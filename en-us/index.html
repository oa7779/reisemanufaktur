Our approach to planning

A good journey begins with attention.

We listen, consider the possibilities, and bring individual wishes together in a coherent journey.

01

What matters to you?

Your travel dates, interests, and preferred pace shape the route—not a long list of stops.

02

How do you like to travel?

The length of each stay, transfers, and accommodations should work together, leaving time where it matters.

03

What can you expect?

Services, availability, and prices are checked for your dates; open questions remain transparent.

Our long-haul journeys are generally planned around total budgets of €5,000 or more per person, including international flights. For shorter trips and smaller budgets, Europa Special Tours offers personally planned getaways.

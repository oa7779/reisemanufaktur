Frequently asked questions

How we plan your short European getaway

Clear expectations before you share your wishes with us.

How do I choose a good base for a short break?

Consider how arrival, accommodation, and activities fit together. On a short trip, short transfers and a conveniently located hotel can leave more time to relax. Check actual travel times for your dates.

When should I plan for events such as Rhine in Flames?

Event dates can change each year, and nearby accommodation may be in high demand. Check the official date for your chosen year, then coordinate transport and lodging.

Are wellness and spa services automatically included?

No. Treatments, opening hours, medical services, and included amenities vary by hotel and offer. The written confirmation for your specific dates defines what is included.

Can I combine relaxation and day trips in four days?

It may work when arrival and excursions do not take too much time. A flexible plan leaves room to rest and considers opening hours, travel time, and days off.

How does a getaway with one home base work?

We find a suitable destination and a hotel to use as your base. If you like, you can start by relaxing there, then take outings into the surrounding area. We tailor travel distances, your hotel, and your daily pace to the time you have available.

Can I combine two days of wellness with two days of exploring?

Yes. This flexible pace is one possible travel idea, not a fixed program. We plan the length of your stay, activities, breaks, and travel arrangements around your wishes and confirmed availability.

What interests can you accommodate?

For example, wellness and spa retreats, cities, history, wine, food, spirits, nature, local traditions, wine festivals, and events such as Rhine in Flames. We check seasonal dates, hotel options, and services for your specific travel period.

What does the travel budget include?

The form asks for your total budget per person, including travel to and from your destination. This is a planning guideline, not a confirmed trip price. You will receive a specific offer only after suitable dates, accommodations, and services have been checked.

Do you arrange child care or health spa services?

We can check family-friendly accommodations and existing services. Child care, treatments, and health services are not automatically available or included in a trip. Hotel terms, children’s ages, dates, and explicit confirmation determine what can be offered.

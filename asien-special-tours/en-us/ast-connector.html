Intelligence with travel context

Understand the journey. Not just the question.

“What matters now?” means something different before departure than after landing. The AI-driven approach behind Connector+ starts with your situation: travel phase, destination region, local time, and the information you have chosen to share provide the context for relevant guidance.

The technical foundation combines context analysis, travel-phase control, and preference logic. The resulting assistance concept prioritizes information rather than giving every feature equal prominence. Your next travel stage, a question about the hotel, or a personal request can then be related meaningfully to your itinerary.

AI supports interpretation and preparation. It replaces neither your advisor's experience nor a confirmed reservation. Binding information, bookings, and changes remain tied to confirmed data and responsible contacts. A useful answer must make sense in the context of your trip, not merely sound convincing in isolation.

Example assistance conversation

“I have just landed. What happens next?”A context-aware answer would bring together the confirmed arrival and transfer arrangements and highlight the responsible contact. If current data is missing, a follow-up question is needed—not an invented pickup time.

Illustrative use case, not a live query you can run on this page.

A connected travel cockpit

Six feature worlds. One personal journey.

Connector+ brings the digital tasks surrounding your trip together. Its modules share the same purpose: offer orientation, make information accessible, and keep a direct route to personal support open.

01 / FLIGHT

Flights & airports

From preparation to the return flight, flight context and travel phase bring relevant topics together. Instead of switching between documents and separate views, orientation starts with your current stage of the journey.

  • Check-in, baggage, and boarding overview
  • Terminal, gate, and airport context
  • Flight cockpit with route and map view

Current gate changes, aircraft positions, and boarding times require available airline, airport, or flight-data sources. The display does not replace announcements at the airport.

02 / JOURNEY

Itinerary, hotel & transfer

An itinerary is about transitions as well as places. The daily programme, arrival, transfer, and hotel belong in a shared flow: what comes next, which information helps, and who is responsible?

  • Travel stages and destination time
  • Hotel information and contact options
  • Meeting points and transfer context

Driver, room, and reservation details apply only when confirmed for your journey. Showing a service is not the same as booking it automatically.

03 / CONNECTIVITY

eSIM & connection

Staying connected on the road is part of preparing for travel. The connectivity area brings connection status, eSIM activation, and suitable installation paths together in a dedicated feature world.

  • eSIM status and activation context
  • QR and installation paths
  • Network status and connection options

Plans, coverage, device compatibility, and provisioning depend on the provider. Mobile, satellite, and eSIM services are not automatically included.

04 / TRAVEL WALLET

Documents & preparation

Tickets, travel documents, and checklists should be accessible where they are needed. Document and media views create a central starting point for preparing for your personal journey.

  • Document previews and travel papers
  • Pre-departure checklists
  • Country, visa, and entry context

We clarify before departure which content is stored locally and available offline. Entry requirements must be checked with the relevant authorities.

05 / PRIVATE SERVICE

AST Privé & concierge

On the personal service level, the focus is your next travel moment rather than the next menu option. The Privé approach brings concierge requests and individually agreed services into one overview.

  • Personal concierge entry point
  • Hotel, dining, and chauffeur context
  • Your AST team and local partner contacts

Premium access, lounges, airport assistance, and reservations require explicit confirmation. They are neither available for every journey nor automatically included.

06 / TRAVEL STORY

Memories & travel journal

Your trip deserves more than an unsorted photo folder. A journal, personal notes, and Travel Story put your moments into a timeline—from first impressions to your favourite place.

  • Photos, notes, and travel events
  • Personal timeline and story views
  • Sharing through supported system features

Local storage is not an automatic cloud backup. Available backup and sharing options are explained separately; you decide what to share.

Spatial assistance · AR · GPS

Your surroundings become information.

Digital orientation does not end at a list of sights. The AR travel guide combines the idea of a camera view with location, viewing direction, and local information. Places and information can be related spatially rather than simply displayed as search results.

Explorer tools complement this approach: a compass, GPS position, altitude, and movement create a technical picture of your surroundings. Quality depends on sensors, reception, and permissions. Inside buildings, GPS alone is not a reliable navigation system.

The documented premium concept ranges from points of interest to a planetarium view based on location and time. Indoor routing, native AR features, and individual additional modules depend on the platform and data and are not universally promised.

The distinction matters: an on-screen hint is an orientation aid, not safety clearance. Local rules, signs, and your guide take priority. A device can help you explore, but it should not encourage you to ignore your surroundings.

The connection to AST expertise

ARIA: the knowledge and service integration vision.

The particular strength lies in combining intelligent assistance with human travel expertise. The planned ARIA/AI-CRM connection is intended to provide approved knowledge and customer context—not create another isolated chat experience.

  1. Capture context

    The starting point is your travel phase, the preferences you have chosen to share, and your specific question. The focus is your current need rather than a blanket recommendation for every traveller.

  2. Put knowledge in context

    An approved ARIA connection is intended to make suitable AST content and confirmed travel information available to assistance. Without that connection, no access to your customer account is claimed.

  3. Connect people

    A request should reach the right contact: your advisor, local agency, hotel, or concierge. Availability and binding services continue to be confirmed by responsible people rather than inferred from a conversation.

  4. Make service traceable

    As a future extension, questions, interests, requests, and the subsequent advice process could become traceable in the customer account. This requires an approved CRM connection and clear data-protection rules.

The ARIA/CRM connection is described here as an integration vision. This does not promise that knowledge retrieval, automatic conversation storage, bookings, or order creation are already running.

Technology that can be explained

Modular by design. Clear about data.

Connector+ is based on a Flutter/Dart application with separate interfaces, controllers, service contracts, and adapters. This structure separates the presentation of a feature from the source supplying its information.

Context, not feature overload

Travel-phase detection and context analysis help arrange views for the situation. Different information matters before a flight than during a transfer or at the hotel. Preferences are intended to refine that selection, not quietly make binding decisions for you.

Device, API & service

GPS and compass provide device signals. Mapping, flight, and partner services provide external information. The AI layer interprets context; your AST team is responsible for personal advice. Adapters connect these layers without implying that every source is equally up to date.

Plan consciously for offline use

Local documents and caching concepts reduce dependence on a connection. This does not mean all content works offline. Live queries, synchronization, and cloud-based assistance require the relevant services and connectivity. We clarify the practical scope before departure.

Permissions with a purpose

Location, camera access, and notifications serve different tasks. The permissions needed by a module and the way data is processed must be explained clearly when you gain access. Not every feature needs the same permissions.

Guidance is not confirmation

AI output can be incomplete or wrong. For flight changes, entry requirements, health, and safety, current authoritative sources count. Reservations, prices, and service commitments need separate confirmation rather than an answer that merely sounds plausible.

Personal access

Discuss supported devices, activation, available modules, and possible additional services with your AST team. This website offers neither a general app-store download nor a promise that every described data source is connected for every journey.

Good to know

Your questions about intelligent travel assistance.

What makes Connector+ an AI-driven travel app?

The product approach combines AI assistance with travel phase, context analysis, and preferences you have chosen to share. Guidance is intended to fit the situation rather than provide only generic answers. The available AI scope depends on the data sources and integrations approved for your journey.

Is ARIA already connected to my customer account?

This page does not promise a production connection to your customer account. ARIA and the AI CRM are described as an integration vision. Knowledge access and conversation documentation require an approved technical connection and defined data-processing arrangements.

Do AI, maps, and documents also work without internet?

That depends on the module. Documents provided locally may be usable independently of live services. Current flight data, external map content, and cloud-based AI need the appropriate connection. Your AST team clarifies what you should prepare before departure.

Are eSIM, concierge, and premium services included?

Not automatically. Access, device compatibility, plans, partner services, and premium scope are discussed personally for your journey. Explicit confirmation is what counts, not the presentation of a module on this website.

Can the app book independently or give binding travel advice?

Automatic bookings or order creation are not promised. AI guidance complements personal advice; binding services must be confirmed. For entry requirements, safety, and health, the relevant authorities and qualified professionals are authoritative.

General travel conditions

The following conditions serve to clearly present and regulate the legal relationships between the customer and AST even after the entry into force of the recasting of travel law, in particular §§ 651 a ff BGB, taking into account the statutory provisions. All cited paragraphs refer to the version of the BGB valid from 1.7.2018.

A right of withdrawal pursuant to §§ 312 ff. Otherwise, the statutory withdrawal and termination regulations apply, see e.g. Paragraphs 5 to 7 of these conditions.

The information on the out-of-court dispute settlement procedure can be found in clause 13.2. of these terms and conditions.

2. Executing air carrier

EU Regulation No 2111 of 14.12.2005 requires tour operators, travel agents and carriers of transport contracts to inform their customers about the identity of each operating airline before the relevant flight is carried out as soon as it is established. Insofar as this is not yet the case at the time of booking, the likely operating airline must first be indicated. In the event of change of the operating airline after booking, the customer shall be informed immediately.

3. Booking a trip from AST

1. A contract is concluded according to the statutory regulation only at the time when completely identical declarations of intent of the parties (offer and acceptance of this offer) are available, whereby acceptance must have taken place in good time. Only statements of interest on both sides (booking request or travel proposal) are not yet an offer, but are merely invitations to submit offers. An offer can be limited in time, otherwise it can only be accepted within the usual period of time, which is 10 days without any special circumstances. A late acceptance represents a new offer, so that the roles can change when submitting the contract declarations. After your booking request, we usually prepare a travel proposal first, which, in consultation with you, ultimately leads to an individual offer in text form under determination of an acceptance period. With your timely acceptance of our offer, which can take place in text or form-free, the travel contract is concluded.

2. Changing or supplementary agreements to the services described and to the travel conditions require an express agreement with AST. It should be made in writing for reasons of evidence. Travel agents shall not be authorised to make any undertaking derogating from the content of the travel description or the travel conditions, or to make any amending or supplementary arrangements.

4. Payment

1. All payments on the travel price, i.e. also the advance payment, are to be made only against delivery of the security note in accordance with § 651 r BGB. You will receive this security note with the travel confirmation/invoice. Upon receipt of the security note, a deposit of 20% of the travel price is due.

2. The balance must be paid no later than 28 days before departure. In the case of contracts concluded later than 29 days before departure, the total travel price is immediately due upon handing over the security note. The travel documents are sent after payment, alternatively the documents can be collected at or after payment.

5. Resignation by the customer/rebooking/substitute

5.1 If there are unavoidable and exceptional circumstances at or near the destination of the trip which significantly affect the performance of the journey or the transport of persons to the destination, you may withdraw from the contract free of charge before the start of the trip. Inevitable and exceptional circumstances exist if they are not subject to the control of the Contracting Party, which refers to them and their consequences could not have been avoided by all reasonable precautions (§ 651 h para. 3 BGB). Such a right to withdraw free of charge also exists under the conditions set out in paragraph 7.4 (price increase above 8%) or in the event of a significant change in an essential part of the travel service.

5.2 Apart from the cases dealt with in point 5.1, you may withdraw at any time before the start of your trip. However, AST then has the statutory right to adequate compensation (§ 651 h para. 1 and 2 BGB), the amount of compensation required must be justified on your request. Since, depending on the booked services of the trip, high compensation amounts may arise in individual cases, we point out in this context to paragraph 8 of these conditions.

5.3 In all cases of withdrawal, AST loses the right to the agreed travel price and must refund any amounts already paid immediately.

5.4 The customer is generally not entitled to change after conclusion of the contract. Only rebooking requests of the customer without legal claim, if their execution is at all possible, are made only as cancellation by the travel participant under the conditions of section 5.2 with simultaneous registration.

5.5 Within a reasonable period of time, but usually no later than seven days before the start of your trip, you can use a permanent data carrier (e.g. letter, e-mail, fax) to demand that a third party named by you enters into the rights and obligations arising from the travel contract. AST may object to entry if the third party does not meet the contractual travel requirements. In the event of entry, original and new participants will be jointly liable as a total debtor for the travel price and the additional costs incurred by the entry. The original travel participant shall be provided with proof as to the amount of additional costs incurred by the entry of the third party. Additional costs may only be required to an appropriate extent and must have actually arisen as an organiser.

10. Rights and obligations in the event of defects

10.1 A lack of travel must be reported immediately (see also 10.5). Remedial requests and notification of defects are to be addressed to our local agency/tour guide (name and address can be found in the travel documents). Where possible and reasonable, they shall be addressed to AST.

10.2 If the journey is not performed in accordance with the contract, the customer may request a remedy from AST. AST may refuse the remedy if it is impossible or involves disproportionate effort, taking into account the extent of the lack and value of the travel service concerned.

10.3 If AST does not provide the requested remedy within a reasonable period specified by the customer without being entitled to do so in accordance with clause 10.2., it may itself provide remedy and demand compensation for necessary expenses. The time limit is unnecessary if AST refuses remedy or needs immediate remedy.

10.4 However, if AST is entitled to refuse the remedial action under point 10.2, the lack of travel concerns a significant part of the travel services, AST must provide adequate replacement services. If this does not achieve an equivalent quality of the trip, AST shall grant a reasonable reduction in the travel price (reduction) according to clause 10.5.. If the replacement services are not comparable to those originally owed or if the reduction offered is not appropriate, the customer may reject the replacement service. In this case or if AST is unable to offer replacement services, the further legal consequences are also directed without notice (point 10.6.) according to § 651 l para. 2 and 3 BGB.

10.5 For the duration of a non-contractual travel service, the customer may: in so far as the remedy has not been thwarted by a culpable omission of the notice of defect, claim a right to a reduction in the travel price (reduction). Restrictions on the legal consequences of a defect in cases of unavoidable exceptional circumstances arise from § § 651 k para. 4 u.5.

10.6 If the journey is significantly affected by a defect, the customer may terminate the travel contract. Before that, it must set a reasonable time limit for remedying the situation. The time limit is unnecessary if AST refuses to remedy or the immediate remedy is necessary.

If the contract has been terminated, AST shall be obliged to take the necessary measures as a result of the termination of the contract, in particular if the contract includes transport, to ensure the return transport without delay; the means of transport used for this purpose must be equivalent to those agreed in the contract. The additional costs for the return transport are borne by AST. However, we retain the right to the (shared) travel price, claims for reduction, compensation and reimbursement of futile expenses in respect of travel services already provided and which are still necessary after termination (for restrictions on claims for damages and reimbursement of expenses see clause 10.5 sentence two). With regard to travel services no longer to be provided, AST's entitlement to the agreed travel price shall be waived; in so far as payments already made must be reimbursed.

However, they are not authorised or authorised to accept or accept claims for reduction or damages with effect against AST.

12. Limitations of liability

1. The contractual liability of AST towards the travel participant for damages that are not bodily damage is limited to the three-fold travel price, unless a damage has been caused culpably by AST or its service providers.


2. Insofar as AST is not based on intent or gross negligence, the liability towards the travel participant for damages due to tort shall be limited to three times the price of the journey for damages that are not bodily damage; however, the amount of liability shall be at least € 4.100,00.

  • Travelers will receive all essential information about the package before concluding the package travel contract.
  • At least one entrepreneur is always liable for the proper provision of all travel services included in the contract.
  • Travelers will receive an emergency telephone number or information about a contact point through which they can get in touch with the tour operator or travel agency.
  • Travelers may transfer the package to another person within a reasonable period of time and possibly at additional costs.
  • The price of the package may only be increased if certain costs (e.g. fuel prices) increase and if this is expressly provided for in the contract, and in any case not later than 20 days before the start of the package. If the price increase exceeds 8% of the package travel price, the traveler may withdraw from the contract. If a tour operator reserves the right to raise prices, the traveler has the right to reduce prices if the corresponding costs are reduced.
  • Travelers may withdraw from the contract without paying a withdrawal fee and receive a full refund of all payments if one of the essential elements of the package, with the exception of the price, is significantly changed. If the contractor responsible for the package cancels the package before the start of the package, passengers are entitled to reimbursement and, in some cases, compensation.
  • Passengers may withdraw from the contract in exceptional circumstances prior to the start of the package without paying a withdrawal fee, for example if there are serious safety problems at the place of destination which are likely to affect the package.

  • In the event of insolvency of the tour operator or, in some Member States, of the travel agent, payments shall be refunded.

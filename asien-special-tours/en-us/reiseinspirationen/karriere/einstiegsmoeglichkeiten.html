Your entry opportunities at Asia Special Tours

In times of digitalization, disruptive change and shortage of skilled workers, we are very interested in investing in our own support for young talent as well as enabling experienced professionals to perform challenging tasks. Only together will we be able to meet the challenges of the future.

All our entry opportunities for pupils, students and professionals are described below. We are looking forward to your application.

PUPILS

We offer the following basic training courses for students. The training courses consist of practical training, which is carried out in our main headquarters in Munich in the respective departments and the theoretical training takes place in the corresponding vocational school.

If we have free seats, we write them out through our job portal. Please check regularly to see if we are looking for trainees.

- Training as a tourism clerk and businessman
- Training as a saleswoman and marketing communications manager

STUDENTS

Below you will find our offers for students. The possibilities are both for our main headquarters in Munich and for our Fililale in Berlin. Current positions are advertised on our job portal.

- Internship in Sales
- Internship in marketing
- Business studies Marketing or Sales
- Dual studies in tourism management

VOCATIONAL INSTITUTES

We are always open to new ideas and fresh wind. Free spirits and visionaries shape the future of our world, which is why we welcome new professionals. Please inform yourself regularly about our job portal according to currently advertised posts.

- Sales assistant
- Marketing Assistant

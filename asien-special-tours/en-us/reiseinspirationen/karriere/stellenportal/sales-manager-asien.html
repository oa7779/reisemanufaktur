Sales & Product Manager (m/w/d) for individual Asia trips

at the earliest possible date of entry, unlimited, in full time at the Munich site

If Asia makes your heart beat faster, you are a sales and organizational talent and you can infect others with your enthusiasm, we absolutely need you in our team! 
With high-quality and individually designed tours, we at Asia Special Tours ensure that our customers can explore this breathtaking continent with all its facets. We value personal service at the highest level.  

We are looking for reinforcements – join us as Sales & Product Manager for individual Asia trips.  

What tasks do you expect?

  • Through knowledge, commitment and passion, you inspire demanding customers sustainably for our high-quality and exclusive dream trips 
  • They accompany demanding customers as contact persons from the first phone call until after returning from the trip. 
  • In cooperation with our local partners, you can create exclusive trips according to the individual wishes of our customers – each trip is an absolute unique one!

What do you get from us?

  • Lots of responsibility with own room for design and independent work
  • Remuneration according to performance and an indefinite employment contract
  • 36-hour week 
  • 6 weeks leave per year 
  • Fam-Trips & Grant for company fitness with EGYM Wellpass  
  • With us you get the opportunity to develop further through a multifaceted activity – we deal with exciting dream trips every day!

What do we want from you?

  • Completed studies or vocational training in the tourism sector 
  • They have professional experience in sales, ideally in the field of premium & luxury travel  
  • You are a passionate service provider: by means of communication strength, professional appearance and honest interest in the wishes of your customers, you are not only a salesman but also a trusted person. 
  • They have a passion for Asia, already bring travel experience and can also get involved in new destinations and sell them  
  • High service orientation distinguishes you 
  • Very good knowledge of German with sovereign written language & reliable English skills  
  • You want to grow beyond yourself: clear objectives, an independent working method and willingness to perform are the key to your success

If you find yourself in the task, we look forward to your meaningful application stating your earliest start date as well as your salary presentation to Mrs. Jana Karcher: passion@~@asienteam.de

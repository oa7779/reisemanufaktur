PONANT – French elegance meets the spirit of discovery

For whom is PONANT the right choice?

PONANT combines luxurious cruises with French elegance and a pleasant personal atmosphere. If you value stylish travel, while attaching great importance to excellent gastronomy, sophisticated design and exceptional routes, PONANT finds one of the most exclusive ways to discover the world from the water. 

The special extra: with PONANT you will discover not only Asia, but the whole world. Thanks to our sister companies Africa Royal Tours and América Special Tours we can combine your cruise with individual land programs on several continents – according to your interests.

What's special about PONANT?

French way of life on board

PONANT is the only French luxury shipping company – and that's exactly what characterizes the experience on board. The cuisine is based on the French gourmet tradition, the wine list is compiled with great care and the ambience also bears a distinctive French signature. Elegance seems natural here and never put on.

Diverse country trips

Carefully compiled excursion programs offer you authentic insights into the culture, nature and way of life of the visited regions. Whether cultural highlights or quiet moments of nature – the selection is tailored to different interests and an upscale travel experience.

Yacht atmosphere

Even on larger ships in the fleet, PONANT offers a travel experience that is more reminiscent of a private yacht than of a classic cruise ship. With a manageable number of guests, spacious outdoor decks and short paths, a personal atmosphere is created in which peace and relaxation are the focus. So you can enjoy the advantages of a luxury cruise without having to do without the exclusivity of small ships.

The ships of PONANT

PONANT stands for small, elegant ships that combine a personal atmosphere with exceptional travel routes. The fleet includes different types of ships, from stylish cruise ships to luxurious icebreakers. Thus each ship offers its own character and opens up special possibilities to discover the world.

The PONANT Sisterships

The PONANT Sisterships form the classic foundation of the fleet. With its manageable size, stylish design and a relaxed atmosphere on board, they are ideal for trips where the ship is just as much an experience as the destination. Short distances, spacious outdoor areas and personal care characterize the feeling of travel. Two ships, among others in Asia, are the Le Soléal and L-Austral. 

A total of four ships belong to the PONANT Sisterships, which change their routes depending on season and season: Le Boréal, L'Australial, Le Soléal and Le Lyrial.

The PONANT Explorers

The six ships of the PONANT Explorers were designed especially for cruises into the tropical terrain. Their concept combines the equipment of a high-quality yacht with the possibilities of an expedition ship. In addition to comfortable areas on board, the focus is above all on the intensive encounter with nature. A special feature is the underwater lounge Blue Eye. Two examples of this category are the Le Jacques Cartier, which takes you to the South Seas, and the Le Lapérouse, which often travels in the Caribbean. 

A total of six ships belong to the category of PONANT Explorers, which also change their routes depending on season and season: Le Lapérouse, Le Champlain, Le Bougainville, Le Dumont d'Urville, Le Bellot and Le Jacques Cartier.

The unique items: Le Commandant Charcot, Le Ponant & Paul Gauguin

In addition to the PONANT Sisterships and the series of PONANT Explorers, three very special pieces belong to the PONANT fleet.

With the Le Commandant Charcot PONANT has created a ship specially designed for the extreme conditions of the polar regions. The luxury icebreaker combines modern expedition equipment, comfortable ambience and high ice class. This combination makes it possible to travel to remote regions of the Arctic and Antarctica. On board, intense expedition experiences meet the comfort typical of PONANT.

The Le Ponant stands for a particularly original form of traveling on the water. The elegant three-master combines the atmosphere of a private sailing yacht with the comfort and service of PONANT. Sailing creates trips with a special feeling of proximity to the sea. Depending on the season and season, she sets sail in the Caribbean - our sister company América Special Tours GmbH will be happy to advise you on an individual country program before or after the cruise.

The Paul Gauguin is specialized in travel in the South Pacific and was designed for special conditions of Polynesian island world. The low draught allows the start of smaller ports and sheltered bays. The ship offers a relaxed setting to get to know the islands, lagoons and cultures of the South Sea in a special way.

Discover PONANT worldwide

Which region and which ship best fits your ideas depends on your personal interests and the respective itinerary. We will be happy to advise you when choosing and combine your cruise with an individually planned pre- or post-program if desired.

  • Asia & Southeast Asia
    Japan, Vietnam, Indonesia, Malaysia, the Philippines and Thailand are among PONANT's many destinations in Asia. The routes combine fascinating cultures and historic cities with tropical islands, nature parks and remote coasts – for example in Halong Bay, Bali or Komodo National Park.
  • Oceania & South Sea
    In Oceania the journeys lead among others to Australia, New Zealand and the island worlds of the Pacific. French Polynesia, Fiji, Vanuatu or the Kimberley region of Australia show very different facets of this extraordinary world region.
  • America & Caribbean
    From the tropical islands of the Caribbean to Central America to South America and Canada, PONANT offers many special routes. Small ports, remote coasts and intense nature experiences combine with destinations such as the Grenadines, Costa Rica, Panama or the coasts of North and South America.
  • Polar regions & expeditions
    For extraordinary expeditions we go to the Arctic and Antarctica. Greenland, Spitsbergen, Iceland and the Northwest Passage are on the program as well as the Antarctic Peninsula, South Georgia and the Falkland Islands.

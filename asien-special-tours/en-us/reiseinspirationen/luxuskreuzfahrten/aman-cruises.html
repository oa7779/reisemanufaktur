Aman Cruises

On the yachts of Aman Cruises you will experience unique luxury cruises through the island world of Raja Ampat, the Komodo Islands and the Spice Islands.

Exclusive luxury cruises with Aman Cruises

Between heaven and earth – surrounded by the glittering sea and absolute peace! Let yourself be taken aboard the unique Aman ships into another world and visit the paradise island world of Indonesia far away from any hustle and bustle. Immerse yourself in dreamlike coral gardens and swim with the dazzling swarms of fish. Look forward to excellent service and an exquisite culinary experience. The two yachts have three or five cabins and guarantee absolute privacy and seclusion. A state-of-the-art diving equipment allows you to enjoy breathtaking dives in the colorful underwater world of Indonesia. You also have the opportunity to obtain a PADI certification. Experienced guides accompany you on land and can organize hiking and kayaking expeditions for you. Stroll through the island world of Raja Ampat, look out for the Komodo Warans in Komodo National Park or discover Spice Islands on the legendary spice trade route. You can decide: travel on fixed dates with other travelers or charter the two ships entirely exclusively for your family or friends on a special occasion or just to enjoy your life with all your senses.

You want to stay longer? Then extend your break in one of the legendary Aman hotels and discover the cultural diversity of Indonesia.

Amandira

When the sails are set, the yacht increases speed and you feel the fresh wind in your face and the feeling of unlimited freedom. Welcome aboard the Amandira! The two-master sailing ship with a length of 52 meters from the bow to the stern, was handcrafted by the Konjo tribe. A traditional Phinisi ship awaits you, combining the ancient romance of the spice trade with the most modern amenities. Exemplary comfort and privacy are waiting for you! Up to 10 people can find space on the Amandira. Three spacious cabins have king-size beds with living area and a bathroom. Two cabins have bunk beds. The 13-person crew consists of a private chef, waiters, a diving instructor and a spa therapist, who will tell you every wish from the lips.

Exclusive luxury cruises with Heritage Line

Decelerate, breathe the peace deeply and dive into your very personal dream world. On a cruise with Heritage Line you will experience Southeast Asia from its most beautiful side. Stroll through the fascinating Halong Bay with the impressive Karstste information and emerald green water – and you're in the middle of it. Discover the many facets of Vietnam and Cambodia from the water in the Mekong Delta and draw new life energy. Always enjoy the luxury on board and feel the magic that surrounds you.

Luxury ship Ginger

Halong Bay, Vietnam

The luxury ship Ginger travels in Lan Ha Bay, in a quiet part of the famous Halong Bay. The first class ship houses a total of 12 spacious suites, all flooded with light and providing undisturbed privacy. Enjoy a romantic breakfast on your private balcony, while the bizarre island world passes in front of your eyes. Warm wooden floors and marble bathrooms, as well as a noble design that combines the traditions of Northern Vietnam with contemporary accents, testify to exclusive elegance. An almost 360-degree light-flooded restaurant with show kitchen is perfectly framed by an outdoor alfresco area. An indoor bar invites you to relax before or after dinner. On the terrace deck is the swimming pool surrounded by comfortable shaded sunbeds. Another highlight of the ship is the large spa area with a steam bath. 

Luxury ship Ylang

Halong Bay, Vietnam

Let yourself be enchanted by the natural beauty of Lan Ha Bay aboard the luxury ship Ylang. An unforgettable cruise on an exceptional ship, with exclusive wellness and lifestyle offers as well as first-class service awaits you. The high-quality equipment in Asian design with French-colonial accents invites you to feel good. Each of the 10 cabins has large panoramic windows, a separate living area and a private balcony. The design of each suite is dedicated to a particular flower and it is equipped with state-of-the-art technology as well as handmade paintings. Start the day relaxed with Tai Chi exercises on the sun deck, paddle a kayak between the impressive karst information or take a hike through Cat Ba National Park. Enjoy the luxurious wellness offer and the first-class Vietnamese cuisine with delicious ingredients from the region. The culinary creations spoil every demanding palate. These days will certainly remain in your memory for a long time.

Luxury ship Violet

Bai Tu Long Bay, Vietnam

Welcome aboard the Violet, one of the youngest gems on the Heritage Line. This exceptional French-Indochinese Art Déco ship is the only one of its kind to sail Bai Tu Long Bay in the Gulf of Tonkin. With 6 spacious, individually designed suites, Italian marble bathrooms, Jacuzzi and spacious balconies, Violet is the ideal place for stylish, romantic river cruises. A spa area, daily Tai Chi, a steam bath and concierge service are available on board. Enjoy culinary highlights on your balcony, in the restaurant with à la carte menus and cooking classes, or relax with cocktails on the deck. Violet offers you a luxurious experience in one of the most unspoilt treasures of the Gulf of Tonkin – Bai Tu Long Bay, with its dramatic limestone formations and calm waters.

Luxury ship The Jahan

Mekong, Indochina

Look out for romantics! The enchanting luxury ship The Jahan takes you on a colonial journey through time. The ship was built in British-Indian colonial style and has a very tasteful interior. Enjoy your high tea quietly while you stroll along the majestic Mekong. The 26 cabins all have a private balcony and a high-quality interior. Amenities on board include the exclusive Viceroy Dining Hall restaurant, the East India Club bar, Apsara Spa spa, and the pool deck with swimming pool and sun loungers. Immerse yourself in the exotic world of Indochina and discover the hidden treasures of Mekong, the longest river in Southeast Asia. 

Luxury ship Jayavarman

Mekong, Indochina

Feel, taste, grasp happiness with your hands - live the new spirit of travel. Deceleration and romantic moments - that's exactly what is waiting for you on board. The magnificent luxury ship Jayavarman takes you on a very special journey. The cruise ship combines a French interior with a tasteful interior of Indochina. All 27 cabins have stylish facilities and a private balcony. On-board amenities include the exclusive restaurant, Henry Mouhot Lounge, Club 1930 Bar, Apsara Spa and a hot tub on the sun terrace. Experience the closeness to nature and enjoy every moment that can be costed while strolling along the majestic Mekong. Let yourself be enchanted by the exotic world of Indochina and discover the hidden treasures of the majestic Mekong, the longest river in Southeast Asia.

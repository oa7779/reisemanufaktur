Exclusive luxury cruises with the Scenic Ecplise

Once in a lifetime. The ultraluxurious Scenic Eclipse with its 6 stars raises the level of previous cruises to a completely new level. A ship's own helicopter and a private submarine guarantee you an unforgettable journey with moments for eternity. Thus, you are guaranteed the highest level of luxury even in the most remote regions of this planet. Dive through the impressive depths of the seas, enjoy unique views over Antarctica from a bird's eye view or the gigantic pampering program on board – there are no wishes left unfulfilled. The luxury yacht is the first discovery yacht in the world.

With a length of 168 meters and a width of 22 meters, a total of 228 guests can board (200 on polar voyages). The luxurious suites convince with elegant design and have a butler service from the standard suite.

Among the incomparable highlights of the Scenic Eclipse are the exclusive service with an almost 1:1 guest crew rate, an extensive excursion offer with expedition parkas and polar shoes and the extensive all-inclusive services. You can experience an all-round visualization in the spa, gym, yoga and Pilates as well as in indoor and outdoor pools. On exclusive excursions with Zodiacs and kayaks, your unbraked spirit of discovery is at its best!

Scenic Neptune

An absolute wow moment is a dive with the submarine Scenic Neptune. The fascinating underwater world in the Caribbean, the numerous shipwrecks in the Mediterranean Sea and the fascinating ice of Antarctica – live in your own world. Up to 6 people can be accommodated in the submarine. With outdoor lighting, the most dazzling colors shine in full splendour and the swivelling platform allows you 360 degrees all-round view (submarine at extra cost, subject to regulatory approval)

All Inclusive

A first-class all inclusive concept awaits you. Culinary top gastronomy with exquisite drinks, a butler service, drinking and service money on board and on land, 550 m2 spa oasis with gym, yoga and Pilates courses as well as airport transfers and hotel overnight stays in advance/after-program.

SeaDream Yacht Club

Exclusive luxury cruises with SeaDream Yacht Club

Mediterranean & Caribbean

"It's yachting, not cruising! Welcome on board the elegant SeaDream I & II. Look forward to a personal and courteous service at the highest level: a team of 95 crew members is available to a maximum of 112 guests around the clock. Enjoy the relaxed and international atmosphere on board. An excellent cuisine and an extensive inclusive offer leave nothing to be desired. In the warm summer months, the twin yachts sail SeaDream I&II with a length of 105 meters small ports and picturesque coves in the Mediterranean. During the cold winter months, the two yachts set course for ports and smaller, unknown islands in the Caribbean, the West Indian Islands. The transatlantic voyages from Europe to the Caribbean and back in spring are particularly spectacular.  

The two yachts have a total of 56 cabins, all with sea views. Culinary treats are "a la minute" freshly prepared for you. You can freely decide whether you want to dine on deck or in your cabin. Choose the finest wines from the SeaDream wine cellar.

SeaDream is the only cruise company to be a certified member of the Thai Spa Association and will spoil you with European and Asian treatments at SeaDream Spa. Let yourself be pampered from head to toe and enjoy the here and now.

Relax your soul and let the thought run wild – you can sleep under the stars in Balinese day beds on deck. Only you and above you the charming star tent.

Get active! Take a bike tour with the captain or stroll through a market with the chef. This is not a dream. This isrea life – this is SeaDream.

With SeaDream to the hidden ports of the Mediterranean

You think a cruise through the Mediterranean is nothing special and always the same? Then let yourself be convinced of the opposite on a trip with the SeaDream Yacht Club. We will take you on new routes to the hidden ports of the Mediterranean, such as: Ikaria in the Aegean, the Croatian island of Vis or Neum on the coast of Bosnia and Herzegovina. Experience moments for eternity – far away from the tourist streams.

Pure Caribbean - West Indian Islands

Do you want to escape the cold of winter and long for warm sun rays on your skin and soft sand between your toes? Then stab with SeaDream Yacht Club in the Caribbean at sea. In addition to turquoise dream bays and hotspots such as St. Barths, you will also discover unknown corners where the huge cruise ships do not operate, such as Tobago Cays on the Grenadines, Sandy Ground on Anguilla or Grande Anse d'Arlet.

Do you want to experience the comfort of the two luxury yachts of the SeaDream Yacht Club for yourself? Then we look forward to your call at the 089 127 091 0 or your inquiry by form!

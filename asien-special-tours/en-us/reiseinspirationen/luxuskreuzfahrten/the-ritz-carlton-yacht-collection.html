The Ritz-Carlton Yacht Collection

On the yachts of The Ritz-Carlton Yacht Collection you will experience unique luxury cruises in the Caribbean, the Mediterranean Sea, Northern Europe, North America or on an Atlantic crossing.

Exclusive luxury cruises with The Ritz-Carlton Yacht Collection

Feel the world! Collect moments of happiness for the soul and expand your consciousness during your luxury cruise on board The Ritz-Carlton Yacht Collection. The three super yachts sail in 2020 for the first time and promise you an unforgettable yacht experience based on the exclusive and luxurious hotel level. The innovative concept convinces with the highest standard of service and comfort and enchants you with a quiet and relaxed atmosphere. Go to the most beautiful places in the world: in the Caribbean, Mediterranean, Northern Europe, North America or on an Atlantic crossing – look forward to inspiring yachting experiences and quiet moments where you can rest deep inside. Only you, the peaceful sound of the sea and the vastness of the horizon.

Lifestyle

The lifestyle on board the The Ritz-Carlton Yacht Collection reflects the existing image of Ritz-Carlton. Luxury, elegance and yet feeling free – the yachts were designed under this aspect. A Rumdum Carefree package awaits you at the highest level - your personal assistant will give you an unforgettable stay. Experience unique landings or relax in the first-class spa on board. Shop on board in exclusive boutiques or enjoy unique feel-good experiences – from the infinity pool to the marina.   

Suites

With 149 suites each in 6 different categories, The Ritz-Carlton Yacht Collection offers the highest level of luxury and modern elegance. All suites have private terraces and specially designed king-size beds, which will enchant you with their special sleep concept in a relaxing sleep. A special highlight are the Owners Suites with their private whirlpools aif the 55m2 terraces. Privacy of the very best and at the same time the feeling of absolute freedom. So that even the smallest wish does not remain unfulfilled, a personal assistant is available in each suite. Enjoy the here and now – we'll take care of the rest.

Culinary experience

In the 5 specialty restaurants on board The Ritz-Carlton Yacht Collection, you can enjoy first-class star cuisine. Whether a-la-carte, fresh seafood from the outdoor grill or 24-hour dining service in your cabin - the excellent star chefs ensure an unforgettable culinary treat. After an eventful day, you can enjoy a refreshing cocktail and listen to live music in the starry night.

The Shore Collection

Tailored travel experiences from The Shore Collection await you. Designed specifically by Thr Ritz-Carlton Yacht Collection, the program includes 5 different themes that are tailored to the interests of guests. Look forward to a lot of insider knowledge and genuine, authentic insights during the landings. Visit a museum outside the opening hours, relax with a yoga session on a private beach or paddle through the rainforest by kayak. Delve deeper, get closer.

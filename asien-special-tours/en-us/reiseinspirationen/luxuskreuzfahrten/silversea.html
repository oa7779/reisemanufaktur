Exclusive luxury cruises with Silversea

Celebrate your life! With over 900 stunning destinations, Silversea's unique luxury ships are heading for secluded ports and remote locations on all 7 continents that will delight all globetrotters. Feeling home far away from home. In a relaxed atmosphere and with a personal butler service, you can feel at home. The butlers are trained according to the highest standards and stand by you around the clock – both on the high seas and in the harbor. The ships of Silversea comprise 50 to 304 suites and stand for intimacy and a relaxed coexistence of guests. Your suite is your private retreat – that's why the Silversea suites are not only spacious, but also very tastefully decorated.

The Silversea cruises are all inclusive cruises: fine wines and spirits, gourmet food, in-suite dining, butler service and all tips are included in the price. Drop and enjoy the sweet nothing-do – we'll take care of the rest!

Fine dining

Your culinary heart will also beat faster with the exquisite creations of world-famous chefs. Enjoy a wide selection of specialty restaurants and varied options to dine. The creativity of the chefs knows no limits. From summer 2020, you will experience a novel culinary concept at Silver Moon: S.A.L.T (Sea And Land Taste). Deep insights into the culinary treasures of the destinations await you.

Destinations

Silversea offers you countless opportunities to travel the 7 continents with over 900 destinations. The small luxury ships can sail narrow waterways to reach the heart of the cities, while other cruise ships have to anchor off the coast.

Body, mind & soul. The versatile themed cruises from Silversea leave nothing to be desired. On culinary trips you will learn from experienced chefs, on the wine tours you will enjoy wine tastings and lectures by famous winemakers and on the opera tours you will experience the most beautiful operas on earth.

For the adventure hungry there are 4 expedition ships that invite you to unique journeys to the most remote places on the planet. Travel to the end of the world and surround yourself with the unique beauties of untouched nature: Russia's Far East, the majestic Galapagos Islands or the enchanting Arctic. Visit the most magical places of our earth on the expeditions and dive into your personal dream world.

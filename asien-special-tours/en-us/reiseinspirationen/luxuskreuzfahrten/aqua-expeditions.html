Exclusive luxury cruises with Aqua Expeditions

Feel freedom, breathe freedom! Feel the power of nature and travel in harmony with yourself. On the unique cruises of Aqua Expeditions you will experience comfort and luxury from the very best. Once you sail by ship on the lifeline of Indochina and let the dreamlike scenery of Mekong affect you. On board a modern yacht, feel the spirit of discovery of past times and experience Indonesia's colorful island world. Or you can dive deep into the Peruvian Amazon and experience the fascination of the lush green rainforest with its incredible wildlife. Whether Indochina, Indonesia or Peru, a luxury cruise with Aqua Expeditions is a unique experience. Break your own boundaries and collect moments for eternity!

Aqua Mekong

Vietnam & Cambodia

Between the floating markets of the Mekong Delta in Vietnam and the colonial ruins of Cambodia, the luxury cruise ship Aqua Mekong offers you international comfort combined with local charm. On board the Aqua Mekong you can expect a feel-good program of the extra class. Equipped with 20 suites spread over two decks, it offers maximum comfort and privacy. Spacious and stylishly furnished, each suite has floor-to-ceiling windows so that you can wake up with a fantastic view. Your culinary heart will also beat faster, because you will be enchanted on board by the exquisite cuisine of star chef David Thompson. 

Aqua Blu

Indonesia

The Aqua Blu is the first and only expedition yacht to travel the paradise island world of Indonesia all year round – with 5-star luxury on board. 15 suites in the style of famous Dutch designer Cor D. Rover await you on the four decks of the luxury cruise ship. What's special: Aqua Blu builds the bridge between contemporary modernity and historical heritage. Originally she crossed the oceans as a British research vessel HMS Beagle. In 2006, she was converted into a private yacht. Restoration and modernization measures in 2019 bring the former research vessel to current world-class standards and allow you to enjoy maximum comfort.

Aria Amazon

Peru

The river cruise ship Aria Amazon was designed specifically for the Peruvian Amazon and the famous Pacaya Samuria National Reserve. Enjoy the fantastic view of the lush rainforest and the mighty Amazon on board the Aria Amazon. There are 16 spacious suites and the best that Peruvian cuisine has to offer. Accompanied by experienced nature guides, you will experience unforgettable experiences in the rainforest of Peru during the excursions. 

Aqua Nera

Peru

The new river cruise ship Aqua Nera will convince you with its distinctive Amazon style. Inspired by the mysterious Blackwater lagoons of the Peruvian Amazon, the ship's 20 suites have been tastefully designed by award-winning architects Noor Design. You'll find dishes from Pedro Miguel Schiaffino, one of Peru's best chefs, known for its refined cuisine made from native Amazonian products. On excursions in small groups and with experienced nature guides at your side, explore the world's richest rainforest.

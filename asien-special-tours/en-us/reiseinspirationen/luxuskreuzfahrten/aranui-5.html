Aranui 5

Luxury cruise on Aranui 5: Discover the beauty of Polynesia on an exclusive cruise on Aranui 5, a converted cargo ship. Enquire now at Asia Special Tours!

Exclusive luxury cruises with the Aranui 5

French Polynesia

"Mana" means the spiritual energy that the gods have distributed over Polynesia. We can't hear you and we can't see you – but we can feel you. Take this energy deep into yourself and discover the enchanting Marquesas islands with the cargo and cruise ship Aranui 5. The ship supplies the inhabitants of the Marquesas archipelago with consumer goods and food. The inhabitants of these remote islands await every arrival on their islands with great longing and welcome you. Become part of this unique Polynesian lifestyle on the journey of your life. Nowhere do you come as close to the way of life of the South Seas as here.

Enjoy the infinite color of the sunset, which is reflected in the ocean when another day ends in the South Pacific. After a three-course dinner, the Aranui band will delight you with the rhythm of Polynesia and teach you to move your hips to the thrilling sounds of Tamure or Tahitian waltz.

During the two-week cruise, which includes both meals and excursions, the modern and comfortable ship will be your home. Discover the magic that has attracted Paul Gauguin, Robert Louis Stevenson, Thor Heyerdahl and Jacques Brel. The cordial crew members who have been on the ship for years will welcome you and familiarize you with their centuries-old customs. Together with the expert and multilingual guest lecturers and guides, you will answer all your questions during your trip through the fascinating island world.

The Aranui 5 was designed to accommodate 254 passengers: 29 spacious suites, 31 Deluxe and Superior Deluxe Staterooms with balcony, three Junior Suites, partly with, partly without balcony and 40 Staterooms, all tastefully and comfortably furnished. The large and family dining room offers excellent French and Polynesian cuisine. The dress code is typically laid-back, no tuxedo, just a fragrant tiare flower behind the ear.

Take a refreshing dip in the pool, exercise your muscles at the gym, relax in the spa or enjoy the lake wind on deck. Time passes quickly, as the guest lecturers tell you about the Marquesas, perfect their dance skills or learn how to braid a hat. In the library you can read how Melville, Stevenson and Heyerdahl described the enticing island world from their own perspective.

The Aranui experience

An adventure trip away from the usual tourist trails. Possibility to immerse yourself in a foreign culture. A way to discover the heart and soul of an old age. This all describes the Aranui experience. As the longest-serving cruise provider in French Polynesia, Aranui Cruises has been making wishes come true for 35 years and shows travelers on the "journey of their lives" the enchanting Marquesas Islands. The ship, the Aranui 5, is special in every respect. The tailor-made cargo and passenger ship, launched in 2015, offers comfort and modern conveniences for a cruise ship, while it is a lifeline for remote areas and supplies small communities away from any civilization. Only 254 passengers will be accommodated on Aranui 5. In this way it can be ensured that the fellow travelers from all over the world can get to know each other in a relaxed setting.

The Aranui Concept

From their first voyage to the present day – and across all generations of Aranui ships – the concept of Aranui Cruises has remained the same: an authentic and traditional offer of Polynesians, from which all of Polynesia benefits. In this sense, almost all employees and crew members are of Polynesian descent; they come from various islands in the five archipelagos. It is a unique experience to be there when they bring their home closer to the guests. You will not experience anything similar on any other ship that crosses our waters. Whether in community facilities, at evening events, during the on-board program or when they help you disembark, you will get to know and embrace our lively crew members and their talents. Some of them are a little shy at first, but in conversation you will quickly find out why the crew is a very important part of the Aranui experience.

Activities

In each port, our guests can choose from a variety of excursions included in the price. For the adventurous, optional activities are also available. For the following excursions, which can be booked on board, there are additional costs:

Diving
Some of the best dive spots in the world are located in the waters around French Polynesia. The three different topographies offer completely different diving experiences. A total of five islands can be dived. While Fakarava, Rangiroa and Hiva Oa are only available for advanced certified divers, Tahuata and Bora Bora also offer beginners their money. Depending on the island and the season, sharks, rays, dolphins, turtles or whales await you – and of course a lot of colorful tropical fish and corals. An adventure playground for all divers!

Glass bottom boat
For those who do not want to dive or snorkel, but still want to see the breathtaking underwater world in the lagoon of Rangiroa, a glass bottom boat is available. With a little luck, a shark or a turtle will also look at you in the middle of the colorful fish and corals!

Roche and shark feeding
In the lagoon of Bora Bora you have the opportunity to get very close to the local sea inhabitants on a typical excursion. So you can snorkel in a coral garden with colorful fish, stand surrounded by playing stingrays in the hip-high water or dive into the azure ocean to meet friendly reef or lemon sharks.

Fishing
Due to their seclusion in the South Pacific, you will find a lush and varied underwater world around the Marquesas. For this reason, the Aranui 5 with the Mokai Nui 2 has a fishing boat for four people on board, with which you can book excursions from all ports with experienced crew members. Tuna, Marlin, Mahi Mahi – these are just some of the fish they can fish. Experience how exciting it is to catch your own dinner, because our chef will prepare your catch for you and your friends in the most delicious way.

Horse riding
On Ua Huka live more wild horses than inhabitants. They came from Chile in the 1850s to the island, which they can now discover from the back of their horses. Wild and free they will feel like the locals.

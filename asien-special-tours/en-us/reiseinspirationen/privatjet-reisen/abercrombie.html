Abercrombie & Kent Private Jet

Close to heaven! Feel the boundless freedom above the clouds and forget your hectic everyday life. Join the Abercrombie & Kent Private Jet and experience a maximum of comfort and style. In a small group of up to 48 people, you can travel on exclusive routes to the most exciting destinations on earth. Insider entrances and cultural events, which are only organized exclusively for you at the invitation of local experts around the world, guarantee unforgettable experiences. Break through your own boundaries and conquer new horizons. Jet from one target to the next. An experienced tour guide team and a dedicated flight crew will take care of you. Let yourself be enchanted by the unique accommodations during the trip, which perfectly reflect the style and spirit of the destination. Traveling through the stomach. Enjoy culinary fireworks - a true feast for the senses.

Travel the most exotic destinations according to a perfect schedule that is tailored to your unique itinerary. The world's handpicked employees from Abercrombie & Kent accompany you from start to finish and ensure a smooth journey. This also includes the dedicated Bell Boy service during the trip, which takes care of your luggage between the individual destinations.

Around the World with Geoffrey Kent

An Inspiring Expedition by Private Jet

Around the world with Geoffrey Kent – the founder of Abercrombie & Kent. Visit exceptional destinations such as Benin in West Africa or Ladakh in India. Experience a tea ceremony in a beautiful Japanese garden, visit a traditional Ladakhi polo match, get deeper insights into African voodoo and much more during a ceremony.

Cruise date: 30 September to 25 February 2020 

2021 Wildlife Safari

Around the World by Private Jet

This journey takes you into the incredible variety of wildlife on this planet. Look out for the Bengal tiger in India, swim in the Philippines with the majestic whale sharks, hike through the lush forests of Rwanda to the impressive Mountain gorillas and experience the unique lemurs that are only available in Madagascar.

Cruise date: 10 January to 02 February 2021

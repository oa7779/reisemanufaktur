Aman Private Jet

Experience a unique journey to Asia with Aman Private Jet: whether Indonesia, China or Indochina, the experts from Asia Special Tours will advise you individually!

Aman Private Jet

Explore the world! The most secluded destinations on Earth are often the most extraordinary. From the mystical peaks of Bhutan to the colorful reefs around Indonesia's legendary spice islands, Aman stands for absolute privacy and comfort. With its first private jet, the luxury resort family provides the highest level of service in the air. The trips are tailored to your needs and interests. A separate jet concierge plans every detail and makes sure that you can enjoy the whole world of Aman even above the clouds. 


Aman's Bombardier Global 5000 is one of the world's most luxurious and spacious private aircraft. The jet can accommodate a maximum of 12 passengers. The aircraft reaches a maximum speed of 924 kilometers per hour and covers distances of up to 9.630 kilometers. The jet is perfect for short and long-haul travel and is ideal for groups and families.

Relax – we'll take care of the rest. Flexible departure times and seamless customs clearances by Aman's staff guarantee relaxed and stress-free days. You can choose your meals before departure. During the entire flight, you have access to your luggage. Of course, a high-speed Wi-Fi connection is available during the flight. In addition to flights, transfers and accommodation in the Aman resorts, all trips include private tours to important cultural sites as well as a range of exclusive privileges in the Aman resorts – from personalized spa treatments and traditional blessing ceremonies to unique culinary experiences. 

Bali & Beyond

Treat yourself to a break and experience the magical island world of Indonesia. "Bali & Beyond" offers you the opportunity to create a tailor-made Indonesian itinerary with up to five Aman destinations. These include Aman's Balinese trio Amandari, Amankila and the Aman villas in Nusa Dua, Amanjiwo overlooking Borobudur in the spiritual core of Central Java and Amanwana, a tent camp on Moyo Island in Floressee. Relax in absolute privacy, travel through fantastically beautiful landscapes and discover the cultural treasures of this unique archipelago.

The Secrets of China

"The Secrets of China" combines stays in Amanfayun, Amandayan and Amanyangyun – from the rural calm of the West Lake of Hangzhou to the thousand-year history of Lijiang to the contemporary vibrant mega metropolis of Shanghai. Immerse yourself in the experience of China with us and feel the harmonious interplay of tradition and modernity up close!

Indochina Past & Present

Experience the multifaceted Indochina that appeals to all your senses and reveals a new world to you. "Indochina Past & Present" offers insights into the ancient culture and magnificent landscapes of Laos, Cambodia and Vietnam – tempting destinations for all Southeast Asian fans. With its proximity to the historic city of Luang Prabang in Laos, the Cambodian temples of Angkor and the Vietnamese Nui Chua National Park, Amantaka, Amansara and Amanoi are perfectly located for an unforgettable tour in the new private jet. Collect memories, not things!

From the Himalayas to the Sea

Immerse yourself in a world full of magic and return to your innermost self. "From the Himalayas to the Sea" includes a trip of at least seven nights through Amankora's five lodges and a three-day stay in Amanpuri. Guests experience the magic of a traditional blessing and the wonders of Bhutan's centuries-old culture. Then we go to the beautiful beaches of Phuket. Sun, beach and sea and you in the middle! Enjoy the sweet nothing-doing and determine your own pace.

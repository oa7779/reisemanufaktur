Four Seasons Private Jet

Your journey of a lifetime wakes you ... luxury, comfort and privacy and you in the middle! As a five-star jet setter, experience an all-round unique Four Seasons experience with exceptional air and ground service. A journey with the Four Seasons Private Jet is perfected down to the smallest detail and gives you countless moments for eternity. Let yourself be enchanted by the first-class service and enjoy exclusive accesses and local expertise. All itineraries include flights with the Four Seasons Private Jet, ground transport, excursions and sightseeing, all meals and drinks, as well as first-class luxury accommodation at the Four Seasons Hotels and Resorts worldwide.

Enter Four Seasons' Service Care – an experience exclusively reserved for the max. 48 passengers of the Four Seasons Private Jet. On board, a team of Four Seasons staff will take care of your well-being, including the Director of Guest Experience, an on-board concierge and an executive boss.

Remote Wonders

Dubai, Hoi An, Angkor Wat, Bangkok, Chiang Mai or Chiang Rai, Seychelles, Rwanda, Serengeti, Dubai

Look out for luxury adventurers! With the "Remote Wonders", you can spend 21 days on an extraordinary journey through Asia and Asia. AfricaAt an exclusive New Year's Eve party in Dubai, champagne corks pop before going on to the enchanting Hoi An, where you not only explore the charming old town and take part in a private dinner under colorful lampions, but also get an exclusive tailor-made garment. After the legendary temples of Angkor Wat you will continue to fly into the fascinating mega metropolis Bangkok. In the island paradise of Seychelles, the catamaran tells us about the turquoise blue water around the dream beach "Petite Anse", before the trip with a crowning finish at the gorillas in Rwanda and an unforgettable dinner with the Massai warriors in the Serengeti National Park end.

Time of travel: 31. December 2020 - 20 January 2021

Ancient Explorer

Miami, Mexico City, Easter Island, Bora Bora, Great Barrier Reef, Koh Samui or Bangkok, Jordan, Pyramids, Athens, Madrid

The 24-day "Ancient Explorer" package begins in sunny Miami. Next stop is Mexico Citywhere you will participate in a Mezcal Masterclass. Next stop: the magic Easter Islandwhere you can explore the mystical volcanic landscape and the famous Moai statues on horseback. Then on a private cruise you will experience the colorful underwater world of the Great Barrier Reef, followed by a stop in the tropical Koh Samui where brave people practice the traditional martial arts "Muay Thai". In Egypt, when you visit the Sphinx and the impressive pyramids of Giza, it goes back to the moving past. The end of the journey will finally take you to Athens and Madrid, where you can enjoy the luxury of two Four Seasons houses. Highlights include a visit to the Acropolis and the Parthenon in Athens and private Flamenco lessons in Madrid.

Cruise date: 05. - 28. October 2021

International Intrigue

Seattle, Kyoto, Hoi An, Maldives, Serengeti, Marrakech, Budapest, St. Petersburg, Paris

A must for all globetrotters! The journey "International Intrigue" takes you to four continents – from vibrant and dazzling cultural capitals, isolated island paradises to the African savannah. Experience the most beautiful places this planet has to offer. Highlights include a seaplane flight across the glittering Seattle skyline, a relaxing Zen meditation in Kyoto, a walking safari in the Serengeti and a visit to the fragrant spice markets in Marrakech.

Cruise date: 24 February to 19 March 2021

Timeless Encounters

Kona, Bora Bora, Sydney, Bali, Chiang Mai or Chiang Rai, Taj Mahal, Dubai, Prague, London

The Four Seasons Private Jet takes off to timeless encounters as part of the 24-day tour Timeless Encounters: destinations include the paradise beaches of Bora Boras and Sydney's world-famous Bondi Beach, the impressive Taj Mahal in India, Hawaiian Big Island and the island of gods. Glitter and glamour await at the end of the journey in Dubai and London.

Time of travel: 19. March to 11 April 2021

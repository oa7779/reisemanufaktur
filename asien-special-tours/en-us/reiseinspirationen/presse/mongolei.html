Mongolia Round Trip - In the Heart of Mongolia

Munich – October 22, 2019. Following the footsteps of Genghis Khan: The latest travel idea of the Munich tour operator Asia Special Tours leads to the heart of Mongolia. Infinite expanses, desert towns and overnight stays in traditional yurt camps convey an authentic image of land and people on this round trip.

The 10-day Mongolia trip begins in the capital Ulaanbaatar. Together with a local tour guide, travelers will go to the center of the city and visit Sukhbaatar Square as well as Gandan Monastery in Tibetan style and Bogd Khan's Winter Palace.

From the living city to the barren desert: The next day is a journey into the desert of Gobi, the northernmost desert in the world. The path leads past endless steppe landscapes and rocky cliffs to the desert town of Mandalgobi. The surrounding rock formations of Zagaan Suvarga are particularly impressive. Due to their color and shape, these are called "white and red stupas" by the locals. Also worth seeing are the rock drawings on Mount Del Uul, which can be dated back to the Bronze Age. The night is spent in traditional yurt camps.

The highlights of the next few days include the longest walking dunes in South Gobis, the Els Sand dune, as well as a stopover in the vulture gorge and of course the visit to the flaming cliffs. With its bright orange color, these are among the most popular attractions in Mongolia.

The Ongi Monastery provides travelers with an insight into the life of Mongolian monks. On a daily stage to the Khogno Khan Camp, you can also enjoy a unique panoramic view of the steppe landscape.

Finally, the travelers reach Karakorum, under Genghis Khan and his successors once the capital of the Mongolian Empire. There the monastery complex Erdere Zuu with its 108 stupas and the white monastery wall draws all eyes on itself. Before the return trip, a last day is available in Ulaanbaatar, which is available for further discovery tours.

The trip serves as an inspiration and can be individually adapted to the wishes of the customers at any time.

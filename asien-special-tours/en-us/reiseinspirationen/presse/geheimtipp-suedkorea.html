Secret tip South Korea – The land of morning silence

Munich – April 11, 2017. South Korea, the country between modernism and tradition, is still considered a secret tip, because South Korea has been spared mass tourism so far and thus was able to preserve its authenticity. Hardly any other Asian travel destination offers so many opportunities to actively experience the beauty of a country as in South Korea.

The small country on the southern tip of the Korean peninsula forms the extreme tip of the Asian continent and can be visited all year round thanks to the temperate climate. South Korea is one of the world's most important industrial nations and has developed into a modern economic center. The landscape in South Korea is characterized by rugged mountains, deep gorges, roaring rivers and lush fields. In the capital city of Seoul, travelers will find historic palaces and city gates as well as impressive shopping centers. Almost 3,500 islands lie off the South Korean coast and contribute to the attractiveness of the country.

The population attaches great importance to the maintenance of cultural rites and customs, which are still lived and celebrated across generations. The combination of tradition and modernity is also a trademark of South Korea. Old and new are not contrasts, but lived culture. This is clearly visible in the capital city of Seoul – between shopping centers and illuminated advertisements there are historic palaces and city gates.

In 2018, the country will also be in focus, as the South Korean city of Pyeongchang will host the Winter Olympics. After the 1988 Summer Games in Seoul, South Korea is now hosting the Olympic Games for the second time.

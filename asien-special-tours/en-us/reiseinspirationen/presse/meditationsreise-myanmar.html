Press release - Meditation trip Myanmar

Press release Myanmar Meditation Journey: On the themed trip from Asia Special Tours, relaxation seekers will find themselves in the land of golden pagodas to the inner center.

Meditation Journey to Myanmar – The Sound of Silence

Munich – May 28, 2019. Escape from the hustle and bustle of everyday life – The new Myanmar trip by the Munich tour operator Asia Special Tours is dedicated to the healing power of meditation in the Meditation Centre Bago. Ancient relaxation methods and ceremonies lead to the inner center in the land of golden pagodas.

In almost no other country in Southeast Asia, Buddhism plays such a prominent role as in Myanmar. Belief in discipline and sacrifice determines people's lives and actions. The 15-day meditation trip to Myanmar stands in the sign of peace and relaxation and is ideal to hide the noise of everyday life along Buddhist sanctuaries.

The beginning of the trip is a visit to Shwedagon Pagoda in Yangon. The largest city in the country has a rich history and an impressive religious legacy. A tour guide leads through the sacred site for Burmese and reports about the rituals of the monks. A highlight is the traditional Oil Lamp Ceremony, where both locals and visitors spark hundreds of oil lamps. The next morning, a Tai Chi teacher receives early risers for a relaxing start to the day. According to the soothing movements, a trip to the Golden Rock is imminent, which according to legend balances on a hairline of Buddhas. Visitors have the opportunity to walk from the base camp along the 1,000-year-old road.

Finally, the ten-day stay in the meditation center is waiting in Bago. There, relaxation seekers immerse themselves in a different world and follow strict rules and the prescribed daily routine of the facility. Early in the morning at 3 o'clock, the day begins with meditation and singing. Between meals, guests return to a state of meditation time and again and dissipate themselves from all the stress of everyday life. To achieve a spiritual experience richer and full of new energy, the journey through Yangon leads back to the homeland.

This Myanmar trip serves as an inspiration and can be customized and extended at any time.

Asia Special Tours expands its portfolio with Oceania and the Silk Road

Munich – August 6, 2018. As part of the Homepage Relaunches, Asia Special Tours GmbH is expanding its target area portfolio and now also offers individual and privately guided trips to Oceania as well as along the Silk Road.

Asia Special Tours stands for tailor-made and high-quality round trips, which are created according to the wishes of customers. Since summer 2018, the target area experts have also been fulfilling individual trips to Oceania and along the Silk Road. For example, the Asia-Organizer's portfolio is expanded to include East and Central Asian countries such as Armenia, Georgia, Iran, Oman, Kyrgyzstan and Uzbekistan. In particular, thematic tours between Orient and Occident as well as diverse country combinations along the Silk Road convince with a combination of exceptional activities and luxurious accommodation.

The island world of the Pacific with New Zealand, Australia and the South Sea destinations Cook Islands, Fiji and French Polynesia can now be explored individually with Asia Special Tours. On the new Oceania homepage you will find varied travel inspirations and island combinations. Whether a train trip through Australia, a car hire trip to the most beautiful boutique hotels in New Zealand or relaxing days with island hopping in the South Seas – the experts from Asia Special Tours meet every customer's requirement.

Asia Special Tours works with selected travel agencies. From the first booking 10% commission will be paid to the travel agency.

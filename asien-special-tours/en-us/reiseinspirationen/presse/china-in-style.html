Luxury Travel to the Middle Kingdom: China in Style

Munich – March 19, 2019. In China, the Far Eastern country of superlatives, modern hotels and first-class service are competing. The latest travel creation of Asia Special Tours leads along the country's highlights at an elevated level – including helicopter flight over Yangshuo and yacht cruise in front of Shanghai's glittering metropolis.

On the 17-day individual trip China in style the Munich tour operator offers an exclusive trip to the most exclusive corners and cultural highlights of the country. The first stop is the luxury hotel The Peninsula Beijing in China's dynamic capital Beijing. This welcomes guests with excellent spa offers and Cantonese as well as French gourmet cuisine. Culinaryly demanding it continues the next day. Travelers taste the famous brandy "Er Guo Tou" and enjoy a champagne picnic on the Great Wall.

The following days are devoted to culture: An expert from the Institute of Cultural Relics Beijing will give insider knowledge about Tiananmen Square and the well-known incense master Zong will tell more about his craft at a workshop. In the Prince's Residence "Qing Wang Fu such travelers can enjoy an afternoon tea. A highlight of every trip to China is a visit to the Terracotta Army in Xi'an. A VIP tour brings visitors as close to the life-size figures as possible.

Before the trip from Xian continues with the express train to Chengdu, there is an excursion to the winery of a famous architect. Chengdu also has the highest density of tea rooms in China, making us the ideal place to attend a traditional tea ceremony. The last few days have particularly unusual activities ready. Travelers hover over Yangshuo by helicopter, discover the Li River on the private bamboo raft and marvel at Shanghai's glittering skyline from a yacht. Finally, art curator Dr. Qilan Shen leads through the prominent art galleries of the metropolis.

This trip serves as an inspiration and can be extended as desired and adapted to customer requirements. Asia Special Tours works with travel agencies on request. From the first booking 10% commission will be paid to the travel agency.

Individual trips in Oman: Asia Special Tours expands offer

Munich – December 17, 2019.  Asia Special Tours from Munich now realizes individual trips to the Sultanate of Oman. Two new example routes serve as an inspiration for a round trip through the oriental trend destination.

A new destination adorns the website of the tour operator Asia Special Tours. Sultanate Oman is the newest country in the portfolio of luxury travel providers. The company presents the new addition in the form of two new travel ideas. With its scenic diversity, Oman is the ideal destination for individual tours, which the tour operator, founded in 1994, specialises in. The extension is also a response to the increased interest of customers.

On the 9-day travel inspiration "Oriental, seductive, unforgettable Travelers move through the highlights in the north of the country. From the capital city of Muscat, the route leads through the mountain villages in Al Hamra to the mountains around Jabal Akhdar. In Wahiba Sands, there is an overnight stay in the desert and at Ras Al Jinz beach in Al Hadd turtles can be observed while laying eggs.

The 18-day travel creation „Deserts, Oases & MountainsIt offers additional time to explore Muscat extensively and familiarize yourself with the Omani traditions. A visit to the largest fortress of the Arabian peninsula in Nizwa and a multi-day trip to the south of the country to the city of Salalah complete the itinerary.

So far, Oman has only been possible in the portfolio of Asia Special Tours as part of a travel combination along the Silk Road, now the country is also exclusively bookable. And not only that: in the future, the offer should continue to grow and add new themed trips to Oman. The company's travel ideas are to be understood as inspiration. Each trip is adapted to the customer's wishes and created by a personal supervisor.

Taste of Japan - Gourmet tour from Asia Special Tours

Munich – 18. ==References== Japan is a true paradise for gourmets and connoisseurs. Over the centuries, Japanese cuisine has become one of the most refined in the world. On this gourmet journey, travelers taste the diversity of the country and also discover Japan's historic culture.

The Round Trip Taste of Japan starts in Tokyo and leads through the Tsukiji market, the largest fish market in the world, through Tokyo with its numerous temples. During a private cooking class, travelers learn how to prepare the soba pasta and then explore the city's street food cuisine.

The high-speed train Schinkansen takes you to the Japanese Alps to Takayama. There is a culinary walk and an overnight stay in a Ryokan, a typical Japanese guesthouse. After a trip to the small village of Shirakawago and a visit to a Sake brewery with tasting, we go to the imperial city of Kyoto. In addition to Kyoto's many cultural attractions, travelers visit the Nishiki market and learn about the various ingredients of Japanese cuisine. Another private cooking class prepares sushi, miso soup and a Japanese salad.

Insights into the traditional country life of Japan are provided on a visit to a peasant family at Lake Biwa. From an introduction to cultivation and harvesting techniques, to the production of mochi rice chicks, to a joint lunch with the family, there is a unique look behind the scenes.

The journey ends with another street food tour through Osaka with its numerous markets, restaurants and specialties such as octopus balls or white cabbage pancakes.

This travel inspiration can be adapted and changed at any time to the wishes and needs of the customers of Asia Special Tours.

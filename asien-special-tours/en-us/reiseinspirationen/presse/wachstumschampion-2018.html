Press release - Asia Special Tours is one of the growth champions 2018

Asia Special Tours was awarded by Focus-Business and is one of the growth champions in 2018. This is the result of the study conducted by the news magazine Focus together with data company Statista, in which the German companies with the highest sales growth were determined.

Focus Business: Asia Special Tours GmbH ranks among the growth champions 2018

This is the result of the study by the news magazine Focus together with the data company Statista, in which the German companies with the highest turnover growth were determined.

Munich, 9 November 2017 – Thomas Baumgartner, Vice President and Marketing Manager of Asia Special Tours GmbH, said about the award: "We are delighted that we are the only tour operator to have made it among Germany's 500 fastest growing companies. My thanks go to our excellent employees for their commitment and to our fantastic customers for their trust in us. It turns out that with our philosophy of focusing on the individual wishes of our customers, we are meeting the pulse of time.

The top list of 500 growth champions lists companies that achieved particularly high percentage sales growth per year between 2013 and 2016. Both privately owned companies and listed companies were taken into account. The companies must be independent (no subsidiaries, no branches), have their headquarters in Germany and in 2013 at least a turnover of 100,000 euros and in 2016 at least a turnover of 1.8 million euros. This is the first time in the history of the euro. From more than 1.9 million Statista has filtered out thousands of companies with above-average growth. Many more were invited to apply for the 2018 growth champions. The companies mentioned in the Focus Top list are awarded the "Growth Champion 2018" award.

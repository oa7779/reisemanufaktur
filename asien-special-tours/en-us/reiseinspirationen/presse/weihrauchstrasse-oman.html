Press release - On the trail of the incense street in Oman

Press release Oman: Asia Special Tours brings customers closer to Oman's beauties as part of a journey along the Frankincense Road.

On the trail of the incense street in Oman

Munich – 02 May 2019. Along the Frankincense Road travel and discover where the merchants of the large caravans got the so-called ancient gold, the incense. The Munich tour operator Asia Special Tours makes this possible and brings the beauty of Oman closer to its customers.

The journey begins in Muscat with a city tour to the Great Mosque, colorful markets, the port and the palace of the Sultan. The next day will take you to Daymaniat Island Reserve. Here travelers have the opportunity to see a variety of fish and, with some luck, turtles on a snorkeling trip. From the coast we continue to the mountains around Jabel Akhdar. In Jabal Akhdar remains sufficient time, the amenities of luxurious Anantara Al Jabal Al Akhdar Resort to enjoy. In addition to yoga lessons and cinema under the stars, the luxury hotel also offers signage and photography courses, hikes and picnics. A visit to the villages of Tanuf and Misfah gives an impression of the original life in Oman and testifies to the history of the country.

It is adventurous when driving through the up to 200 meter high sand dunes in Wahiba Sands. On the other hand, there is cooling in the deep blue and clear water of the Wadis, the numerous natural pools that can be found in the desert. The way back to Muscat leads through Ras Al Jinz at the Gulf of Oman, the fishing village Tiwi and the town of Quriyat with its fortress.

A domestic flight takes travelers to Salalah, where they pass along the Frankincense Road through fishing villages, old castles, and ruins, the river Wadi Darbat and numerous incense trees. A visit to the UNESCO World Heritage Site Ubar is impressive. The city is also referred to as "Atlantis of the Desert" because it literally sunk in the sand. The conclusion of this Oman trip is a city tour in Salalah. 

This Oman trip is part of a Iran-Oman travel combination and serves as inspiration. It can be extended as desired and adapted to customer requirements. Further trips along the Silk Road can be found at www.seidenstrasse-rundreisen.de.

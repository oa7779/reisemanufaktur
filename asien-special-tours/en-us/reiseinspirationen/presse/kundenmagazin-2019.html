25th anniversary: Asia Special Tours presents new customer magazine

Munich – December 05, 2019.  Asia Special Tours and its sister companies Africa Royal Tours and América Special Tours present the sixth edition of their customer magazine Fernweh3. On the occasion of the 25th anniversary, it contains country reports and travel reports as well as a review of the company's roots.

On 72 pages the luxury tour operator Asia Special Tours GmbH together with its sister companies presents interesting travel ideas, exclusive hotels as well as varied activities. The sixth edition of the in-house customer magazine also offers a look behind the scenes at the special milestone of 25 years of Asia Special Tours. Managing Director Rüdiger Lutz tells the story of the company's beginnings, whose success story began a quarter of a century ago with trips to China.

The annual magazine also presents a part of the team and provides readers with first-hand information about their destination trips and expert tips. Of course, customers also speak and report on their diverse experiences in China, Zambia, Namibia, Botswana and Peru. In the course of the expansion, the company also presents Hawaii as a new destination. Another highlight is the brand new 1 million Euro journey: In the private jet, this exclusive journey leads to the most dazzling metropolises in the world.

The Munich tour operator Asia Special Tours and its sister companies specialise in individual long-distance trips to Asia, Africa and America. The travel in the magazine as well as on the website and catalog are considered an inspiration and can be adapted at any time to the wishes of the customers.

Catalog and customer magazine can be ordered free of charge at +49 089 127 091 0 or via the form at the following link: https://www.asia-special-tours.de/service-contact/orderform/

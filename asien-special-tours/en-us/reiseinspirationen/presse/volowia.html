Asia Special Tours discovers new country: the magical Volowia

Munich – 03 January 2019. Autonomous driving, travel guide bots, extraordinary plants, such as the self-idea and spectacular natural wonders, like the giant wave of Lake Leika are waiting for adventurers and tech tourists in the land Volowia newly discovered by Asia Special Tours.

Asia Special Tours has explored Volowia in detail and put together a unique round trip through the exciting country. The 14-day journey starts with a parabolic flight to the capital Illustria, where a travel guide bot is already waiting for guests. The travel guide app takes you on a photo tour through the Technology stronghold. The perfect surf spot is at the legendary Lake Leika, where a huge wave makes every surfer's heart beat faster on time at 11.11.

After a hike on the Zuckerberg, in the mirrored imperial city of Perfetto, it goes into the temple of the weightless feather. In the tropical planning city of Emotia, guests learn more about the earthquake area and how people defy natural violence. On a journey with the sound speed train they arrive in the icy north of Volowia, which hides behind the Emotia mountain range.

A balloon ride over the Pink Desert opens up completely new perspectives. In the Eden crater, the impressive water migration can be observed during the rainy season. With a bit of luck, guests will see the rare two-horn tiger spot Zebra. The Lelucon National Park is also an animal highlight: Zebras and kangaroos do not live together in any other national park in the world. After a visit to the Galabala Islands with a delicious underwater dinner, we continue to the blogguru natives who tell everything about their technology-free life before the journey home at Illustria airport.

The complete itinerary can be found in the appendix and in the new catalog of Asia Special Tours. The catalog can be ordered via this form.

For further information on Volowia, the travel experts from Asia Special Tours will also be at stand 8D40 and f.r.e. at the CMT travel fair in Stuttgart from 12.01.19 to 24.01.19. Munich from 20.02.19 to 24.02.19 at booth A4/519.

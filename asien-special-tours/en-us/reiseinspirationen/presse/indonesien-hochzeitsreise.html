Press release: Indonesia honeymoon – hand in hand to happiness

The Indonesia honeymoon from Asia Special Tours brings newlyweds closer to Bali's unique spirituality and leads on a luxurious sailing yacht through the impressive island worlds around Komodo and Rinca.

Indonesia honeymoon

Hand in hand to happiness

Munich – June 25, 2019. The new Indonesia honeymoon of the Munich Asia expert Asia Special Tours brings newlyweds closer to Bali's unique spirituality and leads on a luxurious sailing yacht through the impressive island worlds around Komodo and Rinca.

The 14-day travel inspiration begins on the Indonesian island of Bali. Three nights spent by the newlyweds in a 5-star luxury resort Amankila on the east coast of Bali and enjoy the impressive nature on a boat trip, a hike as well as romantic dinners in front of fantastic backdrops.

It goes high on a helicopter flight over the island. The volcano Agung, numerous temples and rice plantations as well as impressive coastal sections are admired from a bird's eye view. On a culinary tour in the VW-Oldtimer convertible, honeymooners explore Bali's cuisine as well as the interior of the island. Peace and relaxation await you in the luxurious 5-star Four Seasons Resort Bali at Sayan ==References==

Travelers spend four more days in the south of Bali visiting Uluwatu Temple on the edge of steep cliffs and the picturesque beaches of Padang Padang and Jimbaran. During a Vespa tour through Seminyak, they feel the lively hustle and bustle on the streets up close and have the opportunity to taste regional delicacies.

The honeymooners experience luxury at the highest level during a 3-day private cruise around Komodo and Rinca on board the ALEXA. During guided hikes they get to know the island world and its animal inhabitants, the Komodo Warane. Massages on board the luxury yacht and rest breaks on the sandy beaches of the archipelago provide relaxation. On snorkeling trips there is the possibility to see mantas or turtles with some luck. The cruise is rounded off by dinner and barbecues with typical Indonesian dishes on white sandy beaches.

This honeymoon serves as an inspiration and can be customized at any time according to the wishes of the customers. Since autumn 2018, Asia Special Tours has been realized together with its sister companies Africa Royal Tours and América Special Tours Individual Wedding trips to Asia, Africa and Latin America. Asia Special Tours works with travel agencies on request. From the first booking 10% commission will be paid to the travel agency.

Press release - Slow Travel by Kerala

Press release Slow Travel Kerala: Travel slowly and consciously instead of chasing after every sight - The new travel idea of Asia Special Tours is the theme of "Slow Travel" and leads leisurely through five natural sites in India's Kerala.

Slow Travel through Kerala – consciously experience India

Munich – 30 January 2020. Travel slowly and consciously instead of chasing after every tourist attraction: The new travel idea of Asia Special Tours stands in the sign of "Slow Travel" and leads leisurely through five nature-related places in the Indian Kerala.

The Munich luxury rice organiser takes up the concept of "Slow Travel" on the individual trip through India. Behind it is the thought of taking time and moving carefully through foreign countries to build a deep connection with land and people. Five hidden retreats are on the program on the 17-day journey through the South Indian state of Kerala. Travelers spend at least three days each in an accommodation.

After arriving at the airport, we walk through the intertwined jungle paths of the Malaytoor Forest to the Quiet by the River. The program and tours on site are individually defined for each of the five accommodations and are based on the desired travel speed. Guests can explore the surrounding farms, explore river and sandbanks with their own canoe or enjoy the view from the infinity pool.

The nearest accommodation, Windermere River House in Neriamangalam, is surrounded by two hectares of nature and offers peace and seclusion. Activities include a guided tour of Munnar's tea gardens, visiting the Thattekad Bird Sanctuary or cycling tours through the authentic villages of the neighborhood.

In Kuttikkanam the historian George Abraham gives an insight into the history of South India and from the Philipkuttys Farm in Kottayam the guests explore the famous Kerala Backwaters. At the end of the day, travelers will accelerate for four days at Marari Beach Resort in Mararikulam.

The trip serves as an inspiration and can be individually adapted to the wishes of the customers at any time.

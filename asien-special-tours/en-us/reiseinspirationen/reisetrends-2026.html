Travel Trends 2026

Authentic and personal: We will show you how to travel 2026. Learn more about multi-gene trips, culinary delights and exclusive insider tips.

New ways to discover the world

The way to travel is constantly changing. New trends shape the industry and inspire travelers to new ideas, goals and travel forms. Some developments can already be clearly observed with us and our guests. We present the most important travel trends for the new year.

MULTIGENERATION SERIES

Together on the road – for generations

Families are increasingly choosing to travel with grandparents, parents and children together. For children there are special experiences such as a photo hunt in Vietnam or a Manga drawing course in Japan. Each family travels at its own pace. Various interests can be taken into account and coordinated.

Japan for the whole family
The Soul of Brazil
South Africa & Mauritius - Safari magic and beach jewels

CROSS TRAVEL

On course towards discovery

Modern cruises are currently experiencing increasing demand, especially in the premium and luxury segment. Exclusive brands such as Antarctica21, Silversea or Aqua Expeditions rely on special ships with limited number of guests and individual routes. Expeditions lead to the Galápagos Islands, the South Seas or Easter Island. River cruises, for example on the Mekong through Indochina, are also gaining popularity.

Antarctic cruise
Silversea
Aqua Expeditions

SLOW TRAVEL

Arriving instead of just continuing

We often have to look for sights from one place to the next. In contrast, Slow Travel means staying longer in individual places to experience land and culture deeply. We pay attention to a stress-free pace during all our journeys anyway. On request, however, we will leave you more room to decide on site what and how much you want to discover. For example, on our trip through Kerala in India. There you can spend several days in the same accommodation, explore the quiet backwaters by boat, visit fishing villages, get to know the regional cuisine and let yourself be pampered at the spa.

Kenya's Hidden Treasures
Ecuador Nature Tour - Cloud Forest and Galapagos

NEW LUXURY

Luxury redefined

New Luxury relies on quality, comfort and service, less on magnificent staging. We note that especially small, individual accommodations and offers with special services are increasingly popular. Authenticity is in demand: travelers want to get to know places like they actually are – with culture, everyday life and infrastructure. Our customers especially appreciate activities that offer real insight into local life.

Luxury Tour - Nepal in Style
South America Tour – Magical Andean Highlands
Uganda - On unknown paths

CULINARY DISCUSSION

Enjoy regional cuisine

Travelers are increasingly interested in local specialties, street food, markets or small restaurants away from the mainstream. Individually coordinated culinary experiences are deliberately planned as highlights of a trip. On our numerous trips, we therefore enable cooking classes or even a meal together with locals.

Gourmet Tour Japan - Taste of Japan
Peru - Enjoyment in the land of Inca
Wine, wellness and wild animals in South Africa

ACCOMMODATION AS A RESULT

Special retreats as a travel highlight

Hotels and lodges are increasingly surprising with special designs, special themes or unique locations. Our trips show that such details often make the difference when booking – especially for city trips and nature stays.

Bawah Reserve - Indonesia
One & Only Nyungwe House
Isla Palenque, Panama

PERSONAL TRAVEL MATERIAL

Travel with real contact person

We observe two developments in travel planning that go hand in hand. For easy travel, many customers use digital support through artificial intelligence. This may have its place for easy travel, but for complex long distance travel we observe the opposite trend. Customers appreciate it especially in today's time to talk to a real person. The personal Travel Atelier Advisor offers individual recommendations, time savings, expertise and support before, during and after the trip.

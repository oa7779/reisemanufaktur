Inspiration for your dream trip to Europe

Lake-to-Lake helicopter flight

Como & Lake Geneva from above

How about, for example, a Lake-to-Lake helicopter flight in Italy? Enjoy the Dolce Vita and enjoy spectacular views of Lake Como and Lake Geneva from a bird's eye view. You will stay in style at the 5-star Mandarin Oriental Hotel and enjoy the culinary creations of Chef Vincenzo Guarino.

E-Grand Tour through Switzerland

With the Tesla along impressive pass roads

Always the mountains in view: Go on a journey along the most exciting pass roads in Switzerland. On the E-Grand Tour, take your Tesla rental car on a road trip along 5 Alpine passes, 22 lakes and 12 UNESCO World Heritage sites. In this unique experience, combine the feeling of adventure with soothing relaxation in nature.

Luxury hotels AMANZOE & COMO Castello Del Nero

Blessing for body and mind

If you feel like relaxing and wellness, we recommend the luxury resort AMANZOE on the Greek peninsula Peloponnese or the COMO Castello Del Nero in Tuscany. In the latter, a true refuge for body and soul awaits you with the in-house wellness area COMO Shambhala. Relax with therapeutic treatments, spa and yoga sessions and fine dining excellent with the Michelin star. A revitalizing experience from which you will return strengthened.

Of course, these are just some of the highlights you can experience on a Europe trip with us.

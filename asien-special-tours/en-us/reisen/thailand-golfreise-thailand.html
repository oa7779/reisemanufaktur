Golf Trip Thailand - Welcome to the Gulf Mecca of Asia

Thailand Golf Tour "It's Tee Time - Golf Tour of Thailand" from Asia Special Tours. Ask them about their tailor-made offer.

Itinerary & services

Do you play golf and would like to pursue this passion when traveling? Then we have just the right thing for you! This trip will take you to some of Thailand’s most beautiful golf courses where you can indulge your hobby to your heart’s content. Play the best golf courses in Bangkok, where Tiger Woods has already celebrated great victories, master the most challenging golf courses Chiang Mais against the impressive mountain scenery and convince yourself that Phuket and Hua Hin are also among the best golf destinations in Asia. But not only the sensational golf courses will make your heart beat faster. Thailand also boasts magnificent scenery, fantastic views during the game and a diverse culture. Explore Bangkok, the most vibrant metropolis in Southeast Asia like a local, be captivated by the fabulous temples and bustling markets of Chiang Mais and relax after golfing on the beaches of Phuket or Hua Hin. In your hotels in the extra class you can relax after your days full of impressions and experiences.

1st day Arrival in Bangkok

Welcome to Bangkok – metropolis of the East and Southeast Asia’s most vibrant capital par excellence! Upon your arrival, you will already be expected and welcomed by your tour guide. Be prepared to be enchanted by this urban wonderland. Known in the Thai language as the “City of Angels”, Bangkok is a city with an exciting history and spirituality where you can experience ancient Thai temples stretching magnificently up the shores, colorful palaces sparkling spectacularly in the sunlight, and a river winding through the heart of the city. Fabulous shopping opportunities await you, whether you’re looking for high-quality crafts or excellent bargains in Asia’s most stylish malls. If you love food, you’ll be amazed by the exceptional food you get at every corner of town. Bangkok leaves nothing to be desired! Overnight in Bangkok.

2nd Day Alpine Golf Club: Following in the footsteps of Tiger Woods

After you have been able to get first impressions of the exciting metropolis, today you will have the opportunity to indulge your hobby, golfing. The Alpine Golf Club you will play today is one of the best in Asia. Tiger Woods also had a victory here. The 18 holes make up the most challenging golf course in the Bangkok region – and by far! The Alpine Golf Club is a layout that will put the best to the test. At the same time, it shines with almost picturesque holes and a lush vegetation. Those who play here enjoy an unforgettable experience, where their skills as a golfer are challenged. Therefore, the Alpine Golf Club must not be missing on your golf trip to Thailand! In the evening you can relax in your hotel. Luxury Hotel The Sukhothai Bangkok, with its stylishly furnished rooms in Thai-modern design, attentive, warm service and award-winning spa, is a true refuge of tranquility in the heart of the vibrant metropolis. Let the day end according to your wishes in the middle of lotus ponds and tropical gardens. Overnight in Bangkok.

3rd day Thai Country Club: Golf experience in a class of its own

Even today’s golf day will leave nothing to be desired. You play a round on the 18-hole golf course of the Thai Country Club, which is also one of Bangkok’s best facilities. The design is designed for everyday use by its members as well as large international and even PGA Tour tournaments. PGA Tour victories by Tiger Woods and Vijay Singh have ennobled the prestigious golf course in a sporting way. Thanks to its exemplary presentation and care as well as the exceptionally strong classic Parkland layout, the Thai Country Club is regularly voted among the top five golf courses in Asia. The undulated greens allow many interesting flag positions and thus constantly put the skills of the golfers to the test. Among the 18 holes are some absolute top tracks. Challenging guests like you will appreciate this world-class golf experience. Also treat yourself to a refreshment at the clubhouse. It is regularly voted the best clubhouse in Asia and fulfills every wish! Overnight in Bangkok.

Day 4 Temples, Palaces and Impressive Buddhas

Together with your guide, you will embark on a full-day exploration tour of the multifaceted city today. Your first destination is Wat Phra Kaew, known as the Temple of the Emerald Buddha. Of course, you should not miss the Great Palace. Afterwards, visit the famous temple Wat Pho, which houses more than a thousand Buddha figures, as well as one of the largest solo depictions of Buddha: the reclining Buddha. If you’ve been slowly getting appetite, enjoy your lunch at the beautiful Eat Sight Story Deck restaurant overlooking Wat Arun. Well strengthened, you can then take the longtail boat along the canal of Thonburi and observe the everyday life of Thailand’s “water people”. On your way back, stop at what is probably Bangkok’s most famous symbol, the Temple of Dawn (Wat Arun) with its sparkling towers. In the evening, a real culinary highlight awaits you: a ride on the Manohra Cruise. Let the magnificent backdrop of Bangkok pass by your eyes as you enjoy your delicious dinner. Traditional Thai dances underline the special atmosphere! Overnight in Bangkok.

5th Day Oasis of Tranquility – the luxurious Anantara Chiang Mai Resort

After breakfast, you will be taken to the airport to continue traveling to Chiang Mai. Chiang Mai is also known as the Rose of the North. The city is enchanted by a multitude of magnificent temples and lies in the middle of a beautiful landscape. In the surrounding mountains live in numerous small settlements mountain peoples who have preserved their ancient traditions in the immediate vicinity of this modern metropolis. At the airport you will be received and taken to your dreamlike hotel. Anantara Chiang Mai Resort on the riverfront of Mae Ping River is a luxurious retreat that wonderfully combines modern design with traditional Thai style and colonial style. Enjoy wonderful moments of relaxation and retreat, treat yourself to one of the exclusive Spa rituals or have a cooking class introduce you to the local culture. Thanks to its convenient location in the center of Chiang Mai, the hotel is also the perfect starting point for exploring. If you want, you can stroll independently through the old town with its countless temples, vibrant markets, whimsical cafes, boutiques and galleries. Overnight in Chiang Mai.

Day 6: The Most Beautiful Temples of the City

Chiang Mai is home to many temple complexes, including the Royal Temple of Wat Phra Singh, one of the most popular temples in the city. Particularly important for the great importance of the temple is its unique Buddha statue, which is annually guided to large processions from the temple. Worth seeing is the Viharn by Wat Phra Sing, an impressive roof with many detailed embellishments and golden paintings. Opposite is the elegant Wat Prasat, whose beautiful form is complemented by the impressive art objects within the Viharn. One of the oldest temples in the city and without a doubt the most atmospheric is Wat Phan Tao, a true marvel of teak! Of course, you will also look at the Chedi Luang. After all the temples, it is now time to dive into the bustle of a Thai market. Because nowhere do you learn more about the lived culture of the people than there. The vibrant Warorot market is one of the largest in the city. Stroll along the countless stalls, try out the many delicacies offered and take in the most diverse smells, sounds and colorful pictures. Then you will be driven back to your hotel. Overnight in Chiang Mai.

7th Day Alpine Golf Resort Chiang Mai: golfing in the midst of fantastic nature

Not only Bangkok, but also Chiang Mai has golf courses of the extra class ready for you. You will see for yourself today during a round on the course of the Alpine Golf Resort Chiang Mai. The course is without question one of the golf courses in Chiang Mai that best showcases the natural beauty of the region. The design of the facility was inspired by the mountains on the horizon. No matter where you are, you are guaranteed to enjoy nature in its full glory. The 18-hole course, which measures 6,895 meters starting from the black tees, will challenge experienced players. If you’d rather just enjoy playing golf in beautiful natural surroundings, take the white or blue tees off instead.

8th Day Great Golf Experience at Chiang Mai Highlands

A scenic view, hilly terrain, lots of variety and a great golfing experience is offered by Chiang Mai Highlands Golf Club, whose course was designed by the same team that was also responsible for many Mission Hill layouts in China. The short drive from Chiang Mai to the Highlands leads through an exceptionally beautiful landscape – just sit back and enjoy! The golf course itself was built on an "energy point", the Hermitory of Cuba Sivichai (the monk who founded the famous temple on the Doi Suthep). The result is an exciting and challenging place for players of all experience levels, offering five different tee positions on each hole. From all sides you can enjoy a great view of the entire square and the skyline from mountains in the background. After the "inviting" 1st hole, the space increases in every way and gains strength, beauty and character track by track until you finally reach the 18th hole. The large and hilly greens are not easy to manage. Take on the challenge and prove your strategic approach. The beautiful clubhouse with a pleasant atmosphere offers the necessary relaxation before and after the round. Overnight in Chiang Mai.

Day 9 Back to the South

Phuket or Hua Hin

After spending a few days in Thailand’s beautiful north, return to the south of the country. You will be taken to the airport from where a domestic flight will take you to either Phuket or Hua. Phuket, the largest island in Thailand, is geographically very varied. Your hotel here is Banyan Tree Phuket, nestled in the lagoon landscape. The luxurious resort is an oasis of tranquility and offers the ideal conditions for your perfect holiday. In the coastal town of Hua Hin, on the other hand, stay at Anantara Hua Hin Resort, one of the most luxurious hotels in the area, built in the style of an ancient Thai village right on the beach. Lush tropical gardens surround the hotel and invite you to spend your hours outdoors, while in the rooms and suites you will find all the amenities for a delightfully relaxing holiday. Overnight on Phuket or in Hua Hin.

Day 10: A round of golf in the bathing paradise

Phuket or Hua Hin

Whether you choose the island of Phuket or the coastal town of Hua Hin, both offer ample opportunities to play golf at a high level. Phuket is home to the Laguna Phuket Golf Club, which is in close proximity to Banyan Tree. Since its opening in 2015, the 18-hole par 71 golf course has been awarded twice as "Best Golf Course in Thailand" and once as "Best Golf Course in Asia". Pleasant to play and at the same time challenging, it offers the perfect golf experience for golf fans of all levels. In Hua Hin you play a round on the course of Banyan Golf Club. The first-class championship course with 18 holes impresses with one of the most natural-looking layouts in the country. On almost all tracks, you will get overwhelming views of the mountains and the sea. Each hole has six tea boxes, so the round can be more or less challenging depending on the tee chosen. After a game of golf early in the morning, there is enough time to have a leisurely lunch and spend the rest of the day on the beach or exploring the surrounding area. Overnight on Phuket or in Hua Hin.

Day 11 Red Mountain or Black Mountain Golf Club

Phuket or Hua Hin

On Phuket today, the most exciting and spectacular place on the island is waiting to be played by you. The Red Mountain Golf Club was built on the site of a decommissioned tin mine and is the most hilly course on the island. Since this is the highest golf course on Phuket, you can enjoy breathtaking views from many points on the course during the game. It is also a mountain in Hua Hin: here you play a game on the course of the Black Mountain Golf Club, which owes its name to the black granite rocks of the adjacent mountains. His championship course runs through very hilly terrain. The layout looks very natural because many original features of the environment have been retained. The tracks, accompanied by numerous bunkers with beautiful white sand, offer safe routes on the one hand, but also riskier options for adventurous players. With a fantastic 360-degree panoramic view of the golf course from the clubhouse, you can end your golf experience. The rest of the day is at your disposal to enjoy time on the beach, explore the surroundings or review the experiences of the trip together. Overnight on Phuket or in Hua Hin.

12th day Farewell to Thailand

La gòn kráb/ká and goodbye! Today, unfortunately, it is time to say goodbye to the golfer paradise Thailand. You will be picked up at your hotel and taken to the airport. With precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories of the best golf courses in the country in your luggage, you will go home.

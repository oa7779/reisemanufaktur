Magical Sri Lanka - Experience Intensively

Embark on your very personal adventure across the Spice Island. Culture and nature, history and modernity, adventure and relaxation – all this is close together in Sri Lanka. On your round trip over the colorful island, a diverse mix awaits you: you visit impressive archaeological sites, wander through uniquely beautiful landscapes and immerse yourself in the fascination of Sri Lanka. Whether by tuk tuk, bicycle or jeep – you can not collect the impressions of Sri Lanka more authentically and intensively. You are accommodated in first-class hotels that invite you to dream.

1st day Arrival in Colombo & Tuk Tuk Drive through Colombo

Ayubowan and welcome to Sri Lanka! Upon arrival at the airport, you will already be received and driven to your hotel. Throw yourself right in the middle of the adventure! On an exciting tuk tuk ride it goes across Colombo with its numerous sights, historic buildings and narrow streets. Enjoy many stories from your local Tuk Tuk driver (English) and feel like the “locals” in the middle of this vibrant city. Then we will return to your hotel. Overnight in Colombo.

Day 2: The Secrets of Cinnamon Growing and Virgin White Tea

After breakfast, take a trip to Villa Mayurana near Ahangama. There you will be introduced to the methods of growing and preparing cinnamon before it can be sold on the markets. Afterwards, continue to the Handunugoda tea plantation, the only place in the world where “Virgin White Tea” is grown. You will receive a guided tour of the tea plantation and the associated factory. In the evening you will reach Yala National Park. It is the oldest nature reserve in Sri Lanka. Over 130 bird species such as flamingos, pelicans, peacock and cormorants have settled here; around 30 mammal species roam the area. Among them are elephants and water buffalo. Large parts of the park are reminiscent of the African savannah, while the outer areas are dominated by moist monsoon forests. Overnight in Yala.

Day 3 Jeep Safari at Yala National Park and Overnight at Tent Camp

Today you can take two Jeep safaris and marvel at the diversity of the National Park. Feel the authentic safari feel in your tent camp and let yourself be enchanted by a delicious safari cuisine. Overnight in Yala.

Note:
Please note that we cannot guarantee that you will see the wild animals. Please note that the Jeep safaris in Yala National Park are conducted together with other travelers. In September / October, the Yala National Park is closed for the conservation of flora and fauna.

Day 4 Nuwara Eliya – the city of tea

Enjoy once more the peace and listen to the sounds of the jungle. Then leave Yala National Park and continue on to Nuwara Eliya. The “City of Light” is located in the central highlands of Sri Lanka. The picture book landscape and the temperate climate make this city very special. It is located 1,868 meters above sea level and is considered the most important place for tea production in Sri Lanka. Gracefully towers the highest mountain in the country, the “Pidurutalagala”, over the lush green tea fields of the area. The city carries the nickname “Little England”, because much reminds of the time of the past empire. The British settled here to cultivate tea. Thus, the mountains around Nuwara Eliya are still known as the main growing area of the famous Ceylon tea. Overnight in Nuwara Eliya.

Day 5 Hike to Horton Plains National Park

Today you will visit Horton Plains National Park. The protected area, which is located on the highest mountain plateau in the country, is one of the most important national parks in the country because of its scenic diversity – here you will also find the beautiful Baker’s Waterfall. It is home to a variety of species that live only in this environment. The hills are overgrown by evergreen forests of ancient tree giants. From a steep slope over 1050 meters, which bears the poetic name “World’s End”, in good weather, there is a breathtaking view to the sea. Afterwards you will return to the hotel, where you can rest a little. Overnight in Nuwara Eliya.

Day 6 Train to Kandy & the Sacred Tooth Temple

In the morning you will be taken to Nanu Oya Train Station. From here you travel by train to Kandy. The train journey takes about 3.5 hours and is a real experience. Enjoy the view of the central highlands of Sri Lanka. Gracefully towers the highest mountain in the country, the “Pidurutalagala”, over the lush green tea fields of the area. Arriving in Kandy, take a sightseeing tour with your guide. The main attraction of Kandy is undoubtedly the tooth temple, in which a canine of Buddha is said to be kept in a golden shrine. According to tradition, this tooth emanates spiritual power, making the sanctuary one of the most important pilgrimage sites in the country for Buddhists. Overnight in Kandy.

7th Day Bird Sanctuary Udawattakele & Botanical Garden

Around Kandy there are beautiful landscapes. You can convince yourself of this by a trip to the protected area Udawattakele, where the air is filled with the singing of countless birds. The sanctuary, which also houses three monasteries, not only offers a home for feathered contemporaries, but also deer, bats and giant flying squirrels. In the afternoon, Sri Lanka’s colorful plant world awaits you. You will visit Peradeniya, a good five kilometers west of Kandy, the second largest botanical garden in all of Asia – and without a doubt one of the most beautiful. It was created 700 years ago as a pleasure garden by King Vikramabahu III, but received its current form only in the 19th century by the British. Around 4000 different plant species grow here. Together with the experienced botanist Bandara Palipana, who has worked here for over 30 years, you will walk through avenues of palm trees, past colorful orchids, ponds and cleanly trimmed box trees. Listen with interest to the many stories that Mr. Bandara Palipana has to tell. Overnight in Kandy.

Day 8 - Continue to Madulkelle

Let the day be relaxed and enjoy your breakfast. Then you will be picked up by your guide and drive on to Madulkelle. Your resort is located in the midst of tea plantations and majestic mountains at just under 1000 meters altitude, from where you can enjoy a fantastic view over the mountains. Upon arrival, the rest of the day is at your disposal. Overnight in Madulkelle.

9th day Visit of a tea plantation at Madulkelle

Today is all about tea. Sri Lanka is one of the largest tea exporters in the world and produces black and green tea in a variety of varieties. The first tea plantation was planted in Sri Lanka in the 19th century. On a tea plantation, you can look over the shoulder of the hardworking tea pickers at their work. Afterwards, you will learn everything about this traditional drink in a tea factory, from shrub to harvest. Have a cup of tea tasted before you are brought back to your hotel. Overnight in Madulkelle.

10th day hike in the Knuckles Nature Reserve

After breakfast, set off for your hike in the Knuckles Nature Reserve. The starting point is about 60 minutes drive from your accommodation and is located at an altitude of 1,000 meters. You walk in the company of a naturalist for about 7.5 km (approximately). 4-6 hours) and pass crystal clear waterfalls as well as native flora & fauna. The Knuckles-Ranges bears her name not entirely unfounded. The peaks strung together protrude like the ankles of a fist into the often mist-shrouded sky. When the cloud cover, which covers the summits up to 1,900 meters high, tears open, a spectacular landscape is revealed. For naturalists and biologists, the area is also very interesting due to its unique flora and fauna. Take enough change clothes with you. Overnight in Madulkelle.

Day 11 - Temples of Dambulla

Your journey will take you to Dambulla. Along the way, stop at Alu Vihara Temple, famous for its ancient writings on banana leaves. You will also visit a spice garden in Matale, where you will learn more about the cultivation and processing of various spices. Upon arrival in Dambulla, visit the cave temples of Dambulla. Situated on a hill about 150 meters high, the caves offered protection to King Walagamba during his 14-year exile in the first century. Returning to the throne, he built the largest temple in the country at that time. In total, there are around 80 cave temples in Dambulla, of which you will visit the five largest. In the shrines decorated with historical paintings are more than 150 Buddha statues. Already in the first cave awaits you the largest statue with almost 15 meters. In the second cave, probably the most impressive of the complex, there are well over 100 images of Buddha. With an area of over 2000 square meters, the temple complex is still one of the largest in the country today and was included in the list of UNESCO World Heritage Sites in 1982. Then move to your accommodation. Overnight in Dambulla.

12th day Lion Rock of Sigiriya & Kingdom of Polonnaruwa

Another great highlight of your trip is the Sigiriya Rock (UNESCO World Heritage Site), which is often referred to as the “Eighth Wonder of the World”. On the plateau of a monolith, which rises about 200 meters above the surrounding area, a rock fortress was built in the 5th century. Via a spiral staircase you reach the so-called "cloud girls" - beautiful, well-preserved rock drawings. Over 1200 steps lead up a steep iron staircase to the plateau, where spectacular views await you. The "cloud girls" are about halfway. Of course, you can decide on site how far you want to go. In the afternoon you will continue to Polonnaruwa, where you will transfer to a bicycle. This place was the capital of a powerful kingdom almost a thousand years ago. This period is witnessed by magnificent statues and palaces, impressive stupas, detailed frescoes and last but not least “Gal Vihara”, an imposing rock sanctuary with four Buddha images. In the southern part of Polonnaruwa you will come across the statue of King Parakramabahu I, to whom the city owes its fame. Most of the city’s statues and temples were built during his tenure. Overnight in Dambulla.

13th day Rocks of Pidurangala & Minneriya National Park

Your current destination is Pidurangala - a massive rock formation a few kilometers north of Sigiriya. It looks back on an interesting history, which is closely connected with that of the Sigiriya rock. The Pidurangala rock is more difficult to climb than the Sigiriya rock and thus less known among tourists. Like Sigiriya, Pidurangala was formed by volcanic activity. Already more than 2500 years ago, monks lived here in the caves around the rock. Enjoy a fantastic 360-degree panorama. In the late afternoon, take a private jeep safari in Minneriya National Park. The smallest national park in the country comprises several ecosystems and offers habitats for a species-rich wildlife. Above all, the National Park is known for its elephants who wander around the protected area! Overnight in Dambulla.

14th day of travel home

Even when your holiday is over, the precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories of your trip will accompany you forever. Look forward to letting your friends and family share your experiences in the distance at home. Your guide will take you to Colombo airport for your return flight.

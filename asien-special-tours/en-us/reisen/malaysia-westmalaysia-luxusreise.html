Western Malaysia for All Senses - Culture, Culinary & Wellness

Malaysia Luxury Tour: Experience West Malaysia with all your senses on this individual honeymoon with Asia Special Tours. Ask now!

Itinerary & services

English itinerary text is an offline machine translation of the German original for this team preview. Please confirm travel details with your advisor; the German source remains available through the language switch.

This dreamlike journey takes you to another world. In a varied way, you get to know the diversity of West Malaysia with all your senses. Enjoy your first-class hotels, exciting excursions and the colorful cuisine of the country. Immerse yourself in the exotic culture of Malaysians and go on a taste journey across Asia. Start your journey with a delicious dinner in a cozy and at the same time chic atmosphere, dine at the revolving restaurant Atmosphere 360 with panoramic views of Kuala Lumpur, let rare wines and fine food melt on your tongue in one of the most unique restaurants in the country – in a natural limestone cave – watch the bustle at the Chow Rasta Market and look into the cooking pots of the country at a cooking class in Penang. A particularly beautiful experience is the balloon ride over Putrajaya to sunrise, which dives the city skyline in rich colors. Always set your own pace, which makes the heart and soul wide – and the head free. Relaxing beach days in an excellent bathing hotel round off the trip perfectly.

1st day Perfect travel start: delicious dinner in Bijan

Selamat datang ke Malaysia and welcome to Malaysia. Your journey begins in the capital Kuala Lumpur – called KL for short by its inhabitants. The lovely melting pot of different cultures and religions is also called the Garden City of Lights, because tree-lined streets, parks and public flower gardens transform the city into an urban wonderland that glistens in the evening in the glow of thousands of lights. Upon arrival, you will already be expected and brought to your hotel. Depending on the arrival time, you have some time to look around the area together. In the evening, you will get in tune with a romantic dinner (drinks are excluded) for the coming days. The Bijan is modern in presentation and excellent in taste. Enjoy traditional Malay cuisine in a cozy and at the same time chic atmosphere. Could a journey start better? Overnight in Kuala Lumpur.

2nd day exploring Kuala Lumpur on foot

Look forward to many fascinating impressions of the exciting metropolis. Together with your guide, you will explore Kuala Lumpur on foot and by public transport. You start with a monorail ride to the Chow Kit market, which is rarely visited by tourists. Stroll along the narrow walkways through a maze of stalls loaded with local products of all types and sizes. Continue on to Kampung Bahru, a traditional neighborhood founded in 1899 in the heart of Kuala Lumpur. After a small coffee break, take the light rail to Masjid Jamek, the oldest mosque in the city, and stroll through nearby Little India. In addition, the historic colonial center with the Merdeka Square awaits you. With an authentic Indian banana leaf lunch, you will then fortify for the afternoon program, which will first take you to the "Sze Ya" and the nearby Sri Maha Mariamman Hindu Temple. On the way back to the hotel, stop by the legendary Petronas Twin Towers, from whose observation deck you will have a breathtaking view of Malaysia’s capital. Overnight in Kuala Lumpur.

Day 3rd Balloon Ride Over Morning Putrajaya

Experience the planned town of Putrajaya south of Kuala Lumpur at dawn and watch the sunrise from the air. You will start your adventure at 06:00 with a private transfer from the hotel in Kuala Lumpur to Putrajaya. Strengthen yourself with a cup of tea or coffee and a light snack as you watch the crew prepare. After the safety briefing and an introduction to ballooning by the pilot, it is time to climb on board and take off! Slowly climb over the surrounding buildings and let yourself be enchanted by a sunrise that immerses the city in rich colors – a truly magical experience! Back on solid ground, get a certificate and have your breakfast tasted in a 5-star hotel before you look at the city a little more from the ground. Then return to Kuala Lumpur, where the afternoon is free. In the evening it goes up again: enjoy your dinner in the revolving restaurant Atmosphere 360 with a breathtaking view of the city 282 meters below you. Overnight in Kuala Lumpur.

Day 4 Colonial Charm of Ipoh

Before you make your way to your dreamlike resort, you will first go to Ipoh, a pretty colonial-style city that was once called the “city of millionaires” due to its rich tin deposits. The many beautiful old buildings in Ipoh’s old town still reflect the former wealth of the city today. Its route through the historic old town is about six and a half kilometers long. Depending on the physical condition, however, it can be extended or shortened if desired. Your walk begins at the train station – an excellent example of colonial architecture – and ends at the Town Padang Mosque, built in 1908 in Moghul style. After lunch in Ipoh, continue on to the picturesque Bajaran Hotsprings Retreat. Once there, there is still enough time for initial explorations. For example, how about a visit to the Crystal Cave with amethysts and quartz crystals? It is ideal for energy refueling after a long drive or several days of travel. You can then calmly review the schedule of activities included in the price and sign up for your favorite activity (24 hours notice required). For example, treat yourself to a fish spa or let the soul follow during a yoga hour. Overnight in Ipoh.

5.-6. Day time to relax and enjoy

Without tour guide & chauffeur

After you have gained many exciting impressions of Malaysia’s rich cultural treasures in the last few days, you can now relax completely. For you, an exclusive spa pampering program for two with personal wellness advice and subsequent, three-hour treatment (various treatments to choose from), a private introduction to breathing techniques or meditation in the specially equipped meditation cave as well as a heavenly facial treatment with scented oils are included in the travel price.The Banjaran Hotsprings Retreat also offers countless other possibilities to make your time varied: Swim in the warm thermal water, relax in the thermal steam cave before immersing yourself in an ice bath, visit a temple or a local pomelo farm, or take part in a jungle hike and discover the natural flora and fauna of the region. In the evening, culinary delights await you: dine in one of Malaysia’s most unique restaurants – Jeff’s Cellar. The Pomelo restaurant serves healthy and at the same time very tasty cuisine in a quiet, romantic atmosphere. Overnight in Ipoh.

7th Day Off to the Island – Penang is waiting for you

Without guidance

Let today go on very comfortably, sleep out, have breakfast in peace and enjoy a little more of your dreamlike resort. In the early afternoon you will finally make your way to the beautiful island of Penang, where you will be taken right to your hotel. There, the rest of the day is at your disposal for togetherness and relaxation. Overnight in Penang.

Day 8 With the rickshaw through Georgetown

On a half-day rickshaw ride through Georgetown, discover a rather unusual side of Penang. You start the day with a fabulous dim sum breakfast. Your guide will explain the city’s culinary traditions and Malaysian food culture while tasting the city’s best dim sum, echoing multilingual talk and chopstick chatter in the background. Well strengthened, you will then drive along the culinary route to the Chow Rasta market – the perfect place to observe scenes of daily life. Watch housewives and chefs bargain with vendors for the freshest food and learn about Penang’s daily market culture. Explore the alleys of Penang’s cultural center, admire its historic buildings and religious sites, and marvel at remarkable street art inspired by daily life. Learn about the launch of one of Penang’s largest street art collections and the growing community of local artists who help to emphasize the city’s UNESCO World Heritage-listed personality. With many exciting impressions, you will finally return to the hotel. Overnight in Penang.  

9th Day Cooking Class in the Tropical Spice Garden

Today a very special experience awaits you, which will appeal to all your senses. In the morning you will be picked up at the hotel and drive with your guide to the Tropical Spice Garden, one of the most beautiful places on Penang Island. Explore the lush, natural gardens set on a large, secondary jungle as well as the spice terraces before enjoying a little refreshment in the bamboo garden. Then begin your private cooking class. The cooking school is hidden in a secluded corner of the gardens and is surrounded by jungle and a small herb garden. You also have a great view of the Andaman Sea from here. Under professional guidance, prepare delicious Malaysian, Peranakan or Nyonya and/or Indian dishes against this backdrop. Nyonya cuisine is a mix of Chinese ingredients with various spices and cooking techniques from Malaysian-Indonesian cuisine. The result is Peranakan interpretations of Malaysian and Indonesian food, which are similarly spicy, aromatic and tart. Even if you are already an advanced hobby chef, you will certainly learn some tricks and tricks that make it easier for you to re-cook the dishes at home. A joint lunch, where the cooked delicacies are eaten, concludes the cooking course. Back at the hotel you can freely arrange the rest of the day. Overnight in Penang.

Day 10 - Go to Langkawi

This morning, your driver will pick you up at the hotel and take you to the airport for your flight to Langkawi. In Langkawi you are already expected and brought to your dreamlike hotel. Langkawi is the main island of an archipelago of 99 islands that rise from the clear ocean with fine sandy beaches and lush green forests. The remote location of the island as well as its lush rainforests and untouched beaches make it a dream destination especially for nature lovers. Enjoy a few days of pure relaxation and your beautiful resort The Datai. Your hotel is the perfect place for a romantic and enjoyable time out. Overnight in Langkawi.

11.-14. Day Sweet doing nothing in the island paradise

These days are completely at your disposal to relax once again and celebrate your love. Explore the dreamlike surroundings to your heart’s content, swim in the sea, relax on the beach or simply enjoy your magnificent resort. Just let it go really well! Overnight in Langkawi.

15th day Farewell to Malaysia

Your unforgettable journey of connoisseurs is coming to an end. Even if your holiday is soon over, the precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories from your journey into the realm of the senses will accompany you forever. Look forward to letting your friends and family share your experiences in the distance at home. With countless new impressions in your luggage, your driver will take you to the airport for your return flight home.

Do you long for a break from the hectic everyday life? Then pack your suitcases and gather new strength and energy in Vietnam. Feeling, tasting, seizing happiness with your hands – live the new zeitgeist of travel. This journey is a pleasure for all senses, for body and mind – and thus the ideal journey to find your own self and recharge the empty batteries. 1st day Arrival in Vietnam & Rest oasis Fusion Suites Saigon

Welcome to Vietnam! In Saigon at the airport, you will be expected by your private guide and taken to the hotel. On the streets, an army of mopeds creaks, between bicycle rickshaws meander through. Young people use their mobile phones while in the quiet backyards, believers pray amidst clouds of incense. Your Hotel Fusion Suites Saigon is an oasis of peace. With a daily spa treatment, which is already included in the travel price, you can really be pampered. Enjoy an excellent view from the roof terrace and be inspired by the modern spirit of the city. Relax a little from the journey and try to switch off. Enjoy moments of tranquility during a wellness application and immerse yourself in your very personal dream world, far away from city noise and stress. In the afternoon, a rooftop yoga class awaits you, where you can open your body and find your way to your center. Then return to your hotel. Overnight in Saigon.

2nd Day Culinary Street Food Tour in Saigon

Man is what he eats. According to this philosophy, nutrition is also part of the inner balance. Vietnam is a paradise for foodies. Join Mr. Dittko for a stroll through Saigon’s markets. Mr. Dittko is originally from Germany, has been living in Saigon since 1995 and knows the city like the back of his hand. During a culinary discovery tour, he brings you closer to the sophistication and balance of the local cuisine. In a traditional pharmacy, find out which ingredients and medicinal herbs fit which dish and get to know the delicious little pancakes “Banh Khot”. At lunchtime, you will arrive at a small restaurant dedicated to traditional home cooking. During a visit to the Tan Dinh Market, you will discover the incredible selection of fruits and vegetables, fresh seafood and various types of tofu. At the same time, you will learn about the history, politics, religion and life of the people in the mega-city Saigon. Mr. Dittko will show you the best cooking kitchens and restaurants, which not only offer culinary excellence, but are also always good for an exciting evening with a Saigon beer on the rocks. Overnight in Saigon.

3rd Day Imperial City Hue & Enchanting Alba Wellness Resort

A short domestic flight will take you to Hue. The name Hue means harmony: gracefully reflected in the perfume river, dreaming in the shadow of old palaces and other imperial buildings. A UNESCO World Heritage Site, Hue attracts everyone with its famous citadel, palaces, temples and imperial tombs that bear witness to a great and fascinating past. Together with your guide, take a city tour and see the Thien Mu Pagoda as well as the Imperial Citadel. You will then be taken to your hotel. Opened only in 2018, Alba Wellness Resort is located about 45 km from Hue Airport and is beautifully situated in a 50-hectare tropical forest landscape where natural mineral springs are located. The design concept "alba" means sunrise and combines Vietnamese traditions with Japanese health philosophies. A 2000 square meter Onsen & Spa Center and a deep relaxation through a seven-level Japanese bathing ceremony ensure your inner balance and rest at the highest level. A healthy and balanced diet through your own "alba biofarm" also contributes to your holistic well-being. Pleasant natural sounds and materials, which harmonize perfectly with the green environment, reflect freshness, activity and new beginnings. Overnight in Hue.

Day 4 With meditation, yoga and wellness to yourself

Without tour guide and chauffeur

This day is all about the holistic wellness concept. Let yourself fall and rediscover your inner self. Relax with wellness treatments, take a bike ride into the area and increase your energy through health-conscious nutrition. Enjoy the day and reconcile your healthy body and fresh mind. Overnight in Hue.

Day 5 Exciting Bike Tour in and Around Hue

Enjoy an extensive breakfast and strengthen yourself for today. Your bicycle tour will take you past almost endless rice fields to the impressive Gia Long Tombs. Via Hue’s famous Le Loi Street, you will first pass the French architecture-built Quoc Hoc High School, where many of the country’s well-known personalities, including Ho Chi Minh, were educated. Passing the Khai Dinh tomb with its striking mosaics, you will arrive at Da Ke Handwerkerdorf, where bamboo has always been processed. From here, take a boat across the reservoir to the Gia Long Tombs - well known for its exceptional design and beautiful location. Continue your tour along the pine-lined north bank of the Perfume River and take a ferry to the graves of Thieu Tri, the third king of the Nguyen Dynasty. Continue to the Tu Duc Tombs of the King, who was known for his poetry and chose the site in his honor in asymmetrical forms. A village nearby specializes in the production of incense sticks and bronze casting. After your visit to this village, you will return to Hue where you will be taken to your hotel. Overnight in Hue.

Day 6 Exotic Cooking Class at Tha Om Garden House

Today, a cooking class takes place at the Tha Om Garden House. Among the locals, this place is known as "nha vuon", named after one of Hues' most famous architects. This house is built in the traditional style and has a large and attractive garden with connections to the old royal imperial court. Traditional architecture has long been associated with the natural environment and the ancient beliefs of Feng Shui. During a two-hour course, you will be shown the right cooking techniques and you will learn a lot of interesting things about the different ingredients. Then prepare a selection of Vietnamese dishes, which you can literally melt on your tongue afterwards. Then return to your hotel, where you can arrange the afternoon according to your wishes. Overnight in Hue.

7th day of free time at Alba Wellness Resort

Without tour guide and chauffeur

This day is yours. Start the day with a contemplative meditation and relax in the hotel’s onsen. Lose yourself in the here and now and enjoy the sweet nothing-doing. Overnight in Hue.

Day 8 Over the Cloud Pass to Danang & first-class wellness hotel

After breakfast, leave Hue and continue south on one of Vietnam’s most scenic routes. The so-called “Cloud Pass” offers beautiful views of dreamlike beaches. It is an absolute must for any traveler to cross this beautiful pass with a height of 496m and a length of 20 km. On the left you look down to the sea, on the right into the bizarre, up to 1,000 m high mountains that form the weather divide in central Vietnam. Along the way, stop at the famous Marble Mountains as well as the Cham Museum (the museum is closed on Mondays) before being taken to your hotel in Danang. The first-class luxury hotel Fusion Maia Resort is a perfect refuge for those seeking recreation. It is the first all-inclusive wellness hotel in Asia and provides an unforgettable stay with its exclusive care by the Fusionistas. All wellness treatments are included in the price – whether you want to do something good with an aroma flower bath, a Thai massage or a body scrub: two treatments are guaranteed per day, and you can continue to be pampered depending on the availability of the therapists. During your stay, you will enjoy the luxury of a private villa with its own pool. Overnight in Danang.

Day 9: A Feast for the Senses

Aromatic plants have been used in Vietnam for thousands of years as a traditional remedy to improve health and well-being. Experience the fragrant power of essential oils for body, mind and soul and learn everything you need to know from your personal aromatherapy expert at today’s workshop. As a yoga teacher, chemist and trained aromatherapist for over 10 years, your expert will tell you the history of aromatherapy, essential oils and how they are used effectively. Every odor and oil has a different use and effect on you and your body. Find out for yourself and make your own unique essential oil. Then return to your hotel, where you can end the day contemplatively. Overnight in Danang.

10.-12. Day Regeneration of Body, Mind and Soul

Without tour guide and chauffeur

These days are all about rest and relaxation. Let the soul dangle and enjoy moments when time seems to stop. You have the opportunity to explore the area on your own. The “all-round wellness package” of the Fusion Maia Resort leaves nothing to be desired. Listen to the sound of the sea and enjoy the inner peace of your being. Overnight stays in Danang.

13th day home

Your unforgettable journey to yourself is coming to an end, being picked up by the hotel and taken to the airport from where you will take your home or onward flight. Even if your time out from everyday life is soon over – your inner peace and your satisfaction will accompany you for a long time. Look forward to letting your friends and family share your experiences in the distance at home.

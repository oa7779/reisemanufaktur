Luxury Tour - Nepal in Style

Nepal Travel: Get inspired by this Nepal Travel Inspiration In Style to your own trip with Asia Special Tours. Ask directly here.

Luxurious hotels, the finest restaurants, breathtaking mountain panoramas and exclusive experiences: let yourself be pampered all around on your Nepal trip. With your private chauffeur and guide, discover the fascinating diversity of this unique country – from the cultural treasures of the Kathmandu Valley to the impressive mountains of the Himalayas to the natural wonders of Chitwan National Park. You will explore major attractions such as Durbar Square with its more than 50 temples and monuments, the Swayambhunath Temple – one of the oldest Buddhist temples in the world – and Pashupatinath, one of the most sacred Hindu pilgrimage sites. In Pokhara you can enjoy the idyllic atmosphere on Phewa Lake and spectacular views of the Annapurna mountain range. A special highlight of your trip is staying at the exclusive Shinta Mani Mustang, an exceptional luxury retreat amidst the original Himalayan landscape. Here you will experience the fascinating culture of the Mustang region and impressive mountain panoramas. A private safari in search of the inhabitants of the jungle, a cooking class with a local family and special experiences in the fascinating mountain world of Nepal make this trip an unforgettable experience.

1st day Arrival in Kathmandu

Namaste and welcome to Nepal! Your journey begins in Kathmandu, the capital of Nepal. After landing in Kathmandu, you will be picked up by your driver and taken to your hotel. The rest of the day is freely available to you. Your luxurious accommodation for the next three nights is the magical Dwarika’s Hotel in the heart of Kathmandu. Built in the authentic Newari style, the Heritage House looks almost like a museum to its guests. The spacious 87 rooms and suites are furnished with furniture handmade for the house and beautiful wood carvings on the windows, combining homely coziness with noble elegance. The beautiful courtyard lures with a pool and in the spa you relax with a selection of soothing treatments. Of course, your taste buds in Dwarika’s will also get their money’s worth. Thanks to its very special flair, excellent service and ideal location, Dwarika’s Hotel is the best address in Kathmandu. In the evening, enjoy a six-course meal at Krishnarpan, Dwarika’s Hotel’s specialty restaurant. Overnight in Kathmandu.

Day 2 Explore the most beautiful sides of Kathmandu

Start the day with a visit to Swayambhunath Temple, one of the oldest Buddhist temples in the world. The inner buildings of the sanctuary are said to be about 2,500 years old. Afterwards we will visit the historic old town of Kathmandu, in whose heart is one of the most important cultural landmarks of the city with the magnificent Durbar Square. Where the trade routes once crossed, today you will find over 50 temples and monuments. Especially stand out the temple of the goddess Kumari and the royal palace. From here you will later take a beautiful rickshaw ride through narrow alleys to Thamel, during which you can observe the local life up close. Use some free time there to look around on your own while strolling through the busy district. Later you will meet your guide again and discover Patan, the second of the three fascinating royal cities in the Kathmandu Valley. In the central Patan Durbar Square you will find a collection of finely crafted pagoda temples and stone statues. The former palace of the Malla kings as well as the historical stone baths are surrounded by numerous legends and tell exciting stories from times past. Overnight in Kathmandu. 

3rd day time for interesting encounters

Together with your guide, you will first head to one of the most sacred Hindu pilgrimage sites: Pashupatinath. You can view the complex from the eastern bank of the Bagmati, as entering the temple is strictly forbidden to all non-Hindus. Behind the temple, you will have the opportunity to see the place where the cremation takes place and get in touch with sadhus – Hindu wandering monks. Then visit the 36-meter-high Boudhanath Stupa, one of the most important Buddhist pilgrimage sites in the country. In one of the nearby monasteries you can get in touch with one of the lamas and receive his blessing. To get a lot of new impressions, continue to the old royal city of Bhaktapur. Together with your guide, you will explore the central square of the city. At Pottery Square, you will learn about the ancient craftsmanship of pottery. In addition, you will have the opportunity to try pottery yourself. In the evening, make your way to a local family where you are invited to dinner. Overnight in Kathmandu.

Day 4 - Continue to Pokhara

After breakfast, continue your journey to Pokhara, a place of remarkable natural beauty. The charming city has several beautiful lakes and offers a breathtaking panoramic view of the Annapurna mountain range. The tranquility of the lakes and the splendor of the Himalayas behind you creates a special ambience that has made Pokhara a popular place to relax and enjoy the beauty of nature. Your accommodation for the coming two nights is Dorje's Resort & Spa. Nestled in the green hills above Pokhara, this exclusive eco-luxury boutique resort offers fantastic views of Phewa Lake and the surrounding Himalayan landscape. The 18 spacious rooms and suites have been designed with local natural materials such as wood and stone, combining authentic Nepalese architecture with modern comfort. Thanks to its tranquil location, above the hustle and bustle of Lakeside Pokhara, the resort is an ideal retreat for recreation seekers and nature lovers. Upon arrival, the rest of the day is freely available to you. Overnight in Pokhara.

5th Day World Peace Pagoda with Spectacular Views

After breakfast, you will be picked up and drive together with your guide to the Pumdikot Shiva Statue. From here you can enjoy a breathtaking 360-degree panoramic view of the Pokhara Valley in clear weather. Then continue on to the World Peace Pagoda. The huge pagoda stands on a hill on the southern shore of Phewa Lake and is not only itself an impressive sight, but also offers spectacular views of the Annapurna chain, Manaslu chain, Dhaulagiri chain, Phewa Lake and the city of Pokhara. Afterwards, you can continue to enjoy the scenic beauty while walking down to the picturesque Phewa Lake. Then take a boat tour on Phewa Lake and visit Taal Barahi Temple, located on an island in the lake. In the evening you are invited to a delicious dinner at the Med5 Restaurant. Overnight in Pokha-ra. 

Day 6 Spectacular Mountain Worlds and Arrival at Shinta Mani Mustang

Today, your journey continues into the fascinating Mustang region – a remote Himalayan valley with original landscape, centuries-old culture and spectacular mountain panoramas. The trip to Mustang is an impressive experience through the mountains of Nepal. Please note that road conditions, especially in this region, are not comparable to European standards. Upon arrival in Jomsom, you will be welcomed by your guide and escorted to Shinta Mani Mustang. This extraordinary resort is located at an altitude of around 2,800 meters and is one of the few places in Nepal from which the gigantic Himalayan peaks can be admired up close. Below the resort flows Kali Gandaki, which makes its way through one of the deepest gorges in the world. After checking in to your suite, you can arrive at peace of mind. Let yourself be pampered with soothing applications in the resort spa. For traditional healing methods and authentic well-being, you can take advantage of Amchi’s wellness offerings (experienced practitioners of Tibetan and Himalayan medicine). Overnight in Mustang.

7th day Cultural encounters and nature experiences

Today you will discover the fascinating surroundings of Mustang. Your day starts with a visit to the traditional Thini Village at an altitude of around 2,900 meters. Surrounded by a spectacular mountain backdrop, you will get authentic insights into the everyday life and centuries-old traditions of the local population. Afterwards, a hike leads to the idyllic Dhumba Lake and the nearby Khutsab Terenga Monastery. The trail opens up beautiful views of the Kali-Gandaki Valley, one of the deepest valleys in the world. In the afternoon, explore the charming Marpha Village, known for its traditional stone houses, apple plantations and special atmosphere. Stroll through the narrow, cobbled alleys, discover local craftsmanship and be enchanted by the mix of Buddhist culture and Nepalese village tradition. For lunch, a very special experience awaits you: with Ms. Kamala, a renowned Thakali chef and committed personality of the region, you will enjoy an authentic Thakali menu at her kitchen table and get to know Mustang's culinary specialties. After further exploration of the monasteries, orchards and historical alleys, you will return to your resort with many special impressions. Overnight in Mustang.

Day 8 Excursion to Lubra

Depending on the weather and your personal preferences, today’s excursion will take you to the remote village of Lubra, one of the few surviving Bon villages in the region. Along the impressive Panda Khola River Gorge, you will wander through the fascinating Himalayan landscape to this special place. A local guide will take you on a tour of the village and show you, among other things, the old Gompa, a Buddhist monastery. The hike then leads to another cloister where you will learn about Lubra’s history and the deep-rooted customs of the Bon community. For lunch, enjoy local specialties at a cozy tea shop before heading out to the ruins of an old fortress in the afternoon. From there, impressive views of the valley and the surrounding mountains open up. To conclude your eventful tour, visit a traditional apple garden in the village. Enjoy a freshly squeezed apple juice before returning to your hotel by bicycle or vehicle. Overnight in Mustang.

9th day Chhyama Lake – a hidden jewel

Today, a special nature experience awaits you. In ideal weather conditions, the path will take you through an impressive mountain landscape with spectacular views of the surrounding peaks and valleys. Each step opens up new fascinating perspectives on the majestic Himalayan backdrop. The mountain lake Chhyama impresses with its calm, almost untouched atmosphere. On the lakefront, a stylishly prepared alpine picnic awaits you, arranged by your personal guide and team. Enjoy a delicious lunch in the middle of this unique mountain world and toast with a refreshing glass of wine. After this special time out in nature, hike back to the pick-up point. An unforgettable day full of tranquility, impressive countryside and extraordinary Himalayan moments sounds relaxed. Overnight in Mustang.

Day 10: Tibetan Heritage and Hidden Treasures of the Mustang Valley

After breakfast, you will start your discovery tour with a journey of about one hour to the historic village of Jharkot at 3,519 m altitude. The traditional Tibetan village impresses with its peaceful atmosphere as well as the important old Gompa. From Jharkot you will wander through the picturesque valley, past centuries-old orchards and over traditional suspension bridges. The trail will take you up to the historic village of Jhong, where you will visit a traditional residence and learn about the people’s original life. For lunch, a menu of the Shinta Mani kitchen team awaits you. Then you have the choice: you can experience the landscape on a mountain bike descent or easily drive on to Kagbeni by jeep. There you will visit the important Buddhist monastery Kag Chode Thupten Samphel Ling. Overnight in Mustang.
Optional: Pilgrimage to Muktinath
For those interested in culture, an early tour to the sacred pilgrimage site of Muktinath offers a unique opportunity to immerse yourself in Nepal’s spiritual traditions and symbolically cleanse yourself from sins at the world-famous 108 water fountains.

Day 11 Return to Pokhara

After breakfast at the hotel, you will return to Pokhara today, taking you through the varied landscape of Nepal and offering beautiful insights into nature and rural life in the region. Upon arrival in Pokhara you will move into your hotel. The rest of the day is at your disposal to relax, re-enact the impressions of the past days or enjoy Pokhara at your own pace. In the evening, a joint dinner awaits you at the hotel. Overnight in Pokhara.

12th day boat trip on the Rapti River

The jungle is calling! After breakfast, you will drive to Chitwan – one of the largest jungle and forest areas in Nepal. The park with its swamps and salt forests, which are crossed by rivers and watercourses, has been a UNESCO World Heritage site since 1984. It houses unique ecosystems and is home to more than 55 species of mammals, 525 bird and 50 amphibious species. The park is particularly well known for its population of Indian rhinos and with a lot of luck you might even discover one of the rare Bengal tigers. You will spend the next two nights in Meghauli Serai in the middle of Chitwan National Park. The luxury lodge blends harmoniously into the surroundings, combining noble and chic design with warm pastels and understated natural motifs. After lunch at the resort, take a boat trip on the scenic Rapti River. Enjoy the beautiful landscape around you while looking for the animal inhabitants of the National Park. Afterwards you will experience an atmospheric sundowner at the picturesque confluence of Rapti and Narayani rivers. A rustic dinner rounds off the day perfectly. Enjoy a mix of Nepalese cuisine and barbecue specialties and let yourself be carried away with traditional Tharu dances together with other guests. Overnight in Chitwan.

Day 13: Experiential Day at Chitwan National Park

This morning, fortify yourself with coffee and biscuits before heading out for a private jungle walk through the jungle. Always keep your camera at hand, because with a little luck you will have the opportunity to observe a rhino at close range. It's a really moving moment. After lunch at the resort, another highlight of your trip awaits you: a Jeep safari. You might even spot one of the majestic royal tigers. During the following walk through the nearby Tharu village, you will have the opportunity to catch a glimpse of the traditional village life of this people. Your experienced nature guide will be happy to help you with your understanding. Back at the resort, the impressive day will end with an exclusive candlelight dinner for two. Enjoy the magical atmosphere, the culinary delights and let the experiences of the day pass together. Overnight in Chitwan.

Day 14 Back to the Capital

After a final walk through the jungle, your trip will take you back to the country’s capital. The rest of the day is freely available to you. Use the time for further exploration on your own or relax in your beautiful hotel. Overnight in Kathmandu.

15th day Farewell to Nepal

Even the most beautiful journey comes to an end. During the last few days, you have gathered many unique impressions of the diversity of Nepalese culture and cuisine and can feel almost royal in your noble hotels. The suitcases full of exclusive experiences and lasting memories will take you to the airport, where you will travel home.

Australia Car Rental Tour - Melbourne, Sydney and Great Barrier Reef

Australia car rental trip with Asia Special Tours. Experience the most beautiful sides of Australia from Melbourne to Sydney to Queensland.

Itinerary & services

English itinerary text is an offline machine translation of the German original for this team preview. Please confirm travel details with your advisor; the German source remains available through the language switch.

Majestic landscapes, endless secluded beaches and a unique flora and fauna await the visitor to Australia. It is the differences that fascinate: the vibrant, colorful life in the metropolises, the endless sandy beaches and the deep blue waters of the Pacific. Melbourne is a cosmopolitan metropolis. Here a lively and modern scene has established itself, setting trends in architecture, design, art and gastronomy. Sydney, with its natural harbor basin, is one of the most beautiful cities in the world. We have extraordinary highlights such as a backstage visit to the Opera in Sydney or a balloon ride to the sunrise on the Yarra River near Melbourne for you in the program. Combine these two metropolises with a drive along the east coast from Brisbane to Cairns, which has highlights such as Fraser Island – the largest sand island in the world, the Whitsunday Island Archipelago with its white beaches, the Great Barrier Reef and the tropical rainforest. As a self-driver, you travel individually, flexibly and independently. We book the most beautiful accommodation for you in advance.

1st day Arrival in Melbourne

You will be received at the airport in Melbourne and taken to the hotel. The rest of the day is at your disposal. Arrive and relax from the long flight journey. We recommend a walk through the bustling center of the big city or a visit to the impressive aquarium with its huge shark pool. Overnight in Melbourne.

2nd Day City Tour and Excursion to Phillip Island

Today you will experience two attractions in one day. On the city tour you will get to know the highlights of Melbourne in a relaxing and fun way. Your guide will dive into the architecture and bring the history of this city to life. Enjoy a walk through the park, visit the Cathedral (if allowed) and visit Eureka Skydeck 88, a skyscraper with all-round views of the city. The tour to Phillip Island fills the rest of the day. You’ll see koalas up close in their natural environment and arrive at the Penguin Parade Visitor Center early enough to witness the arrival of the dwarf penguins. Overnight in Melbourne.

3rd day balloon ride over the Yarra River or Hidden Secrets of Melbourne

If you wish, today you will have the opportunity to visit a very special attraction – a balloon ride through the wine region of the Yarra River. It goes before sunrise to the gates of the city, where the hot air balloon is waiting for you. Through the fresh morning air, you can see from a bird’s eye view the fertile hills and valleys of the Yarra River Valley, one of Australia’s most famous wine regions. Enjoy the breathtaking view.
Alternatively, today you can discover the creative scene of the city. In the numerous arcades and hidden alleys of the city center, you will discover with your guide the most innovative shops and restaurants in the Australian metropolis. When it comes to fashion, design, architecture and cuisine, Melbourne, often even more than Sydney, sets the standards in Australia. Enjoy a light lunch on this guided and extraordinary walk through a vibrant big city. Overnight in Melbourne.

Day 4 Excursion to the Great Ocean Road

Today, take a guided excursion to the dramatic cliffs of the Great Ocean Road west of Melbourne and admire unique rock needles separated from the coast in the middle of the sea. In former times, many ships have run aground here. Look for very special animals such as whales, dolphins, echidnas and bandicoots. You’ll have time to stroll along the beaches or have a coffee in the impressive Split Point lighthouse and enjoy the impressive scenery. You’ll stop at Apollo Bay, a quiet coastal town amidst the rolling hillside scenery, and find a selection of cafes and restaurants where you can stop for lunch (not included).
We offer exclusive access to an ancient eucalyptus forest trail located on the Great Ocean Road EcoLodge where you can meet koalas, kangaroos and native birds. As our guest, you will contribute to the Great Ocean EcoLodge conservation program to protect the native Tiger Quolls, Koalas and unique flora of the region. A special day crowned by a visit to the world-famous rock formation of the Twelve Apostles and Loch Ard Gorge. Overnight in Melbourne.

5th day flight from Melbourne to Sydney

Today we will take you from your hotel to Melbourne Airport, where you will board a domestic flight to Sydney. In Sydney, you will be picked up and taken to your hotel in the center.
The rest of the day is at your disposal to experience the world city of Sydney. We recommend a walking tour in the historic district “The Rocks” or a ride on the viewing tower “Sydney Tower” in the heart of the city with a view of the natural harbor. Overnight in Sydney.

6th day Sydney in the luxury classic car of 1964

Your day starts at 9.30 a.m., where you’ll be greeted by your local guide and his buddy Horace – a 1964 vintage car and the most popular luxury car ever made in Australia. Stylishly cruise through Sydney, with the beguiling scent of fine leather in your nose, leaning back into soft lambskin seat covers.
Start in the center with a ride around “The Rocks”. Thanks to your guide, you can discover a thriving inner-city scene where Aboriginal murals and contemporary street art decorate the alleys with turn-of-the-century terrace houses. Get ready for the stories of rising home prices, local architecture, Aboriginal lifestyle and culture. No trip to Sydney would be complete without a visit to the beaches. First, we will take you to a cemetery by the sea that offers breathtaking views of the sandstone coast. From there, you’ll head to three of Sydney’s most famous inner-city beaches: Bronte, Tamarama and Bondi. Cruise through the hills of Sydney’s most exclusive suburbs and arrive at your final destination: a secluded beach with panoramic views of the harbor! Overnight in Sydney.

7th day backstage at Sydney Opera House and flight safari to Cottage Point

You have to get up early because today you can feel like a star of the famous Sydney Opera House. You get admission to the exclusive backstage rooms otherwise reserved for the stars. You will be on stage where celebrities such as Dame Joan Sutherland, Blondie, Pavarotti and Australian Idols have performed. Feel the glamour in the wardrobes and hear the events that take place behind the curtain. A Green Room breakfast reserved for artists and staff is included (closed on Sundays and public holidays). With a seaplane we will take you from Sydney to a quiet bay in 20 minutes to the excellent Cottage Point Inn. This fine, small restaurant is located right on the waterfront in Ku-Ring-Gai Chasing National Park and is known for its exceptional cuisine using Australian specialties. Enjoy a relaxed 3-course lunch before boarding your flight back to Sydney Harbour. Along the way, you’ll have a unique opportunity to take great photos of Harbour Bridge and the Opera House. Overnight in Sydney.

Day 8 Excursion to the Blue Mountains

Today we head west to the Featherdale Wildlife Park, where you get close to the typical Australian wildlife. Today’s destination is the Blue Mountains (World Heritage Site), a more than 1000 m high sandstone plateau with deep gorges and waterfalls. The name comes from the bluish haze that typically lies over the dense eucalyptus forests of the national park. At the Wentworth Falls, explore the Princess Lookout with fantastic views of the trees.
At Echo Point with the world-famous rock formation “The Three Sisters”, visit the Waradah Aboriginal Centre, a cultural center of native Aboriginal tribes. Afterwards we will visit the Govetts Leap viewpoint and enjoy a delicious lunch in a historic inn. The journey continues to Mt Banks and the Mt Tomah Botanical Garden. After crossing the Hawkesbury River, you will find a short stop in the country town of Windsor before returning to the City via Harbour Bridge. Overnight in Sydney.

9th day flight from Sydney to Brisbane

Today we will take you from your hotel to Sydney Airport, where you will board a domestic flight to Brisbane. In Brisbane you will be picked up and taken to your hotel.
The rest of the day is at your disposal to arrive in peace before the round trip begins the next day. Explore South Bank with its cultural centers, locations and galleries. Overnight in Brisbane.

10th day Brisbane to Noosa: In the Beerburrum State Forest and inland lakes - 145 km

Today begins your 14-day car rental tour along the east coast of Australia. During the next 2 weeks, this trip will take you through coastal and port cities, rainforests, spectacular national parks and tropical islands all the way to Cairns and up to the tropical north of Queensland. Take over your rental car today. The size and category of the vehicle will be determined at your booking according to your individual wishes. Once you drive along the Bruce Highway, you’ll reach the Beerburrum State Forest Sanctuary with the Glasshouse Mountains. The coastal road takes you north, passing the beaches Marcoola, Coolum, Peregian and Marcus Beach. Noosa is a place that undoubtedly offers you holidays in a stylish setting. Surrounded by the sea, the river and the rainforest, you can enjoy the beautiful Noosa Beach and relax. But you can also spend the day on a relaxing river cruise on the Noosa River and glide by boat through the inland lake system to the waters of the Everglades. Overnight in Noosa.

Day 11 Noosa to Fraser Island - 199 km

Today’s ride will take you north to the world’s largest sand island – Fraser Island. Fraser Island is a place of extraordinary beauty with its long sandy beaches and the adjacent strikingly colored sand cliffs and the over 100 partially crystal clear, deep blue freshwater lakes, always surrounded by dazzling white sandy beaches.
Board the catamaran and transfer to Fraser Island. In the meantime, your rental car will stop in a guarded parking lot in River Heads. Arriving on Fraser Island, check in at Kingfisher Bay Resort. The rest of the day is at your free disposal. This afternoon you can explore the island on your own. There are a number of short hiking trails around the resort. As soon as it gets dark, the native, nocturnal animals of Fraser Islands show up. You will have the opportunity to take part in a guided night tour with an experienced ranger and get the chance to see some of the very rare animals such as the ‘Acid Frog’ or the insect-eating bat around the resort’s lakes. Overnight on Fraser Island.

12th day Fraser Island, the largest sand island in the world

Today is free to explore Fraser Island. The island offers a rich variety of untamed wilderness and beauty. The dingo, Australia’s well-known wild dog, is at home on Fraser Island. The dingoes are called one of the purest races in the world. Fraser Island is home to 354 documented bird species, making the island a paradise for birdwatchers. Recommended is a visit to the Maheno shipwreck as well as the colored sands of the Pinnacles, Eli Creek and Lake Mackenzie. On the various freshwater lakes of the island, deep blue, clear water meets fine sand that is so incredibly white that it almost blinds the eyes. All these sights can be seen on a booked all-wheel drive tour. Fraser Island’s diverse wildlife also includes the humpback whales that migrate along the coast between August and October. During this time of year, many tours are offered to observe the whales from boats. Overnight on Fraser Island.

Day 13: Fraser Island via sugar cane fields and orchards to Agnes Water - 261 km

After arriving back at River Heads by ferry this morning, head along the Isis Highway to Agnes Water. Bundaberg is surrounded by a large number of sugar cane fields. Make a short stop in Bundaberg, home to the famous Bundaberg rum. In the Bundaberg Rum Bondstore you can not only see how the rum is made, but also taste the rum (optional). Finally, continue on to Agnes Water. Agnes Water are lovely coastal towns, with a wonderful beach, the northernmost surfing beach on the east coast. The natural beauty of the region and the beaches invites you to a well-deserved break. Overnight in Agnes Water.

Day 14 - Agnes Water to Barmoya with stop at Rockhampton - 264 km

There is plenty to see and experience on today’s route, so we recommend a departure from Agnes Waters as early as possible so you have enough time on the way. Rockhampton, or Rocky as it is affectionately known by the inhabitants, is also known as the “Cattle Capital” of Australia, with an estimated number of over 2.5 million cattle in the area surrounding the city! The remarkable historical architecture of Rockhamton is revealed in many public and private buildings, giving the city a unique streetscape. The Rockhampton Botanical Garden is considered one of the most beautiful gardens in Australia. It is over 130 years old and has recently been listed as a monument. Within the garden is also the Rockhampton Zoo, which is home to a variety of native animals. Once you’re ready, leave Rockhampton and follow the road to Henderson Park Farm Retreat, their current accommodation in Barmoya. The family business Henderson Park is a huge cattle farm with more than 1200 cattle and is located about 30 minutes north of Rockhampton. Overnight in Barmoya.

Day 15: Barmoya via Mackay to Airlie Beach, the gateway to the Whitsunday Islands - 473 km

This morning, drive north from Barmoya on the Bruce Highway to Mackay (312 km). The beautiful tropical city of Mackay is excellent for a well-deserved break after the long car journey. The many beaches and parks invite for a leisurely walk. You might also want to have a picnic on the river or a lunch at the port.
Leave Mackay and continue on the Bruce Highway to Airlie Beach and Shute Harbour. Airlie Beach is located on the Whitsunday Passage and is the gateway to the wonderful island world of the Whitsundays, with its total of 74 islands. Stroll through the streets of the village and along the artificially created lagoon, where you can swim without danger. This lagoon is located in a beautiful park with views over the bay. With its many restaurants and cafes and not least because of its unique location, Airlie Beach is one of the most popular holiday destinations in Australia. Overnight in Airlie Beach.

Day 16 Airlie Beach and the Dreamlike Whitsunday Islands

Today you are free all day to enjoy the Whitsundays. We recommend you take a sailing trip and spend the day on one of the islands (optional). One of the most popular excursion destinations is Whitehaven Island with the world famous Whitehaven Beach, one of the most photographed beaches in Australia. Crystal clear water washes around the almost untouched quartz beach and offers a wonderful experience. South of Airlie Beach is Conway National Park, known for its impressively beautiful scenery. In addition, you can often see rock wallabies and a variety of butterflies here. Overnight in Airlie Beach.

Day 17 - Airlie Beach to Townsville - 280 km

Leave Airlie Beach on the Bruce Highway heading north. If you want to take a detour, stop at Bowen, about 40 minutes north of Airlie Beach. Here the scenes of the film “Australia” with Hugh Jackman and Nicole Kidman were shot. From Bowen, the highway continues to the twin towns of Home Hill and Ayr. If you’re interested in butterflies, shells and bugs, the House of Australian Nature on Wilmington Street is the place to be. Townsville is just 87 km north of it. Townsville is home to the Research Center for Marine Biology and the headquarters of the Great Barrier Reef Marine Park Authority. We recommend a visit to the Great Barrier Reef Marine Wonderlands, located at the end of Flinders Street next to Ross Creek. However, visiting Townsville would be incomplete if you missed the 290-meter-high Castle Hill, at the highest point of which there is a viewing observatory. Enjoy the panorama at the top of the hill, where you’ll see the suburbs stretching in all directions and Magnetic Island popping up on the horizon. Overnight in Townsville.

Day 18: Townsville to Mission Beach via the Tully River - 235 km

Continue north on the Bruce Highway towards Ingham. Just outside the city, you’ll find Tylo Wetlands, an oasis for over 200 bird species and other animals. There are extensive hiking trails that allow you to discover the park at your own pace. From Ingham, continue north to Cardwell, overlooking Hitchinbrook Island National Park. The next place is Tully, the place with the reputation of being the wettest in Australia. The Tully River is Australia’s most famous whitewater rafting region. From there it is only a short distance to the village of Mission Beach. Overnight at Mission Beach.

Day 19: Mission Beach at the Great Barrier Reef

In Mission Beach you can do countless things (optional). Visit the Great Barrier Reef or go fishing. For those who love the adventure, there is sea kayaking, white water rafting, diving equipment or try Sky Diving. With kilometers of hiking trails in the rainforest and endless golden beaches, you’ll want to have even more time to explore. Overnight at Mission Beach.

Day 20: Mission Beach to Port Douglas via Wooroonooran National Park and Eubenangee Swamp National Park - 234 km

You can either head straight to Innisfail or take a small detour to Wooroonooran National Park and try out the impressive Mamu Rainforest Canopy walking trail. It is a spectacular 350 m long hike – and suspension bridge path through the rainforest, with observation tower and various hiking trails. Then continue to the 1720-hectare Eubenangee Swamp National Park. This national park is one of the best ecosystems in tropical northern Queensland, with incredible bird watching and numerous rare plants. A 1 km long hiking trail gives you the opportunity to discover the area in an easy way. Your last stop will be the Babinda Boulders, a bathing place for tourists and locals, where you will also find some hiking trails. This place is of great importance for the Aboriginal people. Port Douglas is a famous resort with its emerald sea and golden sand beaches such as the “4 Mile Beach”. There is also a nice fishing port with a spacious pier and the marina is lined with small shops and cafes. Overnight in Port Douglas at a lodge.

21st day Port Douglas with boat ride to the Great Barrier Reef

Today you have the opportunity to experience the Great Barrier Reef (optional). Embark on the ship to get directly to the reef and discover the colorful underwater world of Earth’s largest living creature. You can snorkel, dive or get close to the reef’s fantastic fish and colorful corals in a glass boat. A unique experience. Overnight in Port Douglas at a lodge.

22nd day Port Douglas to Cape Tribulation: Unique jungle backdrop on the Daintree River, gorges and Aboriginal culture - 68 km

Today we go to Cape Tribulation! This morning at 10 a.m., the Dreamtime Gorge Walk is booked for you at the Mossman Gorge Center. It gives you intimate insights into the culture, spirituality and traditions of the Kuku Yalanji clan and their unique connection with their country. After a short drive you will reach the Mossman Gorge, which is a registered World Heritage Site. From there, continue on to the Daintree River. Daintree National Park is one of the largest rainforests in Australia. Since 1998, Daintree National Park and Cape Tribulation National Park have been listed as World Heritage Sites. Thanks to the tropical climate, mammoth trees, orchids, ferns and over 3,500 plant species thrive. You can find here a variety of bird and mammal species, reptiles and, of course, crocodiles. Another popular excursion destination in the region is Cape Tribulation – poet Urwald stretches for many kilometers and comes directly to white, sandy beaches. Crystal clear rivers, welcoming coves and picturesque freshwater bathing sites complete the scenic beauty of Cape Tribulation, a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Overnight in Mossman at a lodge.

23rd day Cape Tribulation to Cairns: Suspended railway in the jungle and tropical fauna and flora - 158 km

Today, drive from Cape Tribulation to Cairns, the center of Queensland’s tropical north. This route is one of the most picturesque panoramic routes in Australia. We recommend a stop at the “Rex Lookout”. Enjoy the magnificent view! Along the way to Cairns there are many small beaches, such as Wangetti Beach.
Next stop is in Kuranda, a small vibrant provincial town surrounded by protected rainforest. You will find markets, hiking trails, a butterfly reserve, a bird reserve and a village that exudes a wonderfully relaxed atmosphere in Kuranda. Kuranda is a popular excursion destination and many visitors take the well-known Kuranda panoramic train or the famous levitation railway (Skyrail), which offers fantastic views of the rainforest and coast. Take the winding road from Kuranda through the Kuranda Ranges to Smithfield and continue on to Cairns, the starting point for the Great Barrier Reef. Overnight stay in Cairns or follow-up program with further beach days in Palm Cove, Port Douglas or a rainforest lodge.

Day 24 Cairns

Today the program ends. Drop off your rental car at the airport and check in for your return or onward flight.

The Pearls of China

China Tour: Enjoy the pearls of China on a round trip with Asia Special Tours. Ask now and experience for yourself!

Itinerary & services

On this beautiful round trip you will experience the pearls of China up close – let yourself be enchanted by the fascinating past and the modern future of the country. Explore the cultural city of Beijing, take a walk on the Chinese wall, experience “old” China while visiting Pingyao and marvel at the famous terracotta warriors. The fascinating blend of tradition and modernity in cosmopolitan Shanghai will captivate you as you embark on a culinary voyage of discovery in Xiamen. The island of Gulangyu lures with enchanting gardens and European flair, while in Nanjing you can admire traditional Hakka roundhouses. Your journey will also take you to the area around Guilin, known for its picturesque Karstberg landscape. Loud domes, as far as the eye can see, each one wrapped in a delicate cloud veil. It is hard to find the right words for this scenery. Best of all: just be amazed! A visit to the bustling metropolis of Hong Kong completes this magical itinerary and gives you unforgettable impressions.

1st day Arrival in Beijing

Welcome to the capital Beijing, where the juxtaposition of hyper-modern architecture and old imperial splendor fascinate. Culture and history compete in the metropolis with mega-shopping and a colorful art scene for the attention of visitors. At the airport, your guide will welcome you and take you to your hotel. After a little refreshment, visit the old town (Hutongs) and experience old Beijing up close. There you can really immerse yourself in the everyday life of the Chinese residents, because because of the cramped living conditions, people transfer many activities to the alleys in front of the houses. Old men play Mah Jongg in the shadows, women clean their vegetables, the hairdresser works outside his shop and merchants do business on the street – just as if the neighborhood were one oversized community. Overnight in Beijing.

2nd Day Imperial Magnificent Buildings: Memory of Past Times

A day full of cultural treasures and remarkable art awaits you. The Temple of Heaven, to which you will first pay your visit, is one of the outstanding testimonies of religious architecture in the Middle Kingdom, from the Ming period (1368 to 1644). An exquisite example of China’s wooden buildings constructed without the help of a single nail is the 39-meter-high round hall of harvest victims. Its three-tiered roof, whose deep blue bricks symbolize the color of the sky, rests on 28 pillars, the four most powerful of which stand in the middle for the four seasons. On your tour you will also discover the center of Beijing, the famous “Forbidden City” with the Imperial Palace. This magical place was the seat of the Chinese emperors of two dynasties – the Ming and the Qing – and for half a millennium was inaccessible to those who did not belong to the court. The crowd is as overwhelming as the architecture, the colorfully painted palace halls, the pagoda-like roofs. Of course, you will also stroll over the Tiananmen Square – 500 meters wide and 880 meters long, the largest square in the world and a monument to the modern history of China that has been stoned since 1989. Overnight in Beijing.

Day 3 The Great Wall is waiting for you!

The most magnificent, most elaborately renovated sacred building in Beijing is the Lamatempel in the northeast of the old town. Originally the private residence of a prince, he was transformed into a monastery when the master of the house became emperor in 1723. In the middle of the 18th century, a center of Tibetan art and religion emerged here. Even today, the temple exudes a strong spiritual force. Just a few steps away, unnoticed by the noisy flow of visitors, the Temple of Confucius invites you to a tranquil tour. The colorful temple is a welcome oasis of silence in hectic Beijing. In the afternoon you will leave for Jinshanling. Ready for a mighty encounter? The most beautiful section of the Great Wall awaits you. To this day, many legends revolve around the spectacular building. Ask your guide about it. What could be better than a walk on the Great Wall at sunset? Overnight in Jinshanling.

4th Day Imperial Flair in Chengde

Early in the morning you will have a second opportunity to experience the Great Wall and all this alone and without a multitude of tourists. After an impressive morning walk on the Wall, continue your journey towards Chengde. Once there, stroll through the spacious summer residence of the Qing Emperor in the afternoon, China’s largest original imperial garden. Work on this overwhelming palace complex began in 1703 and was not completed until 1790. For a hundred years, the court regularly went to Chengde in summer and ruled the empire from there. Here you will find replicas of the gigantic Potala Palace from Lhasa as well as various Tibetan temples and monasteries. The palace complex is located on the northern edge of the city and is surrounded by an imposing ten kilometers long wall that winds over mountains and valleys. The 560-hectare complex includes palaces, temples, halls and pavilions, but also includes beautiful gardens. Overnight in Chengde.

Day 5 “Eight Outer Temples” and “Waschknüppelberg”

In the morning you will visit the “Eight Outer Temples” in Chengde, which together with the Summer Palace are on the UNESCO World Heritage List. Afterwards you climb with a chairlift the curious “Wasschknüppelberg”. Enjoy the view of the temples and marvel at the fascinating rock formation of the “Wasschknüppelberg”. In the afternoon, return to Beijing. Overnight in Beijing.

Day 6 - Express train to Pingyao & village of Zhangbi

Your journey will take you to Pingyao. After breakfast you will be driven to the train station, from where you will take the fast train to the ancient but very lively city. Here, instead of neon lamp signs on the houses, old commercial signs can still be found. In Pingyao, meet your guide and take a trip to the village of Zhangbi together, where you will visit a traditional courtyard. These courtyards are hundreds of years old and represent the typical North Chinese architectural style. Your guide will be happy to tell you more about this masterpiece of architecture. Overnight in Pingyao.

7th Day Historic Old Town of Pingyao: Feeling Ancient China

Pingyao is surrounded by the longest, fully preserved city wall from the Ming period. In the shadow of this more than six kilometers long city wall, one- and two-storey black brick houses duck. Only the stained roofs of the temples stand out. The entire old town of Pingyao was declared a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1997. Stroll through the alleys and admire the city’s picturesque Ming and Qing contemporary courthouses. Then it will be time again for the onward journey. Another fast train ride will take you to the old imperial city of Xi'an. The former capital of eleven dynasties was once the cultural, economic and political center of the Middle Kingdom. Today it is the seat of government of Shanxi Province and the largest city in northwest China. Overnight in Xi'an.

Day 8: Emperor's clay warriors

Immediately in the morning, the trip goes to Lintong, about 35 kilometers east. There are the famous terracotta warriors in line. This impressive tomb at the foot of Li Mountain was discovered by chance in 1974 and dates back to the time of the first emperor Qin Shihuangdi. So far, about 7,200 life-size figures of soldiers and horses have been uncovered – each a unique work of art, because no two warriors are identical. This masterfully designed army shows the troops in full battle order, allowing archaeologists to reconstruct their original lineup. Back in Xi'an, enjoy a bicycle tour along the well-preserved city walls. This originates from the Ming Dynasty and surrounds the city center in the form of a rectangle over a length of 14 kilometers. Although impressive, it seems rather modest compared to the Tang-era wall, as 78 square kilometers of the city, then called Chang’an, were protected. Overnight in Xi'an.

Day 9 - Highlights of Xi'an

Today you will explore Xi'an in all its facets. In the morning you will visit the impressive, ancient stelenwald. The name comes from the fact that here the inscribed stone blocks stand like trees in the forest. The museum now holds a total of over 3,000 steles (from the Han period to the beginning of this century). It is the largest of its kind in China. The museum is a real treasure trove of Chinese calligraphy, but also for many important classical works and documents. Then visit the Great Wild Goose Pagoda, an important Buddhist building from the 7th century. Here the writings of the monk Xuanzang have been preserved, and from the upper floors you have a magnificent view. In the afternoon, stroll through the trendy district "Tang Yuan", a park designed in the style of the Tang Dynasty. The harmonious architecture, gardens and small cafes create a relaxed atmosphere, ideal for a leisurely stroll. Overnight in Xi'an.

10th day glitter metropolis Shanghai & Bummel on the famous Bund

After breakfast, you will be taken to the airport for onward travel. Its destination is the metropolis of Shanghai, which is also called “Gateway to the World”, “Dragon’s Head Metropolis”, “Paris of the East” or “Pearl of the Orient”. After checking in at the hotel and a short refreshment break, you will immediately set out to explore the fascinating city with your guide. During a stroll on the Bund you get a good first overview. On Shanghai’s famous waterfront, old colonial buildings such as the legendary Bank of China (built in 1912) and the old “Peace Hotel” (1930) are nestled against the modern glass and steel towers. The promenade looks particularly impressive from the ship. During your trip on the Huangpu you can see for yourself. Overnight in Shanghai.

Day 11 Shanghai: A city with many facets

Your day begins with a stroll through the economic and high-tech center Pudong, the hallmark of the new Shanghai. Today, the Shanghai Tower, which was completed in 2015, rises 632 meters in height here. It is the second tallest building in the world. From the observation deck you have a sensational view of the city deep below you. An interesting contrast to the modern Pudong is Shanghai's old town. Stroll through the alleys, try a few traditional treats and enjoy the special flair. Continue to the trendy district of Tianzifang with its tangle of power cables over the alleys and the full clotheslines between the houses. The lane labyrinth around Taikang Lu has attracted artists, artisans and young designers. In the Jewish quarter of Shanghai, on the other hand, you walk in the footsteps of the Jews who fled Germany and Austria in the 20th century. In the evening, a very special experience awaits you: a bicycle tour in the former French concession. Your 3-hour tour will take you to the low-traffic areas with colonial streetscape. Let the lively metropolis come to you from a completely new perspective and gain a more intensive insight into everyday Chinese life. Overnight in Shanghai.

12th day excursion to the garden town of Suzhou and the water village of Tongli

Today you will take a trip to the garden city of Suzhou, which enchants with numerous canals and classic gardens. Through the gardens, Suzhou became famous and eventually developed into a cultural and intellectual center. The art of horticulture was to harmoniously design as many landscapes as possible in a confined space, offering both variety and opportunity for relaxation, but at the same time creating the feeling of size and expanse. Afterwards you will drive to the water village of Tongli, where everything still looks like in ancient China at times long gone. While you walk over small bridges, through winding alleys, past wind-leaning, gray-covered cottages, you look over dreamy canals on which junks swim. In the evening you will return to Shanghai. Overnight in Shanghai.

Day 13 Nanjing is waiting for you

After breakfast, leave Shanghai behind and fly south to Xiamen, a port city on the coast of Fujian province. After arriving at the airport, you will continue to Nanjing, about 160 kilometers west – a picturesque area known for its unique Hakka Tulou. These imposing circular clay buildings, which served as residential fortifications, are now UNESCO World Heritage sites and provide fascinating insights into the culture and community life of the Hakka people. In the late afternoon you will reach your hotel. After check-in, you can rest a little before having dinner and deepening your first impressions of this fascinating region. Overnight in Nanjing.  

Day 14 Tradition and History – Visiting the Hakka

Today, immerse yourself in the fascinating world of Hakka. During a full-day visit, you will explore the famous Tulou – impressive, round clay fortifications that once served as residential complexes for several families. These unique buildings are not only architectural masterpieces, but also a living testimony to the Hakka culture. The term "Hakka" literally means "guest people" - a term that refers to the eventful history of this people. Between the 10th and 17th centuries, many Hakka fled northern China from wars and natural disasters to the south and settled in remote areas such as Nanjing. In their new homeland, they saw themselves as guests ("Ha" in Mandarin), which finally gave them the name "Hakka". They are considered to be particularly tradition-bound and cultural pride – an aspect that you can experience in an impressive way in personal contact with the locals. In the evening, return to the hotel and end the day with a leisurely dinner. Overnight in Nanjing.

15th day European flair on the island of Gulangyu

In the morning you leave the Hakka area and return to Xiamen. At noon, take the ferry to the idyllic island of Gulangyu, the most popular excursion destination in the city. The car-free island enchants with its colonial charm, elegant villas and tropical vegetation – a place of tranquility and history, without any urban hustle. Together with your guide, you will explore the only 2.5 square kilometer island on foot. Strolling through narrow streets in the southern European style, you will discover elegant houses with manicured front gardens and enjoy the special atmosphere of this unique place. Take the cable car to Sunlight Rock, the highest point of the island. From here you can enjoy a breathtaking panoramic view over Gulangyu, the Xiamen coastline and the sea. Afterwards, visit Shuzhuang Garden, a private garden created in 1931 that is now a public gem. For lunch, stop at the lovingly restored house “Hai Tian Tang Guo” where you will taste local specialties. If you want, you can also find out more about traditional paper production here. In the evening, immerse yourself in the culinary heart of Xiamen: On a food tour through the lively Zhongshanlu pedestrian zone, you will discover various street food delicacies and regional treats. Overnight in Xiamen.  

16th day rice terraces at Longsheng

Your daily destination is Yangshuo. But also Longsheng with its rice terraces must not be missed. Enjoy on the ride this picturesque landscape with unique rock formations and rice fields. In Longsheng, visit the Longji terrace fields located between Heping Village and Ping’an Village, forming a unique attraction that shines in many colors. Rice fields, as far as the eye can see, up to a height of 880 meters. Like densely woven carpets, they cover the mountain slopes around the village of Ping'an in the southeast of Longsheng. The steps rise steeply to the sky. The first fields were created over 700 years ago. In the vernacular, the agricultural miracle is called “terraces of the dragon bone”. With their rice terraces, the locals created a landscape architecture that combines function and form, utility and aesthetics – more than almost any other cultural landscape. After a short hike in the rice terraces, continue your journey to Yangshuo. The road leads through picturesque fields and opens up a panorama of staggered mountain rows. Overnight in Yangshou.

17th day By bicycle and bamboo raft through the karst cone landscape

Get ready for a day full of varied impressions of the scenic landscape along the Yulong River. After breakfast, head to the village of Jiuxian, known for its beautiful Qing-era homes. Here you will also visit the ancestor temple of the Li family, which also dates back to this period. After this short journey back in time, stroll comfortably to the entrance of the traffic-calmed zone where your bikes are already waiting for you. Along the Yulong River you cycle through the idyllic landscape, where you can hardly see your fill. After about seven kilometers you reach the dock and climb on a bamboo raft. Sit back relaxed and let the scenery pass by. Seen from certain angles, the hills form shapes reminiscent of animals, pagodas or plants. This inspired the local population to give the mountains imaginative names, such as “Moonsichelberg”, “Elephant Nutsberg” or “Horse Painting Mountain”. Back on land, you can walk or cycle through the magical Karstberg landscape back to the parking lot, from where you will be brought to the hotel. Overnight in Yangshou.

18th day of free time in Yangshuo

Without guide and driver

Today is completely at your disposal – design it according to your own wishes.Yangshuo offers countless possibilities: Walk through the charming old town with its small cafés, shops and craft stands or enjoy the relaxed atmosphere with a tea with a view of the Karstbergs. No matter what you choose, today you will have time to experience the beauty of Yangshuo at your own pace. Overnight in Yangshou.

Day 19 Back to Guilin and on to Hong Kong

Your final destination on this trip is the Hong Kong megacity. After breakfast, make your way back to Guilin to travel to Hong Kong by fast train. At the train station you will be met by your German-speaking tour guide. Your chauffeur will take you to your hotel. The rest of the day is at your disposal. Overnight in Hong Kong. 

20th Day Hong Kong - City of Contradictions

Hong Kong is waiting to be discovered by you. In the morning, you will go on a discovery tour together with your guide. The driveway to the 373 meter high Victoria Peak with views of the world-famous Victoria Harbour and Kowloon is simply breathtaking. During the journey along the southern bays of Hong Kong and during a stay at the “Repulse Bay”, Hong Kong shows itself from a completely different side. The local scenery is more reminiscent of the French Riviera than a city of millions. During a visit to the “Swimming Village of Aberdeen” the ubiquitous contrasts of this city become even clearer: For generations, fishermen have lived here on their rather simple houseboats – overlooking the imposing skyline – and the lives of people on land seem to affect them very little. The afternoon is at your disposal for your own discoveries. Overnight in Hong Kong.

Day 21 Farewell to China and Hong Kong

Today, unfortunately, we say goodbye to a fascinating country. You will be driven to the airport from where you will start your onward or return journey.

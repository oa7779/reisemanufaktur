Exclusive Mongolia round trip

Explore the cultural treasures of Mongolia on an individual tour with Asia Special Tours. Now ask me.

Itinerary & services

English itinerary text is an offline machine translation of the German original for this team preview. Please confirm travel details with your advisor; the German source remains available through the language switch.

Mongolia is a country with many faces worth discovering – a mixture of infinite steppe, traditional cattle breeding and progressive urban life, identification with history and connection to modernity. Leave the hustle and bustle of civilization in favor of solitude in the steppes and deserts, watch wild horses in Hustai Nuruu National Park, hear the sand dunes of Khongoryn Els “sing” and get insights into the simple but nature-loving nomadic life. Sharpen your senses for wide steppes, deep gorges, gigantic sand dunes and untouched waters. Because in Mongolia you travel back to originality like nowhere else.

1st day Arrival in Ulaanbaatar

You arrive in Ulaanbaatar early in the morning. Your guide meets you in the airport arrivals hall and takes you to your hotel. After time to rest, your sightseeing begins at Sukhbaatar Square in the heart of the Mongolian capital. Two impressive monuments commemorate the conqueror Genghis Khan and the revolutionary leader Damddiny Sukhbaatar, who secured independence from China in 1921. You also visit Gandan Monastery. Built in the Tibetan style in 1835, it soon became a center of Buddhist learning in Mongolia. During the 20th century it played a special role: having survived the communist destruction of the 30s as one of the few undamaged monasteries, it was allowed to resume limited operations in 1944 as the only monastery in the country. In the afternoon, visit the Museum of History and Ethnography and the Winter Palace of Bogd Khan. Overnight in Ulaanbaatar.

2nd day White and red rock stupas

Your overland journey begins today. Following Mongolian tradition, travel with the sun, initially heading south into the Gobi Desert, the world's northernmost and fifth-largest desert. The road network has improved in recent years, and part of the journey follows the new highway from the capital into the Gobi. At some points you leave the paved road. Enjoy your first impressions of the wide-open landscapes along the way. After about 3½ hours, reach the small desert town of Mandalgobi for lunch and a glimpse into the daily lives of its roughly 10,000 residents. There are shops and simple local restaurants. In the afternoon, reach the mountains of Zagaan Suvarga. Because of their shapes and colors, local people call these impressive formations the white and red stupas. Centuries of erosion have created vast rocky cliffs in shades of pink, red, and orange. Stay in a yurt camp and admire the seemingly endless night sky. Overnight in the yurt camp.

Day 3 The Rock Drawings at Mount Del Uul

After breakfast, take a trip to Mount Del Uul, where you can admire rock drawings from the Bronze Age on four kilometers. Then continue your journey. The endless expanses of the desert and the solitude interrupted only once in a while by a few camels and goat breeders will make you feel like you are somewhere at the end of the world. On hot summer days, it may even happen that a “Fata Morgana” appears in front of you, but dissolves into thin air again at the next moment. Late in the evening you will reach your yurt camp in the middle of the desert, where you will spend the night. Overnight at the yurt camp.

Day 4 Excursion to the Vulture Gorge

A trip to the Vulture Gorge (Yol Gorge) is on your program today. This is located in the Gurwan Saikhan National Park, on the foothills of the Altai Mountains. After visiting a small natural history museum, you will explore the gorge on a short hike and, with a bit of luck, meet the Capricorns and wild sheep living here. After that, you will be returned to your yurt camp. Overnight in a yurt camp.

Day 5 The Sand Dunes of Khongoryn Els

After fortifying yourself at breakfast, set off to marvel at the longest walking dunes, the Khongoryn Els Sand Dunes in South Gobi. In the middle of the landscape, an extraordinary mountain of sand shoots up, which is up to 120 meters high and stretches over 180 km in length. Together with the dark mountain range in the background, there is a particularly beautiful panorama. Unforgettable in addition to this breathtaking sight will also be the meetings with camel breeders and, with a bit of luck, a gazelle herd. If you wish, you can also prove your camel riding talent today and rent one of the “desert ships” (for a fee). Overnight in a yurt camp.

6th Day Flaming Cliffs

During your journey to Bayanzag, we will be accompanied by a landscape of mountains, rocks and sand. We visit one of the most visited sights in Mongolia - the "flaming cliffs", which got their name from their bright orange color. In the 1920s, American scientist and adventurer Roy Chapman Andrews discovered a complete dinosaur nest with eggs here, which led to this place as well as the rest of Mongolia becoming an important place for paleontological research. Overnight in a yurt camp.

7th day Visit to Ongi Monastery

Your next destination is the ruins of the hidden monastery “Ongi”. The monastery once housed over 500 Buddhist monks and is located on the river of the same name. During religious persecutions in the 30In the years before, it was almost completely destroyed and many of the monks living there were sent to labor camps. Today there is a small, new monastery. With a little luck you will meet the monks who take care of the monastery and have the opportunity for a personal conversation. Overnight in a yurt camp.

8th Day Magical Orchon Valley

Today you leave the Gobi Desert and immerse yourself in the mountain steppe landscape of Khangai. In the late afternoon we approach the valley of the Orchon River, which is the longest river in Mongolia with a length of 1,124 kilometers. Overnight at the yurt camp.

Day 9 Khanghai – Orchon Waterfall – Zenkher

In the morning, the journey first leads 50 kilometers to the Orchon waterfall, which falls 24 meters into the depth in the midst of picturesque landscape. During the journey, you will pass mountain steppe landscapes and many yak as well as herds of horses accompanied by their breeders. Depending on your wishes, you can take a trip from here to the temple Tuvkhon, which you can reach after a four-kilometer hike on the top of a mountain. Your hike will be rewarded by a breathtaking view of the mountain landscape in front of you. Late in the evening you will arrive at the hot mineral springs of Zenkher, where you can enjoy a mineral bath under the starry sky when the weather is fine. Overnight at the yurt camp.

10th day Monasteries, craters and a lake

Your day begins with a visit to Zaya Gegeny Monastery in Zezerleg, about 24 km from Zenkher. Then continue to the crater of the extinct volcano Khorgo and the lake Terkhin Zagaan Nuur. The charming environment is perfect for hiking to your heart’s content. It is only about half an hour walk past cold, bizarre lava formations until you can look into the huge crater of the Khorgo. Overnight in a yurt camp.

Day 11: In the footsteps of Genghis Khan

As the sun rises over the infinite expanses of Central Mongolia, head towards Karakorum, under Genghis Khan and his successors capital of the Mongolian Empire. Scientific excavations repeatedly reveal new impressive relics of this multicultural trading metropolis founded in 1220 and the place is still one of the most famous and probably also worth seeing in the country. After visiting the excavation site and museum, you will visit the Erdene Zuu monastery built on the ruins of Karakorum in the 16th century, one of the largest in Mongolia. Unfortunately, only three of the formerly more than 60 buildings have been preserved after the Cultural Revolution, but with a total of 108 stupas and the white border wall, the monastery still offers an impressive picture. Extensive renovation work also helps to restore the shine of the building complex. A detailed tour will take you to the large prayer hall, various temple halls and last but not least through a museum dedicated to the complex. After the visits, continue to the Khogno Khan Mountains. Overnight at the yurt camp.

Day 12: Wild Horses at Hustai Nuruu National Park

In the morning, when climbing a sand dune, you will be amazed by the unique panorama of the steppe desert in front of you. With a little luck, you can also watch wildlife on a temporary lake. You also have the opportunity to hike up a mountain to enjoy the unique view. Afterwards, visit a small temple from the 17th century and leave this magical place in the afternoon. The destination of your day’s stage is the Hustai Nuruu National Park, where you can watch the wild horses Takhi towards evening. Overnight in a yurt camp.

Day 13 Return to Ulaanbaatar

Today your journey will take you back to the capital of Mongolia. The rest of the day is at your disposal and you can use it to stroll around the city or just relax. On request, the Zanabazar Art Museum can also be visited today. Overnight at the hotel in Ulaanbaatar.

14th day Departure from Ulaanbaatar

Today it is unfortunately to say goodbye – surely you have packed your suitcases with many great impressions. You will be driven to the airport from where you will start your return journey.

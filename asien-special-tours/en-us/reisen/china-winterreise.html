Winter travel through China

China Winter Tour: Experience the wonderfully wintery China on this individual China Tour with Asia Special Tours. We are happy to advise you!

Itinerary & services

English itinerary text is an offline machine translation of the German original for this team preview. Please confirm travel details with your advisor; the German source remains available through the language switch.

Experience the winter differently, that is possible in China. Discover the fascinating nature and different cities in the winter flair. With various activities such as ice fishing, skating or even skiing, you can experience the winter Wonderland of China with all the curiosities. Even the great art of ice carving is not denied you, at ice festivals show artfully carved temples and figures that appear stylishly lit in winter splendor. For China fans it is an absolute must to visit this country in an atypical season.

1st day Arrival in Beijing & Flight to the City of Ice

Welcome to China! Today you will first land in Beijing, from where you will take your onward flight to Harbin to the city of ice. Once there, you will already be received and brought to your hotel. The city impresses with architectural masterpieces surrounded by a Russian-looking aura. Some streets and corners are reminiscent of St. Petersburg. The city is in a harsh climate where it is often rainy or snowy. Every year, an ice and snow festival takes place in Harbin. There you can admire meter-high figures, temples and buildings made of ice, which are artfully lit in the dark and thus form a spectacular ambience made of ice. Overnight in Harbin.

2nd Day Morning Mood & Visit to Siberian Tiger Park

Start today with your guide first about the morning market. Your guide will show you some peculiarities and you will be able to observe the local population during their day shopping. Then you see a spectacle where it will get cold from watching. Many of the locals break up the ice of the pools early in the morning and open the winter swimming season. The gifted swimmers show the spectators impressive swimming and diving movements, a definitely unique experience. In order to warm you up again, then drive to the sunny island, which is used as a local recreational area of Harbin. Every year, the largest ice exhibition in the world takes place there. Marvel at the ornately carved figures and temples of ice and snow. Finally, visit Siberian Tiger Park, located on the north bank of the Songhua River northwest of Harbin. With an area of just under 1.5 million m2, it is the world’s largest nature park for wild Siberian tigers. As the natural habitat for the animals is becoming smaller, the Siberian tiger is considered to be in danger of extinction. At Siberian Tiger Park, this is being prevented by targeted breeding and resettlement programmes. Overnight in Harbin. 

3rd day birding at close range

Today you take an excursion by express train to Qiqihaer. There you will visit the nearby Zhalong Nature Reserve. It was established in 1976. Nine of the world’s 15 known crane species are protected in the natural area, many of which were or are threatened with extinction. The most famous of them is the Mandschurenkranich. Like giant dragons, they glide with their enormous span over the marshes and islands. Enjoy the sight of these noble birds before returning to the hotel. Overnight in Harbin.

Day 4 “Ice-fishing” on Lake Chagan

This morning we will continue on to Songyuan. You will drive to Lake Chagan, which has a magical meaning for the locals - Chagan means white and sacred. The huge lake is used by the inhabitants mainly for fishing, as well as in winter. People have always done this with the same tried and tested methods. Every winter a festival takes place here, which reminds of the old tradition of winter fishing. The festival is accompanied by devotional ceremonies, dances and music. After the fishermen make holes in the ice sheet, nets are laid out to trap the large fish. An authentic and unforgettable experience. Overnight in Songyuan.

Day 5 Visit from Changchun & Continue to Jilin

This morning your journey continues via Changchun to Jilin in the middle of the three Northeast provinces. Changchun was once considered the largest and most modern city in Manchuria in the 1930s and 1940s. There you will visit the Imperial Palace, where the last emperor of China “Puyi” served as so-called puppet emperor for eight years after his resignation from Beijing and thus only functioned as a representative office. Your guide will tell you more about the exciting history of this area. Let the unique charm of Chinese and Russian influences affect you. Then drive to Jilin, east of Changchun. Overnight in Jilin.  

Day 6: Discover Jilin as a Natural Wonder

Today you will discover a great natural wonder along the Songhua River. On the island of Rime you can admire the fairytale scenery “Wu Song”. Wu Song means “crystal tree” and this is exactly what the trees and shrubs along the water body look like. Due to rising water vapors, which iron themselves and thus remain attached to the trees as crystals, the spectacle has earned its name. A true winter wonderland, which can be observed especially in January and February. Overnight in Jilin.

7th day onwards to Beijing

After breakfast, you will be taken to the train station, from where you will take the Chinese express train to the metropolis of Beijing. Traveling by express train not only provides you with a fast connection, but also a comfortable and pleasant ride through the countryside. Upon arrival in Beijing, you will already be expected and escorted to your elegant hotel, the Regent Beijing. This luxurious hotel offers you the highest comfort and first-class service, ideal for a relaxing stay in the Chinese capital. After check-in, you can arrange yourself in your room and enjoy the amenities of the house. Overnight in Beijing.

8th Day Once standing on the Great Wall yourself

Ready for a mighty encounter? In the morning, head to the Great Wall at Mutianyu section. The foreigner enjoys the excursion here like a journey through time: from the modern capital beyond the outskirts to the rough mountainous area, where an army of workers once walled an empire. On the wall, together with your guide, you can take a hike and enjoy the views into the surrounding countryside. Then you will return to Beijing. Overnight in Beijing.

9th day of pure winter experiences – skiing in China

Today you set out to make the ski slopes of China unsafe. You will first go to your hotel in Zhangjiakou and experience the next days of skiing at the Genting Secret Garden Resort. From there you can strap on the skis directly and go down the slope. The ski area is located in the Dama Mountains, where you can ski for up to 150 days a year. The area covers 70 kilometers of slopes and almost 90 different departures. From Funpark to the cozy slope, there is something for everyone. You can also easily rent your equipment and participate in ski courses. Look forward to the next few days and experience a slightly different winter sports world. Overnight in Zhangjiakou.

Day 10 & Day 11 Pistengaudi in Chinese

Explore the large and versatile ski area with your ski pass for the next two days. Observe the professionals on the downhill, enjoy hot drinks in the retreats and the completely different flair on Chinese slopes. Overnight in Zhangjiakou.

Day 12 Last Departure & Return to Beijing

Today, you can spend half the day in the ski resort before being taken back to Beijing in the afternoon. Once there, you can move back into your hotel and end the rest of the evening in a relaxed way. You will certainly be a bit exhausted from the fresh winter air. Overnight in Beijing.

13th Day Ice and Fire in Beijing

Beijing, China’s political and cultural center with over 3,000 years of history, has evolved from a city often called the “giant village” to a modern world metropolis since the 2008 Olympic Games. Your tour begins in central Beijing with a visit to the Forbidden City, the impressive Imperial Palace that once served as a residence for the Ming and Qing emperors and was only open to the court for half a millennium. Stroll over Tiananmen Square, the largest square in the world, and visit Coal Hill, which offers a magnificent view of the Forbidden City. In the afternoon, you can skate on Shichahai Lake and watch the wintry activity of the locals. After the fresh air and exercise, enjoy a traditional Mongolian firepot in the evening. This popular, spicy stew is served in a broth of pepper and flower pepper. For those who like it milder, there is also a white broth based on chicken or pig bones. Overnight in Beijing.

Day 14: Farewell to China

Today it is already time to say goodbye to this fascinating country. Surely you have packed your suitcases with many great impressions. You will be driven to the airport, from where you will start your home or onward journey.

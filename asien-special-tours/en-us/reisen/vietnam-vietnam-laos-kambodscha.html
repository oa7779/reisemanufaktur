Tracing Indochina - A Journey Through Laos, Vietnam & Cambodia

Vietnam, Laos, Cambodia Round Trip Combination - Southeast Asia Travel from Asia Special Tours. Trust in the experience of our Asian specialists.

The still very original Laos, the dynamic Vietnam and the mystical Cambodia unite on this extraordinary journey to create an impressive overall picture of Indochina. Between golden shining temples, lively metropolises and a fascinating natural backdrop, a region full of contrasts, depth and beauty unfolds. But this journey goes far beyond the visible: spiritual places, living traditions and warm encounters give you authentic access to people’s everyday lives. Culinary experiences, village visits and special insights into art and craft allow you to experience Indochina with all your senses. Embark on an inspiring journey of discovery through Indochina – a journey that not only connects sights, but above all makes the diversity, culture and way of life of the region tangible.

1st day Arrival in Laos - charming temple world in Luang Prabang

Welcome to Laos! Your journey begins in the fascinating Luang Prabang – a place where history, spirituality and Lao joie de vivre blend in a special way. As you drive to your hotel, you will feel the unique atmosphere of this city, which is one of the best preserved historical places in Southeast Asia. Nestled between the Nam Khan and Mekong rivers, Luang Prabang unfolds a distinctive magic with its golden shining temples and local architecture. No wonder the city was named a World Heritage Site by UNESCO in 1995. After a short rest break, you will discover the old town together with your guide. At Wat Visoun, the oldest still active temple in the city dating back to 1513, you’ll admire antique wooden Buddha figures as well as the striking Stupa That Makmo, affectionately known as the “watermelon stupa.” Continue to Wat Xieng Thong, the most famous and magnificent temple in the city, which impresses with its harmonious architecture and spiritual atmosphere. Your first evening will end in peace – perhaps on a walk through the charming alleys, which appear particularly atmospheric in the warm light. Overnight in Luang Prabang. 

Day 2 Luang Prabang & Pak Ou Cave: Culture, Spirituality and Village Life

After a relaxing start to the day, immerse yourself in the past of the former Kingdom of Laos. The former Royal Palace, now a National Museum (closed on Tuesdays), offers fascinating insights into the lives of the last monarchs. Afterwards, visit Wat Sene, the seat of the Buddhist Patriarch of the city with its gold-decorated facades. The afternoon is all about nature, spirituality and authentic village life. Take a trip to the Pak Ou Caves and visit the small village of Ban Mouang Keo on the banks of the Mekong along the way. During a walk through the village you will encounter the hospitality of the residents. Many make floral arrangements as artistic offerings for the monks - an opportunity to learn more about this deeply rooted practice and to try them out on request. In a tranquil temple, a Buddhist monk welcomes you for a blessing. Then you will head to the Pak Ou caves, which can be reached on a 30-minute hike via a lesser known access or relaxed by boat. In the caves you can expect thousands of Buddha figures. Overnight in Luang Prabang.

3rd Day Noisy Waterfalls from Khoung-Si & Onward Flight to Hanoi

Today you will visit the Khoung Si Waterfalls – one of the most beautiful nature experiences around Luang Prabang. In several stages, the water plunges over bright limestone formations into bright turquoise basins surrounded by dense, tropical jungles. The scenery looks almost fairytale-like and is one of the most beautiful natural backdrops in the region. Over time, numerous small natural pools have formed, which – depending on the water level – also invite you to linger or bathe. Therefore, do not forget to pack your swimwear. Afterwards we say goodbye to Laos. You will be taken to the airport and fly on to Hanoi, the next stop of your trip in Vietnam. Your guide and your chauffeur will warmly welcome you and take you to your hotel. Tomorrow, look forward to getting to know the Vietnamese capital with its green boulevards, magnificent colonial buildings and fascinating temples. Overnight in Hanoi.

Day 4 Charming Hanoi & the Art of Calligraphy

Your tour of the city begins with a walk through the lively “quarter of 36 streets”, the bustling old town of Hanoi. The narrow streets are lined with busy dealers and small workshops. Along the way, visit a traditional pancake bakery where the popular “Banh Cuon” – delicate, steamed rice crepes – are prepared fresh before your eyes. Then you will dedicate yourself to the most important sights of the capital: see the Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum (exterior visit) as well as the former residence of Ho Chi Minh, the traditional wooden house of an ethnic minority, the iconic one-pillar pagoda and the venerable Temple of Literature. In Vietnam, the art of calligraphy has been cultivated for centuries. Today you have the opportunity to meet a calligraphy master in person. He tells you about the history of this traditional art in Vietnam and demonstrates his skill.  
Our “behind-the-scenes” tip: On an excursion, immerse yourself in the centuries-old tradition of Vietnamese Dong Ho painting by visiting the family of the famous master Nguyen Dang Che and learning all about making the artwork from natural materials. Afterwards, you can even design your own picture under guidance.

5th day cruise in the picturesque Halong & Lan-Ha bay

After breakfast, leave Hanoi towards the Halong region. Nearly 2000 limestone islands, emerald water and gently drifting boats create an unparalleled, almost surreal-looking scenery. On board the elegant Orchid Cruise you will experience the impressive island world of the Halong region from a very special perspective. The elegant boutique ship offers spacious cabins with panoramic windows and private balconies, so you can enjoy the spectacular views at all times completely relaxed. The route takes you away from the busy main driveways into the quieter, especially original Lan-Ha Bay with the island of Cat Ba. During the cruise you can expect various stops and activities: paddle by kayak through quiet lagoons or discover the intricacies of Vietnamese cuisine during a small cooking demonstration. In the evening, enjoy a delicious dinner on board and end the day in a relaxed atmosphere. Overnight on board. 

Day 6 - Atmospheric Morning and Flight to Hue

If you are one of the early risers, you can experience the magical Halong Bay at the most beautiful time of day: the rising sun breaks through the early fog and immerses the bay in a magical light. Enjoy your breakfast with a view of the unusually designed rock formations as the ship slowly returns to the port. In the evening we take the plane from Hanoi to Hue in central Vietnam and then to the hotel. The name Hue means harmony: gracefully reflected in the perfume river, dreaming in the shadow of old palaces and other imperial buildings. As a UNESCO World Heritage Site, the city draws everyone into its spell with its famous citadel, palaces, temples and imperial tombs that bear witness to a great and fascinating past. Overnight in Hue.

Day 7 Walking in the Imperial Hue

In the morning, a bicycle rickshaw will take you to the lively Dong-Ba market where you will immerse yourself in a sea of colors, scents and voices. Between fresh vegetables, exotic fruits, spices and small cooking kitchens, it is quickly noticeable why many of the most refined dishes of Vietnamese cuisine originate in Hue. Afterwards, we will cycle to the Nguyen Dynasty Imperial Citadel, whose architecture was inspired by the Forbidden City in Beijing. Located on the northern bank of the Perfume River and once built on an area of around 500 hectares, the complex still impresses today with its monumental gates, palaces and temples. Afterwards, visit Emperor Minh Mang’s majestic mausoleum, which blends harmoniously into the surrounding landscape. In its design, it is reminiscent of the imperial tombs of the Ming Dynasty near Beijing. If you are interested, you will have the opportunity to look over the shoulders of the artisans in a small family business while making the famous Vietnamese cone hats. Overnight in Hue.

8th Day Garden Houses & Over the Cloud Pass to Hoi An

In the morning, visit Hue’s most important religious site, the Thien-Mu Pagoda, also known as the “Heaven Pagoda”. Picturesquely located on the banks of the Perfume River, its seven-storey tower is one of the city's landmarks. Continue to an enchanting garden house that tells the story of the perfume flow. Nestled in a tranquil, sprawling facility surrounded by lush vegetation, an impressive collection of nearly 5,000 artifacts of ancient pottery from the river awaits you. Many of these pieces come from centuries-old finds and are complemented by selected objects from the collection of the Thai family, a significant family of the Nguyen dynasty. After the visit, the garden invites you to a relaxing break with tea and pastries. Then continue your journey over the famous Cloud Pass towards the south. The pass road, which is about 20 kilometers long, winds up to an altitude of 496 meters and offers spectacular views: on the one hand, the wide sea, on the other, the mountain range up to 1,000 meters high. Who would like to make a stop on the marble mountains. In the Cham Museum, you will learn everything you need to know about the culture of the Cham before reaching your daily destination Hoi An. Overnight in Hoi An.

9th Day Discovering Pittoreskes Hoi An & Leisure

In the 17th and 18th centuries, Hoi An was an important trading port. The well-preserved architecture and the relaxed lifestyle have changed little over the centuries, so today a particularly charming, historic town awaits you. In the morning, your guide will accompany you on a tour of the picturesque old town. You will visit old Chinese teak houses and temples, French colonial villas, the approximately 400-year-old Japanese bridge as well as the colorful market. Watch the bustle on the river and stroll through the winding alleys. The rest of the day is at your disposal. Enjoy the amenities of your hotel or explore Hoi An after your very own personal taste. Overnight in Hoi An.

10th day Quirliges Saigon & experience impressive sights

After breakfast, you will be transferred to Danang Airport for your short flight to Saigon. The vibrant metropolis Ho Chi Minh City, affectionately called “Saigon” by the locals until today, is the largest city in Vietnam with around nine million inhabitants. Here life never seems to stand still - between busy streets, modern high-rise buildings and colonial heritage, a city unfolds full of energy, contrasts and dynamism. Your guide will first take you along the elegant Dong Khoi Street, past the impressive city hall and the historic opera house (exterior visit). You will visit the venerable Main Post Office, one of the most beautiful buildings in the city, the Reunification Palace as a symbol of Vietnamese history and the lively Ben Thanh Market In the afternoon you will immerse yourself in a completely different world: In the lively Cholon, the traditional Chinatown of Saigon, you will experience a fascinating interplay of temples, trade and centuries-old Chinese-Vietnamese culture. Overnight in Saigon.

Day 11 Culinary Excursion & Mysterious Tunnel of Cu Chi

Experience Indochina with all your senses! In a region with such a rich culinary tradition, the sense of taste should not be neglected. Guest with an experienced chef, you will delve into the secrets of Vietnamese cuisine. The chef himself has worked in Australia for several years and therefore speaks excellent English. Since fresh ingredients play a central role in Vietnamese cuisine, your cooking class starts with a tour of the in-house vegetable garden. Together with the chef, select the ingredients for your menu. Afterwards, prepare a delicious three-course menu with him – your self-cooked, tasty lunch. In the afternoon, an impressive change of perspective awaits you: visit the famous tunnel system of Cu Chi and learn about the eventful history and extraordinary ingenuity of the Vietnamese population. The tunnel system, which is about 200 kilometers long, acquired great historical importance during the war against the USA. At a depth of up to 25 meters, underground corridors with schools, dormitories and warehouses were built. If you want, you can even enter one of the narrow tunnel entrances and experience the system up close. Overnight in Saigon. 

12th Day Authentic Cruise in the Mekong Delta

Leave the bustling Saigon and drive to Cai Be in the tropical Mekong Delta. The Mekong with its numerous secondary arms shapes not only the landscape, but also the life of the people in the south of Vietnam. An extensive labyrinth of river arms, canals and islands has been determining the rhythm of life here for centuries. Water is a transport route, lifeline and stage of everyday life at the same time. How better to discover this fascinating river labyrinth than from the water? On board the Bassac, a lovingly restored traditional wooden boat with comfortable cabins, you will experience a particularly charming and at the same time decelerated form of travel. Devote yourself completely to the here and now. Lunch will be served on board and the journey begins downstream. In the afternoon, enjoy relaxing hours on deck as the life of the delta passes by. Along the way, stop and visit a shoreside village for authentic insights into local life. In the evening, enjoy a cool sundowner as the sun sets slowly over the river and the scenery dips into a warm light. Dinner will be served on board and the Bassac will be anchored for the night. Overnight on the ship. 

13th Day Floating Markets in the Mekong Delta & Flight to Siem Reap

Enjoy the sunrise with a cup of coffee and a relaxing breakfast on board. Meanwhile, the delta is slowly coming to life. The Bassac glides leisurely towards Can Tho, where you later disembark. Here you will visit one of the famous floating markets of the region. Watch, we offer traders their exotic goods directly from one boat to another. (The floating markets in the Mekong Delta are authentic wholesale markets, for regional products and not tourist markets). The number of boats varies seasonally according to harvest and demand. Due to the increasing expansion of modern markets, it may also happen that at certain times only a few merchant ships are on site.) In the late morning you will be driven back to Saigon by car. From there, fly on to Siem Reap in Cambodia. Welcome to a new chapter of your journey. This city will be your starting point in the next few days to explore the world-famous Angkor Park – an area of about 40 square kilometers and more than 100 temples dating from the 9th to 13th centuries. Upon arrival, you will already be expected at the airport and taken to your hotel. Overnight in Siem Reap.

14th Day Mysterious Temple World of Angkor & Visit to Phare Circus

Today, one of the great highlights of your trip awaits you: the world-famous Angkor Wat Temple. Built in the first half of the 12th century, this UNESCO World Heritage Site is the largest and undoubtedly most important monument to the Angkor Temple Group. The sanctuary is a masterpiece of Khmer architecture and impresses with elaborate reliefs of Hindu mythology as well as nearly 2,000 Apsaras – the so-called “heavenly dancers”. Also worth seeing is Banteay Srei, the “Citadel of Women”, whose filigree sculptures made of pink sandstone are particularly finely worked. Another highlight is Ta Prohm, which was deliberately left in its original state. Huge tree roots wrap around the temple ruins, giving the place an exceptionally mystical atmosphere. Just in time for sunset, climb the Pre Rup Temple to enjoy the view. In the evening you are a guest in a special circus: In the “Phare” young people from poor backgrounds perform. As part of the social project, you will receive training and acrobatics lessons and bring impressive shows to the stage – in Siem Reap and on tours abroad. Overnight in Siem Reap. 

15th day In the footsteps of the Khmer with private picnic

After breakfast, take the tuk tuk to northern Angkor, where you’ll start your day amidst impressive temple worlds. The first stop is Preah Khan – a sprawling facility that once served as a city and important center of Buddhist teachings. From the same era comes Neak Pean, the only “island temple” of Angkor. The finely decorated temples Thommanon and Chau Say Tevoda also testify to the high level of craftsmanship of Khmer architecture and are characterized by their particularly impressive statues. During a private picnic at a temple, you will enjoy the special atmosphere in peace. In the afternoon, continue your visits to Angkor Thom (the big city). Start at the imposing South Gate and finally reach the fascinating Bayon Temple. With its 54 towers and 216 smiling faces of Avalokiteshvara, it is considered one of the most enigmatic temples of the Angkor group. Other attractions are: the Terrace of Elephants, which once served as a ceremonial court and a huge viewpoint for royal audiences and ceremonies, and the Terrace of the Leprosy King with its ornate reliefs. In the evening you return with many impressive pictures and let the day end in peace. Overnight in Siem Reap.

Day 16 Siem Reap and Departure

Choose between different excursion options today: For example, visit the Floating Villages on Tonle Sap. The stilt houses protrude several meters from the shallow water and adapt in a unique way to life on the lake. A picnic in a family home gives you the opportunity to get to know the architecture and everyday life of this special community in an authentic way. If weather permits, then take a boat trip to a remote village where Vietnamese settlers have settled. Alternatively, visit one of the inspiring social and craft projects in Siem Reap, such as the Artisans d’Angkor workshops, where traditional techniques such as wood and stone sculpture are maintained and passed on by local artisans. Afterwards we say goodbye to a fascinating journey. Your chauffeur will take you to the airport, and with many new impressions in your luggage, you will start your journey home. 

Extension of bath: If desired, your trip can be extended by a few relaxing days on the dream beaches of Asia – we are happy to advise you.

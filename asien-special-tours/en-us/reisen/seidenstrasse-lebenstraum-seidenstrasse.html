Silk Road - On ancient caravan paths from Chiwa to Beijing

Silk Road Journey: Fulfill the life dream of a tailor-made journey on the old caravan trails - from Chiwa to Beijing with Asia Special Tours.

Itinerary & services

On the legendary southern route of the Silk Road you travel from Central Asia to China: through the mountain landscapes of Kyrgyzstan you pass the infamous Taklamakan Desert from Kashgar via Turfan to Beijing. Follow the caravan routes of yesteryear and experience the scenic and cultural diversity! Drive through a sea of sand, roam millennia-old cities like the legendary Samarkand, and be enchanted by the oriental charm and splendor of the turquoise shining medres, mosques and minarets. Go on a journey through time through dreamlike landscapes and fulfill yourself as a Silk Road fan a unique life dream!

Day 1 Welcome to the Orient

Early in the morning you will land in Tashkent, be picked up and driven to your hotel. There you will enjoy a rich breakfast. The metropolis of Tashkent is the cultural and economic center of today’s Uzbekistan. Founded by Kangiuy Khoresm under the ancient name Shash, the city looks back on more than 2,000 years of history. To get in the right mood for the Orient, your sightseeing tour starts at the bazaar, where you will get a first impression of the fascinating oriental world. Furthermore, in the old town you will visit the Kukaldash Mosque, the Barak Chan Medrese and the Kaffal Shash Mausoleum. Overnight in Tashkent.

2nd day of free time in Tashkent & flight to the oasis city of Chiwa

Take advantage of your free morning in Tashkent before heading to the airport. Your next destination is Urgentsch. From there you will continue to Khiva, the former capital of Choresmien. You can arrange the evening according to your own wishes and ideas. Stroll through this enchanting city and feel the special atmosphere that surrounds you. Overnight in Chiwa.

Day 3 Khiva - a large open-air museum

Today, get involved in the world in Chiwa, the oasis city on the edge of the Kizilkum Desert. The old town, which is a UNESCO World Heritage Site, is one large open-air museum. Particularly worth seeing are the mighty Kunya Ark fortress and Tash Khauli Palace, whose walls are decorated with magnificent ornaments. Its columned hall is one of the artistic masterpieces of the city. In addition, in the ancient narrow streets you will find numerous medres, palaces and mosques, including the Juma Mosque, whose ceiling is supported by 213 carved wooden columns. Finally, visit the magnificent summer palace of the last emir of Choresmien. A day full of impressions draws to a close. Overnight in Chiwa.

Day 4 - Drive through the Kizilkum desert to Bukhara

About 430 kilometers through semi-deserts and steppes, today's day stage leads you to Bukhara. While driving through the Kizilkum Desert, you will discover that even the apparent wasteland has a lot to offer. You travel along the Amudarja River, the life-giving stream of Central Asia. In addition, your guide will use the time to tell you interesting things about the country and people. So you can look forward to a varied ride. Then you reach Bukhara. The city was once one of the most glamorous commercial centers on the Silk Road. People from all over the world met in the bazaars, and the city developed into an important cultural and spiritual center. Even today, the atmosphere of bygone times is noticeable when you stroll through the narrow alleys of the old town, stroll over the gold market and marvel at the magnificent architecture of the beautifully decorated Oriental architecture. After the long journey, the rest of the day is at your disposal. Overnight in Bukhara.

Day 5 Bukhara - "The Noble One"

After breakfast, you can expect a variety of interesting sightseeings in this medieval city of poetry, legends and fairy tales. Bukhara, "the noble one", belonged like Samarkand and Khiva to the confederation of cities of the golden ring. Among other things, you will visit the Citadel Ark, the Kalon Minaret, the Miri-Arab Medresen and Ulugbek, where Koranic students have been taught for 400 years. Furthermore, you will see the Mosque Choir Minor, the Medresen Divan-Begi and Kukeldasch as well as the bazaar. Experience the Oriental flair up close and let yourself be enchanted. Overnight in Bukhara.

Day 6 “The Place Where the Moon and Stars Meet”

Today, take a trip to the residences and attractions outside the city. You’ll see the 19th-century Sitorai Mokhi Khosa palace complex, as well as “the place where the moon and stars meet”: the Bakr Choir complex consists of a variety of mosques and mausoleums, built in the 16th to 20th centuries. Finally, visit the impressive tomb of Bahouddin Naqshbandi (16th). century. Overnight in Bukhara.

Day 7 - Continue to Chess Risabs: The Green City

On the way from Bukhara to Chessrisabs, visit the city of Qarshi. It was once one of the most important cities of Sogdia. Around Qarshi there are more than 300 ruins from ancient and medieval times. Then continue on to Chessrisabs. The name Chessrisabs means “green city”. It is not only a UNESCO World Heritage Site, but also historically significant as the birthplace of Tamerlane. Overnight in chess risabs.

Day 8 - Welcome to Samarkand in 1001 Nights

Chessrisabs is the birthplace of the great conqueror Timur. The “White Castle”, the Aksaray Palace from the 14th century as well as the Kuk-Gumbaz Mosque, which today is considered the most important Friday mosque, will undoubtedly impress you. In the afternoon, your trip will take you to Samarkand. Scent bazaars, huge mosques and a mysterious atmosphere: this is Samarkand. The city of Samarkand is considered the heart of the legendary Silk Road and looks back on a 2000-year history marked by a rapid change of powers. Overnight in Samarkand.

Day 9 Samarkand - the pearl of the Silk Road

Today, a detailed visit to the magnificent Samarkand is on the program. The city once functioned as one of the most important trading centers on the Silk Road. You will see the 15th century mausoleum Gur Emir, which translates to “Tomb of the Lord”. It was built at the end of the 14th century to the middle of the 15th century as Timur's burial place. Then we head to Registanplatz, the most famous square in Uzbekistan. The ensemble of the three medresen is certainly one of the most striking photo opportunities on your whole journey. In the afternoon you will visit the Bibi Khanum Mosque, which was built on the model of the Thousand-Pillar Mosque of Delhi and at times worked on up to 500 stonemasons. Let the many impressions sink and enjoy a relaxing evening. Overnight in Samarkand.

Day 10: Ulugbek Observatory

You don’t get enough of Samarkand that fast. In the morning, visit the Ulugbek Observatory. Ulugbek, the grandson of Timur, was not only ruler, but also very interested in astronomy. European scientists also built on his findings. Afterwards you will see the Shakhi Zinda Mausoleum – one of the most interesting testimonies to the diversity and beauty of Timurian architecture – as well as the Afrosiab Museum. In the afternoon, visit a silk carpet production factory. Overnight in Samarkand.

11th day Train ride to Tashkent & Onward flight to Kyrgyzstan

Today we will take the train back to Tashkent. At the train station you are already expected by your chauffeur and brought directly to the airport. Now it is time to say goodbye to the enchanting Uzbekistan. Their next destination is Bishkek. Welcome to Kyrgyzstan. Your new guide is already waiting for you and driving you to your hotel. The capital of Kyrgyzstan, which emerged from a caravan station, with its wooden houses and green areas makes you think of a Russian village rather than an Asian metropolis. The city was laid out in the 19th century like a chessboard and is characterized by numerous monuments and classical buildings. Overnight in Bishkek.

12th day Bishkek - the capital of Kyrgyzstan & onwards to Karakol

Early in the morning you will take a short guided tour of Bishkek, where you will get to know the Osch Bazaar and the central square. Afterwards, your journey will take you to Karakol. On the way, stop at the Przhevalskii Museum and visit the Burana Tower. Overnight in Karakol.

Day 13 The red rock formations of sandstone in the Jety-Oguz gorge

Today, in Karakol, you will see the Dungan Mosque and the Russian Orthodox Church, where a cattle market takes place every Sunday. In the afternoon, continue on the old caravan paths to Kotschkor. Along the way, you will pass by sparsely populated places and can visit fantastic red sandstone rock formations in the Jety Oguz Gorge. For a long time, the nomads have been driving their herds of sheep and horses here. You will stop at the beautiful place Skazka (which translates to “fairy tale”). Mystical-looking stone rocks with fairytale-like and bizarre sandstone and clay structures that have their own names like castle, hare, elephant etc. take you into the mysterious world of Kyrgyz nature.

14th day campfire on Lake Son-Kul

In the morning, visit a felt museum with traditional production of shyrdak (fur carpets). Afterwards, your journey will take you to Lake Son-Kul. The lake is located at over 3,000 meters on a high plateau. The day ends wildly romantically: At the campfire you eat dinner and then retreat into a yurt to sleep. Overnight at Lake Son-Kul in a yurt camp.

Day 15 - The old garrison town of Naryn

Have you slept well in your yurt? After breakfast, we continue to the old garrison town of Naryn. The place is located on both sides of the Naryn River on a picturesque gorge. Be sure to have your camera ready. Overnight in Naryn.

Day 16: Over the Chinese border to Kashgar

Today you will make your way to the Kyrgyz-Chinese border. Along the way, visit the Tash Rabat Caravanserei, one of the largest stone buildings in Central Asia. Later in the day, you cross the border and continue to Kashgar. Welcome to China! Enjoy unforgettable days along the Chinese Silk Road. You will be taken to your hotel where you can relax a little. Kashgar was considered the hub of the Silk Road. Here the northern and southern Silk Roads met – and still meet today. This great oasis was once the last stop before the arduous crossing of the Karakorum and Pamir mountains. Overnight in Kashgar.

17th day Kashgar - Silk Road junction

After breakfast, visit the impressive Kashgar: The Id-Kah Mosque is the largest in the city and an Islamic center in Xinjiang Province. It was built in 1798 and later renovated several times until it finally took on its present appearance. On both sides of the entrance stand two ten-meter-high towers. Then visit the tomb of the fragrant concubine, who was brought to Beijing by Qing Emperor Qianlong as a little girl, because the emperor was beguiled by her scent and the fragrant beauty became his concubine. Other Uyghur girls envied her, because hardly any other Uyghur woman was allowed to marry into the imperial family in the Qing dynasty. However, the fragrant concubine paid for this privilege with her life, as she could not get used to the climate in Beijing and died shortly after moving to the imperial capital. Overnight in Kashgar.

18th Day Sunday Market of Kashgar

Today you will have the opportunity to visit the "Sunday Bazaar", which - contrary to its name - is held daily. It is still the largest and most important market in the Orient and one of the most famous markets in China. Divided into two markets—the Sunday cattle market takes place a few miles southeast of the city—thousands of merchants still flock here on horseback, donkey carts, on foot, or any conceivable ride. It is a dusty, loud and photogenic place worth visiting. The rest of the day is at your disposal. Take the time to explore the old town on your own. Overnight in Kashgar.

Day 19 - Through the desert to Hotan

In the morning it is time to pack suitcases again, because your journey will continue along the legendary Silk Road. You drive throughout the day through desert and semi-desert landscapes to Hotan. Let your eyes wander and the many impressions and experiences of the last days affect you. The Kingdom of Hotan was once the most important Buddhist cultural center on the southern Silk Road route. Unfortunately, the sand has only left ruins of it. Overnight in Hotan.

Day 20 The caravan continues through the Taklamakan sand desert

Today you get an idea of how caravans used to travel through the desert – because you also cross the great Taklamakan sand desert. Even in the Bronze Age, even before the Silk Road existed, a trade route along the Hotan River ran across the desert. If you arrive at your yurt camp in the evening and are still fit, you have the opportunity to take a camel ride. Overnight at the camp.

Day 21 – Continue to Aksu – “White Water”

Your journey will take you to Aksu today. Aksu is located between the Taklamakan Desert in the south and the Tianshan Mountains in the north. The name “Aksu” is Turkish and translates to “white water”. The contrasting landscape in this environment will overwhelm you. Overnight in Aksu.

Day 22: On the way to Kucha & visit to the “Thousand Buddha Caves”

After breakfast, the drive continues towards Kucha. On the way, you will pass the so-called “Thousand Buddha Caves”. The Buddhist caves were carved into the overhanging rocks and steep mountain walls from the 3rd century onwards and are the oldest Buddhist caves in China. The earlier frescoes date back to the period of the Southern and Northern Dynasties (420 to 589). The frescoes from the Tang period primarily reflect Buddhist history and teaching. Arriving in Kucha, you will visit the Great Mosque, which was built in the 15th century. It is the second largest mosque in the Xinjiang area after the Id Kah Mosque in Kashgar. Overnight in Kucha.

Day 23 The ruins of the desert city of Subashi

In the morning you will visit the Friday market in Kucha. Then head to Korla and visit the ruins of the 4th-century desert town of Subashi along the way. Unfortunately, they were destroyed in the 12th century. Overnight in Korla.

24th day Underground Canal System and Ruined City of Jioahe

Very close to Korlea is Lake Boston, known for its rich fish fauna. It is the first on your agenda today. Then the journey continues to Turfan. Turfan is also one of the formerly most important stations along the Silk Road. In addition, the Turfan Sink offers a unique scenic panorama: Dry Hot Desert, deep gorges, picturesque mountains and green wooded oases. It is both the deepest and hottest region of China. The city itself is located in the center of an extensive oasis, which is still watered with the help of the underground traditional Karaz system. Karesen are underground canals that were created long ago by the local population due to the extremely dry climate. There are around 1,000 Karesen in Turfan alone. The ruined city of Jiahoe played a major role in the Tang, Song and Yuan dynasties on the Silk Road. Due to the lack of any rain, the up to three storeys of clay buildings were surprisingly well preserved over more than 1,000 years. Overnight in Turfan.

Day 25 “The Flaming Mountain” & Astana Tombs

Today you will take a trip to the Kiziltagh Valley and the legendary “Flaming Mountain”. The mountain got the name because from a distance it looks like it glows in glaring sunlight. At the foot of the Flaming Mountain are the Thousand Buddha Caves of Bezeklik. In this complex of Buddhist cave temples, about 60 caves with important murals are still well preserved. From here, drive to the ruined city of Gaochang, once a military base and ancient capital of the Uyghur Empire. The Astana tombs, located about 40 kilometers southeast of Turfan, were the burial sites for the inhabitants of Gaochang between the years 273 and 778. Since 1959, about 400 graves have been uncovered. Overnight in Turfan.

26th day Oasis Mondsichelsee & Singender Sandberg

In the morning, your chauffeur will take you to the station where you will board the express train to Liuyuan. There you will already be expected and drive on to Dunhuang. The sand dunes of the Taklamakan Desert reach almost to the city. Nearby, among these huge sand dunes, lies the crescent lake, which owes its name to its unmistakable shape. The Ringing Sandberg in the south of the city owes its name to the very special noise created by the friction when the sand dunes slide down. Overnight in Dunhuang.

27th day Extraordinary formations in Yardang National Park

On this day, visit Yardang National Park, a yardang is an erosion form of sediment that resembles a table in shape. For the most part, they owe their appearance to the wind, which has done its job over several million years. Translated from Uyghur, Yardang means “hills of clay”. Let your imagination run wild and you will recognize figures in the formations – for example lions or camels. Almost 400 square kilometers, the national park is large and offers an unreal expanse worth exploring. The most notable attraction is the so-called ghost town – in this area the yardangs resemble an abandoned city. Overnight in Dunhuang.

Day 28 The famous Mogao Grottoes

The highlight of Dunhuang is certainly a visit to the famous Mogao Grottoes. They are among the Buddhist treasures of China and represent a unique example of the development of Buddhist sculpture and painting. Today there are still 492 caves, more than 45,000 square meters of mural painting and 2,450 colored Buddha statues. The design of the caves required a period of more than 1,000 years – it stretched over several dynasties. For this reason, Dunhuang is rightly referred to today as the miracle on the edge of the Gobi desert and a cultural and artistic treasure trove of China. In the afternoon we will continue to Jiayuguan. Overnight in Jiayuguan.

Day 29 Western End of the Great Wall

Most travelers visit the Great Wall in Badaling near Beijing, where it is touristy and not very authentic, but today they visit the western end of the Great Wall. This Hanging Wall owes its name to a slope of 45 degrees, so it almost seems as if it is “hanging” on the mountain. This section of the Great Wall is an extension of the wall at Jiayuguan Pass towards the north and belonged to the ancient defense system. In 1539 it was started to build this section, but of the original 1.5 kilometers unfortunately only about half remains. The piece of wall was built together with the fortress Jiayuguan, built in 1372. Jiayuguan was for a long time the last fortified military garrison – behind it lurked barbarity. The military building is the best preserved on the wall. From there you have a good view of the area north of the Great Wall. After the visit, continue to Zhangye. Overnight in Zhangye.

30th day mountains of red sandstone

Today in Zhangye you can expect a real natural beauty – and a very colorful one at that. Near the city, you can experience a unique natural landscape in the Danxia Geopark. The Danxia Mountains are made up of red sandstones (Danxia literally means “red rock”) that seem to move up and down like waves. They radiate in a wide variety of reds and it almost seems as if the rocks were painted with stripes. Wonderful photos are guaranteed here! In the afternoon, today you will see the temple of the great Buddha. The painted and gilded recumbent Buddha is with 34.5 m length one of the largest of his kind in China overnight in Zhangye.

31st day Kumbum Monastery – sacred site of Tibetan Buddhism

New day, new destination. With the express train, your journey will take you on to Xining. The capital of the province of Qinghai, which has a population of 400,000, is located on a fertile at an altitude of more than 2,000 meters. The history of Xining is over 2,000 years old. Once upon a time, the city was an important hub of the Silk Road. Visit Kumbum Monastery west of Xining, a sacred site of Tibetan Buddhism. The temple complex, which was buried between hills, was the birthplace of Tsongkhapa, the founder of the Yellow Hat place, who was born here in 1357. Exactly two hundred years later, the first temple was built there in his honor. Under Communist rule, the monastery had to close temporarily. However, during the Cultural Revolution, the buildings were protected and reopened in 1979. Today, the monastery is a popular destination for holidaymakers and pilgrims. Overnight in Xining.

32nd Day Interesting Thangka Painting in Tongren

This morning we will take the car to Xiahe. Along the way, visit the Tibetan Institute of Thangka Painting in Tongren. Thangka painting is a Tibetan art form in which bright colors are painted on cotton or silk fabric. The content of a thangka focuses mainly on the subject of Tibetan Buddhism, but also history, famous biographies, customs and science are discussed in thangkas. Admire the beautiful works and exchange ideas with the artists. Then you reach Xiahe. Overnight in Xiahe.

Day 33: Labrang Monastery & Ganjia Grasland

In the morning, visit Labrang Monastery on the Tibetan plateau. According to historical tradition, 3,000 monks lived here during the monastery’s heyday. Today, Labrang Monastery is once again considered an important spiritual center in northwestern China and a higher educational institution of Tibetan Buddhism. The approximately 86-hectare site of the Tibetan-style monastery is home to 48 temple halls of varying sizes as well as more than 500 Buddhist chapels and monk cells. As the most imposing, magnificent and largest building of the monastery, some consider the six-storey Maitreya Hall. The hall houses a 7.4 meter (with base 10 meters) high statue of the future Buddha. Its top floor is a palatial quadrangular pavilion whose roof is made of gilded copper bricks and decorated with gilded copper lions.

Day 34 To the Grottos of the 10,000 Buddhas & Boat Ride on the Yellow River

They leave Xiahe and continue on to Lanzhou. On the way, take a trip to the Bingling Grottoes, which are located about 90 km southwest of Lanzhou. They are one of China’s most famous caves. In the Tibetan language, bingling means "ten thousand Buddhas". As the name suggests, you can also see some Buddah statues here. In the caves there are 694 stone statues, 82 clay sculptures and about 900 square meters of murals that are well preserved. Bingling is famous for the sculptures of the Buddhas. Afterwards, take a boat trip over the Yellow River. Arriving in Lanzhou, we take the fast train to Xi’an. The former capital of eleven dynasties was once the cultural, economic and political center of the Middle Kingdom. Today it is the seat of government of Shanxi Province and the largest city in northwest China. Overnight in Xi’an.

35th Day Impressive Terracotta Army & Great Wild Goose Pagoda

A highlight of every trip to China: the Terracotta Army. It is considered one of the largest archaeological finds of the 20th century. It was discovered by chance by farmers in 1974 and has been on the World Heritage List since 1987. The life-size figures were found near the mausoleum of ruler Qin Shihuangdi, an early Chinese tomb dating back to 210 BC. So far, the archaeologists have excavated around 7,200 figures of soldiers on foot and horseback. It is remarkable that all (!) these earthen warriors are individually designed. It is the representation of a complete army of that time. It is considered certain that there are still other sculptures on the huge site, which has only been exposed to about a quarter so far. You will also visit the Great Wild Goose Pagoda, considered one of the oldest pagodas in China and located in the middle of the Temple of Good Will in the southern suburbs. The Great Wild Goose Pagoda is cone-shaped and with its seven floors a striking landmark of Xi'an. Overnight in Xi'an.

Day 36: Express train to Beijing

You have almost arrived at the destination of your unforgettable journey. This morning you start the last leg of your journey, and your driver will take you to the station where you will board the express train to Beijing. There you will be expected and brought to your hotel. Make the afternoon according to your mood. There are many things to discover in Beijing. For example, borrow a bike and cycle through the Old Town District, where you can take part in the traditional life in the Hutongs. Maybe you like it a bit more historical, then go to the Forbidden City and let the charm of long gone times affect you. Overnight in Beijing. 

Day 37: An Adventure Journey Ends

In time for your return or onward flight, you will be chauffeured to the airport of Beijing. With lots of new impressions, wonderful experiences and great photos in your luggage, you will start your journey home. If you want to extend a few more days in Beijing, we are happy to offer you an exciting program.

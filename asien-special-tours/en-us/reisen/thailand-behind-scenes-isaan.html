Thailand Behind the Scenes - Original Isaan

Thailand Journey: Experience the still untouched northeast of Thailand on the Behind the Scenes journey with Asia Special Tour. Let's talk now.

Itinerary & services

English itinerary text is an offline machine translation of the German original for this team preview. Please confirm travel details with your advisor; the German source remains available through the language switch.

Discover Isaan, the original northeast of Thailand, which holds some of the country’s most important cultural treasures. Deeply connected with their roots, the approximately 20 million inhabitants are still strongly influenced by the centuries-long belonging to the Khmer Empire. Following in the footsteps of this culture, you will encounter testimonies of mystical and spectacular beauty, countless Buddhist temples and shrines as well as ancient traditions. Experience the more spectacular nature of Northeast Thailand from its most beautiful side and enjoy breathtaking views. Of course, culinary experiences should not be missed: try local specialities on the markets. Your journey away from the tourist streams ends with a few relaxing days on the wonderful island of Koh Chang. Give yourself a break and let the soul build again properly.

Day 1 Welcome to Thailand

Welcome to Thailand! At Bangkok Airport, you will be picked up by your private guide and taken to your hotel. The fascinating city, known in the Thai language under the beautiful name “City of Angels”, has even more to offer than ornate sanctuaries and the huge river Chao Phraya used by countless ships. Countless markets, sparkling temples, gigantic shopping malls and millions of Buddha statues: Bangkok is full of life and offers adventures for young and old. The rest of the day is at your disposal. Arrive in peace or take a first small exploring tour through the urban jungle on your own. Overnight in Bangkok.

2nd day Experience colorful market driving

After breakfast, say goodbye to Bangkok and embark on a fascinating journey of discovery through rural Thailand. The start will be a visit to the extraordinary Maeklong market, which is located directly on the railway tracks. Eight times a day, the train passes only a few centimeters past the stands and sweeps sellers along with their goods with a loud signal for a short time from the rails. For the sellers long routine, however, this is a real highlight for visitors. Damnoen Saduak, on the other hand, is the most popular floating market in the area. With a motorized long boat you drive past stilt houses, gardens and temples and finally reach the actual market with its colorful bustle directly on the water. Admittedly, the market is touristy, but still worth a visit due to its long tradition. To get many colorful impressions richer you drive on to Kanchanaburi. There you will visit the famous bridge on the Kwai. Afterwards, we will take the boat to your very special accommodation: The FloatHouse River Kwai. This exclusive river-floating resort is only accessible by water. Overnight in Kanchanaburi. 

Day 3 Natural Wonders and History

Today is a trip to the breathtaking nature of Kanchanaburi on the program. After breakfast, leave with your guide to the Erawan National Park, one of the most beautiful nature parks in Thailand. Density, emerald green forests and spectacular waterfalls characterize the landscape. During a small hike, you will discover one of the famous Erawan waterfalls that stretches across seven levels. Especially refreshing: a jump into the cool water! Therefore, be sure to pack your swimwear, because a bath in one of the natural pools is a real highlight. The national park owes its name to the mythical elephant “Erawan” from Hindu tradition, which is said to have been modeled on the top step of the waterfall with its three foothills. After experiencing nature, enjoy a typical Thai lunch at a local restaurant. In the afternoon, dedicate yourself to a more serious chapter of history and visit the Hellfire Monument. Here you will learn a lot about the construction of the “death railway”, which cost the lives of hundreds of thousands of prisoners of war. Back in your floating hotel you can relax the day in the middle of nature. Overnight in Kanchanaburi. 

4th day Fascinating craftsmanship around Surin

Before you continue your journey, Kanchanaburi offers a special experience: take the Thailand-Burma railway through the picturesque Kwai valley. Let your eyes wander and enjoy the idyllic backdrop. Afterwards we continue by car to Ayutthaya. In the second capital of the kingdom of Siam, the sheer number of large ruins alone testifies to the former splendor of the metropolis, where more than a million people lived at high times. There you will visit the city’s Historic Park, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, and dive deep into the fascination of history among the ruins of the once magnificent metropolis. The remains of numerous temples, palaces and sanctuaries still bear witness to the splendor of the former city of millions. You can also try the local specialty Roti Sai Mai here, which is most easily described as “cotton candy in flat bread”. Overnight in Ayutthaya. 

Day 5 From Old Siam to Northeast Thailand - the Isaan

From Ayutthaya, head east – your destination is Isaan, Thailand’s vast and little-discovered northeast. On the way to the province of Nakhon Ratchasima, also known as “Korat”, make a stop in Saraburi. Here you will visit an important Buddhist pilgrimage site where a revered Buddha footprint is said to be located – a place full of spirituality and silence. Afterwards, the route takes you through the fertile wine-growing area of Khao Yai. Here you will find the traditional PB Valley winery. Surrounded by rolling hills and vines, learn more about Thai viticulture and enjoy a small tasting of fine wines and fresh grape products – a surprising experience in the middle of Thailand. In the late afternoon you will arrive at Nakhon Ratchasima. With around 200,000 inhabitants, the city is one of the largest in the country and is considered the gateway to Isaan – a region that delights with authentic country life, culinary diversity and warm hospitality. Overnight in Nakhon Ratchasima.

Day 6 Khmer Temples & Ancient Banyan Trees

After a refreshing breakfast, leave early for one of Thailand’s most impressive sanctuaries: Phanom Rung Temple. Majestic perched the complex on an extinct volcano at 383 meters altitude and offers not only spectacular views, but also a fascinating journey into the past. The ornately restored Prasat Phanom Rung is a masterpiece of Khmer architecture and was once dedicated to the deity Shiva. In the course of the 14th century, the monastery was finally taken over by a Buddhist monk - there was a change from Hindu temple to Buddhist temple. Afterwards we will continue to another jewel of the Khmer culture: the ruins of Prasat Hin Phimai. This impressive temple complex is the largest of its kind in Thailand and is considered the region’s art historical highlight. On the way back to your hotel, stop at Sai Ngam, a quiet, haunted place under the shade of a banyan tree more than 350 years old. The extensive roots and mystical character make this place an ideal place for a break. In the area, simple but authentic restaurants invite you to a culinary discovery. Be sure to try the typically regional “Pad Mee Korat” – fried rice noodles of the local type, served with fresh papaya salad and sticky rice. Overnight in Nakhon Ratchasima.

Day 7 Through the Heart of Northeast Thailand

Today is a longer, but scenic ride through the gentle hills and rural expanses of Isaan on the program. Your first destination is Khon Kaen, a city with great spiritual significance and impressive temple architecture. Here you will visit the majestic Wat Nong Wang, whose nine-story pagoda towers proudly over Lake Kaen Nakhon. Each floor offers not only artful murals, but also magnificent views over the city and lake – a place full of tranquility and spiritual power. Another architectural jewel is the modern Wat Thung Setthi. With its bright white facade, golden domes and filigree blue ornaments, the temple stands out clearly from the otherwise traditional architecture of the region. The special harmony and purity of this place invites you to stop and enjoy the atmosphere mindfully. In the late afternoon you will reach Udon Thani, a city with a charming mix of tradition and modernity. Here you move into your hotel and let the impressions of the day pass in relaxed review. Overnight in Udon Thani.

8th Day Flower Magic and Rock Legends

Today, an extraordinary day full of natural wonders and spiritual places awaits you. Early in the morning, head south from Udon Thani to Talay Bua Daeng, the famous Red Lotus Sea. From December to February, countless red lotus flowers bloom here and turn the lake into a colorful natural wonder – an experience that you can enjoy particularly intensively during a boat trip. Afterwards we will continue to the Phu Phrabat National Park, a fascinating place full of nature mystique. The impressive rock formations, reminiscent of huge mushrooms, towers or animal figures, were formed by wind and weather and are deeply interwoven with local legends. A walk through this mystical rock landscape will make you feel the magic of the region up close. Before reaching your day’s destination, make a stop at Wat Pho Chai in Nong Khai. Here is one of the most important Buddha statues in the region – a golden figure in the attitude of victory over Mara, which is a place of deep reverence for many believers. In the afternoon you will arrive at the idyllic Pimali Resort, where you can enjoy the tranquility of the area and end the day in a relaxed way. Overnight in Nong Khai.

9th Day Skywalk over the Mekong Valley & Boat Ride

Before continuing towards Chiang Khan, visit the beautiful Wat Pha Tak Sue. From here you have a great view of the Mekong Valley. Are you brave? Then you can also use the nearby Skywalk: the bridge with the glass floor leads in U-shape over 16 meters and offers a spectacular view. Along the Mekong River, continue to the quiet town of Chiang Khan, where you will take a boat trip on the vast river before strolling through the charming town with its pretty teak houses. In the evening, check in at Chiangkhan River Mountain Resort, located right on the riverbank – the perfect place to end this special day in a relaxed way. Overnight in Chiang Khan.

Day 10 Visit to Phu Ruea National Park

Around Chiang Khan there are beautiful landscape motifs that you will see during a visit to Phu Ruea National Park. Enjoy the tranquility of nature and keep your camera ready. A special stop will take you to the charming Café de Meena, located between rice fields. Enjoy a drink in this peaceful backdrop and let the view wander over the countryside – a place to stop and breathe. Afterwards, visit Wat Pa Huay Lad, known for its monumental 19-meter-high white Buddha statue. The temple is embedded in nature and radiates a silent power and spirituality. Continue to the Wat Somdet Phu Ruea, an ornately built teak temple that impresses with its tranquil atmosphere and the Buddha statue “Phra Kring Pawaret”. This place combines traditional architecture with spiritual depth and invites you to linger. Finally, return to your hotel where you can relax and end the day. Overnight in Chiang Khan.

Day 11 - Chiang Khan to Phrae

Early in the morning you set off for an impressive day trip to the traditional city of Phrae. Along the way, a cultural highlight awaits you: a visit to Wat Phra Sri Mahathat in Phitsanulok. In this important temple, you can admire one of Thailand’s most beautiful bronze Buddha statues – a masterpiece of religious art that captivates many believers and art lovers alike. Take time to enjoy the spiritual atmosphere. Then continue your journey through the charming landscape of northern Thailand. In the late afternoon you will arrive at Phrae, one of the oldest cities in the country. The charming place welcomes you with well-preserved colonial buildings, traditional wooden architecture and a pleasant serenity that invites you to arrive and linger. Overnight in Phrae.

Day 12 The road is the goal: On to Chiang Rai!

After breakfast, leave Phrae and make your way to the far north of Thailand, Chiang Rai – the gateway to the fabled Golden Triangle. A first highlight awaits you with a visit to Phae Muang Phi, a natural wonder not far from Phrae. The whimsical, wind-shaped sandstone formations look like they come from another world: mushroom-shaped rocks, eroded towers and rugged sculptures reminiscent of an enchanted fairy-tale landscape. A short walk will take you to a viewpoint with fantastic panoramic views – an ideal place for impressive souvenir photos. Continue to the province of Phayao, where you can take a relaxing break at the idyllic Lake Phayao. The largest freshwater lake in northern Thailand invites you to linger with its peaceful atmosphere – a moment of tranquility before continuing your journey. In the late afternoon, arrive at Chiang Rai, where you can check in at your hotel and end the day relaxing. Overnight in Chiang Rai.

Day 13 Excursion to the legendary Golden Triangle

Today you will discover the Golden Triangle – the triangle of Thailand, Laos and Myanmar – which was once one of the largest transhipment areas for opium in the entire world. From a viewpoint, you can see the foothills of all three nations. During your visit to the Opium Hall you will learn more about the past of this place. Take a boat trip on the Mekong and visit the venerable ruins of Chiang Saen. After a stroll across the border market of Mae Sai, Thailand’s northernmost city, head back to Chiang Rai. There you go in the early evening together with your guide on a fun tour with a customary tuk tuk. You will head for several sights beautifully lit at night – for example, the golden bell tower designed by a famous local artist. There you can attend a small light and sound show. Also visit the statue of King Mengrai. Finally, you can enjoy a delicious meal at an authentic restaurant at the night market and then have the opportunity to stroll across the bazaar before returning to the hotel. Overnight in Chiang Rai.

Day 14 Chiang Mai – the Rose of the North

On the way to Chiang Mai, a very special sanctuary awaits you: Wat Rong Khun, the “White Temple”. The jagged tips of the temple act like white fire, which rebels in the pond. This very complex and partly curious structure is a combination of ancient art and contemporary architecture, the temple was conceived by local artist Chalermchai Kositpipat. It goes further west, you can always make a stop for a photo stop, e.g. at the hot springs of Mae Khajan, where you can cook an egg in the water. Chiang Mai is also known as the Rose of the North. The city is enchanted by a multitude of magnificent temples and lies in the middle of a beautiful landscape. In the surrounding mountains live in numerous small settlements mountain peoples who have preserved their ancient traditions in the immediate vicinity of this modern metropolis. In the evening you can stroll over the famous night market. Overnight in Chiang Mai.

15th day city exploration with charm

Without tour guide & chauffeur

Today you will discover Chiang Mai in a special way – with the traditional Trishaw, the Thai bicycle taxi. The first stop is the Muang Mai Market, a bustling transshipment point for fresh goods of all kinds. Afterwards, visit the venerable Wat Phra Singh, one of the most important temples in the city. Particularly impressive is the delicately decorated Lai Kham Chapel with its ornate murals and carvings in Lanna style. The temple also houses a highly revered Buddha statue, whose spiritual significance is reflected in the annual feast process. Another architectural jewel awaits you with the Wat Chedi Luang. The mighty pagoda of this temple was once the tallest building in the city and is one of the spiritual centers of ancient Chiang Mai. Once there were three temples here, today the Wat Chedi Luang remains as an impressive testimony of bygone times. Finally, you will dive into the colorful bustle of the Warorot market on a guided walk – a place where everyday life pulsates. Be sure to try the typical “Sai Oua”, a spicy, grilled brat sausage from northern Thailand. Overnight in Chiang Mai.

Day 16: Insight into rural life

In the morning, set off on a special day trip: The ride will take you through the green hills of northern Thailand to the idyllic village of Mae Kampong, known for its sustainable lifestyle and warm village community. After the friendly welcome by the residents, you will discover the surroundings together with a local guide. During a walk through the forest and the surrounding tea and coffee plantations you will get exciting insights into the everyday life of the villagers. Along the way, visit the impressive temple of Kanta Prueksa (Wat Mae Kampong), located in the middle of a pond. After saying goodbye to the village, enjoy a local lunch at a local restaurant before continuing to Sankampang’s famous thermal springs. Here you will find a well-deserved traditional Thai massage (1 hour) – the perfect relaxation after an active morning. In the afternoon, return to Chiang Mai, where you can arrange the rest of the day according to your own wishes. Overnight in Chiang Mai.

Day 17 - On to Koh Chang

This morning you will be taken to the airport, from where you will fly on to Trat. Your relaxing bathing can begin. Take the ferry to the island of Koh Chang. The island is one of the largest in the Gulf of Thailand and is characterized by its beautiful, dense rainforest. Look forward to a few relaxing days at your dreamlike resort. The Dewa Koh Chang is located directly on the beautiful Klong Prao sand beach and offers fantastic views of the Gulf of Thailand. The 40 elegantly furnished and in bright colors decorated rooms with balcony are distributed over a two-storey building and overlook the 700 square meter pool with sun terrace, the centerpiece of the complex. Even more stylish, stay in one of the 19 spacious villas and grand villas just steps from the sandy beach and pool overlooking the tropical garden or the Gulf of Thailand. The small spa area with sauna and whirlpool perfectly completes the offer of The Dewa.  

 

18th - 20th day time to dream and relax

(without tour guide & chauffeur)

These days belong only to you. Let your soul really dangle and enjoy moments when time seems to stop. Explore the fascinating underwater world with its colorful fish and corals with diving glasses, take a hike through the dense jungle or borrow a canoe from one of the local providers and explore the island from the water. Or just relax by the pool, read a good book and enjoy the sweet nothing-doing. Overnight on Koh Chang.

Day 21 Farewell to Paradise

Even the most beautiful journey comes to an end. Immerse yourself once again through the delicious breakfast buffet of your resort and enjoy the fantastic view. Then we say goodbye to the island paradise. By ferry you will first return to Trat on the mainland, where a short flight will take you back to Bangkok. With many exciting experiences and impressions of a very special country, you will begin your journey home.

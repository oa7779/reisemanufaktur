Japan Travel - Castles, Temples and Mystical Landscapes

Japan travels to castles, temples and mystical landscapes. Experience your tailor-made Japan trip with Asia Special Tours. Ask now!

Have you already been to Japan and discovered the most famous sights in the country or would like to experience something different, away from the typical routes? Then this trip to the islands of Kyushu and Shikoku will fulfill all your wishes. Explore exciting cities such as Fukuoka, Nagasaki and Kagoshima, which can compete with Tokyo or Kyoto in terms of diversity and modernity. In the former “Warehouse Town” Kurashiki, you will feel transported to the past as you stroll through the old town with its traditional merchant houses, rice stores and stone bridges. You will visit stunning parks and gardens that enchant with their beauty, admire temples that radiate a special spirituality, and discover castles that will impress you. In addition to the rich culture of the two islands, the fantastic nature will astonish you no less. Visit Beppu, famous for its hot springs, traverse the lava field along the Nagisa lava path on Sakurajima Island and take beautiful hikes on the idyllic island of Yakushima. Relaxation will not be neglected: let yourself be pampered in private onsen and enjoy the peace and time by the sea in your exclusive final residence on Yakushima.

1st day Arrival in Japan

Yokoso – welcome to Japan! Your journey begins in Osaka, where you will be welcomed and greeted by an English speaking assistant. Take the train to Kurashiki and then to your first ryokan. The rest of the day is at your disposal. Kurashiki served as the center of warehousing in the region during the Edo period. This is how Kurashiki has earned its name, which literally translates to “lagerdorf”. Stroll through the streets of Kurashiki Bikan and the canal district. Your overnight stay is something very special today: you are a guest in a ryokan. The typical Japanese inns were already built in the Edo period (1603 – 1868). Among the highlights of your stay at Ryokan are meals. Traditionally, many small dishes are served, so you can try a variety of regional and seasonal delicacies. At the end of the day, treat yourself to a relaxing bath in the onsen of your ryokan before you rest on your futon. Overnight in Kurashiki.

2nd day on to Takamatsu

Without guidance

Start your day today with the train ride to Takamatsu. This fascinating city, located at the southern end of Shikoku Island, is the capital of Japan’s smallest prefecture, Kagawa. As the next point of Shikoku to the main island of Honshu, Takamatsu experienced a flourishing period as an impressive castle city during the feudal period. After your check-in, you will have the rest of the day all to yourself to enjoy the charming atmosphere of the city. Stroll through the historic streets and let yourself be seduced by the unique culinary delights of the region. Takamatsu offers a perfect mix of traditional charm and modern lifestyle. Overnight in Takamatsu.

Day 3 Takamatsus Gardens and Bonsais

Without guidance

Make your day according to your wishes. Upon request, we are happy to book excursions for you. Overnight in Takamatsu.

Optional: Tour of Takamatsus Gardens and Bonsai
Meet with your guide in the hotel lobby or at Takamatsu train station. Drive by taxi to a bonsai farm and get insights into the art of miniature trees. After a bento lunch, design your own moss ball under guidance. Then visit the historic Ritsurin Garden with its centuries-old pine trees. 

Optional: A day full of art in Naoshima
Meeting point with your guide at the port of Takamatsu and ferry ride to Naoshima Island. Together you will discover art installations and several “House Projects”. After lunch, visit the Benesse Museum and see Yayoi Kusama’s famous pumpkin artwork. Return by ferry.

Day 4 adventure Kotohira

Without guidance

Enjoy breakfast and get on the train to Kotohira, the quiet city in Kagawa known for its impressive mountain temple. Upon arrival, the rest of the day will be at your disposal. Do you feel the desire for more adventure today? How about the ascent to Kompira-san, an important Shinto and Buddhist complex that venerates the Shinto god of seafarers. The shrine is located on the slopes of Mt. Zozu and can only be reached via a long, steep staircase with a total of 1,368 stone steps. Then enjoy the amenities of your ryokan. In the evening, the multi-course Kaiseki dinner awaits you in the evening, which will spoil you with exquisite, carefully prepared dishes. End the day with a relaxing swim in the private onsen as you enjoy the view of the majestic Mt. Kompira before you bed in your futon for a well-deserved rest. Overnight in Kotohira.

Day 5 Gardens, Temples and an Ancient Castle

Without guidance

The next destination on your trip is the town of Kochi, known for its historic castle and exquisite fish dishes such as the dried bonito. Walking through Godaisan Park is an impressive experience, as is a visit to the nearby Botanical Garden dedicated to Japanese botany father Tomitaro Makino. Just a few steps away is the Chikurin-ji Temple, considered the 31st temple of the famous Shikoku 88 Temple Pilgrimage Route, whose sacred halls hold 16 treasures, including centuries-old statues of Buddhist gods. However, Kochi’s highlight is the iconic castle, which is one of the few historic castles in Japan that have survived all wars, fires and natural disasters since feudal times intact. Its majestic appearance and the history it embodies make the castle a fascinating symbol of the city. Overnight in Kochi. 

Day 6: Travel to Matsuyama

Without guidance

From Kochi, travel on to Matsuyama, which is located in Ehime Prefecture. Ehime is especially known for its pilgrimage routes, hot thermal springs and beautiful cycling routes. Overnight in Matsuyama.

Day 7 Explore Matsuyama

Without guidance

Make this day according to your taste. Discover Matsuyama at your own pace and enjoy the relaxed atmosphere of this charming city. Stroll through the lively shopping streets around Okaido, ride the nostalgic Botchan tram and discover the city’s special features on your own. Overnight in Matsuyama.

Optional: Sightseeing tour of Matsuyama
You will meet your guide and set off for the city together. With the local tram you can reach the famous Matsuyama Castle, one of the twelve remaining original castles in Japan. After lunch, continue to the Ishiteji Temple. Explore the architecture and art of the temple before diving into a long cave that houses numerous Buddhist statues. In the afternoon, continue to the historic Dogo Onsen area. Visit Isaniwa Shrine and climb the 135 steep steps.

Day 8 Hot Springs at Beppu

Without guidance

Today it means saying goodbye to Shikoku Your journey will take you to Kyushu. Your destination is the city of Beppu, known for its hot springs. The route from Matsuyama to Beppu can be covered by train to Yawatahama. From here, take the ferry to Kyushu. After checking in at the hotel, you still have time to relax there or for a short walk through the area. Overnight in Beppu. 

Day 9 Exploring Fukuoka

Get on the train to Hakata Station. Enjoy and discover the diversity of the city according to your personal preferences. Start the day with a visit to the traditional Kushida-jinja. Afterwards, take a detour to the lively Yanagibashi Rengo Ichiba Fish Market and experience the modern side of the city in the afternoon, including a visit to Fukuoka Tower. Early in the evening you will be warmly welcomed by a Hakata Maiko, a young trainee of the traditional arts at the venue. The atmosphere of ancient Japan comes alive through a private performance of classical Japanese dances and traditional “ozashiki asobi” (parlor plays). This is followed by a traditional tea ceremony. Finally, head to the nearby docks where you will board a traditional wooden boat. Overnight in Fukuoka.

Optional: Taste of Fukuoka – Culinary voyage of discovery
Dive into Fukuoka’s cuisine with your guide – from the fish market to local specialties and the famous Yatai street stalls.

Day 10 Discovery Tour of Arita – Porcelain and Craftsmanship

After breakfast, head to Arita Station and join your guide for a walk through the streets of this historic city. Enjoy attractions such as the Izumiyama quarry, which was the source of kaolin clay that sparked Japan’s porcelain revolution, and the rustic tombai walls made from recycled oven bricks. Stop at the elegant Tozan Shrine, dedicated to the protective gods of ceramics and adorning a unique porcelain torii. For lunch, head to a local restaurant that combines traditional gastronomy with Arita’s fine porcelain design. In the afternoon, delve deeper into Arita’s craftsmanship and visit the prestigious Kouraku ceramic oven, where you’ll learn more about the production process on a guided tour. Afterwards, a unique “treasure hunt” experience awaits you: you will have the opportunity to browse in storage rooms full of forgotten antique pieces of porcelain and purchase a memorabilia for your home. At the end of the day, return to Arita Station, say goodbye to your guide and return to Fukuoka. Overnight in Fukuoka.

Day 11 Next stop: Nagasaki

Without guidance

Your next destination is the historically important city of Nagasaki, known mainly for its tragic past. Originally a small fishing village in the county of Nishisonogi, Nagasaki developed into an important port city due to the arrival of the Portuguese in the second half of the 16th century.

Optional: Gunkanjima landing and cruise course  
Upon arrival at the hotel, a boat trip is offered in Hashima, better known as Gunkanjima, a small abandoned island about 15 kilometers off the coast of Nagasaki. For decades, it was an important underwater coal mine and a controversial symbol of forced labor during World War II. Today, the island is famous for its complete decommissioning in 1974 and its fascinating appearance. In July 2015, UNESCO declared Hashima a World Heritage Site due to its outstanding importance for the industrialization of Japan. 

Day 12 Explore Nagasaki

Start the day with an introduction to Nagasaki’s eventful history. First, visit the Peace Park site together with your guide, which gives a strong sense of remembrance with its calm atmosphere and touching monuments. It is a good place to prepare for a visit to the Atomic Bomb Museum, where impressive exhibitions and memories offer deep insights into the city’s tragic past while also revealing its inspiring rebirth and path of peace. Afterwards you reach the Glover hill together, which offers a fascinating perspective on the city. Here you can admire the western-influenced buildings and enjoy the view of the harbor. Continue to the Oura Church, Japan’s oldest Christian church still preserved, symbolizing the influence of European missionaries in Nagasaki. In the Glover Garden, an open-air museum, you will experience the elegance of Western residences. The next stop will take you to the well-preserved Dejima Island, which was Japan’s only gateway to the world during the Edo period. The crowning conclusion is the Megane Bridge, an iconic stone bridge. Overnight in Nagasaki.

Day 13: Travel to Kumamoto

Without guidance

Hop on the train to Kumamoto today, a scenic city that stretches across a wide plain and is framed by Ariake Bay to the west as well as the majestic Central Kyushu Mountains to the east. Enjoy the day according to your taste and discover the heart of the city in peace. Explore Kumamoto on foot or use the tram to visit the impressive Kumamoto Castle and the idyllic Shuizen-ji Joju-en Garden. The Kumamoto Castle, built at the beginning of the Edo period between 1601 and 1607 under the direction of Kato Kiyomasa, is one of the three most important castles in Japan and immerses you in an era long gone. Walking through the Suizenji Joju-en Garden, a park that includes a miniature version of Mt. Fuji and replicas of the 53 post stations on the famous road between Edo and Kyoto, will give you a wonderful view of Japan’s natural and cultural diversity. Overnight in Kumamto.

14th day exploration tour of Kumamoto

Without guidance

What are you doing today? Let your heart guide you and spontaneously decide how you want to spend the day. Relax in the hotel or explore Kumamoto on your own. If you wish, we will gladly book an excursion for you. Overnight in Kumamoto.

Optional Tour: The Legacy of Miyamoto Musashi in Kumamoto
Experience the world of Japanese sword art: In traditional clothing, enjoy matcha and learn about the philosophy of Iaido. Afterwards, you will see a sword demonstration and practice basic techniques in the dojo itself under the guidance of a master.

Day 15 Kagoshima is waiting for you

Without guidance

Your next destination is the city of Kagoshima, which once flourished as a castle town around the residence of Shimazu feudal lords. After your check-in, you still have time to explore the city a little on your own. Overnight in Kagoshima.

Day 16 Kagoshima: From Historic Gardens to Stunning Volcanoes

Your guide awaits you and takes you on an exploration tour of the city. The first stop is the Sengan-en Japanese Garden. Afterwards, you will have lunch at the garden restaurant, where you will taste local specialties. After lunch, take a short ferry ride to Mt. Sakurajima, an active volcano. Small eruptions as well as smoke and ash clouds are commonplace here. With public transport, you can reach Yunohira Observation Point and learn more about the volcano at Sakurajima Visitor Centre. Right next to the center is a park where you can take a foot bath in hot springs. Take the ferry back to the city to visit the shopping district of Kagoshima. Overnight in Kagoshima. 

Optional Tour: Healing Sand Beaches & Hidden Springs from Kagoshima
With your guide you will discover Ibusuki and experience a relaxing sand bath in the warm volcanic sands. Afterwards, head to the idyllic Unagi Onsen Village, where you will learn more about the geothermal springs.

Day 17 - Go to Yakushima

Without guidance

The island is calling! Take the ferry to Yakushima, a subtropical island south of Kyushu. Thanks to its geographical location and scenic beauty, the island is a paradise for hikers and nature lovers. Upon landing in Yakushima, you will be welcomed with the fantastic sight of a huge cedar forest. Some of the gigantic trees are already several thousand years old and create a mystical atmosphere. Your guide awaits you and accompanies you to your accommodation for the next three nights. From your beautiful hotel, the Sankara Hotel & Spa, you have a fantastic view of the sea. The perfect place to enjoy the scent of lush greenery all around and the delicious fresh breeze. The relaxed atmosphere and noble design of the Sankara Hotel & Spa invites you to dream and linger. Just the right hotel for guests with a sense of sophistication like you! Overnight on Yakushima.

18th day hike to Mt. Kuromidake

Today, a full-day hike is on your program. Together with your guide, you will climb Mt. Kuromidake, from the top of which you can enjoy a breathtaking view of the island with its fascinating mountains, lush forests and coastline. The climb is easy to moderate and is suitable for beginners as well as experienced hikers. Again and again you take small breaks on the way to enjoy nature. Once upstairs, enjoy your lunch, a homemade bento lunchbox with fresh island produce. Well strengthened you then make your way back to your hotel. Overnight on Yakushima.

19th Day Walk through the ancient forests of Yakushima

Another hiking day with your guide is waiting for you. Instead of climbing a summit, follow the paths through Yakushima’s dense forests today. Even though this hike is physically less demanding, the backdrop is at least as impressive. The gigantic, often moss-covered trees offer a special sight. In particular, the two ancient tree giants, Buddha Sugi and Yamato Sugi, where your path passes, will captivate you. These impressive trees are already 1,800 and 3,000 years old! Today you will also enjoy a delicious Bento lunch before making your way back to the hotel. Overnight in Yakushima.

Day 20 Back to Fukuoka

Without guidance

Before you leave Yakushima this afternoon, there is still time to enjoy the beauty of the island on a kayaking trip on the Anbo River. The tranquil, wide river is easy to master even for kayaking newbies. Enjoy the island’s forests from a different perspective and look for monkeys and turtles. After a final Bento lunch, you will be taken to Yakushima Airport. During the last few weeks you have collected many impressions of Japan’s culture and scenic beauty that will remain in your memory for a long time. With these pictures in your head and a full heart, you will start your flight home.

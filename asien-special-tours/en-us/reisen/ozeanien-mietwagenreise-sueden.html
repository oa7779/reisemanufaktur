Once in a lifetime to Australia - this dream comes true! Experience the sparsely populated expanse of the 5th continent up close on this individual journey from Adelaide to Sydney. Enjoy the Kangaroo Island natural paradise and marvel at the spectacular rock formations along the Great Ocean Road. Variety offers the lively metropolis Melbourne as well as a trip to Phillip Island to the penguins. Crown this trip with a stay in Sydney: the cosmopolitan city will delight you with its natural harbor and the beaches on the coast.

1st day of travel to Australia

Leave everyday life at home! Today you start your journey to Adelaide, and like any long-distance journey, yours begins with the corresponding long-distance flight. Use the time on the plane to browse a little in your guidebook and tune in to the next few days.

Day 2 Arrival in Adelaide

Welcome to Australia! Upon arrival in Adelaide, you will be picked up at the airport and warmly welcomed by your guide. Together with your chauffeur you drive to your hotel. The rest of the day is at your free disposal. Relax after arriving and take advantage of the amenities of your hotel before embarking on a cultural exploration tour tomorrow! Overnight in Adelaide.

3rd day of free time in Adelaide

This day is free for you. Take the time to explore Adelaide on your own. Adelaide is the capital of South Australia, famous for music, culture, beach life and sporting events. We recommend a walk through the center with a visit to the parks, markets and museums. The Glenelg outskirts, with its extensive beach, can be reached by a tram in half an hour from the city and captivates with its attractive restaurants and shops where you can watch romantic sunsets by the sea. Adelaide is the gateway to the fantastic Flinders Ranges, the famous wine-growing area Barossa Valley and McLaren Vale, and Kangaroo Island Animal Paradise. Overnight in Adelaide.

4th day car rental and ferry ride to Kangaroo Island

Take over your rental car today, there are various models available, for example a Hyundai Elantra and drive to Cape Jervis. Take the ferry to Kangaroo Island. Overnight at Flinders Chase.

5th day Kangaroo Island

Kangaroo Island is Australia's Animal Paradise Number 1Thousands of koalas live on the island, as do kangaroos, wallabies, sea lions and many more. Apart from that, it is the unique landscape with its rugged coastline and white beaches that make Kangaroo Island so popular. Overnight at Flinders Chase.

6th day ferry back to mainland and on to Victor Harbour

Arriving at Victor Harbour around the afternoon, you can take the opportunity to walk along the 17 miles stand or swim in Guichen Bay. Overnight at Victor Harbour.

7th day robe

Today you will continue with your rental vehicle to Robe, about 4 hours south of the Barossa Valley. The area has one of the oldest wine-growing areas in Australia. Marvel at the colorful landscape, best at a beautiful sunset. Breathe the air of a relaxed, Australian coastal town and look forward to the Great Ocean Road. Overnight in Robe.

8.-9. Day of Grampians National Park

The national park has a variety of plant species and is a home for thylacine and mammals. But also different species of birds and insects can be discovered on hikes through the Grampians. It awaits you a breathtaking landscape with mountain ranges and rainforest sections. You can explore nature at various viewpoints such as the Pinnacle, the Reeds Lookout, the Balconies or the Boroka Lookout. Overnight at Halls Gap.

Day 10 - Continue to Australia's spectacular south coast

Today we will head to Port Fairy, the western gateway for the Great Ocean Road. Explore the area on your own or relax in a relaxed, Australian atmosphere at the local restaurants. Overnight in Port Fairy.

11.-12. Fantastic Great Ocean Road

Today you reach the highlight of your car rental tour - the Great Ocean Road with the legendary Twelve Apostles. The road winds close to the coast and is one of the most legendary coastal roads in Australia. Stunning sea views on one side, high mountain ranges and quiet towns on the other, so presents the Great Ocean Road. The highlight is the eight remaining limestone columns of the Twelve Apostles. Overnight in Apollo Bay.

13th day onwards to Melbourne

Melbourne is a cosmopolitan metropolis hosting sporting events such as Formula One and the Australian Open. Here a lively and modern scene has established itself, setting trends in architecture, design, art and gastronomy. The metropolis of Melbourne stands for fashion, hip bars and street cafés, elegant houses and lively nightlife. Some of the most famous historical buildings, such as Flinders Street Station, are impressive testimonies to Australian history. We recommend a walk through the bustling center of the big city or a visit to the impressive aquarium with its huge shark pool. Overnight in Melbourne.

14th day excursion to Phillip Island for the penguins

Your unforgettable day begins with a visit to the Moonlit Sanctuary Conservation Park, where you can experience many encounters with Australian animals such as kangaroos, wombats and echidnas. We will then go to Phillip Island where if time/season permits. We’ll take you to Woolamai Surf Beach for a glimpse of the breathtaking waves and die-hard surfers. We then drive to the dramatic west coast of the island. At the Nobbies, stroll along the boardwalk to experience the waves along this dramatic coastline and perhaps see some seals and some penguin nests. A trip to Phillip Island is not complete without the Black Tie Gala event: the Penguin Parade. You will go to the visitor center to shop, have a coffee and stroll along the promenade to the observation deck. There you can see the little penguins dressed in their tuxedoes and hurry back to find their caves. Overnight in Melbourne.

15th – 16th day Wilson Promontory National Park

Probably the most spectacular national park in Victoria on a peninsula south of Melbourne. 130 km of coastline and over 80 km of hiking trails offer relaxation with a great wildlife and original plant diversity. The national park, which is about 200 kilometers from Melbourne, is very diverse in terms of landscape. The trails lead to secluded coves, bizarre rock formations and through the rainforest. More than 700 plant species grow here. Bird lovers also get their money’s worth. Colorful parrots, wombats and curious wallabies make a rendezvous. Overnight in Foster.

17th day Spectacular coastline around Lakes Entrance

Lake landscape with dolphins: The Gippsland Lakes is the largest network of lakes, marshes and lagoons in Australia with over 400 square kilometers. They extend to the ocean, but are separated from the sea by the dunes at Ninety Mile Beach. From the popular resort of Lakes Entrance, you can reach the beach on foot via a small bridge. Here you can also rent a boat and explore the many islands in the Gippsland Lakes. Along the way, look for dolphins and pelicans. Overnight in Esplanade.

Day 18 – 19 The Saphire Coast in the Southeast

On the Saphire Coast of New South Wales, six hours from Sydney and three hours from Canberra, you can escape the hustle and bustle of everyday life. If you spend your holiday in this coastal region, you can really switch off and enjoy the tranquility of nature and much more: the sparkling sea, the green hillside landscape, the quiet lakes, the cliffs, the caves, the friendly coastal villages and national parks. It is the perfect destination for long walks in nature, water sports as well as good food and excellent wine. Overnight in Merimbula and overnight in Batemans Bay

20th day Onward travel to Sydney and rental car tax

Sydney, with its natural harbor basin, is one of the most beautiful cities in the world and lures visitors with its world-famous icons such as the Harbour Bridge and the Opera House. Sydney is a bustling city and home to the historic district of The Rocks, Circular Quay, Darling Harbour and Bondi Beach. Drop off the rental car here and discover this metropolis on foot or by public transport. Go shopping for yourself and your loved ones at home or see one of the many museums, visit the Sydney Aquarium or drive up the Sydney Tower. Take the ferry from Circular Quay to Manly Beach or visit the famous Bondi Beach. Sydney has more than enough to offer to spend a fantastic day. Overnight in Paddington, Sydney.

Experience the 21st day of Sydney in the luxury classic car from 1964

Your day starts at 9.30 a.m., where you’ll be greeted by your local guide and his buddy Horace – a 1964 vintage car and the most popular luxury car ever made in Australia. Stylishly cruise through Sydney, with the beguiling scent of fine leather in your nose, leaning back into soft lambskin seat covers. After a delightful ride around The Rocks, you will reach your first destination. Thanks to your guide, you can discover a thriving inner-city scene where Aboriginal murals and contemporary street art decorate the alleys with turn-of-the-century terrace houses. No trip to Sydney would be complete without a visit to the beaches. First, we will take you to a cemetery by the sea that offers breathtaking views of the sandstone coast. From there, you’ll head to three of Sydney’s most famous inner-city beaches: Bronte, Tamarama and Bondi. Ask your guide to lower the windows so you can breathe the eucalyptus in the air as you cruise through the hills of Sydney’s most exclusive suburbs. This is how you get to your final destination: a secluded beach with panoramic views of the harbor! Overnight in Paddington, Sydney.

22nd day excursion to the Blue Mountains

Today we head west to the Featherdale Wildlife Park, where you get close to the typical Australian wildlife. Today’s destination is the Blue Mountains (World Heritage Site), a more than 1000 m high sandstone plateau with deep gorges and waterfalls. The name comes from the bluish haze that typically lies over the dense eucalyptus forests of the national park. At the Wentworth Falls, explore the Princess Lookout with fantastic views of the trees. At Echo Point with the world-famous rock formation “The Three Sisters”, visit the Waradah Aboriginal Centre, a cultural center of native Aboriginal tribes. After that, visit the Govetts Leap viewpoint and enjoy a delicious lunch in a historic inn. The journey continues to Mt Banks and the Mt Tomah Botanical Garden. After crossing the Hawkesbury River, you will find a short stop in the country town of Windsor before returning to the City via Harbour Bridge. Overnight in Paddington, Sydney.

23rd day of free time in Sydney

Today is a free day ahead of you. Take advantage of the amenities of your accommodation or make the day on your own. The city also invites with its many small shops for strolling and browsing. Overnight in Paddington, Sydney.

24th day Departure from Australia

Your break from everyday life is coming to an end, and your driver will take you back to Sydney airport for your flight. Even if your holiday is soon over – the precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories of your trip will accompany you forever. Look forward to letting your friends and family share your experiences in the distance at home.

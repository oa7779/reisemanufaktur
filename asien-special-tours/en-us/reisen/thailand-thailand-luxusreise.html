Thailand in style

Thailand Luxury Travel: Experience Thailand with Asia Special Tours In Style. Request directly here and have individual Thailand trips created.

Itinerary & services

English itinerary text is an offline machine translation of the German original for this team preview. Please confirm travel details with your advisor; the German source remains available through the language switch.

Let yourself be impressed by the cultural and historical treasures of Thailand. Enjoy a varied round trip at the highest level, starting in the fascinating metropolis of Bangkok – the city of a thousand impressions. A mess of honking mopeds, tuk tuks and street kitchens, in between Buddhist temples and green resting points. Afterwards, you will head to the Golden Triangle in the north of the country, where the absolute highlight of this round trip awaits you: the luxurious camp nights at Four Seasons Tented Camp. You can relax and unwind your soul in the luxury hotels we have selected for you on Phuket or Koh Samui. Let yourself be pampered and read every wish from the eyes.

Day 1 Sawasdee in Bangkok

Sawasdee – Welcome to Thailand. Upon arrival at the International Airport you will be received personally and without long waiting times the entry formalities will be completed for you. Your German-speaking tour guide and your chauffeur will then take you to your first-class hotel, where you can acclimatize in peace. The rest of the day is at your disposal. When it gets slowly dark, you will be driven to the pier of the Chao Phraya River. Your first evening in Bangkok will end with a romantic dinner cruise. Enjoy a delicious Thai dinner as the glitter metropolis and illuminated temples glide majestically past you. Overnight in Bangkok.

Day 2 Over the Roofs of Bangkok

After an excellent breakfast, you will be picked up by your guide to get to know the most important sights in the city. Highlights include the Wat Phra Keo with the Emerald Buddha, as well as the magnificent Royal Palace, one of the most beautiful examples of Siamese architecture. One of the most beautiful temples is Wat Pho. After lunch at Sala Rattanakosin Restaurant, you can relax on a Klong tour with a rustic longtail boat. The boat tour makes it clear why Bangkok is also called the “Venice of the East”: Since there were hardly any roads in Bangkok until about 1850, the canals were once the most important transport medium in the city. Today, modern teak houses, like traditional ones, line the water and a significant part of everyday life still takes place on the water. You also visit Bangkok's most photographed building: the Temple of Dawn, Wat Arun. In the evening, dinner is served at the Vertigo Restaurant of the well-known Banyan Tree Hotel. Enjoy an excellent dinner and cocktails (not included) while Bangkok is literally at your feet. Overnight in Bangkok.

Day 3 The Many Facets of Bangkok

Bangkok is a fascinating melting pot that combines a variety of cultural influences. This becomes especially clear when visiting the bustling district of Chinatown – Bangkok’s largest “open-air restaurant”: At every corner, merchants sell other specialties of street cuisine. The aromas of various spices and dishes roam through the air and mix with the smells that blow from the small restaurants or from the markets. A very special fragrance prevails at the flower market nearby, where colorful flowers and flowers are sold loose or wound into wreaths and garlands. Along the way, your guide will show you exotic fruits, let you taste Thai street cuisine specialties and tell you about the range of products on offer. After lunch at the Above Riva Restaurant, we will continue with a visit to the Jim Thompson House. If you wish, you can then plunge into the shopping fun to your heart's content in the modern shopping temples at Siam Square. In recent years, Siam Square has seen the emergence of numerous fashionable shopping malls. Overnight in Bangkok.

Day 4 Former Royal City of Ayutthaya

The day begins with a ride to the former summer palace of Banh Pha-In. A decorated collection of pavilions and palaces from different eras and cultural backgrounds gives beautiful photo opportunities there. Afterwards, we continue on to the second capital of the kingdom of Siam: Ayutthaya. The number of large ruins in the historic park allows conclusions to be drawn about the former splendor of the city, where 1 million people are said to have lived at weddings. You will visit the most important of the ruins extensively before returning to Bangkok! Overnight in Bangkok.

5th day onward journey to northern Thailand

After breakfast, leave Bangkok and fly to Chiang Rai in the north of the country. From there, the journey will take you to the fabled Golden Triangle where the borders of Thailand, Laos and Myanmar meet. After about an hour's drive and a 15-minute boat ride, you'll reach the exclusive Four Seasons Tented Camps. Already upon arrival, a breathtaking view of the unspoiled nature of the jungle opens up. The luxurious camp with only 15 stylish tent suites, equipped with handmade furniture and freestanding bathtub, blends harmoniously into the surrounding jungle landscape and offers you an ideal retreat. In addition to relaxing at the idyllic pool or spa, there are numerous activities at your disposal, some of which already include special experiences. In the evening, the Burma Bar invites you to a sundowner with spectacular views. Culinary delights the Nong Yao Restaurant in a stylish ambience. Overnight in Chiang Rai.

Day 6 - A day according to your wishes

This day offers you the opportunity to experience the Four Seasons Tented Camp in all its exclusivity and tranquility. Enjoy the extraordinary “My Elephant & I” experience, where you encounter elephants in a particularly mindful and touching way. Together with experienced mahouts, you will spend time with the animals in their natural environment and gain valuable insights into sustainable elephant conservation and respectful treatment of these fascinating creatures. Complete relaxation is provided by a 90-minute spa treatment that balances body and mind. In the evening you will end the day with an atmospheric sundowner in the elegant Burma Bar before a private wine and cheese tasting or alternatively a Thai charcuterie with local craft beer rounds off this special camp experience. Overnight in Chiang Rai.

Day 7 Excursion to the Golden Triangle

Today you will take a half-day boat trip to the legendary Golden Triangle, the melting pot of three countries. Take advantage of the remaining day for further activities or just enjoy the amenities of your hotel. Overnight in Chiang Rai.

Day 8 Chiang Mai – the Rose of the North

After breakfast, drive through the mountains to Chiang Mai, also known as ‘Rose of the North’. Along the way, visit the extraordinary “White Temple” in Chiang Rai. When visiting a tea plantation, you will learn about tea cultivation and processing. A cup of freshly brewed tea is of course also served. In the afternoon you will arrive at Chiang Mai, where you will be taken to your tasteful hotel. Chiang Mai is also known as the Rose of the North. The city is enchanted by a multitude of magnificent temples and lies in the middle of a beautiful landscape. In the surrounding mountains live in numerous small settlements mountain peoples who have preserved their ancient traditions in the immediate vicinity of this modern metropolis. Overnight in Chiang Mai. Our tip: Stroll in the evening over the night market in the city center or enjoy the luxurious ambience of your hotel.

9th Day Morning Mood in Chiang Mai

The day begins before breakfast today, when you attend a food-giving ceremony that is as common as it is important for Thai Buddhists: you join the groups of believers who are waiting on the side of the road for the monks to give them food. For the monks, this is the only possible food acquisition and for the faithful a good opportunity to acquire good karma for the coming life cycle. After these morning impressions, you will return to your hotel for breakfast. Then your guide will take you on a tour of the city. Although the second most important city in Thailand, Chiang Mai looks quite tranquil compared to Bangkok. The architecture of the countless small temples is mainly influenced by the style of the once mighty Lanna Empire. But the first Burmese influences are also noticeable through the proximity of Myanmar. Proverbial highlight is the visit to the mountain temple Wat Doi Suthep – at an altitude of almost 1,000 meters. Once you have arrived at the top, you can enjoy a panoramic view of the city and its surroundings in beautiful weather. Chiang Mai is also a city of markets and crafts worth exploring. Overnight in Chiang Mai.

Day 10: Design the day according to your interests

Chiang Mai and its surroundings offer a variety of possibilities to design today according to your personal interests. Our suggestions for today: Take a trip to the Doi Inthanon National Park, known for its pleasantly cool climate, lush vegetation and scenic diversity. With 2,565 meters, it is also the highest elevation in Thailand. Picturesque waterfalls, peaceful temples in the middle of the mountain landscape and impressive views of the surrounding mountain ranges characterize this day. The temperatures are very pleasant, sometimes even a bit cool, which is a delightful contrast to the hazy lowlands. With a bit of luck, you will discover colorful butterflies, songbirds, taste locally grown coffee and get insights into the life of the Karen mountain tribes. Or let yourself be introduced to the secrets of Thai cuisine during a cooking class. The day begins with a visit to a local market, where you will learn to choose fresh ingredients and learn more about the typical products of the region. Afterwards, an experienced cook will show you step by step the preparation of traditional dishes. At your personal kitchen workplace, you will put what you have learned into practice before enjoying the four prepared dishes (the cooking course is in English). Overnight in Chiang Mai.

Day 11 - Travel to Phuket or Koh Samui for bathing extension

Enjoy a final breakfast at this beautiful hotel. After that, you will be taken to the airport in Chiang Mai and fly to either Phuket or Koh Samui. Upon arrival, you will be chauffeured to your booked luxury hotel. Overnight on Phuket or Koh Samui.

12-14. Day Phuket or Koh Samui: Enjoying the sun, beach and sea

These days are at your disposal. Relax on the beach with a good book and let the days of this wonderful trip pass again. Overnight on Phuket or Koh Samui. Your hotel on Phuket is the multi-award winning Amanpuri, which will delight you with its breathtaking atmosphere. Located on one of Phuket’s most beautiful beaches, the house spoils you with simple, timeless elegance and first-class, unobtrusive service. The individual villas, typical of the country with the pointed roof, blend harmoniously into the tropical environment and are distributed on the slope. According to the concept of Aman Resorts, your villa offers a reduced, discreet luxury in Thai style. Pure luxury also awaits you at the Aman Spa as well as in the fine restaurants. On Koh Samui, stay at the luxury Four Seasons Resort Koh Samui. It is located in an idyllic hillside on one of the most beautiful beaches of the island on a private bay. The 60 elegantly furnished villas offer the highest possible comfort and privacy as well as a private infinity pool. The unforgettable view of the turquoise Gulf of Siam will never be forgotten. If you manage to detach your eyes from this view, a massage awaits you in the luxurious spa. Overnight stays on Phuket or Koh Samui.

15th day Phuket or Koh Samui – Departure

Today we say goodbye to this unforgettable journey. You will be picked up in person at the hotel and taken to the airport on Phuket or Koh Samui by private air-conditioned vehicle, and you will start your journey home.

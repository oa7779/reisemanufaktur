Family Deluxe - Summer Happiness in Vietnam

Private Family Tour of Central Vietnam: Imperial City Hue, Charming Hoi An, Stylish Train Ride with The Vietage and Relaxed Days by the Sea.

Itinerary & services

English itinerary text is an offline machine translation of the German original for this team preview. Please confirm travel details with your advisor; the German source remains available through the language switch.

Experience a really good time together: This private trip through central Vietnam combines special experiences for the whole family with plenty of space to enjoy and relax. In Hue, immerse yourself in the fascinating history of Vietnam, cycle through the vast lagoon landscape and get creative with traditional craftsmen themselves. In Hoi An is paddled, cooked and carved. Not only is it being watched, but it is also being tried and participated in. And in between, there is enough time to simply not have to do anything. Your private tour guides and carefully selected accommodation ensure that you are comfortable and flexible on the road. Among the special moments of the trip is the stylish train ride by Vietage by Anantara, while the countryside of central Vietnam passes you leisurely. Finally, Zannier Bai San Ho is an extraordinary hideaway in Phu Yen, surrounded by nature and located on a long beach. Here the days belong entirely to you and your loved ones. One thing remains of this trip: a really good time together, filled with common discoveries, small adventures and many new impressions. Whether as a family, together with friends or with several generations: We design your trip according to your personal wishes and fit your travel constellation.

1st day Arrival in Hue – arrive in peace and relax

Welcome to Hue! Upon arrival, you will be received by your personal tour guide and travel to your hotel in a comfortable private transfer. The city of Hue, whose name means “harmony”, welcomes you with a special atmosphere between the perfume flow, old palaces and imperial buildings. Your hotel, Ancient Hue Garden Houses, is idyllic in the historic village of Kim Long, just minutes from the Imperial City. The boutique resort combines traditional Nguyen dynasty architecture with modern comfort, creating an authentic yet stylish temporary home. The lovingly restored garden houses and villas with spacious terraces, high-quality materials and elegant design offer plenty of space to feel at home. The green gardens invite you to stay, while the pool provides a refreshing break in between. Enjoy culinary highlights in the restaurants, relax at the spa or spend precious family moments in the special atmosphere of this historic place. After arriving, the rest of the day is all yours. Arrive in peace, relax by the pool or already discover the surroundings. So everyone can start the journey at their own pace. Overnight in Hue.

Day 2 The Old Imperial City & Sunset in the Lagoon Landscape

Today you will discover the former imperial city of Hue, which is a UNESCO World Heritage site, with its different facets. The first is the Imperial Citadel, where Vietnam’s history comes alive between mighty walls, gates and palaces. Then continue to a traditional garden house of Hue, which was designed according to the principles of harmony and balance and gives a nice insight into the way of life of the region. Then visit the Tu-Duc Mausoleum. With its gardens, pavilions and lakes, it is one of the special testimonies of Hue’s imperial past. The journey to the Chuon Lagoon leads past vast areas of water, rice fields and small villages of rural central Vietnam. During a boat trip, you will explore the lagoon from the water, gain insights into the everyday life of fishing families and discover the stilt houses typical of the region. After a short break at a local restaurant with small snacks, enjoy the special atmosphere of this vast waterscape before returning to your hotel in the evening. Overnight in Hue.

Day 3: Moto Taxi to Hue Craft Villages

In the morning you will discover the rural surroundings of Hue in a special way: With the typical moto taxi, you will go out into small villages where old craftsmanship is maintained until today. You will visit a traditional community house, meet the local people and learn about various local techniques, from mother-of-pearl work and blacksmithing to furnaces for the figures of the “Ong Tao”. In between, there is time for a break with tea or coffee in a small tea house. Then it gets creative. In a village known for its nearly 400-year-old tradition of paper flower making, you will look over the shoulders of a family of craftsmen and get to try it yourself. In the village of Sinh, home to Hue’s traditional woodcuts, a local artist will show you the traditional printing technique before making your own small print as a personal memorabilia. In the afternoon, leave Hue and head to Hoi An via the Cloud Pass. Then arrive at Four Seasons Resort The Nam Hai near Hoi An. Amidst tropical gardens and right on the beach, the resort is a wonderful retreat after an eventful day. Private villas, pools and the spa invite you to end the rest of the day according to your own wishes. Overnight at Hoi An.

Day 4 By basket boat through the water world & picturesque Hoi An

In the morning, an approximately 45-minute boat ride will take you to the port of Cua Dai at the mouth of the Thu Bon River. There you will transfer to a round bamboo boat, as it is used by the fishermen of the region. The “basket boat” owes its name to its unusual shape, which is reminiscent of an inverted walnut shell. The small boats can accommodate up to five people and take you into the hidden canals of the flooded forest: quiet, close to the water and in the middle of the green. Then you take the wheel yourself: With circular paddle movements it goes forward - a fun challenge for the whole family. Afterwards, fortify yourself with a local family with a freshly prepared, regional lunch. In the afternoon you will discover the historic old town of Hoi An. The charming UNESCO World Heritage City delights with its well-preserved alleys and an exciting interplay of Vietnamese, Japanese, Chinese and French influences. During your walking tour, you will see the famous Japanese Bridge, a Chinese temple and the house of Tan Ky, which belonged to a wealthy Vietnamese merchant over 200 years ago and has remained almost unchanged since then. Overnight at Hoi An. 

Day 5 From the market to the kitchen – Cooking experience & creative time in Hoi An

Go to the cooking class! Together with your chef, you start the day at a local market. Between colorful market stalls, you get to know typical ingredients, talk to the dealers and experience the lively activity up close. If you want to, you can also try to negotiate yourself. Afterwards we will visit the community of Tra Que, which is known for its herb gardens. During a walk through the beds, you will harvest fresh herbs and learn about their importance in Vietnamese cuisine and medicine. Well-equipped with fresh ingredients, it goes to the stove. Step by step, you prepare typical Vietnamese dishes under guidance and learn about traditional preparation methods. Afterwards, of course, we try and enjoy together: For lunch, your self-cooked dishes come to the table. In the afternoon, visit a driftwood village in the area around Hoi An. Artful sculptures and decorative pieces are created from washed wood. After a short visit, you can get creative yourself and create your own small artwork under guidance as a special memorial of your time in Hoi An. Overnight at Hoi An.

Day 6 The Vietage Train – Slow Travel in Style

After your private transfer to Danang station, board the “Vietage by Anantara” train. Instead of speed, the exclusive train relies on stylish, slow-paced travel. With only a few guests, an approximately six-hour drive begins. On all squares you can enjoy lodge views of the postcard landscapes of central Vietnam. Lush rice fields, bizarre karst mountains and glittering coastal landscapes pass by. Water buffaloes wallow in the fields, eyeed by white herons. Again and again, historical places such as the dilapidated city walls of Tra Kieu, the coastal town of Quang Ngai and Lake An Khe come into focus as the cradle of Sa Huynh culture. On board you will be spoiled culinary. In the stylish on-board restaurant, Vietnamese classics are interpreted modernly and refined with French influences. On request, a head and neck massage ensures additional well-being. Sit back relaxed and enjoy this special ride. At Dieu Tri Train Station near Quy Nhon, you’ll be expected and taken to your exclusive beach resort, Zannier Bai San Ho in Phu Yen Province. Between rolling hills, tropical vegetation and a long, natural beach, this extraordinary hideaway is beautifully secluded. After the eventful days, you will reach a wonderful place to arrive, breathe and enjoy. Overnight in Phu Yen.

7.-9. Day time for the sea, nature & sweet inaction

The coming days belong entirely to you and your loved ones. Enjoy hours together by the sea and let yourself be drifted by the rhythm of gentle waves. At Zannier Bai San Ho, everyone finds their favorite place. If you want to be active, explore the coastal landscape and the surrounding rice fields during walks or on a bicycle. The nearby bay invites you to snorkel or kayaking, while yoga and meditation provide peace and balance. Cooking classes and encounters in the surrounding villages also offer an opportunity to dive deeper into the Vietnamese lifestyle. Overnight in Phu Yen.

Day 10 Goodbye to Vietnam!

After relaxing days at the sea, today it is time to say goodbye to Phu Yen. Enjoy once again the special ambience of your resort and let your journey end in peace. Depending on the departure time, your transfer to the airport will be to Quy Nhon. Along the way, the landscape of central Vietnam passes you for the last time. With many beautiful memories of a time together full of discoveries and small adventures, you will begin your journey home.

Saudi Arabia Tour – Secrets between Desert and Sea

Experience the magic of Riyadh, Al Ula and the Red Sea on an exclusive, bespoke Saudi Arabia tour – culture meets luxury.

Saudi Arabia, a country that fascinates in the vastness of the desert and amidst impressive metropolises alike, invites you on a journey that brings the history and culture of this kingdom to life. Your discovery tour begins in Riyadh, the vibrant capital that embodies a unique combination of old and new with its futuristic skyscrapers and historic landmarks. Continue on your way to Buraida, where the traditional life of the Saudi provinces is still felt in the markets and old buildings. The desert opens its doors in Hail, a region known for its majestic rocks and the silence of endless sandy landscapes. Al-Ula, with its spectacular rocks and ancient sites, will take you to a time long past as you experience the breathtaking beauty of this historic oasis. Your journey along the Red Sea coast will take you to the fine sandy beach, which with its colors and expanses offers the perfect contrast to the desert. Finally, you will arrive at Jeddah, a vibrant blend of tradition and modernity, where the charm of ancient Arabia meets the cosmopolitan atmosphere of a port city. Let yourself be enchanted by the diversity and hospitality of this extraordinary country and discover the fascination of Saudi Arabia in a very special way.

1st day Arrival in Riyadh

Welcome to Riyadh, the birthplace of modern Saudi Arabia! Upon arrival at the airport, you will be warmly welcomed by our representative and taken to your hotel, where you can rest a little and get the first impression of this fascinating city. Riyadh, the country’s capital, is a vibrant metropolis where the charm of the old world meets the visions of the 21st century. This modern city is not only a thriving financial and business center, but also an emerging cultural hotspot. The province of Riyadh, also known as Al-Wosta, is rich in history – countless fortresses, magnificent palaces and interesting museums tell of a glamorous past. Special highlights are the colorful souks that reflect the lively bustle of the city. Overnight in Riyadh.

Day 2 Riyadh – Past and Modern

The day begins with a visit to Al Masmak Fortress, an imposing landmark from 1865 that plays a central role in the history of Saudi Arabia. It was here that the conquest of Riyadh by Ibn Saud was sealed in 1902, a moment that marked the history of the kingdom. Afterwards, the path will take you to Souq Al-Zal, one of the oldest markets in Riyadh, where you can immerse yourself in the variety of Arabic antiques, robes and beguiling scents. Afterwards you will experience the National Museum, which tells in a fascinating way the history of Saudi Arabia from the earliest artifacts to the most important exhibits from all eras. In the afternoon, visit Diriyah, the birthplace of the first Saudi state, and the UNESCO World Heritage Site Turaif, which is one of the country’s cultural highlights with its traditional adobe architecture and historic buildings. Enjoy the tranquility while walking through the green Al Bujairi Park. At one of the restaurants, you can fortify yourself with traditional dishes before ending the day with spectacular views from the Kingdom Tower skybridge. The view from above opens up an impressive panorama over Riyadh that reflects the modern side of the city. Overnight in Riyadh.

Day 3 Traditional Ushaiger and Buraida

In the morning, drive from Riyadh to Ushaiger, a picturesque village in the heart of the Najd region that looks back on over 1,500 years of history. The narrow streets and well-preserved mud walls offer a wonderful insight into the life of the Bedouins. Nestled in olive trees and date groves, Ushaiger was once an important resting place for pilgrims on their way to Mecca and is now a bustling village where ancient traditions are still maintained. Particularly impressive are the renovated houses with their characteristic triangular windows and ornately carved wooden doors. Continue on to Buraida, where you will visit the Al Bassam Heritage House in the suburb of Unayzah. Built in 1955, the building is an excellent example of traditional Najd architecture and shows how people once lived here. You will also visit the traditional Al Musawkaf Market, which is one of the most important historical landmarks in the region. Immerse yourself in a piece of living history! Overnight in Buraida.

4th day Live camel market in Buraida and on to Hail

Today you leave early from your hotel and drive to the camel market in Buraida, the largest camel market in the world. Enter the hectic main arena where buyers and sellers hold lively auctions and present their camels. The camel market is a living example of local traditions and a fascinating place to immerse yourself in the authentic life of the region. After this impressive experience, we continue towards the northwest to Jubbah. Here are petroglyphs up to 9,000 years old, offering a unique look at the life of the Neolithic period. These drawings show scenes from everyday life, clothing, animals and weapons and represent a valuable cultural heritage. From your experienced guide, you will learn more about the importance of this UNESCO World Heritage Site. On the way to Hail, stop at the historic A'aref Fort, once used to defend the city. There you can marvel at the impressive architecture and historical ambience of the fortress. After this exciting day, you will continue and reach your hotel in Hail. Overnight in Hail.

Day 5 Over the vastness of the desert – On the way to Al Ula

Today is a travel day ahead of you. We will now continue to Al Ula – a fascinating destination that awaits you with its historical and natural beauty. The ride takes you through the vast countryside of the Saudi desert, which is characterized by imposing rocks, rolling hills and endless horizons. During the ride, take short breaks to relax and enjoy the scenery. Along the way, you will have the opportunity to take a look at the different landscapes that make the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia so unique. This ride offers a deep insight into the vastness of the Arabian Peninsula, where time seems to be at a standstill. After arriving in Al Ula, you will still have time to relax at the hotel and prepare for exploring this historic city. Overnight in Al Ula.

Day 6 Mysterious Al Ula

Today, an unforgettable ride to the majestic desert wonders of Al Ula awaits you: First, head to the impressive UNESCO World Heritage Site Hegra (Madain Saleh), known for its ornately carved in the rocks tombs and facades of the Nabataeans. For this exploration, you are stylishly on the road in a Land Rover classic car. Surrounded by the majestic desert landscape, the place offers a fascinating insight into ancient history and culture. Continue to the Maraya, the largest mirror building in the world, which blends harmoniously into the desert landscape and reflects the beauty of Al Ulas. In the old town of Al Ula, which became an important commercial and pilgrimage center in the 12th century, you will admire the well-preserved, fortified houses and walk through the narrow streets that testify to the history of the city and offer a fascinating insight into the former life of the inhabitants. In addition, a visit to the Elephant Rock should not be missed. This is an impressive geological formation formed by natural erosion and forms a breathtaking backdrop in the middle of the desert. Overnight in Al Ula.

Day 7 - From the desert to the glittering Red Sea

After a delicious breakfast, leave Al Ula behind and continue your journey towards the Red Sea. Once on the coast, it continues by speedboat transfer. On the ride, you will enjoy the spectacular views of the crystal clear water and rugged coastal landscape. Upon arrival at the exclusive St. Regis Hotel, you can relax in the resort’s luxurious atmosphere and enjoy the rest of the day in peace. The perfect place to be enchanted by the peaceful surroundings. In the evening you can experience a breathtaking sunset over the Red Sea and look forward to the coming days in this dreamlike environment. Overnight in Red Sea.

8.-9. Day Luxurious Time Out at St. Regis Red Sea Resort

These two days are all about luxurious relaxation and offer you the perfect setting to review the special experiences of the past days in this unique country. Let your soul unwind and enjoy the tranquility of St. Regis Red Sea Resort, located on a private island in the Ummahat archipelago. The exclusive Overwater villas with private pool offer you a retreat for pure relaxation and enjoyment. Take advantage of the resort’s amenities, such as the St. Regis Spa, outdoor pools or the modern fitness center. Culinary delights and the famous St. Regis Butler service ensure that you do not lack anything. Overnight in Red Sea.

Day 10: Farewell to the Red Sea – Onward journey to Jeddah

Before you make your way to the airport, there is still time to enjoy the scenic coastline of your luxurious resort and let the last impressions of the paradisiacal surroundings take hold. A short flight will take you to Jeddah (available on Thursdays or Saturdays only), a vibrant port city that delights with its unique charm and history. After arriving at the hotel, you will have time to settle down in peace or to make your first explorations of the city. Jeddah combines historical architecture with modern structures and invites you to explore them on a first walk. The old town, with its narrow streets and colorful markets, holds many exciting discoveries for you. Immerse yourself in the atmosphere of this fascinating city and be enchanted by its many impressions. Overnight in Jeddah.

Day 11: Discover Jeddah: History, Culture and Stunning Views

Start the day with a short stop at Jeddah Corniche and the Jeddah Water Front before continuing to the Tayebat Museum. Here you will immerse yourself in the 2,500-year history of Jeddah, which comes alive in traditional Hijazi architecture and impressive exhibitions. After the visit, you will head to the “Floating Mosque”, the Al Rahmah Mosque, which is a true architectural masterpiece with its turquoise dome and unique construction. In the afternoon, explore Al Balad, Jeddah’s historic city center, a UNESCO World Heritage Site. You will walk through narrow streets and historic buildings, learning about the history of the city and its importance as a commercial center. At the end of the day, visit the King Fahd Fountain, which offers a spectacular spectacle with a height of 260 meters and impressive light play. Overnight in Jeddah.

12th day Farewell to Saudi Arabia

The time until your planned airport transfer is at your disposal. Enjoy a relaxing breakfast, take a last walk through Jeddah or buy some more souvenirs for your loved ones. Unfortunately, your unforgettable journey through Saudi Arabia is now coming to an end. Even if you say goodbye to this adventure, the fascinating impressions, memories and stories will resonate in you for a long time. Share your experiences with family and friends and let them share the special moments of your journey. In time for your return flight, you will be brought to the airport. We wish you a pleasant journey home and hope to welcome you again soon on another trip with Asia Special Tours!

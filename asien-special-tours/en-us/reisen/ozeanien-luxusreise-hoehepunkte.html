Car Rental Luxury Travel - Ultimate Highlights of New Zealand

New Zealand Luxury Travel: To the ultimate highlights of New Zealand, this travel idea of Asia Special Tours will take you. Here directly request and experience for yourself.

Itinerary & services

In hardly any place in the world awaits such a scenic diversity as in New Zealand. On your round trip from south to north you will explore secluded coves, huge glaciers, dense rainforests, mighty volcanoes and idyllic farmland. Let yourself be carried away into the adventure of New Zealand. They travel in a comfortable vehicle and don’t have to worry about anything other than enjoying the journey to the fullest with all its natural wounds. Overnight stays in high-class lodges as well as selected B&B homes in high-quality restored, historic buildings with wonderful hosts. This trip will give you the ultimate New Zealand experience!

Day 1 Welcome to Christchurch

Upon arrival at the airport, our driver will take you to your pre-booked lodge in a high-quality vehicle. Experience seclusion and historical ambience. High up on the hillside with unobstructed views of the Pacific Ocean and the Kaikoura Ranges in the distance, this charming and secluded rural retreat is a perfect place to arrive. Your stay at Shepherd’s Cottage includes the catering concept “We produce, you serve” for dinner and breakfast. Our chef creates a breakfast menu and a gourmet three-course meal based on any pre-notified eating habits or special wishes. The food comes with simple instructions to your villa so you can just make the finishing touches before enjoying Annandale’s best cuisine. Overnight in Annandale.

2nd day Akaroa Harbour Cruise

Relax and discover marine life with our award-winning Black Cat Cruise Team. Each cruise is supervised by an experienced skipper who has a comprehensive knowledge of nature, wildlife and the history of the marine world in this area. On the cruise, you will take a look at the White Flippered Penguin, the smallest penguin in the world. There are many different bird species, including five different cormorant species and the endangered yellow eye penguin. You’ll see New Zealand’s fur seals basking on the rocks near Seal Bay. Since the port of Akaroa was an active volcano millions of years ago, many spectacular ancient lava flows can be seen. There is also the incredible Scenery Nook, an amazing pink, purple and red volcanic amphitheater that will take your breath away. Overnight in Annandale.

3rd day Car rental and driving to Oamaru

Today your driver will take you to the car rental station in Christchurch, where you will pick up your desired vehicle. Now start your adventure on your own and head to the excellent Pen-y-bryn Lodge in Oamaru. Pen-y-Bryn Lodge (Welsh for the top of the hill) is a small, unique boutique hotel that offers lodge-style accommodation in five elegant and superbly equipped guest rooms with modern bathrooms. Particular is the hillside location with a view of the historic Oamaru and the Pacific Ocean. Pen-y-Bryn (1889) is the largest single-storey wooden house on the South Island of New Zealand and lies amidst 1.5 hectares of landscaped gardens and old trees. The lodge has a pool room, a library, a large salon and a large dining room. There are extensive porches and large terrace areas to relax. Overnight in Oamaru.

Day 4 Dunedin

The university town of Dunedin, New Zealand’s oldest city with Scottish heritage, boasts a unique combination of cultural riches, impressive architecture and modern lifestyle. Dunedin has a justly deserved reputation as one of the best preserved Victorian cities in the Southern Hemisphere. Architecturally outstanding is the massive Flemish Renaissance railway station (built 1904 - 1906) with its impressive mosaics. Dunedin is home to some of New Zealand’s top fashion designers, has many lively cafes and bars and not to forget: the steepest street in the world! Overnight in Dunedin.

Day 5 Explore the Otago Peninsula with Private Guide

Your guide will pick you up today to explore the Otago Peninsula with its spectacular landscape, nature and wildlife. The focus is on native forests, tidal catchments, marine mammals and the Northern Royal Albatross. The itinerary is flexible and is currently coordinated in the morning. Start your excursion on the peninsula with a scenic drive across the country road to the remote side of the Otago Peninsula with its tidal coves, where a variety of birds can be observed depending on tides and season. We will find a nice place for a picnic on the beach or on a viewpoint with wonderful views of the beaches, cliffs, geological specialities and the vast Pacific Ocean (weather-dependent). Further north along the peninsula, the Northern Royal Albatros breeds in the only breeding colony existing on the mainland. This will be another highlight of the day with the option of a one-hour guided tour of the Taiaroa Head Albatross Colony or a one-hour harbor cruise on the MV Monarch to see these majestic birds in flight. Alternatively, a guided tour can be offered at Penguin Place to see rare yellow eye penguins. Overnight in Dundein.

Day 6 Walking Capital Te Anau

Continue on to Te Anau today. The place has made a name for itself as “Walking Capital of the World” and is the ideal base for countless hikes. Fiordland Lodge is a luxurious lodge with ten guest rooms for the discerning traveler. Located on 40 acres of land, Fiordland Lodge is located on a magnificent plot of land with wide views over Lake Te Anau - a rural setting with hilly meadows just 10 minutes from Te Anau Township. In the dining area of the lodge there are large tables made of solid wood, where local dishes are on the menu and is complemented by the wine cellar, from which the appropriate wine can be selected in the evening. Overnight in Te Anau.

Day 7 Your Cruise on the Doubtful Sound

Today, drive from Manapouri across the crystal clear waters of Lake Manapouri to West Arm before boarding a bus and crossing the Wilmot Pass Alpine route through some of Fiordland’s densest rainforests. You will then cruise through the fjord by ship and wind your way through hidden coves and mystical waterways. During the cruise, our knowledgeable guides share with their guests the passion for the region. The fjord is home to bottlenose dolphins and fur seals, both of which are often seen. During the season you can take a look at the rare Fiordland Crested and Little Blue penguins. As the evening approaches, we will anchor at a protected berth. Take the opportunity to explore the coast by kayak or a small boat. In the evening, relax in the salon and enjoy a delicious three-course meal prepared by the chef. The next morning, after the breakfast buffet, there is time for further discoveries before returning to Manapouri. Overnight on the boat (Doubtful Sound).

8th day of Wanaka

Wanaka is something like the quiet little sister of Queenstown: here, too, the area invites for walks, hikes and bike rides, but the city itself is quieter. In front of the dramatic scenery around Wanaka with its impressive mountain and hill landscapes, some of the exterior shots of the epic film trilogy “Lord of the Rings” take place. Lime Tree Lodge is located on a 10-acre property on the edge of Lake Wanaka and offers luxurious 5-star boutique accommodation with breathtaking 360-degree mountain views. The lodge has four luxuriously equipped guest rooms with a bathroom and two private suites, each of which opens with French doors to a large veranda and gardens with magnificent views of the southern Alps. An on-site Helipad offers direct access to the spectacular ski resorts, glaciers and fjords of the region. A pétanque course, croquet lawn, pitch-and-putt golf course, swimming pool and spa pool round off the offer.

Day 9: The Secret of Lake Wanaka – the Buff Weka

Join us as we drive through Lake Wanaka to the remote and magical Mou Waho Island Nature Reserve, a predator-free home of the rare flightless bird Buff Weka. Our Ecoguide will take you on a guided bush walk to reveal the secret of the island, a lake at the top of the island! Enjoy "high tea" overlooking these two lakes - where we will often visit the Buff Weka. Plant a native tree as part of your eco-experience. A unique opportunity to give something back to this special island and the New Zealand environment. Overnight in Wanaka.

10.-11. Tag: Wilderness Lodge Lake Moeraki

The Wilderness Lodge experience combines quality accommodation, excellent food, warm hospitality and an exciting nature experience. Discover wild New Zealand with comfort and style. Few places in the world can match the breathtaking natural scenery of Wilderness Lodge Lake Moeraki. Surrounded by lakes, rivers, rainforest and sea coastline located in the heart of Te Wahipounamu, the southwestern World Heritage area of New Zealand. This is a paradise for nature lovers, active travelers and an oasis in the wilderness. There are 2 guided activities with a local guide included. Overnight at Lake Moeraki.

12th day helicopter flight to the glaciers

Fly over two of New Zealand’s most famous glaciers and land at the top of the Franz Josef or Fox Glacier. Watch the incredible natural phenomenon of the glacier slowly winding from its origin in the mountains high above you into the valleys. Overnight at Lake Moeraki.

13th day Greymouth

The journey continues along the west coast to the southern Alps. As a natural weather barrier, the high mountain ridge is responsible for one of the most extreme precipitation regimes in the country. Above the snow line, large masses of ice collect, forming fast-flowing valley glaciers. The approximately 12 km long Fox and Franz Josef glaciers - unique in temperate latitudes - slide down to an altitude of 300 m above sea level. Overnight in Greymouth.

Day 14 Kaikoura and the Giants of the Seas

Kaikoura is an area marked by the history and legend of the Maori. Off the coast of the village lies a marine environment so rich in nutrients that it attracts some of the greatest creatures we share our planet with. Among them the giant sperm whale, which can grow up to 20 meters long and has the largest brain of a living animal. Not only sperm whales, but also humpback whales (June and July), orca (November to March), the New Zealand Hectors Dolphin, the spirited Dusky dolphins, fur seals and the Royal Albatross can be seen. Overnight in Kaikoura.

Day 15 Through the Cook Water Road to the North

With the ferry you cross over to the North Island. First, glide through the Marlborough Sounds, an area of “drunken” valleys created by the geological subsidence of a central mountain range. The original mountain range is evidenced by steep mountain ridges, which today spring out as narrow peninsulas between deep sea arms. Then drive through the Cook Water Road and, after about three hours of shipping, reach Wellington, the capital of New Zealand. The ferry trip is an experience! Overnight in Kaikoura.

Day 16 Wellington and Zealandia

Wellington is not only the seat of government, but also the artistic and cultural highlight of the country – it houses a variety of art galleries and interesting museums. With more than 300 bars, cafes and restaurants, a lively scene and many culinary highlights await you. Explore the Zealandia Nature Park at Torckelschein while your guide points you to some of the most striking sights and sounds of New Zealand’s jungle. Experience many of the rare and endangered species and see those that only come out at night. See unique species such as Weta, fireflies, Tuatara and Takahē. Your group can see some of the 130 small kiwis that are home to ZEALANDIA - sightings of kiwis are likely on these tours (approximately). 80%, but not guaranteed. Gain insight into New Zealand’s natural history and the importance of the unique species within the Māori culture. The tour ends with a hot Kawakawa (Buschmann tea) in the visitor center. Overnight in Wellington.

17th day Napier

Napier, located directly on the sea, was completely destroyed in 1931 during an enormous earthquake and rebuilt in the Art Deco style of the 1920s/30s. Since then, it has been considered the world’s best preserved city in this striking architectural style. Overnight in Naipier.

Day 18: The Bast Olives of Cape Kidnappers

Gannet Safaris has been taking visitors to the bast boobies for over 35 years. It is the only way to get to these remarkable birds within a few meters. Drive comfortably in an air-conditioned 4WD vehicle through Cape Kidnappers Station, traverse river beds, wide rolling pastures, stalls with natural bush, steep gorges and breathtaking slopes. There is a stop on a spectacular cliff with panoramic views of Hawkes Bay up to the peninsula Mahia. The amazing geology of the area is absolutely fascinating. The journey over 36 kilometers in three hours will be commented on by your driver guide and he will give you a useful introduction to these special seabirds. Enjoy the breathtaking scenery from your position on the edge of the cliffs, 100 meters above the ocean. Here you can calmly observe the bast boobies. Overnight in Naipier.

Day 19 Rotorua – Hot Springs, Sky-High Geysers and Maori Culture

Rotorua in the heart of the North Island, located on the lake of the same name, is rich in Maori culture and known for its bubbling, geothermal activities. Millions of years ago, volcanic eruptions created a fantastic landscape of mountains, lakes and steaming earth. Experience dramatic geothermal energy here, recognizable by the typical sulfur smell. You will have the opportunity to do a variety of activities such as visiting Wai-O-Tapu Ther-mal Wonderland, Waimangu Volcanic Valley and Te Puia Geysers. There is also an important indigenous center, where you can watch Maoris carving their ornate carvings and experience traditional village buildings. Overnight in Rotorua.

Day 20 Rainbow Springs Kiwi Wildlife

Visit Rainbow Springs Wildlife Park with private guide where you’ll see more than a million species of flora and fauna, including rainbow trout, the unique New Zealand Tuatara and Kiwi Bird. You can take a bush walk, visit aviaries with native birds and see Rainbow Springs: over 2 million. liters of pure, fresh spring water. Take a seat in the specially built auditorium and enjoy a demonstration. The exotic parrots do what they do best: play and entertain. A show full of fascinating facts and rousing actions performed by the colorful and sometimes vocal parrots. Not only do the feathered animators fly, some of them will show off their incredible intellect and skill with a range of games and tricks. The Big Splash is a spectacular and intoxicating water fountain. Take a nine-minute journey through time as you explore New Zealand’s ecological development. State-of-the-art animatronics and projection technology bring dinosaurs, the moa, the incredible Haast Eagle and the arrival of humans to life. Overnight in Rotorua.

21st day Northland

The Northland is the northernmost of New Zealand’s sixteen administrative regions. Northland is considered both the Maori and the white settlers as the "cradle of New Zealand". According to Maori legends, Polynesian navigator Kupe landed in Hokianga about 1,000 years ago and many tribes consider him their direct ancestor. James Cook landed here in 1769. The first white settlers in the late 18th century mostly landed in Northland and the first permanent European settlements emerged here. In the village of Kerikeri, Kemp House is the oldest preserved building in New Zealand. Particular historical significance for all of New Zealand is the Treaty of Waitangi, signed at Waitangi on February 6, 1840, which is considered the founding charter of modern New Zealand. Overnight Kerikeri.

22nd Day Dolphin Watching Up Close

Dolphins are fascinating creatures and in the Bay of Islands we are blessed with their attraction all year round. This eco-cruise gives you the opportunity to get into the water and swim with dolphins while playing in their natural environment (as conditions permit). Of course, you can also observe the animals from on board. You will cruise the many remote islands of the bay and gain an insight into this historically significant region. This half-day cruise seeks out the many playful dolphins in the protected waters of the bay. We often see porpoises and common dolphins, baleen whales, killer whales, seals, fish, penguins and other marine and bird species (not guaranteed!). Overnight Kerikeri.

23rd day Northland flight including Cape Reinga

The Half Day Cape Reinga adventure by plane takes place up to twice a day in the summer months, once a day in winter. Fly over the west coast and the world famous 90-Mile Beach before landing at Cape Reinga after an hour. Our guided tour explores the tip of New Zealand for 2, 5 Hours in a comfortable air-conditioned vehicle. Experience the magic of Cape Reinga with local guides, enjoy a light refreshment on a beach near Cape Reinga and, if desired, the thrill of sandsliding the huge sand dunes at Te Paki. The return flight brings the spectacular scenery of the east coast of Northland closer from the air. Many parts of this beautiful coastline are not accessible by road. Overnight Kerikeri.

24th day End of the journey and return or onward flight

Today ends your fantastic New Zealand trip. You will be taken to the airport to board your onward or home flight from there.

A journey along the Uzbek Silk Road

Silk Road Travel - Embark on an individual journey along the Uzbek Silk Road with Asia Special Tours. Ask now!

Itinerary & services

English itinerary text is an offline machine translation of the German original for this team preview. Please confirm travel details with your advisor; the German source remains available through the language switch.

A trip along the Silk Road is a very special experience. Long after your return, you will continue to rave about the colorful medres and outstanding architecture. In the UNESCO World Heritage Sites of Samarkand, Bukhara, Chess Risabs and Khiva, the fairy tales from 1001 nights become reality. By staying with a host family in the Nurata Mountains, you will experience a contrast to the cities and get an authentic insight into everyday life.

Day 1 Arrival in Uzbekistan

Welcome to Uzbekistan! When you arrive in Tashkent, you will already be welcomed by your German-speaking tour guide. Together with your private chauffeur you drive to the hotel, where you can arrive in peace. Overnight in Tashkent.

2nd day of the capital of Uzbekistan

Tashkent is still shaped by Soviet architecture – and a place where you can feel the tension between Europe and the Orient particularly well. After breakfast, your guide will pick you up at the hotel and set off to explore the capital of Uzbekistan together. First, visit Khasti Imon Square with the beautiful Barak Khan Medrese, Tillya Sheikh Mosque and Kaffal Shashi Mausoleum made of simple brick masonry. Here you can take a look at the Quran handwritten by the third Caliph Osman (644 – 656). The Kukeldash Medrese still serves as a Koranic school today. You should not miss an underground metro ride: Tashkent is the only Central Asian city to have a subway, which is also a small architectural and artistic masterpiece. Artworks of a different kind can be admired in the Museum of Applied Art. On the huge Chorsu bazaar you will transport the scents of the oriental spices into the fairy tales from 1001 nights. Overnight in Tashkent.

Day 3 - Continue to Samarkand

In the morning, take a small museum tour and visit other cultural highlights of Tashkent such as the Tamerlan Museum, the Historical Museum and the Navoi Theater. In the afternoon you leave for Samarkand. The city of Samarkand is considered the heart of the legendary Silk Road and looks back on a 2,000-year history marked by a rapid change of powers. Overnight in Samarkand.

Day 4 Samarkand's Most Beautiful Sites

Scent bazaars, huge mosques and a mysterious atmosphere. Your sightseeing tour of this breathtaking city begins with a visit to the Gur Emir Mausoleum, the final resting place of the famous ruler Tamerlan. After a short walk you will reach the heart of Samarkand, the beautiful Registan Square with its three magnificent medres: Ulugbek, Shir Dor and Tillya-Kari. In the observatory of the famous astronomer Ulugbek you can reach for the stars. In the afternoon, it is best to continue your visit with the beautiful Bibi Khanum Mosque. Bibi Khanum was to become the largest mosque in the Eastern world at that time – after all, it is still one of the largest mosques today. In the archaeological site of Afrosiab, a palace was discovered in 1912, which probably belonged to a high official, and numerous terracotta figures have also been found – including an Athena with a helmet. You will continue on your way to the dead city of Shah-I-Zinda, which consists of numerous, finely decorated mausoleums – also the cousin of the Prophet Muhammad is said to be buried here. Of course, you should not miss a stroll over the Oriental bazaar. Overnight in Samarkand.

Day 5 Excursion to Chess Risabs

The name Chessrisabs means “green city”. It is not only a UNESCO World Heritage Site, but also historically significant as the birthplace of Tamerlane. You will visit the impressive Ak Saray Palace, which according to stories was built by Amir Temur and served as his summer residence. Let yourself be impressed by the majolikes and cut mosaics that cover the facade with countless motifs. However, the historic city holds even more attractions such as the Kuk Gumbaz Mosque, the Hazrati Imam Mosque or the tomb of Tamerlan’s son: Yakhangir, who fell off the horse at the age of twelve and died. At the end of this eventful day, visit the Local Lore Museum. Afterwards, return to Samarkand. Overnight in Samarkand.

Day 6 Guest with an Uzbek family

After breakfast, you will leave the unique city and head to the Nurata Mountains. In the village of Asraf, you will be warmly welcomed by your host and familiarized with the family accommodation and its facilities. The house is surrounded by a large farm garden, where you can relax after the sightseeing of the last few days. The guest room can be heated with a wood stove in the cold season – so you will have it cozy. Upon arrival, your guide will guide you through the area. Learn about the architectural style of the local houses and see how the mountain population lives. Lunch, dinner and overnight in the family guest house in Asraf.

7th day Painting Aydarkul Lake

After breakfast, you will have time to explore the place and the surrounding area. You can also take small walks. During the day, you will continue your journey to Bukhara. On the way, you will stop at Lake Aydarkul, which will impress you with a length of 200 kilometers. After you have let the picturesque landscape affect you, you will continue to Bukhara. Overnight in Bukhara.

8th Day Old Bukhara

Bukhara is one of the oldest cities in Central Asia and was once one of the most illustrious commercial centers on the Silk Road. Even today, the atmosphere of bygone times is still felt. Together with your guide, you will first visit the Choir Minor Mosque, the beautiful Bolo Hauz Mosque and the mighty Ark Citadel in the ground plan shape of the constellation “Großer Wagen”. You will see the mausoleum of the former Samanid ruler Ishmael Samani from the 9th and 10th centuries as well as the mausoleum Chashmai Ayub, which translates to “Source of Job”. Another highlight is the Poi Kalon complex, which consists of a minaret, a mosque and the beautiful Miri Arab Medrese. Also worth seeing are the Ulugbek Medrese, named after the famous astronomer, the Abdulaziz Khan Medrese and the Magoki Attori City Mosque. Afterwards you have the opportunity to experience the daily activity in the three overcoupled bazaars up close. Finally, the complex Labi Hauz invites you to linger. Around the water basin “Hauz” several tea rooms are grouped. Once, almost all inhabitants of the city were supplied with water with the help of the water basin. Overnight in Bukhara.

Day 9 - Through the desert to Khiva

In front of you today lies an endless sea of sand, as you drive on an old caravan road through the Kyzylkum Desert to Khiva. On the way, stop for a photo stop on the banks of the Amudarja River. Once you have arrived in Chiwa, the remaining day is at your disposal and you can explore the best preserved city on the Silk Road on your own. Overnight in Chiwa.

10th day Oasis town of Chiwa

After breakfast, you are now completely devoted to exploring Chiwa. The small oasis town in the middle of the desert has preserved the fairy-tale atmosphere of an ancient, oriental city to this day. You will visit the old town of Khiva – Itchan Kala – with the beautifully decorated Islam Khodja Minaret, Pakhlavan Mahmoud Mausoleum and Juma Mosque. You can also look forward to the Mohammed Amin-Khan and Rachim-Khan medres, the Tash-Hauli Khan Castle – where a cobra is said to still guard the last khan’s gold treasure – as well as the Kalta Minor minaret and other fascinating buildings. The fact that Kalta Minor is a “short minaret” in name and form was not planned. Originally, it was even to become the highest minaret in the city. Unfortunately, it could not be further built from a height of 26 meters for static reasons. As you stroll through the old town, you will encounter fine, intricate wood carvings everywhere – an art that has been cultivated in Khiva since ancient times. Keep your camera at hand! Overnight in Chiwa.

Day 11 Ayaz Kala and Topraz Kala

Not only the oasis city of Chiwa has a lot to offer for culture and history lovers. You can convince yourself of this during an excursion into the area when you head to Ayaz Kala. The fortress from the 3rd or 4th pre-Christian century is located on an extensive excavation field. From here you have a good view of the vast desert landscape. Explore the ruins of Ayaz Kala, Kirkkiz Kala and Toprak Kala – Toprak Kala is probably the most archaeologically studied excavation site in the region. Residents were forced to leave their facilities when the Kuschana empire collapsed, and Turkish attacks destroyed the irrigation facilities. Once you have had a quiet look at everything, you will be taken to the airport of Urgentsch and take the plane back to the capital. Overnight in Tashkent.

Day 12 Departure from Tashkent

Today, unfortunately, we say goodbye. With 1001 new impressions and experiences in your luggage, you will be taken to the airport of Tashkent, from where you will start your journey home.

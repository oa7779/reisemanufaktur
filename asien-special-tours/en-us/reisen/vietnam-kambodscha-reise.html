Cambodia Travel - Gods, Glory and Glamour

Embark on a unique journey through time through mysterious Cambodia. Fascinating buildings, eyewitnesses from a bygone era and the power of nature make this country one of the most exciting destinations in Southeast Asia. The legendary temple complex of Angkor, the impressive history of Khmer culture in Phnom Penh and colonial heritage in Battambang – all these are undisputed highlights of the country. But Cambodia is also a real insider tip for architecture and art lovers. From thousands of years old temples to the modern architectural masterpieces and trendy art galleries, you’ll find everything your heart desires here. Experience traditional handicrafts that have been continued for generations and enjoy authentic encounters with the warm locals. You will stay in tasteful design hotels that will surely inspire you. The culmination of this journey is the jungle adventure at the luxurious Shinta Mani Wild Camp, designed by world-famous designer Bill Bensley. Be wild... and immerse yourself in your very personal dream world!

1st Day Arrival & Aperitif and Khmer Food in an Art Gallery

Welcome to Cambodia! Siem Reap is a peaceful and friendly place that has maintained a rather leisurely pace despite tourist popularity - you can convince yourself of this during your first hours. The city is located seven kilometers south of the Angkor temple complex, making it an ideal starting point for exploring. Your guide welcomes you to the airport and accompanies you to your hotel, where you can rest for a while. The Shinta Mani Angkor designer hotel is an upscale boutique hotel by renowned architect Bill Bensley. The total of 39 rooms are very tastefully furnished and designed in a design inspired by the Khmer style. Spend the evening in a contemporary art gallery that presents a modern representation of Khmer culture. While tasting Khmer street food and drinking Khmer beer between aperitif and dinner, you will have the chance to look at the masterpieces of selected painters and photographers of prestigious classics and newer styles. A feast for the senses! Then return to your hotel. Overnight in Siem Reap.

Day 2 Angkor Wat - the largest temple in the world & visit to Phare Circus

The visit of Angkor is a brilliant experience and certainly the highlight of any Cambodia trip. Today you can look forward to the largest sacred building in the world: Angkor Wat. King Suryavarman, revered like a god, erected the unique monument in the 12th century, which is considered a landmark of the once glorious Angkor Empire. Afterwards you will experience Angkor Thom, the last great capital of the Khmer Empire. You can reach the city through the impressive South Gate, a stone gate carved with four gigantic faces and elephants. From here, drive to Bayon Temple, which is right in the middle of the city. Each tower has four faces, each facing in different directions. In the evening, you will be a guest in a very special circus: In the “Phare” young people from poor backgrounds perform, who receive school education and acrobatics lessons through a social project. Although the actors are not yet as routine in everything as professional acrobats, they already bring impressive shows to the stage – in Siem Reap and on tours abroad. Overnight in Siem Reap.

3rd day on the way in the magical temple world of Angkor

Just in time for the beginning of the day you can feel quite royal. Because you can enjoy the sunrise at Srah Srang, "the royal bath". After this early bath in the sunlight, visit Banteay Srei, the only important sacred structure not built by a king – the client was a guru. Accordingly, this is a "miniature temple" - because the large ones were reserved for the rulers. Among the shrines to be seen in Angkor is Ta Prohm, a 12th-century temple. The place of worship was discovered by the French at the end of the 19th century and was deliberately preserved as the restorers found it. At the end of this eventful day, visit the Pre Rup Temple. The temple from the 10th century served as a model for later, larger buildings such as Angkor Wat. It is wrongly one of the less visited sites in the area. In particular, the view from the uppermost pyramid stage to the surrounding rice fields and the lush green jungle is breathtaking. With countless impressions, you will return to your hotel where you can relax. How about a soothing spa application in your hotel? Overnight in Siem Reap.

4th Day Bicycle Tour Chao Srey Vibol & Visit to a Floating Village

It's going to be sporty today. By bicycle, it goes through beautiful nature to the little-visited temple Chao Srey Vibol, which lies in the middle of lush vegetation. Enjoy the peaceful atmosphere and take enough time for the visit. Along the way, learn about the cultural phenomenon of the Betel fruit, which locals harvest and keep in front of their homes for drying. Then transfer to a boat and sail to the floating village of Kompong Phluk, which is located on the largest lake in Cambodia. This lake is home to more fish species than most inland waters in the world, and it is home to an incredible variety of birds, some of them rare and endangered. The floating village is particularly spectacular during and after the rainy season, when the water level is high. At the end of the day, one of the oldest sights in this region is on the agenda: the Rolous Temple Group, whose shrines were largely built in the 9th century – making them one of the oldest, still preserved buildings of Khmer architecture. Overnight in Siem Reap.

5th day Departure to Battambang & ancient stonemason tradition

After eventful days in Siem Reap, we continue to Battambang today. Sit back and relax while driving. Have your camera ready and photograph lush green rice fields and traditional villages with typical Khmer dwellings. Along the way, you will have the chance to observe a few stonemasons who have used techniques that date back to the Angkor era for centuries. Then you reach your goal for the day. Battambang is located in the northwest of Cambodia and is the second largest city in the country. With a rich colonial architecture, a growing art scene that produces more and more Cambodian talents, and a beautiful surrounding landscape, this city is an ideal starting point for sightseeing away from any hustle and bustle. Enjoy your tasteful hotel Bambu Battambang, a paradise for those seeking peace. The hotel is a composition of elements in the Khmer style and the French colonial period. Overnight in Battambang.

Day 6 Authentic Tuk Tuk Tour through the charming Battambang

After breakfast, you will discover the charming Battambang with a tuk tuk, a city with the elegance of the French colonial style. Your exciting tour starts with a visit to a local market where you can admire a variety of local goods. Then we pass the waterfront with the old French buildings from the colonial period until the French governor's house and finally to Wat Tahm-rai-saw, the temple of the white elephant. Next, visit the old pagoda Wat Balat. If you wish, you can take part in daily activities such as making glutinous rice, smoking dried bananas, producing rice wine, making rice paper for the fresh spring rolls, making custom knives or preparing traditional fish paste prahokImmerse yourself in a completely foreign culture and enjoy countless authentic insights into everyday life. The warmth of the locals and the laughing children’s eyes will certainly captivate you. Overnight in Battambang.

7th day Departure to Phnom Penh & visit to various craft shops

Today it is time to pack suitcases again, because your journey will take you on to Phnom Penh, the capital of Cambodia. You start with Pursat, which is famous for its marble quarries. There you will visit a workshop where artists make sculptures from marble. Then continue to Kompong Chhnang, which is located at the mouth of the huge Tonle Sap lake. On the lake you can see various floating villages. Watch the locals doing their pottery work. Then we move on to Phnom Penh. A few kilometers from the capital, climb up Oudong Mountain, which is now a pilgrimage site. Your last stop before arriving in Phnom Penh is in a goldsmith village where experts work with the precious metal. Finally, you will reach Phnom Penh and be taken to your exclusive hotel. Rosewood Phnom Penh is located on the top floors of the Vattanac Capital Tower and symbolically represents modern Cambodia. The Sora Bar on the rooftop terrace and the 20-meter-long Infinity Pool are among the hotel’s first-class amenities. Overnight in Phnom Penh.

Day 8: Discover the new Khmer Architecture Tour with the Bicycle Richa

Together with your guide, you will explore the highlights of Cambodia’s capital. Your first stop is the Royal Palace. The complex also houses the Silver Pagoda, named after the over 5000 kilogram silver plates that grace the floor. Then continue to the National Museum, which boasts a world-leading collection of ancient archaeological, religious and artistic artifacts from the 4th to 13th centuries. Then return to your hotel where you can rest a little. In the afternoon, explore the center of Phnom Penh with a bicycle rickshaw and discover, in addition to colonial buildings, the "new Khmer architecture", which developed from 1953 after the independence of Cambodia. At that time, architecture focused on the conscious combination of regional building traditions and materials with an internationally shaped style. On this unique ride through Cambodia’s capital, you will visit the district around the post office and the main market. On the way, you will have the opportunity to visit some buildings from the inside as well. Overnight in Phnom Penh.

Day 9 Unique jungle adventure

Let the day be relaxed and enjoy an extensive breakfast. Then set off on your great jungle adventure. Kirirom, Bokor and Cardamom are among the oldest national parks in Cambodia and together make up one of the largest nature reserves in Southeast Asia. Untouched estuaries, wild coasts and rainforests with rugged mountain ranges are one of the few untouched habitats for elephants, gibbons and numerous other animal species. And this is where the ultimate luxury camp Shinta Mani Wild by Bill Bensley is located. After a drive of several hours you reach the gate lounge of the camp, where it will be spectacular. A 380-meter zipline takes you via a waterfall to the Landing Zone Bar, where you will be warmly welcomed by your butler (alternatively a Jeep transfer is also possible). The total of 15 extravagant luxury tents are located above a rushing river. Shinta Mani Wild also offers a unique culinary fireworks display. Overnight in Koh Kong.

10.-11. Free time at Shinta Mani Wild

(Without tour guide & chauffeur)

Be wild! Enjoy your precious days in the middle of the jungle and listen to the sounds of nature. How about a trip with the expedition boat through the rainforest? Or would you rather take a short break at the Boulders Spa? It is your precious time and your very private moments. Overnight in Koh Kong.

Day 12 Return to Phnom Penh & Departure

Catch some special moments today and enjoy this paradisiacal environment before returning to Phnom Penh full of unforgettable impressions. From there you will take your flight home or onwards. The special encounters, fascinating experiences and beautiful memories remain in the heart forever. Look forward to sharing the most beautiful experiences of your trip with your loved ones.

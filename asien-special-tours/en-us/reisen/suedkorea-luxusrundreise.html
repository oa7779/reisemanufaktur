Have you ever dreamed of a tailor-made luxury trip through South Korea? Make your dream come true. On your journey, conquer the dazzling capital of the South Korean peninsula and visit the historic border strip between North and South Korea. Of course, you should not miss Korea’s favorites. Prepare the Kimchi National Dish with an expert, discover the natural beauty of Jeju Island on green paths and listen to the sound of the sea and the knocking of your heart. South Korea’s natural jewel also offers white sand beaches – followed by turquoise blue sea, this is the perfect setting to unwind. Day 1 Welcome to Seoul

Welcome to South Korea! Your luxurious dream journey through the multifaceted country begins in the capital Seoul, where you will be welcomed and warmly greeted by your English-speaking driver. Use the rest of the day for first explorations in the vibrant metropolis. Seoul is home to rich cultural treasures dating back several millennia between modern skyscrapers. Overnight in Seoul.

2nd day traditions on track

Today you can enjoy learning more about the traditional Korean clothes and wear them for a few hours. The different shapes and colors reflect the culture and lifestyle of his time one on one and until 100 years ago, “Hanbok” was still worn daily. Nowadays, the country-typical costume is only worn on special occasions. Your exploration begins at Gyeongbokgung Palace, the largest and probably most impressive palace in the city. A big city requires a few kilometers, so you can relax on a rickshaw ride through the historic center of Seoul. View popular and well-known places from the comfort of the bicycle rickshaw, pass narrow streets and discover the daily life of the city. In the Hanokdorf Bukchon you will dive deeper into the past of the city. It is home to hundreds of traditional houses called “Hanok” in Korean. Stroll through the Insadong district with its various art galleries, traditional craft shops and old tea houses. Afterwards, enjoy a cup of herbal tea in a café – just right for your body. After an eventful day, a soothing massage awaits you at the Sulwhasoo Spa. Overnight in Seoul.

Day 3 Between North and South Korea

In the morning, make your way early to the historic Demilitarized Zone (DMZ) – the 250 km long and 4 km wide border strip between North and South Korea: still today a symbol of war and peace, division and reunification. The DMZ was built after the Korean War and runs across the peninsula. By not being allowed to enter the zone, it unconsciously became a refuge for rare animals and plants. On the “Peace Road” drive to the Imjingak Peace Park with the Freedom Bridge. You will see the exhibition hall and the 3rd invasion tunnel through which the North Koreans secretly wanted to enter South Korea. Finally, the Dora platform gives a view over the wide border area up to Gaesong, North Korea’s second largest city. End the rest of the day relaxing at the hotel. How about the popular national drink Soju in the evening? Try Korean specialties at the Slow Brewery and Pub (optional). Overnight in Seoul.

Day 4: The essence of Korea – from kimchi to traditional medicine

Not only culturally, but also culinary South Korea has a lot to offer! The famous national dish Kimchi is part of many Korean dishes and is served almost without exception to any dish. Today, in addition to the preparation of the spicy herb, you will also learn about its history and origin, because kimchi is not only made from Chinese cabbage, but also from numerous other ingredients such as tomatoes or oysters. After the preparation, try your freshly prepared kimchi – traditionally with a portion of pork roast and a typical Korean soybean soup. Freshly strengthened, we continue to Yangnyeong Market, South Korea’s largest spice market. A short walk will take you to the Seoul Yangnyeongsi Museum of Herbal Medicine, where you’ll get a glimpse into the world of traditional Korean healing. As a special experience, you can expect a relaxing herbal foot bath – a feel-good moment for body and soul. At the end of the day, Naksan Park invites you to a walking tour with magnificent views of Seoul. Enjoy the quiet atmosphere and let the day end in the middle of nature. Overnight in Seoul.

5th Day Natural Paradise Jeju & Exclusive Yacht Tour

After impressive days in the dazzling capital, you will continue your journey to Jeju Island today, which is a short domestic flight. There are countless myths surrounding this volcanic island, which has produced its own culture due to its isolated location from the mainland. Scattered all over the island, you will find Dol Hareubangs, figures of lava rock with protruding eyes and no pupils, which are considered fertility symbols. For newlywed Koreans, the island is therefore a popular honeymoon destination. The subtropical climate and fertile volcanic soil produce a rich flora to explore. Upon arrival at the airport, you will be picked up and taken to your hotel, a spacious luxury hotel within walking distance of the beach. The highlight of today is already waiting for you. On an exclusive yacht tour, you can let the fresh sea wind blow around your nose and enjoy the boundless expanse. Let the soul dangle and be sure to enjoy a cool drink on board. Overnight in Jeju. 

Day 6 The Secret of the Emerald Green Volcanic Island

Today you will visit Seongsan Ilchulbong Peak, a crater formed around 100,000 years ago by a seaquake and subsequent volcanic eruption at the bottom of the ocean. With the 99 sharp rocks surrounding the crater, it makes the appearance of a gigantic crown. At the end of the east coast of Jeju Island lies the headland of Seopjikoji, known for its wonderful nature and hiking trails. Especially in spring, a whole field full of bright yellow rapeseed flowers in all its glory shows up here. But also in other seasons it is with its lush green meadows a place worth seeing of the island Jeju. Enjoy the enchanting backdrop and listen to the ocean waves breaking on the coast. This original island, where you can feel the power of nature with all your senses, is simply attractive. At the end of this day, in the historic village of Seongeup, you will get a glimpse into the special culture of the Jeju inhabitants and their traditional customs. Among the special cultural treasures are the houses of the inhabitants, several Confucian shrines and schools as well as former government buildings and stone statues. Overnight in Jeju. 

7th day of free time (without tour guide and chauffeur)

This day belongs only to you. Enjoy the first-class amenities of your hotel and let the soul dangle. If you prefer something more active, you have the opportunity to explore the picturesque island on your own. Overnight in Jeju.

Day 8 - Float over Busan

Today, your journey will take you to Busan – South Korea’s second largest city. A short domestic flight will take you to the lively port city surrounded by a mountain range. After arriving at Busan airport, a very special change of perspective awaits you: During a ride on the famous Songdo Marine cable car, you will experience Busan from a breezy height of 86 meters. Particularly impressive: the so-called Crystal Cabins with glass floor, through which you can admire Busan from any angle. Back on solid ground, visit the colorful Gamcheon Culture Village – an artist village that enchants with its colorful homes, small galleries, cafes and original artwork. Stroll through the narrow streets, discover hidden murals and experience the creative soul of this unique neighborhood. Overnight in Busan.  

Day 9 - Multifaceted Busan

After a leisurely breakfast, head to the Taejongdae observation deck, which is characterised by a rocky coastline lined with dense forests. From there, you can look at the Japanese island of Tsushima with a clear view! In the afternoon, Spa Land Busan awaits you, everything that makes your heart beat faster. With 22 different hot baths in the traditional Korean style as well as 13 saunas, the so-called Jjimjilbangs and an outdoor area, the modern wellness oasis convinces with its diverse offer. Incidentally, the spa receives its water from two springs at a depth of 1,000 meters, with which the hot baths and saunas are operated. Also the outdoor foot bath should not be missed! Let the evening end very relaxed and enjoy the here and now with a soothing and revitalizing hot bath. Overnight in Busan.

10th Day Impressive World Heritage Site in Gyeongju

After breakfast, it is necessary to pack suitcases, as you continue your journey towards Gyeongju. Upon arrival, you will start your exploration tour at Daereungwon Park, better known as Tumuli Park. In front of you, a burial ground spreads with 23 burial complexes of kings and nobles from the Silla dynasty. Your tour will take you to the famous UNESCO World Heritage Site Cheomseongdae. This is considered to be the oldest existing star observatory in Asia from the reign of Queen Seondeok (632-647). Afterwards, your sightseeing tour will take you to the Gyeongju Donggung Palace. The palace was used as the second palace for the Crown Prince along with other annexes. In addition, banquets for important national events and important visitors took place here. The large pond complex “Wolji” is also very reminiscent of the historical past, which states that the pond was built during the Silla Dynasty. Overnight in Gyeongju. 

Day 11: Legendary Cultural Treasures & Temple Overnight

After an extensive breakfast and check-out, you will start the day with a visit to the famous National Museum of Gyeongju, which is loved by tourists and locals alike. Numerous historical and cultural exhibits of the Silla Dynasty take you on an impressive journey back in time. In the afternoon, one last big highlight of your trip awaits you: You will reach Bulguksa Temple, an important cultural site that has been designated a UNESCO World Heritage Site. The Bulguksa Temple represents the temple architecture of the ancient Silla Empire at its best. The temple grounds contain other national treasures, including the main staircases, whose 33 steps symbolically represent the path to enlightenment. To crown your trip, you will spend the night at Bulguksa Temple. During a temple tour, you will learn more about the history and significance of the temple before joining a sound meditation in the early evening and turning your eyes all the way inside. Afterwards you will experience the sequence of a Buddhist evening ceremony and let the special atmosphere affect you. Create your own prayer band before walking around the millennial stupa in the dark and listening to the sound of silence. Overnight in Gyeongju.

12th day Powerful meditation in the morning & new life energy

Early in the morning around 04:30 you start the day with a morning meditation round. Even if it is still the middle of the night, take time to consciously perceive this start in the day. The subsequent Buddhist ceremony is followed by a classic Zen meditation that brings the body and mind to rest. Strengthen yourself at breakfast and then enjoy your cup of tea with a monk. After this impressive time in the temple, you can review all newly gained experiences before traveling on. Your driver is already waiting for you and will take you to the station of Gyeongju. From there you travel by fast train to the metropolis Seoul. The rest of the day is at your disposal. Let your dreamlike journey end according to your wishes. Overnight in Seoul. 

13th day Farewell to South Korea

Even the most beautiful journey will come to an end. After an extensive breakfast, we say goodbye today. Let the bustle of the fascinating Metropople take on yourself again before you are brought to the airport with many new impressions and numerous valuable memories in your luggage. We wish you a good journey home!

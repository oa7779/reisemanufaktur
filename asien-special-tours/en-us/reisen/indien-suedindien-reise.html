South India - Insider Tour of India

Discover colorful South India on this individual India Insider tour of Asia Special Tours and get behind the scenes. Ask now!

Itinerary & services

South India is colorful: On this insider trip, experience the diverse cultural influences, eventful history and varied nature that make it an exciting destination. You will visit ancient temple towns, look behind the scenes of the old merchant houses in Chettinad and sail on a houseboat over the rivers of the backwaters. You will also have numerous opportunities to get in touch with the population – be it visiting small villages, markets or staying on a family farm in Kumarakom. At the end of the trip, a relaxing break awaits you on the picturesque beach of Marari. Of course, you should not miss a performance of the Kathakali dance, which is extremely popular in Kochi, including a make-up ceremony. With expressive eye and hand movements, colorful costumes and colorful makeup, various god stories are brought to the stage by the traditionally male performers. The dance stories are accompanied by shrill music! Let yourself be surprised, because South India will surely enchant you!

1st day Arrival in Chennai & seaside relaxation at Covelong

Namaste and welcome to India! Your journey begins in Chennai, where you will be expected and welcomed when you arrive at the airport. Afterwards, you will immediately drive to the fishing village of Covelong, where you will move into your beautiful hotel for the next two nights. You will sleep at Fisherman’s Cove, part of the Taj luxury hotel group. This dreamlike 5-star house is located right on beautiful Covelong Beach and with its lush gardens, private pools and scenic trails that lead to the sea, it is a true oasis of relaxation. The unique interior combines different styles in a masterful way. Enjoy attentive service and the legendary hospitality that distinguishes Taj hotels. The resort has some of the best restaurants on East Coast Road, where you can enjoy deliciously prepared and attractively presented food from a variety of local and international cuisines. In the J Wellness Circle you can be pampered according to all the rules of art. How could a journey begin more beautifully? Overnight in Mamallapuram.

Day 2 Discover Mamallapuram

Today is all about the old Pallava high culture. You will take a trip to the city of Mamallapuram, whose sights are among the oldest preserved buildings in southern India. Right on the beach is an impressive five-storey temple from the 7th century: the Coastal Temple. Almost 20 meters, the stone sanctuary rises over the coastline. Also worth seeing is the Hindu temple group of the "Five Rathas". Each of the five monuments was carved from a large rock in the middle of the 7th century. All have different style elements. The cave temples (so-called "Mandapas") are especially worth seeing for their sculptures and reliefs. The monumental bas-relief “descent of the aisle” is considered to be the largest and most important bas-relief in the world. It was carved directly into a rock cliff in the 7th century. Over 27 meters wide and almost nine meters high, this monumental work of art tells scenes from Hindu mythology. Towards evening you will return with many new impressions to your hotel in Covelong. Overnight in Mamallapuram.

Day 3 European flair in Puducherry

Today you will make your way to Puducherry, whose cityscape looks astonishingly European. This is not surprising, however, since Puducherry was the capital of French India for 300 years. The old town exudes with its avenues, elegant colonial houses and gardens even Mediterranean flair. Your path will take you past colonial villas, some of which have been converted into charming boutique hotels. A bustling beach promenade runs along the Bay of Bengal and passes through several statues, including a four-meter-high Gandhi monument. Puducherry became known for the Aurobindo Ashram founded in 1926, whose building can be recognized by the gray paint. In the afternoon, a specially trained expert will take you on a tour of the city’s historical heritage. During a walk, take a look behind the scenes of the historic buildings and learn about the Foundation’s efforts to preserve the city’s architectural heritage. Highlights include the beautifully restored pink Church of Our Lady of Angels on Rue Dumas, built in 1865. Overnight in Puducherry.

4th day Impressive temple complexes in Chidambaram & Thanjavur

On your way to Thanjavur, the former capital of the Chola empire, you will make a stop in Chidambaram and stroll through the vast complex of the Nataraja Temple. One of the highlights of your trip awaits you in Thanjavur: Brihadishvara, one of South India’s most important temples and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. The Vimana is crowned by a dome resting on a single granite block of about 80 tons. This mighty stone is said to have been transported over six kilometers with the help of a ramp towards the end of the 10th century. The complex is designed almost symmetrically – it is intended to illustrate the strength and absolute order of its client Rajaraja I. Then continue your journey to Trichy. Overnight in Trichy.

5th Day Temple, Pleasure Moments and Living History

On the way to Chettinad you will visit the impressive Rock Fort Temple in Trichy, whose buildings were partly carved directly into the 83-meter-high rock. Afterwards, you will have some time to stroll through the alleys of one of the oldest cities in the region before continuing your journey to Karaikkudi. Karaikkudi is considered one of the cultural sources of South India. A unique lifestyle is still practised here. Tonight you are a guest at the Visalam Hotel, whose historic building was once a gift from a rich merchant to his daughter. After almost half a century, it now welcomes its guests again in new splendor. The cuisine of the region is also famous throughout India. Convince yourself of its special features during a small, English-speaking cooking demonstration in your hotel. The dishes freshly prepared before your eyes can then be tasted as a delicious meal. Well strengthened, you then set out to explore the surroundings more closely. In the quiet Chettinad you will find many magnificent old merchant houses, which testify to the splendor of bygone times. Visit some of the artisans based here and get insights into their everyday work. Overnight in Chettinad.

Day 6 It happened at a time in Madurai...

Today you will leave Chettinad and continue to Madurai. Enjoy the scenic drive past coconut palms, rice fields and bizarre granite rocks. Along the way, visit a small workshop in Athangudy that produces tiles through a special process. These colorful tiles from Athangudy not only decorate the opulent homes in the region, but are even exported to other parts of the world. Finally, you will reach Madurai, the oldest city in South India. Since the 2nd century BC, Madurai has been a thriving market town, the center of power of many dynasties and has always been the seat of various deities. Join an English-speaking expert on a very special tour and learn about fickle kings and monuments towering high into the sky as you take a look behind the scenes, listen to tales of quirky gods and extraordinary mortals - some dating back many millennia, others more recent. Overnight in Madurai.

7th Day Flower Market & Spiritual Aarti Zeremoni in Minakshi Temple

Come to most other tourists first. Already at sunrise life is in full swing. Become part of this special atmosphere when you stroll over the colorful flower market with your guide early in the morning and immerse yourself in a sea for the senses. Mountains and sacks are towering colorful flowers on the stands, which exude a beguiling scent. Many of these are knotted into ornate garlands and gestures and serve as an offering during temple visits. Later, the cultural highlights of Madurai are waiting to be explored by you. In Thirumalai-Nayaka-Mahal Palace, a piece of Italy awaits you in the middle of India: The palace complex was built by an Italian architect and builds a bridge between Orient and Occident. The highlight of the city, however, is without a doubt the exuberantly decorated Minakshi Temple. The sanctuary is crowned by four extraordinarily richly decorated temple towers, each decorated by more than a thousand colorful stucco figures. Later in the evening, return to Minakshi Temple to attend the evening prayer ceremony called Aarti. A magical event where you experience Indian spirituality up close. Overnight in Madurai.

Day 8 The Scent of Kerala – Spice Plantations in Periyar

After the many cultural treasures in Tamil Nadu, today you reach the state of Kerala, which is very green and fertile. Your destination today is the Periyar Highlands. After arriving at your hotel, your English-speaking expert will take you on a jeep ride to a viewpoint from where you can see the city of Kumily, Periyar National Park, agricultural fields and forests from a bird’s eye view. You will learn about the magnificent Western Ghats, which are among the eight biodiversity hotspots worldwide and the challenge of preserving this enormous biodiversity wealth. During the subsequent visit to a nearby village, you will get an insight into the local agricultural economy and the everyday life of the locals. The Periyar highlands are particularly known for their variety of spices. Join a spice farmer who has already won some awards for best agricultural practices or one of his family members on a tour of the farm and listen to his stories about the variety of Indian spices, the various production processes, the market and the economy. Finally, you can taste a cup of spiced tea and homemade chocolates. Overnight in Thekkady.

9th day Tea culture between Thekkady and Kumarakom

This morning you will take an easy hike with a naturalist guide through the Periyar nature reserve. After breakfast, your journey will take you to Kumarakom on the edge of the famous backwaters – a labyrinth-like network of rivers, canals and lagoons. On the way, stop at a tea plantation (closed on Sundays) and immerse yourself in the history of regional tea cultivation. When visiting a tea factory, you will learn more about the complex processes of tea production. Finally, you will reach Kumarakom, an idyllic place on the shores of Lake Vembanad – the largest freshwater lake in the region. Here you immediately move into your room in your accommodation for the coming nights: the idyllic Coconut Lagoon located on the backwaters. The unique property is only accessible by boat and is surrounded by coconut palms, rice fields and winding canals. Overnight in Kumarakom.

Day 10: One day for free

Without guide and driver

This day belongs entirely to you, so that you can arrange the stay at Coconut Lagoon according to your wishes. Start the morning with a yoga session or discover nature while watching birds. In the afternoon, you can join a traditional cooking demonstration or take a relaxed boat cruise on the lake for sunset. Your accommodation offers numerous opportunities to make the day active or to relax in the Ayurveda Spa and recharge your energy. Overnight in Kumarakom.

Day 11 - Walk and Houseboat through the Backwaters

Assistance by English speaking on-board staff

Before boarding your houseboat, explore picturesque towns of Alapuzzha, whose history and peculiarities you will learn after arriving on a walk with a local. Stroll along the famous 155-year-old Gujarati Street, which is under local heritage protection, stroll across one of the markets and observe everyday life on and around the many canals. Afterwards, enjoy a traditional lunch prepared according to long-established family recipes that reflect the aromas of the region. Then a special highlight awaits you: a boat trip on board a comfortable houseboat through the lagoons of the backwaters. Let the tropical lagoon landscape of Kerala pass by and catch again and again insights into the everyday life of the population. On the way, you always have the opportunity to disembark, visit the villages on the shore and get in contact with the villagers directly. As the night breaks over the backwaters, fishermen light oil lamps and incense on the shores, a magical sight to be enjoyed while serving local Kerala food on deck. Overnight on the houseboat.

12th day off to the beach

After you have gained many exciting impressions of India’s rich cultural and natural treasures during the last few days on your round trip, you can simply relax at the end of your holiday. Your driver will take you to Mararikulam and you will check into the Marari Beach Resort. The rest of the day is at your disposal. Overnight in Mararikulam.

13-14. Pure day of relaxation

Without guide and driver

Surrounded by tropical vegetation and the sound of the ocean, Marari Beach Resort offers cozy villas in traditional Kerala style. You can relax on the private beach, practice yoga and meditation, enjoy Ayurveda treatments or take cooking classes. The resort is an oasis of tranquility and a place to experience the culture and nature of Kerala in an authentic way. Overnight in Mararikulam.

15th day Kochi & Kathakali Dances

Without tour guide & chauffeur

After breakfast, head to the town of Kochi, known for its old castle and delicious fish dishes, such as dried bonito. The port city has long played a key role in the development of trade and transport. In the afternoon after check-in, take a break at your beautiful Heritage Colonial hotel. In the evening, a very special spectacle awaits you. The traditional Kathakali dance performance captivates its viewers with a spectacular mix of drama, dance, music and rituals. In advance, you can look over the shoulder of the actors during elaborate makeup. Overnight in Kochi.

Day 16 The most beautiful sides of Kochi

In the morning you can experience once again the variety of religious influences of the country: you will visit the Christian St. Francis Church, which was already completed in 1516 and the Paradesi Synagogue, built about fifty years later, with its Chinese tiles and Belgian chandeliers. You will also visit the magnificent Mattancherry Palace, built around 1555. In the coronation hall you can inspect clothes, tapestries and turbans of the Rajas. Of course, you should not miss a look at the Chinese fishing nets for which Kochi is known. These heavy wooden structures, which must be operated by at least four men, are only in use here outside of China. The rest of the day is at your disposal. Take the time to explore Kochi on your own or enjoy the amenities of your beautiful boutique hotel. Housed in a former boatyard, the Brunton Boatyard takes you with its unique atmosphere and authentic decor on a little nostalgic journey back in time to the Indo-Portuguese colonial period. Overnight in Kochi.

17th day Farewell to India

At the end of your journey, you will travel home with many new impressions from this land of contrasts. Enjoy the delicious breakfast again and perhaps treat yourself to one last massage. Then your chauffeur will take you to the airport, from where you will travel home or onwards. Who knows, you might return one day. Because this huge country still holds many wonders for you.

Do you dream of a journey that shows Japan in style and elegance? Discover the land of contrasts, where modern metropolises like Tokyo meet centuries-old traditions. Enjoy an exclusive helicopter flight over Tokyo’s impressive skyline, relax in the luxurious hot springs of a traditional ryokan and experience authentic tea ceremonies in scenic temples. Recover your body and mind with a meditation and yoga/pilate class in the midst of Japanese nature. Be indulged in culinary delights and explore the historic temples in Kyoto and the UNESCO World Heritage Site in Nara. This tailor-made trip offers you Japan in its most beautiful form – stylish, exclusive and full of extraordinary experiences.

1st day Arrival in Japan

Welcome to Japan! Your journey begins in the state-of-the-art metropolis of Tokyo, where you will be welcomed and warmly greeted by an English-speaking assistant. This accompanies you to the vehicle that will take you directly to your hotel: the luxurious Mandarin Oriental Tokyo is located in the historic Nihonbashi district, a center of culture, modern attractions and excellent transport links. Mitsukoshimae and Nihonbashi metro stations are just steps away, and Tokyo’s best shopping, restaurant and cultural districts are within easy reach. The elegant rooms combine modern design with Japanese aesthetics, offering breathtaking views of Tokyo’s skyline. Enjoy exquisite cuisine in restaurants offering authentic Japanese dishes, French haute cuisine and creative international specialties, as well as fine tea ceremonies. One highlight is the award-winning Spa on the 37th floor, where you can be pampered while enjoying the panoramic views. A modern fitness center rounds off the wellness offer. Overnight in Tokyo.

2nd day Cosmopolitan Tokyo with breathtaking helicopter flight

Your guide will pick you up from the hotel this morning for a full-day private car exploration tour, your first destination being Asakusa, the center of Tokyo’s “old town.” Visit Sensoji Temple, Tokyo’s oldest Buddhist temple. It is also home to the oldest geisha district in the city. Continue to the Meiji Shrine, one of Japan’s state sanctuaries. Leave the traditional part of Tokyo behind and head to the noble shopping and residential areas of Omotesando and Aoyama. A real eye-catcher in Aoyama is the flagship store of Prada in diamond form. Furthermore, the Eastern Palace Gardens, the Imperial Palace (from the outside) and the famous crossing of Shibuya are on your program – the busiest crossing in the world. In the evening, an absolute highlight awaits you: a helicopter flight over the city. The 40-minute flight over the Ginza, Shinjuku and Akihabara districts will give you a bird’s eye view of Tokyo. You will glide over the skyscrapers of Roppongi Mori Hill and the famous Tokyo Tower. The flight offers fantastic views of Tokyo Skytree, the tallest structure in the country. After landing, your driver will take you back to your hotel. Overnight in Tokyo.

Day 3 Tokyo’s Skyline – Architecture Tour of the World Metropolis

The largest city in the world shines with a characteristic skyline in gigantic dimensions. Futuristic skyscrapers rise above traditional buildings and mystical temples. Today’s day tour of Tokyo is dedicated to the extraordinary design of this urban landscape. A colorful mix of famous sights and shrines, huge shopping malls and even Olympic stadiums await you. You will encounter the names of famous architects such as Tadao Ando, Toyo Ito and Kenzo Tange. With an expert guide at your side, you will learn about Tokyo’s structures and why the city is considered a model of a world metropolis. By the way, there is enough time to get to know the iconic districts of the city better. This tour makes the heart of any architecture lover beat faster. Overnight in Tokyo.

Day 4 From Tokyo to Onsen Paradise

Time to say goodbye to the metropolis of millions. After breakfast, make your way to the quiet town of Shuzenji on the Izu Peninsula, which lures with its hot springs. The scenic drive takes you along Sagami Bay. In Shuzenji, visit the Shuzenji Temple and Hie Shrine. Stroll through the quiet bamboo forest, cross the romantic red bridges over Katsuura-gawa and enjoy the very special atmosphere of this pretty place. Afterwards you will have the opportunity to relax your feet in one of the hot baths along the river. Tonight you will spend in the traditional onsen ryokan Yagyu No Sho, located in the hot springs of Shuzenji, surrounded by a quiet bamboo forest and nature of the Izu Peninsula. The elegant guest house combines classical Japanese architecture, craftsmanship and warm hospitality. The sukiya architectural style rooms offer a quiet atmosphere and are equipped with private onsen baths, partly as open springs outdoors. In the Ryokan you will find indoor and outdoor pools that are accessible around the clock. Culinary you will be spoiled with traditional Kaiseki cuisine. Overnight in Shuzenji.

Day 5 The Amanemu – oasis of peace in the midst of idyllic nature

From the Izu Peninsula, you will travel on to the Ise Peninsula this morning. By private car you will first be taken to the Mishima station, from where you will take the Shinkansen to Nagoya. There you will meet your guide and drive together with the car direction Mie-Prefecture. Along the way, stop at Ise Jingu Shrine, Japan’s oldest Shinto shrine. Here the sun goddess Amaterasu is venerated, who is considered the ancestor of the Japanese imperial house. For lunch, it’s best to head to one of the elegant Japanese restaurants. Then continue to the small town of Futaminoura, where you can marvel at the Meoto Iwa – the “married rocks”. Finally, head to your luxurious accommodation for the next two nights: the dreamlike Amanemu, located in the middle of nature. The peaceful surroundings are nestled in the 24 suites and 4 villas, which impress with their elegant-minimalist style as well as with the picturesque view of the bay or the beautiful gardens. Enjoy a relaxing bath in private onsen or let your sense of taste pamper you with a delicious dinner. Overnight in Shima.

Day 6 Ama divers and coastal magic

So that you can let the many impressions of the last few days affect you and enjoy the amenities of your hotel, today is at your disposal. If you'd rather continue exploring the Ise Peninsula with your guide, they'll pick you up from the hotel early in the afternoon, and first visit Mikimoto Pearl Island, where an Ama diver offers a stunning demonstration. Afterwards, head to the village of Osatsu, known for these Ama divers, where you can visit a diver’s hut and enjoy a delicious grilled seafood meal. After dinner, the trip leads to the Ama Culture Museum and Ishigami Shrine, which traditionally provided protection and good catch for the Ama divers while diving. The last stop of the day is the Toba Observatory, where you can enjoy the breathtaking coastal scenery before being taken back to the hotel. Overnight in Shima.

Day 7: The Old Imperial City of Kyoto

After a relaxing start to the day, leave peaceful Amanemu behind and travel on to the old imperial city of Kyoto. Your driver will first take you to Nagoya station, from where a fast train will take you to Kyoto. Kyoto is one of the most culturally significant sites in the country. Upon arrival and check-in at the hotel, the rest of the day is free for your first explorations. Selected for the next few nights, the Four Seasons Hotel Kyoto is located in the enchanting Higashiyama district of Kyoto, one of Japan’s most traditional and cultural districts, surrounded by temples, shrines and the historic Shakusui-en Pond Garden. This sanctuary combines modern elegance with Japanese serenity. The spacious rooms combine contemporary design with Japanese accents and offer views of either the garden or the city. Culinary awaits you with a selection of excellent restaurants and lounges serving local specialties and international cuisine, as well as a stylish tea house. For your well-being, a first-class spa, indoor pool, whirlpool, sauna, Ofuro bath and a modern fitness center are available. Overnight in Kyoto. 

8th day Zen, tea ceremony and historical sights

A special highlight awaits you today. You will be picked up by private vehicle and meet the host at the entrance of Daitokuji, a sprawling Zen complex. Explore one of these temples, experience the impressive Zen garden and learn about the importance of the mysterious landscape. Step into the host’s traditional wooden house on the edge of the temple complex and experience a tea ceremony. Watch the matcha tea being prepared and learn to enjoy both the moment and taste in each cup. Then continue and explore Kyoto with your guide. After lunch, visit Fushimi Inari Taisha, one of Japan’s most unusual Shinto shrines, crossed by thousands of red Torii gates that line the hiking trails in the forest of Mount Inari. Continue to the Higashiyama district. Enjoy the streets lined with traditional tea houses and craft shops. Visit Kiyomizu Temple and marvel at the architectural masterpiece. Climb the 13-meter-high balcony, built without nails and enjoy the impressive view of Kyoto. Afterwards you will have time to explore the city further. Overnight in Kyoto.

Day 9 Fascinating Nara

Together with your guide you will leave the city in the morning and drive to Nara, the former capital of Japan (710–784). The splendor of that time is still evident today in the great temples, shrines and ruins. Particularly impressive is the “Daibutsu”, the largest bronze Buddha statue in the world, which is venerated in the Todaiji Temple – one of the largest wooden buildings in the world. Along the way to Kasuga Shrine, thousands of stone and bronze lanterns create a very special atmosphere. Another highlight is the Isui Garden, a traditional Japanese garden from the Meiji period that is one of the most enchanting of its kind. In addition to the fantastic backdrop, there are also delightful tea houses where you have the opportunity to enjoy a cup of delicious green teas. In the Naramachi district, an older part of Nara, you will immerse yourself in Japan’s vibrant past. After exploring, return to Kyoto where you can customize the evening – whether it’s on a final walk through the city or a dinner overlooking the Kamo River.

Day 10 From the Golden Pavilion to Gion

This morning, meet your guide to discover more of Kyoto’s attractions. First, visit the famous Kinkaku-ji Temple, also known as the Golden Pavilion, originally built as a villa and later converted into a Zen temple. Afterwards, head to Nijo Castle, one of the few surviving original castles in Japan known for its architecture and beautiful gardens. Finally, stroll through the Geisha district of Gion before returning to the hotel. The afternoon is at your disposal today. Overnight in Kyoto. 

11th day of rest and enjoyment: A relaxed end to your trip to Japan

In the morning you will be picked up by your driver at the hotel and drive to Shiga on the beautiful Lake Biwa where you will meet your English speaking guide. Your walk starts with a visit to the Juge Shrine, which is idyllic in a small forest and offers a wonderful view of Lake Biwa. This place has been cultivated by the locals for centuries and is considered sacred, since a goddess is worshipped here. Enjoy the calm, spiritual atmosphere before moving on to a special meditation and yoga/pilates course. The course takes place in a traditional Japanese farmhouse, where you can rest through breathing exercises and leave all tension behind. After the physical and mental relaxation, a culinary experience awaits you. Together, you will prepare an authentic, healthy Japanese meal using fresh vegetables and rice from your own cultivation. After a relaxed lunch, you will be driven back to Kyoto. The rest of the afternoon is at your disposal. Take the time to reflect on the impressions of your trip to Japan or explore Kyoto further on your own.

12th day Farewell to Japan

The time until the planned airport transfer is at your disposal. Breakfast in peace, take a last walk through the area or get some more souvenirs for your loved ones at home. Soon this time is coming to an end, and your driver will take you to the airport in Osaka in time for your return flight. This beautiful trip to Japan with Asia Special Tours ends here. We wish you a good trip home and look forward to the next adventure together!

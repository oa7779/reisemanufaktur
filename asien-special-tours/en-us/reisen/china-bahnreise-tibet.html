Tibet Journey - Viewing Mount Everest

Tibet China Journey: Explore an unparalleled journey into the majestic Himalayan mountain world with Asia Special Tours. We are happy to advise you!

Tibet is unique. And to stand once personally in the midst of this primordial scenery is a dream for many. With this journey he fulfills himself! Through Chengdu, your path leads you into the majestic mountain world of the Himalayas. In Lhasa, the former capital of Tibet, immerse yourself in the culture of the people and the vibrant presence of Buddhism. Here you will experience the life and rituals of the monks up close, visit spectacular monasteries and memorials, and finally encounter the highest of all peaks – Mount Everest, a place of profound spiritual power. Tibet captivates with its breathtaking expanse, peaceful silence and colorful sensual places. The friendliness of the Tibetans will always bring a smile to your face and make your stay on the roof of the world unforgettable. This is where the country shapes its people, and it is no coincidence that Buddhism has found its home in this mystical region. The tranquility of Tibet invites you to stop and feel the magic of this place in depth.

1st day Arrival in Chengdu

Welcome to China! Your journey begins in Chengdu, home of the pandas and a fascinating culture. At the airport, your personal guide is already waiting for you and driving you to your hotel. There you can first recover from the long flight and make it fresh. Later, your guide will pick you up and take you on a discovery tour of Chengdu’s Old Town. The alleys with their traditional buildings tell stories from times long gone. You will visit a charming local tea house where you can experience the relaxed atmosphere and rich tea culture of the region over a cup of authentic Chinese teas. Afterwards, the visit of the impressive monastery of the Blue Goat is on the program. This monastery is a place of peace and spirituality. After this cultural introduction, you will return to the hotel with many new impressions, where you can arrange the evening according to your wishes. Overnight in Chengdu.   

Day 2 Meeting with the Leshan Giant Buddha

Get ready for a powerful encounter. After breakfast, you will be picked up by your guide and set off for Leshan. The Giant Buddha statue of Leshan, the largest of its kind in the world, is carved into a steep cliff face and rises a proud 71 meters in height. It is an impressive sight from a distance, but it is only up close that you can see the fascinating details that characterize this masterpiece of the Tang Dynasty. After a leisurely walk along the paths that lead to the statue, you will have the opportunity to view the monument from different perspectives. In the afternoon you will return to Chengdu, rich in new impressions and memories. The rest of the day is at your disposal to continue exploring the city on your own or relax in the hotel. Overnight in Chengdu.

Day 3 In the Green Heart of Tibet Overlooking Mount Namjagbarwa

In the morning you will fly to Nyingchi, which is located in eastern Tibet at an altitude of 2,900 meters. This place is ideal for slowly getting used to the altitude. Nyingchi is characterized by an unusually lush vegetation, which stands in strong contrast to the otherwise typical image of Tibet. Surrounded by the massive mountains of the eastern Himalayan foothills, Nyingchi offers breathtaking natural landscapes and one of the deepest gorges in the world: the spectacular bend of the Brahmaputra River that reaches an impressive 3,000 meters into the depths. Enjoy an unforgettable view of the dramatic river bends as well as the majestic Mount Namjagbarwa, perched at an altitude of 7,782 meters above the region. Often referred to as “the most beautiful mountain in Tibet”, this mountain, with its snow-capped peak and surrounding peaks, will offer you an impressive panorama that makes today an unforgettable natural experience. Overnight at Nyingchi.

Day 4 Tsedang – The Cradle of Tibetan Culture

Today you travel by fast train to Tsedang. The railway line between Nyingchi and Lhasa is considered a masterpiece of modern Chinese engineering and even surpasses the famous Xining-Lhasa railway in technical and scenic terms. During the ride, enjoy breathtaking views of the spectacular Tibetan landscape before arriving in Tsedang, the “cradle of Tibetan culture”. In Tsedang, the program includes a visit to Mindroling Monastery. This monastery is one of the six main monasteries of the Nyingmapa, also known as the Red Hat sect. It is not only an important religious center, but also a place where Tibetan traditions are kept alive. In addition to the deep spiritual atmosphere, you will have the opportunity to witness up close how Tibetans make their famous incense sticks – a 100% natural product that has been used in the region for centuries. Overnight in Tsedang.

Day 5: Tibet's oldest temple

Today, together with your guide, visit Samye Monastery, the oldest Buddhist temple in Tibet, built in the 7th century when Buddhism was just introduced to Tibet. This temple is not only a religious center, but also an architectural masterpiece. The design combines different styles from Nepal, China and Tibet, creating a unique and harmonious overall picture. The shape of the entire monastic complex symbolizes the diagram of the universe according to Buddhist teaching and offers you the opportunity to explore the deeper meaning and spiritual aspects of Tibetan culture. During your visit, you will have the opportunity to admire the ornate murals and sculptures that tell the story of Buddhism in Tibet. Then continue your journey and drive to Lhasa, the former Tibetan capital. The city, also called "Place of the Gods", is located on the Kyichu, the river of happiness, and received the nickname "city in the sunshine" because of its numerous sunny days in the year. Overnight in Lhasa.

Day 6: Tracing the Tibetan Faith

Today, your guide will take you to the majestic Potala Palace, the imposing seat of the Tibetan god-kings, perched on the Red Mountain Mar-po-ri and rises 130 meters above Lhasa. Until 1959, the palace served as the residence of all Dalai Lamas. According to legend, the first palace was built in 637. Then your way will take you to Jokhang Temple, the holiest temple of Tibetans in the heart of Lhasa. The Jokhang Temple, also known as Tsuk-Lag-Khang, was once the center of the most important institutions of the Tibetan state. Upon entering the temple, you will immediately be greeted by the distinctive scent of incense and butter lamps that highlights the spiritual atmosphere. From the roof of Jokhang Temple, enjoy a magnificent view over the forecourt and the Barkhor Pilgrimage Road, where believing pilgrims circle the temple and reverently throw themselves to the ground. On both sides of Barkhor Street, numerous small shops invite you to discover Tibetan crafts and purchase souvenirs as you experience the vibrant energy of this sacred place. Overnight in Lhasa.

7th Day Summer Residence of the Dalai Lamas & Philosophy in Tibetan

Today you will visit the Dalai Lama’s former summer palace, known as Norbulingka, set in the middle of a vast garden. This impressive palace was built in the second half of the 18century on the orders of 7Dalai Lamas was built and served as a summer residence for him and his reincarnations. Particularly admirable is the breathtaking throne of the Dalai Lama, which is considered a symbol of his authority and spirituality. In addition, you will have the opportunity to visit picturesque chapels, meditation rooms, the large reading hall and the private rooms of the Dalai Lama and his family members. After your visit, you will continue to the monastery Sera, which 1419 It was founded by students of the Gelugpa Order, also known as Yellow Hats. This former state monastery once formed a flourishing monastic city with several thousand monks. Today, Sera is known for its excellent academy and lively debates. Every day against 16 At the clock, the young monks gather for “discussion lessons”, where they discuss theological questions in ritualized gestures and loud claps in groups of two. This unique experience offers you a fascinating insight into Tibetan philosophy and dynamic teaching tradition. Overnight in Lhasa.

Day 8 Explore Lhasa on your own

Enjoy today’s free time. Stroll through the Barkor Market Square and observe Tibetans in their religious practices. In this place, you can once again calmly immerse yourself in the everyday life of the Tibetans in Lhasa, see the beautifully decorated people in their folk costumes and let the unbroken aspiration of the Tibetans for their Buddhist way of life affect you. Overnight in Lhasa.      

Day 9 Lhasa – Gyantse: Turquoise totally on the way to self-discovery

Today you will leave Lhasa and drive past impressive landscapes through the mountains to Gyantse. The city is located in the fertile plain of the Nyang Chu valley at an altitude of about 4000 meters. A detour will take you to a viewpoint near the sacred Yamdrok Lake, where you will have a lunch break. The turquoise blue lake is reflected in many color shades and you can immediately feel the sanctity that it radiates. Take advantage of the opportunity for a walk along the lake. Then continue on to the “Hero City” Gyantse. Here you will visit the Pelkor Chöde Monastery, famous for its impressive architecture and spiritual significance. Of particular note is the giant stupa (Kumbum), which offers a unique atmosphere with its many small chapels and magnificent murals. During your visit, you will have the opportunity to admire the works of art and learn more about the history of the monastery and its importance for Tibetan culture. Overnight in Gyantse.

Day 10: Travel to Xegar

You are going to Xegar today. On the way, you will visit Sakya Monastery, which is considered the ancestral home of the Sakya sect. This historic monastery has played a significant role in Tibetan history and was crucial for the introduction of Buddhism to Tibet during the reign of the Mongols in the 14th century. The striking gray color of the buildings, due to the special material with which they were built, gives the monastery a special charisma. During your visit, you will have the opportunity to admire the ornate murals, thangkas and impressive statues that reflect the rich history and culture of the Sakya sect. Then continue your journey and enjoy the spectacular views of the surrounding mountains and the breathtaking scenery. Arriving in Xegar, you will have the opportunity to relax and enjoy the evening. Take the time to explore the area on a walk through the city. Overnight in Xegar.

Day 11 - Mount Everest - The Gate to Heaven

Today is a big one. Early in the morning, head to Rungbuk, a place known for its breathtaking views of majestic Mt. Everest, which is the highest mountain on earth at 8,848 m above sea level. Keep the camera ready, because in good weather a truly majestic view awaits you, which will take your breath away. The impressive mountains and unspoilt nature around Rungbuk provide the perfect backdrop to experience the grandeur and grandeur of the Himalayas. Enjoy the moment and let the beauty of the landscape take hold as you admire Mt. Everest. After a day full of unforgettable impressions, you will return to Xegar. Overnight in Xegar.

Day 12: A Geometric View of the World

During the day, you will drive to Shigatse. With the Tashilunpo Monastery, you will visit another important attraction of Tibet today. The monastery with the golden roofs represents the upscale Tibetan architecture and architecture. It attracts thousands of Buddhists every year, with around 600 monks, the monastery is one of the most important religious centers in Tibet. Tashilunpo is also the residence of the Panchen Lama. He is considered a reincarnation of Amitabha, the Buddha of Infinite Light, and is the highest Tibetan dignitary after the Dalai Lama. We also recommend visiting the free market. Let the day end according to your wishes. Overnight in Shigatse.

Day 13 Chengdu: A Visit to the Pandas

Today you fly back to Chengdu. After your arrival, a special highlight is on the program: a visit to the Panda Bear Breeding Station. The Chengdu Research Base of Giant Panda Breeding is one of the most famous facilities for breeding and preserving the endangered giant panda. Here you will have the opportunity to observe these fascinating animals in their natural environment. Learn about the rearing, habitat and protective measures taken to protect these iconic animals. Stroll through the vast facilities where you can see the pandas at different ages – from newborn litters to the playful and curious young. Keep your camera ready to capture the cute pandas as they frolic, eat and relax. Enjoy the rest of the day in Chengdu to discover the city’s culinary delights or visit other attractions. Overnight in Chengdu.

Day 14 Departure – but what remains are unforgettable moments

Today ends your journey on which you could collect numerous impressions. Your guide will take you to Chengdu Airport and say goodbye. You are going home or on your journey.

India and Nepal Round Trip - From Delhi to the Highlands of Nepal

India and Nepal Round Trip - Discover two fascinating countries on this travel inspiration from Asia Special Tours. Ask directly and consult here.

Itinerary & services

English itinerary text is an offline machine translation of the German original for this team preview. Please confirm travel details with your advisor; the German source remains available through the language switch.

Two fascinating countries await you: the contrasting India and Nepal in the midst of the majestic Himalayan mountain range. Let yourself be taken on an unforgettable journey and experience a varied mix of culture and nature. This trip combines the contrasting capital of India with a visit to the world-famous Taj Mahal and spiritual impressions in Varanasi as well as the dreamlike landscape scenery of Nepal. You can also expect exciting safaris and many encounters with locals.

1st day Arrival in Delhi & Market Drive at Moonlight Square

Namaste and welcome to India! Your journey starts in Delhi, where you will be expected and driven to your accommodation upon arrival at the airport. Rest or relax by the pool of your beautiful 1903 Heritage hotel, which boasts colonial charm. In the afternoon, you will travel back in time to Old Delhi, where the Jama Masjid, one of the largest mosques in the world, testifies to Delhi’s past as the capital of Islamic India. Passing the Red Fort, the palace complex of the Mughals, which owes its name to the outer walls of red sandstone, your ride will take you on to the Moonlight Square – Chandni Chowk – one of the oldest and busiest market squares in the city. Here you will experience the typical flair of an oriental bazaar. Even deeper you can immerse yourself in the streets and alleys of Delhi during a rickshaw ride. Your driver will show you the beauty of Old Delhi from a different angle on various routes off the main trails, taking you to traditional shops and bazaars. Overnight in Delhi.

Day 2 Explore New Delhi

After breakfast, meet your guide to explore Imperial New Delhi with its vast avenues and imposing buildings together. Because New Delhi also offers fascinating historical sights: for example, with the 72 Metre-high Qutb minaret and the mausoleum of the great mogul Humayun, which is considered an architectural precursor to the Taj Mahal. Afterwards you will drive a bit through New Delhi and can, among other things, the National Monument India Gate, which is modeled on the Arc de Triomphe in Paris and commemorates the 1World War I was built, marvel. Finally, visit the Gurudwara Bangla Sahib Temple – the largest Sikh sanctuary in Delhi – and return to the hotel for the rest of the day. Take the time to explore Delhi on your own or relax in the hotel. Overnight in Delhi.

Day 3 Evening Mood at the Taj Mahal

From Delhi, drive to Agra. Once a small village on the banks of the Yamuna, thanks to the two great Mughal monarchs Akbar and his grandson Shah Jahan, the city developed into the second capital of the Mughal Empire. The Mughals were not only rulers, but also great architects and their architectural wonders in Agra and the surrounding area can still be admired today. Together with your guide, you will visit the monumental Fort Agra, which is also a UNESCO World Heritage site alongside the world-famous Taj Mahal. Then it is finally time: you experience the sunset at the fairytale Taj Mahal. In incomparable perfection and beauty, this “most expensive proof of love ever” built by Mughal emperor Shah Jahan for his beloved wife Mumtaz Mahal still shines today in white marble. A very special experience that will impress you deeply. Overnight in Agra.

Day 4: Train to Khajuraho

The journey in a private air-conditioned car is undisputedly much more comfortable: Nevertheless, you get to know the country and people on a train trip in a very special way. That's why you're heading to the next stop of your journey - Khajuraho - by train, and if you wish, we'll be happy to arrange your journey to Khajuraho by car. After breakfast, you will be picked up at the hotel and taken to the station for the train ride to Khajuraho. There you will be expected and brought to your hotel. Overnight in Khajuraho.

Day 5 The Temples of Khajuraho & Aarti Ceremony in Varanasi

After breakfast, you will meet with your guide for an exploration tour. Khajuraho is considered one of India’s seven wonders and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. In particular, the temples with erotic depictions of scenes from the Kamasutra enjoy worldwide fame. The shrines were built between the 10th and 12th centuries and their builders liked to spread the legend that they came from the moon. Even if the authors of the temples most likely came from this world: The erotic sculptures and reliefs not only delight the curious voyeur in each of us, but are also impressively crafted. In addition to erotic scenes, everyday situations are also depicted. In the afternoon, you will be taken to the airport for a domestic flight to Varanasi, the holiest site of Hinduism. Varanasi is crowded, dirty and full of spirituality – pure India. Upon arrival, you will be received and taken to your hotel. After the check-in, you will meet with your guide in the evening and experience the spirituality of the place up close at a traditional Aarti ceremony on the banks of the Ganges. Overnight in Varanasi.

Day 6 Varanasi & Sarnath – Coexistence of Hinduism & Buddhism

They greet the morning with a boat trip on the Ganges. Let the magical atmosphere along the sacred river affect you. After breakfast at the hotel, drive from this sacred city of Hinduism to the place where Buddhism was founded over 2,500 years ago: Sarnath. After his enlightenment, Buddha gave his first sermon here. In the Sarnath Museum, learn more about the origins of Buddhism before returning to Varanasi. Then it’s time to explore the fascinating city by daylight. Together with an English-speaking expert, take a walk through the narrow streets of Varanasis, giving you an insight into the life and rich culture of the Bengals who have migrated to Varanasi for centuries. On your way, you will visit hidden shrines, get exciting insights into everyday life on the Ganges and finally reach the ritual bathing place Dashaswamedh. Overnight in Varanasi.

Day 7 Farewell to India & Flight to Kathmandu

This morning, leave the hustle and bustle of Varanasis behind and fly over Delhi to the heart of the Himalayas, Kathmandu. Here you are already expected and brought to your hotel. Kathmandu is Nepal’s largest and most cosmopolitan city – a place where dozens of ethnic groups come together. The city is located at 1,400 m above sea level in a valley surrounded by green mountains with snow-capped, powerful peaks. In the old town are pagodas, narrow, cobbled alleys, anciently carved windows and the stone sanctuaries backdrop for loud and lively life. The Kathmandu Valley is currently home to seven UNESCO World Heritage sites. The rest of the day is at your disposal. Take the time to already look around Nepal’s capital on your own or just let the soul dangle. Overnight in Kathmandu.

8th day Temples and palaces in Kathmandu

Start the day with a visit to Kathmandu’s Old Town, which undoubtedly centers on Durbar Square. Especially stand out the temple of the goddess Kumari and the royal palace. From here, take a beautiful rickshaw ride through narrow alleys to Thamel, where you can watch local life up close. Use some free time there to look around on your own while strolling through the busy district. Then reunite with your guide and visit the Swayambhunath Temple, one of the oldest Buddhist temples in the world. You will also visit the most sacred Hindu pilgrimage site: Pashupatinath. You can view the complex from the eastern bank of the Bagmati, as entering the temple is strictly forbidden to all non-Hindus. Then visit the 36-meter-high Boudhanath Stupa, one of the most important Buddhist pilgrimage sites in the country and one of the largest Buddhism sanctuaries worldwide. In one of the nearby monasteries, you can get in touch with one of the lamas, the Buddhist teachers, and receive his blessing. Overnight in Kathmandu.

Day 9: The jungle is calling

After breakfast, you will drive to Chitwan – one of the largest jungle and forest areas in Nepal. The Royal Chitwan National Park covers an area of 932 square kilometers. Chitwan used to be a private hunting ground of the Maharajas of Nepal. The park with its swamps and salt forests, which are crossed by rivers and watercourses, has been a UNESCO World Heritage site since 1984. It houses unique ecosystems and is home to more than 50 species of mammals, 525 species of birds and 55 species of amphibians. Among others, deer, monkeys, wild elephants, leopards, lip bears and crocodiles live here. The park is particularly famous for its population of Indian rhinos and with a lot of luck you might even discover one of the rare Bengal tigers. After lunch at the resort, spend the rest of the day with various jungle activities arranged by your lodge. Back at the resort, an experienced naturalist guide will tell you a lot of interesting things about the park’s flora and fauna during a slideshow before ending the day with a relaxing dinner. Overnight in Chitwan.

Day 10 Varied Day in Chitwan National Park

Nature is calling! Immerse yourself in the fascination of the wilderness and its inhabitants. You can explore the park with guided jeep safaris, walks, canoeing or elephant rides from very different perspectives.
You can also visit the local Tharu villages and watch the elephants feeding and bathing up close. Back at the resort, experience a cultural performance of the Tharu community. Let yourself be enchanted by the dances and songs and for the moment be taken completely into the world of the tribe. Overnight in Chitwan.

Day 11 - Continue to Pokhara

After breakfast, continue your journey and continue to Pokhara, a place of remarkable natural beauty. The charming city has several beautiful lakes and offers a breathtaking panoramic view of the Annapurna mountain range. The tranquility of the lakes against the splendor of the Himalayas creates a special ambience that has made Pokhara a popular place to relax and enjoy the beauty of nature. Overnight in Pokhara.

12th day Majestic summit, colorful bazaars & boat trip on Phewa Lake

Getting up early is worthwhile today! Because if the weather permits, you will experience a fantastic sunrise over the peaks of the Himalayas from the village of Sarangkot. You will hike to this highest possible viewpoint and let the view wander over the Pokhara Valley. After this spectacle of nature, you will first return to your hotel to strengthen yourself at breakfast for the day. Together with your guide you will stroll over the old bazaar of Pokhara. You will find everything here, from edibles to dresses and cosmetics to gold. In the afternoon, during your hike to the village of Damsadi, you can expect once again fascinating viewpoints and spectacular landscapes. Drink the famous “butter tea” with the inhabitants. Back in Pokhara, you can relax on a boat tour on Phewa Lake. Phewa Lake, considered the most beautiful of the three inland lakes in the Pokhara Valley, lies at an altitude of 742 meters above sea level and is framed by a fascinating mountain landscape. Back on land, stroll along the shores of the lake with your guide before returning to your hotel. Overnight in Pokhara.

Day 13 Back to Kathmandu

This morning your trip will take you back to the capital of the country. There, the rest of the day is freely available after arrival. Use the time for further exploration on your own or relax in your hotel. Overnight in Kathmandu.

14th day Departure

Now your varied journey through the colorful India and the Himalayan state Nepal ends. Enriched by many impressions from two fascinating countries, take your return flight home.

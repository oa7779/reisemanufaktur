Saudi Arabia Journey - The Highlights of the Legendary Kingdom

Experience an exclusive, customized Saudi Arabia journey: from Riyadh to Al Ula to Jeddah – culture, history and desert magic included!

The Saudis themselves call their country the “kingdom of the desert”, and indeed the vast, endless sandy landscape shapes the image of this fascinating country. In Saudi Arabia, tradition and modernity meet in an impressive way. On your journey you will discover not only the vibrant life of metropolises like Riyadh, but also the unspoilt beauty of the desert, which is one of the oldest and most mysterious landscapes in the world. Visit historic sites such as the UNESCO World Heritage Sites Diriyah and Al-Ula, and immerse yourself in the impressive architecture of modern skyscrapers. Your journey will take you to Tabuk, a region full of history and unspoilt nature, and Medina, where history and tradition come alive. Jeddah is a fascinating mix of traditional charm and modern influences while enjoying the backdrop of the Red Sea. Let yourself be enchanted by the hospitality and culture of a country that still has many secrets to offer.

1st day Arrival in Riyadh

Welcome to Riyadh, the birthplace of modern Saudi Arabia! Upon arrival at the airport, you will be warmly welcomed by your driver and taken to your hotel where you can rest a little and get the first impression of this fascinating city. Riyadh, the country’s capital, is a vibrant metropolis where the charm of the old world meets the visions of the 21st century. This modern city is not only a thriving financial and business center, but also an emerging cultural hotspot. The province of Riyadh, also known as Al-Wosta, is rich in history – countless fortresses, magnificent palaces and interesting museums tell of a glamorous past. Special highlights are the colorful souks that reflect the lively bustle of the city. Overnight in Riyadh.

Day 2 Riyadh – Past and Modern

Start today with a visit to Diriyah, the historical origin of the first Saudi capital. The Al Turaif district, a clay village declared a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2010, provides fascinating insights into Saudi heritage. Then head to the National Museum, where you can discover the rich history of the kingdom. After lunch, visit the Al Masmak fortress, which played a significant role in an attack by Ibn Saud in 1902. Particularly worth seeing are the maps and photographs from the years 1912-1937. Then stroll over the Souq Al-Zalb, one of Riyadh’s oldest traditional markets, where you can discover Arabian scents, jewelry and antiques. In the afternoon, the spectacular skybridge of the Kingdom Tower awaits you. This unique glass bridge, designed with a lot of creativity, offers breathtaking views of the city. Inspired by world-famous landmarks such as the Gateway Arch in St. Louis, the Sydney Harbour Bridge and the Eiffel Tower, it offers you an unrivalled panorama. Overnight in Riyadh.

Day 3 In the footsteps of history in Tabuk

In the morning, fly to Tabuk and start your exploration tour with a visit to Hejaz Station from the early 20th century, one of Saudi Arabia’s best-preserved stations. Covering an area of 80,000 square meters, there is a workshop, a crafts center and a building that houses a locomotive, a freight car and numerous Ottoman relics. Continue to the Ottoman castle from the 16th century. Today, it is a museum that exhibits numerous historical artifacts from the Ottoman period and offers plenty of information about the history of Tabuk, including links to Prophet Muhammad as well as famous travelers such as Ibn Battuta. The castle has a mosque on the ground floor, an open courtyard and stairs leading to the mosque and the observation towers on the second floor. Outside are cisterns that once caught water from a spring from which the Prophet Muhammad allegedly drank. After an eventful day, drive back to the hotel to rest. Overnight in Tabuk.

Day 4 As an explorer on the way in Wadi Dissah

Continue your journey towards Wadi Dissah, one of Saudi Arabia’s secret natural wonders. Arriving in the valley, you will be served a rich lunch. Then take a hike through the impressive 15-kilometer canyon that runs through the Jebel Qaraqir massif. Wadi Dissah was known only to a few explorers for a long time before the nearby road and satellite imagery made the valley accessible to the world. Particularly fascinating are the 500-meter-high rock walls and the dramatic panorama that you can enjoy from the peaks of the canyon. The watercourse that flows through the western part of the wadis provides the lush vegetation with water, so that palm trees and tall grasses thrive in the valley. After exploring Wadi Dissah, continue your journey towards Al Ula. Overnight in Al Ula.

Day 5 Hegra and Al Ula – Historic Treasures and Natural Wonders

Today you will visit the impressive UNESCO World Heritage Site Hegra (Madain Saleh), known for its ornately carved in the rocks tombs and facades of the Nabataeans. Surrounded by the majestic desert landscape, the place offers a fascinating insight into ancient history and culture. Continue to the so-called Maraya, the largest mirror building in the world, which blends harmoniously into the desert landscape and reflects the beauty of Al Ulas. In the old town of Al Ula, which became an important commercial and pilgrimage center in the 12th century, you will admire the well-preserved, fortified houses and walk through the narrow streets that testify to the history of the city and offer a fascinating insight into the former life of the inhabitants. In addition, a visit to the Elephant Rock should not be missed. This is an impressive geological formation formed by natural erosion and forms a breathtaking backdrop in the middle of the desert. Overnight in Al Ula.

6th day drive to Medina and discovery of the Holy City

After breakfast, you will travel to Medina, where you can visit an exhibition in the complex of the Prophet's Mosque and take impressive pictures of this sacred place. Afterwards, visit Qiba Street, which connects the Prophet Mosque and Quba Mosque - the first mosque in the Islamic world - (only outdoor visits are possible for both mosques). This street is now a pedestrian street and known for its traditional grocery stores. A special experience awaits you at noon: you are invited to dinner with a local family, where you can experience Saudi hospitality up close and enjoy local specialties such as the popular buff – a bag of dough filled with ground beef. The rest of the day is at your disposal. Overnight in Medina.

7th day train ride to Jeddah and visit the old town

After a pleasant fast train ride, arrive at Jeddah and continue your day with an exploration tour of Al Balad, the historic old town of Jeddah. Al Balad, founded in the 7th century, was once the center of the city and is now a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Your tour will take you through the narrow, winding alleys where the old buildings are preserved with their unique window designs and doors, partly built of shells and stones. You will visit one of the traditional buildings that reflects the historical architecture. As you stroll through the streets, learn about the history of Jeddah trade and how pilgrims got their supplies of clothing and food before continuing on their way to Mecca. Another highlight of the city are the numerous ancient mosques, some over 300 years old and still in use as religious sites. At the end of the day, visit King Fahd’s Fountain, the tallest water fountain in the world, which rises an impressive 260 meters into the air and shines in spectacular light at night. Overnight in Jeddah. 

8th Farewell to Saudi Arabia

The time until your planned airport transfer is at your disposal. Enjoy a relaxing breakfast, take a last walk through Jeddah or buy some more souvenirs for your loved ones. Unfortunately, your unforgettable journey through Saudi Arabia is now coming to an end. Even if you say goodbye to this adventure, the fascinating impressions, memories and stories will resonate in you for a long time. Share your experiences with family and friends and let them share the special moments of your journey. In time for your return flight, you will be brought to the airport. We wish you a pleasant journey home and hope to welcome you again soon on another trip with Asia Special Tours!

New Zealand Rental Car Tour - Highlights in the Land of Long, White Cloud

New Zealand Rental Car Tour: Experience all the highlights of the North and South Islands with Asia Special Tours. The land of the long, white cloud awaits you!

Hardly any other country unites such an impressive variety of landscapes in such a small area as New Zealand. From the geothermal wonders of the North Island to the dramatic fjords and glaciers of the South Island, a natural backdrop of unique beauty unfolds. Travel flexibly by rental car through one of the most fascinating countries in the world and discover New Zealand’s highlights at your own pace.

Day 1 Arrival in Auckland

Kia Ora! Today you reach Auckland. This starts your rental car tour on the North Island. Upon arrival at Auckland Airport, pick up your rental car and drive to your accommodation. Your room stands out 14 The clock is available. Use the rest of the day for relaxation or explore Auckland if you are still enterprising despite jet lag. Auckland is considered a melting pot of European, Asian and South Pacific cultures as well as indigenous Maori heritage. This variety brings with it a wealth of unique restaurants and shopping opportunities. Auckland’s waterfront location has earned the city the nickname “City of Sails”. Don’t miss the imposing Sky Tower in the heart of the city. Of a height of 328 It is the highest tower in the southern hemisphere. In addition to the skyline, dozens of dormant volcanic cones around Auckland shape the cityscape. Overnight in Auckland.

Day 2 Northland: Bay of Islands

Today you drive from Auckland via the Harbour Bridge and travel along the "Hibiskus Coast" to the scenic and charming Northland. The route is lined in many places by impressive New Zealand Christmas trees (Pohutukawa). Passing Whangarei, the heart of the Northland region, you can reach the picturesque Bay of Islands in the subtropical north in the evening. In Waitangi, near Paihia, is the important Treaty House where the Treaty between the British Crown and the Maori was signed in 1840. Overnight in Kerikeri.

3rd Day Nature Experience Bay of Islands

Kerikeri is the largest village of the Bay of Island. The place is often referred to as the “cradle of New Zealand” because it is where the country’s first mission station was built and some of the oldest surviving buildings in the state stand. Experience the spectacular natural beauty of the Bay of Islands on an approximately four-hour cruise. You have the opportunity to observe dolphins, whales, seals and numerous seabirds in their natural habitat. During a stay at Otehei Bay on Urupukapuka Island, you can swim, explore the island and, with luck, discover rare native bird species. You will also learn about the marine mammal sanctuary and the Island Song project, which is working with local Maori tribes to restore natural island ecosystems. Overnight in Kerikeri.

Day 4 In the Kingdom of Kauri Trees

Today your journey continues to Aranga. In the Waipoua Forest you can admire the gigantic Kauri trees, which reach a height of up to 50 meters. The oldest and most famous representative of this tree species, Tane Mahuta ("Lord of the Forest"), is said to be as old as our Christian era. You can learn more about these impressive giants at the Kauri Museum in Matakohe, where there are also numerous exhibits made of Kauri resin – a substance similar to our amber. Overnight in Aranga.

Day 5 The Beauty of the Coromandel Peninsula

After breakfast, drive over Auckland to the sparsely populated Coromandel Peninsula. The region impresses with its varied landscape of white sand beaches, original rainforests and picturesque coastal villages. The peninsula, which is characterized by a mountain range of volcanic origin, offers numerous opportunities for hiking, swimming and relaxing. Enjoy the relaxed atmosphere and the charming mix of nature, coastline and lively art and culture scene. Overnight in Coromandel.

Day 6 Rotorua and Maori culture

Today you leave the Coromandel Peninsula, drive through the fertile kiwi plantations around Te Puke and finally reach Rotorua. The city is the geothermal center of New Zealand and delights with a unique landscape of hot springs, steaming crevices, seething mud pools and impressive geysers. At the same time, Rotorua is considered one of the most important centers of Maori culture. You will visit Mitai Maori Village and experience a traditional waka (canoe) on the Wai-o-Whiro River, fascinating performances with singing, dancing and stories of the first settlers, as well as the demonstration of a hangi cooking food in an earth oven. Then enjoy a rich buffet. A guided walk also provides insights into the way of life of the Maori people, their medicinal plants and the history of an old fortified village. An authentic experience that brings the culture and traditions of the Maori in an impressive way. Overnight in Rotorua.

7th day Geothermal wonders Rotoruas

Rotorua, located in the heart of the North Island on the lake of the same name, is rich in Maori culture and known for its impressive geothermal activities. Millions of years ago, massive volcanic eruptions created a unique landscape of mountains, lakes and steaming thermal areas. Explore the Waimangu Volcanic Valley, the youngest geothermal area in the world. Well-developed paths lead through a fascinating landscape with crater lakes, hot springs and steaming crevices. Then enjoy a 45-minute boat ride on Lake Rotomahana, where you can learn about the 1886 eruption of Mount Tarawera and admire geothermal phenomena visible only from the water. Shuttle buses are available for transportation within the park. The combination of walk and boat trip takes around three hours and offers an impressive nature experience in a unique volcanic landscape. Overnight in Rotorua.

8th day drive to Tongariro National Park

Today you will leave Rotorua and travel on to Tongariro National Park, the oldest national park in New Zealand and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Along the way, you will pass the volcanic plateau of the Central North Island with its wide plains, rivers and lakes. Even from a distance, impressive views of the striking volcanic peaks of Tongariro, Ngauruhoe and Ruapehu offer. In October 1996, there were still heavy ejections. The barren crater landscape also served as a location for "Mordor" in "The Lord of the Rings". The region is one of the most spectacular volcanic landscapes in the country and has a great spiritual significance for the Maori. Take advantage of the rest of the day for initial explorations or enjoy the unique backdrop of this impressive mountain world. Overnight in Tongariro NP.

9th day Tongariro Alpine Crossing - Hike

Today is all about the impressive volcanic landscape of Tongariro National Park. The shuttle will take you early in the morning to the starting point of the famous Tongariro Alpine Crossing, considered one of New Zealand’s most beautiful day hikes. The route leads through a spectacular alpine landscape with fascinating volcanic craters, cooled lava fields, emerald green lakes and grandiose views of the volcanoes of the national park. In the afternoon, return transfer from the end of the hike back to your accommodation. Overnight in Tongariro NP.

Day 10 Wellington & Zealandia at night

Today you drive along the charming Kapiti coast south to Wellington, the capital of New Zealand. The city is not only the seat of government, but also a cultural and creative center of the country. Numerous art galleries, museums and a lively café and restaurant scene shape the cityscape. In the evening, a special nature experience awaits you in the award-winning Zealandia reserve. During a guided night walk (together with other guests) you will go in search of the inhabitants of the New Zealand night forest. With a little luck, you can observe the rare dwarf kiwi in its natural habitat. In addition, fireflies, Tuatara (bridge lizards), giant locusts, takahe, coca parrots or the New Zealand forest owl (ruru) can often be discovered. During the tour, you will learn about New Zealand’s unique wildlife and its importance in Maori culture. The atmospheric ending is a cup of hot Kawakawa tea in the visitor center. Overnight in Wellington.

Day 11 - From the North to the South Island

Today, take the Interislander ferry from Wellington on the North Island to Picton on the South Island. The approximately three-hour ferry ride through the Cookstraße is considered one of the most beautiful in the world and is an experience in itself. First, traverse the often rough Cook Road before the ferry enters the scenic Marlborough Sounds. This fascinating network of water arms and forested hills was created by the flooding of former river valleys. Steep mountain ridges today stand out as narrow peninsulas between the deep-cut sea bays and offer an impressive backdrop. During the 92-kilometer crossing, enjoy the delightful views of the coastal landscapes and the unique nature before you reach the South Island with your vehicle. Overnight in Kaiteriteri.

12th day Abel-Tasman National Park

Fifty kilometers of impressive coastal landscapes, golden yellow beaches, fascinating animals – this is the Abel Tasman National Park. Already the arrival is an experience: On the boat trip (together with other guests) to Medlands Beach you can with a little luck observe fur seals in their natural habitat. After that, wander through the pristine coastal forest of Abel Tasman National Park and enjoy spectacular views from the famous Falls River Bridge. Overnight in Kaiteriteri.

Day 13 Hokitika – The Heart of New Zealand Jade

After breakfast, today we continue on to the wild west coast of the South Island. Hokitika experienced a rapid development during the Gold Rush between 1865 and 1895 and was at that time a bustling gold mining town with numerous hotels and saloons. Today, the charming coastal town is best known for processing the famous New Zealand green stone. From the valuable Nephrite jade, artful jewelry and traditional Maori artworks are created here. After checking in at the hotel, design the afternoon to suit your tastes as you explore the charming town. Overnight in Hokitika.

Day 14 Fox & Franz Josef – Tour over the ice giants of the Southern Alps

The journey continues along the west coast to the southern Alps. As a natural weather barrier, the mountains here provide some of New Zealand’s highest rainfall levels. Above the snow line, enormous masses of ice collect from which impressive valley glaciers are created. The Fox and Franz Josef glaciers, which are approximately 12 kilometers long, extend – unique in temperate latitudes – down to about 300 meters above sea level. Experience the spectacular glacier landscape from a very special perspective on a helicopter flight over the Fox and Franz Josef Glacier. During the flight, breathtaking views and excellent photo opportunities offer the majestic peaks of Mount Cook and Mount Tasman. A special highlight is the landing in the eternal snow of the Southern Alps. Alternatively, thanks to their comparatively low position, the glaciers can also be explored up close on various hiking trails. Overnight in Franz Josef.

Day 15 On panoramic roads through the Southern Alps

A particularly nice excursion destination in the early morning is Lake Matheson at Fox Glacier. At dawn, the mostly calm water surface almost perfectly reflects the summits of the Southern Alps and provides one of the most famous photo opportunities in the region. After breakfast, you will leave the west coast and drive into the impressive mountains of the southern Alps. Your gateway to this spectacular landscape is the small village of Haast, where the river of the same name flows from the mountains into the Tasmanian Ocean. Before you cross the Alps via the Haast Pass, a stop at Ship Creek Walk is worthwhile. On wooden walkways, explore an original swamp forest that gives a sense of what New Zealand looked like before the arrival of European settlers. The route then leads through a varied landscape of rainforest, rivers and mountain panoramas. In the afternoon you will reach the resort of Wanaka, picturesquely located on the shore of the lake of the same name. Take the time for a short walk along the waterfront or a stroll through the charming place. From here it is not far to Queenstown, which can be reached in the late afternoon. There you move into your hotel for the coming nights. Overnight in Queenstown.

16th Day Queenstown: Adventure and Natural Enjoyment

Today you have time to experience Queenstown individually. The city lies picturesquely on the shores of crystal clear Lake Wakatipu and is framed by the impressive peaks of the Southern Alps. It is considered the adventure capital of New Zealand and offers an impressive variety of activities throughout the year. The region delights with a spectacular natural backdrop, which can be explored on numerous hiking and panoramic routes. Cyclists will also find ideal conditions here: The offer ranges from relaxed rides along the lakes and river valleys to challenging mountain bike routes. If you want to take it more quietly, you can play a round at one of the excellent golf courses in the region or visit the renowned wineries of the Central Otago area. Overnight in Queenstown.

Day 17 Te Anau: Hiking, discovering, enjoying

Your journey will take you to Te Anau, the gateway to Fiordland National Park – New Zealand’s largest and one of its most original and least developed national parks. Hardly any region of the country embodies the term wilderness more impressively than Fiordland. To the west, 14 fjords cut into a spectacular landscape of ancient rainforests, rugged mountain ranges and snow-capped peaks. The charming town of Te Anau has made a name for itself as “Walking Capital of the World” and offers the ideal starting point for numerous hikes and nature experiences in the impressive landscape of the Fiordland National Park. Enjoy today according to your own ideas. Overnight in Te Anau.

Day 18 Milford Sound – The Eighth Wonder of the World

With the Milford Sound, one of the absolute highlights of your New Zealand trip awaits you today. After about a two-hour drive, you will reach Milford Sound, the famous fjord in Fiordland National Park that Rudyard Kipling once called the “Eighth Wonder of the World”. During a relaxing, approximately 1.5-hour boat trip, you will discover the entire length of Milford Sound and immerse yourself in the fascinating nature of this unique region. Passing by steep towering rock walls, lushly overgrown mountain slopes and impressive waterfalls, a backdrop of extraordinary beauty opens up for you. With a bit of luck, you will encounter dolphins and seals along the way or, depending on the season, even the rare Fiordland cap penguins. The experienced crew adapts the route flexibly to current weather conditions and wildlife observations, so you can always experience the fjord from its most beautiful side. Enjoy unforgettable impressions in one of the most spectacular natural landscapes in New Zealand. Overnight in Te Anau.

Day 19 Historical Dunedin

Today you will continue to Dunedin, the impressive university town founded by Scottish settlers. One of its landmarks is the impressive Flemish Renaissance-style train station with its ornate mosaics, which should not be missed when visiting. In addition, it is home to the steepest street in the world, the famous Baldwin Street. Once Dunedin was the richest city in New Zealand: Already in 1863 it had gas-lit streets and in 1879 a tram was opened, following the model of the Californian cable cars, which facilitated transport to the higher parts of the city. In addition, the city rightly enjoys the reputation of being one of the best preserved Victorian cities in the southern hemisphere. Overnight in Dunedin.

20th Day Meeting with the Yellow Eye Penguins

Today you will visit a protection and breeding station for the rare yellow eye penguins on the Otago Peninsula. During an approximately 90-minute guided tour, you will get exciting insights into the lifestyle of this endangered animal species as well as into the extensive nature conservation measures for its preservation. The highlight is the trip to a protected nature reserve. On a guided walk about 2 km long through the charming coastal landscape you have with a little luck the opportunity to discover yellow-eye and dwarf penguins, seals, sea lions as well as numerous coastal and land birds. An unforgettable nature experience on the spectacular coast of the Otago Peninsula. Overnight in Dunedin.

21st day Natural wonders between coast and high mountains

Today you will leave Dunedin and follow the coast to Moeraki. Here you will find the famous Moeraki Boulders, mysterious, almost perfectly spherical boulders scattered on the windstorm beach. Continue to Oamaru and then inland through the impressive landscapes of Mackenzie Country. The route leads past the bright turquoise glacier lakes of the region. The views become more and more spectacular the closer you get to your daily destination: the Aoraki / Mount Cook National Park. The home of New Zealand’s highest mountain fascinates with its alpine backdrop of snow-capped peaks, mighty glaciers and almost untouched nature – a region that captivates nature lovers from all over the world. Overnight in Twizel.

Day 22 Christchurch: The perfect travel destination

This morning you will have the opportunity to take a scenic flight over Tasman Glacier or an impressive boat ride on the Glacier Lake (neither included) before cruising across the vast Canterbury Plains to Christchurch. Our personal recommendation to conclude your journey: a special culinary experience on board the Christchurch Tram. During an approximately 2.5-hour tour of the city center, you will enjoy selected specialties and drinks from New Zealand. This combines historical charm and modern culinary culture to create an unforgettable experience in an exclusive ambience. Let your New Zealand trip end in style on this special evening and enjoy a successful farewell to a fascinating country. Overnight in Christchurch.

23rd day Delivery of the rental car and return flight

Today ends your eventful journey through New Zealand. After unforgettable impressions, drive to Christchurch Airport, where you will return your rental car and travel home. We wish you a good return flight!

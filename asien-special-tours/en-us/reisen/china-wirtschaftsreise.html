China Economic Trip - The Digital Dragon

China Business Trip: Discover the digital dragon China on an individual trip with Asia Special Tours. Ask now!

Itinerary & services

English itinerary text is an offline machine translation of the German original for this team preview. Please confirm travel details with your advisor; the German source remains available through the language switch.

China on the rise – no other country is transforming into a high-tech nation at such a breathtaking pace. Embark on an extraordinary journey that will make the heart of any tech enthusiast and business enthusiast beat faster. Your journey begins in the glittering metropolis of Shanghai, where you’ll tour the world’s largest container ship port and the newly built port city created specifically for this stunning project. Suzhou is home to innovative robotics company ECOVAS and China’s first robot museum where you can enjoy interactive experiences with intelligent robots. A visit to the Autostadt Anting offers you the opportunity to experience a robo-taxi and visit the innovative robo-parking garage. But nowhere is the impressive economic upswing more noticeable than in Shenzhen, China’s newest mega-metropolis. In just 40 years, the former fishing village has developed into a vibrant city with 12 million inhabitants and is now considered China’s Silicon Valley, home to the world’s largest electronics market and the most innovative companies. But also the old China with its rich history does not fall short on this trip. Let yourself be carried away by the exciting mix of millennia-old cultural assets and modern China of the 21st century!

1st day Arrival in Shanghai

Welcome to the Megacity Shanghai! You will be picked up from the airport and taken to your hotel. In the afternoon, explore the city on a first round trip. Overnight in Shanghai.

2nd Day City of Contrasts – Multifaceted Shanghai

Shanghai has a lot of things to see for you. After a walk through the old town, visit the trendy district of Tianzifang. The lane labyrinth around Taikang Lu has attracted artists, artisans and young designers. An interesting contrast is the economic and high-tech center Pudong, the trademark of the new Shanghai. It’s hard to imagine that there is still a middle of 80Years ago stood little more than ailing harbor facilities! Today it stands out among other things Shanghai Tower 632 meters in height. It is the second tallest building in the world after Burj Khalifa. From the observation deck, you will have a sensational view of the city deep below you before you can feel the high-speed feeling of Shanghai on your own while riding the Transrapid. The evening is at your disposal. Whether you want to stroll through the busy streets, enjoy the city’s culinary diversity in one of its numerous restaurants or relax in one of the chic bars or cafes – the possibilities are almost unlimited. Overnight in Shanghai.

3rd day Technological innovations and maritime history

Today a real highlight awaits your visit. A 35-kilometer bridge will take you to Yangshan Deep Sea Port, arguably the largest container ship port in the world. Then visit the neighboring state-of-the-art port city of Ling Gang Xin Cheng. This newly planned city is a symbol of China’s vision of the urban future and impresses with innovative architecture and advanced technology. Here you have the special opportunity to take a ride on an autonomous robo-bus and experience the mobility of the future up close. In the afternoon, visit the China Maritime Museum, the largest maritime museum in the country. Here, immerse yourself in the exciting and long history of Chinese seafaring, from historic ships and ancient trade routes to the latest technological developments. Impressive models and interactive exhibitions will give you an in-depth insight into the central role of shipping for China and the global economy. Overnight in Shanghai.

4th Day Robot Museum in Suzhou

On an excursion to Suzhou, visit the innovative robot company ECOVAS with its associated robot museum. China’s first robot museum shows intelligent robots from around the world, including the US, France, UK, Japan and Korea. In the six sections of the museum, you will learn about the historical development of robots, learn about the wonders of robotics and interact with intelligent androids in a range of activities. The highlight of the museum, however, is the life-like robot San Bao acquired exclusively by NASA. He has considerable artificial intelligence, speaks more than 30 languages and even sleeps like a human being! With many new impressions of an age in which robots will no longer be indispensable from everyday life, return to Shanghai. The day will end with a visit to the Digital Art Museum “teamLab Borderless”. The three-dimensional exhibition is truly limitless: hundreds of projectors create fascinating digital worlds, impressive light sculptures and works of art that seem to come from another world. Overnight in Shanghai.

5th day Experiencing the future of mobility in Anting

Today you will visit the Autostadt Anting, which is about 50 kilometers west of Shanghai. Here you can expect a fascinating experience in the world of modern mobility. You will have the opportunity to experience a robo-taxi – an autonomous vehicle that takes you safely and comfortably to your destination. This innovative means of transport gives you a direct insight into the future of travel. Another highlight is the robo parking garage, where the latest technology for automatic parking of a vehicle is used. Watch vehicles parked without human intervention and learn about the efficient systems that make this technology possible. After your visit to Anting, you will return to Shanghai where the afternoon is at your disposal. Overnight in Shanghai.

Day 6 Shenzhen – China’s tech metropolis

Next stop on your trip is Shenzhen, which you can reach by domestic flight. Shenzhen is one of the fastest growing cities in the world and is of particular importance to foreign investors due to its status as a special economic zone. In the afternoon you will have the opportunity to visit the city planning museum. Here you will get a comprehensive insight into the impressive development of this dynamic metropolis, which has transformed from a small fishing village to a global hub for technology and innovation. In addition, you can expect an industrial exhibition that illuminates the history and influence of industry on the city and the region. Discover the different sectors that have contributed to Shenzhen’s economic flourishing and learn about the latest trends and technologies in the industry. This visit will give you the opportunity to experience Shenzhen’s fascinating transformation and gain a better understanding of the city’s urban developments and industrial advancements. Overnight in Shenzhen.

Day 7 Experience the future in Shenzhen

Today you have the unique opportunity to take part in a test flight with a robo-air taxi and experience the fascinating future of urban transport up close. Then board the BYD-Skyrail, an innovative and automated elevated rail system that takes you safely and comfortably to your next destination. This modern transport solution offers you not only a comfortable ride, but also a breathtaking view of the impressive urban landscape. Another exciting experience awaits you with the food delivery drone: be surprised how your meal is flown directly to you and discover the exciting possibilities that technology opens up in modern food delivery. These experiences turn your stay into an unforgettable adventure, marked by innovation and progress. Overnight in Shenzhen.

Day 8 Discover Nantou: From historic alleys to creative hotspots

Start your day by visiting Nantou Old Town, where history and culture meet. Stroll through the picturesque alleys, admire the well-preserved historical buildings and learn about the traditional past of this charming district. After exploring the old town, you can expect the creative highlights of the trendy district. Here you will find vibrant art installations, trendy cafes and unique shops that reflect the modern culture of Shenzhen. Afterwards you have the remaining time free. Take this opportunity to explore the area on your own, dine at one of the local restaurants or simply enjoy the atmosphere of this exciting district. Whether you want to relax or make new discoveries, the day offers you the freedom to make the most of your stay. Overnight in Shenzhen.

Day 9 Farewell to China

Today we say goodbye to the fascinating high-tech nation of China. Your journey begins with an impressive ride over two monumental bridges: the Shenzhen-Zhongshan Bridge and the Zhuhai-Macau-Hongkong Bridge, which run parallel across the mouth of the Pearl River. These architectural masterpieces offer not only spectacular views, but also a unique insight into China’s modern infrastructure. Afterwards you will travel to Hong Kong, where you will finally start your journey home.

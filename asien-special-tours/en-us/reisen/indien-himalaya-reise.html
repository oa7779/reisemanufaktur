Time out in the Himalayas

Experience the fascinating mountain world of India and enjoy the best hotels in the Himalayas on this trip from Asia Special Tours.

Itinerary & services

In everyday life, you sometimes wish to stop the time. Even if we can not offer you this service: This trip comes very close to the feeling. Set amid the monumental expanses of the Himalayas, visit villages that seem like you’ve turned the clock back. Your hike through the Kumaon region, which is little developed by tourists, will give you the opportunity to experience the Indian country life and the impressive natural treasures of the country as authentically as only a few have been granted. Here you will experience what backpackers are looking for and that without having to drag the backpack. Because this journey creates the difficult balancing act between amenities and authenticity: your luggage will be taken to the next stop of your hike, you will stay in traditional houses that have been carefully extended with modern amenities. Pure comfort will then offer you stays at two of the most beautiful resorts in the Himalayas: the 360° Leti, which perched solitude spectacularly above the mountains at 2400 meters altitude, and the Ananda in the Himalayas, one of the world's best spa resorts, which has already been honored by celebrities such as Bill Gates and Oprah Winfrey.

1st day Arrival in Delhi

India’s capital is colorful, loud and teeming with people. This is where your journey begins, which will offer you a contrast program to the bustle of the capital: vast landscapes, soothing tranquility and days beyond all hustle and bustle. Before continuing your train journey towards the mountainous region of Kumaon tomorrow, spend one night in Delhi. Upon arrival at the airport, you will already be expected and driven to your hotel. Take advantage of the rest of the day to explore the Indian metropolis: Delhi in a fascinating way combines modern city life with its thousands of years of eventful history. Stroll over Chandi Chowk, one of the largest bazaars in India, admire the monumental Friday Mosque or taste the delicacies of India’s street cuisine. Overnight in Delhi.

2nd day train ride to the Himalayan Mountains

Early in the morning, take the train east to Kathgodam. During the ride, you can watch the Holy Ganges pass by and finally watch the foothills of the Himalayan Mountains get closer on the horizon. In Kathgodam you will be picked up by your guide and the driver and together make your way deeper into the mountain landscape. The destination of your trip is the Almora region, about 1,600 meters above sea level, where in the middle of the idyllic mountain scenery lies the village of Chitai. From here your walk will begin, which will take you through various traditional villages and beautiful landscapes in the next few days. Your luggage will be brought to your accommodation for you so that you can enjoy your stroll through the mountains undisturbed. After visiting the temple dedicated to Golu Devata, the god of righteousness, embark on your two-hour trek towards the sleepy place of Deora. There you will stay in a traditional village house that has been carefully renovated, so you won’t have to miss out on modern amenities like a warm shower. Take a stroll through the village and get to know its inhabitants while a chef prepares an authentic Indian dinner especially for you. Overnight in Deora.

Day 3 Insights into Country Life

After breakfast, continue your tour through the unspoilt mountains of Kumaon. The first stop on your hike is the village of Chirala, where you will get a glimpse of the village's children's world while visiting a school . Afterwards, the path will take you down through the forest into a valley where the trees begin to thin and give you a view of farmers working in their fields and children playing at the creek. Along a water channel past a mill and rice terraces, the path winds up the mountain again. Depending on your condition, you can walk a piece to the picnic area for lunch by car or climb a fairly steep hill for an hour and a half. Your luggage is always transported with a vehicle, if you do not feel up to the effort at any point of the hike, you can also rely on motorized travel for part of the route. Often, however, a break is enough to come to new strengths – since you are on the way with your personal tour guide, you alone determine the pace of the hike. After eating, the trail continues through rhododendron forests to Jwalabanj, which you reach in the afternoon. Overnight in Jwalabanj.

Day 4 The mountain panorama of the Himalayas

Early in the morning, set off for a short hike to a viewpoint that spreads a fantastic panorama of the highest peaks of the Indian Himalayas ahead of you. Watch as the morning sun dips the surrounding mountains into gentle shades of red and yellow and take in the peace and expanse of this landscape. After the descent, a cozy breakfast awaits you in Jwalabanj. Take your time before you set off for a four to five-hour hike around the village today. Overnight in Jwalabanj.

Day 5 Arrival at 360° Leti

After a few days of exploring Kumaon on your hikes, you now expect two days of rest and relaxation. You will continue your journey to the spectacularly located Resort 360° Leti. After a half-hour hike, you drive a bit by car and finally climb a mountain path to your accommodation. At an altitude of more than 2,400 meters, nothing but the monumental mountains of the Himalayas surrounds you. Your resort has only four private pavilions, so you have the untouched nature almost to yourself. The architecture of the hotel combines the traditional architectural style of the region with modern elegance: wood and natural stone convey a rustic warmth, the spacious windows offer breathtaking views on all sides. Overnight at 360° Leti.

Day 6 - 7 Relaxation at 2,400 meters altitude

Without tour guide & chauffeur

In the morning, you can watch the sunrise over the mountains through the spacious window front of your bungalow without having to fight back even the pashmina ceilings of your bed. Here far away from everyday life – or rather far beyond it – the days take their course quite peacefully. Thanks to the spectacular natural scenery, it is already an unforgettable experience to simply enjoy the view from a deckchair. Depending on your mood, you can take smaller walks in the area – including a guide and picnic basket. You will experience even more rest during guided yoga or meditation exercises. With meals, spoil the resort’s gourmet chefs with exquisite food and wine. And at the end of the day, watch the sunset from your bungalow’s terrace over some of the world’s tallest peaks as a campfire quietly crackles. Overnight at 360° Leti.

Day 8: An Evening at the Camp Fire

After a leisurely breakfast, set off for a slightly longer drive. After about five hours – the view always compensates for the long way – you reach the village of Kana. If you wish, you can take a short hike from here before ending the evening at the campfire. Overnight in Cana.

9th Day Fascinating Wildlife at Corbett National Park

Start the day with a short walk to a nearby temple. Then after a week in the heights of the Himalayas, your journey will take you back to lower realms. Your driver will take you to Corbett National Park. If you wish, you can already learn more about the flora and fauna of the region from your guide: located in the foothills of the Himalayas, the reserve is India's oldest nature reserve and also the first to be dedicated specifically to preserving the highly endangered tigers. In the forests, valleys crossed by streams and on the high plateaus of the parks live a multitude of animals, whose habitat is increasingly circumcised elsewhere: herds of the imposing Indian elephant roam the meadows, gharial galleys sunbathe on the banks of the streams, tigers lurking in the undergrowth. In the evening you will have the opportunity to make your first explorations in the jungle with the nature expert of your hotel. Overnight in Corbett National Park.

Day 10: Travel to Narendra Nagar

On a safari early in the morning, today you will have the opportunity to observe fascinating animals in their natural environment. If luck is very favorable to you, you may even be able to look a tiger in the eye. However, the King of the Jungle is unfortunately one of the most camera-shy residents of the National Park. After breakfast at your resort, leave the sanctuary behind and continue your journey along the foothills of the Himalayas to the northeast. Your destination is Narendra Nagar, a small town that offers a breathtaking view of the lowlands of Haridwar at 1300 meters, through which three rivers wind. In the early twentieth century, the incumbent British viceroy had the palace of Maharaja extended here, which over the years housed many important political figures such as Indira Gandhi. Even since the building was converted into a luxurious spa resort in 2000, the stream of illustrious guests has not stopped: Oprah Winfrey, Bill Gates, Kate Winslet and Prince Charles have already been pampered here. You too will get your money’s worth in one of the best spa resorts in the world. Overnight in Narendra Nagar.

Days 11-14 at one of the best spa resorts in the world

“Coming to Ananda is more of a pilgrimage than a visit,” says Oprah Winfrey about her stay at the resort. The spa offering of Ananda is deeply rooted in Indian healing. For you, one of 5 wellness programs is already included. Choose from Yoga, Destress, Active, Renew and Detox. These all-inclusive packages are programs tailored to individual health goals and following a holistic approach, expertly led by a team of experienced Ayurvedic doctors, trained therapists, nutritionists, yogis and spa chefs. With guided exercises in yoga and meditation, thousands of years-old traditions will help you achieve peace and quiet. The resort’s highly qualified doctors and therapists combine ancient traditional knowledge with the latest findings from medical research and integrate influences from all over the world into your spa program. Your room with a private balcony offers you a beautiful view over the surrounding mountain landscape. In the restaurant you can expect refined dishes with ingredients from your own organic garden. The unusual combination of Oriental and Californian cuisine is a very special treat. Overnight in Narendra Nagar.

Day 15 Farewell to India

After two weeks of retreat, fascinating insights into strange worlds and hikes through unspoilt landscapes, your break from everyday life is coming to an end. You will be taken to the airport in the afternoon for your flight to Delhi. From Delhi you start your home or onward journey.

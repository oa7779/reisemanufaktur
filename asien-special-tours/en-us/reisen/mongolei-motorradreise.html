Mongolia Motorcycle Tour - On the Journey to Happiness

Mongolia Motorcycle Tour: Feel the freedom and discover the endless expanses of Mongolia on this motorcycle tour of Asia Special Tours

Itinerary & services

English itinerary text is an offline machine translation of the German original for this team preview. Please confirm travel details with your advisor; the German source remains available through the language switch.

Cross Mongolia, the land of unlimited freedom, with your motorcycle and be impressed by the fascinating natural spaces. Swirled dust and clattering motors - uphill and downhill combined with infinite expanse awaits you in Mongolia. Experience an unforgettable adventure in the Wild West and focus on your sporting tour and a unique experience. Your motorized companion bears the name Cross C450, with which you will start your dream journey well equipped. You will be off-road driving over gravel, sand, stones and gravel. Of course, there are always on-road routes. Please prepare for a very simple infrastructure, which will be compensated with many beautiful experiences and breathtaking views. Feeling freedom, breathing freedom is the motto of your Mongolia motorcycle trip to happiness.

1st day Arrival in Ulaanbaatar

Welcome to Ulaanbaatar, the capital of Mongolia. Upon arrival at the airport, you will be welcomed by your tour guide and first taken to your hotel. While visiting the Gandan Monastery and a historical museum of Mongolia, you will not only get to know the multifaceted country more intensively, but also make the cultural visits an ideal starting point for your dream trip. Overnight in Ulaanbaatar.

Day 2 The Journey of Your Life

On the road to happiness! Today the motorcycles are prepared and your big overland journey into the Gobis desert begins. Did you know that it is the northernmost and at the same time fifth largest desert in the world? Following Mongolian tradition, take the sun south into the Gobi Desert. During the journey you can enjoy the first impressions of the landscape and already breathe in the unlimited freedom. Today’s day stage starts comfortably on mostly asphalt roads until you reach your first yurt camp. Of course, there is also a bit of off-road so you can get a little warm for the next few days. Arriving in the mountains of Baga Gazriin Chuluu, move into your yurt camp and admire for the first time the infinite starry sky that spreads over you like a blanket of stars. Overnight at the Baga Gazar yurt camp. Day stage: about 260 km

Day 3 – The path is the destination – Zagaan Suvarga

Feel freedom, breathe freedom! After a short drive (approx.) 70 km) you reach the small town of Mandalgobi, the provincial capital of Mittelgobi - Aimag. Immerse yourself in the everyday life of this small desert town and feel the coziness of the country. Of course, now is also a good opportunity to refuel your motorcycle enough before it goes on into the desert with a new adventure spirit. The endless expanse and solitude, interrupted only once in a while by a few camels and goat breeders, gives the feeling that you are somewhere at the end of the world. Enjoy this feeling of luxury and give your full attention to nature. The impressive rock formations Zagaan Suvarga, called “white and red stupas” by the locals due to their color and shape, are waiting for you. After an eventful ride and insane 250 new kilometers on the tachometer, it is now time to relax and regenerate. Enjoy the independence of your dream journey and feel the strengthening power of nature. Overnight at Tsagaan Suvraga Jurtencamp. Day stage: about 250 km.

Day 4 Unforgettable Adventure – South Gobi

In absolute isolation, another day starts in the Gobi desert. After having enough energy at breakfast, the first stage will take you to Mount Del Uul, where 4 km of Bronze Age rock drawings stretch. Enjoy a sporty underground, as your ride will take you on a rocky slope over small hills along the Gobi desert. In the afternoon and after a proud 270 kilometers you reach your yurt camp in the middle of the boundless desert. Overnight at Khanbogd Jurtencamp. Day Stage: about 270 km

Day 5 In harmony with nature – discover the Vulture Gorge

So close to nature! Today is a trip to the Vulture Gorge on your program. This is located in the Gurwan Saikhan National Park, on the foothills of the Altai Mountains. After visiting a small natural history museum, discover the impressive gorge and, with a bit of luck, meet the Capricorns and wild sheep living here, then return to your yurt camp where you can recharge in the afternoon for tomorrow's stage. Overnight at Khanbogd Jurtencamp. Day stage: about 30 km

Day 6 On the way towards sand dunes

Towards the adventure! After fortifying yourself at breakfast, set off to marvel at the longest walking dunes, the Khongoryn Els Sand Dunes in South Gobi. In the middle of the landscape, an extraordinary mountain of sand shoots up, which is up to 120 meters high and stretches over a fabulous length of 180 km. Together with the dark mountain range in the background, there is a particularly intense panorama. Do not forget to capture this dreamlike backdrop for eternity. In addition to this breathtaking sight, the meeting with camel breeders will be unforgettable - with a little luck you will also meet a gazelle herd. Overnight at Gobi Discovery Jurtencamp. Day stage: about 180 km  

Day 7 Khongor Sand Dunes

Create the power of inner peace and let it be relaxed. This day, you will make it to your mood. For example, take a round around the large sand dunes. This unique backdrop makes you feel the fascinating desert experience even more intensely. Of course, you can also relax and gather new energy for the next stages. Overnight at Gobi Discovery Jurtencamp.  

Day 8 Ready, Set, Go!

Let the soul dangle in the land of the steppe. Today you can look forward to a very special highlight of your trip. The Flaming Cliff is a rock formation that is transformed into a blood-red sea of color in the evening sun and thus becomes a real eye-catcher! Swipe on your motorized darling and drive into the magical desert forest of Bayanzag. Overnight in the Mongol Gobi Jurtencamp. Day stage: about 150 km

Day 9 Visit of two pieces of cultural jewelry

A new day – a new goal! On hot summer days it may happen that a mirage appears in front of you, but at the next moment it dissolves into thin air. In the Mittelgobi desert you will find the ruins of two larger 19th century monasteries located on the Ongi River. Overnight in the Ongiin Nuuz Jurtencamp. Day stage: about 160 km

Day 10 Karakorum – UNESCO Historical & Cultural Heritage

As the sun rises over the infinite expanses of Central Mongolia, head towards Karakorum. The multicultural trading metropolis, which was founded in 1220, is still one of the most famous and probably worth seeing places in the country. After an impressive visit to the excavation site, you will visit the Erdene Zuu monastery, founded in the 16th century, one of the largest in Mongolia. Of the formerly more than 60 buildings, only three have survived after the Cultural Revolution. With a total of 108 stupas and a white border wall, the monastery still offers an impressive picture. After an outstanding day stage with a sporty 270 km you reach your nocturnal domicile. Overnight at Urguu-Kharkhorin Jurtencamp.

Day Stage: about 270 km

11th Day Up Together – Khogno Khan

Moments for eternity. Today you will continue your journey to the Khogno Khan Mountains. After climbing the sand dune, a unique panorama awaits you. Welcome to the steppe desert gate. Your path will take you on, past the sand dunes of Mongol Els, where you will jet through the sand with your motorcycle. Gather moments of happiness with drive on your off-road route! If you like it a bit more sporty, there is also the possibility to hike up the mountain. Of course, you will be rewarded with a unique view. Overnight at Khogno Khan Jurtencamp. Day stage: about 100 km

12th Day Hustain Nuruu National Park

Today you will get to know another treasure of Mongolia. Hustai Nuruu National Park is best known for its free-living Takhi or Przewalski horses, the ancestors of all horse breeds living today. With a little luck, you can watch the wild horses towards evening when they make their way to the potions. Enjoy precious moments in this magical place in nature. Overnight at Camp Hustai Tuv Jurtencamp. Day stage: about 230 km

13th Day Together to the goal – Ulaanbaatar

Today it is final sprint on your motorcycle trip to happiness. The last 100 km are waiting for you, because when you arrive in Ulaanbaatar it is important to say goodbye to your motorized companion. Let the evening go quietly and look back with pride on 2000 km of off- and on-road across Mongolia. Overnight in Ulaanbaatar.

Day 14 Farewell to Mongolia

Today your trip to Mongolia comes to an end and it means saying goodbye to this fascinating country. Surely you have packed your suitcases with many new impressions and an unforgettable motorcycle adventure in Mongolia is one of your travel treasures. Your driver will take you to Ulaanbaatar airport, from where you will board your return flight. We wish you a pleasant journey home.

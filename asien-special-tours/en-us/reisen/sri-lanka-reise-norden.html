The forgotten north of Sri Lanka

Discover the forgotten north of Sri Lanka with its natural and cultural beauties on this individual tour of Asia Special Tours.

Away from the tourist trails, discover unique landscapes and multifaceted animal giants - this is the credo of your personal dream trip to the northern tip of Sri Lanka. Feel the magical power of nature and conquer the highlights of Sri Lanka’s often underestimated natural jewel. Immerse yourself in the fascinating culture of the country and explore one or the other insider tip of the glamorous island. In the picturesque Wilpattu National Park, you will experience the species-rich wildlife and be captivated by the impressive natural spectacle. You stay in first-class hotels, which of course leave no wishes unfulfilled. Convince yourself of unparalleled retreats and collect moments of happiness for the soul.

1st day Arrival in Sri Lanka

Today your journey begins through the north of Sri Lanka. Upon arrival at Colombo airport, you will be picked up and taken to your hotel in Thriappane. After the approximately 4.5 hour drive, you can rest a little after arrival. Your charming boutique hotel is idyllic surrounded by emerald green lush rice fields and a small pond and offers an ideal starting point to consciously enjoy the oasis of tranquility. The tastefully furnished villas all have a private pool. Immerse yourself in your very personal dream world, where you are in the absolute center of attention. Overnight in Thriappane.

2nd day Anuradhapura UNESCO World Heritage Site

In the morning you will travel to Anuradhapura to explore the former capital of Sri Lanka. Anuradhapura is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and is Sri Lanka’s first kingdom. According to the ancient Sinhalese chronicle 'Mahavansa', the kingdom was founded in the fifth century BC. Upon arrival, you will be greeted by the bright white dome-shaped stupas or dagobas, the religious monuments Ruwanweliseya and Jethawanarama. These two stupas have survived over two millennia and the mighty Jethawanarama is just as large as the pyramids in Giza. Next, admire the 'Samadhi' Buddha statue, a granite statue of Buddha in meditative pose - a true masterpiece. Afterwards, you will receive a blessing near the sacred Sri Maha Bodhi tree – which was brought from India over 2,500 years ago and is considered the oldest tree in the world. Overnight in Thirappane.

3rd Day Dream Beaches in Trincomalee

After breakfast, your trip will take you to the port city on the north coast of Sri Lanka. Upon arrival in Trincomalee, you can take it easy and enjoy the world-class amenities of Uga Jungle Beach Resort. This unique luxury resort is like a stylish tree house and invites you to dream and relax. 49 chalets with traditional and modern elements will certainly make your heart beat faster. The exotic environment also has a lot to offer and impresses with a magnificent view of the neighboring lagoon and offers fascinating insights into the legendary flora and fauna of the north. Overnight in Trincomalee.

Day 4 - 6 - Let the soul dangle

Without guidance

Enchanting days lie ahead of you. Enjoy this unforgettable time under the sign of relaxation and relaxation. Start the day comfortably, let yourself be inspired by the amenities of your luxury hotel and take long walks on the beach. Collect memories, not things! Overnight in Trincomalee.

Day 7 Jaffna – Sri Lanka’s original north

After breakfast, leave the scenic coast and drive up to the original north of the island. The exotic peninsula Jaffna on the northern tip of Sri Lanka is inhabited mainly by the Tamil population of the country and impresses with an unmistakable environment and history. Some of the most fertile fields on the island are found in Jaffna and offer in particular mangoes, palmyrapalms and red chili peppers and spicy curries. After arriving at the hotel, she takes a boat to the historic Fort Hammenhiel in the afternoon. The rest of the day is at your disposal. Overnight in Jaffna.

Day 8 Historic Jaffna

After breakfast, you will go by boat to the nearby island of Delft, where you can comfortably explore the island in the Tuk Tuk and let the idyllic surroundings affect you. One of the most important Hindu temples in Jaffna district is Nallur Kandaswamy Kovil. The sacred temple, with its striking towers decorated with the images of deities of Hinduism, is venerated by adherents of all religions. Continue to the Keerimalai pond, whose springs are considered therapeutic and contain medicinal water. The site itself is of historical and religious importance. Then visit Nagadeepa Temple, an ancient Buddhist temple in Nagadeepa. A ferry ride back to the mainland of Jaffna will take you to Dambakola Patuna, the seaport. Let the afternoon relax at the famous Casuarina beach and hear only the sound of the sea and feel the white sandy beach under your feet. The rest of the day is at your disposal. Overnight in Jaffna.

Day 9 Discover the highlights of Mannar

Today you continue your journey towards Mannar. Your exploration tour starts with a historic highlight, the 450-year-old Mannar Fort and this is not only one of the oldest structures in the region, but was once considered a guard post to monitor the vital area for the ancient silk and spice routes. From here, you will also see a huge monkey bread tree or affectionately referred to as the upside down tree. Also, the religious harmony of Mannar does not go undetected by you while admiring three very different places of worship. The temple Mathota Raja Maha Viharaya is one of the few Buddhist temples in the region. Pass Thiruketheeswaram Temple, an important Hindu temple, before reaching the Catholic Church. Mannar is the ideal place for bird watching between November and early April. The wide open flood plains, salt marshes, mangroves and sand dunes with a mixture of salt water and fresh water create the perfect salinity balance to grow the food sources for the migratory birds. Enjoy the approximately 4-hour nature hike and let the impressions of the picturesque landscape affect you. End the day relaxingly as you explore the well-known Adams Bridge, a connection of sandbanks, coral reefs and islands in the strait between Sri Lanka and India. Overnight in Mannar.

Day 10 Sanctuary in Mannar

In the morning, visit the donkey clinic and sanctuary in Mannar. Donkeys used to be part of everyday life in Mannar, but more recently there have been many conflicts with people. This sanctuary takes care of abandoned donkeys and brings them back to the community. It is believed that donkeys were brought to Sri Lanka by merchants on the Somali coast as a cheaper alternative to horses or camels. They used to be part of the budget, now many of them have been abandoned, which has led to problems. Many are sick, injured and starved, but with the help of this sanctuary they will be cared for and hopefully put on the path of recovery. The sanctuary not only helps donkeys, but also examines the healing effects of donkey-assisted therapy on children with special needs. The rest of the day is at your disposal. Overnight in Mannar.

11th Day Untouched Nature in the Wilpattu National Park & Overnight at the Tent Camp

Today you leave Mannar and continue your journey to Wilpattu. Breathtaking natural landscapes and boundless freedom are already waiting for you. Once you arrive, there will be plenty of time to explore your home before going on safari in Wilpattu National Park in the evening. The Wilpattu National Park is located in the northwest of the island and is one of the largest and at the same time one of the oldest national parks. As you drive through the park, you will notice many small natural lakes or "Willu". Be sure to pay attention to the spotted deer, wild boars and wild buffaloes roaming through the park. If you’re lucky, you might be able to watch the sloth bears from Sri Lanka eating on the berries of Palu trees in June and July. Recognizing the elusive Sri Lankan leopard is difficult, but not impossible for the naked eye. Thanks to the coastal belt on the edge of the park, as well as the natural lakes, owls, eagles, storks, peacocks and icy birds, you can also see them floating through the air or wading through the water. The Wilpattu Safari Camp has four tents and offers plenty of peace and relaxation. Enjoy the here and now and let the evening end relaxed in the camp. Overnight in Wilpattu.

12th day Jeep Safari in Wilpattu National Park

Getting up early is especially rewarded today. In the morning, set off on a morning safari and experience the first hours of the day in the multifaceted Wilpattu National Park. Then you return to the camp and can recharge your energy. With a bit of luck, you can watch the multitude of wildlife in the park in the afternoon and feel the authentic safari feeling up close. After this impressive day, enjoy the amenities of your camp. How about in the evening, for example, with a cool drink under the stars? Collect precious moments in this magical place. Overnight in Wilpattu.

13-14. Tag Beach pearl Kalpitiya

Without guidance

Change of scene - in the morning continue your journey towards Kalpitiya. Now it is time to simply relax and review the impressive moments of your dream journey. Treat yourself to the special and listen only to the sound of the sea and the knocking of your heart. The boutique hotel welcomes you in a unique environment and offers with spacious and tastefully furnished tents just the right oasis for deceleration and endless freedom. Feeling, tasting, seizing happiness with your hands – live the new zeitgeist of travel.
Have you ever dreamed of seeing dolphins up close? This dream could come true the next morning. Take a boat out to sea and experience the delicate marine animals in their natural environment. (Optional and weather dependent, only available from November to March). Overnight in Kalpitiya.

Day 15 “Angampora” – 2000 years old martial art

After breakfast, it is time to leave for the last stop of your dream trip. After arriving in Colombo, you can relax before continuing to Kaduwela in the afternoon. There you will find an authentic insight into the martial art “Angampora”, which is passed down through generations of generations and combines self-defense techniques, combat and sacred rituals. This is a unique opportunity to visit a traditional Angampora village in the suburbs of Colombo, where this ancient martial art is still very much alive today. Upon your arrival, you will be greeted by an Angampora Guru who will explain and demonstrate this ancient art. Next, you are invited to see an exciting performance that demonstrates the defense techniques and grips. The sacred rituals are performed at a drumbeat, which is used for training, showing and fighting. Afterwards, you will also learn about the vital art of meditation and relaxation. For the crowning conclusion, you have the opportunity to learn a few movements for yourself! Overnight in Colombo.

16th day home

Even the most beautiful journey will come to an end. You will be taken to the airport and even if your holiday is over – the precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories of your trip will accompany you forever. Look forward to letting your friends and family share your experiences in the distance at home. Your driver guide will take you to the airport for your return flight.

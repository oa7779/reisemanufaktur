Family Adventures in Bali with Stopover in Singapore

Family trip Bali: Experience your individual Indonesia round trip with Asia Special Tours. Ask now without obligation!

Itinerary & services

English itinerary text is an offline machine translation of the German original for this team preview. Please confirm travel details with your advisor; the German source remains available through the language switch.

Embark on your very personal family adventure in Bali. A stopover in the glittering world metropolis Singapore, at the start of the trip, offers many exciting experiences for young and old. Afterwards, Bali is waiting for many adventures that this versatile journey has to offer you. Your path leads you not only to Ubud, the cultural heart of the island, but also to beautiful temples and the green rice fields of Bali. The hospitable Balinese will warmly welcome you. During a snorkeling trip, you will immerse yourself in the fascinating underwater world with all its dazzling colors. Enough adrenaline is provided by an exciting rafting tour and the suspension bridges in the Bali Treetop Adventure Park. Afterwards, let your soul really dangle and enjoy the precious time with your whole family on the beach of Nusa Dua.

1st day Arrival in Singapore

Welcome to Singapore. Upon arrival, you will be expected by your chauffeur and taken to your hotel. Relax a little or go on a first exploration tour. The city is a melting pot that in particular combines cultural influences from Chinese, Indian, British and Malaysian culture. Gather first impressions of this dazzling metropolis and let yourself drift. Overnight in Singapore.

2Sentosa Island & Gardens by the Bay

After an extensive breakfast, you will be picked up by your guide and drive to the island of Sentosa. Start with a cable car ride that gives you a spectacular view of the Singapore skyline and the port from a bird’s eye view. Afterwards you will experience the exciting Skyline Luge adventure, where you whizz with your sled over the unique tracks. The SA Aquarium, the world’s largest oceanarium, awaits unique animal encounters. After returning to the mainland, you will have the opportunity to rest at the hotel before your next discovery tour begins in the evening. This time it is in Singapore’s green heart – to the Gardens by the Bay. Visit the two spectacular winter gardens. The Cloud Forest impresses with tropical vegetation, ferns, orchids and the tallest indoor waterfall in the world, which rushes from 35 meters into the depth. The highlight will be the light and music show “Garden Rhapsody”, where the iconic supertrees shine in colorful light and move at the beat of the music. Finish your day with a ride on the Singapore River in a traditional bumboat. At the estuary of the river is watched by the majestic Merlion - a mythological creature who is half lion, half fish. Overnight in Singapore.

3rd Day Animal Encounters at Singapore Zoo

After breakfast, your day starts with a visit to the Singapore Zoo – one of the most beautiful and close to nature zoos in the world. Nestled in lush tropical vegetation, this award-winning wildlife park offers more than 2,800 animals from over 300 species a species-appropriate home in open, spacious habitats. Experience the savannahs of Africa, the dense rainforests of Asia or the rugged rock landscapes of Ethiopia and observe the animals in an environment modeled on their natural habitats. A special highlight for families is the KidzWorld – home of Ranger friends. Here, interactive learning is in the foreground: children can discover the wildlife in a creative way while playing, climbing and splashing. From water games to sensory experiences, there is something for every age group. Full of experiences and impressions, you will return to your hotel in the afternoon. The rest of the day is at your disposal – ideal for relaxing or exploring the city on your own. Overnight in Singapore.

4th day Flight to Bali

Today we say goodbye to Singapore. You will be taken to the airport and fly on to Bali. Selamat datang and welcome to the Island of the Gods! Upon your arrival in Denpasar, you will already be expected and welcomed by your guide. Afterwards you will be driven from your chauffeur to the hotel in Sanur. After checking in, you and your family are free to spend the rest of the day. Sanur is one of the most famous and largest places in East Bali. Here you can immerse yourself in Balinese everyday life for the first time. Walk along the seafront promenade, find cooling in the clear waters of the Indian Ocean and be enchanted by the wonderful odour of incense sticks, sweet food, tropical fruits and the mystical sounds of gamelan music. Sanur is especially suitable for families with children, as the sea here falls particularly flat, so the swimmers among them have to wait for the tide. Sanur is also a wonderful starting point to explore the island. Arrive now in peace, tomorrow you will begin your exploration of Bali! Overnight in Sanur.

Day 5 - Life in Sanur

Start today with a relaxed breakfast. Around 14:00 you will be picked up by your guide and chauffeur. You will start your excursion with a visit to one of the few museums in Sanur, which is located directly on the beach. The Belgian artist Le Mayeur reached the island by ship in 1935 and moved into a small house in Sanur. He found his muse in his wife Ni Nyoman Pollok, a Balinese dancer. Afterwards, a local fisherman takes you on board his traditional boat, a jukung, out at sea. Arriving at Semawang Beach, you will continue on foot to the most famous Italian ice cream parlor Sanurs, where you can taste a few balls. Then visit the well-hidden Blanjong Temple. With the national means of transport, the Bemo, you drive back to the center of Sanur and pay a visit to the Pasar Senggol, the night market, where all visitors sit on long benches or colorful stools around the stalls and can taste the various local delicacies. How about Ikan Goreng, a baked fish, or for the kids the wonderfully sweet Pisang Goreng, fried bananas? After this eventful day, you will return to your hotel in the evening. Overnight in Sanur.

Day 6 Wave Riding at Sanur Beach

Waves, beach and sea... a sparkling pleasure awaits you! After breakfast, today you will head straight to the beach, where you will take part in a two and a half hour surf lesson. First, you will receive a brief safety briefing as well as an introduction to the theory of surfing. Then you can try it yourself. What fun for the whole family! Then return to the hotel where the rest of the day is free. Relax or swim a few rounds in the pool. Overnight in Sanur.

7th day Visit of the Jatiluwih rice terraces

After a relaxing breakfast, head to the Pemuteran region. Your first stop is the Jatiluwih rice terraces, a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Enjoy the endless views of green rice terraces and learn about the traditional techniques used by Bali’s farmers to water the fields and produce rice. Continue to the Bedugul Market, where you’ll discover a variety of local products and scented flowers used for ceremonies and offerings. The evening will then be relaxed in your next hotel. Overnight in Pemuteran.

Day 8 Bali's fascinating underwater world

Today you can expect an impressive journey into the underwater world of Bali. You will be taken by boat to the island of Menjangan. A snorkeling trip through the species-rich coral garden opens up a whole new world for you. Between corals and anemones in the warm, crystal-clear water there are fish of all colors and forms. Afterwards, explore the island of Menjangan with a small walk. With a little luck, you can observe the deer that is home to here. Around noon you will find strengthening in your lunch box. Enjoy your rest on the beach before returning to your hotel in Pemuteran. Overnight in Pemuteran.

Day 9 on the way to Ubud

Ran to the nets! With a small motorboat you go out to the sea and can help the locals with traditional fishing. The caught fish can then be prepared on the beach for lunch. In the afternoon you will continue to Ubud. The ride is very scenic. Through green rice fields, mountains and clear rivers it goes into the heart of Bali. During your journey, you will stop at the water temple Ulun Danu, which is located in the mountains on Lake Bratan. This temple is dedicated to Dewi Danu, the deity of water. In the late afternoon you will reach your hotel in Ubud. Relax in your hotel or go on a small exploring tour. Ubud is characterised by many temples, museums, art galleries and a far-reaching artistic heritage, as Western artists and intellectuals were often invited there in the 1930s. The place will delight you with its idyllic location, rice fields and lush jungles! Overnight in Ubud.

Day 10 Visit to the Monkey Forest

After breakfast, you will begin your visit in the heart of Balinese culture. First, visit Ubud’s traditional market. The locals come to the market early in the morning every day to run their errands. In addition to food for everyday use, the Balinese also need small offerings for their gods and house spirits. So small cakes, various tropical flowers and incense sticks end up in the baskets of the women, who then usually carry it on their heads. Afterwards, your guide will take you to another highlight of Ubud: the "Monkey Forest". The tropical vegetation with its moss-covered stones is home to about 140 - 200 long-tailed macaques. Stroll through the forest, discover numerous hidden temples and beware of the monkeys that are constantly on prey! The monkeys can quickly become thieves and then it takes a lot of skill to get the things back. In the afternoon you will drive to Tegallalang, where you can admire the magnificent landscape around Ubud during a two-hour hike. Along rice fields and picturesque villages, you will get a good insight into the traditional rice cultivation and rural life of the Balinese. Overnight in Ubud.

11th day rafting tour on the Ayung River

Start the day well strengthened, because today is another adventure on the program. Together with your family, take an exciting rafting tour along the Ayung River. After a short safety introduction, take the paddles in hand and rush through the refreshing water. Over obstacles, through narrow gorges and with a breathtaking view of the rice terraces, you finally reach the end point where a delicious lunch is waiting for you. Afterwards, you will be driven to your bathing hotel in Nusa Dua and have free time for the rest of the day. Your hotel, which combines tradition and modernity, impresses above all with its dreamlike gardens as well as an outstanding service. Several pools and five restaurants make it easy for you to switch off from everyday life and let your soul dangle! Overnight in Nusa Dua.

12.-15. Day of beach holidays

Without tour guide and chauffeur

After you have gained many exciting impressions of the rich cultural and natural treasures of Bali as well as its friendly inhabitants during the last few days on your round trip, you can relax once again at the end of your holiday. Go the days slowly, make time for a good book or the sweet nothing-doing. Overnight in Nusa Dua.

16th day Departure from Indonesia

Your time out from everyday life is coming to an end. Even if your holiday is soon over, the precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories of your trip will accompany you forever. Look forward to letting your friends and family share your experiences in the distance at home. Your driver will take you to the airport for your return flight.

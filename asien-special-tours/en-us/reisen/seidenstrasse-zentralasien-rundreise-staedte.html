Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Uzbekistan and Turkmenistan - Unforgotten Cities

Central Asia Round Trip: Discover unforgettable cities along the Silk Road with Asia Special Tours, your Asia specialist. Ask now!

Itinerary & services

English itinerary text is an offline machine translation of the German original for this team preview. Please confirm travel details with your advisor; the German source remains available through the language switch.

Many different faces, cultures, traditions and landscapes characterize the four Central Asian countries Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Uzbekistan and Turkmenistan. Let yourself be enchanted by magnificent cathedrals, oriental markets, imposing mosques and richly decorated medres that lie on your itinerary along the ancient Silk Road – as well as mountain lakes, deserts and mountains.

1st day Arrival in Kazakhstan

Welcome to Kazakhstan! At the airport of Almaty, your German-speaking guide awaits you and drives you to your hotel. Get a little fresh – then go: your guide will show you Panfilov Park and the beautiful Zenkov Cathedral, built only of wood. Other items on the agenda are the State Museum and the Museum of Kazakh musical instruments. Afterwards, take a trip to the Medeo sports complex, about 17 kilometers before Almaty. Overnight in Almaty.

Day 2 Almaty – Lake Issyk-Kul (Kyrgyzstan)

You leave Almaty, cross the Kyrgyz border at noon and travel onwards via Kordai Pass to Lake Issyk-Kul – the second largest mountain lake in the world. He is the pride of Central Asia. The rest of the day is at your disposal. Overnight at Lake Issyk-Kul.

Day 3 Karakol – Sary-Oi

In the morning you will visit Karakol. The city was founded in 1869 as a Russian officer settlement and later built in the Russian-colonial style. Visit the wooden Dungan Mosque. It was built in 1910 by the Chinese-Muslim dungeons expelled from China. The architectural style of the mosque is more reminiscent of a Buddhist temple or pagoda. You will also visit the wooden Russian Orthodox Church. Then drive to Sary-Oi on the north bank of Lake Issyk-Kul. Along the way, take a trip to the Semenov Gorge and the Grigoriev Gorge with beautiful views of the slopes covered with Tienshan fir trees, snow-capped peaks and emerald green pastures. In good weather you have a wonderful view of the high mountain lake. Overnight in Sary-Oi.

Day 4: Sary-Oi – Bishkek

Your journey today for you to Bishkek. Along the way, you will pass the Burana Tower, whose secrets have not been revealed for centuries. Your guide will introduce you to some mysterious legends. Then you will reach the capital of Kyrgyzstan, which emerged from a caravan station. The town of Bishkek was laid out in the 19th century like a chessboard and is characterized by numerous monuments and classical buildings. You will visit the Historical Museum, Old Square and Manas Square. The end is a visit to the ZUM shopping center, where you will find everything from antiques to fashionable clothing. Stroll to your heart’s content in this shopping center of a different kind. Overnight in Bishkek.

Day 5 Bishkek - Taras (Kazakhstan)

In the morning, your guide will show you the picturesque Osh Bazaar, the most important market in the capital. Here the mood of a real Asian bazaar still prevails. Afterwards, your journey will take you across the Kazakh border to Taras, which already looks back on a history of more than 2000 years. Overnight in Taras.

Day 6 Taras - Turkestan

On your way to Turkestan, you will visit the mausoleum of Aisha Bibi, whose exterior walls are based on the texture of lace. 60 different patterns were carved into the terracotta tiles. Here, in the 11th or 12th century, a noblewoman found her final resting place. Then travel on to Turkestan and visit the Badbadzha Khatun Mausoleum, a museum depot with ancient treasures of the Kazakh people. Arriving in Turkestan, round off the day with a visit to the magnificent Ahmed Yasaviy Mausoleum with colorful outer walls and turquoise dome.

Day 7 Turkestan – Tashkent (Uzbekistan)

From Turkestan, travel to Tashkent, the multicultural capital of Uzbekistan. Along the way, you will visit the ruins of Otrar, a ghost town on the Silk Road. The once flourishing city with turbulent history, along with the Eurasian part of the Silk Road, lost its importance and was finally abandoned. Walking through the ruins, you can find signs of history everywhere. Afterwards, cross the Kazakh-Uzbek border. Your chauffeur will take you directly to your hotel. Tashkent is still shaped by Soviet architecture – and a place where Europe and the Orient merge. Overnight in Tashkent.

Day 8 Tashkent – Samarkand

After breakfast, take the “Sharq Express” to Samarkand. Once there, your guide will take you to the hotel, where you will first get a little fresh and then explore the fabled city on the Silk Road. First you visit the Registanplatz. Enjoy the beautifully decorated and magnificent medres of Ulugbek, Shir Dor and Tillya-Kari standing here. You will continue on your way to the dead city of Shah-I-Zinda, which consists of numerous, finely decorated mausoleums – also the cousin of the Prophet Muhammad is said to be buried here. Your tour will end with the Gur Emir Mausoleum, the final resting place of the famous ruler Tamerlan. A highlight is certainly the bustling Siyob market, a typical oriental trading center. Spend the evening with an Uzbek family and prepare a traditional dinner menu together.

Day 9 Stunning Samarkand

Continue the visit of Samarkand! The second day also has some special sights and places ready for you. First, you’ll see the Ulugbek Observatory, named after Tamerlan’s grandson and famous astronomer. Look for the stars here and be amazed at the means by which the planets could already be determined back then. You will also visit the ancient city of Afrosiab – now an excavation site. Here was discovered in 1912 a palace, which probably belonged to a high official. Afterwards you will see the Khoja Donionor Mausoleum and Khazrati Khizr Mosque. The afternoon is at your disposal. Explore the Oase City on your own. Overnight in Samarkand.

Day 10 Samarkand – Urgut – Tersak – Samarkand

This morning you drive to the Oriental markets of Urgut. Afterwards, the journey continues to the village of Tersak. Here you will meet the family of a teacher and can talk to them – with the help of your guide – about life in Europe and Asia. Your chauffeur will take you back to Samarkand, where you can review the day’s experiences with a leisurely meal in the evening. Overnight in Samarkand.

Day 11 Samarkand – Chess Risabs – Bukhara

Along the old Silk Road you travel to Bukhara today – with a detour to the “green city” of chess risabs. There, your guide will show you the ruins of Ak Saray, the “white palace”. The Kok-Gumbaz Mosque with its beautiful blue dome from 1435 is also an important building and today serves as a Friday mosque. Afterwards you will continue to Bukhara and directly to your hotel. Overnight in Bukhara.

Day 12: Brilliant Bukhara

Today you will get to know the beautiful city of Bukhara and its multitude of attractions. In Bukhara you will experience a comparatively untouched medieval city that is full of life. You will visit, among other things, the impressive Ark fortress of Bukhara in the form of the constellation “Big Wagon” and the Nadir-Devon-Begi-Medrese. The latter was first planned as a caravanserai and offers an unusual mix of styles. Next, your tour will take you to the Mausoleum Chashma Ayub with the Holy Fountain, the Bolo Mosque, the Kalon Mosque and the Miri Arab Medrese. You will also visit the twin medres of Ulugbek and Abdul Aziz Chana as well as the Ismail Samanid Mausoleum. Also worth seeing is the complex Labi Hauz, which invites you to linger: Around the water basin “Hauz” several tea rooms are grouped, in which the Bucharers meet for the game of dominoes. Once, almost all inhabitants of the city were supplied with water with the help of the water basin. The afternoon is at your disposal. Towards evening you will visit a folklore show in the Medrese-Nodir-Devanbegui. Overnight in Bukhara.

Day 13 Around Bukhara

An excursion into the surroundings of Bukhara will first take you to the summer palace of the last emir, Sitorai Mokhi Khosa. After visiting the pond-grown palace complex, visit the Bakhauddin Nakhshbandi ensemble, which includes a shrine for Muslim pilgrims and the mausoleum. Every day pilgrims come to the grave and spend the night on site – a piece of lived Islam comes alive before your eyes. Afterwards, your guide will show you the choir-Bakr-Necropolis before returning to Bukhara. You can use the afternoon for your own explorations. Stroll through the alleys once more on your own and watch the lively activity at Lyabi-Hauz-Platz over a glass of tea. A good tip is also to visit a traditional hammam (not included). Overnight in Bukhara.

Day 14 Bukhara – Farap – Mary (Turkmenistan)

After breakfast, head towards Turkmenistan. Across the border, your new guide awaits you and travel to Mary together. On the way there you will pass the Sundukli Barchans landscape, the Amudarya River and the Karakum Desert. In Mary you will be taken to your hotel immediately. Overnight in Mary.

Day 15 Mary – Merw – Ashgabat

After breakfast, visit Merw. The historic city consists of a succession of ancient cities that developed for over 2,500 years in the heart of an oasis in the Murgab River Delta. In total, the cities covered an area of 1,000 hectares. Since 1999, this site has been a World Heritage Site. Visit everything in peace – then fly on to Ashgabat. Of course, your guide will welcome you there and drive you to the hotel. Overnight in Ashgabat.

Day 16 Ashgabat

Today your guide will show you the capital of Turkmenistan: you will see the Independence Park, the Monument of Neutrality, the earthquake memorial and the Ertogrul Ghazi Mosque. Other highlights include a visit to the National Museum of Turkmenistan and the historic Nisa site, which was declared a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2007. The archaeological remains vividly illustrate the cultural influences from Central Asia and the Mediterranean world. The empire of the Parthians with its capital Nisa was one of the most powerful and influential civilizations of the ancient world and a strong rival to the Romans, preventing the expansion of the Roman Empire towards the east. If you wish, visit the Turkmenbashy Mosque and then return to Ashgabat. Overnight in Ashgabat.

17Ashgabat – Fire Crater Darwaza

Today you will travel to the Karakum Desert, where another very special highlight of Turkmenistan awaits you. Towards evening you reach the fire crater Darwaza. Here you will find the spectacular sight of a 50 to 100 meter cave in the middle of the barren Karakum desert. Geologists discovered a cave filled with natural gas in 1971. So that no toxic methane can escape, the gas was ignited – and still burns today. Overnight in gas crater Darwaza.

Day 18 Kunja Urgentsch – Khiva (Uzbekistan)

On the way to the Uzbek border, stop at Kunja-Urgentsch, a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2005 and located on the edge of the Karakum Desert. For several centuries, Kunja Urgentsch was considered one of the most important trading centers in Central Asia. You will visit, among other things, the Fachriddin Mausoleum, a funeral mosque, and the 62-meter-high minaret Gutluk Temir from the 15th century – still today the highest in Central Asia. The buildings date back to the period of the choresmic Renaissance in the 14th century. Here you will also visit the Dasch-Kala caravanserai and the mausoleum of Il-Arslan, the oldest monument in the city. Then drive over the Turkmen-Uzbek border to Khiva. Overnight in Chiwa.

19th Day Fairytale Chiwa

Today we dedicate ourselves entirely to the ancient city of Khiva, where time seems to stand still. Visit Juma Mosque, Pahlavan Makhmud Mausoleum and Tosh Khovli Palace. Today you will also discover the Shergozi-Khan and Alloquli Khan medres, as well as the Kalta Minor minaret and the Uch Avlya Mausoleum. A special highlight for most Khiva visitors is the dreamlike old town of Ichan Kala, which resembles an open-air museum and fairy tales from 1001 nights. It has been a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1990. Behind every corner and behind every gate, new backdrops open up, be it the courtyards of the mosques, sparkling minarets in the sun, or carved wooden doors or a view into small workshops where the inhabitants still pursue traditional crafts. Chiwa has preserved the fairytale atmosphere of an ancient, oriental city to this day. Overnight in Chiwa.

Day 20 Khiva – Urgench – Tashkent

In the morning you will be taken to Urgentsch airport and fly back to Tashkent. Your guide is already waiting for you at the airport. Use the remaining day again to get to know the capital better. Stroll over the huge Chorsu bazaar and visit the Kukeldash and Barak Khan medres, as well as the Khazrati Imam complex. Other highlights of your tour today: the Medrese Abduraim Sheikh, the Museum of Applied Arts and a visit to the Tamerlane Museum of Tashkent. Overnight in Tashkent.

Day 21 Tashkent

Today, unfortunately, we say goodbye. You will be picked up at your hotel and taken to the airport. With 1001 unforgettable impressions and at least as many photos in your luggage, you will start your journey home.

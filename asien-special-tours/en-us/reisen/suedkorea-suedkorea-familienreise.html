South Korea for the whole family

Between K-Pop-Fever and old palaces: South Korea is the perfect, safe destination for young and old. Ask for a tailored tour now!

Itinerary & services

English itinerary text is an offline machine translation of the German original for this team preview. Please confirm travel details with your advisor; the German source remains available through the language switch.

South Korea is a country full of contrasts and as if made for a family adventure together. Between futuristic metropolises and old royal palaces, K-pop and centuries-old traditions, silent temples and lively markets, you will experience a new facet of the country every day. Again and again, it is also necessary to become active: at the Korean cooking class, at K-pop dancing or during first attempts in taekwondo or archery. But there is also a lot to experience outside the cities. Dense bamboo forests, vast coastal landscapes and historical villages form a varied contrast to the lively city life. Sometimes you take the rail bike along the sea, sometimes high above the rooftops of Seoul or on the tracks of long-gone kingdoms. This creates a family trip that combines culture, nature and shared experiences in a special way and leaves enough space for the moments you will talk about for a long time.

Day 1 begins your family adventure

Welcome to South Korea! After landing, you will be welcomed at the airport by your driver and taken to your hotel in Seoul. Already during the journey, young and old can take their first curious glances at the fascinating metropolis: Shining street trains, modern high-rises and the bustling hustle and bustle awaken the anticipation of the coming days. Arriving at the hotel, it means first of all arriving in peace, taking a breath and letting the first impressions affect you. Maybe there is still some time for a first small stroll through the area before the next morning your family adventure in South Korea really begins. Overnight in Seoul. 

Day 2 Seoul: Between Tradition and Modernity

Today you will get to know the many facets of Seoul. With your guide you will first visit the impressive Gyeongbokgung Palace. Between magnificent halls, imposing gates and expansive courtyards, you will follow in the footsteps of the Korean kings and feel almost as if you are transported into a historical film. In the National Folk Museum, the history of Korea comes alive in a vivid way. Then explore the charming alleys of Samcheong-dong. Small shops, creative boutiques and cozy cafes invite you to discover and linger. In the traditional district of Insadong, you can look for beautiful souvenirs before visiting the Hanok village of Bukchon. Here it is particularly impressive how harmoniously tradition and modernity merge in Seoul. In the afternoon, visit the UNESCO World Heritage-listed Jongmyo Shrine, one of Korea’s most important ancestral shrines. Finally, a special experience awaits you for the whole family at the lively Gwangjang market. Between colorful market stalls and enticing scents, try the popular Korean mung bean pancake Bindaetteok during a street food tasting and discover Korean cuisine in a pleasurable way. Overnight in Seoul. 

Day 3 K-Pop and culture up close

Between royal history, colorful Hanboks and the world of K-pop, a day full of contrasts awaits you today. With your guide you will experience how fascinating tradition and modern pop culture meet in Seoul. In a Hanbok studio you slip into the traditional costume of Korea. Perfectly dressed, you will then go to the professional photo shoot against the backdrop of the Gyeongbokgung Palace. This creates special family photos. After that, you will move from the royal past to modern Seoul. The HiKR Ground inspires a creative world of interactive exhibitions, K-pop and digital media art visitors of all ages. In the trendy district of Hongdae, it becomes active: During a private K-pop dance class, you learn the choreography of a well-known song and try out some moves yourself with lots of fun. Walking through Hongdae’s lively streets, you’ll feel the young, creative energy of the district. In one of the photo studios, it becomes entertaining again in front of the camera and original memory pictures are created. The evening will end in relaxation at a local restaurant. With typical Korean specialties, you can review the experiences of the day. Overnight in Seoul.

Day 4 Seoul to Enjoy and Marvel

During a cooking class, you will get to know the world of Korean cuisine together with other guests. Under the guidance of an experienced chef, you will prepare traditional dishes yourself and learn about typical ingredients. At the following lunch, enjoy your creations. In the futuristic Dongdaemun Design Plaza, the Korean capital shows its creative side. Exceptional architecture and modern design set exciting contrast points to the traditional impressions. Then visit Sungkyunkwan University, whose history dates back to the time of the Joseon Dynasty. In the trendy Gangnam, the COEX Mall hosts the Starfield Library with its meter-high bookshelves. Even higher up is the Lotte World Tower. From the Seoul Sky Observatory, the metropolis of millions is at your feet. To finish, stroll along the picturesque Lake Seokchon or browse the Jamsil Underground Shopping Mall. Overnight in Seoul. 

Our tip (extra charge): Take part in a calligraphy workshop and design your own scroll under the guidance of an experienced calligraphy artist.

Day 5 The Art of Archery and Fascination Jeonju

Goodbye, Seoul! After breakfast, leave with your guide to Suwon. The Hwaseong Fortress begins a journey into Korea’s past. Powerful fortress walls, historic sites and four large gates that lead to all directions tell of Korea’s past. A particularly beautiful photo motif offers the picturesque pavilion Hwahongmun over the watercourse of the fortress. Then you get active yourself: Depending on the weather conditions, you will receive an introduction to Gukgung, the traditional Korean art of archery. You get to know the basic techniques and can then prove your skills yourself. In the afternoon, travel to Jeonju. Between winding alleys and traditional hanok houses, you will get a feel for the Korea of bygone times. On a walking tour, visit Gyeongijeon Shrine and learn about the beginnings of the Joseon Dynasty. In the Hanji Paper Museum, you will learn about traditional Korean papermaking. The Jeondong Cathedral with its special architecture contrasts with the Hanok houses. Finally, the lively Nambu market returns once again between market stalls and cooking kitchens. Overnight in Jeonju.

6th Day Nature Experiences on the way to Yeosu

Today, your journey continues to the south of Korea. In the idyllic Juknokwon Bamboo Park you will walk through dense bamboo groves and enjoy the soothing tranquility of this green oasis. A completely different landscape awaits you a little later on the famous Metasequoia Tree Avenue, whose majestic rows of trees invite you to a relaxing walk. In Suncheon, Korea’s nature shows once again from a new side. In the vast Suncheonman National Garden, colorful flower beds, lovingly designed themed areas and waterscapes alternate. In the nearby Suncheonman Wetland Reserve, wooden walkways lead right into the middle of wide reed landscapes and protected nature. Here you can let the view wander and with a little luck observe the wildlife of the region. Afterwards, a little journey back in time: In the Naganeupseong Folk Village, traditional straw roof houses and winding alleys give a vivid impression of how Korean families lived centuries ago. In the late afternoon you will finally reach the charming coastal town of Yeosu. If you like, the day ends with an optional yacht ride. Overnight in Yeousu.

7th day Rail bike ride and colorful Busan

Today, on the way to Busan, a mix of coastal landscapes, movement and culture awaits you. At the start, it is immediately active: During a rail bike ride, you “bike” along a former railway line at a leisurely pace along the sea. Then visit the impressive Hyangiram Hermitage, located on a cliff. Via stairs and small rock paths you can reach this Buddhist temple, where wide views over the sea and the offshore islands open up. Then continue your family trip to the lively port city of Busan. Upon arrival, explore the colorful Gamcheon Culture Village. Colorful houses, imaginative works of art and winding alleys make the walk a small adventure. How about a family competition for the most original photo motif? To finish, stroll along the famous BIFF Street, known for its connection to the Busan Film Festival. Between street food stands, small shops and the lively hustle and bustle let the atmosphere of the port city affect you. Overnight in Busan.

Day 8 Between Bamboo Forests and Coastal Magic

Today you get to know Busan from its green and maritime side. Your first excursion will take you to the vast, mystical-looking Ahopsan Forest. On shady paths you walk through dense bamboo groves. Then we go out to the coast. With the Haeundae Beach Train you drive directly along the sea, past beaches, small coves and the varied coastal landscape of Busan. On Dongbaekseom there is time for a walk in the fresh sea air before visiting the modern APEC Nurimaru House. After a stop at the UN Peace Memorial Hall, a little thrill awaits at Oryukdo Skywalk: The glass jetty juts out over the cliffs and opens up the view of the deep blue ocean below you. Finally, it will be colorful and surprising: In the Arte Museum Busan, light, sounds and digital art merge into fascinating worlds of experience. Let yourself be enchanted by the artistic stagings. Overnight in Busan.

Our tip (extra charge): As a scented memory, you will create your own personal perfume in a private perfume workshop – refined with the exclusive touch “Haeundae Salt” as a very special souvenir of your time in Busan.

Day 9 In the footsteps of the Silla Kings in Gyeongju

Today your journey will take you to Gyeongju, the former capital of the Silla Empire. Hardly anywhere else does the past of Korea seem as present as here. High up on the mountain, the famous Seokguram Grotto (UNESCO World Heritage Site) awaits you with its impressive Buddha statue. Then visit the Bulguksa Temple, one of the most important temples in the country. The elaborately designed and harmonious complex invites you to stop for a moment and enjoy the tranquility of this place. In Tumuli Park, you will encounter the world of the Silla Kings in an impressive way: The mighty grass-covered burial mounds seem almost like a mysterious hillside landscape. Finally, visit the Gyeongju National Museum. Between precious treasures and ornate finds, many of the impressions of the day come together to form a vivid picture of the once mighty Silla Empire. Overnight in Gyeongju. 

Our tip (for an extra charge): For an extra portion of fun and action, an optional visit to the Gyeongju World theme park, where varied attractions and thrills provide the perfect family change to the cultural program.

10th day Coastal views and royal past – Pohang and Gangneung

Today you will see the east coast of South Korea with an exciting mix of sea, extraordinary architecture and royal past. First, head to Pohang for Homigot Sunrise Square, South Korea’s easternmost point. Here you will marvel at the famous sculpture “Hands of Harmony”, which rises symbolically from the sea, and let the view wander far across the ocean. Afterwards, the Hwanho Park Space Walk offers an architectural experience of a special kind. The futuristic construction winds at airy altitude over the park. Those who are free of dizziness can look forward to a little thrill again here. The journey continues to Yeongwol to the historic Jangneung Tomb. Here you will change from the modern present back to Korea’s royal past. The sprawling complex is one of the royal tombs of the Joseon dynasty and brings to life another chapter in the country’s eventful history. In the afternoon you will finally reach Gangneung. The charming coastal town welcomes you with a relaxed atmosphere and forms the conclusion of an eclectic day between coast, modernity and history. Overnight in Gangneung. 

11th day mountain worlds, temple art and ceramic tradition

Today it goes high up into the impressive mountain world of the Seoraksan National Park. After a walk on Anmok Beach, the cable car will take you up to Gwongeumsanseong Fortress. Already during the journey, the view of the surrounding mountains opens up before you arrive at the top to enjoy the wide panorama. Then visit the Shinheungsa Temple, which blends harmoniously into the wooded slopes of the National Park. In the midst of nature, you will get to know a quieter, spiritual side of Korea and let the peaceful atmosphere of this traditional temple complex affect you. Your journey will take you to Icheon, the center of Korean ceramic art. In the traditional Icheon Pottery Village, you will encounter a craft that has been maintained and handed down here for generations. Between workshops and artfully crafted ceramics, you’ll be able to discover up close the creativity and craftsmanship behind the often-filigree works; in a beautiful conclusion that brings you another facet of Korean culture before returning to Seoul. Overnight in Seoul. 

Day 12: Goodbye, South Korea!

How quickly the past days have vanished! Until your airport transfer, the remaining time is free. Perhaps there is still an opportunity for one last little walk or a special souvenir before it is called to say goodbye. Then your driver will take you to the airport. Behind you are days full of shared discoveries, new perspectives and special experiences between tradition and modernity. This is how your family trip through South Korea comes to an end. However, the memories of this time together will accompany you for a long time and perhaps already awaken the anticipation of the next travel adventure together. Goodbye and see you soon!

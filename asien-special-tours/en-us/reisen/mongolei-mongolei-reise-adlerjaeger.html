Mongolia Travel - Guest with Eagle Hunters and Reindeer Breeders

This idea of Asia Special Tours takes you to the Eagle Hunters and the last real reindeer breeders of Mongolia. This is an inquiry.

Itinerary & services

English itinerary text is an offline machine translation of the German original for this team preview. Please confirm travel details with your advisor; the German source remains available through the language switch.

Endless expanses, spectacular landscapes and absolute freedom. All this awaits you on your journey through a fascinating country that will inspire you with its extraordinary natural beauty and the friendliness of its inhabitants. Leave the hustle and bustle of civilization in favor of solitude in the steppes and expanses and get fascinating insights into the simple but nature-loving nomadic life. They travel by land to the almost untouched west and thus cross a large part of Mongolia. The Altai Mountains are among the most adventurous areas of the country that you can explore by jeep. Enjoy the endless expanses of Mongolia’s “Wild West” and enjoy exciting encounters with eagle hunters, shamans and reindeer breeders. Please prepare for a very simple infrastructure, which will be compensated with many beautiful experiences and breathtaking views.

1st day Arrival in Ulaanbaatar

Welcome to Ulaanbaatar, the capital of Mongolia. Your guide greets you at the airport and first takes you to your hotel. You then begin your sightseeing at Sukhbaatar Square in the heart of the city. Two impressive monuments commemorate the conqueror Genghis Khan and the revolutionary leader Damddiny Sukhbaatar, who secured independence from China in 1921. Gandan Monastery is also worth a visit. Built in the Tibetan style in 1835, it soon became a center of Buddhist learning in Mongolia. During the 20th century it played a special role: having survived the communist destruction of the 30s as one of the few undamaged monasteries, it was allowed to resume limited operations in 1944 as the only monastery in the country. Depending on the time available, also visit the Museum of History and Ethnography and the Winter Palace of Bogd Khan. Overnight in Ulaanbaatar.

2nd Day Fascinating Khogno Khan Mountains

Today begins your great overland journey. Your current destination, the Khogno Khan Nature Reserve, is located in the heart of Mongolia, directly on the main road Ulaanbaatar – Karakorum. Here you can experience very different nature zones – from mountains of granite to dune landscapes – which makes the area special. After arriving, there is enough time for a nice hike such as the sand dunes Elsen Tasarkhai, which are very close by. Here you also have the opportunity to rent camels and explore the sand dunes with the "desert ships" (for a fee). You spend the night for the first time in a yurt camp and can admire the infinite starry sky above you. Overnight in the Khogno Khan Mountains.

3rd day volcanic crater Khorgo and the "White Lake"

The journey continues to the crater of the extinct volcano Khorgo and the lake Terkhin Zagaan Nuur, which is 2,600 meters above sea level. The charming environment is perfect for hiking to your heart’s content. It is only about half an hour walk past cold, bizarre lava formations until you can look into the huge crater of the Khorgo. Finally, you will be taken to your yurt camp for the night. Overnight in Terkhin Zagaan Nuur.

Day 4: The path is the goal

Today is a day of travel ahead of you, as you head towards the Altai area. Even if you will be driving in the car for a long time today, you can enjoy the beautiful scenery on the way. You will cross rivers and passes and reach Uliastai city in the evening. Here you will stay for a change in a hotel. The city is about 1,100 kilometers from Ulaanbaatar and lies in the valley where the Chigestei and Bogd rivers converge. Still in 2010, the city with 24,276 inhabitants was one of the 10 most populous cities in Mongolia, but the number of inhabitants dropped enormously since then. Overnight in Uliastai.

Day 5 Mongol Els - Sand dunes as far as the eye can see

In the morning you will drive to the dunes of Mongol Els, which are among the largest dunes in the world. Their foothills reach as far as Russia! Here you take a nice walk. From up here, you have a fantastic view of Lake Ereen Nuur, which lies in the middle of the dunes. Since it is quite swampy, caution should be taken when walking there. Overnight in Ereen Nuur.

Day 6 - Continue west

Today you will continue to approach the Altai area. Here the landscape appears ever wilder. You will reach the large Khar Us Lake ("Black Water Lake"), which is 1,157 m above sea level and home to many bird species. With a width of 26 kilometers and a length of 72 kilometers at its longest point, the lake is the second largest freshwater lake in Mongolia. Take a nice walk along the shore and enjoy the beauty of nature. In the evening they will pitch their tents on the lakeside. Overnight at Khar Us Lake.

Day 7: The Sacred Mount Tsambagaraw

Today’s destination is the sacred mountain Tsambagaraw, one of the most important and impressive mountains in Mongolia. The glacier mountain is 4,195 meters high and covered with eternal snow. If you wish, you can hike here a little to enjoy the beautiful view of the summit. There are also many ethnic minorities in this area, including Uriankhais and Kazakhs. Overnight in Tsambagaraw.

Day 8 Into the highest province of Mongolia

Today you will travel to the westernmost province of Bayan-Ulgii, the highest province in Mongolia bordering both Russia and China. This region is inhabited by the minority of Kazakh nomads. These have preserved their traditions to this day and still live in the characteristic, richly decorated yurts. Hardly anywhere else has Kazakh culture been as well preserved as in the remote west of Mongolia. Finally, you reach the Sagsai River where you pitch your tent for the night. Overnight in Sagsai-Sum.

Day 9 Visiting the Eagle Hunters

The ride now takes you to the western area of Altai Tavan Bogd National Park and to the beautiful crystal clear mountain lakes of Khurgan and Khoton, which are connected. To reach this region, you will have an impressive highland crossing with breathtaking views. Tonight is spent with a Kazakh nomadic family on the edge of Lake Khoton. This, like Lake Khurgan, is fed by many rivers and streams that originate in the Altai Mountains. The landscape around the lakes is fantastically beautiful and invites you to hikes with the picturesque backdrop of the snow-capped mountains, green meadows on their foothills and the larch forests. Tavan Bogd National Park protects a variety of plants and rare animals in this desert and mountain wilderness. Here you also have the opportunity to visit an eagle hunter family. The season of eagle hunting begins in autumn with the first snow. Nevertheless, you can talk to the eagle hunters and learn a lot of interesting things about their lives. Overnight in Khoton/Khurgan Lakes.

Day 10 with the shamans

You will travel via high passes and beautiful landscapes to the sacred Mount Shiveet Khairkhan, which is located in the upper valley of Tsagaan Gol in the northwestern part of the Mongolia-Altai Mountains. It is surrounded by archaeological monuments dating from the Bronze Age (early third millennium BC) to Turkic (6th-9th century). C.E.C.). Here you have time for a small hike. Then visit a Tuwa family. The Tuwa are among the Turkic ethnic groups and still practice a form of shamanism today. However, shamanism is changing drastically through contact with Western esoteric neoshamans. The intensive exchange with this new stream of shamanism will possibly falsify and overlay the original traditions of this people. Nevertheless, the visit to the shamans is a special and unique experience that will remain in your memory for a long time. Overnight in Altai Tavan Bogd.

Day 11 - Uureg Nuur Lake

Today you travel on to the Uureg Nuur Lake. The round, drainless lake has a diameter of 18 km and lies between the Kharkhiraa massif and the Zagaan Schuwuut uul. You will spend the night on the south side in a yurt camp. Overnight at Uureg Nuur Lake.

Day 12 Oasis in the middle of the desert: Lake Khyargas

A new day, a new goal. Your journey will now take you to Lake Khyargas, which lies in the middle of a climatically extreme desert landscape. Along the way, take a look at Lake Uws, the largest lake in Mongolia. It is located in the northernmost mountain sink of Central Asia and is up to 20 meters deep. The water of the lake is slightly salty, very clear and rich in fish. Once you have arrived at Lake Khyargas, there are opportunities for beautiful walks. Overnight at Khyargas Lake.

13th day time for beautiful walks

The landscape of Lake Khyargas is simply too beautiful to go further. Therefore, you can use today to further explore the dreamlike surroundings. Overnight at Khyargas Lake.

Day 14 - Small village of Tosontsengel

Between Altai and Khangaigebirge lies the large Mongolian basin. You will travel through an interesting region, which has hardly touched touristic so far and is only sparsely populated. Your destination is the small village of Tosontsengel, which is picturesquely located in a plain between mountains in the immediate vicinity of the Ider River. With a temperature of minus 45 degrees in winter, the village is one of the coldest places in the country. Overnight in Tosontsengel.

15th day drive to Khuvsgul Lake

After breakfast, make your way to Lake Khuvsgul, the most beautiful lake in all of Mongolia. Due to its size (136 kilometers long, 20 to 40 meters wide and up to 262 meters deep), it is also one of the most important freshwater reservoirs in the country. Surrounded by many mountain ranges and mountain ranges, it offers a dreamlike backdrop for walks. Overnight at Khuvsgul Lake.

Day 16 Insight into the Life of Reindeer Breeders

Today you have the opportunity to visit the reindeer nomads. The unique nomads specialized in the breeding of reindeer centuries ago and live like their ancestors today, but also from the animals. The domesticated animals are kept like pets and enable people to live through their milk, meat and warm fur. From your yurt camp it is only a few kilometers to the summer camp of reindeer breeders, which you can walk. Overnight at Khuvsgul Lake.

17th day of free time at Khuvsgul Lake

This day is freely available to you. Take the opportunity to explore the surroundings of the lake on your own. Parts of the Hovsgol National Park have been declared strictly protected areas, as you can still find the endangered snow leopard here, as well as its favorite prey, Argalsh and Asian Capricorn, which are also threatened with extinction. The park is also excellent for bird watching, as there are around 245 different bird species here. The plant world is also surprisingly diverse and the lake and rivers are popular fishing areas, including Baikal Omul. Today you have ample opportunity to hike on the lake and in the taiga forest, as well as to beautiful boat trips (for a fee). Overnight at Khuvsgul Lake.

18th day drive to Bulgan Province

Today you will leave the beautiful Khuvsgul Lake and continue to the province of Bulgan. The landscape of the province is a mixture of dry grass steppe in the south, extensive coniferous forests in the north and grain and vegetable growing areas in the fertile river valleys of Selenge. The mountain range of the Burengiin Nuruu, which is up to 2,058 meters high, divides the province into two parts: in the northern, wooded part live numerous wild animals such as moose, bears, musk, deer and marmots; in the lush grasslands of the southern part and on the forest edges grows a large variety of wild flowers, berries and medicinal herbs. The fertile pastures are also ideal for livestock breeding and more than one million animals feed on them. Bulgan Province is particularly known nationwide for the best fermented mare milk (Airag) and the tastiest wild berries. Absolutely try! Overnight in Bulgan Province.

19th day Monastery Amarbyasgalant

The last stage of your round trip through Mongolia will take you to Amarbayasgalant today. Amarbayasgalant is known for its monastery, which is one of the three largest monastic complexes in Mongolia. Overnight in Amarbayasgalant.

Day 20 Return to Ulaanbaatar

This morning you will visit the Monastery of Amarbayasgalant. The more than 40 temples built in the early 18th century were once a center of Buddhism and many monks made pilgrimage here to marvel at the complex. The partially destroyed monastery still offers many monks a home today and was named a World Heritage Site in 1996. Then make your way back to Ulaanbaatar, where you will arrive in the evening. Overnight in Ulaanbaatar.

Day 21 Farewell to Mongolia

Today your trip to Mongolia comes to an end and it means saying goodbye to this fascinating country. Surely you have packed your suitcase with many nine impressions. Your driver will take you to Ulaanbaatar airport, from where you will board your return flight.

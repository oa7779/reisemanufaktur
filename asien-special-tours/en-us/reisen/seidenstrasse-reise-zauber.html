Iran Journey – Magic of the Orient

Iran Travel - Experience the magic of the Orient on your individual Iran journey with Asia Special Tours. Ask here and experience the Silk Road for yourself.

Itinerary & services

English itinerary text is an offline machine translation of the German original for this team preview. Please confirm travel details with your advisor; the German source remains available through the language switch.

Welcome to Iran – one of the most fascinating destinations in the fairytale Orient. Let yourself be enchanted by a country that is struggling with many prejudices and of which you do not know much. On your round trip you will see magnificent mosques, impressive archaeological sites from several centuries and authentic insights into a foreign culture. The unique hospitality of the Iranians and the contact with the locals will delight you! But not only the cultural highlights await you, but also unique and atmospheric accommodation. Whether under an open starry sky in a Bedouin tent, in a traditional caravanserai along the Silk Road or in the tasteful boutique hotels - everywhere you feel the magic of the country. Immerse yourself in the fairy tale from 1001 night and look forward to indelible impressions that Iran will leave with you!

1st day Arrival in Tehran

Salam and welcome to the charming Iran. Upon your arrival in Tehran, you will be expected by your guide and then driven to your hotel where you can relax from your flight or explore the area a little on your own. Overnight in Tehran.

2nd Day Unique Museum Collection in Tehran

The vibrant metropolis is awakening! After breakfast, you and your personal guide will embark on your first exploration of the city. Good starting points for visiting Tehran are the Carpet Museum and the National Museum. You will start with the National Museum of Iran, which has the largest collection of artifacts in Persia and one of the most important collections of historical objects in the world, with over 300,000 ancient objects. The Carpet Museum lets you share in the magic of 1001 nights. Afterwards, stroll over the Great Bazaar and immerse yourself in the colorful hustle and bustle. With a length of about 10 kilometers and 30,000 shops and workshops, the Grand Bazaar is one of the largest in the world – almost a city within a city. In the evening, a special highlight awaits you: from the TV tower Borj-e Milad you have a fantastic view over the city. At 435 meters, it is the highest tower in the country and the sixth tallest television tower in the world. In the revolving restaurant of the Milad Tower, you can taste Persian delicacies while the city is at your feet. The ideal ending for this day. Overnight in Tehran

3rd day Flight to Qeshm Island

Let the day go on comfortably and enjoy your breakfast before you continue on a sightseeing tour. A visit to Saadabad Palace, the first summer residence of the Qajarian kings, allows you to share in the Oriental flair. The extensive complex comprises 18 palaces: the white and green palaces are particularly worth seeing. The white palace is the largest with 54 rooms and 5000 square meters. In the green palace you can still admire the noble equipment of the Shah period. Depending on the remaining time, it is also worth a visit to the Niavaran Palace. Mohammed Reza Shah built it in the 60s in a modern style. Afterwards you will be taken to the airport and fly to the island of Qeshm on the Persian Gulf. Once there, you will be picked up and driven to your hotel. Overnight stay in Qeshm

4th day Forests, caves and coasts on the island of Qeshm

The island of Qeshm is the largest island in the Persian Gulf and is especially worth seeing due to its natural beauty. The main town is the port city of Qeshm, which belongs to the Qeshm Free Trade Zone. In ancient times, the island was known as Alexandria. Due to their strategically important location, many empires later fought for control of Qeshm, such as the empire of Elam, the Umayyads, Abbasids, British and Portuguese. The western part of the island is largely part of the Qeshm Geopark, the only one in Iran. Today your guide will show you the most beautiful sides of the island and take you to the Kharbas caves, the Hara mangrove forest, the Portuguese fort and the turtle coast, where the protected loggerhead turtles lay their eggs in March. Then return to your hotel. Overnight stay in Qeshm

5th day boat trip to the rock island of Hormoz

Today you will explore the pretty island of Qeshm with your guide a little further, then you will take the boat to the rocky island of Hormoz 18 kilometers east. Particularly worth seeing here are the multicolored rocky landscapes with the red iron oxide and salt deposits or the ruins of the former Portuguese fortress on the northern tip. Later in the day, the boat will take you back to Qeshm Island. Overnight stay in Qeshm

Day 6: Onward journey to Shiraz and first explorations

From the island of Qeshm it goes today by plane back to the mainland, more precisely to Shiraz. With Shiraz, many people associate two things: it is the city of poets and the city of gardens and parks. You will start your visits with a walk to the impressive Karim Khan fortress, in the center of the city. Afterwards, visit the garden area with the grave mausoleum of Hafez, a well-known Iranian poet, and especially happy and unhappy lovers visit the grave of the poet. Incidentally, Goethe also quoted some verses of Hafez in his work “West-Eastern Divan”. About 2.5 kilometers from the Hafez Mausoleum, the Saadi Mausoleum stands at the end of Bolvar-e Bustan in a garden complex. Saadi was a scholar who joined the Suhrawardi Sufi Order and made long trips abroad before dying in Shiraz in 1292. Built in his honor, the modern overcoupled tomb with a high canopy supported by eight pillars was only built in 1952. Overnight stay in Shiraz

Day 7 City of Roses and Nightingales

After breakfast, visit the Vakil Karim Khan Zand residence building. The Vakil complex with the Vakil Mosque and the Vakil Bazaar is one of the most famous urban complexes of Islamic architecture in Iran. The domed Vakil bazaar is one of the most beautiful in the country and still offers a rich range of spices, textiles and crafts. Here you can practice wonderful haggling. The visit to the famous Nasir-al-Molk Mosque is also on the program of your visit today. With a stroll through the famous Delgosha Paradise Garden and a visit to Khajuy-e Kermani, from where you have a beautiful view of Shiraz, you can end the day perfectly. Overnight stay in Shiraz

Day 8 Persepolis & the Rock Tombs of Naqsh-e-Rostam

Today you will take a trip to the Old Persian Persian (UNESCO World Heritage Site), the ancient Achaemenid capital of the Persian Empire, first built in 520 BC by Darius I. Even today, the ruins of the former palace city bear witness to its former splendor. After an extensive visit, head to Naqsh-e-Rostam and visit the necropolis of the Achaemenids, which can be seen in the form of rock tombs on the steep mountain slope. Of course, you will also visit the impressive rock reliefs of Naqsh-e-Rajab. Then we continue on to Mehriz. In the afternoon you will reach your day’s destination and immediately embark on a small city tour that will take you to the Bagh-e Palevanpur Garden, which is a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Finally, you will reach the Zein-o-Din caravanserai located in the middle of the desert. Feel transported back to the time of the caravans, when they moved with precious goods through the desert and night in the caravanserai along the Silk Road. Enjoy the silence under the starry sky. They stay in semi-common rooms. Individual cabins can be separated by curtains. The toilets and washing facilities shall be used jointly. Overnight in Mehriz

9th Day City Tour in Yazd

In the morning, continue your journey to Yazd, one of Iran’s oldest cities at the foot of the 4000-meter-high Shir Kuh Mountain. Surrounded by two large deserts of the country (Dasht-e Kavir and Kavir-e Lut) and built in traditional clay construction, the city will surely delight you. Here you will first visit the important fire temple of the Zoroastrians, whose open flame has been kept burning continuously for more than 100 years. Afterwards, your sightseeing tour will take you to the Jameh Mosque (Friday Mosque), the Amir Chakhmaq Complex and the “Towers of Silence”, also called Dakhmeh. From the minaret of this complex, at sunset you have a particularly beautiful view of this fascinating mud brick town. You should not miss a visit to a Zurkaneh, a so-called “powerhouse”. Here, the Iranian men practice in traditional martial arts exercises. Among other things, heavy wooden clubs are used as devices and the exercises are partly combined with dance-like inserts. Overnight in Yazd

Day 10 Via Meybod to Isfahan

A new day, a new goal. Along the desert edge, we continue towards Isfahan. Along the way, visit Chak Chak, a sacred pilgrimage site of the Zoroastrians. It lies in the solitude of the desert on a mountain slope, where a spring makes itself felt by dripping sounds – Chak Chak. Some of the spring water runs through the modern fire temple, where Zoroastrians from around the world meet in mid-June. Only a few tourists find the way here, which is worthwhile because of the silence and the landscape. In addition, visit the old village of Kharanagh. Arriving in Meybod, visit the Narin Castle. Then you finally reach the magical Isfahan, another highlight of your journey. Here you can still see two of the most impressive bridges in the city: the Si-o-se Pol or Pol-e Allahverdi Khan Bridge, as well as the much admired Khaju Bridge with its 23 arches, built around 1650, where thanks to its two storeys you can cross the river on two levels. The river with its bridges is a very popular meeting point in the evening. Let the day end relaxed and mix with the sociable locals. Overnight in Isfahan

11th day Cultural metropolis of Isfahan

To call Isfahan a wonder of the world is no exaggeration. The city, which is thought to have been founded in the days of Tahmourass and Keykavous, is full of splendor and culture. Your visit to the cultural metropolis starts best at Imam Khomeini Square. It is the second largest place in the world – after Tiananmen Square in Beijing. Around him you can see the imposing Imam Mosque, the Sheikh Lotfollah Mosque (also called Women's Mosque, since ladies were also allowed) as well as the palaces Chehel Setoon and Alighapoo. The name of the latter means "High Gate", because the palace forms the entrance to the palace district, which lies west of the square. The palaces are surrounded by one of the most beautiful, oldest and oriental bazaars in Iran with interesting handicrafts and objects of art. Paradise is found in the garden of Hasht-Behesht Palace – the name of the palace refers to the Paradise Garden and is open to the public. Afterwards you will see two of the most impressive bridges in the city: the Si-o-se Pol Bridge, as well as the much admired Khaju Bridge with its 23 arches built around 1650. Let the day end relaxed and enjoy the cultural metropolis. Overnight in Isfahan

Day 12: Explore Isfahan

Today you will visit the historic Friday Mosque, a 1,000-year-old association of pre- and Islamic architecture and art, the Christian Armenian Vank Cathedral in the district of Djolfa built by Shah Abbas the Great for Armenian settlers. Then, south of the river, see the fire temple of the small Zarathustic community, which has never recovered from the persecutions under Shah Soltan Hoseyn in the early 18th century and currently counts less than 500 members. Overnight in Isfahan

Day 13 - Go to the desert!

Early in the morning you leave for the desert. In the Zagros Mountains, you will walk with a local guide to your current day’s destination: a Bedouin village. There you move into your night camp and taste various Iranian treats together with the Bedouins. Overnight in Koohrang.
Note: Please note that the overnight stay is very easy. We recommend you take warm clothes with you, as it can get very cold at night.

Day 14 Visiting Abyaneh

After a leisurely breakfast with the Bedouins, continue your journey to Abyaneh. Here the red-brown mud brick houses of the village nestle on a slope. During a walk you can hear the villagers speak an ancient Persian dialect, and a small mosque with a blue dome invites you to visit. In the vicinity of the village there are some hiking trails that give a beautiful view of the village panorama. Overnight stay in Abyaneh

15th day Kashan City Tour

We will head to the final destination of your trip to Iran. You will drive to Kashan, a city on the desert edge of the large salt lake. In no other Iranian city is there such a high density of citizen houses. Because Kashan was a city of rich merchants thanks to the carpet trade, rose water and ceramics production. Their spacious construction and the rich equipment of the rooms are absolutely worth seeing and give an insight into the life of the Iranian bourgeoisie. The Tabatabai house you are looking at today is a spacious Qajaren-era property that includes several residential buildings and wide courtyards equipped with water basins. Also court Ivane and own Hamams belong to the property. Afterwards, take a look at the Fin Garden, where you’ll feel the magic of Iran’s oldest surviving garden as you stroll along the water basins, flowers and shrubs. Overnight in Kashan

16th day Departure from Iran

On your journey back to Tehran, you have some time to stop in Qom. In the metropolis of Shia Islam, you will have the opportunity to feel the special atmosphere in the sanctuary Fatima Masumeh. She belonged to the prophet family and the tomb, which was erected in her honor from 819 onwards, has been further expanded over the years, as well as lined with tiles and mirror mosaics. Back in Tehran, unfortunately, it is time to say goodbye to this fascinating country. They are taken to the airport and fly back home with 1001 new impressions in their luggage. The memories and photos of your exciting time in Iran will bring you a lot of joy in everyday life at home for a long time.
Note: Non-Muslims are not allowed to enter the inside of the shrine in Qom, only outside visit is possible.

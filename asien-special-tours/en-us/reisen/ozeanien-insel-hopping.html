Island Hopping - Pure Joy of Life on the Fiji Islands

Fiji Journey: Experience pure joy of life with your island hopping on the Fiji Islands with Asia Special Tours. Ask and enjoy them.

“Bula” (Welcome) to the Fiji Islands. Fiji is an archipelago with over 300 islands and fulfills all your South Sea dreams. The main island of Viti Levu offers all the modern amenities for your island stay and has many highlights ready for you. True South Sea feeling, however, can be found on the offshore archipelagos of the Mamanucas and Yasawas: white beaches, turquoise lagoons and wavering palm trees. Here you will quickly find relaxation in water sports activities such as snorkeling, diving and surfing. Or you can enjoy the sweet doing nothing on the beach. But it’s not just the dream beaches that are addicted to Fiji. It is also the unique happiness of the Fijians, coupled with their warm hospitality. Experience the powerful culture of the Melanesians, which consists of singing, dancing and pure joie de vivre - it's worth it!

1st day Arrival in Viti Levu with VIP Express Service

Bula! Today you reach Nadi, the international airport of the main island of Viti Levu, and enjoy our VIP Express service. Our on-site staff will be waiting for you directly upon leaving the plane and bypassing the regular entry through a separate accelerated entry counter. This saves you unnecessary waiting times and get to your dream island faster. Afterwards, a private transfer will take you directly to the ferry, with which you will be traveling for about 1.5 hours to your island resort. Alternatively, we can arrange a helicopter transfer: a unique opportunity to experience the Pacific island world of Fiji from the air. Overnight on Matamanoa Island.

2.-4. Day stay Mamanuca Islands

The small island of Matamanoa is located 30 km west of Nadi in the group of Mamanucas. The almost deserted white beaches and the crystal clear water with a front coral reef make the very personally managed resort complex a real idyll. In the middle of tropical surroundings, the complex has 13 rooms and 20 Boers (bungalows) as well as restaurant, swimming pool, umbrellas and beach loungers. Includes: windsurfing, sailing, catamaran, snorkelling, kayaking, tennis, beach volleyball, boccia, table tennis. In addition, for a fee: boat trips, diving, deep sea fishing, massage. Children aged 16 and over are welcome. Overnight on Matamanoa Island.

Day 5 - Ship to the Yasawa Islands

Today, your speedboat picks you up directly from Matamanoa and takes you to neighboring Beachcomber Island. From there, take the ferry to Naukacuvu Island for your next island stay at Paradise Cove Resort. So you save yourself the return trip to the main island and enjoy the Pacific island world only from the boat. Overnight on Yasawa Island.

6.-7. Day stay Yasawa Islands

Paradise Cove is a 5-star resort in the Yasawa Islands, beautifully nestled in a dreamlike bay on the island of Naukacuvu. You are surrounded by fine sand and crystal clear sea just steps from your spacious villa. Enjoy the resort, the beach, three swimming pools, miles of sand, two bars and fantastic food. Discover the cultural interaction with the nearby village, explore our beautiful reefs and snorkel with the Giant Manta Ray's. Our Padi dive shop can show you a variety of great dive sites or just relax on our quiet island with a full day spa, where time is determined only by the level of the sun and the sound of the waves. Overnight at Yasawa Island

8th day return or onward journey from Viti Levu

Today, the ferry will take you to the main island of Viti Levu and a private transfer awaits you at the port to take you back to Nadi International Airport. Sit back on the plane and let the past days pass mentally. These memories will accompany you throughout your life.

From Kuala Lumpur to Lombok - Between Tiger States and Tropical Paradise

Indonesia Malaysia Round Trip: Travel from Kuala Lumpur to Lombok and discover tiger states and tropical paradises with Asia Special Tours.

An exciting journey full of contrasts is waiting for you. At the beginning of your trip, you will get to know the vibrant metropolises of Kuala Lumpur and Singapore and let yourself be driven by the rhythm of these world cities. In the historic city of Malacca, you will get a taste of colonial history and be pampered with culinary delights. On the still very original island of Lombok you will experience the bustle of the markets and feel the mystical tranquility in Indonesia’s traditional temple complexes. At the end of the trip you can really relax on the enchanting Gili Islands, because the islands are certainly still among the insider tips for all those seeking rest. Look forward to a tropical paradise in small format!

1st day Arrival in Kuala Lumpur

Welcome to Kuala Lumpur. Upon arrival, you will be expected by your guide and taken to your hotel. Relax a little or go on a first exploration tour. In the evening there will be a great start to your trip. You will visit the famous Petronas Twin Towers and head to the “Skybridge” and observation platform. This tallest two-storey bridge, which connects the two towers on the 41st and 42nd floors, offers a great view of the capital of Malaysia. After an atmospheric light show at KLCC Park, a delicious dinner awaits you at Tamarind Hill, where you can relax in an elegant ambience. You will then be returned to your hotel. Overnight in Kuala Lumpur.

2nd day experience Kuala Lumpur up close

After breakfast, you will explore the city like a “local” with public transport together with your guide and experience Kuala Lumpur up close. You will visit Kampung Baru, the oldest quarter of the city, where the traditional wooden houses can still be seen today. After visiting a market where merchants mainly advertise meat, fish, vegetables and fruits, you will fortify yourself in a traditional restaurant with a delicious Curry Laksa, a spicy noodle soup. In the afternoon, take the tram to the historic center of the city, where you can see buildings from the late 19th century. In the bustling Chinatown, you will learn more about the Chinese community and its culinary influences on Malay cuisine. In a 100-year-old restaurant, you can taste a soothing Chinese green tea. The day full of treats is coming to an end and it goes back to the hotel, where you can end the evening relaxed. Overnight in Kuala Lumpur.

Day 3 Through the Malay Colonial City

Today you will leave the capital and continue to Malacca on the west coast. The churches, squares and fortifications testify to the eventful history of the city. Together with your guide, you will embark on a very individual exploration tour. Special highlights are the central Red Square with Christ Church and the bell tower. You will also walk past the ruins of St. Paul’s Church and Porta de Santiago – the gateway to the fortress La Formosa, built by the Portuguese in 1511. Just a stone's throw apart, you can see the representatives of the three main religions of Malaysia: a church, a Buddhist Taoist temple and a Hindu temple. Appropriately, they are on Harmony Street. Strolling across Jonker Street, you’ll see all kinds of souvenirs – from real bargains to high-priced goods. The Baba Nyonya Heritage Museum, located in the former residence of an extended Chinese family, offers exciting insights into the living and living conditions of the past. At the end of the day, enjoy a relaxing river cruise that offers you new perspectives on the historic city and rounds off the day. Overnight in Malacca.

Day 4 - Go to Singapore

In the morning we will continue to Singapore, where you will be taken to your hotel. Welcome to this dazzling metropolis. The city is a melting pot that combines in particular cultural influences from Chinese, Indian, British and Malay culture. The modern city-state developed rapidly into a flourishing industrial nation in the second half of the 20th century. In the evening, together with your guide, you will make a first city tour. Pass the Financial District and drive to Chinatown. The city-state’s largest ethnic group settled first here, yet in the middle of Chinatown there is one of the oldest Hindu temples in the city. Then stroll to Lau Pa Sat, the historic Telok Ayer Market – an architectural jewel in the heart of the city. In this impressive building, experience Singapore’s diverse food culture at its best: try your hand at local specialties and enjoy an authentic dinner amidst the bustling ambience. Overnight in Singapore.

Day 5 - Discover Singapore's highlights

Today we continue with the sightseeings of this impressive city. Drive through the Civic District and pass landmarks such as the Padang, the Cricket Club, the historic Parliament Building, and the City Hall and Supreme Court. A photo stop at Merlion Park will give you great views of Marina Bay and Singapore’s landmark – the Merlion, a mythical creature with lion’s head and fish body. Continue to the National Orchid Garden in the Botanic Gardens – Singapore’s “green lung” and UNESCO World Heritage Site. Admire the colorful variety of orchids in the midst of tropical gardens. In Little India, immerse yourself in the vibrant flair of this colorful neighborhood before crowning the tour with a visit to Sands Sky Park on Marina Bay Sands – an architectural masterpiece with spectacular panoramic views of the city. After returning to the hotel, you have free time and can get a little fresh. In the late afternoon, visit the futuristic Gardens by the Bay. In the air-conditioned glass halls, you can marvel at Mediterranean plant landscapes and cloud forest tropical vegetation as well as the highest indoor waterfall in the world. Finally, the impressive light and sound show “Garden Rhapsody” awaits you at the famous Supertrees – an unforgettable experience under the night skies of Singapore. Overnight in Singapore.

6th day discover Singapore on your own

Today is your free day. Explore Singapore at your own pace—whether it’s shopping along Orchard Road, visiting the National Museum, or taking a culinary discovery tour of the city’s Hawker centers. A trip to the offshore island of Sentosa or a walk on the Singapore River are also worthwhile. Overnight in Singapore.

7th day onwards to Indonesia

Today we say goodbye to Singapore. You will be picked up from your hotel and fly on to Lombok, where you are already expected by your guide. Afterwards, you will continue by car to your hotel in Sire Beach. What a contrast to the last lively days in Singapore! Enjoy the tranquility on Lombok now and take an extensive walk on the beach. Overnight in Lombok.

Day 8: The Original Lombok

After breakfast, drive to Tetebatu, located in the south of Rinjani National Park. Here you get to know the everyday life in a typical village and take sightseeing tours through the fruit and vegetable plantations. A short stop at Monkey Forest awaits you, where with a bit of luck you will see the active primates. In addition, a visit to the Tibu Topat waterfall is on your program. A local lunch will be served at a local restaurant in Tetebatu. In the afternoon you will dive further into the still very original and crafted Lombok. Each of the villages has specialized in a craft. First, drive to Loyok, the place where bamboo is processed. Then the tour continues to Masbagik, the place of pottery. Full of impressions and experiences, it goes back to the hotel, where you can end the day very relaxed. Overnight in Lombok.

Day 9 Lomboks Treasures

In the morning, visit Kebon Roek Market. Here you can feel the life of Lombok! Observe the locals who go about their daily activities. Between vegetables, spices, fish, meat and small snacks, there are also small shops where real treasures are offered! Afterwards, visit the Lingsar Temple on the outskirts of Mataram, where you can discover the fascinating syncretism between Balinese Hinduism and Lomboks Waktu Telu. Built in 1714 by King Anak Agung Ngurah, the temple complex includes shrines connected to the sacred mountains of both islands. Another highlight is the sacred eel in the watering system of holy water sources; try to attract it with a boiled egg. The appearance of the eel is considered particularly auspicious. At the Tengarra Museum, you will learn more about Lombok’s history. A local will guide you through the museum and answer all questions. In the afternoon, stop at the Mayura Water Palace, a historic site of the former Karangasem Kingdom. Then return to your hotel. Overnight in Lombok.

Day 10 - Boat to the Gili Islands

The tropical paradise is waiting for you! After an extensive breakfast, you will be picked up from the hotel and drive along the coast to the boat dock. After about a 30-minute boat ride, you head to the first island. What a backdrop! Gili Nanggu is surrounded by crystal clear, turquoise waters, white sand beaches and tropical coconut trees. Literally immerse yourself in the colorful underwater world and enjoy the refreshing snorkeling excursion. On the island of Gili Sudak, a culinary treat awaits you. In a simple restaurant there is a freshly caught grilled fish, plus a magnificent panorama of this enchanting island world. Relax on the beach or walk through the white sand before heading to Gili Meno. Once there, a Balinese horse-drawn carriage (Cidomo) will take you to your beautiful resort. Here you can swim in the turquoise blue water and lie in the sun on untouched beaches. Overnight in Gili Meno.

11.-14. Day Day Relaxation on the Gilis

Enjoy the island paradise and feel like Robinson Crusoe! On the white sand beach drink a coconut, snorkel, bathe and relax. Do what you want to do. A great experience is the sunset over the neighboring island of Bali. Overnight on the island of Gili Meno.

15th day Departure from the island paradise

Your time on the island is coming to an end. In the morning, take a motorboat to Teluk Kode, Lombok (duration about 1 hour). Here you will be met by your chauffeur and taken to Lombok airport. Even if your holiday is over – the precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories from your trip will accompany you forever. Look forward to letting your friends and family share your experiences in the distance at home.

Between jungle and beach paradise - The natural treasures of southern Thailand

Thailand Nature Tour: Explore Southern Thailand’s natural treasures with your Thailand Expert Asia Special Tours. Ask now and enjoy!

On this fantastic round trip through the south of Thailand with a bathing stay in Khao Lak or Phuket, we present the country at its most beautiful and show you why southern Thailand is considered a holiday paradise. Your tour of Thailand through the south of the country begins in vibrant Bangkok, which also bears the nickname “Venice of the East”. Here you will discover exciting sights, such as the Royal Palace and the fantastic Emerald Buddha Temple, before exploring the Thai metropolis by bicycle. Afterwards, head to River Kwai, where you’ll stay in a floating hotel and visit Erawan National Park. The tour of Thailand will then take you via Hua Hin and Chumporn to Khao-Sok National Park. Here you will spend the nights in an adventurous way in luxurious safari tents and go on a cloth feel with the amiable national animal of Thailand – the elephant. After a two-day visit to the stunning National Park with a jungle hike and Kayaktour, you will find the dream beaches of southern Thailand where you can relax to your heart’s content.

1st day Grand travel start: Dinner Cruise on the Chao Phraya

Welcome to Thailand! At Bangkok Airport, you will be picked up by your private guide and taken to your hotel. The fascinating city, known in the Thai language under the beautiful name “City of Angels”, has even more to offer than ornate sanctuaries and the huge river Chao Phraya used by countless ships. Countless markets, sparkling temples, gigantic shopping malls and millions of Buddha statues: Bangkok is full of life and offers adventures for young and old. The rest of the day is at your disposal. Arrive in peace or take a first small exploring tour through the urban jungle on your own. In the evening, the first big highlight of your trip awaits you: a relaxed dinner cruise (together with other guests) on board the Manohra, a rice bark in vintage style. Enjoy delicious Thai specialties as you comfortably sail along the Chao Phraya River and the evening-lit backdrop majestically passes you by. Overnight in Bangkok.

Day 2 Bangkok's magnificent temples and bustling waterways

After an extensive breakfast, your personal guide will bring you closer to his city. The magnificent Royal Palace is a must for any visitor to the capital. Even today it serves as a backdrop for state receptions and royal ceremonies. Here is also the temple of the highly venerated Jade Buddha. Not far away is Wat Pho Temple, considered the oldest “university” in Thailand and known for its traditional massage school. It also houses the gigantic reclining Buddha. If you can’t get enough of all the temples, one of which is more beautiful than the other, we still recommend visiting the white marble temple (Wat Benjamaphorn) and the Temple of the Golden Buddha (Wat Trimitr). After the lunch break, you can relax with a ride on one of the typical longtail boats through Bangkok’s extensive canal system – the so-called klongs. Life still plays out on, on and in the water here. As you pass through stilt houses, cooking kitchens and gardens, you get an authentic impression of how Bangkok has come to be known as the “Venice of the Far East”. Overnight in Bangkok.

3rd day exploring Bangkok by bicycle

Anyone who has ever been to Bangkok, it is certainly difficult to connect a bicycle tour with the capital. Start in the morning for a relaxing bike ride at Benjasiri Park, right in the heart of Thailand’s capital. From here, cycle through the bustling streets to the traditional Khlong Toei Market, one of the city’s largest fresh markets. The trail then takes you past the simple but vibrant settlements of the Khlong Toei slum – an impressive, honest view behind the shining facades of Bangkok. At the pier, board a boat and set sail over Chao Phraya, Thailand’s mighty river of life. On the other hand, you enter a completely different world: the green peninsula Bang Krachao, also known as the “Green Lung of Bangkok”. A highlight is a visit to Sri-Nakhon-Khuean-Khan Park, a green oasis with small lakes, wooden walkways and bird watching towers. The route continues through villages where time seems to stand still. On weekends, you might be lucky enough to visit the floating market Talad Nam Pueng. After your return, the rest of the day will be at your disposal. Relax in your hotel or continue your journey of discovery individually. Overnight in Bangkok.

Day 4 Colorful Market Running at Bangkok’s Gates

A visit to Bangkok is not complete without at least one market visit, because the colorful activity should be experienced by yourself. Leave Bangkok west and head to Damnoen Saduak, where the most popular floating market in the area is located. With a long boat you explore the canals and finally reach the actual market with its colorful bustle directly on the water. Admittedly, the market is touristy, but due to its long tradition it is still worth a visit. Keep the camera ready, because there are hundreds of photo opportunities. Later you will continue your journey and finally reach your day’s destination: Kanchanaburi on the River Kwai. Sit back during a ride on a bamboo raft along the jungle-lined river bank and let the picturesque landscape pass by. Then you will be taken to your “floating hotel”. You will spend the night at The FloatHouse River Kwai, which, swimming on the river surrounded by dense jungles, can only be reached by boat. Just wonderfully idyllic and something very special! Overnight on the River Kwai.

5th Day Painting Erawan National Park

Today you can expect an excursion into the dreamlike nature of Kanchanaburi. After breakfast, head to Erawan National Park, known for its emerald green, dense jungle and roaring waterfalls. There you will take a small hike that leads you to one of the waterfalls. You should not forget your swimwear, because after the hike it will do you good to jump into the water. After that, lunch will taste even better. In the afternoon, take a short trip on the Thailand-Burma railway along the scenic Kwai Valley and visit the memorial at Hellfire Pass.

Day 6 Along the coast to Hua Hin

Your journey will take you south along the tropical coast. On the way, stop at the former summer palace in Petchaburi. The complex was built on a hill above the city, houses an interesting collection of antiques and offers a beautiful view over the area. In the afternoon, arrive at Hua Hin and check in at your hotel. You can use the remaining day to relax on the beach or enjoy the amenities of your hotel. Overnight in Hua Hin.

Day 7 Visit to Sam Roi-Yot National Park

Your further path south takes you to the fascinating national park of Sam-Roi-Yot. The high-contrast nature reserve consists of sea, sand and marsh areas as well as a massif of limestone crossed by grottoes that rises up to 650 meters in height. Numerous bird species, mammals and amphibians live here; the park also serves as a resting area for many migratory bird species. On the way, you will also visit one of the numerous pineapple and coconut plantations. A proverbial highlight is a visit to the Temple complex on Spiegelberg, from where you can see large parts of the coastline of southern Thailand on clear days. Then continue on to Chumphon, where you will spend the night. Overnight in Chumphon.

Day 8 From Chumphon to Khao-Sok National Park

From Chumphon, the ride passes exotic landscapes to Khao Sok National Park, where you check into Elephant Hills Camp (Elephant Camp). This is Thailand’s first comfortable jungle camp in the middle of the largest rainforest area in southern Thailand. You will stay in one of the luxurious tents, all equipped with a double bed, fan and private bathroom. After the check-in, the highlight of the camp is already waiting for you, because it goes to the elephants. Learn from the naturalist guides interesting things about the life and social behavior of the good-natured giants as well as about the important importance of the mahout in this partly lifelong friendship. Then you get the unique opportunity to interact with the fascinating animals. They chop market-fresh fruit into mouth-appropriate pieces of elephant, watch the pachyderms in the mud bath and scrub the brown, mud-covered skin clean with the help of coconut fibres. Riding tours are not offered for the protection of animals. After a well-deserved snack, set off for a relaxing kayaktour along the Sok River. Overnight at Elephant Hills (Elephant Camp).

Day 9: One night in the floating luxury tent camp

Spend your second night at Elephant Hills at Rainforest Camp on Lake Cheow Lan – one of the few floating luxury tent camps on this planet and a unique haven of tranquility. Search for wildlife in the jungle, take a motorized longboat cruise on scenic Cheow Lan Lake, visit a local market or just relax at camp. Here you will experience the beautiful nature of southern Thailand at its best! Overnight at Elephant Hills (Rainforest Camp).

Day 10 - From the jungle to the sea

The new day welcomes you with the sounds of the jungle. Listen to the gibbons screaming high up in the trees as the fog reveals the true beauty of the lake. Immerse yourself in the cool water and then strengthen yourself with a rich breakfast. This starts with a guided canoe ride on the small arms of Lake Cheow Lan. Look out for rhino birds and various primates roaming in the trees. Then it is time to say goodbye to this wonderful jungle paradise. After a final lunch at the camp overlooking the lake, continue your journey to Khao Lak or Phuket, where you can relax after all the adventures of the past days and revisit the collected experiences in peace. For a few days, your life will feel like a postcard subject – how could travel be even more beautiful? Your accommodation for the coming nights is either the exclusive boutique hotel The Sarojin in Khao Lak, which is beautifully quiet and located directly on an eleven kilometer long sandy beach. Or the enchanting Anantara Layan Phuket Resort & Spa on a quiet section on picturesque Layan Beach overlooking the Andaman Sea. Overnight in Khao Lak or Phuket.

11.-13. Day of pure relaxation in the beach paradise

These days belong only to you. Make the time according to your personal preferences and let it go really well! Dream the days by the pool, explore the colorful underwater world on snorkeling trips or treat yourself to soothing spa treatments. There are no limits to your perfect holiday. Overnight in Khao Lak or Phuket.

Day 14 Farewell to Paradise

Even the most beautiful journey comes to an end. Immerse yourself once again through the delicious breakfast buffet of your resort and enjoy the view of the beautiful surroundings. Then we say goodbye to the island paradise. Your driver will take you to the airport, from where you will travel home with many exciting experiences and colorful impressions of a very special country.

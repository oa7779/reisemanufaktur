Tour of West Malaysia, Borneo & Brunei

Malaysia Round Trip - Experience the most beautiful sides of West Malaysia, Borneos and Bruneis on an explorer trip with Asia Special Tours. Ask now!

On this trip you will get to know the most beautiful sides of West Malaysia, Borneos and Bruneis. Explore the multi-faceted world metropolis of Kuala Lumpur, stroll through one of the oldest jungles in the world, the Taman Negara and stroll through the green tea plantations of the Cameron. In Borneo, you will find exciting encounters with the diverse animal and plant world: experience orangutans up close and get authentic insights into the life of the Iban on the Batang Ai. In Bako National Park, you will also have the rare opportunity to observe nose monkeys and monitors in their natural environment. The Sultanate of Brunei boasts the largest water village in the world and its unique atmosphere. At the end of your trip, two special highlights await you: an exotic cooking course and the impressive sight of the majestic Mount Kinabalu.

1st day Arrival in Kuala Lumpur, a city with many facets

Welcome to Malaysia! Your journey begins in the capital Kuala Lumpur, where you will be welcomed and warmly greeted. Together you drive to your hotel. Depending on the arrival time, you can start with first sightseeings or rest a little at the hotel. Kuala Lumpur is a diverse city due to its diverse cultural influences – Malaysians, Chinese gold prospectors, Indian immigrants and British colonialists: Modern shopping malls can be found here as well as colorful market stalls, Chinatown as well as Little India. In the evening, enjoy a stylish dinner at the renowned Bijan Restaurant. In an elegant setting with Asian design elements, traditional Malay dishes are served – refined, modernly interpreted and prepared with great attention to detail. The successful combination of contemporary style and warm atmosphere makes this evening a special culinary experience. You will then be taken back to your hotel. Overnight in Kuala Lumpur.

2nd day experience Kuala Lumpur authentically

Together with your guide, you will set out to explore the versatile city on a city tour. You will travel to the nearest LRT station and experience Kuala Lumpur from the perspective of the locals: while taking public transport, you will reach Kampung Baru, one of the oldest Malay villages in the heart of the modern metropolis. During a walk through Kampung Baru, discover traditional Malay houses, lively markets and gain exciting insights into the city’s cultural heritage. Then visit the Masjid Jamek Mosque, the oldest mosque in the city. Continue to Chinatown, where you will visit the "Sze Ya" Temple and the nearby Sri Maha Mariamman Hindu Temple. Knowledge makes you hungry, so strengthen yourself in a local restaurant and choose between two culinary highlights: discover the aromatic variety of Baba Nyonya cuisine with its exciting combination of Chinese and Malay influences or enjoy classic Indian specialties from North or South Indian cuisine. Well strengthened, take the LRT again to the famous Petronas Twin Towers. Afterwards, you will be taken back to your hotel or have the opportunity to discover Kuala Lumpur on your own. Overnight in Kuala Lumpur. 

Day 3 From the urban jungle to the rainforest

In the morning you will leave the metropolis Kuala Lumpur and be picked up at your hotel. An approximately four-hour drive takes you through the diverse landscapes of Malaysia to Labu Sentral, the gateway to the rainforest. With a traditional long boat, you drive upstream, deep into the dense rainforest. The passing, lush vegetation and the tranquility of nature make this trip a special experience. In the early afternoon you will reach your lodge in the heart of the rainforest. Once there, you can relax a little. After dinner at the resort, take a night walk through the jungle. Your guide knows the area like the back of his hand and with a bit of luck you will see some of the nocturnal animals living here on the site. The sounds of the jungle will eventually accompany you to sleep. Overnight at Taman Negara National Park. 

Day 4 Through the Treetops of the Rainforest

The new day welcomes you with the sounds of the jungle. After breakfast at the lodge, meet your guide and set off for a hike through the jungle. Your path leads you up to Teresek Hill, the highest point in the area. Once you reach the top, you will be rewarded with a spectacular view – one of the oldest in the world. In clear view, the view even reaches Gunung Tahan, the highest mountain in the region. During your hike, your guide will explain to you different plants and small animal species. Afterwards, the famous Canopy Walk will take you through the tree canopy over a length of 530 meters up to 40 meters. From this perspective, you will experience the fascinating flora and fauna of the rainforest even more intensively. Back at the resort you will enjoy a relaxed lunch and let the previous impressions affect you. In the afternoon they will take the boat upstream to the Lata Berkoh waterfall. While passing small rapids, enjoy the untouched nature along the river. Once you have reached your destination, a natural pool invites you to a refreshing cooling. After this varied day, you will return to your resort, where a dinner is already waiting for you. Overnight in Taman Negara.  
 

Day 5 From the Dense Rainforest to the Tea Plantations

After breakfast, leave the rainforest and head to the Cameron Highlands, Malaysia’s most famous mountainous region in Pahang state. At an altitude of around 1,500 meters, a pleasantly cool climate awaits you as well as a landscape of gentle hills, tea plantations and colonial charm. The region was established in the late 19th century during British colonial times as a retreat for colonial administration to escape the tropical heat of the lowlands. Upon arrival, enjoy a stylish high tea at the traditional The Smokehouse. Built in 1937, the Tudor-style property once served as a stylish country house for British expats and has retained its original character to this day. Dark wooden beams, antique furniture, crackling fireplaces and lovingly manicured gardens give the house its distinctive charm. With a cup of tea and fine pastries, let the colonial flair take on itself and enjoy the peace and quiet of this unique mountain landscape. Overnight in the Cameron Highlands. 

Day 6 Back to Kuala Lumpur

After breakfast with a view of the countryside, you will be picked up by your experienced naturalist guide at the hotel and drive to one of the famous tea plantations of the Cameron Highlands. Nestled in gently curved, emerald green hills, your experience begins with a guided walk through the sprawling tea terraces. You will learn interesting facts about the history of tea cultivation. Afterwards, take an exclusive look behind the scenes of a tea factory. Step by step follow the path of the tea leaf. The pleasurable conclusion is a tea tasting with a view to the surrounding plantations. Taste different exquisite teas and sharpen your sense of fine taste nuances and different characters. In the afternoon, continue your journey towards Kuala Lumpur. Along the way, stop at the impressive Batu caves – one of the most important Hindu sanctuaries in the country. Over 272 steps you reach the imposing limestone cave, which houses colorful temples and shrines. Observe the lively religious activity and let the special atmosphere of this place affect you. Afterwards, drive back to Kuala Lumpur, where you will end the day. Overnight in Kuala Lumpur.

Discover Kuching 7th Day – Historical Heritage & Culinary Diversity

After breakfast, your driver will take you to the airport. From there, fly on to Kuching. Upon arrival, you will be welcomed and drive directly to the city center, where your guide is already waiting for you for an exciting Heritage City tour. A culinary highlight awaits you at the start: try the famous Sarawak Laksa – an aromatic noodle soup with shrimp, fish or chicken in a spicy coconut broth. Then explore the historic India Street. Continue to the city mosque, the oldest mosque in the city, which with its golden domes is impressively perched on the banks of the Sarawak River. During your walking tour you will have the opportunity to taste local specialties from small street stalls or traditional Kopitiams. You will visit important historical sites such as the Brooke-era Old Kuching Courthouse and the Pavilion Building. A photo stop at the Square Tower and a walk along the Main Bazaar will give you an authentic insight into historic Kuching. Another highlight is a visit to the approximately 175-year-old Tua Pek Kong Temple. You will also get an insight into the Chinese tea culture by visiting one of the oldest tea houses in the city. The rest of the day is at your disposal. Overnight in Kuching. 

Day 8 Visiting the "Wild Man of Borneo"

Today you can expect a big highlight of your trip: You will experience one of our closest animal relatives up close. After breakfast, head to the Semenggok Orangutan Rehabilitation Center where you can see the “Wild Man of Borneo” up close in his natural environment. The aim of the rehabilitation center is to re-accustom orangutans who have been freed from captivity or found injured in the rainforest to a life in the wild. With a bit of luck, you will observe the fascinating primates up close – especially during feeding times. A short walk through the rainforest will also give you a first impression of Sarawak’s impressive nature. Then continue your journey towards Simanggang (Sri Aman). Along the way, make a stop in Serian and visit the local market. Continue to a pepper farm, where you will learn more about the cultivation and importance of pepper as an important export commodity Sarawak. At lunchtime you will reach the small village of Lachau near the Indonesian border. Here you can enjoy a simple, typical lunch in a local restaurant. After arriving in Simanggang, the rest of the day will be at your disposal. Overnight in Simanggang 

9th Day Guest at the Iban – Traditions on Batang Ai

After breakfast, head towards Batang Ai Dam. There you get together with your travel companion in a traditional long boat that leads you over the calm water of the reservoir through an impressive natural backdrop. Your destination is an authentic longhouse of the Iban, one of Borneo’s indigenous peoples. Upon arrival, you will be warmly welcomed by the residents. During a guided tour of the Langhaus you will get fascinating insights into the way of life, traditions and social structures of the Iban. A special highlight is the performance of a traditional war dance, in which the dancers perform in historical robes – accompanied by the sounds of old Chinese gongs that bear witness to past trade relations. Afterwards, you will have the opportunity to taste the home-brewed rice wine “Tuak” and get into conversation with your hosts. A traditional lunch will round off your visit in culinary style, after which you’ll be able to take action yourself and try out the blowpipe, the once feared hunting weapon of the Iban. In the afternoon, return by longboat to the pier at Batang Ai Dam. From there you will drive back to your hotel, where you can relax and end the day. Overnight in Simanggang. 

Day 10 Back to Kuching

After breakfast, leave Simanggang and drive back to Kuching. Along the way, take a relaxing lunch break at a local restaurant and enjoy once again the variety of regional cuisine. In the afternoon you will reach Kuching. The rest of the day is at your disposal: simply enjoy the amenities of your hotel and let your experiences pass in peace. Overnight in Kuching.

11th day jungle adventure in Bako National Park

Together with your guide, you will leave early in the morning for the Bako National Park. First, you will head to the fishing village of Bako, where you will board a speedboat. The approximately 30-minute drive takes you along the river to the sea and finally to Bako National Park, where you will take a beautiful hike through the park’s diverse vegetation. If you move quietly, you may get to see nose monkeys, silver hood langurs or dragons – not to mention the many species of birds you flutter around. After a simple lunch in the park canteen, you will learn more about the unique flora and fauna of the reserve. On a plank path through the rainforest and mangrove forest, you can look for different species of monkeys and the imposing rhino birds. In the evening, enjoy your dinner in the National Park before retreating to your basic accommodation. With a little luck, curious macaques around your accommodation can be observed at dusk. Listen to the sounds of the jungle and experience the special atmosphere of the tropical rainforest at night. Overnight in Bako National Park.

Day 12: Last Discoveries in Bako National Park

In the early morning, take advantage of the special atmosphere of the national park: Together with your guide, you will embark on a last stalwart through the rainforest. In the cool morning hours, the chances are particularly high to observe rare bird species as well as the characteristic proboscises in the wild. Afterwards, return to the parking canteen and enjoy your lunch before it’s time to say goodbye to the impressive natural scenery. With the boat you drive back to the fishing village of Bako, where your driver is already waiting for you. From here we go back to Kuching. In the afternoon you will reach the city and end your adventure in the Bako National Park with many unforgettable impressions. Overnight in Kuching. 

13th day time for relaxation

This day is free to enjoy the amenities of Cove55 or explore the fascinating surroundings of the Santubong Peninsula. Relax at the infinity pool overlooking the sea or take advantage of the proximity to the exclusive Damai Golf & Country Club for a round of golf between rainforest and coast before ending the evening relaxing at the stylish Cove Bar. Overnight in Kuching.

Day 14 - Go to Brunei

A new day, a new goal. After breakfast, you will be taken to Kuching Airport for the flight to Miri. From Miri you will be driven in about 2.5 hours to Bandar Seri Begawan, the capital of the Sultanate of Brunei. Upon arrival, the rest of the day is freely available to you. Overnight in Bandar Seri Begawan.

15th day exploration tour of Bandar Seri Begawan

After breakfast, you will meet with your guide at the hotel and embark on a city tour of Bandar Seri Begawan, the capital of Brunei. Their first stops are the Royal Ceremonial Hall (Lapau) and the Old Parliament Building (Dewan Majlis), which give an insight into the political and ceremonial history of the country. Afterwards, visit Tamu Kianggeh Market on the Kianggeh River, where you will experience local products and the daily life of the locals. As you progress, discover the traditional craftsmanship of Bruneis at the Malay Technology Museum. After that, the tour will take you to the Royal Regalia Museum, built on the occasion of the silver anniversary of Sultan Hassanal Bolkiah’s throne, featuring impressive insignia, gifts and personal memorabilia of the Sultan. An architectural highlight awaits you with the magnificent Omar Ali Saifuddien Mosque. After lunch, continue your visit with the Jame’ Asr Hassanil Bolkiah Mosque, whose 29 golden domes are symbolic of the Sultans of Brunei. In the afternoon, embark on a boat trip to the world’s largest water village, Kampong Ayer. Here you have the opportunity to visit one of the typical houses on stilts there. Overnight in Bandar Seri Begawan. 

Day 16: The Green Heart of Borneo

Together with your guide, you will set off in the morning for an unforgettable adventure into the unspoiled wilderness. Over the architecturally impressive, 30-kilometer-long Sultan-Omar-Ali-Saifuddien-Bridge, the path leads you to the Temburong district. After a warm welcome with coffee and tea, hop on a traditional Temuai long boat that takes you deeper into the rainforest. Arriving at the park center, the gateway to Ulu Temburong National Park opens for you. On wooden walkways that gently protect the delicate ground vegetation, you wander through the dense green. The absolute highlight awaits you high above the giant of the jungle: the spectacular Canopy Walk takes you up into the treetops and gives you a completely breathtaking view of the rainforest. On the way back you will experience the river up close. Enjoy refreshing waterfalls, a natural fish spa in the middle of nature or become active during rafting or kayaking. For the crowning end, a delicious lunch will be served directly on the riverbank at Rainforest Lodge. After a relaxing break, where you can enjoy the magical backdrop once more in peace, you will return to Bandar Seri Bega-wan.

17th day Flight to Kota Kinabalu

Today you can take a relaxed approach. Enjoy a leisurely breakfast, take another walk through the area or relax by the pool. Around noon, your flight to Kota Kinabalu will take you to Bandar Seri Begawan Airport. At the airport of Kota Kinabalu you are already expected and brought to your hotel. The rest of the day is at your disposal. Make the most of your time – whether it’s relaxing after the trip, taking a first stroll through the city or letting the relaxing atmosphere of Kota Kinabalu feel good. Overnight in Kota Kinabalu. 

18th day Sabah culinary experience – cooking class in the Kiulu Valley

In the morning, meet your guide at the hotel and ride together into the idyllic landscape of the Kiulu Valley. Nestled between the river and the rainforest, a very special experience awaits you: a cooking class that brings you closer to the traditional cuisine of Sabah. In a rustic outdoor kitchen, you will be warmly welcomed – accompanied by a refreshing glass of lemon grass. Guided by local chefs, immerse yourself in the culinary world of Kadazan Dusun and other indigenous communities. You will learn not only the preparation of traditional dishes, but also the stories and cultural backgrounds that are closely linked to them. Together, you prepare typical specialties, including Hinava (a finely seasoned raw fish lettuce), Pinsakan (traditionally preserved fish), an aromatic wild ginger tufau salad, and grilled vegetables and rice – an integral part of every meal in Borneo. You will discover traditional cooking techniques and the respectful handling of ingredients. Finally, you can taste your self-cooked food before being returned to your hotel in Kota Kinabalu. Overnight in Kota Kina-balu. 

Day 19 Discovering Sabah's Natural Diversity

Early in the morning, your journey to the original Inland Borneo begins. The ride will take you through an impressive landscape of rolling hills, rice fields and small villages until you reach Sabah Tea Plantation – one of the few certified organic tea plantations in Malaysia. On site, you will get insights into the cultivation and processing of the tea and then enjoy a wide view over the valley in the restaurant of the plantation. Then continue your journey to the Poring Hot Springs. Nestled in lush vegetation, the hot springs invite you to a relaxing walk. The natural thermal pools, surrounded by bamboo, are considered soothing and provide a perfect opportunity for recreation. Just a few kilometers away, the Kipungit waterfall awaits you – an impressive natural highlight. Alternatively, you have the opportunity to experience the Canopy Walk, a suspension bridge high above the rainforest floor. Here you will get a unique view of the tree canopy. After a lunch break, continue the drive towards Mount Kinabalu National Park. On a guided walk, discover the fascinating plant world of this UNESCO World Heritage site and enjoy the view of the majestic Mount Kinabalu – the highest peak in Southeast Asia. In the late afternoon you will return to your hotel and be free to spend the rest of the day. Overnight in Kota Kinabalu. 

20th day Borneo's wildlife up close

Leave the hustle and bustle of Kota Kinabalu behind and leave for the Beaufort region of Borneo. Upon arrival at Klias River Lodge, you will be greeted with a refreshing welcome drink and a brief introduction to your upcoming nature adventure. Together with an experienced naturalist guide, board a local motorboat to start an exploration tour on the Klias River. They glide through a mangrove system and learn details about these ancient plants. Look for the rare nose monkeys in the treetops along the riverside and take the chance to spot silver langurs or macaques. Also worth a look at the sky: With a little luck you can spot exotic birds such as the majestic rhino bird or the azure blue ice bird. As soon as the sun sets, the absolute highlight of this excursion awaits you: the evening sky is lit up by the magical blinking of countless fireflies. After this enchanting natural spectacle, return to the shore where a delicious barbecue dinner awaits you at the lodge. Afterwards, the transfer will take place back to Kota Kinabalu. Overnight in Kota Kinabalu. 

Day 21 Farewell to Borneo

After breakfast, it is time to say goodbye to Borneo. Your driver will take you to Kota Kinabalu Airport, from where you will board your flight home. Surely you will remember for a long time this eventful journey that took you to the most beautiful places of West Malaysia, Borneos and Bruneis. From the bustling world metropolis of Kuala Lumpur to the famous tea plantations of the Cameron Highlands, to ancient rainforests with their biodiversity of exotic animals and plants, and the largest water village in the world in the Sultanate of Brunei – you have seen and experienced a lot. If you want to relax and enjoy the beautiful beaches of Borneo, we are happy to arrange a bath extension in one of the dreamlike resorts on the island of Gaya near Kota Kinabalu for you.

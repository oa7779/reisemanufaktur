Georgia Tour - In the Land of the Golden Fleece

Welcome to the land of the Golden Fleece! On this enchanting journey, experience Georgia’s natural beauties, explore the emerging capital of Tbilisi and its scenic surroundings. With a farming family, you will get authentic insights into everyday life and taste the local wines for which Georgia is known worldwide. You drive along the old Silk Road to traditional cities and immerse yourself in the land of the Golden Fleece in West Georgia. Walk here on the mysterious traces of Greek mythology, which tells of the legendary gold richness of the former Colchers. An absolute highlight is a visit to the breathtaking region of Svanetia. Finally, relax on the beautiful Black Sea coast. Embark on an unforgettable journey through a fascinating country and let all your senses prevail!

Day 1 Tbilisi – Discovering the Historic Heart of Georgia

Gamarjoba and welcome to Georgia! Upon arrival at Tbilisi airport, you will be expected by your guide and driven to your hotel. Relax from the journey and rest a little. Then go on a tour of the city. They start at the Metechi Church from the 13th century. Then continue to the equestrian statue of the city founder Wachtang Gorgassali. From here you can enjoy a beautiful view of the Mtkwari River, the old town and the surroundings of Tbilisi. By cable car you will then float up to Nariqala fortress from the 4th century. From there, the trail will take you on foot down to the famous medieval sulfur baths. On your way, you will encounter the religious diversity of Tbilisi: you will visit the synagogue and the historic Sioni Cathedral, where the legendary vine cross of Saint Nino is preserved. Saint Nino once introduced Christianity to Georgia. Also the Antschischati Church, the oldest church in the city from the 6th century, should not be missed. Finally, visit the Treasury of the Historical Museum where you can admire the goldsmith art from the time of the Golden Fleece. At the end of the day, you will have dinner at a Georgian restaurant before spending your first night in Tbilisi. Overnight in Tbilisi.

Day 2: Signagi “city of love” & secrets of Georgian wine

After breakfast, you will leave Tbilisi and make your way to the Kachetien region, the traditional center of Georgian winegrowing. On the way, you will reach the city to Signagi, the “city of love”. Signagi towers above the infinite Alasani plane. Stroll through the narrow streets and enjoy the special atmosphere of this city. Then visit the Monastery of Bodbe with the Church of Saint Nino, one of Georgia’s most important religious sites and idyllic in the middle of gardens. Around noon, continue to a nearby village. Here you are invited to a farming family for a rich Georgian lunch of homemade wine. In the village of Velsziche, you will be introduced to the secrets of Georgian winemaking. You will be amazed how different it is from the European one! Of course, you also taste the white and red wines. After entering the world of Georgian wines, continue on to Tsinandali. There you will visit the historic Princely House of Tsinandali, the former residence of the Chavchavadze noble family, surrounded by a beautiful park and known as one of the cultural centers of Georgian nobility. Overnight in Tsinandali.

3rd Day Discover Cacheties – Markets, Monasteries and Wine Tradition

Today you will travel to Telavi and visit the well-known farmers market. Then visit the 11th century Alaverdi Cathedral. The Alaverdi Cathedral – or Church of George – is not only the highest, but also the most delicate church in Georgia. The architects have renounced elaborate jewelry and yet the Alaverdi Cathedral looks special. Your journey will take you to the Chareba wine factory. The exceptional wine cellar is built into a long tunnel – in Georgian “Gvirabi” – and serves as a naturally air-conditioned wine warehouse. During a guided tour of the underground wine cellars, you will learn more about Georgian wine production and taste two selected quality wines. Lunch will then be served in a restaurant above the wine cellars overlooking the surrounding countryside. In the afternoon, visit the historic fortified church of Gremi, once part of the old royal city and a significant architectural monument of the region. Afterwards we will visit the Mosmieri winery in Kisiskhevi, where traditional Georgian winemaking methods are combined with European techniques. During another tasting, you will get to know three selected wines of the house. In the late afternoon return to the hotel. The evening is at your disposal. Overnight in Tsinandali.

4th Day Georgia's Religious Center & Visit to the Armed Church of Ananuri

The time-honored Mzcheta – former capital of Georgia and still today the religious center of the country – is definitely worth a visit. You will visit the Dschwari Church (6th century) and the Swetiz-Choveli Cathedral (11th century). century. This masterpiece of Old Georgian architecture owes its name – “life-giving tribe” – to a legend about the body coat of Christ. A resident of Mzcheta brought the shirt of Christ to Mzcheta after the crucifixion. At the place where it was buried, a tree with a trunk sprouted up, donating a healing balm. This is where the church was built. Afterwards you will drive along the Georgian Army Road to Stepantsminda, which is located in the higher elevations of the Great Caucasus. Along the way, visit the fortified church of Ananuri, built in the 17th century. The journey leads through a scenic very charming area to higher elevations of the Great Caucasus. Overnight in Stepantsminda at 1700 m.

Day 5 Hike to the Gergeti Trinity Church

Lace up your hiking shoes, because an active day is ahead of you! An interesting walk will take you to the small Gergeti Trinity Church, perched at an altitude of 2,170 meters. The hike is exhausting, but it is worth it: the view into the valley, the view of the highest glacier of the Caucasus - the 5.047 m high Kasbeg (if it shows), the sight of the small chapel in front of the majestic mountains and the spiritual atmosphere in this sacred place make the visit an unforgettable experience. Your walk will take you to the Gweleti waterfall. Through an idyllic valley, you walk on a narrow path along a mountain river surrounded by rugged rocks and impressive nature. With a little luck, even eagles can be observed. In the late afternoon, you are a guest of an artist who shows you the elaborate, traditional felt production and invites you to a cozy cup of tea. The day will end at dinner. Overnight in Stepantsminda.

6th day cave town Uplisziche & visit to the spa town Borjomi

After breakfast, drive along the ancient Silk Road to the Kartli region and reach Gori, the birthplace of Stalin. There you will first visit his birthplace (from the outside) and then continue to the cave town Uplisziche, which was founded in the 1st century BC and led through a branch of the legendary Silk Road. Do you recognize the city structure with the many streets, the theater and the various palaces? Then continue on to the Mesheti region. In Borjomi, the well-known mineral water spa, take a walk in Borjomi Park and visit the mineral water spring. Overnight in Borjomi.

7th day Impressive cave town Wardsia & visit to Rabat Fortress

A must on any Georgia trip is a visit to Wardsia. The impressive cave town of Wardsia consisted of 3,000 cave dwellings and offered shelter to over 50,000 people. It was built in the golden age of Georgia, in the 12th century. The artistically decorated rooms are connected by long corridors, which were knocked out of the rock by human hands over 800 years ago. Back in Achalziche, visit the Rabat Fortress, located directly on the Potskhovi River and perched above the city. The fortress was built in the 9th century and has been recently restored. Overnight in Borjomi.

Day 8 In the Land of the Golden Fleece

In the morning, leave Achalziche and continue on to the province of Imereti in West Georgia, the land of the Golden Fleece. According to legend, gold was laundered here in the gold-rich region around Kolchis. The inhabitants laid sheepskins in rivers where the gold was caught. Along the way, visit a grotto with interesting rock formations. Then you reach Kutaissi, the second largest city in the country. Here you will visit the Cathedral of Bagrati (11.) century) perched on a hill. It was not until 1952 that the restoration work on the church, which had been severely damaged by the Ottomans in 1692, began. Its interior was once covered with mosaics, the columns graced white marble. A good impression of the decor richness offers the restored facades. During a walk, you will discover the center of Kutaisi with its small squares and lively streets. In the evening, let the day end with a meal. Overnight in Kutaissi.

9th day Monastery of Motsameta & adventurous drive to Swanetia

After breakfast, visit the Kutaisi Farmers Market, where local merchants offer fresh fruit, vegetables, spices and local specialties. Then visit the 11th century Monastery of Motsameta. The small monastery is impressively located on a rocky outcrop above the deep gorge of the Rioni River and offers a magnificent view of the surrounding landscape. Then head to Svanetia - a spectacular mountainous region in the Caucasus known for its snow-capped peaks, mighty glaciers and centuries-old traditions. On the way to Mestia you will visit the Enguri Dam, which with a width of 750 meters is one of the most impressive buildings in the Caucasus. In the afternoon you will reach Mestia. Overnight in Mestia.

10th day walk in the village of Uschguli & lunch with the locals

Start the day early, because one of the highlights of your trip is coming! You drive to Uschguli (2400m), the highest permanently populated place in Europe. In this place, high mountains, fortified towers and ancient traditions combine to form a total work of art. In Uschguli you will take a walk through the village and can let the special atmosphere work in peace. You will visit the Lamaria Church (12th century) with the shchara (5068m), the highest mountain in Georgia, in the background. Enjoy a lunch with a local family where you will learn many exciting stories about this special place. In the evening, return to Mestia. Overnight in Mestia.

11th day Port town of Batumi on the Black Sea

Today you will leave Mestia and drive to the capital of the Adshara region to Batumi on the Black Sea. Batumi is the third largest city in Georgia and an important seaport. Batumi inspires with its cultural diversity: Georgians, Armenians, Russians, Jews and Turks have lived side by side here for centuries. The harmonious juxtaposition of churches, synagogues and mosques still shapes the cityscape today. There you will take a guided tour of the botanical garden, which is just outside the city. During the guided tour, you will be able to discover more than 5,000 plant species from around the world. You will then be taken to your first-class hotel. The historic old town district is only a few minutes walk away. Let the day end with a leisurely evening walk. Overnight in Batumi.

Day 12: The White Pearl of Georgia

Today you will explore the fascinating coastal town of Batumi, often referred to as the “white pearl of Georgia”. Your walk starts at Piazza Square, probably the most famous square in the city. Here you can admire the ornate mosaics, colorful facades and elegant glass decorations that make the place a vibrant center of culture and encounter. Afterwards, the path will take you to Europe Square, another architectural highlight of the city. In addition to magnificent historical buildings, the old astrological clock immediately catches the eye. You will also see the statue of the Medea, the most famous figure in the region, whose myth is closely linked to Batumi and the historical landscape of Kolchi. Finally, visit the Batumi Cathedral of the Mother of God. The church was originally built as a Catholic house of worship and today impresses with its architecture and its central importance for the religious culture of Batumi. Overnight in Batumi.

13th day Departure from Georgia

Your break from everyday life is coming to an end today. Even if your holiday is soon over, the precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories of your trip will accompany you forever. Look forward to letting your friends and family share your experiences in the distance at home. Your driver will take you to the airport for your return flight.

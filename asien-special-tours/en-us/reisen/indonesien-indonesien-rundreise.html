Java and Bali Tour - Coffee, Volcanoes and Magic Temples

Java and Bali round trip - coffee, volcanoes and magical temples. Discover Indonesia with this travel idea of Asia Special Tours. Ask now!

Itinerary & services

English itinerary text is an offline machine translation of the German original for this team preview. Please confirm travel details with your advisor; the German source remains available through the language switch.

Java is the smallest, but at the same time the most important of the Great Sunda Islands. Experience the treasures of Java and then enjoy the tranquility in the National Park on the island of Bali. The varied program lets you immerse yourself in the culture of Java and at the same time shows you the incomparable natural beauties. Your journey takes you past lush green tropical landscapes and in the middle of imposing volcanic landscapes. In Menjangan on Bali, relaxing days await you when you can relax and let the rich impressions take effect.

1st day Arrival in Yogyakarta

Upon arrival at Yogyakarta Airport, you will be welcomed by your guide and taken to your hotel. The rest of the day is at your disposal. Overnight in Yogyakarta.

Day 2 Explore Yogyakarta

After breakfast, together with your local guide, you will discover the Sultan’s city of Yogyakarta. Stroll on hidden alleys to the Kraton (Sultan’s Palace) and immerse yourself in the heart of the city. Contrary to expectations, the arrangement follows, the at first glance very confusing plant, strict cosmological ideas. You will also visit the resort’s Taman Sari pleasure castle, after which you will make your way to the Hindu Prambanan Temple. The three main temples of this complex are dedicated to the gods Shiva, the destroyer, Vishnu, the keeper, and Brahma, the creator, thus representing the Hindu trinity. After you have admired the beauty of the temple in peace, you will return to the hotel where you can end the day in peace. Overnight in Yogyakarta.

Day 3 The Buddhist Temples of Borobudur

Today, you will make your way to Borobudur – the largest Buddhist temple in the world – very early to see the sunrise in this mystical setting. This pyramid-shaped temple dates back to the Buddhist history of the country and is now a World Heritage Site. Then continue on to Candi Rejo. Once there, exchange the car for a "Dokar", a local horse-drawn carriage, and visit the village of Candi Rejo. Take part in a small music lesson and see how delicious Krupuk is made from the cassava root. Then your driver will take you to your next hotel. Overnight in Magelang.

4th day of free time

Without tour guide and chauffeur

This day is free for you. Take the time to play a round of golf or explore the region on your own. Enjoy this oasis of peace and relaxation! Overnight in Magelang.

Day 5 The Magelang Coffee Plantations

After breakfast, visit the local coffee plantations. You get to know the differences between Robusta and Arabica coffee, get insights into the roasting of the beans and can of course enjoy a delicious cup of coffee at the end. Together with your guide, you will continue to Gedong Songo, a centuries-old Hindu temple complex located at 1,200 m altitude. The temples are among the oldest in Java. Finally, you will continue to Solo, where the rest of the day is available for free. Overnight in Solo.

Day 6 Solo - the cultural center of Java

Solo, together with the heavy star city of Yogyakarta, is considered the center of Javanese culture. The city, where the number of Becaks (bicycle rickshaw) exceeds that of cars, holds a peaceful juxtaposition of Chinese temples, mosques and churches. The Triwindu, an antique market in the center of the city, is a great starting point to watch the friendly banter of traders. Everywhere in the labyrinth of stands and shopfronts, there is loud haggling and trade. Next door is the Sultan's Palace Mangkunegaran. The palace could hardly be more contrary to the bustle of the market: Quiet, lush gardens, gilded pavilions and water games. In the afternoon, visit the Sukuh Temple at the foot of Mount Lawu. Overnight in Solo.

Day 7: Train to Bromo

After an early breakfast, you will be taken to the train station, from where you will continue by train to Mojokerto. In order to bridge the long distances on Java, you drive particularly well by train. On a ride on the air-conditioned express train, you can enjoy a view of the beautiful landscapes of central Java, which is a particularly fertile agricultural area. Upon arrival in Mojokerto, you will be picked up and transported to Bromo. The road leads up to 1868m through artfully adapted vegetable terraces on the mountain slopes. Overnight in Bromo.

Day 8 Sunrise at Mount Bromo

Early in the morning, around 03:30, start your volcano tour on Mount Bromo to admire the sunrise (as far as the weather permits). You should definitely bring a warm jacket to protect yourself from cold temperatures. First it goes by jeep up to a viewpoint. From here you will admire the sunrise. Then take the jeep through the unique landscape at the foot of the volcano. Now it is still a sea of sand and 250 steps to overcome, until you have a fantastic view. In the light of the rising sun, the landscape looks like it is from another planet. After this impressive experience, your guide will take you back to the hotel where you can have breakfast and get some fresh. Then continue to Ijen, on the edge of the rainforest of Ijen National Park. The landscape is characterized by rice terraces and volcanoes. Overnight in Ijen.

Day 9 Good Morning Ijen

In the morning you start the excursion to the crater first by all-wheel drive vehicle. For the next hour and a half, you will walk along a shady path with breathtaking views up to the edge of the crater. You set the pace of the hike yourself. In addition, a ranger hut offers an opportunity for a small break halfway. Arriving at the crater, take as much time as you like and explore the surrounding area before heading downhill. In hard work, local workers reduce sulphur here. Back at the hotel you have time to take a refreshing bath in the pool. In the afternoon, head to the port of Ketapang where you will take the ferry to Gilimanuk and continue on to Menjangan. Overnight in Menjangan.

10.-11. Day free time in Menjangan

Without tour guide and chauffeur

Enjoy the sun, beach and sea and the amenities of your hotel surrounded by nature. Go the days slowly, make time for a good book or the sweet nothing-doing. If you have the sense of activities, the underwater world invites you to small snorkeling or diving trips. Or would you prefer to be pampered in the spa area? Overnight in Menjangan.

Day 12 Menjangan - Departure

Without guidance

Today, unfortunately, we have to say goodbye again. Soak in the peace once more before being taken relaxed and relaxed to Denpasar Airport, where you will take your flight home.

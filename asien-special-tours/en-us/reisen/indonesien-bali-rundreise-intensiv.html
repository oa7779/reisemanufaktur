Bali Round Trip Intensive - So Close to the Gods

This Bali tour takes you very close to the gods: Enjoy adventure and relaxation on your individual Bali tour with Asia Special Tours.

Embark on a very special adventure and treat your body and soul to a break! A perfect mix of activities and recreation, nature and culture await you. You are accommodated in first-class hotels that invite you to dream. Whether by bike, on foot, on the surfboard or under water - you can not collect the impressions of Bali more authentic and intense. In between, there is enough space for a flexible time division and very private moments. The program is also great for a honeymoon of a different kind!

1st day Arrival in Denpasar & Bali by night

Selamat datang and welcome to the Island of the Gods! Upon arrival in Denpasar, you will be expected by your guide and driven to your hotel in Seminyak. After checking in, you have time to recover. Take a magnificent beach walk or swim the first lanes in the pool. In the evening, join your guide on a street food tour of Seminyak. On a Vespa you will head to a trendy bar, where you will cozy the evening with a refreshing cocktail. After the sun sets, you will be served a delicious dinner. Watch the bustle on the crowded streets of Seminyak. For dessert, leave the city and drive to a trendy restaurant in the middle of rice fields. In this enchanting setting, enjoy your sweet dessert before returning to the hotel. Overnight in Seminyak.

2nd Day Visit to Uluwatu Temple & Romantic Dinner

Waves, beach and sea... a sparkling pleasure awaits you! After breakfast, today you will head straight to the beach, where you will take part in a two and a half hour surf lesson. First, you will receive a brief safety briefing as well as an introduction to the theory of surfing. Then you can try it yourself. Jumping on the board, maintaining balance and surfing the wave – that’s fun! Then return to the hotel where you can rest. In the afternoon, visit Uluwatu Temple, which stands on top of a spectacular cliff and is one of Bali’s most impressive temples. In the evening there is a very special highlight on the program: Experience an exclusive dinner in a secluded cave in a romantic atmosphere. The cave is located on a private beach where you will enjoy a beautiful sunset over the sea. A 5-course menu to the sounds of the sea noise awaits you. Overnight in Seminyak.

Day 3 Free time in Seminyak

This day is full of rest and relaxation. Let the soul dangle and enjoy the moments when time seems to stop. Make the day according to your taste. Overnight in Seminyak.

Day 4 Bicycle through the rice fields of Jatiluwih

It's going to be sporty! After breakfast, you will be picked up and drive on north to Munduk. On a bicycle tour you get to know Bali today in a very special way. Your first stop is the rice fields of Jatiluwih. They have been designated a World Heritage Site by UNESCO. Together with your guide, you will explore the area and learn all about Balinese rice cultivation. Over beautiful panoramic roads and through dense rainforest it goes uphill and downhill by bicycle until you reach your destination. At an altitude of about 850 meters is the mountain resort Bedugul. The tranquil Lake Beratan fills the ancient crater of Mount Batukaru. Its beautiful temple Ulun Danu Bratan on the waterfront is dedicated to Dewi Danu, the deity of water. Then you get back into your car and are taken to your hotel. Overnight in Munduk.

5th Day Trekking Tour in Munduk

Munduk is home to Bali’s coffee and cocoa plantations. Succulent green rice fields and numerous waterfalls also line the paths of the area. The pretty place already enchanted Danish colonial rulers, who built their residences here in 1903. On a two-hour hike, you will pass through some villages where you will get deep insights into simple country life. Enjoy a cup of coffee from the region. Then we continue to Menjangan, in the still very original northwest of Bali. This quiet place with its beaches of dark volcanic sand has its own charm. Overnight in Menjangan.

6th day of free time in Menjangan

This day is yours alone. Enjoy the sun, beach and sea and the amenities of your hotel surrounded by nature. Take the day slowly, make time for a good book or the sweet nothing-doing. Or would you prefer to be pampered in the spa area? Overnight in Menjangan.

Day 7 Excursion to West Bali National Park

An impressive journey into the underwater world of Bali awaits you today. You will be taken by boat to the island of Menjangan. A snorkeling trip through the species-rich coral garden opens up a whole new world for you. Between corals and anemones in the warm, crystal-clear water there are fish of all colors and forms. Afterwards, explore the island of Menjangan with a small walk. With a little luck, you can observe the deer that is home to here. Around noon you will find strengthening in your lunch box. Enjoy your rest on the beach before returning to your hotel in Pemuteran. Overnight in Menjangan.

Day 8 - Continue to Kintamani

After an extensive and relaxed breakfast, you will have some time to enjoy your first-class hotel complex. Then you will be picked up by your guide and drive on to your next destination. You will visit the village of Sangsit and the impressive temple Pura Beji, dedicated to the goddess of rice and known for its traditional sculptures. For lunch, head to a local restaurant in Kintamani. Surrounded by mountains and lush green forests, Kintamani is your charming hotel. Enjoy this quiet and peaceful place or take a stroll through the surroundings framed by the imposing Mount Batur. Overnight in Kintamani.

Day 9 On to the summit of Bali

Today it is necessary to get up early to experience the sunrise from the Batur volcano. The mountain is quite good to climb with a normal fitness level. From the parking lot to the summit, it is 600 meters of altitude and it takes two to three hours. The effort and getting up early are worth it: the spectacle at sunrise is great. With a halfway clear view, you can first see a patrol on the horizon behind the neighboring mountain Gunung Abang and the Gunung Agung. Finally, the sun shows up – first the peak, then it rises relatively quickly and it becomes bright. The view of Lake Batur, over the caldera and the lava fields on the volcanic slope is great. Back at the hotel, your breakfast will taste even better than usual. Afterwards you will leave for the cultural heart of Bali, Ubud. This city is characterized by many temples, museums, art galleries and a far-reaching artistic heritage. Because in the 1930s, Western artists and intellectuals were often invited there. The place will delight you with its idyllic location, rice fields and lush jungles! Overnight in Ubud.

10.-15. Day free time in Ubud

These days are at your disposal. Take the time to explore Ubud on your own. The COMO Shambala Estate Hotel offers the best conditions for a few relaxing days to bring body and mind into harmonious balance. Enjoy your holiday with a Balinese relaxation program, a wellness application or a workout in the jungle gym. The holistic concept of the hotel including yoga teachers, dietitians and Ayurvedic doctor ensures your inner balance. A balanced diet and a high-quality cuisine round off the perfect stay.

16th day Departure from Bali

Your time out from everyday life is coming to an end. Even if your holiday is soon over, the precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories of your trip will accompany you forever. The day is at your disposal before you are taken to the airport for your return flight.

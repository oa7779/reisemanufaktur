China Garden Travel - In line with Feng Shui

China Garden Tour: Experience Chinese horticulture in perfection on this individual China trip with Asia Special Tours. Ask now!

Do you love exotic gardens and stately parks? Then let yourself be enchanted by the Chinese garden art in its perfection. The interplay of water, fire, earth, wood and metal can be found in the Chinese garden design at Feng Shui. As a retreat and feel-good oasis, you can really drop in the gardens and feel the chi, the flowing energy as the center of Chinese garden teaching. You will visit the impressive summer residence in Chengde, where you will experience an almost perfect example of Chinese garden art. The enchanting nature in the Huangshan Mountains is considered a prototype of Chinese gardens and will also captivate you with its beauty. Of course, the unique garden city of Suzhou with all its famous gardens cannot be missed on this trip. Experience the more than 5000 years old tradition of Chinese garden art and experience complete harmony in the most enchanting gardens in China!

1st day Arrival in Beijing

Welcome to China! Your trip starts in Beijing, where you will be expected by your guide when you arrive at the airport. As a political and cultural center of the country, Beijing can look back on a 3000-year history, which is reflected in a globally unique cultural heritage. Thanks to the 2008 Olympic Games, China’s capital, often dismissed as a giant village, has transformed into a modern world city. Just as the emperors once built their city as a reflection of the universe, China today has created a capital for itself as a reflection of a new, great country. You will be brought to your hotel and can first relax a little from the long journey. Overnight in Beijing.

Day 2 Beijing’s Summer Palace – a masterpiece of Chinese gardening

After breakfast, you will be picked up by your guide and go on a discovery tour. Tanzhe Temple is located in the middle of the densely forested west mountains, about 1 hour drive from the city center. It is one of the oldest Buddhist temples in Beijing. During the subsequent dynasties, the complex was extended again and again and now consists of various halls, courtyards, pavilions and pagodas. To experience unique architecture, continue on to the Summer Palace. The complex is a rare masterpiece of Chinese horticultural art that will enchant you as a garden lover. The magnificent Summer Palace on the West Mountains near Beijing is the only surviving imperial garden. In it, the perfect dream of Chinese garden architecture is realized: an ensemble of palaces and temples, pavilions and pagodas with a total of 3,000 rooms on 290 hectares. One feels how the Chinese emperors and their court lived. With many impressions you return to your hotel, where you can end the evening according to your own wishes. Overnight in Beijing.

Day 3 In Imperial Traces Through the Forbidden City

Before you leave Beijing, you must definitely visit the Forbidden City with the Imperial Palace. This magical place was the seat of the Chinese emperors of two dynasties – the Ming and the Qing – and for half a millennium was inaccessible to the population who did not belong to the court. The crowd is as overwhelming as the architecture, the colorfully painted palace halls, the pagoda-like roofs. Ready for a mighty encounter? The Great Wall is waiting for you! For you, it is the Jinshanling section, which is about 120 km from Beijing. The 6.5 km long section is smaller and narrower than the Great Wall in Badaling or Mutianyu, but much more original and surrounded by a fantastic landscape. A cable car ride will take you up to the wall. Once you have arrived at the top, you have the opportunity to explore this gigantic building on a hike. Back at the starting point, we go down with the cable car again. Overnight in Jinshanling.

4th day Enchanting garden art at the summer residence in Chengde

After breakfast, continue to Chengde, where one of the most outstanding examples of garden art awaits you. Once there, stroll through the spacious summer residence of the Qing Emperor, China’s largest original imperial garden. For a hundred years, the court regularly went to Chengde in summer and ruled the empire from there. Here you will find replicas of the gigantic Potala Palace from Lhasa as well as various Tibetan temples and monasteries. The palace complex is located on the northern edge of the city and is surrounded by an imposing ten kilometers long wall that winds over mountains and valleys. The complex includes palaces, temples, halls and pavilions, but also includes beautiful gardens. Take a look around and have your camera ready. Overnight in Chengde.

Day 5: Travel to Huangshan in the “Golden Mountains”

Today you will first be brought back to Beijing by car. From there you will continue with the fast train to Huangshan, where you will be expected and transported to your hotel in the village of Bishan. Use the rest of the day to explore Bishan and look forward to the next few days in mystical nature. Overnight in Bishan. 

6th Day Art and Culture in Anhui Province & Idyllic Bike Tour

On your excursion today, get to know more about the people of the region and their ways of life and enjoy the fascinating natural landscape. You will first visit the villages around Huangshan in Anhui province - it is considered the poorest region of the east. In earlier times, this area was called Yixian and was a historically very prosperous city and home to many businessmen of the country. Thus, you will discover magnificent and noble houses here, which were considered a sign of prosperity. Many of the buildings are over 200 years old and decorate themselves with the filigree art of timber construction. These houses can still be found in the surrounding villages. Discover these noble houses there as witnesses of a prosperous era. Then swing on the bike and take a bicycle tour along green rice fields in the idyllic village of Bishan. Overnight in Bishan.

Day 7: One night over the clouds on Huangshan

Mysterious, bizarre and beautiful mountain regions are many in China. But hardly any is comparable to the imposing beauty of Huangshan. The Huangshan is world famous for its "four distinctive features": strangely grown pines, strangely shaped rocks, the sea of clouds beneath its peaks and hot springs. The special geological structure and the composition of the summits are typical features of this landscape. With the cable car you will float up to the summits of the Yellow Mountain, whose beauty has been sung by numerous poets and made painters dream. Although the main summit is less than 1,900 meters high, the steep rocks offer an eventful hiking trail. Even in dense fog, this area is delightful with its rocks, bamboo groves and ancient, gnarled pines. You will spend the night on the mountain today and, with a little luck, enjoy a beautiful sunset. A pure enjoyment moment! Overnight on the Huangshan.

Day 8: From the Light of Dawn to the Garden City of Suzhou

Good morning! Today the day greets you at high altitude. Get up early to enjoy the magical morning mood in peace and watch as the early fog around the summits is dipped in color. Let this moment affect you before you take another hike on the mountain. Then take the cable car back down into the valley. In the late afternoon, you will be taken to the train station to travel on to Suzhou by express train. In the evening you will reach the garden town of Suzhou. The city enchants with its numerous canals and classic gardens. Through the gardens, Suzhou became famous and eventually developed into a cultural and intellectual center. The art of horticulture was to harmoniously design as many landscapes as possible in a confined space, offering both variety and opportunity for relaxation, but at the same time creating the feeling of size and expanse. Overnight in Suzhou.

9th Day Harmony in Suzhou’s Gardens & Private Bonsai Trial Course

Today you will visit two typical gardens of the city of Suzhou. First, visit the garden "Lion Forest", known for its diverse rock formations. Afterwards we go to the garden "Fischermeister". There you will meet a bonsai master. During a private bonsai trial course, you will learn more about Chinese bonsai art: how is a bonsai tree grown and cared for, how are the bonsai plants integrated in a garden, what is the point of this art? Take advantage of the valuable time and find detailed answers to your questions. Overnight in Suzhou.

10th Day Taihu Stones in the Chinese Garden

Stones are also an important element in the Chinese garden. These originate exclusively from Lake Taihu, 60km southwest of Suzhou. The Taihu stones, due to their unique characteristics and formations, are still the most popular decorative material of a Chinese garden or park. Today you will visit a Taihu Stone Market and inquire about the art of grinding and integration. Afterwards, take a trip to the Dongshan Peninsula on Lake Taihu, the third largest inland lake in China. In Dongshan, visit the Qiyuan Garden, which is beautifully located on Lake Taihu. It seems as if the lake is immediately integrated into the garden. Let yourself be enchanted by this ingenious art of architecture before returning to Suzhou. Overnight in Suzhou.

11th day Visits to the Dragonhead metropolis Shanghai

In the morning you will start the last stage of your journey. The fast train goes to Shanghai. Once there, your guide will welcome you and take you to your hotel. What a contrast to the last few days! The first highlight of the day is the ascent to the impressive Shanghai Tower, which is one of the tallest skyscrapers in the world at 632 meters. From the observation deck, you’ll enjoy stunning views of the city and the bustling Huangpu River. Afterwards, visit the City Planning Museum, where you will get interesting insights into Shanghai’s impressive development and planning. Here you will learn more about the history, architecture and future visions of this dynamic city. After visiting the museum, a relaxing Huangpu river cruise awaits you. Enjoy the view of the glittering skyline as you sail past the city and admire the impressive lights of the skyscrapers. Your day will end with a stroll through the historic French concession, a charming neighborhood known for its shady streets, elegant buildings and unique flair. Here you can relax in one of the cozy cafés and let the atmosphere of this fascinating city affect you. Overnight in Shanghai.

Day 12 Excursion to Guangfulin Ruins Park & Chenshan Botanical Garden

Today, take a trip to Songjiiang, a suburb of Shanghai, where you will visit the Guangfulin Ruins Park. Here you will find a tasteful park with modern design, traditional architecture and rural charm. Afterwards you will visit the Chenshan Botanical Garden, which with its infinite number of exotic plants is the largest botanical garden in all Asia. With many impressions you will return to Shanghai, where you will be taken to your hotel. Overnight in Shanghai.

13th day of free time in Shanghai

Without tour guide and chauffeur

This day belongs to you alone. Enjoy your free time in this mega-metropolis and let yourself drift. Shanghai still has an infinite amount to offer and is waiting to be discovered by you. Overnight in Shanghai.

Day 14: Farewell to China

Today, unfortunately, it means saying goodbye to a fascinating country from which you will take many lasting impressions with you. You will be taken to the airport from where you will start your onward or return journey.

Family Adventures - On Treasure Hunt in Vietnam and Cambodia

Vietnam Cambodia Travel: Go treasure hunting with your family in Vietnam and Cambodia with Asia Special Tours. Ask now!

Fernweh as a family? No problem - embark on your very personal family adventure through Vietnam and Cambodia. There are many exciting adventures waiting for you, big and small, and unique moments that take you into your dream world. You will not only experience the main sights of Vietnam and Cambodia, but also see many more facets that remain hidden to most visitors. They are guests with local families and thus experience an authentic insight into rural life and the traditional craftsmanship that has been handed down for generations. Always set your own pace, which makes the heart and soul wide – and the head free. At the end of an eventful family holiday, you will once again let yourself rest completely. Spend your precious time with your whole family on the magnificent beach on Phu Quoc and leave any everyday stress far behind.

1st day Arrival in Hanoi & visit to a pancake baker

Welcome to Vietnam! In Hanoi, you will already be expected by your guide and taken to your hotel. After a rest break, take a walk through the “36 alleys” of the old town, into the vibrant life of the Vietnamese. You will visit a traditional pancake baker in the heart of the old town. Here you can see (and of course try) how the pancakes “Banh Cuon” are made. The typical steamed treats are made of rice flour and have little in common with the European pancakes – but you will find out about that on site. Then you will meet a water puppet manufacturer who has maintained this artisanal tradition of North Vietnam for 7 generations. Your host welcomes you to his house and shows you how he makes the water dolls. Of course, he will also show you a private demonstration with his dolls. Hidden behind a stage, he lets the water puppets move to different rhythms. Try it with your family and stage your own play. With many first impressions you return to the hotel. Overnight in Hanoi.

2nd Day City Tour of Hanoi & Guest with a calligraphy master

In the morning, start your full-day discovery tour by visiting the Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum (from outside). It is a tribute to the architecture of the Lenin Mausoleum in Moscow, a traditional stilt house and the one-column pagoda built in the 11th century. Then visit the Ethnographic Museum, which exhibits 54 ethnic groups of the country. In the afternoon, we will head to the Temple of Literature, which is located in the place where the first University of Vietnam was founded. With its ancient courts, altars and rooftops, the Temple of Literature is a special example of Vietnamese architecture. Here you will meet a calligraphy master who is particularly familiar with this art form. He uses the so-called “four treasures of the study room”: ink pen, ink stone, paper and brush. You will learn about the history of calligraphy and can admire his talent. He will gladly answer all your questions. Overnight in Hanoi.

3rd day Luxurious cruise through the charming Halong Bay

Today follows another highlight of the journey for big and small. You leave Hanoi and head to Halong Bay, where you board a junk. On deck, let the view wander over the unique limestone landscape, which has been declared a World Heritage Site by UNESCO. More than 1,000 rocks, grottoes and islands stick out of the turquoise blue water. Enjoy a fresh lunch on board as you gradually sail through the magical bay. You will explore the labyrinth of the bay with its many limestone rock islands and caves. For visiting the Grotte Sung Sot and Titop Island, disembark and enjoy magnificent views over the bay. After a delicious dinner of fresh seafood, you will experience an unforgettable sunset amidst a dreamlike backdrop. Overnight in Halong Bay.

Day 4 End of the Cruise & Onward Flight to Central Vietnam

If you are one of the early risers, you can experience this place at the most beautiful time of day: The rising sun breaks through the early fog and immerses the bay in a magical light. Refuel with a few tai-chi exercises for the day. During breakfast, you will gradually cruise back to Halong. After a delicious brunch on board, arrive at Halong early at noon, where you will leave the boat. Your chauffeur will take you to Hanoi Airport, from where you will fly to Hue in central Vietnam in the evening. The name Hue means harmony: gracefully reflected in the perfume river, dreaming in the shadow of old palaces and other imperial buildings. A UNESCO World Heritage Site, Hue attracts everyone with its famous citadel, palaces, temples and imperial tombs that bear witness to a great and fascinating past. Overnight in Hue.

5th day Visit to the Imperial Citadel & Dragon Manufacturing

In the morning you will visit the impressive Imperial Citadel. This World Heritage site was created following the example of the Forbidden City in Beijing. Once upon a time, behind impressive walls and a moat of ten kilometers in length, the members of the Nguyen dynasty’s imperial family lived here in stately palaces lined with pretty gardens and ornate temples. In the afternoon, we will visit a dragon factory located three kilometers from Hue. There you will have the chance to get to know the traditional techniques of this craft and create your own dragon. The dragon making is special in Hue due to the special design and spiritual message, such as the desire for a rich harvest or good weather, especially after the rainy season. After that, you will head to a huge open field to test the dragon yourself. Maybe a few local children will join you to let the dragons rise with you. Of course, you can keep your dragon as a souvenir afterwards. Overnight in Hue.

Day 6 Over the Cloud Pass into the charming Hoi An

After breakfast, you can enjoy the breathtaking scenery on the drive over the scenic mountain roads that offer spectacular views of the coast. Halfway between Danang and Hue lies the so-called Cloud Pass built by the French. In good weather you can see here to the coast! The destination of your day tour is the old picturesque port city of Hoi An. The ancient city center of this once important commercial port is very well preserved, so you can see here buildings from several centuries that testify to the different cultural influences to which Hoi An was exposed. You feel the life of the old colonial period in almost every alley. You will visit the Japanese Bridge, the Chinese Temple and the House of Phung Hung. Overnight in Hoi An.

Day 7 Guest at a rice farmer & adventurous basket boat ride

An adventurous day for the whole family is ahead of you. Rice is the number one staple food in much of Asia. But how is the rice actually grown? Today, you can learn more about rice cultivation and get a glimpse of this centuries-old agricultural tradition. A Vietnamese farmer will explain to you directly on the field how the grain is grown and processed. At lunch with the farming family, you will get a personal insight into Vietnamese rural life in a casual atmosphere. In the afternoon we will take a 45-minute boat trip to the seaport of Cua Dai at the mouth of the Thu Bon River. Then transfer to a special boat called thung chai (“basket boat”). This small, round boat made of bamboo is used by local fishermen and can accommodate up to five people. With the small boat you will explore the less accessible canals and discover the idyllic landscape. Try it yourself and try to keep the balance with circular paddle movements. This is really not an easy task, but it will certainly be fun for you and your children! Overnight in Hoi An.

8th day Flight to Siem Reap & Leisure

Enjoy your free morning and take a leisurely beach walk. Afterwards, you will be taken to the airport and fly on to Siem Reap. This city will be your family’s starting point in the next few days to explore the world-famous Angkor Park with about 40 square kilometers and more than 100 temples dating from the 9th to 13th centuries. In Siem Reap you will be expected by your guide and brought to your hotel. The rest of the day is at your disposal. Visit one of the lively markets of the city or go to one of the interesting museums. Overnight in Siem Reap.

9th Day Experience Local Village Life & the Ruined City of Angkor Thom

Today you will learn more about the local village life of the locals. You start your experience by making your own traditional breakfast and being shown the different stages of rice noodle production. Then head to a village that specializes in the production of bamboo sticky rice and the harvesting of coconuts. Help prepare the bamboo filling and try the tasty Cambodian snacks. Afterwards, visit a family that specializes in handicraft from palm leaves. The residents will show you how to make such a piece of craft and of course you will also have the opportunity to take home your own work of art as your personal souvenir. In the afternoon, your guide will take you to the ruined city of Angkor Thom, once the last capital of the Great Khmer Empire in the 13th century. You can reach the city through the impressive South Gate, a stone gate carved with four gigantic faces and elephants. From here, drive to Bayon Temple, which is right in the middle of the city. A special feature of this monument are the 54 towers representing the 54 provinces of the Great Khmer Empire. Overnight in Siem Reap.

Day 10 Spectacular Angor Wat & Flight to Saigon

After breakfast, another highlight of your trip awaits you: the world-famous Angkor Wat Temple. Built in the first half of the 12th century, this World Heritage Site is the largest and undoubtedly most important monument of the Angkor Temple Group. Angkor Wat is said to represent the peaks of Mount Meru, home of the gods. The sanctuary is a masterpiece of Khmer architecture: within the vast complex you will find beautifully designed carvings of figures from Hindu mythology as well as almost 2000 Apsaras, so-called “heavenly dancers”. Your guide will be able to tell you a lot of interesting things about the mythological meaning of the ornaments and statues. Among the shrines to be seen in Angkor is Ta Prohm, a 12th-century temple. The place of worship was discovered by the French at the end of the 19th century and was deliberately preserved as the restorers found it. The semi-decomposed state and the overgrown roots of the surrounding tree giants give the place a mystical atmosphere. With many impressions of today, you will be taken to the airport and fly on to Saigon. There you will be met by your guide and taken to your hotel. Overnight in Saigon.

Day 11 With the Vespa through the busy streets of Saigon

Your adventurous exploration of Saigon begins early in the morning by visiting a park where locals enjoy practicing their morning gymnastics. Afterwards, the traditional Vietnamese breakfast awaits you: the noodle soup Pho. Also, treat yourself to a coffee before you rush into the bustle of the awakening city. From the back seat of a Vesparoller, you will experience the turbulent metropolis in a particularly authentic way – but without having to maneuver through chaotic traffic. During the ride, your driver will show you the highlights of the city, such as the Reunification Palace, the Main Post Office, the neo-Romanesque Cathedral of Notre Dame, the Opera House and the City Hall. Then we continue with a visit to Cholon, which means “Great Market”. Cholon is Saigon’s “Chinatown”, a bustling and colorful district. Visit the Thien Hau Temple and Binh Tay Market. Overnight in Saigon.

12th day Flight on Phu Quoc & Start of bathing stay

After eventful days with many exciting adventures for big and small, it is now time for a relaxing bathing stay. You will be taken to Saigon Airport to fly to Phu Quoc. There you will be picked up by the hotel’s shuttle and taken to the hotel. The chic Chen Sea is the perfect place for a quiet and relaxed bathing holiday without much hustle. Located in a small bay, the resort has just 36 villas, all with great sea views scattered in a tropical garden area. Here you will feel comfortable with your family. Overnight at Phu Quoc.

13-14. Day Sun, beach and sea

Without tour guide & chauffeur

Phu Quoc offers a postcard landscape that invites you to dream. These days are all about rest and relaxation. Let the soul dangle and enjoy moments when time seems to stop. You have the opportunity to explore the area on your own or spend leisure hours on the beach or in the complex of your hotel. Enjoy your precious family time. Overnight stays on Phu Quoc.

15th day Departure from Vietnam

Today, unfortunately, we say goodbye to two fascinating countries and their lovable inhabitants. You will be taken to the airport of Phu Quoc and fly back to Saigon, where you will take your home or onward flight. There are countless experiences and impressions in your luggage that will accompany you and your family throughout their lives.

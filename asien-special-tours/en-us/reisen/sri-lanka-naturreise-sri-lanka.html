Natural resources of Sri Lanka

Sri Lanka Nature Tour: Experience impressive natural treasures on your individual Sri Lanka trip with Asia Special Tours. Ask them in a moment.

Sri Lanka makes the hearts of nature lovers beat faster. Here you experience infinite diversity in the smallest space: savannahs, rainforests, mountain ranges and fine sandy beaches are united on an area that is not even as big as Bavaria. Many exciting encounters with the species-rich wildlife will also inspire you. However, Sri Lanka also looks back on a long history to which the country owes fascinating archaeological sites. Let yourself be taken back in time and explore the testimonies of ancient civilizations and the ruins of magnificent palaces. Relaxing days on the beach round off this diverse journey.

1st day Arrival in Sri Lanka

Today your journey through Sri Lanka begins. Upon arrival at Colombo airport, you will be picked up and taken to your hotel in Anuradhapura, where you will be able to take some rest after arriving before going on your first safari tomorrow. Enjoy your tasteful boutique hotel and feel transported back to the golden times of the former kingdom. Overnight in Anuradhapura

2nd Day Jeep Safari in Wilpattu National Park & Lost Kingdom of Anuradhapura

The nature and wildlife of Sri Lanka are the focus right at the beginning of your trip: After breakfast, you will leave together with your guide to the Wilpattu National Park, the largest national park in the country. On a jeep safari, with a bit of luck, you’ll see mongooses, peacocks, elephants, wild boar, crocodiles and turtles. However, the protected area is particularly well known for its leopard population. In the afternoon, Anuradhapura is waiting for you. In addition to the ruins of a lost kingdom, the city also houses various sacred sites – such as the Ruwanweliseya-Dagoba, one of the highest stupas in Sri Lanka. It was built in the second century BC. According to legend, the poplar dough, which is now part of a temple complex, is just as old: Siddharta Gautama is said to have already sat under it – so you can probably see the oldest tree in the world here. Overnight in Anuradhapura

3rd day Spice Garden Matale & Tooth Temple in Kandy

Your journey will take you to Kandy. Along the way, visit a spice garden in Matale where you’ll learn about growing and processing various spices. Then you reach the former royal city of Kandy, which lies in the middle of the central mountains about 500 meters above sea level. Despite its size, it is still very much influenced by ancient traditions. The last king to rule here was Sri Wickrama Rajasinghe - then the British took power from 1815. In the middle of the city lies a lake that is perfect for a walk around. The main attraction is undoubtedly the tooth temple, in which a canine of Buddha is said to be kept in a golden shrine. Tradition has it that this tooth emanates spiritual power, making the sanctuary one of the most important pilgrimage sites in the country for Buddhists. Overnight in Kandy.

Day 4 The Secrets of Tea Growing

After breakfast, continue to Nuwara Eliya. The City of Light is located 1,868 meters above sea level in the central highlands of Sri Lanka. The picture book landscape and the temperate climate make them very special. Gracefully perched the highest mountain in the country, the “Pidurutalagala”, above the lush green tea fields of the area – Nuwara Eliya is the most important place for tea production in Sri Lanka. The city also bears the nickname “Little England”, because much reminds of the time of the past empire. Once upon a time, the British settled here to cultivate tea. Thus, the mountains around Nuwara Eliya are still known as the main growing area of the famous Ceylon tea. When visiting a tea factory, you can convince yourself of the quality. The lush green hills are also a beautiful backdrop for walks. For golf lovers, we are happy to arrange a round on the wonderful 18-hole golf course (optional). Overnight in Nuwara Eliya

Day 5 Excursion to Horton Plains National Park

Today you will visit Horton Plains National Park. The protected area, which is located on the highest mountain plateau in the country, is one of the most important national parks in the country because of its scenic diversity – here you will also find the beautiful Baker’s Waterfall. It is home to a variety of species that live only in this environment. The hills are overgrown by evergreen forests of ancient tree giants. From a steep slope over 1050 meters, which bears the poetic name “World’s End”, in good weather, there is a breathtaking view to the sea. Afterwards you will return to the hotel, where you can rest a little. Overnight in Nuwara Eliya

Day 6 - Continue to Udawalawe

On the way to Udawalawe, stop at Buduruwagala. As unspeakable as the name is, so telling is it. “Budu” in Sinhala means Buddha, “ruwa” means image and “gala” means rock. What sounds simple, however, turns out to be a masterpiece. It is the great stonemasonry and the secluded location in the middle of the jungle that makes up the magic of Buduruwagala and that it is off the tourist trampling path. Then you reach your place for today. Overnight in Udawalawe

7th day Sri Lanka’s pachyderms & safari in Udawalawe National Park

Today you take a safari in the Udawalawe National Park, which was declared a protected area in 1972. He is known for his large elephant populations. Other species that live in Udawalawe National Park are water buffaloes, wild boar, muntjaks, axish deer and mongooses. The park consists mainly of open grasslands and forests that you explore on a safari. Afterwards you will visit the project Elephant Transit Home Athuru Sevana. Here the elephants live in freedom, but are fed three times a day. On this occasion you can get closer to the fascinating animals. Overnight in Udawalawe.

Day 8 The Sinharaja Nature Reserve

A new day, a new destination: the next protected area on your trip is the Sinharaja Nature Reserve. For many, the Sanctuary of Sinharaja ("Lion King") is home to the mythical lion that the Sinhalese see as their ancestor. Over 60 percent of the trees here are endemic. Many of the animals are only found here, especially some bird species. The rainforest is one of the most beautiful in the world – if time permits, you can explore it today on a first short hike. Enjoy the view of the forests and tea plantations directly from your lodge. Overnight in Sinharaja

9th Day Hikes in the Sinharaja Nature Reserve

Today you will use it to explore the rainforest of the protected area on various hiking trails with a knowledgeable naturalist guide. You will learn a lot about the rare flora and fauna of the region. Along the way, you will pass by some observation points, where you will be lucky to see wildlife. Overnight in Sinharaja

10th Day Colonial Heritage in Galle & Mangroves by Madu Ganga

After breakfast, we continue on to the coast towards Galle. Along the way, take a boat trip through the mangroves of the Madu Ganga. Countless animals live here, the region is a true biodiversity hotspot. You will also get an interesting insight into the everyday rural life of Sri Lanka. Then visit Galle, where Dutch architecture mixes with South Asian tradition and tropical feeling. The city in the southwest of Sri Lanka was the most important port in the country before the arrival of the Portuguese in the 16th century. However, the peak in development reached Galle under Dutch rule. The current landmark is the old Dutch fort with its fortress walls and bastions. The old town inside the fortification was added to the list of World Heritage Sites in 1988. Together with your guide, you will make a round through the fort, where you will also admire the large lighthouse. In the late afternoon you will reach Hikkaduwa on the west coast. Overnight in Hikkaduwa.   

11.-13. Day Beach Stay in Hikkaduwa

These days are all about rest and relaxation. Let the soul dangle and enjoy moments when time seems to stop. You have the opportunity to explore the area on your own or spend leisure hours on the beach or in your hotel. Overnight in Hikkaduwa.

14th day of travel home

Now your break from everyday life is coming to an end. You will be taken to the airport and even if your holiday is over – the precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories of your trip will accompany you forever. Look forward to letting your friends and family share your experiences in the distance at home. Your driver guide will take you to the airport for your return flight.

Sri Lanka - Time out in harmony

Sri Lanka Ayurveda Travel - Experience a break in harmony with Asia Special Tours on your Sri Lanka round trip. Ask directly here!

Leave the hustle and bustle of everyday life behind you and do something good for your body and your mind. We will take you to the unique landscapes and bring you closer to the fascinating culture of Sri Lanka. On this trip, explore the island’s exciting past and visit impressive World Heritage sites. Sri Lanka is also a paradise for gourmets: traditional recipes, fresh ingredients and a refined combination of flavours make even simple food feast. Stay in world-class wellness hotels beautifully nestled in the peaceful nature of Sri Lanka and perfect retreats for unforgettable days.

1st day Arrival in Sri Lanka

Today your journey through Sri Lanka begins. Upon arrival at Colombo airport, you will be picked up and taken to your hotel in Sigiriya where you can rest once you arrive. Your charming hotel is idyllic nestled on a small lake in the middle of rice fields. The villas are very elegantly furnished with teak flooring and natural materials. Enjoy unadulterated nature and pure peace. Overnight in Sigiriya.

2nd Day Lion Rocks of Sigiriya & Mighty Kingdom Polonnaruwa

In the morning, a first highlight of the trip awaits you. The Sigiriya Rock is often referred to as the “Eighth Wonder of the World”. On the plateau of a monolith, which rises about 200 meters high above the surrounding area, a rock fortress was built in the 5th century. Since in former times a stone lion towered at the entrance of the bastion, this place is called "lion rock" in the local language. However, only two paws of this statue survived over the centuries. Today you can walk through a pleasure garden here, which still houses the remains of fountains, pavilions and a monastery complex. In the afternoon, continue your journey. Polonnaruwa was the capital of a powerful kingdom nearly a thousand years ago. Today, statues and palaces, impressive stupas and detailed frescoes still bear witness to the former splendor. In the southern part of Polonnaruwa you will come across the statue of King Parakramabahu, to whom the city owes its fame. Most of the city’s statues and temples were built during his tenure. You will explore the ancient city in a relaxed way by bicycle. After visiting this historical site, you will return to Sigiriya. Overnight in Sigiriya.

3rd day country life in Diyakapilla & Safari in Minneriya National Park

A trip to the idyllic village of Diyakapilla brings you closer to rural life in Sri Lanka. A short drive leads along the old pilgrimage route over the Gallinda hill chain. Arriving in the village, visit the local primary school and shrine (Wednesday and Saturday). A walk of two to three kilometers leads along a pilgrimage path through forests, agricultural areas and rice fields. By the wayside, you will always see viewpoints that serve to protect the harvest from wildlife. You will also learn how seasonally available cereals and vegetables such as rice, sweet potatoes, cassava or finger millet are harvested by traditional methods and prepared into tasty dishes such as roti, "Katta Sambol" or "Pol Sambol". Before you start your journey, enjoy a lunch prepared by a family from the village. Then we will return to your hotel. In the afternoon, take a jeep safari in Minneriya National Park. The national park is home to many fascinating animals and is best known for its large number of elephants. Around a water tank there are deer, monkeys and elephants. Overnight in Sigiriya.

Day 4 Spice Garden Matale & Tooth Temple in Kandy

Your journey will take you to Kandy. Along the way, visit a spice garden in Matale where you’ll learn about growing and processing various spices. Then you reach the former royal city of Kandy, which lies in the middle of the central mountains about 500 meters above sea level. Despite its size, it is still very much influenced by ancient traditions. The last king to rule here was Sri Wickrama Rajasinghe - then the British took power from 1815. In the middle of the city lies a lake that is perfect for a walk around. The main attraction is undoubtedly the tooth temple, in which a canine of Buddha is said to be kept in a golden shrine. Tradition has it that this tooth emanates spiritual power, making the sanctuary one of the most important pilgrimage sites in the country for Buddhists. Overnight in Kandy.

5th Day Authentic Cooking Class in Kandy & Visit of an Ayurveda Doctor

Experience Sri Lanka with all your senses. With the Tuk Tuk you will first go to the Manikkumbura General Market, where you will get the various ingredients for your lunch today. Get to know the variety of different vegetables grown by the villagers. Afterwards, Suzy shows you how to prepare a traditional meal from five different curries, which are then cooked on firewood. From scraping off the coconut to pounding the sambol with old tools, you’ll learn all about local cuisine. Afterwards, let yourself taste the delicious food. Full of new impressions, return to your hotel where you will meet an Ayurvedic doctor. This answers all questions about herbal medicine, e.g. which herbs are particularly helpful against cold or flu. Overnight in Kandy.

6.-8. Free time in Kandy

Enchanting days lie ahead of you. Your lifestyle hotel Santani is the perfect place for all Ayurvedic and wellness lovers. Every day an excellent wellness offer and yoga classes await you. Feel the special concept of the hotel “ambalama” (place of rest) and immerse yourself in your personal dream world. Overnight in Kandy.

9th day Departure to Koggala

After breakfast, leave the cultural triangle of Sri Lanka and head south where you will be taken to your beach hotel. The magical Hotel The Fortress Resort & Spa is the perfect place for a few wonderful days on the south coast of Sri Lanka. The luxury hotel is modeled on the Dutch fort and is located about 17 kilometers from the historic city of Galle. It combines the country’s colonial influences with Sri Lankan motifs and local furniture, reviving the old splendor of days gone by. The total of 53 rooms are harmoniously and tastefully furnished with dark wooden furniture and have an open bathroom. Enjoy excellent cuisine and the tastefully designed spa area. Overnight in Koggala.

10.-12. Day bathing in Koggala

These days are all about rest and relaxation. Let the soul dangle and enjoy moments when time seems to stop. You have the opportunity to explore the area on your own or spend leisure hours by the sea or in the facilities of your hotel. Overnight in Koggala.

13th day home

Now your break from everyday life is coming to an end. You will be taken to the airport and even if your holiday is over – the precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories of your trip will accompany you forever. Look forward to letting your friends and family share your experiences in the distance at home. Your driver guide will take you to the airport for your return flight.

Sumatra Round Trip - Adventures and Jungle Kings

Sumatra Tour: Go on a jungle adventure with Asia Special Tours and discover the natural treasures of Sumatra. Ask now!

Explore the magnificent nature and cultural treasures of Sumatra Island. The exciting journey from Medan to Padang connects the highlights of the island. Variety and variety is guaranteed on this trip! Experience the friendly inhabitants up close and immerse yourself in the tradition and culture of the Batak and Minangkabau. Small adventures can be experienced on the back of elephants and during a rainforest hike. The bicycle tour through the highlands of Minangkabau offers you unique insights into the life of the local population. See for yourself why Sumatra is called the great stage of Indonesian natural spectacles!

1st day Arrival in Medan

Welcome to Sumatra! Upon arrival at the airport, you will be greeted by your guide and escorted to your hotel. The rest of the day is at your disposal. Overnight in Medan.

Day 2 From the City to the Jungle

After breakfast, your guide will pick you up for a tour of the city and visit the Sultan's Palace, whose interior decor is captivated by the clever combination of traditional Malay cultural elements with Islamic, Spanish, Indian and Italian styles. You will get a deeper insight into the Muslim culture of the country when you visit the Great Mosque. Also a colorful Chinese temple is on your program. Then drive along the scenic route via Binjai to Tangkahan, a small settlement in the jungle on the edge of Gunung Leuser National Park. During the approximately 4-hour journey, there are always opportunities for stops. Overnight in Tangkahan.
Note: Please note that accommodation in Tangkahan is very simple. Alternatively, you can also visit Tangkahan as a day trip from Bukit Lawang, where a higher quality lodge is available.

3rd day elephant safari and river adventure

Today is all about the lovely pachyderms – the elephant of Sumatra. In the elephant camp you will meet a mahout who will tell you everything you need to know about his protégé. Then it is time for a bath with the elephants. Help scrub the elephants properly. You'll probably get one or the other shower, and then you'll go on an hour-long elephant trek to explore a part of the National Park. In the afternoon we will continue with the Jeep towards Bukit Lawang. The approximately three-hour, adventurous ride leads through plantations and rural areas (note: The road leads for the most part over bumpy dirt roads). You are welcome to let your chauffeur know at any time and he will stop so that you can take photos in special places. After arriving at your lodge, you can listen to the sounds of the rainforest in the evening or explore the surrounding area. Overnight in Bukit Lawang.

Day 4 Visiting the Orang Utans

It will be animal again, because today you go in the footsteps of the orangutans. You will take a half-day hike, where you will penetrate deep into the jungle of Gunung Leuser National Park. Good fitness is required for this! With a bit of luck, you will be rewarded with the sight of wild orangutans and other monkey species. Around noon you will reach your lodge again, where you can rest a little. In the afternoon, visit Timbang Lawang Eco Farm, which is committed to nature conservation and agriculture. Did you know how brown sugar is made and how gum trees are tapped? Overnight in Bukit Lawang.

Note: Camera and video fees are to be paid on site. Please note that we cannot guarantee that you will see the animals.

Day 5 - The Highlands of Berastagi

An approximately 6-hour varied ride will take you to the Berastagi highlands. The place is located at about 1300 m altitude and is framed by the two volcanoes Sinabung (2417 m) and Sibayak (2172 m). The diamond bataks live here in their traditional houses with the striking saddle roofs – eight families find space in one house. Along the way, stop at Mary Annai Velangkanni Church, which is an example of Indo-Mogul architecture. Arriving in Berastagi, you will end the day with a stroll over the fruit market with its exotic offer. Overnight in Berastagi.

Day 6 Fascination Tobasee

After breakfast, your journey will take you to Lake Toba. On the way, you will first stop in Dokan, a traditional village of Karo-Batak. You can also make a photo stop at the picturesque Sipisopiso waterfall, which plunges from 120 m into a deep gorge. In Pematang Purba, visit the magnificent wooden palace of former Batak King Simalungun while in Simarjarunjung you will enjoy a first fantastic panoramic view over Lake Toba, which is one of the deepest and largest crater lakes in the world. Around the afternoon you reach the shore and set off by boat (approx.) 1 hour) to Samosir, a volcanic island in the middle of Lake Toba. Overnight on Samosir.

Day 7: The Secrets of Samosir

Today you will explore the island of Samosir together with your guide. Your path will take you to different locations of the 40 km long and 20 km wide island. Visit the village of Ambarita, known for its megalithic chairs and tables that served as a gathering place. They are also said to have played a role in earlier cannibal rituals. In the north of the island is the small village of Simanindo with a museum. There you will also see a traditional dance performance of the Batak Toba before continuing your way to the village of Tomok, where you will visit the tomb of King Sidabutar. In Panguruaran there are sulphur-containing thermal springs. During your tour, you will always stop in beautiful places to enjoy the landscape and marvel at the grave monuments of the local population. Let yourself be captivated by the unique landscape of Lake Toba. If desired, you can hold at a local school and get to know the everyday life of the children. You may also see women weaving the traditional “Ulos” along the way. Overnight on Samosir.

8th day drive to Padang Sidempuan - The path is the destination

From the island it goes by ferry and car back to Parapat and on to Padang Sidempuan. You drive through the beautiful and fertile mountain landscape. Your view wanders over green rice fields, deep gorges, lush green jungles, villages and small markets along the way. Along the way, stop at Toba Batak village Jangga Dolok and visit a pineapple, carnation and coffee plantation. Stroll through the traditional market in Balige Sigur, see the hot springs in Sipoholon and walk along the banks of the Suralla River. Overnight in Padang Sidempuan.

Day 9 - About the Equator

Today you explore the land of the Minangkabau. Your journey continues south through spectacular scenery. In a small botanical garden, learn about tropical herbs, spices, and their uses. Your next stop is the large Muslim school of Purbabaru. In the late afternoon, cross the equator at Bonjol. From here it is about another 1½ hours to Bukittinggi, home of the Minangkabau. The region is known for its distinctive architecture, spicy cuisine, matriarchal traditions, and welcoming people. Overnight in Bukittinggi.

Day 10 In the land of Minangkabau

In the morning, travel with your guide to Puncak Lawang, which is located in the hills and offers a magnificent view of Lake Maninjau. There you will take a short walk through the dense jungle down to the lake. Along the way, stop at a local café to enjoy the beautiful scenery. Arriving in the village, you will be driven to lunch at a restaurant on the lakefront. In the afternoon, we will travel via Kota Gadang, known for his silver jewelry work, back to Bukittinggi. There you have time to visit the city. The city was founded during the colonial period in the 19th century. You’ll start at the bustling Pasat Atas Market, located directly opposite the Jam Gadang bell tower, the city’s landmark. Then we go to the former Dutch fort “De Kock”. Impressive is the view into the Sianok Canyon. After a short break, enjoy a traditional Minangkabau dance performance in the center of Bukittinggi in the evening. Overnight in Bukittinggi.

Day 11 Bicycle Tour of Rice Fields

Today, look forward to experiencing the country and its people up close on a bicycle tour. You will drive to the village of Kamang, where you will start your round trip. They cycle along rice terrace fields, mountains and small villages and get on this occasion again and again insights into everyday life. Maybe you also want to keep the farmers on the side of the road and talk to them? Your guide will gladly help with the exchange. After a picnic, continue cycling to Gadut, from where you will be transported back to your hotel by car. Overnight in Bukittinggi.

Day 12 Bukittinggi - Padang

After breakfast, drive from Bukittinggi to Padang to the airport and stop at the nature reserve on the Anai waterfall along the way. At the airport you will be said goodbye by your guide and go home or onwards.

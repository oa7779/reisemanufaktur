Family Adventures in Thailand

Thailand Family Travel: Experience the highlights of Thailand with the whole family on your individual Thailand trip with Asia Special Tours. This is an inquiry.

Embark on your very personal family adventure in Thailand. The country holds many exciting experiences for young and old to look forward to. Your journey will not only take you to Bangkok, the capital and cultural heart of the country, but also to beautiful temples and the picturesque mountain landscape around Chiang Mai, the “rose of the north”. The hospitable Thais will warmly welcome you. On a trip to the picturesque Erawan National Park, you can splash to your heart’s content in the azure water basins. In Kanchanaburi, a childhood dream comes true when you are allowed to go on touch with the gray giants. Of course, a trip with the iconic Tuk Tuk should not be missing. Enough adrenaline is provided by an exciting canoe ride on the Kwai river surrounded by jungle. Around Chiang Mai there are fascinating encounters with the monks, small hikes through picturesque mountain landscapes and traditional villages, exciting market visits and an overnight stay in the sweet Lisu Lodge, which in addition to its idyllic location also attracts great activities for the whole family. Afterwards, let your soul really dangle again and enjoy the precious time with your whole family on the white sand beach on Phuket.

Day 1 Welcome to Thailand

Welcome to Thailand! At Bangkok Airport, you will be picked up by your private guide and taken to your hotel. The fascinating city, known in the Thai language under the beautiful name “City of Angels”, has even more to offer than ornate sanctuaries and the huge river Chao Phraya used by countless ships. Countless markets, sparkling temples, gigantic shopping malls and millions of Buddha statues: Bangkok is full of life and offers adventures for young and old. The rest of the day is freely available to you. Arrive in peace or take a first small exploring tour through the urban jungle on your own. Overnight in Bangkok.

2nd day Bangkok as the locals explore

Get ready for a day full of colorful impressions of the multi-faceted metropolis. Together with your guide, hop on the Skytrain, also known as BTS, and cruise through the city to the River Chao Phraya. There you will board a boat and take a short drive to the famous temple Wat Pho with its more than a thousand Buddha figures and the gigantic reclining Buddha, next to which you suddenly feel tiny. Continue on foot to the magnificent Royal Palace, from where rulers ruled the country for centuries. Here you will feel like kings and queens from ancient times: everything glitters and sparkles wherever the eye looks. Something else dazzled by all that gold will fortify you over lunch at a local restaurant. On a subsequent boat trip on one of the major canals (Klongs), you will learn why the Thai capital is also called the “Venice of the East”. A large part of everyday life still takes place on and off the water. At the end of this eventful day, you finally make your way back to your hotel by BTS (Skytrain) or taxi. Overnight in Bangkok.

Day 3 Colorful Market Drive in and Around Bangkok

A visit to Bangkok is not complete without at least one market visit, because the colorful activity should be experienced by yourself. A particularly unusual location awaits you at the Maeklong market directly on the railway tracks. Eight times a day, the train passes by the stands only a few centimeters and sweeps sellers along with their goods from the rails with a loud signal for a short time. For the sellers has long been routine, but for visitors this is a real highlight that you can not miss. Damnoen Saduak, on the other hand, is the most popular floating market in the area. With a motorized long boat you drive past stilt houses, gardens and temples and finally reach the actual market with its whirlwind and swarm directly on the water. Back in Bangkok, you’ll have a little time to refresh yourself in your beautiful hotel pool. Opposite 18 At the clock, you will be picked up at the hotel and roar on one of the iconic tuk tuks on three wheels through the streets of evening Bangkok. Of course, you can also taste some of the Thai delicacies. You will also visit the colorful flower market and enjoy the view of the now illuminated sights – without hectic bustle. Overnight in Bangkok.

4th day bathing fun in Erawan National Park

After all the bustle in the city, a trip into nature awaits you today. After breakfast, you will be picked up from the hotel and drive on to Kanchanaburi. You will visit Erawan National Park, known for its emerald green, dense jungle and roaring waterfalls. There you will take a small hike that leads you to one of the waterfalls. You should not forget your swimwear, because after the hike it will do you good to jump into the water. Also the afternoon program is sporty-active, you take a canoe tour. Depending on your mood, you can either paddle leisurely down the Kwai River surrounded by lush jungles or face the challenge and paddle up the river. A fun and safe activity for everyone! Then you will be taken to your “floating hotel”. You will spend the night at The FloatHouse River Kwai, swimming on the river surrounded by dense jungle, which can only be reached by boat. Just wonderfully idyllic and something very special! Overnight in Kanchanaburi.

Day 5 - A day with the elephants

Today, in the lush jungle of Kanchanaburi, treat yourself to a time-out to get in touch with nature. Enter the kingdom of gentle giants and discover what the perfect day in an elephant’s life looks like: a mahout shows you how to feed the animals. You can pet them and play with them in the river before following them into the forest for more exciting insights into their world. A shared experience that promises unforgettable memories. Overnight in Kanchanaburi.

Day 6 - Continue to the North

Before making your way back to Bangkok, first visit the beautiful Kaeng lava cave, known for its chambers with impressive formations of stalactites and stalakmites. Here you can also observe numerous bats living in the cave and turning their loopings gracefully between the dripstones. In Bangkok, you will be taken to the airport to travel on to Chiang Mai by domestic flight. Once there, you will immediately be brought to your hotel and can freely arrange the rest of the day. Overnight in Chiang Mai.

7th day Chiang Mai & surroundings by bike

Strengthen yourself with an extensive breakfast before swinging onto the saddle. South of the city, pick up your bike and head off to explore the vibrant Chiang Mai and its surroundings. You’ll get to know all the different aspects of life in and around the city: from the bustling and historic center to the lively local markets and slum apartments. During this exciting bike tour, your guide will tell you all about the history, customs and local culture that make Chiang Mai a special place. Overnight in Chiang Mai.

Day 8 Excursion to Doi Inthanon National Park

Today we go out into the beautiful nature around Chiang Mai. You will spend the day in the Doi Inthanon National Park, known for its numerous waterfalls and the various mountain peoples living there. The Doi Inthanon is the southernmost foothill of the mighty Himalayas and the highest mountain in Thailand. The national park is a paradise for nature lovers and just perfect for beautiful walks. Enjoy the picturesque waterfalls, the breathtaking views of the mountains around and the beautiful temples that blend into the mountainous landscape. The vegetation is beautifully green and with a bit of luck you will discover some songbirds and pretty colorful butterflies fluttering through the air. The park is only about an hour’s drive from the city, but the surrounding area is completely different. During your visit to the National Park, you will also encounter some of the Karen mountain tribes that live on the mountain slopes and can taste locally grown coffee. Temperatures are almost always pleasant, sometimes even a bit cool, but this can be a welcome change from the hazy flatlands. Therefore, be sure to pack clothes that keep you nicely warm! Towards evening you will return to Chiang Mai. Overnight in Chiang Mai.

9th Day Food Donation to the Monks & Iconic Wat Phra That Doi Suthep

Early in the morning, visit Ton Payom Market, the city’s largest market located 10 minutes from Chiang Mai’s center. Here the locals at the stands eagerly buy gifts for the monks. Then you will attend a ceremony that is as common as it is valuable for Thais. They wait with the faithful on the side of the road for the passing monks and donate food to them. For the monks, it is the only permitted purchase of bread and the donors receive thereby merits for the next life cycle. After a little free time in the early afternoon, visit the city’s landmark just outside on a hillside: Wat Phra That Doi Suthep with its sun-shining golden Chedi. From up here, you also have a unique view of the city and the surrounding countryside. Experience a unique private blessing and witness the monk’s songs: an awesome and exotic experience! Afterwards you will return to your hotel in Chiang Mai.

10th day overnight in the middle of a dreamlike nature

Today you will visit the only tea plantation in Thailand where tea is still made in traditional handicraft. Upon arrival, you will be warmly welcomed and introduced to your personal guide for the tea garden. Together with an experienced picker, you will go to the tea bushes where you will learn how to pick fresh tea leaves by hand – of course you can help yourself. Afterwards you will visit the roasting station, where you will experience the craftsmanship of the tea up close. A delicious Thai lunch with mostly organic ingredients awaits you in the teahouse’s restaurant. In the afternoon you will reach the Lisu village. The Lisu Lodge, an oasis of tranquility, invites you to relax, hike or bike in clear mountain air. After a traditional welcome drink, the team introduces you to various activities for the afternoon. Before you decide, your Lisu guide accompanies you on a walk through the village. Among other things, you will visit the Ton Lung Temple and the herb gardens. Encounters with local families will provide you with exciting insights into the life of the Lisu community. The day ends with a dinner full of regional specialties. Overnight in Mae Taeng.

Day 11 Back to Chiang Mai

Taste your breakfast at Lisu Lodge with a view of the beautiful nature. Afterwards you will return to Chiang Mai, where the rest of the day is free to explore the city a little further on your own. Overnight in Chiang Mai.

12th day off to the island!

After collecting countless impressions of Thailand’s rich culture and natural beauty during the last few days, it is now time for some relaxation. Phuket, the largest island in Thailand, is perfect for this with its beautiful beaches. Your driver will take you to the airport and you will fly directly to Phuket. There you will spend the coming days at the beautiful Anantara Layan Phuket Resort & Spa on the stunning west coast of the island. The resort is located on a quiet section on the picturesque Layan Beach overlooking the Andaman Sea and is a dreamlike tropical holiday oasis. Stay in one of the 30 luxuriously equipped rooms and suites in modern Asian style or in one of the 47 villas with their own private pool. From your private balcony or terrace, you will enjoy fantastic views of the tropical gardens and the shimmering blue of the bay. The best restaurants on Phuket are waiting for you right at the resort. Overnight in Phuket.

13-15. Day Family time by the sea

These days belong only to you as a family. Make the time according to your personal preferences and let it go really well! Your resort offers numerous facilities and leisure activities that leave nothing to be desired. Dream days at the beach pool or get to know something new. For example, how about an exciting water sport, a taste lesson in Muay Thai kickboxing or an exotic cooking class? On excursions on water and land, explore Phuket’s fascinating nature and culture. There are no limits to your perfect holiday. Overnight in Phuket.

Day 16: Farewell to Paradise

Even the most beautiful journey comes to an end. Immerse yourself once again through the delicious breakfast buffet of your resort and enjoy the view of the beautiful surroundings. Then we say goodbye to the island paradise. Your driver will take you to Phuket Airport, from where you will travel home with many exciting experiences and colorful impressions of a very special country.

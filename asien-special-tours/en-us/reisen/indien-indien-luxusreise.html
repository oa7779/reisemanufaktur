1st day Arrival in India

Delhi – old cultural city, modern metropolis. Upon arrival, you will be expected at the airport and brought to your hotel by your private chauffeur: the iconic luxury hotel The Oberoi is located in the heart of the Indian metropolis of New Delhi. The interiors of the 220 elegant rooms and suites are inspired and tastefully decorated by Sir Edwin Lutyen’s New Delhi architecture. All rooms and suites have large panoramic windows offering sensational views of the Delhi Golf Course or the UNESCO World Heritage-listed Humayun Mausoleum. The three excellent restaurants fulfill all the culinary needs imaginable: enjoy the great international cuisine from sushi to pizza at threesixty°, let yourself be pampered with authentic Indian cuisine at Omya by internationally renowned chef Alfred Prasad or treat your taste buds at Baoshuan with delicious Chinese specialties by world-famous chef Andrew Wong. At the open-air rooftop bar Cirrus – one of the hottest and noblest bars in Delhi – you can end the day in style with innovative cocktails and breathtaking views. Of course, the 5-star house also has an exclusive spa area. The rest of the day is at your disposal. Overnight in Delhi.

Day 2: Experiencing Delhi with all your senses

Immerse yourself in the history of 2,000 years old culture. With its rich archaeological history built over generations of kingdoms, Delhi is a melting pot of cultures. The 5-sense tour of Delhi offers the unique opportunity to pamper your senses to your heart’s content. Stroll through narrow streets and let the capital of India affect you. Did you know that red sandstone and white marble were two of the most commonly used building materials in the Mughal period? Immerse yourself in the scent of nature as you visit an 8th generation Ittar studio where you can smell a wide variety of ittars and make your own unique blend. Afterwards, get a taste of Delhi’s typical cuisine: If there is a heritage of the world’s popular cuisine in Delhi, it’s butter chicken. The globally popular dish has its modest origins in a small village in Punjab. At the end of the day, embark on a spiritual journey in Delhi’s greatest Gurudwara, the city’s famous Sikh temple, and be enchanted by the sounds. Dinner will be served at the Imperial Hotel: the in-house Spice Route has been voted one of the ten best restaurants in the world by Condé Nast Traveler. Overnight in Delhi.

Day 3 - The most beautiful sides of Delhi

In the morning, you will dedicate yourself once again intensively to the most beautiful sights of Old Delhi and visit the Jama Masjid, one of the largest mosques in the world. Passing the Red Fort, the palace complex of the Mughals, your ride will take you on to the Moonlight Square – Chandni Chowk – one of the oldest and busiest market squares in the city, where you will experience the typical flair of an Oriental bazaar. At Raj Ghat, where the father of the nation was cremated, you can pay homage to Mahatma Gandhi. A contrast to Old Delhi with its good 1.500Years of history form the imperial city of New Delhi with its wide avenues and imposing buildings. 1931 Designed by the British occupiers as a modern garden city, it now houses the government district, presidential house and the National Monument India Gate, modeled on the Arc de Triomphe in Paris and commemorating the 1World War I was built. But also the urban area of New Delhi offers fascinating historical sights: for example, with the 72 Metre-high Qutb minaret and the mausoleum of the great mogul Humayun, considered an architectural precursor to the Taj Mahal. Finally, visit the Gurudwara Bangla Sahib Temple – the largest Sikh sanctuary in Delhi.

Day 4 Agra - Witness of the Mughal Age

After breakfast, leave Delhi and make your way to Agra. Once a small village on the banks of the Yamuna, thanks to the Mughal monarchs Akbar and his grandson Shah Jehan, the city developed into the second capital of the Mughal Empire. The Mughals were also great builders and their best architectural wonders for Agra and its surroundings can still be admired today. Your luxurious accommodation for the next two nights is The Oberoi Amarvilas, from where you will have a fantastic view of the fairytale Taj Mahal, which you will already look forward to visiting. The first Oberoi hotel not only enchants with its excellent location, but also with sensational service and impressive architecture. The 102 rooms and suites are tastefully decorated and exude Indian flair. There are some elements in the design that pay respect to the splendor of Mughal palaces. Refresh yourself in the delightfully cool pool before enjoying fine international and Indian specialty cuisine at one of the exquisite restaurants or relaxing with a cocktail at Agra’s only bar with Taj Mahal views. The Oberoi Amarvilas was rightly named “Best Resort Hotel in India” at the 2018 Travel + Leisure World’s Best Awards. Overnight in Agra.

5th Day Morning Yoga Hour Overlooking the Taj Mahal

Today the time has come: you see the fairytale Taj Mahal with your own eyes. When early in the morning the fog hangs over the water basins of the garden and the sun rises slowly over the magnificent structure is the best time to visit the impressive monument. In incomparable perfection and beauty shines this “most expensive proof of love ever” built by Mughal emperor Shah Jahan for his beloved wife Mumtaz Mahal. If you wish, you can admire the majestic structure during a private yoga hour on a hill just 150 meters away. With new energy, visit two other masterpieces of Mughal architecture: the monumental Fort Agra, which is also a UNESCO World Heritage Site alongside the world-famous Taj Mahal, and the Itmad-ud-Daulah Mausoleum, nicknamed “Baby Taj”. It was completed two years before the foundation stone of the Taj Mahal and served as an architectural model. After this impressive day, immerse yourself in the culinary tradition of Agras over a romantic dinner on the private balcony of your room while enjoying breathtaking views of the gardens and the distinctive silhouette of the Taj Mahal. Overnight in Agra.

6th day ghost town Fatehpur Sikri

On your way to Ranthambore, visit the ruined city of Fatehpur Sikri, which is one of the most important testimonies of Mughal architecture. With red sandstone bricks, the Grand Mogul Akbar had his capital built here in the 16th century, but it had to be abandoned shortly after completion due to a lack of water. It has been preserved almost ghostly to this day and combines two opposites: the figurative architecture of the Hindus and the geometrical architecture of Islam. Ranthambore National Park has a reputation for providing the best way to observe shy tigers in the world. In 1980, the area was transferred to a nature reserve for endangered animals. Over the years, the cats have shifted their activities to the day and reduced their fear of humans, so that very good observation possibilities arise. Your accommodation at the National Park is The Oberoi Vanyavilas, whose structure resembles a picture book fantasy. It offers you luxury tents that contrast with nature due to their exclusive facilities. The distinctions of the fine tents of the luxury hotel and the Indian jungle are combined as you sit on your sun deck and absorb nature and atmosphere in peace. Overnight in Ranthambore. Overnight in Ranthambore.

Day 7: In the footsteps of the Tigers

The tiger, once king of the jungle, is severely threatened. Spend all day on private safari tours in the National Park (morning and afternoon) and experience India’s exciting wildlife. You have the opportunity to observe animals that have become very rare on our planet. Your chances are not bad to see one or more specimens of the majestic Bengal tigers. But of course not only tigers populate the national park, whose rich plant life also ranks around the ruins of a fort from the 10th century. With a bit of luck, you can also watch leopards and hyenas. Swamp crocodiles live in the shallow waters and the air is filled with the song of countless birds. It is best to always keep your camera handy. Overnight in Ranthambore.

8th day time to enjoy

Without tour guide & chauffeur

Today you can simply let your soul dangle and drift – this day is yours alone. Breakfast at your leisure, treat yourself to a massage at the luxurious spa of your resort or go on a boat trip on the Chambal River in search of the endangered Gharial – a crocodile species that lives only in Nepal and northern India. Alternatively, we are happy to organize more private Jeepsafaris for you. Overnight in Ranthambore.

9th day Romantic sunset in dreamlike backdrop

After a few beautiful days in the wilderness of Ranthambore National Park, you will return to civilization. Your next destination is Jaipur, capital of the Indian state of Rajasthan. In the late 19th century, the city was painted in shades of pink in preparation for the visit of the British king – in Rajasthan’s tradition, this is the color of hospitality. To this day, Jaipur is still called the “Pink City”. Let yourself be transported by the charming city on a journey back in time into an era of glamour, royalty and tradition. The next three nights will be spent in the breathtaking The Oberoi Rajvilas. Make your Indian dream of enchanted palaces and exotic gardens come true. Simple elegance welcomes you in the individually furnished feel-good rooms. A fireworks display of aromas awaits you in the elegant restaurant. In the spa area, luxurious applications bring your soul back to balance. In the evening, a very special experience awaits: an exclusive sunset cocktail against the backdrop of the magnificent Naila Fort. Your Oberoi driver drives you in a noble car around Jaipur to the magnificently restored 18th-century Naila Fort. Enjoy delicious cocktails in front of this unique backdrop and watch the sunset. Overnight in Jaipur.

Day 10 Perfect start to the day: Hot air balloon ride over Jaipur

Early in the morning, a highlight of your trip awaits you: a hot air balloon ride. Enjoy glide weightlessly over castles and palaces and admire the mountains, lakes and villages of Rajasthan from above. Observe the daily life of India from a different perspective, admire hidden treasures from airy heights and marvel at the incredible interplay of colors and contours of the sky. After breakfast at the hotel, explore the new part of Jaipur far from the bustle of the old town. In a gorgeous vintage car, you will ride on wide streets and avenues past some of the sights of modern Jaipur such as Birla Mandir Temple. Your journey ends at Narain Niwas Palace, one of the best heritage hotels in the city. The hotel is known for the culinary expertise of the hotel operators who belong to the once powerful Kanota family. Take the opportunity to take an interactive cooking class with a family member to try two family recipes that the family guards like a treasure. Afterwards you can taste your own prepared dinner and also try the liqueur, which was once exclusively distilled for and by the Kanota family. Finally, you will be driven back to your hotel. Overnight in Jaipur.

Day 11 Magic Experience: A Traditional Aarti Ceremony

After breakfast, a masterpiece of Mughal architecture awaits you: the imposing Fort Amber perched on a steep mountain slope north of Jaipur. With the Jeep you drive up the winding pavement road to the main gate of the facility. Behind it you can expect the public audience hall, the magnificent Ganesha Gate and the Spiegelpalast. From the galleries and terraces you have a wonderfully wide view over the valley and the mountain world of the Aravalli chain. On the way back, you will stop at the “Palace of the Winds”, behind whose ornate facade the ladies of the court observed the activity on the road. Jaipur itself also impresses with magnificent architecture. You will visit the magnificent City Palace, which witnesses an era of abundance and in part still serves today as the residence of the former Maharaja. After a free afternoon, a special experience awaits you in the evening: in the private 18th-century Shiva Temple, you will experience the spirituality of India up close at a traditional Aarti ceremony. The temple belonging to the Oberoi is located on a small island and can be reached via a bridge that leads over a pretty water lily pond. In a solemn atmosphere, burning oil lamps are offered to the god there - a truly magical moment with goosebumps guaranteed. Overnight in Jaipur.

12th day Pilgrimage Place Pushkar

Your journey will take you to Udaipur, the “Venice of the East”. Along the way, stop at Pushkar, one of India’s most important pilgrimage sites. The place is located on the sacred Pushkar lake, where many devout Hindus pilgrimage every year to wash themselves clean and bless their family with health. Pushkar is home to one of India’s rare Brahma temples. In addition, every year in November the largest known market in the world takes place here – the camel market or Pushkar Fair – to which hundreds of thousands of people come. Once in Udaipur, you will be taken to your hotel: The Oberoi Udaivilas. This luxury hotel is located directly on the shores of Lake Pichola and offers fantastic views over the water. The unique architecture with light-flooded corridors and elegant domes reflects Udaipur’s floor plan. The 87 rooms and suites, furnished with magnificent furniture and inspired by the Mewar style, offer the best possible comfort and fantastic views. In some rooms and suites you can enjoy the luxury of a private or semi-private pool. Upon arrival, the rest of the day is freely available to you. Leave your body and mind with a treatment at the first-class spa and enjoy palate delights in one of the excellent restaurants overlooking Lake Pichola as well as the City Palace. Overnight in Udaipur.

Day 13 The most beautiful sites Udaipurs & cooking with the masters

Udaipur is dominated by its lakes – this is why the romantic city is also called “Venice of the East”. The royal past is reflected in the rich ornaments on the doors of houses in the Old Town, palaces, gardens and monuments. The highlight of Udaipur is its city palace: one part of the winding building complex is open to the public as a museum, another serves as a residence for the acting head of Mewar, another houses a luxury hotel. Also extremely worth seeing is the Jagdish Temple, surrounded by a defensive wall, a Vishnu sanctuary in the traditional style. Above all, pay attention to the figure friezes that run in ribbons around the temple with expressive depictions of elephants, dancers and musicians. Of course, in a city whose nickname contains the word “Venice”, you will also take a private boat trip on the picturesque Lake Pichola. Towards evening, you will dive deeper into the secrets of Rajasthan’s cuisine during a culinary experience in a class of its own. Let one of the chefs at The Oberoi Udaivilas show you the exquisite kitchen garden and introduce you to the variety of Indian spices. Afterwards, you are invited to prepare a sensational menu with the master personally. Overnight in Udaipur.

Day 14 The impressive Jain temples of Ranakpur

Today you can look forward to a trip to the surroundings around Udaipur. Your day begins with a ride to the impressive Kumbalgarh fortress, majestically perched on a mountain ridge. Afterwards we continue through the Aravelli mountains to the famous Jain temples of Ranakpur. The temple complex is characterised by a great love for detail: a forest of columns supports the dome decorated with intricate geometric patterns and figures of goddesses. The temple has 29 rooms and 1444 columns, the architecture of which reflects the Jain myths and their symbolism. It is said that not a single pillar resembles another. Jainism is one of the original religions of India. His teaching is based on finding the way to liberation and happiness by living a nonviolent and renounced life. Along the way, enjoy a gourmet picnic lunch before visiting the Adivasi villages of Bheels, Meenas and Garasiyas and catch a glimpse of the cultural practices there. In the evening you will return to your hotel in Udaipur. Overnight in Udaipur.

15th day flight to Delhi and onward travel to Gurgaon

In the morning, your chauffeur will take you to the airport to board your flight to Dehli. Upon arrival at Delhi airport, you will be warmly welcomed and greeted. Afterwards you will be driven to your exclusive accommodation The Oberoi Gurgaon. The hotel stands out for its unrestricted luxury as well as its elegant and classic design. Your room has a fascinating panorama. The interior is designed with attention to detail and invites you to relax and feel good with the highest level of comfort. Here enjoyment is offered to all the senses. With soothing wellness treatments at the Oberoi Spa, you can let the soul dangle. The 24-hour butler service also offers additional convenience. For dinner, you will be taken on a unique taste journey and spoiled with exquisite delicacies in the excellent, hotel-owned restaurant “Threesixtyone”. Overnight in Gurgaon.

Day 16 Back to Delhi and Farewell to India

Even the most beautiful journey comes to an end. During the last few days you have gathered many unique impressions of the diversity of Indian culture and cuisine and can feel like queens in your fairytale hotels. The suitcases full of exclusive experiences and lasting memories will take you to the airport where you will travel home.

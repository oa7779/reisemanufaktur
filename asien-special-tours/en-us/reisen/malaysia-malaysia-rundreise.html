Malaysia Round Trip - Actively experiencing the highlights of West Malaysia

Malaysia Round Trip from Asia Special Tours. Experience the highlights of West Malaysia actively on your individual Malaysia tour. Ask now!

Do you like to be active in sports and are you always on the move when traveling? Then this trip is just right for you. Explore the ancient trading city of Malacca by bicycle and the multifaceted metropolis of Kuala Lumpur on foot, wander through the mystical moss forest, the dense mountain forest and the beautiful tea plantations in the Cameron Highlands and explore the Malaysian rainforests with their fascinating wildlife on land and water. From the highest point of Teresek Hill in Taman Negara National Park, you will be rewarded with a magnificent view for the effort of climbing. Of course, you also have time in between to relax a little. With many adventures and lasting impressions richer you return to your home after almost two weeks.

Day 1 Start of your trip - Welcome to Malaysia

Welcome to Malaysia! Your journey begins in the capital Kuala Lumpur, where you will be welcomed and warmly greeted. Together, you will drive on to the charming colonial city of Malacca. A colorful mixture of peoples gives Malacca a very special atmosphere. After arriving at the hotel, the rest of the day is free. Take a first stroll through the area or relax in the hotel from your arrival. Your hotel for the next two nights is The Majestic Malacca. In the original mansion from the 1920s, the old architecture blends harmoniously with modern style components. The spacious spa area is the ideal place to find peace and relaxation after a city walk. The city hotel is located in the heart of Malacca on the shores of the historic Sungai Malacca River, which used to be navigated by merchant ships from all over the world. Overnight in Malacca.

2nd day to explore Malacca by bike

Your day starts actively – with an approximately three-hour bicycle tour through the old trading city. The trail takes you mostly off the roads in a loop around and through the city, which is about twenty kilometers long. Even for less trained cyclists, the track is good to master. Along the way, your experienced mountain bike guide will show you some of the most beautiful areas of the city. Take your time to properly enjoy the surroundings and your tour. Also stop in between to rest or chat with the locals. The tour is as flexible and unique as you. After lunch, take a closer look at the city with your guide and visit the central Red Square with Christ Church and the bell tower. You’ll also see the oldest functioning Chinese temple in Malaysia and walk past the ruins of St. Paul’s Church and Porta de Santiago. A special highlight is the shipping on the Malacca River. Sit back and enjoy Malacca from a different angle. Overnight in Malacca.

Day 3 Back to the Capital

After breakfast, make your way back to Malaysia’s vibrant capital. On the way, you will make a stopover in the small village of Cantik, where you will get an insight into the rural life of the region. During a walk through the village you will notice the traditional stilt houses of the region. The inhabitants of the village will show you how to extract rubber from rubber trees, grow rice and for what different applications they use palm oil. Your guide will be happy to help you get in touch with the villagers. After lunch at a local restaurant (optional), continue your drive to Kuala Lumpur. Overnight in Kuala Lumpur.

4th day discover Kuala Lumpur on foot

Look forward to many fascinating impressions of the exciting metropolis. Together with your guide, explore Kuala Lumpur like a “local” on foot and by public transport. You will start with the Masjid India market and explore the area also affectionately known as "Little India". Stroll along the Jalan Tun Perak towards Jamek Mosque, the oldest mosque in the city. With an authentic Indian banana leaf lunch, you’ll fortify yourself for the afternoon program, which will first take you to the Sultan Abdul Samad building in Moorish style. Its 41-meter-high bell tower is nicknamed "Big Ben of Malaysia". Then stroll through Independence Square, where the Malay flag was hoisted for the first time in the country’s history after independence from Britain on 31 August 1957. You will also see the Cathedral of Mary, the first brick church of the native states on the Malay Peninsula. From here, it’s a short walk to the Central Market, which showcases local crafts. Also take a look at two of the city’s oldest Chinese and Hindu temples, as well as Chinatown. Overnight in Kuala Lumpur.

5th Day Juicy Green Cameron Highlands

Before continuing your journey, the Batu Caves, an important Hindu sanctuary, await your visit. A staircase with 272 steps leads to this large, natural limestone cave. You will also make a short stop at the Royal Selangor Tin Tableware Factory. Then drive along the winding road to the Camero Highlands, located at an altitude of 1,500 meters above sea level. The colonial rulers already appreciated the fresh mountain air as a change from the tropical heat in the flatlands. Not only is it pleasantly cool here, but you also have a wonderful view of the lush green hillside landscape with its fertile tea plantations, small villages and waterfalls. In addition, many ethnic tribes still live in the local forests today. After your arrival, the rest of the day is freely available to you. Overnight in the Cameron Highlands.

Day 6 through the dense mountain forest

Enjoy your breakfast in the pleasant mountain air – it tastes even better. Well fortified, climb into the Land Rover and drive along the scenic hilly tea plantations towards Gunung Brinchang, the highest mountain in the Cameron Highlands at a height of 2,000 meters. Up there you can let the glances wander and marvel at the surrounding landscapes from a bird’s eye view. Walking through the moss-covered, ancient forest, your guide will show you some of the most exotic plants in the world such as pot plants, orchids, ferns, spices and medicinal plants. You will be amazed at the variety of plants you will discover here. In the afternoon a small hike through the dense mountain forest awaits you. You will learn from your hiking guide a lot of interesting things about the valuable medicinal plants growing here and the ecosystem of the rainforest. Since in many places the sunlight does not penetrate to the ground, it is easier to wander through the relatively light undergrowth. So you can look for the valuable medicinal plants. After your hike, you will return to your hotel richer by many impressions. Overnight in the Cameron Highlands.

7th Day Night Jungle Hike

No visit to the Cameron Highlands is complete without a visit to the famous tea plantations. That’s why you head to one this morning to learn more about the process of tea making. The view over the wonderful surrounding tea plantations is simply enchantingly beautiful and the perfect start to the day. Of course, you also have to try a cup of tea before then breaking into the Belum Valley. On the way, stop at Kuala Kangsar, the ancient Sultan city of Perak. There you will see the Ubudiah Mosque, one of the most beautiful mosques in Malaysia. You will also see Malay College, which used to host only the sons of princely Malay families, as well as Iskandariah Palace and the oldest rubber tree in the Malaysian Peninsula. In the afternoon, you will drive through small villages to the Belum Valley. Enjoy the original landscape along the way and see the locals doing their day work. Finally, reach your hotel on the island of Banding. After dinner, a special experience awaits you: You will take a nightly hike through the jungle to observe the diverse wildlife of Malaysia. Overnight at Banding Island.

Day 8 All day in the rainforest

The Belum Valley has rich natural resources that you will explore today. You start the day with a boat trip through the valley. In addition to a large number of exotic animals, the “Orang Asli”, natives of the area, who have preserved their tribal traditions, also live here. In addition, take a small hike where you will find a Rafflesia in full bloom with a bit of luck. The largest flower in the world can expel flowers up to one meter in diameter. At a salt lick you may discover some of the forest’s animal inhabitants, such as Asian elephants, tigers and gibbons. Afterwards we will take the boat to Sira Basah, where you will take a small hike into the jungle. Encourage yourself with a picnic overlooking the river before being taken by boat to an idyllic, small waterfall. Refresh yourself in the cool water and listen to the sounds of the jungle. After this day full of colorful impressions of the Malaysian jungle, you will finally return to your resort. Overnight at Banding Island.

9th day on to the coast

The coast is calling! After breakfast, make your way to Kuala Terengganu, where you will arrive in the afternoon. Use the rest of the day to explore the area or relax at your resort. Terrapuri Resort consists of lovingly restored, traditional wooden houses nestled beautifully among palm trees in the seaside lagoon. The name Terrapuri translates to “Land of Palaces”. The ground plan of the resort is actually based on the Terengganu Palace from the 17th century. A beautiful patch of earth to let the soul dangle. Overnight in Kuala Terengganu.

Day 10 Traditional Malay craftsmanship

Along the coast we continue to Kuantan. On the way, you will visit a few villages and have the opportunity to immerse yourself in the everyday life of the inhabitants. Then take a small boat trip and see how brass goods are made in the traditional way. In the afternoon you will get an insight into the weaving of Pandan leaves, for example in bags and mats. Richer by many exciting impressions, you finally arrive in Kuantan. Overnight in Kuantan. 

Day 11 - Longboat through the Taman Negara Rainforest

From the hotel you will be taken this morning to the pier of Kuala Tembeling. Once there, board a traditional longboat that takes you deeper along the river into the rainforest of Taman Negara National Park. The journey itself is already a real experience. Sit back and enjoy the wonders of nature that will unfold before your eyes. Exotic fish shimmer under the surface of the water and in the lush green you might spot a tarana. Arriving at the boat dock, you only separate a few stairs from your jungle resort. After dinner at the resort, take a night walk through the jungle. Your guide knows the area like the back of his hand and with a bit of luck you will see some of the nocturnal animals living here on the site. The sounds of the jungle will eventually accompany you to sleep. Overnight in Taman Negara.

12th day jungle hike in Taman Negara National Park

Good morning, Malaysia! The new day welcomes you with the sounds of the jungle – perfect to start an active program full of energy. After breakfast at the lodge, meet your guide and embark on a hike through the jungle that will take you to the highest point of Teresek Hill. From here you have a fantastic view over the rainforest of the National Park, one of the oldest jungles in the world. During your hike, your guide will explain to you different plants and small animal species. For more exciting information about the flora and fauna of Malaysia get on a boat that takes you to the waterfall Lata Berkoh. Enjoy this little adventure on the river with its rapids. It will be a refreshing experience! After this eventful day, your delicious dinner awaits you at the resort. Overnight in Taman Negara.

Day 13 Back to the capital and farewell to Malaysia

After a final breakfast, accompanied by the sounds of the jungle, make your way back to civilization. What remains are memories of great adventures and exciting encounters with the diverse flora and fauna of the country. By long boat you will return to the Kuala Tembling dock and from there by car back to Kuala Lumpur, where you will arrive in the afternoon. Your driver will take you directly to the airport for your flight home. Or would you rather spend one or two more days in the multicultural metropolis? We are happy to arrange this for you.

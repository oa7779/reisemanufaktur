Gulf paradise Vietnam

Vietnam Golf Tour: Golf Paradise Vietnam of Asia Special Tours. Ask here directly for your tailor-made Vietnam trip!

Itinerary & services

English itinerary text is an offline machine translation of the German original for this team preview. Please confirm travel details with your advisor; the German source remains available through the language switch.

Are you a passionate golfer and would like to pursue your hobby when traveling? Then this trip is made for you. You will travel to the best and most beautifully landscaped golf courses in Vietnam. With a view of the sea or in the middle of a tropical plant, you can work on your handicap. But not only the sensational golf courses will make your heart beat faster. Of course, do not miss the cultural beauties of Vietnam. The charming Hanoi roam as well as the bustling Saigon. You will get to know the picturesque port town of Hoi An and walk past imperial tombs in Hue. And as you sail through the famous Halong Bay and the fertile Mekong Delta, you can just sit back and enjoy. You will stay in first-class hotels with exclusive service, which leave few wishes unfulfilled. Break away – the golf paradise Vietnam is waiting for you!

1st day Arrival in Vietnam & first orientation tour of Hanoi

Welcome to Vietnam! At Hanoi airport, you will be welcomed by your guide and taken to your hotel. In the exclusive Sofitel Legend Metropole, you will feel transported back to the colonial period. The “Grande Dame” by Hanoi exudes the charm of long gone times. This elegant French colonial-style house is located in the middle of the historic city center, making it the ideal starting point for your visits to Vietnam’s capital. A luxurious spa with pool as well as several very good restaurants offer relaxation and enjoyment after sightseeing. A first orientation walk (optionally you can also take a walk in a typical bicycle rickshaw) leads you through the old town of Hanoi, the “quarter of 36 alleys”. With its tree-lined avenues, French colonial architecture, peaceful lakes and pagodas, this city offers many highlights, the first of which you can already see today. Overnight in Hanoi.

Day 2: Discover Hanoi from a different angle

After breakfast, you will be picked up by a Freiburg journalist and former restaurant owner who has lived in Hanoi for several years and is well versed in the city. Together you walk through the historic old town and learn a lot about politics, religion and the social coexistence of the Vietnamese in their narrow houses. Immerse yourself in a tangle of old, small alleys that is probably unique throughout Asia. How is it living here? What do Vietnamese people buy for dessert in the market? What are the property prices? Here in the heart of Hanoi you will learn more about the Vietnamese soul. True to the Vietnamese motto, “the best food is on the street,” try a traditional bun cha (grilled meatballs in a fish soup with plenty of fresh herbs and salads). Then visit the Military Museum with a 10-minute cinematic screening of the historic victory of the Vietnamese over the French in Dien Bien Phu. The path also takes you past the mausoleum. On Ba Dinh Square you will get some background information about the cult of personality around “Uncle Ho”, why he has to lie in a glass coffin. From there it is not far to the Temple of Literature. Overnight in Hanoi.

3rd day of golf at Kings Island Mountain View

Without guidance

After a restorative breakfast, you leave for 18-hole King’s Island golf course, The first golf course in North Vietnam. The course is located about 45 kilometers from Hanoi on the banks of the reservoir Dong Mo, in the shadow of mountain Ba Vi. The 350-hectare King’s Island Golf Resort and Country Club is one of the most scenic golf destinations in the country. After playing golf, return to Hanoi in the late afternoon. Overnight in Hanoi.

4th Day Cruise through the World Heritage Site of Halong Bay

Today, another highlight awaits you: a junk cruise through the breathtaking Halong Bay. While driving to Halong, enjoy the sight of the agricultural areas of the Red River delta. See water buffalo at work and everyday Vietnamese life. Nearly 2,000 islands lie in the bay, from whose emerald green waters massive rock formations of limestone rise. The place has made it to Hollywood as a setting: In the 1974 James Bond film “The Man with the Golden Colt”, the agent goes on a criminal hunt. You will experience the magical atmosphere in this picturesque environment but quite relaxed. Around noon, you will board a junk where you will be greeted with a welcome drink. Afterwards, there will be a lunch of fresh seafood on board as you sail through the magical bay. During various landings you can explore the “Surprise Grotto” or enjoy a beautiful all-round view of the scenery. Overnight on board the junk.

5th day of golf at Chi Linh Star and Golf Country Club

Without guidance

If you are one of the early risers, you can experience the magical Halong Bay at the most beautiful time of day: The rising sun breaks through the early fog and immerses the bay in a magical light. If you wish, you can gather strength for the day during a few tai chi exercises or still join the excursion of the ship program. While you have breakfast in peace, slowly cruise back to the harbor. In the late morning you have solid ground under your feet again. On the way back to Hanoi, you can enjoy a round of golf at Chi Linh Star and Golf Country Club in front of a scenic setting. After making the 18 holes, return to Hanoi. Overnight in Hanoi.

6th Day Flight to Central Vietnam & Charming La Residence Hotel & Spa

Let this day go on calmly. Have a leisurely breakfast or make one more round of the city, where life is already busy in the early morning. At noon you will be taken to the airport and fly on to Hue in central Vietnam. The name Hue means harmony: gracefully reflected in the perfume river, dreaming in the shadow of old palaces and other imperial buildings. A UNESCO World Heritage Site, Hue attracts everyone with its famous citadel, palaces, temples and imperial tombs that bear witness to a great and fascinating past. You are already expected at the airport and taken to your Hotel La Residence Hotel & Spa. The design of the hotel takes up the Art Nouveau style of the former governor’s house in noble materials and forms. With great care, the charm, comfort and atmosphere of a classic colonial villa could be preserved. While each room offers all the modern amenities, the days gone by are palpable in its noble design and attention to detail. Overnight in Hue.

Day 7 On foot, by bike and boat on imperial tracks through Hue

Hue is idyllic between the mountains and the sea. Its gardens, lakes and canals were once sung by poets – today it is a UNESCO World Heritage Site. In the morning you will visit the famous Imperial Citadel, which is located on the banks of the Perfume River. Modeled on the forbidden city in Beijing, this place bears witness to Hue’s glamorous past as the capital of the Nguyen dynasty. By bicycle along the perfume river, you get to the Thien-Mu Pagoda, a Buddhist monastery dating back to 1601. Legend has it that at night on this hill an old woman was found wearing a long red dress and green trousers, claiming that this place belonged to a deity and demanded that a pagoda be built in the name of that deity. This is why the building is called "The Pagoda of the Ancient Goddess". If possible, the monks living here will gladly give you closer insights into their daily lives and their faith. On the way to the pagoda, stop at one of the typical garden houses for which Hue is famous. These facilities are subject to precise geometric specifications in order to give visitors a relaxed feeling. Then a boat trip will take you to the Emperor’s Tomb of Minh Mang – based on the Chinese model. Overnight in Hue.

8th day of golf at Laguna Lang Co Golf Club

Without guidance

Over the scenic cloud pass you drive further south. In good weather you can see here to the coast! On the way, stop for another round of golf in Laguna Lang Co Golf Club, which was opened only in March 2013. Designed by Nick Faldo, the course is located about an hour north of Danang, directly on the sea and thus offers an impressive backdrop – you have to expect a slight breeze here. The 18-hole golf course is equally suitable for experienced players and beginners. Afterwards you will reach the exquisite luxury hotel Four Seasons The Nam Hai. The hotel offers everything you need to leave everyday life behind and enjoy the here and now to the fullest. Nestled in a beautiful tropical garden, the stylish villas offer an elegant-puristic design based on the Feng Shui principle. Contemporary Vietnamese works of art and high-quality materials provide a pleasant feel-good ambiance in the villas. Enjoy luxury and comfort at the highest level. Fine restaurants, a very attentive service, several pools and an excellent spa area leave nothing to be desired. Overnight in Hoi An.

9th day Discovering picturesque Hoi An & time out by the sea

The tranquil merchant town of Hoi An has never lost its old charm. Holidaymakers feel the life of the old colonial period in almost every alley. Today, Hoi An is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and the old town is a car-free zone. On a city tour, you will discover the picturesque old port city, which was the most important port in Vietnam in the 16th century. During your walking tour, you will visit the Japanese Bridge, Chinese Temple and Phung Hung House, among others. By the way, Hoi An is also the city of tailors and lamps – at least the latter you will quickly see for yourself if you stroll through the streets. The rest of the day is at your disposal. For example, a stroll through local markets is great for buying some souvenirs at home, make yourself cozy in one of the many small restaurants and cafes or enjoy sun, beach and sea and an exclusive spa in your dream hotel. Overnight in Hoi An.

10.-11. Day Golf at Montgomerie Golf Club Links

Without guidance

These two days are all about the game of golf on the course of the Montgomerie Links Golf Clubs Vietnam. It lies between Danang and Hoi An and was designed by 2010 eight-time Order of Merit and Ryder Cup winner Colin Montgomerie. The course is repeatedly recognized as one of the most elite golf courses in Asia and challenges the best players in the world. It is also suitable and playable for all players with medium and high handicap. Overnight in Hoi An.

12th day Flight to Saigon & City Tour of the bustling Saigon

They fly further south to Saigon. Modern skyscrapers stand side by side in Vietnam’s largest city with colonial buildings and traditional temples. On the streets an army of mopeds crackles, in between bicycle rickshaws drive. Young people make phone calls with their mobile phones, in the quiet backyards, believers pray amidst clouds of incense. Let these contrasts work on you during your full-day city tour. Among the most famous attractions are the Presidential Palace, the War Museum, Notre-Dame Cathedral, the old Saigon Post Office and the former City Hall. Your guide, whom you meet at Saigon airport, can tell you all kinds of things. You will then be taken to your exclusive luxury hotel Park Hyatt, which is undisputedly one of the top addresses in Saigon. The rooms and suites are furnished in the noble, classical French colonial style and were extensively renovated in 2015. The multi-award-winning hotel restaurants are among the best restaurants in all of Vietnam and make for an unforgettable culinary feast. The elegant Park Hyatt combines class with the highest comfort, excellent service and best location in the heart of the city. Overnight in Saigon.

13th Day Golf at Long Thanh Golf Resort & Visit to A&O Show

Take a deep breath and take a break from the hustle and bustle of Saigon. About an hour from the Megacity is the 36-hole golf course Long Thanh Golf ClubOn the 350-hectare site, you can wander between your tees and see a unique panorama. The area was designed by the world-renowned company Golf Plan – with palm gardens and waterfalls. 18 of the 36 holes were created on a hill and are particularly challenging, the rest can also master ambitious new golfers. For a particularly stylish ending of this evening, the visit to the A-O-Show in Saigon. This unique art form, in an innovative symbiosis of music, acrobatics and effects, also known as the “New Circus”, gives an insight into the soul and cultural heritage of the Vietnamese. Overnight in Saigon.

14th day of golf at Vietnam Golf & Country Club

Without guidance

Your goal today is to Vietnam Golf & Country Club. It is the first 36-hole golf course in the country, about an hour from the center of Saigon. The area had to be changed only slightly in order to serve as a golf course – here you experience a particularly natural golf enjoyment. Then return to Saigon. How about a spectacular ending of the day at one of Saigon’s hottest bars? The exclusive Chill Skybar is located at breezy altitude on the 25th floor of the AB Tower and is the perfect place for an unforgettable sundowner. Overnight in Saigon.

15th day off on a cruise in the enchanting Mekong Delta

The Mekong with its secondary arms shapes the landscape in the south of Vietnam just as much as the lives of its inhabitants. Palms weigh in the wind, the bananas hang hard on the perennials. Water buffaloes sow on rivers, women row through narrow canals. Many people live on or on the water and make a living from fishing. Others grow rice, fruits and vegetables on land. In the morning, head towards Cai Be, the starting point for your cruise on the “Bassac”: the best and most convenient way to explore the Mekong Delta. While you are served a welcome drink, the ship departs slowly and after lunch you can relax on deck before visiting a traditional village. Here you will not only learn more about working in the fruit and rice fields, but also have the opportunity to talk to the locals and get to know the special lifestyle in the delta. Back on board there is still some time to end the day at a sundowner. After dinner, the Bassac anchors at Tra On’s floating market for the night. Overnight on the ship.

16th Day Floating Market of Cai Rang & Departure

Enjoy the sunrise on board and fortify yourself with an extensive breakfast. Meanwhile, the ship sails towards Can Tho and continues to the floating market of Cai Rang. Incidentally, the goods offered for sale here can be recognized by the specimens that dangle on the boats as an exhibit of high posts. Not only delicacies of every kind, also craftsmanship is offered. After a detailed visit to the market, you will be taken by small boat to the pier of Ninh Kieu, from where you will return to Saigon, where your journey ends. We are happy to submit a proposal for a subsequent bathing extension, for example on the island of Phu Quoc or Con Dao.

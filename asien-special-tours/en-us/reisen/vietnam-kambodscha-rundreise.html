Cambodia Behind the Scenes

Cambodia Tour behind the scenes with Asia Special Tours. Inquire directly here and have your individual Cambodia trip created.

This enchanting journey will take you on new paths to the highlights of Cambodia and also some insider tips from this great country. Of course, the legendary temple world of Angkor will also captivate you, but you will also get to know the “beyond” and experience all facets of Cambodia. Enjoy authentic insights into the typical everyday life of the population and look forward to unique encounters with the warm hosts. You will discover places that only a few tourists can find – and in very different ways. Especially on the bicycle and on your boat trips you will experience a lot! In between, there is enough space for a flexible time division and very private moments. Embark on the great adventure through Cambodia and travel between impressive history and the unadulterated present.

1st day Arrival in Siem Reap & Cambodian Welcome Dinner

Welcome to the enchanting Cambodia! Upon arrival in Siem Reap, you will be expected at the airport by your guide and chauffeur and escorted to the hotel. Siem Reap is an up-and-coming small town – and will be your starting point in the next few days to explore the world-famous Angkor Park with about 40 square kilometers and more than 100 temples dating from the 9th to 13th centuries. After a short rest break you get to know the small town in the late afternoon on an orientation walk. The name “Siem Reap” goes back to the victory of the Khmer over the Siamese in the 17th century: “Place of defeat of the Siamese”, as the name is literally translated. In the evening, we invite you to a welcome dinner at a local restaurant, where you can taste typical Cambodian dishes and end your first day of vacation in a relaxed way. Overnight in Siem Reap.

Day 2: Discover Angkor with the Tuk Tuk & Picnic between the Temples

After breakfast, you will not only get to know magnificent temples, but also a typical vehicle of the region: with a tuk tuk, set off for the temple complexes of Angkor and in the morning see the unique brick sculptures of Prasat Kravan, the Srah Srang reservoir, also known as “the royal baths”, Banteay Kdei and East Mebon. Lunch is a picnic in the middle of Angkor’s temple landscape. Enjoy the special atmosphere and let the many impressions of the morning sink in. In the afternoon, something very special awaits you: Angkor Wat, the largest sacred building on earth, which you have certainly been looking forward to visiting for a long time – and rightly so. King Suryavarman, revered like a god, erected the unique monument in the 12th century, which is considered a landmark of the once glorious Angkor Empire. The intoxicatingly beautiful wall reliefs and the powerful dimensions of Angkor Wat are simply breathtaking. Overnight in Siem Reap.

3rd day Adventure bike ride around Angkor

Enjoy an extensive breakfast and strengthen yourself for the day, because it will be sporty. By bicycle you can explore Angkor Thom today. This was the last capital of the Great Khmer Empire in the 13th century – its remains are well preserved. Right in the center of the city you will meet the Bayon Temple. A special feature of this monument are the 54 towers representing the 54 provinces of the Great Khmer Empire. Each tower has four faces, each facing in different directions. The Ta Phrom plant sunk in the jungle is unfortunately less well preserved, but it is precisely the decay that makes the magic spell. The conclusion of this culturally rich day is the smaller but no less impressive temples Ta Nei and Preah Khan. Then return to your hotel, where you can end the evening according to your wishes. Overnight in Siem Reap.

Day 4 - Bicycle beyond Angkor & insights into everyday life

Today it goes out into the little-developed hinterland of the province of Siem Reap. First, the tour will take you to Banteay Samre. There you get on a bicycle and cycle through the idyllic landscape. Your route will take you through Khmer rice fields and villages, offering insights into Cambodia’s rural life. Have your camera ready and try to get in touch with the friendly locals – your guide will be happy to help you. After a refreshing lunch, your guide will take you by car to Bantey Srei, the "Citadel of Women", decorated with very filigree stone carvings in red glowing sandstone. There you wander through the jungle and can let this special atmosphere work in peace before going back to the hotel. Overnight in Siem Reap.

Day 5: The Cambodian lifeline: the enchanting Tonle Sap Lake

Today you will visit the remote temple of Beng Mealea, which is overgrown by dense jungle. Then continue to visit a floating village on the Tonle Sap. Then visit a local family where you will have lunch together. On your walk through the village, you will see the everyday life of its inhabitants – among them a family that breeds crocodiles. Back in Siem Reap, you will have time to stroll through the market or admire the skills of local artists and craftsmen in the “Artisans d’Angkor” workshops. In the evening, you will be a guest in a very special circus: In the “Phare” young people from poor backgrounds perform, who receive school education and acrobatics lessons through a social project. Although the actors are not yet as routine in everything as professional acrobats, they already bring impressive shows to the stage – in Siem Reap and on tours abroad. Overnight in Siem Reap.

Day 6 on the way to Phnom Penh

In the morning, drive through the dreamlike landscape of Cambodia to Phnom Penh. The ride lasts all day, so you’ll make some interesting stops along the way (depending on your time and interest): marvel at Sambor Prei Kuk or Banteay Prey Nokor temples – or stroll across the exotic spider market in Skuon. Take advantage of the car ride and let what has already been experienced sink and pass in review before you plunge into a new adventure. In the late afternoon you will reach Phnom Penh, the economic center of the country. Overnight in Phnom Penh.

7th day Experience nature with the bicycle on the Mekong Islands

Let the day go quietly and enjoy your breakfast. A boat trip will take you to the Mekong Island of Koh Oknha Tey in the morning. As a delightful contrast program, you will explore by bicycle the original life beyond the lively capital Phnom Penh. Far away from motor clatter, big streets and urban life, you will encounter rich nature, interesting people and their way of life. Of course, there is also time to take a deep breath. With a great view of Phnom Penh, it goes back to the capital. Overnight in Phnom Penh.

Day 8 Experience the charm of Phnom Penh

Today you will visit Phnom Penh, a big city with provincial charm. The tour includes a stroll through the central market with the high dome, a visit to the National Museum and the Royal Palace. Built in 1813, it is a mirror of Khmer architecture from many smaller buildings of various Khmer architectural styles. From the hill Penh, which gave the city its name, and the stupa located there at an altitude of 27 meters, take a view from above of the capital of Cambodia. In the evening you will visit the Apsara dance school, see how the students learn this classical dance and immerse themselves once again in the world of Cambodian music. You also have the opportunity to learn the art of traditional Cambodian dances yourself. After a short introduction and exciting insights into the history of art, you can apply what you have learned directly. Afterwards you will have the unique opportunity to try on classic Cambodian costumes. Enjoy your newfound skills and capture this magical experience photographically so you can share this unforgettable day with your family and friends. Overnight in Phnom Penh.

9th Day Authentic Island Idylles on Koh Trong Island

In the morning, leave Phnom Penh and continue to the northeast of the country. Sit back and enjoy the panoramic ride over Kampong Cham to Kratie. Kampong Cham is a provincial capital amidst fertile rice fields, in whose port goods of all kinds are handled. Various boats come and go and you get an unadulterated insight into the simple everyday life of the Khmer. Afterwards, the journey continues to Kratie, an old French colonial city. A boat will take you on a leisurely journey to Koh Trong Island. In this idyllic place, you will discover the traditions and customs of the locals during a bicycle tour. The varied island offers a floating Vietnamese village with vegetable gardens, farmers with their oxen in the rice fields, Khmer pagodas, fish farms, traditional Khmer stilt houses, orchards and much more. Overnight on the island Koh Trong near Kratie.

10th day swimming in the picturesque crater lake Yeak Laom

In the morning, continue your journey to Rattanakiri. This province is the northernmost region of the country and is often referred to as the Wild East. In the afternoon, the magnificent scenery on Lake Yeak Laom invites you for a walk or a refreshing swim. The 700,000-year-old crater lake is nestled in the dense jungle and has a beautiful green color. Ethnic minorities venerate the lake as a sacred place. Relax and drive back to your hotel. Overnight in Rattanakiri.

11th Day Experience Original Cambodia & Secrets of Ancient Cultures

Today you can look forward to a piece of authentic Cambodia and a journey back in time into a world of ancient cultures. This original and little-known Cambodia is densely overgrown with jungles and is inhabited by several minority populations, including the Tampous, Jaraïs and Kroeungs. You will stop in the village of Tampou minorities, where you will see their houses built only for women or men. Your guide will introduce you to the secret of this culture. Through primeval forests, the boat will take you to the village of Koah Peak and the village of Ka Choan, known for their totems. Full of impressions, return to your hotel. Overnight in Rattanakiri.

Day 12 Excursion to Lumphat & Undiscovered Landscapes

Today you drive through large rubber plantations and old zircon mines to Lumphat. You will be surprised by the size of the waterfall with its seven floors. Don’t forget to pack your swimsuit, as the refreshing water invites you to swim. On the way to the south, traces of the American bombing near Lumphat are still visible, as are the remains of an old colonial school near the river Sre Pok. On the way back, visit a traditional village before returning to your cozy hotel. Overnight in Rattanakiri.

13th day Market visit in Banlung & Leisure in Phnom Penh

After visiting the Banlung market, where different tribes offer their goods, your chauffeur will take you back to Phnom Penh. Afterwards relax in the hotel or stroll comfortably along the waterfront. Overnight in Phnom Penh.

14th day of travel home

Your unforgettable journey is coming to an end, and you will be picked up and taken to the airport. Even if your holiday is soon over – the precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories of your trip will accompany you forever. Look forward to letting your friends and family share your experiences in the distance at home.

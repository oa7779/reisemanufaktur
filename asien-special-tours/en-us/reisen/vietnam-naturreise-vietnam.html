Vietnam - Wonders of Nature

Vietnam Nature Tour: Experience the wonders of nature in Vietnam on an individual round trip with Asia Special Tours. Ask now without obligation!

Itinerary & services

English itinerary text is an offline machine translation of the German original for this team preview. Please confirm travel details with your advisor; the German source remains available through the language switch.

Get to know the tropical rainforest of Vietnam with its wonderful flora and fauna – one of the most exotic habitats on earth. By boat, by bicycle and on foot it goes through spectacular karst landscapes and through juicy green rice fields. With a junk you drive through the magical island world of Halong Bay and in Phong Nha National Park you explore gigantic caves. Also in the south of Vietnam, nature holds some treasures for you: in the Nam Cat Tien National Park you will experience an incredible variety of animals and plants and the dreamlike backdrops of the Mekong let you look at yourself from the ship. Experience places of complete tranquility and hear the heart of nature beating loud.

1st Day Arrival in Vietnam & Exciting City Tour of Hanoi

Welcome to Vietnam! At the airport of Hanoi, your guide will warmly welcome you and take you to your hotel, where you can arrive in peace. Afterwards, a half-day city tour brings you closer to the highlights of Hanoi: the one-pillar pagoda, the Temple of Literature, the Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum and the Opera House await you. In addition, a visit to Hoan Kiem Lake with the Turtle Pagoda should not be missed. Surrounded by beautiful trees, old colonial buildings and parts of the Old Town, the “Sea of the Returned Sword” is a popular refuge for Hanoians of all ages. According to legend, a turtle lives here, which once lent a king a magic sword with which he could defeat the enemies of Vietnam. A ride with a typical bicycle rikschka through the old town, the “quarter of 36 alleys” rounds off your sightseeing tour.

2nd day on the way in the picturesque Mai Chau valley

After breakfast, drive to Mai Chau, passing small villages, rice paddies and sugar cane fields. The place is nestled very idyllic in a beautiful valley. For several centuries, the relatives of the tribe of White Thai have lived here. Thais are known for their harmonious community and still maintain many of their centuries-old traditions in modern times. You reach the village of minorities around noon and enjoy a delicious lunch. Then ride your bike from the lodge and begin your tour of discovering the daily life of ethnic minorities in the area. You cycle through peaceful villages with traditional pile buildings, past lush green rice fields, where the locals farm with the help of water buffaloes. You can choose between different routes depending on your physical condition and personal preferences. Overnight in Mai Chau.

Day 3rd Bicycle Tour of Villages & Boat Ride in Van Long Nature Reserve

Leave the beautiful Mai Chau Valley and continue through scenic landscapes towards Ninh Binh. Let your eyes wander and keep your camera ready. Around noon you swing back on your bike and cycle comfortably on a dam along the Boi River. Along the way, you will pass through some villages and their curious inhabitants, who will gladly give you insights into their daily village life. Nearby is the Van Long Nature Reserve, a place of extraordinary beauty. The seemingly unreal landscape is characterized by rainforests and limestone rocks, which are reflected in the water of the rice fields and rivers. The rocks have the same origin as the peaks of Halong Bay and Ninh Binh. You will explore the reserve on board small wooden boats. These so-called sampans are skillfully maneuvered by paddlers with their feet. So sit back relaxed and enjoy the view. Exoticism is guaranteed! Finally, you drive to your hotel. Overnight in Ninh Binh.

Day 4: Cuc Phuong National Park

The age of nature plays a big role today: Accompanied by a nature guide of the national park, start a tour to over 50 meters high tree giants. Cuc Phuong is the oldest nature reserve in Vietnam and also serves as a rescue center for many natural species. You will see many rare trees and plants there, which are protected and cared for in this place. You can take a short hike into the jungle and immerse yourself in this incredible natural landscape. Take the time to enjoy the surroundings and atmosphere of this national park and feel the power of nature before heading back to Ninh Binh. Overnight in Ninh Binh.

Day 5 Aboard the luxury Orchid Cruise through Halong Bay

Enjoy your breakfast in the hotel and let the day go on comfortably. Then leave Ninh Binh and continue on to Haiphong. Be enchanted on board the luxury ship Orchid from the most beautiful side of Halong Bay. The upscale ship with high-quality equipment and first-class service ensures an unforgettable cruise. The total of 18 cabins have large panoramic windows and private balconies. They are equipped with a dark wooden floor, western design and state-of-the-art technology. Those days will surely be remembered for a long time. At lunchtime, the cruise ship sets off for the islands of Con Vit and Gia Luan – impressive limestone islands that rise from the sea. Afterwards we continue to Cat Ba Island and Lan-Ha Bay. Relax on board or paddle into the sunset with a kayak. Before dinner, you can attend a traditional cooking class with the chef. Overnight on the ship.

Day 6 With the dayboat on a voyage of discovery through Halong Bay

Feel the magical atmosphere on board your ship early in the morning. With tai chi exercises on the sun deck, you awaken the spirits of life and can start the day completely relaxed. Afterwards, board a day boat to explore the community of Viet Hai. In the village, you can ride a bicycle on rural paths and through the rainforest. Along the way, you can make a stop to visit a local home and experience the idyllic country life. Once you are back on the dayboat, your lunch will be served. In the afternoon, visit Lan-Ha Bay, where you can comfortably paddle across the sea with a kayak before returning to Orchid Cruise. Enjoy an exclusive dinner and let the evening end relaxing. Overnight on the ship.

Day 7 With the canoe in the Sang Toi cave & tea ceremony on board

Early in the morning you will experience the absolute silence and clear air of Halong Bay. Let it be completely relaxed and take time for your breakfast. Then we go back on an exploratory tour. With a canoe you go to the Sang Toi cave, where you can see bizarre rock formations. Back on board, take part in an exclusive tea ceremony. While you’re having a tasty brunch, slowly return to the port. Late in the morning, leave the ship and return to Hanoi. A short domestic flight will take you to Dong Hoi. Your guide awaits you at the airport and accompanies you to your hotel in Phong Nha National Park. The National Park is one of the most beautiful in Vietnam. It covers an area of around 86,000 hectares and lies directly on the border with Laos. In 2003, it was included in the list of World Heritage Sites of Vietnam by UNESCO. In the midst of this great backdrop you will spend the next few days. Overnight at Phong Nha National Park.

8th Day Pure Adventure: Cave exploration in Phong Nha National Park

After breakfast, take a boat trip on the Son River and sail through beautiful landscapes. When you arrive at the cave entrance and cross it, you will feel as if you are getting into the mouth of a huge monster. First, visit the impressive Phong Nha Cave, the longest cave in Southeast Asia. After returning to the pier, head to Thien Duong Cave, another fantastic cave and the largest of its kind in central Vietnam. In this cave, the stalactites are particularly diverse and depending on the perspective and imagination you can see different forms and images in them. Then return to your hotel. Overnight at Phong Nha National Park.

9th day of free time at Phong Nha National Park

Enjoy your free day in the middle of nature. How about a leisurely bicycle tour or go on an exploration tour with a kayak. Listen to the sounds of the National Park and absorb the peace and silence deep within yourself. Overnight at Phong Nha National Park.

10th day Flight to Saigon & Citytour in the urban jungle

Change of scene: You leave the national park and set off for the urban jungle of Saigon. From Dong Hoi you fly to the south of Vietnam, to Saigon. Your guide is already waiting for you and accompanies you to your hotel. The city builds a bridge between cultures and epochs: modern high-rises stand side by side with colonial buildings and traditional temples. On the streets, an army of mopeds crackles, and bicycle rickshaws wind between them. Young people use their mobile phones while in the quiet backyards, believers pray amidst clouds of incense. With your guide, visit the Presidential Palace, Opera, Notre-Dame Cathedral, the old Saigon Post Office and former City Hall. Many famous buildings with domes, turrets and ornaments bear witness to Saigon's past as a French colony - you will certainly not soon forget them. Also on the shopping street Dong Khoi you will stroll and can drift. Overnight in Saigon.

11th Day Biodiverse Diversity at Nam Cat Tien National Park

Today you drive to Nam Cat Tien National Park, about 150 kilometers from Saigon. In this rainforest you will find an incredible variety of birds and mammals, including an estimated 230 bird species. But even the large animals are no less impressive: with a little luck you can encounter Indochina tigers, Asian elephants, wild boar, deer, gibbons and a rare rhino species. Your guide knows well about the residents. Enjoy your time in the middle of unspoiled nature and listen to the sounds of the national park. Overnight at Nam Cat Tien National Park.

12th day Picturesque hike to Crocodile Lake

In the morning you walk six kilometers through the forest to the Krokodil Lake. On your way, you will have the opportunity to observe a large number of animals. And on the lake - you already suspect it - you meet with a little luck its namesake. Photo motifs in abundance! Overnight at Nam Cat Tien National Park.

Day 13 Return to Saigon & Leisure

In the morning, take a hike in the heart of Nam Cat Tien National Park and visit the Gibbons Protection Center before returning to Saigon. Your guide will take you to your hotel, where you can arrange the rest of the day according to your wishes. How about a shopping trip through the bustling shopping streets of the city? Overnight in Saigon.

14th day off on a cruise in the enchanting Mekong Delta

The Mekong with its secondary arms shapes the landscape in the south of Vietnam just as much as the lives of its inhabitants. Palms weigh in the wind, the bananas hang hard on the perennials. Water buffaloes sow on rivers, women row through narrow canals. Many people live on or on the water and make a living from fishing. Others grow rice, fruits and vegetables on land. In the morning, head towards Cai Be, the starting point for your cruise on the “Bassac”: the best and most convenient way to explore the Mekong Delta. While you are served a welcome drink, the ship departs slowly and after lunch you can relax on deck before visiting a traditional village. Here you will not only learn more about working in the fruit and rice fields, but also have the opportunity to talk to the locals and get to know the special lifestyle in the delta. Back on board there is still some time to end the day at a sundowner. After dinner, the Bassac anchors at Tra On’s floating market for the night. Overnight on the ship.

Day 15 The Floating Market of Cai Rang – Home or Onward Travel

Enjoy the sunrise on board and fortify yourself with an extensive breakfast. Meanwhile, the ship sails towards Can Tho and continues to the floating market of Cai Rang. Incidentally, the goods offered for sale here can be recognized by the specimens that dangle on the boats as an exhibit of high posts. Not only delicacies of every kind, also craftsmanship is offered. After a detailed visit to the market, you will be taken by small boat to the pier of Ninh Kieu, from where you will return to Saigon, where your journey ends. We are happy to submit a proposal for a subsequent bathing extension, for example on the island of Phu Quoc or Con Dao.

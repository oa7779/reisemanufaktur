Have you long wanted to face a new challenge with an open attitude? Then you can try something very extraordinary on this trip. Free your mind and tune your body for the high art of kung fu. In a Chinese kung fu school, you will get the opportunity to take part in an intense workout and train your body for balance, coordination skills and agility. You also learn to fixate your mind for a certain moment and to face the martial arts willingly and disciplined. Surely you will not be spared one or the other muscle soreness. But focus on yourself and your body and you will soon be able to guess where the seemingly inhuman movements and achievements of the Kung Fu masters come from. They succeed only through absolute devotion from the deepest heart and full of conviction. Take these life wisdoms and be curious to see what place kung fu will take in your future life.

Day 1 Arrival in Beijing & Impressive Temple of Heaven

Welcome to China! Your journey begins in Beijing, the exciting capital of the “Middle Kingdom”. At the airport, your personal guide is already waiting for you and driving you to your hotel. As a political and cultural center of the country, Beijing can look back on a 3000-year history, which is reflected in a globally unique cultural heritage. Thanks to the 2008 Olympic Games, China’s capital, often dismissed as a giant village, has transformed into a modern world city. The Temple of Heaven, which you will visit today, is one of the outstanding testimonies of religious architecture in the Middle Kingdom, from the Ming period (1368 to 1644). An exquisite example of China’s wooden buildings constructed without the help of a single nail is the 39-meter-high round hall of harvest victims. Its three-step roof, whose deep blue bricks symbolize the color of the sky, rests on 28 columns representing seasons, months and hours of traditional Chinese rituals. Overnight in Beijing.

2nd Day Imperial Fine Buildings & Chinese Martial Arts

A day full of cultural treasures and remarkable art awaits you. On your tour today you will discover the famous “Forbidden City” with the Imperial Palace. This magical place was the seat of the Chinese emperors of two dynasties – the Ming and the Qing – and for half a millennium was inaccessible to those who did not belong to the court. The crowd is as overwhelming as the architecture, the colorfully painted palace halls and the pagoda-like roofs. Of course, you can also stroll over Tiananmen Square – with a width of more than 500 meters and a length of 880 meters, it is the largest square on earth. Afterwards you will get the first insights into the great martial arts of the Chinese in a Kung Fu Institute. There you can watch the students during a training. Overnight in Beijing.

Day 3 The Great Wall & Visiting a Kung Fu Show

Ready for a mighty encounter? In the morning, head to the Great Wall at Mutianyu section. The foreigner enjoys the excursion here like a journey through time: from the modern capital beyond the outskirts to the rough mountainous area, where an army of workers once walled an empire. On the Wall, you will take a hike together with your guide. Enjoy the spectacular view and let your eyes wander. In the evening, visit a kung fu show in Beijing. You will be impressed by the Chinese martial arts that became famous in films by Jackie Chan or Bruce Lee. Take a trip to the cradle of Chinese kung fu and follow the acrobatic and dance scenes. Overnight in Beijing.

Day 4 Sacred Site of Buddhism & Musical Performance

This morning you will be taken to the station and take a fast train to Luoyang. The city was the residence of emperors from nine different dynasties (ca.) 1000 BC to 960 AD) and still today reflects a history full of splendor and glory. You will visit the Longmen Grottoes from the Tang period. These grottoes are sacred sites of Buddhism that count as UNESCO World Heritage sites. When the Toba moved their residence to Luoyang in 492, they continued their creativity in cave temple construction at the Dragon Gate “Longmen”, where the Yi River flows into the Luohe Valley. It is here that artists have created a formidable Buddhist sanctuary in around 1,350 grottoes and 750 niches. Marvel at the impressive and imposing works of figures and symbols. Then you will be taken to Shaolin. In the square next to your hotel, you will visit a musical performance from Zen Buddhism. Overnight in Shaolin.

5th Day Morning Tai Chi Course & Visit to the Famous Shaolin Monastery

You start the day with a tai chi course at your hotel. Let the movements work on you and look forward to this active start to the day. Afterwards, you can eagerly prepare for your excursion to the famous Shaolin Monastery. 1,500 years before Jackie Chan’s canvas career, the monks were already practicing the spectacular martial arts here. Over time, the monastery was destroyed and rebuilt several times, including during the Cultural Revolution. Only a few monks live here, most of them were expelled. With the general liberalization of religious practice, the Buddhist monks were also allowed to practice freely here again. Embrace this atmosphere at this famous birthplace of kung fu and look forward to your own teaching lessons. You will then return to Luoyang and take the fast train to Qufu, where you will be taken to your hotel next to Kung Fu School. Overnight in Qufu.

Day 6 – 10 Learning the Great Art of Kung Fu Yourself

Look forward to your kung fu training program. The school is geared towards traditional Shaolin Kung Fu and trains students from all over the world. It is located near the city of Qufu, which is also the birthplace of Confucius. The masters are Shaolin warrior monks who have dedicated their lives to kung fu. The training sessions often take place outdoors in a peaceful environment. In the next few days, in addition to theories and rituals of kung fu, you will also get to know first basic movements such as kicks and steps. Also included are power training and various stretching exercises as well as health care through fitness and meditation. Finally, you will be able to represent some kung-fu forms in movement sequences. This includes, for example, the Wu Bu Quan - a box movement consisting of five step combinations. Through training, you will quickly notice how your strength, agility and coordination improve and build mental strength by learning self-defense and control over your mind. Training can be combined with various elective courses. For example, you can take courses in Mandarin, calligraphy, Buddhism or acupuncture and massage. Overnight in Qufu.

Day 11 Free day for recovery

Today you can really recover from the extensive training. Process your gained impressions and feel your body in a new formation now. Your school is located in Shimen Mountain National Park, a beautiful forest area with many larger and smaller rock formations. There you can look around in peace and enjoy nature. The park is an important place of faith for Buddhism, Taoism and Confucianism. Overnight in Qufu.

Day 12: In the Footsteps of Emperor Confucius

Confucius, who as a great philosopher, statesman and educator is one of the ten great sages of China’s history, calls Qufu his hometown. Today you will visit the UNESCO World Heritage Sites of the city. These include the Temple of Confucius, the mansion of Confucius called ‘Kong’ and the cemetery of this great popularity. The temple dates back to 478 BC, making it an important ancient site in China. In honor of the Emperor Confucius, he was built one year after his death to show honor and respect. His teachings and philosophies profoundly shaped Chinese culture. Marvel at this architectural masterpiece for the venerable hero of China. Overnight in Qufu.

13th – 17th day Kung Fu – Training Week Part II

After these two days of recovery, your training rituals continue. They work out five hours a day, three in the early morning and two in the afternoon or evening. Kung Fu is strongly influenced by Buddhism. The term kung fu or gong fu means to solidify the performance and perfection obtained through hard work and complete dedication in the training of body and mind. The core idea of kung fu involves a deep expression to spiritual goals of awakening and perfection. It testifies to a spiritual wisdom that forms the basis of teaching methods. This results in the often unimaginable and seemingly inhuman movements and achievements that a kung fu master has mastered. They succeed only through absolute devotion from the deepest heart and full of conviction. Take up these life wisdoms and be curious to see what place kung fu will find in your life. Overnight in Qufu.   

Day 18 With new energy for body and mind, the journey continues

Today you leave Qufu and your training school with a feeling of being reborn. Enjoy all the precious days you have learned so much new about your body and mind. Today you are traveling by train back to Beijing. There you will be expected and brought to your hotel, where you can process the impressions of the last few days. Overnight in Beijing.

19th Day Goodbye to China

Today it is already time to say goodbye to this fascinating country. Surely you have packed your suitcases with lots of great impressions and maybe some kung fu elements. You will be driven to the airport, from where you will start your home or onward journey.

Take a look behind the scenes of fascinating northern India and discover Rajasthan and Uttar Pradesh in a particularly intense and stylish way. This extraordinary journey combines the most famous sights of the country with authentic encounters and unforgettable moments. Highlights include exceptional accommodation that is part of the journey itself: stay in magnificent palaces, elegant boutique hotels and some of India’s most beautiful homes. Fall asleep overlooking the Taj Mahal, spend a night in a 300-year-old palace still inhabited by the former ruling family and be pampered by the legendary service and unique atmosphere of your carefully selected luxury hotels. In Varanasi, the holiest city of Hinduism, you will finally experience the spiritual soul of India. Between the Ghats on the Ganges, thousands of years of rituals and the incomparable energy of this city, India shows itself in its most original and at the same time most touching form. An unforgettable journey full of contrasts, inspiring encounters and special moments, compiled with the utmost care by our India experts for you.

1st Day Arrival in India & Evening Discovery Tour of Old Delhi

Namaste and welcome to India! Your journey begins in Delhi, where you will be expected and transported to your hotel upon arrival at the airport. Take the time to arrive and relax, such as by the 50-meter pool of your stylish luxury hotel. In the heart of vibrant Delhi and yet far from the hustle and bustle of the big city lies The Lodhi – a stylish oasis of peace. Spacious rooms and suites, contemporary design and exclusive location near the Lodhi Gardens make the hotel one of the most elegant addresses in the Indian capital. After dark, immerse yourself in the vibrant life of Chandni Chowk, the historic heart of Old Delhi. The name means “lunar light place” and dates back to the Mughal period, when a water basin reflected the light of the moon. Together with your guide, you will explore the winding alleys in which the authentic everyday life of the city unfolds between busy activities, cooking kitchens and centuries-old trading houses. You will learn exciting stories about the district’s past and discover historical facades and buildings that have survived the transformation of this fascinating district to this day. Overnight in Delhi. 

Day 2 - A day full of contrasts

An exciting contrast to Old Delhi with its more than 1,500 years of history is the Imperial New Delhi with its wide avenues and representative magnificent buildings. The capital, planned by the British in 1931 as a modern garden city, now houses the government district, the presidential palace and the India Gate, which was modeled on the Arc de Triomphe in Paris and commemorates the Indian soldiers who died in the First World War. But New Delhi also impresses with important historical sights. These include the 72-meter-high Qutb Minar and the mausoleum of the great mogul Humayun, which is considered an architectural precursor to the Taj Mahal. In addition, visit the Gurudwara Bangla Sahib, the most important Sikh sanctuary of Delhi. In every Sikh temple worldwide, a so-called “Langar”, a free communal kitchen, belongs to the lived tradition. Every day, thousands of people, regardless of origin, religion or social status, are provided with fresh meals. During your visit, you will get a glimpse of the impressive communal cuisine, where food is prepared around the clock and distributed to those in need as well as pilgrims. Overnight in Delhi. 

Day 3 In the Footsteps of the Mughal Emperors

After breakfast, make your way to Agra. Once a small village on the banks of the Yamuna, the city developed under the great Mughal rulers Akbar and his grandson Shah Jahan to the second capital of the Mughal Empire. The Mughal emperors were not only powerful rulers, but also passionate builders. Arriving in Agra, move into your room at the luxurious The Oberoi Amarvilas. Inspired by the imposing architecture of the Mughal period, elegant rooms and suites, terraced gardens, reflecting water basins and an extraordinary view of the most famous monument in the world inspire. Then meet your guide, who will introduce you to two of Agra’s most important architectural treasures. You will visit the monumental Agra Fort, which is a UNESCO World Heritage Site alongside the world-famous Taj Mahal. Not only the magnificent complex itself impresses, but also the magnificent view from the fortress walls of the Taj Mahal. Another architectural jewel is the Itmad-ud-Daulah Mausoleum, often referred to as the “Baby Taj”. It was completed before the Taj Mahal and in many ways served as an architectural inspiration for the later masterpiece. Overnight in Agra. 

Day 4 The Taj Mahal and Pink City Jaipur

Today it is finally time: you experience the fairytale Taj Mahal with your own eyes. The early hours of the morning are considered the best time to visit when the rising sun dips the white marble into a gentle light. As “the most expensive proof of love ever” Mogul emperor Shah Jahan had this masterpiece built for his beloved wife Mumtaz Mahal. On your way to Jaipur, visit the impressive ruined town of Fatehpur Sikri. The former capital of Grand Mogul Akbar was built in the 16th century from red sandstone, but soon after its completion due to water shortages abandoned. In the afternoon you will reach Jaipur, the famous “Pink City”. During an evening walk with your guide, discover the authentic life of the city. Taste regional specialties at popular street food stands and watch silversmiths, jewelry artists and other craftsmen at work. Then move to your hotel for the next few nights. The Rambagh Palace was once the residence of the Maharajas of Jaipur. Surrounded by magnificent Mughal gardens, elegant courtyards and historic architecture, the legendary Palace Hotel combines royal history with timeless luxury. Overnight in Jaipur.

5th day Jaipur with all your senses

Getting up early is worthwhile, because today you can expect another masterpiece of Mughal architecture: the imposing Fort Amber perched on a steep mountain slope north of Jaipur. With the Jeep you drive past artfully landscaped Mughal gardens over a winding pavement path up to the main gate of the facility. On the way back to the city, you make a photo stop at the famous “Palace of the Winds”, behind whose ornate facade the ladies of the court once observed the bustle on the streets. Jaipur itself also inspires with impressive architecture. You will visit the magnificent City Palace. Just a few steps away, the Jantar Mantar Observatory welcomes you with soothing peace. In the afternoon, another special experience awaits you: a visit to a performance at the legendary Raj Mandir Cinema. Even if you will not understand every word, the atmosphere is incomparable. The Bollywood feeling can nowhere be experienced more authentically than here. After all these today’s highlights, did you get an appetite for homemade Indian specialties? Perfect, because you are already expected by your warm hosts for a small cooking class. During the subsequent meal together there is time for interesting conversations. Overnight in Jaipur. 

Day 6 Blue Facades and Royal History

From the “Pink City” Jaipur, continue to Jodhpur, also known as the “Blue City” due to the color of its houses. The city was founded in 1459 by Rao Jodha, the head of a Rajput clan. Jodhpur once owed its wealth and rapid growth to the trade in opium, sandalwood and copper. After check-in at the hotel, the rest of the day is at your disposal. Perched high above Jodhpur is the magnificent Umaid Bhawan Palace, one of the last great palaces in India and to this day partly the residence of the former royal family. Surrounded by vast gardens, the property run by Taj Hotels combines majestic architecture and timeless luxury. If you wish, explore the alleys around the Ek Minar Masjid together with your guide. There, immerse yourself in a traditional craftsmanship closely associated with Rajasthan: the Bandhani art, also known as the tie-dye technique. Learn about the colorful dyeing techniques that have been passed down for generations and discover how much skill and patience is required to create the ornate patterns on the fabrics. Overnight in Jodhpur. 

7th day Bishnoi village and the most beautiful sides of Jodhpur

Away from the hustle and bustle of the city, you will get insights into the life of a very special community. The Bishnoi are considered pioneers of nature conservation in India and have been working for centuries to protect trees and wildlife, if necessary even using their own lives. Founded in the 15th century by Guru Jambheshwar, the community lives by strict principles of respect for humans, animals and nature. Afterwards you will discover the most beautiful sides of Jodhpur. The city is dominated by the imposing Mehrangarh fortress, which sits majestically on a rock about 130 meters above the city. From here, a spectacular view of the bright blue sea of houses Jodhpur opens up. At the foot of the fortress lies the elegant marble mausoleum Jaswant Thada, built in honor of the popular ruler Jaswant Singh II. Afterwards, immerse yourself in the lively city life. With the tuk-tuk you drive from Mehrangarh Fort into the winding alleys of the old town. The blue labyrinth holds numerous discoveries: traditional spice markets, dye shops, puppet builders and many more encounters with the authentic everyday life of this impressive desert metropolis. Overnight in Jodhpur. 

Day 8 Guest with a noble family

A new day, a new goal. Your journey will take you to the charming village of Chanoud, which is about a two-hour drive from Jodhpur. The Chanoud Garh is a magnificent, over 300-year-old mansion that is still run today by the descendants of the former ruling family. The lovingly restored property combines the charm of an authentic heritage hotel with the warm hospitality of a family residence. Curving archways, marble colonnades, ornate Jharokha windows, extensive courtyards and well-kept gardens tell of past times and bring the dazzling history of the house to life in an impressive way. In the afternoon, set off for a jeep ride through the rural area. Passing the settlements of the Rabari, a traditional shepherd and nomadic community, you will reach a silvery shining salt basin that impresses especially in the warm light of the late afternoon. The area is also a popular resting place for numerous migratory birds. As the sun sinks behind the Aravalli hills, enjoy the peaceful mood of the desert landscape with tea and small snacks. Overnight in Chanoud. 

Day 9 The Jain Temples of Ranakpur

On the way to Udaipur, the next stop of your journey, you will stop at the Jain Temples of Ranakpur (without a tour guide, only audio guide allowed), a temple complex that is characterised by great attention to detail. A forest of pillars supports the dome decorated with intricate geometric patterns and figures of goddesses. The temple has 29 rooms and 1,444 columns, the architecture of which reflects the Jain myths and their symbolism. It is said that no pillar is the same as another. Jainism is one of the oldest religions in India. His teaching is based on the search for liberation and bliss through a nonviolent and renounced life. In the afternoon you will reach the romantic lake town of Udaipur. Located directly on the picturesque Lake Pichola, the impressive The Leela Palace with its ornate arches, filigree stone carvings and opulent courtyards reminds of the glamorous time of the Maharajas. Already the stylish arrival by boat over the lake makes the stay a special experience. The elegantly designed rooms and suites offer fantastic views of Lake Pichola, the famous City Palace or the Aravalli mountains. Culinary highlights and an exquisite spa ensure the highest level of well-being. Overnight in Udaipur. 

Day 10 Between palace splendor and lake romance

Udaipur is characterized by its lakes and therefore also bears the nickname “Venice of the East”. The small streets of Udaipur can be explored particularly well by bicycle. Cycle along the lakes, temples and palaces with your guide and immerse yourself in the bustling life of this contrasting city. Visit the small shops of goldsmiths, cobblers and potters, sample specialties of Indian street food and explore vegetable and spice markets. The pace of your tour is up to you alone. In the afternoon, visit the City Palace, the architectural highlight of Udaipur. One part of the winding building complex is open to visitors as a museum, another serves as a residence for the acting head of the Mewar dynasty, while another part houses an exclusive luxury hotel. Of course, a boat trip on Lake Pichola should not be missed in Udaipur. In your private boat, you will glide over the scenic lake that forms the heart of the city. Sit back and enjoy the views of the surrounding mountains as well as the magnificent palaces and historic buildings along the shore. Overnight in Udaipur.

Day 11 - Off to the holiest city of Hinduism: Varanasi

Today, your journey will take you to Varanasi, considered the holiest city of Hinduism. In the morning you will be taken to the airport and fly on to Varanasi via Delhi. The city on the Ganges has thousands of years of religious tradition and has attracted believers from all over India for more than 2,500 years. Varanasi is intense, chaotic and at the same time profoundly spiritual - India in its most original form. Pilgrims perform ritual washings in the Ganges, cows stroll through the winding alleys and merchants offer their goods. Here you will experience the country in all its authenticity and vibrancy. Stay at the magnificent BrijRama Palace. The palace decorated with ornate Maratha elements is one of the oldest landmarks of Varanasis and is located directly on the famous ghats of the Ganges. Just arriving by the hotel’s own boat gives a royal feeling and opens up fascinating views of the spiritual life on the river. BrijRama Palace is a true gem of palace architecture and delights with its exceptional location, spectacular views of the Ganges, excellent service and a unique flair full of history, magic and romance. Overnight in Varanasi. 

12th Day Sacred Sites and Moving Aarti Ceremony

Now it is time to discover the impressive city of Varanasi in daylight. For centuries it has been regarded as an important center of knowledge and Sanskrit scholarship. During your visit to the prestigious Benares Hindu University, founded in 1916 to promote Indian art, culture and Sanskrit research, you will learn about an important part of this cultural heritage. On the extensive campus are the New Vishvanath Temple dedicated to the god Shiva and the Bharat Kala Bhavan, one of the most important university museums in India. The impressive collection includes over 100,000 exhibits, including precious miniature paintings, sculptures, textiles, manuscripts and artisanal treasures from different eras. On the way to the Old Town, you will pass the famous Durga Temple, also known as the “Monkey Temple”, as well as the Bharat Mata Temple with its unique marble relief of the Indian subcontinent. In the evening, a particularly moving experience awaits you: the atmospheric Aarti ceremony at Dasaswamedh Ghat. Amidst chants, incense fragrance and flickering lights, you will experience the spiritual atmosphere on the banks of the sacred Ganges up close. Overnight in Varanasi.

13th Day Morning Boat Ride on the Ganges

They greet the morning with a boat trip on the Ganges. Thousands of believers gather daily at the Ghats to bathe and pray. In front of the silhouettes of temples and palaces in the gentle morning light, they let candles float on banana leaves across the river – an impressive and unforgettable spectacle. In the afternoon, take a trip to Sarnath, the place where Buddha preached his first sermon after his enlightenment more than 2,500 years ago, establishing Buddhism. In the Sarnath Museum (closed on Fridays) you will get exciting insights into the origins of this world religion. After returning to Varanasi, you will have time for individual discoveries in the winding alleys of the old town or for a relaxing end to the day at the Ganges. Overnight in Varanasi. 

Day 14 Farewell to India

At the end of your journey, you will travel home with many unforgettable impressions from this fascinating land of contrasts. After a delightful breakfast, your chauffeur will take you to the airport, from where you will first fly back to Delhi. They say goodbye to India. But who knows, maybe one day you will return. Because this impressive country holds countless more wonders and discoveries for you.

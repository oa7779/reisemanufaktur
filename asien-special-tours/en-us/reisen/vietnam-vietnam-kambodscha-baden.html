Vietnam & Cambodia Round Trip - The highlights of Indochina with bathing stay in Phan Thiet

Vietnam Cambodia Tour: Experience the highlights of Indochina with a bathing stay in Phan Thiet. The experts of Asia Special Tours will be happy to advise you.

Get to know the most famous sights of Vietnam and Cambodia! On a unique junk trip, you will experience the UNESCO World Heritage site Halong Bay up close. You will continue your journey over the former imperial city of Hue to the metropolis Saigon to the capital of Cambodia and onwards to Siem Reap to visit the truly royal complexes of Angkor. Finally, you can relax on the beach of Phan Thiet and watch the fishermen catch their catch early in the morning. Always set your own pace and enjoy moments for eternity.

1st day Arrival in Hanoi & visit of a traditional pancake baker

Welcome to Vietnam! In Hanoi, you will be welcomed by your guide and taken to your hotel. After a short rest, take a walk through the “36 alleys” of the old town, into the vibrant life of the Vietnamese. You will visit a traditional pancake baker in the heart of the old town. Here you can see (and of course try) how the pancakes “Banh Cuon” are made. The typical steamed treats are made of rice flour and have little in common with European relatives – but you will find out about that on site. Afterwards, visit Hanoi’s main attractions: the Mausoleum (from outside) and Ho Chi Minh’s private residence, the wooden ethnic minority house and the one-pillar pagoda. Continue to the Temple of Literature, Vietnam’s first university. Afterwards, you will get to know the diverse culture of Vietnam in the Ethnographic Museum. After a day full of impressions, you will return to your hotel where you can end the evening relaxed. Overnight in Hanoi.

2nd Day Luxurious Cruise in the Enchanting Halong Bay

After an extensive breakfast, leave Hanoi towards Halong Bay to board your junk. Almost 2000 islands, emerald water and the sails of the junks make Halong Bay in the Gulf of Tonkin the most beautiful in Vietnam. She became famous for the James Bond film "The Man with the Golden Colt". You will experience a fascinating cruise through this environment and visit the interior of the colorfully lit surprise grotto. On an island you moor and explore the cave Bo Nau. Afterwards, you can relax with a drink on deck or in the lounge chair on board. While enjoying your dinner, experience the breathtaking sunset in the bay. Overnight on the junk.

Day 3 End of the Cruise & Flight to the Magic Hoi An

Feel the magical atmosphere on board your ship. Early in the morning you will experience an absolute silence and clear air in Halong Bay. After getting up, enjoy a freshly brewed coffee and a fresh seafood brunch on board before leaving the junk. Let yourself once again be captivated by the dreamlike scenery. Afterwards you will return to Hanoi for your onward flight to Danang. Upon arrival, you will be driven to Hoi An. Hoi An was an important commercial port in the 17th and 18th centuries, its architecture and relaxed lifestyle have changed little over the years, so a charming little town awaits you here. Your charming hotel is located on a beautiful beach where you can relax perfectly. Overnight in Hoi An.

4th Day Get to know Hoi An & free time in Hoi An

Today, take a sightseeing tour of the historic city center. Of course, the program includes a visit to the 400-year-old Japanese bridge and a stroll over the colorful market. The rest of the day is at your disposal. Enjoy the amenities of your hotel and treat yourself to a little time out. Or are you more likely to go on another adventure? Your guide will be happy to give you a few suggestions. Overnight in Hoi An.

Day 5 Over the Cloud Pass to Hue & Visit of the Imperial Tombs

Visit the famous marble mountains as well as the Cham Museum today before heading on to Hue. The journey takes you on the so-called Cloud Pass towards the north. This is not just any road: on the right you look down to the sea, on the left into the bizarre, up to 1,000 meters high mountain range. In the afternoon you will get a taste of history and visit the impressive tombs of former emperors. You will visit the Mausoleum of Emperor Minh Mang, a sprawling architectural complex consisting of 40 buildings and home to palaces, temples and pavilions, among others. Do not miss the tomb of Khai Dinh. The last mausoleum of the Nguyen Dynasty was built between 1920 and 1931 on a mountain slope in the village of Chau Chau. The long staircase to the tomb is lined with stone dragons. Inside you can expect imposing ceiling paintings and ceramic frescoes. Overnight in Hue.

6th Day Quirliger Market Visit & Riksha Cruise through the Imperial City of Hue

After breakfast, stroll through the lively market of Dong Ba and discover the fresh produce from the area. Afterwards, enjoy a boat ride on the romantic perfume river and discover Hue’s most famous religious site, the Thien Mu Pagoda, which is Vietnam’s tallest pagoda with seven floors. In the afternoon, wander through the imposing Imperial Citadel, where a total of 13 Nguyen dynasty emperors resided and ruled from 1802 to 1945. After exploring the ruins of this wonderful monument, take a bicycle rickshaw through the old town inside the citadel, which takes you to Lake Tinh-Tam, where the Emperor relaxed. Around the lake full of lotus flowers, many cafes invite you to stay. Afterwards, drive to the airport for the flight to Saigon. Check in at your hotel. The metropolis Ho Chi Minh City – still called “Saigon” by the locals today – is Vietnam’s largest city with about seven million inhabitants. 24 hours a day pulsates life here, because everyone is busy shopping and trading, studying, working or just enjoying the day. Overnight in Saigon.

7th day Exciting City Tour of the dazzling Saigon

Good morning Saigon! Your tour will take you past the main shopping street (Dong Khoi), the former town hall and the old opera house (view from outside). Visit Notre-Dame Cathedral, Main Post Office, Reunification Palace and the bustling Ben Thanh Market. In the afternoon, head to Cholon, which means “Great Market”, which is also home to the Chinese colony. You will visit the Thien Hau Temple, the Binh Tay Market and a lacquerware factory. Thien Hau is especially a focal point for the local women, as it is dedicated to the goddess of fertility and the goddess of mothers. Overnight in Saigon.

Day 8 Visits to Phnom Penh – “The Pearl of Southeast Asia”

Today you leave Vietnam and embark on a new adventure in Cambodia. You will be taken to the airport and fly on to Phnom Penh. The city was once called the “Pearl of Southeast Asia” and was one of the most beautiful cities in the Orient. Together with your guide, you will explore the highlights of Cambodia’s capital. Your first stop is the Royal Palace. It dates back to 1866 and is the last palace built during the French colonial period. The complex also houses the Silver Pagoda, named after the over 5000 kilogram silver plates that adorn the floor. Its original name is Wat Prakeo – “Temple of the Emerald Buddha”. The sanctuary houses a collection of Buddhas in gold, silver, crystal and bronze. Continue to the National Museum, which boasts a world-leading collection of ancient archaeological, religious and artistic artifacts from the 4th to 13th centuries. Over 5000 exhibits show the cultural richness of the past kingdom. Another attraction is the bat colony in the roof of the building! Then return to your hotel. Overnight in Phnom Penh.

Day 9 Angkor Wat: the largest temple in the world

After breakfast, fly to Siem Reap. The small town is the ideal starting point for exploring the world famous Angkor Park. In the afternoon you will find the largest religious temple in the world, the magnificent Angkor Wat: a masterpiece of Khmer architecture and probably the most fantastic temple complex in the world. Finish your tour at the ancient city of Angkor Thom, the last capital of the Great Khmer Empire. From here you will reach the Bayon Temple, which is located in the middle of the city. Your path continues to the Terrace of Elephants, from where the royal family followed the festivities on the Great Square, right next to the terrace of the Leprosy King. It is believed that the bodies of high dignitaries were burned here. Capture these countless photo motifs with your camera – you will think back on these experiences for a long time. Overnight in Siem Reap.

Day 10 Angkor's Temple & Insights into Rural Daily Life

Getting up early is definitely worthwhile today: After breakfast, you drive to the magnificent temple Banteay Srei to admire it in the most beautiful morning light. Banteay Srei is the best preserved temple in Cambodia. On the way back you can stop in small villages and observe the daily life of the villagers. Use this to try palm wine and sugar (seasonal) and other local products. In the afternoon, you’ll experience a delightful contrast to the well-preserved Banteay Srei as you visit the Ta Prohm Temple – its semi-decomposed state and the tree giants’ roots looming through the temple give it a mystical atmosphere. Overnight in Siem Reap.

11th day return flight to Saigon & start of bathing in Phan Thiet

After experiencing and seeing a lot in the last few days, now is the time to relax. First you fly to Saigon. Once there, you will be driven to the coast to Phan Thiet, where your exclusive beach hotel is waiting for you. The afternoon is already at your disposal – and your bathing stay begins. Overnight in Phan Thiet.

Day 12 - 14 Recreation in Phan Thiet

Without tour guide and chauffeur

These days are at your disposal, enjoy a refreshing bath in the sea, relax on the dreamlike beach or explore the surroundings. Whatever you choose: Everyday life will seem very far away. Overnight in Phan Thiet.              

Day 15 Farewell to Vietnam

Even the most beautiful journey comes to an end. With many new impressions, you will be taken to Saigon Airport, from where you will take your flight home – lasting memories will accompany you.

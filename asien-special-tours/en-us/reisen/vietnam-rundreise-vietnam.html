Vietnam Round Trip - Visit to the Country and People

Vietnam Tour: Visit to the country and people. Discover the beauty of Vietnam. The experts of Asia Special Tours will be happy to advise you.

Immerse yourself in one of the most exotic cultures in Asia and be enchanted by the magic of Vietnam. We invite you to experience not only the most beautiful regions of the country, but also the people living there unadulterated and authentic. You will get closer to the whole variety of Vietnam: you will see lush rice terraces and impressive karst mountains in the north, sail through the dreamlike Halong Bay, walk through the bustling metropolises Hanoi and Saigon and learn a lot in the historic cities Hue and Hoi An. In each region you will meet hosts who will bring you closer to Vietnam in their own personal way. Go behind the scenes and experience an aspiring nation trying to find the perfect balance between its ancient culture and modern life.

1st day Arrival in Vietnam & first orientation walk through Hanoi

Welcome to Vietnam, one of the most exotic countries in Asia! At Hanoi airport, you will be welcomed by your guide and taken to your hotel. A first orientation walk (optionally you can also take a walk in a typical bicycle rickshaw) leads you through the old town of Hanoi, the “quarter of 36 alleys”. With its tree-lined avenues, French colonial architecture, peaceful lakes and oriental pagodas, this city offers many highlights, the first of which you can already see today. Let the evening end relaxed. Overnight in Hanoi.

Day 2 - Discover the highlights of Hanois & visit a water puppet manufacturer

The modern capital of Vietnam has preserved the mystery and charm of past centuries. Narrow alleys and traditional shops, lakes, parks, boulevards and monuments invite you to explore. As part of your city tour, you will visit the Temple of Literature – Vietnam’s first university, the One-Pillar Pagoda, the Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum, Ho Chi Minh’s House, the Ethnography Museum and Hoan Kiem Lake with the Turtle Pagoda. Surrounded by beautiful trees, old colonial buildings and parts of the Old Town, the “Sea of the Returned Sword” is a popular refuge for Hanoians of all ages. According to legend, a turtle lives here, which once lent a king a magic sword with which he could defeat the enemies of Vietnam. Then you will meet a water puppet craftsman who has been upholding this artisanal tradition of North Vietnam for 7 generations. Your host welcomes you to his house and shows you how he makes the water dolls. Of course, he will also show you a private demonstration with his dolls. Hidden behind a stage, he lets the water puppets move to different rhythms. Overnight in Hanoi.

3rd day Lunch with the locals & scenic bike tour through dry Halong Bay

After breakfast, leave the bustling Hanoi and travel on to Ninh Binh. On the way you will encounter one of the most beautiful landscapes in Vietnam. You will stop at a village in Nho Quan where you will enjoy your lunch with the locals. Enjoy an authentic insight into everyday life and try to exchange a little with the help of your guide. In the early afternoon, continue to the village of Gia Hung. Here you take a pleasant bike ride (ca.) 8 km) on the dike along the river Boi, where you will discover the villages of the delta and get to know their inhabitants. Keep your camera ready and stop whenever and wherever you want. Upon arrival in Van Long, you will be driven on a traditional rowing boat – a sampan – through rice fields, past spectacular stone peaks and through canals. What a fairytale backdrop – and you in the middle of it. Overnight in Ninh Binh.

4th day Dreamlike cruise through the World Heritage Halong Bay

Today, another highlight awaits you - the fascinating Halong Bay. Almost 2000 islands, emerald water and the sails of the junks make this bay in the Gulf of Tonkin the most beautiful in Vietnam. She became famous for the James Bond film “The Man with the Golden Colt”. The Vietnamese name Vinh Ha Long translated to “bay of the submerging dragon”. Towards late morning you board a traditional wooden junk. Enjoy a fresh lunch on board as you gradually sail through the magical bay. You will explore the labyrinth of the bay with its many limestone rock islands and caves. For visiting the Grotte Sung Sot and Titop Island, disembark and enjoy magnificent views over the bay. After a delicious dinner of fresh seafood, you will experience an unforgettable sunset amidst a dreamlike backdrop. Overnight in Halong Bay.

5th Day Movie Ready Bay from Bai Tu Long & Adventurous Train Ride to Lao Cai

Feel the magical atmosphere on board your ship. Early in the morning you will experience the absolute silence and clear air of Halong Bay. With tai chi exercises on the sun deck, you awaken the spirits of life and can start the day completely relaxed. Your junk will take you to Bai Tu Long Bay today. Here you can once again admire an impressive landscape with karst rocks and scattered islands – this also reached film fame: as a backdrop for the film “L’Indochine”. Around 11 a.m., you will reach the dock in Halong and return to Hanoi from there. You can spend the afternoon according to your wishes. How about a leisurely stroll through the lively old town or relax in one of the many charming cafes? In the evening, leave behind the colorful hustle and bustle of the capital and head to Sapa, the quiet mountainous country in northern Vietnam. Here you will take a ride on the night train. Allow yourself to sleep in your night compartment by the gentle rattling of the train. Overnight on the train.

Day 6 Hike through the magnificent mountain landscape & deep insights into the everyday life of the mountain peoples

In the morning you will arrive in Lao Cai, where you will be taken to your hotel for breakfast. Enjoy your magical hotel in the middle of a dreamlike mountain scenery. After strengthening, take a hike through the magnificent mountain scenery to the village of Cat Cat, where the Black Hmong live. There you will have the opportunity to meet locals and learn more about their daily life. For the members of this tribe, the family comes first: To have a family is to be happy. Having none means being lost, is a Hmong proverb. Your next stop will be the village of Y Linh Ho before returning to Sapa. Overnight in Sapa.

Day 7 Guest at the Tay & Colorful Market Visit

Also this day you will actively spend in a magnificent environment. You will walk along lush rice terraces to Ta Van (1,200 meters) where you will meet the Tay minority. The Tay are the second largest ethnic minority in the multi-ethnic state of Vietnam, which officially recognizes 53 ethnic minority groups. They live especially in the north of Vietnam and have preserved an original religion that venerates nature and contains a strong ancestral cult. Experience their traditional culture and immerse yourself in another world. By the way, the most interesting way to understand the everyday life of these people is a visit to the colorful markets in the mountain villages. Watch the colorful activity and keep your camera ready. Afterwards, return to Lao Cai and take the night train back to Hanoi. Let the moving impressions of the last few days affect you and look forward to many more highlights on your journey through the enchanting Vietnam. Overnight on the train.

Day 8: In Imperial Traces through Hue & Boat Ride on the Perfume River

Arrive early in the morning in Hanoi, where you will be taken to the hotel to freshen up and have breakfast. Afterwards you drive to the airport and fly on to Hue. Hue is idyllic between mountains and the sea. Its gardens, lakes and canals were once sung by poets – today it is a UNESCO World Heritage Site. After your arrival in Hue, you will get a picture of the former imperial city for yourself. After a boat trip on the “Perfume River” you will reach the beautifully located Thien-Mu Pagoda, a Buddhist monastery whose complex houses the highest pagoda in Vietnam. Not to be missed is a visit to the famous citadel. The fortress was built in the early 19th century and is modeled on the forbidden city in Beijing. Emperors and subjects, heaven and earth, man and nature: everything should be in balance. Let yourself be impressed by the forbidden purple city that housed the palaces of the emperors and empresses. The Thai Hoa Palace, the halls of Mandarin and the nine dynastic urns can be walked in a short time. At the end of the day, stroll over the Dong-Ba market, the center of Hue city and let the day end relaxed according to your wishes. Overnight in Hue.

9th Day Exotic Cooking Class in Hue & Over the Cloud Pass to Hoi An

Today you have the opportunity to learn at home with a Vietnamese couple how to prepare authentic home cooking. The private cooking class takes place in the garden house of your hosts, who will cook with you a selection of traditional dishes of the region and show you what combinations of flavors can later impress your friends and family at home. At lunch, enjoy your self-cooked dishes along with other specialties that your hosts serve you. Afterwards, you can enjoy the breathtaking scenery on the drive from Hue over scenic mountain roads that offer spectacular views of the coast. Halfway between Danang and Hue lies the so-called Cloud Pass built by the French. In good weather you can see here to the coast! The destination of your day tour is the old picturesque port city of Hoi An. The ancient city center of this once important commercial port is very well preserved, so you can see here buildings from several centuries that testify to the different cultural influences Hoi An was exposed to. Overnight in Hoi An.

10th day walking tour of the charming Hoi An & Lantern Workshop

The tranquil Hoi An has never lost its old charm. Among other things, you will visit the Japanese Bridge, the Museum of Sa Huynh Culture with archaeological finds from the time before the Cham, the Tran family chapel and the Phuoc Kie Pagoda. In the afternoon you will still have the opportunity to visit a lantern workshop in the “City of Tailors and Lampions” and learn how the filigree colorful works of art are produced. Overnight in Hoi An.
Would you like to experience even more unique encounters? how about a visit to the vegetable village of Tra Que, a tour with local fishermen or a day behind the scenes of rice cultivation in Vietnam? Overnight in Hoi An.

11th Day City Tour in Saigon & Evening Vespa Tour

After breakfast, leave Hoi An and fly on to Saigon. Among the most famous attractions are the Presidential Palace, Notre-Dame Cathedral, the old Saigon Post Office and the former City Hall. In addition, you will get to know the Reunification Palace – the former independence palace of the South Vietnamese president from the 19 60s. Of course, visit Cholon – Saigons Chinatown. Here you will visit the Thien-Hau Pagoda and Binh Tay Market, a wholesale market where everything from dried food to T-shirts to electronics is sold. A special highlight awaits you in the evening: discover the nightlife of Saigon in the back seat of a Vesparoller. Your driver will take you safely through the turbulent traffic of the metropolis. They roar over wide streets, which are just so teeming with large families on mofas and through narrow alleys, past small shops and snack bars. During the ride, stop more often to try the typical food. Some peace from the hustle and bustle of the city can be found in a hidden backyard café before plunging into the hustle and bustle of a live music bar. Overnight in Saigon.

12th Day Picturesque Boat Tour of the Mekong Delta & Lunch with Locals

Today you will leave the bustling Saigon and drive into the fertile Mekong Delta. The Mekong with its secondary arms shapes the landscape in the south of Vietnam just as much as the lives of its inhabitants. Palms weigh in the wind, the bananas hang hard on the perennials. Water buffaloes sow on rivers, women row through narrow canals. Many people live on or on the water and make a living from fishing. Others grow rice, fruits and vegetables on land. Upon arrival in Ben Tre, you will take a boat trip on the river of the same name. You will make a short stop at a local coconut sweets factory before visiting a brick plant and yet another coconut factory where the coconuts are opened by hand. Continue by boat on a smaller canal until you finally stop to visit the coconut gardens of a local home. Then take a smaller boat, which is controlled by a local rower, and sail on the canal. After leaving the boat, go on a bike to discover the rural surroundings and stop for lunch at a local, beautiful restaurant where you can enjoy some traditional Mekong dishes.In the afternoon, walk to a small dock where your boat to Ben Tre is waiting for you. Afterwards you will drive to Can Tho, where you will be taken to your charming hotel. Overnight in Can Tho.

13th Day Floating Market in Cai Rang & Leisure in Saigon

Your boat will take you through dreamlike landscapes to the floating market of Cai Rang. This is the most beautiful of its kind in the entire Mekong Delta. Here you can look over the shoulders of the locals when trading exotic goods and – if you like – haggle yourself. Afterwards you will leave the Mekong Delta and return to Saigon, where you will be taken to your hotel. Your unique journey is slowly coming to an end and it is time to toast the many unique experiences. How about a cocktail in one of the city’s trendy rooftop bars? Overnight in Saigon.

Day 14: Farewell to Vietnam

Today, unfortunately, we say goodbye to a fascinating country. You still have time until the transfer to the airport, from where you take your home or onward flight. You will be accompanied by beautiful impressions that will surely sweeten your everyday life often.

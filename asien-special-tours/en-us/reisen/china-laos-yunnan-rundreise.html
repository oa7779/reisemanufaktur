Laos and Yunnan Tour - On the legendary Tea and Horse Road

Laos and Yunnan round trip on the legendary tea and horse road with your specialist Asia Special Tours. Here directly inquiries and individual advice.

Are you looking for a trip away from the tourist trail? This Laos-Yunnan journey takes you through some of the most impressive areas of Asia with breathtaking natural beauty. Noisy waterfalls, clear lakes, huge karst mountains and spectacular rice terraces are among the grandiose landscapes you will encounter. A bit of the way you travel on the legendary Tea and Horse Road, where tea and spices were once traded in one direction and salt and horses in the other. In addition, you will cross one of the most important tea-growing regions in China, where the well-known Pu’er tea is produced. Experience deep insights into the everyday life of ethnic minorities along the way and immerse yourself in a completely different world.

1st day Arrival in Laos & visit of a traditional Baci ceremony

Welcome to Laos! After arriving in Luang Prabang, you will be taken to the hotel and on the way you can get your first impressions of the best preserved traditional city in Southeast Asia. The charming city with historic temples and colonial buildings lies between the Nam Khan and Mekong rivers and was declared a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1995. After a short rest, you are invited to participate in a Baci ceremony. "Baci" is a traditional Buddhist ritual performed at significant events such as birth, weddings or important stages of life. The Lao believe that each person has 32 souls. They are the guardians over different parts of the body and over health. A white cotton thread is cut into short pieces and knotted around your right wrist. This gesture connects the souls and represents your attachment to Laotian culture, then returns to your hotel where you can end the evening in a relaxed way. Overnight in Luang Prabang.

2nd Day Waterfalls of Khoung Si & Charming Luang Prabang Discover

Drive to the Khoung Si Waterfalls, which are located south of Luang Prabang. The multi-stage waterfalls of limestone formations and numerous turquoise water basins have become one of the most popular excursion destinations in North Laos. Numerous small, natural limestone pools that have formed through water erosion over time invite you to a refreshing bath. So don’t forget to pack your swimwear. Then return to Luang Prabang. Your voyage of discovery through Luang Prabang will take you to the most characteristic temples, to the top of Mount Phousi, to the city center and later to Hmong Market. You’ll also be sure to explore the city’s oldest temple, Wat Visoun, the magnificent National Museum and the Wat Xieng Thong Temple. Enjoy your time in this charming city and soak up the spiritual atmosphere deep within yourself. Overnight in Luang Prabang.

Day 3 Traditional Crafts & Explore the Pat-Tok Cave

After breakfast, leave Luang Prabang and continue on to Nong Khiaw. Along the way, visit a village known for its cotton weave art. This craft has been taught there for almost 300 years and gives you deep insights into this ancient tradition. Then visit Pat-Tok Cave, which is located outside of Nong Khiaw on a single rock. At the time of the war, it was used as a headquarters for protection for the villagers. The entrance of the cave is about 50 meters above the ground and can be reached via steps. Once you arrive in the cave, you will see that inside it resembles an impressive dome on two levels and offers amazing views of the surrounding mountains and rice fields. Overnight in Nong Khiaw.

Day 4 Guests with ethnic minorities

Today you will travel on to the Oudomxay region. The surrounding mountains extend far to China, Vietnam and Laos. The area is characterized by a magnificent mix of lush, green landscapes and your view is inevitably drawn to the breathtaking mountain scenery. On the journey to Muang La, you will visit various places inhabited by ethnic minorities. Immerse yourself in a completely foreign world and try to get in touch with the friendly residents. Your guide will be happy to help you. Then reach your daily destination Muang La. Your accommodation is the enchanting Muang La Resort, which has a spa, sauna and steam bath (especially from November to March it gets very cold here in the evening). The resort is next to a natural hot spring and every evening a small pool is filled with the water of the hot spring. The evenings here are a delight: you will be spoiled with a 5-course menu that combines Asian and European cuisine, candlelight and the sounds of nature. Overnight in Muang La.

5th day Picturesque walk to a Khmu village

Lace up your walking shoes! You start in the morning towards Pak Khai. Enjoy a wonderful view of the Nam Pak River, rice fields and small villages along the river shore. On a small mountain trail that follows a cool mountain stream, your hike begins through lush vegetation. Determine your own pace and enjoy the silence of nature. Along the way, you will have the opportunity to observe the farmers at their work in the rice fields. Afterwards, leave the forest and arrive at the village of Ban Phavie. The beautiful Khmu village is located on the top of a mountain. Your lunch will be served today in this village. Try to communicate with the villagers and enjoy the deep insights into rural village life. After a short rest, continue your hike and visit some nearby waterfalls (weather dependent). In the late afternoon you will return to your hotel, where you can treat yourself to a well-deserved rest. Overnight in Muang La.

6th day Visit to Nam Kat Yola Pa Park & refreshing bath in the waterfall

Today you will travel to the south to visit Nam-Kat-Yola-Pa Park. Immediately after arrival, you can relax in nature and enjoy the fresh air. The tour begins with a short drive in an electric car along winding, narrow roads that lead past dense forests. After about 15 minutes you will reach the starting point for a hike on the nature trail to the waterfall. Don’t forget to bring your swimwear with you, because the cool water invites you to a refreshing bath. Overnight in Oudomxay.

7th day Border Crossing to China & Botanical Garden of Menglun

After breakfast, you will be taken to Boten from Nam-Kat-Yola-Pa Park. Then it is time to say goodbye to the enchanting Laos. At the Chinese border of Mohan, you are already expected by your Chinese guide and continue to Menglun. You will travel through the tropical Xishuangbanna in the deep south of Yunnan, which is mainly characterized by its humid-tropical climate. Over ten ethnic minorities live in this region, wedged between Laos and Myanmar. Once in Menglun, visit the tropical Botanical Garden with over 3000 plant species. Overnight in Menglun.

8th day on the legendary Tea and Horse Road

Today’s ride will take you through one of China’s most important tea-growing areas. Especially known for this region is the Pu’er tea, which is also becoming increasingly popular in Europe. In the village of Yiwu you can convince yourself of the excellent quality of the tea. The place is also considered a starting point for the legendary Tea and Horse Road. This, like the Silk Road, was an extensive trade network that connected Yunnan with the Tibetan highlands and India. Then reach your daily destination Jiangcheng, where you will be taken to your hotel. Overnight in Jiangcheng.

9th Day Picturesque Overland Voyage & Ethnic Minorities

The path is the goal. Your full-day car ride will take you along the Lixianjiang border river to Yuanyang today. Keep your camera ready and enjoy the impressive landscapes. On the way, stop in Lvchun and visit the ethnic cultural museum. Lvchun is called “the green spring” in Chinese and therefore very appropriately describes the place. It is also home to the largest branch of the Hani minority. Then we continue to Laomeng, which is very well known for its Sunday market. All six local minorities meet here every Sunday in their traditional costumes and head decorations. Arriving in Yuangyang, you will be taken to your hotel. Overnight in Yuangyang.

10th day UNESCO rice terraces of Yuangyang

Getting up early is an absolute must today! In beautiful weather, you can experience a breathtaking sunrise over the impressive rice terraces. Keep your camera ready. You will certainly not forget this sight so quickly. Back at the hotel, you can have breakfast in peace and start the day with a good cup of coffee or tea. Then take a hike with spectacular views of the rice terraces and the surrounding villages. Countless rice terraces, some of which extend from horizon to horizon, provide you with infinite photo opportunities. Take enough time and enjoy the impressive landscape. Overnight in Yuangyang.

11th Day Impressive Garden Art and Confuciu Temple in Jianshui

Today you will travel to Jianshui, whose history dates back to the Jin Dynasty (256 – 420). Later, Janshui also played a role in the kingdoms of Ningzhou and Nanzhao. During the Yuan Dynasty (1279 – 1368), Jianshui (Lin An) was a major military outpost, contributing to the emergence of today’s city. Together with your guide, you will walk through the magnificent garden of the Zhu family. Then visit the idyllic Temple of Confucius, which dates back to 1285 and is the second largest Temple of Confucius in China. With a cozy stroll through the old town you can end the day wonderfully. Overnight in Jianshui.

Day 12 Bizarre-Beautiful Stone Forest & Onward to Kunming

After breakfast, we continue to Shilin. A very special forest awaits your visit: the “trees” of this almost 26 000 Hectares of large area are narrow, bizarrely shaped rock needles that protrude between five and thirty meters in height. On twelve square kilometers you can admire different rock formations. On the rock walls you can see corals, because the geological origin of the stone forest lies in a disappeared sea. These natural karst formations emerged around 200 million Years when the earth's crust rose and the water slipped. Noisy waterfalls, clear lakes and karst caves complement the impressive and bizarre landscape. One 1.200 Metre long, safely marked hiking trail winds through the stone labyrinth past fantastic creatures reminiscent of people, animals, swords and pagodas. The trail leads to the Lotus summit, from where you can see the stone forest in its full size. Then you reach the final destination of your journey. Kunming, the “city of eternal spring” and capital of Yunnan Province, is already waiting for you. As the name suggests, Kunming spoils its guests with a mild climate all year round. Overnight in Kunming.

13th day Departure from China

Your unforgettable journey is coming to an end. You will be picked up and taken to Kunming Airport. Even if your holiday is soon over – the precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories of your trip will accompany you forever. Look forward to letting your friends and family share your experiences in the distance at home. Would you like to discover even more of the original beauty of southwest China and experience the cultural diversity of Yunnan’s mountain peoples?

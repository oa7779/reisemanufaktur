Sulawesi Round Trip – Symphony of Culture and Nature

Sulawesi Round Trip - Symphony of Culture and Nature. With Asia Special Tours, discover the fascinating island of Sulawesi during your private Indonesia trip.

Itinerary & services

English itinerary text is an offline machine translation of the German original for this team preview. Please confirm travel details with your advisor; the German source remains available through the language switch.

On this trip, discover the fascinating island of Sulawesi. Unforgettable adventures, enchanting natural beauties and a cultural diversity are waiting for you! In Tanah Toraja you will encounter the fairytale land of a thousand years old tradition. Let yourself be enchanted by the strange architecture and testimonies of religious ceremonies! On this trip you will also discover the beauty of the unique nature of Sulawesi. On Gangga Island you can really relax and immerse yourself in the colorful underwater world of the Celebessee with its great biodiversity. This trip is our insider tip for every Asian fan!

1st day Arrival in Makassar

Welcome to the charming island of Sulawesi. After your local guide welcomes you, head to your hotel in Makassar together. The rest of the day is at your disposal. Makassar is the fifth largest city in Indonesia, yet it has retained its original charm. Visit the harbor with its many fishing boats and watch the bustle. Overnight in Makassar.

2nd day On the way to Rantepao

Today you drive along the “Transsulawesi Road” to Toraja. Along the way, visit a traditional village of the Bugis, a tribe known for the typical “Phinisi” boats and its houses built on stilts. Your path will take you to the mountains of Sulawesi, one of the most beautiful regions of the whole island. In Rantepao you move into your hotel in the late afternoon. Overnight in Rantepao.

3rd - 4th Day Two-Day Toraja Tour: Rituals and Traditions

In the next two days you will explore the region around Tanah Toraja. This area is unique because visiting the Toraja offers fascinating insights into their ancient culture. You will visit original villages with the traditional houses “Tongkonan”. As a distinguishing feature, the houses have an oversized saddle roof in boat form. Usually they are also colorfully decorated. While visiting the rock tombs carved in limestone, you will have the opportunity to delve deeper into the culture of the Toraja. In front of the entrances of the tombs are so-called Tau Tau. These are wooden figures representing the deceased. With a little luck, you can even attend a funeral ceremony. Also one of the local markets, where regionally typical products and handicrafts are traded, is on your program. In the mountainous region around Batutumonga, you will visit impressive mountain villages of the Toraja during an approximately two-hour hike. You will get unique insights into the life and coexistence of the people. Your walk leads past ornate rice terraces and traditional family homes. In addition, there are magnificent views of the beautiful nature and landscape of this region. Overnight stays in Rantepao.

Day 5 Into the former center of the Wajo Kingdom

After breakfast, leave for Sengkang, the former capital of the Wajo Kingdom. On your way there are always interesting stops for photography and staying. Today, Sengkang is the center of traditional buginese silk weaving. Not far from the city center is the well-known lake Tempe. You will be taken to your hotel where you can relax a little from the ride. Overnight in Sengkang.

6th Day Floating Houses on Lake Tempe

Today it is time to get up early! With a traditional fishing boat, you will cross the picturesque lake Tempe at dawn. Enjoy the peaceful atmosphere with a cup of coffee or tea and watch how slowly the day awakens. Nowadays there are still a few floating houses inhabited by the locals. With some great photo opportunities in your luggage, you will return to the hotel where you can have breakfast in peace. Then continue on to the east coast of Sulawesi. In the evening you will reach the small town of Bira with its snow-white beaches and crystal clear water. Overnight in Bira.

7th day snorkeling on Liukan Island

Paradise is waiting for you! In the morning we will take a traditional fishing boat to the nearby island of Liukan. Explore the fascinating underwater world with its colorful fish and corals with diving glasses. After this magnificent refreshment, visit a small fishing village and enjoy your picnic. In the afternoon you have enough time to put on fins and goggles again or just relax on the beach. Then return to your hotel in Bira. Overnight in Bira.

Day 8 Back to Makassar

Enjoy another extensive beach walk before returning to Makassar around noon. Along the way, visit Tana Beru, where the famous “Phinisi” boats are made. You will also stop at the Bissappu falls before reaching Makassar in the evening. Overnight in Makassar.

9th day Flight to Manado

After breakfast, you will be taken to the airport and fly to Manado, in the far north of the island. There you will be expected and brought to your hotel. The rest of the day is at your disposal. How about a visit to the Museum Negeri Propinsi Sulawesi Utara with its traditional costumes and everyday objects of ethnic groups? Or stop by the 19th-century Chinese temple. Overnight in Manado.

Day 10 Hike to Lokon Empung Volcano

Today the hiking shoes are laced. You will leave Manado and drive to the starting point for the hike to Lokon Empung Volcano, one of the most active volcanoes in Indonesia. The further you walk, the more vegetation changes. The jungle gives way to the tall grass until it turns into pure rock. After about 3 hours you reach the crater rim, where you can enjoy a fantastic view of the surrounding volcanic landscape and Tomohon. After a picnic, you will return to Tomohon (descent about 1 hour) where you will be taken to your hotel. Overnight in Tomohon.

Day 11 in Tangkoko National Park

In the morning, visit the Tasikoko Wildlife Rescue Center and learn about the endangered wildlife of the Indonesian archipelago. Alongside wildlife rescue and rehabilitation, the center runs educational programs to raise local awareness of Indonesia's valuable biodiversity. Continue to Tangkoko National Park, which covers about 90 km². On your walk, discover dense rainforest, hornbills, and the calls of black macaques high in the trees. With luck, you may see tarsiers, described in the original itinerary as the world's smallest monkey species. After this nature experience, drive to your hotel in the Lembeh Strait. Overnight in the Lembeh Strait.

12th day Departure to Gangga Island

After breakfast, you will be taken to the harbor, where the boat will continue on Gangga Island. Crystal clear water, dreamlike sandy beaches and excellent diving areas are waiting for you! Their sprawling resort is nestled in shaded coconut palms and has comfortable bungalows, as well as a welcoming seawater swimming pool and spa area. The dreamlike white sand beach invites you to romantic walks. Overnight at Gangga Island.

13-14. Day relax on Gangga Island

These days are all about rest and relaxation. Let the soul dangle and enjoy moments when time seems to stop. We recommend a snorkeling or diving trip where you will encounter an enchanting underwater world with its great biodiversity. Overnight stays on Gangga Island.

15th day Gangga Island - Manado - Departure

Today it is unfortunately to say goodbye to this fascinating island and its still so unknown treasures. You will take the boat back to Manado, where you will be taken to the airport. Afterwards, your home or onward flight is waiting for you.

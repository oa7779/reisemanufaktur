This trip combines insights into the cultural and natural treasures of Sri Lanka as well as the everyday life of the locals with a detour to the tropical bathing paradise. After spending a week visiting the impressive cultural sites and enchanting landscapes of ancient Ceylon, fly on to the Maldives. The bright white sandy beaches are one of the places of longing par excellence. Surrounded by turquoise blue sea and towering over green palm trees, they radiate an almost unreal peace and beauty. A first-class hotel selection with exclusive service and maximum comfort leaves little to be desired. Immerse yourself in your personal dream world and visit the tropical paradise in the Indian Ocean.

Day 1 Arrival in Colombo

Your tour of Sri Lanka begins in the state capital, Colombo. Upon arrival at the airport, you will be welcomed by the local guide and escorted to your hotel in Colombo. In the afternoon, take a small sightseeing tour of the capital. Among other things, you will visit the lively markets of the multi-ethnic Pettah district and end the day in a relaxed way. Have a cool beer at the Dutch Hospital and take a look at the chic shop windows. Smell the scent of incense and listen to the bells of the Gangarama Temple. Overnight in Colombo.

2nd Day Authentic Country Life & Cave Temples of Dambulla

Today you will travel to Dambulla After arriving you will visit the famous rock cave temple. Situated on a hill about 150 meters high, the caves offered protection to King Walagamba during his 14-year exile in the first century BC. Returning to the throne, he built the largest temple in the country at that time. In total, there are about 80 cave temples in Dambulla. In the decorated shrines there are more than 150 Buddha statues. With an area of over 2000 square meters, the temple complex is one of the largest in the country and was included in the list of UNESCO World Heritage Sites in 1982. Afterwards you will be taken to your charming hotel in Sigiriya. Enjoy a lot of privacy and exclusive service in this quiet oasis surrounded by peaceful nature. Overnight in Sigiriya.

3rd Day Walk in Diyakapilla & the Mighty Kingdom of Polonnaruwa

A trip to the idyllic village of Diyakapilla brings you closer to Sri Lanka’s rural life and authentic cuisine. A short drive leads along the old pilgrimage route over the Gallinda hill chain. Once in the village, take a walk along a pilgrimage trail that runs through forests, agricultural areas and rice fields. You will learn how seasonally available cereals and vegetables are harvested according to traditional methods and prepared into tasty dishes. Before you start your journey, enjoy a lunch prepared by a family from the village. Then we will return to your hotel. In the afternoon, a trip into the past awaits you: Polonnaruwa was the capital of a powerful kingdom almost a thousand years ago. This period is witnessed by magnificent statues and palaces, impressive stupas, detailed frescoes and last but not least “Gal Vihara”, an imposing rock sanctuary with four Buddha images. In the southern part of Polonnaruwa you will come across the statue of King Parakramabahu I, to whom the city owes its fame. Then we will return to your hotel. Overnight in Sigiriya.

4th day Lion Rocks from Sigiriya & Tooth Temple in Kandy

The Sigiriya Rock is often referred to as the “Eighth Wonder of the World”. On the plateau of a monolith, a rock fortress was built in the 5th century AD. Since in former times a stone lion towered at the entrance of the bastion, this place is called "Lion Rock" in the local language. However, only two paws of this statue survived over the centuries. At half-height some frescoes of women have been preserved – of these “cloud girls” are said to have decorated the walls of about five hundred. Afterwards you will visit a spice garden on the way to Kandy in Matale. Kandy, the second largest city in Sri Lanka and former royal residence lies in the middle of the central mountains about 500 meters above sea level. Despite its size, it is still very much influenced by ancient traditions. The main attraction is undoubtedly the tooth temple, in which a canine of Buddha is said to be kept in a golden shrine. According to tradition, this tooth emanates spiritual power, making the sanctuary one of the most important pilgrimage sites in the country for Buddhists. In the evening you are invited to a cultural show with various dances of Sri Lanka. Overnight in Kandy.

Day 5 The Secrets of Tea Growing in Nuwara Eliya

You will leave Kandy and continue on to Nuwara Eliya in the central highlands of Sri Lanka. Gracefully perched the highest mountain in the country, the “Pidurutalagala”, above the lush green tea fields of the area. The city bears the nickname “Little England”, because much reminds of the time of the past empire. The British settled here to cultivate tea. Thus, the mountains around Nuwara Eliya are still known as the main growing area of the famous Ceylon tea. The weather in Nuwara Eliya also occasionally shows up from its British side due to the high location of almost 2000 meters and brings cool nights with it. Along the way, visit a tea plantation and learn a lot about this traditional drink. Of course, a tea tasting should not be missing! Overnight in Nuwara Eliya

Day 6 - Continue to Yala National Park

Today you will continue your journey to the southeast of Sri Lanka. Here you will find a testimony of the country’s rich natural resources: the Yala National Park. It is the oldest nature reserve in Sri Lanka. Over 130 bird species such as flamingos, pelicans, peacock and cormorants have settled here. Around 30 mammal species roam the area. For example, they include elephants and water buffaloes. Large parts of the park are reminiscent of the African savannah, while the outer parts of the park are dominated by moist monsoon forests. Your accommodation - the Uga Chena Huts - is just 5 minutes from Yala National Park. The design reflects the regional architecture in harmony with the surroundings of the jungle. Overnight in Yala.

Day 7 Jeep Safari in Yala National Park

Following in the footsteps of the leopards, explore the National Park early this morning as well as during the afternoon hours on a Jeep safari. The rest of the time you can enjoy in your beautiful resort quite relaxed. Overnight in Yala.

8th day Stelzenfischer and colonial heritage in Galle

The next destination of your trip is Galle. On your way there, you will have the opportunity to observe stilt fishermen at work. From narrow piles, anchored not far from the shore in the sea arc, they fish sardines, mackerel and small herring. Galle, located in the southwest of Sri Lanka, was the most important port in the country before the arrival of the Portuguese in the 16th century. However, the peak in development reached Galle under Dutch rule. The current landmark is the old Dutch fort. The old town inside the fort was added to the World Heritage List in 1988. Overnight in Galle.

9th day Flight to the Maldives

After you have gained many impressions of the country’s natural and cultural treasures on your round trip through Sri Lanka, relaxing days in the island paradise of the Maldives await you at the end of your trip. You will be taken to the airport of Colombo, where a short flight will take you to Male. With the speedboat you continue to your resort. The elegant Anantara Dhigu Maldives Resort has 110 villas and suites and is decorated with natural materials in typical Maldivian style. Several restaurants offer culinary variety. An infinity pool, fitness center and a wide range of water sports facilities including PADI diving school round off the offer. The exclusive spa area, which is located on stilts above the water, provides unforgettable experiences. Leave any everyday stress behind and listen to the sound of the sea. Overnight in the Maldives.

10.-14. Day bathing in the Maldives

The bright white beaches of the Maldives are one of the places of longing par excellence. Surrounded by turquoise blue sea and towering over green palm trees, they radiate an almost unreal peace and beauty. Enjoy in the middle of this paradisiacal backdrop a few days away from everyday life. Of course, you can extend or shorten your stay as you wish. Overnight stays in the Maldives.

Day 15 Maldives – Departure

Today, your time out from everyday life is coming to an end. Even if your holiday is over, the precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories of your trip will accompany you forever. Look forward to letting your friends and family share your experiences in the distance at home. With the speedboat it goes back to Male, where you take your flight home.

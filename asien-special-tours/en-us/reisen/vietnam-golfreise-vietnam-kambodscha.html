Golf dreams in Vietnam and Cambodia

Vietnam and Cambodia Golf Tour: Experience Vietnam and Cambodia on your individual journey with Asia Special Tours. Ask them in a moment.

Itinerary & services

English itinerary text is an offline machine translation of the German original for this team preview. Please confirm travel details with your advisor; the German source remains available through the language switch.

Are you a passionate golfer and would like to pursue your hobby when traveling? Then this trip is made for you. You will not only experience the most spectacular golf courses in Indochina, but also immerse yourself in breathtaking natural landscapes and grandiose cultural monuments. Play in the most beautiful squares of Vietnam and Cambodia and let your views wander over white beaches, green overgrown karst rocks and gentle hillside landscapes. In between, there is enough space for a flexible time division and very private moments. Enjoy a perfect combination of golf, culture and nature and treat yourself to a break.

1st day Arrival in Hanoi & first orientation walk

Your golf trip through Vietnam and Cambodia begins in the Vietnamese capital with its green boulevards, French colonial-style buildings and fascinating temples. The historic old town of Hanoi is a tangle of small alleys that is probably unique throughout Asia. You can still see a few really old houses and shops, but they have to give way to modernity due to increasing profit pressure at a rapid pace. Upon arrival at Hanoi airport, you will be expected by your guide and taken to your hotel. Back in the colonial period you will find yourself in the exclusive Sofitel Legend Metropole. The “Grande Dame” by Hanoi exudes the charm of long gone times. This elegant French colonial-style house is located in the middle of the historic city center, making it the ideal starting point for your visits to Vietnam’s capital. A luxurious spa with pool as well as several very good restaurants offer relaxation and enjoyment after sightseeing. A first orientation walk (optionally you can also take a walk in a typical bicycle rickshaw) leads you through the old town of Hanoi, where you can collect many first impressions. Overnight in Hanoi.

2nd day exploration tour of the charming Hanoi

The modern capital of Vietnam has preserved the mystery and charm of past centuries. Narrow alleys and traditional shops, lakes, parks, boulevards and monuments invite you to explore. That is what you will do today. As part of your city tour, you will visit the Temple of Literature – Vietnam’s first university, the One-Pillar Pagoda, the Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum, Ho Chi Minh’s House, the Ethnography Museum and Hoan Kiem Lake with the Turtle Pagoda. Surrounded by beautiful trees, old colonial buildings and parts of the Old Town, the “Sea of the Returned Sword” is a popular refuge for Hanoians of all ages. According to legend, a turtle lives here, which once lent a king a magic sword with which he could defeat the enemies of Vietnam. Let the evening end comfortably and enjoy the first-class amenities of your hotel. The Sofitel Legend Metropole is a house with an eventful history, many traditions and famous guests who have come in and gone out. Join the hotel historian on a 45-minute guided tour of the hotel grounds and learn more about the eventful past. The guided tours take place twice a day (usually 17:00 and 18:00). Overnight in Hanoi.

3rd day of golf at the Royal Golf Club Ninh Binh

Look forward to the first golf game of your trip today. After breakfast, your chauffeur will take you to the Royal Golf Club Ninh Binh. After playing the 18 holes, you can relax in the late afternoon on a rowing boat ride through rice fields and the spectacular rock formations of the “Dry Halong Bay”. You will then be taken to your hotel. Enjoy the tasteful Emeralda Ninh Binh Resort, which is beautifully integrated into nature in the middle of the rice fields and impresses with a very good service and tasty cuisine. Overnight in Ninh Binh.

Day 4 Experience the fascination of Halong Bay

After breakfast, a UNESCO World Heritage site awaits your visit: you drive to the fascinating Halong Bay. Almost 2000 islands, emerald green water and the sails of the junks make this bay in the Gulf of Tonkin one of the most beautiful photo opportunities in Vietnam. The Vietnamese name Vinh Ha Long translated to “bay of the submerging dragon”. Late in the morning you board a traditional junk. The Violet Junk is named after the beautiful violet Chirita, a delicate, violet plant that grows nowhere else than on the rocky slopes of Halong Bay. Like its namesake, the Violet is small and exquisite. With only six luxurious cabins, it offers an exclusive view of the wonders of Halong Bay. Enjoy a fresh lunch on board as you gradually sail through the maze of this magical island world. During a delicious dinner with fresh seafood, you will experience a unique sunset in the middle of this dreamlike backdrop. Overnight in Halong Bay.

Day 5: Chi Linh Star and Golf Country Club

If you are one of the early risers, you can experience the magical Halong Bay at the most beautiful time of day: The rising sun breaks through the early fog and immerses the bay in a magical light. If you wish, you can gather strength for the day during a few tai chi exercises or still join the excursion of the ship program. While you have breakfast in peace, slowly cruise back to the harbor. In the late morning you have solid ground under your feet again. On the way back to Hanoi, you can enjoy a round of golf at Chi Linh Star and Golf Country Club in front of a scenic setting. After making the 18 holes, return to Hanoi. Overnight in Hanoi.

Day 6: Travel to Hoi An & First-class Four Seasons The Nam Hai

Let this day go on calmly. Have a leisurely breakfast or make one more round of the city, where life is already busy in the early morning. During the day, you will be picked up by your chauffeur and taken to the airport to fly on to Danang in central Vietnam. At Danang Airport, you will be met again and taken to your hotel. The exquisite luxury hotel Four Seasons The Nam Hai offers everything you need to leave everyday life behind and enjoy the here and now to the fullest. Nestled in a beautiful tropical garden, the stylish villas offer an elegant-puristic design based on the Feng Shui principle. Contemporary Vietnamese works of art and high-quality materials provide a pleasant feel-good ambiance in the villas. Enjoy luxury and comfort at the highest level. Fine restaurants, a very attentive service, several pools and an excellent spa area leave nothing to be desired. Listen to the sound of waves on the untouched sandy beach Ha My, balance your body with yoga exercises or play a few rounds of golf. Overnight in Hoi An.

7th Day Golf Club Montgomerie Links

After a refreshing breakfast, it is for you to Golf Club Montgomerie LinksLocated between Danang and Hoi An, it was designed by 2010 eight-time Order of Merit and Ryder Cup winner Colin Montgomerie. The course is repeatedly recognized as one of the most elite golf courses in Asia and challenges the best players in the world. It is also suitable and playable for all players with medium and high handicap. Swing your golf club and clear your head and wide your heart. Overnight in Hoi An.

8th day free of charge

Feel freedom, breathe freedom. Today is at your disposal. Let the soul relax, enjoy the sun, beach and sea or take advantage of the numerous amenities of your hotel. Overnight in Hoi An.

9th day Flight to Siem Reap & feel-good ambiance in your hotel

By flight, your journey will continue to Siem Reap, which will be your starting point in the next few days to explore the world-famous Angkor Park with about 40 square kilometers and more than 100 temples dating from the 9th to 13th centuries. Welcome to Cambodia! Your guide is already waiting for you and will take you to your first-class hotel. The luxurious 5 star Hotel Belmond La Résidence d'Angkor is the perfect choice for a few unforgettable days in Siem Reap. Before you embark on a new adventure, relax by the pool or let yourself be pampered with a soothing spa application. Take some time off and let it go well. Overnight in Siem Reap.

Day 10: Discover Mystical Angkor

With the Tuk Tuk you reach one of the cultural highlights of your trip in the morning: the world-famous Angkor Wat Temple. Built in the first half of the 12th century, this World Heritage Site is the largest and undoubtedly most important monument of the Angkor Temple Group. The sanctuary is a masterpiece of Khmer architecture: within the vast complex you will find beautifully designed carvings of figures from Hindu mythology as well as nearly 2000 Apsaras, so-called “heavenly dancers”. In the afternoon, visit the ruined city of Angkor Thom, once the last capital of the Great Khmer Empire in the 13th century. The well-preserved remains of the city lie amidst the dense jungle and are surrounded by an eight-meter-high wall that is four kilometers long on each side, forming a perfect square. You enter the city through the impressive South Gate, a stone gate carved with four gigantic faces and elephants. From here, drive to Bayon Temple in the middle of town. A special feature of this monument are the 54 towers representing the 54 provinces of the Great Khmer Empire. Each tower has four faces, each facing in different directions. Let the evening end calmly and relaxed. Overnight in Siem Reap.

11th day of golf at Angkor Golf Resort

Without guidance

After breakfast, your driver will take you to Angkor Golf Resort, which was named Cambodia’s best golf course in 2014. The 18-hole golf course has a par of 72 and challenges golfers of every level. In the evening, you will be a guest in a very special circus: In the “Phare” young people from poor backgrounds perform, who receive school education and acrobatics lessons through a social project. Although the actors are not yet as routine in everything as professional acrobats, they already bring impressive shows to the stage – in Siem Reap and on tours abroad. Overnight in Siem Reap.

12th day of golf at Phokeethra Golf Club

Without guidance

Even today, use Siem Reap as a backdrop for a game of golf at Phokeethra Golf Club. The 18-hole course is crisscrossed by extensive fairways and greens. Natural lagoons and sandholes put your skills to the test. For variety, a mixture of par 3, 4 and 5 tracks provides. Then return to your hotel. How about a visit to a modern art gallery or an interesting museum? Overnight in Siem Reap.

13th day Onward travel to Phnom Penh & exciting city tour

Your journey will take you to Phnom Penh today. Sit back and let your eyes wander during the overland journey. Upon arrival, explore the highlights of Cambodia’s capital. Your first stop is the impressive Royal Palace. It dates back to 1866 and is the last palace built during the French colonial period. The complex also houses the Silver Pagoda, named after the over 5000 kilogram silver plates that adorn the floor. The sanctuary houses a collection of Buddhas in gold, silver, crystal and bronze. Continue to the National Museum, which boasts a world-leading collection of ancient archaeological, religious and artistic artifacts from the 4th to 13th centuries. Over 5000 exhibits show the cultural richness of the past kingdom. Another attraction is the bat colony in the roof of the building! You will then be taken to your hotel. The luxurious Raffles Le Royal is undisputedly one of the top addresses in the city. Enjoy the historic ambience and first-class service of this hotel. Overnight in Phnom Penh.

14th day of golf at Garden City Golf Club

Today you can go back to your passion of playing golf. You play at the Garden City Golf course, which opened its 97 acres to players in November 2013. The 18-hole golf course is well equipped with a modern spa, pool, restaurant and fitness room. Then return to your hotel. Visit one of the city’s trendy bars or stroll along the waterfront. Overnight in Phnom Penh.

15th day Flight to Saigon & extravagant Hotel The Reverie

The next stop of your trip, Saigon, is a short flight away. The city builds a bridge between cultures and epochs: modern high-rises stand side by side with colonial buildings and traditional temples. On the streets, an army of mopeds crackles, and bicycle rickshaws wind between them. Young people use their mobile phones while in the quiet backyards, believers pray amidst clouds of incense. Your guide is already waiting for you at the airport and will take you to your hotel. The exclusive Hotel The Reverie is one of the most extravagant hotels Saigon has to offer. The hotel is a total work of art and is rightly one of the Leading Hotels of the World. Equipped with Italian designer pieces, the hotel offers beautiful rooms that give you all unforgettable views of the Saigon skyline with floor-to-ceiling windows. A modern lifestyle coupled with the highest standard of hospitality awaits you. Culinary delights such as the romantic restaurant R&J, named after Shakespeare’s romance Romeo and Juliet, take you into your personal dream world. Centrally located in the heart of the city, Hotel The Reverie is the perfect starting point for your stay in Saigon. Overnight in Saigon.

16th day street food tour with Mr. Dittko

Join Mr. Dittko for a stroll through Saigon’s markets. Mr. Dittko is originally from Germany, has been living in Saigon since 1995 and knows the city like the back of his hand. During a culinary discovery tour, he brings you closer to the sophistication and balance of the local cuisine. Get to know the delicious little pancakes “Banh Khot”, which are rolled together with herbs into a lettuce leaf and then dunked in the hearty “sweet” fish sauce. And find out what all the culinary influences the Vietnamese have taken over from their “occupiers”. “An com chua?” – “Already eaten rice?”: This is how people greet each other here at lunchtime. To do just this, you will stop at a small restaurant where you have dedicated yourself to the traditional home cooking. During a visit to the Tan Dinh Market, you will discover the incredible selection of fruits and vegetables, fresh seafood and various types of tofu, which are delivered daily fresh from neighboring regions. At the same time, you will learn about the history, politics, religion and life of the people in the mega-city Saigon. In no other city in the country can you discover the whole range of Vietnamese cuisine as well as here. Mr. Dittko will show you the best cooking kitchens and restaurants in Saigon. Overnight in Saigon.

17th Day Long Thanh Golf Club & Visit to the A&O Show

Without a guide

Take a deep breath and take a break from the hustle and bustle of Saigon. About an hour from the megacity is the 36-hole Long Thanh Golf Club. On the 350-hectare area you can wander between your tees the view over a unique panorama. The area was designed by the world-renowned company Golf Plan – with palm gardens and waterfalls. 18 of the 36 holes were created on a hill and are particularly challenging, the rest can also master ambitious new golfers. For a particularly stylish ending of this evening, the visit to the A-O-Show in Saigon. This unique art form, in an innovative symbiosis of music, acrobatics and effects, also known as the “New Circus”, gives an insight into the soul and cultural heritage of the Vietnamese. Overnight in Saigon.

18th day of golf at Vietnam Golf & Country Club

Without guidance

Your destination today is the Vietnam Golf & Country Club. It is the first 36-hole golf course in the country, about an hour from the center of Saigon. The area had to be changed only slightly in order to serve as a golf course – here you experience a particularly natural golf enjoyment. Then return to Saigon. How about a spectacular ending of the day at one of Saigon’s hottest bars? The exclusive Chill Skybar is located at breezy altitude on the 25th floor of the AB Tower and is the perfect place for an unforgettable sundowner. Overnight in Saigon.

19th day Flight home from Saigon

Even when your holiday is over, the precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories of your trip will accompany you forever. Look forward to letting your friends and family share your experiences in the distance at home. Your guide will take you to Saigon Airport for your return or onward flight.

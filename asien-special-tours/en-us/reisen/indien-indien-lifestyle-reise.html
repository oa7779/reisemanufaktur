Lifestyle and Culture Travel - The Colors of India

India Lifestyle and Culture Tour: The Colors of India. Experience the colorful facets of India with Asia Special Tours. Ask now!

India – a country as multifaceted and full of colors as hardly any other. The vast state offers a fascinating mosaic of exotic life forms, architectural masterpieces and regional dishes and culinary styles that reflect the rich religious and cultural history of the country. The magnificent temples of Jaipur, the cosmopolitan Mumbai and the picturesque landscape of Kerala – they alone are worth a trip. During your trip, you will also immerse yourself in the different lifestyles of Indians – from lunchbox suppliers to fashion designers and Bollywood choreographers – feel the pulse of India on the bustling bazaars and go shopping with a local fashionista through Delhi. Of course, with the enormous multifacetedness of Indian cuisine, special culinary experiences must not be missed: visit an Indian couple in their pretty home for a cooking class of a special kind, try out the best street cuisines in Mumbai and let an Ayurvedic doctor introduce you to the secrets of the Ayurvedic diet. So intense and up close only the few Indian travelers experience the country.

1st Day Typical Indian: Saree, Henna & Excellent Food

Delhi – old cultural city, modern metropolis. The capital of India welcomes you. Upon arrival, you will be expected at the airport and brought to your hotel by your private chauffeur. Taj Mahal Hotel Delhi is located in the heart of the city, making it an ideal starting point for exploring. The magnificent Mogul architecture, the dramatic facade of pink Dholpur sandstone, immaculately maintained gardens and the spacious pool create the atmosphere of a luxurious oasis of peace. Here, splendor meets restrained elegance. Antiques, precious works of art, traditional accents and colors merge with modern style. Enjoy the fantastic view of the city from your room and let yourself go really well in the coming days. In the late afternoon, the first highlight of your trip awaits you: in the chic and modern Hauz Khas you are a guest with Neha and her husband Anmol, the operators of the “Saffron Palate”. After a warm welcome, it becomes really Indian. Together you prepare some traditional dishes and breads, get to wrap yourself in a saree and get decorated hands and feet with stylish henna paintings. Finally, you can taste your self-cooked dinner in a leisurely round before being returned to your hotel. Overnight in Delhi.

2nd day exploration tour through the urban jungle

After breakfast, you will embark on an exploration tour through the urban jungle, which will take you to Hauz Khas again. There you will visit an Oriya temple, unique in its architecture and belonging to the ethnic community of Orissa. Continue to the bookshop Sri Books, where you will meet Mr. KD Aryan, who has already been featured in a BBC documentary. Listen to his story and browse old books and movie posters. Exclusive shops and boutiques with designer jewelry and clothing, paintings and prints, restaurants and cafes in the old Havelis invite you to linger. You can also find some art galleries here. Finally, you will get to the gardens near the ruins of a madrassa. Perhaps you will meet the friendly old gentleman who often comes here to play cards and likes to be persuaded into an improvised Urdu lesson. After a snack in one of the charming restaurants, you will stroll over the Aurobindo market. In the afternoon, explore Shahpur Jat, a place of creativity and art. Discover the facets of the urban village with hidden little shops and try a chai in one of the countless tea kitchens. With many new impressions, you will finally return to your hotel. Overnight in Delhi.

Day 3 Preparations for an Indian Wedding

Indian weddings are grandiose and colorful celebrations that often last several days. You will visit a market that offers everything around weddings. You will visit a fashion studio to learn more about the colors and fabrics of wedding outfits and current wedding trends. Under the expert guidance of an expert, you will participate in an “Indian Wedding Stitch Workshop”. Afterwards, you can learn an Indian wedding dance at an exclusive dance workshop that lasts 30 minutes. Are you ready for some culinary highlights? Taste yourself through delicious dishes served at a classic Indian wedding. Your next item on the agenda: shopping. In case you don’t have much interest in shopping, this is also a great opportunity to watch soon-to-be married couples and their excited families. At dinner in the trendy restaurant “Indian Accent” you will end the day relaxed. The Indian Accent combines international ingredients and cooking techniques with Indian traditions to create a masterful symbiosis of tastes. Michelin Star chefs are also regularly invited to the “Indian Accent” to curate new menus. Overnight in Delhi.

Day 4 Art, Culinary and Culture in Jaipur

A new day, a new goal. Your journey will take you to Jaipur, the vibrant capital of the Indian state of Rajasthan. In the late 19th century, the city was painted in shades of pink in preparation for the visit of the British king – in Rajasthan’s tradition, this is the color of hospitality. To this day, Jaipur is still called the “Pink City”. After checking in at the hotel, you will meet your guide for a stroll through the local markets where you can chat with the dealers and get an insight into their daily work. Be sure to try some of the culinary delights of the region such as Pakoras, Aloo Tiki or Samosas. However, the city has also preserved some craft traditions that have been passed down from generation to generation. Take the opportunity to look over the shoulders of silversmiths, jewelry makers and other local artisans at work. Of course, you will also see the iconic “Palace of the Winds”, behind whose ornate facade the ladies of the court watched the bustle on the road. After this impressive walk, you will be returned to your hotel. Overnight in Jaipur.

5th Day Cinema, Tea and a Mighty Fort

Today, a true masterpiece of Mughal architecture awaits you: the imposing Fort Amber perched on a steep mountain slope north of Jaipur. With the Jeep you drive up to the main gate of the facility. The public audience hall, the magnificent Ganesha Gate and the Spiegelpalast will amaze you. From the galleries and terraces you also have a wonderful view over the valley and mountains of the Aravalli chain. At noon, stop at Spice Court Restaurant, one of the city’s most popular restaurants. Here you can not only fortify yourself with a delicious lunch for the afternoon, but also get the opportunity to chat with the chef. Your afternoon program will take you to the magnificent City Palace, which witnesses an era of abundance and in part still serves as the residence of the former Maharaja, as well as the Jantar Mantar Observatory, which houses the largest sundial in the world. Later you are invited to the Heritagehotel Dera Mandawa for afternoon tea. Enjoy the cozy, familiar atmosphere as you chat with the master of the house, a great-grandson of the builder and member of the Sadhani clan. In the evening, a performance awaits in India's oldest cinema, the Raj Mandir cinema. Even if you will not understand much, the visit is worthwhile, because nowhere can you feel the Bollywood feeling as well as here. Overnight in Jaipur.

6th Day Culinary Discovery Tour of Mumbai

Next destination on your trip is Mumbai, India’s largest city and one of the world’s most populous metropolises. Mumbai is also the center for Indian film production – Bollywood. After breakfast, your driver will take you to the airport for your domestic flight to Mumbai. Once there, you will first be brought to your hotel and can relax a little. In the evening, you’ll be picked up and taken to the popular recreational and festive grounds at Chowpatty Beach, where you’ll dive into the local street kitchen. From Pani Puri to Sev Puri and Golas to Pav Bhaji and Mumbai Sandwich, Mumbai’s street cuisine has evolved over the years and is an excellent reflection of the city’s vibrant and ever-changing culture. Be sure to try a few dishes and let the taste of Mumbai melt on your tongue. It accompanies you an English-speaking guide. Overnight in Mumbai.

7th day Mumbai at dawn and the Dabbawalas

Your day starts at dawn and gives you a fantastic insight into everyday life in Mumbai. Immerse yourself in the vibrant city life directly after sunrise. Watch the newspaper vendors and the bustle in the wholesale market. Afterwards we will visit the Sassoon Docks, where one of the largest fish markets in Mumbai takes place. In the later hours of the morning, everyday life also awakens. Back at the hotel, rest with breakfast before a walk through Dharavi takes you through Asia’s largest slum – home to nearly one million people. It is located in the middle of the city, which is rather unusual for slums. Even otherwise, Dharavi only partially corresponds to the prejudices about slums: It is a busy area with an estimated economic value of 500 million US dollars. Here, ceramics and textiles are produced and traded with recycled goods. On this tour you will get to know the workplaces and houses and experience the enthusiasm of the locals. In addition, an English-speaking expert explains the importance of the Dabbawalas: During the week in Mumbai about 200,000 dabbas, food containers made of sheet metal, are delivered. The food carriers work without technical aids. See how the Dabbawalas wind their way through the busy traffic of the metropolis to drive out the lunchboxes for the employees. Overnight in Mumbai.

8th day Mumbai with all your senses

Get ready for a special tour of the city that will appeal to all your senses. Your walk today is a tribute to the city in its entirety: the historical sights, the people and their culture. Stroll past magnificent colonial-era buildings with your guide, breathe in the scent of exotic spices at the famous Mumbai Spice Market and dance to cheerful Bollywood music with your personal choreographer. For lunch, enjoy a delicious dish of traditional Konkan cuisine, which is also home to Mumbai. The Konkan cuisine uses many spices, chilli powder and coconut in all conceivable forms and creates from it fiery-spicy palate delights. But even if you prefer it less spicy, the Konkan kitchen is just the right thing to spoil your sense of taste. Overnight in Mumbai.

Day 9: Insight into the world of Mudras & Kathakali dance performance

Another domestic flight will take you to Kochi today, which is best known for its old castle and delicious fish dishes, such as dried bonito. Once you’ve checked in at your hotel, head out on a first exploration tour together with your guide. You will visit the Chinese fishing nets, which are only used here outside of China. These heavy wooden structures, which must be operated by at least four men, are a fascinating sight especially when the morning sun is reflected in the ocean. Then stroll a little over the spice market. In the evening, visit a traditional Kathakali dance performance – a spectacular mix of drama, dance, music and ritual. Beforehand, you will meet a Kathakali dance master in his private studio who will introduce you to the world of mudras or ritual hand gestures. You will also learn some of the traditional mudras yourself and learn how much health benefits they can be in your daily life. A South Indian dinner at The Grand restaurant, which opened in 1963, brings the day to a relaxing end. Overnight in Kochi.

10th day Meeting with an Ayurvedic doctor

Kochi offers visitors everything that makes Kerala so worth seeing in concentrated form: beautiful coastal landscape, impressive buildings and lively life. Together with your guide, visit Fort Kochi, considered the oldest European settlement in India. In the afternoon you will have the unique opportunity to learn from a knowledgeable Ayurvedic doctor about the holistic health system Ayurveda. The traditional Ayurveda can be translated as the knowledge (veda) of long life (ayur). The most important element of the holistic system, which also includes massages, special cleansing treatments (panchakarma) and physical exercises (yoga), is nutritional teaching. Matched to the respective constitutional types, the appropriate foods ensure a healthy body and a balanced mind. During your meeting with the Ayurvedic doctor, you will receive an introduction to the basic principles of Ayurvedic dietetics, learn why each Ayurvedic meal should contain the six flavors sweet, sour, salty, tart, bitter and spicy, and prepare a balanced menu under guidance, while you taste it, there is still enough time to continue to communicate with the Ayurvedic doctor. Overnight in Kochi.

11th day boat trip on the picturesque Vembanad Lake

In Kochi you can also experience the variety of religious influences of the country: you will visit the Christian church of St. Francis, which was already completed in 1516, and the Paradesi Synagogue (closed on Fridays/Saturdays) built about fifty years later with its Chinese tiles and Belgian chandeliers. The once large Jewish community in Kochi is still witnessed by the Jewish quarter in Mattancherry. Afterwards, visit the Mattancherry Palace, built by the Portuguese in 1555 and presented to the Raja von Kochi as a sign of goodwill. The palace is also known as the Dutch Palace, since it was extensively renovated by the Dutch almost a century later. After a delicious dosa lunch at a local restaurant, head to Kumarakom on the edge of the famous backwaters – a labyrinth-like network of rivers, canals and lagoons in southern Kerala. Here you immediately move into your room in your accommodation for the coming nights. In the evening, take a leisurely boat trip on scenic Lake Vembanad, the largest freshwater lake in the region. Sit back and enjoy the delightful scenery as the sun slowly sinks on the horizon, dipping the land into gentle shades of red and yellow. Overnight in Kumarakom.

12th day of free time in Kumarakom

Without tour guide & chauffeur

These days belong entirely to you. Treat yourself to a soothing Ayurveda massage, relax at the swimming pool with fantastic views of Lake Vembanad or explore the area on your own. If you would like, we are also happy to organize another exploring tour for you. How about a trip to Philipkutty's Farm, idyllic on the Backwaters. The Mathew family has been cultivating the land for several generations, growing mangoes, coconut palms, pepper and bananas, and the owners will warmly welcome you and show you their home. Then you can choose from a variety of activities: explore the plantations on a guided tour, take a walk/bike tour of the nearby village, try your fishing luck or go bird watching. Boat tours, yoga, Ayurveda and a cooking demonstration are also offered. Overnight in Kumarakom.

Day 13 Back to Kochi and Farewell to India

Even the most beautiful journey comes to an end. During the last few days, you have gathered many unique impressions of the diversity of Indian culture and cuisine and gained an insight into the different lifestyles of Indians. With suitcases full of experiences and lasting memories, you will be taken to the airport where you will begin your journey home.

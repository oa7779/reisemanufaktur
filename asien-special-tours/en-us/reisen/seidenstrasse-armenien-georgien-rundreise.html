Georgia & Armenia Tour - Hidden Cultural Treasures of the Caucasus

Georgia and Armenia Round Trip - Discover the hidden cultural treasures on an individual round trip with Asia Special Tours. Ask directly here.

A varied journey is waiting for you, which includes both a lot of culture and the impressive landscape and excellent food of Armenia and Georgia. Christian churches and monasteries, mysterious cave cities and enchanting castle ruins await your visit. Numerous culinary experiences make your gourmet heart beat faster: In Armenia you taste brandy, in Georgia it must of course be wine. In addition, you are a guest with a local family and can look forward to a truly rich feast. Let yourself be transported into the fascinating world of the Caucasus with its mighty mountains and warm people.

1st day Arrival and first visits in and around Yerevan

Welcome to Armenia! Upon arrival at Yerevan airport, you will be expected by your local guide and driven to your accommodation. After a short rest, you will go on a city tour in Yerevan. Armenia’s capital was founded on the hill Arin Berd in 782 BC, making it one of the oldest cities in the world. First you will get to know the Republic Square, which was built in traditional Armenian architecture. Afterwards you will visit the Government House, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and the National Gallery of Paintings. Then your journey will take you to Etschmiatsin, which is about 20 kilometers from Yerevan. The Cathedral of Etschmiatsin is known as the center of the Armenian Gregorian Church and is considered one of the first Christian churches in the world. You have the opportunity to meet a parish priest. On the way back to Yerevan, visit the Temple of Zvartnot, a pearl of 7th-century architecture and UNESCO World Heritage Site. Afterwards you will return to your hotel, where you can end the day relaxed. Overnight in Yerevan.

2nd day Religious sites around Yerevan

After breakfast, drive to the Monastery of Choir Virap, located at the foot of Mount Ararat and near the closed border with Turkey. The importance of the monastery is due to Grigor, the enlightener. According to legend, he is said to have introduced Christianity in Armenia and is thus considered the first Armenian Catholic. Afterwards you will visit the Momik Wine Cube, where you will immerse yourself in the thousands of years old Armenian winemaking tradition during a wine tasting. When tasting selected wines, you will discover the typical aroma profiles and peculiarities of this traditional wine region. A joint lunch with regional specialties will round off the culinary experience harmoniously. Afterwards, your journey will take you to the Amaghu gorge. There is Noravank, perhaps the most beautiful Armenian monastery. With its reddish facade, it is hardly distinguishable from its rocky background at sunset. It is also considered a large religious and cultural center of the 12th century and is located in a place that also impresses with its breathtaking nature. Full of impressions, return to Yerevan. Overnight in Yerevan.

3rd Day Archaeological Findings in Yerevan & Brandy Tasting

Today you will dedicate yourself once again to the Armenian capital, because there is still much to discover. At Republic Square, visit the Historical Museum, which exhibits important archaeological collections from the Stone Age to the Middle Ages. In front of the museum is a large fountain, also known as “Singing Fountains”. Afterwards you will visit Zizernakaberd, a hill on which the memorial and museum for the victims of the genocide of Armenians in 1915 are located. Then a special highlight awaits you! Embark on a journey around the history of the brandy and learn everything you need to know about the production and maturation of the noble drop. During a tasting, convince yourself of the warm-velvet aroma and quality of the famous Armenian brandy. Overnight in Yerevan.

4th Day Cave Temple Geghard & Traditional Bort Manufacturing

Freshly strengthened, start the new day. Your path today will take you to the “Matenadaran”, which contains historical documents from Europe and Asia as well as from many centuries. Matenadaran means library in Old Armenian. Of inestimable value are the ancient manuscripts preserved here. The first copy of the Bible and the Gospels also fascinates with their miniatures. Afterwards you will drive around pagan temple Garni from the 1st century. The temple is a picturesque monument from the Hellenistic period, which bears witness to the ancient Armenian architecture. Lunch at a local home, you will have the opportunity to get acquainted with the secrets of the Armenian Lawash bread baking process and the “Tonir”, a traditional barbecue. A pleasure for all the senses! In the afternoon you will visit the mysterious Geghard cave monastery, which is one of the most important pilgrimage sites in Armenia. The founder is said to be Saint George, patron of the Armenian Apostolic Church. The monastery is also considered a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Finally, return to your hotel in Yerevan. How about a leisurely evening walk through the busy streets of the city? Overnight in Yerevan.

Day 5: Enchanting Sevan Lake & Medieval Haghpat

After breakfast, head north. Your first stop is the enchanting Lake Sevan. After all, this huge lake makes up five percent of Armenia’s land area, lies 2,000 meters above sea level and is the second highest freshwater lake in the world. On a peninsula there is a monastery, which you will visit afterwards. Then we continue to the architectural monastery complex of Haghpat, which is considered one of the most outstanding works of medieval Armenian architecture and is also a UNESCO World Heritage Site. On the way, you strengthen yourself with a picnic and can let the many impressions sink a bit. In the afternoon, your trip will take you to the Armenian-Georgian border, where your Georgian guide is already waiting for you. Your daily destination is the Georgian capital Tbilisi, where you will be taken to your hotel. At the end of the day, enjoy delicious delicacies in a traditional restaurant. Overnight in Tbilisi.

6th Day City Tour in the Emerging Capital Tbilisi

The exciting city of Tbilisi is waiting to be discovered by you. Together with your guide, you will go on a discovery tour. The old town offers you many sights that can be developed on a short walk. They start at the Metechi Church from the 13th century. Then continue to the equestrian statue of the city founder Wachtang Gorgassali. From here you can enjoy a beautiful view of the Mtkwari River, the old town and the surroundings of Tbilisi. By cable car you will then float up to Nariqala fortress from the 4th century. From there, the walk will take you down to the famous medieval sulfur baths, whose warm springs are closely interwoven with the city’s founding legend. On your way, you will encounter the religious diversity of Tbilisi: you will visit the synagogue and the historic Sioni Cathedral, where the legendary vine cross of Saint Nino is preserved. Also the Antschischati Church, the oldest church in the city from the 6th century, should not be missed. Finally, visit the Treasury of the Historical Museum where you can admire the goldsmith art from the time of the Golden Fleece. At the end of the day, you will have dinner at a Georgian restaurant. Overnight in Tbilisi.

7th Day Romantic Signagi and wine tasting in Kisichewi

Today you will explore the “City of Love”: Signagi is located in the heart of Kachetien and perched above the infinite Alasani plain. The narrow alleys and beautiful wooden balconies also contribute to the naming and create an unforgettable atmosphere. Pay particular attention to the ensemble of houses built in classical-South Italian style, combined with typical Georgian elements! Then visit the Monastery of Saints Nino in Bodbe. It was built on the orders of King Mirian III at the place where the Capadoc nun was buried in the 4th century – that saint to whom the Christianization of Georgia is attributed. At lunchtime, you will be with a local family and enjoy traditional Georgian specialties in an authentic atmosphere. Well strengthened, you will then continue to a farm where you can taste homemade organic wines and learn more about the centuries-old Georgian wine tradition. The tradition of viticulture has been cultivated for more than 7,000 years and originates in Georgia. Convince yourself of the skills of the winemakers during a wine tasting and learn all about the secrets of the different grape varieties. Overnight in Tbilisi.

8th day Old venerable Mzecheta & ride on the Georgian Army Road

After breakfast, your trip will take you to Mzcheta. The time-honored, former capital of Georgia and religious center of the country is definitely worth a visit. Visit the Dschwari Church (6th century) and the Swetiz-Choveli Cathedral (11th century). century. This masterpiece of Old Georgian architecture owes its name to the “life-giving tribe” to a legend about the body coat of Christ. A resident of Mzcheta brought the shirt of Christ to Mzcheta after the crucifixion. At the place where it was buried, a tree with a trunk sprouted up, which donated a healing balm. This is where the church was built. Afterwards, you will continue along the well-developed Georgian Army Road over the Cross Pass to Kasbegi. The route winds through a scenic area further and further up to the higher elevations of the Great Caucasus. Finally, you reach the small town of Kasbegi. Overnight in Stepantsminda.

Day 9 Hike to the Trinity Church

Lace up your hiking shoes, because an active day is ahead of you! An interesting walk will take you to the small Gergeti Trinity Church, perched at an altitude of 2,170 meters. The hike is exhausting, but it is worth it: the view into the valley, the view of the highest glacier of the Caucasus - the 5.047 m high Kasbeg (if it shows), the sight of the small chapel in front of the majestic mountains and the spiritual atmosphere in this sacred place make the visit an unforgettable experience. After a lunch break, you will be a guest in the afternoon with an artist who shows you the elaborate, traditional felt production and invites you to a leisurely cup of tea. The day will end at dinner. Overnight in Stepantsminda.

10th day Cave town Uplisziche & Land of the Golden Fleece

After breakfast, drive along the ancient Silk Road to the Kartli region and reach Gori, the birthplace of Stalin. There you will first visit his birthplace (from the outside) and then continue to the cave town Uplisziche, which was founded in the 1st century BC and led through a branch of the legendary Silk Road. Do you recognize the city structure with the many streets, the theater and the various palaces? In the afternoon you will reach the province of Imereti in West Georgia. Welcome to the land of the Golden Fleece! In Kutaissi, the third largest city in the country, you will take a first walking tour and get an impression of its rich history and lively atmosphere. In the evening, let the day end with a meal. Overnight in Kutaissi.

Day 11: Discover Kutaissi and its karst caves

A new exciting day lies ahead of you. After breakfast, visit the lively Kutaissi Farmers’ Market, where local merchants offer fresh fruit, vegetables, aromatic spices and numerous local specialties – an authentic insight into Georgian everyday life. Afterwards, visit the 11th century Bagrati Cathedral “Mary Slept” perched on a hill. The once magnificent church was severely damaged by the Ottomans in 1692 before extensive restoration work began from 1952. Today, the reconstructed facades in particular give an impression of the former splendor with mosaics, white marble and ornate details. Then continue to an impressive karst cave located near Kutaissi. Several caves are connected by underground rivers. The cave complex fascinates with its beautiful formations of stalagmites and stalactites, which are especially staged by means of lighting technology. In the afternoon, return to Kutaissi and use the remaining day for free. Overnight in Kutaissi.

12th day Visit to Rabat and Sapara Monastery

Today you will continue to the Mesheti region. In Borjomi, the well-known mineral water spa, take a walk in Borjomi Park and visit the well-known mineral water spring. The mineral water is a popular drink of Georgians and is offered everywhere. Then we move on to Achalziche. Here you will visit the Rabat Fortress, located directly on the Potskhovi River and perched above the city. The fortress was built in the 9th century and has been elaborately restored in recent years and today shines in new splendor. Afterwards, one of the most famous South Georgian monasteries awaits you – the Sapara Monastery from the 13th century. You will see that the monastery lives up to its name, which translates as “hidden”. Once upon a time, the monastery housed 12 churches, three of which are still preserved today. The visit to the monastery is also particularly worthwhile because of the beautiful views around it. Overnight in Achalziche.

13th day Cave town Wardsia and unique mountain landscapes

After breakfast, your trip will take you to Wardsia. The cave town of Wardsia consisted of 3,000 cave dwellings and offered shelter to over 50,000 people. It was built in the golden age of Georgia, in the 12th century. The artistically decorated rooms are connected by long corridors, which were knocked out of the rocks by human hands more than 800 years ago. At noon you will be invited to dinner with a family below the caves, where you can taste traditional Georgian specialties. Then drive back to Tbilisi via the Tsalka Pass. Enjoy the unique mountain landscapes of the small Caucasus on the way. The area is hardly populated, sometimes you can see small villages and shepherds with many herds of cattle and sheep. Particularly beautiful are the lakes nestled between the bare hills. In the evening you will reach Tbilisi where you will be taken to your hotel. Take advantage of your last evening to stop at one of the restaurants in the alleys of the illuminated old town and enjoy the delicious Georgian cuisine. Overnight in Tbilisi.

14th day Departure from Georgia

Your break from everyday life is coming to an end today. Even if your holiday is soon over, the precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories of your trip will accompany you forever. Look forward to letting your friends and family share your experiences in the distance at home. Your driver will take you to the airport for your return flight.

Iran & Oman Journey - From Silk Road to Frankincense Road

Iran and Oman Travel - Be inspired by Asia Special Tours to your individual Silk Road round trip. We look forward to hearing your request!

Itinerary & services

English itinerary text is an offline machine translation of the German original for this team preview. Please confirm travel details with your advisor; the German source remains available through the language switch.

Uncover the secrets of ancient trade routes and feel the magic of the Orient. Travel in Iran along the Silk Road, which has connected Europe and Asia for thousands of years. On the incense road in Oman, you will learn where the traders of the large caravans got the ancient gold - the incense. Experience the colorful activity on the bazaars, discover old oasis cities, magnificent buildings and fascinating cultures. Let yourself be enchanted by the hospitality of the locals and breathe in the intense smells of the Orient. You will explore spectacular deserts and impressive landscapes such as the island of Hormoz or Qeshm. A very special experience is the luxury hotel Anantara Al Jabal Al Akhdar Resort, on Oman’s “green mountain”. Enjoy the silence in this magical place and dream the fairy tale of 1001 nights!

1st day Arrival in Mashhad

Salam and welcome to the charming Iran. Upon arrival in Mashhad, you will already be expected by your driver guide and taken to your hotel. Mashhad is considered one of the seven holy sites of Shia Islam because it is home to the holy Gouharshad Mosque. The city is a political and religious center; annually they visit numerous Shiite pilgrims. The special religious significance of Mashhad for Iran stems from the fact that the (eighth) Imam Reza buried here is the only one of the twelve Shia imams whose tomb is located on Iranian soil. During a city tour, visit the Holy Shrine Imam Reza (from outside), a building complex containing the mausoleum of Imam ar-Ridā. This complex forms the center of tourism in Iran; it is visited annually by 15 to 20 million pilgrims. Afterwards we will visit the Goharshad Mosque, an important Persian mosque from the 15th century. The construction of the classical mosque with a four-Iwan courtyard took 12 years. Overnight in Mashhad.

2nd day excursion to the Robat-i Sharaf caravanserai

After breakfast, head to the Robat-i Sharaf caravanserai on the road between Nishapur (now Neyshabur, Iran) and Merv (now Mary, Turkmenistan). As one of the most important caravanserai on the Silk Road, Robat-i Sharaf dates back to the rule of Sultan Sanjar. Unlike the usual caravanserai used by ordinary people, Robat-i Sharaf was a palatial caravanserai also used by kings. It has two courts: the first for operating units and keeping animals, and the inner court for housing the king, his wives and other officials. Then return to Mashhad. Overnight in Mashhad.

Day 3 Visit to the Jami and Molana Abubakr Mausoleum

In the morning, head to Torbat-e-Jam and visit the Sheikh Ahmad-e-Jami Mausoleum. Here you will see a collection of religious buildings, mosques, houses and tombstones around the central tombstone of Sheikh Ahmad-e Jami, who lived in Torbat-e Jam between 1048 and 1141. The name of the city was changed to Torbat-e-Jam because of the Jami Mausoleum. Afterwards, visit the Mausoleum of Molana Abubakr in Taybad before returning to your hotel in Mashhad. Overnight in Mashhad.

Day 4 - Continue to Sabzevar via Neyshabur

Today you continue your journey to Sabzevar. Along the way, stop at Neyshabur where you will visit the Attar Mausoleum. Nishapur, now Neyshabur or Neyshabour, is a city in a high mountain range in Razavi-Chorasan province in Iran. Through them leads the Silk Road. Abu Hamed Abu Bakr Ebrahimi, better known by his stage name Farid ud-din and Attar of Neyshabur, was a great poet and Gnostic of Iran. Its mausoleum was tastefully decorated with colored tiles and carvings. The inside is covered with plaster. The tomb of the famous poet is located 6 km west of Neyshabur, near Imamzadeh Mahrooq and the tomb of Omar Khayyam, which you will visit afterwards. Then continue to Sabzevar to the Molla Hadi Sabzevari Mausoleum. Hadi Sabzevari or Haj Mulla Hadi Sabzevari (1797-1873) was a famous Iranian philosopher, mystic, theologian and poet. His tomb is a must-see sight in Sabzevar. Overnight in Sabzevar.

5th day Visit of Azmiyeghan and onward travel to Tabas

After breakfast, travel on to Tabas. On the way, visit the village of Azmiyeghan. Here you can learn how the village is cultivated in the shade of the palm trees. Then continue to your hotel in Tabas. The oasis town is best known for its ancient citrus plants and has a magnificent 300-year-old public garden, Baghe-golshan, with a beautiful fountain. Overnight in Tabas.

Day 6 Explore Tabas and its surroundings

Today you will explore the surroundings of Tabas together with your driver guide. You will visit the village of Morteza Ali and the gorge located there, as well as the large salt lake of Khur. Overnight in Tabas.

Day 7 - Go to Kerman

In the morning you will continue your journey to Kerman. On the way, stop to visit the village of Isfahak. Then you reach Kerman. Known for centuries in particular for its carpet production, Kerman looks back on an eventful history. Already in the writings of ancient Greece, the place is mentioned under the name Karamani. Even the world traveler Marco Polo fascinated the city very much. In his travel descriptions he describes the quality and beauty of the carpets from Kerman. The city then became the center of carpet production. Near the city are the important ruins of the city of Jiroft. Overnight in Kerman.

Day 8: The Wind Bumps of Shahdad

Your journey will take you to the village of Shahdad on the edge of the Lut Desert. Nearby is the hill Gamdom Beryan, which is considered the hottest place in the world with a temperature of up to 67 degrees Celsius in the shade. About half an hour's drive from Shahdad, you will visit some of the fascinating Jardangs, Kalouts or wind bumps - a geomorphological, table-shaped form of erosion in loose sediments created by wind and water erosions over time. From a distance, they resemble buildings. The area also invites for small hikes. Also, do not miss the cypress of Sirch, which with an estimated age of 4,000 to 4,500 years is considered one of the oldest living things on earth. According to a legend of the Zoroastrians, the tree of Zarathustra was planted. Therefore, it is considered a pilgrimage site for Zoroastrians. Overnight in Shahdad.

Day 9 Safari in the Lut Desert

One of the highlights of your journey awaits today: a safari in the Lut Desert. Covering 166,000 km², Lut is Iran's largest desert. To the southwest, the Zagros Mountains contribute to the arid conditions; to the north lies the country's other great desert, the Kavir. Annual rainfall is less than 50 mm. With summer surface temperatures reaching 70.7 °C, it is one of the hottest places on Earth. Erosion has shaped the southwest, while deposits in the southeast have created dunes up to 420 m high. Unlike the Sahara, the original itinerary reports no discoveries showing earlier human settlement, fossils, or other evidence of former life here. These hostile conditions leave Lut uninhabited today. Nomads spend the summer in the mountains south of Lut and move toward the Persian Gulf in winter. Overnight in Shahdad.

Day 10: Mahan continues on Qeshm Island

On your trip to Bam, stop in Mahan and visit the Shah Nematollah Vali Mausoleum and Bagh-e Shazde Garden (Prince Garden), considered the most beautiful garden in the country. After arriving in Bam, you will visit the Citadel, the world’s largest construction of raw bricks and clay, before being taken to your hotel. Overnight in Bam.

11th day Port of Bandar Abbas

In the morning, visit the Bronze Age excavation site Konar Sandale before heading to the next stop of your journey: Bandar Abbas, one of Iran’s most important port cities, offering special natural and historical attractions. In addition to the historic port, there is an old spa that has been used for medical purposes. It is said that Marco Polo used this healing water for the healing of his skin diseases. Overnight in Bandar Abbas.

12th day boat trip to Hormoz Island

After breakfast, take a boat trip through the Strait of Hormuz. Its name is the rock island of Hormoz, which lies in the northern part of the strait. It lies between Iran and the Omani exclave of Musandam. Since ancient times, the Strait of Hormuz has been an important shipping route. All shipping traffic to and from the oil ports of Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain, Iraq, the United Arab Emirates and Iran passes through them, plus most of the Saudi Arabian traffic. Since a road closure would block the supply of significant parts of the Middle East oil areas, it is of global strategic importance (about a quarter of the global oil supply). On the island of Hormoz, visit the museum of Ahmad Nadalian, who is internationally known for his environmental art projects. His works refer to a variety of symbols from ancient rituals and mythologies with new interpretations on contemporary themes. Particularly worth seeing here are the multicolored rocky landscapes with the red iron oxide and salt deposits or the ruins of the former Portuguese fortress on the northern tip. Overnight on Hormoz Island.

13th day Forests, caves and coasts on the island of Qeshm

Also today you take a boat tour, this time to the island of Qeshm. The island of Qeshm is the largest island in the Persian Gulf and is especially worth seeing due to its natural beauty. The main town is the port city of Qeshm, which belongs to the Qeshm Free Trade Zone. In ancient times, the island was known as Alexandria. Due to their strategically important location, many empires later fought for control of Qeshm, such as the empire of Elam, the Umayyads, Abbasids, British and Portuguese. The western part of the island is largely part of the Qeshm Geopark, the only one in Iran. Today, your guide will show you the most beautiful sides of the island and take you to the Hara mangrove forest and the Valley of the Stars. Overnight on Qeshm Island.

14th day sightseeing tour on Qeshm Island

Continue your sightseeing tour on the island of Qeshm and see, among other things, the valley of Chahkouh and the port of Laft, a port village with traditional houses. The shore and calm water are reminiscent of the Caribbean Islands. You will also visit the small island of Naz, which disappears into the sea at high tide. Overnight on Qeshm Island.

15th day Onward journey to Shiraz - City of Roses and Nightingales

Today you will fly from Qeshm Island to Shiraz. Arriving in Shiraz, you will be taken to your hotel. At the end of the day, stroll over the colorful bazaar, absorb the foreign aromas and try your luck haggling. You will also visit the tomb of Hafis. Overnight in Shiraz.

16th day Travel to Dubai and drive to Dibba Musandam

In the morning you will be taken to Shiraz Airport, from where you will fly on to Dubai. Once there, you will be expected and taken to your hotel, Six Senses Zighy Bay. After the many new impressions from Iran, you will spend a few relaxing days here before continuing your journey through Oman. Overnight in Dibba Musandam.

17-18. Day of free time

Without driverguide

These days are all about rest and relaxation. Take advantage of the amenities of your hotel, relax by the pool or take a trip to the area. Your resort, Six Senses Zighy Bay, offers you a variety of options (partly for a fee). Visit the Grand Canyon of Musandam or explore the area on a mountain bike tour. Also hikes, fishing or a snorkeling are possible. Overnight in Dibba Musandam.

19th day exploration in Muscat

In the morning you will be taken to Dubai Airport, from where you will fly on to Muscat. Upon arrival at the airport, you will first be taken to your hotel and in the afternoon you will begin your city tour of Muscat. First, a photo stop will take you to the Great Mosque before visiting Omani Market. Your guide will be happy to help you with your actions. A visit to the Bait Al Zubair Museum will give you an insight into Oman’s former way of life. In addition to traditional crafts, the private collection of the old Omani trading family Zubair is on display. This collection includes historical photographs, documents, jewelry and everyday objects. At the port is the Centre for the Administration of the Omani State. In addition to the palace authorities and government buildings, the palace of Sultan Qaboos can also be found here. You will visit the building located at the front of the bay. If you let your view wander over the area, the two fortresses Djalali and Mirani immediately catch your eye. They were created by the Portuguese at the end of the 16th century for protection against Turkish invasions. Overnight in Muscat.

20th day snorkeling excursion in the Daymaniat Island Reserve

Today you will find an unforgettable highlight of your trip, as you will go on a snorkeling excursion in the Daymaniat Island Reserve. Here you have the opportunity to see a variety of fish and, with a bit of luck, turtles. Overnight in Muscat.

Day 21 - Mountains around Jabel Akhdar

A unique photo motif is the large mosque in Muskat. It was built between 1995 and 2001 by Sultan Qabus and since then bears his name. Due to its size of about four hectares, the mosque is one of the largest in the world and at the same time one of the most important buildings of Oman. Then continue your journey to Nizwa. Above the rooftops of Nizwa, the largest fortress of the Arabian peninsula was built in 1668. It was once used as the headquarters of Iman. Today, from up there, you have a wonderful view of the buildings and the bustling market. Your further visit will take you past the round towers of the fortress up to the "own" museum. You will also visit the goat market of Nizwa. Afterwards, on your way through the desert, you will pass the old oasis town of Birkat al Mauz. Here are still old ruins of a former settlement. Continue the journey to Jabel Akhdar, called “green mountains” in Arabic. Overnight in Jabal Akhdar.

22nd day visits to Tanuf and Misfah

Today you take a trip to the surrounding area. You will first visit the village of Tanuf, which was destroyed in the war of Al Jabal in 1953. Learn more about the history of Oman here. In addition to Jabreen Castle, you will also visit Fort Bahla, which cannot be visited but is worth a stop. In the small town of Misfah, unlike most Omani cities, you will still find many of the old houses made of reddish clay. Walk through the alleys and take advantage of the chance to watch the women grind flour, bake bread in the traditional way, extract oil from the cores of the Shua tree, roast coffee or make natural remedies from sandalwood. Also known are the beautiful field terraces that surround the mountain together with the old buildings. Only a few kilometers away is Misfat. Here you will still find traditional houses and alleys and multi-storey, clay-built houses. Overnight in Jabal Akhdar.

23rd day of free time

Without driverguide

This day is free for you. Relax in your hotel and enjoy the amenities of your hotel. In addition to yoga lessons and cinema under the stars, Anantara Al Jabal Al Akhdar also offers drawing and photography classes, hikes, village visits and picnics. Soak up the many impressions of the last few days and enjoy the sweet nothingness. Overnight in Jabal Akhdar.

Day 24 The Sand Dunes of Wahiba Sands

Today we continue on to the desert to Wahiba Sands. Here you will experience an adventurous ride through white and red sand dunes that rise up to 200 meters. In the desert there are many animals and plants. Here also live the Bedouin and Nomads, who will surely meet you again and again with their camels. Then you experience the direct contrast to your desert experience. Jump into deep blue and clear waters and cool down in the middle of the desert. Oman is known for its numerous wadis, which are natural pools that can be found in the desert. The Wadi Bani Khalid lies within the Wahiba Desert. In addition to the large natural pool, you will find numerous smaller and colder pools just a few minutes away. At the end of the day, you will be taken to Ras Al Jinz. The place is known for the Green Sea Turtle, which lays its eggs here. At night, take a guided tour to the beach where, with luck, you can witness this intimate moment. Overnight in Ras Al Jinz.

25th day return to Muscat and flight to Salalah

After breakfast, continue your journey to Sur. Once there, drive directly to the fishing port to watch the fishermen at work. Here you will visit a factory where the traditional dhows, handmade wooden boats, are made. Then your journey will slowly take you back to Muscat. On the way, visit Tiwi, a small charming fishing village. A small fort marks the entrance to this small wadi. Stroll through banana plantations and date palms along a small path. During your ride you will see the Bimah Sinkhole, which was created by the collapse of a mountain and today is filled with crystal clear turquoise water. Your final stage of today will take you past the town of Quriyat, which is protected by its 18th-century fortress. In the evening you will be taken to the airport and fly on to Salalah. Overnight in Salalah.

26th day on the road in East and West Salalah

Your day begins with a visit to the ancient fishing village of Taqah, famous for its dried sardines and traditional old Dhorari houses. There you will visit the old castle, the former residence of the former Welsh of the region. Then visit the ruins of the once famous city of Sumharam (Khor Rohri). The city was once an important port on the coast of Dhofar and one of the most important trading towns along the incense road. After that, you will see the evergreen Wadi Darbat where you can take a walk. To the west of Salalah, visit the Qara Mountains and the tomb of the Prophet Job. A detour will take you to the vast beach of Mughsail. Here, the waves blow the water through natural holes. At the end of the rocky beach there is a cave-like entrance. On your way, incense trees line the streets. Take a short walk around the area before returning to your hotel. Overnight in Salalah.

Day 27 A visit to the "empty quarter"

Today's excursion will take you to the so-called "empty quarter", Rub Al Khali, Dhofar's most important site. You start with an all-wheel drive vehicle into the untouched dunes. On your way through the Qara Mountains you will find spectacular views. In Thumrait, a former Bedouin settlement, take a short break before continuing your journey through the Al Nejd debris desert. You will visit the Lost City - Ubar, which counts as a UNESCO World Heritage Site. The city is now considered the "Atlantis of the Desert" because it is literally sunk in the sand. Explore one of the largest sand deserts in the world. On your way back to Salalah, stop at Wadi Dawka, also a UNESCO World Heritage Site, also known as Wadi of the Weincense Road. It is also known for hosting the largest number of incense trees in the region. Overnight in Salalah.

28th Day City Tour in Salalah

After breakfast, take a half-day city tour in Salalah. First, you will visit the Museum of the Incense Land. Here you will find out everything you need to know about the Weauchstraße. The museum was opened in 2007 and is located in the Archaeological Park Al Balid, another UNSECO World Heritage site in Salalah. Then stop for a photo stop at Sultan Qaboos Mosque and see the palace of His Majesty the Sultan. Finally, visit the bazaar of the city and stroll through the alleys or get last souvenirs. Overnight in Salalah.

29Tab Departure from Oman

Your time out from everyday life is coming to an end. Even if your holiday is soon over, the precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories of your trip will accompany you forever. Look forward to letting your friends and family share your experiences in the distance at home. You will be taken to the airport from where you will begin your journey home.

Nepal Round Trip - In the Heart of the Himalayas

Nepal Round Trip - In the heart of the Himalayas, this travel idea will take you away from your specialist Asia Special Tours. Make an appointment and give advice.

On this trip you will explore Nepal in all its facets: from the hustle and bustle of the big city Kathmandu up to the lonely heights of the Himalayas. You will discover places of deep religious significance and walk over colorful markets, you will tack on the heels of wild animals and go in ancient palaces looking for traces. On the way, you always have the opportunity to get in touch with the local population and get an insight into their lives. Treat yourself to two weeks in which Nepal’s fascination will also captivate you.

1st day Arrival in Nepal

Namaste and welcome to Nepal! After landing at Kathmandu airport, you will be picked up by your driver and taken to your hotel. Upon arrival, the rest of the day is freely available to you. Depending on the time of day, you can use the time to look around on your own or to recover from your arrival. Overnight in Kathmandu.

2nd day Temples and palaces in Kathmandu

Start the day with a visit to Kathmandu’s Old Town, which undoubtedly centers on Durbar Square. Where the trade routes once crossed, today you will find over 50 temples and monuments. Especially stand out the temple of the goddess Kumari and the royal palace. From here you will later take a beautiful rickshaw ride through narrow alleys to Thamel, during which you can observe the local life up close. Use some free time there to look around on your own while strolling through the busy district. Then reunite with your guide and visit the Swayambhunath Temple, one of the oldest Buddhist temples in the world. In the central Patan Durbar square you will find a collection of finely crafted pagoda temples and stone statues. The old palace of the Malla kings and the ancient stone baths are each associated with legends and episodes of history. Overnight in Kathmandu

3rd day Hinduism meets Buddhism

They haven’t seen everything Kathmandu has to offer. Together with your guide, you will first head to one of the most sacred Hindu pilgrimage sites: Pashupatinath. You can only view the complex from the eastern bank of the Bagmati, since all non-Hindus are strictly forbidden to enter the temple. Behind the temple, you will have the opportunity to see the place where the cremation takes place and get in touch with sadhus – Hindu wandering monks. Then visit the 36-meter-high Boudhanath Stupa, one of the most important Buddhist pilgrimage sites in the country and one of the largest Buddhism sanctuaries worldwide. Buddhist believers have been making pilgrimages here for centuries to circle the building at dawn and dusk. The stupa rests on an octagonal pedestal with embedded prayer wheels, from the top four pairs of Buddha’s eyes watched the visitors. In one of the nearby monasteries you can get in touch with one of the lamas and receive his blessing. The rest of the day you can arrange according to your ideas and explore Kathmandu independently. Overnight in Kathmandu.

Day 4: The Jungle is Calling

After breakfast, you will drive to Chitwan – one of the largest jungle and forest areas in Nepal. The Royal Chitwan National Park is considered the best organized in the country and covers an area of 932 square kilometers. Chitwan used to be a private hunting ground of the Maharajas of Nepal. The park with its swamps and salt forests, which are crossed by rivers and watercourses, has been a UNESCO World Heritage site since 1984. It is home to unique ecosystems and is home to more than 50 species of mammals, 525 bird and about 50 amphibious species. Among others, deer, monkeys, wild elephants, leopards, lip bears and crocodiles live here. The park is particularly well known for its population of Indian rhinos and with a lot of luck you might even discover one of the rare Bengal tigers. After lunch at the resort, spend the rest of the day with various jungle activities arranged by your lodge. Back at the resort, experience a cultural performance of the Tharu community. Let yourself be enchanted by the dances and songs and for the moment be taken completely into the world of the tribe. Overnight in Chitwan.

Day 5 Varied Day in Chitwan National Park

Nature is calling! Immerse yourself in the fascination of the wilderness and its inhabitants. You can explore the park with guided jeep safaris, walks, walks or canoeing from very different perspectives. You can also visit the local Tharu villages and watch the elephants feeding and bathing up close. Back at the resort, experience a cultural performance of the Tharu community. Let yourself be enchanted by the dances and songs and for the moment be taken completely into the world of the tribe. Overnight in Chitwan.

Day 6 Lumbini - Birthplace of the Historical Buddha

Today it becomes spiritual: After breakfast, travel to Lumbini to the birthplace of Gautama Buddha, who saw the light of day here in 623 BC. Lumbini, in southwestern Nepal, conjures up a sacred atmosphere – a place that millions of Buddhists around the world venerate as much as Christians worship Jerusalem and Muslims worship Mecca. After arriving in Lumbini, you can still use the daylight for photography and immediately visit the sacred garden, a small complex located at the southern end of Lumbini. All the most important historical monuments and archaeological finds are kept there. Among other things, you will discover the Mayadevi Temple: Maya Devi gave birth to the child Buddha on the way to her parents' home in Devadaha. You will also visit the Ashoka Pillar and the famous sacred pond Puskarni. The birthplace of Lord Buddha can also be visited. Your sightseeing tour is conveniently done by E-rickshaw. Overnight in Lumbini.

Day 7 - Painting Pokhara

After breakfast, you will travel to Pokhara today. Along the way you will get to see the typical landscapes of Nepal and the typical small villages. Pokhara is one of the most visited places in Nepal after Kathmandu. It is known for its wonderful lakes Pewa, Begnas and Rupa, whose springs originate in the Annapurna glacier mountains in the Himalayas. It also offers you magnificent views of the summit of Machhapuchhre. After arriving in Pokhara, the rest of the day will be at your disposal. Overnight in Pokhara

8th day Majestic summit and a picturesque lake

Getting up early is worth it today! Early in the morning, head to Sarangkot, where you can experience a breathtaking sunrise over the Himalayas. With clear visibility, the snow-capped peaks and slopes of the mountains shine in the gentle, golden light of the morning sun. Then enjoy a scenic gondola ride on the Annapurna cable car from Sarangkot to Sedi. After a delicious breakfast at your hotel, your sightseeing tour of the valley begins. Your first destination is the Pumdikot Shiva statue, from where you can enjoy a spectacular view of the Annapurna mountain range. Continue to the World Peace Pagoda on the south shore of Phewa Lake, which also offers impressive panoramas. The day will end relaxing on a boat trip across the lake, with a short stop on the island to visit Barahi Temple. Overnight in Pokhara.

9th day Bergdorf Bandipur

The next stop of your trip is the mountain village of Bandipur. The settlement is located on a narrow saddle between two mountain peaks, which is just wide enough for a main road framed by residential houses. The gardens of the houses already lead steeply down the hill. After your arrival, you will have time to explore the place and its surroundings. Visit the Bindyabasini Temple, dedicated to the city’s protective deity – Durga. Maybe you recognize something, because the wood carvings and the metalwork copy the decorations on old pagodas from the Kathmandu Valley. During the tour you will also have the opportunity to discover vibrant bazaars, visit traditional houses and gain a fascinating insight into the daily life of the villagers. Overnight in Bandipur.

Day 10 - Go to Dhulikhel

Today it goes high: you drive to Dhulikhel, which is located at an altitude of just under 1,550 meters. Formerly an important part of the ancient trade route to Tibet, Dhulikhel enjoyed a glorious past. In the beautiful buildings and intricate wood carvings in the commercial streets as well as in the wonderful temples, history is still visible today. The Newari settlement is also known for the fascinating views of the Himalayas, beautiful picnic areas and temples of various deities. In and around the old town you can find both Hindu temples and Buddhist stupas. After checking in at the hotel, the rest of the day will be at your disposal for your own explorations of this small, historic city. Overnight in Dhulikhel.

11th Day Temples, Markets and Monasteries

After breakfast, take a short tour of the city where you will discover the most important sights such as the Narayan and Harisiddhi Temple, decorated with ornate carvings. Your guide will then take you to the old bazaar where you will experience the colorful market activity and show you the Bhagwati Temple, which is located on a hill in the western part of Dhulikhel. From here you can enjoy a magnificent view of the city and the surrounding countryside. After that, take a detour to Namobuddha Monastery, a place of tranquility and spirituality that enchants visitors with its history and impressive views. In the afternoon, continue your journey to Nagarkot. Overnight in Nagarkot.

Day 12 Sunrise and Changu Narayan Temple

If you get up this morning, you will be rewarded with a breathtaking sunrise over the mountains. Nagarkot is one of the most picturesque places in the Bhaktapur area and known for its spectacular views of the Himalayas when the weather permits. After breakfast, take an easy walk to Changu Narayan Temple, an outstanding example of Nepalese pagoda architecture. The temple, considered the oldest of its kind in the valley (from the 4th century), is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and is dedicated to the god Vishnu in his incarnation as Narayan. Particularly impressive is the statue with ten heads and ten arms, a masterpiece of stonemasonry from the 5th century. Then continue your journey to Bhaktapur. After check-in at the hotel, the afternoon is free to explore the city on your own. In the evening, make your way to a local Newari family where you are invited to dinner. Watch as Momos – very popular meat or vegetable filling pasta bags in Nepal – are prepared. Then let the eventful day end with a meal together. Overnight in Bhaktapur.

13th day Bhaktapur and potter workshop

Bhaktapur “The City of Admirers” is the best preserved medieval city in the Kathmandu Valley and impresses with its religious architecture. The narrow streets, stone streets and red brick houses lead to temples and courtyards, while daily life is characterized by traditional crafts such as pottery, weaving and woodworking. The main attractions include the 55-window palace, the Golden Gate, Nyatapola Temple and Mini Pashupati Temple. Visit the Pottery Square where you can observe the traditional pottery process and get creative yourself. Afterwards, your trip will take you back to the capital of the country. After arriving at the hotel, the afternoon is freely available. Use the time for further exploration on your own, browse the markets and small shops for souvenirs for your loved ones at home or relax in your beautiful hotel. Overnight in Kathmandu.

Day 14 Farewell to Nepal

Even the most beautiful journey comes to an end. During the last few days, you have gathered many unique impressions of the diversity of Nepalese culture, the impressive mountains and the diverse flora and fauna of Chitwan National Park. The suitcases packed with indescribable experiences and lasting memories will take you to the airport to start your journey home.

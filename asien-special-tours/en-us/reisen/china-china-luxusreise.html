Do you dream of the Far East and do not want to do without anything? This unique luxury journey takes you to another world and shows you the most beautiful spots of China. Unforgettable experiences, unique moments and exciting adventures meet you on this luxurious round trip at the highest level: an atmospheric sparkling wine picnic on the Great Wall, a spectacular helicopter flight over the karst landscape of Yangshuo or an exclusive cruise in front of the glittering evening backdrop of Shanghai. You will meet art and culture experts who let you look deep behind the scenes through their insider knowledge and accompany you on hidden paths far away from the tourist flows. In between, there is enough room for a flexible time management and your very private experiences. Stay in the best hotels in China: every hotel on this route is unique and will delight you with exceptional service and extravagant exclusivity.

1st day Arrival in Beijing & first class The Peninsula Beijing

Your dream journey begins in Beijing. You will already be expected at the airport by your private guide and chauffeured to the hotel. The Peninsula Beijing is located in the heart of Beijing and is the absolute top address in China’s capital. From here it is only a few minutes to historical sights such as the Forbidden City, Tiananmenplatz and the National Theatre. Behind the impressive facade in Chinese style, the luxury hotel combines Oriental and Western elements and offers its guests the best of both worlds. With its elegant style, first-class service and state-of-the-art technology, The Peninsula Beijing sets the highest standards of design, luxury and comfort. The culinary offer also makes the heart of every gourmet beat faster. The restaurant “Jing” serves contemporary French cuisine and is one of the best restaurants in the city. You can also enjoy excellent Cantonese cuisine, international and Asian specialties as well as delicious drinks and fine wines. Arrive in peace and relax a little from the journey. How about a massage in the excellent spa area of the hotel (spa application not included in the travel price) or relax with a cup of tea in the tea lounge? Overnight in Beijing.

2nd day sparkling wine picnic on the Great Wall & booze tasting

After an extensive breakfast, the first big highlight of your trip is waiting for you! In the morning, head to the Great Wall at Mutianyu section. The foreigner enjoys the excursion here like a journey through time: from the modern capital beyond the outskirts to the rough mountainous area, where an army of workers once walled an empire. On the wall, together with your guide, you will take a hike and enjoy the unique atmosphere during a romantic sparkling wine picnic. Take it upon yourself and taste the unforgettable moment. Then you leave and drive back to Beijing. On the way back you will visit the traditional distillery Niu Lan Shan, where the famous brandy "Er Guo Tou" is distilled. Convince yourself of the quality of this fine wine. . In the evening you can pamper your palate at the Cantonese restaurant "Huang Ting" or taste the famous Beijing duck in the "Country Kitchen" of the Rosewood Hotel. Overnight in Beijing.

3rd Day Private Tai Chi Course & the Forbidden City with an Expert

Many Beijingers get up very early and come to the parks to do sports. Especially Tai Chi is very popular. Your personal tai chi master awaits you at the Coal Hill Park and guides you through a series of slow, graceful movements and exercises. Enjoy the relaxing movements in this unique environment and create new energies for the day. After a refreshing breakfast at the hotel, start your sightseeing tour today. Stroll with an expert over Tiananmen Square – 500 meters wide and 880 meters long, it is the largest square in the world. Enjoy the expert’s technical explanations in the Forbidden City with the impressive Imperial Palace. This magical place was the seat of the Chinese emperors of two dynasties – the Ming and the Qing – and for half a millennium was inaccessible to those who did not belong to the court. The crowd is as remarkable as the architecture and pagoda-like roofs. Let the many impressions sink first and let the rest of the day end according to your ideas. How about a cocktail at the rooftop bar or go eat Beijing duck in one of the best restaurants in town? Overnight in Beijing.

4th day incense workshop & stroll through the Hutongs

After breakfast, an incense workshop awaits you, where an expert will take you into the world of China’s fragrances and traditions. Under the title “The Four Famous Incenses as a Miniature of Chinese civilization”, you will learn about the cultural significance and history of these special fragrances, which are deeply rooted in Chinese culture. In the afternoon, you’ll still have time for a relaxing stroll through the Hutongs – the traditional narrow streets of Beijing that offer an authentic insight into old city life. During your stroll through these historic districts, you will also visit an antique market where you will have the opportunity to discover artwork, ancient treasures and curiosities that reflect China’s rich history and culture. Overnight in Beijing.

Day 5 - Express train to Xi'an & stroll through the old town

Today you will leave Beijing and travel on to Xi'an by express train. The former capital of eleven dynasties was once the economic and political center of the Middle Kingdom. Today it is the seat of government of Shanxi Province. You are already expected at the train station and accompanied to your luxury hotel. The Sofitel Legend Peoples Grand Hotel in the heart of Xi’an welcomes you. Opened in 1953 as a state guest house, the luxury hotel uniquely conveys the atmosphere of the former capital. The beautiful garden area invites you to linger and is the perfect backdrop for daily tai chi classes. A butler takes care of your well-being around the clock. After a short rest break, stroll through the old town and admire the Great Mosque. During your walk, a view of the bell and drum tower should not be missing. The 36-meter-high bell tower in the center is the landmark of the city. The drum tower of 1370, which has already been restored several times, used to announce the closing of the city gates through its drumming. Of course, you can also see the city wall - the best preserved in all of China. For dinner, you are invited to the Nobel restaurant “Shi San Chao” and are spoiled with regional delicacies. Overnight in Xi’an.

6th Day Fascination Terracotta Army & Leisure in your luxury hotel

A highlight of every trip to China: the Terracotta Army. It is considered one of the largest archaeological finds of the 20th century. It was discovered by chance by farmers in 1974 and has been on the World Heritage List since 1987. The life-size figures were found near the mausoleum of ruler Qin Shihuangdi, an early Chinese tomb dating back to 210 BC. So far, archaeologists have unearthed around 7,200 figures of soldiers on foot and horseback. It is remarkable that all (!) these earthen warriors are individually designed. You will also visit the Great Wild Goose Pagoda, considered one of the oldest pagodas in China and located in the middle of the Temple of Good Will in the southern suburbs. The Great Wild Goose Pagoda is cone-shaped and with its seven floors a striking landmark of Xi'an. The rest of the day is yours. Do you want to discover even more or prefer to relax in your luxury hotel? The Bar Louis XIII with Remy Martin makes rare Louis XIII Fortress cognac and other specialties. The spa of the hotel includes three treatment rooms, as well as a room for traditional Chinese foot massages and takes care of your physical and mental well-being (spa treatments not included in the travel price). Overnight in Xi’an.

Day 7 With the express train to Chengdu & tea ceremony in the tea house

After breakfast, take the fast train to Chengdu. The capital of Sichuan province is a modern city, but its origins go back far into the past. The fascinating past of the city is clearly noticeable in the numerous, well-preserved temples to this day. And yet the city seems young and alive. Especially pretty gardens and tea houses shape the face of the city, making Chengdu one of China’s most popular cities. Nowhere are there so many tea shops as in Chengdu. In a tea house, an authentic tea ceremony awaits you. Look forward to your exclusive hotel for the next few days. Upper House Chengdu is a particularly beautiful luxury hotel with unrivalled charm. Go on a unique journey through time and let yourself be enchanted. In the evening, enjoy a private show of traditional folk arts from Sichuan. These include mask changes, fire spits and handshade play. Exchange views with the artists and learn more about the tradition of the area. Overnight in Chengdu.

8th Day Experience Sichuan with All Senses

Today, your guide will take you on an exciting journey through the history of Sichuan’s cuisine. Visit the Sichuan Cuisine Museum, the world’s first museum dedicated entirely to traditional cuisine. Here you will not only learn a lot of interesting things about the traditional cuisine of the province, you can also smell, taste, feel and even make it yourself. On the approximately 2.7 hectare exhibition area, the main hall shows more than 2,500 years old cooking utensils excavated in Sichuan and many books, notes and pictures about Sichuan cuisine. Afterwards, visit the market with a chef and shop for your dinner today. Arriving at the restaurant, prepare your own food, which you will then melt on your tongue. After many culinary impressions, return to your hotel. Overnight in Chengdu.

Day 9 Yangshuo: The perfect symbiosis of landscape and experience

A short domestic flight will take you to the next region to Guilin, where you are already expected. Together with your guide and chauffeur, you will continue to Yangshuo, where you will be taken to your luxury hotel. Located in the midst of the jade green mountains, Banyan Tree Yangshuo is a place of complete peace and harmony. Experience the natural beauty of Yangshuo with its iconic karst cone mountains, rice terraces and picturesque Li River. The excellent facilities, excellent restaurants and the breathtaking surroundings guarantee a feel-good experience from which you will live for a long time. With a sunset cocktail, let the peaceful atmosphere take on itself while the sun sinks behind the imposing karst mountains. In the evening you will enjoy a breathtaking performance. At the impression show Liu Sanjie, you will captivate over 800 actors simultaneously with a fantastic combination of light, singing, acrobatics and trick technique. Nevertheless: The secret star of this unique spectacle is the Li River surrounded by Karstbergen. Overnight in Yangshuo.

10th day Spectacular helicopter flight over Yangshuo

Slowly you realize why everyone is so enthusiastic about this area: green rice fields and a panorama with staggered mountain rows that stand like a sine curve behind the other. Loud domes, as far as one can see, each one wrapped in a delicate cloud veil. It is hard to find the right words for this scenery. Best of all: just be amazed! On an adventurous helicopter flight (duration 25 minutes) you will explore this beautiful region of karst landscapes from above. Enjoy the sight of the unique rock formations and lush green rice fields that stretch beneath you and let the magic of this landscape take a deep look. The rest of the day is at your disposal to continue exploring the area at your own pace. In the evening, let the magnificent cuisine of the Banyan Tree Hotel accompany you through the evening. Overnight in Yangshuo.

11th day flight to Shanghai & exclusive yacht cruise on Huangpu

Today we return to Guilin and fly to the mega-metropolis of Shanghai. Your guide will take you to your luxury hotel Capella Shanghai Jian Ye Li. The chic boutique hotel pays homage to the city’s cultural heritage and celebrates the special architectural style of the golden era of the 1920s to 30s. At the heart of the hotel are the 55 Shikumen villas with one, two and three bedrooms, which combine the historic courtyard in Shanghai style with the Parisian design flair. The contemporary elegance of the villas’ interior contrasts with the accents of Chinoiserie, while creating a magnificent symbiosis of tradition and modern luxury. A personal assistant reads every wish from your eyes. In the fine dining restaurant, you can enjoy creative, French star cuisine, while the boulangerie pampers your nose and taste buds with delightfully scented bread and pastries. The highlight of today is a two-hour yacht cruise on Huangpu, where you can enjoy a unique atmosphere overlooking the glittering skyline. For this purpose, cool drinks and snacks are served. Overnight in Shanghai.

12th Day Art Tour of Shanghai

Without tour guide and chauffeur

Enjoy an exciting half-day art tour of Shanghai’s prominent art galleries today. The rest of the day is at your disposal. Do you want some relaxation and relaxation away from the bustle of the city? Your hotel’s award-winning Auriga Spa, named after the constellation of the carrier, whose main star is Capella, is a health and wellness temple of the extra class that celebrates caring for the body with a diverse range of uses and treatments (spa applications not included in the travel price). Plenty of variety is provided by a colorful range of activities and experiences such as cooking, cocktail mix and painting classes, yoga lessons or rum tastings. Surely you have enough ideas for the free afternoon. Overnight in Shanghai.

Day 13 Full of Contrasts: Shanghai from Above & Walk through the Old Town

Shanghai still has so much to offer and is waiting to be discovered by you. Shanghai's landmark is the Oriental Pearl Tower, the television tower measures a proud 468 meters, the Shanghai Tower even 632 meters. Completed in 2015, the tower is the second tallest building in the world after the Burj Khalifa – with the observation deck for visitors even higher than that of the Burj Khalifa. Go upstairs and let the view wander over the city from the observation platform. But Shanghai has more to offer than skyscrapers, business districts and crowded tourist attractions. There is still an old and original Shanghai, just as it was before the big dawn in the 90s. Stroll through the alleys together with your guide, try a few of the treats offered at the stands and let yourself be carried away by the pure joy of life of the people. Afterwards, let the evening go out in the top restaurant of the hotel “Le Comptoir de Pierre Gagnaire” and revisit the dream trip in peace. Overnight in Shanghai.

Day 14: Farewell to China

Today, unfortunately, it means saying goodbye to a fascinating country from which you will take numerous lasting impressions with you. You will be taken to the airport from where you will start your onward or return journey.

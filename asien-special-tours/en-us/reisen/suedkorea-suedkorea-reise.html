South Korea Travel - Maritime South Korea

South Korea Travel - Enjoy the maritime South Korea on this travel idea of Asia Special Tours. Ask now and get individual advice.

Itinerary & services

English itinerary text is an offline machine translation of the German original for this team preview. Please confirm travel details with your advisor; the German source remains available through the language switch.

On this trip you will get to know in particular the beautiful cities and villages on the coasts of South Korea, which will enchant you with their diversity. Experience the unique landscape of Watt and Röhricht in the bay of Suncheon, take beautiful walks along the sea shore and explore the coast of Yeosus by rail bike. During a trip with the longest cable car in South Korea, you will get a magnificent view of the small island groups off the Korean coast. Further highlights await you in the maritime botanical garden Oedo Botania with its lush plant variety and on the impressive cliffs of Taejongdae. While cruising, experience Busan from a new perspective before visiting the unusual underwater tomb of King Munmu. The legendary cliff temple Haedong Yonggungsa is also one of the special stops on your journey. Your impressions will be rounded off by visiting traditional markets, especially the Jagalchi fish market with its diverse range of freshly caught specialties from the sea. Your trip ends in style at the Korea House in Seoul during a special dinner with Korean haute cuisine.

1st day start of your journey

Welcome to South Korea! Your journey through the Tiger State begins in the capital Seoul, where you will be welcomed and warmly greeted by your English-speaking driver. Use the rest of the day for first explorations in the Asian metropolis. Seoul has a long historical tradition as the capital of various dominions, dating back to pre-Christian times. Thus, the place among modern skyscrapers houses rich cultural treasures from several millennia. Overnight in Seoul.

Day 2 Seoul – fascinating juxtaposition of old and new

Today you will experience up close in what a fascinating field of tension between history and modernity Seoul moves. Their first destination is the Changdeokgung Palace, which still exists today in its original form and served as a residence of the royal family and a place of recreation. The wonderful garden complex Huwon on the palace grounds beautifully represents Korean garden art. During the rickshaw ride through the historic center of Seoul, you can view popular and well-known places from the comfort of your bike. In the Hanokdorf Bukchon you will dive deeper into the past of the city. It is home to hundreds of traditional houses called “Hanok” in Korean. Want to know more about the history of the country? In the Korean National Museum you will get a detailed picture. It exhibits artifacts of the prehistoric and modern ages. Finally, visit Garosugil Boulevard, a street train surrounded by Gingko trees in the Gangnam district. The mix of boutiques, cafes and bars, creates a unique ensemble that draws one or the other comparison with the trendy district of Soho, New York. Here you can end your day in a stylish and relaxed way. Overnight in Seoul.

3rd day Culinary delights and overnight in a hanok

In the morning, head to Jeonju, known nationwide for its authentic and local cuisine. While the rest of the city was modernized in the course of modern times, Jeonju Hanok Village retained its historical charm. Here your first destination is Jeondong Cathedral, built in the European-Romanesque style. Just a few minutes walk away is the Gyeonggijeon Shrine, which is one of Korea’s national treasures. Knowledge makes you hungry! What would be better than a few samples at the countless stalls and bistros in Korea’s most famous city for smugglers? During your culinary exploration tour, you will get a taste of the diversity of Korean cuisine. Whether Korean muzzles, so-called Mandus, with a refreshing pumpkin rice drink or Jeonju’s famous Imsil cheese on the spit (slightly comparable to Halloumi cheese), it will definitely be a delight! The perfect end of today is the overnight stay in a traditional Korean hanok. The lovingly designed property is operated by a Korean couple. Overnight in Jeonju.

Day 4 Mystical Mountains, Bamboo Forests and Fermented Sauces

Not only culturally, but also scenic South Korea has a lot to offer! Your journey will take you to the mystical Maisan Mountains, where you will visit the Buddhist Temple Tapsa (in English: “Pagoda Temple”). Flanked by dramatic Tafoni rock walls, it has over 80 cone-shaped stone pagodas, some of which reach a height of up to nine meters. At noon on your way, visit the village of Sunchang at the foot of Mount Amisan, famous nationwide for producing the popular and well-known red sauce Gochujang, which is used in almost every Korean dish. In the Sunchang Jangryu Museum you will learn more about the fermented sauces, as well as about red pepper and soybean paste. Now that you know almost everything about these important ingredients of Korean cuisine, your lunch will surely taste even better. Well strengthened drive on to Damyang, the city of bamboo. Here you will visit the Juknokwon Bamboo Park. The oldest trees are up to 300 years old! During your walk, you can catch a view of the Damyang River and Metasequoia Avenue. The avenue is rightly called the most beautiful avenue in Korea, as it forms a dreamlike scenery at any time of year. Overnight in Gwangju.

5th day tea plantations, historic villages and fantastic coastal landscapes

Before continuing your trip to Yeosu, take a trip to the Boseong tea plantations that spread like lush green carpets across the hills. Learn a lot of interesting things about the history of Korean tea culture. Then walk through the historic village of Nagan Eupseong, the only one in the country still surrounded by an old city wall. If you stroll along the fortress wall, no detail of the settlement will escape you, where 280 people still live today. Around Suncheon – your next destination – you can expect unique landscapes of Watt and Röhricht. The marshy area of Suncheon Bay is home to around 150 bird species, including some endangered specimens. In addition, many migratory birds make a stop here on their way around the globe. In the evening you will be taken to your hotel in the coastal town of Yeosu. Overnight in Yeosu.

Day 6 Fresh sea breeze in Yeosu

After a leisurely breakfast, take a morning walk along the breakwater from the port of Yeosu to Odongdo Island. The island is home to about 70 species of wild flowers. The most spectacular is a visit to the camellia blossom, which turns the island into a scarlet sea of flowers from November to April. Yeosu is also known for its treasures from the sea. In particular, the fresh oysters that grow on the oyster banks off Yeosu's coast should not be missed. On the fish market you will find a large selection of all kinds of fish and can have them freshly prepared on site. Well strengthened, a ride with the rail bike along the coast awaits you. The afternoon will end with a visit to Hyangiram Temple. It served as a base camp for Buddhist monks who supported Admiral Lee Sun-shin during the Japanese invasion. You will then be returned to your hotel. Overnight in Yeosu.

Day 7 With Korea's longest cable car on Mt. Mireuksan

Your journey will take you to the small fishing town of Tongyeong. To get an overview of the little paradise, you go with Korea’s longest cable car high up to the 461 meter high mountain Mireuksan. Arriving at the top, you will be rewarded with a fabulous view of the small island groups that grace the Korean coast. Is the name Isang Yun familiar to you? While visiting the memorial and the museum, you will learn a lot about the life’s work and music of the world-renowned composer. If you wish, it will continue musically afterwards when you visit the Tongyeong Concert Hall (optional). The concert hall in traditional shoebox design offers the best conditions for excellent acoustics. We are happy to take care of concert tickets for you. Overnight in Geoje.

8th day Flower splendor in the maritime botanical garden Oedo-Botania

To admire South Korea’s colorful floral splendor, take a trip to Oedo-Botanica Island today. During the 20-minute ferry crossing, you can admire the rocky island of Haegeumgang, which bears a resemblance to the diamond mountains located in North Korea. The island is part of the Hallyeo Marine Park, an official nature reserve in Korea. Once you reach your destination, enter the wonderland Oedo-Botania. It is a maritime botanical garden in the Hallyeo Marine Park with a wonderful view of the Southern Sea and the surrounding landscape. The various gardens show an exotic flower world from different countries. The wonderful surroundings offer you a great backdrop for your holiday photos. In the afternoon, head to the next stop of your trip: Busan, South Korea’s second largest city. Overnight in Busan.

Day 9 A city with many facets: fascinating Busan

After a leisurely breakfast, you’ll learn about the country’s past on your city exploration tour as you visit the UN Memorial and Peace Park. These honor the UN soldiers from 16 nations who participated in the Korean War (1950-1953) and died in battle. Then head to the Taejongdae observation platform. In the afternoon, immerse yourself in the colorful bustle surrounding BIFF Square – named after the famous Busan International Film Festival. Here you will find the so-called “Road of Handprints”, dedicated to Asian movie stars, as well as numerous street food stands and small boutiques. Try some typical snacks along the way. Well fortified drive to the nearby Jagalchi fish market, a landmark of the city. Hardly any other market in East Asia offers such a wide selection of fresh fish and seafood as this one. With a cruise you will end the day relaxed. Afterwards you will be taken to your hotel. Overnight in Busan.

Day 10 The legendary cliff temple

Their first destination today is the fabled temple Haedong Yonggungsa, which is called the Aquatic Sanctuary of Buddhism. According to legend, the great Buddha of mercy lives in solitude in the vastness of the ocean and appears on the back of a dragon. The underwater tomb of King Munmu should not be missed either. It is located on a small rock island in the East Sea and worth a visit just because of the dreamlike surroundings. On your way to Gyeongju, you will also make a stop at Daewangam Park, from where an approximately one-kilometer long hiking trail through a pine forest leads to the Ulgi lighthouse. Enjoy the fantastic view of the impressively beautiful coastline with its rustic rock formations before continuing your journey to the former capital of the Silla Empire. In the middle of the city center of Gyeongju, the tombs of 23 rulers of the Silla period rise in Tumuli Park. Here you can not only walk among the artificial, green hilltops, but also visit one of the gigantic burial mounds. At the end of the day, treat yourself to a stroll through the exotic garden landscape along the Volji pond. Overnight in Gyeongju.

11th Day Crowning Closure in Seoul

After your check-out, you will be taken to Gyeongju Train Station, from where you will take the high-speed KTX train back to South Korea’s capital. The rest of the day is at your disposal until the evening one last big highlight of your trip awaits you: You visit the Korean House to crown your trip with an exclusive dinner of Korean haute cuisine. Now let yourself be pampered with culinary and enjoy your last evening in South Korea with all your senses. Overnight in Seoul. 

12th day Farewell to South Korea

Today, unfortunately, we are already saying goodbye. Let the bustling bustle of the fascinating Metropople take on yourself again before you are brought to the airport with many new impressions. We wish you a good journey home!

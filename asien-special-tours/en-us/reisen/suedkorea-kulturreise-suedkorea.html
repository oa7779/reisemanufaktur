Culture, wellness and cuisine of South Korea

This trip offers something of everything: vibrant cities, venerable temples, historical sites, culinary experiences and unique natural treasures. Explore South Korea in all its facets – and relaxed. You will explore the mega-metropolis of Seoul, which will impress you with its contrasts and walk in Gyeonju in the footsteps of the Silla Empire. During your street food tour in Busan you will have the opportunity to taste regional specialty directly. On the wonderfully beautiful volcanic island of Jeju you can enjoy unique nature: you marvel at thousands of pretty camellia trees, take a pleasant hike on the Olle path along the coast and admire the island during a cruise from the water. Soothing spa and wellness treatments as well as culinary delights in the best restaurants allow you to recharge between peace and strength. At the end of your trip, you can relax on the beach and simply let your soul unwind.

1st day Arrival in South Korea

Welcome to the Land of Morning Silence! Your journey through the tiger state begins in the capital Seoul, where you will be welcomed and immediately taken to your hotel. Take advantage of the rest of the day to relax after your arrival or make your first explorations on your own in the Asian metropolis. Seoul has a long historical tradition as the capital of various dominions, dating back to pre-Christian times. Between modern skyscrapers hide rich cultural treasures from several millennia. Overnight in Seoul.

2nd Day Tradition Meets Modernity in Cosmopolitan Seoul

Today you set out to explore some of the most beautiful sights in the city. Your first destination is the Gyeongbokgung Palace (UNESCO World Heritage Site), the former main palace and residence of the royal family. After a rickshaw ride through the historic center of Seoul, immerse yourself in the Hanok village of Bukchon deeper into the city’s past. It is home to hundreds of traditional houses called “Hanok” in Korean. After you have already gathered many impressions of traditional Korea this morning, we will now continue cosmopolitanly in the trendy “in-quarter” Cheong-dam-dong. Afterwards, you should treat the mind and body to some relaxation. In the SPA of the Sulhwasoo Flagship Store you can feel really good. Traditional Korean brand Sulhwasoo opened its first flagship store in the luxury shopping district of Cheong-damdong in Gangnam district. Choose between three different spa applications. Then it’s best to close your eyes and just enjoy. Overnight in Seoul.

3rd day try excellent vegetarian temple cuisine

You start the day with a visit to Gilsangsa Temple, hidden in the middle of the well-off Seongbuk-dong district amidst luxury objects - a true oasis in the bustle of the city. Afterwards, take a culinary journey into Buddhist faiths. Balwoo Gongyang serves light temple cuisine based on traditional recipes handed down in Korean temples for centuries. It is the first restaurant to receive a Michelin star with vegetarian temple cuisine. During your subsequent visit to a traditional tea café in Insadong Alley, you can really get your immune system going with medicinal herbal teas. In the Yangnyeongsi Museum of Alternative Medicine you will learn more about alternative healing methods. Among the exhibits are also very rare, as well as highly toxic medical products. You will deepen your knowledge on a stroll through the Seoul Yangnyeong Market, known for its selection of very special ingredients for alternative medical treatment. Nearby is Gyeongdong Market, one of the largest ginseng and herbal markets in Korea. Overnight in Seoul.

Day 4 In the footsteps of the Silla Empire

In the morning you will be taken to the train station, from where you will travel by high-speed train KTX to the former capital of the Silla Empire, Gyeongju. Once there, the first thing to do is visit Tumuli Park in the middle of the city center, where the burial mounds of 23 rulers of the Silla period rise. Here you can not only walk among the artificial, green hilltops, but also visit one of the gigantic burial mounds. Inside it are more than 11,000 grave goods as well as the crowns of the king. In the immediate vicinity is the Cheomseongdae Observatory, the oldest existing star observatory in Asia. The 362 stones used for construction represent the 362 days of the lunar year. At the end of the day, take a stroll through the exotic garden landscape along the Volji pond, once part of the palace. Overnight in Gyeongju.

Day 5: Legendary Cultural Treasures and Wellness in Hot Springs

Your day begins with two UNESCO World Heritage sites that will surely impress you. The Bulguksa Temple represents the temple architecture of the ancient Silla Empire at its best. On the temple grounds are other national treasures such as the main stairs with 33 steps, which represent the 33 steps for enlightenment. The famous Seokguram Grotto was dug into the mountain wall of Tohamsan Mountain in the 8th century. Inside it is a Buddha statue overlooking the East Sea. Then it is already time to set off for your next stop: Busan, the second largest city in South Korea. Here you will find the SPA Land Centum City, a modern wellness oasis where you can enjoy a break from the here and now. The spa receives its water from two springs at a depth of 1,000 meters, which operates 22 different hot baths and 13 different saunas. Also the outdoor foot bath should not be missed! Another special feature is that the SPA is located in the Shinsegae Centum City department store, which is listed in the Guinness Book of Records as the world’s largest shopping complex. Overnight in Busan.

6th Day Culinary Delights in Busan

In the morning we will first go to the Jagalchi fish market. Even today, women sit on the roadside and sell the catch of the day at small stalls. Hardly any other market in East Asia offers such a wide selection of fresh fish and seafood as this one. For foodies and fish lovers definitely a must. The cuisine continues at the same time: immerse yourself in the colorful activities around BIFF-Platz – named after the famous Busan International Film Festival. Here you will find the so-called “Road of Handprints”, dedicated to Asian movie stars, as well as numerous street food stands and small boutiques. On the go, try some typical snacks like Bindaetteok (hearty mung bean pancake) and Eomuk, the popular fish cake – a real local specialty. Then it goes up to the Busan Tower. From Busan's landmark, enjoy a wonderful view of the uniquely located city deep below you. The rest of the day is at your disposal to discover the city further according to your mood. Overnight in Busan. 

Day 7 The island is calling!

After just under a week on the South Korean mainland, you will travel on to South Korea’s secret jewel, the subtropical island of Jeju, which you will reach after a short domestic flight. Countless myths revolve around this volcanic island, which has produced its own culture due to its isolated location from the mainland. Scattered all over the island, you will find Dol Hareubangs, figures of lava rock with protruding eyes and no pupils, which are considered fertility symbols. For newlywed Koreans, the island is therefore a popular honeymoon destination. Upon arrival at the airport, you will be picked up and immediately visit Jeju’s oldest traditional market, Dongmun Market. Be sure to try the exotic Hallabong fruit there. She is known for her sweet and soft pulp – a wonderful taste experience! Afterwards, the Hallim Park attracts with its various beautiful gardens. Stroll along Palmenallee or visit the Jeju Stone Garden, the Bonsai Garden or the Water Garden. With these colorful impressions in mind, you will then be brought to your hotel. Overnight in Jeju.

Day 8: Insight into life as a Buddhist monk

Let yourself be inspired by Korean Buddhism and Jeju’s nature today. You will visit the Yak-chonsa Temple, with its impressive height of 30 meters, one of the largest temple complex on Jeju. Nearby is the Dharma Hall (Gulbeopdang), which has been carved into a natural cave. At Camelia Hill, you can admire 6,000 camellia trees of 500 different species. But there are also palms, wild flowers and other plants here. An ideal place to simply let your soul dwell and enjoy the holiday to the fullest. Afterwards you will continue to the O'sulloc Green Tea Museum, where you will learn about the cultivation and history of the famous green tea and have the opportunity to taste different types of tea. The day ends with a walking tour to the Suwallbong summit, a viewpoint from which you can enjoy breathtaking views of the coast and surrounding countryside. Overnight in Jeju. 

Day 9 On the Olle path through Jejus beautiful landscapes

Breakfast today, because your day will be active! On the volcanic island with its unique and varied vegetation, 21 hiking trails (Olle paths) and 5 secondary routes were created over the years. The paths lead through forests, dreamy villages or pass along the coasts and beaches. On all routes you immerse yourself in Jeju’s culture and experience the wonderful nature. This morning you will head to the starting point of your hike along the Olle path No. 6 (degree of difficulty: easy), which begins at the sea arm Soesokkak. This is where the Pacific Ocean and river water meet. The special feature of this route is the varied nature and the unique ecosystem of Jeju. The hike takes you to the Cheonjiyeon Waterfall, traverses beautiful forests and fields, continues along the wonderful coastline and finally ends at the rustic rock formation Oedolgae. The approximately 20-meter-high columnar rock was formed about 1.5 million ago. Years after a volcanic eruption. Today you can already see the first pines on the rock. Overnight in Jeju.

Day 10 A day in the sign of the element “water”

A visit to Jeju would not be complete without a detour to the Yongmeori coast and the surrounding Sanbangsan mountain. The Yongmeori coast is one of the most scenic spots on Jeju's south coast. With its unique rock formations formed over millions of years, it offers a spectacular backdrop for photographers, nature lovers or the family album. Shipping offers the best opportunity to view the volcanic island from a different angle. Enjoy the fresh sea breeze as you explore the southern part of the island by water. During the trip you will see the mountain Sanbangsan, the tufa walls, several small island groups and much more. You have experienced a lot in the last few days and have earned a SPA program at the WE Hotel. With the unique concept of serving as a pure healing oasis, it is the first of its kind on the island. Tucked away in a hilly region, the resort represents a quiet, zen-like patch of earth. Among other things, the resort offers hydrotherapies, from which you can choose one. If a water therapy is not to your taste, we are happy to organize another treatment for you. Overnight in Jeju.

Day 11 - The fascinating world of Haenyeo female divers

Another impressive day lies ahead of you. In the morning, visit Hamdeok Beach, where turquoise blue water, black lava rock and fine sandy beach provide a dream setting for a refreshing walk along the coast. Afterwards, immerse yourself in the fascinating world of traditional Jeju divers – the Haenyeo. These particular women who are merely equipped with diving glasses, a wetsuit and a knife, scratching shells from the stones and diving for shellfish in the depths of the sea. Without an oxygen bottle, you can do without breath at a depth of about 15 meters up to three minutes. Then head to Seongsan Sunrise Peak, a crater formed around 100,000 years ago by a seaquake and a subsequent volcanic eruption at the bottom of the ocean. A climb is rewarded with a breathtaking view over the island and the vast sea. At the end of this day, in the historic village of Seongeup you will get a glimpse into the special culture of the Jeju inhabitants and their traditional customs. Overnight in Jeju.

12-13. Day of free time in Jeju

Without tour guide and chauffeur

Make this day your own pleasure. Explore the island on your own or enjoy the amenities of your luxurious hotel. Overnight in Jeju.

Day 14 Dinner Over the Clouds

Today, unfortunately, it is time to say goodbye to the island paradise Jeju. In the morning you will be taken to the airport, from where you will fly back to the metropolis of Seoul. Once there, you will be taken to your hotel. The rest of the day is at your disposal. Let yourself drift through the city and buy one or the other souvenir for your loved ones at home. With a dinner above the clouds, you will say goodbye to this beautiful country. The Walking on the Cloud is located on the 59th floor of Building 63, but it does not only impress with its outstanding views over the Han River and Seoul, but especially with its exquisite kitchen. Here delicacies from all over Europe are served at the highest level. Ideal to end your dreamlike journey with an excellent dinner with a fantastic view. Overnight in Seoul.

Day 15 Farewell to South Korea

Let the bustling bustle of the fascinating Metropople take on yourself again before you are brought to the airport with many new impressions. We wish you a good journey home!

The “Venerable Beauty” – this means the name of Sri Lanka. The island looks back on a long history, whose heritage is manifested in impressive archaeological sites. Richly decorated temples, venerable monasteries and the remains of monumental ancient palaces rise above beautiful landscapes. Sri Lanka offers a perfect mix of cultural highlights and natural delight. Sattgreen rainforests, picturesque mountain landscapes and bright white sand beaches invite you to walks. Discover the island on a round trip as diverse as the country itself.

Day 1 Arrival in Colombo

Your journey through Sri Lanka begins in Colombo. Here you will be warmly welcomed by your tour guide on arrival at the airport and brought to your hotel. Take advantage of the first day to relax from your arrival or make your own explorations in Colombo. Overnight in Colombo.

Day 2 Explore Colombo

Today is dedicated to the dynamic city of Colombo, which has long been characterised by its strategic location on one of the most important maritime trade routes. Currently, the city, like the whole island, is experiencing a new upswing after years of stagnation. One of Colombo’s known as the back of his hand is Mark Forbes. He has Danish, British and German roots, but has lived in Colombo for a long time. During a joint city tour, he will show you the most interesting places and tell you a lot about the historic past and today's everyday life in this emerging metropolis. Your walk will take you past historic colonial buildings to the bustling Pettah Market. (Note: The walk is usually personally guided by Marc Forbes). When booking, you will know if it is available on your desired date. In addition, you will be accompanied by your personal guide. In the afternoon, visit the colorful Gangaramaya Temple and the multi-ethnic Cinnamon Garden district. Overnight in Colombo.

Day 3 The Cave Temples of Dambulla

On the way east, visit the cave temples of Dambulla. Situated on a hill about 150 meters high, the caves offered protection to King Walagamba during his 14-year exile in the first century BC. Returning to the throne, he built the largest temple in the country at that time. In total, there are about 80 cave temples in Dambulla. In the shrines decorated with historical paintings are more than 150 Buddha statues. Already in the first cave awaits you the largest statue with almost 15 meters. In the second cave, probably the most impressive of the complex, there are well over 100 images of Buddha. With an area of over 2000 square meters, the temple complex is still one of the largest in the country today and was included in the list of UNESCO World Heritage Sites in 1982. Then you will be driven to your hotel. Overnight in Habarana.

Day 4: Lion Rocks of Sigiriya & Minneriya National Park

This morning, a highlight of the trip awaits you. The Sigiriya Rock is often referred to as the “Eighth Wonder of the World”. On the plateau of a monolith, which rises about 200 meters high above the surrounding area, a rock fortress was built in the 5th century. Since in former times a stone lion towered at the entrance of the bastion, this place is called "lion rock" in the local language. However, only two paws of this statue survived over the centuries. The ruins of the once colossal palace complex give a very good idea of how spectacularly the majestic building towered over the surrounding countryside. Around the huge rock once a moat of water stood. Today you can walk through a pleasure garden here, which still houses the remains of fountains, pavilions and a monastery complex. In the afternoon, take a jeep safari in Minneriya National Park. The national park is home to many fascinating animals and is best known for its large number of elephants. Around a water tank there are deer, monkeys and elephants. After this exciting excursion, you will be driven to your hotel. Overnight in Habarana.

5th Day Country Living in Diyakapilla & Mighty Kingdom Polonnaruwa

A trip to the idyllic village of Diyakapilla brings you closer to rural life in Sri Lanka. A short drive leads along the old pilgrimage route over the Gallinda hill chain. Once in the village, visit the local primary school and shrine. A walk of two to three kilometers leads along a pilgrimage path through forests, agricultural areas and rice fields. You will learn how seasonally available cereals and vegetables are harvested and prepared using traditional methods. Your lunch will be prepared by a family from the village, and then you will continue your journey. Polonnaruwa was the capital of a powerful kingdom nearly a thousand years ago. Today, statues and palaces, impressive stupas, detailed frescoes and last but not least “Gal Vihara”, an imposing rock sanctuary with four Buddha images still bear witness to its former splendor. You will explore the ancient city in a relaxed way by bicycle. After visiting this historical site, head to Passikudah beach where you will spend some relaxing days. Take a look at the first impressions of your journey and relax in your first-class hotel complex. Overnight in Passikudah.

6.-8. Day relaxation on the beach of Passikudah

Without guidance

Sit back and enjoy the sun, beach and sea in the beautiful Passikudah. Read a good book and enjoy doing the sweet nothing. Or how about sailing across the deep blue waters of the Indian Ocean? We are happy to make you an offer. Overnight in Passikudah.

Day 9 The Old Royal City and the Tooth Temple of Kandy

Your journey will take you to Kandy today. Kandy, the former royal city, lies in the middle of the central mountains about 500 meters above sea level. Despite its size, it is still very much influenced by ancient traditions. The main attraction is undoubtedly the tooth temple, in which a canine of Buddha is said to be kept in a golden shrine. According to tradition, this tooth emanates spiritual power, making the sanctuary one of the most important pilgrimage sites in the country for Buddhists. The temple was built in several sections between 1687 and 1782 to protect and preserve the dental relic. In the evening you are invited to a cultural show with different dances of Sri Lanka. Overnight in Kandy.

10th day Train ride through tea plantations towards Nuwara Eliya

You leave Kandy and take the train for about four hours through beautiful landscapes towards Nuwara Eliya. The train finally ends in Nanu Oya, from there it continues with the car. The “City of Light” is located in the central highlands of Sri Lanka. The picture book landscape and the temperate climate make this city very special. It is located 1,868 meters above sea level and is considered the most important place for tea production in Sri Lanka. Gracefully towers the highest mountain in the country, the “Pidurutalagala”, over the lush green tea fields of the area. The city carries the nickname “Little England”, because much reminds of the time of the past empire. The British settled here to cultivate tea. Thus, the mountains around Nuwara Eliya are still known as the main growing area of the famous Ceylon tea. Overnight in Nuwara Eliya.

Day 11 Hike to Horton Plains National Park

Today you will visit Horton Plains National Park. The protected area, which is located on the highest mountain plateau in the country, is one of the most important national parks in the country because of its scenic diversity – here you will also find the beautiful Baker’s Waterfall. It is home to a variety of species that live only in this environment. The hills are overgrown by evergreen forests of ancient tree giants. From a steep slope over 1050 meters, which bears the poetic name “World’s End”, in good weather, there is a breathtaking view to the sea. When visiting a tea factory, you will gain insights into the production of the delicious drink: from harvest to final refinement. You can also try yourself in tea picking. Afterwards you will return to the hotel, where you can rest a little. Overnight in Nuwara Eliya.

Day 12: Travel to Yala National Park

On the way to the next stop of your trip, you will stop at Ella in the highlands of Sri Lanka. Before you continue your journey, you can stroll a little through the small town. In the southeast, a testimony of the country’s rich natural resources awaits you in the afternoon: the Yala National Park. It is the oldest nature reserve in Sri Lanka. Over 130 bird species such as flamingos, pelicans, peacock and cormorants have settled here; around 30 mammal species roam the area. Among them are elephants and water buffalo. Large parts of the park are reminiscent of the African savannah, while the outer areas are dominated by moist monsoon forests. You will explore this diversity on a Jeep safari. Overnight in Yala.

13th Day Pilgrimage City Kataragama and Colonial Heritage in Galle

Today you will first visit Kataragama, an important pilgrimage site where a large pilgrimage celebration in honor of the god of the same name takes place in July/August. But also during the rest of the year, the many shrines are worth a visit. Afterwards we continue to Galle, where Dutch architecture mixes with South Asian tradition and tropical feeling. The city in the southwest of Sri Lanka was the most important port in the country before the arrival of the Portuguese in the 16th century. However, the peak in development reached Galle under Dutch rule. The current landmark is the old Dutch fort with its fortress walls and bastions. The old town inside the fortification was added to the list of World Heritage Sites in 1988. Together with your guide, you will make a round through the fort, where you will also admire the large lighthouse. Overnight in Galle.

Day 14: Onward journey to the west coast to Negombo

After breakfast, you will leave the enchanting south coast and get back to the starting point of your journey: your driver guide will take you to Negombo, a coastal town near the international airport. Negombo was once the center of cinnamon cultivation by Dutch colonialists and is now a popular tourist spot with its beaches, exquisite seafood cuisine and lively nightlife. Enjoy a last walk to the sunset on the beach. Overnight in Negombo.

15th day home

Today, your time out from everyday life is coming to an end. Even if your holiday is over, the precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories of your trip will accompany you forever. Look forward to letting your friends and family share your experiences in the distance at home. Your driver guide will take you to the airport for your return flight

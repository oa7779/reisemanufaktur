Uzbekistan - a country of the highest craftsmanship

Uzbekistan Travel - Experience a country of highest craftsmanship with this trip from Asia Special Tours. Ask here for your personal trip!

Itinerary & services

English itinerary text is an offline machine translation of the German original for this team preview. Please confirm travel details with your advisor; the German source remains available through the language switch.

Following in the footsteps of Uzbek art, the path leads from Tashkent via the oasis city of Bukhara to the artist village of Gijduvan. There you will witness the masterful ceramic production. During your journey, you will also be introduced to the secrets of silk carpet weaving, and in the big cities, art accompanies you at every turn. Because the magnificently decorated mosques and palaces amaze every visitor. In order to bring a unique souvenir as a memory, you should plunge into the turmoil of the Oriental bazaars.

Day 1 Arrival in Uzbekistan

Welcome to Uzbekistan! When you arrive in Tashkent, you will already be welcomed by your German-speaking tour guide. Together with your private chauffeur you drive to the hotel, where you can arrive in peace. Overnight in Tashkent.

Day 2 Explore the capital of Uzbekistan

Tashkent is still shaped by Soviet architecture – and a place where you can feel the tension between Europe and the Orient particularly well. Enjoy a discovery tour through the capital of Uzbekistan, which will bring you closer to the ancient squares and buildings, but also the modern part of the 2,000-year-old city. First, visit Khasti Imon Square with the beautiful Barak Khan Medrese, Tillya Sheikh Mosque and Kaffal Shashi Mausoleum made of simple brick masonry. Here you can also take a look at the Quran, which was handwritten by the third Caliph Osman (644 – 656). Afterwards you will visit the Kukeldash Medrese, which still serves as a Koranic school today. You will get an impression of the everyday life of the Uzbeks while walking over the huge oriental Chorsu bazaar. Immerse yourself in the bustle and let yourself be enchanted by the scent of oriental spices and fruits. In the afternoon, visit the Museum of Applied Arts, which provides an interesting overview of Uzbekistan’s rich traditional handicrafts. Afterwards you will discover the Neustadt with the famous Independence Square, and the magnificently designed metro stations are also worth a detour. Overnight in Tashkent.

Day 3 - Continue to Bukhara

In the morning you fly to Bukhara. The oasis city was formerly considered the Islamic center of Central Asia and also one of the most important cities along the Silk Road. Visit the most magnificent sights of Bukhara. You will see the mausoleum of the former Samanid ruler Ismael Samani from the 9th and 10th centuries as well as the beautiful Bolo Hauz Mosque. Other highlights are the gigantic Ark Citadel in the ground plan shape of the constellation “Great Chariot” and the Poi Kalon complex, which consists of a minaret, a mosque and the beautiful Miri Arab Medrese. Also worth seeing are the Chashma Ayub Mausoleum with the Holy Fountain and the Ulugbek Medrese, which was named after the famous astronomer. In addition, you have the opportunity to experience the daily activity in the three overcoupled bazaars up close. During your guided tour, of course, the Abdul-Aziz-Khan-Medrese and the Magokki-Attori Mosque, which was rebuilt several times – during excavations, archaeologists found remains from the tenth century. Overnight in Bukhara.

Day 4 Traditional Puppet Play in Bukhara

This morning, your guide will show you more sights in the Bukhara area. You will visit the summer palace of the last emir of Bukhara – Sitorai mohi khosa – as well as the Choir Minor Mosque with its 4 minarets. The city is famous for its traditional puppetry. You are invited to a manufactory still dedicated to this special craft, and the master will explain to you the production process and tradition of the theatre. The dolls can even perform Uzbek dances. You will be amazed and will not be able to stop a smile. Overnight in Bukhara.

5th Day Masterful Ceramics in Gijduvan

After breakfast, drive to the small town of Gijduvan, 46 kilometers away, best known for its excellent ceramic craftsmanship. The ceramic styles there are particularly beautiful to look at due to the elaborate ornaments and combinations of geometric figures. In the schools you can watch the ceramic production live. Then continue the journey to Samarkand. Along the way, visit the Sardoba Malik cistern and a caravanserai in Navoi. Overnight in Samarkand.

Day 6: Legendary Samarkand

Scent bazaars, huge mosques and a mysterious atmosphere: this is Samarkand. Your sightseeing tour of this breathtaking city begins with a visit to the Gur Emir Mausoleum, the final resting place of the famous ruler Tamerlan. After a short walk you will reach the heart of Samarkand, the beautiful Registan Square with its three magnificent medres: Ulugbek, Shir Dor and Tillya-Kari. In the afternoon, it is best to continue your visit with the beautiful Bibi Khanum Mosque. Bibi Khanum was to become the largest mosque in the Eastern world at that time – after all, it is still one of the largest mosques today. In the archaeological site of Afrosiab, a palace was discovered in 1912, which probably belonged to a high official, and numerous terracotta figures have also been found – including an Athena with a helmet. You will continue on your way to the dead city of Shah-I-Zinda, which consists of numerous, finely decorated mausoleums – also the cousin of the Prophet Muhammad is said to be buried here. After the stars you can finally reach in the Ulugbeks observatory. And of course you should not miss a stroll over the Oriental Siab bazaar. Overnight in Samarkand.

7th day excursion to Urgut

Leave the fairytale Samarkand with your guide to visit the town of Urgut near the Zeravshan mountain range. The city is located near the Tajik-Uzbek border and is famous for its plane tree grove, which contains more than 1,000 years old trees, whose circumference can reach up to 16 meters. The Choir Chinor Garden, with its plane trees, was formerly used as a school. Here you have the opportunity to chat with locals. Afterwards, enjoy a stroll over the lively and colorful bazaar of Urgut before returning to Samarkand. Overnight in Samarkand.

Day 8 Back to Tashkent

In the morning there is still time to visit a silk carpet factory, where you will learn everything about the production, from the silkworm to the finished, artfully designed carpet. Then the journey leads you back to the capital. During the ride you can stop at the Amir-Temur gate and the honey market in Jizzakh. Here people from nearby villages meet to offer their products. From May to June there is flower honey, in the following two months you can try “cotton honey” – a variety that is collected during the cotton bloom. Also with tasty apples, pears, plums and kaki you can stock up here. However, you will search for potatoes and onions in vain – everyone grows them themselves. Back in Tashkent, you have time again to organize the last hours in Uzbekistan according to your wishes. Stroll over the Chorsu Bazaar or through a few shops. You will certainly find a souvenir for yourself or for those at home here. Overnight in Tashkent.

9th day Departure from Tashkent

Today, unfortunately, we say goodbye. With 1001 new impressions and experiences in your luggage, you will be taken to the airport of Tashkent, from where you will start your journey home. The unique memories will always remain for you and often beautify everyday life.

Ladakh - On the Roof of the World

Discover Ladakh on a private round trip: Leh, Buddhist monasteries, Himalayan passes, Nubra Valley, Pangong Lake and selected walks.

Itinerary & services

How does it feel to stand on the roof of the world and overlook the Himalayas to the horizon? In Ladakh, the legendary "Land of High Passes", you can expect spectacular mountain landscapes, centuries-old monasteries and encounters with Buddhist culture, which is still deeply rooted in its traditions. Follow the course of the Indus through fascinating valleys, discover remote villages and experience the unique atmosphere of the Himalayas up close. Easy to medium hikes always lead you to places that are far from the well-known routes. From the green oases of the Nubra Valley to the shimmering Pangong Lake, every day opens up new perspectives on a region full of contrasts and beauty. Carefully selected accommodation with local charm offers space to arrive, breathe and relax. Again and again there is time to let the expanse of the mountain world affect itself and to experience the special magic of Ladakh consciously. A journey into the soul of Ladakh.

1st day Arrival in New Delhi

Namaste and welcome to India! Upon arrival at Delhi airport, you will be warmly welcomed by your guide. A friendly welcome and the support during the first steps on site ensure a relaxed and successful start to your journey. Then head to your airport hotel. After check-in, you have time to recover from your arrival and arrive in peace. Depending on the arrival time and your wish, we are happy to organize a first city tour where you can get to know the many facets of Delhi. Overnight in New Delhi. 

Day 2 From Delhi to the Himalayan Silence

Go to the North of India! In the morning, head to the airport and fly to Leh. Immerse yourself in one of the most spectacular mountain regions in the world. Already during the approach, breathtaking views of the snow-capped peaks of the Himalayas are offered with a clear view. Deeply cut valleys, rugged mountain ranges and the barren beauty of the high mountain landscape suggest why Ladakh is often called the “Land of High Passes”. Prayer flags dancing in the wind and white stupas on the mountain slopes give the region its distinctive face. After landing in Leh at an altitude of around 3,500 meters, you will be warmly welcomed and driven to your stylish accommodation, the exclusive Dolkhar Resort. With its successful combination of sustainable design and regional architecture, the resort reflects Ladakh’s unique character. So that you can get used to the altitude, the rest of the day is all about acclimatization. Take it easy, enjoy the fresh mountain air and the first impressions of this fascinating Himalayan region. Overnight in Leh.

Day 3 Between prayer songs and mountain panoramas

The day begins with a visit to the impressive Thiksey Monastery, one of Ladakh’s most important Buddhist centers. Smoke is in the air, while from the prayer halls the deep chants of the monks resound. During the morning prayer ceremony you will experience the special spiritual atmosphere of the monastery. Then take an easy hike along the Indus to the village of Shey, the former residence of the Ladakh kings. The remains of the former palace tower over fertile fields and tell of the rich history of the Kingdom of Ladakh. At lunch, enjoy a hotel-provided lunch package. Afterwards you will drive to the famous Hemis Monastery, the largest and most important monastery in the region. In addition to its impressive architecture, it houses a valuable collection of Buddhist art treasures. From Hemis, a pleasant ascent leads to the secluded Gotsang Cave of Meditation. For centuries, it served monks as a place of silence and retreat. In the late afternoon, return to Leh. To end the day, visit Shanti Stupa, high above the city. From here you have a wide view over Leh, while the evening sun dips the landscape into warm hues of gold. Overnight in Leh.

Day 4 Cultural Treasures in the Indus

Following the course of the Indus, you will travel together with your guide through a fascinating landscape of rugged gorges and ochre-colored mountain slopes. Your first stop is the over 1,000-year-old Alchi Monastery, which with its extraordinary murals and unique Kashmiri influence is one of Ladakh’s most important cultural treasures. Then visit the picturesque Likir Monastery, which is visibly towering over an imposing gilded Maitreya Buddha statue. After the visits, you will be served lunch at the renowned Alchi Kitchen, which interprets traditional Ladakh flavors in a modern way. In the afternoon you will arrive at Uletokpo, a quiet village on the banks of the Indus, nestled in the spectacular mountain world of West Ladakh. Here you can get your accommodation at Ule Ethnic Resort. With its traditional architecture, the resort blends harmoniously into the natural environment. At the end of the day there is a relaxing walk along the river. Overnight in Uletokpo.

Day 5 - Walking through the original Ladakh

Ladakh away from the well-known routes is on your program today. Your walk will take you through the mountain landscape around Uletokpo and past farms, barley fields and small settlements that reflect the traditional life of the region. The destination of the day is the remote village of Tar, which has retained its original character to this day. You will have the opportunity to talk to the local population and learn more about the traditions and ways of life in rural Ladakh. The hike always offers delightful views of the surrounding mountains and gives a feeling for the extraordinary silence of this region. In the late afternoon, return to Uletokpo. Let the impressions of the day end relaxed and enjoy the evening in your accommodation. Overnight in Uletokpo. 

6th Day Natural Paradise Hemis National Park

Your adventure today takes you to the secluded Rumbak Valley, which is considered the gateway to Hemis National Park. The protected mountain region is one of the last largely untouched landscapes of the Himalayas and is home to numerous wild animals, including blue sheep, bearded vultures and, with a bit of luck, even the rare snow leopard. During a hike through the impressive high mountain landscape, you will experience the originality of this region. Wide valleys and rugged mountain slopes characterize the landscape and make Rumbak a paradise for nature lovers and hiking lovers. The secluded location in the middle of the mountains makes the resort an ideal starting point for nature and wildlife observations. Afterwards, there is time to let the unique surroundings have a calm effect on you and admire the impressive high mountain backdrop. Overnight in Rumbak. 

Day 7 From the mountain wilderness back to Leh

The day begins with another hike through the impressive landscape of the Hemis National Park. Against the backdrop of majestic mountain peaks, you will experience the special silence of high alpine nature. With a little luck, wildlife can be observed again on the way. After lunch, you and your guide will return to Leh. The capital of Ladakh will once again welcome you with its authentic charm. The rest of the afternoon is at your disposal. Take the time for a leisurely stroll through the lively alleys of the old town, visit one of the charming cafes or enjoy the amenities of your accommodation. Overnight in Leh.

Day 8 Over the Cloud Pass into the Nubra Valley

One of Ladakh’s most spectacular rides awaits you today. Via the Khardung La, one of the highest passes in the world at an altitude of 5,359 meters, you can reach the picturesque Nubra Valley. The magnificent views of the mountain ranges of the Karakorum and Zanskar make the journey an unforgettable experience. After crossing the pass, the landscape changes noticeably. Green oases, apricot groves and small villages shape the image of the Nubra Valley, which has always been an important hub along historical trade routes. From Diskit you take a hike to Hunder. The trail passes through traditional settlements and agricultural land and provides authentic insights into the daily life of the region. Overnight in Nubra.

9th day Monasteries, dunes and caravan paths

Discover the cultural and scenic highlights of the Nubra Valley today. Your first stop is the traditional Diskit Monastery, which sits impressively above the valley. Particularly impressive is the monumental Maitreya Buddha statue, which looks far beyond the plain and is one of the most famous landmarks in the region. Then explore the famous sand dunes of Hunder, an extraordinary landscape of gentle dunes and rugged mountain ranges. Here you will encounter the characteristic two-hulled trampling animals that once accompanied the caravans along the ancient Silk Road and still shape the image of the Nubra Valley today. The rest of the day is at your disposal to let the peaceful atmosphere of the Nubra Valley and the fascinating landscape affect you. Overnight in Nubra.  

Optional (extra charge): Quad ride through the sand dunes of Nubra  
Experience pure adventure amidst spectacular landscapes on an ATV ride through the golden sand dunes of the Nubra Valley. On high-performance off-road vehicles, you will explore the desert landscape and experience breathtaking views of the mountains. 

Day 10: Wonders of Colour at Lake Pangong

Today’s highlight is Lake Pangong, whose unique colors and impressive expanse make it one of the most spectacular natural wonders of the Himalayas. Located at an altitude of around 4,350 meters, the lake extends beyond the borders of India to Tibet and impresses with its ever-changing color play. Depending on the time of day and light incidence, the water shimmers in different shades of blue and turquoise and forms an impressive contrast to the barren mountains of the area. Upon arrival, you will move into your accommodation at Desert Blue Pangong. Against the spectacular backdrop of the lake, the property impresses with its exceptional location in the middle of the impressive landscape of Ladakh. After lunch, there is time to let the soothing seclusion of this place affect you. Use the evening for a walk along the lake shore and enjoy the fascinating scenery. As the sun slowly disappears behind the surrounding peaks and the last rays of light dance across the water, Lake Pangong unfolds its very special magic. Overnight in Pangong.

Day 11 Sunrise at the Lake & Return to Leh

Early in the morning you will experience the sunrise on Pangong Lake. When the first rays of sun hit the surface of the water and immerse the lake in shimmering shades of blue and gold, the unique beauty of this natural wonder is revealed from its most impressive side. After breakfast, you will return to Leh. The route leads over the Chang La Pass and offers once again magnificent views of the fascinating high mountain landscape of Ladakh. Arriving in Leh, the rest of the afternoon is at your disposal. Take the time for final explorations, a stroll through the old town or relaxing hours in your accommodation. In the evening, a festive farewell dinner awaits you, during which you will review the highlights of your Ladakh journey in an atmospheric atmosphere. Overnight in Leh.

Day 12 From the roof of India back to the capital

After many days in the mountains of Ladakh, you will return to Delhi today. You will be taken to the airport of Leh and fly to the Indian capital. During the flight, the imposing peaks of the Himalayas will once again pass under you and offer unforgettable views of the enormous mountain world. For your last night in India, you will again move into a room in a comfortable airport hotel. The remaining day is at your disposal. We are happy to organize an interesting city tour through Delhi or a varied evening program for you to end your trip with an atmospheric atmosphere. Overnight in New Delhi.

13th day Departure from India

Without tour guide and chauffeur

Your adventure in India is coming to an end, and let the impressions of this extraordinary journey revive the fascination of this unique region. Look forward to sharing your experiences, stories and most beautiful photos with your loved ones and keeping the memories of this unforgettable journey alive for a long time. Depending on the departure time, you will go to the terminal on your own. We say goodbye and look forward to accompanying you on your next dream trip!

China Journey - Rediscovering Yunnan's Diversity

China Journey: Explore Yunnan’s diversity with Asia Special Tours and re-experience the land of the middle. Here you can request and consult your individual trip to China.

Itinerary & services

English itinerary text is an offline machine translation of the German original for this team preview. Please confirm travel details with your advisor; the German source remains available through the language switch.

More varied and authentic, China could hardly be. On this journey you will encounter numerous wonders, stories, cultures and legends from earlier times. Be it bizarre rock formations as from another world in Kunming, spectacular and mystical mountain landscapes in lush nature in the Gaoligong National Park, the encounter with people of different cultures and ethnicities of China or the beyond famous monastery Songtseling in the time-honored Shangri-La. Enjoy the variety on this journey and let yourself be captivated by the multifaceted beauty of China.

Day 1 Arrival in Kunming & Welcome to Eternal Spring

Your journey through beautiful southern China begins in the Chinese province of Yunnan, in the city of Kunming. At the airport, your personal guide and your chauffeur are already waiting for you. Literally translated, Yunnan means “south of the clouds”. You will see, sometimes the promise is fulfilled in the name. Kunming spoils its guests with a mild climate all year round. Relax from the long flight and enjoy the rest of the day according to your wishes. Depending on the arrival time, your guide can still take a trip with you. Overnight in Kunming.

2nd day Bizarre rock formations in the stone forest

Your guide welcomes you today, and you first travel to Shilin for a natural highlight: the famous Stone Forest. Its extraordinary maze of towering rock pinnacles suggests mythical creatures and animals. Across an area of 12 km², discover formations such as Ashima, a young beauty said to have turned to stone. Corals are visible on the rock faces, recalling the prehistoric sea in which this landscape originated. Walk through this stone garden and discover different figures as your perspective changes. Then continue to Jianshui. Overnight in Jianshui.

3rd day Romantic gardens and visit to the Temple of Confucius

Today you will explore Jianshui, whose history dates back to the Jin Dynasty (256 – 420). Later, Jianshui also played a role in the kingdoms of Ningzhou and Nanzhao. During the Yuan Dynasty (1279 – 1368), Jianshui (Lin An) was a major military outpost, contributing to the emergence of today’s city. Together with your guide, you will visit the Temple of Confucius, a huge temple complex dating back to 1285, which is the second largest Temple of Confucius in China. Then walk through the magnificent garden of the Zhu family, which is a typical flower wonder from the southern province. Finally, visit the village of Tuanshan, which is considered a cultural monument. The whole village consists of the elaborately built and beautiful wooden buildings from the 19th century. Enjoy the original atmosphere, because not many tourists get lost in this village. Finally, continue on to Yuanyang today. Overnight in Yuanyang.

4th day Rice terraces in the Southern Highlands & ethnic minorities

Getting up early is an absolute must today! In beautiful weather, you can experience a breathtaking sunrise over the rice terraces. Enjoy this absolutely impressive moment - a sight that will surely be remembered. Back at the hotel, you can have breakfast in peace and start the day with a good cup of coffee or tea. Then take a hike with spectacular views of the rice terraces and the surrounding villages. The travel terraces extend from their lowest point at 144 meters up to 2,000 meters. Countless deep green fields, which extend as if from horizon to horizon, give you an infinite number of photo opportunities. Take enough time and enjoy the wonderful landscape. You also have the opportunity to visit a village of the Hani minority and learn a lot about the traditional rice cultivation. Overnight in Yuanyang.

5th day Gaoligong Nature Reserve & adventurous Vinetree Tented Resort

Today is a slightly longer journey ahead of you, but you will have great looks out the window. From Yuanyang we will first go back to Kunming. Take a flight to Tengchong. There, a guide will already await you and take you to Gaoligong National Park in your exceptional accommodation, the Vinetree Tented Resort. There you will spend the night in well-equipped, fixed tent bungalows. The Gaoligong National Park is probably one of the largest contiguous primordial forests in China. Since it extends over many altitude meters, the area has a very diverse flora and fauna. While there is still a tropical climate at the foot of the mountain, the summit is covered by snow. Look forward to the next few days in wild nature. You will certainly be able to observe one or the other bird and maybe even a wild animal. Use the time to enjoy the pure nature in peace and let your soul dangle. Overnight in Gaoligong.

Day 6 Hike through the lush natural landscape

After breakfast, you will have the opportunity to explore the tranquil and impressive nature of the Gaoligong Mountains on a hike. The National Park is considered a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve due to its rich diversity of plants and animals. The area is about 2,300 meters. Today you wander in the national park about 7 km and 400 meters through the landscape. The paths are historical trade routes that served for the exchange of goods between Burma and China in the 17th century. Relax in this original atmosphere. The rest of the day is at your disposal. Enjoy the views of the surrounding land and how it is slowly immersed in a mystical fog at sunset. Overnight in Gaoligong.

7th day Beautiful high altitude path in Gaoligong National Park

Today you can lace up your hiking shoes again. You walk over the most beautiful mountain ridge of the countryside. This trekking tour is about 15 km long and you cover about 600 meters of altitude. Enjoy standing on the roof of these mountains and enjoying the great, wide views. Do not forget your camera, there will surely be some unique photo moments. Back in your tent lodge you can spend the rest of the day according to your wishes. Put your legs up and listen to the birds’ singing – enjoy the peace and seclusion. Overnight in Gaoligong.

Day 8 Shangri-La - legendary "City of Bliss"

In the morning you leave this beautiful mountain world again. From Tengchong, take a domestic flight to the city of Shangri-La, located at 3,200 meters. For a long time, no one knew exactly where “Shangri-La” – a legendary Buddhist place of bliss – lies. Since 2002, it has been known: in Zhongdian, the gateway to Tibet, which was renamed Shangri-La. There you will already be expected by your tour guide and warmly welcomed. So you can already, while the chauffeur takes you to the hotel, first facts and anecdotes about this area. The remaining day is at your disposal, so that you can arrive in peace and acclimatize. Overnight in Shangri-La.

Day 9 Visiting Songtseling Monastery & Shangri-La National Park

After breakfast, another highlight of your trip awaits you: Songtseling Monastery. The 300-year-old Tibetan Lama Monastery is one of the 13 famous monasteries in the Tibetan territory of Kham. . The complex, founded by the 5th Dalai Lama, covers an area of 33.3 hectares and is modeled on the style of the Potala Palace in Lhasa – hence it is also known as the “little Potala Palace”. The murals and wood carvings in the monastery are real art treasures. The prayer rooms are used by about 800 monks and are permeated by the scent of thousands of incense sticks – an absolutely spiritual place. After your visit to the temple, you will go out into nature. The landscape of Shangri-La National Park awaits you. Especially enchanting is the Bitahai Lake, which invites to hikes. The lake lies at an altitude of 3,540 meters and is fed exclusively by the meltwater of the surrounding mountain ranges. The peculiarity of the water body is that its surface is covered by a 50 centimeter thick, floating layer of grass. Overnight in Shangri-La.

10th day View into deep gorges: the Tiger Jump Gorge

The next destination on your trip is the city of Lijiang, which is a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Striking in the street scene are decorative men in traditional robes, women in blouses and pale blue aprons. These are the Naxi who have settled in Lijiang for more than 1,000 years. Originally they lived as nomads on the Tibetan plateau. On the way, take a look into one of the deepest gorges on earth. The Tiger Jump Gorge is an impressive 3,900 meters deep and about 16 kilometers long. In some places, however, it measures only 10 meters wide. According to legend, a tiger is said to have skipped the gorge here long ago to escape its hunters. Almost vertically, the cliffs hang threateningly over the raging waters of the Jinshajiang (River of the Golden Sands). Steps struck in the rock lead down to the rushing stream, where you can witness this unique natural spectacle. Overnight in Lijiang.

11th Day Historic Old Town of Lijiang & Detour to the Naxi Villages

Start the day with a detour to Yuhu village, which is one of the Naxi villages in the area. There you can visit the former home of the botanist and linguist Joseph Rock, who lived here in the 30s and 40s. Then explore the beautiful old town of Lijiang. Between the large water wheel at the entrance and the central square, there is busy activity in the restaurants, cafes and shops from morning to late at night. The houses are a feast for the eyes: two-storey wooden skeleton buildings with brick walls and natural foundations with artfully carved door panels and flower-decorated courtyards. In the north of the village you will find the pond of the Black Dragon (Heilong Tan), surrounded by old pavilions, where in windless weather the 13 peaks of the up to 5,600 meters high massif of the Jadedrachenberge and Yufeng Temples are reflected in the clear water. A sight to melt down. Overnight in Lijiang.

Day 12 On the Famous Tea Horse Trade Route

You will continue to Shaxi today. Once upon a time, Shaxi was a busy place, a caravan stop on the tea-horse trade route between Yunnan and Tibet. Salt, tea and horses were exchanged on this route and trade brought prosperity to the region. But when political conditions changed in China in the 1950s, this era came to an end. Trade on the historic route came to a standstill, the place fell into oblivion. It was only at the turn of the last millennium that the jewel was rediscovered. Shaxi is characterized by a special culture. Most of the inhabitants are Bai, a local ethnic minority that has its own customs and rites. You wander along the famous trade route and can imagine how busy it used to be. Afterwards you will hike to the Buddhist Steingrotten in the Steinschatzberg. Overnight in Shaxi.

Day 13: Admire Dali's landmarks

After breakfast, you continued your journey to Dali. The town lies between the long Erhai Lake in the east and the impressive, mostly snow-capped peak chain of the Cangshan Mountains (up to 4,122 meters) in the west. For centuries, the city was a metropolis of a kingdom independent of China that temporarily extended to Sichuan. Dali's landmarks are the three white pagodas of Chong-Sheng-Si Temple, whose highest tower measures 46 meters, counts 16 floors and offers a magnificent picture in clear weather in front of the mostly snow-capped peaks. The temple in the background is a typical example of the traditional architecture of Yunnan province. Afterwards we continue to Xizhou, a typical village of the Bai minority. Together with your guide, you can admire the traditional old houses and stroll over the lively market where there is a lot to buy. From vegetables to local specialties such as wild mushrooms or bee honey, to souvenirs and local delicacies, you will discover a lot. Overnight in Dali.

Day 14 Visiting the village of Bai Nationality & the nunnery

Today you have an exciting day ahead of you. In the morning you will visit the beautifully located nunnery Ji Zhao at the foot of the Cangshan Mountains. It has become known mainly because of the charming nature that surrounds it and the beautifully landscaped gardens. You can also taste the nuns’ vegetarian lunch and experience how they spend their everyday life in the monastery. Don’t be afraid to talk to women. Afterwards you will continue to the village of Zhoucheng, where you will learn about the fascinating culture of the Bai ethnic minority. Afterwards you will be taken to the train station of Dali, from where you will return to your starting point in Kunming. There you will be expected as usual and brought to your hotel. Overnight in Kunming.

Day 15 Farewell to China

Today it is already time to say goodbye to this fascinating country. Surely you have packed your suitcases with many new impressions. You will be driven to the airport, from where you will start your home or onward journey.

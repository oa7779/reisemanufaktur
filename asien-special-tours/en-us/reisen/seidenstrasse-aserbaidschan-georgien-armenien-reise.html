Azerbaijan, Georgia and Armenia Journey - Between Caspian Sea and Caucasus

Azerbaijan, Armenia and Georgia awaits you on this trip from your specialist Asia Special Tours. Ask directly here.

Look forward to a very special journey through three mysterious countries. Travel the still little visited Caucasus region between Europe and Asia and walk between Christianity and Islam. Spectacular landscapes and impressive cultural sites of great historical importance await you. You’ll start in the emerging metropolis of Baku on the Caspian Sea, continue to travel through enchanting Georgia and finish your trip in Yerevan with a view of the majestic mountain Ararat. Enjoy the tasty, local cuisine of the Caucasus and experience for yourself during the warm encounters with the locals why every guest is regarded as "a gift from God".

1st day Arrival to Azerbaijan

Welcome to Azerbaijan! Upon arrival in the capital Baku, you will be expected by your guide. As the chauffeur takes you to the hotel, you can already learn first facts and anecdotes about the city, where modernity and tradition merge completely harmoniously. Overnight in Baku.

2nd Day Tradition & Modern in Baku & Sunset Boat Tour

After breakfast, set off for a city tour together with your guide. This will take you to the old town: “Icheri Sheher” is the name of the walled quarter with its narrow streets, historical caravanserai, mosques and the Schirvan Chess Palace - the last residence of the rulers of Schirvan State. Then visit the Maiden Tower from the 12th century and catch a glimpse of the imposing villas of the oil barons that were built in the years of the oil booms (1870-1914). Afterwards, a special experience awaits you: a boat trip on the Caspian Sea. During the ride, you can admire the famous Flame Towers, the flag square with the big flag of Baku and the Crystal Hall from the water as the sun slowly sinks into the sea. How could you end the day better? Back on land you will be taken to your hotel. Overnight in Baku.

Day 3 Ateshgah “Place of Fire” & Burning Mountain Yanar Dag

Let the day go quietly and enjoy your breakfast. Today you can get an idea of why Azerbaijan is rightly called the "Land of Fire". Devote the morning to exploring the Absheron Peninsula and visiting Ateshgah (Place of Fire) - a Zoroastrian fire temple. In addition, a curious natural phenomenon awaits you: the burning mountain Yanar Dag. The gas flowing from the hills has been burning since time immemorial. Because the deposits are so inexhaustible, they may continue to burn for several centuries. Afterwards, you will continue to Gobustan to visit the rock paintings (UNESCO Cultural Heritage) there. Finally, return to Baku. Overnight in Baku.

Day 4 Mosques & Craft Center in Northwestern Azerbaijan

In the morning, you will leave the metropolis of Baku and drive to the northwest of Azerbaijan to the city of Sheki. Along the way, visit Juma Mosque and Yeddi Gumbez Mausoleum in Shemakhi, as well as Diri Baba Mausoleum in Maraza. You will also visit the small town of Lahij, which was built in the 5th century BC. To this day, it has been able to preserve its importance as a center for trade and crafts. Some pieces from Lahij can even be seen in the Louvre and the Museum of Bern. In the evening you will finally reach Sheki, one of the oldest cities in Azerbaijan. Overnight in Sheki.

5th day Medieval castle complex Gremi & wine tasting in Kvareli

Today you are leaving for the Georgian border. At the Azerbaijani-Georgian border near Lagodechi, say goodbye to your guide and continue with your Georgian guide to the castle complex Gremi. Afterwards you will continue to Kvareli, where you will get authentic insights into the everyday life of the locals on a farm and see how the traditional Georgian bread is baked. Enjoy your tasty lunch with homemade food and freshly baked bread. Afterwards, a tasting with the best branded wines awaits you in the Khareba wine cellar. Well strengthened, continue to Tsinandali, where you will visit a 19th-century princely house. Overnight in Tsinandali.

6th day nunnery Bodbe and picturesque Signagi

In the morning you will drive to the nunnery Bodbe (4.-8.) This is where the Apostle Nino – she Christianized Georgia – is buried. Finally, you will reach the picturesque town of Signagi, which invites for a walk. The whole city is surrounded by a double wall with 28 towers, which was the symbol of the Free City Signagi. The houses are built in the classical-South Italian style with typical Georgian elements – a memorable combination. Then continue on to Tbilisi. Overnight in Tbilisi.

7th Day City Tour in Tbilisi: Cultural Diversity in the Smallest Space

Get to know the cultural diversity of Tbilisi in the smallest space. Your guide will pick you up for your city tour. All the interesting monuments in the old town are close together, so you don't have to walk far. First we go to the Metechi church from the 13th century. Then your guide will show you the equestrian statue of the city founder Wachtang Gorgassali. From here you have a great view of the Mtkwari River, the old town and the surroundings of Tbilisi. Then continue, passing the sulphur baths (if you wish, make a stop here too), to Nariqala Fortress from the 4th century. In addition, on the program: the synagogue, the main church, the Sioni Cathedral with the vine cross of Saint Nino and the oldest church in the city, the Antschischati Church. You will also visit the Treasure Chamber in the Historical Museum and a jewelry workshop where you can marvel at the ancient techniques of goldsmithing. A day full of new impressions is coming to an end. How about a leisurely evening walk through the bustling alleys of the city? Overnight in Tbilisi.

8th day Georgia's religious center Mzcheta & cave town Uplisziche

The time-honored Mzcheta – former capital of Georgia and still today the religious center of the country – is definitely worth a visit. You will visit the Dschwari Church (6th century) and the Swetiz Choveli Cathedral (11th century). century. This masterpiece of Old Georgian architecture owes its name – “life-giving tribe” – to a legend about the body coat of Christ. A resident of Mzcheta brought the shirt of Christ to Mzcheta after the crucifixion. At the place where it was buried, a tree with a trunk sprouted up, donating a healing balm. This is where the church was built. Along the old Silk Road, you will continue to Gori in the Kartli region, where you will visit the cave town of Uplisziche. The city structure with several streets, a theater and various palaces is easy to understand. Then return to Tbilisi. Overnight in Tbilisi.
 

9th Day Monastery Complex Haghpat & Guest at the Molokans

After breakfast, travel to the Armenian border near Sadachlo. There you will cross the border and be welcomed on the Armenian side by your new guide. Another highlight of your trip is waiting for you: the monastery complex of Haghpat (976 AD) is one of the outstanding works of medieval Armenian architecture and a Unesco World Heritage Site. On the way to Dilijan, take a detour to the village of Fioletovo. This is where the original Christians of the country live, who call themselves Molokans. With a resident of the village you are invited to tea, where you can learn a lot about the culture and customs of the people living here. Then you reach Dilijan, one of the famous spa towns of Armenia. The locals say: "If Paradise had mountains, forests and mineral springs, it would be like Dilijan". Due to the natural beauty of Dilijan, it is also called "little Armenian Switzerland". Overnight in Dilijan.

10th day Armenian Crossstone & visit to Lake Sevan

In the morning in Diljian, take a leisurely walking tour of the old part of the city and visit an art studio for wood and ceramics. Then drive to the Parz lake. At the upper reaches of the river Aghstev is the monastery Goshavank from the 12th - 13th. century. It is located in the middle of dense oak forests, surrounded by gorges with small but turbulent mountain rivers. It is known for its particularly interesting Armenian crossstone. Then continue on to Lake Sevan. This huge lake, which is up to 5% of the area of Armenia, lies about 2,000 meters above sea level and is the second highest freshwater lake in the world. In the evening you will arrive at Yerevan, the capital of Armenia, where you will be taken to your central hotel. Overnight in Yerevan.

11th Pure Culture Day in Yerevan

After breakfast, embark on a city tour of one of the oldest cities in the world. Visit the Republic Square, the Government House, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, the Central Post Office and the National Gallery. Near the Republic Square, you will also visit the historical museum and its artifacts dating back to the Stone Age. Finally, visit the memorial dedicated to the victims of the 1915 genocide and the associated museum. After your return to the hotel, you can let the many impressions affect you and end the evening relaxed. Overnight in Yerevan.

12th Day Pagan Temple Garni & Cave Monastery Geghard

Today, your path will take you to the pagan temple Garni (1st century), a picturesque monument from the Hellenistic period and an impressive piece of ancient Armenian architecture. Here you will take an approximately one and a half hour hike through the gorge of Garni to the Symphony of Stones. Lunch is served at a local home in Garni, where you will have the opportunity to watch the baking process of traditional Lavash bread. Not far from the temple stands the cave monastery Geghard, which is one of the most important pilgrimage sites in Armenia. The founder is said to be Saint George, patron of the Armenian Apostolic Church. On the way back to Yerevan, stop at the Armenian Central Archive "Matenadaran". Here are historical documents from Europe and Asia, including the first copy of the Bible. Overnight in Yerevan.

Day 13 Excursion to Echmiadzin & Brandy Tasting

In the morning, your chauffeur will take you to Echmiadzin, about 20 kilometers from Yerevan. The Cathedral of Echmiadzin is known as the center of the Armenian-Gregorian Church. It is considered one of the first Christian churches in the world. Then visit the Temple of Zvartnot, a pearl of 7th-century architecture and UNESCO World Heritage Site. You can enjoy your lunch today in an art school. Finally, return to Yerevan and visit a factory where brandy is produced. Learn more about the interesting ripening process and the secrets of storage. Convince yourself and try the much-praised Armenian brandy! In a relaxed atmosphere, you will end the evening. Overnight in Yerevan.

14th day Monasteries around Yerevan & wine tasting

Another great highlight of your trip is imminent: the monastery choir Wirap. The monastery is a sanctuary of the Armenian Apostolic Church and pilgrimage site, which is closely linked to the spread of Christianity in Armenia. Here is the deep pit where Saint Gregory the Illuminator (first head of the Armenian Apostolic Church) was imprisoned for 13 years. In the background of the monastery rises the Biblical Mount Ararat. Although this extinct volcano is located in Turkey, it is the national symbol of Armenia and adorns the country’s coat of arms. Afterwards you will visit the Momik Wine Cube, where you will immerse yourself in the thousands of years old Armenian winemaking tradition during a wine tasting. You will get to know the characteristic grape varieties of the region and learn about the terroir in the Ararat and Vayots-Dzor area as well as about traditional and modern methods of winemaking. When tasting selected wines, you will discover the typical aroma profiles and peculiarities of this traditional wine region. A shared lunch of regional specialties will round off the culinary experience harmoniously, returning to Yerevan towards evening. Overnight in Yerevan.

15th day Departure from Armenia

Your time out from everyday life is coming to an end. Even if your holiday is soon over, the precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories of your trip will accompany you forever. Look forward to letting your friends and family share your experiences in the distance at home. Your driver will take you to the airport for your return flight.

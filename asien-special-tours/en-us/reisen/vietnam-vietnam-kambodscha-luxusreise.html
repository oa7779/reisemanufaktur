Vietnam and Cambodia - luxury for the soul

Vietnam and Cambodia Travel: Discover Vietnam and Cambodia on a luxurious round trip with Asia Special Tours. Ask now!

Do not dream your life, live your dream. Take a break from everyday life and travel to the most beautiful corners that Vietnam and Cambodia have to offer. Stay in the most luxurious hotels and ships of Indochina: each one on this route is unique and will delight you with exceptional service and exclusivity. You will travel on historical trails and visit important UNESCO World Heritage sites. Dreamlike landscapes, fascinating cities and impressive temples accompany you on this hand-picked round trip. In between, there is enough space for a flexible time division and very private moments. An absolute highlight is the luxurious cruise with the first-class Aqua Mekong, on which hardly any wishes remain unfulfilled. Break your own boundaries and conquer new horizons... and collect moments for eternity.

1st day Arrival in Hanoi & first class hotel Sofitel Legend Metropole

Welcome to Vietnam! Immediately after your landing at Hanoi airport, your all-round carefree package begins. After leaving the plane, you will be received by your guide in the airport building. The entry formalities are now handled by the airport staff for you. Then your personal chauffeur drives you with the limousine to the exclusive Hotel Sofitel Legend Metropole. The “Grande Dame” by Hanoi exudes the charm of long gone times. This elegant French colonial-style house is located in the middle of the historic city center, making it the ideal starting point for your visits to Vietnam’s capital. A luxurious spa with pool as well as several very good restaurants offer relaxation and enjoyment after sightseeing. Your room is also ready for early arrival, so that you can relax after arrival. If you want to stand your feet a little, then walk around Hoan Kiem Lake in the heart of Hanoi. Your personal guide is at your disposal with lots of information. Surrounded by beautiful trees, old colonial buildings and parts of the old town, the lake is a popular residence for Hanoians of all ages. Overnight in Hanoi.

2nd Day Insider City Tour with Christian Oster through Hanoi

After breakfast, Mr. Oster, a journalist and former restaurant owner, picks you up, and Mr. Oster has lived in Hanoi for several years and is well-versed in the city. Together you walk through the historic old town. Meanwhile, learn about politics, religion and the social coexistence of the Vietnamese in their narrow houses. Immerse yourself in a tangle of old, small alleys that is probably unique throughout Asia. Here in the heart of Hanoi you will learn more about the Vietnamese soul. True to the Vietnamese motto, “the best food is on the street,” try a traditional “bun cha” (grilled meatballs in a fish soup with plenty of fresh herbs and salads). Afterwards you will visit the military museum and learn more about the background to the Vietnam War and why the Vietnamese are hardly interested in it anymore. The trail will take you past the Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum. On Ba Dinh Square, Mr. Oster gives you some background information about the cult of personality around “Uncle Ho”, why he has to lie in a glass coffin and why the socialist government currently needs him so badly. In the Temple of Literature, you will learn about Confucius and his influence on Vietnamese society today. Overnight in Hanoi.

3rd day Exclusive cruise on your private junk

In the morning you will be transferred by private transfer to Halong Bay. Arriving in Tuan Chau, board your private cruise. After you have checked in and been greeted by the crew, move into your cabin. While cruising slowly through the bay, enjoy a delicious lunch on board. The on-board staff is entirely up to you. This also applies to the choice of your food and where you want to eat your meals – be it in the dining room, in the cabin or on the deck. Do you want to treat yourself to something very special? Then we are happy to arrange a private Candle Light Dinner in a cave or on a quiet beach for you (optional). Overnight on the ship.

Day 4 On a voyage of discovery through Halong Bay

Feel the magical atmosphere on board your ship early in the morning. With tai chi exercises on the sun deck, you awaken the spirits of life and can start the day completely relaxed. Then enjoy your breakfast in peace. Decide according to your mood which goals you want to head for today and how you want to spend the day. How about visiting one of the numerous grottoes, anchor in a private bay, delve deeper into the mysterious island world by kayak, visit swimming villages, try fishing, treat yourself to a soothing massage or just relax on board and let yourself drift. Your small cruise will be completely adapted to your wishes and ideas. Sit back and enjoy it. Overnight on the ship.

5th Day Morning Tai Chi on Board & Exclusive Luxury Hotel Amanoi

Early in the morning you will experience the absolute silence and clear air of Halong Bay. Let it take a very relaxed approach and use today again the opportunity for a tai chi hour in the middle of karst rocks. During breakfast, you will leisurely sail back to the port. The seaplane will take you back to Hanoi, from where you will fly on to Nha Trang. There you will be expected and brought to your hotel. Immerse yourself in the dream world of Amanoi, located in one of the most beautiful coves in Asia. The luxury Hideaway is located in the middle of Nui Chua National Park on the east coast and can be reached from Cam Ranh. Amanoi means “place of peace” and is the first hotel of the renowned Aman luxury hotels in Vietnam. Enjoy untouched tropical landscapes, rugged coastlines with beautiful rock formations and secluded sandy beaches. The 31 pavilions and villas feature an elegant, modern style combined with traditional Vietnamese elements. Relax at the Zen-inspired Aman Spa and enjoy panoramic views of the magnificent scenery that surrounds you in the Infinitypool. Overnight in Nha Trang.

6.-7. Day free time in Nha Trang

Without tour guide and chauffeur

These days are all yours alone. On a hike, discover the untouched wilderness with countless wild animals and plants or paddle with a kayak into a secluded bay. Take a break and enjoy your precious time. Overnight in Nha Trang.

Day 8: Elegant luxury hotel Park Hyatt & Citytour through the bustling Saigon

Today, leave Nha Trang behind and travel on to Saigon. The city builds a bridge between cultures and epochs: modern high-rises stand side by side with colonial buildings and traditional temples. On the streets, an army of mopeds crackles, and bicycle rickshaws wind between them. Young people use their mobile phones while in the quiet backyards, believers pray amidst clouds of incense. You will be expected at the airport and taken to your hotel. Undisputedly, the exclusive luxury hotel Park Hyatt is one of the top addresses in Saigon. The multi-award-winning hotel restaurants are among the best restaurants in all of Vietnam and make for an unforgettable culinary feast. Your guide will show you the highlights of the city such as the Reunification Palace, the Main Post Office, the neo-Romanesque Notre Dame Cathedral, the Opera House and the City Hall. In Cholon - Saigon's Chinatown, immerse yourself in a bustling and colorful district. Visit the Thien Hau Temple and the bustling Binh Tay Market. In the evening, we invite you to a dinner at the luxurious restaurant “The Deck” on the banks of the Saigon River. Overnight in Saigon.

9th Day Street Food Tour of Saigon & Fascination AO Show at the Opera House

Join Mr. Dittko for a stroll through Saigon’s markets. Mr. Dittko comes from Germany, has lived in Saigon since 1995 and knows the city like the back of his hand. During a culinary discovery tour, he brings you closer to the sophistication and balance of the local cuisine. In Vietnam, each food is assigned a ying or a yang property. Find out in a traditional pharmacy which ingredients and medicinal herbs fit which dish. And find out what culinary influences the Vietnamese have taken over from their “occupiers”. At Tan Dinh Market you will discover the incredible selection of fruits and vegetables, fresh seafood and various kinds of tofu. At the same time, you will learn about the history, politics, religion and life of the people in the megacity . In no other city in the country can you discover and experience the full range of Vietnamese cuisine as well as here. In the evening, visit the wonderful A-O show at Saigon Opera House. This unique art form, in an innovative symbiosis of music, acrobatics and effects, gives an insight into the soul and cultural heritage of the Vietnamese. Overnight in Saigon.

Day 10 Start of the luxury Aqua Mekong cruise

Today your luxurious cruise starts on the Aqua Mekong. Once with the ship sail on the lifeline of Indochina and let the dreamlike backdrops of the Mekong affect you. Between the floating markets of the Mekong Delta in Vietnam and the colonial ruins of Cambodia, the luxury ship Aqua Mekong offers international comfort combined with local charm. On board the luxury ship Aqua Mekong, a feel-good program of the extra class awaits you. Equipped with 20 suites spread over two decks, it offers the highest level of comfort and privacy. As the first luxury ship on the Mekong, it offers a modern fitness center, a library, a small cinema and a spacious observation deck. With an outdoor lounge including plunge pool, the deck invites you to sunbathe and relax. Exotic cocktails are served at the rooftop bar. Soothing massage treatments await you in the luxurious spa. In the spacious lounge and dining area, you will be spoiled by the exquisite cuisine of Star Chef David Thompson. A special highlight are also the ship's own bicycles, which can be rented for shore visits. Overnight on board the Aqua Mekong.

Day 11 on the Majestic Mekong

Enjoy the sunrise on board and fortify yourself with an extensive breakfast. The Mekong with its secondary arms shapes the landscape in the south of Vietnam just as much as the lives of its inhabitants. Palms weigh in the wind, the bananas hang hard on the perennials. Water buffaloes sow on rivers, women row through narrow canals. Many people live on or on the water and make a living from fishing. Others grow rice, fruits and vegetables on land, visiting typical villages along the Mekong and tasting the local fruits that thrive in this area. Do you prefer it more sporty? Join us on a 17-kilometer bike ride, and back on board you’ll find a first-class dinner as the sun slowly disappears on the horizon. Overnight on board the Aqua Mekong.

12th Day Floating Market in Chau Doc & Border Crossing to Cambodia

Today you will visit the floating market of Chau Doc. Incidentally, the goods offered for sale here can be recognized by the specimens that dangle on the boats as an exhibit of high posts. Not only delicacies of every kind, also craftsmanship is offered. Then drive to Sam Mountain, which is also called the sacred mountain. From here you have a magnificent view over the delta. Optionally, you can also take a bicycle tour again or accompany the ship’s cook on a rickshaw ride to the colorful Tan Chau market. Then return to the ship. After lunch, you can relax on the sun deck and really let your soul dangle. Or would you rather take part in a cooking class? Enjoy the amenities of your ship as you cross the Cambodian border. Overnight on board the Aqua Mekong.

Day 13 Khmer Crafts & Visit to a Remote Village

Start the day comfortably and enjoy an extensive breakfast. In the morning you will have the opportunity to observe the locals in their traditional Khmer craftsmanship, the dyeing and weaving of the typical scarf. For a discovery tour past Buddhist temples and stilt houses, bicycles are at your disposal. After lunch, visit the tamarind trees of Preah Prosop with a small dinghy and visit a very remote village that has hardly changed with the passage of time. After this little journey back in time, you will return to the ship where you can end the evening relaxed. Overnight on board the Aqua Mekong.

14th day Visit of Phnom Penh & exclusive luxury hotel Amansara

In the morning, leave the ship and visit the exciting sights of Cambodia's capital with a tuk tuk. Your first stop is the Royal Palace, the last palace built during the French colonial period. The complex houses the Silver Pagoda, named after the silver plates that grace the floor. Then head to the National Museum, which boasts a world-leading collection of artifacts from the 4th to 13th centuries. Then your cruise ends and you fly on to Siem Reap. Siem Reap is an up-and-coming small town – and will be your starting point in the next few days to explore the world-famous Angkor Park with about 40 square kilometers and more than 100 temples from the 9th to 13th centuries. You are already expected at the airport and brought to your hotel. Stay on the outskirts of Siem Reap at the exclusive luxury hotel Amansara. This luxurious boutique hotel used to be the guest house of King Norodom Sihanouk and is located not far from the UNESCO World Heritage Site. Experience for yourself the welcoming atmosphere of this unique hotel and its lush garden.

15th Day Spectacular Temple of Angkor & Visit to an Art Gallery

Today, one of the highlights of your trip awaits you: the world-famous Angkor Wat Temple. Built in the first half of the 12th century, this World Heritage Site is the largest and undoubtedly most important monument of the Angkor Temple Group. The sanctuary is a masterpiece of Khmer architecture: within the vast complex you will find beautifully designed carvings of figures from Hindu mythology as well as almost 2000 Apsaras, so-called “heavenly dancers”. Your guide will be able to tell you a lot of interesting things about the mythological meaning of the ornaments and statues. Then visit the ruined city of Angkor Thom. The well-preserved remains of the city lie in the middle of the dense jungle and are surrounded by an eight-meter-high wall. You can reach the city through the impressive South Gate, a stone gate carved with four gigantic faces and elephants. From here, drive to Bayon Temple, which is right in the middle of the city. A special feature of this monument are the 54 towers representing the 54 provinces of the Great Khmer Empire. In the evening, visit a contemporary art gallery that presents a modern representation of Khmer culture. Overnight in Siem Reap.

16th Day Yoga Class at a Temple & Helicopter Flight Over Angkor

Say goodbye to all stress! In the shadow of a hidden temple, you can switch off in complete silence during a 75-minute yoga session and forget about any everyday stress. They are taught to bring themselves into a state of rest and peace. A yoga teacher will help you open the mind to meditation and solidify the balance of your soul. Take the energy and power of this unique experience with you and enjoy your journey to the fullest. Then a very special experience awaits you. Explore the world’s largest temple from the air. Your flight experience starts at Siem Reap Airport, where you will meet your pilot. He will explain all the details of the excursion and give you a security introduction. Then begins the 15-minute flight over Angkor Wat, its surrounding temples and the floating village on the shores of Tonle Sap Lake! The rest of the day is at your disposal. Read a good book or visit the first-class spa area in your hotel. Overnight in Siem Reap.

17th day Departure from Cambodia

Unfortunately, today ends your unique journey through two of the most fascinating countries in Southeast Asia. Your hotel room is available until your departure. You will be picked up from your hotel and taken to Siem Reap Airport, where you will board your flight home. Even if your holiday is soon over – the precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories of your trip will accompany you forever. Look forward to letting your friends and family share your experiences in the distance at home.

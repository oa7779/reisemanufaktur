India Luxury Tour at Airy Highs in the Noble TUTC Camps

India Luxury Tour in breezy altitudes with overnight stays in the noble TUTC camps in the heart of Ladakh with your expert Asia Special Tours. This is a question.

Silence. Width. Peace. Hardly any part of the world offers a stronger contrast to the restlessness of everyday life than the monumental mountains of the Himalayas. On this trip you will explore the Indian part of the Himalayan Mountains in the Ladakh High Valley, which is already geographically and culturally part of the Tibetan plateau. Ladakh fascinates not only through grandiose landscapes, but also through its lively monastic life, which finds its climax every year in colorful festivals. You can expect breathtaking views of wide valleys and snow-capped peaks, intimate insights into the everyday life of the mountain dwellers and unique experiences that will remain in your memory for a long time, such as a Buddhist morning prayer ceremony at sunrise in the castle-like monastery Tikse. You can also look forward to two very special Ultimate Travelling Camps accommodations, offering the perfect combination of a nomadic lifestyle with all the amenities of a luxury hotel in a picturesque setting. Luxury camps leave nothing to be desired, and your personal butler is there for you around the clock. Your journey begins and ends in India's capital Delhi, whose most beautiful sides you can explore together with your guide.

1st day Arrival in Delhi

Your journey starts in Delhi, where you will be expected and driven to your accommodation upon arrival at the airport. Rest first or relax in your hotel. The Taj Mahal Hotel is located in the heart of the city, making it an ideal starting point for exploring. The magnificent Mogul architecture, the dramatic facade of pink Dholpur sandstone, immaculately maintained gardens and the spacious pool create the atmosphere of a luxurious oasis of peace. Enjoy the fantastic view of the city from your room and let yourself go really well. In the early afternoon, meet your guide to explore the imperial city of New Delhi with its vast avenues and imposing buildings. Because New Delhi also offers fascinating historical sights: for example, with the 72-meter-high Qutb minaret and the mausoleum of the great mogul Humayun, which is considered an architectural precursor to the Taj Mahal. Overnight in Delhi.

2nd day Old Delhi: a journey back in time to the past

After an extensive breakfast in the hotel, you will go on a journey through time in Old Delhi, where the Jama Masjid, one of the largest mosques in the world, testifies to Delhi’s past as the capital of Islamic India. Passing the Red Fort, the palace complex of the Moguls, which owes its name to the outer walls of red sandstone, your ride will take you on to the moonlight square – “Chandni Chowk” – one of the oldest and also busiest market squares in the city. Experience here the typical flair of an oriental bazaar. Even deeper you can immerse yourself in the streets and alleys of Delhi during a rickshaw ride. Afterwards, visiting Bangla Sahib Gurudwara, the city’s famous Sikh temple, you will get insight into the traditions of this religion that originated in India. During your visit to Sikh Temple, you will also visit the communal cuisine where food is prepared and served around the clock for free for thousands of people. Back at the hotel, the rest of the day is at your disposal. Overnight in Delhi. 

Day 3 Luxury in the High Himalayas

After breakfast, you will be taken to the airport to travel on to Leh by domestic flight. Once there, you will be taken to your accommodation in Tikse. Immerse yourself in the tranquility of the area and enjoy a traditional Ladakhi reception. The rest of the day is at your disposal to get used to this altitude. If you wish, you can take a walk through the village of Tikse and get a first impression of the life of the local population. Or you can enjoy the peace and comfort of the luxury camp. Located in the beautiful Leh Valley, Chamba Camp Thiksey is a tribute to Ladakh’s lifestyle. The 11 exquisitely furnished cream-coloured tents exude the charm of unattached nomad life combined with exceptional luxury. Each tent is spacious and has a bedroom, private bathroom and porch area. The large windows allow you to enjoy the beautiful and idyllic mountain world around. The dining tent provides culinary experiences of the extra class. Overnight in Tikse.

Day 4 Excursion to Leh

The morning belongs only to you. Sleep first and enjoy your breakfast in peace. Are you a bird lover? How about an early morning walk through the dreamlike landscape with the naturalist of the camp (optional). With a bit of luck, you will be able to observe a large number of birds in the Trans-Himalayan region. After lunch at the camp, visit Matho Monastery, which is hidden in a side valley. The monastery is famous mainly for its wintry Nagrang festival, during which the oracle Rong Btsan reveals itself in two monks. Then explore together with your guide the most beautiful sides of the city of Leh. Stroll through the winding alleys of the old town at the foot of the old fortress towards the south, where the Neustadt joins. Your walk ends near the village of Chubi, where you will be picked up and driven to the widely visible Shanti Stupa perched on a hill. From up here, enjoy spectacular views of the city before returning to your accommodation. Overnight in Tikse.

Day 5 Insights into Rural Life

After a first cup of tea or coffee, start the day with a visit to Tikse Monastery, arguably the most impressive complex in this part of the Indus Valley. Built around 1440, the Yellow Hat Gompa is a beautiful example of the castle-like monasteries that have shaped Ladakh’s cultural landscape since the 15th century. There you will have the privilege to participate in the Buddhist morning prayer ceremony at sunrise. After a hearty breakfast at the camp, the rest of the morning is freely available. In the afternoon, visit the famous monastery of Hemis, founded in the 17th century. Here young lamas are taught in literature, history, philosophy, yoga and tantra. Alternatively, after an early breakfast, you can take a trip to one of Ladakh’s most scenic passes (optional): Wari La, the world’s fourth highest passable road! After lunch in nature, ride your bike down the route to the green oasis of the village of Shakti, where you will finish your bike ride and return to your camp. Overnight in Tikse.

Day 6 Continue to the Champa Camp Diskit

After breakfast, your journey will take you to Diskit via the Khardung La Pass, which is considered the highest road in the world. From the pass you have a beautiful view into the Nubra Valley. Along the way, stop at the village of Khardung to fortify yourself with a packed lunch for the onward journey that takes you past the famous sand dunes of Nubra. Finally, you reach the village of Diskit and are immediately brought to your camp. Luxury Camp Champa Camp Diskit, which like Chamba Camp Thiksey is one of the exclusive camps of The Ultimate Travelling Camps, lies in the midst of the dramatic glacier landscape of the Nubra Valley at an altitude of 3,100 meters. Facilities include a dining tent with indoor and outdoor seating, a library of board games and an organic vegetable garden. Each of the eight tents has a private porch and is beautifully decorated with canopy beds, wooden chandeliers and nomadic-style furniture. Even the bathrooms do not miss any luxury. Overnight in Diskit.

Day 7: Insights into village life & camel ride through the sand dunes

Today gives you unique insights into traditional village life in the beautiful Nubra Valley. With your guide, you will travel to the picturesque village of Sumur, which lies on the banks of the Nubra River. There you get to know life in the region and are initiated into the special initiative of the Nubra Women’s Alliance. This has set itself the goal of increasing the status of women in the countryside and strengthening local culture and agriculture. A nice drive through the farmland of Sumur will take you to a traditional farm where you can enjoy a hearty lunch in a family atmosphere. There you will get fascinating insights into the rural life in this region and learn about its rich history. In the evening, a special experience awaits you: a ride through the sand dunes on the back of a Bactrian camel. Let the dreams of the merchants, mercenaries and monks who once traversed these dunes in search of gold, glory and God come alive. Finally, return to your camp for the night. Overnight in Diskit.

8th day Diskit according to your taste

(Without tour guide & chauffeur)

This day you can create your own ideas. After a leisurely breakfast, you can hike to your heart’s content, cycle, try your hand at archery or visit other impressive monasteries, of which the region still has many to offer. Or how about a Ladakhi cooking session with one of the camp’s chefs or a picnic in the midst of scenic nature? We also recommend a trip to the village of Turtuk (optional), located on the Shyok River, which is inhabited by the Balti Muslim ethnic group. Many of them have amazingly blue eyes, chestnut hair and rosy cheeks, making them distinctly different from the Mongoloid Ladakhis. Stroll through the picturesque place with its traditional homes and enjoy the delicious apricots grown in the region. You should also visit the last border mosque from the 16th century as well as the Turtuk Gompa from the 11th century. After the picnic lunch, you will finally be returned to your camp. Overnight in Diskit.

9th Day Morning Prayer at Diskit Monastery & Back to Tikse

Early in the morning, drive to Diskit Gompa, perched on a hilltop above the primeval village of Diskit, considered the oldest and largest Buddhist monastery in the Nubra Valley. The prayer room is one of the most beautiful in all of Ladakh and also the view into the valley is breathtaking. There you are invited to start the day with a special prayer near the towering Maitreya Buddha. After breakfast at the camp, make your way back to the Leh Valley via the Wari La Pass. Along the way, you’ll probably see some shepherds grazing their Paschmina goats or the shaggy yaks grazing in the meadows along the streams. Of course, there is also enough time for photo stops. Upon arrival at Chamba Camp Thiksey, the rest of the day is free to relax in your luxurious tent or camp and once again enjoy the spectacular nature with the starry night skies. Overnight in Tikse.

Day 10 return flight to Delhi

Today it is time to say goodbye to the breathtaking and enchanting mountain world of Ladakh. Your driver will take you back to Leh, from where you will fly back to Delhi. After check-in at the hotel, the rest of the day is freely available to you. Relax and enjoy the amenities of your hotel. We are also happy to organize an exploration tour in India's capital for you. Overnight in Delhi.

Day 11 Farewell to India

Now your exciting and impressive journey through India is coming to an end. Many impressions of the fabulous cultural and natural treasures of the Ladakh high valley will bring you to the airport and take your onward or return flight.

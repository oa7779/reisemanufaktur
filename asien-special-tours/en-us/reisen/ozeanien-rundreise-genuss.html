Australia round trip - metropolises, exquisite pleasures and natural spectacle

Australia Round Trip - Explore metropolises, culinary delights and tremendous natural spectacles in Australia with this round trip of Asia Special Tours.

Itinerary & services

Majestic landscapes, endless secluded beaches and a unique flora and fauna await the visitor to Australia. It is the differences that fascinate: The vibrant, colorful life in the metropolises, the endless sandy beaches and typical Australian animals such as koalas, emus, kangaroos and wombats. In addition, there are magical landscapes and unmistakable nature experiences. A large continent awaits you with the rush of rushing waves on majestic, unspoiled beaches and the magical silence of the Outback. A rich, endemic wildlife and exciting natural spectacle will accompany you on your journey. They will record with all their senses the sounds and scents of a continent at the other end of the world.

Day 1 Arrival in Adelaide

You will be received at the airport in Adelaide and taken to the hotel. The rest of the day is at your disposal. Arrive and relax from the long flight journey. We recommend a walk through the center with a visit to the parks, markets or museums. Overnight in Adelaide.

2nd Day Wines and Specialties of McLaren Vale

One of South Australia’s best kept secrets is the fertile McLaren Vale, where grapes, almond plantations and olive groves grow near the sea. In addition to wine, the Mediterranean climate has favored the cultivation of almonds and olives in the plantations. Today you will go on a personal tour with a special wine guide including a ride along the beautiful coast. You will have time to visit the Dridan Fine Arts Gallery, owned by renowned Australian artist David Dridan. Lunch at a well-known McLaren Vale restaurant, where you can enjoy the local cuisine with fresh regional produce. Take part in a private tasting at the Coriole winery, where grapes have been cultivated since 1920 and the first Shiraz was bottled in 1970. Coriole has earned a reputation for producing first-class, full-bodied red wines. Enjoy excellent wines in a beautiful environment overlooking the sea. Other tastings at other well-known family wineries such as d'Arenbergs, Wirra Wirra and Pennys Hill. Overnight as the day before in Adelaide.

3rd day flight to Kangaroo Island with its sea lion colony

In Adelaide, you will be taken to the airport to reach your flight to Kangaroo Island. Your guide will pick you up from the airport. Explore Kangaroo Island's varied landscape from the Cygnet River Valley on the north coast, through the central plateau to the rugged south coast. Walk through the bush with your guide and get to know some of the unique island plants. Enjoy a delicious lunch in a private setting with some of the island’s fresh produce, including native fish, salads and fine wines, before heading on to the south coast. Experience the sea lions up close on a beautiful sandy beach in Seal Bay Conservation Park. Dinner with local products. Overnight at Kangaroo Island.

4th Day Nature Experience at Kangaroo Island

Today, explore Kangaroo Island’s highlights and the lesser-known backtracks of Flinders Chase National Park. See a wide variety of wildlife species such as kangaroos, wallabys, opossums, goannas, koalas, platypuses and many birds, including rare Cape bar geese. Visit a seal colony at Cape du Couedic and admire the amazing rock formations Admirals Arch and The Remarkable Rocks, huge natural granite sculptures sculpted by winds, rain and salt from the Southern Ocean. Our picnic consists of local products and we also enjoy Australian wines. Depending on the time of year, the afternoon may include a visit to Grassdale in the heart of Kelly Hill Conservation Park, the secluded pools of the Rocky River, or a rugged bay on the south coast. Your time on Kangaroo Island comes to an end and the guide takes you to Kingscote Airport. Your privately chartered plane flies directly to Port Lincoln in the sunset. Your guide will pick you up from the airport and take you to your Port Lincoln hotel. The Eyre Peninsula is known as Australia’s Seafood Frontier and in the next 3 days you will experience local produce from the region! Overnight in Port Lincoln.

5th day Seafood of the Eyre Peninsula and cruise offshore

Your guide will pick you up at the hotel, where he will give you an overview of the exciting things that will come! Then you get a first impression of the Eyre Peninsula, where you cruise on board the "Electric Boat Tesla Marina". This is followed by a tempting lunch of local seafood at an award-winning fish restaurant. With a full belly, we drive to the Whalers Way, the oldest rock in South Australia that offers a magnificent view of the rough coastline. Continue to Mikkira Station, home of the Koalas of the Eyre Peninsula. After an eventful day, we will enjoy dinner at Port Lincoln Harbor. Overnight as at the lecture in Port Lincoln.

Day 6: Visiting the Sea Lions and an Oyster Farm

Today, you can have one of Australia’s best interactive wildlife experiences and swim with the playful Australian sea lions. This is a half-day cruise (optional) to the crystal clear waters of Seal Cove off Port Lincoln, where the young sea lions will greet your arrival with great excitement! Swim or snorkel in the company of these inquisitive animals or just watch the drift from board. But if you prefer to stay on dry land, we offer an alternative route that offers the best of the lower Eyre Peninsula including a four-wheel drive tour in the Wanna Sand Dunes. After lunch, we drive to Coffin Bay to watch the oyster harvest. The Coffin Bay Oyster Tour is the only one of its kind in Australia. Here you will learn about the oyster industry and try fresh Pacific oysters fetched directly from the sea. Then we head back to Port Lincoln, where we gather for another good dinner and to enjoy local wines. Overnight as at the lecture in Port Lincoln.

7th day cage diving with the Great White Shark or visiting Coffin Bay National Park

Today you have two activities to choose from. If you are looking for an adventure on the Eyre Peninsula, you can have the unique experience of cage diving with great white sharks. This is a full-day tour and an adrenaline experience you will never forget! If you’d rather stay ashore, we’ll have a full day to visit the pristine Coffin Bay National Park and experience some up-close encounters with nature at Lincoln National Park and Glen Forest Wildlife Park. We will learn about the history and culture of the area by visiting a number of local museums and art galleries and then it’s time to relax with wine and beer tastings at the local vineyards and breweries. At the end of your stay, we will enjoy fresh oysters and local wine on a deserted and unspoiled beach before admiring the magnificent scenery of the Eyre Peninsula for the last time and driving back to the airport. Your transfer back to your accommodation in Adelaide is organized. Overnight Adelaide.

8th day flight from Adelaide to Melbourne

Today we will take you from your hotel to Adelaide Airport, where you will board a domestic flight to Melbourne. In Melbourne, you will be picked up and taken to your hotel in the center. The rest of the day is at your disposal. We recommend a walk through the bustling center of the big city or a visit to the impressive aquarium with its huge shark pool. Overnight in Melbourne.

9th Day City Tour and Excursion to Phillip Island

Today you will experience two attractions in one day. On the city tour you will get to know the highlights of Melbourne in a relaxing and fun way. Your guide will dive into the architecture and bring the history of this city to life. Enjoy a walk through the park, visit the Cathedral (if allowed) and visit Eureka Skydeck 88, a skyscraper with all-round views of the city. The tour to Phillip Island fills the rest of the day. You’ll see koalas up close in their natural environment and arrive at the Penguin Parade Visitor Center early enough to witness the arrival of the dwarf penguins. Overnight in Melbourne.

10th day balloon ride over the Yarra River or Hidden Secrets of Melbourne

If you wish, today you will have the opportunity to visit a very special attraction – a balloon ride through the wine region of the Yarra River. It goes before sunrise to the gates of the city, where the hot air balloon is waiting for you. Through the fresh morning air, you can see from a bird’s eye view the fertile hills and valleys of the Yarra River Valley, one of Australia’s most famous wine regions. Enjoy the breathtaking view. Alternatively, today you can discover the creative scene of the city. In the numerous arcades and hidden alleys of the city center, you will discover with your guide the most innovative shops and restaurants in the Australian metropolis. When it comes to fashion, design, architecture and cuisine, Melbourne, often even more than Sydney, sets the standards in Australia. Enjoy a light lunch on this guided and extraordinary walk through a vibrant big city. Overnight in Melbourne.

Day 11 Excursion to the Great Ocean Road

Today, take a guided excursion to the dramatic cliffs of the Great Ocean Road west of Melbourne and admire unique rock needles separated from the coast in the middle of the sea. In former times, many ships have run aground here. Look for very special animals such as whales, dolphins, echidnas and bandicoots. You’ll have time to stroll along the beaches or have a coffee in the impressive Split Point lighthouse and enjoy the impressive scenery. You’ll stop at Apollo Bay, a quiet coastal town amidst the rolling hillside scenery, and find a selection of cafes and restaurants where you’ll stop for lunch. We offer exclusive access to an ancient eucalyptus forest trail located on the Great Ocean Road EcoLodge where you can meet koalas, kangaroos and native birds. As our guest, you will contribute to the Great Ocean EcoLodge conservation program to protect the native Tiger Quolls, Koalas and unique flora of the region. A special day crowned by a visit to the world-famous rock formation of the Twelve Apostles and Loch Ard Gorge. Overnight in Melbourne.

12th day flight from Melbourne to Sydney

Today we will take you from your hotel to Melbourne Airport, where you will board a domestic flight to Sydney. In Sydney, you will be picked up and taken to your hotel in the center. The rest of the day is at your disposal to experience the world city of Sydney. We recommend a walking tour in the historic district “The Rocks” or a ride on the viewing tower “Sydney Tower” in the heart of the city with a view of the natural harbor. Overnight in Sydney.

Experience Sydney on the 13th day in the luxury classic car from 1964

Your day starts at 9.30 a.m., where you’ll be greeted by your local guide and his buddy Horace – a 1964 vintage car and the most popular luxury car ever made in Australia. Stylishly cruise through Sydney, with the beguiling scent of fine leather in your nose, leaning back into soft lambskin seat covers. Start in the center with a ride around “The Rocks”. Thanks to your guide, you can discover a thriving inner-city scene where Aboriginal murals and contemporary street art decorate the alleys with turn-of-the-century terrace houses. Get ready for the stories of rising home prices, local architecture, Aboriginal lifestyle and culture. No trip to Sydney would be complete without a visit to the beaches. First, we will take you to a cemetery by the sea that offers breathtaking views of the sandstone coast. From there, you’ll head to three of Sydney’s most famous inner-city beaches: Bronte, Tamarama and Bondi. Cruise through the hills of Sydney’s most exclusive suburbs and arrive at your final destination: a secluded beach with panoramic views of the harbor! Overnight in Sydney.

14th day backstage at Sydney Opera House and flight safari to Cottage Point

You have to get up early because today you can feel like a star of the famous Sydney Opera House. You get admission to the exclusive backstage rooms that are otherwise reserved only for the stars. You will be on stage where celebrities such as Dame Joan Sutherland, Blondie, Pavarotti and Australian Idols have performed. Feel the glamour in the wardrobes and hear the events that take place behind the curtain. A breakfast in the Green Room, reserved for artists and employees, is included. With a seaplane, we will take you from downtown Sydney to a quiet bay at the excellent Cottage Point Inn in 20 minutes. This fine, small restaurant is located right on the waterfront in Ku-Ring-Gai Chasing National Park and is known for its exceptional cuisine using Australian specialties. Enjoy a relaxing 3-course lunch before boarding the seaplane again and taking your flight back to Sydney Harbour. Along the way, you will have a unique opportunity to take great photos of the Harbour Bridge and the Opera House to finally land again in Rose Bay. Overnight in Sydney.

15th day excursion to the Blue Mountains

Today, head west to Featherdale Wildlife Park, where you’ll get close to the Australian wildlife. Today’s destination is the Blue Mountains, a more than 1000 m high sandstone plateau with gorges and waterfalls. The name comes from the bluish haze that typically lies over the eucalyptus forests of the national park. At the Wentworth Falls, explore the Princess Lookout with fantastic views. At Echo Point with the world-famous rock formation “The Three Sisters”, visit the Waradah Aboriginal Centre, a cultural center of indigenous tribes. Afterwards we head to the Govetts Leap viewpoint and enjoy lunch in a historic inn. The journey continues to Mt. Banks and the Mt. Tomah Botanical Garden. After crossing the Hawkesbury River and a stop in the Windsor, we return to Sydney. Overnight in Sydney.

16th Day Sydney Pure

Spend today in Sydney at your free disposal. Go shopping for yourself and your loved ones at home or see one of the many museums, visit the Sydney Aquarium or drive up the Sydney Tower. Take the ferry from Circular Quay to Manly Beach or visit the famous Bondi Beach. Sydney has more than enough to offer to spend a fantastic day. Overnight in Sydney.

17th day return flight from Sydney

Today the program ends. We will pick you up from the hotel and take you to the airport. Farewell and return or flight.

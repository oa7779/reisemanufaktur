India Travel - Temple Wonderland Karnataka with time out on the Andamans

India Travel: Explore the numerous temples in Karnataka and enjoy a break in the Andamanes with Asia Special Tours. We are happy to advise you.

Itinerary & services

English itinerary text is an offline machine translation of the German original for this team preview. Please confirm travel details with your advisor; the German source remains available through the language switch.

Enjoy a very special journey through regions of India that are less well known for tourism but rich in cultural and natural treasures. Your journey begins in Hyderabad, sometimes called “Cyberabad” because of its booming software industry. Together with an expert, you will walk in the footsteps of the ruling family Nizam, which has had a lasting impact on the history of the city. The further course takes you to small, but for the history of India extremely important places. Admire mighty forts, enchanting temples, and impressive cave temples with masterfully crafted sculptures that rank among the best in India. Of course, you should not miss the scenic beauty of South India. Let your views in Chikkmagaluru wander over the coffee plantations and the gentle hillside landscape and go on safari in Nagarhole National Park near Kabini, known for its biodiversity. At the end of your trip, you can relax to your heart’s content on the dreamlike beaches of the Andamanes. Thanks to its idyllic location and the 50 modern luxury villas on stilts under a canopy of coconut and palmyra trees, the beautiful resort Taj Exotica Andamans offers the perfect conditions to let the soul relax.

1st day Arrival & tour in the footsteps of the great rulers

Namaste and welcome to India! Your journey begins in Hyderabad, where you will be expected and driven to your accommodation upon arrival at the airport. Rest first or relax in your beautiful luxury hotel. The city of Hyderabad with about six million inhabitants is nestled in a beautiful landscape and was founded only at the end of the 16th century by Muhammad Quli Qutb Shah. Named after its queen, Hyder Mahal, the modern city is sometimes called “Cyberabad” because of its booming software industry. In the afternoon, explore the city with an English-speaking expert in the footsteps of the Islamic ruling family Nizam. It is impossible to visit Hyderabad without experiencing the splendor of this dynasty that fascinated the whole world. Learn interesting things about their magnificence and sophistication and listen to stories about the eccentricities of the individual rulers of this house. During your tour, you will see what the family has achieved during their reign and hear how they have indelibly shaped this city. With many exciting impressions, you will finally return to your hotel. Overnight in Hyderabad.

2nd day palaces, temples and the old fortified city of Golkonda

Together with your guide, you will dedicate yourself today to exploring the most beautiful sides of Hyderabad. Your first destination is Charminar, the center of the city. Built by the city’s founder, this four-tower triumphal arch commemorates the end of the plague in 1593 and represents the distinctive Qutb Shahi style, offering a good view of the city. At the Mecca Mosque, 800 workers have built a whole 77 years. It is one of the oldest and largest mosques in the whole country. The Qutb Shahi Tombs characterize the development of a modern architectural style composed of a mixture of Hindu, Pathan and Persian architecture. You will also visit the Chowmohalla Royal Temple Complex, known for its unique style and elegance. The proverbial highlight of the day is the fortified and ruined city of Golkonda, which was the capital of the Sultanate of the same name from the sixteenth to seventeenth centuries. Located 11 kilometers west of Hyderabad on a 120-meter-high granite hill, it is particularly known for its extraordinary acoustics: a sound at one of the gates can be heard up to a kilometer away at the highest point of the fortress. Overnight in Hyderabad.

Day 3: Bidar to Gulbarga

After breakfast, leave Hyderabad and make your way to Gulbarga. Along the way, stop at Bidar nearly 1,000 meters high and visit the Bidar Fort built on the edge of the plateau, which reflects the rich and eventful history of the place. Here you will get a deep insight into the historical background of Bidar and the changing ways of life of its inhabitants over time. The walls, bastions, gates and watchtowers of the fortress are well preserved and are among the most stylish in all India. Bidar also looks back on a long connection with Sikhism. You will also visit the Guru Nanak Jhira Sahib Shrine, built in 1949 and dedicated to the first Sikh Guru, Guru Nanak. After the Golden Temple in Amritsar, Guru Nanak Jhira Sahib Shrine is the most important sanctuary of the Sikhs. After arriving in Gulbarga and checking in at the hotel, you will immediately set out to visit one of the most beautiful examples of ancient Islamic architecture in Karnataka with the Gulbarga Fort. The Jama Masjid in the Persian style within the fortress is also absolutely worth seeing. The construction of the mosque is considered unique in India, as the courtyard has been completely covered with elegant domes. Overnight in Gulbarga.

Day 4 Exceptional Architectural Creativity in Bijapur

A new day, a new goal. Your journey will take you to the historic city of Bijapur, founded between the 10th and 11th centuries. The city was also once called Vijayapura or the “city of victory” and experienced exceptional architectural creativity under the Adil Shahi dynasty. Here you will find a very special example of Islamic architecture in India: The Mausoleum Gol Gumbaz. The size of its dome is surpassed worldwide only by St. Peter's Basilica in Rome. The massive vault is not supported by pillars and is designed so that even the slightest whisper can be heard at a long distance. From the gallery around the dome you have a fantastic view over the city. Then visit Ibrahim Rauza, a beautiful tomb built by Adil Shah II for his Queen Taj Sultana. It is known for its extremely decorative carvings and even more beautiful than the Sultan’s Tomb. Another highlight is the Jama Mosque of Bijapur, the largest and oldest mosque in Dekkan. Adil Shah I began construction of the huge house of worship after his triumphant victory over the empire of Vijayanagar. However, it was never completed. The most beautiful features of the mosque are its highly proportional dome and the elaborately painted prayer niche. Overnight in Bijapur.

Day 5 The Four Cave Temples of Badami

Continue to the north of Katarnaka, to the old capital Badami. Badami lies on the shore of the artificially created Lake Agastya at the foot of a red sandstone rock. In the midst of this spectacular natural backdrop, the city impresses in particular with its monumental stone and cave temples, richly decorated with carved Hindu deities. Upon arrival and check-in at the hotel, spend the afternoon visiting the four cave temples, the largest and most beautiful of which is dedicated to Vishnu. In the caves there are rectangular halls with sanctuaries in the rear part. The sculptures on ceilings and pillars as well as the sculptures of the individual deities are among the best in India. Overnight in Badami.

Day 6 Testimonies from Seven Centuries

On the way to the next stop of your journey, stop at Aihole, which has only about 2,500 inhabitants but is considered the cradle of Indian temple architecture. The small town is home to over 100 ornately crafted and detailed Hindu and Jain temples dating back seven centuries, of which the oldest – Ladhkhan Temple – dates back to the 5th century. In the early 6th century, the capital of the Chalukya Empire was located here, but it was moved to Badami a few decades later. The religious significance of the place was preserved, so that new temples were built here until the 12th century. The sheer abundance of temple buildings is overwhelming! Pattadakal, a place 13 kilometers from Aihole, also houses a historic temple complex. The richly decorated sanctuaries from the 7th and 8th centuries have been a World Heritage Site since 1987. After the visit, continue your journey to Hampi. Overnight in Hampi.

Day 7 Hampi - City of Temples

The city of Hampi counts as one of the most fascinating ruined cities in the world a UNESCO World Heritage Site. However, the wild beauty of the granite landscape around is almost as impressive as the variety of ruins. From the 14th to 16th centuries, Hampi was the capital of the powerful Vijayanagar kingdom. In 1564, the empire lost a decisive battle: much of the sprawling capital was destroyed, most of the tens of thousands of inhabitants killed or expelled. Many of the temple buildings in this world heritage site are still preserved today and tell in an impressive way about the splendor of the past. Together with your guide, explore the ruins of the once flourishing city of Hampi. You will visit the famous Vittala Temple with its stone chariot and Lakshmi Narasimha – a huge statue of the goddess Narasimha and one of the most beautiful examples of sculpture in Hampi. You’ll also see the stables that once housed the royal elephants and the King’s Scale – a tall stone building where rulers could outweigh gold and jewels on holidays. These were then distributed among brahmins and poor. Overnight in Hampi.

Day 8 Chikkmagaluru: hills and coffee plantations as far as the eye can see

Today you will make your way to Chikkmagaluru. Chikkmagaluru is called "the city of the youngest daughter", as it was given to the youngest daughter of Rukmangada as a dowry. The small town is an important center for education and commerce and popularly known as the coffee country of Katarnaka. Among the most important attractions, besides the Hoysala-style Kodandarama Temple, are some mosques, a water castle and the Roman Catholic Church of St. Joseph. Together with your guide, you will visit the highest peak in Katarnaka with an altitude of 1,930 meters above sea level: Mullayanagiri in the afternoon. The summit is known for its peaceful ambience and the rugged beauty of nature all around. Let the glances wander and enjoy the fantastic view. From up here you can also see some coffee plantations that are very close by. To learn more about coffee cultivation in the region, visit the Coffee Museum. Here, from picking the beans to pulverizing, you can observe all the processes of making the “black gold”. Overnight in Chikkmagaluru.

Day 9 Excursion to Belur and Halebid

Today awaits you a further exciting excursion into the past: With the immense abundance of relief friezes, monumental sculptures and filigree decor, the temple buildings of Belur are among the most artistically important in India. Especially the Chenna Kesava Temple, built in the 12th century, is magnificently decorated with figures of people, animals and deities that seem almost alive! Afterwards, visit Halebid. Halebid is now only a small village, but it was once the capital of the Hoysala dynasty. At the beginning of the 14th century began the descent of the once so glittering city. But even today, fascinating sanctuaries such as the Hoysaleshwara and Kedareshwara temples testify to the eventful past. Halebid’s monuments are among the masterpieces of Hindu art. In the evening, return to Chikkmangaluru. Overnight in Chikkmagaluru.

10th day Evolve Back Kuruba Safari Lodge – Oasis in the midst of picturesque nature

Today your journey will take you to Kabini, which is best known for the beautiful Nagarhole National Park. The park is home to a variety of animal species, including about 250 different species of birds. After arriving at the hotel, you can arrange the rest of the day according to your expectations. Enjoy the amenities of your picturesque resort, situated in the middle of the varied landscape, and get to know luxury with a traditional imprint. The design of the resort is inspired by the villages of the surrounding tribes and also shows the 37 guest houses the successful combination of traditional craftsmanship and modern amenities. From the popular or tribal decorated furniture to small things, such as the lampshades made of bottle squashes, the complete furnishings blend into a harmonious whole. A delightful reading lounge, a traditional Ayurveda spa and the Infinity Pool are designed to relax and regenerate your body, soul and mind. Overnight in Kabini.

Day 11 on Safari in the National Park

After breakfast, you will have the opportunity to explore the area on a boat safari (together with other guests) on the Kabini River from the water. With a little luck, you can observe Asian elephants, different bird species or swamp crocodiles. Always keep your camera handy. After lunch at the resort, enjoy an exciting jeep safari (together with other guests) in Nagarhole National Park. Nestled in the Nilgiri Mountains, the park, also known as Rajiv Gandhi National Park, covers about 640 square kilometers. Together with two other parks, it forms the largest protected area complex in southern India. Between the bush and rainforest areas, in addition to axish deer, monkeys, Sambar deer and four-horn antelopes, tigers, leopards, lip bears and Asian wild dogs have their home here. However, the park is particularly famous for its considerable elephant population, which gather during the dry season especially on the reservoirs. Overnight in Kabini.

Day 12 Mysore – a city with many facets

After a last breakfast with river views, we go back to civilization. Your next destination is the city of Mysore, which brings together a variety of attractions in a confined space: prehistoric sites, monuments, museums, castles, temples, mosques and churches. The cityscape is characterized by magnificent palaces, majestic buildings, extensive gardens and boulevards. This is where shimmering silk, scented sandalwood and masterful crafts are made. In the afternoon, join a local English-speaking expert for a walk in the heart of what is one of India’s most pedestrian-friendly cities. See testimonies of British colonial rule, learn interesting about the influences of the Maharajas on the development of the city and experience the colorful activity in the markets and bustling alleys of the old town. This tour is an excellent introduction to the special features of Mysore and helps you connect various events of world affairs with the city history. Overnight in Mysore.

13th day Mysore and the surrounding area to explore by bicycle

Start the day with a relaxed bike tour of the city. Together with your local English-speaking guide, take a look into the life of different communities and enjoy a cup of chai with the locals. Your tour will take you past beautiful colonial buildings and give you an opportunity to explore the city at your own pace. With a traditional South Indian breakfast, you will finally end your tour. Well strengthened, then take a trip to Somnathpur, 33 kilometers east of Mysore, where you can visit a fantastic example of Hoysala art. The Keshava Temple dedicated to Vishnu was built in the 13th century by Somantha, a Hoysala commander, and has three shrines whose halls and inner sanctuaries are covered with intricate and very detailed carvings, friezes and stonework depicting gods, goddesses, animals and scenes from various epics. Back in Mysore, of course, you must not miss the Palace of Mysore – one of the largest castles in the country. The building was designed by an English architect and completed in 1912. Overnight in Mysore.

Day 14 - Srirangapatnam to Bengaluru

On your way to Bengaluru, visit Srirangapatnam, the capital of Tipu Sultan. The small town played an important role in the region since its foundation in the 10th century and was the central point in the political development of South India. The fort was built in 1454 under the Vijayanagar kings, 150 years later the last Vijayanagar king gave power to the Hindu Wodeyars of Mysore who made it their capital. In the second half of the 18th century, it became the capital of Hyder Ali, who defended it against the Marathas. Since ancient times, Srirangapatnam was also a sacred place of Vishnu, to which the Ranganatha Temple is dedicated. In the summer palace decorated with ornate arches, you can admire murals depicting Tipu Sultan, his father Hyder Ali and episodes from their lives. The tomb of Hyder Ali and the surrounding gardens are also worth noting. Finally, you will reach Bengaluru, where you will be taken to your hotel immediately. Bengaluru combines contrasts: tradition and modernity, nature and technology. Because of its numerous parks, it got the nickname “Gartenstadt”. However, the place with the tranquil parks is also the most important center of the IT industry in India. Overnight in Bengaluru.

15th day to the island paradise

After breakfast, fly to Port Blair. After the entry formalities, take the ferry to Havelock Island. The island is located about 57 kilometers from Port Blair and is known for its beautiful beaches. Upon arrival, you will be taken to your hotel. The afternoon is at your disposal. The next five nights will be spent at the luxury hotel Taj Exotica Resort & Spa Andamans. Surrounded by a strip of mangroves and a river overlooking the beach, the resort is a tribute to the remote Andaman and Nicobar Islands, which range 700 kilometers from the southern tip of Burma to the north of Sumatra. The resort was designed to showcase and maintain the island’s natural resources. With its 50 modern luxury villas on stilts under a canopy of coconut and palmyra trees, it is a timeless haven of peace and relaxation. The design of the almost 150 square meter residences in sustainable plantation wood is inspired by the houses of the Jarawa and offers all the comforts you would want from a luxury hotel. Wi-Fi Internet in the lobby connects with the outside world, while the unobtrusive service creates a customized atmosphere for privacy and romance. Overnight at Havelock Island.

16-20. Pure Day Relaxation at Havelock Island

Without tour guide & chauffeur

These days belong only to you. Make the time according to your personal preferences and let it go really well! Listen to the singing of birds, treat yourself to an extensive massage, or relax at the huge infinity pool. A beautiful water lily pond invites you to stay. Do you have a sense of variety? Then try your hand at yoga, charter a boat or take advantage of the numerous water sports opportunities. Want to see more of the surroundings? Wonderful: The area is very easy to explore if you rent a bike from the hotel or paddle through the mangroves by kayak. In the three restaurants you can immerse yourself in sensational culinary experiences or indulge in beauty and wellness treatments in the spa area. If you would like, we are also happy to organize exciting excursions for you, such as a trip to the elephant beach with snorkeling or a dive. Overnight at Havelock Island.

Day 21 Farewell to Paradise

Even the most beautiful journey comes to an end. Immerse yourself once again through the delicious breakfast buffet of your resort and enjoy the view of the beautiful surroundings. Then we say goodbye to the island paradise. The ferry will first take you back to Port Blair, from where you will return by plane to Bengaluru. There you will go home after 21 days with many unique experiences and colorful impressions of a very special country.

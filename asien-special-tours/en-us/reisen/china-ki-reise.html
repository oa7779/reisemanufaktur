China’s metropolises vibrate at the beat of the future. Between drone taxis and learning algorithms, new forms of economic activity and decision-making are emerging. All this connected by one force: Artificial intelligence. On this curated AI expert tour of China, you will experience what AI really means: as a tool for entrepreneurial efficiency and as a catalyst for innovation and societal change. Accompanied by an accomplished, German-speaking AI expert, you’ll meet thought leaders and visit innovative tech companies. At the same time, there is room for the quiet moments – for example when visiting the water village of Zhujiajiao or during a night boat trip in front of Shanghai’s sea of lights. On this group trip with like-minded people, new approaches and partnerships are created.

1st day Starting into the future

In Shanghai, you will gain in-depth insights into the research centers of major technology and AI companies. Guangzhou offers you fascinating perspectives on urban air mobility, modern skyline architecture and the historic heart of South China – from visiting EHang to evening shipping on the Pearl River. Shenzhen, once a small fishing town, today a world-class innovation hub, opens the doors to start-ups, tech giants and the vibrant old town. The finale will take you to Hong Kong, a world metropolis that artfully combines East Asian business dynamics and British history.

2nd Day City of Contrasts - Multifaceted Shanghai

After arriving in Shanghai, the transfer to the city takes place with the Transrapid. Although the technology originated in Germany, the project impressively shows how China has further developed this technology and implemented it on a large scale – a first encounter with China’s ambitions in modern infrastructure. Afterwards check-in at the hotel and time to rest. In the evening, an atmospheric Huangpu river cruise on an exclusively chartered boat is on the program. As the lights of the skyline pass by, enjoy a welcome dinner on board. A successful start to get to know each other in a relaxed atmosphere and attune to the coming days, which are dominated by exchange and insights into China’s developments in the field of artificial intelligence. Overnight in Shanghai.

 

Day 3 Shanghai: Deep Learning & Future Labs

Today, immerse yourself in the center of Chinese AI research. First, visit SenseTime, one of the world’s leading companies in Artificial Intelligence. You’ll gain insights into deep learning, computer vision, multimodal models and experience the largest AI data center in Asia firsthand. Then we move on to the Shanghai Foundation Model Innovation Center, one of the largest AI research centers in China. The center was established to drive the development of so-called foundation models – large voice and multimodal models that can combine text, image, video and audio. Here, universities, start-ups and technology companies are working on applications for industry, medicine, urban planning and finance. The rest of the day is at your disposal – for example, for a walk through the old town with its alleys and tea houses. In the evening, enjoy a stylish dinner in a revolving restaurant with panoramic views of Shanghai’s imposing skyline. Overnight in Shanghai.

 

4th day Apollo self-driving and car showroom

The day begins with a visit to Baidu, Inc., one of China’s largest technology companies, known as the leading search engine and pioneer in artificial intelligence. The Apollo platform develops solutions for autonomous driving and operates test fleets in several Chinese cities. Technologies such as software control, sensor integration and applications for robotaxis will be presented. Then take a look at a car showroom of a premium EV brand, such as Zeekr. Zeekr is a premium electric brand of the Geely Group, founded in 2021 and specializing in the upscale segment. In modern showrooms, often in shopping malls or business complexes, models such as the Zeekr 001 or the Zeekr 009 are presented. You will gain insights into technology, design and digital equipment. In the afternoon, visit the water village of Zhujiajiao. The historic village on the outskirts of Shanghai is known for its ancient canals, stone arch bridges and well-preserved traditional architecture. Overnight in Shanghai.

 

5th Day Guangzhou – Future of Urban Air Mobility

Flight to Guangzhou. Here you will experience one of the most exciting developments in the field of new transport technologies and get fascinating insights into the mobility of the future. Visiting EHang, currently the only company in the world with commercially operating drone taxis, you will learn about innovative concepts of autonomous aircraft. On site, you will gain exciting insights into technology, security architecture and the practical implementation of urban air mobility – a significant step towards tomorrow’s cities. In the evening, an atmospheric cruise on the famous Pearl River awaits you, where the modern skyline of Guangzhou can be experienced impressively from the water. Overnight at the White Swan Hotel. This traditional luxury hotel is one of the most famous addresses in Guangzhou and convinces with its excellent location directly on the Pearl River on the historic Shamian Island. Elegant rooms, first-class service and the impressive view of the river make the stay particularly pleasant.

Day 6 Guangzhou – Skyline, History & Mobility of the Future

The day begins with the driveway to the impressive Guangzhou Tower, one of the most striking landmarks in the city. From here a fascinating panoramic view over the modern metropolis of millions and the pearl river opens up. Afterwards you will discover the historic colonial districts as well as the traditional Chinese old town, where the old and new Guangzhou meet in a particularly impressive way. Afterwards, the journey to Shenzhen (approx.) 130 km. With a test drive on the innovative BYD SkyRail mono rail, you can already experience an exciting insight into the urban mobility of the future. Overnight at Aden Hotel Shenzhen Nanshan. This high-quality 5-star hotel is located in the modern district of Nanshan, one of Shenzhen’s most important technology and innovation centers. The excellent location, stylish rooms and an upscale international standard make it an ideal address for this professional tour.

Day 7 Shenzhen: Smart City, Robotics & Electronics Paradise

You will gain insight into the work of Intellif, a provider of technological solutions in the field of artificial intelligence and smart city applications. The company develops systems for smart cities, including facial recognition, data analytics and digital infrastructure. Then explore the trendy district of Nantou Old City, a historic district where traditional architecture meets modern creative projects. The combination of old town structures and new urban culture makes Nantou a vivid example of Shenzhen’s dynamic urban development. Finally, immerse yourself in the bustle of the Huaqiangbei electronics market, one of the largest electronics markets in the world. Huge malls offer an enormous variety of electronic components, devices and technical innovations.

 

8th day tech giant Hong Kong–Zhuhai–Macau Bridge

A boat tour will take you to the Hong Kong–Zhuhai–Macau Bridge, the longest sea crossing in the world with a total length of about 55 kilometers. The link between Hong Kong, Zhuhai and Macau consists of several bridge sections, a 6.7 km long submarine tunnel and artificially created islands – an impressive engineering feat. The rest of the time is free. If it is timely, there are numerous possibilities in Hong Kong: a walk along the Tsim Sha Tsui promenade overlooking the skyline, the Victoria Peak for panoramic views, a visit to the markets in Mong Kok or a detour to the West Kowloon Cultural District with its modern museums. In the evening, the cross-border transfer to Hong Kong Airport (HKG) will take place for the return flight or your onward journey.

 

Day 9 Homecoming & Outlook: Inspirations for the Future

This morning in Frankfurt – or at your respective home airport – you will not only land new contacts, but above all deep insights into China’s dynamic AI industry. Let the experience work: Which ideas have moved you particularly? Where are new opportunities for your company? How can AI technologies enrich your everyday working life – or perhaps even change your private life? This journey opens up new perspectives – for your entrepreneurship as well as for your view of the future.

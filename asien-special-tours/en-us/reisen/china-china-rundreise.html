Day 1: Arrival in Beijing

Welcome to China! Your journey begins in Beijing, the exciting capital of the “Middle Kingdom”. At the airport, your personal guide is already waiting for you and driving you to your hotel. The rest of the day is at your disposal. As a political and cultural center of the country, Beijing can look back on a 3000-year history, which is reflected in a globally unique cultural heritage. Thanks to the 2008 Olympic Games, China’s capital, often dismissed as a giant village, has transformed into a modern world city. Just as the emperors once built their city as a reflection of the universe, China today has created a capital for itself as a reflection of a new, great country. Take the time to take a look around. Overnight in Beijing.

2nd day Imperial magnificent buildings & nostalgic treasures

A day full of cultural treasures and remarkable art awaits you. The Temple of Heaven, to which you will first pay your visit, is one of the outstanding testimonies of religious architecture in the Middle Kingdom, from the Ming period (1368 to 1644). An exquisite example of China’s wooden buildings constructed without the help of a single nail is the 39-meter-high round hall of harvest victims. Its three-tiered roof, whose deep blue bricks symbolize the color of the sky, rests on 28 pillars, the four most powerful of which stand in the middle for the four seasons. On your tour you will also discover the center of Beijing, the famous “Forbidden City” with the Imperial Palace. This magical place was the seat of the Chinese emperors of two dynasties – the Ming and the Qing – and for half a millennium was inaccessible to those who did not belong to the court. Dedicate the afternoon to a diverse variety of works of art and antiques, as the Panjiayuan flea market awaits you. This is the largest flea market in China, which has a fabulous 3,000 stalls. Discover the unstoppable offer of nostalgic collectibles, fancy crafts and perhaps even one or two souvenirs. Overnight in Beijing.

3rd day Monastery life & impressive architecture

Beijing has many more treasures for you. Today, with your guide, you will take a trip to Tanzhe Monastery, which is idyllic embedded in the mountains west of Beijing. The monastery, which is around 1,700 years old, is the oldest temple complex in the region around Beijing and much older than the capital Beijing itself. For many emperors, the first task after their enthronement was to pray to Buddha in the Tanzhe Monastery. Due to its location outside Beijing, however, the monastery remains hidden to most tourists. Not so to you. Stroll through the extensive temple complex and feel the special atmosphere of this place. To experience unique architecture, head to the only Imperial Garden still preserved today: the Summer Palace on the West Mountains near Beijing. In it, the perfect dream of Chinese garden architecture is realized: an ensemble of palaces and temples, pavilions and pagodas with a total of 3,000 rooms on 290 hectares. One feels how the Chinese emperors and their court lived. Overnight in Beijing.

Day 4 Beijing Highlight: The Great Wall is waiting for you!

This morning you will first visit one of the churches based in Beijing. Then one of the biggest highlights of your trip to China awaits you: the Great Wall at the Mutianyu section. To this day, many legends revolve around the spectacular building. It is often claimed that the wall is the only human edifice visible to the naked eye from the moon. The foreigner enjoys the excursion here like a journey through time: from the modern capital beyond the outskirts to the rough mountainous area, where an army of workers once walled an empire. Would you like to enjoy the unique atmosphere of the Great Wall also at sunset and rising? When everything is immersed in warm shades of red and the tourist flows are still far away? Then an overnight stay at the eco-retreat “The Brickyard” is just right for you. From each of the rooms you can enjoy a magnificent view of the Great Wall. Since you are not going back to Beijing today, you have enough time for a hike along the wall. So you experience the Great Wall much more intensively than most other China travelers. Overnight in Mutianyu.

5th Day Continue to Xi’an & Stroll through the Oriental Night Market

You can make the morning as you wish. Sleep in, enjoy your breakfast in peace or take another walk on the wall and watch the sunrise over this dreamlike backdrop. At noon, you will be picked up from the hotel and taken to the station for your train ride to Xi’an. The former capital of eleven dynasties was once the cultural, economic and political center of the Middle Kingdom. Today it is the seat of government of Shanxi Province and the largest city in northwest China. Upon arrival, you will already be expected and received. There is still enough time for a walk through the Muslim quarter with the Oriental night market. The colorful, loud whirlwind is reminiscent of the bazaars of the East. Let yourself be drawn into the busy activity and try a variety of fancy food at the stands. Overnight in Xi’an.

Day 6 - The Emperor's clay warriors

Immediately in the morning, the trip goes to Lintong, about 35 kilometers east. There are the famous terracotta warriors in line. Discovered by chance in 1974, the gigantic tomb at the foot of Li Mountain was built by the first emperor Qin Shihuangdi. So far, archaeologists have excavated around 7,200 figures of soldiers on foot and horseback. Remarkable: All these earthen warriors are individually designed, no two are alike – an artistic masterpiece! It represents a complete army of that time. Since the troops have been deployed in battle order, their localization can be approximately reconstructed. Therefore, it is certain that there are still other sculptures on the huge site, which has only been exposed to about a quarter so far. Back in Xi’an, take a small bike ride on the city wall – the best preserved in all of China – which forms a rectangle around the city center about 14 kilometers long. The Ming Dynasty city fortification is still an impressive sight today, but modest compared to the Tang era. At that time, the wall enclosed 78 square kilometers of the city called Chang'an at that time. Overnight in Xi'an.

7th day Painting Panorama – high up Xiang Gong Shan

In the morning you will be driven to the airport, from where you will take your onward flight to Guilin. Once there, you will already be welcomed by your personal guide. Together you set off for a car ride to the panoramic mountain Xiang Gong Shan before continuing on to Yangshuo. After a short hike on the mountain, you will of course be rewarded with a magnificent view from the Karstbergen and the River Li-. Now it becomes clear why everyone is so enthusiastic about the area around Guilin: The road leads through fields and opens up a panorama of staggered mountain rows. Loud ridges, as far as one can see, each individual enveloped by a delicate cloud veil. It is hard to find the right words for this scenery. Best of all: just be amazed! Overnight in Yangshuo.

8th day By bicycle and bamboo raft through dreamlike landscapes

Your day begins actively! In the morning, explore the picturesque landscape on a guided bicycle tour. Sit back comfortably on the subsequent bamboo raft ride on the Yulong River, a quiet side arm of the Li River. They are still in a dream setting. Seen from certain angles, the hills form shapes reminiscent of animals, pagodas or plants. This inspired the local population to give the mountains imaginative names such as Mondsichelberg, Elephant Risselberg or Horse Painting Mountain. This unique landscape will surely delight you! Overnight in Yangshuo.

Day 9 Yangshuo on your own

Without tour guide & chauffeur

This day you can do as you wish. Enjoy this unique atmosphere again today, relax, borrow a bike or just go for a walk. Overnight in Yangshuo.

10th day walking in the rice terraces

From the picturesque Karst Kegelbergen around Yangshuo, your journey will take you on to Longsheng. Here you can expect rice fields as far as the eye can see. Like densely woven carpets, the Longji terrace fields cover the mountain slopes to an altitude of 880 meters. The stairs rise steeply to heaven. The first fields were created over 700 years ago. In the vernacular, the agricultural miracle is called “terraces of the dragon bone”. With their rice terraces, the locals created a landscape architecture that combines function and form, utility and aesthetics like hardly any other cultural landscape. After arriving at the rice terraces, explore them on an easy hike. Overnight in Longsheng.

Day 11 Into the glitter metropolis of Shanghai

Your last destination on this trip is the modern metropolis of Shanghai. Other names for Shanghai are “Gateway to the World”, “Dragon Head Metropolis”, “Paris of the East” or “Pearl of the Orient”. In the morning you will be driven to the airport in Guilin, from where you will fly to Shanghai. Upon arrival at the airport, you will be picked up and taken to your hotel. The colonial past and the ambitious future of Shanghai will inspire you. Together with your guide, you will immediately start your first exploration tour through this interesting city with a walk on the Bund. Shanghai’s famous waterfront is nestled by old colonial buildings such as the legendary Bank of China (built) 1912) and the old “Peace Hotel” (1930) to the modern towers of glass and steel. The promenade looks particularly impressive from the ship. During your trip on the Huangpu you can see for yourself. A stroll across the famous shopping street Nanijing Road rounds off the day. 20In his years, Nanjing Road was compared to Broadway – and even today something of this very special flair has remained. Overnight in Shanghai.

Day 12 Spectacular Prospects & European Traces

Start the day with sensational views. You will ride on the Shanghai Towers, completed in 2015, which measures a proud 632 meters. From the observation deck of the second tallest building in the world, you have a fantastic view of the city. Stroll back on solid ground through the old and original Shanghai, which was able to preserve its charm of time before the big dawn in the 90s. Shanghai also has some European trajectories to offer: In the 19th century, many French businessmen came to Shanghai and designed an elegant neighborhood: the former French concession. Even today, you can enjoy the flair of that time and almost forget which metropolis you are in when seeing some neoclassical buildings. In the evening, a very special experience awaits you: a bicycle tour in the former French concession. Your 3-hour tour will take you to the low-traffic areas with colonial streetscape. Let the lively metropolis come to you from a completely new perspective and gain a more intensive insight into everyday Chinese life. Between parks, historical sites and local cafés, you will surely find more unforgettable impressions. Overnight in Shanghai

13th day Excursion to the Garden City of Suzhou and the Water Village Tongli

Today you will take a trip to the garden city of Suzhou, which enchants with numerous canals and classic gardens. Through the gardens, Suzhou became famous and eventually developed into a cultural and intellectual center. The art of horticulture was to harmoniously design as many landscapes as possible in a confined space, offering both variety and opportunity for relaxation, but at the same time creating the feeling of size and expanse. Afterwards you will drive to the water village of Tongli, where everything still looks like in ancient China at times long gone. While you walk over small bridges, through winding alleys, past wind-leaning, gray-covered cottages, you look over dreamy canals on which junks swim. In the evening you will return to Shanghai. Overnight in Shanghai.

Day 14: Farewell to China

Today it is already time to say goodbye to this fascinating country. Surely you have packed your suitcases with many great impressions. You will be driven to the airport, from where you will start your home or onward journey.

Japan, a winter fairy tale

Japan Tour: Experience the wintery Japan on your individual tour with Asia Special Tours, your Asia expert. Ask now!

Japan in winter – a land full of magic, quiet beauty and wild nature! On this trip, you will experience the snowy expanses of Hokkaido, from the elegant red crown cranes in Tsurui to the majestic Giant Sea Eagles on a boat tour to the graceful singing swans on the hot waters of Lake Kussharo. You will explore the impressive national parks Shiretoko, Daisetsuzan and Akan Mashu with their spectacular mountain landscapes, glittering lakes and steaming volcanic springs. Insights into the culture of the Ainu bring you closer to the harmonious interaction of people with nature, while in Sapporo you discover culinary specialties and experience the city from extraordinary perspectives Stunning views from Mt. Okura, fascinating hikes on snowy paths and the observation of rare animals turn the journey into an enchanting winter adventure. Enjoy relaxed moments in the silence of nature and let yourself be enchanted by the beauty of Hokkaido. A winter fairy tale that will accompany you for a long time.

1st day Arrival in Japan

Irasshaimase and welcome to Hokkaido! Your journey begins in Kushiro in the northeast of the island, where you will be met by an English speaking driver. It will take you to your hotel in the idyllic village of Tsurui, known as the home of Manchure cranes. After returning here in the 1920s, Tsurui is now considered one of the best places to observe these majestic animals in their natural environment. The coming days are all about unforgettable nature and animal encounters. Overnight in Tsurui.

Day 2 From Tsurui to Shiretoko Rausu

After breakfast, meet your English-speaking driver and make your way to the Tsurui Ito Protected Area. Here you have the opportunity to admire Japan’s largest bird up close. With their graceful movements in the snow and the melodic calls during courtship, the cranes enchant everyone. In winter, around 300 animals gather here, which are fed in the vast fields – a sight that remains deeply remembered. Afterwards, your adventure will take you to the UNESCO World Heritage National Park Shiretoko up to the small fishing village Rausu. During the journey you will have the opportunity to enjoy the impressive volcanic landscape and wild coastal regions. In Rausu you will experience the local culture and have time for a little exploration in the village. The name “Shiretoko” comes from the Ainu language and means “end of the world” – an apt expression for this remote and wild region. Here, brown bears, sea eagles and orcas live in their natural environment. Overnight in Shiretoko Rausu.

3rd day Majestic Giant Eagle and Natural Paradise Shiretoko

One highlight chases the next. After breakfast, you will meet your driver in the hotel lobby, who will shortly take you to the port, where a boat is already available for an exciting birdwatching trip. On the open sea you will experience a rare natural spectacle: imposing giant sea eagle, of which there are only about 5,000 specimens worldwide. These majestic birds glide down to land on ice floes or linger in the coastal forests. After returning to the port, the route continues along the coast into the rugged landscape of the Notsuke Nature Reserve. This unusually shaped, narrow headland protrudes far into the sea and provides habitat for foxes, deer and numerous winter birds. After a more than two-hour drive, you will reach the west side of the Shiretoko Peninsula. During the drift-ice season, the port is closed here, but the spectacular views of the wintry coastal landscape make this special place a real highlight of your trip. Overnight in Shiretoko Utoro.

4th day snowshoe hike in the five-lake winter landscape

Once again today you are attuned to a fascinating encounter with nature. After breakfast, you will meet with your local guide and set off into the fascinating nature together. The destination is the natural wonder of the Shiretoko Five Lakes – a place of silence that is only accessible to few visitors in winter. Equipped with snowshoes, you will wander through the snow-covered landscapes of the UNESCO World Heritage Site and experience a unique winter backdrop. Depending on the weather conditions, a view of the silver-gloss Shiretoko mountain range opens up along the way. For even more flexibility, an English-speaking driver is at your disposal throughout the day. If you want to discover more places in the area after the excursion or simply move around comfortably, he will always take you wherever you like. This leaves you the freedom to arrange the day according to your wishes. Overnight in Shiretoko Utoro.

Day 5: Akan Mashu National Park

Get ready today for a fascinating adventure in the Akan Mashu National Park. Together with your English-speaking driver, you will set off to explore one of Japan’s oldest national parks. The region is known for crystal clear lakes, hot springs, dense forests and a diverse flora and fauna. Within the 90,000-hectare parkland are three impressive lakes: the mystical Mashu Lake, the vast Kussharo Lake and the Akan Lake. Your journey will first take you to Lake Kussharo, the largest crater lake in Japan, where majestic swans find refuge in the warm thermal springs in winter. Nearby, the active volcano Io-zan rises, whose steaming sulfur springs offer an impressive, almost unreal scenery. Continue to Lake Akan, famous for its simmering mud springs, the so-called “bokke”, and the Marimo bullet algae. This rare species of algae forms into beautiful green balls that can reach the size of a football. A day full of impressive nature experiences that combine the volcanic power and quiet beauty of Hokkaido in a special way. Overnight in Akan.

Day 6: Encounter with the Ainu culture

In the morning you will meet your local Ainu guide in the hotel lobby, who will lead you to the village of Ainu Kotan. More than 100 members of the indigenous community live here. Your guide will tell you his personal story of growing up in this culture. During a walk through the surrounding forests, you will learn how the Ainu live in harmony with nature. They worship the “Kamuy”, the God in all things that accompanies them in everyday life. Openly and honestly, your companion also tells of the challenges that the Ainu are facing to this day and the successes they have achieved in the fight for recognition. Finally, visit a gallery that specializes in local woodwork. This gives you a very personal insight into the artistic heritage of the Ainu. Afterwards you will return to the city and have the choice: Either go back to the hotel or take the opportunity to explore the area on your own. An English-speaking driver is at your disposal throughout the day. Overnight in Akan.

Day 7 Daisetsuzan National Park: Sounkyo Gorge and Peak Views

Your adventure takes you to the heart of Hokkaido today. Your English-speaking driver is ready and together you set off for the largest national park in Japan, the Daisetsuzan National Park. With its imposing mountains such as Mt. Asahidake, deep gorges and untouched nature, it captivates every nature lover. Particularly impressive is the Sounkyo Gorge, a 24-kilometer gorge surrounded by steep cliff walls and home to some famous waterfalls such as the “Ginga” and “Ryusei” Falls. In winter, the gorge can be easily admired from the viewpoints along the road. Mt. Kurodake, one of the most beautiful peaks of the Daisetsu mountain range, also awaits you with breathtaking views. With the cable car and the chairlift you can easily reach the 5th station, where wide panoramic views over the mountains open up and with a little luck also the local wild squirrels of the region can be observed. After these impressive nature experiences, continue to the city of Asahikawa, where the day ends. Overnight in Asahikawa.

Day 8 Arrival in Sapporo and culinary discoveries

Today you will travel with your English-speaking driver to Sapporo, the capital of Hokkaido and also the fifth largest city in Japan. In the heart of the city lies the sprawling Odori Park, a 1.5 km long promenade at the end of which rises the Sapporo TV Tower. Just a few steps away is the famous Sapporo Clock Tower, one of the oldest buildings in the city and today a museum that offers exciting insights into Sapporo’s history. The service of your driver ends at the hotel before you leave for a very special enjoyment program in the evening. Together with a local guide, immerse yourself in the city’s culinary diversity for three hours. Depending on your tastes, the tour will take you to the best addresses for Hokkaido’s specialties: from spicy Genghis Khan Lamb Barbecue to the famous Butter Ramen and fresh seafood. Finally, you have the opportunity to try a typical Shime parfait made of creamy Hokkaido cream. Then say goodbye to your guide and continue the evening according to your mood. Overnight in Sapporo.

Day 9 Sapporo between tradition, sport and enjoyment

After breakfast, meet your guide in the hotel lobby and travel together with the subway to the quiet district of Maruyama. From there, you can take a taxi to Mt. Okura, an insider tip under the viewpoints of Sapporos. With a bit of luck, you’ll even catch a glimpse of the ski jumpers training. Even if there is no training or competition, it is still possible to take the ski lift up to the summit and feel how exciting and breathtaking it must be to take the jump from the hill towards the city center. At the foot of the hill, the Sapporo Olympic Museum commemorates the 1972 Winter Olympics, and then takes you to the central wholesale market, where you can enjoy a fresh seafood lunch or other local specialty. Afterwards, we will take the train to the Sapporo Beer Museum. Here you will learn why beer brewing is firmly intertwined with the city’s history and have the opportunity to enjoy a freshly tapped glass of beer. Back in the center of Sapporo, walk with your guide through the bustling streets. He will give you valuable tips for your own explorations, the best restaurants and hidden corners of the city. Finally, say goodbye to your guide. The rest of the evening is free to discover Japan’s northern capital at your own pace or return to the hotel relaxed. Overnight in Sapporo. 

Day 10: Farewell to Sapporo

Today you have free time until the return flight – enjoy it to your taste, whether for last purchases, a leisurely walk or simply to relax in the hotel. The express train will take you directly to New Chitose Airport for your onward journey to board your return flight. Your winter trip through Hokkaido ends here. The unique impressions, the breathtaking nature and the encounters with people and animals will remain deep in your heart. Look forward to sharing your photos and stories from this wonderful adventure with family and friends.

With yoga, Ayurveda and vegan nutrition on the journey to self

India Yoga Journey - With Asia Special Tours you will find peace, deceleration and self-discovery in India as well as yoga, Ayurveda and vegan diet for yourself.

You need time for rest and deceleration and want to do something good for yourself and your body with enjoyable vegan food? Then you will love the beautiful South of India! The state of Kerala offers with the picturesque Vembanad Lake, the winding backwaters and the idyllic mountain landscape around Munnar the perfect environment for your journey to self. Bring body, mind and soul into harmonious balance during yoga and meditation, enjoy the unadulterated nature on a pleasant hike and get to know the healthy Indian cuisine at a cooking demonstration. During the trip, you will also get valuable insights into central elements of Ayurveda: relax with personalized Ayurvedic massages and treatments and be introduced to the Ayurvedic nutritional teaching. Throughout the trip, vegan food is organized for you in the visited hotels. Of course, cultural experiences should not be missing on your trip. Let a local lady show you the hidden architectural treasures of Alapuzzha, explore the multifaceted Fort Kochi district, which looks back on a long and exciting history, and be enchanted by a traditional Kathakali dance performance. At the end of your journey, you can expect seven nights at the luxurious Shreyas Yoga Retreat. Enjoy soothing peace and find yourself at yoga outdoors, guided meditations and Ayurveda treatments under the care of experienced Indian specialists.

Day 1 Sit back and enjoy: Boat trip on Lake Vembanad

Namaste and welcome to India! Upon arrival in Kochi, you will be expected and transported to your hotel in the magical backwaters of the Kerala region. After a break, the first highlight awaits you in the evening: a boat trip with a motorized canoe on Lake Vembanad. This gives you unique insights into life on and off the water and presents the lake in all its beauty. Best of all: just sit back and enjoy! Your accommodation for the next three nights is the luxury hotel Purity above the lake, which offers numerous authentic experiences including yoga and Ayurveda. The bright and spacious rooms with their handcrafted contemporary yet traditional design form a dialogue between past and present. Here you can decelerate wonderfully and find yourself. Overnight in Muhamma. 

Day 2 - Houseboat through the Backwaters

After a delightfully fresh and balanced breakfast, start the day with another boat trip. Aboard a comfortable houseboat, explore the labyrinthine network of rivers, canals and lagoons that make up the famous backwaters. The backwaters are also habitat of many different animal and plant species that you can admire during your boat trip. With a little luck, you will discover, for example, otters who frolic in the water or monkeys who frolic in the trees on the lake shore. Back at the hotel you will be pampered with a heavenly foot massage after this impressive day. Overnight in Muhamma.

Day 3 Explore with a local Lady Alapuzzha

A visit to the city of Alapuzzha must not be missed. Their backwaters are world famous and attract many tourists, but very few know the architectural marvels that this city holds. Together with an English-speaking local who knows their city like the back of his hand, you will explore the city mainly on foot. Stroll through the streets, admire hidden churches, temples and mosques along the way, and pass abandoned homes where Gujarati shops once flourished. Her volunteer guide is not a trained tour guide, but she loves her city and is happy to share her heritage and culture with you. The walk ends with a small refreshment at the home of a volunteer city guide. After a short break at the hotel, visit the coconut fibre factory near Muhamma, which is organized as a cooperative. Here you will learn all about the manufacturing process of the versatile fibers. Overnight in Muhamma.

Day 4: Perfect start to the day with meditation and yoga

Your day begins with meditation and yoga. This expands the soul and brings the body and mind into harmonious balance. After a restorative breakfast, it’s time to leave the backwaters behind. The next destination on your trip is the city of Munnar, known mainly for its tea plantations and scenic mountain scenery. Some of the tea fields are located at an altitude of up to 2200 meters – making Munnar one of the highest tea growing regions in the world. Due to the altitude and climatic conditions, the tea from Munnar is of the best quality; of course, it is best to convince yourself of this even with a cup of tea! Arriving in Munnar, you will immediately be taken to your hotel: the idyllic boutique resort Spice Tree located in the middle of a picturesque mountain landscape. Here, in this oasis of tranquility, you will feel a positive transformation and well-being that cannot be expressed in words. Overnight in Munnar.

5th day hills and tea plantations as far as the eye can see

The beauty of Munnar is best enjoyed on a pleasant hike. Munnar will definitely delight you as soon as the surrounding natural scenery spreads in front of you. Your path leads through lush, green grasslands, tea and coffee plantations and over rolling hills. On the way, you always have the opportunity to let the breathtaking view affect you. On clear days you can even catch a view of Anaimudi, the highest mountain in southern India. You may also encounter some representatives of the numerous bird species that are native to the region. Enjoy the time in nature and strengthen yourself with a picnic. Against this backdrop, it tastes so good. If you wish, you can visit the tea museum after the hike. It is the first of its kind in India and clearly conveys the elaborate production process of tea. Overnight in Munnar.

Day 6 Kochi, the “Queen of the Arabian Sea”, awaits you

Breakfast in peace, breathe in the fresh mountain air with a yoga lesson and enjoy once again the fantastic view. With new energy, you will later make your way back to Kochi, which is best known for its old castle. The picturesque old port city consists of several islands and peninsulas and is also praised in literature as the “Queen of the Arabian Sea”. Your accommodation for the next three nights also pays homage to the rich past of the place. The stylish Brunton Boatyard Hotel resides in the former Victorian boatyard right next to Kochi harbor and takes you with its unique atmosphere and authentic decor on a little nostalgic journey back in time to the Indo-Portuguese colonial era – a time when pepper from Kerala was as valuable as gold. High ceilings, historic furniture, terracotta floors and a lot of wood determine the decor – a tasteful mixture of English, Portuguese and Dutch influences. Overnight in Kochi.

Day 7 With an expert in the footsteps of colonial rulers in Fort Kochi

Today is dedicated to exploring the bustling Fort Kochi district, which holds countless testimonies of its rich cultural past. You will accompany an English-speaking expert on a walk that will take you to the impressive fort, the Christian St. Francis Church, the Parade Square and the Imperial Bank of India. Along the way, you will pass numerous historic buildings that revive the period of European colonial rule before your mind’s eye. Of course, you should not miss the famous Chinese fishing nets. These heavy wooden structures, which must be operated by at least four men, are a truly fascinating sight. Finally, visit the port of Fort Kochi, where small fishing boats roam. The rest of the day you can do as you wish. Stroll a bit through the lanes of Fort Kochis or let the hotel relax with a soothing Ayurveda application (optional). In the evening, enjoy your dinner at the hotel’s own pier overlooking the harbor – an enjoyable experience for all the senses! Overnight in Kochi.

Day 8: Experience Kochi with all your senses: Cooking demonstration & Kathakali dances

Kochi is known not only for its rich cultural past, but also for its diverse cuisine. Vegans get their money's worth here and can look forward to countless meat- and fish-free pleasures. Together with an experienced chef at the Brunton Boatyard Hotel, you can buy vegetables, herbs and fragrant spices at the local market. If you wish, you can also try yourself in the art of acting. Back at the hotel, the professional will introduce you to the secrets of Indian cuisine and show you how the purchased spices unfold their full aroma. Of course, you can also taste the wonderfully scented dish. Well strengthened you can then go on another exploration tour through the city or treat yourself to an Ayurveda application. In the evening, a very special spectacle awaits you. You will attend a traditional Kathakali dance performance that delights its viewers with a spectacular mix of drama, dance, music and rituals. In advance, you can look over the shoulder of the actors during elaborate makeup. Overnight in Kochi.

Day 9 Shreyas Yoga Retreat: in harmony with nature & your own body

Without guidance

With many new impressions in your luggage, you will be taken to the airport after breakfast, from where you will continue to Bengaluru. There you will already be expected and brought to your dreamlike resort. Spend the next few days in harmony with nature and your own body. The Shreyas Yoga Retreat offers the ideal conditions for this. The resort is located about an hour from Bengaluru in the middle of nature. The air is filled with bird voices, no noise disturbs the gentle soundscape of the wilderness. Upon arrival, an experienced doctor will advise you on your individual needs, to which the applications and activities will be tailored in the following. In addition, the healing artists have an open ear for you and are happy to advise you on questions about your health. Overnight in Bengaluru.

10. -15. The Day of Resurrection and Resurrection

Without tour guide & chauffeur

These days belong entirely to you. Let the soul dangle and find outdoor yoga (together with other guests), guided meditations and Ayurveda treatments under the care of experienced Indian specialists for a soothing inner peace and yourself. The friendly and attentive service of the resort does the rest: the staff is always cautious to ensure that none of your wishes remains open – while showing a great love for detail. Between the applications and exercises, you can retire to the pool with a book from the comprehensive library. If you want to do something good not only yourself, but also others, you can, for example, participate in a food donation for a nearby orphanage. The vegan food is cooked partly from vegetables from your own garden. During a course you can learn more about the preparation of dishes. Overnight at Shreyas Yoga Retreat.

16th day Farewell to India

Even the most beautiful journey comes to an end. Full of new energy, relaxed and with a new self-image say goodbye to India. Your driver will take you to Bengaluru Airport, from where you will travel home. But who knows, maybe one day you’ll come back here.

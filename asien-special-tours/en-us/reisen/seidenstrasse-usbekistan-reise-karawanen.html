On the paths of the great caravans in Uzbekistan

Following in the footsteps of restless caravans, discover the unique culture and nature of Uzbekistan. A varied and varied journey awaits you as you follow the old caravan roads in Uzbekistan with us. Once mighty commercial centers on the ancient Silk Road, cities like Bukhara and Samarkand are still home to stunning buildings that will make your trip an unforgettable experience. Highlights such as a camel ride, the overnight stay in a yurt, the fertile Ferghana valley and the Aydarkul Lake should not be missing.

Day 1 Arrival in Uzbekistan

Welcome to Uzbekistan! When you arrive in Tashkent, you will already be welcomed by your German-speaking tour guide. Together with your private chauffeur you drive to the hotel, where you can arrive in peace. Overnight in Tashkent.

2nd day drive to the Ferghana Valley

At the beginning of your journey, the trip will first take you to the Ferghana Valley. By train you first go to Kokand – the “city of the winds”. Kokand was once an important hub along the Silk Road and with 300 mosques of religious center of the Ferghana Valley. Here you will visit the Juma Mosque with its 98 imposing wooden columns, which is now a museum. Continue to visit the beautiful Khudajar Khan Palace (closed on Mondays), which is already a candy from the outside: its facade measures 70 meters and has been decorated with countless arabesques and patterns of different styles and materials. The Modari Khan Mausoleum and the one-story Norbutabi Medrese are other sights of the city. Later you will continue on your way to Ferghana. After your arrival, a short sightseeing tour will take you to Al-Ferghaniy National Park and the Regional Theatre. Overnight in Ferghana.

3rd day of craftsmanship in Margilan and Rishtan

A day of craftsmanship awaits you! Not far from Ferghana is Margilan, the center of the Uzbek silk industry. You will visit a silk weaving mill and learn all about the production process of the noble fabrics. You will also visit the bustling bazaar, Chakar Mosque and Said Akhmad Khodja Medrese, then continue on to Rishtan. The city is famous for its ceramic workshops. Here you will be introduced to the traditional Uzbek art of ceramic manufacturing. Across the country, there are various schools that use their own techniques and patterns. Then you will be driven through the mountains to Tashkent. Overnight in Tashkent.

4th day of Uzbekistan's capital

Tashkent is still shaped by Soviet architecture – and a place where you can feel the tension between Europe and the Orient particularly well. Take advantage of today to get to know the capital of Uzbekistan. First, visit Khasti Imon Square with the beautiful Barak Khan Medrese, Tillya Sheikh Mosque and Kaffal Shashi Mausoleum made of simple brick masonry. The Kukeldash Medrese still serves as a Koranic school today. You will also visit the Museum of Applied Art. You should not miss an underground metro ride: Tashkent is the only Central Asian city to have a subway, which is also a small architectural and artistic masterpiece. On the huge Chorsu bazaar you will transport oriental smells and spices into the fairy tales from 1001 nights. Overnight in Tashkent.

Day 5 - Continue to Samarkand

In the morning, visit other cultural highlights of Tashkent such as the Amir Timur Museum, the Historical Museum and the Navoi Theater. In the evening, take the high-speed Afrosiyob train to Samarkand. The city of Samarkand is considered the heart of the legendary Silk Road and looks back on a 2,000-year history marked by a rapid change of powers. Overnight in Samarkand.

Day 6 Samarkand's Most Beautiful Sites

Scent bazaars, huge mosques and a mysterious atmosphere: this is Samarkand. Your sightseeing tour of this breathtaking city begins with a visit to the Gur Emir Mausoleum, the final resting place of the famous ruler Tamerlan. After a short walk you will reach the heart of Samarkand, the beautiful Registan Square with its three magnificent medres: Ulugbek, Shir Dor and Tillya-Kari. In the afternoon, it is best to continue your visit with the beautiful Bibi Khanum Mosque. Bibi Khanum was to become the largest mosque in the Eastern world at that time – after all, it is still one of the largest mosques today. Of course, you should not miss a stroll over the Oriental Siab bazaar. Overnight in Samarkand.

7th day excursion to chess risabs

After breakfast, travel via Takhta Karachi Pass to the town of Chessrisabs, whose name means “green city”. Chessrisabs is not only a UNESCO World Heritage Site, but also historically significant as the birthplace of Tamerlane. You will visit the impressive Ak Saray Palace, which according to stories was built by Amir Temur and served as his summer residence. Let yourself be impressed by the majolikes and cut mosaics that cover the facade with countless motifs. However, the historic city holds even more sights such as the Kuk-Gumbaz Mosque or the tomb of Tamerlan’s son: Yakhangir, who fell off the horse at the age of twelve and died. In the afternoon, return to Samarkand. Overnight in Samarkand.

Day 8 Stunning Samarkand

You haven't seen everything yet! Your guide will continue with you today to visit the breathtaking city. You can reach for the stars in the Ulugbek Observatory. Afterwards, you will search for traces in the ancient city of Afrosiab – now an excavation site. Here was discovered in 1912 a palace, which probably belonged to a high official and whose mud walls were built in the 6th and 7th centuries. It is fairytale in the necropolis of Shahi Zinda. Everywhere you will find floral and star décor: the Samarkander ornament. In this complex, the cousin of Prophet Muhammad is said to be buried. After a lot of new impressions, visit the Local Lore Museum at the end of the day. Overnight in Samarkand.

Day 9 Samarkand – Nurata – Yangigazgan

From Samarkand, today’s journey will first take you to Nurata: a well-known pilgrimage site where you will visit the Chil Ustun Mosque and the ruins of Alexander the Great’s fortress. The place is also known for its chashma spring – it is sacred along with the fish floating in the water and the destination of many pilgrims. After that, you will be escorted to the yurt camp of Yangikazgan settlement, which is located in the Kyzylkum Desert. A highlight of your excursion is the unforgettable camel trekking tour through the sand dunes of the desert, where you can prove your talent. Very special accommodation awaits you here, because you will spend tonight in a traditional yurt. The evening will end with a dinner at the campfire, accompanied by folk songs, under the starry sky of Uzbekistan. Overnight in Yangigazgan.

Day 10 Refreshing Aydarkul Lake

After spending an exciting night in the middle of the desert, head to scenic Aydarkul Lake, which will impress you with a length of 200 kilometers. If it is warm enough, you can swim a little, or just relax. Afterwards you will drive to the city of Bukhara, which was once one of the most glamorous commercial centers on the Silk Road. Along the way, you can make a stop in Gijduvan, famous for its ornate ceramics. Overnight in Bukhara.

Day 11 - Brilliant Bukhara

The oasis city was formerly considered the Islamic center of Central Asia and also one of the most important cities along the Silk Road. Even today, the atmosphere of bygone times is still felt. Today is dedicated to exploring the magical city from 1001 nights. Together with your guide, you will first visit the Choir Minor Mosque, the beautiful Bolo Hauz Mosque and the mighty Ark Citadel in the ground plan shape of the constellation “Großer Wagen”. You will see the mausoleum of the former Samanid ruler Ishmael Samani from the 9th and 10th centuries as well as the mausoleum Chashmai Ayub, which translates to “Source of Job”. Other highlights include the gigantic Poi Kalon complex, which consists of a minaret, a mosque and the beautiful Miri Arab Medrese. Also worth seeing are the Ulugbek Medrese, named after the famous astronomer, the Abdulaziz Khan Medrese and the Magoki Attori City Mosque. Finally, the complex Labi Hauz invites you to linger. Around the water basin “Hauz” several tea rooms are grouped, in which the Bucharers meet for the game of dominoes. Once, almost all inhabitants of the city were supplied with water with the help of the water basin. Overnight in Bukhara.

Day 12: More miracles from 1,001 night

After breakfast, your guide will show you even more of Bukhara. You will get to know the summer palace of the last emir of Bukhara – Sitorai mohi khosa – as well as the ensemble Bakhauddin Nakhshbandi. This tomb is visited daily by pilgrims and gives you an insight into the living Islam. After visiting the Chor-Bakr-Mausoleum, you can arrange the rest of the time according to your wishes. Overnight in Bukhara.

Day 13 - Through the desert to Khiva

An exciting ride ahead of you today. On an ancient caravan road, cross the Kyzyilkum Desert, cruising the Amu-Darja River to reach the ancient city of Khiva. On the way, there are always opportunities for photo stops. After accommodation at your hotel in Chiwa, you can familiarize yourself with this breathtaking city on a first walk. Overnight in Chiwa.

Day 14 Excursion to Ayaz Kala & Toprak Kala

Not only the oasis city of Chiwa has a lot to offer for culture and history lovers. You can convince yourself of this during an excursion into the area when you head to Ayaz Kala. The fortress from the 3rd or 4th pre-Christian century is located on an extensive excavation field. From here you have a good view of the vast desert landscape. Explore the ruins of Ayaz Kala and Toprak Kala – Toprak Kala is probably the most archaeologically studied excavation site in the region. Residents were forced to leave their facilities when the Kuschana empire collapsed, and Turkish attacks destroyed the irrigation facilities. Once you have looked around you will return to Khiva with many new and impressive impressions. Overnight in Chiwa.

Day 15: Fairytale Khiva

After breakfast, you are now completely devoted to exploring Chiwa. The small oasis town in the middle of the desert has preserved the fairy-tale atmosphere of an ancient, oriental city to this day. You will visit the old town of Khiva – Itchan Kala – with the beautifully decorated Islam Khodja Minaret, Pakhlavan Mahmoud Mausoleum and Juma Mosque. Enjoy the medres of Mohammed Amin-Khan and Rachim-Khan, the castle of Khan Tash-Hauli – where a cobra is said to still guard the gold treasure of the last Khan – and other fascinating buildings. Everywhere in the old town you will encounter fine, intricate wood carvings – an art that has been cultivated in Khiva since ancient times. Keep your camera at hand! In the evening you will be taken to Urgentsch Airport, from where you will return by domestic flight to Tashkent. Overnight in Tashkent.

16th day Departure from Tashkent

Today, unfortunately, we say goodbye. With 1001 new impressions and experiences in your luggage, you will be taken to the airport of Tashkent, from where you will start your journey home. The beautiful memories will always remain for you and often beautify everyday life.

The fairy tales of 1,001 nights become reality as you travel from the fertile Ferghana Valley to the oasis cities of the Kyzylkum Desert. Enjoy unforgettable moments such as a camel ride, dinner in a madrassah, a yurt night and your stay with a host family. These are rounded off by visits to the most beautiful fortresses, mosques and mausoleums of Uzbekistan.

Day 1 Arrival in Uzbekistan

Welcome to Uzbekistan! When you arrive in Tashkent, you will already be welcomed by your German-speaking tour guide. Together with your private chauffeur you drive to the hotel, where you can arrive in peace. Overnight in Tashkent.

2nd day Multifaceted capital

You can dedicate your first day in Uzbekistan completely to the sights of the multi-faceted capital. First, visit Khasti Imon Square with the beautiful Barak Khan Medrese, Tillya Sheikh Mosque and Kaffal Shashi Mausoleum made of simple brick masonry. Here you can take a look at the Quran handwritten by the third Caliph Osman (644 – 656). The interesting Museum of Applied Art and the Kukeldash Medrese are also on your program. In the afternoon, continue your exploration in the modern part of the city and visit Independence Square, the Tashkent subway with its ornately designed metro stations and the huge Chorsu bazaar, whose Oriental smells and spices will transport you to the fairy tales from 1001 nights. Overnight in Tashkent.

Day 3 Via Kokand to Ferghana

At the beginning of your journey, the trip will first take you to the Ferghana Valley. By train you first go to Kokand – the “city of the winds”. Kokand was once an important hub along the Silk Road and with 300 mosques of religious center of the Ferghana Valley. Here you will visit the Juma Mosque with its 98 imposing wooden columns, which is now a museum. Continue to visit the beautiful Khudajar Khan Palace (closed on Mondays), which is already a candy from the outside: its facade measures 70 meters and has been decorated with countless arabesques and patterns of different styles and materials. The Modari Khan Mausoleum and the one-story Norbutabi Medrese are other sights of the city. Later you will continue on your way to Ferghana. After your arrival, a short sightseeing tour will take you to Al-Ferghaniy National Park and the Regional Theatre. Overnight in Ferghana.

4th day Craft Centre Ferghana Valley

A day of craftsmanship awaits you! Not far from Ferghana is Margilan, the center of the Uzbek silk industry. You will visit a silk weaving mill and learn all about the production process of the noble fabrics. But the city has more to offer, as you will see as you stroll across the bazaar, visit the Chakar Mosque and visit the Said Achmed Khodja Medrese. Then continue on to Rishtan. The city is famous for its ceramic workshops. Here you will be introduced to the traditional Uzbek art of ceramic manufacturing. Across the country, there are various schools that practice their own techniques and patterns. Through the beautiful mountain landscape you drive back to the Uzbek capital. Overnight in Tashkent.

Day 5 in Karakalpakstan

Explore the Autonomous Republic of Karakalpakstan. From Tashkent you fly to the capital Nukus. From here, drive to Muynak on the edge of the Aral Sea. The former fishing village Muynak was originally located on the southern shore of the Aral Sea – now the town is about 200 kilometers from the current lake shore. Fishing had to be abandoned and a large part of the inhabitants emigrated. Here you will visit the ship's cemetery, which opens up the view of the now endless desert and serves as a memorial. On your return trip, you will make a detour to the ruins of the tomb city of Mizdakhan. The seven-step ladders, lying on many graves, symbolize the transition from one world to the other; the stones stacked up correspond to certain wishes. Overnight in Nukus.

Day 6 From Nukus to Khiva

After breakfast, visit the Museum of Savitskiy. It houses numerous art treasures, including the world’s second largest collection of Russian avant-garde art, one of the largest and most interesting collections of applied art and archaeological finds in Central Asia. Then continue to Khiva, where you can use the rest of the day at your free disposal. Overnight in Chiwa.

7th day Oasis town of Chiwa

Today you will explore the oasis city of Chiwa – one of the best preserved cities on the Silk Road. The former capital of the 17th century Khiva Khanati has preserved the fairy-tale atmosphere of an ancient, oriental city to this day. You will visit the old town of Khiva – Itchan Kala – with the beautifully decorated Islam Khodja Minaret, the Palvan Kara complex and the medre of Mohammed Rachim Khan. In the afternoon, continue your exploration tour and visit Kunya Ark Fortress and Pakhlavan Mahmoud Mausoleum. By the way, the fact that the city’s landmark, Kalta Minor, is a “short minaret” according to its name and shape, was not originally planned. It was even to become the highest minaret in the city. Unfortunately, it could not be further built from a height of 26 meters for static reasons. The Medrese Allakuli Khan not only has beautiful architecture to offer, but is especially interesting if you want to see and acquire craftsmanship. Because inside them are small craft companies. Carpet weavers and silk weavers will gladly share their arts. Overnight in Chiwa.

Day 8 - Through the desert to Bukhara

While driving through the Kyzylkum Desert, you will discover that the apparent wasteland has a lot to offer. You travel along the Amudarja River, the life-giving stream of Central Asia. In addition, your guide will use the time to tell you interesting things about the country and people. So you can look forward to a varied ride. Overnight in Bukhara.

Day 9 Bukhara's Miracle

Visit the most magnificent sights of Bukhara. You will see the mausoleum of the former Samanid ruler Ismael Samani from the 9th and 10th centuries, Chashmai Ayub and the beautiful Bolo Hauz Mosque. Other highlights are the gigantic Ark Citadel in the ground plan shape of the constellation “Great Chariot” and the Poi Kalon complex, which consists of a minaret, a mosque and the beautiful Miri Arab Medrese. Also worth seeing is the Ulugbek Medrese, which was named after the famous astronomer. In addition, you can experience the daily activity in the three overcoupled bazaars and at the silk market up close. Your guide also introduces you to the secrets of the complex Labi Hauz, which invites you to linger: Around the water basin “Hauz” several tea rooms are grouped, in which the Bucharers meet for the game of dominoes. Once, almost all inhabitants of the city were supplied with water with the help of the water basin. The fitting conclusion of a perfect day is the dance performance in a medrese. Overnight in Bukhara.

Day 10 The surroundings of Bukhara

In the surroundings of Bukhara there are other sights that should not be hidden from you. After visiting the Choir Minor Mosque, your guide will introduce you to the secrets of the Summer Palace of the last Emir of Bukhara – Sitorai mohi khosa – and show you the Mausoleum Choir Bakr as well as the Bakhauddin Nakhshbandi Ensemble. This tomb is visited daily by pilgrims and gives you a glimpse into the living Islam. Finally, you return to Bukhara and can arrange the rest of the day according to your wishes. Overnight in Bukhara.

11th day camels and yurts

From Bukhara, the journey first takes you to Nurata. Along the way, you can make a stop in Gijduvan, famous for its ornate ceramics. Nurata is a well-known pilgrimage site where you will visit the Chil Ustun Mosque as well as the ruins of Alexander the Great’s fortress. The place is also famous for its chashma spring – it is sacred along with the fish floating in the water and the destination of many pilgrims. After that, you will be escorted to the yurt camp of Yangikazgan settlement, which is located in the Kyzylkum Desert. A highlight of your excursion is the unforgettable camel trekking tour through the sand dunes of the desert, where you can prove your talent. Very special accommodation awaits you here, because you will spend tonight in a traditional yurt. The evening will end with a dinner at the campfire, accompanied by folk songs, under the starry sky of Uzbekistan. Overnight in Yangikazgan.

12th Day Rural Life in Uzbekistan

After spending an exciting night in the middle of the desert, head to scenic Aydarkul Lake, which will impress you with a length of 200 kilometers. Here you can fortify yourself on the shore with a delicious picnic and have a little time to relax and dream. In the afternoon we will continue to the Nurata Mountains. Here you get to know the rural life of the locals and can explore the landscape. Accommodation and overnight stay in a very simple guest house in Sentyab.

Day 13 - Go to Samarkand

Today you continue your journey to Samarkand. Scent bazaars, huge mosques and a mysterious atmosphere: this is Samarkand. Once you have arrived here, you will see the main sights of the city. Start with the Gur Emir Mausoleum, the final resting place of the famous ruler Tamerlan. After a short walk you will reach the heart of Samarkand, the beautiful Registan Square with its three magnificent medres: Ulugbek, Shir Dor and Tillya-Kari. Overnight in Samarkand.

Day 14: Stunning Samarkand

You haven't seen everything yet! Today your guide will continue with you the sightseeing of the breathtaking Samarkand. Your path leads you to the dead city of Shah-I-Zinda, which consists of numerous, finely decorated mausoleums – also the cousin of the Prophet Muhammad is said to be buried here. The Ulugbek Observatory invites you to reach for the stars. You will be amazed how exactly the celestial bodies could already be determined at that time. Also the sight of the beautiful Bibi Khanum Mosque will not be forgotten so quickly. Bibi Khanum was to become the largest mosque in the Eastern world at that time – after all, it is still one of the largest mosques today. Your guide will tell you which legend surrounds its creation. Afterwards you will visit the archaeological site Afrosiab and of course you can not miss a stroll over the Oriental bazaar. After a lot of new impressions, visit the Local Lore Museum at the end of the day. Overnight in Samarkand.

15th Day Back to Tashkent

Your journey will take you back to the capital Tashkent. Along the way, you can enjoy a special treat: at the Jizzakh honey market, people from nearby villages meet to offer their products. From May to June there is flower honey, in the following two months you can try “cotton honey” – a variety that is collected during the cotton bloom. Also with tasty apples, pears, plums and kaki you can stock up here. However, you will search for potatoes and onions in vain - everyone grows them themselves. The rest of the day is at your free disposal. Take advantage of the last afternoon to visit the numerous bazaars and squares of Tashkent. Overnight in Tashkent.

16th day Departure from Tashkent

Today, unfortunately, we say goodbye. With 1001 new impressions and experiences in your luggage, you will be taken to the airport of Tashkent, from where you will start your journey home. The unique memories will always remain for you and often beautify everyday life.

Culinary Tour of Thailand - Thailand for Connoisseurs

Thailand Round Trip: Go on a culinary round trip through Thailand and discover the country’s delicacies. Ask now!

Thailand is a paradise for gourmets and connoisseurs: magnificently exotic, diverse and a true firework of fragrances and aromas. Thai cuisine is rightly one of the best in the world. On this trip you will taste the diversity of the country: you will enjoy a relaxed dinner river cruise on board the elegant Supanninga on Chao Phraya, let yourself be introduced to the secrets of the different herbs and spices, immerse yourself in the colorful bustle of the markets and learn all about the Thai national dish when visiting a rice farm. During your overnight cooking course at the Thai House, you will prepare popular Thai dishes by hand under expert guidance and also get an interesting insight into the life of a Thai family. Other culinary highlights of your trip await you in Chiang Mai, where you will experience a traditional cantoke dinner and dive into the tasteful street food of northern Thailand on a street food tour. Of course, in addition to so much cuisine, the culture of the country is not neglected.

1st Day Elegant Dinner Cruise on Chao Phraya

Welcome to Thailand! At Bangkok Airport, you will be picked up by your private guide and taken to your hotel. Known in Thai as the “City of Angels”, Bangkok is a city with an exciting history where you can experience ancient temples, colorful palaces, and a river that winds through the heart of the city. The rest of the afternoon you can arrange according to your own ideas. Arrive in peace or take a first small exploration tour on your own. In the evening, a first big highlight awaits you: a unique dinner cruise (together with other guests) on the Chao Phraya River. Enjoy authentic Thai food, real hospitality and a majestic view of the Chao Phraya River. Enjoy the view of the illuminated skyline and palaces that pass by the shore. Overnight in Bangkok.

Day 2 Infinite Variety of Thai Cuisine

Get ready for a day full of colorful impressions of the diversity of Thai cuisine. In the morning we head to the district of Bang Rak, whose name in German means “village of love”. Here you can find everything that the gourmet heart desires: from Chinese duck, to typical Thai coffee or tea, classic Thai dishes to halal dishes that are not only tasted by Muslims. By the way, you will learn a lot about the history of the district. Afterwards you will drive to the time-honored shopping center “Old Siam”, where an entire floor is dedicated to food. You can also try one or the other and see how the individual delicacies are created. Of course, you can’t miss a stroll through Chinatown, Bangkok’s “largest open-air restaurant”! At the end of the day you should treat yourself to a drink in the exclusive bar “Vertigo”, where the whole city is at your feet! Relax with a delicious cocktail or glass of wine (not included) and enjoy the breathtaking views of nightly Bangkok and the Chao Phraya River. Simply ideal to review the experiences of the day. Overnight in Bangkok.

Day 3 - Immersion in the world of herbs and spices

After breakfast, you will be picked up from the hotel and drive about an hour by motorized long boat to Thai House. In small groups (together with other guests, up to a maximum of 10 people) you will first be introduced to the secrets of the different herbs and spices before you set off to conjure up various popular dishes under expert guidance. Even if you are already an advanced hobby chef, you will certainly learn some tricks and tricks that make it easier for you to re-cook the dishes at home. A dinner together, where the cooked delicacies are eaten, concludes the first part of the cooking class. Tonight you will spend in the cooking school accommodation, a peaceful oasis of tranquility away from the hustle and bustle of the capital. The Thai House is unique and authentic, embodying features that can only be found in traditional Thai architecture. Overnight in Nonthaburi (simple "Homestay" accommodation). If you want it a bit more chic and would rather spend another night in the comfortable Anantara Riverside Bangkok, we are happy to organize an alternative cooking course in the well-known restaurant “Blue Elephant” with a joint market visit for you. Overnight in Bangkok.

Day 4 Delicacies of Thai “Street Kitchen”

Early in the morning you will head to a typical market for fresh food, where you will shop together with the cook and then return to the Thai House for breakfast. Around noon, head back to Bangkok and visit the Sam Phraeng district, which is one of Bangkok’s most charming neighbourhoods with its quiet, charming and village-like atmosphere. The inhabitants appreciate their district in particular as a culinary paradise, where all kinds of delicacies from the area of “street kitchen” await you. For example, try different types of pasta, traditional desserts or homemade soy milk. Here is guaranteed for every taste the right one. Overnight in Bangkok.

Day 5 Colorful Market Running at Bangkok’s Gates

Today you will leave Bangkok and delve deeper into Thailand’s bustling market. A particularly unusual location awaits you at the Maeklong market directly on the railway tracks. Eight times a day, the train passes by the stands only a few centimeters and sweeps sellers along with their goods from the rails with a loud signal for a short time. For the sellers long routine, however, this is a real highlight for visitors. Damnoen Saduak, on the other hand, is the most popular floating market in the area. With a motorized long boat you drive past stilt houses and temples and finally reach the actual market with its colorful bustle directly on the water. On the way back to the metropolis, visit the magical little temple Wat Bang Kung. In 300 years, a huge banyan tree has grown over the shrine with its golden Buddha inside, which can still be entered today. Dinner is served in the romantic restaurant “Sala Rattanakosin”, where you will be served exquisite dishes of traditional Thai or international cuisine. It also offers fantastic views of the iconic Wat Arun (“Temple of Dawn”) perched on the opposite bank. Overnight in Bangkok.

Day 6 - Continue to the North

Leave Bangkok and continue north. Your journey will first take you to Ayutthaya, the former capital of the Siamese kingdom of the same name. There you will visit the city’s Historic Park, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, and dive deep into the fascination of history among the ruins of the once magnificent metropolis. The remains of numerous temples, palaces and sanctuaries still bear witness to the splendor of the former city of millions. You can also try the local speciality Roti Sai Mai here, which is most easily described as “cotton candy in flat bread”. In the “monkey city” Lopburi you can then observe hundreds of curious primates. Finally, you will reach Phitsanulok, where Wat Mahatha is one of the most beautiful Buddha statues in Thailand. At the end of the day, visit the night market, which offers a very special delicacy for the brave: fried insects! Overnight in Phitsanulok.

Day 7 on a Thai rice farm

To learn how to win the Thai national dish, visit a rice farm today. You slip into the traditional clothes of the rice farmers and dive directly into their everyday life. You will get exciting insights into the variety of rice varieties as well as the traditional methods of cultivation and harvesting. Afterwards, enjoy a local lunch on site. In the afternoon, you will continue to Lampang, where you will visit the beautiful teak temple Wat Phratat Lampang Luang, one of the oldest teak temples in the world. Overnight in Lampang. 

8th day Kantoke Dinner – a feast for all the senses

A new day, a new goal. Your journey today takes you to Chiang Mai, which is also known as the “Rose of the North”. Along the way, visit a local market near Lampang, stop at a pineapple plantation, and have lunch tasting a Northern Thailand specialty: Khao Soy, a dish of pasta and chicken in a savory coconut curry sauce. Finally, you will reach the city of Chiang Mai, which is charmed by a multitude of magnificent temples and surrounded by beautiful scenery. After checking in at the hotel, you will have some time to relax before a classic cantoke dinner awaits you in the evening. Typical dishes of northern Thailand are framed by traditional dances of the mountain peoples. A feast for all the senses! Overnight in Chiang Mai.

9th day Food donation to the monks & last street food tour

Early in the morning, you will attend a ceremony that is as common as it is valuable for Thais. They wait with the faithful on the side of the road for the passing monks and donate food to them. For the monks, it is the only permitted purchase of bread and the donors receive thereby merits for the next life cycle. After your own breakfast, drive out of the city to one of the many coffee and tea plantations. Of course, you can also taste the freshly brewed products. Back in Chiang Mai, on a final street food tour you will get an impression of the variety of street cuisines in northern Thailand. Overnight in Chiang Mai.

Day 10: Farewell to Thailand

Your unforgettable journey of connoisseurs through Thailand is coming to an end, but the precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories from your journey into the realm of the senses will accompany you forever. Look forward to letting your friends and family share your experiences in the distance at home. With countless impressions of the fascinating variety of Thai cuisine and one or the other recipe in your luggage, your driver will take you to the airport for your return flight home.

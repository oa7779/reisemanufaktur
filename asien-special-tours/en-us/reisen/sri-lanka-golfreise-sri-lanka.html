Day 1 Arrival in Colombo

Ayubowan and welcome to Sri Lanka! Upon arrival at the airport, you will be met and driven to your hotel in Colombo. Relax a little from your flight and enjoy your free time. Alternatively, on a city tour with your chauffeur in the evening, you can get a first impression of the impressive city of Colombo. Overnight in Colombo.

2nd day of golf at Royal Colombo Golf Club

After a restorative breakfast, swing your golf club. Did you know that Sri Lanka has three of the best 18-hole golf courses in the whole of South Asia? The legendary Royal Colombo Golf Club was founded over 130 years ago and is the second oldest Royal Golf Club outside of the UK. The nearly 6 kilometers long course is varied and has a few challenging water hazards. The tree-lined fairways and greens are well maintained and make the heart of any golfer beat faster. After playing golf, you will be taken back to your hotel. Overnight in Colombo.

3rd day Departure to Kandy

Start the day relaxed. In the morning you will be picked up and drive to Kandy. Optionally, we are happy to arrange a private helicopter transfer to Kandy. The former royal city of Kandy, lies in the middle of the central mountains about 500 meters above sea level. Despite its size, it is still very much influenced by ancient traditions. The last king to rule here was Sri Wickrama Rajasinghe, then from 1815 the British took power here. In the middle of the city lies a lake - perfect for a walk around. You will be taken to your hotel and can enjoy the amenities of your hotel in the afternoon. Overnight in Kandy.

Day 4 The Botanical Garden of Peradeniya with a botanist

Today, Sri Lanka’s colorful plant world awaits you. You will visit Peradeniya, a good five kilometers west of Kandy, the second largest botanical garden in all of Asia – and without a doubt one of the most beautiful. It was created 700 years ago as a pleasure garden by King Vikramabahu III, but received its current form only in the 19th century by the British. Around 4000 different plant species grow here. Together with the experienced botanist Bandara Palipana, who has worked here for over 30 years, you will walk through avenues of palm trees, past colorful orchids, ponds and cleanly trimmed box trees. Listen with interest to the many stories that Mr. Bandara Palipana has to tell. The main attraction is undoubtedly the tooth temple, in which a canine of Buddha is said to be kept in a golden shrine. According to tradition, this tooth emanates spiritual power, making the sanctuary one of the most important pilgrimage sites in the country for Buddhists. The temple was built in several sections between 1687 and 1782 to protect and preserve the dental relic. In the evening you are invited to a cultural show with various dances of Sri Lanka. A day full of impressions and experiences comes to an end. Overnight in Kandy.

5.-6. Playing Day Golf at Victoria Golf Club

On these two days you can pursue your passion, playing golf. Victoria Golf Club is located about 1 hour from Kandy and is one of the “100 most beautiful golf courses in the world”. Your hotel for the next few days is located on the grounds of the Victoria Golf Club. Let yourself be enchanted by the colonial charm and special atmosphere. The award-winning 18-hole course, designed by Donald Steele, covers 500 hectares. Dreamlikely nestled in the picturesque hillside landscape and tropical vegetation, this golf course is certainly one of the highlights of your Sri Lanka trip. Overnight in Kandy.

7th day Departure to Nuwara Eliya

After breakfast, leave Kandy and continue on to Nuwara Eliya. The City of Light, Nuwara Eliya, is located in the central highlands of Sri Lanka. The picture book landscape and the temperate climate make this city very special. It is located 1,868 meters above sea level and is considered the most important place for tea production in Sri Lanka. Gracefully towers the highest mountain in the country, the “Pidurutalagala”, over the lush green tea fields of the area. The city carries the nickname “Little England”, because much reminds of the time of the past empire. The British settled here to cultivate tea. Thus, the mountains around Nuwara Eliya are still known as the main growing area of the famous Ceylon tea. Overnight in Nuwara Eliya.

8th day of golf in Nuwara Eliya

Today you can play golf again. Playing golf in Nuwara Eliya means being transported back more than 100 years to the colonial times of Sri Lanka. The Nuwara Eliya Golf Club was founded in 1889 and is one of the oldest golf courses in Asia. This historic and traditional golf course is surrounded by the lush green tea plantations of the area. The 18-hole course is located on a 40-hectare area and lies at 1830 meters above sea level. Overnight in Nuwara Eliya.

Day 9 To the South Coast to Hambantota

In the morning, make your way to the south coast of Sri Lanka. From the hilly highlands we go to Hambantota. Enjoy the overland journey and make photo stops when and where you want. In the afternoon, you will arrive at Hambantota and be taken to your hotel. Shangri La’s Hambantota Golf Resort & Spa is located on a spacious complex and offers numerous activities such as: Water sports, gym, bicycle rental and its own 18 golf course. The golf course is landscaped very differently and thus provides a lot of variety. Optionally, we are happy to organize a private helicopter transfer to Hambantota for you. Overnight in Hambantota.

10.-13. Day bathing holidays in Hambantota

Without guidance

These days are yours. Take a break and let yourself be really good. Treat yourself to a soothing wellness application, let yourself be pampered by the hotel’s culinary delights or relax at one of the three swimming pools. Overnight in Hambantota.

Day 14 Return to Colombo

Today it is called packing suitcases, because it goes back to Colombo. Take a look back at the many new impressions of this wonderful journey and enjoy your last evening in Sri Lanka. Optionally, we are happy to organize a private helicopter transfer to Colombo for you. Overnight in Colombo.

15th day Departure from Sri Lanka

Even when your holiday is over, the precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories of your trip will accompany you forever. Look forward to letting your friends and family share your experiences in the distance at home. Your guide will take you back to Colombo International Airport for your return flight.

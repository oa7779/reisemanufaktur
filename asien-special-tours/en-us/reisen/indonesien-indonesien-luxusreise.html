Their accommodations are the exclusive Aman resorts, which with their first-class service create exceptional retreats that leave hardly a wish unfulfilled. Always set your own pace, which makes the heart and soul wide – and the head free. Bring your body and mind in harmony and forget about any everyday stress. Decide for yourself whether you want to be pampered with a relaxing wellness application, admire the colorful biodiversity of the underwater world on a snorkeling trip or whether you want to be enchanted by the cultural treasures of Indonesia. 1st day Arrival in Yogyakarta and onwards to Magelang

Selamat datang and welcome to Java! Upon arrival in Yogyakarta, you will be expected and driven by your chauffeur to Magelang. After a two-hour drive, you will reach your luxury hotel for the next few days. When you arrive, feel the enchanting magic of this special place. Amanjiwo means “peaceful soul” and is located in a natural amphitheater surrounded by the Menoreh hills and the plain of Kedu. Enjoy the view of Borobudur, the largest Buddhist temple in the world, whose architecture was considered a model for Amanjiwo. The 36 tasteful and elegant suites are arranged in two crescents around the main building and offer you a magnificent view of the surrounding lush green travel fields. Forget about everyday stress and immerse yourself in your personal dream world. Overnight in Magelang.

2-4. Day Temple world of Borobudur, bicycle tour and recreation

These days are all about the incomparable natural beauty and the cultural treasures of central Java. Always set your own pace and enjoy your very private moments. Visit the fascinating temple world of Borobudur and learn about the Buddhist history of Indonesia. This pyramid-shaped temple is now a World Heritage Site. It is said that the many terraces with stone reliefs show the faithful the way to nirvana. Or take a bicycle tour to the surrounding hills and rice terraces. If you’re more into relaxation and tranquility, swim a few laps in the 40-meter Infinity Pool and enjoy the amenities of your world-class hotel. Overnight in Magelang.

5th Day Flight to Bali and Driving to Ubud

After an extensive breakfast, it is necessary to pack suitcases. You will then be taken to Yogyakarta Airport, from where you will fly on to the neighboring island of Bali. Upon arrival in Denpasar, you will already be expected and brought to Ubud by your chauffeur. Your luxury hotel Amandari is beautifully located high above the Ayung Valley with fantastic views of the surrounding jungle and rice terraces. Amandari means “peaceful spirits” and is modeled on a traditional Balinese village. The detached villas and suites with private gardens and open-air bathtubs leave nothing to be desired. Relax a little from your short flight and use yoga to reconcile your body and mind. Overnight in Ubud

6.-8. Day Art & Culture, Hike and Balinese Dance Course

Ubud is characterized by many temples, museums, art galleries and a far-reaching artistic heritage. Because in the 1930s, Western artists and intellectuals were often invited there. The place as a starting point for the next few days will inspire you! Mix with the locals and visit a lively local market with delicious delicacies. Do you prefer it sporty? Then take a trekking tour through the lush green rice fields around Ubud. You will also have the opportunity to learn a piece of Balinese culture yourself during a dance course. Of course, rest and rest do not fall short on these days. Let every wish be read from the eyes and enjoy your days in Bali, the island of the gods. Overnight in Ubud.

9th day Flight to Sumbawa and transfer to Moyo Island

Today your wonderful journey continues. From your chauffeur, you will be taken to the airport and fly on to Sumbawa. Here you are already expected and continue on Moyo Island, surrounded by unspoilt nature and the marine reserve of the Floressee. Amanwana means “peaceful forest” and is the only resort on the island. Surrounded by powder-white sandy beaches, crystal clear waters and unspoilt coral reefs, this tropical paradise offers luxury at the highest level. The 20 exclusive tents are furnished with elegant furniture and Indonesian artwork. There is also a lot to be said for culinary. Traditional recipes and freshly caught ingredients directly from the sea make the heart of every gourmet beat faster. Enjoy a first-class service and spend a few enchanting days that you will not soon forget. Overnight on Moyo Island. Note: You can also fly from Bali by seaplane directly to Moyo Island. We are happy to make you an offer for this.

10.-14. Day holiday paradise Moyo Island

Let your soul really dangle and enjoy moments when time seems to stop. Explore the fascinating underwater world with its colorful fish and corals with diving glasses. Take a jungle hike to a waterfall and a bat cave in the heart of the island. Read a good book, relax on the beach and enjoy the sweet nothing-doing. Overnight on Moyo Island.

15th day Departure from Moyo Island

Your unforgettable journey draws to a close; you will be picked up and taken to Sumbawa Airport. Even if your holiday is soon over – the precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories of your trip will accompany you forever. Look forward to letting your friends and family share your experiences in the distance at home.

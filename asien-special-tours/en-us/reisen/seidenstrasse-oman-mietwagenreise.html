From impressive fjords to magical desert landscapes, discover the beauty of Oman on your individual Oman rental car journey. Ask now!

On this Oman rental car trip, you will cross a multifaceted country full of charming coastal towns, magical desert camps, breathtaking mountain ranges and green oases. Get to know a new culture, meet the hospitable residents and soak up the feeling of 1001 nights. Your unrivalled journey begins with a unique cruise through the fjord landscape of northern Oman. Then discover the Sultanate with the rental car at your own pace and with completely flexible daily arrangements. Live the new zeitgeist of travel and feel the magic of the Orient. A dream of sand and sea is waiting to be discovered by you.

1st day Arrival in Oman

Welcome to Oman! Upon arrival, you will already be expected and brought to your hotel. Look forward to exciting days full of new impressions! Oman offers you what your heart desires: beaches, deserts, a wildlife and a primordial flora and fauna. Overnight in Muscat.

2nd day trip to northern Oman

In the morning, a short flight into northern Oman will take you to Khasab. Here you are already expected and driven to the port. You board your yacht and start the unforgettable journey through the fjords of Oman. It is not without reason that this natural paradise is also called the “Norway of the Orient”. In the early evening, the anchor is thrown and you have time for snorkeling, swimming, fishing or kayak driving. Let the soul dangle and begin your dream journey. Overnight on board.

Day 3 The Norway of the Orient - Drive through the fjords

Today you enjoy the day on the water and your yacht. You will drive through the beautiful fjord landscape, past islands that tell stories from ancient times. When visiting small villages that can only be reached by sea, you can feel the historical zeitgeist. In the afternoon you will have time to swim and snorkel the colorful underwater world up close. Overnight on board.

Day 4 Mountain Safari by Jebel Harim

At breakfast you will already be greeted by an enchanting sunrise. In the morning you have some time to swim before getting ready for a mountain safari through Jebel Harim. From here, it is early afternoon by land back to Khasab and your luxurious hotel. The rest of the day is at your disposal. Overnight in Khasab.

Day 5 Back to Muscat

In the morning, you will be taken to Khasab airport and fly back to Muscat. Here you will receive your rental car and can move into your wonderful hotel on the beach. Take advantage of the rest of the day to discover Muscat and stroll over the vibrant souk. A visit to one of these traditional markets will enchant you into the shining world of the Orient. Overnight in Muscat.

Day 6: Bimah Sinkhole and Wadi Shab

After breakfast, continue your Oman rental car trip to Sur. You will drive through the village of Quriyat, which is guarded by an imposing fortress from the 18th century. Then head to Fins Beach and Bimah Crater, a natural hole filled with blue-green seawater. Your next destination is the Wadi Shab. Beautiful turquoise-blue basins offer a welcome and impressive change from the otherwise barren landscape. Hop on a boat and cruise the wadi before taking a 45-minute hike through the area. Overnight in Ras Al Jinz.

7th day Turtles, oases and desert paradise

Today begins in the early hours of the morning and offers a special highlight of your Oman car rental trip. They will oppose 04.45 Clock picked up by rangers at your hotel and place an approximately 15 A minute walk in. The destination of the excursion is the green turtles that can be found in the tranquility of Ras Al Jinz beach – a truly extraordinary animal encounter. You will be back in time for breakfast. Then continue on to the desert to Wahiba Sands. Before you reach it, make a stop at Wadi Bani Khalid. Jump into deep blue water and cool down in the middle of the desert. Oman is known for its numerous wadis or natural pools. Sand and water are never far apart. Make another stop at Wadi Bani Khalid. If you like it sporty, you can also go for a swim here. Enjoy the here and now in a dreamlike setting. Overnight in Wahiba Sands.

8th Day Desert Magic in the Expanses of Oman

Today is at your disposal. Take a desert safari, try surfing on the dunes or ride on a dromedary. Of course, you can also listen to the silence of the desert and enjoy every precious moment in this special place. Overnight in Wahiba Sands.

Day 9 Over the rooftops of Nizva

Today, your journey will take you to the Jebel Akhdar Mountains. First you will visit the city of Nizwa with the fort of the same name. High above the roofs of the city, the largest fortress of the Arabian Peninsula was built in 1668. It was once used as the headquarters of Iman. Today, from up there, you have a wonderful view of the buildings and the bustling market. Your next visit will take you past the round towers of the fortress up to the "own" museum. You will also visit the goat market of Nizwa. Afterwards, on your way through the desert, you will pass the old oasis town of Birkat al Mauz. Here are still old ruins of a former settlement. Continue the journey to Jebel Akhdar, called “green mountains” in Arabic. Overnight in Jebel Akhdar.

10. -11. Day Mountains around Jebel Akhdar

The next few days are at your disposal. Relax in your unique hotel or take advantage of the many excursion opportunities. Hiking tours, climbing routes, visiting the surrounding villages and much more will surely give you an unforgettable time. Overnight in Jebel Akhdar.

12th Day Luxurious Beach Days at Al Bustan Palace in Muscat

Welcome to your last destination of your dream journey. You leave the majestic mountain world and drive back to Muscat. There you will reside at the Al Bustan Palace, a real jewel in Muscat. Between the impressive Hadschar mountains towers this renowned luxury hotel, which shines among other things with its magnificent, oriental construction. With its 250 rooms and suites, the resort offers an extra class retreat. Feel the freedom and immerse yourself in the magical world of the Orient. During a walk on the private beach or a soothing spa application, you will feel the inner peace and can simply let yourself fall. Let yourself also be pampered culinary and convince yourself of the arts of the tea sommelier. Just listen to the sound of the sea and discover moments of happiness for the soul. Overnight in Muscat.

13-14 Day Recharge & Relax

After you have gained many exciting impressions of the cultural and natural treasures of Oman as well as its friendly inhabitants on your round trip, you can relax once again at the end of your holiday. You will spend a few days on the beach of Muscat. Take your time to create new energy and let the experiences of your unique Oman car rental trip pass in peace. Overnight in Muscat.

15th day Departure from Oman

Today is at your disposal. After check-out, you can continue to use the public areas of the hotel. I am happy to arrange a late check-out so that you can use your room until your departure (at an additional cost). Your time out from everyday life is coming to an end. Even if your holiday is soon over, the precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories of your trip will accompany you forever. Look forward to letting your friends and family share your experiences in the distance at home. Your driver will take you for your return flight to the airport in Muscat.

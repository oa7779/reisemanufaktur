Australia Round Trip - Tropical Queensland on the Great Barrier Reef and metropolis Sydney

Australia Round Trip - Enjoy tropical nature with white beaches and the metropolis of Sydney with Asia Special Tours. This is a question.

Majestic landscapes, endless secluded beaches and a unique flora and fauna await the visitor to Australia. It is the differences that fascinate: the vibrant, colorful life in the metropolises, the endless sandy beaches and the deep blue water of the Pacific Ocean with the creature Great Barrier Reef, which is visible even from space. Let yourself be transported back to prehistoric times in the oldest rainforest of the, the Daintree Rainforest north of Cairns, explore Kakadu National Park and enjoy the hustle and bustle of one of the most beautiful cities in the world: Sydney.

Trip information

Itinerary

19 days
Darwin – Kakadu National Park – Arnhem Land – Cairns – Daintree National Park – Cape Tribulation – Cooktown – Port Douglas – Kuranda – Orpheus Island – Great Barrier Reef – Sydney

  • Aboriginal rock painting, mangrove migration with Aborigines

  • Bush hike and visit to a bush cabin
  • Kuranda by floating railway over rainforest and scenic railway
  • Helicopter flight over the Great Barrier Reef

Day 1 Arrival in Darwin

You will be received at the airport in Darwin and taken to the hotel. The rest of the day is at your disposal. Arrive and relax from the long flight journey. We recommend a walk through the center or a visit to the markets.

Day 2 - All-wheel drive car to Kakadu National Park

Your guide will pick you up from your hotel at 8 a.m. in your luxurious, fully equipped, air-conditioned four-wheel drive car. The first stop is Fogg Dam Conservation Reserve, an extensive open wetland where many colorful birds live during the dry season. After a drive over the Marrakai Plains and Adelaide as well as Mary River, we take our morning tea or coffee break at Purple Mango Cafe. Then take the Arnhem Highway for a cruise on the famous Corroboree Billabong - part of the Mary River System. There are countless bird species to see here as well as the large saltwater crocodile. Later, drive over the old Jim Jim 4WD Track into Kakadu National Park. You will have the opportunity to observe the diversity of birds and wildlife along the waterways and we will visit the Warradjan Aboriginal Cultural Centre.

3rd day Australian jungle and Aboriginal rock paintings

This morning after breakfast we will head to Nanguluwur, where there is great rock art and a settlement of local Aboriginal people to discover. These sites are less well known, so you’ll be off the tourist trails. You will see some excellent examples of rock art, including the rare and ancient "dynamic figures", "X-ray" style art and the so-called art of the "contact period", which represents the first contact of Aboriginal people with the whites. Your guide will be happy to explain these unique testimonials to you. After lunch, we drive south on a 4WD track to the Maguk Gorge (Barramundi Gorge) and spend the afternoon in this fantastic area. At the end of a rainforest walk, you can swim on request and refresh yourself in the natural diving pool under the cascade of a waterfall.

4th Day Billabongs in Magela Wetlands and Aboriginal Art

Today, after breakfast, continue north along the Magela Wetlands and follow a steep step where you will take a guided tour of the Aboriginal people on the eastern Alligator River. You will enjoy a fantastic view of the Arnhem Escarpment and get to know many aspects of the local Aboriginal culture. After a short boat trip, with special permission from the traditional owners, we will head to Arnhem Land, where we will visit a small Aboriginal community called Gunbalanya. Here you can meet the local artists and weavers and watch them at work. From the settlement, there is a magnificent view of the Arnhem Land wetlands. After lunch, we start our journey back to Darwin and see more wetlands and billabongs along the way. We take a break at Bark Hut Inn and come back to Darwin in the late afternoon.
NOTE: We reserve the right to change this program due to weather conditions, seasonal road conditions or Aboriginal cultural reasons.

Day 5 flight from Darwin to Cairns

Today we will take you from your hotel to Darwin Airport, where you will board a domestic flight to Cairns. At the airport in Cairns, you will be picked up and taken to your hotel in the center. The rest of the day is at your disposal to explore Cairns. Stroll along the Esplanade with its sandy beach lagoon or enjoy the local and international cuisine in one of the countless cafes and restaurants.

6th Day Fauna and Flora of the Rainforest with Aboriginal Culture

Today you will embark on one of Australia’s most beautiful coastal roads, reaching Cooya Beach to meet members of the Kubirri Warra tribe and join one of their beach, wattage and mangrove walks. They follow the traditions of their ancestors who have led their traditional lives for thousands of years. Today, the tribe teaches its guests how to throw a spear, hunt and watch the land and its wildlife. Enjoy a little refreshment before continuing to Daintree Village, where you can enjoy a one-hour boat ride with crocodile and wildlife viewing. Now have a picnic before visiting the spectacular Niau Falls on a secluded 450-acre private property reserved exclusively for this tour. The falls are an untouched natural wonder waiting to be discovered. You will see strange plants and learn about the French settler family and its history. It is also time to swim (if desired) in this magical rainforest. Leave the falls and head to Cape Tribulation crossing the Daintree River.

Day 7 Wilderness at Daintree National Park and Cooktown

Have a leisurely breakfast before discovering the heart of Daintree National Park on Bama Way. Drive on the 4WD Bloomfield Track today, where you will cross the wilderness with its rivers and mountain ranges. Arrive in Wujal Wujal and visit Bloomfield Falls accompanied by the Walker family who are happy to share their knowledge of this special and sacred place with you. You will then continue to Helenvale, where you will have lunch at Lion’s Den Hotel, a famous Australian bush bar founded in 1875. After lunch, visit the Black Mountains, a unique geological attraction that is at the center of mysterious stories and plays a major role in Aboriginal legends. Continue to Cooktown, where you’ll enjoy great 360-degree panoramic views from Grassy Hill on Cooktown, the Coral Sea and the Endeavour River.

8th Day Rock Art and the Tradition of the Nugalwarra Clan

After breakfast, head to the Aboriginal Rock Art sites of Nugalwarra and meet with Nugalwarra Elder, Willie Gordon. Willie, the traditional storyteller of the Nugal-Warra clan, takes you to the rock paintings of his ancestors in a stunning landscape high in the hills above Hope Vale. You will visit six sites, including the Cave of Reconciliation and an ancestral birthplace where Willie tells the stories behind the art. Stories that can only be told by the elder of the Nugal clan. He explains how the paintings bear witness to the essence of life and the teachings of his people. With his broad smile and contagious laughter, Willie gives you an amazing insight into Aboriginal society and its spirituality and lets them share in the songs of his people. The tour includes an approximately 30-minute bush hike on easy terrain. Water is available and cameras are welcome. Return to Cooktown for a delicious lunch at the Nature Powerhouse Museum before taking a ride across the backbone of the Great Dividing Range and experience the spectacular scenery of the Far North Queensland Outback, finally reaching Port Douglas.

9th day Port Douglas

Spend a quiet day at your booked resort today. In Port Douglas, the rainforest meets the beach. You can have a relaxing beach day or take another trip to the rainforest, for example to Mossman. Let it go well, not least in the fantastic restaurants with delicious dishes of fresh fish and seafood of all kinds.

10th day floating train, historic train and rainforest in Port Douglas

Today, a unique experience is on the agenda. They will be picked up at their Port Douglas resort and taken to the Skyrail Rainforest Cableway departure station. You ride a gondola over the huge treetops of the jungle and have the opportunity to take guided walks with the local rangers at two stations. You will enter overwhelming viewpoints and get an insight into the history of this wilderness, which is a UNESCO World Heritage site. Finally, you will reach Kuranda, a village in the middle of the jungle with many restaurants and market stalls, where you will have enough time to stay quiet. Back then with the Kuranda Scenic Railway, a historic railway line with great views of the coast around Cairns. Keep an eye out for the falls along the way and enjoy the stops at the Barron River Gorge. They will be taken back to their resort in Port Douglas.

Day 11 Great Barrier Reef from Above

What would a stay in Queensland be without the Great Barrier Reef experience? This highlight of Australia is worth it that you fully enjoy it. Today you drive from Port Douglas in a good hour to the airport in Cairns, where a helicopter is ready for you. On the 1.5-hour flight to their destination, Orpheus Island, you will fly over the Great Barrier Reef and experience this natural wonder of the world in all its extent and full splendor. Tropical islands between lagoons and colorful corals can be viewed extensively from a bird’s eye view on this flight and possibly spot one or the other large ocean dweller from the air.  

12th day Island Stay Orpheus Island

Orpheus Island with its exclusive and private lodge is located right in the protected area of the Great Barrier Reef. You can hardly get closer to the reef. The excellent resort is known for its exclusivity with a maximum of 42 guests, no day visitors are allowed. You can swim in the infinity pool or visit one of the nearby beaches. Enjoy the excellent food and relax at the spa. The island is of course an Eldorado for water activities of all kinds such as snorkeling, diving, fishing etc. Take her private tender boat and sail to her personal favorite beach or have a romantic dinner served for a couple right on the beach. All this and much more can be experienced in this unique island lodge.

13th Day Relaxation at Orpheus Island

Another day to fully enjoy your stay at the Great Barrier Reef and relax. Let the sun determine its rhythm and the warm water of the Pacific rinse around its skin. Look at colorful corals and exotic fish under water. A special feature of the island is the breeding station for giant clams, which are known for their colorful colors. Enjoy the delights of the excellent island cuisine and relax with a massage under the palm trees.

14th day helicopter flight to Cairns and onward flight to Sydney

The return flight from your island dream is on the way. Today you can experience the mighty Great Barrier Reef from the air once again as you take off from Orpheus Island by helicopter and fly to Cairns. Here you board the plane to Sydney. The rest of the day is at your disposal to experience the world city of Sydney. We recommend a walking tour in the historic district “The Rocks” or a ride on the viewing tower “Sydney Tower” in the heart of the city with a view of the natural harbor.

Experience the 15th day of Sydney in the luxury classic car from 1964

Your day starts at 9.30 a.m., where you’ll be greeted by your local guide and his buddy Horace – a 1964 vintage car and the most popular luxury car ever made in Australia. Stylishly cruise through Sydney, with the beguiling scent of fine leather in your nose, leaning back into soft lambskin seat covers. Start in the center with a ride around “The Rocks”. Thanks to your guide, you can discover a thriving inner-city scene where Aboriginal murals and contemporary street art decorate the alleys with turn-of-the-century terrace houses. Get ready for the stories of rising home prices, local architecture, Aboriginal lifestyle and culture. No trip to Sydney would be complete without a visit to the beaches. First, we will take you to a cemetery by the sea that offers breathtaking views of the sandstone coast. From there, you’ll head to three of Sydney’s most famous inner-city beaches: Bronte, Tamarama and Bondi. Cruise through the hills of Sydney’s most exclusive suburbs and arrive at your final destination: a secluded beach with panoramic views of the harbor!

16th day backstage at Sydney Opera House and flight safari to Cottage Point

You have to get up early because today you can feel like a star of the famous Sydney Opera House. You get admission to the exclusive backstage rooms that are otherwise reserved only for the stars. You will be on stage where celebrities such as Dame Joan Sutherland, Blondie, Pavarotti and Australian Idols have performed. Feel the glamour in the wardrobes and hear the events that take place behind the curtain. A Green Room breakfast reserved for artists and staff is included (closed on Sundays and public holidays). With a seaplane, we will take you from downtown Sydney to a quiet bay at the excellent Cottage Point Inn in 20 minutes. This fine, small restaurant is located right on the waterfront in Ku-Ring-Gai Chasing National Park and is known for its exceptional cuisine using Australian specialties. Enjoy a relaxing 3-course lunch before boarding the seaplane again and taking your flight back to Sydney Harbour. Along the way, you will have a unique opportunity to take great photos of the Harbour Bridge and the Opera House to finally land again in Rose Bay.

Day 17 Excursion to the Blue Mountains

Today we head west to Featherdale Wildlife Park, where you get close to the typical Australian wildlife. Today’s destination is the Blue Mountains (World Heritage Site), a more than 1000 m high sandstone plateau with deep gorges and waterfalls. The name comes from the bluish haze that typically lies over the dense eucalyptus forests of the national park. At the Wentworth Falls, explore the Princess Lookout with fantastic views of the trees. At Echo Point with the world-famous rock formation “The Three Sisters”, visit the Waradah Aboriginal Centre, a cultural center of native Aboriginal tribes. Afterwards we will visit the Govetts Leap viewpoint and enjoy a delicious lunch in a historic inn. The journey continues to Mt Banks and the Mt Tomah Botanical Garden. After crossing the Hawkesbury River, you will find a short stop in the country town of Windsor before returning to the City via Harbour Bridge.

18th day of Sydney Pure

Spend today in Sydney at your free disposal. Go shopping for yourself and your loved ones at home or see one of the many museums, visit the Sydney Aquarium or drive up the Sydney Tower. Take the ferry from Circular Quay to Manly Beach or visit the famous Bondi Beach. Sydney has more than enough to offer to spend a fantastic day. 

19th Day Home Travel from Sydney

Today the program ends. We will pick you up from the hotel and take you to the airport. Farewell and return or onward flight

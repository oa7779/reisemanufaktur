Highlights of New Zealand's North Island and Cook Island's South Sea Dream

New Zealand South Sea Trip: Experience the highlights of the North Island and the Cook Islands South Sea Dream on a personalized trip with Asia Special Tours.

In hardly any place in the world awaits such a scenic diversity as in New Zealand. On your round trip through the North Island you will explore secluded coves, dense rainforests, mighty volcanoes and idyllic farmland. Let yourself be carried away into the adventure of New Zealand. Make a dream come true and visit the Cook Islands afterwards. Rarotongas coastline with 32 kilometers is surrounded by a dreamlike lagoon, where it teems with all kinds of colorful fish. The interior is characterized by fascinating volcanic peaks in the middle of tropical forests. Aitutaki has magnificent white beaches and authentic Polynesian culture. The island is surrounded by a spectacular lagoon and world famous as the pearl of the South Seas.

Day 1 Arrival in Auckland

Kia Ora! Today you reach Auckland. Upon arrival at Auckland Airport, you will be transferred to your hotel with a private transfer. Your room stands out 14 The clock is available. Use the rest of the day for relaxation or explore Auckland if you are still enterprising despite jet lag. Auckland is considered a melting pot of European, Asian and South Pacific cultures as well as indigenous Maori heritage. This variety brings with it a wealth of unique restaurants and shopping opportunities. Auckland’s waterfront location has earned the city the nickname “City of Sails”. Do not miss the imposing Sky Tower, located in the heart of the city. Of a height of 328 It is the highest tower in the southern hemisphere. In addition to the silhouette, these buildings shape dozens of dormant volcanic cones around Auckland. Overnight in Auckland.

2nd Day City Highlights and the Beauty of the Coromandel Peninsula

Today you will be picked up from the hotel and start your private city tour of Auckland. You will experience the highlights of this versatile metropolis and explore the nearby suburbs, islands and volcanoes of New Zealand’s largest city. Then take over the rental car of your choice and start your personal self-drive tour through the North Island of New Zealand. Not far from Auckland attracts the sparsely populated Coromandel Peninsula with its natural rainforest. 400 kilometers of white sand beaches, rustic villages, and a rainforest-covered interior make for a relaxing holiday atmosphere, far away from big city life. The Coromandel Peninsula is characterized by a mountain belt of volcanic origin. The contrast between beaches and lush green cultivated land gives this area a special scenic appeal. Cycling, swimming and hiking combined with a cuisine of fresh seafood and a vibrant artist community all add up to a great travel experience. Overnight on the Coromandel Peninsula.

Day 3 - Marine Protected Area TE WHANGANUI A HEI

Explore the first marine reserve on the Coromandel Peninsula today. It has the official name “Te Whanganui A Hei” (Cathedral Cove). The ship Seafari II is an aluminum pontoon boat with 2 glass-bottomed viewing windows. It is “Safe Ship Certified” and has a fully enclosed cabin for your comfort. Your crew is fully trained to ensure your safety and fun on board our ships. Dolphins, penguins and fur seals are absolutely magical if they take their time to visit us in the bay. Come on board and explore the wonderful marine world on our unique ECO adventure. Enjoy the view of the seabed area "Te Whanganui A Hei" with glass floor. If desired, you can snorkel. The equipment, including suits, will be provided to you on board free of charge. Overnight on the Coromandel Peninsula.

Day 4 Welcome to the Shipping

You will say goodbye to the Coromandel Peninsula and head to Matamata, where you will discover a piece of Middle-earth: On your 2-hour guided tour, you will visit the film set of the trilogies “Lord of the Rings” and the “Hobbit”, which could be better known as “Auenland”. During your tour, you will see the Hobbit caves, The Green Dragon Inn, The Mill and many more highlights. Via the port city of Tauranga in the 'Bay of Plenty', main cultivation area for kiwifruit, it goes on to Whakatane. The city, which is characterised by Maori history, lies with its beautiful harbor directly on the sea. Overnight in Whakatane.

Day 5 The active island volcano White Island and Rotorua

Experience a 5- to 6-hour adventure to explore New Zealand’s only active marine volcano – White Island. Located 49 km off the coast, this is one of the most accessible volcanoes in the world. Visitors can walk near seething steam holes, bubbling mud pits, hot volcanic streams and a lake full of steaming acids. The bright colors of yellow and orange, created by the rich sulfur content of the island, make the island a dream for photographers. Continue on to Rotorua. A volcanic lunar landscape, hot springs, seething mud ponds and a vibrant Maori culture: Rotorua is a fascinatingly foreign piece of earth. In the evening, visit Tamaki Maori Village, where you can enjoy a cultural show with hangi, the traditional food of the Maori. Overnight in Rotorua.

Day 6 Rotorua via Lake Taupo to Napier

Go to Taupo today. North of Taupo is Huka Falls, a dramatic river landscape. Lake Taupo is a crater lake that was once formed by a massive eruption of the Taupo volcano. The lake is larger than Lake Constance and a popular holiday destination. Enjoy the relaxed atmosphere on the shore of the lake. Board the Cruise Cat to the famous Maori Rock Carvings, which can only be reached by boat, and the scenic bays of Lake Taupo. An informative commentary will be provided on board about the volcanic history and Maori mythology surrounding Lake Taupo. Enjoy the magnificent views of secluded coves and the spectacular snow-capped mountain peaks of Tongariro National Park. Napier, located directly on the sea, was completely destroyed in 1931 during an enormous earthquake and rebuilt in the Art Deco style of the 1920s/30s. Since then, it has been considered the world’s best preserved city in this striking architectural style. Overnight in Napier.

Day 7 Wellington - the capital of New Zealand

Worth seeing is the surroundings of the Parliament with the former government buildings, of which the oldest from 1876 is considered the second largest timber construction in the world. Opposite is the current government. Architecturally striking is the modern cultivation, known as the “bee basket”. One should not miss a ride in the capital by cable car – the historic tram that goes up to the Botanical Garden. From here there is also a beautiful view of the city and its port. Overnight in Wellington

8th Day National Museum Te Papa and Weta Cave Workshop

For a glimpse into the country’s culture, today we have planned a visit to Te Papa, New Zealand’s National Museum, which conveys much worth knowing about the country through its innovative and highly interactive exhibition. Join the local crew on an exclusive Weta Cave Workshop tour behind the scenes of the world-renowned New Zealand film industry in the afternoon. See the impressive effects created for “The Lord of the Rings”, “The Hobbit”, “District 9” and many more movies. Stand on one of our stages. See through the windows that allow a direct view of the artists at work. Overnight in Wellington.

Day 9 - Ferry to Kapiti Island

To perfectly showcase the Kapiti Island phenomenon, take a day tour to Waiorua Bay at the northern end of Kapiti today. Here you can experience a unique balance between nature conservation, ecology and local cultural history. Experience the peace and quiet of the New Zealand bush on one of the hiking opportunities, interrupted only by birdsong. You can expect to see some of our most beautiful and endangered birds up close. Overnight in Paraparaumu.

10.-11. Tag Crater landscape Mordor

The Tongariro National Park contains three still active volcanoes, which are venerated as sacred by the Maori in several places of worship in the area. The wise foresight of Maori chief Te Heuheu Tukino laid the foundation for the establishment of the fourth oldest national park in the world. Lava casts flowed from several eruption channels for thousands of years. In October 1996, there were still heavy ejections. The barren crater landscape also served as a location for "Mordor" in "The Lord of the Rings". Enjoy this great landscape on your own. Overnight in Tongariro National Park

Day 12: Waitomo Caves to Auckland

Make your way back to Auckland today and stop at Waitomo. The Waitomo Caves are a limestone and fireflies cave. Millions of years of Earth's history can be read from the formations. With a guide, you can only see some fireflies up close in the “Cathedral”, the largest room of the cave. As soon as the lamps are turned off, the cave ceiling glows in thousands of small lights like the Milky Way. The "glowworms" are not related to the European St. John's beetle, but are the larva of a small insect that attracts and eats other insects with its light. Your guide will tell you many fascinating stories and show you the impressive natural wonders that will be remembered throughout your life. Your last day in New Zealand ends with a highlight: Enjoy your 3-course menu at the Auckland Skytower at an altitude of 190 meters with a stunning view over the metropolis of Auckland. Overnight in Auckland.

13th day Rental car and flight to Rarotonga over the date line

Today you drop off your rental car at the airport and check in for your flight to the Cook Islands. Do not be surprised: you will arrive a day earlier than you departed. Why that? The date line you fly over runs through the middle of the Pacific Ocean and so you arrive a day earlier than you departed. Crazy! Upon arrival in Rarotonga, you will be picked up and taken to your reserved beach bungalow on the white, fine sandy Titikaveka beach. Overnight in Rarotonga.

14-16. Day Adventure in Paradise

The scenic island, known locally as "Raro", is the largest and by far the most popular of the Cook Islands. The interior is characterized by fascinating volcanic peaks amidst lush tropical forests. Rarotonga’s 32-kilometer coastline is surrounded by a warm, shallow, aquamarine blue lagoon and there are virtually no multi-story houses on the island. There is a wide range of tourist activities, including snorkeling, diving, kite surfing, hiking, scenic flights and much more. With all its attractions, Rarotonga is an ideal place to soak up some sun, enjoy the relaxed atmosphere of the island and experience thousands of years of Polynesian history and culture. Overnight in Rarotonga.

17th day flight from Rarotonga to Aitutaki

With a private transfer today it goes back to the airport in 20 minutes. Climb an Air Rarotonga machine and enjoy the unique view of the tropical, green Rarotonga with its peaks contrasting with the deep blue of the Pacific Ocean and the turquoise lagoon. The approach to Aitutaki is indescribable! Your private driver will take you to your South Sea hideaway in one of the most famous lagoons in the world: Aitutaki! Overnight in Aitutaki.

Day 18 The South Seas in Perfection: Aitutaki

Just 45 minutes by plane from the main island of Rarotonga, this little paradise offers you peace and tranquility away from the hustle and bustle of the modern world. It has magnificent powder-white beaches, tropical rainforests, and authentic Polynesian culture. The island in the South Pacific is surrounded by a spectacular lagoon and is perfect for snorkeling and swimming because of the shallow, warm water. There are many small islets (Motus) in the lagoon and it is full of tropical fish and corals. Aitutaki is home to only about 2100 inhabitants, most of whom live in the capital Arutanga. The infrastructure on the island is simple. Overnight in Aitutaki.

19th day day cruise through the lagoon

Cruise with the 21-meter boat today 6 hours through the lagoon of Aitutaki to the upstream motus such as One Foot Island. A full day to absorb the indescribable colors of this South Sea paradise. Snorkel through the colorful coral world of the lagoon and relax on the snow-white beaches. A delicious buffet of local and international delicacies awaits on board. Your host will sweeten the day with authentic Polynesian stories and you will have a lot of fun on this trip with international audiences. The tour is run by a local operator and directly benefits the local community. And don’t forget your passport: One Foot Island offers its own post office and gives a unique stamp on the passport on request. A unique souvenir! Overnight in Aitutaki.

20th day Return or onward travel from Aitutaki

Today, your private driver takes you to the airport and you fly back to Rarotonga. Sit back on the plane and let the past days and weeks pass mentally. These memories will not be forgotten for a lifetime. After landing at Rarotonga International Airport, take your connecting flight home or to your next destination.

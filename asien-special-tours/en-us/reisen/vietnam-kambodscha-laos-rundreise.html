Cambodia & Laos Round Trip - Lifeline Mekong & Mysterious Temples

Let yourself be seduced by the countless cultural and scenic highlights in Laos and Cambodia. Feel the unforgettable magic in the legendary Angkor Wat, immerse yourself in the unique water world of the 4,000 islands, enjoy the beauty of the picturesque Bolaven Plateau high above the Mekong Valley and let yourself be captivated by the diversity of Phnom Penh. Get to know and love these two countries and their amiable inhabitants.

Day 1 Welcome to Luang Prabang

Welcome to Laos! Upon arrival at Luang Prabang Airport, you will be warmly welcomed by your personal guide and taken to your hotel. Already on the journey you can get first impressions of the probably best preserved traditional city in the whole of Southeast Asia. Enjoy the free time and stroll through the charming alleys of the city or relax in your hotel. Overnight in Luang Prabang.

2nd Day Alms of the Monks & rushing Kuang Si Waterfalls

For early risers, it is a special event to observe the orange-clad monks at their morning fundraising collection. At sunrise, the monks leave their pagodas to receive food from the inhabitants of Luang Prabang. If you would like to participate, your guide will help you prepare food donations and deliver them to the monks. Then climb the 328 or 410 stairs (depending on the entrance dial) up the Temple Mount Phou Si for an overwhelming view of Luang Prabang. During a stroll over the local market, immerse yourself in the morning’s bustling atmosphere. Afterwards, you will first go back to your hotel so that you can have breakfast in peace. During the morning, your guide will take you on a city tour. Visit Wat Visoun, the oldest temple still in use in Luang Prabang. Don’t miss Luang Prabang’s most famous temple, Wat Xieng Thong. In the afternoon, take a trip to the beautiful Khuong Si Falls, which invites you to a refreshing swim. A beautiful hiking trail through the forest leads you back to the car. Overnight in Luang Prabang.

3rd day boat trip to the mystical Buddha caves of Pak Ou

First of all, visit the former Royal Palace today – it houses the National Museum, which will bring you even closer to the culture and history of the country. Around noon you will take a small boat tour on the Mekong, the lifeline of Southeast Asia. Along the way, stop at the picturesque village of Thin Hong to get a better impression of daily life. Then you reach the interesting caves of Pak Ou. The limestone caves are located directly on the shore cliffs of the Mekong, about 25 kilometers north of Luang Prabang and are one of the most important Buddhist pilgrimage sites in Laos. On the way back to Luang Prabang, visit the craft village of Ban Xang Khong, also known as the “paper village”. There you will discover many typical Lao creations from Sa-Papier and observe craftsmen at work. Stop at the “Artisans du Mekong”, an art gallery that presents works by local artists. Your highlight: You will be welcomed by a craftsman of the village who will reveal to you the secrets of Sa-paper production. In a workshop with the local craftsman, you create your own lantern. Then you will reach Luang Prabang. Overnight in Luang Prabang.

Day 4 The picturesque limestone landscape of Vang Vieng

After breakfast, head south to Vang Vieng, a small town on the banks of the Nam Song River surrounded by extraordinary limestone rocks. The approximately seven-hour drive leads through a magnificent mountain landscape over numerous serpentines with breathtaking views. Around Vang Vieng, a variety of caves dig into the mighty Karst mountains. Some of the caves house Buddha figures and other religious symbols. A sunset cruise on the Nam Song River rounds off the day. Overnight in Vang Vieng.

Day 5 Cozy Capital Vientiane

Your goal today is Vientiane. In the sleepy capital of Laos you can still feel the colonial flair. Your guide will show you the most interesting places: Wat Simuang, Vientiane’s holiest place and Wat Sisaket, the seat of the Buddhist community. This pagoda houses hundreds of Buddha figures made of wood, bronze and stone. The temple Wat Pra Keo originally housed the Jadebuddha, now seen in Bangkok. You will also pass by That Luang Stupa and Patuxai Victoria Gate, known as the “Arc de Triomphe” of Laos. How about a stroll for the senses about the local market? The perfect ending in the evening is a walk on the Mekong at sunset. Overnight in Vientiane.

Day 6: Enchanting South Laos & Mysterious Ruins of Wat Phu

In the morning you will be taken to the airport and fly on to Pakse in southern Laos. After the eventful days in the north of the country, a charming journey through the scenic south awaits you. At the airport you are already expected and drive immediately to Champassak, to the impressive ruins of Wat Phu. Together with the cultural landscape of Champasak, they are part of the UNESCO World Heritage Site. This site was built in the 10th century under Angkor ruler Jayavarman IV as a Shiva sanctuary – before the establishment of Angkor Wat in Cambodia, which you will visit later. Enjoy the evening in your hotel directly on the Mekong. With a refreshing swim in the infinity pool, you can also let the view wander over the river landscape. How about a sundowner cocktail in this peaceful atmosphere? Overnight in Champassak.

Day 7: A world of 4,000 islands

A new day, a new goal. Today, the largest waterfalls in Southeast Asia, called Khone Phapheng, are waiting for you. By boat you will explore the unique island world of the Mekong. Here, where the Mekong finds its widest extent, over the millennia a landscape has emerged which, depending on the water level, produces only a few large and countless small islands in the river, some with a dimension of a few square kilometers, others only a few square meters in size. Drive to the Liphi Falls and the fishing village of Ban Khone. Enjoy the natural beauties and observe everyday life in this extraordinary region. After a 2.5-hour drive, you will reach Champasak in the evening. Overnight in Champasak.

8th Day Painting Bolaven Plateau

After breakfast, you will arrive at Paksong, the country’s most important place for coffee production and the gateway to the Bolaven Plateau, which is located at an altitude of 1,300 meters. Various ethnic groups live on the plateau, most of them living from agriculture. They grow coffee, tea and spices on fertile soil, and the French originally planted coffee, bananas and rubber trees on the plateau. The coffee that is grown in this area today is known all over the world. Far from the main tourist routes, you will experience the beauty of nature in South Laos. In the Bolaven Plateau, high above the Mekong Valley, there are also numerous rivers and streams that pour into the valley as waterfalls. Some of them you will visit today, such as the waterfalls Tad Fan, Tad Yuang and Tad Pha Suam. Overnight at the Bolaven Plateau.

9th day Change of scenery: from Laos to Cambodia

Now it is time to say goodbye to the magical Laos. You will be driven to Pakse airport and fly on to Cambodia. A new great adventure begins! The city of Siem Reap is your starting point in the next few days to explore the world-famous Angkor Park with about 40 square kilometers and more than 100 temples from the 9th to 13th centuries. In Siem Reap you will be expected by your guide and brought to your hotel. Let the evening end comfortably and look forward to the next few days. Overnight in Siem Reap.

Day 10: Angkor Wat and Jungle Temple Ta Prohm

Right in the morning, when it is still quiet, we head to the world-famous Angkor Wat Temple. Built in the first half of the 12th century, this World Heritage Site is the largest and undoubtedly most important monument of the Angkor Temple Group. The sanctuary is a masterpiece of Khmer architecture. Your guide will be able to tell you a lot of interesting things about the mythological meaning of the ornaments and statues. Among the shrines to be seen in Angkor is Ta Prohm, a 12th century temple. The place of worship was discovered by the French at the end of the 19th century and was deliberately preserved as the restorers found it. The semi-decomposed state and the overgrown roots of the surrounding tree giants give the place a mystical atmosphere. After so much culture, let the view once again wander into the distance as you enjoy the sunset at Pre Rup Temple. Overnight in Siem Reap.

11th Day Culture and Circus Art in Siem Reap

Today you will visit Angkor Thom – the “big city”. Start at the famous South Gate and see the 12th-century Bayon Temple. It is considered one of the most enigmatic temples of the Angkor group, consisting of 54 divine towers, decorated with 216 smiling faces. Other attractions include the Elephant Terrace, which once served as a ceremonial court and a huge viewpoint for royal audiences and ceremonies, and the “Terrace of Leper King”, a seven-meter-high platform. Banteay Srei, the “Citadel of Women”, impresses with its pink sandstone sculptures. In the evening, you will be a guest in a very special circus: In the “Phare” young people from poor backgrounds perform, who receive school education and acrobatics lessons through a social project. Although the actors are not yet as routine in everything as professional acrobats, they already bring impressive shows to the stage – in Siem Reap and on tours abroad. Overnight in Siem Reap. 

12th Day Swimming Villages & Lunch with a Family

Enjoy an extensive breakfast and let the day go on comfortably. Then set off to the impressive Tonle Sap Lake. The Tonle Sap is the liquid heart of Cambodia. Here, the stilt houses project several meters above the shallow water into the air. During a picnic in the house of a family, you have the opportunity to get to know the architecture and everyday life of these special villages quite authentically. If the weather conditions are favorable, then take a boat to a remote village where Vietnamese settlers have settled. Overnight in Siem Reap.

Day 13 in the midst of the bustle of the capital Phnom Penh

When visiting Cambodia, of course, the capital must not be missing. Once lived on the banks of the Mekong a lady named Penh. One day, when she saw a tree trunk floating on the river, she ordered her servant to bring it to land. And lo and behold, inside the tribe were five Buddha statues. The lady immediately poured a hill (phnom) to erect a pagoda for the sanctuaries. Thus, according to legend, Phnom Penh, today's state capital, was founded. After reaching Phnom Penh by domestic flight, your guide will show you the city’s treasures, such as the Royal Palace and the Silver Pagoda, named after the over 5,000 kilogram silver plates that grace the floor. Another attraction is the National Museum, which boasts a world-leading collection of ancient archaeological, religious and artistic artifacts from the 4th to 13th centuries. Over 5,000 exhibits show the cultural richness of the past kingdom. Overnight in Phnom Penh.

14th day Departure or extension to Cambodian coast

Your unforgettable journey draws to a close; you will be picked up and taken to Siem Reap Airport. Even if your holiday is soon over – the precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories of your trip will accompany you forever. If you do not yet want to say goodbye to Cambodia, we recommend that you spend some more relaxing days on the Cambodian coast. We are happy to make you an offer.

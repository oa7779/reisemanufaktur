Travel a country of superlatives and let yourself be inspired by the gigantic China! The perfect juxtaposition of hypermodern architecture and old, traditional magnificent buildings awaits you. Exciting culture and ancient history compete for the bet with mega shopping malls and a colorful art scene. The rapidly developing China delights every architecture - and art lover. 1st day Arrival in Beijing & walk through Sanlitun district

Welcome to China! Your trip starts in Beijing, where you will be expected by your guide when you arrive at the airport. As a political and cultural center of the country, Beijing can look back on a 3000-year history, which is reflected in a globally unique cultural heritage. Thanks to the 2008 Olympic Games, China’s capital, often dismissed as a giant village, has transformed into a modern world city. Just as the emperors once built their city as an image of the universe, China’s rulers of the 21st century created their capital as an image of a new, powerful China. You will be taken to your hotel, where you can first relax a little from your arrival. Then take a walk in the Sanlitun district, where there are many restaurants, bars and boutiques. Let your first evening in China end comfortably. Overnight in Beijing.

Day 2 Beijing's Mega-Buildings & Imperial Traces Through the Forbidden City

After breakfast, take a city tour of Beijing’s mega-built buildings. Architectural masterpieces include the State Theatre, CCTV Tower, Soho CBD and of course the Olympic grounds where you can see the famous bird’s nest and water cube. Keep your camera ready, because you will find photo opportunities in abundance. In the afternoon, visit the Forbidden City with the Imperial Palace. This magical place was the seat of the Chinese emperors of two dynasties – the Ming and the Qing – and for half a millennium was inaccessible to those who did not belong to the court. The crowd is as overwhelming as the architecture, the colorfully painted palace halls, the pagoda-like roofs. Also impressive are the powerful names of the magnificent buildings: “Palace of Heavenly Purity” is called one temple-like complex, the “Gate of the highest harmony” another, another gate in turn pays homage to the “divine military art”. Of course, you will also stroll over the Tiananmen Square – 500 meters wide and 880 meters long, the largest square on earth. Overnight in Beijing.

3rd day Glory Building Heaven Temple & Red Brick Art Museum

Many Beijingers get up very early and come to the parks to do sports. Especially tai chi is very popular, especially among older people. If you want, join the locals and try your hand at one or the other tai chi exercise as well. The Temple of Heaven, to which you will pay your visit this morning, is one of the outstanding testimonies of religious architecture in the Middle Kingdom, from the Ming period (1368 to 1644). An exquisite example of China’s wooden buildings constructed without the help of a single nail is the 39-meter-high round hall of harvest victims. Its three-step roof, whose deep blue bricks symbolize the color of the sky, rests on 28 pillars: the four most powerful in the middle represent the four seasons; the twice twelve columns arranged in a double ring represent the twelve months, as well as the two-hour traditional Chinese day segments. In the afternoon, visit the Red Brick Art Museum, the best modern art gallery in Beijing. An expert will accompany you through the museum and bring you closer to the specifics. Overnight in Beijing.

Day 4 Great Wall at Mutianyu

Today, one of the biggest highlights of your trip to China awaits you: the Great Wall at the Mutianyu section. To this day, many legends revolve around the spectacular building. It is often claimed that the wall is the only human edifice visible to the naked eye from the moon. The foreigner enjoys the excursion here like a journey through time: from the modern capital beyond the outskirts to the rugged mountainous area, where an army of workers once walled an empire. Since you are not going back to Beijing today, you have enough time for a hike along the wall. So you experience the Great Wall much more intensively than most other China travelers. Overnight in Mutianyu.

5th day of free time at the Great Wall & train ride to Pingyao

After breakfast you will have some free time in Mutianyu and you can deepen your discoveries from the previous day before you are picked up. You will travel by fast train to Pingyao, which you will reach in the afternoon and are already expected there at the station. A transfer will take you to the old town of Pingyao. Pingyao is a lively and ancient city at the same time. Here you will find old commercial signs on the houses instead of neon lamp signs. The city is surrounded by the longest, fully preserved city wall from the Ming period. In the shadow of the high, more than six kilometers long wall are one- and two-storey black brick houses. Overnight in Pingyao.

6th Day Traditional Chinese Architecture & Chinese Court Houses

Enjoy Pingyao with its wonderful cultural history. The city was founded in the 14th century and its architectural style impressively reflects feudal China. As the trade routes changed in the middle of the 19th century due to industrialization, Pingyao lost importance. In the 20th century, the city was too poor to demolish the walls that hindered traffic – as almost all other cities did. Fortunately! The city center was preserved and today you can still feel how ancient China lived. The entire old town was declared a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1997. Among other things, the city temple is very worth seeing. Then take a trip to the Hofburg of the Wang family. In Pingyao and its surroundings, there are still many typical Chinese court houses built in the Ming and Qing Dynasty according to the Yin-Yang theory, representing the highest level of traditional architecture. You will see numerous embellishments, among others. Stone figures, on the roof, at the door, in the courtyard, etc. and learn more about their symbolic meanings. You can also admire other Chinese arts here such as calligraphy, ink painting and wood carving. It can be said that a Chinese courthouse can be regarded as a cultural composition of all Chinese arts. Then return to Pingyao. Overnight in Pingyao.

7th day Visit with a pasta master & flight to Hangzhou

Today you will visit a pasta master. The province of Shanxi is the cradle of Chinese pasta. Here the pasta are not only food, but also works of art that are shaped in different figures. These are also prepared for important ceremonies and festivals and then sacrificed. In the afternoon we will head to Taiyuan, where you will be driven to the airport. Your next destination is Hangzhou. There you will already be expected by your guide and brought to the hotel. Overnight in Hangzhou.

8th day walk on the West Lake & visit to a traditional pharmacy

You will get to know the most beautiful sides of Hangzhou. Your tour begins spiritually with the Buddhist Temple of the Refuge of Souls. Walk through the temple and admire the Buddhist figures on the opposite mountain slopes, including the Laughing Buddha of Hangzhou. An outstanding attraction of the provincial capital is the West Sea. Artificially landscaped dams and islands where pastures and apricot trees grow complement the grace of the lake. Pavilions and tea houses line the shore. During a walk along the lake you have enough time to let the unique garden landscape affect you. Visit Hangzhou without visiting a tea plantation? – This is almost impossible. Finally, the dragon fountain tea “Lonjingcha” is produced in Hangzhou: perhaps the best green tea in China. Afterwards, you will walk through the old town and be introduced to the secrets of Traditional Chinese Medicine in a pharmacy. Overnight in Hangzhou.

9th Day Masterly Structures by Wang Shu Discover with an Expert

This day is dedicated to the works of Wang Shu, a world-famous architect and winner of the 2012 Pritzker Prize, who will be accompanied by an architecture expert to get even deeper into the impressive buildings. You will visit the village of Wencun in the county town of Fuyang, which is a model for the environmentally friendly and traditional conversion of an old village. In addition, visit the campus of the Chinese Academy of Art in Xiangshan District. Overnight in Hangzhou.

Day 10 Traditional Water Village and Cultural Hotspot Wuzhen

Today it is time to pack suitcases again, because your journey will take you on to Suzhou. On the way, visit the water village of Wuzhen. The East Chinese village is already more than 1,300 years old. The traditional water village of the Qing Dynasty is about 120 km from Shanghai, is located on the large Imperial Canal and is crossed by many small canals. Here, enchantingly narrow streets and picturesque water channels wind along the houses, painting a romantic picture of ancient China. Stone bridges span the waterways. In Wuzhen it seems that all life takes place on the water, as the small rivers represent the lifelines of the village and connect the village with the outside world. Wuzhen is also an increasingly important cultural place. Since 2013, the Wuzhen Theater Festival has been held annually (in October), which is now one of the most important cultural events in the country. Worth seeing numerous exhibitions, such as the Muxin Art Museum, a representative art gallery. Finally, you reach your daily destination Suzhou. Look forward to your special hotel for the next few days: The Designhotel Youxiong is the former residence of the Bai family, from which the world-famous architect I.M. Pei is from. Mr I.M. Pei designed the pyramid in front of the Louvre in Paris. Overnight in Suzhou.

Day 11 The Garden City of Suzhou & Bricks as Artwork

After breakfast, the garden town of Suzhou awaits you. The city enchants with its numerous canals and classic gardens. Through the gardens, Suzhou became famous and eventually developed into a cultural and intellectual center. The art of horticulture was to harmoniously design as many landscapes as possible in a confined space, offering both variety and opportunity for relaxation, but at the same time creating the feeling of size and expanse. You will visit the "Museum of Imperial Kiln Brick", a completely unknown but highly interesting museum. See how bricks are made, which were used at that time only for the Imperial Plants, among others for the Forbidden City. The complete production process, which lasts more than one year, is vividly presented. Brick of this type is called "Golden Bricks" and they are only found in Suzhou. They were synonymous with the most enduring building material in ancient times. They are also considered a work of art because they bring with them characteristics such as constancy, serenity, clarity and cultural symbolism. They have been the favorite material and artistic decoration of Chinese scholars for centuries. Overnight in Suzhou.

12th day “Roots of Shanghai” at Guangfulin Ruins Park & Masterpiece Intercontinental Shanghai Wonderland

In the morning we will continue to Songjiang, a suburb of Shanghai. There you will visit the Guangfulin Ruins Park. Ruins from about 4000 years ago were excavated here in the 1950s. Therefore, Guangfulin is also known as the "Roots of Shanghai". The ruin park is directly connected to the excavation site. Alone the very aesthetic design and the perfect combination of old and modern are worth a visit. The next architectural wonder awaits you: the spectacular Hotel Intercontinental Shanghai Wonderland. The hotel is located in a 100-meter-deep abandoned quarry. 17 of the total 19 floors are below sea level and 4 floors are under the water of an artificially created lake. The construction of the hotel took 12 years and was once considered impossible. The masterpiece was designed by world-leading design firm Atkins. This hotel will surely make your heart beat faster. Overnight in Shanghai.

Day 13 Into the glitter metropolis of Shanghai

Your last destination on this trip is the modern metropolis of Shanghai. The colonial past and the ambitious future of Shanghai will inspire you. Together with your guide, you will immediately start your exploration tour through this interesting city. Opposite the Bund is Pudong in the east of the city: a district of Shanghai that has sprung up in a few years, where today banks, corporations and luxury hotels reside in skyscrapers. Shanghai's landmark is the Oriental Pearl Tower, the television tower measures a proud 468 meters, the Shanghai Tower even 632 meters. Completed in 2015, the tower is the second tallest building in the world after Burj Khalifa. Go upstairs and let the view wander over the city from the observation platform. Then look forward to the Digital Art Museum “teamLab Borderless”, an interactive museum in which you as a visitor yourself become part of the art At the end of your eventful day, a very special experience awaits you: a bicycle tour through the former French concession. Let the lively metropolis come to you from a whole new perspective and gain a more intensive insight into Chinese everyday life.

14th day of free time in Shanghai

Without tour guide and chauffeur

This day is free for you. What are you doing today? Relax, shop or go on a sightseeing tour – decide! How about a stroll over the famous Nanjing Road, which is the largest shopping street in the world and the most popular in all of China? Enjoy the day in this brilliant mega-metropolis. Overnight in Shanghai.

Day 15 Farewell to China

Today, unfortunately, it means saying goodbye to a fascinating country from which you will take numerous lasting impressions with you. You will be taken to the airport from where you will start your onward or return journey.

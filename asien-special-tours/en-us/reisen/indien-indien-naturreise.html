India Nature Tour - The Tiger Route

India Nature Travel: Discover the noble cat on this individual India travel idea. The Tiger Route can be adapted to your wishes at any time.

Itinerary & services

English itinerary text is an offline machine translation of the German original for this team preview. Please confirm travel details with your advisor; the German source remains available through the language switch.

It is estimated that our planet now has only 3,000 to 5,000 wild tigers, most of them living in protected areas. The king of the jungle has lost much of his empire, but not of his fascination. On this journey, you will explore the habitat of this powerful predator in India’s national parks. Many other fascinating animals are also native here: look for leopards, Indian rhinos, gurs, wild elephants, the Nilgais, Asia's largest antelopes, Chinkara gazelles, toothed and horse deer, wild dogs, jackals, crocodiles and countless bird species. You explore the wilderness by jeep and take walks in nature. The exciting city of Delhi, the elegant jaipur, many magnificent temples and, of course, the unforgettable Taj Mahal complete the experience. If you are a great bird lover and would like to observe indigenous bird species in addition to the tigers and other large wildlife, we are happy to organize a visit to the Bharatpur Bird Sanctuary for you.

1st day Arrival in India & first explorations in New Delhi

Namaste and welcome to India! Your journey starts in Delhi, where you will be expected and driven to your accommodation upon arrival at the airport. Rest first or relax in your hotel. In the early afternoon, meet your guide to explore the imperial city of New Delhi with its vast avenues and imposing buildings. Because New Delhi also offers fascinating historical sights: for example, with the 72-meter-high Qutb minaret and the mausoleum of the great mogul Humayun, which is considered an architectural precursor to the Taj Mahal. Overnight in Delhi.

2nd day Old Delhi: a journey back in time to the past

After an extensive breakfast in the hotel, you will go on a journey through time in Old Delhi, where the Jama Masjid, one of the largest mosques in the world, testifies to Delhi’s past as the capital of Islamic India. Passing the Red Fort, the palace complex of the Moguls, which owes its name to the outer walls of red sandstone, your ride will take you on to the moonlight square – “Chandni Chowk” – one of the oldest and also busiest market squares in the city. Experience the typical flair of an Oriental bazaar here: The traffic noise is mixed with the echo of prayers and the haggling of businessmen. Even deeper you can immerse yourself in the streets and alleys of Delhi during a rickshaw ride. Your driver will show you the beauty of Old Delhi from a different angle on various routes off the main trails, taking you to traditional shops and bazaars. Afterwards you will drive a bit through New Delhi and can, among other things, the National Monument India Gate, which is modeled on the Arc de Triomphe in Paris and commemorates the 1World War I was built, and marvel at the presidential house. Back at the hotel, the rest of the day is at your disposal. Overnight in Delhi.

Day 3 craftsmanship in the “pink city” Jaipur

From Delhi, travel to Jaipur, the fast-growing capital of Rajasthan state. With a university, theatres, cinemas, museums, a zoo and numerous art monuments, the city is both an industrial city and a cultural center. In the late 19th century, the city was painted in shades of pink in preparation for the visit of the British king – in Rajasthan’s tradition, this is the color of hospitality. To this day, Jaipur is still called the “Pink City”. Upon arrival, the afternoon is at your disposal to take a stroll over the market, for example. The city has also preserved some craft traditions that have been handed down from generation to generation. You can observe this during a walk with an English-speaking expert around Ghat Darwaza and Vishveshwar ji. During this special evening walk, you mix with the locals and try culinary delights such as Pakoras, Aloo Tiki, Samosas and sweets at the most popular street kitchen stalls in the city. Along the way, you’ll see silversmiths, jewelry makers and other local craft artists. Overnight in Jaipur.

Day 4: Powerful Amber Fort

After breakfast, a true masterpiece of Mughal architecture awaits you: the imposing Fort Amber perched on a steep mountain slope north of Jaipur. With the Jeep you drive past the artistically landscaped Mogul Garden, the winding pavement road up to the main gate of the complex. Behind it you can expect the public audience hall, the magnificent Ganesha Gate and the Spiegelpalast. From the galleries and terraces you also have a wonderfully wide view over the valley and the mountains of the Aravalli chain. On the way back to the city you will see the “Palace of the Winds”, behind whose ornate facade the ladies of the court observed the bustle on the street. Jaipur himself also knows how to impress with magnificent architecture. You will visit the magnificent city palace, which bears witness to an era of abundance and in part still serves today as the residence of the former Maharaja. A short walk away, the Jantar Mantar Observatory welcomes soothing tranquility. The accuracy of the instruments – including the world’s largest sundial – is still impressive. Here you can end the impressive day completely in peace. Overnight in Jaipur.

Day 5 - Go to Ranthambore

It is time to get to know the natural beauty of the country in addition to the cultural wonders of India. After breakfast, make your way to Ranthambore. Ranthambore National Park has a reputation for providing the best way to observe shy tigers in the world. In 1980, the area was transferred to a nature reserve and game reserve for endangered animals. Over the years, the cats have shifted their activities to the day and reduced their fear of humans, so that very good observation possibilities arise. Upon arrival, they will be brought to their accommodation immediately. The comfortable resort “Khem Villas”, with about four hectares, is a perfect starting point for nature exploration in the Ranthambore National Park. In the open grasslands you can observe desert foxes, jackals and many different birds. Khem Villas offers a lot of comfort and is equipped with all amenities to relax after a safari. The delicious meals are prepared from organic products and purely vegetarian. Overnight in Ranthambore.

Day 6: The Tigers on the Track

The tiger, once king of the jungle, is severely threatened. Spend the day on private safari tours in the National Park (morning and afternoon) and experience India’s exciting wildlife. You have the opportunity to observe animals that have become very rare on our planet. Your chances are not bad to see one or more specimens of the majestic Bengal tigers. But of course not only tigers populate the national park, whose rich plant life also ranks around the ruins of a fort from the 10th century. With a bit of luck, you can also watch leopards and hyenas. Swamp crocodiles live in the shallow waters and the air is filled with the song of countless birds. It is best to always keep your camera handy. Overnight in Ranthambore.

7th Day Evening Mood at the Taj Mahal

On the way to the next stop of your trip, visit the ruined city of Fatehpur Sikri, which is one of the most important testimonies of Mughal architecture. With red sandstone bricks, the Grand Mogul Akbar had his capital built here in the 16th century, but it had to be abandoned shortly after completion due to a lack of water. Nevertheless, it has been preserved almost ghostly to this day. Finally, you reach your goal: Agra. Once a small village on the banks of the Yamuna, thanks to the two great Mughal monarchs Akbar and his grandson Shah Jahan, the city developed into the second capital of the Mughal Empire. Together with your guide, you will visit the monumental Fort Agra, which is also a UNESCO World Heritage Site alongside the world-famous Taj Mahal, as well as the Itmad-ud-Daulah Mausoleum, nicknamed “Baby Taj”. Then it is finally time: you experience the sunset over the fairytale Taj Mahal. In incomparable perfection and beauty, this “most expensive proof of love ever” built by Mughal emperor Shah Jahan for his beloved wife Mumtaz Mahal still shines today in white marble. A very special experience that will impress you deeply. Overnight in Agra

8th Day Train Ride – The Temples of Khajuraho

Today you get to know the country and people on a train ride in a very special way. You will be driven to the train station early in the morning and enjoy a delicious breakfast on board. After a few hours on the train you reach Jhansi station shortly before noon. There you will already be expected and driven to your new destination Khajuraho. After check-in, meet with your guide to explore the most beautiful sides of the city together. Khajuraho is considered one of India’s seven wonders and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. In particular, the temples with erotic depictions of scenes from the Kamasutra enjoy worldwide fame. The shrines were built between the 10th and 12th centuries and their builders liked to spread the legend that they came from the moon. Even if the authors of the temples most likely came from this world: The erotic sculptures and reliefs not only delight the curious voyeur in each of us, but are also impressively crafted. In addition to erotic scenes, everyday situations are also depicted. By visiting the evening sound and light show, which shows the history, philosophy and craftsmanship of this archaeological goldmine, you will end the day. Overnight in Khajuraho.

Day 9 Excursion to Panna National Park and on to Bandhavgarh

The day begins with an exclusive jeep safari in Panna National Park, 31 kilometers from Khajuraho, the most important sanctuary in the north-central highlands of India. The park with its deep gorges, waterfalls and dense teak forests is home to the majestic tiger. Even if you will only get to see the big cats with a lot of luck, the excursion into the landscape traversed by a huge canyon is always worthwhile. Chances are good that you can observe Nilgau antelopes, Sambar deer, wild boar or crocodiles here. But leopards, wolves, hyenas, jackals and lip bears are also at home here. After breakfast at the hotel, continue your journey to Bandhavgarh, where you can end the day in your beautiful accommodation. Samode Safari Lodge – a member of Relais & Chateaux Hotels – is located on the edge of the National Park and offers ideal conditions for relaxing days in the wilderness thanks to its idyllic location. Elegantly and comfortably designed with many natural materials, the interior reflects the character of the lodge and the national park. The daily changing menus provide a lot of culinary variety. Overnight in Bandhavgarh.

Day 10 Safari in Bandhavgarh National Park

Bandhavgarh National Park is located in the Vindhya hills and is home to spotted red deer, black bucks, wild oxen, lip bears, horse deer, Nilgau antelopes, leopards and tigers. The diverse bird life includes piroles, parakeets, rhino birds and partridges. Today you go morning and afternoon on private safaris in the national park. Experience the untouched nature up close and enjoy the stay in this very special place. Overnight in Bandhavgarh.

Day 11 - On to the Kanha Tiger Reserve

After another morning Jeep safari in Bandhavgarh National Park, your journey will take you to the Kanha Tiger Reserve – one of the largest national parks in the country. A little more luck on your safari you will need to watch tigers, Asian wild dogs, hyenas, panthers and gaure – the largest living representatives of cattle. More than 200 species of birds roam the park. During an evening walk, listen to their chanting or relax at the resort. Her accommodation is Chitvan Jungle Lodge, which blends harmoniously into the surrounding jungle landscape and offers breathtaking views of the wildlife around. The lodge’s 18 spacious and tastefully decorated suites are designed according to the five elements of nature – water, earth, fire, air and emptiness and designed so that you can enjoy a magnificent view of the forest or grassland from the bed of each room. The restaurant serves you fresh for you, with food from the in-house organic farm, prepared dishes of local and international cuisine. If you prefer to dine together, the prudent hotel staff will gladly arrange a private dinner (optional). Overnight in Kanha.

12th day safari in the Kanha Tiger reserve

Today you go morning and afternoon on a private safari in the national park. It is best to always keep your camera ready, many of the shy animals show only briefly. Overnight in Kanha.

13th day Wilderness up close – the Jamtara Wilderness Camp

Today, leave Kanha behind and make your way to Pench. Set in the forests of today’s Pench National Park is the story of the famous jungle book written by British-born writer Rudyard Kipling at the end of the 19th century. Upon arrival, the rest of the day will be at your disposal. Your accommodation is the luxury tent camp Jamtara Wilderness Camp located in the village of Jamtara, near Pench National Park, in the heart of rural Pench. It is the only camp in India with its own entrance to a national park. Surrounded by tall Arjuna trees and an enchanting old Banyan, each of the 10 tent rooms offers magnificent views of the forest – the perfect backdrop for your jungle experience. In addition to the dreamlike views, the interior of the tents captivates with beautiful wooden floors, chic furniture in colonial and art deco style, rich Indian fabrics and brocades as well as private bathrooms and large, comfortable beds. The ever-changing dinner venues around the property create different ambiences where you can enjoy the lush Indian cuisine. Overnight in Pench.

Day 14 Safari Time in Pench National Park

Today, take advantage of the day to explore Pench National Park further with private jeep safaris (one in the morning and one in the afternoon). In the time between safari rides, you can walk through the village and experience rural life up close or relax in the camp. Overnight in Pench.

Day 15: From Pench National Park to Tadoba National Park

After breakfast, your journey will take you to the next national park: Tadoba Andhari Tiger Reserve, the oldest and largest national park in the Indian state of Maharashtra. There live on an area of more than 100,000 square kilometers, which are largely covered by densely forested hills, besides an estimated 60 tigers a variety of mammals such as leopards, lip bears, Gaure and various other ungulates. Crocodiles lurk in the waters, through the forest meander pythons and cobras. Just on the banks of the river you can observe a variety of water birds, including many endangered species. You will spend the next two nights at Svasara Jungle Lodge. The property offers a variety of comfortable rooms designed in harmony with the natural environment. The lodge attaches great importance to sustainability and environmental protection, which is reflected in the architecture and services offered. Overnight in Tadoba.

Day 16 On safari in Tadoba-Andhari Tiger Reserve

Today you dedicate yourself fully to exploring the National Park. In the morning and afternoon you go on a private safari in the national park. Experience the untouched nature up close and look for the shy cats. The chances are not bad here to actually spot one of the beautiful animals. Overnight in Tadoba.

Day 17 Back to Delhi

After breakfast, you will be taken to the airport in Nagpur to fly back to Delhi from there. In the capital, you will be driven to your hotel at the airport and make the rest of the day according to your taste. Overnight in Delhi.

18th day Farewell to India and its fascinating wildlife

After almost three eventful and impressive weeks on the trail of the majestic tigers, it is now time to say goodbye to India, which has certainly delighted you with its beauty and its species-rich wildlife. With many exciting impressions and fantastic photos of your trip in your luggage, you will be taken to the airport and take your return flight. Who knows, maybe one day you will return here.

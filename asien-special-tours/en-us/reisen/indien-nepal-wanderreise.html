Nepal Travel - Himalayas, jungles and ancient royal cities

Experience the many facets of Nepal with visits to the Himalayas, the jungle and ancient royal cities on this individual Nepal trip with Asia Special Tours.

Breathtaking mountain panoramas, friendly people, deep spirituality, species-rich wildlife, impressive architecture and unique hikes – all this and much more is Nepal. Nestled between the two giants India and China, the highest country on earth offers unforgettable travel experiences and unique opportunities for hikes in incomparable nature. Enjoy getting to know the most beautiful sides of the capital Kathmandu, go in search of rhinos, monkeys and crocodiles in Chitwan National Park and enjoy the view of Mount Manaslu, the eighth highest mountain in the world from the mighty Gorkha Durbar fortress. During your trip, you will always catch glimpses of the locals’ lives when you visit a Tharu village in Chitwan National Park or have dinner with a Newari family at their home in Kathmandu. A special highlight of the trip is certainly the three-day hike in the middle of the spectacular mountain world of the Annapurna mountain range. The day stages are easy for everyone to cope with and offer numerous opportunities for impressive photos. The lodges where you stay during your hike are simple by standard, but neat and professionally run. From each of the three lodges, you can also enjoy sensational views of the spectacular mountain panorama. You will not be able to help but fall in love with this fascinating country.

1st day Arrival in Kathmandu and on to Nagarkot

Namaste and welcome to Nepal! After landing at Kathmandu airport in the morning, your driver will pick you up and take you to your Nagarkot hotel. The small settlement of Nagarkot is dreamlike at an altitude of 2,200 meters and offers one of the best viewpoints to see the peaks from Annapurna to Everest. After arrival, the rest of the day is freely available to you. Enjoy the spectacular sunset over the Himalayas.

Day 2 Active Day: Yoga, Hiking, Culture and Enjoyment

Welcome the new day with a yoga hour. Balance your body and soul during your one-hour Hatha yoga class with warm-up, stretching and breathing exercises as well as meditation. Afterwards you strengthen yourself with an extensive breakfast for the day. Afterwards, head to Telkot and hike to Changu Narayan Temple, the oldest Vishnu temple in the Kathmandu Valley. The hike takes about two to three hours and offers a breathtaking view of the surrounding mountains. In the afternoon, head to the ancient royal city of Bhaktapur, built in the 9th century by King Anand Dev. Together with your guide, you will stroll through the fascinating city. However, the main attraction of the small town is inevitably the central square, which is lined by a multitude of fascinating buildings from well over a thousand years of city history. Experience pottery, where you can observe the traditional production process of local families and create your own hand. Relax, you’ll finally make your way to a local Newari family where you’ll be invited to dinner. Watch as Momos – very popular meat or vegetable filling pasta bags in Nepal – are prepared. With many new impressions, you will finally be brought to your hotel in Kathmandu. Overnight in Kathmandu.

Day 3 Continue to the former royal city of Gorkha

Your driver will pick you up in the morning and together you drive west, to the small town of Gorkha. The journey there is already an experience. The old royal city is located at an altitude of almost 1,100 meters and was once home to the Shah dynasty and famous Gurkha soldiers. From 1996 to 2009, Gorkha was named Prithbhinarayan in honor of King Prithvi Narayan Shah, who was born here and is considered the founder of the state of Nepal. After the abolition of the monarchy, the name was changed. Gorkha is particularly famous for its magnificent Royal Palace Gurkha Durbar, which was once the residence of the Shah dynasty. After arrival, there is still enough time to take a stroll around the traditional street market. Overnight in Gorkha.

Day 4 The Mighty Fortress of Gorkha

Together with your guide, you’ll visit the Gorkha Museum and the 16th-century Gorkha Durbar, which is considered by many to be the crowning glory of Newar architecture, and is a fortress, palace and temple all in one. Miraculously, the main structure survived the earthquake of 2015, but the damage was great. Thanks to its location on a mountain ridge high above Gorkha, the Temple Palace offers fantastic views. Let your eyes wander over the surrounding valleys and enjoy the view of Mount Manaslu on a clear day, which is the eighth highest mountain in the world at 8,163 meters. The museum’s collection is located in the magnificent Tallo Durbar, a Newari-style palace built in 1835. While the collection is limited, it is a pleasure to explore the old corridors with beautiful crafts, historical objects and the 3.5-hectare garden. After the visits, you can make the rest of the day free. Overnight in Gorkha.

5th day boat trip on Phewa Lake

Next destination on your trip is Pokhara, a place of remarkable natural beauty. The charming city has several beautiful lakes and offers a breathtaking panoramic view of the Annapurna mountain range. The tranquility of the lakes and the splendor of the Himalayas in the background creates a special ambience that has made Pokhara a popular place to relax and enjoy the beauty of nature. After checking in at the hotel, take a boat tour on Lake Phewa, the second largest lake in the country. The lake, considered the most beautiful of the three inland lakes in the Pokhara Valley, is framed by a fascinating mountain landscape. Sit back relaxed in the boat and enjoy the fantastic view. During the journey, stop and visit the island temple “Taal Barahi”. Back on land, stroll along the shores of the lake with your guide before returning to your hotel. Overnight in Pokhara.

Day 6 Start of your walk

After breakfast you will be picked up by your hiking guide and after an approximately one-hour drive you will reach the starting point of your hike. On the way, you can always make photo stops to capture the breathtaking panoramas of Annapurna photographically. Upon arrival at the village of Dhawa, you will begin your two- to three-hour hike to Birethanti, a small village at the foot of the Modi Khola Valley, which is one of the main stops on the Annapurna Circuit Treks. On your leg today, follow a ridge in the lower foothills of the Annapurnas to the Modi River Valley where Birethanti Lodge is located, your accommodation for the night. The lodge offers a magnificent view of the sacred and unspoiled Fishtail Mountain, or Machhapuchhre as it is called in the local language. After lunch at the lodge, you will have the opportunity to visit the nearby village of Birethanti or spend the afternoon exploring the surrounding river valley. Overnight in Birethanti.
Your walk today: easy, 5 kilometers, about 2 – 3 hours

Day 7: Insight into Nepalese village life

After a leisurely breakfast, you will leave the lodge and walk through small villages and through numerous stairs of natural stone up to Majgoan Lodge in the Gurung village of Majhgaun, which is located at an altitude of 1,400 meters. Built in the traditional Nepalese style, the lodge offers spectacular views of the surrounding valley and mountains from each of its eleven rooms. The lodge has an extensive plot of land with pretty garden where you can relax and enjoy the magnificent view. After lunch at the lodge, your guide will escort you to the village of Tanchok, where you will visit a traditional style house that has been converted into a museum. Here you will find out how the local people still live and work today. With a dinner at the lodge you will end the day relaxed. Overnight in Majhgaun.
Your hike today: easy to medium, 4 kilometers, about 2 hours

Day 8 Through villages and subtropical forests to Dhampus

Today you will wander through the unspoilt village of Patlekhet and continue through subtropical forests with tree orchids, rhododendrons and magnolias. Along the way, you will stop at the village of Pothana at 1890 m, where many Tibetan traders live and sell their souvenirs. After about 4.5 hours you will reach the Dhampus Lodge. After lunch at the lodge, you can take a walk through the old village of Dhampus. From the lodge you have a magnificent view of the entire Annapurna mountain massif. Overnight in Dhampus.
Your hike today: easy to moderate, 11 kilometers, about 4.5 hours

Day 9 Back to Pokhara

Your last stage takes you along the mountain ridge, over wooded hills and through the terraced farmland downhill into the valley (1,000 meters). Finally, you will meet your chauffeur, who will bring you back to Pokhara in about 30 minutes by car. We recommend treating yourself and your muscles to a relaxing full-body or foot massage (optional) in your hotel or letting your soul dangle over a bath in the Jacuzzi (optional). Overnight in Pokhara.
Your walk today: easy, 3 kilometers, about 1.5 hours

Day 10: The jungle is calling

After breakfast, you will drive to Chitwan – one of the largest jungle and forest areas in Nepal. The Royal Chitwan National Park is considered the best organized in the country and covers an area of 932 square kilometers. The park with its swamps and salt forests, which are crossed by rivers and watercourses, has been a UNESCO World Heritage site since 1984. Among others, deer, monkeys, wild elephants, leopards, lip bears and crocodiles live here. The park is particularly well known for its population of Indian rhinos and with a lot of luck you might even discover one of the rare Bengal tigers. After lunch at the resort, you will spend the rest of the day with various jungle activities arranged by your lodge together with other guests. For example, you can visit a small village of the Tharu ethnic group to get a sense of what their world and life look like. Back at the resort, experience a cultural performance of the Tharu community. Let yourself be enchanted by the dances and songs and for the moment be taken completely into the world of the tribe. Overnight in Chitwan.

Day 11 Varied Day in Chitwan National Park

Nature is calling! Immerse yourself in the fascination of the wilderness and its inhabitants. You can explore the park with guided jeep safaris, walks or canoe rides from very different perspectives. Back at the resort, experience a cultural performance of the Tharu community. Let yourself be enchanted by the dances and songs and for the moment be taken completely into the world of the tribe. Overnight in Chitwan.

Day 12 Back to the Capital

After an extensive breakfast, your trip will take you back to the capital Kathmandu, which looks back on a long history. As early as 800 BC, the region established itself as a flourishing commercial center. Over the centuries, the area came under the control of various powers and was thus exposed to a wide variety of cultural influences. After arriving at the hotel in the afternoon, the rest of the day is freely available. Use the time for first explorations on your own or relax in your beautiful hotel. Overnight in Kathmandu.

Day 13 Explore the most beautiful sides of Kathmandu

Start the day with a visit to Kathmandu’s Old Town, which undoubtedly centers on Durbar Square. Where the trade routes once crossed, today you will find over 50 temples and monuments. Especially stand out the temple of the goddess Kumari and the royal palace. From here you will later take a beautiful rickshaw ride through narrow alleys to Thamel, during which you can observe the local life up close. Later, visit the Swayambhunath Temple, one of the oldest Buddhist temples in the world. The inner buildings of the sanctuary, which has undergone many changes over the centuries, are said to be about 2,500 years old. In the central Patan Durbar square you will find a collection of finely crafted pagoda temples and stone statues. The old palace of the Malla kings and the ancient stone baths are each associated with legends and episodes of history. Overnight in Kathmandu.

Day 14 Farewell to Nepal

Even the most beautiful journey comes to an end. During the last few days, you have gathered many unique impressions of the diversity of Nepalese culture, the impressive mountains and the diverse flora and fauna of Chitwan National Park. The suitcases packed with indescribable experiences and lasting memories will take you to the airport to start your journey home.

Java and Bali – On Special Paths

The magic of Indonesia's most famous islands is waiting for you. Let yourself be captivated by the exotic flair and travel through fantastically beautiful landscapes, past lush green rice terraces, where you can observe farmers at their day work. Experience the hustle and bustle of the markets and feel the mystical tranquility of Indonesia’s traditional temple complexes. Feel the fascination of Java and Bali in different ways – on foot, by bicycle, by train or by car and enjoy the encounters with the locals!

1st day Arrival in Yogyakarta

Welcome to Java. Upon arrival in Yogyakarta, you will be picked up at the airport and welcomed by your guide. Together you drive to your hotel. The rest of the day is at your free disposal. Once you arrive, relax and take advantage of your hotel’s amenities before embarking on a cultural exploration tour tomorrow. Overnight in Yogyakarta.

2nd Day Bicycle Tour & Prambanan Temple

After breakfast, take a bike to the Prambanan Temple. As you cycle along the narrow but well-paved path, you will pass through fields where farmers go about their daily work and children let their dragons rise. Let yourself be impressed by how these people have preserved their traditional cultivation methods to this day and cultivate their land by hand. After about 2.5 hours of driving, you will reach Prambanan Temple, a highlight of Hindu Javanese temple architecture. In the evening, take a ride with a traditional oxcart “Pedati” to the surrounding villages. Afterwards, a cultural highlight will be on the program: The presentation of Ramayana, a classic Javanese ballet performance that depicts the Hindu epic with more than 100 dancers. Before that, you will be pampered with authentic food as part of the Ramayana Ballet. Overnight in Yogyakarta.

Day 3rd Sunrise at Borobudur

Today your day begins with an unforgettable sunrise on Dagi Hill, giving you a unique view of the majestic Borobudur Temple – the largest Buddhist temple in the world. Enjoy this quiet moment at a picnic breakfast. Then explore the impressive temple and immerse yourself in its rich history. After a coffee break at a local restaurant, continue your trip to Kaliurang. Along the way, stop at the Ulen Sentalu Museum (closed on Mondays). Lunch can be enjoyed at Kopi Klotok Restaurant, surrounded by rice fields and with wonderful views of the Merapi Volcano. Afterwards, take an exciting jeep tour to the lava fields of Kinahrejo, where the Sisa Hartaku Mini Museum illuminates the dramatic events of the 2010 volcanic eruption and the infamous "Ring of Fire". In the evening you will return to Yogyakarta, where you can end the day in peace. Overnight in Yogyakarta.

Day 4: Train to Mojokerto

After an early breakfast, you will be taken to the train station, from where you will continue by train to Mojokerto. In order to bridge the long distances on Java, you drive particularly well by train. On a ride on the air-conditioned express train, you will enjoy a view of the beautiful landscapes of central Java, which is a particularly fertile area. Upon arrival in Mojokerto, you will be picked up and transported to Bromo. The road leads up to 1868m through artfully adapted vegetable terraces on the mountain slopes. Overnight in Bromo.

Day 5 Sunrise at Mt. Bromo

Early in the morning – before sunrise – start your volcano tour on Mount Bromo to admire the sunrise there (as far as the weather permits). You should definitely bring a warm jacket to protect yourself from cold temperatures. First it goes up by jeep. Then it is still a sea of sand and 246 steps to overcome until you have a fantastic view. In the light of the rising sun, the landscape looks like it is from another planet. After this immersive experience, your guide will take you back to the hotel to have breakfast. Then continue to Ijen, on the edge of the rainforest of Ijen National Park. The landscape is characterized by rice terraces and volcanoes. Overnight in Ijen.

Day 6 Hike to the Ijen Crater

Today, too, we need to get up early. You’ll start the day with a drive through idyllic villages and coffee and carnation plantations to finally get to one of Java’s last remaining rainforests. Here, where huge ferns and trees of gigantic proportions cover the slopes, you can experience the untouched flora and fauna up close. The next two hours you will climb on foot, along a shady path with breathtaking views, up to the edge of the crater (2883m). Once you have arrived at the crater, take your time to let the sulfur fields and impressions affect you. The spectacular sight of the turquoise-green sulfur lake with rising smoke plumes immediately compensates for the effort of the steep ascent. Finally, you begin the descent. Then you have time to relax and let the view wander over the fantastic landscape again. Overnight in Ijen.

7th Day to Bali

After breakfast at the hotel, drive to the port to take the ferry to Bali. From there, the journey continues towards the artist village Ubud. Your journey will take you through the lush nature of Bali, past plantations and rice terraces. Spend the rest of the day according to your wishes. Your hotel is located just outside the bustling city in the middle of nature. Take a look back at the impressions of the past few days as you leisurely pull your lanes in the pool. Overnight in Ubud.

Day 8 Between Rice Fields and Temples

You will be picked up by your guide and leave Ubud. Afterwards, an expert will introduce you to the secrets of Balinese naturopathy on the "Eco Walk", a walking trail of about 5 km in the middle of the rice fields. You will walk through the herb and spice gardens and learn about their diverse uses. During your hike through the rice fields, you will pass by small rivers and tropical forests. After visiting Goa Maya, a sacred cave with an impressive underground cave system, your tour will end at a local restaurant. In the afternoon, visit the water temple Tirta Empul. Surrounded by dense jungle, the faithful bathe here in the water, which according to legend has a cleansing effect.Then drive through green rice fields and picturesque villages to the temple Gunung Kawi, which was built in the 11th century. Gunung Kawi consists of ten rock incisions representing memorial sites. Enjoy the atmosphere at this still rather undiscovered temple! In the evening you will be back at your hotel. Overnight in Ubud.

9th day of free time in Ubud

This day is yours! Enjoy your great hotel to the fullest or visit one of the many art galleries that Ubud has to offer. Overnight in Ubud.

10th day Ubud – Departure

Your time out from everyday life is coming to an end. Even if your holiday is soon over, the precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories of your trip will accompany you forever. The day is at your disposal before you are taken to the airport for your return flight. Or would you like to relax on the beautiful beaches of Bali? We are happy to make proposals in this regard.

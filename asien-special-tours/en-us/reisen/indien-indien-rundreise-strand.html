India Round Trip - Between Taj Mahal and Golden Beaches

India Round Trip: Between Taj Mahal and golden beaches, this India round trip takes you to some of India’s highlights. Ask and consult here.

You have been traveling in the distance many times, but India is still a white spot on the map for you? This trip is ideal for gaining an insight into the diversity of the subcontinent. Let yourself be impressed by the highlights of the Golden Triangle – consisting of the cities of Delhi, Agra and Jaipur – and experience the spiritual Varanasi as well as the multifaceted Mumbai. Marvel at the fairytale Taj Mahal, experience a traditional Aarti ceremony and embark on a culinary discovery journey on a street food tour in Mumbai. At the end of your trip, relaxing days await you on the golden beaches of Goa.

1st day Arrival in India & first explorations in New Delhi

Namaste and welcome to India! Your journey starts in Delhi, where you will be expected and driven to your accommodation upon arrival at the airport. Rest first or relax in your hotel. In the early afternoon, meet your guide to explore the imperial city of New Delhi with its vast avenues and imposing buildings. Because New Delhi also offers fascinating historical sights: for example, with the 72 Metre-high Qutb minaret and the mausoleum of the great mogul Humayun, which is considered an architectural precursor to the Taj Mahal. Afterwards you will drive a bit through New Delhi and can, among other things, the National Monument India Gate, which is modeled on the Arc de Triomphe in Paris and commemorates the 1World War I was built, and marvel at the presidential house. New Delhi has been 1931 laid out by the British occupiers as a modern garden city and today houses the government district and many imposing buildings from the colonial days of the city. Overnight in Delhi.

2nd Day Time Travel to the Past & Magic Water Show

After an extensive breakfast in the hotel, you will go on a journey through time in Old Delhi, where the Jama Masjid, one of the largest mosques in the world, testifies to Delhi’s past as the capital of Islamic India. Passing the Red Fort, the Mughal palace complex that owes its name to the outer walls of red sandstone, your ride will take you on to Raj Ghat, where the father of the nation – Mahatma Gandhi – was cremated. You will also visit Gurudwara Bangla Sahib Temple – the largest Sikhs sanctuary in Delhi and Akshardham Temple, which is the world’s largest Hindu temple complex even in the Guinness Book of Records. After dark, a special highlight awaits you: a demonstration of the breathtaking Sahaj Anand water show – a fireworks display of light, water, music and drama. Enjoy this captivating presentation. Overnight in Delhi.

3rd day masterpieces of Mughal architecture in Agra

Traveling in a private air-conditioned vehicle is definitely more comfortable. Nevertheless: On a train journey you experience the country and people in a very special way. This morning you will be driven to the train station to continue traveling to Agra. Once a small village on the banks of the Yamuna, thanks to the two great Mughal monarchs Akbar and his grandson Shah Jahan, the city developed into the second capital of the Mughal Empire. The Mughals were not only rulers, but also great builders and their best architectural wonders in Agra and its surroundings can still be admired today. Together with your guide, you will visit the monumental Fort Agra, which is also a UNESCO World Heritage Site alongside the world-famous Taj Mahal, as well as the Itmad-ud-Daulah Mausoleum, nicknamed “Baby Taj”. It becomes romantic on the following walk through the pretty garden area Mehtaab Bagh or “Moonlight Garden” on the other shore of the Yamuna. From here, you have a breathtaking view of the Taj Mahal and can end the day relaxing while watching the sunset over the fairytale structure. Overnight in Agra.

Day 4 Sunrise over the Taj Mahal

Now it is finally time: you see the fabulous Taj Mahal with your own eyes. When early in the morning the fog hangs over the water basins of the garden and the sun rises slowly over the magnificent structure, the best time to visit the impressive monument is. In incomparable perfection and beauty, this “most expensive proof of love ever” built by Mughal emperor Shah Jahan for his beloved wife Mumtaz Mahal still shines today in white marble. On the way to the next stop of your trip, visit the ruined city of Fatehpur Sikri, which is one of the most important testimonies of Mughal architecture. With red sandstone bricks, the Grand Mogul Akbar had his capital built here in the 16th century, but it had to be abandoned shortly after completion due to a lack of water. Nevertheless, it has been preserved almost ghostly to this day and combines two opposites: the figurative architecture of the Hindus and the geometrical architecture of Islam. In the afternoon you will reach Jaipur. Overnight in Jaipur.

Day 5: Mighty Amber Fort

This morning, another masterpiece of Mughal architecture awaits you: the imposing Fort Amber perched on a steep mountain slope north of Jaipur. With the Jeep you drive up to the main gate of the facility. Behind it you can expect the public audience hall, the magnificent Ganesha Gate and the Spiegelpalast. On the way back to the city you will see the “Palace of the Winds”. Jaipur himself also knows how to impress with magnificent architecture. You will visit the magnificent City Palace, which witnesses an era of abundance and in part still serves today as the residence of the former Maharaja, as well as the Jantar Mantar Observatory, just a few steps away, where you can admire the largest sundial in the world. However, the city has also preserved some craft traditions that have been passed down from generation to generation. You can observe this during a walk with an English-speaking specialist around Ghat Darwaza and Vishveshwar ji. During this special evening walk, you mix with the locals and try culinary delights such as Pakoras, Aloo Tiki, Samosas and sweets at the most popular street kitchen stalls in the city. Along the way, you’ll see silversmiths, jewelry makers and other local craft artists. Overnight in Jaipur.

6th day Flight to Varanasi

After breakfast, you will be taken to the airport in the morning. Today, your journey will take you onwards to the city of Varanasi, considered the holiest site of Hinduism. The city on the Ganges has a long religious tradition. For more than 2,500 years, believers have been making pilgrimages here. Varanasi is crowded, dirty and full of spirituality – pure India. Pilgrims bathe in the Ganges, cows walk through the alleys, merchants offer their goods for sale – here the country awaits you in an intoxicating authenticity. You are already expected at the airport and brought to your hotel. Then drive to the place where Buddhism was founded over 2,500 years ago: Sarnath. After Buddha had found his enlightenment, he gave his first sermon here. In the Sarnath Museum, learn more about the origins of Buddhism before finally returning to Varanasi. In the afternoon, take a Bengali Tola walk. Enjoy insider insights into the symbiotic life of the river inhabitants of Varanasi. Overnight in Varanasi.

7th Day Morning Boat Ride on the Ganges & Aarti Ceremony

They greet the morning with a boat trip on the Ganges. Thousands of believers come daily to the shores in the morning to bathe and pray. Let the magical atmosphere work on you. After you have strengthened yourself at breakfast in the hotel, it is time to explore the fascinating city in detail with your guide. From ancient times Varanasi was a city of learning and center of the great Sanskrit scholars. You will visit India’s oldest university, Benares Hindu University, which was founded in 1916 for the study of Indian art and culture and Sanskrit. The Durga Temple, which you pass on your way to the old town, is one of the prescribed stops for pilgrims, as is the neighboring modern Tulsi Manas Temple. Finally, your guide will show you the Bharat Mata Temple with a large marble relief representing a map of India as well as the Gyanvapi Mosque. In the evening, during a traditional Aarti ceremony at St. Dasaswamedh Ghat, you will experience the spirituality of the place up close. In a solemn atmosphere, the faithful offer small ships with burning lights and flowers. After this magical event, return to your hotel. Overnight in Varanasi.

8th Day Culinary Discovery Tour of Mumbai

A new day, a new goal. After breakfast, you will be taken to the airport from where another domestic flight will take you to Mumbai, India’s largest city and one of the world’s most populous metropolises. Once there, you will first be brought to your hotel and can relax a little. In the evening, you’ll be picked up and taken to the popular recreational and festive grounds at Chowpatty Beach, where you’ll mingle with the locals and immerse yourself in the local street kitchen. From Pani Puri to Sev Puri and Golas to Pav Bhaji and Mumbai Sandwich, Mumbai’s street cuisine has evolved over the years and is an excellent reflection of the city’s vibrant and ever-changing culture. Be sure to try a few dishes and let the taste of Mumbai melt on your tongue. Overnight in Mumbai.

9th Day Impressive Caves, Dabbawalas & Magnificent Buildings

In the morning, visit Mani Bhavan, Mahatma Ghandi’s home in Mumbai. Here began in 1919 the first mass movement in the struggle for freedom against the British rulers. The house therefore stands for the unique Indian freedom struggle and is now a museum. On your subsequent drive through the city, you’ll see many colonial-era buildings such as the Supreme Court, university and museum buildings, the Tower of Silence, and the beautiful Jain Temple. From an English-speaking expert you will learn the meaning of the Dabbawalas: During the week, about 200,000 dabbas, food containers made of sheet metal, are delivered in the Indian metropolis Mumbai. See how the Dabbawalas wind their way through the busy traffic of the metropolis to drive out the lunchboxes for the employees. In the afternoon you will visit the caves of Elephanta, which you can reach by boat. These five temple caves date back to the 8th century and are a UNESCO World Heritage Site. The large Elephanta cave can be reached via steps flanked by elephant figures. The temple hall behind it is surrounded by side courtyards and several chapels. Then return to Mumbai. Overnight in Mumbai.

Day 10 - Go to Goa

Without guidance

After you have gained many exciting impressions of India’s rich cultural treasures in the last few days on your round trip, you can simply let your soul unwind at the end of your holiday. You will fly to Goa where golden beaches are waiting for you. The Alila Diwa offers the best conditions for relaxing days in a luxurious ambience. The 5-star hotel is located in the midst of lush rice fields and combines contemporary elegance with traditional Goa design to create an exceptional experience in the luxury hotel industry. The sloping roofs, ceiling-high columns, airy verandas and quiet courtyards give the resort a distinctive character and make it a true oasis of tranquility. The 153 tastefully furnished rooms and suites offer a great deal of space and all the comfort you would like from a luxury hotel. Enjoy the view of the beautiful tropical garden or make yourself comfortable on your private terrace. The numerous gastronomic facilities – including the award-winning Indian specialty restaurant Spice Studio – will do more than satisfy your palate. Overnight in Goa.

11-14 days of pure relaxation at Alila Diwa Goa

Without tour guide & chauffeur

These days belong only to you. Make your time as you wish and just let it go really well. Breakfast in peace, relax at the endless infinity pool overlooking the rice terraces or at the quiet, golden sand beach, which you can reach in a few minutes' walk or with the free hotel shuttle. Or how about a visit to Spa Alila? The specialized treatments combine the wisdom of ancient Asian healing methods, the goodness of ancient beauty recipes and the healing effects of natural ingredients. You will feel like you are newborn. Would you rather explore the area or be active? Very good! The Alila Diwa Goa offers numerous excursions into the area such as market and temple visits, beautiful hikes, but also pottery or cooking classes and private film screenings in the in-house cinema.

Day 15 Back to Mumbai

Enjoy once again the delicious breakfast in this dreamlike backdrop before you are taken to the airport for the return flight to Mumbai. Upon arrival at the hotel, the rest of the day is free to explore the fascinating city a little further on your own. Overnight in Mumbai.

16th day Farewell to India

After almost two eventful and impressive weeks, it is now time to say goodbye to India. Relaxed and richer with many experiences, you will be brought to the airport and take your return flight.

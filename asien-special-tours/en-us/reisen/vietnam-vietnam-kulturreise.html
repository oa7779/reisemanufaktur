1st day Arrival in Vietnam & first orientation tour of Hanoi

Welcome to Vietnam! At Hanoi airport, you will be welcomed by your guide and taken to your hotel. After a short rest, you can take a rickshaw ride or walk through the old town of Hanoi, the “quarter of 36 alleys” between Dong Xian Market and Hoan Kiem Lake. With its tree-lined avenues, French colonial architecture, peaceful lakes and oriental pagodas, this city offers many highlights from which you can already get a first impression today. Then return to your hotel, where you can end the evening relaxed. Overnight in Hanoi.

2nd Day Tai Chi at Hoan Kiem Lake & Visit to a lacquerware workshop

If you are an early riser and want to experience something typically Vietnamese this morning, then meet an experienced tai chi master with whom you will do joint exercises at Hoan Kiem Lake. This way the new day can start wonderfully. Explore the Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum (from outside), the Temple of Literature – Vietnam’s first university – and the One Pillar Pagoda. This is not only one of the oldest pagodas in the city, but also has an extraordinary construction: instead of resting on a foundation, the wooden temple rests on a three-meter-high column in the middle of a lake. In the afternoon, visit a private lacquerware workshop in a restored house where you will learn all about the traditional but also the modern works of art. Of course, you can also swing the brush yourself and make your own lacquered goods. An evening performance by the famous Water Puppet Theatre rounds off this artistic day, where you can follow everyday scenes from Vietnamese life – the (mis) fate of rice farmers and fishermen, for example – or from the history of the country. Overnight in Hanoi.

3rd day rowing boat ride & bicycle tour in the “Dry Halong Bay”

After breakfast, we drive from Hanoi to Ninh Binh. Along the way, you’ll encounter one of Vietnam’s most beautiful landscapes, so keep your cameras ready. You will stop at a village in Nho Quan, where you will be invited to lunch with a local family. In the early afternoon, continue to the village of Gia Hung. Here you take a relaxed bike ride (ca.) 8 km) along the river Boi, where you will discover the villages of the delta and get to know their inhabitants. After arriving in Van Long, you can sit back on a one-hour boat ride. In a small rowing boat you wind through green rice fields and can enjoy fantastic views of the steep towering karst rocks that have earned the region the name “Dry Halong Bay”. Overnight in Ninh Binh.

4th day Luxurious cruise through the charming Halong Bay

Today follows another highlight of the journey: from Ninh Binh, you drive into Halong Bay and board a junk. On deck, let the view wander over the unique limestone landscape, which has been declared a World Heritage Site by UNESCO. More than 1,000 rocks, grottoes and islands stick out of the turquoise blue water. Enjoy a fresh lunch on board as you gradually sail through the magical bay. You will explore the labyrinth of the bay with its many limestone rock islands and caves. For visiting the Grotte Sung Sot and Titop Island, disembark and enjoy magnificent views over the bay. After a delicious dinner of fresh seafood, you will experience an unforgettable sunset amidst a dreamlike backdrop. Overnight in Halong Bay.

Day 5 End of the Cruise & Onward Flight to Central Vietnam

If you are one of the early risers, you can experience this place at the most beautiful time of day: The rising sun breaks through the early fog and immerses the bay in a magical light. Refuel with a few tai-chi exercises for the day. During breakfast, you will gradually cruise back to Halong and have the opportunity for short excursions on the way. After a delicious brunch on board, arrive at Halong early at noon, where you will leave the boat. Your chauffeur will take you to Hanoi Airport, from where you will fly to Hue in central Vietnam in the evening. The city is known for its magnificent monuments of the Nguyen Dynasty (1802 to 1945). Overnight in Hue.

Day 6 Discover the Imperial Hue

Hue is idyllic between mountains and the sea. Its gardens, lakes and canals were once sung by poets – today it is a UNESCO World Heritage Site. In the morning you will visit the famous Imperial Citadel, which is located on the banks of the Perfume River. Modeled on the forbidden city in Beijing, this place bears witness to Hue’s glamorous past as the capital of the Nguyen dynasty. In the afternoon, visit the tombs of the Emperor Minh Mang – laid out after the Chinese model – as well as the Tu-Hieu Pagoda, which lies on a crescent-shaped lake full of lotus flowers. Overnight in Hue.

7th Day Cooking Class in Hue & Over the Cloud Pass to Hoi An

Today you have the opportunity to learn at home with a Vietnamese couple how to prepare authentic home cooking. The private cooking class takes place in the garden house of your hosts, who will cook with you a selection of traditional dishes of the region and show you what combinations of flavors can later impress your friends and family at home. At lunch, enjoy your self-cooked dishes along with other specialties that your hosts serve you. Over the scenic cloud pass you drive on to the south, picturesque town of Hoi An. In good weather you can see here to the coast! Along the way, visit the Cham Museum with unique exhibits on the culture of this minority. You will stop by the Marble Mountains, where you can also walk into some grottoes where Buddhist sanctuaries have been built for centuries. Overnight in Hoi An.

8th day fishing with the locals & being creative in picturesque Hoi An

Together with local fishermen, you will start the day and learn about their traditional fishing techniques during a cruise on the river. During your subsequent walking tour, immerse yourself in the history of the city of Hoi An, which was declared a World Heritage Site by UNESCO. Intensive insights into the local craftsmanship can be gained while visiting a pottery and a small lampion factory. There you will also have the opportunity to get creative yourself and make a colorful souvenir. Overnight in Hoi An.

Day 9 Saigon – modern and traditional

From Danang, fly on south to Saigon today. Modern high-rise buildings stand side by side in Saigon with colonial buildings and traditional temples. On the streets, an army of mopeds crackles, in between a bicycle rickshaw drives. Young people make phone calls with their mobile phones, in the quiet backyards, believers pray amidst clouds of incense. Let these contrasts work on you during your full-day city tour. Among the most famous attractions are Notre-Dame Cathedral, the old Saigon Post Office and the former Town Hall – the latter richly decorated buildings are unmistakably from colonial times. Then we continue with a visit to Cholon, which means “Great Market”. Cholon is Saigon’s “Chinatown”, a bustling and colorful district. Visit the Thien Hau Temple and Binh Tay Market. Overnight in Saigon.

10th Day History in Cu Chi & Adventurous Vespatour through Saigon

Explore the branched tunnel system of Cu Chi, 65 kilometers northwest of Saigon. Already the journey past the typical rice fields is an experience. However, the vastness and beauty of the landscape is less noticeable at your next program point – but it is all the more exciting: The tunnels of Cu Chi served as a strategic retreat for the Vietcong during the Vietnam War. A special highlight awaits you in the evening: discover the nightlife of Saigon in the back seat of a Vesparoller. Your driver will take you safely through the turbulent traffic of the metropolis. They roar across wide streets teeming with large families on mopeds and people who somehow manage to transport refrigerators on a two-wheeler, and through narrow alleys, past small shops and snack bars. During the journey – attracted by the scent of the street kitchen – you stop more often to taste the typical food. Some peace of mind from the hustle and bustle of the city can be found in a hidden backyard café where you should try Vietnamese coffee before plunging into the hustle and bustle of a live music bar. Overnight in Saigon.

11th Day Mekong Delta & Romantic Cruise on Bassac

After breakfast, leave with chauffeur and guide to the Mekong Delta. Around noon, board your ship and enjoy a tropical welcome drink before moving into your cabins. The Mekong with its secondary arms shapes the landscape in the south of Vietnam just like the lives of its inhabitants. How better to explore the delta than from a ship? On board the Bassac – a traditional wooden boat with comfortable cabins – you will experience an extremely charming and authentic cruise. You will stay in air-conditioned cabins with double glazed windows and a small separate bathroom. Turn it off completely and let your eyes wander. Enjoy Vietnamese delicacies on board, sip a refreshing cocktail on the sun deck and immerse yourself in the daily life of the locals during landings. Overnight on the ship.

Day 12 Floating Markets & Flight to Phu Quoc

Enjoy the sunrise on board and fortify yourself with an extensive breakfast. Meanwhile, the ship sails towards Can Tho and continues to the floating market of Cai Rang. Incidentally, the goods offered for sale here can be recognized by the specimens that dangle on the boats as an exhibit of high posts. Not only delicacies of every kind, also craftsmanship is offered. Then set off for a little paradise as you fly from Can Tho to Phu Quoc. There you will be picked up by the hotel’s shuttle and taken to your hotel. Stay at the romantic La Veranda Resort Phu Quoc, one of the most beautiful hotels on the island. The boutique hotel enchants with a beautiful garden area and impresses with its colonial style, reminiscent of a French mansion. The rooms are furnished with dark wooden furniture and exclusive materials and provide a magical feel-good atmosphere with warm colors and mosaic tiles. The hotel also has a lot to offer in culinary terms. Enjoy international and local cuisine, as well as barbecues directly on the sea. The great beach guarantees you every evening dreamlike sunsets and atmospheric walks. Overnight at Phu Quoc.

13-16. Day bathing holidays in Phu Quoc

(Without tour guide and chauffeur)

These days are all about rest and relaxation. Let the soul dangle and enjoy moments when time seems to stop. You have the opportunity to explore the area on your own or spend leisure hours on the beach or in the facilities of your hotel. Overnight stays on Phu Quoc.

Day 17 - Phu Quoc - Travel Home

Today, unfortunately, it is time to say goodbye to a fascinating country and its lovable inhabitants. You will be taken to the airport of Phu Quoc and fly back to Saigon, where you will take your home or onward flight. There are countless experiences and impressions in your luggage that will accompany you throughout your life.

The Old Ceylon and the Wonder World of Dubai & Abu Dhabi

Sri Lanka and Dubai Travel: Enjoy Sri Lanka with a stopover in Dubai and Abu Dhabi. Let your trip be created by Asia Special Tours.

Itinerary & services

Do you want to leave a time-out and any everyday stress behind? Then this luxury trip is just right for you. We invite you to get to know the ancient Ceylon and the wonders of the United Arab Emirates with us. On your round trip over the colorful island of Sri Lanka, you will experience a diverse mix of impressive archaeological sites and uniquely beautiful landscapes. After a few enchanting days in Sri Lanka, a contrast program awaits you. You’ll head to Dubai’s skyscrapers and canyons and Abu Dhabi’s architectural masterpieces. On a desert safari, learn the secrets of 1001 nights and let your soul dangle at the desert resort of Al Maha. You will stay in luxury hotels of the extra class, which will delight you with exceptional service and full of exclusivity. Unforgettable moments and certainly unique encounters await you, which will remind you of this dream trip for a long time.

1st day Arrival in Colombo and Departure to Anuradhapura

Ayubowan and welcome to Sri Lanka! Upon arrival at the airport, you will be welcomed and drive to the cultural triangle of Sri Lanka. Arriving in Anuradhapura, you will be taken to your hotel. Relax a little from your arrival and enjoy the amenities of your hotel. Overnight in Thirappane near Anuradhapura.

2nd day safari in Wilpattu National Park & visit to Anuradhapura

Early in the morning we head to Wilpattu National Park, the largest national park in the country. On a jeep safari, with a bit of luck, you’ll see mongooses, peacocks, elephants, wild boar, crocodiles and turtles. However, the protected area is particularly well known for its leopard population. In the afternoon, visit Anuradhapura. Since people have already settled here around 400 BC, the place is one of the oldest continuously inhabited cities on earth. The well-preserved ruins of the ancient Sinhala civilization still bear witness to this flowering period. In addition to the ruins of a lost kingdom, the city also houses various sacred sites – such as the Ruwanweliseya-Dagoba, one of the highest stupas in Sri Lanka. It was built in the second century BC. According to legend, the poplar dough, which is now part of a temple complex, is just as old: Siddharta Gautama is said to have already sat under it – so you can probably see the oldest tree in the world here. Overnight in Thirappane near Anuradhapura.

Day 3 The rock fortress Sigiriya “Lion Rocks”

Today is the next highlight of the trip: you will visit the Sigiriya Rock (UNESCO World Heritage Site), also known as the “Eighth Wonder of the World”. On the plateau of a monolith, which rises about 200 meters above the surrounding area, a rock fortress was built in the 5th century. Since in former times a stone lion towered at the entrance of the bastion, this place is called "lion rock" in the local language. Via a spiral staircase you reach the so-called "cloud girls" - beautiful, well-preserved rock drawings. Over 1200 steps lead up a steep iron staircase to the plateau, where spectacular views await you. The "cloud girls" are about halfway. Of course, you can decide on site how far you want to go. Overnight in Sigiriya.

4th day excursion to Polonnaruwa & Minneriya National Park

In the morning, take a trip to Polonnaruwa. It was the capital of a powerful kingdom nearly a thousand years ago. This period is witnessed by magnificent statues and palaces, impressive stupas, detailed frescoes and last but not least “Gal Vihara”, an imposing rock sanctuary with four Buddha images. In the southern part of Polonnaruwa you will come across the statue of King Parakramabahu I, to whom the city owes its fame. Most of the city’s statues and temples were built during his tenure. In the afternoon, an animal adventure awaits you: take a private jeep safari in Minneriya National Park. The smallest national park in the country comprises several ecosystems and offers habitats for a species-rich wildlife. Above all, the National Park is known for its elephants who wander around the protected area! Overnight in Sigiriya.

5th day Cave Temple of Dambulla & Spice Garden of Matale

After breakfast, visit the cave temples of Dambulla. In total, there are around 80 cave temples, of which you will visit the five largest. With an area of over 2000 square meters, the temple complex is still one of the largest in the country today and was included in the list of UNESCO World Heritage Sites in 1982. Your journey will take you to Kandy. Along the way, visit a spice garden in Matale where you’ll learn about growing and processing various spices. Then you reach the former royal city of Kandy, which lies in the middle of the central mountains about 500 meters above sea level. In the middle of the city lies a lake that is perfect for a walk around. The main attraction is undoubtedly the tooth temple, in which a canine of Buddha is said to be kept in a golden shrine. According to tradition, this tooth emanates spiritual power, making the sanctuary one of the most important pilgrimage sites in the country for Buddhists. Afterwards you will be taken to your stylish boutique hotel, which is far from any hustle and bustle just outside Kandy. Overnight in Kandy.

Day 6 The Botanical Garden of Peradeniya with a botanist

After breakfast, Sri Lanka’s colorful plant world awaits you. You will visit Peradeniya, a good five kilometers west of Kandy, the second largest botanical garden in all of Asia – and without a doubt one of the most beautiful. It was created 700 years ago as a pleasure garden by King Vikramabahu III, but received its current form only in the 19th century by the British. Around 4000 different plant species grow here. Together with the experienced botanist Bandara Palipana, who has worked here for over 30 years, you will walk through avenues of palm trees, past colorful orchids, ponds and cleanly trimmed box trees. Listen with interest to the many stories that Mr. Bandara Palipana has to tell. The rest of the day is at your disposal. Overnight in Kandy.

7th day train ride to Hatton

Let the morning go quietly in your hotel. Then you will be taken to Peradeniya train station. From here, the journey continues for you to Hatton by train. The train journey takes about 3.5 hours. Enjoy the unique, deep green nature and the view of the central highlands of Sri Lanka. Arriving at Hatton Train Station, take the car to your extraordinary hotel. The Ceylon Tea Trails is located at an altitude of 1,250 meters and is the first tea resort in the world. Enjoy your absolute privacy here with a first-class butler service and an excellent gourmet cuisine. Let your eyes wander over the Golden Valley and the tea-growing area. The landscape with mountains, lakes and waterfalls is truly breathtaking. Soak this special atmosphere deep within yourself. Overnight in Hatton.

Day 8: The Secrets of Tea Growing and Migration

Today is all about tea. You will visit a tea plantation and tea factory to learn all about this traditional drink. This activity organizes your hotel and is conducted in English. Where do the plants thrive best, how are they harvested and what are the different varieties? Let yourself taste a delicious cup and learn why Sri Lanka is one of the largest tea exporters in the world. In the afternoon, together with a naturalist, you will take a hike through the enchanting landscape. On a hill above the Dunkeld Bungalow, you can fortify yourself with a four-course picnic - overlooking the bright green tea fields. Overnight in Hatton.

9th day Departure to Kotugoda

After an extensive breakfast, leave the highlands in the morning and continue on to Kotugoda, your last stop on Sri Lanka. You will arrive at your hotel in the early afternoon. Enjoy your free time in your tasteful boutique hotel, a former colonial-era mansion. Overnight in Kotugoda

10th day Flight to Dubai & Welcome in 1001 nights

Today it is time to say goodbye to Sri Lanka, because you fly on to Dubai. Welcome to Dubai, a city of superlatives with luxury hotels, gigantic shopping malls and white sand beaches. You are already expected and brought to your hotel. Immerse yourself in 1001 nights, because your hotel is truly fairytale. Welcome to the luxury hotel One & Only Royal Mirage. On an area of 26 hectares, the hotel is beautifully nestled in a lush garden landscape on a white private beach of Jumeirah. Your room is located in The Palace Hotel Complex, which reawakens the magic and magic of ancient Arabia. The total of eight restaurants offer you an unforgettable culinary journey through the tastiest cuisines in the world. The outstanding spa area and the traditional Oriental Hamam leave nothing to be desired. Overnight in Dubai.

11th Day City Tour in Dubai – City of Modernity

Dubai is dominated by powerful skyscrapers. The Burj al Arab became particularly well known worldwide: the sail-shaped structure is the most luxurious and expensive hotel in the world. Luxury is omnipresent in Dubai. However, the city also offers some cultural highlights, such as the Dubai Museum, which gives an overview of life before the oil boom. Afterwards, take a water taxi over Dubai Creek to the Spice Market, where you will have the opportunity to buy spices from all over the world. As a crowning conclusion, the Burj Khalifa awaits you. The highest building in the world at 828 meters, towers over a sea of high-rise buildings. Enjoy a spectacular sunset before returning to your hotel. Overnight in Dubai.

12th day to discover Dubai on your own

Without tour guide and chauffeur

Today is at your disposal - make this day according to your wishes and ideas. For example, how about new explorations in the metropolis of magnificent elegance? If you’d rather take a relaxed approach, you can make extensive use of the wonderful amenities of your luxurious hotel. Recharge and relax in the heart of Dubai. Overnight in Dubai. 

Day 13: Drive to Al Maha Desert Camp

After breakfast, leave the glittering high-rise world of Dubai and head to the desert for the luxurious Al Maha. Experience the contrast program to the urban jungle here. Peace, seclusion and luxury at the highest level are waiting for you. Al Maha offers first-class service, state-of-the-art comfort and very tasteful villas with exquisite Arabian furnishings. Enjoy the starry sky and look at desert sand as far as the eye can see. Overnight in the desert.

14th day Desert adventure and pure relaxation

These days are yours. Feel the magic and magic of the desert and let your soul dangle. How about a camel safari or archery? Or would you rather take a walk? Discover the amazing diversity of life in the desert. Overnight in the desert.

15th day Departure to Abu Dhabi

Today you will leave the desert and continue on to Abu Dhabi, the capital of the United Arab Emirates. As the culmination of your unforgettable journey, Emirates Palace is waiting for you, a luxury hotel of superlatives. A first-class service, exclusive rooms with state-of-the-art comfort and an award-winning cuisine will give you an unforgettable stay. Relax on the private sandy beach or in the luxurious pool landscape and let yourself be enchanted. Overnight in Abu Dhabi.

16th Day City Tour and Visit to Louvre Abu Dhabi

After breakfast, we will go on a city tour of Abu Dhabi. The city is located on an offshore island in the Persian Gulf and is one of the most modern cities in the world. On your tour you will learn more about the fascinating mix of ultra-modern city feeling and oriental flair. Of course, visit the recently opened Louvre Abu Dhabi, a spectacular museum complex that spans a 180-meter-wide stardom. You will then be returned to your hotel. Enjoy your last evening and review the last eventful days with an extensive beach walk. Overnight in Abu Dhabi.

17th day Departure from Abu Dhabi

Your time out from everyday life is coming to an end. Even if your holiday is soon over, the precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories of your trip will accompany you forever. Look forward to letting your friends and family share your experiences in the distance at home. Your driver will take you to the airport for your return flight.

Sri Lanka in style

Sri Lanka Travel: Discover the most beautiful places on this Asia Special Tours tour and enjoy the luxury of the most exclusive hotels.

This dreamlike luxury trip takes you to another world and shows you the most beautiful spots of Sri Lanka. Ancient Ceylon looks back on a long history to which the country owes fascinating archaeological sites. Let yourself be taken back in time and explore the testimonies of ancient civilizations, fascinating sanctuaries and the ruins of magnificent palaces. Let yourself be impressed by the cultural and historical treasures of the island and look forward to a varied round trip at the highest level. In between, there is enough space for a flexible time division and very private moments. Stay in the most exclusive hotels in Sri Lanka: every hotel on this route is unique and will delight you with exceptional service and exclusivity.

1st day Arrival in Colombo and drive to Kotugoda

Ayubowan and welcome to Sri Lanka! Upon arrival at the airport, you will be welcomed and driven to your hotel in Kotugoda. You can move into your room right away and rest a little. Relax a little from your flight and enjoy your free time at your tasteful boutique hotel, a former colonial-era mansion. Overnight in Kotugoda.

2nd day Departure to Sigiriya & Cave Temple from Dambulla

After breakfast, head towards Sigiriya. On the way, visit the cave temples of Dambulla. Situated on a hill about 150 meters high, the caves offered protection to King Walagamba during his 14-year exile in the first century. Returning to the throne, he built the largest temple in the country at that time. In total, there are around 80 cave temples in Dambulla, of which you will visit the five largest. In the shrines decorated with historical paintings are more than 150 Buddha statues. Already in the first cave awaits you the largest statue with almost 15 meters. In the second cave, probably the most impressive of the complex, there are well over 100 images of Buddha. With an area of over 2000 square meters, the temple complex is still one of the largest in the country today and was included in the list of UNESCO World Heritage Sites in 1982. After that, it is only a short drive to your luxurious accommodation in Sigiriya. The design and concept of your hotel is all about water gardens. Enjoy a lot of privacy and exclusive service in this quiet oasis surrounded by peaceful nature. Overnight in Sigiriya.

Day 3 Excursion to Polonnaruwa & Minneriya National Park

In the morning, take a trip to Polonnaruwa. It was the capital of a powerful kingdom nearly a thousand years ago. This period is witnessed by magnificent statues and palaces, impressive stupas, detailed frescoes and last but not least “Gal Vihara”, an imposing rock sanctuary with four Buddha images. In the southern part of Polonnaruwa you will come across the statue of King Parakramabahu I, to whom the city owes its fame. Most of the city’s statues and temples were built during his tenure. A very special spiritual experience that you will surely remember for a long time is the blessing of a Buddhist monk. In the afternoon, an animal adventure awaits you: take a private jeep safari in Minneriya National Park. The smallest national park in the country comprises several ecosystems and provides habitats for a species-rich wildlife. Above all, the National Park is known for its elephants who wander around the protected area! Overnight in Sigiriya.

Day 4 The Lion Rock Sigiriya

Today you can look forward to a very special experience - a balloon trip over Sri Lanka. Early in the morning you will be taken to Kandalama, the starting point of your balloon trip. Keep the camera ready, because breathtaking views of the wild landscape under you are guaranteed while you leisurely float there at airy altitude. You will remember this impressive experience for a long time and always look back with enthusiasm. Then return to your hotel where you can rest and relax. In the afternoon, visit the Sigiriya Rock (UNESCO World Heritage Site) up close. On the plateau of a monolith, which rises about 200 meters above the surrounding area, a rock fortress was built in the 5th century. Since in former times a stone lion towered at the entrance of the bastion, this place is called "Lion Rock" in the local language. Via a spiral staircase you reach the so-called "cloud girls" - beautiful, well-preserved rock drawings. Over 1200 steps lead up a steep iron staircase to the plateau, where spectacular views await you. The "cloud girls" are about halfway. Of course, you can decide on site how far you want to go. Overnight in Sigiriya.

5th Day Spice Garden Matale & Tooth Temple in Kandy

Your journey will take you to Kandy. Along the way, visit a spice garden in Matale where you’ll learn about growing and processing various spices. Then you reach the former royal city of Kandy, which lies in the middle of the central mountains about 500 meters above sea level. Despite its size, it is still very much influenced by ancient traditions. The last king to rule here was Sri Wickrama Rajasinghe - then the British took power from 1815. In the middle of the city lies a lake that is perfect for a walk around. The main attraction is undoubtedly the tooth temple, in which a canine of Buddha is said to be kept in a golden shrine. According to tradition, this tooth emanates spiritual power, making the sanctuary one of the most important pilgrimage sites in the country for Buddhists. Afterwards you will be taken to your stylish boutique hotel, which is far from any hustle and bustle just outside Kandy. Overnight in Kandy.

Day 6 The Botanical Garden of Peradeniya with a botanist

After breakfast, Sri Lanka’s colorful plant world awaits you. You will visit Peradeniya, a good five kilometers west of Kandy, the second largest botanical garden in all of Asia – and without a doubt one of the most beautiful. It was created 700 years ago as a pleasure garden by King Vikramabahu III, but received its current form only in the 19th century by the British. Around 4000 different plant species grow here. Together with the experienced botanist Bandara Palipana, who has worked here for over 30 years, you will walk through avenues of palm trees, past colorful orchids, ponds and cleanly trimmed box trees. Listen with interest to the many stories that Mr. Bandara Palipana has to tell. The rest of the day is at your disposal. Overnight in Kandy.

7th day train ride to Hatton

Let the morning go quietly in your hotel. Then you will be taken to Peradeniya train station. From here, the journey continues for you to Hatton by train. The train journey takes about 2.5 hours. Enjoy the unique, deep green nature and the view of the central highlands of Sri Lanka. Arriving at Hatton Train Station, take the car to your extraordinary hotel. The Ceylon Tea Trails is located at an altitude of 1,250 meters and is the first tea resort in the world. Enjoy your absolute privacy here with a first-class butler service and an excellent gourmet cuisine. Let your eyes wander over the Golden Valley and the tea-growing area. The landscape with mountains, lakes and waterfalls is truly breathtaking. Soak this special atmosphere deep within yourself. Overnight in Hatton.

Day 8: The Secrets of Tea Growing and Migration

Today is all about tea. You will visit a tea plantation and tea factory to learn all about this traditional drink. This activity organizes your hotel and is conducted in English. Where do the plants thrive best, how are they harvested and what are the different varieties? Let yourself taste a delicious cup and learn why Sri Lanka is one of the largest tea exporters in the world. In the afternoon, together with a naturalist, you will take a hike through the enchanting landscape (duration: about 2 hours). On a hill above the Dunkeld Bungalow, you can fortify yourself with a four-course picnic - overlooking the bright green tea fields. Overnight in Hatton.

Day 9: Continue to Yala National Park

After breakfast, leave the highlands. In the southeast of Sri Lanka, a testimony of the rich natural resources awaits you today: the Yala National Park. Yala National Park is the oldest nature reserve in Sri Lanka. Over 130 bird species such as flamingos, pelicans, peacock and cormorants have settled here. Around 30 mammal species roam the area. Among them are elephants and water buffalo. Large parts of the park are reminiscent of the African savannah, while the outer areas are dominated by moist monsoon forests. In this enchanting setting is the luxury Wild Coast Tented Lodge Camp, with 28 tasteful tents modeled on Cocoons. In the evening, take a first Jeep safari and go on a discovery tour. Overnight in Yala.

Day 10 Jeepsafari through Yala National Park

Today you can take two Jeep safaris and marvel at the diversity of the National Park. Enjoy the rest of the day the amenities of your luxury accommodation and be enchanted by an exquisite safari gourmet cuisine. Sip your cocktail on the sand dunes and enjoy the sunset over the Indian Ocean. Overnight in Yala.
Note: Please note that we cannot guarantee that you will see the wildlife and that the Jeep safaris in Yala National Park will be conducted together with other travelers.

Day 11 - Continue to Tangalle

After exploring the cultural and natural treasures of Sri Lanka, it is time to relax. Treat yourself to the special and enjoy your time out by the sea. Continue to Tangalle, where you will be taken to your exclusive hotel. Your modern luxury hotel offers you a feel-good atmosphere at the highest level. Designed by star architect Geoffrey Bawa, the tasteful suites are equipped with local materials. A special highlight of the evening is a private barbeque dinner on the beach. Enjoy the romantic atmosphere under the open stars. Overnight in Tangalle.

12th - 15th day beach holiday in Tangalle

Without guidance

Let the soul dangle and enjoy pure relaxation and relaxation on the 800-meter-long palm-lined beach! How about a spiritual yoga class or a snorkeling trip? Immerse yourself in your personal dream world and let yourself drift. Overnight in Tangalle.

16th day Departure from Sri Lanka

Even when your holiday is over, the precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories of your trip will accompany you forever. Look forward to letting your friends and family share your experiences in the distance at home. Your guide will take you back to Colombo International Airport for your return flight.

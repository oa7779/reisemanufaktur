Original Samoa - Journey to Upolu and Savai'i

Discover the dream beaches and authentic culture of Upolu and Savaii on your tailor-made Samoa trip with Asia Special Tours. Now ask me.

Samoa offers you an unadulterated and original South Sea experience. You will encounter ancient Polynesian culture, warm people and delicious local cuisine. A very traditional lifestyle shapes the country to this day. Outside of Apias, the capital, people still live in their original villages, in open huts without walls or windows. In the former German colony, whose history is proud here, you can immerse yourself in the rich, colorful culture, meet the warm-hearted locals, visit magical waterfalls and lush tropical forests, or simply relax on pristine beaches and swim in crystal clear water. However you choose, the uniqueness and authenticity of Samoa will energize your heart and imagination. A journey you will remember forever.

1st day Arrival in Samoa

After arriving at Faleolo International Airport in the South Sea paradise of Samoa, you will be personally welcomed and taken to your reserved suite on the sandy beach in about 5 minutes, where you can relax immediately.

2.-4. Day to Stay at Sheraton Samoa Beach Resort

Your stay at Sheraton Beach Resort will delight you. The 100 m long sandy beach is right in front of the resort, which was completely renovated in 2015. For your relaxation, there is the Manaia Polynesian Spa area, several pools, the Dolphin Kids Club for the kids and the Sheraton Fitness Center. The La Penina golf course borders directly on the resort. Tennis courts are at your disposal as well as all kinds of water sports activities. Your well-being is also ensured by 5 different restaurants. There is no boredom here. Upon request, we will arrange a city tour of Apia, 35 km away, where you can visit Fugalei Markets, the historic Mulinu’u Peninsula, the Robert Louis Stephenson Museum, the Maileiani Factory, the Mulivai Cathedral and the Papase’ea Sliding Rocks.

5th day by ferry to Savai’i

Today, your driver will pick you up from the resort and take you to the ferry where you will transfer to Savai’i.

6.-7. Day your stay at Le Lagoto Resort & Spa

In the north of the island of Savaii, near the village Fagamalo directly on the beach is your accommodation. The facility is about 25 minutes from the ferry port. Le Lagoto Resort & Spa is located on a secluded white sand beach and offers 10 traditional Samoan-style bungalows (fales) with a seafront restaurant, bar, infinity pool and day spa. You will feel very comfortable in the personal and warm atmosphere. There is a small shop, which is separated from the complex only by the street. The fales are all equipped with a terrace with sunbeds, air conditioning, double bed, a bath with hot water shower, mosquito nets, refrigerator, TV, telephone as well as coffee and tea cooking facilities. You can borrow kayaks for free. A dive base organizes dives, at the tour desk of the facility you can book activities such as swimming with turtles or excursions about the island.  

Day 8 Return to Upolu

Today, your driver will take you back to the international airport with the help of the ferry. Sit back on the plane and let the past days pass mentally. These memories will accompany you throughout your life.

Gourmet tour - Vietnam à la Carte

Vietnam Gourmet Tour: Experience Vietnam’s culinary side on a gourmet tour with your Vietnam Exoerten Asia Special Tours. Ask now!

Itinerary & services

Experience Vietnam from its most delicious side! Culinary highlights from Garküche to Haute Cuisine await you – from Saigon to Hanoi. Hardly any Asian cuisine is as diverse and tasty as Vietnamese cuisine. From south to north you will wander through many different regions with their various culinary specialties. You will take private cooking classes, get insights into the food culture from an ethnologist, go on gastronomic walks through Saigon and Hanoi and visit an organic vegetable farm. In Dalat, a cup of coffee at a private coffee roaster and a tasty, typical Vietnamese dinner await you. What more could the culinary heart wish for? Stay in the best hotels in Vietnam: every hotel on this route is unique and will delight you with exceptional service and full of exclusivity. What are you waiting for? Depart for your very personal dream journey.

1st day Arrival in Saigon & Sundowner cocktail and authentic dinner

Welcome to South Vietnam! Upon arrival in Saigon, you will be welcomed by your guide and taken to your luxury hotel. Undisputedly, the exclusive luxury hotel Park Hyatt is one of the top addresses in Saigon. The multi-award-winning hotel restaurants are among the best restaurants in all of Vietnam and make for an unforgettable culinary feast. After a short rest break, you will already take a first orientation walk through the city, if you like. Strike for the days ahead with a sunset cocktail on the terrace of the Park Hyatt Hotel. : Your first culinary encounter will be at Hoa Tuc Restaurant, which has a lot to offer lovers of authentic Vietnamese cuisine. Overnight in Saigon.

2nd Day Culinary Walking Tour of Saigon

Join Mr. Dittko for a stroll through Saigon’s markets. Mr. Dittko is originally from Germany, has been living in Saigon since 1995 and knows the city like the back of his hand. Today he brings you closer to the sophistication and balance of the local cuisine. In Vietnam, each food is assigned a ying or a yang property. Find out in a traditional pharmacy which ingredients and medicinal herbs fit which dish. Get to know the delicious little pancakes “Banh Khot”, which are rolled into a salad leaf together with various herbs before consumption and then tunked into the hearty “sweet” fish sauce. And find out what all the culinary influences the Vietnamese have taken over from their “occupiers”. “An com chua?” – “Already eaten rice?”: This is how people greet each other here at lunchtime. During a visit to the Tan Dinh Market, you will discover the incredible selection of fruits and vegetables, fresh seafood and various types of tofu. At the same time, you will learn about the history, politics, religion and life of the people in Saigon. Mr. Dittko will show you the best cooking kitchens and restaurants, which not only offer culinary excellence, but are also always good for an exciting evening over a Saigon beer on the rocks – “Ars Vivendi” à la Vietnam. Overnight in Saigon.

Day 3 With bike and boat on the way in the magical Mekong Delta

After breakfast, your chauffeur will take you to Cai Be, where you will embark on your comfortable rice bark around noon and begin your two-day river cruise along the mighty Mekong. The Mekong with its secondary arms shapes the landscape in the south of Vietnam just like the lives of its inhabitants. How better to explore the delta than from a ship? On board the Bassac – a traditional wooden boat with comfortable cabins – you will experience an extremely charming and authentic cruise. You will stay in air-conditioned cabins with double glazed windows and a small separate bathroom. Turn it off completely and let your eyes wander. Enjoy Vietnamese delicacies on board, sip a refreshing cocktail on the sun deck and immerse yourself in the daily life of the locals during landings. Overnight on board the vessel.

Day 4 The Floating Market from Cai Rang & Return to Saigon

If you like, get up early and see how the Mekong Delta comes back to life. Watch the morning light and listen to the many bird voices. Today you continue your cruise and drive through the small side arms. Especially beautiful is the floating market of Cai Rang, the largest and most colorful in the whole delta. See how goods change hands from boat to boat and how customers haggle at the best price. Your boat trip will take you along typical houses, green gardens and past shipyards. Around noon you will leave your floating hotel and be chauffeured back to Saigon. Your guide will be happy to show you everything interesting about Saigon’s (street) kitchen if you want. Overnight in Saigon.

Day 5 The Vegetable City of Dalat & Visit to a Private Coffee Roasting Company

Today, after a short flight, you will get to know Dalat, the main growing area for vegetables and fruit. Stroll through Dalat’s colorful market, whose product variety amazes gourmets and world-renowned restaurant critics such as Patricia Wells. Refresh yourself in between with a popular local drink on the roadside, which you have certainly never tried before. Have you heard of the avocado milkshake (sinh to bo)? Dalat is also a growing area for coffee and tea. You can then enjoy the freshly prepared coffee in a private roasting company and find out about the cultivation of the export hit. At the Thanh Thuy restaurant, you can enjoy your dinner, for which the chef uses only the best products grown in Dalat. Overnight in Dalat.

Day 6 Visit to the City of Eternal Spring & a lonely island feel at the exclusive Six Senses Ninh Van Bay

Today you will visit the air resort Dalat, founded in 1912. The former mountain station from the colonial period has colorful parks, lakes, gardens and waterfalls. Due to its pleasant climate, it has earned the name “City of Eternal Spring”. You will start your tour with a walk at Xuan Huong Lake in the heart of Dalat. Stroll through the French Quarter and visit the Cathedral. Afterwards you will visit the old mountain railway station with the cog train that Dalat connected to Tham Cham at the beginning of the 20th century. In the late afternoon, your chauffeur will take you on a panoramic ride to Nha Trang. Located in the beautiful bay of Ninh Van, this unique Hideaway of the Six Senses Group is only accessible by boat. Surrounded by white sand beaches, tropical vegetation and impressive rock formations, the resort offers a unique atmosphere. The 59 spacious villas of the Six Senses Ninh Van Bay are built in a mixture of modern and native Vietnamese architecture and grant a lot of privacy due to their special location: nestled in rock formations, quietly located in a tropical garden or high above the water overlooking the bay, each villa is an oasis of tranquility. Overnight in Nha Trang.

7th day rafting at Nha Trang & free time in your dream hotel

After an extensive breakfast, you will be active in the morning on a rafting tour on the Cai River. Paddle over the rapids and enjoy the pure nature. After that, you have earned a seafood barbecue on the beach. The rest of the day you can do as you wish. Enjoy the amenities of your hotel and let yourself drift. Overnight in Nha Trang.

Day 8: Flight to Danang & Magic Hoi An

A new day, a new destination: In the morning you fly to Danang in central Vietnam. Here you are already expected and drive on to the magical Hoi An. The ancient city is teeming with ancient houses and temples that are still preserved in their original condition. If you’re hungry for your walking tour of the city, enjoy Cau Lau and White Rose, two traditional specialties Hoi Ans, whose recipes are only handed down internally from generation to generation. In the afternoon, you can take an easy bike ride with your guide to the magnificent surroundings of Hoi An and visit the vegetable gardens. Or would you rather spend time in your exquisite luxury hotel? Four Seasons Resort The Nam Hai offers everything you need to leave everyday life behind and enjoy the here and now to the fullest. Nestled in a beautiful tropical garden, the stylish villas offer an elegant-puristic design based on the Feng Shui principle. Enjoy luxury and comfort at the highest level. Fine restaurants, a very attentive service, several pools and an excellent spa area leave nothing to be desired. Overnight in Hoi An.

Day 9 Over the Cloud Pass to Hue & Vegetable Carving Course

When the fishing boats come back early in the morning, onlookers are offered an impressive spectacle. Be there when the boats arrive and the freshly caught goods are loaded – the atmosphere is unique. If you have had breakfast at the hotel, drive over the Cloud Pass to Hue with a stop at the Marble Mountains. Once in Hue, it is worth a visit to the citadel. The fortress was built in the early 19th century and modeled on the forbidden city in Beijing. You will also be impressed by the Thai Hoa Palace, the halls of Mandarin, the “nine dynastic urns” and the forbidden purple city. In the evening you will attend the cooking demonstrations and a vegetable carving class by Mrs. Ton Nu Ha. The vital seventy-year-old Grande Dame comes from a respected family whose members have served as cooks for generations. The family is famous for their private extensive collection of recipes from the Nguyen Dynasty. Afterwards, you will enjoy the refined cuisine of the Imperial City during dinner in the ambience of a typical garden house. Overnight in Hue.

10th Day Romantic Boat Trip on the Perfume River & Onward Flight to Hanoi

Sit back relaxed as you take a boat trip on the Perfume River to the Imperial Tombs of Khai Dinh and Tu Duc. The Emperor Tu Duc is said to have been a proven gourmet and promoted the culinary arts significantly. It is thanks to him that the ornately decorated and refined dishes of Hue enjoy a high reputation. In the late afternoon, fly from Danang to Hanoi. There you will be picked up by your private tour guide and taken to your hotel. Back in the colonial period you will find yourself in the exclusive Sofitel Legend Metropole. The “Grande Dame” by Hanoi exudes the charm of long gone times. This elegant French colonial-style house is located in the middle of the historic city center, making it the ideal starting point for your visits to Vietnam’s capital. A luxurious spa with pool as well as several very good restaurants offer relaxation and enjoyment after sightseeing. Overnight in Hanoi.

Day 11 Culinary Tour of Hanoi

Today you will visit the Temple of Literature – Vietnam’s first university – and the Tran Quoc Pagoda on the West Sea. Afterwards you will walk through the 36 craft alleys of the old town. Today, as a gastronomic scout, she accompanies Christian Oster, a Freiburg journalist and former restaurant owner who has lived in Hanoi for several years. Mr. Oster takes you on an amusing culinary stroll through one of the most interesting cities in Southeast Asia. For lunch, he invites you to a simple but delicious snack in his favorite cooking kitchen in a hidden alley. If you still want to taste a culinary speciality, you will have the opportunity to enjoy the famous chocolate buffet at the Hotel Metropole Sofitel. A special Hanoi experience with style awaits you in the evening. First, visit the famous traditional water puppet theater, which brings you closer to the Vietnamese faith, customs and practices of the locals with classical performances and traditional music. Afterwards, in a restaurant you have the opportunity to get to know the tasty cuisine of the mountain peoples. Enjoy a culinary journey of discovery with traditional grill dishes, fruit wines and wild mountain herbs. Overnight in Hanoi.

Day 12: Exotic Cooking Class in Hanoi & Dinner at a Fusion Restaurant

After breakfast, you will take part in a private and entertaining cooking class. You will visit a typical market where you will smell many different flavors and buy the necessary ingredients for your lunch. Try acting for yourself – your guide will be happy to help you. Together you will prepare the food in a private restaurant on the Red River and enjoy it afterwards. The rest of the day you will be freely available. For example, how about a rickshaw ride through the old town of Hanoi? Or do you prefer to relax a little at your hotel? You have a choice. In the evening, your guide will arrange a special dinner at the “D’ Clover”. Let the evening end tastefully and enjoy the here and now. Overnight in Hanoi.

13th day Fairytale cruise in Halong Bay

In the morning you will leave the bustling city of Hanoi and drive to the fascinating Halong Bay. Almost 2000 islands, emerald green water and the sails of the junks make the Bay in the Gulf of Tonkin the most beautiful in Vietnam. She became famous for the James Bond film "The Man with the Golden Colt". The Vietnamese name Vinh Ha Long translated to "Bay of the Submerging Dragon", whose bizarre idyll was named a World Heritage Site by UNESCO in 1994. Late in the morning, board a traditional wooden junk. The Violet Junk is named after the beautiful violet Chirita, a delicate, violet plant that grows nowhere else than on the rocky slopes of Halong Bay. Like its namesake, the Violet is small and exquisite. With only six luxurious cabins, it offers an exclusive view of the wonders of Halong Bay. Enjoy a fresh lunch on board as you gradually sail through the enchanting Halong Bay. You will explore the labyrinth of the bay with its many limestone rock islands and caves. To visit the Grotte Sung Sot and Titop Island, disembark. After a delicious dinner of fresh seafood, enjoy an unforgettable sunset amidst a dreamlike backdrop. Overnight in Halong Bay.

Day 14 Movie-ready Bay from Bai Tu Long & Return to Hanoi

Feel the magical atmosphere on board your ship. Early in the morning you will experience the absolute silence and clear air of Halong Bay. Welcome the day with a hearty breakfast on board. Today your junk will take you to the Bay of Bai Tu Long, where you can once again admire an impressive landscape consisting of karst rocks and scattered islands, which already served as a backdrop for the film “L’Indochine”. Around 11 a.m., you will reach the dock in Halong and return to Hanoi from there. Around noon, your private chauffeur will return to Hanoi, where you can use your afternoon for free. The evening will finally end with a farewell dinner in peace. Overnight in Hanoi.

Day 15 Farewell to Vietnam

Today we say goodbye to a fascinating country. You will be taken by your driver to the airport for your home or onward flight. You have countless unique memories in your luggage – and plenty of culinary ideas that will always beautify your everyday life.

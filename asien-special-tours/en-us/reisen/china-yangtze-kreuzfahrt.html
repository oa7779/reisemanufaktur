Journey along China’s southern lifeline with Yangtze cruise

Discover China’s southern lifeline with wonderful landscapes on a Yangtze cruise. The experts of Asia Special Tours will be happy to advise you.

Itinerary & services

Get ready for numerous superlatives that await you on this journey. In addition to the legendary Great Wall and the famous terracotta army, this also includes the Three Gorges Dam, humanity’s largest dam project. The mighty river on which it was built, the Yangtze, with an incredible 6,380 kilometers, is the longest river in the People's Republic and the southern lifeline of China. Enjoy a four-day cruise aboard the Yangzi Explorer, the most luxurious ship on the river, where you can not only see the massive dam with your own eyes, but also let fascinating landscapes pass by. After a detour to the former capital Xi’an, delve deeper into the natural beauty of southern China as you visit Yangshuo, popular for its picturesque karst cone rocks. Not only can you enjoy a beautiful bike ride through idyllic landscapes and a relaxed bamboo raft ride, but also a demonstration of the spectacular Liu Sanjie Impression Show and a helicopter flight high above this breathtaking backdrop. Your journey ends in the glittering metropolis of Shanghai, the largest city in China with over 20 million inhabitants. These and many other highlights make your trip very special. Embark on the adventure of China and enjoy your time in one of the most spectacular countries in the world.

1st day Arrival in Beijing

Welcome to China! Your journey begins in Beijing, the exciting capital of the “Middle Kingdom”. At the airport, your personal guide is already waiting for you and driving you to your hotel. The rest of the day is at your disposal. Our suggestion: Make a little fresh and then stroll through the old town. If you stroll through the so-called Hutongs, you will experience old Beijing up close. There you can really immerse yourself in the everyday life of the Chinese residents, because due to the cramped living conditions, many activities are transferred to the alleys in front of the houses. Old men play Mah Jongg in the shadows, women clean their vegetables, the hairdresser works outside his shop and merchants do their business on the street – just as if the neighborhood were one oversized community. You can explore the alleys on foot or by bicycle rickshaw. Overnight in Beijing.

2nd day Pleasures for all senses: Culture, Crafts & Peking ducks

A day full of cultural treasures and remarkable art awaits you. Start your tour in the heart of Beijing with the famous “Forbidden City” and the Imperial Palace. This magical place was the seat of the Chinese emperors of two dynasties and for half a millennium inaccessible to all who did not belong to the court. The crowd is as overwhelming as the architecture, the colorfully painted palace halls, the pagoda-like roofs. Before you dedicate yourself to ancient treasures in the afternoon, stroll a little over Tiananmen Square. With an impressive 500 meters wide and 880 meters long, it is the largest square in the world. In the afternoon, dedicate a colorful variety of works of art and antiques, as the Panjiayuan flea market awaits you. This is the largest flea market in China, which has a fabulous 3,000 stalls. Discover the unstoppable offer of nostalgic collectibles, fancy crafts and perhaps even one or two souvenirs. How could you end the day better than with special, culinary delights? In the evening, you will be invited to a Beijing duck meal at the famous restaurant Da-Dong. The famous Beijing duck will demand your full attention. Overnight in Beijing.

Day 3 Picnic on the Great Wall

What would a stay in Beijing be without a visit to the legendary Great Wall? To this day, many legends revolve around the spectacular building. It is often claimed that the wall is the only human edifice visible to the naked eye from the moon. The foreigner enjoys the excursion here like a journey through time: from the modern capital beyond the outskirts to the rough mountainous area, where an army of workers once walled an empire. Together with your guide, you will travel to the Mutianyu section and a dream will come true. Once to stand on the Great Wall. To make this experience even more unique, we organize a picnic with sparkling wine and all kinds of delicacies for you. Enjoy the treats, let the view wander over the breathtaking landscape around and toast you, today and all the unique impressions. Only a few travelers to China experience the mighty building so intensely and exclusively. You will return to Beijing in the afternoon. Overnight in Beijing. 

4th day Express train to Yichang & start of your Yangtze cruise

By fast train, you can travel quickly and – thanks to a seat in business class – comfortably to the next stop of your journey: Yichang. There you will be picked up at the train station and taken to the port. Your cruise begins on the massive Yangtze, China’s southern lifeline. You board the Yangzi Explorer, the top 5 star cruise ship on the Yangtze River and move into your luxurious cabin with private balcony. The luxury boutique-style ship offers not only the largest and best equipped cabins, but also the best and most personalized service. Thanks to a small number of guests with a maximum of 124 passengers and a crew-passenger ratio of 1:1, you will always be taken care of. The total of 62 cabins and suites are bright and tastefully decorated A special highlight is the private balcony, which gives you fantastic views of the fascinating landscape of southern China. Sit back and embark on a journey upstream along the section famous for its beautiful scenery and historical and cultural significance to China. In the evening you will be officially welcomed with an excellent dinner. Overnight on the ship.

5th Day Gigantic Three Gorges Dam

Start the day aboard Yangzi Explorer with tai chi before fortifying yourself at the rich breakfast buffet for today’s program. First, you will learn more about the region’s mammoth project: the Three Gorges Dam. The aim and purpose of the dam, which is more than 180 meters high and two kilometers wide, is to make a major contribution to energy production, to reduce the risk of flooding on the Yangtze River and to bring the prosperity of the country, which for a long time concentrated on the coastal regions, to the inland country. After lunch, leave the ship for a trip to the Three Gorges Dam. Back on board, listen to an exciting lecture on traditional Chinese medicine as you cruise through the Xiling Gorge. In the evening, a special welcome reception by the captain and an excellent a la carte dinner await you. With a delicious drink at the Explorer Bar, you can end the day in style. Overnight on the ship.

Day 6 A country between tradition and modernity

After breakfast, transfer from your modern cruise ship to a traditional long boat. As you row through the breathtaking gorge, you’ll discover signs of early settlements. Look for the coffins hanging high up in the limestone rocks. You will also visit a traditional village, where you will get an insight into the traditional life in the countryside. In the simple mud huts that serve as a home for the people of this region, the meat for drying hangs on hooks in the kitchen and in the bedrooms stand coffins. Although modern technology in the form of satellite dishes has also found its way here, people are proud of their cultural heritage. Back on board, lunch will be served. You can arrange the afternoon according to your ideas. Relax on the sun deck and enjoy the ride through the spectacular gorges, or take part in one or the other activity offered by the ship. After dinner, learn more about the 816 underground project before ending the day with live music or a visit to the in-flight cinema. Overnight on the ship.

Day 7 Hidden Caves and Sunken Rocks from Fuling

After breakfast, you have the choice between two excursions. You will visit either the 816 Underground Project or the White Cranestone Underwater Project. The 816 Underground Project is the world’s largest artificial military cave, a secret project hidden in the Fuling mountains for 50 years. Alternatively, accompany the onboard staff to the White Cranestone Underwater Project, China’s first underwater museum. It was created to protect the characters, poems and inscriptions carved into the White Crane Stone, documenting changes in the Yangtze water level for around 1,200 years. During the construction of the Three Gorges Dam, the White Crane Stone sank permanently. An arched glass cover, through which you can admire the stone carvings and inscriptions, save them from destruction today. An exciting contrast is the subsequent stroll over the local market, where you will get an insight into the rich culinary culture of the region. After lunch on board, the afternoon is once again at your disposal. In the evening, a sumptuous farewell dinner and a live concert await you. Overnight on the ship.

Day 8 Farewell to Yangzi Explorer & Onward Journey to Xi’an

Enjoy one last breakfast aboard the Yangzi Explorer before finally disembarking in Chongqing and being welcomed by your private guide. This ends your eventful and relaxing Jangtse cruise. But your journey is far from over. After a round trip through Chongqing’s old town, you will be taken to the train station and continue your journey by fast train to Xi’an. The former capital of eleven dynasties was once the cultural, economic and political center of the Middle Kingdom. Today it is the seat of government of Shanxi Province and the largest city in northwest China. At the train station you are already expected and brought to your hotel. Overnight in Xi’an.

Day 9 - The Emperor's clay warriors

Immediately in the morning, the trip goes to Lintong, about 35 kilometers east. There are the famous terracotta warriors in line. Discovered by chance in 1974, the gigantic tomb at the foot of Li Mountain was built by the first emperor Qin Shihuangdi. So far, archaeologists have excavated around 7,200 figures of soldiers on foot and horseback. Remarkable: All these earthen warriors are individually designed, no two are alike – an artistic masterpiece! It represents a complete army of that time. Since the troops have been deployed in battle order, their localization can be approximately reconstructed. Therefore, it is certain that there are still other sculptures on the huge site, which has only been exposed to about a quarter so far. Back in Xi’an, take a small bike ride on the city wall – the best preserved in all of China – which forms a rectangle around the city center about 14 kilometers long. Afterwards, stroll a little through the city center before you can expect either a muzzle banquet at De Fa Chang Restaurant or a specialty dinner at Shi San Chao in the evening. Overnight in Xi’an.

10th day Landscapes as from the fairy tale book

In the morning you will be driven to the airport, from where you will take your onward flight to Guilin. Once there, you will already be welcomed by your personal guide. Together you set off for a car trip to Yangshuo. Now it becomes clear why everyone is so enthusiastic about the area around Guilin: The road leads through fields and opens up a panorama of staggered mountain rows. Loud domes, as far as one can see, each one wrapped in a delicate cloud veil. It is hard to find the right words for this scenery. Best of all: just be amazed! Overnight in Yangshuo.

Day 11 Stunning Experience: Impression Show Around the Li River

Your day begins actively! In the morning, explore the picturesque landscape on a guided bicycle tour. Sit back comfortably on the subsequent bamboo raft ride on the Yulong River, a quiet side arm of the Li River. They are still in a dream setting. Seen from certain angles, the hills form shapes reminiscent of animals, pagodas or plants. This inspired the local population to give the mountains imaginative names such as Mondsichelberg, Elephant Risselberg or Horse Painting Mountain. This unique landscape will surely delight you! In the evening you will enjoy a breathtaking performance. At the impression show Liu Sanjie, for which we have reserved seats in the best seating category, over 800 actors captivate you with a fantastic combination of light, singing, acrobatics and trick technique. Nevertheless: The secret star of this unique spectacle is the Li River surrounded by Karstbergen. Overnight in Yangshuo.

Day 12 From the Karst Kegelbergen to the glitter metropolis Shanghai

The morning is at your disposal. Sleep in, have breakfast and treat yourself to a spa application in your beautiful hotel. A special highlight is the opportunity to experience the treatments outdoors. Amidst the breathtaking landscape of Yangshuo, you can relax in the open air while listening to the soothing sound of the Li River and the singing of birds from the surrounding forests. Perfect to enjoy its beauty once again. Around noon you will be picked up at the hotel and taken to Guilin Airport. From there, you will continue by domestic flight to Shanghai, the metropolis with sea breeze. Other names for Shanghai are “Gateway to the World”, “Dragon Head Metropolis”, “Paris of the East” or “Pearl of the Orient”. Upon arrival at the airport, you will be picked up and taken to your hotel. The colonial past and the ambitious future of Shanghai will inspire you. Overnight in Shanghai.

13th day China's booming metropolis Shanghai

You will begin your exploration of Shanghai in Pudong, a district that has sprung up in just a few years and now houses banks, corporations and luxury hotels in skyscrapers. There you will drive up to the observation platform of the 632-meter-high Shanghai Tower. Then your guide will show you the old town. The old and original Shanghai, as it was before the big dawn in the 90s. In the Jewish quarter of Shanghai, on the other hand, you will find traces of the forgotten ghetto in which Jews who fled Germany and Austria had to live in the most primitive conditions in the 20th century. Shanghai also has some European trajectories to offer: In the 19th century, many French businessmen came to Shanghai and designed an elegant neighborhood: the former French concession. In the evening you will discover them on a bicycle tour. Your 3-hour tour will take you to the low-traffic areas with colonial streetscape. Let the lively metropolis come to you from a completely new perspective and gain a more intensive insight into everyday Chinese life. Between parks, historical sites and local cafés, unforgettable impressions await you. Overnight in Shanghai.

14th day excursion to Suzhou and Tongli or shopping to your heart's content

Today you will take a trip to the garden city of Suzhou, which enchants with numerous canals and classic gardens. Through the gardens, Suzhou became famous and eventually developed into a cultural and intellectual center. The art of horticulture was to harmoniously design as many landscapes as possible in a confined space, offering both variety and opportunity for relaxation. Afterwards you will drive to the water village of Tongli. While you walk over small bridges, through winding alleys, past wind-leaning, gray-covered cottages, you look over dreamy canals on which junks swim. Would you rather use the day to shop at the best boutiques and malls in the city to your heart’s content? The metropolis of millions offers ample opportunity for this. Your driver and guide will be at your disposal throughout the day, taking you to the best addresses. During a farewell dinner in a Nobel restaurant on the Bund, you will end the day in style. Enjoy the breathtaking view and let your journey pass together once again. Overnight in Shanghai.

Day 15 Farewell to China

Today it is already time to say goodbye to this fascinating country. Surely you have packed your suitcases with many great impressions. You will be driven to the airport, from where you will start your home or onward journey.

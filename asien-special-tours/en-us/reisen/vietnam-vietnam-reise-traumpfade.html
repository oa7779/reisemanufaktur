Feel, taste and grasp happiness with your hands – experience the dream trails of Vietnam from north to south and meet the absolute highlights of the country. Forget all the everyday stress and immerse yourself in your personal dream world. You will travel through breathtaking landscapes, fascinating cultures and exciting cities. Get an idea of both old and new Hanoi, experience the fantasy world of Halong Bay, the romance and tranquility of Hue and Hoi An, the beach paradise of Nha Trang, the atmosphere of the highlands of Dalat and the vibrant Saigon. The contrast to the metropolis Saigon is provided by the lush green areas of the Mekong Delta that you will explore. A first-class hotel selection with plenty of space and time for private moments makes the trip your personal dream trip.

1st day Arrival in Vietnam & Leisure in Hanoi

Welcome to Vietnam! You will be greeted at Hanoi Airport by your private guide and taken to the exclusive Hotel Sofitel Legend Metropole. The “Grande Dame” by Hanoi exudes the charm of long gone times. This elegant French colonial-style house is located in the middle of the historic city center, making it the ideal starting point for your visits to Vietnam’s capital. Relax a little from the journey or jump into the turmoil. Narrow alleys and traditional shops, lakes, parks, boulevards and monuments invite you to explore. Overnight in Hanoi.

2nd day Exciting city tour and rickshaw ride through Hanoi

Today, Hanoi’s main attractions are on the agenda: Ho Chi Minh’s Mausoleum (from the outside) and his private residence: a wooden bungalow modeled on the Tai’s stilt houses that Ho Chi Minh moved into in 1958. The simple building is idyllic on a small artificial lake, in the middle of a beautiful garden with powerful mango and orange trees. Also on the same site is the one-stake pagoda, built under King Ly Thai in 1049, and another highlight is the visit to the Temple of Literature, built in 1070 for the veneration of Confucius, which only a few years later made its name as Vietnam's first university. In the afternoon you will get closer to the diverse culture of the country in the Ethnographic Museum. Be sure to check out the back of the museum: here are the 54 different ethnic groups in Vietnam – your guide will be happy to explain more. At the end of the day you will visit with a rickshaw the old town district with the lively “36 alleys” and the Hoan Kiem Lake. With many impressions you will return to your luxurious hotel, where you can end the evening relaxed. Overnight in Hanoi.

3rd day Boat trip dry Halong Bay & Guest with a local family

After an extensive breakfast, you will be picked up and set off for Van Long. Enjoy the beautiful scenery and take the opportunity to shoot some photos of the endless rice fields. After arriving in Van Long, board a traditional sampan boat and glide between the imposing karst mountains. Immerse yourself in this picture book landscape and let your eyes wander. Then change to a bicycle and cycle across the dike to a typical house in this area, where you will experience a special lunch. You are a guest of a local family who prepares you a delicious meal. Take advantage of this opportunity to get to know real life and rejoice in the authentic and genuine insights. Overnight in Ninh Binh.

4th day Fairytale cruise through Halong Bay

Today, another highlight awaits you - the fascinating Halong Bay. Almost 2000 islands, emerald water and the sails of the junks make this bay in the Gulf of Tonkin the most beautiful in Vietnam. Around noon you will arrive at Halong Town, where you will board a traditional junk. The Violet Junk is named after the beautiful violet Chirita, a delicate, violet plant that grows nowhere else than on the rocky slopes of Halong Bay. Like its namesake, the Violet is small and exquisite. With only six luxurious cabins, it offers an exclusive view of the wonders of Halong Bay. Enjoy a fresh lunch on board as you gradually sail through the enchanting Halong Bay. You will explore the maze of the bay with its many limestone rock islands and caves. To visit the Grotte Sung Sot and Titop Island, disembark. After a delicious dinner of fresh seafood, enjoy an unforgettable sunset amidst a dreamlike backdrop. Overnight in Halong Bay.

5th day Movie-ready Bay of Bai Tu Long & Flight to the Imperial Hue

Feel the magical atmosphere on board your ship. Early in the morning you will experience the absolute silence and clear air of Halong Bay. Today your junk will take you to the Bay of Bai Tu Long, where you can once again admire an impressive landscape consisting of karst rocks and scattered islands, which already served as a backdrop for the film “L’Indochine”. Afterwards, a short flight will take you to Hue in the center of Vietnam. A UNESCO World Heritage Site, Hue attracts everyone with its famous citadel, palaces, temples and imperial tombs that bear witness to a great and fascinating past. Upon arrival, you will be picked up by your private guide and taken to your exclusive Hotel La Residence. The design of the hotel takes up the Art Nouveau style of the former governor’s house in noble materials and forms. With great care, the charm, comfort and atmosphere of a classic colonial villa could be preserved. While each room offers all the modern amenities, the days gone by are palpable in its noble design and attention to detail. Overnight in Hue.

6th day bicycle tour to Phu Mong and city tour in Hue

In the morning you will take a city tour including a bike ride in the countryside. The driver will take you to the meeting point near the center of Hue, where you will receive your bikes. Then you can enjoy a leisurely bike ride to the village of Phu Mong. Admire the tranquil, magnificent scenery around Hue: rice fields, orchards and vegetable gardens as far as the eye can see. Afterwards, continue towards the Thien-Mu Pagoda. Before you reach your destination, make a stop at one of the typical garden houses for which Hue is famous. After visiting the pagoda, head towards the mausoleum of Emperor Minh Mang, an emperor of the Nguyen Dynasty. In the afternoon, visit the Hue Citadel and walk through the narrow alleys and wide, shaded magnificent streets of the old Vietnamese city. Based on the forbidden city in Beijing, this place on an area of 500 hectares testifies to Hue’s glamorous past as the capital of the Nguyen dynasty (1802 – 1945). Afterwards you will have the opportunity to visit the Dong-Ba market. Then return to your hotel, where you can end the evening according to your wishes. Overnight in Hue.

7th Day Imposing Marble Mountains & Drive Over the Cloud Pass to Hoi An

Your daily destination today is Hoi An. On the way, visit the famous marble mountains. Each of these hills symbolizes one of the five elements of the universe: water, wood, fire, ether and earth. From this backdrop, continue on the Hai Van Cloud Pass, one of Vietnam’s most beautiful panoramic roads, to Hoi An. There you will discover on a city tour, the picturesque old port city that was the most important port in Vietnam in the 16th century. Among other things, you will visit the Japanese Bridge, various temples worth seeing and an old house. Hoi An is the city of tailors and lamps. A stroll through local markets is great for buying some souvenirs for your home. You will then be taken to your magical Four Seasons Resort The Nam Hai hotel. The exquisite luxury hotel offers everything you need to leave everyday life behind and enjoy the here and now to the fullest. Nestled in a beautiful tropical garden, the stylish villas offer an elegant-puristic design based on the Feng Shui principle. Overnight in Hoi An.

8th Day Bicycle Tour of Duy Vinh Village & Leisure in Hoi An

A half-day excursion will allow you to enjoy the charm of the Duy-Vinh landscape on foot and by bike. This morning, take a bike ride and cross the Cam Kim Bridge to visit the village of Kim Bong, also known as “Zimmermannsdorf”. There you will have the opportunity to see the local techniques of a carpenter from the village in his workshop. Afterwards, take a bike ride and visit the village of Duy Vinh, which is famous for weaving mats from Riedgras and certainly one of the most beautiful villages in this area. During the 13-kilometer route, you will enjoy the landscapes with rice and bamboo fields as well as shady paths. In the village you will discover the traditions and the special know-how that is passed down from generation to generation. You will continue your exploration on a leisurely bike ride through idyllic villages. Finally, get back on board the boat to return to Hoi An. The rest of the afternoon is at your disposal. How about a first-class spa treatment or a game of golf? Overnight in Hoi An.

Day 9 Paradise bathing at the exclusive L’Alyana Ninh Van Bay

Enjoy the peaceful morning hours in your dreamlike hotel. Unfortunately, it also means packing suitcases again. After a short flight, you will arrive at Nha Trang, a beautiful coastal town surrounded by white sand beaches and fascinating islands. You will be taken to your exclusive hotel L’Alyana Ninh Van Bay, which is very picturesque on an unspoiled peninsula. A short boat transfer will take you to another world where you can forget all everyday stress. Overnight in Nha Trang.

10th day snorkeling trip to the magical island world around Nha Trang

You have already seen a lot of above-ground Vietnam – that’s why you get to know some special features below the water surface. For snorkelling, head out to the sea to discover the magical island world around Nha Trang. The crystal clear water and the colorful underwater world will delight you. But also numerous other activities such as diving, jet skiing, windsurfing and parasailing are possible – you just have to decide. Overnight in Nha Trang.

11th day of free time in Nha Trang

Without tour guide and chauffeur

This day is yours alone. Enjoy the top-notch amenities of your hotel, read a good book and let your soul dangle. Overnight in Nha Trang.

Day 12 Dalat – the City of Eternal Spring & Visit to Crazy House

Relax on the beautiful beach in the morning before traveling to Dalat in the afternoon. Dalat is a romantic city with many European-style villas and extensive pine forests on the Langbian plateau at 1,500 meters altitude. Due to its pleasant climate, it has earned the name “City of Eternal Spring”. You will start your tour with a walk on Lake Xuan Huong. Stroll through the French Quarter and visit Dalat Cathedral. After that, the art gallery Hang Nga, often called “Crazy House” by locals, is on your program. Already from the outside it seems with the mysterious window caves more like a haunted magic mountain. The rooms inside this quirky work of art also appear in the truest sense of the word “crazy”: curved stairs, winding corridors, mysterious caves as well as brightly colorful furniture and sculptures await you. Then visit the old mountain railway station; here you can see an old Russian steam train. The former summer palace of the last Vietnamese Emperor Bao Dai should not be missing on your sightseeing tour. Overnight in Dalat.

13th day exploration tour around Dalat & onward flight to Saigon

Today you are dedicating yourself to Dalat’s surroundings. A quiet atmosphere prevails at the Truc Lan Pagoda, a monastery from which you have a beautiful view of the surrounding mountain world. Afterwards you will continue to the heart of the forest and to a village inhabited by the ethnic minority Darahoa. This is your chance to learn more about the daily lives of these people. In the afternoon, return to Dalat and visit elegant villas of the former colonial city in a landscape with huge pine forests. Your tour will first take you to the Flower Market before continuing on to the summer residence of Bao Dai, the last ruler of the Nguyen Dynasty. Your last stop will take you to an old train station built in 1932 by two French engineers, but which is no longer in use today. Then we take the plane to Saigon. You are already expected at the airport and taken to your luxury hotel. Undisputedly, the exclusive Hotel Park Hyatt is one of the top addresses in Saigon. The rooms and suites are furnished in the noble, classical French colonial style. The award-winning hotel restaurants are among the best restaurants in Vietnam. Overnight in Saigon.

Day 14 Exotic Street Food Tour with Mr. Dittko

Join Mr. Dittko for a stroll through Saigon’s markets. Mr. Dittko is originally from Germany, has been living in Saigon since 1995 and knows the city like the back of his hand. During a culinary discovery tour, he brings you closer to the sophistication and balance of the local cuisine. Find out in a traditional pharmacy which ingredients and medicinal herbs fit which dish. Get to know the delicious little pancakes “Banh Khot”, which are rolled into a salad leaf together with various herbs before consumption and then tunked into the hearty “sweet” fish sauce. “An com chua?” – “Already eaten rice?”: This is how people greet each other here at lunchtime. To do just this, you will stop at a small restaurant where you have dedicated yourself to the traditional home cooking. During a visit to the Tan Dinh Market, you will discover the incredible selection of fruits and vegetables, fresh seafood and various types of tofu. Mr. Dittko will show you the best cooking kitchens and restaurants, which not only offer culinary excellence, but are also always good for an exciting evening over a Saigon beer on the rocks – “Ars Vivendi” à la Vietnam. Overnight in Saigon.

15th day Excursion to the Mekong Delta & Guest with a local family

After breakfast, leave Saigon and take a trip to the picturesque Mekong Delta. In Ben Tre you get on a boat and drive along the river of the same name. Discover the lives of the inhabitants who make their living on the river. To make sure this doesn’t just happen in the drive-by, make a few stops and see how people make coconut sweets. Continue by boat on a smaller canal surrounded by coconut palms until you finally stop to visit the coconut gardens of a local home. There you can try some tropical fruits. Then take a smaller boat and ride on the canal. After leaving the boat, go on a bike to discover the countryside and stop for lunch at a local restaurant where you can enjoy some traditional Mekong dishes. In the afternoon, walk on a narrow path to a small dock where your boat to Ben Tre is waiting for you. After many impressions in the peaceful nature, you will return to the lively city of Saigon. Overnight in Saigon.

Day 16: Farewell to Vietnam

Today, unfortunately, it is time to say goodbye to Vietnam. With many new impressions, you will be taken to Saigon Airport, from where you will take your flight home – lasting memories will accompany you.

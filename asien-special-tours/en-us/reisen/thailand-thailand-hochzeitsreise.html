Perfect Thai Honeymoon

Thailand honeymoon: Perfect Thai Honeymoon is an idea for your honeymoon to Thailand with Asia Special Tours. Ask and consult here.

This dreamlike honeymoon takes you to another world. In a varied way, you get to know the beauty of Thailand with all your senses. Enjoy first-class hotels, exciting excursions and the colorful cuisine of the country. You’ll start your journey in Bangkok, which boasts magnificent temples, a colorful flower market and sensational rooftop bars. A particularly romantic experience is the dinner cruise on the majestic river Chao Phraya. Enjoy a lush menu as you leisurely cruise past the illuminated skyline along the river. Explore the charming city of Chiang Mai, also known as the Rose of the North. Since love goes through the stomach, you will be introduced to the secrets of Thailand’s diverse cuisine during a private cooking class. Of course, a visit to the Wat Doi Suthep – the landmark of Chiang Mai – should not be missed. With a traditional Thai wedding ceremony, you confirm your recently closed bond for life in the homeland. After all the new impressions of Thailand’s diverse culture, it is time for some togetherness. You will spend two nights in the idyllic The Surin on the dream island of Phuket, which boasts tropical nature and complete privacy. In the noble-rustic Six Senses Yao Noi with fantastic views of the picturesque backdrop of the limestone formations of Phang Nga Bay, you can relax.

Day 1 Welcome to Thailand

Welcome to Thailand! Your beautiful honeymoon begins in Bangkok, one of the most exciting cities in Asia. At the airport, you will be picked up by your private tour guide and taken to your hotel. Arrive in peace, take a first exploration tour or enjoy togetherness. Centrally located on the banks of the great river Chao Phraya, Anantara Bangkok Riverside Resort & Spa combines the thrill of urban life with the charm of tropical luxury. Characterized by Thai tradition and the legendary service of Anantara, this exotic property is an oasis of tranquility with fantastic views of the city. The entire immaculately maintained hotel complex is characterized by a simple and at the same time elegant Asian style. You will be pampered in the middle of lush tropical gardens, in the luxurious Oasis Spa with a heavenly massage according to all the rules of art. Taste experiences of a special kind guarantee a private Candle Light Dinner as the sun sets over the city. You will enjoy an unforgettable dinner specially designed for you and experience the “City of Angels” with all your senses. Immerse yourself in the unique harmony of luxury and nature offered to you in this city oasis. Overnight in Bangkok.

Day 2 Discover the most beautiful sides of Bangkok

Get ready for a day full of colorful impressions of the multi-faceted metropolis. Together with your guide, you will first travel to the magnificent Royal Palace, from where rulers ruled the country for centuries. Here you will almost feel like kings and queens from ancient times: everything glitters and sparkles wherever the eye looks. In the immediate vicinity is Wat Phra Keo, one of the most important temples in the whole country. Numerous ceremonies are still held here today, in which the royal family participates. In addition, the temple houses the beautiful Emerald Buddha, whose contemplation hardly comes out of amazement. Equally beautiful to look at is the famous temple Wat Pho with its more than a thousand Buddha figures and the gigantic reclining Buddha, next to which you suddenly feel tiny. In the evening, a real culinary highlight awaits you: a unique dinner cruise on board the Manohra (together with other guests) on the Chao Phraya River. Enjoy a lush menu as you leisurely cruise past the illuminated skyline and palaces along the river. The well-equipped noble bar will spoil you with cocktails and tingling champagne (not included). Overnight in Bangkok.

Day 3 Colorful Flower Splendor & Thai Wedding Candy

The morning begins with color: immerse yourself in the bustle of the Pak Klong Talad – Bang-kok’s famous flower market. Between the narrow streets and lush stands, you will observe dealers, customers and florists. Locals negotiate, laugh, shop – a lively spectacle that impressively shows the significance of flowers in Thai everyday life and culture: whether as an offering in temples or as a sign of silent symbolism. Afterwards you get into a typical tuk tuk that takes you to the “Old Siam” shopping mall. A sweet treat awaits you here: traditional Thai sweets, often served at weddings, are available for tasting. After the sweet snack, continue to Wat Benchamabophit, one of Bangkok’s most beautiful temples. Known under the name “Marmortempel” it was also built from this noble material. Finally, your driver will take you to your hotel where you can get fresh. Today you can enjoy your dinner in the star restaurant “Nahm” of the COMO Metropolitan Bangkok Hotel with fantastic views of the beautiful garden area. With a drink at the Vertigo & Moon Bar Rooftop at Banyan Tree Hotel Bangkok, the day can be relaxed. Overnight in Bangkok.

Day 4 - The most magnificent temples of Chiang Mais

After breakfast, you will be taken to the airport to continue traveling by domestic flight to Chiang Mai. There you will be greeted by your guide and driven to the hotel first. The city is enchanted by a multitude of magnificent temples and lies in the middle of a beautiful landscape. In the surrounding mountains live in numerous small settlements mountain peoples who have preserved their ancient traditions in the immediate vicinity of this modern metropolis. During your first exploration of the fascinating city, you will first dedicate yourself to some of the most beautiful temples today. Especially old is Wat Suan Dok, who is already visible from a distance with his almost fifty-meter-high stupa. On the grounds of the temple there is also a radiant white mausoleum, which houses the ashes of members of the Royal Family of Chiang Mai. Just outside the city center is Wat Jed Yod, a stone sanctuary with seven towers built in the 15th century. The landmark of the city is a little further out on a hill: Wat Phra That Doi Suthep with its golden chedi. From up here, you also have a unique view of the city and the surrounding countryside. Overnight in Chiang Mai.

5th day Private cooking course with market visit

Today, a special experience awaits you: as love passes through the stomach, you will be introduced to the secrets of Thailand’s diverse cuisine during a private cooking class. First, accompany the chef to the market, where you together buy the ingredients you need later. Back in the kitchen, watch the professional prepare the individual dishes before you try it yourself. The menu varies daily and is always adapted to a specific region or theme, so you can choose for yourself what you want to prepare. Of course, you can also taste your self-enchanted 3-course menu together afterwards! The rest of the day you can freely design. Continue exploring Chiang Mai on your own or spend a relaxing afternoon for two in your dreamlike hotel. Located near the Ping River, 137 Pillars House Chiang Mai is one of the Small Luxury Hotels of the World and a true haven of tranquility. Relax on your large terrace overlooking the tropical gardens, allow yourself to be pampered in the magnificent spa area according to all the rules of art or take a bird’s eye view of Chiang Mai on a helicopter flight (not included in the travel price). Overnight in Chiang Mai.

Day 6 Traditional Lanna Wedding Ceremony

In order to reaffirm the bond of marriage already concluded in the homeland, today we are organizing a North Thai Lanna wedding ceremony for you. They are dressed in traditional robes and once again symbolically married in the style of the northern Lanna. In a beautiful Lanna-style house, you will be dressed in traditional robes and once again symbolically married in the style of the northern Lanna. A flower garland is wrapped around both of your hands as a sign of closeness and connection, followed by other traditional rituals. At the end of the ceremony, you plant a love tree together with the wish that it will grow and flourish as well as your love. During a lunch accompanied by traditional dance and singing, you can once again enjoy the richness of Thai cuisine and toast to your marital happiness. The afternoon is at your disposal. Overnight in Chiang Mai.

7th day Tropical Island Paradise on Phuket

Without tour guide & chauffeur

After you have gained many exciting impressions of the rich cultural treasures of Thailand as well as its friendly inhabitants in recent days, you certainly want to enjoy some togetherness. Once you have arrived on the dreamlike island of Phuket, you can simply let go, relax your soul and let all the precious experiences of the last few days resound in peace. For your time out, we have chosen a very special place: the exclusive The Surin Phuket. This stylish resort is tucked away on the quiet west coast of the island, right on Pansea Beach. Surrounded by lush tropical nature and with wonderful views of the Andaman Sea, you will experience a paradise as from the picture book. In the evening, a very special highlight awaits you: a romantic dinner by candlelight in the elegant Blue Elephant Restaurant, known for its fine Thai cuisine. At the end of this relaxing day, enjoy a delicious cocktail at the bar if you wish. Overnight in Phuket.

8th day enjoying time for two

Without tour guide & chauffeur

Today belongs to you alone. Sleep in, have breakfast until noon and just let yourself drift today. If you have a sense of activity or culture, you can take part in one of the excursions that the resort offers. For fitness lovers, a gym is available directly by the pool – perfect for an invigorating workout with a view. Or how about gentle movement on the beach or in the water? The Surin Phuket invites you to sustainable water sports – without motors, but with all the more natural connection. Kayaks, paddleboards or snorkels are at your disposal for free and provide you with a relaxing pleasure in harmony with the breathtaking environment. The resort is actively committed to the protection of the coastal landscape and the Andaman Sea – so that future generations can also experience this paradisiacal nature. There are no limits to your perfect day. Overnight in Phuket.

Day 9 - Go to the beach paradise Koh Yao Noi

Without tour guide & chauffeur

Enjoy a last delicious breakfast at The Surin before being taken by private van to Phuket Yacht Haven Marina for a private speedboat ride to Six Senses Yao Noi. You will spend the next five nights at Six Senses Yao Noi with fantastic views of the scenic backdrop of the limestone formations of Phang Nga Bay. In the typical Six Senses style, nature and luxury complement each other to form a true refuge in the midst of magnificent nature. The 55 pool villas in a noble rustic style are embedded in the natural, tropical vegetation and offer the highest level of privacy – spiraling up a small hill. Immerse yourself in your personal infinity pool in front of your villa and enjoy the view from your private terrace. The professional Six Senses spa area offers a holistic range of relaxing massages and baths as well as invigorating peels. Of course, culinary delights are not neglected either. Tonight, with romantic candlelight, let yourself taste a delicious dinner specially arranged for you in your private villa. Overnight at Koh Yao Noi.

10th-13th day of pure romance on Koh Yao

Without tour guide & chauffeur

Make these days completely according to your personal ideas. Do you have a sense of variety? Then try your hand at yoga, play a round of tennis or take advantage of the numerous water sports opportunities. Typical activities such as cooking classes and Thai boxes are also offered. Want to see more of the surroundings? Wonderful: The area is very easy to explore if you rent a bike from the hotel. Some local restaurants are also accessible. During these days, a 60-minute couples massage is also included for you, after which you will feel wonderfully refreshed and relaxed. The three excellent hotel restaurants and the wine cellar pamper you with all kinds of culinary delights. At Cinema Paradiso, you can end the day romantically with a movie under the stars. Could travel be even better? Overnight at Koh Yao Noi.

Day 14 Farewell to Paradise

Even the most beautiful journey comes to an end. Immerse yourself once again through the delicious breakfast buffet of your resort and enjoy the view of the beautiful surroundings. Then we say goodbye to the island paradise. A private speedboat will first take you to the marina of Phuket, where your driver will wait for you and take you to the airport. After 14 days, you will travel home with many romantic experiences and colorful impressions of a very special country.

Palaces, temples and dream beaches - get to know Thailand's most beautiful sides

Thailand Tour: Experience Thailand’s most beautiful palaces, temples and dream beaches with Asia Special Tours, your expert for individual tours.

Itinerary & services

English itinerary text is an offline machine translation of the German original for this team preview. Please confirm travel details with your advisor; the German source remains available through the language switch.

On this extensive Thailand tour, we will show you the most beautiful and exciting sides of the country. During your three-day stay in Bangkok, you will explore the Thai metropolis, also known as the "Venice of the East", extensively. Marvel at the fascinating Temple of Dawn and the magnificent Royal Palace, explore rural Bangkok by bike and enjoy fantastic views from the Golden Mountain. As you continue your journey, immerse yourself in the bustle of Thai markets, visiting the former capital Ayutthaya and exploring the fabled Golden Triangle and Chiang Mai, the “Rose of the North”. A very special experience is also the visit of a Thai family, which gives you an insight into their everyday life. To crown your trip, you can relax on the dream beaches of Khao Lak or Koh Samui and revisit the countless newly gained impressions in peace. Determine your own rhythm and let yourself drift – these days belong to you alone.

1st day Perfect start: a dinner cruise on the Chao Phraya

Welcome to Thailand! At Bangkok Airport, you will be picked up by your private guide and taken to your hotel. Known in the Thai language as the “City of Angels”, Bangkok is a city with an exciting history where you can experience ancient temples stretching magnificently up the shores, colorful palaces sparkling spectacularly in the sunlight and a river winding through the heart of the city. The rest of the afternoon you can arrange according to your own ideas. Arrive in peace or take a first small exploration tour on your own. In the evening, a first big highlight awaits you: a unique dinner cruise (together with other guests) on the Chao Phraya River. Enjoy a sumptuous Asian dinner as you leisurely cruise past the illuminated skyline and palaces along the river. So you get first impressions of the multi-faceted city. Overnight in Bangkok.

2nd day Temples, palaces and impressive Buddhas

Bangkok’s most beautiful sights are waiting to be discovered by you. Together with your guide, you will embark on a varied tour through the multifaceted metropolis. Their first destination is the magnificent Royal Palace, an absolute “must see” from which rulers ruled the country for centuries. Here you can also admire the temple of Jade Buddha. Not far away is the famous temple Wat Pho, which houses more than a thousand Buddha figures and one of the largest solo depictions of Buddha: the reclining Buddha. After lunch, a longtail boat ride through the canals on the west bank of Chao Phraya will give you an idea of why Bangkok is nicknamed “Venice of the Far East”. Finally, head to one of Bangkok’s most extraordinary temples, the Temple of the Golden Mountain. Over 400 steps take you to the center of the complex, the great golden chedi, which houses a relic of Buddha and also gives you a fantastic view over the city. Overnight in Bangkok.

Day 3: Explore Bangkok away from the crowds by bicycle

The words “bicycle” and “Bangkok” hardly fit together at first glance. Too hectic, too crowded, too many cars – this is how the capital presents itself almost every day. But once you have crossed the river Chao Phraya, you quickly think you are in a completely different city. Coconut groves, small waterways, temples, parks and almost village communities let you quickly forget the towers of glass and steel and the eternal convulsion of the metropolis. Here you get to know Bangkok from a completely different side. Overnight in Bangkok. Note: English-speaking guide during the bicycle tour; in high season the tour takes place together with other travelers. 

Day 4 Experience colorful market driving

After breakfast, you will leave Bangkok and immerse yourself in the bustling market of Thailand. A particularly unusual location awaits you at the Maeklong market directly on the railway tracks. Eight times a day, the train passes only a few centimeters past the stands and sweeps sellers along with their goods with a loud signal for a short time from the rails. For the sellers long routine, however, this is a real highlight for visitors. Damnoen Saduak, on the other hand, is the most popular floating market in the area. With a motorized long boat you drive past stilt houses, gardens and temples and finally reach the actual market with its colorful bustle directly on the water. Admittedly, the market is touristy, but still worth a visit due to its long tradition. To get a lot more colorful impressions, continue to Kanchanaburi, where you first look at the infamous bridge on the Kwai. During a visit to the Hellfire Monument, you will learn about the construction of the “death railway”, which cost the lives of hundreds of thousands of prisoners of war. You will spend the night at The FloatHouse River Kwai, swimming on the river surrounded by dense jungle, which can only be reached by boat. Just wonderfully idyllic and something very special! Overnight in Kanchanaburi.

Day 5 - The Old Royal City of Ayutthaya

Before you continue your journey, Kanchanaburi offers a special experience: take the Thailand-Burma railway through the picturesque Kwai valley. Let your eyes wander and enjoy the idyllic backdrop. Afterwards we continue by car to Ayutthaya. In the second capital of the kingdom of Siam, the sheer number of large ruins alone testifies to the former splendor of the metropolis, where more than a million people lived at high times. Take a lot of time to visit the Historic Park – it’s worth it! In the evening, your guide will take you to the hotel. Overnight in Ayutthaya.

Day 6 - Continue to the North

A culture-packed day is ahead of you, rejoice! Your journey now takes you to the north of the country. On your way to Sukhothai, stop at Saraburi. It is said that there is a footprint of Buddha to admire here. In the “monkey city” Lopburi you can watch hundreds of curious primates frolicking around. The Wat Mahta of Phitsanuloke is also worth a trip. Here, one of the most beautiful Buddha statues in the country has its home. Finally, you will reach Sukhothai, where you will immediately be taken to your hotel. Overnight in Sukhothai.

7th day Sukhothai, cradle of Thai culture and spiritual center

Sukhothai is considered the cradle of Thai culture and spiritual center of the kingdom. Take plenty of time to explore the Sukhothais Historic Park and the sister town of Sri Satchanarai in detail. Both parks are part of the UNESCO World Heritage Site, but differ greatly from each other atmospherically. With many pictures of the magnificent facilities in mind, you will continue to Lampang, where little Wat Phra Keow is on your program. Overnight in Lampang.

Day 8 Chiang Rai – the gateway to the Golden Triangle

Start today with a visit to Wat Phrathat Lampang Luang, one of the oldest teak temples in the world. On your way to Chiang Rai, stop at Phayao. The unique, rural city on Thailand’s largest inland waters is really worth seeing! However, since you still have some plans today, you will continue your journey soon. Arriving in Chiang Rai, the gateway to the Golden Triangle, you will get to know the most beautiful sides of the city on a round trip. Admire, among others, the Wat Phra Kaew temple, which once housed the famous Jadebuddha. Today it can be seen as a national sanctuary in the King's Temple of the same name in Bangkok. Also worth seeing and nearby are the temples Wat Phra Singh, a prime example of traditional Lanna architecture, and Wat Mung Muang, who miraculously performed during the bombing raids on Chiang Rai. 2World War I was unscathed. Then a very special sanctuary awaits you: Wat Rong Khun, the "White Temple". This modern masterpiece of temple architecture with its dazzlingly white, richly decorated facade almost looks like it is made of frosting. Overnight in Chiang Rai. 

Day 9 Excursion to the legendary Golden Triangle

Discover the Golden Triangle – the triangle of Thailand, Laos and Myanmar – which was once one of the largest transhipment areas for opium in the entire world. From a viewpoint, you can see the foothills of all three nations. During your visit to the Opium Hall you will learn more about the past of this place. A trade of a different kind still flourishes today at the former drug transshipment hub: Laotians who use a gap in customs law at the border sell all sorts of bizarre whiskey creations and other souvenirs. Take a boat trip on the Mekong and visit the venerable ruins of Chiang Saen. After a stroll across the border market of Mae Sai, Thailand’s northernmost city, head to Wat Doi Tung, which is located on a hill on the northwestern outskirts where you can relax with a beautiful view of the city and the Kok River. Overnight in Chiang Rai. 

Day 10 With the longboat down the coke

A new day, a new goal. With the motorized long boat you drive down the Coke River towards Thaton. You will pass small mountain villages, bamboo huts and wind your way through the jungle. Finally, you reach your day’s goal. The small village of Thaton is only a few kilometers from the Burmese border. Overnight in Thaton.

Day 11 Chiang Mai, the “Rose of the North”

Enjoy a delicious breakfast at your accommodation before the journey to Chiang Mai continues. Look forward to that day! During your drive to Chiang Mai, stop at Wat Tham Chiang, where you will visit the caves of Chiang Dao. The extensive system is partly walkable, impresses with bizarre formations and serves in parts as a natural temple. Arriving in Chiang Mai, you will get a first overview on an exploration tour of the most famous temples. Your guide will take you to Wat Chedi Luang as well as the Royal Temple of Wat Phra Singh, one of the most popular temples in the city. Particularly important for the great importance of the temple is its unique Buddha statue, which is annually guided to large processions from the temple. The proverbial highlight of the day is probably the visit to the mountain temple Wat Doi Suthep. Situated at an altitude of 1,000 meters, the temple impresses with a large golden pagoda and – in good weather – with a fantastic panoramic view. In the evening you stroll over the night market. Overnight in Chiang Mai.

Day 12 Visiting a Thai Family

Today you can expect very special and authentic experiences, because you are visiting a Thai family that gives you an insight into their everyday life. At the end of the day, you will most likely take something more with you than “just” beautiful memories. You will learn interesting things about the history and tradition of your host family and will be initiated – privately – in the preparation of a typical northern Thai lunch. During the day, there are always opportunities for lively conversations with your hosts, to exchange ideas and to pursue one of the most popular activities – not only Thais: food! Overnight in Chiang Mai.

13th day Chiang Mai and exploring the surrounding area by bike

With the bike ride out into nature around Chiang Mai. You start near the famous Tha Phae Gate and cycle through quiet side streets. Admire ancient temples, traditional markets and residential areas in historic neighborhoods. As soon as you leave the famous city walls behind, the “Rose of the North” shows its other, peaceful facet. In the afternoon you can relax a little and relax in your hotel. Overnight in Chiang Mai.  
Note: English-speaking guide during the bicycle tour; in high season the tour takes place together with other travelers. 

14th day on to the beach paradise

Without guidance

Now it is time for some relaxation. With many new impressions and experiences in your luggage, you will be taken to the airport of Chiang Mai from where you will travel to Khao Lak or Koh Samui. The multifaceted Khao Lak enchants with its peaceful atmosphere and paradisiacal sandy beaches. But Koh Samui, the second largest island in Thailand, is also known for its dreamlike, cocospal-fringed beaches. Sarojin Khao Lak is an exclusive luxury boutique beach resort with only 56 rooms. Beautifully located on a secluded, eleven kilometers long beach, you can relax here. Exceptional, romantic dining experiences and soothing spa massages spoil all your senses. The Anantara Bophut Koh Samui Resort also leaves nothing to be desired. Sunbathe at the 30 meter long infinity pool or in the white sand of Bophut. The luxurious spa invites you to relax and dream, while the resort’s restaurants seduce you with delicious dishes of Thai and Western cuisine. How could a holiday be more beautiful? Overnight in Khao Lak or on Koh Samui.

15-20. Day time to enjoy

Without guidance

“Between heaven and earth” – these days belong to you alone. Determine your own rhythm and let yourself drift. Start the day with a rich breakfast and realize your island dreams. Paddle with a kayak over the crystal clear water, marvel at the colorful underwater world on a snorkeling trip or take a trip to boat or land in the area. There are dreamlike days waiting for you, which you will wish will never end. Overnight in Khao Lak or on Koh Samui.

Day 21 Farewell to Paradise

Even if you find it difficult to leave this magical place, your journey is now coming to an end. Enjoy your breakfast with sea views, swim and snorkel extensively before you are taken to the airport for your flight home. The many memories and photos from your wonderful trip will make you remember them for a long time. Who knows, maybe one day you will return there.

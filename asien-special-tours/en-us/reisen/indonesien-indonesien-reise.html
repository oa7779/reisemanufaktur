Best of Indonesia - A magical island world

Indonesia Island Hopping - Best of Indonesia. From West to East, travel through Indonesia on this travel creation of Asia Special Tours. Ask now!

Itinerary & services

English itinerary text is an offline machine translation of the German original for this team preview. Please confirm travel details with your advisor; the German source remains available through the language switch.

Have you been dreaming of a trip to Indonesia for a long time and would like to get to know the highlights of this diverse island nation? Then this trip is just right for you. Your adventure begins on Su-matra, where lush rainforests and animal encounters await you. On Java, visit the world-famous temple complexes of Borobudur and Prambanan and get deep insights into the traditional life of the locals. Immerse yourself in the culinary delights of Bali and experience the unique culture on the Island of the Gods. Let yourself be captivated by the exotic flair of Indonesia, travel through beautiful landscapes and feel the mystical tranquility in the traditional temple complexes. Always travel at your own pace and enjoy exceptional retreats. Another highlight awaits you in Komodo National Park, where you will encounter the famous Komodo monitors and explore the island world in front of Flores. For the crowning end, spend relaxing days on the original island of Sumba. Far from the hustle and bustle of everyday life, you can enjoy fantastic beaches, exceptional nature and the special tranquility of one of Indonesia’s most exclusive retreats.

1st day Arrival in Medan and Departure to Bukit Lawang

Upon arrival at Medan Airport, you will be warmly welcomed by your guide. The capital of the province of North Sumatra is one of Indonesia’s most important international gateways and impresses with a mixture of Malay, Chinese and colonial influences. During a short city tour, you’ll discover some of the metropolitan’s major attractions, including the magnificent Maimoon Palace, the Great Mosque, and a traditional Chinese temple. The well-preserved historical buildings from the colonial period also provide an insight into the eventful history of the city. Then drive to Bukit Lawang on the edge of Gunung-Leuser National Park. The approximately four-hour, adventurous ride leads through plantations and rural areas. After your arrival at your lodge, there is time to let the relaxed atmosphere of the rainforest village affect you. Enjoy the first impressions of the tropical jungle and listen to the fascinating sounds of the rainforest. Overnight in Bukit Lawang.

2nd day In the footsteps of the orangutans in Gunung-Leuser National Park

It will be animalistic, because today you go in the footsteps of the orangutans. You will take a half-day hike, where you will penetrate deep into the jungle of Gunung Leuser National Park. Good fitness is required for this! With a bit of luck, you’ll be rewarded with the sight of free-living orangutans and other monkey species. After this impressive nature experience, you will reach the Bohorok River. On a traditional tire hose, let yourself be relaxed by the current through the lush jungle landscape back towards Bukit Lawang. Around noon you will reach your lodge again, where you can rest a little. In the afternoon, visit Timbang Lawang Eco Farm, which is committed to nature conservation and agriculture. Did you know how brown sugar is made and how gum trees are tapped? Overnight in Bukit Lawang.

Day 3 The Highlands of Berastagi

After breakfast, head to the village of Timbang Lawang, where you’ll get a glimpse of northern Sumatra rural life. Here you will experience the traditional production of tofu and palm sugar, which is still carried out according to traditional methods. Afterwards, the journey will take you to the highlands of Berastagi in about five hours. Located at an altitude of about 1,300 meters, the town is known for its pleasantly cool climate and spectacular location between the volcanoes Sinabung (2,417 m) and Sibayak (2,172 m). In the late afternoon, you will have the opportunity to stroll through Berastagi’s colorful fruit market. Exotic fruits, regional specialties and the lively activity of the traders give an authentic impression of local everyday life. Overnight in Berastagi. 
 

Day 4 Hike to Mount Sibayak Volcano

Early in the morning you will be picked up and taken to the starting point of the ascent of Mount Sibayak volcano. After a one-hour ascent, you will reach the summit where you can admire the smoking sulfur springs in a moon-like landscape. After descending, you will return to your hotel on the same path. The rest of the day is at your disposal in Berastagi. For example, visit the traditional Karo Batak village of Lingga with its characteristic long houses or the Lumbini Nature Park, known for its impressive golden pagoda. If volcanic activity permits, it is also possible to visit a viewpoint overlooking the majestic Sinabung Volcano. Overnight in Berastagi.

Our tip: You want to experience even more of the enchanting island of Sumatra? Then extend a few more days on the beautiful Toba Lake with the volcanic island of Samosir.

Day 5: Flight to Yogyakarta and journey to Borobudur

After a few eventful days on Sumatra, you will return to Medan today and be taken to the airport. Then fly on to Yogyakarta on Java, where you are already expected. You will be taken to your hotel. Relax in the peaceful and tranquil surroundings of your tasteful hotel and get ready for the next few days on the charming island of Java. Overnight in Borobudur.

Day 6 Sunrise in Borobudur & Luxury Mesa Stila

Today, before sunrise, make your way to Borobudur, the largest Buddhist temple in the world. In the mystical atmosphere of the morning, you will experience how the first rays of sun dip the temple complex into a warm light. Let this special moment affect you and enjoy the tranquility of this place. The pyramid-shaped temple is one of the most important testimonies to the Buddhist history of Indonesia. It is said that the many terraces with stone reliefs show the faithful the way to nirvana. Then return to your hotel for breakfast and enjoy a relaxing morning. Later, you will be taken to the exclusive MesaStila Resort. Nestled in a historic coffee plantation and surrounded by tropical nature as well as the majestic volcanoes of central Java, an extraordinary retreat for body and mind awaits you. The complex combines colonial heritage with modern comfort and includes lovingly restored historical buildings, including the former home of the plantation owner. Take advantage of the rest of the day to enjoy the amenities of the resort. Overnight in Magelang.

7th day of free time in Magelang

This day is free for you. Take the time to explore the region on your own or enjoy the amenities of your exceptional hotel. Take a guided tour of the plantation, where coffee has been grown for almost 100 years, and learn about traditional coffee production. Relaxation promises the renowned Hammam Spa, where you can be pampered with coffee from your own plantation. Overnight in Magelang.

8th Day Culinary Experience in Yogyakarta

Enjoy an extensive breakfast and start the day comfortably. Around noon you will leave for Yogyakarta. Where you will be taken to your hotel in the heart of the city. In the evening, you will be picked up and driven to the renowned Gajah Wong Restaurant. In a stylish setting, enjoy a dinner of Indonesian specialties. Located directly on the river, the restaurant combines excellent cuisine with a special atmosphere and forms the perfect setting for an enjoyable evening in Yogyakarta. Then you will be returned to the hotel. Overnight in Yogyakarta.

Day 9 Yogyakartas Cultural Treasures and the Temples of Prambanan

After breakfast, discover the cultural treasures of Yogyakarta. At the Sonobudoyo Museum, you’ll get exciting insights into Javanese culture with historic Wayang dolls, ornate masks and traditional Gamelan instruments. Afterwards, visit the Kraton, the magnificent Sultan’s Palace of Yogyakarta and the Sultan’s residence to this day. The extensive facility provides a fascinating insight into the history, traditions and courtly life of Java. Depending on the day of visit, you can also experience traditional dance or music performances. Afterwards, explore the city authentically on a traditional Becak ride through the historic Kauman district to the Taman Sari Water Palace. The former royal bathing facilities with their pavilions and water basins are among the most beautiful sights in the city. In the afternoon, Prambanan is another cultural highlight. The majestic temple complex from the 9th century is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and is considered one of the most important Hindu sanctuaries in Southeast Asia. The impressive temple towers dedicated to Brahma, Vishnu and Shiva tell of the rich history and spiritual significance of Java. In the late afternoon, return to your hotel. Overnight in Yogyakarta.

10th day Flight to Bali

Today we say goodbye to the cultural treasures and impressive landscapes of Java. Your onward flight will take you to the neighboring island of Bali, which has been captivating travelers for generations with its unique mix of spirituality, nature and joie de vivre. Upon arrival at Denpasar airport, you will be warmly welcomed and taken to Ubud, the cultural heart of the island. Here, the COMO Uma Ubud is a luxurious retreat that is harmoniously embedded in the lush jungle landscape. Inspired by the architecture of Balinese temples, the resort combines timeless elegance with authentic island charm. Use the rest of the day to arrive calmly. Relax by the pool, listen to the sounds of nature and enjoy the special atmosphere of your hotel surrounded by tropical greenery. Overnight in Ubud.

Day 11: Following the Trail of Balinese Spirituality

Today, a special experience awaits you. Early in the morning you will make your way to Pura Lempuyang, one of the most important and sacred temples in Bali. The name means “the highest lantern” and refers to the great spiritual significance of this plant. During a walk of about two and a half hours, follow a forested path up to over 1,000 meters. The climb requires a good condition, but rewards with grandiose views. With clear visibility, the panorama ranges from the majestic Mount Agung to the rugged east coast of Bali with its tranquil coves and shimmering sea. After a little reinforcement, you will receive the traditional blessing of a Hindu priest in this special place and experience an authentic insight into the spiritual traditions of the island. Then descend the approximately 1,700 steps and enjoy a well-deserved lunch. In the afternoon, visit the Water Palace Tirta Gangga, whose name means “Water of the Ganges”. The former royal complex impresses with artfully landscaped water basins, fountains and lush gardens and is one of the most beautiful sights of East Bali. Then return to your hotel and enjoy the evening in a relaxed atmosphere. Overnight in Ubud.

12th day Ubud between tradition, art and cuisine

Today you will discover the cultural treasures of Ubuds. Your day begins in the Sacred Monkey Forest, a haunted complex with centuries-old temples and free-living macaques. From there you will go to the Saraswati Temple, whose lotus pond and filigree architecture are among the most beautiful photo opportunities in the city. In the heart of Ubud, visit Ubud Palace before strolling through the market. Between artfully crafted crafts, colorful textiles and local specialties, you will experience the flair of the city. An insight into the art and culture of Bali is offered by the renowned Agung Rai Museum of Art (ARMA). For lunch, enjoy the special atmosphere of the stylish Tanah Gajah. After a break at your hotel, an extraordinary culinary experience awaits you in the early evening. In a nostalgic VW convertible you drive through the hills and rice terraces around Ubud. Overlooking the Sayan Valley, enjoy sunset cocktails and creative fusion tapas while the tropical landscape is immersed in warm evening light. Afterwards, the evening will take you to an elegant restaurant on the Campuhan River. Here you will end the day enjoyably with a multi-course dinner before returning to your hotel or continuing the evening in Ubud as you wish. Overnight in Ubud.

Day 13 Through the Green Highlands of God's Island

Today you will discover the diverse landscapes of the Balinese highlands. The prelude is the King's Temple of Mengwi, one of the most beautiful temples on the island. From there, the journey continues to Jatiluwih, where the famous rice terraces protected by UNESCO run like a green carpet over the hills. On an e-bike tour, experience this unique cultural landscape up close and get insights into the traditional Subak irrigation system that has shaped the island’s agriculture for centuries. After lunch, green rice terraces and gentle hillside landscapes gradually change to the mountain world of Bedugul. On the shores of Lake Beratan, the Ulun-Danu Temple awaits one of Bali's most famous landmarks. Picturesquely situated on the water and dedicated to the goddess of water, the temple complex seems to float almost on the lake. In the late afternoon, continue your journey towards the south coast. Before reaching Seminyak, visit the famous Tanah Lot Temple, built on a rock formation off the coast. As one of the most important sea temples in Bali, it is deeply rooted in the mythology of the island and offers a particularly impressive backdrop. Then continue and move into your hotel. Overnight in Seminyak.

14th day free time in Seminyak

This day is yours. Enjoy the beach and sea and the amenities of your hotel. Take the day slowly, make time for a good book or the sweet nothing-doing. Overnight in Seminyak.

15th day Flight to Labuan Bajo

After breakfast, the next adventure awaits you! They are taken to Denpasar airport and fly to Labuan Bajo on the island of Flores, where boom boats and flower-decorated houses line up. You can immediately see why the Portuguese gave the island the name “Cap of Flowers” in the 16th century. Overnight in Labuan Bajo.

16th day boat trip to Komodo National Park

Leinen los - look forward to today's excursion. With the boat you travel to the lesser-known island of Rinca. The goal of the day is to meet the Komodo-Waran. With a length of three meters and a weight of 90 kilograms, this is the largest lizard in the world. A ranger will take you through the forests and savannahs to the highest point in the area for a magnificent view of the island panorama. On the way, you often see one or more Komodo monitors. Back on board you will have lunch and continue on to the island of Komodo. With your boat you will continue to “Pink Beach”, a beach whose sand shimmers slightly pink. There is time to discover the fascinating underwater world while swimming or snorkeling and enjoy the unique backdrop. In the afternoon, return to your hotel in Labuan Bajo. Overnight in Labuan Bajo.

Day 17: Flores returns to the coast of Bali

The morning is at your disposal. In the afternoon you will fly back to Bali and then be taken to your hotel in Seminyak. Oberoi Bali is located on one of the most beautiful beaches in Bali. With only 74 villas and pavilions, the luxury hotel is a small peaceful oasis far from any hustle and bustle. The rooms are built in the traditional Balinese style, from where you can enjoy a fantastic view of the beach or the tropical garden. Take advantage of the rest of the day to enjoy the relaxing atmosphere of the resort, relax by the pool or take a stroll along Seminyak’s long sandy beach. Let the day end with an atmospheric sunset over the Indian Ocean. Overnight in Seminyak.

Day 18: Flight to Sumba

As the culmination of your journey, one of Indonesia’s most original and fascinating islands awaits you. After transfer to the airport, you will fly to Sumba, about 400 kilometers east of Bali. The island delights with gentle hillside landscapes, traditional villages, unspoilt beaches and a culture that is still firmly rooted in its centuries-old traditions. Here you get your exclusive domicile, the Cap Karoso. Situated on a sprawling sandy beach, the stylish resort combines modern architecture with Sumba’s natural beauty. Clear lines, sustainable design and a special attention to local craftsmanship create an atmosphere of relaxed elegance and contemporary luxury. Enjoy the view of the turquoise blue sea, relax by the pool or explore the surrounding area. The resort has its own organic farm, which provides many ingredients for the excellent cuisine and underlines the close connection to the nature of the island. Let the impressions of your journey pass in this extraordinary environment and enjoy the special tranquility and originality of Sumba. Overnight in Sumba.

19-22. Day time out on Sumba

Without tour guide/chauffeur

These days belong to you alone. Determine your own rhythm and let yourself drift. Let your soul really dangle and enjoy moments when time seems to stop. Explore the fascinating underwater world with its colorful fish and corals with diving glasses. Take a jungle hike or sail by boat over the expanses of the sea. Forget your everyday life and immerse yourself in your very personal dream world. Overnight in Sumba. 

23rd day Farewell to Sumba and return to Bali

Today we say goodbye to this magical place. In the afternoon, your return flight will go to Bali, where you will be taken to your hotel. Enjoy your last evening in Indonesia once again to the fullest, to let this incredible journey pass with all impressions. Overnight in Seminyak.

24th day Departure from Indonesia

Your time out from everyday life is coming to an end. Even if your holiday is soon over, the precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories of your trip will accompany you forever. Look forward to letting your friends and family share your experiences in the distance at home. Your driver will take you to the airport for your return flight.

Mongolia Round Trip - Blue Pearl

Tour Mongolia: Get to know the secret of the Blue Pearl with Asia Special Tours. Request your individual Mongolia trip here.

Enjoy an exciting round trip through a fascinating country that will delight you with exceptional natural beauty and the friendliness of its inhabitants. Experience a mixture of infinite steppe, traditional cattle breeding and urban life, from identification with history and the connection to modernity. Leave the hustle and bustle of civilization in favor of solitude in the steppes and deserts, listen to the sand dunes of Khongoryn Els "sing" and get insights into the simple but nature-loving nomadic life. During your journey you will discover the infinite expanse again and again on hikes. The absolute highlight is the “Blue Pearl” – the beautiful Khuvsgul Lake located in the north of the country. Here you have time to relax. Enjoy the dreamlike landscape during walks on the lake shore or a boat tour. At the “Mongolian Sea” – as the lake is also called – you can simply let your soul dangle and switch off completely.

Day 1 Welcome to Mongolia!

Welcome to Mongolia! You are met at Ulaanbaatar Airport and taken to your hotel. Your sightseeing then begins at Sukhbaatar Square in the heart of the Mongolian capital. Two impressive monuments commemorate the conqueror Genghis Khan and the revolutionary leader Damddiny Sukhbaatar, who secured independence from China in 1921. Gandan Monastery is also on your itinerary. Built in the Tibetan style in 1835, it soon became a center of Buddhist learning in Mongolia. During the 20th century it played a special role: having survived the communist destruction of the 30s as one of the few undamaged monasteries, it was allowed to resume limited operations in 1944 as the only monastery in the country. In the afternoon, visit the Winter Palace of Bogd Khan. Overnight in Ulaanbaatar.

2nd day Dense forests and high mountains in Khan Kentii

Today begins your great overland journey. You will leave the Mongolian capital and drive to the densely forested and mountainous protected area of Khan Khentii National Park. Khan Khentii National Park surpasses with its 12.000 For many square kilometers a German federal state is in size and despite its proximity to the Mongolian capital is largely undeveloped; its center forms the with 2.800 The highest mountain in eastern Mongolia is the Asralt Khairkhan. Spend the night for the first time in a traditional Mongolian yurt. The Ger-Camp Princess Lodge lures not only with its first-class location, but also with a sauna. Overnight in Khan Khentii National Park.

3rd day time for beautiful walks

Well strengthened use today to explore the landscape of Khan Kentii National Park on foot. Their destination is Günjin Süm, the tomb of a daughter of Manchu Emperor Kangxi. Today, only the main temple and tomb of the princess are preserved from the once large complex. Nevertheless, the excursion is always worth it! After your visit to the tomb, you will return to your yurt camp. Overnight in Khan Khentii National Park.

Day 4 The Rock Landscape of Terelj

Your next destination is Terelj National Park, which is about an hour from the Princess yurt camp. Take advantage of the afternoon to explore the beautiful landscape of Terelj National Park on foot. In the mountains north of Tuul you will come to the landscape that made Terelj famous. Some of the rocks have names such as "Kauernder Mann" or "Turtle", the name of which will surely be immediately revealed to you. Your hike will take you to the "Turtle" Rock. If you wish, you will also have the opportunity to explore the landscape further on the back of a horse (optional booking on site). Perhaps you will also discover a brown bear, but certainly one or the other of the 250 native bird species. There is even a meditation stamp here. Overnight in Terelj National Park.

Day 5 - Continue to explore Terelj National Park

Take advantage of the day to explore the landscape of Terelj National Park on foot or on horseback (optional booking on site). Or you can spend the day in your yurt camp and enjoy the peace and quiet. If you wish, visit the Ger Museum or relax in the garden. The beer garden lures with cool Mongolian drinks, a barbecue and a fantastic view of the Terelj Valley. Overnight in Terelj National Park.

6th Day White and Red Rock Stupas at Zagaan Suvarga

Now you continue your overland journey through Mongolia. Following Mongolian tradition, take the sun south into the Gobi Desert. It is the northernmost and at the same time fifth largest desert in the world. In recent years, the road network has been improved, so that you can cover part of the route on the new highway, which leads from the capital to the Gobi. In some places you deviate into the terrain, while driving you can enjoy first impressions of the vast landscape. For lunch, you will arrive in the small desert town of Mandalgobi – an ideal opportunity to immerse yourself briefly in the everyday life of the inhabitants. There are also shopping opportunities here and you can have lunch in a simple restaurant. In the afternoon you will reach the mountains of Zagaan Suvarga. Due to their color and shape, these imposing rock formations are called “white and red stupas” by the locals. Over centuries, gigantic rocky cliffs have been created, which today shine in a wide variety of colors such as pink, red and orange. You stay again in a yurt camp and can admire the infinite starry sky above you at night. Overnight in Zagaan Suvarga.

7th Day Ancient Rock Drawings at Mount Del Uul

Continue through the desert to Mount Del Uul, where you can admire rock drawings from the Bronze Age on four kilometers. The endless expanses of the desert and the solitude interrupted only once in a while by a few camels and goat breeders will make you feel like you are somewhere at the end of the world. On hot summer days, it may even happen that a “Fata Morgana” appears in front of you, but dissolves into thin air again at the next moment. Late in the evening you will reach your yurt camp in the middle of the desert, where you will spend the night today. Overnight in South Gobi.

8th day Excursion to the Vulture Gorge and on to the sand dunes

Before you continue to the sand dunes, a trip to the vulture gorge (Yol gorge) is on your program. This is located in the Gurwan Saikhan National Park, on the foothills of the Altai Mountains. After visiting a small natural history museum, you will explore the gorge on a short hike and, with a bit of luck, meet the Capricorns and wild sheep living here. Then set off to see the longest walking dunes, the Khongoryn Els Sand Dunes in South Gobi. In the middle of the landscape, an extraordinary mountain of sand shoots up, which is up to 120 meters high and stretches over 180 km in length. Together with the dark mountain range in the background, there is a particularly beautiful panorama. Unforgettable in addition to this breathtaking sight will also be the meetings with camel breeders and, with a bit of luck, a gazelle herd. If you wish, you can also prove your talent in camel riding today and rent one of the “desert ships” (optional booking on site). Overnight in Khongoryn Els.

Day 9 The Flaming Cliffs

Spend the morning with another hike through the sand dunes and enjoy the spectacular scenery. After lunch, leave for Bayanzag. During the journey you will be accompanied by a landscape consisting of mountains, rocks and sand. You will visit one of the most famous sights in Mongolia - the "flaming cliffs", which got their name because of their bright orange color. In the 1920s, American scientist and adventurer Roy Chapman Andrews discovered a complete dinosaur nest with eggs here, which led to this place as well as the rest of Mongolia becoming an important place for paleontological research. Finally, you will reach your camp for the night, where you will rest in your yurt. Overnight in Bayanzag.

Day 10 Visiting Ongi Monastery

Your next destination is the ruins of the hidden monastery “Ongi”. The monastery once housed over 500 Buddhist monks and is located on the river of the same name. During religious persecutions in the 30In the years before, it was almost completely destroyed and many of the monks living there were sent to labor camps. Today there is a small, new monastery. With a little luck you will meet the monks who take care of the monastery and have the opportunity for a personal conversation. Overnight at Ongi Monastery.

Day 11 Fascinating Khogno Khan Mountains

The Khogno Khan Nature Reserve – your current destination – is located in the heart of Mongolia, right on the main road Ulaanbaatar – Karakorum. The special feature of the area is that here nature lovers and active holidaymakers can experience various natural zones, from mountains of granite to dune landscape. After arriving, there is enough time for a nice hike such as the sand dunes Elsen Tasarkhai, which are very close by. Here you also have the opportunity to rent camels and explore the sand dunes with the "desert ships" (for a fee). Overnight in the Khogno Khan Mountains.

Day 12: In the footsteps of Genghis Khan

As the sun rises over the infinite expanses of Central Mongolia, head towards Karakorum – under Genghis Khan and his successors capital of the former Mongolian Empire. Scientific excavations repeatedly reveal new impressive relics of this multicultural trading metropolis founded in 1220 and the place is still one of the most famous in the country. After visiting the excavation site and museum, you will visit the Erdene Zuu monastery built on the ruins of Karakorum in the 16th century, one of the largest in Mongolia. Unfortunately, only three of the formerly more than 60 buildings have been preserved after the Cultural Revolution, but with a total of 108 stupas and the white border wall, the monastery still offers an impressive picture. Extensive renovation work also helps to restore the shine of the building complex. A tour will take you to the large prayer hall and various temple halls. Overnight in Karakorum.

Day 13 Over Zezerleg to Khorgo Crater

After breakfast, continue via Zezerleg to Khorgo Terkhiin Zagaan Nuur National Park. There are mainly the crater of the extinct volcano Khorgo and the lake Terkhin Zagaan Nuur. The charming environment is perfect for hiking to your heart’s content. It is only about half an hour walk past cold, bizarre lava formations until you can look into the huge crater of the Khorgo. Overnight in Khorgo National Park.

14th day time to enjoy

You can make this day according to your own ideas. Spend time in your yurt camp, enjoy the peace and quiet, take walks through the beautiful surroundings or get interesting insights into the life of the nomads as you look over their shoulders in their activities. Overnight in Khorgo National Park.

Day 15 Into Mongolian Switzerland

Now you come to another vegetation zone, the taiga. In the area, which is largely forested with coniferous trees, you will visit “Mongolian Switzerland”. The landscape is actually similar to the Alps: fir trees grow on steep slopes, a green alb nestles between two mountain ranges and the rocks rise high up into the sky. The area is also rich in wildlife. With a bit of luck, you will see, for example, red deer, musk, Argalishafe and various bird species. The flora is also diverse and makes the heart of plant lovers beat faster. Finally, you get to your yurt camp in the middle of this magical landscape. Overnight in Khuvsgul Province.

Day 16 The “Blue Pearl” – in the sun glittering Khuvsgul Lake

Today your journey will take you through the villages of Moron and Khatgal, where you will each stop to visit them. Today’s destination is the “Blue Pearl” – Khuvsgul Lake, probably the most beautiful lake in all of Mongolia. Due to its size one of the most important freshwater reservoirs in the country and also known under the name “Mongolian Sea”. Many fish and birds find the ideal habitat in and around the lake with its crystal clear water. Once there, the rest of the day is at your disposal. Take a small walk along the shore or relax in your yurt camp. The dreamlike nature will inspire you. Overnight at Khuvsgul Lake.

17th Day Enjoying the Idyll at Lake Khuvsgul

Without guidance

Today at Khuvsgul Lake you spend according to your wishes. Take a boat trip (optionally bookable on site) or further hikes along the lake shore and get to know the different medicinal plants and wild flowers that occur there. Overnight at Khuvsgul Lake.

Day 18 - Drive along the Selenge River

Time to say goodbye to the beautiful Khuvsgul Lake. Continue on to the Selenge River, which you reach in the afternoon. During your journey, you will cross beautiful landscapes. With its approximately 615 kilometers of flow within Mongolia, the Selenge is the fifth longest river in the country, the remaining 409 kilometers are located in Russia. Numerous animals live in and around the river, in spring and autumn also many migratory birds. On the way, stop at the deer stones Uushigiin, about 25 kilometers from the city of Murun. Overnight in the Bulgan Province.

19th Day Impressive Monastery Amarbayasgalant

Drive past Erdenet, the third largest city in Mongolia. The modern, industrial city is particularly important for Mongolia due to copper mining and accounts for the majority of the country’s copper export. After lunch, continue your journey to Amarbayasgalant Monastery, which was nominated as a World Heritage Site by UNESCO in 1996. This early 18th-century monastery impresses with its homogeneous architecture and rich collection of Buddhist artwork. Today, it is undeniably one of the most important Buddhist centers in Mongolia. This building also fell victim to the communist waves of destruction in the 1930s, in which many monks were executed and numerous religious treasures were irrevocably destroyed. In the course of the reforms from 1990 onwards, however, the monastery then experienced an extensive restoration period and today again houses over 60 monks and novices. Overnight in Amarbayasgalant.

Day 20: Wild Horses at Hustai Nuruu National Park

Your journey will take you to the Hustai Nuruu National Park, home to numerous bird species with its typical Mongolian forest and steppe landscape. It is also home to the wild horses called Takhi or Przewalski, which died out in the wild in the 1960s. However, some animals had previously been brought into zoological gardens in Europe, so that descendants of these animals could be reintroduced into the steppes of Mongolia since the 1990s. In the evening you can observe the animals when they come to the waterholes for drinking. Overnight at Hustai Nuruu National Park.

Day 21 Back to the Capital

Today we return to Ulaanbaatar. After arriving in the capital, the rest of the day is at your disposal. Use the time, for example, to buy some souvenirs for your loved ones at home. Overnight in Ulaanbaatar.

22nd day Farewell to Mongolia

Today, unfortunately, it is time to say goodbye to this fascinating country – surely you have packed your suitcases with many new impressions. You will be driven to the airport from where you board your return flight. We wish you a good journey home!

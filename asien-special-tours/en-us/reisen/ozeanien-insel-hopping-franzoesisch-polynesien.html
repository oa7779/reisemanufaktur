Island Hopping French Polynesia - The Pearls of the South Seas

South Sea Island Hopping: Discover the pearls of the South Seas on an individual journey to French Polynesia with Asia Special Tours. Ask now!

Exotic flower scents and a touch of vanilla are the perfume of these paradisiacal islands in the Pacific. Soundful names like Tahiti, Moorea and Bora Bora await you – who wouldn’t dream of it? Relax in the beautiful island resorts lined with turquoise sea. As part of France, these islands have a noticeable French flair. Tahiti is the largest of the social islands, including the dreamlike islands of Moorea and Bora Bora. Each of these islands has a rugged volcanic heart and is rich in forests, ferns and waterfalls. Coral reefs full of colorful fish protect shallow, incredibly blue lagoons - ideal for bathing, water sports or simply enjoying. Typical for French Polynesia are the Overwater Bungalows. Imagine waking up and being able to jump into the sea directly from your own terrace.

1st day Arrival in Tahiti

Ia Ora! Today you reach Papeete, the international airport of the main island of Tahiti. Our on-site staff will be waiting for you directly at the airport. A private transfer will take you directly to the booked resort. Overnight in Tahiti.

2nd day Island paradise Tahiti

With its majestic mountain peaks, Tahiti, the largest island of French Polynesia, towers like a graceful queen over the sea. The mountainous interior is decorated with gorges, rivers and high waterfalls. The majority of the inhabitants live on the coast, which left the heart of the island almost untouched and primitive. Papeete, which means “water basket”, was once a meeting place for the Tahitians who brought their fresh water there. Your stay at InterContinental Tahiti, an internationally renowned hotel, marks the start of an unparalleled journey through the Polynesian island nation. Overnight in Tahiti.

3rd day by plane to Raiatea

Today, your private driver will pick you up and take you to nearby Papeete Airport, where you will take off with Air Tahiti to Raiatea. Arriving in Raiatea, you will continue by boat to Le Taha’a Island Resort & Spa and its first highlight: Your Taha’a Overwater Suite. Overnight in Raiatea.

4.-5. Day Paradise Stay at Le Taha’a Island Resort

Raiatea is still the cradle of Polynesian culture. This island is the second most important economic center of Polynesia and an important marina, hosting numerous sailing ships and charter companies. It is also the only island in Polynesia that has a navigable river: Faaroa. There are still many traces of her past as a great spiritual center, including the famous Marae Taputapuatea, the most important pilgrimage site in Polynesia, nowadays also very significant for both Maoris and Hawaiians. Other peculiarities of Ra’iateas: on Mount Temehani sprouts a unique flower in the world: the Tiare Apetahi; the island shares the crystal clear waters of its lagoon with its sister and neighbor Taha’a, where your resort is located. Overnight in Raiatea.

Day 6 by plane to Bora Bora

Today, take the boat back to Raiatea Airport and check in to Bora Bora. With another flight over the Pacific island world with its indescribably intense colors, you reach your next destination with Bora Bora. A boat will take you to nearby St. Regis Bora Bora, one of the most famous hotels in the world! Overnight in Bora Bora.

7th - 9th day stay at the Overwater Superior Villa on Bora Bora

Bora Bora has without a doubt one of the or even the most beautiful lagoon in the world. Bora Bora is a jewel flaunted on the blue velvet of the Pacific Ocean, a romantic island where each hotel has created a private paradise to welcome guests for your dream vacation. The resort offers tranquility and intimacy in luxurious bungalows that stand on the perfect turquoise of the lagoon. Watching the sun’s magnificent reddish glimmer as it sinks into the ocean behind Mount Otemanu is an unforgettable spectacle. Overnight in Bora Bora.

10th day return flight to Tahiti

Worth seeing is the surroundings of the Parliament with the former government buildings, of which the oldest from 1876 is considered the second largest timber construction in the world. Opposite is the current government. Architecturally striking is the modern cultivation, known as the “bee basket”. One should not miss a ride in the capital by cable car – the historic tram that goes up to the Botanical Garden. From here there is also a beautiful view of the city and its port.

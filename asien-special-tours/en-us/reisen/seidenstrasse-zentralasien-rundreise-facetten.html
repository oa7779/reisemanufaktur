Turkmenistan, Uzbekistan, Kyrgyzstan, Kazakhstan - The many facets of Central Asia

Central Asia Round Trip from Asia Special Tours. Discover the facets of Turkmenistan, Uzbekistan and Kyrgyzstan on this individual round trip.

This comprehensive tour of Central Asia includes the most beautiful places in four out of five Central Asian countries: Turkmenistan, Uzbekistan, Kyrgyzstan and Kazakhstan. Your travel program is as varied as the countries themselves. You will witness a traditional concert with a fashion show in a medre in Bukhara and you will be able to reach for the stars at the Samarkand observatory. If you want to satisfy your cultural interest, you will witness two great UNESCO World Heritage Sites: the cities of Mary and Bukhara.

1st day Arrival in Ashgabat

Welcome to Turkmenistan! Arriving in Ashgabat, your personal guide is waiting for you and driving you to your hotel. Depending on the arrival time, there is still some time after check-in for first explorations on your own. Overnight in Ashgabat.

Day 2 - The most beautiful sides of Ashgabat

Your tour begins with a city walk through the capital of Turkmenistan: Ashgabat. Turkmenistan's capital is located in an oasis in the Karakum desert. Unlike the name “lovely city” suggests, Ashgabat has evolved from a Russian military base. In the severe earthquake of 1948, the city was almost completely destroyed, rebuilt and since 1991 – when it became the capital – redesigned. Your guide will first take you to the excavation site of Nisa, the first capital of the Parthians, which has been a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2007. If you wish, visit the National Museum of History before lunch. Other highlights will be on the agenda in the afternoon: the Arch of Neutrality, the Carpet Museum, the Presidential Assembly, the Monument of Independence and the Great Mosque in Kiptschak. Overnight in Ashgabat.

Day 3: Day trip to Mary and Merw

After breakfast, your chauffeur will take you to the airport of Ashgabat, as you will take a trip to the city of Mary. Once there, take your guide to the ruins of Merw, an oasis town 20 kilometers away. Here you will visit the Erk Kala settlement, the Gyaur-Kala fortress, the 38-meter-high mausoleum of Sultan Sanjar, the small and large Gyz-Kala fortress and many other UNESCO World Heritage sites. In the afternoon you will fly back to Ashgabat. Overnight in Ashgabat.

Day 4 Into the Oasis City of Khiva (Uzbekistan)

Today they fly over the Karakum Desert to Daschoguz. But you are not yet at the destination of your today’s journey: We continue to Kunja-Urgentsch. Located on the edge of the Karakum desert, it has been a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2005 and for several centuries was one of the most important trading centers in Central Asia and the magnificent capital of the Choresmic Empire. That is why you can admire many important buildings here. For example, the Kutlug-Timur Minaret, which is 67 meters high and thus the highest minaret in all of Central Asia. Also interesting: the Dash-Kala Caravanserei and the Mausoleum of Il-Arslan, the oldest monument in the city. Afterwards, cross the Turkmen-Uzbek border and continue to the oasis city of Khiva. Overnight in Chiwa.

Day 5 - Fairytale Chiwa

In the small oasis town in the middle of the desert, time seems to stand still. Chiwa has preserved the fairytale atmosphere of an ancient, oriental city to this day. Today you can look forward to a sightseeing tour that takes you through the old town of Itchan Kala, which is a UNESCO World Heritage site. You will feel like you are in a dream from 1,001 night. First of all, you will visit the imposing Kunya Ark fortress, the Islam Khodja Minaret, the Palvan Kara complex and the Rafanek Medrese. In the afternoon, continue exploring the city founded in the 6th century AD and visit Juma Mosque, beautiful Tash Hauli Palace – where a cobra is still said to guard the gold treasure of the last Khan – Uch Avlioli Mausoleum, Kutluq Murad Inaq Medrese and many other impressive buildings.

Day 6 - Through the desert to Bukhara

In front of you lies the Kyzylkum Desert, an almost endless sea of sand. Here you must – may! – pass through on your journey along the Silk Road to Bukhara. Drive along the Amudarja River, the life-giving stream of Central Asia. Along the way, your guide will tell you a lot of interesting things about the country and people. So you can look forward to a varied ride. Overnight in Bukhara.

Day 7: Brilliant Bukhara

Bukhara was once one of the most splendid commercial centers on the Silk Road. People from all over the world met in the bazaars, and the city developed into an important cultural and spiritual center. The UNESCO World Heritage City has over 140 well-preserved buildings and monuments, all of which are worth exploring. Dedicate the morning to the Ismael Samani Mausoleum – the final resting place of the former Samanid ruler – the impressive Ark fortress and the Bolo Hauz complex. Together with the water basin (Hauz) and the minaret, a closed assembly was created. Later you will visit other impressive oriental buildings. You will visit, among others, the Poi-Kalon Ensemble, the Taqi Bazaar Dome and the beautiful Miri-Arab and Ulugbek Medrese. The end of this unforgettable day is a dinner in the Nodir-Divonbegi-Medrese, accompanied by a concert performance and a fashion show. Overnight in Bukhara.

Day 8 On the “Royal Road” to Samarkand

They leave Bukhara behind and drive on the “king road” to Samarkand. During the journey, you will pass some interesting places: visit a 39-meter-high minaret in Vabkent, and stop in the city of Gijduvan to see the Ulugbek medrese in the important center for trade and crafts. You will also visit the Rabat i Malik caravanserai in Karmana before finally arriving in Samarkand. Overnight in Samarkand.

Day 9 Into the Uzbek capital: Tashkent

Samarkand is one of the oldest cities in the world and was included in the list of UNESCO World Heritage Sites in 2001. Enjoy a sightseeing tour of the city once conquered by Alexander the Great. The program includes the Gur Emir Mausoleum, the beautiful Bibi Khanum Mosque and the Shah I Zinda Necropolis, which consists of numerous finely decorated mausoleums – also the cousin of the Prophet Muhammad is said to be buried here. At the bazaar you will experience even more oriental flair. You can also look forward to the afternoon. You will visit the Ulugbek Observatory and the famous Registan Square, which is framed by the Shir Dor, Tillya Kari and Ulugbek medres. Then take the “Registan” train to Tashkent. Overnight in Tashkent.

Day 10 - Continue to Bishkek (Kyrgyzstan)

Once you have arrived, you will leave the capital of Uzbekistan again today to fly to Bishkek. After lunch, visit the State Museum of History, Ala Too Park, some craft shops and the Osch Bazaar. Overnight in Bishkek.

11th day Dreamlike landscapes around Bishkek

For a trip, leave the city of Bishkek today and get out into the nature of Kyrgyzstan. Enjoy the scenic idyll and tranquility in the Ala Archa National Park. Located about 40 kilometers outside of Bishkek, it is a popular excursion spot for weekend picnics and hikers. There are more than 20 small and large glaciers, about 50 mountain peaks within the park and two smaller rivers springing from the glaciers. Surely there is a goal for you! Afterwards, visit the legendary Burana Tower before returning to the capital. Overnight in Bishkek.

Day 12: Final destination: Almaty (Kazakhstan)

Today, your journey will take you north to explore another Central Asian country. The journey passes through the Korday Pass before crossing the Kyrgyz-Kazakh border after lunch. In Almaty, the former capital of Kazakhstan, which today is an important center for culture, science and business. Overnight in Almaty.

13th day Almaty

A very special program awaits you today: your chauffeur will take you to the best skiing center in Central Asia. With the cable car you travel to an altitude of 3,200 meters. You will also visit the Medeo skating stadium, located in the picturesque Lesser Almatinka gorge. Finally, you will return to Almaty to visit Panfilov Park, Zenkov Cathedral, State Museum of History, Central Mosque which accommodates 7,000 worshippers and Kazakh Museum of Musical Instruments. Overnight in Almaty.

Day 14 Farewell to Central Asia

Your eventful journey is now coming to an end. After breakfast, your chauffeur will take you to Almaty airport and you will fly back to your home country with many great memories and photos in your luggage.

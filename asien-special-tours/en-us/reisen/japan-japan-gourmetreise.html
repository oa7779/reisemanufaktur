Gourmet Tour Japan - Taste of Japan

Japan Travel - Enjoy the culinary highlights of Japan on this gourmet trip from Asia Special Tours, your Japan specialist in the heart of Munich.

Itinerary & services

English itinerary text is an offline machine translation of the German original for this team preview. Please confirm travel details with your advisor; the German source remains available through the language switch.

Japan is a true paradise for foodies and connoisseurs. Over the centuries, Japanese cuisine has become one of the most refined in the world. She consistently relies on the three pillars of high-quality dishes, artful preparation and excellent taste. Regional and seasonal products are more popular than in most industrialized countries and you taste it: everything is wonderfully fresh and full of taste. One reason for the long life expectancy of the Japanese and a delicious one at that. On this trip you will taste the diversity of the country: you will taste the freshest sushi, learn how to make Japanese soba noodles, experience the flair of a traditional izakaya, roam the markets, let the special specialties of the Japanese Alps melt on your tongue, taste the national drink sake, visit a farm and get to know the extravagant world of Japanese street food. Of course, in addition to so much cuisine, the culture of the country is not neglected.

1st day Arrival in Japan

Your journey begins in Tokyo, where you will be welcomed and warmly greeted by an English-speaking assistant. This accompanies you to the vehicle available for you, which brings you to your hotel. The rest of the day is at your disposal: time to make first explorations on your own in this breathtaking world metropolis. Overnight in Tokyo.

2nd day Stroll through the Tsukiji outdoor market & sushi breakfast

Today you can look forward to a day full of diverse impressions of the exciting metropolis. Your guide will wait for you in the foyer of your hotel and will be the first to take you to Tsukiji Market. Even though the world-famous fish market has been relocated to the island of Toyosu, the Tsukiji outdoor market with traditional shops and world-class sushi restaurants remains a lively hub for visitors and the local population to enjoy delicious food and buy local specialties. Stop at one of the restaurants for a super-fresh sushi breakfast. Empowered, head to Hamarikyu Park, a veritable oasis in the midst of a sea of skyscrapers. In Asakusa, visit the Sensoji Temple, Tokyo’s oldest Buddhist temple. It is also home to the oldest geisha district in the city. A short walk will take you to the nearby Kappabashi district, where all kinds of everyday items are produced and marketed. Finally, your guide will take you to Ameyoko, a street market with hundreds of merchants and market cries. Here you should try a Japanese crepe. Overnight in Tokyo. 

Day 3: Experiencing Tokyo with all your senses

Start the day with a culinary experience where you can take care of yourself: You will attend a private soba cooking class. Her teacher is either Akila Inouye, founder and head chef of the Tsukiji Soba Academy, or one of his graduates. After a demonstration by the teacher, you will prepare your own soba pasta dough, which you will then cut into noodles 1.6 millimetres thick. Finally, the noodles are cooked and eaten in the tachigui style (literal translation: standing and eating) – in the Japanese way. In the evening, you will be introduced more deeply into the culinary diversity of Japan. Tokyo offers a huge offer. Whether street food, small Japanese pubs or luxurious star restaurants: the gastro scene really leaves nothing to be desired. Also, not only Japanese specialties are offered, but also Western and exotic food. Together with an English-speaking food expert, you’ll take a look behind the scenes of hidden izakayas – small Japanese pubs, restaurants and bars. Of course, you will not return to your hotel hungry. Overnight in Tokyo.

Day 4 The Japanese Alps

Without guidance

The train continues to Takayama, which is located in the Japanese Alps. First, take the world-famous Shinkansen high-speed train, which can travel up to 260 kilometers per hour. Then change to the Hida Wide View Express. This ride is one of the most scenic in Japan. Finally, you will reach Takayama. Walking through the streets of this idyllic place feels like a journey into the past. Behind the traditional facades you will find small shops, souvenir shops, restaurants and bars. Line up at one of the popular snack stalls and try the famous Hida beef sushi. Your overnight stay is something very special today: you are a guest in a ryokan. The typical Japanese inns were already built in the Edo period (1603 – 1868). Staying here is especially worthwhile to experience Japanese hospitality. Among the highlights of a stay at Ryokan are meals. Tonight you will enjoy a multi-course Kaiseki dinner. Let the day end with a relaxing bath in the private onsen. Overnight in Takayama.

Day 5 Culinary Walking Tour of Takayama

After a typical Japanese breakfast, your guide will pick you up at your ryokan and take you for a walk through the charming old town. This environment is characterised by traditional houses, shops and restaurants that have been able to preserve their original flair. The tradition of Takayama is best experienced on a culinary tour. Your guide will take you to the city’s most popular places, where you will have numerous opportunities to sample some of the regional specialties such as miso, rice dumplings in soy sauce, Japanese sweets and locally produced sake. Until noon you will learn a lot about Takayama’s cuisine and history. Afterwards, take a trip on your own up the Japanese Alps to the small village of Shirakawago. Nestled in the charming scenic backdrop of dense forests, houses in the style of Gasshozukuri are grouped here, whose up to 60 degree steep, reet-covered roofs can also withstand the heavy snow masses in winter. Stroll through the streets of the picturesque village and take yourself back to the village’s peasant past. A particularly beautiful view can be enjoyed from the Shiroyama observation platform. In the afternoon we will return to Takayama. Overnight in Takayama. 

Day 6: The Old Imperial City of Kyoto

Without guidance

Your next destination is Kyoto, Japan’s cultural capital, which you can reach by train. Unlike many other cities, Kyoto has been largely spared war damage. This allowed the city to preserve its cultural heritage and is considered an epitome of Japanese culture. After your arrival, the rest of the day will be free. Explore the city on your own or opt for the optional half-day trip. Overnight in Kyoto.
Optional: Introduction to Japanese Culture
Immerse yourself (together with other guests) in the traditional world of Japan on a half-day cultural experience tour in Kyoto. Start with a visit to a Machiya, a traditional wooden house. Then you have the option to put on a kimono. Another highlight awaits you at the meeting of a local tea master. This experience is rounded off by an introduction to the art of origami.

 

Day 7 Kyoto from its most beautiful side

Today you will visit one of Kyoto’s most famous landmarks: the Fushimi Inari Shinto Shrine. A magnificently decorated building houses the goddess of rice, while thousands of orange torii gates lead up the mountain. Afterwards, take the train to Sanjusangendo, an impressive Buddhist temple. Inside, a fascinating collection of 1001 life-size golden Buddha statues awaits you. After the visit, take a taxi to Teramachi, where you will have lunch at a local restaurant. In the afternoon, visit Nijo Castle. Known for its ornately painted walls and the “tonguishing nightingale floors” that warn intruders, you will experience life at the court of the samurai ruler. Take the metro to the eastern foothills of Kyoto to Nanzenji Zen Temple, a quiet retreat where monks have lived in peaceful seclusion since 1291. Admire the famous Zen stone garden. After an eventful day, say goodbye to your guide and have the opportunity to continue exploring Kyoto on your own. Overnight in Kyoto.

8th day Market visit and traditional cooking course

The morning is at your disposal before you delve into the world of Japanese cuisine in the afternoon. Meet your guide and stroll through the Nishiki market, also known as "Kyoto's Cuisine". Let yourself be inspired by the stands with fresh ingredients and spices. Your guide will explain how these traditional products are used in Japanese cuisine and help you select the freshest ingredients for your subsequent cooking class. After the market visit, you will continue to a Machiya, a traditional wooden house, where you can learn all about the art of sake making during a sake tasting and try different varieties. Then enter the kitchen and let a local chef introduce you to the preparation of classic dishes. Learn the art of rolling sushi, preparing a delicious miso soup and a refreshing fruit salad as you learn about the eating habits of the Japanese. After the delicious, self-prepared dinner, say goodbye to your chef and enjoy the evening atmosphere of Kyoto or let your guide escort you back to the hotel. Overnight in Kyoto.

9th day Culinary excursion to the beautiful Lake Biwa

Start this day with another special culinary experience on the beautiful Lake Biwa, where you will meet your guide. Together you stroll through the place and taste the wonderfully fresh spring water, which is said to be responsible for the fantastic taste of the rice grown here. Afterwards, visit a local farming family who will give you an insight into traditional Japanese country life and introduce you to the cultivation and harvesting techniques of the region. These consist mainly of the rice grown on the farm. The rice is processed with a large wooden hammer until it reaches the perfect tough consistency – great fun for young and old. Surely you have now got a decent appetite and deserved a hearty lunch. The farmer’s wife serves you a homemade chicken sukiyaki, a simple but extremely tasty Japanese specialty. We wish you a good appetite. Then return to Kyoto. Overnight in Kyoto.  

Day 10 Osaka - a Mecca for gourmets and foodies

Without guidance

Your journey will now take you to Osaka, Japan’s culinary capital. The highlights of the city are certainly its extravagant and fun-loving inhabitants as well as the fantastic food. The city’s motto is “kuidaore”, which means “food until you drop” – and you’ll quickly realize that it’s not that hard. Locals are particularly proud of specialties such as Okonomiyaki (Japanese white cabbage pancake) and Takoyaki (octopus balls). After arriving, there is plenty of time for a first exploration of the city, where you can already taste some of the local delicacies.

11th Day Street Food Tour of Osaka

You can make the day according to your ideas. In the evening, your Schlemmertour through Osaka awaits you. Your guide will take you on a “food tour”. There is no city where you could get to know the extravagant and colorful world of Japanese food better than in Osaka. The city’s motto is “kuidaore” or “food until you drop.” The first stop is a traditional Tachinomi, a sociable standing bar serving delicious specialties such as delicately stewed miso beef, freshly pickled fish and steamed chicken with spicy ponzu sauce. Continue to an Izakaya where golden brown frit-fried Kushikatsu skewers – juicy meat, crisp vegetables and delicate shrimp – are waiting for you. For this, a glass of sake fits perfectly from the diverse selection. The crowning conclusion is a charming home cooking restaurant, which spoils with hearty yakitori skewers and fragrant odes, a hearty stew of vegetables, tofu and fish. Strengthened, you stroll a little further through the district with its countless small bars and immerse yourself in the lively nightlife of Osaka. Overnight in Osaka. 

12th day Farewell to Japan

The time until the planned airport transfer is at your disposal. Breakfast in peace, take a last walk through the area or buy some more souvenirs for your loved ones at home. Then your time out from everyday life is already coming to an end. With valuable memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories, you say goodbye to Japan. Share your wonderful experiences and experiences with your friends and family at home. Your driver will take you in time for your return flight to the airport. This ends your beautiful trip to Japan with Asia Special Tours. We wish you a good journey home! Goodbye!

Vietnam Round Trip - Adventure and Sea

Vietnam Nature Tour: Experience the natural beauty of Vietnam on your individual Vietnam tour with Asia Special Tours. Ask now!

Itinerary & services

Start the great Vietnam adventure and look forward to countless cultural highlights and natural beauties. Witness the early Vietnamese history, experience impressive cultural monuments and explore enchanting karst and jungle landscapes. On foot, by bicycle or by boat you will discover the most beautiful sides of Vietnam up close and unadulterated. Immerse yourself in the spectacular cave world at Phon Nha-Ke Bang National Park and sail with a junk through the breathtaking Halong Bay. After so many different impressions, you can relax during your bathing in Hoi An and enjoy the here and now.

1st day Arrival in Vietnam & City Tour through charming Hanoi

Welcome to Vietnam. At the airport of Hanoi you will already be expected and warmly welcomed by your guide. During the subsequent city tour, you will see the well-known sights of Hanois. Vietnam’s capital is a city full of lakes, shaded boulevards and public parks. Drive to Ba Dinh Square to visit the Presidential Palace. Of course, a visit to Hoan Kiem Lake and the Jadeberg Temple (Ngoc Son) with the striking dragon heads should not be missed. Surrounded by beautiful trees, old colonial buildings and parts of the old town, the “Sword returned lake” (Hoan Kiem Lake) is a popular place to stay for Hanoians of all ages. According to legend, a turtle lives here, which once lent a king a magic sword with which he could defeat the enemies of Vietnam. You might see this episode later in a traditional performance. Because in the evening you will visit a performance of the famous water puppet theater. Here you can follow everyday scenes from Vietnamese life – the (mis) fate of rice farmers and fishermen, for example – or just from the history of the country. Overnight in Hanoi.

2nd day on the way in the World Heritage Halong Bay

After breakfast, leave Hanoi by land towards Halong Bay. Enjoy the sight of the agricultural areas of the Red River delta and let rice terraces, water buffalo and the day-to-day Vietnamese village life pass by. In Halong city you exchange your means of transport and board a beautiful junk. Nearly 2,000 islands lie in the bay, from whose emerald green waters massive rock formations of limestone rise. This spectacular backdrop has made it to Hollywood as a setting: In the 1974 James Bond film “The Man with the Golden Colt”, Bond went on a criminal hunt here. However, you will experience the fascinating nature in this picturesque environment quite relaxed and will make various landings during small stops. While enjoying your dinner, you will experience an enchanting sunset over the bay. Overnight on the junk.

Day 3 Atmospheric Halong Bay & Onward Flight to Vinh

Feel the magical atmosphere on board your ship. Early in the morning you will experience the absolute silence and clear air of Halong Bay. With tai chi exercises on the sun deck, you awaken the spirits of life and can start the day completely relaxed. Enjoy an extensive breakfast as bizarre rock formations pass you by. Around noon you will leave the junk and then return to Hanoi airport. In the evening, fly on to Vinh to spend tomorrow. Overnight in Vinh.

4th day Discovering magnificence in Vinh & sniffing history

Vinh once exerted great political and military influence – the Tay Son dynasty even considered making it the capital. The city also developed into an important industrial center during the French occupation. Unfortunately, many buildings were destroyed – including during the Vietnam War, as Vinh was strategically important as the starting point of the Ho Chi Minh Trail. In Vinh, visit the village of Lang Sen as well as the Su-Nu Pagoda and other attractions. If time is enough, take a detour to the quiet beach of Cua Lo. Overnight in Vinh.

Day 5 Away from the tourist trails in Dong Hoi

Leave Vinh in the morning and continue on to Dong Hoi. Along the way, you will pass through numerous villages, observe rural life and stop at one time or another to take a souvenir photo. On the beach Da Nhay you will enjoy a fantastic view of the picturesque landscape of this region, which has been traveled by only a few tourists until today. Overnight in Dong Hoi.

6th Day Untouched Nature in Phong Nha-Ke-Bang National Park & Visit to Mysterious Caves

Today you will visit one of the most beautiful national parks in Vietnam, Phong Nha-Ke Bang National Park. It covers an area of about 86,000 hectares and lies directly on the border with Laos. In 2003, it was included in the list of World Heritage Sites of Vietnam by UNESCO. You will visit the famous Phong Nha Cave as well as the even larger Thien Duong Cave and explore the fascinating landscape of the park by boat. In the caves you will find yourself face to face with some superlatives: the oldest and largest karst formation (about 400 million). Years) with the largest cave system (65 – 70 kilometers) and the longest underground river in the world (about 7.7 kilometers). Overnight in Dong Hoi.

7th day Pure history in the demilitarized zone & impressive tunnel system Vinh Moc

On your way to the Imperial City of Hue, visit the demilitarized zone that separated North and South Vietnam after the 1954 Geneva Conference. Here you can visit the famous tunnel system Vinh Moc and get a memorable impression of the Vietnam War. In the tunnels, the residents found refuge and a hiding place in the war. Bunkers and military cemeteries are reminders of the former battles. Afterwards, visit the Quang Tri Citadel and take a walk through the village of Phuoc Tich to get to know more about life in rural Vietnam. Then you reach Hue and are taken to your hotel. Overnight in Hue.

8th day Picturesque bicycle tour to the village of Phu Mong & boat ride through the Imperial Hue

After breakfast, explore the city and its surroundings. From Hue’s center, embark on a leisurely bicycle tour to the village of Phu Mong. On your way through the magnificent Hues countryside, rice fields, fruit and vegetable gardens pass you by. You will stop in one of the typical garden houses for which Hue is famous. The facilities are subject to precise geometric specifications in order to give visitors a feeling of inner peace. Your bike ride will end at the Thien-Mu Pagoda, a Buddhist monastery whose complex houses Vietnam’s tallest pagoda. By boat, your visit will take you to the magnificent Mausoleum of Emperor Minh Mang, a vast architectural complex consisting of 40 buildings and including palaces, temples and pavilions. Afterwards you will visit the imposing Imperial Citadel, where a total of 13 emperors of the Nguyen Dynasty resided and ruled from 1802 to 1945. Modeled on the forbidden city in Beijing, this place bears witness to Hue’s glamorous past as the capital of the Nguyen dynasty (1802-1945) over an area of 500 hectares. At the end of the day you will have the opportunity to visit the Dong-Ba market, where numerous exotic fruits and vegetables as well as spices are offered. Overnight in Hue.

Day 9 Over the Cloud Pass to Hoi An

The overland journey from Hue to Hoi An is one of the most scenic routes in Vietnam. The so-called Cloud Pass with a height of 496 meters and a length of 20 kilometers offers one of the most breathtaking views in the whole country: on the right, the country falls off to the sea, on the left, the mountain range up to 1,000 meters high rises into the sky, which forms the weather divide in central Vietnam. On the way, stop at the Linh Ung Pagoda on the Son Tra peninsula before continuing to Hoi An. The city was an important commercial port in the 17th and 18th centuries. Its architecture and relaxed lifestyle have changed very little over the years, so that a charming little town awaits you here. Arriving in Hoi An, take a walk through the old town and feel the charm of the town up close. Overnight in Hoi An.

10th – 13th day bathing stay in Hoi An

Without tour guide and chauffeur

The following days are completely at your disposal. Relax at the beach or pool of your hotel and recharge your energy. Enjoy moments of freedom and feel how time seems to stop. Hoi An also invites further exploration, as it is considered a city of tailors and lamps. You should definitely try the local cuisine! Overnight in Hoi An.

14th day Departure from Vietnam

Your time out from everyday life is coming to an end. Even if your holiday is soon over, the precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories of your trip will accompany you forever. The day is at your leisure before you are taken to Danang Airport for your return flight.

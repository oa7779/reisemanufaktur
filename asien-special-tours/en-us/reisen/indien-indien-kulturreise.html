Between tea plantations and mountain monasteries

India Cultural Tour - Discover India between tea plantations and mountain monasteries in the beautiful Sikkim region. Get advice from Asia Special Tours.

The fascinating East of India awaits you with all its facets. In Darjeeling, enjoy the soothing tranquility of the Himalayan Mountains with its Buddhist monasteries that exude a special spirituality. Around Darjeeling, a lot still appears “very British”: the endless, well-kept tea plantations, the museum steam train, the colonial houses covered with green corrugated plate and also the fog that often runs through the valleys. However, the sensational view of the third highest mountain on earth, Kanchenjunga, is unique. As you travel, you will advance into the former kingdom of Sikkim and ever more remote areas of the Himalayas. You will see the ruins of old palaces, snow-capped peaks, sacred lakes and venerable Buddhist monasteries. During this trip, you will get to know India from a side that is hidden from most travelers.

1st day Arrival in India

Namaste and welcome to India! Your journey starts in Delhi, where you will be expected upon arrival at the airport and driven to your accommodation near the airport. Rest first or relax in your hotel. If you would like, we are also happy to organize a tour of the city for you. Overnight in Delhi.

Day 2 – Continue to Darjeeling – the “Queen of the Hills”

Early in the morning you will be picked up from your hotel and driven to the airport for your flight to Bagdogra. From here you will continue by car to Darjeeling, which is located at over 2,000 meters altitude on a steep mountain slope. The city is often referred to as the "Queen of the Hills". Green hills, covered by plantations, characterize the landscape in the foothills of the Himalayan Mountains. Numerous tea gardens produce tea here with very different character and aroma. Darjeeling, the capital of the region of the same name, enjoys worldwide fame due to its high-quality tea. Your accommodation for tonight is the luxurious Heritage Hotel The Elgin in the heart of the city. The building already looks back on more than 125 years of history and once served the Maharaja of Cooch Behar as a summer residence. The 25 luxury rooms overlooking the Himalayan mountains also leave nothing to be desired in terms of luxury, comfort and special charm. Overnight in Darjeeling.

Day 3rd Sunrise on Tiger Hill & Narrow Gauge Train Ride

Before sunrise, set off to experience the break of the day at Darjeeling’s main attraction, Tiger Hill. Slowly the sun dips the rugged rock formations of the mountains into reddish light. In clear distance view, you can even see the third highest mountain on earth, Mount Kanchenjunga, as well as Mount Everest. After breakfast, you will experience pure nostalgia on a Darjeeling Himalayan Railway: to this day, the passenger train with the brocade-related seats is pulled by a steam locomotive through the beautiful mountain panorama of Darjeeling. In Ghoom you will visit the Buddhist monastery of the same name, whose colors can already be seen from afar. It is located at an altitude of 2,500 meters and houses murals, ancient texts, as well as a huge gilded statue of Maitreya Buddha. By car you will return to Darjeeling, where with your guide you will visit the Nadia-Himalayan Animal Garden, which is committed to the conservation of endangered animal species of the high mountains. Then visit the Himalayan Mountaineering Institute, which was established in Darjeeling. You will also visit the Tibetan Craft Centre. Overnight in Darjeeling.

Day 4 - Continue to Yuksom, Sikkim's first capital

Your journey will take you to the sleepy village of Yuksom in the Indian state of Sikkim, which was once an independent kingdom. Yuksom, once the first capital of Sikkim, has a special place in history. Here, in 1642, three lamas came together from different directions across the Himalayas to crown the first religious head of Sikkim, Chogyal Phuntsog Namgyal. The stone crown can still be seen in Norbugang Park today. Phuntsog Namgyal ruled over a vast territory that was many times the size of Sikkim today. After checking in at the hotel, you will visit the crown throne together with your guide. Under a wooden cover in front of the throne, you will also see a huge footprint, which is said to have come from one of the lamas. Afterwards you will drive to the nearby Kathok lake, which used to have a ritual meaning as well: from the lake was once taken the water for the anointing during the coronation ceremony. Overnight in Yuksom.

5th Day Ancient Monasteries, Sacred Lakes and Stunning Waterfalls

Today begins with a walk to Dubdi Monastery, which sits on the top of a hill about an hour from Yuksom. It is one of Sikkim’s oldest monasteries and was built by the followers of the Nyingmapa sect of Tibetan Buddhism. The way there winds through lush forests and offers picturesque views of the mountain panorama. After breakfast at the hotel, you will make your way to Pelling. Along the way, stop at Lake Khecheolpalri, which nestles in the hilly landscape and is fluttered by countless prayer flags. The lake is considered by Hindus and Buddhists alike as one of Sikkim’s most sacred lakes. Also on the Rambi and Kanchenjunga waterfalls leads your way past. Finally, you reach the small town of Pelling. At an altitude of 2,150 meters, Pelling lies in the middle of beautiful nature against the background of the Kanchenjungas. Upon arrival, you will take an approximately one-hour hike to Sanga Choeling, one of the oldest monasteries in Sikkim. Overnight in Pelling.

6th day Gigantic statues outside the gates of the small town of Namchi

In the morning, visit Phemayangtse Monastery, home of the Nyingmapa Order. Although it has been renewed several times, it is still the second oldest and most important monastery in Sikkim. After a short drive, you will reach the ruins of Rabdentse, the remains of the former capital of the Sikkim Kingdom. From here the rulers of 1670 up to 1814 Your Mountain Kingdom, which is only 1975 It was incorporated into India. Then continue your journey and head towards Namchi (or Namtse). On the way you will visit the huge, over 40 Meter-high statue of Guru Padmasambhava – the patron saint of Sikkim. The impressive statue with its shimmering copper and bronze paint dominates the wooded mountain ridge of Samdruptse hill about eight kilometers from Namchi and is supposedly even visible from Pelling. Almost greater is the great. 33 Metre high Shiva statue perched on the opposite Solophok hill. The statue stands in the middle of a vast complex of guest houses, temples and pagodas, which includes replications of the Choir Dam – India’s four sacred Hindu pilgrimage sites. After the visit, you will be taken to your hotel in Namchi. Overnight in Namchi.

Day 7 - The Buddha Park of Ravangla

A new day, a new goal. Your journey will continue towards Gangtok, capital of the former Kingdom and current state of Sikkim. Along the way, visit the Temi Tea Garden, known for the high quality of its tea, as well as the Ravangla Buddha Park, part of the Rabong Gompa Monastery complex, a centuries-old pilgrimage site. At the heart of the Buddhapark is a nearly 40-meter-high Buddha statue, which was created on the occasion of 2,550. It was consecrated in 2013 by the 14th Dalai Lama. Finally, you reach Gangtok, which is located on a mountain ridge at 1,640 meters altitude. Overnight in Gangtok.

8th day Buddhist worlds, gardens and traditional craftsmanship

Today is dedicated to exploring gangtoks. Their first destination is the Buddhist monastery Rumtek, which in its present form was created in the 1960s at the initiative of the 9th Karmapa as a copy of a monastery near Lhasa. The monastery is the seat of the head of the Kagyupa sect of Tibetan Buddhism. Behind the main monastery is the elaborately furnished Karmae Nalanda Institute of Buddhist Studies, which attracts students from all over the world. Your guide will be happy to help you with the translation if you want to know more about life in the monastery. The beautiful Saramsa garden is full of colors. Originally intended for fruit cultivation, it was later known for the cultivation of the medicinal plant Ipecacuanha. Today it houses an orchidarium and is popular as a leisure garden. At the Namgyal Institute of Tibetology you can delve deeper into the thought world of Buddhism. After visiting the monasteries of Enchey and Dro Dup Chen, you will get an insight into the craftsmanship of the region. You will visit the Institute of Traditional Arts and Crafts, where, among other things, handmade paper, wood carvings and bags are sold. Overnight in Gangtok.

9th day excursion to Tsomgo Lake

After breakfast, you will take a trip to Lake Tsomgo, or Lake Changu, which with its deep blue water is a real feast for the eyes. Already the drive through the picturesque mountain world is an experience and leads you past several waterfalls. The natural glacier lake is fed by the melted snow of the surrounding mountains and offers a spectacular sight at any time of year. Lake Tsomgo is particularly charming in winter, when the ice surface glistens in the sun and around the snow-capped peaks of the mountains shine. The pink rhododendrons, blue and yellow poppy flowers, different types of primroses and purple sword lilies then make for a real color rush. Numerous myths and legends revolve around the lake, which is venerated by Hindus and Buddhists as a “holy lake”. If the water of the lake had a dark hue, the future would be dark and gloomy, full of unrest. A bright shade, on the other hand, promised happiness and peace. Even today, faith healers pilgrimage to the lake to pray there. But people are not the only visitors. It is also a popular stopover for migratory birds and home to numerous bird species such as the Rostgans and the blood pheasant. Overnight in Gangtok.  

Day 10: Travel to Kalimpong

Today, your journey will take you to Kalimpong, a quiet place at 1,250 meters altitude in eastern Himalaya. Already the untouched landscape on the way there will inspire you. Kalimpong is famous for its orchid and tree nurseries: oaks, maples and chestnuts stretch as far as the eye can see. Finally, you reach Kalimpong. The small town was once a flourishing trading center between Tibet and India. Through missionaries she underwent a clear Scottish influence for a long time. In addition, Tibetans and Nepalese settled here over time, and in the afternoon you will have time to explore the small town on your own. Overnight in Kalimpong.

11th Day Monasteries, Markets and Orchids in Kalimpong

During your visit to the place, you will always come across the numerous cultural influences that unite in Kalimpong. In the morning, visiting Zang Dhok Palri Phodang Monastery on the hill Durpin offers fantastic panoramic views of the city and its surroundings. The prayer room inside the monastery is decorated with beautiful murals. Founded at the beginning of the 20th century, the Garden Tharpa Choling Monastery on Tirpai Hill, which you will visit afterwards, is one of Kalimpong’s oldest monasteries. It has a collection of rare Buddhist manuscripts and artifacts. Significantly older is the Bhutanese Thongsa Gompa Monastery, which was built in 1692 and is therefore the oldest monastery in Kalimpong. The meditation halls of the monastery have been completely renovated and are decorated with beautiful murals, including the clearly erotic representation of the union of the divine pair of opposites. After these further insights into life in local Buddhist monasteries, your guide will take you to the Kalimpong market. Let yourself be taken into a world of colors and scents! Finally, walk through the magnificent orchid and tree nurseries where you can end the day in a relaxed way. Overnight in Kalimpong.

Day 12 - Back to Delhi via Bagdogra

After exploring the impressive mountain world of Darjeeling and Sikkim, your driver will take you to Bagdogra Airport from where you will return to Delhi by domestic flight. Here you will be driven to your airport hotel and can arrange the rest of the evening as you wish. Overnight in Delhi.

13th day Farewell to India

Just under two weeks you have explored the fascinating Indian states of Darjeeling and Sikkim in all their facets: from the enchanting landscapes of Darjeeling with tea plantations and lush green hills to the quiet monasteries of the Himalayan region. Now your break from everyday life is coming to an end, and your driver will take you back to the airport today for your flight.

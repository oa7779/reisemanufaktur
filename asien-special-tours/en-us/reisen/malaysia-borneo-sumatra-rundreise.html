Borneo & Sumatra - In the Land of Orangutans

Borneo Sumatra Tour: In the land of the orangutans you will experience the most beautiful sides of Malaysia and Indonesia with Asia Special Tours. Ask now!

Itinerary & services

English itinerary text is an offline machine translation of the German original for this team preview. Please confirm travel details with your advisor; the German source remains available through the language switch.

The islands of Borneo and Sumatra are considered natural paradise par excellence thanks to their species-rich rainforests. Here you have the unique opportunity to get close to our closest animal relatives, the orangutans, in their natural environment – a unique and fascinating experience! On your varied round trip over the two islands, on Sumatra you will find untouched primordial forests, rubber plantations, traditional villages, the culture of the Batak, colorful markets and the beautiful Toba Lake, which you will explore by bicycle and boat. Borneo will fascinate you with its species-rich rainforests, mysterious mangroves, breathtaking views and a variety of exotic animals. In the midst of the flora and fauna of the jungle, you will feel like you are in another world.

Day 1 Welcome to Sumatra

Selamat datang and welcome to Sumatra! At Medan Airport, your guide and chauffeur are already waiting for you. With a population of three million, the provincial capital is one of the international gateways to Indonesia and the fourth largest city in the country. Together with your guide, you will take a small city tour of Medan. The city is an interesting melting pot of Chinese, Indian, Malay and local Batak culture. You will see the Maimoon Palace, the Great Mosque, a Chinese temple and the Old Town. Then continue directly to Bohorok. On the way you have the opportunity to visit the rubber plantations. The journey time is about four and a half hours. Gunung Leuser National Park is one of the largest national parks in Indonesia with about 800,000 hectares of untouched jungle. The park is a UNESCO World Heritage site and offers a retreat for many endangered animal species – including the orangutans. Overnight in Bukit Lawang, Sumatra.

Day 2 Visiting the Orangutans

Today, the first big highlight of your journey on the trail of orangutans awaits you: you have the unique opportunity to observe the rare animals in one of their last natural habitats. An approximately three-hour walk will take you through the species-rich flora and fauna of the tropical rainforest. With a bit of luck, you’ll see wild orangutans, gibbons, macaques, flying squirrels, peacocks and rhino birds along the way. After lunch, you will be picked up with a traditional “bekak” for a visit to the Timbang Lawang Eco Farm. On a subsequent tour of the area, you will learn more about how brown sugar is produced and the coveted rubber is obtained. With many new impressions, you will finally return to your accommodation. Overnight in Bukit Lawang, Sumatra.

Day 3 - Discover Bukit Lawang

You can spend today as you please. Take it easy and enjoy your lodge or explore the Bukit Lawang area on a walk. Overnight in Bukit Lawang, Sumatra.

Day 4 Highlands of Berastagi

Today it goes high for you. A 6-hour drive will take you to the Berastagi Highlands today. The village is located at about 1,300 meters above sea level and is surrounded by the two volcanoes Sinabung and Sibayak. Stop along the way at Mary Annai Velangkanni Church, an example of Indo-Moghul architecture. Then take advantage of the afternoon in Bergastani to stroll through the famous fruit market and taste exotic fruits. Overnight in Berastagi, Sumatra.

5th Day Gigantic Lake Toba

Another natural spectacle awaits you: Lake Toba. This is the largest and deepest crater lake in the world – about twice as big as Lake Constance. The approximately eight-hour drive leads past plantations and the waterfall Sipiso-Piso. Along the way, you will also see a traditional village of the Karo-Batak and the Simalungun Royal Palace, which stands on 20 wooden pillars. In the afternoon, take the ferry from Parapat to the island of Samosir, which is located in the middle of Lake Toba. Overnight in Samosir, Sumatra.

Day 6 Exploring Samosir

Today you will explore Samosir together with your guide. Enjoy the traditional Batak houses, Tomok’s tombs and Ambarita’s megalithic stone chairs. You can get a further insight into the Batak culture at a dance performance in Simanindo. Then you have a choice. How about a walk or an exploration of the area by bicycle? Of course, you can also end the day very relaxed in the hotel. Overnight in Samosir, Sumatra.

Day 7 Back to Medan

Today is a travel day ahead of you. In the morning you will drive along the south coast of Lake Toba past rice fields, huge tombs of the Batak kings and through the highlands back to Medan. On the way, there are always opportunities for stops. After arriving in Medan, the rest of the day will be at your disposal. Overnight in Medan.

Day 8: Multifaceted Kuching

Now it is already time to say goodbye to the unique island of Sumatra. But your journey is far from over! After breakfast, you will be taken to the airport and fly via Kuala Lumpur to Kuching, the capital of the Malaysian state of Sarawak on the island of Borneo. In addition, the city is the gateway to the natural wonders in the dense rainforests of Borneo. Upon arrival at the airport, you will be warmly welcomed and, together with your guide, set off for a small city tour. You will see the old Gambir Spice Street with its markets, the Malay quarter with the mosque, the oldest Chinese temple of Sarawak and the “Kuching Waterfront” overlooking buildings that testify to Sarawak’s history. A short boat ride on a traditional sampan over the Sarawak River will take you to Fort Margherita, which testifies to the former greatness of the White Raja. After visiting the fort, you will be taken to your hotel. Overnight in Kuching.

Day 9 Visiting the "Wild Man of Borneo"

Today awaits you another encounter with one of our nearest animal relatives. You will drive to the Semenggok Orangutan Rehabilitation Center, where you can see the “Wild Man of Borneo” up close in his natural surroundings. The aim of the rehabilitation center is to re-accustom orangutans released from captivity or found injured in the rainforest to a life in the wild. During feeding time, you can see the orangutans up close. Later, continue your journey and make your way to the Borneo Highlands Resort, which is located at an altitude of 1,000 meters above sea level and surrounded by pristine rainforest. With its surrounding forest areas and the 177 bird species registered there so far (including subspecies), it is considered a paradise for bird lovers.After lunch (strict vegetarian) you will take an approximately one-hour hike through the rainforest with your guide, where you can observe the colorful bird life. When the darkness has broken over the rainforest, embark on a night walk where, with a bit of luck, you will encounter some nocturnal animals such as flying lemurs, rod insects and sleeping birds. Overnight in Padawan.

10th day Night boat trip through the rainforest

Good morning, Borneo! The new day welcomes you with the sounds of the jungle – perfect to start full of energy in an eventful program. Early in the morning, meet your guide for a shared hike to the viewpoint with magnificent views of the Indonesian side of Borneo (Calimantan). This place is ideal for observing the diverse bird life of the forest. After breakfast at the resort, the morning is freely available. In the early afternoon, make your way to Permai Rainforest Resort, located at the foot of Mount Santubong. A next highlight awaits you in the evening: a nightly boat trip through the mangrove forest, which makes an even more mysterious impression at night. Enjoy the beautiful sight as the sun sinks over the mangrove forest. In the glow of flashlights, you may discover the reflection of crocodile eyes that light up like little flashes in the water. In addition, countless fireflies gather in the mangrove trees, which turn them into glittering Christmas trees. After this unforgettable boat ride deep into the heart of the pristine Sarawak, return to your resort. Overnight in the Permai rainforest.

11th day boat trip to the Irrawaddy dolphins

After a restorative breakfast, start the day with another introduction to Sarawak’s unique wildlife. You will take a boat trip to the Irrawaddy dolphins, who present themselves in all their beauty on the hunt. Look for flying fish, because they usually announce the arrival of the dolphins! After this once-in-a-lifetime experience, continue your journey to the islands of Satang, which, alongside the Talang-Talang Islands, are the only egg-laying sites for Sarawaks turtles. Of course, you can also relax on the islands. Look back at the experiences of the day, soak in the sun and swim or snorkel in the clear water around the island. Relaxed, you will go back on board your boat in the afternoon and make your way back to your resort. You will pass a rock formation in the middle of the lake, which is used by sea birds for egg-laying and nesting. Although you can not enter this rocky island, the boat is slow, so you still have the opportunity to observe the many birds from a distance. In the evening you go on a night walk through the rainforest. Overnight in the Permai rainforest.

12th day excursion to Bako National Park

Another unique Borneo National Park awaits you. Together with your guide, you will leave early in the morning for the Bako National Park. First, head to the picturesque fishing village of Bako, where you board a speedboat. The approximately 30-minute drive takes you along the river to the sea and finally to Bako National Park, where you will take a beautiful hike through the park’s diverse vegetation. If you move quietly, you may get to see nose monkeys, silver hood langurs or dragons – not to mention the many species of birds you flutter around. After a simple lunch in the park canteen, you will learn more about the unique flora and fauna of the reserve. On a plank path through the rainforest and mangrove forest, you can look for different species of monkeys and the imposing rhino birds. The excursion ends with another boat trip from the National Park’s docking point back to the fishing village of Bako. From there you will be driven by car back to your accommodation. Overnight in the Permai Rainforest.

Day 13: Farewell to Borneo

After a final breakfast, accompanied by the sounds of the jungle, make your way back to civilization. What remains are countless memories of great adventures and exciting encounters with the diverse flora and fauna of Sumatra and Borneo. In particular, the impressive orangutans, which you were able to experience up close, will certainly remain in your memory for a long time. You will be taken to Kuching Airport, where you will say goodbye to your guide. Perhaps one day you will return here.

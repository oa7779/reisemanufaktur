Malaysia travel - nature experience Borneo with bathing stay

Borneo Nature Travel - Experience unique nature experiences with Asia Special Tours on your individual Malaysia trip. This is a question.

Experience the fascinating diversity of Borneo on a journey full of extraordinary nature experiences and unforgettable encounters. Observe orangutans, nose monkeys, Irrawaddy dolphins and sea turtles in their natural environment, explore the mysterious cave worlds of the UNESCO-protected Gunung Mulu National Park and discover some of the oldest rainforests on our planet. Between the cultural treasures of Kuching, the spectacular landscapes around Mount Kinabalu and the species-rich national parks Sarawaks and Sabahs, you will experience all the beauty of Borneo. The perfect conclusion is relaxed days on the dreamlike beaches of Sabah, where you can end the experiences of your trip in a tropical atmosphere.

1st day Arrival in Kuching

At Kuching International Airport you will be warmly welcomed by your guide. Together you drive to your resort in the Damai area. After checking in, the rest of the day is at your disposal to arrive in peace and enjoy the tropical atmosphere. Your resort is set in a spectacular natural setting at the foot of legendary Mount Santubong. Here, the rainforest-covered foothills of one of the oldest tropical rainforests on earth connect with the beaches of the South China Sea. Enjoy upcoming adventures in one of the most fascinating natural paradises in Southeast Asia and unforgettable encounters with the unique wildlife of Borneo. Overnight in Kuching.

2nd Day Encounters with the wildlife of Borneo in Bako National Park

Together with your guide, depart early in the morning for Bako National Park, Sarawak’s oldest national park. After the trip to the fishing village of Bako, you will transfer by speedboat to the national park. The approximately 30-minute boat ride leads along the coast and already offers first impressions of the impressive landscape. In the National Park you will explore the diverse habitats on selected hiking trails. With a bit of luck you will discover nose monkeys, silver hood longurons, monitors and numerous bird species in their natural environment. After a simple lunch in the park canteen, continue your explorations and learn about the reserve’s unique flora and fauna. On wooden jetties through mangrove and rainforests, look for more wildlife, including various ape species and the impressive rhino birds. In the afternoon you will return by boat to the fishing village of Bako. From there you will return to Kuching to your hotel. Overnight in Kuching. 

Day 3 Guest at the "wild man Borneos"

Today you can expect a special highlight of your trip: the encounter with one of our closest animal relatives. After breakfast, head to the Semenggoh Wildlife Centre, a renowned orangutan rehabilitation center. Here, animals that have been freed from captivity or found injured in the rainforest are carefully prepared for a life in the wild. During a short walk through the rainforest you reach the feeding platform. With a bit of luck, the impressive primates can be observed up close when they appear from the surrounding forest for feeding. After the visit, return to Kuching. In the afternoon, on a guided tour, you will explore the charming capital of Sarawak with its cultural and historical sights. Highlights include India Street, Main Bazaar and the over 175-year-old Tua Pek Kong Temple, one of Borneo’s oldest Chinese temples. Follow in the footsteps of James Brooke on a traditional sampan ride on the Sarawak River. While enjoying local snacks, magnificent views of the Kuching Waterfront, Astana, Fort Margherita and the picturesque Malay villages along the river open up. Overnight in Kuching. 

4th day Mangrove forest and turtle island

In the morning, head to the coastal village of Santubong, where you will board a motorized boat. During the drive through the mangrove landscape of Kuching Wetlands National Park, the fascinating wildlife of Borneo opens up. With a bit of luck you will discover mud jumpers, different bird species, monkeys and other inhabitants of this unique ecosystem. At the mouth of the Santubong and Salak Rivers, look for the rare Irrawaddy dolphins native to these waters. The further journey leads along the coast to the habitats of the nose monkeys before reaching Satang Island. The island is known for its sea turtle conservation project dedicated to the preservation of these endangered animals. Afterwards, you will have the opportunity to snorkel in the clear waters around the island and explore the colorful underwater world. In the afternoon, return to the mainland and drive to your hotel. Enjoy the rest of the day according to your own ideas and let the diverse nature experiences end relaxed. Overnight in Kuching.

Day 5: Travel to Mulu

In the morning you will be taken to the airport and fly on to Miri. Upon arrival, your guide will welcome you and escort you to the Mulu Marriott Resort & Spa, idyllic on the edge of UNESCO-protected Gunung Mulu National Park. Then start your discoveries in the National Park. You will first follow the three-kilometer long plank path through the typical Borneo dipterocarp and swamp forest to the “Lang ́s Cave”. Cave curtains and the wonderful drip formations characterize the beauty of this small and unique cave. The path through the cave itself is only 25 meters short and leads on to “Deer Cave”, which has the largest cave entrance in the world. As you enter, you will notice the typical smell of the millions of bats that live in this cave. Every night they leave their shelter to go for food. After about one kilometer you reach the southern entrance, which is also known as “Garden of Eden”. At an observation point, you can observe the bats as they leave the cave. This is an event that I am sure you will remember for a long time. At nightfall, make your way back to your resort. Overnight in Mulu.

6th Day Spectacular Caves of Mulu National Park

Today you will take a traditional long boat up the Melinau River to the “Wind Cave”. This spectacular cave is famous for its large, slender stalactites and stalagmites. The cave owes its name to the cool air currents that can be felt at the entrance. The “Clearwater Cave” has the longest underground water system (106 km) in Southeast Asia. Enjoy the clear water in the natural basin that formed outside the cave. After returning to the park headquarters, another highlight awaits you in the afternoon: a walk along the Baumkronenweg, which gives you fascinating views of the lush vegetation of the rainforest. The rest of the afternoon is at your disposal. Take the time to relax or explore the unique nature of the National Park on your own on one of the well-signed hiking trails. In the evening, return to your resort and end the day in a relaxed atmosphere. Overnight in Mulu. 

Day 7 - Continue to Kota Kinabalu

Today, say goodbye to the Borneo rainforest and fly to Kota Kinabalu, the capital of Sabah. Upon arrival, you will be met by your guide and escorted to your hotel. Take advantage of the remaining day to enjoy the relaxed atmosphere of the coastal city. Overnight in Kota Kinabalu. 

Day 8: Sabah's Natural Diversity

Early in the morning your journey to the original interior of Borneo begins. Passing rolling hills, rice fields and small villages, you will reach Sabah Tea Plantation, one of the few certified organic tea plantations in Malaysia. During a tour, you will get insights into the cultivation and processing of the tea and enjoy the wide view over the green hillside landscape. Then visit the Poring Hot Springs, nestled in tropical vegetation. The natural thermal springs invite for a relaxing walk. Alternatively, explore the Kipungit Waterfall or the spectacular Canopy Walk, which opens up impressive views of the treetops high above the rainforest floor. After lunch, continue to Kinabalu National Park, a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2000. Located at the foot of Mount Kinabalu, 4,102 meters high, the park is one of the most biodiverse protected areas in Southeast Asia. On a guided walk, you will discover the fascinating plant world of the park and enjoy, as long as the weather plays along, magnificent views of Mount Kinabalu, the highest elevation between the Himalayas and Papua New Guinea. In the late afternoon, return to your hotel. Overnight in Kota Kinabalu.

Day 9 Discover Kota Kinabalu

After a relaxing breakfast, discover the capital of Sabah. Dreamlikely located between the South China Sea and the foothills of Mount Kinabalu, Kota Kinabalu combines maritime flair, eventful history and cultural diversity. Together with your guide, you will explore the city’s main attractions and learn about its development from the former colonial settlement of Jesselton to the modern capital of Sabah. A special highlight is the visit to the colorful Filipino Market on the waterfront, where local specialties, handcrafted souvenirs and the bustle of everyday life meet. At the end of the day, enjoy the relaxed atmosphere on the waterfront. Overlooking the sea, the offshore islands of Tunku Abdul Rahman Marine Park and the slow-setting sun, Kota Kinabalu is at its most beautiful. Overnight at Kota Kinabalu.

10.-12. Day time to enjoy

After your round trip, you will now spend some relaxing days near Kota Kinabalu. Enjoy the sun, beach and sea and the amenities of your hotel. Shangri La Rasa Ria Resort is nestled in the tropical landscape of the nature reserve on Pantai Dalit beach. Treat yourself to a relaxing massage at the spa, take advantage of the diverse water sports offer or play a game of golf on the 18 hole golf course. The resort is guaranteed to leave no heart’s desire open. Overnight at Kota Kinabalu.  

Tip: Would you rather spend your bathing on a picturesque island? Then the luxury resort Bungaraya Island Resort is for you. The charming wooden villas in the traditional style of Sabah nestled in the lush vegetation and offer magnificent views of the heavenly-white crescent-shaped sand beach. We are happy to make you an offer!  
 

Day 13: Farewell to Borneo

Today we say goodbye. After impressive nature experiences, unforgettable wildlife encounters in the rainforest and relaxing days on the beach, you will be taken to Kota Kinabalu airport and will start your journey home or onwards with many valuable memories.

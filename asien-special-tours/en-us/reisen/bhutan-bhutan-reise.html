Do you like to travel intensively? In just eight days, this itinerary will explore the highlights of the Buddhist Kingdom of Bhutan: its peaceful capital, ornate monasteries and, of course, the breathtaking natural backdrop of the Himalayas. During a two-day hike through the mountains, you will then immerse yourself in the beauty of this country, enjoying breathtaking views and moments of silence.

1st day Arrival to Paro

The Buddhist kingdom of Bhutan, through its long seclusion, has preserved its own identity like no other nation. Look forward to a week in which you immerse yourself in the fascination of a foreign lifestyle in the midst of spectacular natural scenery. Your journey begins by arriving at Paro Airport, the only international airport in the country. In good weather, you will have a breathtaking view of the Himalayan Mountains up to Mount Everest when you land. Due to the towering mountain peaks around the runway, take-off and landing is considered so difficult that only a few specially trained pilots are allowed to fly into Paro. After landing, you will be expected and driven to your hotel in the capital Thimphu. The first day in the unfamiliar high-altitude air should be tackled slowly so as not to overwhelm your body. Your resort offers the best conditions for a relaxing afternoon: stay at Pemako Thimphu, which has an in-house spa area. Overnight in Thimphu.

Day 2 The tranquil capital Thimphu

Thimphu is one of the smallest capitals in the world and – next to the seat of government of the island state of Palau – the only capital without traffic lights. You will drive above the city through the Mothitang district to Takin Reserve, a small protected area where you will discover Bhutan’s national animal: the Takin – an idiosyncratic creature with the body of a cow and the head of a goat. According to legend, it was created by magic by the Divine Madman, an eccentric Buddhist saint. The journey continues to Sangay Gang, where the BBS Tower rises. Here, countless prayer flags flutter in the wind. If you wish, you can take a short, easy hike to Wangditse Monastery from here. Back in town, visit the National Memorial Chorten, a monument to world peace and prosperity dedicated to the third king of Bhutan. Insights into the country’s art and culture can be obtained by visiting the Institute of Traditional Arts and Crafts and the Folk Museum. Overnight in Thimphu.

Day 3 Over the Dochula Pass to Punakha

In the morning you leave Thimphu and leave for Punakha. Your path takes you over the Dochula Pass (3050 meters), from where you have an impressive view of the towering peaks of the Himalayan Mountains in beautiful weather. Arriving in Punakha, a walk to the temple Chhimi Lhakhang offers you a beautiful view of the valley. The view from your hotel room will also inspire you: the Uma with only eleven rooms and the traditional architecture provides a very special atmosphere. Overnight in Punakha.

Day 4 The magnificent monastery fortress of Punakha

In the morning, take a hike to Khamsum Yulley Namgyal Chorten. From the temple, a wide view of the Punakha Valley opens up – a perfect place for quiet moments and insights into the spiritual depths of Bhutan. The hiking trail leads through rice fields, over a narrow suspension bridge and finally up the hill to the temple. The path is gently rising and lined with magnificent natural scenery. In the afternoon, one of the most impressive buildings in the country awaits you: the Punakha Dzong, built in 1637 by legendary state founder Shabdrung Ngawang Namgyal. The monastery fortress served for centuries as both the religious and administrative center of Bhutan. Despite several fires and a serious earthquake, the Dzong has been lovingly restored and today shines in old splendor. Nestled between two rivers, it is one of the most beautiful and impressive dzongs in the country – a masterpiece of Bhutanese architecture and spiritual symbolism. Overnight in Punakha.

Day 5 Return to Paro

After breakfast, we will return to Paro via the Dochula Pass. Along the way, enjoy a traditional lunch at a farm over 300 years old. You will also visit the Shrine of Kyichu Lhakhang, a complex consisting of two identical temples built on a small ledge. Around the sanctuary, countless colorful prayer flags blow in the wind. Then visit the National Museum of Bhutan, housed in a rounded building dating back to 1649. The massive, shell-shaped structure with up to 2.5 meters thick walls once served as a watchtower and today impresses not only for its extraordinary architecture, but also for its diverse collection of religious, cultural and historical artifacts. Overnight in Paro.

Day 6 Hike to Bumdra Monastery

After experiencing many of the kingdom’s highlights, today you will embark on a two-day hike through the fascinating Himalayan mountain world. Your tour starts in Sang Choekor, where your load ponies are already waiting for you. After a climb of about two hours, you will reach an enchanting clearing hung with prayer flags, offering a unique view of Paro and the Do-Chhu Valley. In the distance, you can catch a view of Choechong Tse Lhakhang Temple, which is another two hours away on foot. After another stage, which leads you steeply up to ruins and prayer flags, you reach an ancient forest that must be crossed. Their camp for the night is secluded near the Bumdra Monastery. After lunch, you can visit the monastery and climb a nearby mountain peak with a total height of 4000 meters. With a clear view, you have a breathtaking view over the Himalayas from here. Overnight at the altitude camp at 3800 meters.
Note: Overnight in the tent on a camp bed with sleeping bag and blankets, separate sanitary facilities, warm water in the evening & morning, kitchen tent.

Day 7 Hike to Tiger's Nest

Well strengthened start today’s day hike. Over the monastery of Zangtopelri, which is also called "heaven on earth", today's hike downhill leads you to the unique and world-famous monastery of Taktshang (Tigernest). According to legend, the second Buddha, Guru Padmasambhava (also called Rinpoche), flew here on the back of a tigress and meditated. It is on this rocky outcrop that the impressive monastery was built, which has become a landmark of Bhutan. The view from here is truly dizzying! From the monastery you start the last stage of your trekking tour and begin an approximately one-hour descent. Once downstairs, you will already be expected and driven back to your hotel in Paro. Overnight in Paro.

Day 8 Departure from Paro

After a week in the Kingdom of Bhutan, your journey will end, from which you will surely take home many precious memories. Your driver will take you to the airport for the return flight via Delhi.

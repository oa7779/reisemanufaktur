The Little Sunda Islands - Sailing cruise to the Komodo dragons

Indonesia island hopping to the small Sunda Islands. Experience with Asia Special Tours an unforgettable sailing cruise to the Komodo Varanas. Ask now!

Itinerary & services

English itinerary text is an offline machine translation of the German original for this team preview. Please confirm travel details with your advisor; the German source remains available through the language switch.

Would you like to feel like Robinson Crusoe? Then visit the charming little Sunda Islands, which will surely make your heart beat faster. The mystical tranquility you experience on the mountains and crater lakes, lush rice terraces, pink dream beaches and various excursions to traditional villages - this trip has everything to offer. Meet the world’s largest lizard, Komodo Waran, and see dreamlike coral gardens and colorful aquarium fish snorkeling. The absolute highlight of this trip is the cruise on the exclusive sailing ship Katharina. Shipper through the paradisiacal island world and immerse yourself in your very personal dream world!

Day 1 Arrival at Flores

Welcome to Flores. Upon arrival in Maumere, you will already be expected by your guide. Together you drive to a local restaurant, where you can first strengthen yourself a little. On the way to Moni, stop at beautiful Koka Beach. Take off your shoes, walk through the soft sand and let your vacation begin. Then you will be driven to your hotel. Enjoy the peaceful tranquility in your hotel or take a walk through the small town of Moni. Overnight in Moni.

Day 2 Sunrise at Kelimutu

Even before the first rays of the sun illuminate the sky and clouds of fog steaming up from the valleys and forests, you drive today to the summit of the volcano Kelimutu. In the language of the locals, the Kelimutu is also called “Mountain of Spirits”. It is therefore not surprising that this place was once the setting for spiritual rituals. After an approximately 30-minute walk, you will enjoy a magnificent panorama and a breathtaking sunrise that is reflected glitteringly in the three uniquely coloured lakes. The three different colored lakes are particularly impressive, because they shimmer due to the mineral content of the water in brilliant aquamarine up to ochre red and chocolate brown. After this natural spectacle, return to your hotel and enjoy a rich breakfast. Then leave Moni and continue along the coast to Riung. Here are dreamy white sand beaches waiting for you. Overnight in Riung.

Day 3 Excursion to the “17 Islands”

Today, discover with your local guide the dazzling underwater world of Pulau Tujuhbelas National Park. The National Park of the "17 Islands" offers a paradisiacal island landscape with white sand beaches and turquoise water - a dreamlike natural backdrop. Snorkel in the crystal clear water and marvel at the unspoiled coral reef and the turmoil of colorful fish. If the water level is not too low, visit bat island Ontoloe. Here you can observe colonies of flying foxes that live in the mangrove trees by the thousands and fly up droves at sunset. Then return to Riung. Overnight in Riung.

Day 4 Traditional Life in Bajawa

On the way to Bajawa, today you can make a very relaxing stopover in Soa to enjoy a dip in the hot springs of Mangeruda in the shade of the surrounding coconut palms. Around noon you will reach Bajawa - a small, friendly mountain town located at 1,100 meters between forested volcanic peaks. Bajawa is one of the most traditional towns on the island. Five different tribes, each with its own language, customs, faith and dress, belong to the Ngada people. The tribe lives as it did hundreds of years ago in thatched wooden houses. Enjoy a typical lunch at a local restaurant before visiting the impressive Ogi Waterfall. During a walk across the market you can get to know the colorful life here in the center of the original settlement of the Ngada. Overnight in Bajawa.

5th Day Country Living in Bena

In the morning you can expect exciting insights into a rural life that is so completely different from our Western everyday life. In the traditional village of Bena, the old houses are flanked by impressive megalites. In addition, the Volcano Inerie in the background provides a breathtaking backdrop. Hike up to the top of a mountain to get a magnificent view of the village, the volcano and the blue Savu Lake. Then the journey continues to Ruteng. The landscape is becoming greener and the climate milder. In this lively, Catholic mountain village, the minority of the Manggarai is also at home. In the afternoon, visit Ranamese Lake, which is located at 1,220 meters altitude in a nature reserve. The dense forest is home to many bird species, including wild macaws. Your next stop is the limestone cave in Liang Bua, where a potential Homo genus was discovered in 2003, the “Homo Floresiensis”. Overnight in Ruteng.

Day 6 drive to Labuan Bajo

After breakfast, take a short walk through the rice fields of Ruteng. Then the drive continues to the westernmost point of the island, Labuan Bajo, where boom boats and flower-decorated houses line up. You can immediately see why the Portuguese gave the island the name “Cap of Flowers” in the 16th century. Together with the guide you will visit the Batu Cermin cave. At the end of the day, you will be enchanted by the sunset overlooking the small islands between Flores and Komodo. Overnight in Labuan Bajo.

7th - 8th day Departure to the island paradise - Start of the cruise

Today it says: Welcome on board the Katharina, a stylish sailing ship and your floating home for the coming days. Once the anchor is cleared, your dream cruise begins through the fascinating island world of the Indonesian archipelago – a journey between natural wonders and cultural treasures. Already on the first day a highlight awaits you: the legendary island Komodo, the only home of the legendary Komodo dragons. These impressive lizards, up to three meters long and weighing up to 70 kilograms, seem like creatures from a bygone era. They roam leisurely through the barren landscape – an encounter that leaves wonder and awe. Later, glide into the crystal clear waters of Komodo National Park. Your first snorkeling tour will take you to coral reefs that glow in all colors. With a bit of luck, join graceful sea turtles silently moving through shallow water – a quiet, magical moment under the tropical sun. Overnight on board.

Day 9 - 10 From pink shores to mystical crater lakes

Today, you wake up off the coast of an uninhabited dream island – a white sandy beach traversed by shimmering pieces of red coral, dips the shore into delicate shades of pink and gives it the magic of a “pink beach”. Spend the morning in this paradisiacal setting: swimming, paddling and snorkeling along the front coral reef make these hours an unforgettable experience. Then take a course on an active volcanic island, where an extraordinary snorkeling adventure awaits you. From the seabed, fine bubbles of volcanic origin rise between the corals. Later, explore another volcanic island at the heart of which is a sunken crater lake – filled with salt water after the massive eruption of Mt. Tambora in 1815. Amidst this mystical landscape, you will have the opportunity to swim in the crater lake, kayak or paddle relaxed – an impressive conclusion to a day between natural wonders and volcanic history. Overnight on board.

Day 11 - 12 In touch with whale sharks

Today is a day where you can experience something unforgettable! With a bit of luck, you’ll have the unique opportunity to swim with whale sharks – the majestic, gentle giants of the oceans. These impressive but completely harmless animals regularly stay in this bay. Responsible cooperation with local fishermen, who operate their traditional fishing platforms here and thus attract the animals, provides a special opportunity to meet them in the wild. A sighting can of course not be guaranteed – after all, they are wild animals – but the chances are good and we hope for a meeting. Later in the day, visit the island of Moyo. After a short walk through the village, you will reach a hidden waterfall – a delightful place to swim in the clear, cool water and end this impressive day. Overnight on board.

13th - 14th day Insights into island life & marine adventure

Today your journey will take you to a small volcanic island where you will be taken to a traditional PinesBoat bumping – an impressive example of Indonesian boat building art made directly on the beach. During a walk through the charming village, you will get authentic insights into the everyday life of the inhabitants. Get in touch with the friendly islanders and discover artful, hand-woven textiles. Back in Komodo National Park, you’ll have the opportunity to snorkel and explore secluded beaches. A special highlight is the visit of a coral restoration project. The day will end with an atmospheric barbecue directly on the beach. Under the wide starry sky, enjoy freshly prepared local specialties and the sound of traditional music – a festive ending with the entire boat crew! Overnight on board.

15th day Departure from Bali

Enjoy one last time the morning hours on board while the ship is quietly anchored. After breakfast, a scenic ride will take you to the mountain village of Melo, where an impressive performance of the traditional Caci dance awaits you. In this ritual duel, two fighters meet with a whip and shield in a powerful, rhythmic dance accompanied by traditional music – a living testimony to the culture of Westflore. Back on board, you will have one last lunch together. Afterwards it is time to say goodbye – to the crew, your travel companions and the experiences of this impressive journey. You disembark and embark on the journey. An unforgettable journey with many unique impressions comes to an end - you disembark and are taken to Denpasar Airport.

Tigers, Maharajas & Bollywood - The Many Facets of India

India Tour: Walk in the footsteps of Tigers, Maharajas and Bollywood during your individual India Tour with Asia Special Tours.

As much as India is known for its inspiring heritage, culture and traditions, the country is also famous for the dazzling and glamorous Bollywood that produces more than 1,600 films per year. Embark on a journey through the enchanting and multifaceted Rajasthan with its magnificent palaces and impressive fortresses, its scenic beauty and charming small towns and villages facing Mumbai’s cosmopolitan culture. Sleep in the most beautiful palaces of Rajasthan, such as Maharaja and Maharani of bygone times, with a little luck see the majestic royal tiger in the wild and let yourself be enchanted by the glamour of the Bollywood world.

1st day Arrival in India & colorful bustle on the moonlight spot

Namaste and welcome to India! Your journey starts in Delhi, where you will be expected and driven to your accommodation upon arrival at the airport. You move into your room at The Imperial Hotel – also known as the “museum hotel” because each floor is dedicated to an artist whose works decorate the walls. The rooms and suites reflect the period of the British Raj. Enjoy an exclusive hotel with culinary delights and exceptional flair. In the afternoon, you will travel back in time in Old Delhi, where the Jama Masjid, one of the largest mosques in the world, testifies to Delhi’s past as the capital of Islamic India. Passing the Red Fort, the palace complex of the Mughals, which owes its name to the outer walls of red sandstone, your ride will take you on to the moonlight square – Chandni Chowk – one of the oldest and also busiest market squares in the city, where you will experience the typical flair of an Oriental bazaar. Even deeper you can immerse yourself in the streets and alleys of Delhi during a rickshaw ride. Your driver will show you the beauty of Old Delhi from a different angle on various routes off the main trails, taking you to traditional shops and bazaars. Overnight in Delhi.

2nd day temples, palaces and an ancient fortress

The imperial city of New Delhi with its wide avenues and imposing buildings was 1931 created by the British occupiers as a modern garden city. Today it houses the Government District, the Presidential House and the National Monument India Gate, modeled on the Arc de Triomphe in Paris and commemorating the 1World War I was built. But also the urban area of New Delhi awaits you with fascinating historical sights: for example, with the mausoleum of the great mogul Humayun. Your guide will also show you the Lotu Temple of the Baha'i, a tolerant world religion that originated in Iran, constructed in the form of a lotus flower, as well as the vast Lodi Gardens, which house several tombs of rulers of the Lodi Dynasty. One highlight is the Fort Purana Qila perched on a small hill east of the India Gate with the Qila-i-Kuhna Mosque, which is still well preserved today. Impressive in a very different way is the Akshardham Temple, decorated with statues of important personalities, divine figures of Hindu history and stone elephants, which brings you closer to the essence of Hindu culture and spirituality. The mighty structure is even in the Guinness Book of Records as the world’s largest Hindu temple complex. Overnight in Delhi.

Day 3 The fairytale Taj Mahal

Early in the morning you leave for the small, little-known village of Ajabgarh on the edge of the Aravelli mountains. With the express train you first go to Agra, which today is world famous mainly thanks to the Taj Mahal, a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Of course, you should not miss a look at this fabulous building. If you wish, you can also visit the ruined city of Fatehpur Sikri, which is also one of the most important testimonies of Mughal architecture. Finally, reach your exclusive luxury retreat for the next two nights: the Aman Bagh, which belongs to the luxury hotel group Aman. Complete in every respect, the 5-star house nestles itself in the picturesque Aravelli hillside landscape and is a true oasis of luxury and tranquility. The 40 suites and pavilions with a spacious terrace, which open up to a colorful garden, pay tribute to the detail of the Mughal architects. The marble interiors are decorated with magnificent wall frescoes and give the former palace an elegant charm. Enjoy exquisite seasonal dishes of Indian and international cuisine in the stylish restaurant, refresh yourself in the large swimming pool or treat yourself to a massage at the noble spa. Overnight in Ajabgarh.

Day 4 Time to Relax

Without tour guide & chauffeur

Today gives you the opportunity to really dangle your soul and enjoy the sweet inaction. Or do you feel like exploring the beautiful surroundings that invite you to hikes, temple visits, picnics and much more? For example, how about a morning yoga class in the ruins of Fort Bhangarh? After breakfast, we recommend a trip to one of the surrounding villages or to the fort of Ajabgarh, whose mamor facade and courtyard framed by 24 columns still offer a majestic sight today. The Ajabgarh is accessible on foot, by Jeep or Camel and offers you a magnificent view of Lake Jai-Sagar. Overnight in Ajabgarh.

5th Day Ranthambore National Park – Home of the Tigers

Today, head to Ranthambore National Park, which has a reputation for providing the best way to observe shy tigers in the world. In 1980, the area was transferred to a nature reserve and game reserve for endangered animals. Over the years, the cats have shifted their activities to the day and reduced their fear of humans. With a little luck, you have very good chances to see the majestic animals. Upon arrival, you will be immediately transferred to your hotel: the luxurious Aman-I-Khas, which is also one of the Aman Resorts. Treat yourself to the very best of what Ranthambore National Park has to offer in terms of accommodation. Feel transported back to times when the Mughals still ruled and went hunting with their court. Ten stately tents, which offer you all the comforts imaginable and are very stylishly and elegantly furnished, are grouped on a spacious complex around a communal area that is perfect in the evening to sit comfortably by the campfire. If you want more privacy, just make yourself comfortable on your private terrace and listen to the sounds of the jungle. Overnight in Ranthambore.

Day 6 on Jeepsafari in Ranthambore National Park

The tiger, once king of the jungle, is severely threatened. Spend all day on private safari tours in the National Park and experience India’s exciting wildlife. You have the opportunity to observe animals that have become very rare on our planet. Your chances are not bad to see one or more specimens of the majestic Bengal tigers. But of course not only tigers populate the national park, whose rich plant life also ranks around the ruins of a fort from the 10th century. With a bit of luck, you can also watch leopards and hyenas. In the shallow waters live marsh crocodiles and the air is filled with the song of countless birds. It is best to always keep your camera handy. Overnight in Ranthambore.

Day 7 Pure Luxury at Samode Palace

From the Ranthambore National Park, your journey continues to the small town of Samode, which is about 40 kilometers north of Jaipur. Tucked away on the edge of the town stands a palace in the style of architecture of the Mughal era – a true jewel of Indo-Saracene palace architecture. In 1987, the palace was transformed into a luxury hotel, which is one of the most beautiful hotels in India. Here you will spend the next two nights and feel like Maharaja and Maharani from days gone by. The 5-star house not only has 35 luxuriously furnished rooms, but also a sloping pool with views of the surrounding mountain world. In the courtyard there is an excellent restaurant with terrace that seduces your senses with delicious Indian and Western cuisine. Enjoy a gentle healing massage at the spa, sink into the lush bed in your room or take your time exploring the fairytale palace, almost 500 years old. Special highlights of the magnificent building are the reception hall with murals and small mirrors decorated walls as well as the mirror room above, which gives an overwhelming impression of abundance and splendor. Overnight in Samode.

Day 8 Excursion to Jaipur – the “Pinke City”

Enjoy a trip to Jaipur, the fast-growing capital of Rajasthan state. In the late 19th century, the city was painted in shades of pink in preparation for the visit of the British king – in Rajasthan’s tradition, this is the color of hospitality. To this day, Jaipur is still called the “Pink City”. Here you will find a true masterpiece of Mughal architecture: the imposing Fort Amber, perched on a steep mountain slope north of Jaipur. With the Jeep you drive past the artistically landscaped Mogul Garden up the winding pavement road to the main gate of the complex. Behind it you can expect the public audience hall, the magnificent Ganesha Gate and the Spiegelpalast. From the galleries and terraces you also have a wonderfully wide view over the valley and the mountains of the Aravalli chain. Also the Rambagh Palace is absolutely worth a visit. Once home to the Maharaja of Jaipur, the luxurious palace surrounded by beautiful gardens is now a luxury hotel known as the “Jewel of Jaipur”. You will also visit the magnificent City Palace, which witnesses an era of abundance and still serves as the residence of the former Maharaja, as well as the Jantar Mantar Observatory. The accuracy of the instruments – including the world’s largest sundial – is still impressive. Overnight in Samode.

Day 9 Jodhpur, the “Blue City”

Your next destination is the city of Jodhpur, which is also called the “Blue City” because of the color of its houses. It was founded in 1459 by Rao Jodha, head of the Rajput clan. The city owes its wealth and rapid growth to the trade in opium, sandalwood and copper. The city is dominated by the huge historical fortress Meherangarh, which perched majestically on a rock 130 meters above the city and offers spectacular views over the bright blue tangle of houses. Considering the colossal dimensions, British writer Rudyard Kipling described this place as “the work of giants”. At the entrance to the fortress stands the marble mausoleum Jaswant Thada for the popular ruler Jaswant Singh II (1878-1895), who freed Jodhpur from robbers, set up irrigation systems and boosted the economy. Spend the night at the Umaid Bhawan Palace Hotel, located on a hill just outside the city. Just the driveway to this exclusive property will arouse a truly royal feeling in you. The Maharaja of Jodhpur himself lives here and occasionally holds polo tournaments with elephants. Let yourself be transported here to a bygone time, when the British Empire still spanned the entire world. Overnight in Jodhpur.

10th day Luxury in harmony with nature

Today, a very special nature experience awaits you far away from the big cities of India. You will drive to Suján Jawai luxury camp in the middle of untamed wilderness. The 9 luxurious tents offer plenty of space and privacy and are nestled in the breathtaking moonscape of the steppe Rajasthans. Here, in the ancient hills of Jawai, man and leopard have lived together in harmony for centuries. Inspired by the granite hills of the region, the camp offers a modern style but remains authentic and true to its location. Together with the owners, designer Michael Aram created a new quasi-Bauhaus safari style with this luxury camp that opened in 2013, which torn many interior decorators to storms of enthusiasm. In order to protect this unique nature, the management has set itself the goal of operating as sustainably and environmentally friendly as possible. What African luxury camps have been practicing for some time is becoming a pioneer in India at Jawai Leopard Camp – pure luxury and a minimal ecological footprint! The owners of Jawai have managed to create a contemporary luxury camp that combines subtle opulence with a strong sense of adventure. You can feel this sense of adventure on your afternoon Jeep safari. Go in search of the spotted big cats and keep your camera always at hand. Overnight in Jawai.

Day 11: In the footsteps of leopards: Jeep safari at dawn

If you have not yet had the luck to see one of the elegant big cats, you have the opportunity again today. Even before dawn, you leave for another Jeep safari under the cover of darkness. Look for the yellow-green glitter in the rugged mountains. These are the eyes of a leopard! Back at the camp, the rest of the day is at your disposal. First of all, have a quiet breakfast, enjoy the view from your beautiful porch or treat yourself to a soothing treatment at the spa. Routine Ayurvedafans will be pleased that thanks to the small size and highly qualified employees, it may be possible to respond a little more to personal needs in the wellness area. Would you rather explore the culture and natural beauty of the area? How about a trip to the 15th-century Kumhargarh Fort, surrounded by a wall of about 36 kilometers – the second longest wall in the world after the Great Wall of China – or another Jeep safari? Overnight in Jawai.

12th day boat trip on the picturesque Lake Pichola

Your next stage will take you to Udaipur, which is dominated by its lakes – this is why the romantic city is also called “Venice of the East”. The royal past is reflected in the rich ornaments on the doors of houses in the Old Town, palaces, gardens and monuments. Along the way, make a stop at the Jain temples of Ranakpur, a temple complex marked by great attention to detail: a forest of columns supporting the dome decorated with intricate geometric patterns and figures of goddesses. At noon, stop at the chic colonial-style Mountbatten Lodge and enjoy lunch under a 400-year-old banyan tree. Arriving in Udaipur, take a private boat trip to get attuned to the city, whose nickname contains the word “Venice”. They sail over the artificially created Pichola Lake. During a stopover at Jag Mandir Palace, Hollywood feeling comes up: The building was the scene of such well-known films as "Tiger von Eschnapur" and James Bond's "Octopussy". In the middle of the lake is also your fairytale palace hotel: the famous Hotel Taj Lake Palace, which has already been seen in many films and TV series, especially "Octopussy" and "The Jewel in The Crown". Built in 1746 as an amusement palace by Maharana Jagat Singh II, it is now considered one of the most romantic hotels in the world. Overnight in Udaipur.

Day 13 With the classic car through Udaipur

Udaipur has so much more to offer, so dedicate the morning entirely to exploring its most beautiful sides. You will be driven in style in a vintage car from the Vintage Car Museum of Udaipur. Truly royal is the sight of the magnificent Udaipur City Palace. One part of the winding building complex is open to the public as a museum, another serves as a residence for the acting head of Mewar, another houses a luxury hotel. Also extremely worth seeing is the Jagdish Temple, surrounded by a defensive wall, a Vishnu sanctuary in the traditional style. Above all, pay attention to the figure friezes that run in ribbons around the temple with expressive depictions of elephants, dancers and musicians. Of course, you should not miss a trip to Gangaur Ghat, which is one of the most beautiful places in Udaipur. Finally, the garden complex Saheliyon-ki-Bari invites with its lotus ponds, fountains and pavilions for extensive walks. The rest of the day you can design according to your own ideas. Explore Udaipur a little further on your own or enjoy the amenities of your dreamlike hotel. Overnight in Udaipur.

14th day Bollywood calls

This morning, a domestic flight will take you to Mumbai, the last stop on your journey. With a population of 12.5 million, Mumbai is the largest city in India and one of the most populous metropolises in the world. Together with your guide, you will take a city tour that will first take you to a very special laundry: at the Dhobi Ghats, around 10,000 people clean the laundry of hotels, restaurants and other major customers every day. You will also see Chhatrapti Shivaji Terminus Train Station in the style of Victorian neo-Gothicism, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, and the city’s seafront landmark: the Gateway of India. Finally, stroll through Crawford Market, Mumbai’s shopping mall. Here you can buy just about everything and get carried away into the bustle of a typical Indian market. Mumbai is also the center for Indian film production – Bollywood. If you wish, during a one-hour Bollywood dance workshop with a well-trained trainer (optional), you will get a first glimpse of the “madness” that constitutes Mumbai. Their hotel, The Taj Mahal Palace is much more than a luxury hotel, it is a national institution – and an Indian myth. It is one of the legendary hotels of the Orient. Overnight in Mumbai.

Day 15 Behind the Scenes of the Indian Dream Factory

After breakfast, delve even deeper into the crazy world of Bollywood. Knallig colorful, full of pathos and never embarrassed by a dance interlude – that’s how you know Bollywood movies. Around 250 of these stripes are filmed annually in Mumbai. On your full-day tour of a part of Bollywood Film Studios, take a look behind the scenes of the Indian Dream Factory: you will tour some of the sets and witness a film recording. You can also deepen your Bollywood dance skills and try your own sound recordings. Meet the professionals of the entertainment industry such as actors, journalists, musicians and dancers who come up with personal and authentic stories and knowledge. This excursion offers not only cineastes an exciting insight into the heart of modern Indian culture. Overnight in Mumbai.

16th day Farewell to India

Tigers, palaces and Bollywood – over the past few days you have gathered many colorful impressions of the diversity of Indian culture and its impressive wildlife. But even the most beautiful journey comes to an end. The suitcases packed with unique experiences and lasting memories will take you to the airport, where you will begin your journey home. Who knows, maybe one day you will return here.

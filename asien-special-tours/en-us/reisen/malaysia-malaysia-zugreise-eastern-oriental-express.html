Malaysia Train Tour - With the legendary Eastern & Oriental Express through Malaysia

Embark on a unique Malaysia train journey with the legendary Eastern & Oriental Express. Let the expert advise you now.

Itinerary & services

English itinerary text is an offline machine translation of the German original for this team preview. Please confirm travel details with your advisor; the German source remains available through the language switch.

Do you enjoy relaxed travel by train and have long dreamed of a ride on the famous Eastern & Oriental Express? This journey takes you on a very special ride on the legendary luxury train. Explore the fascinating metropolis of Singapore as well as the colonial city of Georgetown on the Malaysian island of Penang. Sit back and enjoy the fantastic view from the panoramic window of your noble cabin while driving. In the evening, dine in the elegant restaurant cart that serves fusion specialties created by internationally recognized chefs. The bar wagon will then spoil you with excellent cocktails with atmospheric piano music. Your hotel in Singapore is also very fine and leaves nothing to be desired.

1st day start of your adventure

Welcome to Singapore! Today, the fascinating city-state is a microcosm of glittering skyscrapers and lush green spaces that merge with testimonies of its colonial and Chinese influenced past. Upon arrival at the airport, you will already be expected and received. Your driver will take you to your hotel for the coming night. Depending on the arrival time, you can start with first sightseeings or rest a little at the hotel. In the evening, the first big highlight of your trip awaits you: an exclusive dinner at the Singapore Flyer, the second largest Ferris wheel in the world. Indulge in the delights of an excellent 4-course menu (Western or Chinese cuisine to choose from), let your personal waiter take care of you and enjoy the breathtaking views of Marina Bay – all in the comfort of your private gondola. After this unique experience, which is unrivalled not only in Singapore, you will be returned to your hotel. Overnight in Singapore.

Day 2 Singapore - Gemas

On the road with the Eastern & Oriental Express

After a relaxing breakfast at the hotel, you will have some time to rest or discover Singapore on your own. Later, drive to Woodlands Station, where your unforgettable train journey through Malaysia begins. On the platform, the legendary Eastern & Oriental Express awaits you. The elegant interior of the train is beautifully decorated with exquisite inlays and shiny teak work. For you, a comfortable and elegant state cabin with air conditioning, private bathroom and large panoramic window is reserved. Your cabin steward takes care of your well-being around the clock. While the train leisurely leaves the border of Singapore, you can see the picturesque landscape from the colonial-style panoramic car at the end of the train. In the evening, dine in the elegant restaurant cart that serves fusion specialties created by internationally recognized chefs. Afterwards, treat yourself to a cocktail in the bar car and enjoy the sociable atmosphere with atmospheric piano music. In your cabin transformed into a bedroom, you finally make yourself comfortable. Overnight on the train.

Day 3 Taman Negara National Park - Kuala Lumpur

On the road with the Eastern & Oriental Express

After a peaceful night on board, you wake up in the morning with a view of the impressive nature of Malaysia. As the train glides through the dense, tropical landscape, breakfast will be served in your cabin. After lunch on board, you will arrive at Merapoh Train Station. A special adventure begins from here: a trip to the legendary Taman Negara National Park, one of the oldest tropical rainforests in the world – around 130 million years old. This species-rich natural paradise with its wide rivers, rolling hills and lush jungles is home to an impressive variety of wildlife including water buffaloes, macaques, tapirs and rhino birds. In the heart of the park, you will meet dedicated villagers and organizations dedicated to protecting the last wild tigers and their habitats. Eastern & Oriental Express supports these initiatives as part of its long-standing partnership with Save Wild Tigers. Back on board, an atmospheric evening awaits you. Enjoy an exquisite dinner in the dining car, followed by entertainment in the elegant Piano Bar Car. Overnight on the train.

Day 4 West Coast Line - Penang Island

On the road with the Eastern & Oriental Express

Today you will reach Penang Island, a melting pot of cultures. You can choose from various excursions to discover Penang according to your personal interests. Lovers of cuisine immerse themselves in the world of Peranakan cuisine. The excursion starts with a visit to a spice shop and typical grocery store before you create your own hand in an interactive cooking class. If you are passionate about art and culture, choose a guided walk through the UNESCO-protected old town of Georgetown. Here you will find magnificent temples, a contemporary artist’s studio, a Chinese opera and an artfully designed puppet house. Alternatively, discover Penang individually on a private rickshaw ride. At Butterworth you will return on board, where a delicious lunch awaits. For curious explorers, there is also an excursion to the city of Ipoh, a former tin capital. Enjoy the last evening on board where a festive dinner awaits you. Overnight on the train.

Day 5 Johor Bahru & Arrival in Singapore

On the road with the Eastern & Oriental Express

After a last breakfast in its own compartment with fresh croissants, fruits, coffee, tea and juice, it is time to say goodbye to the historic luxury train. As the Eastern & Oriental Express travels through Malaysia’s southernmost state, Johor, tropical rainforests and scenic coastal landscapes move past your window. Shortly thereafter, the train crosses the narrow strait and reaches its destination: Singapore. At the train station you will already be expected by your tour guide and brought to your hotel. The rest of the day is at your disposal – a nice opportunity to relax or discover other facets of the city at your own pace. Overnight in Singapore.

Day 6 Discover Singapore

After breakfast, set off for a full-day exploration of Singapore – accompanied by your tour guide. Start with a drive through the Civic District, the historic heart of Singapore. Passing the Padang, the Cricket Club, the old Parliament Building, the Supreme Court and City Hall, you will learn about the colonial past and modern development of the city. At Merlion Park you will stop – with magnificent views of Marina Bay and the landmark of Singapore: the iconic Merlion statue, a mythical creature with lion’s head and fish body. Continue to the historic Chinatown. Here you will visit the impressive Buddha Tooth Relic Temple and Museum. Afterwards, visit the National Orchid Garden, nestled in the sprawling Singapore Botanic Gardens, a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Here you will marvel at one of the largest orchid collections in the world with over 60,000 plants. Around noon, a very special cultural experience awaits you at The Intan, a private museum in the heart of the Joo Chiat district. Founder Alvin Yapp personally guides you through his lovingly furnished home. The tour ends with a homemade Peranakan lunch inspired by his mother’s recipes. The rest of the day is at your disposal. Overnight in Singapore.

Day 7 Departure or Extension at Malaysia’s Dream Beaches

Your time out from everyday life is coming to an end. Even if your holiday is soon over, the precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories of your trip will accompany you forever. The day is at your disposal before you are taken to the airport for your return flight. Or do you want to relax on the beautiful beaches of Malaysia? We are happy to make proposals in this regard.

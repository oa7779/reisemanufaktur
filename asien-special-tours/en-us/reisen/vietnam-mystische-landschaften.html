Let yourself be enchanted by the mystical landscapes and magical places in Laos. Feel the special charm of this little-traveled country and smile with the hospitable Laotians for the bet. On this trip, you will get to know the cultural and scenic highlights of Laos and get genuine and authentic insights into the still very original everyday life in the villages of the various minorities. Experience wonderful Laos with all its facets: impressive colonial architecture in Luang Prabang and Vientiane, ethnic diversity from north to south, unspoiled river landscapes along the Mekong and not forgetting an excellent cuisine with many exotic dishes. Soak up the peace and serenity of Laos

1st Day Enchanting Luang Prabang with a UNESCO expert

Welcome to Laos! After arriving in Luang Prabang, you will be taken to the hotel and on the way you can get a first impression of what is probably the best preserved traditional city in Southeast Asia. The charming city with historic temples and colonial buildings lies between the Nam Khan and Mekong rivers and was declared a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1995. You are invited to explore Luang Prabang with UNESCO expert Mr. Francis Engelmann. You will meet him for the first time for a short thematic introduction, where you will learn about history, architecture and the temples in Luang Prabang. After the theoretical introduction, it is time to discover Luang Prabang live! A guide will accompany you throughout the day as you visit the mystical temples and monuments of Luang Prabang, such as the oldest temple in the city – Vat Visoun, the Temple of the Royal City – Vat Xieng Thong or Vat Sene. Later, you will meet Francis Engelmann again, who is happy to answer all your questions and give you further information on certain topics. With many first impressions you return to your hotel. Overnight in Luang Prabang.

2nd Day Boat Tour to the Mystical Buddha Caves of Pak Ou

Enjoy breakfast in your hotel and let the day go on comfortably. Then visit the former Royal Palace. Today it houses the National Museum, which will bring you even closer to the culture and history of the country. Around noon you will board for a small boat tour on the Mekong. You will stop at the picturesque village of Thin Hong, where you will get a first impression of daily life. The boat will take you to the interesting caves of Pak Ou. The limestone caves are located directly on the shore cliffs of the Mekong, about 25 kilometers north of Luang Prabang and are one of the most important Buddhist pilgrimage sites in Laos. Once upon a time, there were over 5,000 statues in the two cave temples that found their way here through donors. On the way back to Luang Prabang, visit Ban Xangkhong. This village is known for its handmade paper. As you can see, the traditional craft flourishes in the Luang Prabang area. Take a walk through the village and visit Hmong Market. Afterwards you will reach Luang Prabang, where you can relax the evening. How about a drink at a bar or visit the lively night market, where many Laotian specialties are offered. Overnight in Luang Prabang.

3rd Day Visit to Khmu Village & Hike to Idyllic Waterfalls

Early in the morning, the monks move through the streets in their striking orange coats and are supplied by the citizens with rice and other food donations. An impressive spectacle! After breakfast, stroll across the colorful market and make your way to the Buddhist temple Wat Mai, which with its five-story ornate roof is the largest temple in Luang Prabang. Then leave Luang Prabang and reach Ban Nong Heo after a 50-minute drive, where you will have some time to discover the charming village of Khmu. Then hike through lush mountain forests and plantations to the Khoung Si Waterfalls. After a 1.5-hour hike in beautiful nature, you will reach Ban Thapene, a village located at the entrance to Khoung Si Waterfalls. The turquoise water, which flows partly over terraced basins down the slope, invites you to bathe. Then return to Luang Prabang, where at the end of today you will ascend the Temple Mount Phou Si to enjoy the sunset with an overwhelming view of the city and Mekong. Overnight in Luang Prabang.

4th day guesswork in the mysterious level of clay pitchers

In the morning you will leave the charming Luang Prabang via a mountainous road. During the winding drive, enjoy panoramic views from the finest and observe everyday life in the small villages along the streets. Despite the destruction caused by the Vietnam War, which had spread to Laos, the landscape is still relatively untouched. Your goal is the plain of clay pitchers, a mysterious archaeological site. Here are stone jugs from half to three meters in size and of different shape and shape. The heaviest – “King’s Cup” – brings a proud 6 tons on the scale. The age of the pitchers is estimated to be 1,500 to 2,000 years and they are said to be funeral ruins, but of which people is unclear. Take your time and visit the huge clay pitchers in peace – maybe you can solve the mystery of the origin? Afterwards you will reach your hotel in Xieng Khouang. Overnight in Xieng Khouang.

Day 5 Guest in Vientiane - Visits to the Laotian capital

After breakfast, stroll through the central market of Xieng Khouang and watch the bustle. Then a short flight will take you to Vientiane. The sleepy-looking capital of Laos impresses with shady avenues and colorful markets. Located on the banks of the Mekong and within sight of Thailand, it is characterized by temples and buildings from the colonial period. You will begin your tour of the city with the oldest temple, Wat Si Saket. This temple complex shows you original Laotian architecture. Hundreds of Buddha statues made of stone, wood and bronze can be seen from a gallery set in the surrounding wall. On the way to the famous Stupa That Luang, you will make a photo stop at Patuxai Monument, the Laotian version of the Arc de Triomphe. The gate honors the royal troops who died in the Indochina War. After many impressions, the day will end on the Mekong shore, where you can enjoy an atmospheric sunset. Walk a little along the waterfront and let yourself drift. Overnight in Vientiane.

Day 6 Colorful Market Visit & Sculptures and Statues in Buddha Park

During a leisurely walk, you continue your city tour in the morning. You will see the temples Wat Ong Teu, seat of the second patriarch of Laotian Buddhism, and Wat Inpeng with its impressive frescoes depicting the Indian Ramayana epic. Then visit the centerpiece of Vientiane - the Central Market. From fresh vegetables to antiques - everything you can imagine is sold here. You have lunch at your disposal. Relax in your hotel or stroll through the lively streets of the city. In the afternoon, visit the famous Buddha Park, about 25 km outside Vientiane. Here are about 200 Buddhist and Hindu statues and images, which are intended to recreate different eras. The sculpture park with meter-high concrete figures was created in 1958 by the Laotian artist Boun Leua Soulilat. Then return to Vientiane, where you can arrange the evening as you wish. Overnight in Vientiane.

7th day On to the south to the temple complex of Wat Phu

In the morning you will be taken to the airport and fly on to Pakse in southern Laos. After the eventful days in the north of the country, a charming journey through the scenic south awaits you. You are already expected at the airport and immediately drive on to Champasak, to the impressive ruins of Wat Phu. Together with the cultural landscape of Champasak, they are part of the UNESCO World Heritage Site. This site was built in the 10th century under the Angkor ruler Jayavarman IV as a Shiva sanctuary – before the establishment of Angkor Wat in Cambodia. Let the special atmosphere of this historically valuable area affect you and visit the different levels of the complex. Overnight in Champasak.

Day 8 Visiting Coffee Farmers & Picturesque Bolaven Plateau

After breakfast, head to Paksong, the Laotian capital of coffee production and the gateway to the Bolaven Plateau, which is located at an altitude of 1,300 meters. Various ethnic groups live on the plateau, most of them living from agriculture. They grow coffee, tea and spices on fertile soil, and the French originally planted coffee, bananas and rubber trees on the plateau. The coffee that is grown in this area today is known all over the world. The Bolaven Plateau is rich in hidden treasures. With a Jeeptour you find the most beautiful places. After an adventurous ride into the heart of the plateau, you will be greeted by a passionate coffee farmer who will invite you to his land. Together you will walk through its plantations and learn all about traditional coffee cultivation and further processing. After a short hike along small paths past plantations, fields and forests, it’s time for a refreshing lunch. Enjoy the peaceful surroundings while enjoying some typical Laotian snacks. Then we take the Jeep to a bamboo village of the ethnic minorities Laven and Souay. You will feel like you are traveling to the past. Walk through the village and try to get in touch with the warm residents. Of course, your guide will be happy to help. Overnight in Champasak.

9th Day By boat through a world of 4000 islands

Today, in the very south of the country, you immerse yourself in an almost surreal region – the so-called area of the 4000 islands. Here, where the Mekong finds its widest extent, over the millennia a landscape has emerged which, depending on the water level, produces only a few large and countless small islands in the river, some with a dimension of a few square kilometers, others only a few square meters in size. You will find the largest waterfalls in Southeast Asia, called Khone Phapheng. By boat you will explore the unique island world of the Mekong. You will also drive to the Liphi Falls and the fishing village of Ban Khone. Enjoy the natural beauties and observe the life of the locals in this extraordinary region. Overnight in Champasak. 

Day 10: Farewell to Laos

Your unforgettable journey draws to a close; you will be picked up and taken to Pakse Airport. Even if your holiday is soon over – the precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories of your trip will accompany you forever. Look forward to letting your friends and family share your experiences in the distance at home.

Armenia Journey - Cradle of Christianity

Armenia Travel - Cradle of Christianity of Asia Special Tours. Experience Armenia up close and individually. Ask here for your private trip to Armenia!

Itinerary & services

English itinerary text is an offline machine translation of the German original for this team preview. Please confirm travel details with your advisor; the German source remains available through the language switch.

Depart to the largely undiscovered Armenia and experience the country with its religious and historical sites. Try the highly acclaimed Armenian brandy and ride the longest cable car in the world to an architectural masterpiece. Immerse yourself in the world of Christian origins and discover the cultural, historical and natural beauty of the country during your journey! Experience Armenia from its most delicious side and learn about the thousands of years old culinary traditions. We also invite you not only to discover the most beautiful regions of the country, but also to feel the warm hospitality of Armenians up close.

1st day Arrival in Yerevan

Bari galoust and welcome to Armenia! Upon arrival at Yerevan airport, you will be expected by your local guide. So you can already, while the chauffeur takes you to the hotel, first facts and anecdotes about the city. Armenia’s capital was founded on the hill Arin Berd in 782 BC, making it one of the oldest cities in the world. Overnight in Yerevan.

2nd day Exciting city tour in Yerevan

After you have fortified yourself with an extensive breakfast, take a city tour of Yerevan. First you will get to know the Republic Square, which was built in traditional Armenian architecture. There you will visit the Historical Museum, where important archaeological collections from the Stone Age to the Middle Ages are on display. In front of the museum is a large fountain, also known as the “Singing Fountain”. Afterwards you will visit the Government House and the Ministry of the Federal Foreign Office. Then you will dedicate yourself to the central post office and the national gallery of paintings. Finally, visit the Zizernakaberd, a hill on which the memorial and the museum for the victims of the genocide of Armenians in 1915 are located. After your return to the hotel, you can let the many first impressions affect you. How about a leisurely evening walk through the busy streets of Yerevan? Overnight in Yerevan.

3rd day priests in Etschmiatsin, cooking class and brandy tasting

Today, your journey will take you to Etschmiatsin, about 20 kilometers from Yerevan. The Cathedral of Etschmiatsin is known as the center of the Armenian Gregorian Church and is considered one of the first Christian churches in the world. In the monastery you will meet a priest who will certainly be happy to answer many of your questions. A subsequent cooking class will teach you how to make gata, a traditional sweet bread. You can taste your lunch in an art school. Afterwards, visit the Temple of Zvartnot, a pearl of 7th-century architecture and also a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Finally, return to Yerevan and visit a factory where brandy is produced. Learn more about the interesting ripening process and the secrets of storage. Convince yourself and try the much-praised Armenian brandy! In a relaxed atmosphere, you will end the evening. Overnight in Yerevan.

Day 4: Religious Sites and Departure to Jermuk

After breakfast, you will first drive to the Monastery of Choir Virap, which is located at the foot of Mount Ararat and near the closed border with Turkey. The importance of the monastery is due to Grigor, the enlightener. According to legend, he is said to have introduced Christianity in Armenia and is thus considered the first Armenian Catholic. Afterwards, your journey leads you to the Amaghu gorge. There is Noravank, perhaps the most beautiful Armenian monastery. With its reddish facade, it is hardly distinguishable from its rocky background at sunset. It is also considered a large, religious and cultural center of the 12th century and is located in a place that also impresses with its breathtaking nature. Then you will continue to Jermuk, where you will be taken to your hotel. Go on a little exploration or relax in your hotel. Overnight in Jermuk.

5th day Driving trip to Tatev Monastery

The town of Jermuk is mainly known for its mineral springs, which thanks to their chemical composition can be used in the treatment of some diseases. During a walk through the city, you will visit the Gallery of Minerals and learn more about the applications and effectiveness of natural sources. The cable car goes up afterwards! It is also known as the “wing of Tatev”, a whopping 5.7 kilometers long and opened in 2010. Enjoy the ride, which saves you a thirty-minute winding car ride on narrow roads. The cable car takes you to the medieval monastery Tatev, which is located in the village of the same name in the district of Sjunik. The monastery was built in the 10th century and resembles an architectural masterpiece. It has a turbulent history behind it, was home to poor monks and once owned hundreds of villages, was destroyed and rebuilt. Below, the Vorotan rushes and supports the flair of this secluded place. After you have strengthened yourself from the many impressions over lunch, return to Jermuk. Overnight in Jermuk.

6th day Visit of a caravanserai & enchanting Sevan Lake

Your journey will take you via the Selim Pass to a caravanserai. Truly overwhelming is the view you can take from the caravanserai to the pass and mountain ranges of Vajot’s Dzor. The caravanserai is very worth seeing. It is the best known and best preserved in all of Armenia. Here Christian and Islamic architecture are combined – two inscriptions prove this. Then continue on to Lake Sevan. This huge lake makes up five percent of Armenia’s land area, 2,000 meters above sea level, making it the second highest freshwater lake in the world. On a peninsula there is a monastery, which you will visit afterwards. Finally, you reach Dilijan, a famous spa town in Armenia. In Armenia it is said: "If Paradise had mountains, forests and mineral springs, it would be like Dilijan". Due to its natural beauty, it is often referred to as “little Armenian Switzerland”. Overnight in Dilijan.

7th Day Experience Tradition in Fioletowo

In the morning, take a walking tour of the old town district of Dilijan. Afterwards you will continue to the village of Fioletovo, where the early Christians live. Over a cup of tea at a local house, you can learn about the village’s inhabitants who call themselves “Molokanan”. Immerse yourself in the customs and customs that the minority continues to maintain today! Afterwards, your journey will take you to the medieval monastery of Saghmosavank, which is located on the breathtaking, deep gorge of Ashtarak. Continue on to Ashtarak, one of the oldest cities in Armenia. There you will visit the small family church Karmravor with a seventh century brick roof. It measures just seven square meters – incredible that this building even has a dome! Despite its size, you can also feel the authentic Christianity here. Finally, return to Yerevan. Overnight in Yerevan.

8th day Painting Temple Garni and Mystical Cave Monastery Geghard

Your current path will take you to the pagan temple Garni from the 1st century. The temple is a picturesque monument from the Hellenistic period and bears witness to ancient Armenian architecture. Not far from the temple stands the cave monastery Geghard, which is one of the most important pilgrimage sites in Armenia. The founder is said to be Saint George, patron of the Armenian Apostolic Church. The monastery is also considered a UNESCO World Heritage Site. At lunch at a local house, you will have the opportunity to familiarize yourself with the secrets of the Armenian Lawash bread baking process and the “Tonir”, a traditional barbecue. Finally, return to Yerevan where you will visit the “Matenadaran”, which contains historical documents from Europe and Asia and from many centuries. Matenadaran means library in altarmenic. Of inestimable value are the ancient manuscripts preserved here. The first copy of the Bible and the Gospels also fascinates with their miniatures. Afterwards you will be taken to the hotel, where you can end the evening comfortably. Overnight in Yerevan.

9th day Departure from Armenia

Your time out from everyday life is coming to an end. Even if your holiday is soon over, the precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories of your trip will accompany you forever. Look forward to letting your friends and family share your experiences in the distance at home. Your driver will take you to the airport for your return flight.

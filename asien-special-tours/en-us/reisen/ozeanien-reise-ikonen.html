Australia Journey - Australian Icons

Australia Journey to the impressive icons of Australia. Ask Australia specialist Asia Special Tours now!

Majestic landscapes, endless, secluded beaches, a rich culture and history as well as a unique flora and fauna await visitors to Australia. In Australia, it is the colors that fascinate: glowing red in the heart of the continent and deep blue water on the Great Barrier Reef, visible even from space. Sydney, one of the most famous cities in the world with its natural harbor basin and opera house, lures visitors with historical attractions and modern appearance. This adventure trip to the UNESCO World Heritage Sites is an ideal combination of the highlights of the 5th continent. The program is perfect for independent travelers who do not want to drive themselves, but want to travel individually without a group.

Trip information

Itinerary

15 days
Sydney – Uluru (Ayers Rock) – Alice Springs – Cairns – Palm Cove – Cooktown

1st day Arrival in Sydney

You will be welcomed at the Sydney airport by our guide and taken to the hotel. The rest of the day is at your disposal. Arrive and relax from the long flight journey. We recommend a walk from the Botanical Garden to the Opera House. 3 nights in Sydney

2nd Day City Tour and Port Cruise “Sydneysider Harbour Odyssey”

Your guide welcomes you at the hotel. After a tour of the Mrs. Macquarie’s Point overlooking Opera and the Harbour Bridge, take a morning tour of the City and suburbs of Sydney with its parks and romantic coves. At noon we will take you to the pier at Cockle Bay on Darling Harbour, where you will set off for a cruise through Port Jackson with a small motor catamaran. On board you will find a delicious lunch buffet and drinks. In Darling Harbour, you will arrive in the afternoon 16.30 Arrive again at the clock (return to the hotel on your own).

Day 3 Excursion to the Blue Mountains

Today we head west to the Featherdale Wildlife Park, where you get close to the typical Australian wildlife. Today’s destination is the Blue Mountains (World Heritage Site), a more than 1000 m high sandstone plateau with deep gorges and waterfalls. The name comes from the bluish haze that typically lies over the dense eucalyptus forests of the national park. At the Wentworth Falls, explore the Princess Lookout with fantastic views of the trees. Continue to Leura, where you will visit the Everglades Historical House & Gardens and learn about the history of the house and gardens during a short walk. Afterwards, enjoy your lunch in a traditional restaurant. Continue to Echo Point and visit the famous rock formation “The Three Sisters”. Continue to Blackheath to the Govetts Leap viewpoint at the Grand Canyon. You will visit the Ranger Station at Govetts Leap, where you will learn more about the gorges and the creation of the Blue Mountains. After a day filled with impressions in nature, we go back to Sydney.

4th day visit of Kata-Tjuta with sunset at Uluru

In the morning we will pick you up from the hotel and take you to the airport. After a 3-hour flight, Ayers Rock Airport is located at Uluru in the red heart of the continent. Your driver guide welcomes you at the airport. Today we go to the world-famous rock formations to Kata-Tjuta (Olgas). Consisting of 36 stone domes, this massive can be explored in a 1-2 hour hike through the Valley of Winds. Then drive to Uluru to celebrate the highly anticipated sunset with a traditional glass of sparkling wine. Overnight at Ayers Rock Resort.

Day 5 Sunrise at Uluru and Drive to Kings Canyon

Early in the morning, drive to Uluru to experience the sunrise. We take a tour around the Monolith and visit the award-winning cultural center. After short hikes to waterholes and cave rock paintings of the Aboriginal people, we continue to Kings Canyon. Overnight at Kings Canyon.

Day 6 - Mereenie Loop to Glen Helen

In the morning, take a 2-3-hour hike across the canyon plateau, from the sandstone domes of the “Lost City” to the oasis of the “Garden of Eden”. Then drive on the dusty Mereenie Loop Road through a fantastic desert landscape to Glen Helen. Overnight in Glen Helen.

Day 7: Western MacDonnell Ranges and Alice Springs

After a walk to Glen Helen Gorge, take a 1-2 hour hike through the rough Ormiston Gorge. The journey then continues along the MacDonnell Ranges to Ellery Creek Big Hole and Simpsons Gap. After a short stroll to the waterhole, you will arrive at Alice Springs, where you will visit the historic telegraph station. After the view from ANZAC Hill follows the hotel transfer. Overnight in Alice Springs.

Day 8 Flight from Alice Springs to Cairns

Around noon, your guide will take you to the airport. After about 3 hours you will land in Cairns. Your guide welcomes you and takes you to the hotel in Palm Cove, a small village 20 km north of Cairns on a two-kilometer long sandy beach. 3 nights in Palm Cove.

9th day Kuranda Village

Pick up from the hotel and transfer to the cable car station. Soon they float with the Skyrail over the treetops of the rainforest up into the mountain village of Kuranda, where our guide is waiting for you. Afterwards you will experience the decorated train station in the rainforest, followed by a stroll to the market stalls. The visit to the Rainforest station with the Pamagirri Aboriginal Centre is a highlight in the program. Then take the minibus back down to the coast (if you want, you can also take the historic train back, your driver will pick you up at the station).

Day 10: Great Barrier Reef

Today’s program is without our guide: you will be picked up from the hotel and taken to the marina in Cairns, where you will board a boat for a day trip to the Great Barrier Reef. You can swim on the reef, snorkel or just laze. Lunch, snorkeling equipment and a semi-submarine are included. This excursion is English speaking and unaccompanied!

Day 11 - Quinkan Country to Cooktown

Today, head to the Great Dividing Ranges with a coffee stop at a roasting plant in Mareeba, then an opportunity to spot waterfowl on Lake Mitchell. Continue through the former gold fields on the Palmer River to Split Rock in Quinkan Country to admire the approximately 20,000-year-old Aboriginal rock paintings. In the afternoon you will reach the Rainforest Lodge, your accommodation for 2 nights. Relaxation is trendy or alternatively a stroll in the rainforest. 2 overnight stays near Cooktown in a lodge.

12th day Cooktown

After breakfast, pass Black Mountain to Cooktown, where Captain Cook set the flag for England in 1770. The view from Grassy Hill over the Endeavour River and the Coral Sea is unique. A short drive will take you to Quarantine Beach, idyllic in a beautiful bay. In the afternoon, a visit to the James Cook Museum, a former convent (boarding school). On the way back, visit the famous Lions Den Pub in Helensvale before returning to the lodge.

Day 13: Via Daintree National Park to Palm Cove

After breakfast, leave the oasis in the woods and ride on the rough and dusty Bloomfield Track overlooking the nearby barrier reef to the Bloomfield River. At Cape Tribulation, admire tree giants and mangroves. After crossing by car ferry, you’ll be lucky to see saltwater crocodiles on a boat trip on the Daintree River. From Port Douglas, it goes back on the coral coast to Palm Cove and Cairns. 2 nights of Palm Cove.

Day 14 Cairns

Today you have the opportunity to explore Cairns on your own. The transfer bus will take you to 10.00 Pick up the clock and drop off at Cairns on the esplanade. Swim in the lagoon, visit the markets (until) 16.00 Clock) or the Marlin Marina at the port. In the year 2017 The Cairns Aquarium was reopened with typical marine inhabitants of the Great Barrier Reef and freshwater fishing of the rainforest. In the afternoon, 17.00 At the clock we return by bus to the hotel in Palm Cove. Alternatively, you can also have a beach day today or just relax at the swimming pool.

15th day Departure from Cairns

Today the program ends after breakfast. We will pick you up from the hotel and take you to the airport. Farewell and return or flight.

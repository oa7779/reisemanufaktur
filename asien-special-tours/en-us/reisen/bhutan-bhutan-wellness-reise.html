Treat yourself to the special and set off for the enchanting kingdom of Bhutan. Here you will find a certain untouchedness that will captivate and inspire you from the first moment. The everyday life and beliefs of the Bhutanese are deeply connected with myths and rituals. Experience a deep spirituality and the regeneration of your mind in the land of bliss. You will visit magnificent monasteries and Buddhist pilgrimage sites, nestled in breathtaking natural beauty. Steer your well-being with a hot stone massage. Take a look behind the scenes and experience the lively traditions up close. Walk in the footsteps of Buddha and find your very personal path to happiness.

1st day Arrival in Paro & Yoga hour in Thimphu

Your tour starts in Paro, the only international airport in the country. In good weather, you will have a breathtaking view of the Himalayan Mountains up to Mount Everest when you land. After your landing, you will already be expected and brought to Thimphu by your driver. After a short break in your hotel, your first encounter with Bhutanese culture awaits you: a visit to the Takin Reserve. The Takin – a peculiar goat-like animal – is the national animal of Bhutan. Then visit the National Memorial Chorten, one of the most spiritually significant monuments in the country. Built to commemorate the third king, His Majesty Jigme Dorji Wang-chuck – also known as the “father of modern Bhutan” – the chorten serves as a monument to peace and prosperity. Finish the day with a relaxing stroll through Thimphus craft shops. In the state craft center, as well as in numerous small shops of the city, you will find a wide selection of colorful, hand-woven textiles, traditional masks, carved wooden objects and other ornate handicrafts. Overnight in Thimphu.

2nd day Traditional monk blessing & making your own mandalas

Today it is necessary to get up early, because a special experience awaits you. At a traditional ceremony, you will be blessed by an experienced monk. Afterwards you can enjoy your breakfast to the fullest. In the morning, you will meet a color expert who will report on the spiritual journey of life. After all your questions have been answered, you can watch as the monks start working on their mandalas with colored rock powder. Then it goes on, you drive to the monastery Pangrizampa. Here you have the opportunity to participate in a personal astrological reading. Back in Thimphu, the Institute of Traditional Arts and Crafts awaits you. In the Textile Museum you can convince yourself of the fascinating weaving art made of Bhutanese fabrics. Afterwards, a visit to one of the most impressive landmarks in the country: the Buddha Dordenma. In the evening, the circle of today closes: They visit again the monks who have completed their mandalas in the meantime. At the end of this day, visit Trashi Chho Dzong, the seat of Bhutan’s government and highest religious authorities. After a day full of impressions, a soothing massage awaits you at the end. Overnight in Thimphu.

Day 3 Over the Dochula Pass to Punakha

After breakfast, leave Thimphu and drive towards Punakha. The route takes you at an altitude of 3050 meters over the breathtaking Dochula Pass. The ridge is lined with countless colorful prayer flags that flicker in the high wind. Along the way, stop at Lamperi Recreational National Park, a protected area with fascinating flora and fauna. Finally, you will reach Punakha, which is located in a picturesque valley. In the afternoon you can take a walk around the Temple of Fertility, Chhimi Lhakhang. It goes back to Saint Drukpa Kuenley, who received the nickname “Divine Mad One” for his unorthodox methods of propagate the Buddhist faith – through singing, humor and offensive behavior. He is said to have introduced in Bhutan, among other things, the practice of painting phallic symbols on the walls of the house to drive away evil spirits – this custom you will see many times on your way to the sanctuary. The pilgrims who visit the monastery are traditionally hit on the head with a 25 centimeter long wooden phallus. After an eventful day, a soothing traditional “hot stone” massage awaits you. Overnight in Punakha. 

Day 4 Unique Monastery Fortress & Visit to Nalanda Monastery School

Today you will take a small hike through rice, chilli and cabbage fields to the Chörten Khamsum Yuelley Namgyal, which was built by the Queen and stands for peace and stability in a fast-paced time. You will also visit the Dzong of Punakha, a unique monastic fortress located at the confluence of the Po Chu and Mo Chu, popularly known as the “Palace of Happiness”. During a picnic, you can fortify yourself a little and enjoy the view of the Dzong of Punakha. In the late afternoon, visit the Sangchen Dorji Lhundrup Chholing Monastery, perched on a hill above the Punakha Valley. This spiritual place is inhabited by a community of Buddhist nuns – here you have the opportunity to talk to some of them. Next to the temple is an ornately designed stupa, as well as numerous slate carvings depicting religious figures and scenes – filigree masterpieces of traditional craftsmanship. Overnight in Punakha. 

Day 5 Return to Paro & Spiritual Butterlamp Ceremony

After breakfast, we return to the starting point of your trip to Paro. Along the way, enjoy a traditional lunch at a farm over 300 years old. You will also visit the Shrine of Kyichu Lhakhang, a complex consisting of two identical temples built on top of a ledge. Around the sanctuary, countless colorful prayer flags blow in the wind. A very special experience awaits you afterwards: in a small temple you light untough butter lamps and are accompanied by a monk. The butter lamp represents the dispersion of darkness and uncertainty. Enjoy the spiritual moment and let your mind run wild. At the end of the day, visit Bhutan’s National Museum, housed in the former Ta Dzong Watchtower above Paro Dzong. The striking round building from 1649 impresses with up to 2.5 meters thick walls and reminds in its form of a shell. After the 2011 earthquake, the museum was extensively restored and reopened in 2019 as the most important museum in the country. Overnight in Paro.

Day 6 Hike to the Tigernest

Look forward to today, because an absolute high point is before you. You will take a hike through Bhutan’s impressive mountain world. Your destination is the famous “Tigernest Monastery” (Taktshang), which – 900 meters above the valley – nestles against the rugged rock faces of the Himalayas. The name comes from a legend that the second Buddha, Guru Padmasambhava, flew here to meditate on the back of a tigress. The place is a famous pilgrimage site for Buddhists and one of the most sacred places in the Himalayas. Once you have arrived at the top, enjoy the dreamlike view of the valley in front of you. Overnight in Paro.

Day 7 Departure from Paro

Today your spiritual journey through the fascinating Kingdom of Bhutan ends. Richer with many precious memories, you will now take your return flight.

Are you a passionate golfer and would like to pursue your hobby when traveling? Then this trip is made for you. You will not only experience the most spectacular golf courses in Java, but also immerse yourself in the cultural fascination of Java. The enchanting temple complexes Borobudur and Prambanan, the contact to the hospitable locals and the beautiful landscapes will delight you. As a crowning conclusion, a bathing stay of the extra class awaits you. Feel like Robinson Crusoe and experience the feeling of seclusion – at Kura Kura Resort you will be able to do so! Let your soul really dangle and hear only the sound of the sea.

1st day Arrival in Singapore

Welcome to Singapore. Upon arrival, you will be expected by your chauffeur and taken to your hotel. Relax a little from your arrival or go on a first exploration tour. In the evening you will be picked up by your guide. Take a ride on a traditional bumboat along the Singapore River to discover Singapore’s historical roots. This tour offers the ideal opportunity to understand the central importance of the river for the development of Singapore as a port city. Historic buildings come in exciting contrast to the imposing skyscrapers of the present day. At the end, you will find the majestic Merlion, the legendary creature of lions and fish that guards the estuary and is known worldwide as the iconic symbol of Singapore. At the end of the day, a delicious seafood dinner awaits you before you are brought back to the hotel. Overnight in Singapore.

2nd Day City Tour with Little India, Chinatown and Dim Sum Lunch

After breakfast, a half-day guided tour will give you the opportunity to explore the city-state of Singapore. Drive through the historic Civic District, past the Padang, the historic Parliament Building, City Hall and Supreme Court. Continue to Singapore’s landmark, the Merlion. Then visit Kampong Glam, where the Sultans Mosque’s golden dome shines and Arab Street delights with Oriental flair and colorful shops. A technical masterpiece awaits you when you visit Sands Sky Park – high above the city’s rooftops, you will float 200 meters above sea level and look out over futuristic Singapore. Pass the Financial District and drive to Chinatown. Enjoy a traditional lunch, a dim sum lunch where many Chinese treats are served in small bamboo baskets. The afternoon is at your disposal. Overnight in Singapore.

Day 3rd Flight to Yogyakarta and Leisure

Today it is called packing suitcases. After an extensive breakfast, you will be picked up by your chauffeur and driven to the airport. Then fly on to Yogyakarta on Java. The island is the smallest but at the same time the most important of the Great Sunda Islands. Your guide is already waiting for you and accompanies you to your hotel. The rest of the day is at your disposal. Enjoy your tasteful hotel complex in a first-class location to the city center. Overnight stay in Yogyakarta

4th day Sultan City Yogyakarta & the thousand temples of Prambanan

After breakfast, you will discover together with your guide the sultan city of Yogyakarta. Stroll through hidden alleys to the Kraton (Sultan’s Palace) and immerse yourself in the heart of the city. Contrary to expectations, the arrangement follows, the at first glance very confusing plant, strict cosmological ideas. You will also visit the resort’s Taman Sari pleasure castle, after which you will make your way to the Hindu Prambanan Temple. The three main temples of this complex are dedicated to the gods Shiva, the destroyer, Vishnu, the keeper, and Brahma, the creator, thus representing the Hindu trinity. After admiring the beauty of the temple in peace, head back to the hotel where you can end the day in peace. Overnight stay in Yogyakarta

5th day Merapi golf course and onwards to Borobudur

It's tea time. On one of Java’s most spectacular golf courses, you can swing your golf club today. Merapi’s 18-hole golf course extends at 6,370 meters with enchanting views of the Merapi Volcano. Each hole of the golf course has been carefully placed and is therefore considered a special challenge. Then continue to your hotel, from where you can look directly at the Temple of Borobudur. Enjoy your pretty boutique hotel in this peaceful and tranquil environment. Overnight stay in Borobudur

Day 6 sunrise over Borobudur and onwards to Magelang

Today your day begins before dawn. From Dagi Hill, watch as the first rays of the sun dip the mighty Borobudur Temple into a golden light. The clear morning air, the picnic breakfast and the quiet atmosphere give this moment an almost meditative power. Afterwards, you will embark on an in-depth exploration of the impressive temple complex. On the way back, stop at the smaller temples of Pawon and Mendut, which together with Borobudur form a cultural three-star. After the check-out, continue towards Mount Sumbing, where you will take a leisurely walk through lush green rice fields and charming villages. The goal is the secluded Selogriyo Temple – a modest building whose quiet location in the midst of nature makes its special appeal. In the afternoon, visit the OHD Museum in Magelang, which opens up new perspectives with its impressive collection of modern Indonesian art. Then your driver will take you to your next hotel. Surrounded by eight volcanoes, tropical landscapes and located in the middle of a coffee plantation, it is a true refuge for body and soul. The colonial-style complex also includes historic buildings such as the original house of the plantation owner from 1828 and a former train station building. Overnight in Magelang.

7th day Visit to a coffee plantation & relaxation in Magelang

After breakfast, visit the local coffee plantations. You get to know the differences between Robusta and Arabica coffee, get insights into the roasting of the beans and can of course enjoy a delicious cup of coffee at the end. The rest of the day is at your disposal. Take the time to play a round of golf or explore the region on your own. Enjoy this oasis of peace and relaxation! Overnight in Magelang.

8th day railway history

After a leisurely breakfast in the hotel, it is time to say goodbye to your oasis. Together with your guide, visit the Ambarawa Railway Museum, built in 1873 during the Dutch colonial period by King Willem I. The museum houses 21 steam locomotives, four of which are still in operation, as well as historical collections such as old telephones, telegraphs and signalling equipment. Finally, continue on to Semarang. The journey leads past lush greenery, small villages and rolling hills – a final opportunity to enjoy the scenic diversity of central Java. Look forward to new impressions in a city that combines colonial heritage and modern dynamism in its own unique way. Overnight in Semarang.

9th day flight to Karimun Jawa and boat transfer to Kura Kura Resort

After a few eventful days, a bathing stay of the extra class is now on the program. They are taken to the airport and fly to the main island of Karimun Jawa. Afterwards, take the speedboat to the Kura Kura Resort. The resort is located in the Karimun Jawa archipelago and covers up to 27 islands. The long journey is worthwhile, because you are waiting for a tropical paradise with coconut trees, turquoise water and white sand beaches - as in a picture book. Kura Kura Resort consists of 18 luxurious pool villas and 15 cottages. The warm service of the hotel staff and the peaceful atmosphere enchant every seeker. Overnight at Kura Kura Resort.
Note: The flight to Karimun Jawa takes place only on certain days. If desired, the aircraft can also be chartered. Kura Kura Resort is open from April to November.

10-14. Day holiday paradise Kura Kura Resort

Without guidance

“Between heaven and earth” – these days belong to you alone. Determine your own rhythm and let yourself drift. Spend a day like Robinson Crusoe on an uninhabited island, paddle over the crystal clear waters with a kayak and marvel at the colorful coral reefs with their countless sea creatures on a snorkeling trip. Forget your everyday life and immerse yourself in your very personal dream world. Overnight at Kura Kura Resort.

15th day Departure from Kura Kura Resort

Today, unfortunately, it means saying goodbye again, even if it is difficult for you to leave this magical place. In the afternoon, you will take the speedboat and plane back to Semarang on Java, where you will take your home or onward flight. Even if your holiday is soon over – the precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories of your trip will accompany you forever. Look forward to letting your friends and family share your experiences in the distance at home.

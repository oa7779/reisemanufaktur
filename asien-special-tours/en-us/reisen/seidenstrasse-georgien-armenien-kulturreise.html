Georgia & Armenia Travel - Mystical Mountain Worlds and Captivating Culture

Armenia Georgia Travel - Experience the mystical mountain worlds and captivating cultures on this individual Silk Road journey from Asia Special Tours.

Are you looking for a very special trip away from any tourist flows? Then travel the two enchanting countries of Georgia and Armenia, nestled in a dreamlike mountain backdrop between the majestic Ararat and the snow-capped peaks of the Caucasus. Walk in Georgia, the “Balcon of Europe” in the footsteps of over 3000 years of history and visit in Armenia, the oldest Christian country on earth, impressive monasteries in breathtaking mountain scenery. On your tour, you will not only experience the history of the peoples, but also get to know the modern culture in the emerging metropolises of Tbilisi and Yerevan. Deep insights into everyday life and culinary delicacies round off this varied journey. Enjoy countless highlights in two still largely undiscovered countries!

Day 1 Welcome to Georgia & Tbilisi City Tour

Welcome to Georgia. Upon arrival in Tbilisi, you will be expected at the airport and taken to the hotel, where you can immediately move into your room and sleep in. Have breakfast and let the day go on comfortably. Then your guide will pick you up for your city tour. The bustling capital has many sights to offer. All the interesting architectural monuments in the old town are close together, so they can be easily reached by a short walk. First, the Metechi Church from the 13th century is on the program. Then your guide will show you the equestrian statue of the city founder Wachtang Gorgassali. From here you have a great view of the Mtkwari River, the old town and the surroundings of Tbilisi. Then continue past the sulfur baths to the 4th century Nariqala fortress. In addition, on the program: the synagogue, the main church, the Sioni Cathedral with the vine cross of Saint Nino and the oldest church in the city, the Antschischati Church. At the end of the day, enjoy delicious delicacies in a traditional restaurant and taste the varied Georgian cuisine. Overnight in Tbilisi.

2nd day nunnery Bodbe & Picturesque town Signagi

In the morning you will drive to the nunnery Bodbe (4.-8.) This is where the Apostle Nino – she Christianized Georgia – is buried. Then look forward to the picturesque town of Signagi, which invites you for a walk. Signagi is located in the heart of Kachetien and perched above the infinite Alasani plain. The narrow alleys and beautiful wooden balconies also contribute to the naming and create an unforgettable atmosphere. The old city wall is also very well preserved. Pay particular attention to the ensemble of houses built in classical-South Italian style, combined with typical Georgian elements! In the late afternoon you will reach Tsinandali and get a very exclusive insight into a princely house from the 19th century. Overnight in Tsinandali.

Day 3 Religious Insights and the Art of Winemaking

Today your journey will take you to Telavi, where you can stroll comfortably over a farmers market and observe the local life very well. Then visit the Cathedral of Alaverdi (11th). century) and the fortified church of Gremi (16. century. The Alaverdi Cathedral – or Church of George – is not only the highest, but also the most delicate church in Georgia. And it works only through these features – the builders have renounced elaborate jewelry. How about a glimpse into Georgia’s culinary treasures? When visiting a winery, you will taste both traditional Georgian and European wines and learn about the country’s millennia-old winemaking tradition. For lunch, you will be hosted by a local family and enjoy a traditional Georgian meal with fresh ingredients from your own garden. Take a seat at the table of your hosts, taste food prepared according to family recipes and experience the Georgian hospitality and way of life up close. In the afternoon, a traditional wine cellar awaits you, where you can taste more Georgian wines. Overnight in Tsinandali.

Day 4 Traditional Crafts in Tbilisi

Start the day comfortably! Via the Gombori Pass you will continue to Tbilisi, where you will learn more about the crafts of goldsmiths. Stroll through the old town and let the Georgian capital have an effect on you. Overnight in Tbilisi.

5th Day Ancient Mzecheta & Mysterious Cave Town Uplisziche

Today, Tbilisi leave again and continue to Mzcheta. The time-honored, former capital of Georgia and religious center of the country is definitely worth a visit. Visit the Dschwari Church (6th century) and the Swetiz-Choveli Cathedral (11th century). century. This masterpiece of Old Georgian architecture owes its name to the “life-giving tribe” to a legend about the body coat of Christ. A resident of Mzcheta brought the shirt of Christ to Mzcheta after the crucifixion. At the place where it was buried, a tree with a trunk sprouted up, which donated a healing balm. This is where the church was built. Afterwards you will drive along the old Silk Road to the Kartli region and reach Gori, the birthplace of Stalin. There you will first visit his birthplace (from the outside) and then continue to the cave town Uplisziche, which was founded in the 1st century BC and through which a branch of the legendary Silk Road led. Do you recognize the city structure with the many streets, the theater and the various palaces? In the afternoon you will reach the province of Imereti in West Georgia. Welcome to the land of the Golden Fleece! Overnight in Kutaissi.

6th Day Impressive Gelati Monastery & Panorama in Swanetia

After breakfast, you will get to know Kutaissi more closely on a short city tour. Then visit the idyllic Motsameta Monastery from the 11th century, which offers you a beautiful view into the deep gorge. Then set off for Svanetia, a stunning mountainous region with ice-crowned peaks and the most unusual traditions. On the way to Mestia you will visit the Enguri Dam, which with a width of 750 meters is one of the most impressive buildings in the Caucasus. Overnight in Mestia.

7th day walk in the village of Uschguli & lunch with locals

Start the day early, because one of the highlights of your trip is coming! You drive to Uschguli (2400m), the highest permanently populated place in Europe. In this place, high mountains, fortified towers and ancient traditions combine to form a total work of art. In Uschguli you will take a walk through the village and can let the special atmosphere work in peace. You will visit the Lamaria Church (12th century) with the shchara (5068m), the highest mountain in Georgia, in the background. Enjoy a lunch with a local family where you will learn many exciting stories about this special place. In the evening, return to Mestia. Overnight in Mestia.

8th day return to Kutaissi and free time

From Mestia we return to Kutaissi. Upon arrival, visit the 11th-century Bagrati Cathedral, perched high above the city and one of the most important landmarks in the region. The rest of the day you can design according to your own wishes. Relax a little and let the many impressions of the last few days affect you. Or take a leisurely stroll through the streets of Georgia’s third largest city and let yourself drift. Overnight in Kutaissi.

Day 9 On the way to Borjomi

After breakfast, continue towards Achalziche. On the way there you will visit the Ubissa Church. In the simple church you can discover some of the most beautiful frescoes in Georgia. Then you reach the well-known spa town of Borjomi. During a walk through the sprawling Borjomi Park, you will visit the famous mineral water source and have the opportunity to taste the water directly on site. The afternoon is at your disposal – enjoy the relaxed atmosphere of the park or take a ride on the cable car and let the view wander over the surrounding countryside. Overnight in Borjomi.

10th day Cave town Wardsia & border crossing in Bavra

A new day, a new goal! You leave Borjomi and continue south. When visiting the cave city of Wardsia, you can see how 3,000 cave apartments could provide shelter to 50,000 people. It was built in the golden age of Georgia in the 12th century. You will find artistically decorated rooms, connected by long corridors. These were carved out of the rock by human hands over 800 years ago. The ambience is further enhanced by dripping ice-cold water from the bare rock. In the afternoon, you will say goodbye to your Georgian guide, because in Bavra you will cross the border into Armenia. Your Armenian guide is already waiting for you and you will continue to Gyumri. On a small sightseeing tour you can get to know the second largest city in Armenia. Overnight in Gyumri.

11th day Experience tradition in Fioletowo & enchanting spa town Dilijan

Today you will drive to the village of Fioletovo, where the early Christians are located. Over a cup of tea at a local house, you can learn about the village’s inhabitants who call themselves “Molokanan”. Immerse yourself in the customs and customs that the minority continues to maintain today! Finally, you will reach Dilijan, a famous spa town in Armenia. In Armenia it is said: "If Paradise had mountains, forests and mineral springs, it would be like Dilijan". Due to its natural beauty, it is often referred to as “little Armenian Switzerland”. Together with your guide, you will take a tour of the old town district of Dilijan. Overnight in Dilijan.

12th day Sevansee “the blue pearl of Armenia”

After a leisurely breakfast, continue on the Selimpass towards Lake Sevan. This huge lake makes up five percent of Armenia’s land area, is 2,000 meters above sea level and is the second highest freshwater lake in the world. On a peninsula there is a monastery that you can visit. Afterwards we will continue to Goris, in the southeast of Armenia. Overnight in Goris.

13th day Exhilarating drive to Tatev Monastery

The cable car is going high today! It is also known as the “wing of Tatev”, a whopping 5.7 kilometers long and opened in 2010. Enjoy the ride, which saves you a thirty-minute winding car ride on narrow roads. The cable car takes you to the medieval monastery Tatev, which is located in the village of the same name in the district of Sjunik. The monastery was built in the 10th century and resembles an architectural masterpiece. It has a turbulent history behind it, was home to poor monks and once owned hundreds of villages, was destroyed and rebuilt. Below, the Vorotan rushes and supports the flair of this secluded place. After you have strengthened yourself from the many impressions over lunch, continue to Jermuk, where you will stay overnight. Overnight in Jermuk.

Day 14 Visit two pieces of jewelry - Noravank Monastery and Virap Choir

A day full of highlights is coming. First of all, we head to the Amaghu gorge, where perhaps the most beautiful Armenian monastery, Noravank Monastery, stands. With its red facade, it is hardly distinguishable from its rocky background at sunset. From here, continue to the Monastery of Choir Virap, located at the foot of Mount Ararat. No monastery in Armenia is so strongly associated with the legend of St. Grigor as this one. According to legend, he is said to have introduced Christianity in Armenia and is thus considered the first Armenian Catholic. Then finally you reach the last stop of your enchanting journey. Welcome to Yerevan, Armenia’s vibrant capital. How about a leisurely evening walk through the busy streets of the city? Overnight in Yerevan.

15th day Painting Temple Garni & Mystical Cave Monastery Geghard

Your current path will take you to the pagan temple Garni from the 1st century. The temple is a picturesque monument from the Hellenistic period and bears witness to ancient Armenian architecture. Not far from the temple stands the cave monastery Geghard, which is one of the most important pilgrimage sites in Armenia. The founder is said to be Saint George, patron of the Armenian Apostolic Church. The monastery is also considered a UNESCO World Heritage Site. At lunch at a local house, you will have the opportunity to familiarize yourself with the secrets of the Armenian Lawash bread baking process and the “Tonir”, a traditional barbecue. Finally, return to Jerevan where you will visit the “Matenadaran”, which contains historical documents from Europe and Asia and from many centuries. Matenadaran means library in Old Armenian. Of inestimable value are the ancient manuscripts preserved here. The first copy of the Bible and the Gospels also fascinates with their miniatures. Afterwards you will be taken to the hotel, where you can end the evening comfortably. Overnight in Yerevan.

16th day Exciting City Tour of Yerevan

After strengthening yourself with an extensive breakfast, today you will dedicate yourself to the capital of Armenia and take a city tour of Yerevan. First you will get to know the Republic Square, which was built in traditional Armenian architecture. There you will visit the Historical Museum, where important archaeological collections from the Stone Age to the Middle Ages are on display. In front of the museum is a large fountain, also known as the “Singing Fountain”. Afterwards you will visit the Government House and the Ministry of the Federal Foreign Office. Then you will dedicate yourself to the central post office and the national gallery of paintings. Finally, visit the Zizernakaberd, a hill on which the memorial and the museum for the victims of the genocide of Armenians in 1915 are located. After your return to the hotel, you can let the many impressions affect you. Overnight in Yerevan.

Day 17 A day in Ashtarak & Brandy tasting in Yerevan

Today you will take an excursion to Ashtarak. In the center of the city of Ashtarak (in German “tower”) stands the small family church Karmravor with a tile roof from the seventh century. It measures just seven square meters – incredible that this building even has a dome! Low houses and outstanding church towers characterize the entire townscape. Also worth visiting two other church complexes near the city: Saghmosavank and Hovhannavank, which are among the most beautiful monasteries of the Middle Ages. After a tasty lunch, on the way back to Yerevan, you will see the unique red-gray striped and richly decorated church of St. George Mughni, which dates back to the 17th century. Back in Yerevan, you will visit a factory where brandy is produced. Learn more about the interesting ripening process and the secrets of storage. Convince yourself and try the much-praised Armenian brandy! In a relaxed atmosphere, you will end the evening and toast an unforgettable journey through the enchanting Caucasus with its captivating culture.

18th day Departure from Yerevan

Your time out from everyday life is coming to an end. Even if your holiday is soon over, the precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories of your trip will accompany you forever. Look forward to letting your friends and family share your experiences in the distance at home. Your driver will take you to the airport for your return flight to Yerevan.

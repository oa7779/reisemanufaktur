Malaysia Travel - Experience Colorful Diversity

On this trip you will get to know West Malaysia with all its facets. Thanks to its various cultural influences, the country is an ideal destination, especially for Asian newcomers. Immerse yourself in the hustle and bustle of the captivating metropolis Kuala Lumpur and enjoy the enchanting nature in the Cameron Highlands as a balancing pole of calm. Here you will also get authentic insights into tea production when visiting a tea plantation. Also the dreamlike view from the highest mountain in the region must not be missed! A special highlight is also the charming island of Penang, which you can explore by bike. After a few wonderful days of pure relaxation on the beach of Dungun, you finally make your way home with many exciting impressions in your luggage and well recovered. An all-round successful journey that will make you feel like exploring the exciting continent of Asia.

1st day start of your journey

Welcome to Malaysia! Your journey begins in the capital Kuala Lumpur, where you will be welcomed and warmly greeted. Together you drive to your hotel. Depending on the arrival time, you can start with first sightseeings or rest a little at the hotel. Kuala Lumpur is a diverse city due to its diverse cultural influences – Malaysians, Chinese gold prospectors, Indian immigrants and British colonialists: Modern shopping malls can be found here as well as colorful market stalls, Chinatown as well as Little India. Overnight in Kuala Lumpur.

Day 2 Vibrant metropolis Kuala Lumpur

After breakfast, enjoy a half-day city tour where you will get to know the most beautiful sides of Kuala Lumpur. A visit to the metropolis is best started with the legendary Petronas twin towers. Continue to the Royal Palace, which is also a sought-after photo motif. Also on your sightseeing program is the National Monument, which was erected in memory of the fallen soldiers of the Malay freedom struggle. You will drive past the National Mosque and the old railway station building to Independence Square. Here you will also see the Sultan Abdul Samad building, known for its elaborate architecture. You can arrange the afternoon according to your own ideas. Around half-past seven, set off on an exciting tour of the evening Kuala Lumpur with its dazzling skyscrapers and lively afterlife. Your exploration will take you to the night market of Chinatown and the Sri Mahamariamman Temple. After a dinner of typical Malaysian specialties, a cultural performance will provide you with a taste of Malaysian art forms. Overnight in Kuala Lumpur.
Note: Please note that the National Mosque cannot be visited on Friday.

3rd Day Legendary Batu Caves

The next destination on your trip is the green Cameron Highlands. Along the way, stop at the famous Batu caves, an important Hindu sanctuary. A staircase with 272 steps leads to this large, natural limestone cave. Watch the followers pay homage to their respective deities! Continue along the winding road to the Cameron Highlands, about 1,500 meters above sea level. The colonial rulers already appreciated the fresh mountain air as a change from the tropical heat in the flatlands. Here it is not only pleasantly cool, you also have a wonderful view of the lush green hillside landscape with its fertile tea plantations, small villages and waterfalls. In addition, many ethnic tribes still live in the local forests today. After your arrival, the rest of the day is freely available to you. Overnight in the Cameron Highlands.

Day 4: It's Tea-Time in the Cameron Highlands

Fresh and well-strengthened, climb into Land Rover and ride along the scenic hilly tea plantations towards Gunung Brinchang. With a height of 2,000 meters, it is the highest mountain in the Cameron Highlands and offers you the opportunity to admire the surrounding landscapes from a bird’s eye view. Walking through the moss-covered, ancient forest, your guide will show you some of the most exotic plants in the world such as pot plants, orchids, ferns, spices and medicinal plants. You will be amazed at the diversity you will discover here. But no visit to the Cameron Highlands would be complete without a visit to the famous tea plantations. You will drive down the mountain again and visit a tea factory to learn more about the process of making tea. Of course, you also have to take the opportunity and try a cup of tea. The view over the wonderful surrounding tea plantations is simply enchantingly beautiful and rounds off your day perfectly. With these wonderful impressions you will be brought back to your hotel. Overnight in the Cameron Highlands.

Day 5 Impressive Cave Temple & Drive through Mangrove Forest

A new day, a new goal. On the way to Penang, take a trip to the cave temple of Sam Poh Tong, which is near the village of Ipoh. It is one of the largest of its kind in the whole country. The entrance is decorated with colorful statures and portals, in the interior of the cave between impressive stalakmites and stalactites there are numerous Buddha statues as well as faithful Buddhists who are immersed in meditation. On the way there is also the city of Kuala Kangsar, whose beautiful mosque is worth a detour. Another highlight of the place is the Iskandariah Palace. Afterwards you will continue to Kuala Sepetang, where you will set off for a mangrove ride. They follow the waterways used by local fishermen for their journey to the open sea. With a little luck, you can see dolphins. Sit back and watch the daily lives of fishermen harvesting cockle hearts and running their fish farms. From here we continue to Penang. Overnight in Penang.

Day 6 - Bicycle through Penang

Today begins actively! In the morning, set out to explore the UNESCO city of Georgetown with its World Heritage sites, traditional trading sites and Penang’s famous Hawker Food on two wheels. The entire bike ride takes about 3.5 hours. Once you have returned your wheels, your guide will take you to the largest Buddhist temple in this region because no visit to Penang is truly complete without it. The Kek Lok Si Temple is located in the center of Penang Island in a village called Air Hitam (Black Water) and is also known as the Pagoda of the Ten Thousand Buddhas, or the Monastery of Highest Bliss. The striking architecture is a mix of Thai, Burmese, Indian and Chinese elements, and every nook and corner is a photographer’s paradise. After a short walk you will reach Penang Hill, the highest point of the island. With the cable car you can float up the hill and enjoy a fantastic panoramic view of Georgetown and the mainland. While you’re at it, try to discover the two Penang Bridges! After the tour, we return to the hotel. Overnight in Penang.

Day 7 From the West to the East Coast

Today is a travel day ahead of you. After breakfast, drive to the east coast of Malaysia, Kota Bharu. The journey is about seven hours depending on the traffic. Enjoy the journey through the varied landscape. After check-in at the hotel, the rest of the day is at your disposal. Kota Bharu is the royal capital of Kelantan State. Translated from Malay, the name of the city means as much as "The New Fortress". You will quickly notice how it comes to this magnificent name. In addition to many Thai-style Buddhist temples, mosques and museums as well as the unique architecture of the Sultan’s Palace, you will find other treasures worth seeing in the city center. Overnight in Kota Bharu.

8th day Land and people around Kota Bharu

After a leisurely breakfast, take an exploration tour that also takes you to some sights outside of the city. For example, the production of the flying kites is explained to you. Then visit a fishing village where you can learn more about the country and its people. The locals like to show you their, passed down through generations, fishing methods. When visiting a British bunker from the 2World War II, learn more about the historical facts of Malaysia. Finally, you will go to the famous market Siti Kaddijah. Let yourself be carried away by the busy activity and try your luck in haggling before you are brought to Dungun in your dreamlike hotel for the next few days. Overnight in Dungun.

9.-11. Day Recreation and pure relaxation

Without tour guide and chauffeur

After the varied impressions on your trip so far, the motto for the coming days is rest and relaxation on the dreamlike beach. Let us review the journey again. Your resort - the Tanjong Jara - offers perfect conditions: it is located directly on the long, sandy beach near the village of Dungun. There are no restaurants or shops in the immediate vicinity. The idyllic resort is modeled on the style of 17th century Malaysian royalty. Due to its proximity to nature, it is the ideal place to reconcile body and mind. Your accommodation has two restaurants, a bar and two swimming pools. Overnight in Dungun.

12th day Farewell to Malaysia

Many eventful days with countless and unforgettable impressions are behind you. Your journey through diverse Malaysia is now coming to an end, and the time until your departure is at your disposal. Enjoy an extensive breakfast and review the last days. Then your driver will take you to the airport, where you will start your journey home. Would you like to extend your time out on the beach or get to know the fascinating wildlife of Borneo? We are happy to make you an offer.

It's Tee Time - Golf Tour of Southern China

China Golf Tour: It's Tee Time - Golf Tour of the South of China with your expert Asia Special Tours. Here directly inquiries and individual advice.

Itinerary & services

English itinerary text is an offline machine translation of the German original for this team preview. Please confirm travel details with your advisor; the German source remains available through the language switch.

Do you play golf and would like to pursue this passion when traveling? Then we have just the right thing for you! This trip will take you to some of the most beautiful golf courses in China, where you can indulge your hobby to your heart’s content. Play on the world’s largest golf resort – Mission Hills Resort in Shenzhen – walk in the footsteps of some of the most famous golf legends and play the only par 6 hole in China. But not only the sensational golf courses will make your heart beat faster. The magical province of Yunnan also lures with a magnificent landscape, fantastic views during the game and a diverse culture. Stroll through Lijiang, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, visit the stone forest and get fascinating insights into an important episode of Chinese colonial history. Finally, your trip will take you to the tropical island of Hainan. After the daily rounds on the most popular of the 16 courses that the island has to offer, the rest of the day is at your disposal to enjoy yourself.

1st Day True Golfer Paradise: The Mission Hills Resort

Welcome to China! The starting point of your journey in the footsteps of golf legends is Hong Kong, from where you will immediately travel to Shenzhen. The city is located in the south of the Chinese province of Guangdong and borders the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region. Shenzhen is one of the fastest growing cities in the world and is an important city for foreign investors due to its status as a special economic zone. The city also has a lot to offer you as a golfer: with the Mission Hills Golf Club, the largest golf course in the world has been built in Shenzhen. The World Cup of Professional Golfers has been held here since 2007. You’ll spend the next three nights at Mission Hills Resort, China’s only five-star resort integrated into a golf course. Each of the more than 500 beautiful rooms and suites represents its own interpretation of a tropical golf and leisure paradise. In addition to the 12 world-class 18-hole courses, the resort offers four clubhouses, Asia’s largest spa oasis, 51 tennis courts with professional coaches and some fine dining restaurants that will bring you true taste buds. At Mission Hills Resort, you’ll enjoy comfort and luxury at its finest. Overnight in Shenzhen.

Day 2 First round on the course of your favorite golfer

Today, the first round is already waiting for you on one of the 12 golf courses of the Mission Hills Golf Club. With this impressive number, the golf resort has secured an entry in the Guinness Book of Records. Each of the courses was personally designed by one of the most famous golf legends in the world. When selecting the architects, the resort has carefully taken care to hire at least one designer from each continent. The result is 12 unique courses that promise you golfing at the highest level. Surely the choice of your first place will not be easy for you. It is best to choose the one of your favorite golfer. The square architects are: Jack Nicklaus, David Duval, David Leadbetter, José Maria Olazábal, Nick Faldo, Annika Sörenstam, Jumbo Ozaki, Vijay Singh, Ernie Els, Greg Norman, Peter Dye and Zhang Lianwei. Starting times can be guaranteed on eight golf courses (Norman and World Cup Course on request, Pete Dye is only accessible to “Premium” members and Zhang Lianwei is an 18 hole par 3 course). Overnight in Shenzhen.

Day 3 To a second round of golf at Mission Hills

After breakfast, your second round is on the agenda at one of the magnificent seats. Four 18-hole golf courses and the Par 3 course are located right next to the Golf Hotel (World Cup Course, Els Course, Vijay Course, Ozaki Course). Two of them can also be recorded in the evening thanks to floodlights. For five golf courses (Leadbetter Course, Norman Course, Duval Course, Olazábal Course and Annika Course) registration is made at the Dongguan Clubhouse, to which a shuttle bus from the hotel and back every 15 minutes from 06:00 in the morning. The journey time is about 20 minutes. The Faldo Course and the Pete Dye Course (only for premium members) can also be reached by shuttle in 15 minutes. The bus runs every 30 minutes. Also here there is a clubhouse and a very nice restaurant. Overnight in Shenzhen.

Day 4: Last round in Shenzhen and on to Lijiang

Today you play your last round on one of the courses at Mission Hills Resort. Afterwards, you will be taken to the airport and travel on to Lijiang by domestic flight. The city at 2,500 meters altitude is a UNESCO World Heritage Site. If you imagine it without tourists, it would truly be a journey back in time to the past century. Upon arrival in Lijiang, you will be expected by your tour guide and taken to your hotel. The next three nights will be spent at the Hotel Indigo Lijiang Ancient Town, located on the strategic south portal of the former tea road, which was driven by horse caravans. In this hotel, the culture of caravanserai blends with local folklore to create an appealing decor and design. The rooms are furnished with characteristic works of art and creative unique pieces. In the charming restaurant, which looks like it lies among snow-capped mountains, you can enjoy authentic dishes that reflect the Naxi tradition. The “Me Space” with gigantic copper bird cages and seating from real horse saddles invites you to linger. If you wish, you can also attend a tea ceremony at the hotel’s teahouse. Overnight in Lijiang.

Day 5 Excursion to Tiger Jumping Gorge

After spending the last few days playing golf, take a look into one of the deepest gorges on earth: the Tiger Jumping Gorge! The gorge is an impressive 3,900 meters deep and about 16 kilometers long, but in some places it measures only 10 meters wide. According to legend, a tiger is said to have skipped the gorge here long ago to escape its hunters. Almost vertically, deep down to the Jinshajiang (River of the Golden Sands), the cliffs hang threateningly over the raging water. Overnight in Lijiang.

6th day of golfing and discoveries in Lijiang Old Town

In the morning, a round of golf awaits you at Lijiang Ancient City Lakeside Golf Course, a challenging par-72 course that stretches for an impressive 7 km. Nestled in the breathtaking scenery around Lijiang, this golf course offers not only a sporting challenge but also magnificent views of the surrounding mountains and scenic lake. The course is known for its carefully designed fairways and greens that blend harmoniously into the natural environment. After a morning on the golf course, in the afternoon you will have the opportunity to explore the historic old town of Lijiang. With its cobbled alleys, traditional wooden houses and charming bridges, the old town invites you to take a leisurely stroll. The old town, which has been designated a World Heritage Site by UNESCO, with its winding streets, lively squares and many small shops, exudes a special atmosphere that invites you to discover and linger. Overnight in Lijiang. 

7th day Gigantic mountain panorama of the Jadedrachen snow mountain

Lijiang has another world-class golf course that you can enjoy today: the Lijiang Jade Dragon Snow Mountain Golf Course designed by China’s No. 1 golfer Zhang Lianwei with fantastic views of the Jade Dragon Snow Mountain. This golf course has also earned an entry in the Guinness Book of Records as currently the longest par-72, 18-hole course worldwide. With its location at an altitude of 3,100 meters, it is also the highest golf course in the world. Due to the altitude, the air is thinner here, which makes the ball fly about 20% further and makes the teeing a very special experience, which is deepened by the surrounding, gigantic mountain landscape. Playing golf here is fun, challenging and at the same time relaxing! On the way back to Lijiang you will visit the former residence of the Austrian-American researcher, geographer, linguist and botanist Joseph Francis Rock (or Joseph Franz Karl Rock). His former home is now a museum, which exhibits images of Lijiang in the 1920s to 1940s, partly photographed by Rock himself. Overnight in Lijiang.

8th day onwards to Kunming

Now leave Lijiang behind and travel by plane to Kunming. Upon arrival, you will be taken to your hotel. Spring City Resort is one of the top 10 golf resorts in China. It boasts two championship golf courses, one designed by professional golfer Jack Nicklaus, the other by Robert Trent Jones Jr. Spring City Golf & Lake Resort is located about a 40-minute drive from Kun-ming on scenic Yang Zhonghai Lake. The year-round mild climate makes Spring City a popular destination for golfers from all over the world. All golf lodges and villas offer fantastic views of the garden area. Overnight in Kunming. 

Day 9: A round on the Mountain Course

After breakfast, you will enjoy a round trip on the Jack Nicklaus-designed Mountain Course (18-hole) of Spring City Golf & Lake Resort. This course is located on a hill overlooking Yang Zhonghai Lake. So you can enjoy a spectacular view during your game. A wonderfully inspiring and soothing sight as the challenging course will demand a lot from you and bring out all your skills. Afterwards, the rest of the day is at your disposal. Overnight in Kunming.

10th Day Spectacular Lake Golf Course & Stone Forest

After mastering the Mountain Course yesterday, today is a morning round on the Lake Course designed by Robert Trent Jr. 18Hole Square is located on the shores of Yang Zonghai Lake and is world famous for its narrow fairways, intersecting trenches, green terraces and breathtaking views of the lake. A difference in height of approximately 140 meters between the hole on a hill 1 and the lakeside hole. 9 is a very particular challenge. In the afternoon you can look forward to the impressive stone forest. The “trees” of this almost 26.000 Hectares of large area are narrow rock needles that rise between five and thirty meters in height. On twelve square kilometers you can admire different rock formations, for example Ashima, allegedly a young beauty frozen to stone. On the rock walls you can see corals, because the geological origin of the stone forest lies in a disappeared sea. One 1.200 Meter-long hiking trail winds through the stone labyrinth. Overnight in Kunming.

Day 11 Between Kunming's Charm and Sanya's Coastal Magic

Today you will discover the highlights of Kunming on an exciting city tour. Your first destination is the Railway Museum, which will give you a glimpse into the history of Chinese railways. Here you can admire historical locomotives and wagons and learn more about the development of rail transport in China. Afterwards you will visit the old town of Kunming, which with its traditional buildings and narrow streets gives a real feel for the local culture and history. Stroll through the historic streets, experience the bustle of the locals and enjoy the charm of this ancient city. The next stop is the lively flower and bird market, a real delight for the senses. The market is known for its magnificent floral arrangements and the multitude of exotic birds offered here. Stroll through the colorful stands, breathe in the fresh scents and let yourself be carried away by the lively atmosphere. In the afternoon you will be taken to the airport, from where you will continue to Sanya. Upon arrival in Sanya, you will be expected by your guide and taken to your hotel. Overnight in Sanya.

Day 12 First Round in Tropical Paradise

Start the day with a round of golf at Yalong Bay Golf Club’s 18-hole course, which can be reached in five minutes with the free hotel shuttle. The par-72 golf course with a length of 7,189 yards is another example by US architect Robert Trent Jr. and also ranks among the 10 best golf courses in China. Its typical shape is reminiscent of a dragon claw. The slightly hilly terrain has been redesigned with native sand, creating the effect of natural sand dunes as they existed here a few hundred years ago before rice fields were planted. In addition to challenging fairways with Bermuda grass, the course also offers a driving range with 54 tee seats, which is built in typical Hainan style. After the game of golf, the rest of the day is at your disposal. Overnight in Sanya.

Day 13: Testing the only par 6 hole in China

Today also has a highlight for you: a game on the course of the Sun Valley Golf Club designed by JMP Design (Ian Costello, Mark Hollinger and Robert Moore) and opened in 2004. This 27-hole championship course consists of three 9-hole courses distributed among the hillside, lake and man-made sand dunes. The natural terrain was expanded to create a Front-9 Lake and a Back-9 Desert Course. At least seven holes are slightly elevated, allowing for an impressive view of Yalong Bay, while five more overlook huge desert bunkers. With 1,100 square meters, you will also find the largest green in China and hole 18, the only par-6 in China, will crown the course. An unforgettable gaming pleasure! The rest of the day is at your disposal to enjoy the island once again according to your own ideas. Overnight in Sanya.

Day 14: Farewell to China

Today we say goodbye to China. Even if your holiday is soon over – the precious memories, unforgettable impressions, beautiful photos and exciting stories from your journey on the footsteps of golf legends will accompany you forever. Look forward to letting your friends and family share your experiences in the distance at home. Your driver will take you to Sanya airport for your return flight. We wish you a good journey home!

Thailand, Laos and Cambodia - Once in a Lifetime Luxury Tour

Thailand, Laos and Cambodia Luxury Tour - Enjoy your Once in a Lifetime experience on this luxury country combination tour of Asia Special Tours.

Bury your phone deep in your suitcase and forget the trivialities of everyday life for a certain time. Unique experiences, unique moments and exciting adventures meet you on this luxurious round trip at the highest level. Let yourself be impressed by the cultural and historical treasures of Thailand, Laos and Cambodia and experience wonderful natural beauties. Stay in the most luxurious hotels and ships in Southeast Asia: each one on this route is unique and will delight you with exceptional service and exclusivity. You always set your own pace and your privacy is the focus. A paradisiacal bathing stay on the island of Koh Yao Noi completes this unforgettable journey. In absolute seclusion, you live here in your own world, listening only to the sound of the sea and the knocking of your heart.

1st Day Arrival in Bangkok & Helicopter Transfer to Peninsula Hotel

Welcome to Bangkok. You will already be received at the airlift and escorted to the immigration counter. After receiving your luggage and completing the entry formalities, you will take the helicopter directly to your hotel. Enjoy the first views of the vibrant metropolis on the flight before landing on the Peninsula Hotel Helipad. In addition to the perfect location, this house is characterized by the spacious swimming pool and the exclusive spa area. The first-class service of the house reads every wish from the eyes. In the evening, a very special experience awaits you. With a speedboat you go to Villa Chakrabongse. Experience the quiet and privileged lifestyle of a bygone era while you are served a welcome cocktail. Afterwards, the authentic Thai cuisine will enchant you with original family recipes from the royal palace kitchen. Strike on an unforgettable holiday and let the tasty Thai cuisine literally melt on your tongue. Overnight in Bangkok.

2nd day shopping with a personal fashionista & meeting with the florist Sakul Intakul

Enjoy an extensive breakfast before jumping into your exclusive shopping experience. Your personal fashionista is already waiting for you in the hotel lobby. You will visit exquisite designer shops and look behind the scenes of the show rooms. To crown your shopping tour, a specially arranged gourmet experience awaits you. In the afternoon, head to the trendy Thong Lor – Ekkamai district, where Bill Bensley’s design studio and outsider gallery are located in a hidden alley. The Bangkok-based star designer works from here on extraordinary projects throughout Southeast Asia. His most famous works include the Shinta Mani Hotels in Cambodia and the Four Seasons Resorts in Chiang Rai, Chiang Mai and Koh Samui. In addition to his work as an architect and designer, Bill Bensley is passionate about art – his paintings and sculptures can be admired in the Outsider Gallery. During a private visit, you will be warmly welcomed by one of its team members and enjoy a refreshing cocktail and fine snacks in a relaxed atmosphere. In this inspiring setting, you’ll learn more about Bensley’s creative visions and versatile artistic work. Overnight in Bangkok.

3rd day Heavenly views and first class cuisine in Chiang Mai

After breakfast, leave the glittering metropolis of Bangkok and fly on to Chiang Mai in northern Thailand. You are already expected at the airport and taken to your exclusive hotel 137 Pillars House Chiang Mai. This exceptional hotel offers a perfect blend of historic Thai architecture and modern design. The proverbial highlight of today is a visit to the Temple Mount Wat Doi Suthep. From the complex at an altitude of almost 1,000 meters, you can enjoy a wonderful view over the city and its surroundings. Over 306 steps climb up to the Temple Gate, which is guarded by two menacing nagas (snakes). Start the evening in style with a sunset cocktail at one of Chiang Mais’ most spectacular rooftop bars, offering panoramic views of the city immersed in golden light. Afterwards, an exquisite culinary experience awaits you at Belén, curated by Michelin-starred chef Paulo Airaudo, where European elegance meets the aromas of northern Thailand – a perfect combination of sky-high views and first-class cuisine. Overnight in Chiang Mai.

Day 4 Spectacular Balloon Ride Over Chiang Mai

Today you can look forward to a very special experience - a balloon trip over Chiang Mai. Early in the morning you will be taken to the starting point. Enjoy a cup of coffee while the crew makes the balloon ready to go. After a brief pilot safety briefing, get into the basket and your balloon ride begins. Keep the camera ready, as breathtaking views of the countryside below you are guaranteed while leisurely hovering at airy altitude over lush green rice fields, the lush Ping River valley and traditional Thai villages. After about 1-1.5 hours, you will start the descent before you have solid ground under your feet again. A traditional “champagne toast” is the perfect end to this unique experience. Then return to your hotel where you can rest and relax. The rest of the day is yours alone. Overnight in Chiang Mai.

Day 5: Four Seasons Tented Camp

(Without tour guide and chauffeur)

Tucked away in the beautiful landscape of the Golden Triangle lies the dreamlike Four Seasons Tented Camp Golden Triangle, the perfect refuge from the rest of the world. On unique excursions, discover the pleasures of interacting with rescued elephants in semi-natural habitat, explore spectacular mountain trails and bamboo jungles, and relax in elegant luxury tents. The camp offers luxurious tent accommodation with breathtaking views. For maximum privacy, we are happy to reserve the particularly idyllic location, Explorer's Lodge for you (extra charge on request). First-class culinary experiences such as a private breakfast at the highest point of the resort or a stylish picnic in nature, as well as a bath in the natural pool overlooking the jungle, private yoga classes and a wide range of soothing massages and innovative spa treatments amidst the idyllic jungle panorama complete the excellent offer of the luxury camp. Overnight in Chiang Rai.

Day 6 – 7 Free time in the Golden Triangle

(Without tour guide and chauffeur)

In the coming days, you can relax here remotely from the outside world and participate in various activities. As part of a Mahout training, you can experience the good-natured pachyderms up close and learn how to guide “your” elephants through the lush green jungle. An experience that will surely always be remembered. Do not miss the big elephant bathing – a wet-happy fun for all sides. In the evening, the Burma Bar invites you to a sundowner. Enjoy the surroundings and the unique camp! Overnight in Chiang Rai.

8th day Start of the ultra-luxurious cruise on the Mekong

After breakfast, you will be picked up and drive to Chiang Saen where you will board the ultra-luxurious Gypsy. After a short safety briefing, Leinen is off. Let yourself drift with the currents of the Mekong and spend light-hearted days in complete privacy. Gypsy is a brand new, ultra-exclusive river ship that brings you closer to the beauty of the Upper Mekong in a very personal way. The ship is privately chartered for you, is 41 meters long and about 5 meters wide and offers you an all-round carefree package: all drinks and food are included. You decide when and where to stop and whether you want to make landings. There are a total of five crew members, including the captain and the chef, who provide an unforgettable service. In the late afternoon you will reach the village of Pak Tha, where you will anchor for the night. Enjoy your first-class dinner and your first evening on board the ship. Overnight on the ship.

9th day on the way on the majestic Mekong

Early in the morning you will enjoy an absolute silence and wonderfully clear air on the Mekong. Let it be quite relaxed and gather forces for the day during a Buddhist meditation. Enjoy your breakfast on board as you cruise south. Along the way, visit the Kamu village of Ban Houy Phalam, known for its weaving and scarf production. Back on board, a delicious lunch awaits you. In the afternoon you will reach the small village of Pakbeng, where you will anchor for the night. If you wish, you may like to go ashore or simply relax on the ship. In the evening, your crew will once again prepare an unforgettable dinner on board. Overnight on the ship.

Day 10 Mystical Pak-Ou Caves & End of Cruise

Wake up in the morning with the first rays of sunlight and start the day with a yoga lesson. Enjoy your breakfast as your ship sets off on its way – always to the south. In Ban Baw you are invited to a whisky tasting. At noon, the chef on board prepares a delicious lunch before your eyes, which you can enjoy to the fullest. Then you reach the sacred Pak Ou caves, which are among the most important Buddhist pilgrimage sites in Laos. Inside it are thousands of gold-coated Buddha statues. Take enough time for the visit. In the afternoon you will reach the charming Luang Prabang. You say goodbye to the ship and will be taken to your hotel. The Luxury Hotel Sofitel Luang Prabang is already waiting for your visit. The hotel is set in a centuries-old colonial villa, beautifully nestled in a tropical garden. Still, a colonial elegance lies in the air and invites you to escape into the romance of the good old days. Overnight in Luang Prabang.

Day 11: Explore Luang Prabang with a UNESCO expert & cocktail on the Mekong shore

Start the day with a meditation or swim a few rounds in the pool. Freshly strengthened, you will meet UNESCO expert M. Francis Engelmann, who has been actively involved in the city’s conservation process since 2001. Together you will take a tour in which you get to know the architectural heritage of Luang Prabang intensively – from both religious and civic perspectives. The walk mainly leads through the lesser-known parts of the city, including picturesque alleys that offer a more traditional atmosphere of the World Heritage City. Mr. Engelmann will infect you with his passion for history, urbanism and anthropology. He knows the city better than anyone else! Then return to your hotel where you can relax. In the late afternoon, Lao delicacies are served on the shores of the mighty Mekong. Enjoy a refreshing cocktail and watch the sun gradually disappear behind the mountains. Feel the magic that lies above the spiritual Luang Prabang. Overnight in Luang Prabang.

12th Day Waterfalls of Khoung Si & Romantic Dinner

In the morning, head to the beautiful waterfalls of Khoung Si: The turquoise water, which flows partly over terraced basins down the slope, invites you to swim. A hiking trail through the tropical forest will take you back. Then, unfortunately, it is time to say goodbye to the magical Laos. They are taken to the airport and fly on to Cambodia. Welcome to Siem Reap. The emerging small town will be your starting point in the next few days to explore the world-famous Angkor Park. Nestled in a beautiful garden and surrounded by lush green travel fields, lies a magical villa where an exclusive dinner is organized for you. Take a romantic walk and find a cozy place where you can enjoy a cocktail. After that, you will be spoiled with a candlelight dinner under the open stars. Then return to your luxurious hotel. The elegant Hotel Phum Baitang is the perfect retreat amidst lush gardens and travel fields. Overnight in Siem Reap.

13th Day Spectacular Temple of Angkor & Visit to Art Gallery

Around 05:00 you will be picked up from your hotel and drive to the Archaeological Park of Angkor still in the dark. At the spectacular Angkor Wat you will find a suitable place and observe how soon afterwards the sun rises over the back of the temple and bathes the Angkor Wat in the warm morning light. Then visit the temple together with your guide, who will tell you a lot of interesting things about the mythological meaning of the ornaments and statues. Then head to the picturesque Srah Srong Baray, built in the 10th century. In this small oasis of peace, a champagne breakfast is served on a quiet corner of the lake. Then return to your hotel where you can rest a little. In the afternoon, visit the ruined city of Angkor Thom. Then you return to the hotel, where you can let the many impressions sink first. In the evening, visit a contemporary art gallery that presents a modern representation of Khmer culture. Overnight in Siem Reap.

Day 14 Yoga lesson at a temple

Say goodbye to all stress! In the shadow of a hidden temple, you can switch off in complete silence during a yoga session and forget about any everyday stress. They are taught to bring themselves into a state of rest and peace. A yoga teacher will help you open the mind to meditation and solidify the balance of your soul. The rest of the day is at your disposal. Read a good book or visit the first-class spa area in your hotel. We are also happy to take you to Angkor Golf Resort, In 2014, it was named Cambodia’s best golf course. The 18-hole golf course has a par of 72 and challenges golfers of every level. Overnight in Siem Reap.

15th Day Welcome to Paradise & Back to Nature

After the unforgettable impressions of the last few days, it is now time for pure rest and relaxation at the highest level. You will be taken to the airport for your onward flight to Phuket and travel on to the tropical paradise of Koh Yao Noi. Welcome to TreeHouse Villas – a retreat for barefoot luxury and natural elegance! Experience unforgettable days in untouched nature. Each of the tree house villas extends over two floors and is designed with great attention to detail. The open design, natural materials and the harmonious mix of style and comfort give the villas an unrivalled feel-good atmosphere. Hanging bridges take you to the other areas of the complex, where first-class service and warm hospitality await you. Enjoy your spacious private pool area and let your soul really dangle. Traditional recipes and freshly caught ingredients directly from the sea make the heart of every gourmet beat faster. Enjoy a first-class service and spend a few enchanting days that you will not soon forget. Overnight on Yao Noi.

16th – 19th day Paradise break on Koh Yao Noi

These days are all yours alone. Turn off completely and enjoy moments when time seems to stop. Explore the fascinating underwater world with its colorful fish and corals with diving glasses. Relax on the beach and enjoy the sweet nothing-doing. Overnight at Koh Yao Noi.

20th day Departure from Koh Yao Noi

Unfortunately, today you have to say goodbye to this paradise island again. By boat you will return to Phuket, from where you will begin your journey home. Even if your holiday is over – the precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories from your trip will accompany you forever.

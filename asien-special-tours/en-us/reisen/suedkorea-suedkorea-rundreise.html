South Korea Tour - A Country with Many Facets

South Korea Round Trip from Asia Special Tours - A country with many facets. Let Asia advise experts and discover the fascinating South Korea for yourself!

Itinerary & services

English itinerary text is an offline machine translation of the German original for this team preview. Please confirm travel details with your advisor; the German source remains available through the language switch.

Vibrant cities, unspoiled nature and a rich history: South Korea has many facets. On this two-week trip, you will get to know the tiger state from different sides: You will plunge into the hustle and bustle of the metropolis Seoul, rest in style during overnight stays in traditional Hanok guest houses, feel the vastness of unique landscapes in the national parks and visit places steeped in history. Your taste buds will also get their money’s worth during this trip. They immerse themselves in the bustle of the markets and try local delicacies. At the end of your trip, you will not only return home with many new impressions, photos and souvenirs, but also with a wealth of memories that will make you feel like bringing a piece of South Korea into your everyday life for a long time to come.

1st day Arrival in South Korea

Welcome to the Land of Morning Silence! Your journey through the tiger state begins in the capital Seoul, where you will be welcomed and warmly greeted. Use the rest of the day to relax a little from your flight or make your first explorations in the Asian metropolis. Seoul has a long historical tradition as the capital of various dominions, dating back to pre-Christian times. Thus, the place among modern skyscrapers houses rich cultural treasures from several millennia. Overnight in Seoul.

Day 2 Vibrant metropolis Seoul

Together with your guide, set out to explore some of the most beautiful sights in the city. Their first destination is the Gyeongbokgung Palace (“Palace of Bliss”), which was once the main palace and residence of the royal family. On foot we continue to the Samcheongdong district. Small cafés, individual shops, antique shops and art galleries paired with the surrounding nature create a beautiful scenery that invites you to dream and linger. Not far away is Insadong Alley, known for traditional craft shops, small galleries and tea houses. In the Hanokdorf Bukchon you will dive deeper into the past of the city. It is home to hundreds of traditional houses called “Hanok” in Korean. Then head to Korea’s oldest market, Gwangjang Market. After getting to know a part of traditional Seoul, it’s time to explore the futuristic side of the city. Drive to the Dongdaemun Design Plaza, DDP for short. The complex consists of an exhibition hall, a design museum and other galleries. The perfect place to end the day relaxed. Overnight in Seoul.

Day 3 Impressive plant diversity in the “Garden of Morning Silence”

Not only Seoul itself, but also the surrounding area has a lot to offer. That’s why you head to the province of Gyeonggi-do, where the garden of morning silence lures. This is one of Korea’s largest botanical gardens and enjoys great popularity. From the promenade surrounded by a refreshing pine forest to the 330,000 square meter flower garden, the Arboretum shows all the diversity of the plant world. Afterwards, your journey will take you to Pocheon, where you will visit the Sansawon brewery – a traditional distillery specialising in the production of Makgeolli and other rice wines. Here you will get exciting insights into the craft of Korean alcohol brewing. In the late afternoon you will return to your hotel where you can end the day in a relaxed way. Overnight in Seoul. 

4th day Culinary delights in Jeonju and overnight in a hanok

It’s time to leave Seoul behind. In the morning, head to Jeonju, a city considered the culinary heart of Korea. She is particularly known for her authentic cuisine, especially the famous Bibimbap, which you enjoy today for lunch in a local restaurant. While the rest of the city was modernized in the course of modern times, Jeonju Hanok Village retained its historical charm. Here your first destination is Jeondong Cathedral, built in the European-Romanesque style. Just a few minutes walk away is the Gyeonggijeon Shrine, which is one of Korea’s national treasures. In the afternoon, explore Nambu Market where you can immerse yourself in the bustle of a traditional market and discover regional specialties or handmade goods. The perfect end of today is the overnight stay in a traditional Korean hanok. The lovingly designed property is operated by a Korean couple. Overnight in Jeonju. 

Day 5 Through the Bamboo Forest of Damyang

On your way to Namwon, stop at Damyang, the city of bamboo. Here you will visit the Juknokwon Bamboo Park. The oldest trees are up to 300 years old! During your walk, you can catch a view of the Damyang River and Metasequoia Avenue. The avenue is rightly called the most beautiful avenue in Korea, as it forms a beautiful scenery at any time of year. Afterwards, your journey will take you to the mystical Maisan Mountains, where you will visit the Buddhist temple Tapsa (in German: “Pagoda Temple”). Flanked by dramatic Tafoni rock walls, it has over 80 cone-shaped stone pagodas, some of which reach a height of up to nine meters. Your physical well-being is also taken care of here: the road from the south entrance leads past a number of culinary delicacies. One specialty is Heukdwaeji Deung Galbi Gui, pig ribs smoked in a tongrill over charcoal. For vegetarians, the Sanchae Bibimbap is recommended, a bowl of rice and various wild herbs. Overnight in Namwon.

6th day Traditional monastic life and colorful street art

After a leisurely breakfast, head to the next stop of your trip: Busan, South Korea’s second largest city. On your way lies the famous Haeinsa Temple, founded in 802 by the monks Suneung and Ijeong. To this day, monks lead their ascetic lives here. The temple houses 15 cultural monuments and about 200 private art treasures, which have been included in the UNESCO World Heritage Site. Among them are the famous Tripitaka Koreana, with over 80,000 printing blocks the most comprehensive collection of Buddhist writings in Southeast Asia. Arriving in Busan, visit the Gamcheon Cultural District. Its colorful houses are nestled on a mountain slope, which earned the district the name Machu Picchu Busan. You will also discover street art elements, because here art and reality merge. Stroll through the many small alleys where galleries, small cafes and shops invite you to linger. Overnight in Busan.

Day 7 A city with many facets: fascinating Busan

There is a lot to see and discover in Busan. This morning, you will embark on a full-day exploration tour of the multifaceted city. You will learn about the country’s past during your visit to the UN Memorial and Peace Park. These honor the UN soldiers from 16 nations who participated in the Korean War (1950-1953) and died in battle. Afterwards, immerse yourself in the colorful bustle surrounding BIFF Square – named after the famous Busan International Film Festival. Here you will find the so-called “Road of Handprints”, dedicated to Asian movie stars, as well as numerous street food stands and small boutiques. Well fortified drive to the nearby Jagalchi fish market, a landmark of the city. Finally, you'll head to the Taejongdae observation deck, where the environment is characterised by a rocky coastline lined with dense forests. From there, you can look at the Japanese island of Tsushima with a clear view! Overnight in Busan.

 

Day 8 The legendary temple Haedong Yonggungsa

Their first destination today is the fabled temple Haedong Yonggungsa, which is called the Aquatic Sanctuary of Buddhism. According to legend, the great Buddha of mercy lives in solitude in the vastness of the ocean and appears on the back of a dragon. It is more secular in the Onggi pottery village of Oegosan. The production still takes place quite traditionally in Lehmöfen, where the goods have to be distilled for 15 days at a time. On the village grounds there is also a small museum, in which the history and production of the special pottery art is illustrated. At Hanok Dodongsanbang, take time off with Korean tea and lunch before continuing your journey to the former capital of the Silla Empire. In the middle of the city center of Gyeongju, the tombs of 23 rulers of the Silla period rise in Tumuli Park. Here you can not only walk among the artificial, green hilltops, but also visit one of the gigantic burial mounds. In the immediate vicinity is the Cheomseongdae Observatory, the oldest existing star observatory in Asia. At the end of the day, treat yourself to a stroll through the exotic garden landscape along the Volji pond. Overnight in Gyeongju.

9th day Discovering legendary cultural treasures

Your day begins with two UNESCO World Heritage sites that will surely impress you. The Bulguksa Temple represents the temple architecture of the ancient Silla Empire at its best. On the temple grounds are other national treasures such as the main stairs with 33 steps, which represent the 33 steps for enlightenment. The famous Seokguram Grotto, which you will visit afterwards, was dug into the mountain wall of Tohamsan Mountain in the 8th century. Inside it is a Buddha statue facing the East Sea. Continue to the Jukdo market, which has now developed into one of the largest markets in the province. In Guryeongpo, travel back in time to a South Korea once occupied by Japanese colonial rulers. In the “Guryeongpo Street of Contemporary Cultural History” you will find buildings of Japanese construction from the 1930s and discover the Japanese influences of the then occupied Korea. Finally, drive to the beach of Guryeongpo to review the daily events on a small walk on the 400-meter-long beach and to end the day relaxed. Overnight in Gyeongju.

Day 10 Confucius and traditional village life in Hahoe

You can get a glimpse of Confucianism while visiting the Confucian school Byeongsan Seowon. It was founded by Confucian students to commemorate the scholar Seong-nyoung Yu (1542-1607). Seong-nyoung Yu was highly regarded for his lyrics and personality and was also buried here. You will then get an authentic insight into traditional life in the village of Hahoe. It is one of South Korea’s UNESCO sites and convinces by preserving Korean architecture and culture. It owes its name “village surrounded by water” to the river Nakdong, which winds around the village. Then head to the nearby Buyongdae cliff, from which a spectacular panoramic view of the entire Hahoe village and the surrounding river landscape opens up. You will end your eventful day with an overnight stay at the traditional Hanok Guesthouse Rakkojae. Situated in the middle of the historic village, it offers you a unique opportunity to fully immerse yourself in the lives of the people of that time. Overnight in Andong. 

Day 11 Discoveries Along the East Coast

This morning you leave for Samcheok, where you will actively start the day with a one and a half hour ride on the rail bike along the coast. Afterwards, you will head to Gangneung - the venue for the Olympic Winter Games 2018 - and dedicate yourself to part of Korea's recent history. You start with Tongil Park, also known as the Reunification Park. Here you will have the opportunity to visit the North Korean Navy submarine, which was stranded in 1996, and learn more about the geopolitical history of Korea. Continue your journey to Sokcho, an idyllic coastal town where you will visit the port of Daepohang. Enjoy the maritime atmosphere and fresh sea air before you are taken to your hotel, where you can end the evening in peace. Overnight in Sokcho. 

12th day Legendary Seoraksan Mountains

After breakfast, depart for the legendary Seoraksan Mountains. Seoraksan National Park is undoubtedly one of the most beautiful landscapes South Korea has to offer. From green coniferous forests rise peaks, crowned by unique rock formations and through the valleys flow crystal clear water courses. In your exploration today, however, you will not only find unique nature: in the middle of the park lies the Shinheungsa Temple, over which a bronze Buddha stands almost 15 meters high. A ride on the cable car will take you up to the mountain fortress Gwongeumseong, which has overlooked the surrounding rock peaks for centuries. However, not only in airy altitudes, the Seoraksan National Park offers unique landscapes. Among the valleys Jujeongol is particularly idyllic, among the most beautiful waterfalls of the area are the Biryong Falls. Overnight in Sokcho.

Day 13 Back to the Capital

Your journey is slowly coming to an end and you will return to your starting point: Seoul. Upon arrival, you will immediately immerse yourself in the vibrant life of the city. First, visit the famous Myeongdong Shopping Street, a paradise for shopping lovers where you can drift through the numerous boutiques and stalls. In the evening, a special experience awaits you: the Nanta Performance. The songless musical is about four cooks who have to create a wedding banquet within an hour. The show includes acrobatics, magic tricks, comedy, pantomime and the audience is also involved. The first international performance took place in 1999 at the Edingburgh Festival Fringe, where it was awarded as best performance. Since then, NANTA has already been performed in 18 countries. Overnight in Seoul. 

Day 14 Farewell to South Korea

The time until the planned airport transfer is at your disposal. Unfortunately, your time out from everyday life is already coming to an end. Even if your holiday is over – the precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories of your trip will accompany you forever. Your driver guide will take you in time for your return flight to the airport. This ends your beautiful journey through the “Land of Morning Silence” with Asia Special Tours. We wish you a good journey home!

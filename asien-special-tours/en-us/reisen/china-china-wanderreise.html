On this beautiful hiking trip, experience the many facets of Yunnan Province up close – be impressed by the indescribable natural beauty and immerse yourself in the life of some minority residents here. Visit Songtsenling Monastery, one of the 13 famous monasteries in the Kham Tibetan area, enjoy the sunrise over the mountains around the Tibetan village of Benzilan and climb the base camp of Mt. Kawagebo, one of the most sacred mountains of the Kham Tibetans. They wander through one of the deepest gorges on Earth – the Tiger Jump Gorge – and look for the rare black stump nose monkeys that only exist in Yunnan. Finally, your trip will take you to the UNESCO World Heritage City of Lijiang.

Day 1 Shangri-La - legendary "City of Bliss"

Welcome to Shangri-La, a city situated at 3,200 meters. For a long time, no one knew exactly where “Shangri-La” – a legendary Buddhist place of bliss – lies. Since 2002, it has been known: in Zhongdian, the gateway to Tibet, which was renamed Shangri-La. There you will already be expected by your tour guide and warmly welcomed. So you can already, while the chauffeur takes you to the hotel, first facts and anecdotes about the city. The remaining day is at your disposal, so that you can arrive in peace and acclimatize. Overnight in Shangri-La.

Day 2 Visit to Songtseling Monastery

This morning, the first highlight of your trip awaits you when you visit Songtseling Monastery just outside the city. The 300-year-old Tibetan Lama Monastery is one of the 13 famous monasteries in the Kham region of Tibet. Several hundred monks perform their Buddhist exercises in the prayer halls. Founded by the 5th Dalai Lama, the complex covers an area of 33.3 hectares and is modeled on the style of the Potala Palace in Lhasa – which is why it is also known as the “little Potala Palace”. Every year, according to the Chinese traditional lunar calendar, the festival of Gedong is celebrated here, an important religious festival of Tibetans, which should herald a happy new year. After your temple visit, you will embark on a first small hike that will take you to the Dabao Temple. Built on a rugged mountain slope at 3,300 meters, the temple is one of the oldest Buddhist sanctuaries in Shangri-La. Not much of the temple itself has been preserved since the Cultural Revolution; the sacred environment with its lush hills, wind-flopping prayer flags and old pine trees is all the more worthwhile. Overnight in Shangri-La.

Day 3 At the upper reaches of the Yanggtse

Your journey will take you to Feilaisi near Deqin today. During the approximately five-hour drive through charming landscapes, you will pass the large bend of the Jinsha River at the upper reaches of the Yangtze River and visit the Tibetan village of Benzilan. Due to its location between Yunnan and Tibet, the place was once an important stop on the ancient tea and horse trade route. Today, Benzilan is best known for its local crafts. Overnight in Feilaisi.

Day 4 Hike to Mt. Kawagebo

Ready for your upcoming hike? Before that, be sure to watch the sunrise over the majestic mountain world around Deqin, because how better to greet the day? Afterwards, head to the hot springs at Xidang, where your hike begins. This first takes you about 12 kilometers uphill to the 3,700 meters Nantsong La Pass and from there about 6 kilometers downhill to the village Yubeng at 3,400 meters altitude. Yubeng will be the starting point for your day hikes of the next two days. Overnight in Yubeng.

5th Day Walk to the Holy Waterfall

Today you can look forward to a not too exhausting day hike to the Holy Waterfall. The track is mostly quite flat. Shortly before the waterfall, a pass at an altitude of 3,500 m is to be crossed. After a rest in front of this magnificent backdrop, make your way back and return to your accommodation in Yubeng in the afternoon. Overnight in Yubeng.

Day 6 Hike to Mt. Kawagebo Base Camp

Mount Kawagebo, 6,740 m high, is one of the eight great sacred mountains in the Tibetan region. To this day, his summit is unconquered, because all mountaineering expeditions have failed so far. For cultural and religious reasons, no further attempts are currently allowed. For the Kham Tibetans, Mount Kawagebo is sacred and an important pilgrimage site. Your current destination is the base camp of Mt. Kawagebo, located at 3,600 meters. Enjoy the walk through magnificent, largely untouched landscapes with fantastic views. The path runs over flat pastureland into the mountains. Then the path leads about 400 meters through a jungle with huge trees – at altitudes over 3000m a real special feature. After you have crossed the pass at 3,700 meters of altitude, you will go downhill to Base Camp. Depending on your condition, you can hike about 2 km further to a beautiful glacier lake. After a well-deserved rest, you will hike back to Yubeng to your guest house. Overnight in Yubeng.

Day 7 Through the Ninong Gorge

After two wonderful days, leave Yubeng behind today. Together with your guide, you will hike downhill for about 16 kilometers until you reach the Mekong River at 2,300 m. Along the way, you will cross the Ninong Gorge with countless ponds and waterfalls that line your path. Via meadows and past steep mountain walls, you will finally reach the town of Ninong, where you will be picked up by your driver. Together you will go to Cizhong. Since the arrival of French missionaries about 100 years ago, the idyllic place has been Catholic. The Catholic Church of Cizhong in particular bears witness to this. Tonight is spent in the pretty Songtsam Cizhong Lodge at 2,200 meters. The lodge is located very close to the Catholic Church of Cizhong and offers a beautiful view of the dreamlike surroundings. The rooms are decorated in Tibetan style and all have their own private terrace where you can end the day wonderfully and relax. Also provided for the physical well-being: in the hotel's restaurant you can taste a delicious Chinese dinner. Overnight in Cizhong.

Day 8 Along the Mekong to Cizhong

In addition to the Catholic faith, the French missionaries also brought the wine to the place on the Mekong River. Before continuing your journey, first visit the church of Cizhong and wander over a winery, where you can certainly taste one or the other drop. In the afternoon, drive through fantastic mountain landscapes to Tacheng. Also tonight you sleep in a Songtsam Lodge. The pretty property is located in the middle of extremely fertile farmland and enjoys a wonderfully pleasant climate. All nine rooms are very spacious and yet cozy. Large windows and a private balcony allow you a fantastic view of the rice terraces. The lodge is decorated in an elegant design with Chinese art and style elements. Overnight in Tacheng.

Day 9: Nearby the Black stump-nosed monkeys

This morning it is especially worth getting up early: together with your guide you will visit a nature reserve where you can observe the black stump nose monkeys. This species of monkey lives only in southwest China and is almost as valuable to the country as the Great Pandas. Unfortunately, black stump nose monkeys are today among the acutely endangered primate species and are therefore protected. The population currently living in Southeast Xizang, Tibet and Northwest Yunnan is estimated to be around 1,500 animals. Later you will continue to the Tiger Jumping Gorge, one of the deepest gorges on earth. The gorge is an impressive 3900 meters deep and about 16 kilometers long. In some places, however, it measures only 10 meters wide. According to legend, a tiger is said to have skipped the gorge here long ago to escape its hunters. Almost vertically, the cliffs hang threateningly over the raging waters of the Jinshajiang (River of the Golden Sands). From Qiaotou, you will always hike easily uphill via the “22 sweeps” to the Tea Horse Guesthouse, where you will spend the night. Overnight in the Tiger Jumping Gorge.

Day 10 Through the Tiger Jumping Gorge

Breakfast vigorously. The current hiking stage first leads up along the mountain ridge and leads shortly before your destination finally downhill to the guest house. Overnight in the Tiger Jumping Gorge.

11th day Walnut shark – the narrowest part of the gorge

The last day of your hike through the impressive Tiger Jumping Gorge will take you down to the riverbank for about 1.5 hours, where you can enjoy a gigantic view of the gorge. Afterwards you hike for about 1.5 hours slightly uphill to the quiet village called “Walnusshain”, which invites you to rest with its terrace fields, numerous walnut trees and wooden or stone houses. Here you will meet your driver who will take you to Lijiang. The city at 2,500 meters altitude is a UNESCO World Heritage Site. If you imagine it without tourists, it would truly be a journey back in time to the past century. Striking in the street scene are decorative men in traditional robes, women in blouses and pale blue aprons. These are the Naxi who have settled in Lijiang for more than 1,000 years. Originally they lived as nomads on the Tibetan plateau. Its originally matriarchal social structure is astonishing, but it is now also more the commercial tone.

12th day UNESCO World Heritage Site Lijiang

Start the day with an extensive breakfast. Whether you want Western or traditional Chinese breakfast, you will definitely find something delicious. Well strengthened, your guide will take you on a stroll through the historic old town of Lijiang. Between the large water wheel at the entrance and the central square, called Sifang, there is busy activity in the restaurants, cafes and shops from morning to late at night. The houses are a feast for the eyes: two-storey wooden skeleton buildings with brick walls and natural foundations with artfully carved door panels and flower-decorated courtyards. In the north of the village you will find the pond of the Black Dragon (Heilong Tan), surrounded by old pavilions, where in windless weather the 13 peaks of the up to 5,600 meters high massif of the Jadedrachenberge and Yufeng Temples are reflected in the clear water. The rest of the day is at your disposal to explore the pretty city a little further on your own or to buy suitable souvenirs for your loved ones at home. Overnight in Lijiang.

Day 13 Departure from Yunnan

Today we say goodbye to Yunnan. With many precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories from your trip, you will leave Lijiang. Look forward to letting your friends and family share your experiences in the distance at home. Your driver will take you to Lijiang Airport for your return flight home.

Tasmania Car Rental Trip - Natural Beauty in Southern Australia

Tasmania car rental trip: Experience unique nature on your individual car rental trip through Tasmania with Asia Special Tours. This is a question.

Embark on a journey to the southernmost part of Australia. In Tasmania, the largest island on the red continent, unparalleled nature awaits you in all its facets. From spectacular mountains, to original national parks and idyllic bays with blue water – nature lovers get their money’s worth here. With the rental car you hike at your own pace from one highlight to the next and are completely flexible and independent. We book hand-picked accommodation along your itinerary in advance. Feel the freedom of Australia and look forward to a journey from which you will tell your friends and family enthusiastically for a long time.

1st day Arrival in Hobart and city walk

Welcome to Tasmania! Finally, your long-awaited adventure begins. Upon arrival at Hobart airport, you will take your rental vehicle directly and drive comfortably to your hotel in the Tasmanian capital. The elegant Islington Hotel combines historic charm with modern luxury. The lovingly restored 1847 villa delights with an extraordinary art collection and magnificent views of Mount Wellington. In the afternoon, discover Hobart on an atmospheric walk (in a small group) through the historic old town. Your local guide will take you on an exciting journey back in time and tell fascinating stories about the personalities who shaped the development of the city – from the early settlement site to the lively port city of today. You will discover impressive listed buildings and learn about the people and events behind these historic landmarks. Overnight in Hobart.

2nd Day Bruny Island Cruise – Spectacular Nature Experiences

Experience an unforgettable day trip (together with other guests) to Bruny Island and discover the impressive coastal landscape on a cruise. After transfer by bus and ferry you will reach the island and start a three-hour boat tour along the rugged coast. Passing some of Australia’s tallest sea cliffs, the journey leads to imposing rock formations and deep ocean caves. With a bit of luck, you can observe seals, dolphins and numerous seabirds. The specially built boats offer the best view of the spectacular surroundings through covered outdoor areas and enable an intense nature experience. On board, enjoy a lunch with regional specialties. In the late afternoon, return to Hobart. Overnight in Hobart. 

Day 3 - Continue to the West Coast

Today, your journey from Hobart will take you to the idyllic Strahan Village. A box seat on the west coast of Tasmania: your accommodation Risby Cove delights with exclusive suites directly on the water, spectacular sunsets over Macquarie Harbour and an atmosphere full of tranquility, nature and enjoyment. Strahan is considered the gateway to the Franklin-Gordon Wild Rivers National Park World Heritage Site, where the wild rivers Collingwood and Franklin rush through dense rainforest and converge in the majestic Gordon River. Once an important port for logging and maritime trade, Strahan has retained its quiet charm and impresses with its picturesque waterfront location. The narrow streets, historic buildings and cozy cafés convey an authentic coastal flair. Explore the unspoilt nature around Strahan or enjoy a relaxing walk along the waterfront with fresh sea air. Strahan combines history, nature and relaxed small-town life in a unique way. Overnight in Strahan.
(Driving distance: 300 km, driving time: about 4 hours). 30 minutes)

4th Day Gordon River Cruise – Rainforest, History and Wild Coasts

After breakfast, an adventure on the Gordon River awaits you on a boat trip (together with other guests). Already in the morning your journey will start over the Macquarie Harbour to the imposing Hells Gates, where the power of the South Ocean will be felt. Along the way, you will pass fish farms and get interesting insights into the sustainable breeding of salmon and trout. Then glide through the quiet landscape of the UNESCO World Heritage-listed rainforest, whose ancient trees are reflected in the calm waters of the Gordon River. During a landing, you will discover a more than 2,000-year-old Huon Pine – one of the oldest trees in Australia. Another highlight is the visit of Sarah Island, a former penal colony, whose eventful history brings your guide to life with exciting stories. During the journey you will enjoy a delicious lunch on board. In the afternoon, return from the cruise. Overnight in Strahan.

Day 5 Moina – gateway to the wilderness around Cradle Mountain

You will leave Strahan and continue towards Moina, a small village on the edge of the Tasmanian wilderness, north of Cradle Mountain National Park. The route leads through the varied landscape of West Tasmania, past rainforests, rolling hills and wide valleys that offer a first taste of the natural splendor around Cradle Mountain. Moina is a quiet, natural starting point for further exploration of the region. Overnight in Moina. 
(Driving distance: 150 km, driving time: about 2 hours). 30 minutes) 

Day 6: Cradle Mountain National Park

Day free of charge

Wake up in the heart of the wilderness: Surrounded by ancient rainforests, Lemonthyme Wilderness Retreat offers a unique retreat in Tasmania’s nature. Enjoy the tranquility far away from everyday life and with a little luck observe the fascinating wildlife of the island in its natural environment. Cradle Mountain is protected by UNESCO and one of the most beautiful hiking areas in Australia. From day hikes to the multi-day hike along the Overland Track, there is something for everyone. In any case, you will find yourself in the middle of untouched nature, where it is teeming with wallabys and other animals. This day is free for you to explore. Overnight in Moina.

Day 7 From the Wild to the Coast

Your journey continues from the impressive mountains of Cradle Mountain to the northeast coast of Tasmania to Bridport. The ride offers a varied landscape change: you leave the pristine wilderness of the island’s interior and pass green farmland, rolling hills and typical Tasmanian landscapes before reaching the coastal region. Bridport is home to the unique atmosphere of Barnbougle, one of Australia’s most famous golf destinations. The two spectacular golf courses The Dunes and Lost Farm are nestled in a wild, original coastal landscape with gentle dunes, wide sea views and an impressive natural backdrop. Overnight in Bridport. 
(Distance: 200 km, driving time: about 3 hours) 

8th Day All Day Golf or Leisure in Bridport

Today you will design according to your personal wishes and have the opportunity to enjoy the special atmosphere of the region around Bridport. Nature lovers can explore the dreamlike coastal landscape, take relaxed walks or simply let the tranquility and expanse of Tasmanian nature affect themselves. For golf lovers, there is an opportunity for an unforgettable game on the famous Barnbougle Dunes Golf Course. Nestled in a spectacular dune landscape on the coast of Tasmania, the course is one of the most beautiful golf courses in Australia. A relaxing day where you can discover the beauty of Tasmania at your own pace or pursue your passion for golf in exceptional surroundings. Overnight in Bridport. 

9th Day New Horizons: On to the Freycinet Peninsula

Today you will continue your journey with the rental car and drive towards the Freycinet Peninsula on the east coast of Tasmania. Along the way, experience the tranquil beauty of rural Tasmania and make individual stops depending on your interest. Your destination is the spectacular region around Freycinet National Park. The harmonious combination of turquoise sea, white sand beaches and the striking granite mountains of the Hazards makes this area one of the most beautiful natural paradises in Tasmania. In the afternoon, arrive at Wineglass Bay Estate, where you can relax amidst the impressive scenery of the Freycinet Peninsula. The property consists of a few detached luxury retreats that are modern and stylishly designed. Spacious window fronts open up impressive views over the lagoon and the surrounding bush landscape and combine the comfort of the accommodation harmoniously with the unique natural backdrop. Overnight in Coles Bay. 
(Distance: 200 km, driving time: about 3 hours) 

Day 10 Tasmania’s Coastal Jewel – Wineglass Bay

Enjoy an unforgettable nature experience at a four-hour eco-cruise. You will drive through the impressive landscape of the Freycinet region and discover the national park from a very special perspective. During the ride, you will enjoy breathtaking views of the rugged coastal landscape, turquoise coves, impressive rock formations and, with a bit of luck, the diverse wildlife of Tasmania. Your experienced local captain will provide exciting insights into the geology and history of the region. The highlight of the cruise is a stay at the world-famous Wineglass Bay. In front of the dreamlike backdrop of one of the most beautiful beaches in the world, you can enjoy a delicious lunch with regional specialties. Then we return to Coles Bay. Overnight in Coles Bay.

11th day of free time and evening penguin watching

The day is free to explore the impressive scenery of Freycinet National Park on your own. Take a hike and discover the unique landscape with its pink granite mountains, dense bushscapes and spectacular viewpoints. In the evening, a special nature experience awaits you (together with other guests). At dusk, visit a penguin colony on Tasmania’s east coast and see the little penguins waddle to their nests after returning from the sea. The tour was created by locals to help protect the penguin population while allowing respectful observation of these fascinating animals. With a little luck, you will experience the charming inhabitants of Tasmania up close. Overnight in Coles Bay.

Day 12 Returns to the Capital

Today your journey will take you back to Hobart for your last stop in Tasmania. You leave the beautiful Freycinet region and drive with your rental car through the varied interior of the island. Upon arrival in Hobart, you will move into your hotel at the city’s picturesque harbor. The MACq 01 Hotel is located directly on the historic waterfront and is the ideal starting point to discover the cultural heart of the Tasmanian capital. At the end of your trip, enjoy the special atmosphere of Hobart, stroll along the harbor promenade or let yourself be pampered by the diverse gastronomy with fresh regional specialties. Overnight in Hobart. 
(Driving distance: 180 km, driving time: about 2 hours). 30 minutes) 

Day 13: Farewell to Tasmania

Even the most beautiful journey comes to an end. Today we say goodbye to Tasmania and the unforgettable experiences of the past days. After a last relaxing breakfast, drop off your rental car at the airport and start your journey home or onwards. Take advantage of the last moments to review the varied impressions of your trip – from the spectacular nature of the national parks to the impressive coastal landscapes to the special encounters and experiences on the way. With many beautiful memories in your luggage, you will fly from Hobart to your next destination or back home.

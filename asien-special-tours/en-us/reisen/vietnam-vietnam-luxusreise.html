Luxury Travel - Vietnam in Style

Vietnam Luxury Tour: Discover the most beautiful sides of Vietnam on this luxurious Vietnam trip from Asia Special Tours. Now ask me.

Itinerary & services

This special Vietnam trip combines stylish comfort with authentic experiences and takes you through one of the most fascinating countries in Southeast Asia in a very personal way. Enjoy a carefully curated itinerary full of privacy, comfort and surprising moments – with exciting behind-the-scenes insights, personal encounters and cultural highlights. This creates a varied mix of discovery and enjoyment. At the same time, there is consciously enough room for flexibility and individual design. You decide for yourself your pace, have time for personal discoveries or simply enjoy in peace the amenities of your high-quality accommodations, which give each stage a special setting. A paradisiacal bathing stay on the island of Con Dao completes this unforgettable journey. Enjoy the harmony with yourself and collect countless moments for eternity.

1st day Arrival in Vietnam: Welcome to Hanoi

Welcome to Hanoi! Immediately after your landing, your personal all-round carefree experience begins. Already in the airport building you will be warmly welcomed by your tour guide or the airport staff. While all entry formalities are done for you and your luggage is brought to the vehicle, you can arrive quite relaxed. Your personal chauffeur awaits you and takes you to your exclusive hotel, the legendary Sofitel Legend Metropole Hanoi. The “Grande Dame” of the Vietnamese capital enchants with its elegant French colonial style and the charm of bygone times. A luxurious spa, pool area and excellent restaurants invite you to relax and enjoy after sightseeing. Your room is also ready for early arrival, so you can arrive in peace and recharge your energy. If you feel like a first walk, we recommend a tour around the idyllic Hoan-Kiem Lake. Surrounded by colonial buildings and shaded trees, the lake is a popular meeting place for Hanoians of all ages. Your personal guide will gladly accompany you with exciting background information and individual tips. Overnight in Hanoi.

2nd Day Insider City Tour with Christian Oster

After breakfast, Christian Oster, a Freiburg journalist and former restaurant owner, has lived in Hanoi for many years and knows the city like the back of his hand. Together you walk through the historic old town. You will get exciting insights into Vietnamese politics, religion and the social coexistence of people. How is it living here? What do Vietnamese people buy for dessert in the market? What are the property prices? True to the Vietnamese motto “The best food is on the street” try a traditional “Bun Cha” – grilled meatballs in a spicy sauce. Then visit the Military Museum, where a ten-minute film screening shows the victory of the Vietnamese over the French in Dien Bien Phu on May 7, 1954. You will learn the background to the Vietnam War and why this chapter of history in Vietnam is discussed only marginally today. The trail will take you past the Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum. On Ba Dinh Square, Mr. Oster tells you about the cult of personality around “Uncle Ho”. In the Temple of Literature, you will learn about Confucius and his influence on Vietnamese society today and the social role of men and women in Vietnam. Overnight in Hanoi.

3rd Day In Style Through Lan Ha Bay: Cruise Aboard the Ylang

In the coming days, the fascinating Lan Ha Bay awaits you – emerald green water, impressive karst formations and hidden coves. On board the Ylang, a luxury boutique ship with only 10 cabins, enjoy exclusive wellness and lifestyle offerings. This special route through the less frequented Lan-Ha Bay along the island of Cat Ba is in no way inferior to the backdrop of the famous Halong Bay. Your private chauffeur and guide will take you to the marina. By speedboat you transfer to the ylang. Each cabin offers panoramic windows, a separate living area and its own balcony. After lunch on board, visit the village of Viet Hai. On a relaxed bicycle tour through winding alleys and the green surroundings, you will get insights into the life of the villagers. If you like it more relaxed, you can use an electric shuttle. Back on the ship you can expect tea tastings, soothing massages (for a fee) or time on the sun deck. Dinner will spoil you with exquisite local specialties. At the end of the day you have the choice between meditations, a film in the theater or even a night fishing experience. Overnight on the ship.

Day 4 - Second day on board

Those who love the early hours of the morning will start the day amidst a magical backdrop at sunrise with tai chi exercises on deck. Afterwards, enjoy your breakfast in peace as the ylang takes course on the southern tip of Cat Ba Island. There, a dinghy will take you to a particularly authentic fishing village where people live in close connection with the water. In the Cat-Ba National Park you have the opportunity to become active on a hike through jungle-like landscapes and discover the diverse flora and fauna. Those who like it quieter, simply relax on board and let the breathtaking natural backdrop take effect. After a lunch on board, you will have another opportunity to visit a scenic bay - whether for a relaxing walk on the beach or a kayak tour. Back on the ship, an atmospheric evening awaits you as the sun slowly sinks behind the horizon – a perfect moment to stop and enjoy. Overnight on the ship.

Day 5 Arrive, Relax and Enjoy at Four Seasons Nam Hai

In the morning, once again enjoy the peaceful atmosphere on board the Ylang. Meanwhile, the ship slowly glides back to the port. Back on shore, your private chauffeur will welcome you and take you to Hanoi Airport for your onward flight to central Vietnam. Your exclusive resort for the coming days is the luxurious Four Seasons The Nam Hai – a place of pure relaxation to fully enjoy the here and now. In the middle of a beautiful tropical garden, right on the endless sandy beach, elegant, puristic design based on Feng Shui principles awaits you in the stylish villas. Depending on your personal preference, you have the choice between villas with and without private pool, secluded in the garden or with sea views. We are happy to advise you on this. Contemporary Vietnamese artworks and high-quality materials create a harmonious feel-good ambiance in which luxury and serenity blend perfectly. Fine restaurants, attentive service, several pools and an excellent spa area leave nothing to be desired. Overnight at Hoi An.

Day 6 Painting Hoi An & Sunset on the Thu Bon River

Start the day calmly and enjoy the comfort of your resort – whether it’s a refreshing round in the pool, a walk on the beach or a soothing wellness application. Later, your private guide will take you on a trip to the enchanting port city of Hoi An. The old town, UNESCO World Heritage Site, enchants with its winding alleys, historic buildings and the authentic charm of centuries past. Together you will discover ancient Chinese teak houses and temples, colorful markets, the 400-year-old Japanese Bridge and French colonial villas. Then board a traditional wooden boat. Glide leisurely on the Thu Bon River while serving refreshing cocktails and small snacks. The setting sun shrouds Hoi An in a warm, magical light, and the city’s first colorful lamps shine. Overnight at Hoi An 
Our recommendation (for an additional fee): Over a private dinner at the idyllic Cam Thanh, experience up close the creative fusion cuisine and inspiring life story of top chef Duc Tran.

Day 7 - 8 or be active? You have a choice...

These days belong entirely to you. Enjoy the amenities of your hotel and moments of pure relaxation with Yoga & Wellness. In addition, we provide you with exclusive experiences that we tailor to your interests – for example:  
Do you love golf?  
We arrange for you access to the first-class golf courses in the Da Nang region. We especially recommend the Montgomerie Links Golf Club. 
Are you passionate about culture and history?  
Then we recommend a trip to the imperial city of Hue. A ride over the spectacular Cloud Pass leads you to the former imperial city of Vietnam. Your personal guide will accompany you for a full day and open the doors to the fascinating history of the Nguyen Dynasty. 
The temples of My Son are a paradise for explorers and photographers 
Nestled in an impressive mountain landscape, you will discover the mysterious ruins of the former temple city of Champa. Protected as a UNESCO World Heritage Site and surrounded by lush nature, this place offers fascinating insights into Vietnam’s religious and cultural heritage.
 

9th Day Saigon – Luxury, Culture and River Romanticism

Today you leave Hoi An and travel on to the vibrant heart of South Vietnam – to Saigon. The city builds a fascinating bridge between cultures and eras: modern high-rises stand side by side with colonial buildings and traditional temples. At the airport, you will be met and taken to your first-class hotel. The exclusive Park Hyatt is undisputedly one of the top addresses in the city. It combines timeless elegance, class and excellent service with the best location in the heart of Saigon. The multi-award-winning restaurants of the house pamper you with culinary highlights. After a short rest, explore the highlights of the city with your personal guide: the imposing Reunification Palace, the historic Main Post Office, the elegant Opera House and the City Hall. In the late afternoon, a private Sunset Cruise awaits you on the Saigon River. While you glide relaxed over the water, you leave behind the lively bustle of the city. With cool drinks, the impressions of the day can affect you. As the sun slowly sinks behind the horizon, a magical backdrop unfolds: the warm light of the evening sun immerses the surroundings in golden colors. Overnight in Saigon.

Day 10 Saigon after your Gusto & Fascination AO Show at the Opera House

Take advantage of today to discover Saigon according to your taste - together with your guide and chauffeur. Spontaneously vote on what you want. The Dong Khoi Street, for example, invites you to a shopping stroll with modern boutiques and accessory shops of Vietnamese designers. If you are interested in traditional crafts, it is recommended to visit a lacquerware factory where you can look over the shoulders of the artisans. Another highlight is the lively Ben Thanh market. 
Our “behind-the-scenes-tip” (optional): Would you like to experience Saigon even more intensively? With your personal guide, explore authentic District 5 and the historic Cho Lon district of China off the classic routes—with spiritual pagodas, a traditional pharmacy, and coffee at a local café.

In the evening we have a special experience ready for you: the visit of the A-O show at the Saigon Opera House. This extraordinary staging, an innovative symbiosis of music, acrobatics and effects will give you a glimpse into Vietnam’s cultural heritage. Overnight in Saigon. 

11th day Flight to Con Dao – Paradise Island Hideaway

After breakfast, a short flight will take you to Con Dao – a true island jewel. Turquoise water, bright sandy beaches in front of an impressive mountain panorama and a soothing tropical silence await you at this special place. At the airport, you will be welcomed and taken to your private oasis, Six Senses Con Dao, where exclusivity, closeness to nature and highest comfort blend harmoniously. The resort is inspired by a traditional Vietnamese fishing village and interprets its architecture in a modern, stylish way. The spacious villas with private pools and fantastic sea views offer absolute privacy. The Six Senses Con Dao is a multi-award-winning retreat for conscious luxury travelers, which convinces with sustainable architecture and respectful treatment of nature. Shoes off, feet in the sand – and arrive at the moment. Enjoy an exclusive break by the sea. Overnight at Con Dao. 

12-14. Luxury, tranquility and exclusivity at Six Senses Con Dao

Enjoy the sun, sea and fine sand under your feet as well as the exclusive comfort of your first-class resort. Time seems to pass more slowly here – ideal for a good book, barefoot doing nothing and moments that are all yours. If you feel like discovering in between, you can expect wonderful experiences around the island: crystal clear water invites you to relaxed snorkeling excursions and - depending on the season from about May to October - there is a rare opportunity to observe sea turtles in their natural habitat. At Six Senses Con Dao, tranquility, privacy, sustainable luxury and authentic nature experiences blend perfectly. Overnight stays on Con Dao.

Day 15 Farewell to Vietnam

Today your journey is slowly coming to an end. Enjoy once again the tranquility, the gentle sound of the sea and the breathtaking views from your villa before a short flight back to Saigon, from where you will embark on your home or onward journey. With many unique memories in your luggage, you will finally board your plane. Goodbye and until the next adventure together!

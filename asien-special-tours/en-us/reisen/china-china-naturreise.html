On this wonderful round trip you will get to know the greatest treasures of the natural world of China. You will hardly have time to get out of the amazement. We will show you the great wonders of nature and you will feel like you are in a different world at the sight of whimsical rock formations, mystical and endless seeming mountain worlds and lush green nature amidst valleys and villages. Take a break and recharge your energy and strength in these powerful natural landscapes. With the conclusion of this journey in the modern and vibrant metropolis Shanghai, you will get to know a completely different side of China with all its many different facets. Keep these lasting impressions and experience the power of nature up close.

1st day Arrival in Chengdu & first city orientation

Welcome to China! Your journey begins in Chengdu, where you will be expected and driven to your accommodation upon arrival at the airport. The capital of Sichuan province is a modern city, but its origins go back far into the past. The deep history of the city is clearly noticeable in the numerous, well-preserved temples to this day. Here the old and the new China have come together harmoniously. Especially pretty gardens and tea houses shape the face of the city, making Chengdu one of China’s most popular cities. After a short break in the hotel, you will take a tour of the city together with your guide. You can get a first impression of Chengdu while also visiting the trendy Kuanzhai Alley district. It is an old-fashioned neighborhood with houses in traditional architectural style, with original restaurants, tea shops and boutiques. Colorful, lively and always well attended. Overnight in Chengdu.

Day 2 Visiting the Great Buddha of Leshan

Today, visit one of China’s most important historical sites in Leshan. The place became known for its stunning 71-meter-high Buddha on the banks of Minjiang. Buddhist monks already carved it into the rock face in the 8th century over a period of more than 90 years. The Buddha is still in a remarkably good condition. The head of the huge figure alone is almost 15 meters high and provided with over 1000 helical hair knots. On its wide foot, there is room for more than 100 people. According to tradition, the Buddha was built on the initiative of a monk from Lingyun Monastery, which is located above the statue. The Buddha was built to appease the roaring river. Let yourself be enchanted by this powerful symbol, created by pure handicraft. Overnight in Chengdu.

Day 3 On the way to the traditionally revered panda bears

Today you drive northwest into the wild nature to Wolong. On the way, take a detour to the Dujiangyan irrigation plant. It is the oldest irrigation system in operation in China. Although the plant has been repeatedly renewed and improved, its basic principle has not changed to this day. Already in the Han Dynasty, the water of the river provided more than 100,000 hectares of fertile land, which is why it is now a UNESCO World Heritage Site. After you have reached Wolong, you can already look forward to the well-known and very popular panda bears. Wolong is not only the place of discovery of panda bears in the 1860s, but also still the optimal spot around the animal beauties. The park is located in a picturesque valley and offers the cute pandas a generous and beautiful habitat of forests, clear lakes and meadows by making them feel poodle comfortable. On a night tour in the Panda breeding station you will experience the animals at night, because they are more active than during the day. The plant is home to around 80 of the endangered bears. Observe the snuffy creatures and you will quickly understand the pride of the Chinese about this native species. Overnight in Wolong.

Day 4 - Express train to the fascinating mountain world of Jiuzhaigou

Today your onward journey with the modern express train goes into the fascinating mountain world of Jiuzhaigou. During the comfortable train ride, you will enjoy varied landscapes that slowly pass from fertile plains to the imposing mountainous region of western China. Upon arrival in Jiuzhaigou, you will be greeted by your local guide and embark on a guided walking tour of the famous Jiuzhaigou National Park together. The UNESCO World Heritage Site delights with turquoise blue lakes, roaring waterfalls, deep forests and snow-capped peaks. Away from the well-known viewpoints, you will experience the impressive natural scenery intensively and learn from your guide interesting facts about the unique landscape, flora and culture of the region. Overnight in Jiuzhaigou.

Day 5 A natural paradise of turquoise blue lakes and waterfalls

Continue your discovery tour through the impressive Jiuzhaigou National Park. Together with your guide, you will explore more fascinating landscape sections of this unique UNESCO World Heritage site. On varied paths you walk through dense forests, along crystal-clear lakes and past spectacular waterfalls, which are among the most beautiful natural scenery in China. Enjoy the peace and pristineness of this mountain world and let the impressive impressions of the Jiuzhaigou National Park once again consciously affect you before returning to your accommodation at the end of the day. Overnight in Jiuzhaigou.

Day 6 Landscape cinema at the train window – from Sichuan to Zhangjiajie

After breakfast, take the fast train back to Chengdu. During the train journey, varied landscapes pass you – from the mountainous regions of Sichuan to the lively regions around the metropolis of Chengdu. Here you change and continue your journey with the express train towards Zhangjiajie. The long train ride offers you the opportunity to enjoy the passing natural scenery relaxed and to review the experiences of the past days. Only in the evening will you reach Zhangjiajie, where you will already be expected by your driver and taken to your hotel. Overnight in Wulingyuan National Park.

Day 7 On paths through bizarre rock formations and lush nature

The landscape is famous for its rugged mountain landscape with vertical columns of rock and rich vegetation. The Wulingyuan National Park combines three areas: Zhangjiajie Forest Park and the two nature reserves Suoxiyu and Tianmenshan. Due to erosion and weathering, an interesting topography has developed here. Several thousand peaks of red sandstone rise to the sky, the structure of their rock layers is clearly visible. It seems as if they were made up of many giant rocks stacked on top of each other. After breakfast, drive to Tianzi Mountain. With a cable car we go to the summit. The journey opens, as far as the weather allows, a beautiful view of the surrounding landscape. Once you have reached the summit of Tianzi, a detour to He-Long Park follows, from where you will have an unforgettable view. The electric bus then continues to the landscape zone Yuanjiajie in the north of the park. From the plateau you overlook a deep valley and a chain of mountain peaks – the foresight seems endless. Overnight in Wulingyuan National Park.

Day 8 In the footsteps of scenic wonders

After breakfast, head to the impressive Huangshizhai (“Yellow Stone Highlands”), one of the most beautiful viewpoints in Zhangjiajie National Forest Park. Comfortably, you can take the cable car up to the high plateau, from where breathtaking panoramic views of the characteristic sandstone columns and the forested mountain landscapes of the region open up. At the summit, take an approximately two-hour hike along the scenic Golden Whip Stream Scenic Route and experience the unique natural backdrop up close. The path leads past bizarre rock formations, deep gorges and lush vegetation and offers impressive photo opportunities again and again. At the highest viewpoint of the forest park, you can expect numerous spectacular landscape attractions, including the famous “Southern Sky Gate” and the “Stargazer Terrace”. Overnight in Wulingyuan National Park.

Day 9 Along the Sky Path of Glass

In the morning, return to Zhangjiajie and make your way to the impressive Tianmenshan (“Heaven Mountain”). Together with your guide, you will experience one of the most spectacular cable car rides in the world: over a distance of around 7.5 kilometers, the train overcomes a difference in altitude of about 1,300 meters and takes you up to the summit of Tianmenshan. During the journey, breathtaking views open up. Once upstairs, explore the spectacular summit region on an approximately two-hour hike. Along the panoramic paths, you can expect impressive views and two special highlights: the glass heavenly paths on the west and east sides of the mountain. The transparent jetties along the rock walls provide a unique experience and enable fascinating views into the depths as well as to the majestic natural backdrop around Zhangjiajie. Afterwards, take the long escalators in several stages down to the famous Tianmen Gate, the legendary “Gate to Heaven”. At the end of this day, a parking bus will take you back to the valley via the spectacular mountain road – the famous “road of 99 curves”, which winds with numerous serpentines along the mountain and once again provides unforgettable views. Overnight in Zhangjiajie.

Day 10: Travel to Huangshan in the “Golden Mountains”

Today we take the fast train via Changsha to Huangshan, where you will be expected and taken to your hotel in the village of Bishan. Take advantage of the rest of the day and look forward to the next few days in a mystical nature. Overnight in Bishan.

11th Day Art and Culture in Anhui Province

On your excursion today, get to know more about the people of the region and their ways of life and enjoy the fascinating natural landscape. You will first visit the villages around Huangshan in Anhui province - it is considered the poorest region of the east. In earlier times, this area was called Yixian and was a historically very prosperous city and home to many businessmen of the country. Thus, you will discover magnificent and noble houses here, which were considered a sign of prosperity. Many of the buildings are over 200 years old and decorate themselves with the filigree art of timber construction. These houses can still be found in the surrounding villages. Discover these noble houses there as witnesses of a prosperous epoch. Then swing on the bike and take a bicycle tour along green rice fields. Overnight in Bishan.

Day 12: A Night Over the Clouds on Huangshan

Mysterious, bizarre and beautiful mountain regions are many in China. But hardly any is comparable to the unique beauty of Huangshan. He was once the favorite subject of all Chinese painters. The Huangshan is world famous for its "four distinctive features": strangely grown pines, strangely shaped rocks, the sea of clouds beneath its peaks and hot springs. The special geological structure and composition of the summits are typical features of this landscape. With the cable car you will float up to the summits of the Yellow Mountain, whose beauty has been sung by numerous poets and made painters dream. Although the main summit is less than 1,900 meters high, the steep rocks offer an exciting hiking trail. Even in dense fog, this area is delightful with its rocks, bamboo groves and ancient, gnarled pines. You will spend the night on the mountain today and, with a little luck, enjoy a beautiful sunset. If you get up tomorrow morning, you can also look at the sun when it rises. A pure enjoyment moment! Overnight on the Huangshan.

Day 13: From the Light of Dawn to the Globes of Shanghai

Good morning! Today the day greets you at high altitude. Get up early to enjoy the spiritual morning mood in peace and watch as the early fog around the summits is dipped in color. Let this moment take hold of you before you set off for your last stop of the journey. The way down the cable car takes over for you. Once downstairs, your guide will take you to the train station, from where you will catch the express train that will take you to the metropolis of Shanghai. Once there, your guide will welcome you and take you to your hotel. What a contrast to the last few days! Overnight in Shanghai.

Day 14 The Dragonhead metropolis Shanghai

Look forward to exploring Shanghai. During a stroll on the Bund you get a good first overview. On Shanghai’s famous waterfront, old colonial buildings such as the legendary Bank of China (built in 1912) and the old “Peace Hotel” (1930) are nestled against the modern glass and steel towers. The promenade looks particularly impressive from the ship. During your trip on the Huangpu you can see for yourself. Opposite the Bund is Pudong in the east of the city: a district of Shanghai that has sprung up in a few years, where today banks, corporations and luxury hotels reside in skyscrapers. It is also home to the 632-meter-high Shanghai Tower, which offers the second highest observation platform in the world. Drive upstairs and let the view wander over the city - a great experience. The beautiful old town of Qibao is part of the Minghang district and is a magnificent mirror of Shanghai’s past. Finally, visit the trendy district of Tianzifang. The lane labyrinth around Taikang Lu has attracted artists, artisans and young designers. Many shops and galleries with funny clothes, jewelry and original souvenirs can be found here. Overnight in Shanghai. Overnight in Shanghai.   

Day 15 Farewell to China

Today, unfortunately, it means saying goodbye to a fascinating country from which you will take numerous lasting impressions with you. You will be taken to the airport from where you will start your onward or return journey.

In the heart of Mongolia

Mongolia Travel: This travel creation of Asia Special Tours takes you through the heart of Mongolia. Experience the hidden treasures. Now ask me.

This journey through beautiful Mongolia offers you not only cultural highlights but also unforgettable impressions of the endless expanse of the Asian country. You will meet locals who breed goats or camels, welcome you to ancient monasteries and introduce you to their traditional way of life. The overnight stays in Mongolian yurt camps round off your impression of land and people. Desert cities, flaming cliffs, impressive rock carvings and the longest walking dune of South Gobi will surely enchant you. Join us on a discovery tour and get to know the legendary Mongolia.

1st day Arrival in Ulaanbaatar

Welcome to Ulaanbaatar, the capital of Mongolia. Your guide greets you at the airport and accompanies you to your hotel. You then begin your sightseeing at Sukhbaatar Square in the heart of the city. Two impressive monuments commemorate the conqueror Genghis Khan and the revolutionary leader Damddiny Sukhbaatar, who secured independence from China in 1921. Gandan Monastery is also worth a visit. Built in the Tibetan style in 1835, it soon became a center of Buddhist learning in Mongolia. During the 20th century it played a special role: having survived the communist destruction of the 30s as one of the few undamaged monasteries, it was allowed to resume limited operations in 1944 as the only monastery in the country. Depending on the time available, also visit the Museum of History and Ethnography and the Winter Palace of Bogd Khan. Overnight in Ulaanbaatar.

Day 2 The Red and White Rocks of Zagaan Suvarga

Your overland journey begins today. Following Mongolian tradition, travel with the sun, initially heading south into the Gobi Desert, the world's northernmost and fifth-largest desert. The road network has improved in recent years, and part of the journey follows the new highway from the capital into the Gobi. At some points you leave the paved road. Enjoy your first impressions of the wide-open landscapes along the way. After about 3½ hours, reach the small desert town of Mandalgobi for lunch and a glimpse into the daily lives of its roughly 10,000 residents. There are shops and simple local restaurants. In the afternoon, reach the mountains of Zagaan Suvarga. Because of their shapes and colors, local people call these impressive formations the white and red stupas. Centuries of erosion have created vast rocky cliffs in shades of pink, red, and orange. Stay in a yurt camp and admire the seemingly endless night sky. Overnight in the yurt camp.

Day 3 The Rock Drawings at Mount Del Uul

After breakfast, take a trip to Mount Del Uul, where you can admire rock drawings from the Bronze Age on four kilometers. Then continue your journey. The endless expanses of the desert and the solitude interrupted only once in a while by a few camels and goat breeders will make you feel like you are somewhere at the end of the world. On hot summer days, it may even happen that a “Fata Morgana” appears in front of you, but dissolves into thin air again at the next moment. Late in the evening you will reach your yurt camp in the middle of the desert, where you will spend the night. Overnight at the yurt camp.

Day 4 Excursion to the Vulture Gorge & Khongoryn Els Sand Dune

After fortifying yourself at breakfast, set off to marvel at the longest walking dunes, the Khongoryn Els Sand Dunes in South Gobi. On the way there you make a stop at the vulture gorge (Yol gorge). This is located in the Gurwan Saikhan National Park, on the foothills of the Altai Mountains. After visiting a small natural history museum, you will explore the gorge on a short hike and, with a bit of luck, meet the Capricorns and wild sheep living here. Then continue your journey. In the middle of the landscape, an extraordinary mountain of sand shoots up, which is up to 120 meters high and stretches over a length of 180 km. Together with the dark mountain range in the background, there is a particularly beautiful panorama. Unforgettable in addition to this breathtaking sight will also be the meetings with camel breeders and, with a bit of luck, a gazelle herd. If you wish, today you can also prove your talent in camel riding and rent one of the “desert ships” (for a fee). Overnight at the yurt camp.

Day 5: Flaming Cliffs

The onward journey to Bayanzag leads through a landscape consisting of mountains, rocks and sand. Arriving in Bayanzag, you will visit one of the most visited attractions in Mongolia - the "Flaming Cliffs", which got their name from their bright orange color. In the 1920s, the American scientist and adventurer Roy Chapman Andrews discovered a complete dinosaur nest with eggs here, which led to this place as well as the rest of Mongolia becoming an important place for paleontological research. Overnight at the yurt camp.

Day 6 Visit to Ongi Monastery

Your next destination is the ruins of the hidden monastery “Ongi”. The monastery once housed over 500 Buddhist monks and is located on the river of the same name. During religious persecutions in the 30In the years before, it was almost completely destroyed and many of the monks living there were sent to labor camps. Today there is a small, new monastery. With a little luck you will meet the monks who take care of the monastery and have the opportunity for a personal conversation. Overnight at the yurt camp.

Day 7 Khogno Khan Mountains

The destination of your day’s stage is Khogno Khan Camp near Karakorum. When climbing a sand dune, the panorama of the unique steppe desert will delight you. With a little luck, various wild animals can be observed on a temporary lake. Overnight at the yurt camp.

Day 8: In the footsteps of Genghis Khan

As the sun rises over the infinite expanses of Central Mongolia, head towards Karakorum, under Genghis Khan and his successors capital of the former Mongolian Empire. Scientific excavations repeatedly reveal new impressive relics of this multicultural trading metropolis founded in 1220 and the place is still one of the most famous and probably also worth seeing in the country. After visiting the excavation site and museum, you will visit the Erdene Zuu monastery built on the ruins of Karakorum in the 16th century, one of the largest in Mongolia. Unfortunately, only three of the formerly more than 60 buildings have been preserved after the Cultural Revolution, but with a total of 108 stupas and the white border wall, the monastery still offers an impressive picture. Extensive renovation work also helps to restore the shine of the building complex. A detailed tour will take you to the large prayer hall, various temple halls and last but not least through a museum dedicated to the complex. After the visits, you will return to Khogno Khan. Overnight at the yurt camp.

Day 9 Back to Ulaanbaatar

Today your journey will take you back to the capital of Mongolia. The rest of the day is at your disposal or you can use it for further visits to the capital. Overnight in Ulaanbaatar.

Day 10 Departure

Today it is unfortunately to say goodbye – surely you have packed your suitcases with many nine impressions. You will be driven to the airport from where you will start your return journey.

1st day Arrival in Beijing & time for first explorations

Welcome to China! Your adventure for the whole family begins in Beijing, the exciting capital of the "Middle Kingdom". At the airport, your personal guide is already waiting for you and will take you to your hotel. As a political and cultural center of the country, Beijing can look back on a 3000-year history, which is reflected in a globally unique cultural heritage. Thanks to the 2008 Olympic Games, China’s capital, often dismissed as a giant village, has transformed into a modern world city. Just as the emperors once built their city as an image of the universe, China’s rulers of the 21st century created their capital as an image of a new, powerful China. On your tour you will discover the center of Beijing, the famous "Forbidden City" with the Imperial Palace. This magical place was the seat of the Chinese emperors of two dynasties - the Ming and the Qing. The crowd is as overwhelming as the architecture, the colorfully painted palace halls and the pagoda-like roofs. Overnight in Beijing.

2nd day Chinese for beginners & Beijing ducks

Today you can unpack your language skills and get to know a few words of this language that is so foreign to us. In the morning, you will be taken to a language institute where you will take part in a half-day topical Chinese language course. You will have fun getting to know a few Chinese words. Afterwards you can prove your knowledge on a shopping tour. Your family will receive a shopping list of typical items, where you can search for different items in a classic Chinese supermarket. It will certainly be a lot of fun for everyone. You will be amazed at how much your children can remember and what there is to buy in Chinese supermarkets. Your guide will then tell you if you have found the right items. In the evening you are invited to dinner. The famous Beijing duck will demand your full attention. After a complicated process, it is dried, marinated and finally cooked over the open fire. The highlight of this dish is the crispy tasty skin, which is shaped piecemeal into thin rolls at the table. Let them taste it. Overnight in Beijing.

Day 3 Beijing Highlight: The Great Wall is waiting for you!

Ready for a mighty encounter? In the morning, head to the Great Wall at Mutianyu section. With a cable car you drive up to the impressive building. The foreigner enjoys the excursion here like a journey through time: from the modern capital beyond the outskirts to the rough mountainous area, where an army of workers once walled up a wealth. Once you have arrived at the top, you have the opportunity to explore this gigantic wall on a small hike and enjoy the all-round view. Downstairs it goes with sow and braus in the summer toboggan run. Great fun for young and old! Overnight in Beijing.

Day 4: Continue to Xi’an & Bicycle Tour on the City Wall

In the morning, you will be picked up from the hotel and taken to the station for your train ride to Xi'an. Upon arrival, you will already be expected and received. Afterwards, take a small bike ride on the city wall – the best preserved in all of China – which forms a rectangle around the city center about 14 kilometers long. The city fortifications from the Ming Dynasty are still an impressive sight today. Enjoy a traditional dough bag dining in the evening: artfully shaped bags made of wheat flour, in all possible forms and with the most diverse fillings will surely meet your taste. They are classically steamed in bamboo baskets, but can also be cooked or fried in hot oil. Good appetite! At the end of the day, visit Grand Tang Everbright City, a modern neighborhood that boasts stunning light shows, ornate installations and historic performances. Traditional architecture and state-of-the-art technology combine to give you an unforgettable experience. Overnight in Xi'an.

Day 5 - The Emperor's clay warriors

In the morning, the trip goes to Lintong, about 35 kilometers east. There are the famous terracotta warriors in line. Discovered by chance in 1974, the gigantic tomb at the foot of Li Mountain was built by the first emperor Qin Shihuangdi. So far, archaeologists have unearthed around 7,200 figures of soldiers on foot and horseback. Remarkable: All these earthen warriors are individually designed, no two alike - an artistic masterpiece! It represents a complete army of that time. Since the troops have been deployed in battle order, their localization can be approximately reconstructed. Therefore, it is considered certain that there are still other sculptures on the huge terrain, which has only been exposed to about a quarter so far. In the nearby terracotta warrior reproduction factory, you will see how the famous warriors are recreated by over 40 technicians. Their children are invited to participate in a workshop where they can design miniature terracotta warriors themselves from clay. Overnight in Xi'an.

Day 6 - Express train to Chengdu

On a train ride with the spectacular fast trains of China it goes quickly on to Chengdu. The capital of Sichuan province is a modern city, but its origins go back far into the past. The great history of the city is clearly noticeable in the numerous, well-preserved temples to this day. And yet the city seems young and alive. Here the old and the new China have come together harmoniously. Especially pretty gardens and tea houses shape the face of the city, making Chengdu one of China’s most popular cities. Your guide will take you on a visit to the Chengdu Museum of Nature, which is one of the most important museums in the country. With around 70 million different exhibits, the museum offers an impressive collection ranging from ancient fossils to flora and fauna to gigantic dinosaur skeletons. In the evening, a culinary highlight awaits you: a traditional Sichuan firepot, also known as a hotpot. This famous dish of the region is characterized by its spicy broth and the variety of fresh ingredients, which you can cook at will even in the broth. The Sichuan firepot is a feast for the senses and offers a rich flavor range ranging from fiery pungent to mild. Overnight in Chengdu.

Day 7 Experience a day as a panda bear caretaker

Today will be one of the highlights for you and your children. You will visit the Wolong Panda Bear Breeding Station, which takes care of the elaborate care, preservation and research of the cuddly creatures. In various courses you can help the professionals. The activities range from tidying up the enclosures, to fodder preparation and feeding to planting bamboo and trees. From the caregivers you will learn a lot about the valued, Chinese national animal: what care he needs, what his habitat is and how he lives together with humans. For this purpose, you will be shown documentation and you will get insights into the breeding and research station. In the end, everyone can also participate in a small quiz, where what they have learned is tested right away. Look forward to this special day with particularly sniffy creatures. Overnight in Chengdu.

8th day Painting panorama & deep green rice terraces

In the morning you will be taken to the airport from where you will take your onward flight to Guilin. Once there, you will already be welcomed by your personal guide. Together you set off for a car trip to Yangshuo. Now it becomes clear why everyone is so enthusiastic about the area around Guilin: The road leads through fields and opens up a panorama of staggered mountain rows. In the afternoon, explore the scenic landscape on a guided bicycle tour. This ends at the boat dock, where you transfer to a raft. Sit back comfortably on the subsequent bamboo raft ride on the Yulong River, a quiet side arm of the Li River. They are still in a dream setting. Seen from certain angles, the hills form shapes reminiscent of animals, pagodas or plants. This inspired the local population to give the mountains imaginative names such as Mondsichelberg, Elephant Risselberg or Horse Painting Mountain. This unique landscape will surely delight you! Overnight in Yangshuo.

9th Day Meet Kung Fu & Free Afternoon

Today, you will learn more about the famous Chinese martial arts Kung Fu. From films by Jackie Chan or Bruce Lee, the Chinese martial arts from Kung Fu to Karate became known. You too can test your fighting skills today. During a private Kung Fu course, you will learn about first steps, stepps or boxing movements and practice your balance and coordination skills. At the end, you may already be able to demonstrate a sequence of kicks and stepps. The rest of the day you can do as you wish. Enjoy the green nature around Yangshuo, or relax - for example at the hotel's swimming pool. Overnight in Yangshuo.

Day 10 On to the glitter metropolis Shanghai

Your last destination on this trip is the modern metropolis of Shanghai. Other names for Shanghai, by the way, are "Gateway to the World", "Dragon Head Metropolis", "Paris of the East" or "Pearl of the Orient". In the morning you will be driven to the airport in Guilin, from where you will fly to Shanghai. Upon arrival at the airport, you will be picked up and drive to your hotel with the famous Transrapid at high speed. There you can arrive in peace. Your accommodation is located directly on the waterfront, where you can take a first exploring tour. On the lively river you will discover the elements of the old and new city. Impressive views of the modern skyline of skyscrapers on one side and romantic Yu gardens on the other show you the flourishing atmosphere in the metropolis. Overnight in Shanghai.

Day 11: From the heights of the Shanghai Tower to the depths of the sea

On the Friendship Highway, which leads to Kathmandu, your journey will take you on to Gyantse. The ride takes you through one of the most spectacular landscapes in the world. Along the way, a detour will take you to a viewpoint near the sacred Yamdrok Lake, where you can picnic and get some legs. The turquoise blue lake, set in many color shades, is one of the four sacred lakes of Tibet and offers a truly spectacular sight. The trail takes you down the impressive Karola Pass at over 5,000 meters into the city of Gyantse. More than in other cities, ancient Tibet has been preserved in Gyantse with its white houses and colored window cladding. Here you will visit the monastery complex Palkhor Chöde, an 18 religious and residential building in the old town. Impressive is especially the Kumbum, the only walk-in relic shrine that has survived the times. From the simple gods on the lower levels, one comes up to the Most High, the origin of all being. Whoever celebrates the stupa clockwise and shows his honor to the deities in each chapel of the four floors, can increasingly purify his consciousness during the ascent – at least faith. Overnight in Gyantse.

12th day time for yourself and your family

Today is only in the sign of your family! Enjoy the hours together without a program and explore Shanghai in all its facets on your own. Overnight in Shanghai.

Day 13: Farewell to China

Today it means for your family to say goodbye again to this fascinating country. Surely you have packed your suitcases with many great impressions. You will be driven to the airport, from where you will start your home or onward journey.

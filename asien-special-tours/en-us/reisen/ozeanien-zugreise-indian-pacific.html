Australia Train Journey - From Perth to Sydney by Indian Pacific

Australia Train Journey: Enjoy all the highlights from Perth to Sydney on this luxurious train journey from Asia Special Tours. Ask them about it and give them advice.

Some of Australia’s best beaches, the busiest markets, wide parks and world-class restaurants offer Perth, the gateway to the southwest. Giant trees and whales, as well as world-class wines, art galleries, boutiques and the best surf spots can be found in this region. With your own car, you travel in the southwest individually, flexibly and independently. We book the most beautiful accommodation for you in advance. Once back in Perth, continue the journey in the legendary Indian Pacific, a train of superlatives. Enjoy the amenities of Platinum service on your leisurely drive through one of the most secluded areas on Earth, Nullarbor Plain.

1st day Arrival in Perth

You will be received at the airport in Perth and taken to the hotel. The rest of the day is at your disposal. Arrive and relax from the long flight journey. We recommend a walk through the bustling center with a visit to the vast, inner-city Kings Park. Overnight in Perth.

Day 2 Excursion to Rottnest Island by helicopter

Have you heard of the Quokkas? These cute short-tailed kangaroos the size of domestic cats are the undisputed stars of Rottnest Island. You can find countless, funny Quokka selfies on the Internet and today you have the opportunity to shoot a few on the island itself. The 11 x 4.5 km large and close to the coast attracts visitors with great beaches and paradisiacal nature. There are the southernmost corals in the world to see as well as dolphins and sea lions. Motorized traffic is prohibited and you are on foot or by bicycle. You will be picked up from their Perth accommodation and visit the Perth Mint and Kings Park, from where you will have fantastic views over Perth, the Swan River and Darling Scarp. The lively harbor town of Fremantle near Perth offers a wide selection of great seafront restaurants where you can enjoy your lunch. They fly by helicopter to Rottnest Island. On a guided bicycle tour you have time to enjoy the beach and go swimming. Afterwards you will fly by helicopter back to Perth and will be taken to your hotel. Overnight in Perth.

Day 3: Perth via Bunbury to Busselton

Take over your rental car today. The size and category of the vehicle will be determined at your booking according to your individual wishes. Mandurah is a popular holiday destination south of Perth. The estuary here was once a meeting place for Aborigines. In Bunbury, Boulters Lookout offers impressive panoramic views of the city. Here you will find some excellent hotels and cafes for your lunch break. When visiting Bunbury, make sure you don’t miss a visit to the Dolphin Discovery Centre, which is just a few kilometers from the city center. There you can experience bottlenose dolphins (Great Bottlenose Dolphins), who regularly visit Koombana Bay. From here, head to Busselton, one of the oldest cities in Western Australia. Busselton is a seaside resort and at the same time the center of this agricultural region. It is located 241 km south of Perth on the coast of Geographe Bay and on the Vasse River. The wineries around Margaret River are easily accessible from here. Overnight in Margaret River

Day 4 Margaret River Region & Leeuwin Naturaliste National Park

Today is at your disposal. An underwater observatory is located at the very end of the pier in Busselton and on a guided tour you have the opportunity to observe more than 300 marine animals through eleven windows at different depths. Drive to Dunsborough and 13 kilometers along Cape Naturaliste Road to reach the Cape Naturaliste Lighthouse. Built in 1903, the Cape Naturaliste lighthouse offers an untouched view of the rugged coastline and the Leeuwin Naturaliste National Park. From June to December, the whales make their annual trek along the coast to get to their summer feeding ground in Antarctica. The rugged sea and moon-like landscape of the western headlands are a stark contrast to the gentle coastal scenery and tranquil lake of Bunker Bay to the east. Ngilgi Cave is named after its history with the Australian natives and offers a collection of stalagmites and dripstones. A guided tour of the cave is recommended. Or spend the afternoon visiting wineries, tasting gourmet specialties and exploring the beaches. Overnight in Margaret River.

Day 5 Cape Leeuwin Lighthouse and Karri Forest

Leave south on one of the most scenic itineraries in Western Australia, Caves Road. Don’t miss a stop for a walk through the impressive Boranup forest and visit one of the caves. The Cape Leeuwin Lighthouse, located south of Augusta, is the southernmost point where the Indian and Southern Oceans meet. In the lush rainforests around Pemberton you will find some of the largest hardwood trees in the world. Enjoy a forest walk or take the Pemberton Tram, which winds around some of the world’s largest trees. For a spectacular view, venture up Pemberton’s climbing trees. The large trees – “Karri” – are Pemberton’s biggest attraction and very impressive. Warren National Park has the Dave Evans Bicentennial Tree. From here you can see the lush Karri forest from a bird’s eye view, even from a platform halfway up. Pemberton has become a gourmet and wine region and you can visit some wineries for wine tasting and indulge in the local delicacies, including Marron and Trout. Overnight in Pemberton.

Day 6: Pemberton via Walpole to Albany

Walpole is located 120 kilometers south of Pemberton. Drive deeper into the forest on Hilltop Road and look for the giant Giant Tingle Tree. On your way to Denmark, make sure you stop at the Giant Tree Top Walk in Walpole-Nornalup National Park! There is a floating bridge that runs 38 m above the forest floor. Surrounded by 20,000 hectares of towering trees, it is an exciting yet relaxed way to get to know the magical southern forest and admire the grandeur of these towering giants. Before heading to Albany, we recommend stopping at Elephant Rock, a massive rounded rock formation. The surrounding countryside of Albany and the beaches are very beautiful. Let yourself be transported back in time and explore the history of Albany’s penal prison, old taverns, whaling ships, settler huts and listed houses. You can end the day by tasting fresh marine specialties! Overnight in Albany.

7th day Albany with beaches, rugged coast and its whale history

Today is free to explore Albany and its surroundings. We have a number of suggestions for you to make the most of your day. Be sure to check out the Brig Amity here, a replica of the ship that brought Albany its first settlers and cargo. Drive to Torndirrup National Park, known for its natural attractions along the rugged coastline. Very close by is Natural Bridge, a fascinating granite formation in the shape of a bridge. Whales can be watched playing and frolicking in King Georg Sound during the season from July to October, and you can either watch them from the shore or take part in a booots trip. In Middleton Beach you will find five kilometers of untouched white sand beach. After lunch, visit the Black Mountains, a unique geological attraction that is at the center of mysterious stories and plays a major role in Aboriginal legends. Continue to Cooktown, where you’ll enjoy great 360-degree panoramic views from Grassy Hill on Cooktown, the Coral Sea and the Endeavour River. Overnight in Albany.

Day 8: Albany to Perth

Leave in a northern direction to Perth. Right next to Mount Barker is a Banksia farm where you’ll find scenic wildflowers in a five-acre garden, alongside thousands of native Australian plants. You can participate in a visit here. Further north of Mount Barker is Stirling Range National Park – the only Alpine area in Western Australia and it’s famous for its colorful wildflowers. Five of the mountain peaks are over 1500 meters high and often shrouded in fog. Perth, founded by British settlers in 1829, has old buildings such as the Government House, Town Hall and His Majesty’s Theatre. The oldest park is Stirling Gardens, the oldest building is the Old Courthouse. You may also feel like going to the beach and driving to one of the 19 beaches that belong to Perth. Take the West Coast Highway along the coast and explore Scarborough, Hillary's Harbour and Sorrento in northern Perth or Cottesloe in the south. Popular and good restaurants can be found in Subiaco, Mt Lawley, Leederville and Northbridge. Overnight in Perth.

Day 9 - On the road with the Indian Pacific

Take a journey from the Indian Ocean to the Pacific Ocean through rolling hillside landscapes, outback resorts as in the wild west, lush vineyards and historic places. You will travel a total of 4352 km on this remarkable route. Off Train Excursions are welcome breaks on the way. The Platinum service offers its guests a transcontinental holiday with luxurious extras such as Platinum transfers, breakfast in the cabin and access to the Platinum Club. During the day, your cabin is configured as a private lounge with seating and a table with sofa. At night, the lounges are converted into a comfortable bedroom with a double bed or two single beds. What if the hunger comes? Head out of your cabin to the Platinum Club, where an all-inclusive menu of board chefs is prepared with fresh local dishes. Our seasonal menu offers delicious dishes such as saltwater barramundi and grilled kangaroo fillet. The Platinum service includes all meals such as breakfast in the room, lunch and dinner on board. Vegetarian or special diet cuisine is available. Overnight on the Indian Pacific train.

Day 10: Outback of Nullarbor Plain

Upon awakening, you will see the wide horizon behind the window - you have already arrived at the edges of Nullarbor Plain. A wide, blue sky awaits you, the silence of the desert landscape, interrupted only by the kangaroo packs or a circling bird of prey. Once you’ve refreshed yourself and enjoyed a relaxing breakfast, get off the train and make a short stop at Cook. Once a bustling railroad community, now a ghost town, Cook gives new meaning to the phrase “in the middle of nowhere.” Get a sense of the seclusion of the Outback as you wander through Cook’s abandoned buildings and learn about the city’s fascinating history. Overnight on the Indian Pacific train.

Day 11 Arrival in Adelaide

In the morning you will reach the train station in Adelaide and be taken to the hotel. The rest of the day is at your disposal. Arrive and look around. We recommend a walk through the center with a visit to the parks, markets or museums. Overnight in Adelaide.

12th Day Wines and Specialties of McLaren Vale

One of South Australia’s best kept secrets is the fertile McLaren Vale, where grapes, almond plantations and olive groves grow near the sea. In addition to wine, the Mediterranean climate has favored the cultivation of almonds and olives in the plantations. Today you will go on a personal tour with a special wine guide including a ride along the beautiful coast. You will have time to visit the Dridan Fine Arts Gallery, owned by renowned Australian artist David Dridan. Lunch at a well-known McLaren Vale restaurant, where you can enjoy the local cuisine with fresh regional produce. Take part in a private tasting at the Coriole winery, where grapes have been cultivated since 1920 and the first Shiraz was bottled in 1970. Coriole has earned a reputation for producing first-class, full-bodied red wines. Enjoy excellent wines in a beautiful environment overlooking the sea. Other tastings at other well-known family wineries such as d'Arenbergs, Wirra Wirra and Pennys Hill. Overnight in Adelaide.

13th day Dolphin Watch Cruise

Embark on board the Temptation this morning and see wild dolphins off the coast of Adelaide up close. The sighting is guaranteed! Watch the animals swim near the catamaran, just an arm’s length away. During the cruise you will see up to 3 or 4 different species of dolphins. If you want to go into the water on a cloth feel with the marine mammals, this is possible here (advance booking required!) The afternoon is at your free disposal. You can enjoy fresh seafood in Glenelg, a suburb of Adelaide right on the beach, or go shopping in the city. Or visit one of the museums. I'm sure you can think of something. Overnight in Adelaide.

14th day flight to Kangaroo Island: Three days in Australia’s zoo without fences

In Adelaide, you will be taken to the airport to reach your flight to Kangaroo Island. Your guide will pick you up from the airport and head to Seal Bay Conservation Park. Here you will learn how the Australian sea lion survives the rigors of the southern ocean. Continuing south, enjoy a pleasant, easy walk through the bush and come out at the foot of the spectacular and huge sand dunes of Little Sahara. From the south coast we cross the rural areas in the middle of the island via Parndana Wildlife Park. This park has been set up as an "animal shelter" where sick, injured, orphaned or unwanted animals find a home. Lunch is a picnic of delicious and fresh ingredients, complemented by a selection of drinks. The north coast beaches of Snellings and Stokes Bay are perfect for a walk. Enjoy the sound of waves breaking on the untouched sand. Later in the day, Lathami National Park awaits. Watch the kangaroos and wallabies. This is also the habitat of the Glossy Black Cockatoo and the Echidna (stingy ant-eater). The first day ends with your return to your island accommodation. Overnight at Kangaroo Island.

Day 15: Flinders Chase National Park and Hanson Bay Sanctuary

Today we drive to Rocky River, the headquarters of Flinders Chase National Park. Kangaroos lounge here, and Cape Barren geese quench their hunger with grass. Admirals Arch, an ancient limestone formation near the ocean, is a perfect resting place for a colony of active New Zealand seals. Continue on the spectacular coast from Cape du Couedic to Remarkable Rocks, where the forces of nature knock sculpture out of granite. Now it’s time for a BBQ of marinated steak, onions and potatoes prepared by your guide. This is complemented by fresh salads, local wines and other refreshments. After dinner, we head to Hanson Bay Wildlife Sanctuary, 9,500 hectares of protected land with an abundance of koalas easily seen hanging in the local "Tasmanian Blue Gum" trees. Your guide will take you on a bush-lined path to 'Grassdale'. Here you can observe packs of kangaroos. You will also hear the many parrots and other bird species that live in the tall rubber trees. Enjoy the majestic scenery of Point Ellen, where you can search for shells on the beach and possibly spot a penguin in its colony. Overnight at Kangaroo Island.

16th day Pelicans, first settlers and delicacies on Kangaroo Island

Today, visit the town of American River and watch the pelicans. We take a walk along the ocean, through the tall Sheoak and Gum trees. Go east of the island and explore the picturesque coastal town of Penneshaw. On the way, we can stop for a wine tasting at Sunset Winery. Enjoy your picnic lunch here with roasted beef fillet, fresh salads and delicious fruit among the Paper Bark trees that line the Chapman River. Enjoy another glass of wine and stroll along the beautiful Antechamber Bay to search for penguins and mussels. Climb the oldest lighthouse in South Australia at Cape Willoughby on the far eastern tip of the island. Perhaps a visit to the Emu Ridge Eucalyptus Distillery or Clifford's Honey Farm can pique your interest. Finish the day by the sea, sit on the rocks in Kingscote and marvel at the pelican that floats in the air waiting for its daily food. Sit back now and think of her best moments on Kangaroo Island as you head back to the airport and check in for your return flight to Adelaide. Overnight in Adelaide.

17th day The surroundings of Adelaide with its German history

Today we will take you for half a day into the lovely scenery of the Adelaide Hills, including the breathtaking views from the top of Mount Lofty. Visit the Cleland Wildlife Sanctuary and the historic municipality of Hahndorf, which is proud of its German heritage. Many galleries and craft shops are housed in the old historical cottages. The rest of the day is for their relaxation, before tomorrow it goes on with the Indian Pacific to Sydney. Overnight in Adelaide.

Day 18 With the Indian Pacific to the mine Broken Hill Outback

This morning we return on board the legendary Indian Pacific train. During lunch, watch the willow trees turn into a hard mallee bush as they approach Broken Hill. Known as "Silver City", Broken Hill is one of Australia's legendary outback locations characterized by wide streets, country pubs, the Royal Flying Doctor Service and School of the Air. Here you can visit the studio of the late Pro Hart or see the miners’ monument, where the harsh reality of working in the Broken Hill mines is shown throughout the centuries. Or let the drag queens entertain you at the Palace Hotel, referring to the cult film “Priscilla, Queen of the Desert”. As the train winds through the magnificent Blue Mountains, there is plenty of opportunity to sit back and enjoy the stunning scenery. You have already traveled from the Indian Ocean to the Pacific through some of the most impressive landscapes on Earth. Overnight on the Indian Pacific Train

19th day Arrival in Sydney

Arrival at the train station and transfer to the booked hotel. The rest of the day is at your disposal to experience the world city of Sydney. We recommend a walking tour in the historic district “The Rocks” or a ride on the viewing tower “Sydney Tower” in the heart of the city with a view of the natural harbor. Overnight in Sydney.

20th day excursion to the Blue Mountains

Today we head west to Featherdale Wildlife Park, where you get close to the typical Australian wildlife. Today’s destination is the Blue Mountains (World Heritage Site), a more than 1000 m high sandstone plateau with deep gorges and waterfalls. The name comes from the bluish haze that typically lies over the dense eucalyptus forests of the national park. At the Wentworth Falls, explore the Princess Lookout with fantastic views of the trees. At Echo Point with the world-famous rock formation “The Three Sisters”, visit the Waradah Aboriginal Centre, a cultural center of native Aboriginal tribes. Afterwards we will visit the Govetts Leap viewpoint and enjoy a delicious lunch in a historic inn. The journey continues to Mt Banks and the Mt Tomah Botanical Garden. After crossing the Hawkesbury River, you will find a short stop in the country town of Windsor before returning to the City via Harbour Bridge. Overnight in Sydney.

Experience the 21st day of Sydney in the luxury vintage car from 1964

Your day starts at 9.30 a.m., where you’ll be greeted by your local guide and his buddy Horace – a 1964 vintage car and the most popular luxury car ever made in Australia. Stylishly cruise through Sydney, with the beguiling scent of fine leather in your nose, leaning back into soft lambskin seat covers. Start in the center with a ride around “The Rocks”. Thanks to your guide, you can discover a thriving inner-city scene where Aboriginal murals and contemporary street art decorate the alleys with turn-of-the-century terrace houses. Get ready for the stories of rising home prices, local architecture, Aboriginal lifestyle and culture. No trip to Sydney would be complete without a visit to the beaches. First, we will take you to a cemetery by the sea that offers breathtaking views of the sandstone coast. From there, you’ll head to three of Sydney’s most famous inner-city beaches: Bronte, Tamarama and Bondi. Cruise through the hills of Sydney’s most exclusive suburbs and arrive at your final destination: a secluded beach with panoramic views of the harbor! Overnight in Sydney.

22nd Day Flight Safari to Cottage Point

With a seaplane we will take you in 20 minutes flight time from the hectic city center of Sydney to a quiet bay at the excellent Cottage Point Inn. This fine, small restaurant is located right on the waterfront in Ku-Ring-Gai Chasing National Park and is known for its modern and exceptional cuisine using Australian specialties. Enjoy a relaxed 3-course lunch with excellent wines before boarding the seaplane again and taking your flight back to Sydney Harbour. Along the way, you will have the unique opportunity to take great photos of the Harbour Bridge and the Opera House from the air and finally land again in Rose Bay. Overnight in Sydney.

23rd day Departure from Sydney

Today the program ends. We will pick you up from the hotel and take you to the airport. Farewell and return or flight.

On this journey, the following applies: The path is also the goal! With the express trains of China you travel through the country. You will see the beauty of the country passing by. Over mountains past lush green nature, through steppe-like terrain along rivers, lakes and the gigantic cities of China you will discover a lot. Experience this unique feeling with China’s high-speed trains to travel long distances in a short time and enjoy the comfort of the trains. The stays in Beijing and Shanghai show you the polarizing China of tradition and modernity. Cultural highlights such as the Great Wall and the Terracotta Army at Xi’an will give you breathtaking moments. And the hilly tea province around Hangzhou will reveal to you the beauty of this preciousness. Look forward to a multifaceted journey.

1st day Arrival in Beijing

Welcome to China! Your journey begins in Beijing, the exciting capital of the “Middle Kingdom”. At the airport, your personal guide is already waiting for you and driving you to your hotel. The rest of the day is at your disposal. As a political and cultural center of the country, Beijing can look back on a 3000-year history, which is reflected in a globally unique cultural heritage. Thanks to the 2008 Olympic Games, China’s capital, often dismissed as a giant village, has transformed into a modern world city. Just as the emperors once built their city as a reflection of the universe, China today has created a capital for itself as a reflection of a new, great country. Take the time to take a look around. Overnight in Beijing.

2nd Day Imperial Magnificent Buildings & Contemporary Art

A day full of cultural treasures and remarkable art awaits you. The Temple of Heaven, to which you will first pay your visit, is one of the outstanding testimonies of religious architecture in the Middle Kingdom, from the Ming period (1368 to 1644). An exquisite example of China’s wooden buildings, constructed without the help of a single nail, is the 39-meter-high round hall of harvest victims. On your tour you will also discover the center of Beijing, the famous “Forbidden City” with the Imperial Palace. This magical place was the seat of the Chinese emperors of two dynasties – the Ming and the Qing – and for half a millennium was inaccessible to those who did not belong to the court. An overwhelming architectural masterpiece. Stroll through the streets with your guide and be impressed by the overwhelming scenery of modernity and tradition. You can also try a culinary highlight of the city today – the Peking Duck. This famous dish is truly celebrated. The duck is dried and marinated and then cooked slowly over the open fire. The tasty meat and especially the crispy skin, which is turned into mouth-sized rolls at table, are an absolute taste experience. Overnight in Beijing.

3rd Day Once standing on the Great Wall

Get ready for a powerful encounter. The Great Wall is waiting for you! To experience them far away from the tourist crowds, travel to Gubeikou’s Great Wall, some 130 kilometers from Beijing. This section was once built along a mountain range to protect the gateway to the capital from the Mongols and Manchurs. Along with the sections at Shanhaiguan and Juyongguan, Gubeikou was central to defense against incursions from the north. Of the wall with its watchtowers, fortresses and other military buildings are today mainly ruins left, because this section was not restored. It is therefore still beautifully authentic and surrounded by a fantastic landscape. The view of the curved wall winding over the ridges of Yanshan is simply breathtaking! Explore this gigantic structure on a two- to three-hour hike and enjoy the view. After this unique experience, you will return to Beijing. Overnight in Beijing.

Day 4: Express train to Pingyao in ancient China

In the morning you will be driven to the train station, from where you will take the fast train to Pingyao. The ancient but very lively city, where old commercial signs can still be found instead of neon luminous signs on the houses, is surrounded by the longest, completely preserved city wall from the Ming period. In the shadow of this more than six kilometers long city wall, one- and two-storey black brick houses duck. Only the stained roofs of the temples stand out. The entire old town of Pingyao was declared a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1997. Together with your guide, you will also visit the picturesque Ming and Qing Age courthouses of the city, as well as the ancient Chinese town hall of the Yamen Hof and the old bank “Rishengchang”. Stroll through the streets and enjoy this unique atmosphere. Overnight in Pingyao.

5th day Visit to the village of Zhangbi & trendy district in Xi’an

Today you will leave Pingyao towards Xi’an. But before you start, visit the old family castle of the Wang family in the small village of Zhangbi. It was created in the 6th century and has thus lived through many epochs of Chinese history. The magnificent underground tunnel system of the plant initially served military purposes. Today, many locals use the tunnels as spacious grain and storage stores. Today you can visit the tunnel maze and be impressed by the traditional, North Chinese construction. Afterwards, take the fast train from Pingyao station to Xi’an. There you will already be expected and brought to your hotel. In Xi'an is the trendy district "Grand Tang Everbright City". During an evening visit, experience impressive light shows, ornate installations and lively historical performances. Surrounded by traditional architecture and state-of-the-art technology, immerse yourself in historical heritage while enjoying vibrant activity and modern entertainment. Overnight in Xi’an.

Day 6 - Emperor's clay warriors & Chinese delicacies

Today, another big highlight of your trip to China awaits you: the Terracotta Army. Early in the morning, when many tourists turn around again in bed, you will be picked up at the hotel and set off on a very special exploration tour. This brings you closer to the historical background of the city in a unique way. You will head to Lintong, about 35 kilometers east, where the famous terracotta warriors are in line. It represents a complete army of that time. Since the troops have been deployed in battle order, their localization can be approximately reconstructed. Therefore, it is considered certain that there are still other sculptures on the huge terrain, which has only been exposed to about a quarter so far. In the nearby terracotta warrior reproduction factory, you will see how the famous warriors are recreated by over 40 technicians. These are exhibited, for example, in the Terracotta Army Museum. For dinner you can try a Chinese national dish: the famous, steamed dough bags “Jiaozi” that resemble our muzzles. The fillings vary from vegetables to meat to fish and the bags are tucked into soy sauce before consumption. Overnight in Xi'an.

7th day train ride to Hangzhou

Your journey continues today to Hangzhou. With a fast train you will arrive there and be brought to your hotel. Hangzhou is the capital of the Zhejiang region and known throughout the country for its special West Lake and popular as a destination. The artificially landscaped West Lake Xihu charms with its artfully landscaped gardens and nestles itself as a green oasis in the cityscape. The region around the metropolis of Hangzhou is beautiful and surrounded by rivers, canals and lakes. The area is considered the home of tea and silk in China. Hangzhou is also considered a traditional center for literature and art. Overnight in Hangzhou.

8th day Imperial waters while visiting Hangzhou

Today you will get to know the most beautiful sides of Hangzhou with your guide. In the morning you will visit the Monastery of Soul Refuge, one of the most beautiful temples in southern China. The monastery was founded in 328 AD by an Indian monk. According to legend, he chose this place because he thought the mountain was a sacred peak flown there from his homeland. You will also explore the West Lake as the main attraction of the provincial capital today. Artificially landscaped dams and islands where pastures and apricot trees grow complement the grace of the lake. Pavilions and tea houses line the shore. During a walk along the lake you have enough time to let the unique garden landscape affect you. The city also forms the southern end of the Kaiserkanal and was thus the starting point for the flow of goods into the rest of China. Especially silk, tea and fabrics were shipped from the once rich trading city. Over time, the canal reached a length of 1,800 kilometers to Beijing. Let yourself be impressed by this impressive part of Hangzhou today. Overnight in Hangzhou.

Day 9 - The famous dragon fountain tea from Hangzhou's hill country

Visit Hangzhou without visiting a tea plantation? – This is almost impossible. Finally, the dragon fountain tea “Lonjingcha” is produced in Hangzhou: perhaps the best green tea in China. Enjoy a hike through the tea plantations in the hill country and learn more about the popular national drink on the side. Enjoy the breathtaking views over the endless tea plantations. Then leave the province of Zhejiang and take the fast train to the metropolis of Shanghai. Look forward to a fascinating world there too. You are already expected at the train station and brought to your hotel. Design the rest of the day according to your ideas, explore the area and walk along the famous Bund promenade. Overnight in Shanghai.  

10th day glitter metropolis Shanghai

Your last destination on this trip is the modern metropolis of Shanghai. The colonial past and the ambitious future of Shanghai will inspire you. You will start your tour on Shanghai’s famous waterfront. There, old colonial buildings such as the legendary Bank of China (built in 1912) and the old “Peace Hotel” (1930) are nestled against the modern glass and steel towers. During your cruise on the Huangpu you can see for yourself. Opposite the fret is Pudong. There you have the opportunity to admire the city from the Shanghai Tower, at 632 meters altitude. Afterwards you will visit the trendy district of Tianzifang with its impressive labyrinth of lanes. Afterwards, your eventful day will take you to the interactive Digital Art Museum “teamLab Borderless”, where you yourself as a visitor become part of the art. The three-dimensional exhibition is truly limitless: hundreds of projectors create fascinating digital worlds, impressive light sculptures and works of art that seem to come from another world. Let the colorful color spectacle work on you! Overnight in Shanghai.

11th day excursion to the garden town of Suzhou and the water village of Tongli

Today you will take a trip to the garden city of Suzhou, which enchants with numerous canals and classic gardens. Through the gardens, Suzhou became famous and eventually developed into a cultural and intellectual center. The art of horticulture was to harmoniously design as many landscapes as possible in a confined space, offering both variety and opportunity for relaxation, but at the same time creating the feeling of size and expanse. Afterwards you will drive to the water village of Tongli, where everything still looks like in ancient China at times long gone. While you walk over small bridges, through winding alleys, past wind-leaning, gray-covered cottages, you look over dreamy canals on which junks swim. In the evening you will return to Shanghai. Overnight in Shanghai.  

Day 12: Farewell to China

Today we say goodbye to this fascinating country. Surely you have packed your suitcases with many lasting impressions and experiences. You will be driven to the airport, from where you will start your home or onward journey.

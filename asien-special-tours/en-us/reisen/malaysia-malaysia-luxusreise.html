Malaysia and Singapore in Style

Malaysia Travel - Malaysia & Singapore In Style by Asia Special Tours. Experience your customized trip to Malaysia. Ask now!

Have you dreamed of a trip to Malaysia for a long time and would like to travel the country exclusively? Do you not only want to visit the most beautiful sights, but also treat yourself to some very special experiences and a few relaxed days on a dreamlike beach? Then this trip is just right for you. Start your journey with an exclusive 4-course dinner in the world’s second-largest Ferris wheel with breathtaking views of Singapore before discovering the city’s charming east. A visit to the private Peranakan Museum “The Intan” followed by an authentic lunch will round off the experience and continue on to Malacca, where colonial charm and vibrant history await you. In Kuala Lumpur you will experience the colorful bustle of markets, street food tours by Vespa, impressive temples and modern architecture. One highlight is the stylish dinner at Tamarind Springs Restaurant. Enjoy pure relaxation at the luxurious Banjaran Hotsprings Retreat and experience an extraordinary dinner in the middle of an impressive limestone cave. After two days on the multi-faceted island of Penang, let yourself be kissed by the sun on the dreamlike beaches on Langkawi. How could travel be even more beautiful?

1st day Grandioner Reisestart: Private dinner with a view

Welcome to Singapore! Your journey begins in Singapore. Today, the fascinating city-state is a microcosm of glittering skyscrapers and lush green spaces that merge with testimonies of its colonial and Chinese influenced past. Upon arrival at the airport, you will already be expected and received. Your driver will take you to your hotel for the next three nights. Depending on the arrival time, you can start with first sightseeings or rest a little at the hotel. In the evening, the first big highlight of your trip awaits you: a romantic and exclusive dinner at the Singapore Flyer, the second largest Ferris wheel in the world. Indulge in the delights of an excellent 4-course menu (Western or Chinese cuisine to choose from), let your personal waiter take care of you and enjoy the breathtaking views of Marina Bay – all in the comfort of your private gondola. After this unique experience, which is unrivalled not only in Singapore, you will be returned to your hotel. Overnight in Singapore.

Day 2 An extraordinary discovery tour through Singapore's East

This morning you will meet your driver who will take you to Joo Chiat Place – the starting point for an extraordinary experience: a ride in the stylish vintage Vespa sidecar. During this approximately two-hour tour, you will discover the hidden sides of eastern Singapore – from small alleys and colorful street art to the ornate Sri Senpaga Vinayagar Temple to the pastel-colored Peranakan houses of Koon Seng Road. Afterwards, you will explore the lively districts of Joo Chiat and Katong on foot. You will also get insights into the everyday life of local residents and visit one of the oldest housing estates in the city. Along the way, you will pass the impressive National Stadium in Kallang Bay. A highlight of the day is a visit to The Intan, a multi-award-winning private museum devoted entirely to Peranakan culture. Situated in the middle of the residential area, passionate collector and host Alvin Yapp has transformed his own home into a lovingly curated treasury. During a personal guided tour, you will experience unique historical artifacts, ornate porcelain, traditional clothing and personal stories. At the crowning end, an authentic Peranakan lunch awaits you. Overnight in Singapore.

Day 3 Colonial Charm in Malacca

After breakfast, leave Singapore behind. Your driver will take you to the charming colonial city of Malacca. Your hotel for the coming night is The Majestic Malacca. This original 1920s mansion elegantly combines classical architecture with modern design elements. After checking in, you have some free time. In the evening, your culinary adventure begins through the diverse influences of Malacca. Along the way, you’ll find numerous local restaurants that showcase the region’s culinary diversity. Some serve traditional Malay dishes, while others reflect the influences of foreign traders who came to Malacca in the last 2,000 years. Your taste experience begins with Satay Celup, a hot peanut fondue where you dip meat and vegetable skewers. Then enjoy the flavors of Indian cuisine in a traditional specialty restaurant where the dishes are prepared in clay pots over an open fire. Your culinary foray will take you on to Chinese dim sum and a variety of street food specialties. The evening will end in a café on the canal. Overnight in Malacca.

Day 4 From Colonial Architecture to Peranakan Culture in Malacca

Today you will explore the most important historical and cultural sites of Malacca. The history of Malacca is best explored on foot, alternatively a rickshaw can be used for an extra charge. Your tour begins with A Famosa, the oldest surviving example of European architecture in Southeast Asia. Then climb St. Paul’s Hill to visit St. Paul’s Church before walking down to the Stadthuys, one of the best examples of Dutch colonial architecture. For lunch, enjoy the cuisine of a Teochew restaurant and try a Portuguese cake from one of the street vendors. In the afternoon, immerse yourself in the world of Peranakan culture. At the Baba & Nyonya Heritage Museum, you will experience the lives of Chinese families. Stroll through the old town, visit Chinese clan houses and discover traditional crafts. In a centuries-old shop, you can admire the colorful, pearl-studded Nyonya silk shoes while a talented calligrapher immortalizes your name in Chinese characters. Also, learn and try Chinese teas in a traditional tea salon. Continue to Chen Hoon Teng Temple. Take part in the traditional incense ceremony. After a day full of history, culture and tradition, you will continue on to Kuala Lumpur.

Day 5 Kuala Lumpur: Experience Hidden Treasures of the City Up close

Start early in the day to discover Kuala Lumpur on foot and by public transport. Together with your guide, you will make your way to the traditional Kampung Baru district. From the Pintasan Saloma Bridge you can enjoy a view of the city skyline. Walking through the district, you will discover traditional Malay wooden pile houses. Continue to the Chow Kit district, where you will explore the market of the same name. In the Little Indonesia district, you will find flavors and dishes that show exciting parallels and differences to the local cuisine. Take a break with a freshly foamed Teh Tarik, the popular Malaysian national drink, and try perhaps the best Pan Mee noodles in town. In the afternoon, a new chapter of your discovery tour begins: a walk through Kuala Lumpur’s Chinatown. Along the way, discover colorful murals that tell the story of the 1960s Chinese community and enjoy a cocktail overlooking Petaling Street, considered Kuala Lumpur’s birthplace. Your guide will customize the course of the evening according to your preferences. Overnight in Kuala Lumpur.

Day 6 Kuala Lumpur: Meeting of Cultures and Religions

After breakfast, leave Kuala Lumpur and head to the famous Batu caves. Climb the 272 colorful steps to the impressive cathedral cave, which houses the sanctuary of Murugan, a revered Hindu deity. Enjoy spectacular views of the city and observe the lively monkeys. Back in Kuala Lumpur, visit the magnificent Thean Hou Temple, one of Malaysia’s oldest and largest Buddhist temple complexes. Then your way through the Seegarten with its tropical vegetation leads you to an architectural landmark of the city: the modern National Mosque. Finally, stop at the world-famous Petronas Twin Towers, Kuala Lumpur’s distinctive landmark, for a photo stop. After a day full of cultural and spiritual impressions, return to the hotel and have time to relax before ending the evening in a stylish atmosphere. For dinner, enjoy an exquisite menu at Tamarind Springs – a restaurant known for its romantic location on the edge of a lush forest and fine Indo-Chinese cuisine. Here nature, taste and atmosphere merge into an unforgettable experience. Overnight in Kuala Lumpur.

Day 7: Kuala Lumpur to Ipoh

Today you will leave Kuala Lumpur and drive to Ipoh. The city is one of Malaysia’s cultural highlights and impresses with colonial architecture, impressive limestone formations, an excellent gastronomic scene and a relaxed atmosphere. Once Ipoh was one of the most important and prosperous tin mining cities in the world, today the city combines history and modern lifestyle in a unique way. Explore the historic old town together with your guide. The walk leads to the most important sights and gives insights into the eventful past of the city. Highlights include the magnificent colonial-style train station, the 1908 Town Padang Mosque and the Birch Memorial Clock Tower. Along the way, take a break at a traditional coffee shop and experience the special flair of the restored old town. Former commercial buildings have been transformed into stylish cafes and small boutiques, while ornate murals tell the story of Ipoh vividly. The final stage is Concubine Lane, known for its architecture, antique shops and small shops. After check-in, the rest of the day is free to enjoy the tranquility and exclusive atmosphere of your resort. Overnight in Ipoh.

8th day Luxury, hot springs and cuisine in Ipoh

Enjoy a day of relaxation at The Banjaran Hotsprings Retreat, nestled in a 260 million-year-old limestone valley near Ipoh. Surrounded by majestic limestone mounds, geothermal thermal springs, natural caves and tropical rainforest, the exclusive resort offers a unique retreat for recreation and well-being. Enjoy a traditional Urutan Malaysia massage at the resort’s spa. This typical Malaysian treatment uses rhythmic, flowing movements and targeted pressure to release tension and restore the natural balance of body and mind. In the evening, a special culinary experience awaits you at Jeff's Cellar. This extraordinary restaurant is located deep inside a limestone cave that is 260 million years old and is one of Malaysia’s most unique gourmet addresses. Surrounded by impressive rock formations and atmospheric candlelight, you will enjoy an exquisite dinner in an unparalleled atmosphere. Overnight in Ipoh.

Day 9: Travel to Penang

After breakfast, leave Ipoh and head to Kuala Kangsar, which is still considered the center of traditional craftsmanship. Here you will get insights into the production of Labu Sayong, an artfully crafted clay vessel traditionally used to cool water. Watch the craftsmen at their filigree work or try your hand at the potter’s disc. After lunch, visit the workshop of the renowned Keris master Pak Mazin, whose family has practiced the forging trade for four generations. The traditional Keris, a Malay dagger with great cultural significance, is closely linked to the legends and traditions of the country. Learn more about its history, symbolism and manufacture. Then continue your journey to Penang. Upon arrival, visit the impressive Kek Lok Si Temple, one of the largest Buddhist temples in Southeast Asia. The extensive temple complex combines Chinese, Thai and Burmese architectural elements and impresses with ornate pagodas, well-kept gardens and the imposing Kuan Yin statue, the goddess of mercy. Stroll through the complex and enjoy the peaceful atmosphere of this spiritual place. Overnight in Penang.

Day 10: The Many Facets of Penang

Experience the fascinating mix of cultures, history and culinary delights on a full-day exploration of Penang. Your tour starts in George Town, the historic heart of the city and UNESCO World Heritage Site. As you stroll through the Old Town, learn from your guide about Penang’s rich history shaped by international traders. The tour starts at St. Georges Church, the oldest Anglican church in Southeast Asia. From here you will walk along the famous “Street of Harmony”, where mosques, temples and churches of different faiths have coexisted peacefully for generations. Stroll through Armenian Street, known for its colorful street art, creative galleries and small boutiques. Another highlight is a visit to the magnificent Khoo Kongsi, one of Malaysia’s most impressive clan houses. Afterwards you will enter the Pinang Peranakan Mansion, a magnificent mansion that impressively presents the fascinating culture of the Peranakan. At the end of the tour, enjoy a short drive on the traditional Trishaw through the historic streets. Your ride ends in a cozy Nyonya cafe. The rest of the afternoon is at your disposal. Overnight in Penang.

Day 11 Explore nature around Penang

In the morning, take the historic funicular to Penang Hill and enjoy magnificent views of George Town and the Malaysian mainland. At The Habitat Penang Hill, you will discover a 130-million-year-old rainforest with its impressive flora and fauna. On the approximately 1.6 kilometer long nature trail you will learn more about the extraordinary biodiversity of the highland forest as well as the importance of nature and species protection. Highlights include the Canopy Walk with spectacular views over Penang’s wooded hills, as well as the Tree Top Walk, the highest observation deck on the island with an impressive 360-degree panorama. Afterwards, there is time for a short break at the Habitat Café (at an additional cost) before you take the funicular back to the valley and on to your hotel. In the evening, a culinary highlight awaits you at the elegant Kebaya restaurant, which combines traditional Nyonya cuisine with modern French techniques. Enjoy elaborately prepared dishes such as otak-otak, duck confit with sambal and slowly cooked beef yield in a stylish, romantic ambience. Overnight in Penang. 

12th day Langkawi – tropical beauty and relaxing wellness

In the morning, your driver will pick you up at the hotel and take you to the airport to fly to Langkawi. The island, known as the Jewel of Kedah, belongs to an archipelago of 99 islands. Surrounded by crystal clear, emerald green water and lined with fine sandy beaches, Langkawi delights with its unspoilt nature, which has brought the island the status of a global geopark. Mystical legends and mysterious places also give the tropical island a special charm. Upon arrival at your hotel, you can let the tranquility and beauty of the surroundings affect you. A special highlight is the Datai Spa, idyllic on a small, splashing creek and embedded in the scent and sounds of the rainforest. Let yourself be taken on a sensual journey of relaxation and rejuvenation – a 90-minute massage will help you to balance your body and mind and enjoy the tropical atmosphere of Langkawi to the fullest. Overnight in Langkawi.

13th Day Dinner Cruise on Langkawi

Spend the day as you wish and enjoy the amenities of your resort, The Datai, nestled in the wilderness of an untouched, 10 million-year-old rainforest. The dense, tropical forest is home to an impressive variety of flora and fauna, while the resort’s private walk will take you straight to Datai Bay’s white sand beach, which has been named one of the ten most beautiful beaches in the world by National Geographic. In the late afternoon, a special experience awaits you on the Andaman Sea: After a short transfer to the marina, you will board a private 38-foot catamaran. While sailing towards the southern sub-islands of Langkawi, enjoy a refreshing cocktail and watch the sun set over the sea. Relax in the saltwater hot tub, take a refreshing dip in the sea and then enjoy a delicious BBQ dinner on board as the sky slowly wraps up in the colors of twilight. After this unforgettable evening at sea, you will return to the pier and be taken back to your hotel. Overnight in Langkawi.

14th day of pure relaxation in Langkawi

(without tour guide & chauffeur)

Once again, this day is completely at your disposal to explore the dreamlike surroundings to your heart's content, swim in the sea, relax on the beach or enjoy your magnificent resort. Let it go really well! To crown your holiday, a personal dinner by candlelight on the beach awaits you. Your butler welcomes you with a glass of champagne, while the gentle sea breeze and spectacular sunset form the perfect backdrop. Enjoy your dinner served by your butler. The evening ends atmospheric with roasted marshmallows and a hot chocolate drink – an unforgettable conclusion to your journey. Overnight in Langkawi.

15th day Farewell to Malaysia

Your exclusive journey is coming to an end, and you’ve seen a lot and explored the most beautiful sides of Malaysia and Singapore in style. Surely you will remember this trip for a long time, which struck one high point after another. With countless new impressions and beautiful photos in your luggage, your driver will take you to the airport for your return flight home.

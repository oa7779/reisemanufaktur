The "Beautiful Island" Taiwan - Between Ancient Traditions and Picture Book Landscapes

Taiwan Travel: Experience the beautiful island of Taiwan with this round trip from Asia Special Tours. Inquire directly here and have individual Taiwan travel created.

Experience an unknown piece of the Far East! The exciting combination of nature and culture makes every Taiwan tour a very special experience. You’ll start your journey in the exciting capital of Taipei and then head to scenic Sun-Moon Lake. Visit Taiwan’s ancient capital Tainan, the port city of Kaohsiung and the magical Kenting National Park before traveling on to the rugged east coast. A highlight will certainly also be the Taroko Gorge in the national park of the same name. On the northeast coast of the country, good times await you when you try yourself in the art of gold washing. After almost two weeks, you will return to where your journey began – to the capital Taipei, which has much more to offer. Enjoy the fascinating metropolis from a bird’s eye view and visit the city’s most important Daoist temple. This trip will show you the most beautiful sides of Taiwan. You will be amazed by the diversity!

Day 1 Start of your trip on the “beautiful island”

Welcome to Formosa, the “beautiful island”! Your journey begins in Taipei, where you will be welcomed and warmly greeted by your guide. So you can already, while your driver takes you to the hotel, first facts and anecdotes about the city. Arrive in peace and explore the surroundings. The rest of the day is at your disposal. Overnight in Taipei.

2nd day Visiting the bright blue Sun-Moon Lake

To get to know Taiwan's beautiful landscapes, leave Taipei behind and travel with your guide to one of the absolute highlights of the country: the picturesque sun-moon lake located in the mountains. The lake received this name because its shape resembles the Chinese characters of the sun and moon. Its beautiful landscape makes it one of the most popular recreational areas in the country. It is located in the center of Taiwan at an altitude of 762 meters. On the way, small stops are planned at the Ci En Pagoda and in the village of Ita Thao. After arrival, there is still enough time for a relaxed boat trip (together with other guests) to really enjoy the dreamlike surroundings. Overnight at the Sun-Moon Lake.

3rd Day Experience Colonial Tainan up close

Your next destination is Tainan, Taiwan’s ancient capital that shines with numerous attractions and Dutch colonial history. During your exploration tour, you will first visit Fort Zeelandia, proudly perched on the banks of the Suriname River in the shade of centuries-old mahogany trees. Built between 1624 and 1634 by the Dutch East India Company, Fort Zeelandia served as the governor's seat. Today it is one of the first stone buildings in Paramaribo city center, which consists largely of wooden houses. Visit the Dutch settlement with its striking red bricks amidst tropical vegetation. Continue to Anping Tree House, a former warehouse into which Banyan trees have grown over the last few decades. Marvel at the fascinating structures that resulted from it. A river trip through Sichare's marsh area (together with other guests) is also planned for you today. A walk through Shennong-Old Street, lined with pretty old houses, and a visit to the Old Hayashi Department – the city’s oldest department store – round off the eventful day.

Day 4 Tainan – Modernity and the past united

You haven’t seen everything Tainan has to offer. The city still has some beautiful sides for you. Your guide will show you the National Museum of Taiwanese Literature, Koxinga Shrine and the Temple of Confucius. Instead of countless statues of gods, there are doctrinal tablets here. The temple is considered the oldest temple of Confucius and the "first school" in Taiwan. Then visit Fort Provintia, built in 1653 by the Dutch colony and still a true beauty today. And this despite the fact that the buildings have been destroyed and rebuilt several times over the centuries. Especially in the evening light, the old walls reveal their charm. The Chihkan Tower of Fort Provintia, surrounded by a small, lovingly landscaped park, always offers a beautiful photo opportunity. With many new impressions, you will then return to your hotel. Overnight in Tainan.

Day 5 Walking in British Traces in Kaohsiung

Today you will travel on to Kaohsiung, Taiwan’s most important port and capital of the south. Along the way, stop at Ten Drum Culture Village where you’ll listen to a percussion concert. Arriving in Kaohsiung, visit the former British Consulate and the Art Center at Pier 2. Today, the old port site is a venue for exhibitions, concerts and cultural activities. Discover Taiwan's art scene here. Then sit back relaxed on a boat trip (together with other guests) on the “love river” and enjoy Kaohsiung’s backdrop from a different angle. At the end of the day, stroll a little over the Liuhe Night Market. Here a multifaceted offer of culinary delicacies awaits you. Taste Jiaozi pastry bags, teppanyaki, tangyuan, firepot and many more traditional dishes of Taiwan. Overnight in Kaohsiung.

6th day to enjoy natural beauty Kentings

After an extensive breakfast, head to the beautiful Kenting National Park. Taiwan’s tropical southern tip offers warm temperatures all year round. Here native, as well as introduced exotic plants thrive equally. Azur blue water and white beaches invite you to swim and snorkel. The Oluanpi Lighthouse and the Longpan Park are also worth a visit and of course not missing in your exquisite program. Afterwards you will be taken to your beautiful hotel, where you can make yourself comfortable. Overnight in Kenting.

7th day to discover Taitung by bicycle

Your journey will take you to the beautiful city of Taitung on the Pacific Ocean. Enjoy the fantastic nature of Taiwan’s rugged east coast. In addition to natural rock formations, a real natural spectacle awaits you: an upward flowing water body. Afterwards, the beautiful area around Mt. Guanshan is waiting to be discovered by bicycle. Cycle past lush green rice fields and enjoy the views of the surrounding mountains. The 12-kilometer route is suitable for every fitness level and scenic. Overnight in Taitung.

Day 8: Enchanting Taroko National Park & Picturesque Carp Lake

Over the almost Alpine-seeming East Rift valley it goes on to Taroko. Taroko National Park, one of eight national parks in Taiwan, was named after the Taroko Gorge. In it flows the gurgling Liwu River. Along the way, you will make a photo stop at the monument of the Northern Tropic Circle, also known as the “Turning Circle of Cancer”. Also a detour to the Matai-an swamp areas is on your program. At the end of the day, you will cross the picturesque Karpfensee. The calm, clear water is surrounded by mountains all around. In good weather, paragliders often float from the 601 meter high Karpfenberg to the east into the valley. With this beautiful sight you will end the day. Overnight in Taroko.

9th day Adventure in the Taroko Gorge

Today you have the entire day to explore the Taroko Gorge. The gorge was created by a narrow mountain river, which has milled into the surrounding mountains over the millennia, and now winds through forested rugged mountains. High above, gigantic rock walls pile up, while far below the river flows over huge marble rocks. In addition to the natural wonder, numerous temples and pagodas await the visitor. Overnight in Taroko.

Day 10 Experience Traditional Art in Yilan

The Taroko National Park area also includes the Qingshui cliffs. The coastal road leads here towards the mountain Liushishi, which is also called Sixty Stone Mountain. In August and September, millions of daylilies bloom on the hills – an overwhelmingly beautiful sight. Finally, leave the national park behind and continue on to Yilan. Visit the National Centre for Traditional Arts in Yilan for a glimpse of traditional Taiwanese art. Afterwards you will be taken to your hotel. Overnight in Yilan.

11th day Gold washing on the Northeast Coast & Taipei from a bird’s eye view

Now continue on to the northeast coast of the country. A Mecca for Gold Diggers! With the Jinguashi Gold Museum and the chance to try your hand at gold washing, you can expect good times here. Then pay a visit to the town of Jioufen and then continue on to Taipei. At the end of the day, experience Taipei from a bird’s eye view: you drive onto the observation deck of the 101 Tower, which until 2007 was the tallest building in the world and was replaced by the Burj Khalifa in Dubai. Overnight in Taipei.

Day 12 Experience Taipei's most beautiful sites

Take advantage of today to fully devote yourself to exploring the exciting city. Your sightseeing tour will first take you to the National Palace Museum, which displays the collections of Chinese emperors from the Forbidden City in Beijing. It is considered one of the best addresses in the world. You will also visit the Pao-An Temple, considered the most important Daoist temple in Taipei. It was built in 1760 and owes its name to a godlike Emperor from the 10th century, whom people venerate for his medical abilities as a protective god. In the courtyard of the temple, performances of Taiwanese opera take place from March to June and from September to December. A stroll across Dihua Street offers a glimpse of traditional Chinese trades such as bamboo shops, rice mills, white-and-lantern shops, and rounds off your day perfectly. In the colorful bustle of the street, you can taste delicacies and make a good purchase especially with textiles. Overnight in Taipei.

13th day Farewell to Taiwan

The time until departure is available for further exploration in Taipei. Then it is time to say goodbye to Taiwan. Even if your holiday is now over – the precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories of your trip will accompany you forever. Look forward to letting your friends and family share your experiences in the distance at home. We wish you a good journey home!

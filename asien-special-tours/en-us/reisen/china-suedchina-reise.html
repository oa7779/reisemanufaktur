This journey could be painted, so rich in colors and fascinating structures China presents itself here. Bizarre mountain formations, countless karst cone mountains and steep rice terrace fields attract the eye in their spell. Embark on a journey to the south of the country and learn about some of the most beautiful landscapes in southern China. You will also get amazing insights into the ancient Chinese culture. Temples, ancient towns, once important trade routes and traditional villages of various minorities offer numerous photo opportunities on this beautiful journey.

Day 1: China is calling...

Your journey through the beautiful south of China begins in the Chinese province of Guangxi, in the city of Guilin. This part of southern China is known for its beautiful karst mountain landscape, which will remain in your memory for a long time. At the airport, your personal guide and your chauffeur are already waiting for you. Together you will immediately make your way to Yangshuo. At least now it becomes clear why everyone is so enthusiastic about the area around Guilin: The road leads through picturesque fields and opens up a panorama of staggered mountain rows. It is difficult to find the right words for this scenery. Overnight in Yangshuo.

2nd day By bicycle and bamboo raft through the karst cone landscape

Get ready for a day full of varied impressions of the scenic landscape along the Yulong River. After breakfast, head to the village of Jiuxian, known for its beautiful Qing-era homes. Here you will also visit the ancestral temple of the Li family. After this short journey into the past, stroll comfortably to the entrance of the traffic-calmed zone, where your bikes are already waiting for you. Along the Yulong River you cycle through the idyllic landscape, where you can hardly see your fill. After about seven kilometers you reach the dock and climb on a bamboo raft. Sit back relaxed and let the scenery pass by. Seen from certain angles, the hills form shapes reminiscent of animals, pagodas or plants. This inspired the local population to give the mountains imaginative names, such as “Moonsichelberg”, “Elephant Nutsberg” or “Horse Painting Mountain”. Back on land, you can walk or bike through the picturesque countryside back to the parking lot, from where you will be taken to the hotel. Overnight in Yangshuo.

3rd day Riderraces as far as the eye can see

Also Longsheng with its rice terraces must not be missed – a landscape-unique idyll. Enjoy on the ride this picturesque landscape with unique rock formations and rice fields. In addition, you will get an impression of life in the countryside, because there are numerous minorities living in this area who have maintained their traditional ways of living and living. In Longsheng, visit the Longji terrace fields, which form a unique attraction that shines in many colors. For many photographers a very coveted motif: rice fields, as far as the eye can see, up to an altitude of 880 meters. Like densely woven carpets, they cover the mountain slopes around the village of Ping'an in the southeast of Longsheng. The steps rise steeply to the sky. The first fields were created over 700 years ago. With their rice terraces, the locals created a landscape architecture that combines function and form, utility and aesthetics more than almost any other cultural landscape. Absolutely worth seeing! After arrival, you will take a small hike through the rice terraces together with your guide and can enjoy the beautiful landscape particularly well. Overnight in Longsheng.

Day 4 Visiting the Dong

Your journey will take you to Zhaoxing, one of the most beautiful villages of the Dong minority. On the way, stop at Sanjiang, which is also inhabited by members of the Dong people. Here you will visit the drum tower and the breathtaking wind-and-rain bridge, built in 1916 and with a length of 76 meters. It is an excellent example of the architecture of the Dong, who are especially famous for their filigree wooden structures. Finally, you will reach the village of Zhaoxing, which lies beyond the river Linxi in a wide, flat valley. In this village, with the exception of a few modern buildings in the outskirts, there is a variety of traditional buildings for which it is praised throughout China. Overnight in Zhaoxing.

5th Day Impressive Architecture of the Dong and Miao

The village of Zhaoxing consists of five districts, all inhabited by different clans. Each district has the three famous treasures of Dong architecture: its own drum tower, its own theater and a wind-and-rain bridge. The Dong produce and still wear the traditional indigo blue clothing that is soft-knocked and coated with protein to impregnate them against mosquitoes. This ethnic group is also known for its ornate chants, in which bird voices are often imitated. In addition to the Dong, many members of the Miao minority live here. The Miao speak so many different dialects that it can even be difficult for them to converse with members of other families. Many of their traditions are already over a thousand years old! Together with your guide you will visit some villages of the Miao minority, such as the village of Langde Shang Miao, which comprises 50 wooden houses that are densely packed in a depression. In the middle there is a pond and dance floor, which was laid out in concentric circles around a tree trunk decorated with buffalo horns and painted with dragons. Overnight in Zhaoxing.

Day 6 By express train and car on to Wanfenglin

Today is a travel day ahead of you. You will first be taken to the Congjiang station, from where you will travel to Panzhou by express train. From there you will be picked up and get back on the car and drive about 2.5h to Wanfenglin. Overnight in Wanfenglin.

Day 7 Excursion to the Maling Gorge

The small, somewhat remote market town of Xingyi in the extreme southwest corner of Guizhou province is surrounded by low, round limestone mounds and shallow rice fields. Together with your guide, take a trip to the 15-kilometer Maling Canyon. You might almost think you were in a backdrop for a fantasy movie. The canyon was cut into the rock by a raging river and is considered one of the most beautiful in the world. On the left and on the right, steep rock faces protrude into the sky and springs bubbly down mossy rock faces in miniature waterfalls. A beautiful circular path with several bridges takes you in about two to three hours in a zigzag down to the water, upstream along the river course and through natural tunnels. You will be amazed at how many waterfalls you discover along the way. Take a closer look at the rocks: through a special chemical process, they form very special red shapes that look like large scales or stone fern fronds! Take time to explore the area in peace and enjoy nature. Afterwards, they will be brought to their accommodation. Overnight in Wanfenglin.

Day 8 “In the forest of 10,000 Kegelberge”

Today is dedicated to exploring the beautiful Wanfenglin National Park, also known as the “Forest of Ten Thousand Cone Mountains”. The area with its karst cone landscape is a bit reminiscent of Yangshuo, but it is less known and even more beautiful. The many mountain peaks here are pyramid-shaped and stand close to each other. During your walk, you will feel like you are in a Chinese bonsai garden. The landscape is especially beautiful in spring, when the rapeseed blossoms and turns the fields into a yellow sea of flowers. Overnight in Wanfenglin.

Day 9 Bizarre-Beautiful Stone Forest

A very special forest awaits your visit: the stone forest near Shilin. The “trees” of this nearly 26,000-hectare area are narrow, bizarrely shaped rock needles that rise between five and thirty meters. On twelve square kilometers you can admire different rock formations, for example Ashima, allegedly a young beauty frozen to stone. On the rock walls you can see corals, because the geological origin of the stone forest lies in a disappeared sea. These natural karst formations were formed around 200 million years ago. Years when the earth's crust rose and let the original water sink. Noisy waterfalls, clear lakes and karst caves complement the impressive and bizarre landscape. A 1,200 meter long, safely marked hiking trail winds through the labyrinth. The trail leads to the Lotus summit, from where you can see the stone forest in its full size. Afterwards, you will be taken to Kunming Airport to travel further by domestic flight to Shangri-La. The small town, located at 3,200 meters, is the gateway to Tibet and already strongly influenced by Tibetan influences. Overnight in Shangri-La.

Day 10 Spiritual Temples and Magic Shangri-La

Just outside Shangri-La is the 300-year-old Tibetan Monastery of Songtsenling. The Lama Monastery is one of the 13 famous monasteries in the Tibetan area of Kham and is also known as the “little Potala Palace”. His murals and wood carvings are true treasures of art. Several hundred monks perform their Buddhist exercises in the prayer halls imbued with the scent of thousands of incense sticks – an absolutely spiritual place. After your visit to the temple, you will go out into nature. The lovely landscape of Shangri-La National Park awaits you. Especially enchanting is the Bitahai Lake, which invites to small hikes. The lake lies at an altitude of 3,540 meters and is fed exclusively by the meltwater of the surrounding mountain ranges. The peculiarity of the water body is that its surface is covered by a 50 centimeter thick, floating layer of grass. Overnight in Shangri-La.

11th day View into one of the deepest gorges on earth: the Tiger Jump Gorge

The next destination on your trip is the city of Lijiang, which is a UNESCO World Heritage Site. If you imagine it without tourists, it would truly be a journey back in time to the past century. Striking in the street scene are decorative men in traditional robes, women in blouses and pale blue aprons. These are the Naxi who have settled in Lijiang for more than 1,000 years. Originally they lived as nomads on the Tibetan plateau. On the way, take a look into one of the deepest gorges on earth. The Tiger Jump Gorge is an impressive 3,900 meters deep and about 16 kilometers long. In some places, however, it measures only 10 meters wide. According to legend, a tiger is said to have skipped the gorge here long ago to escape its hunters. Almost vertically deep down to the Jinshajiang (River of the Golden Sands) their cliffs hang threateningly over the raging water. Steps struck in the rock lead down to the rushing stream, where you can witness this unique natural spectacle. Overnight in Lijiang.

12th Day Historic Old Town of Lijiang & Detour to Yuhu Village

Start the day with a detour to Yuhu village, where you can visit the former home of botanist and linguist Joseph Rock. Then explore the beautiful old town of Lijiang. Between the large water wheel at the entrance and the central square, there is busy activity in the restaurants, cafes and shops from morning to late at night. The houses are a feast for the eyes: two-storey wooden skeleton buildings with brick walls and natural foundations with artfully carved door panels and flower-decorated courtyards. In the north of the village you will find the pond of the Black Dragon (Heilong Tan), surrounded by old pavilions, where in windless weather the 13 peaks of the up to 5,600 meters high massif of the Jadedrachenberge and Yufeng Temples are reflected in the clear water. A sight to melt down. Overnight in Lijiang.

Day 13: Admire Dali's landmarks

Today, your journey will continue to Dali, one of Yunnan’s most popular destinations. From Lijiang, the express train will take you to Dali. Dali's landmarks are the three white pagodas of Chong-Sheng-Si Temple, whose highest tower measures 46 meters, counts 16 floors and offers a magnificent picture in clear weather in front of the mostly snow-capped peaks. Especially when the ensemble is reflected in the small pond: a wonderful photo motif! The temple in the background is a typical example of the traditional architecture of Yunnan province. Of course, a detour to the picturesque Erhai Lake, whose shape resembles a huge ear and gave the “Ear Lake” its name, should not be missing. In the late afternoon you will be taken to the train station and make your way back to Kunming. In the evening, a very special “sea” awaits you – not from water, but from flowers. The flower market in Kunming offers you an incomparable color splendor. Overnight in Kunming.

14th Day Historic Old Town of Kunming and Enjoy Panorama

After a strong breakfast, you start the day full of energy. Your exploration will take you to the charming old town of Kunming, also known as the “city of spring”. This lively city attracts several million tourists every year who appreciate its unique mix of culture and modern lifestyle. In the afternoon, take a small hike up Xishan, also known as Westberg. From the viewpoints of the Westberg you have a breathtaking view over the city of Kunming as well as the picturesque Dianchi Lake. The fresh mountain air and the impressive views provide a wonderful opportunity to enjoy the beauty of the area and take photos. After the hike, you will relax back to the hotel. The rest of the evening is at your leisure to continue exploring the city or simply enjoying the amenities of your hotel. Overnight in Kunming.

Day 15 Farewell to China

Today it is already time to say goodbye to this fascinating country. Surely you have packed your suitcases with many new impressions. You will be driven to the airport, from where you will start your home or onward journey.

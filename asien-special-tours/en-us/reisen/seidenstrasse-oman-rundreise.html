Deserts, Oases & Mountains – The Highlights of Oman with Salalah

Oman Tour: Explore the multifaceted Sultanate of Oman with its deserts, oases and mountains as well as the legendary Salalah region with Asia Special Tours.

The journey takes you to the highlights of Oman and opens the doors to a dreamlike oriental world. Enjoy a variety of landscapes, such as deserts, impressive mountain ranges and green oases and learn about the deep-rooted history of the Sultanate. Your journey begins in the colorful capital Muscat, where tradition and modernity meet. You will explore rural life and get an impression of the everyday life of the locals. In the impressive mountains around Jebel Akhdar you will find peace before making your way to the desert. You go on an exciting desert safari and spend a night under the starry sky. An animal encounter with turtles awaits you on the Gulf of Oman. In the second part of your trip you will have enough time to get to know Salalah in all its facets. The magic of the Orient is waiting for you!

1st day Arrival in Oman

Welcome to Oman! Upon arrival, you will already be expected and brought to your hotel. Look forward to exciting days full of new impressions! Oman offers you what your heart desires: beaches, deserts, a wildlife and a primordial flora and fauna. Overnight in Muscat.

Day 2 Explore in Muscat

You start at the large mosque of Muscat, which forms a unique photo motif. Due to its size of about four hectares, the mosque is one of the largest in the world and at the same time one of the most important buildings of Oman. Afterwards you will drive along the tree-lined Embassy road. Your path will take you to the Royal Opera House, which reflects Oman’s unique contemporary architecture. In addition to a spacious luxurious concert theatre, the Royal Opera House includes an arts center for music, theatre and opera production. Dive into the oriental worlds before continuing on to the Muttrah district. The coastal road, also known as Corniche, forms the border with the old town. It takes you along the main port of Oman, Mina Al Sultan Qaboos and past the souk of Muttrah. During a visit to the Bait Al Zubair Museum, you will get an insight into Oman’s former way of life. In addition to traditional crafts, the private collection of the old Zubair trading family is on display. At the port is the center for the administration of the Omani state. Here you can visit the building located at the front of the bay, but only from the outside. If you let your view wander over the area, the two fortresses Djalali and Mirani immediately catch your eye. Overnight in Muscat.

3rd day Old, Omani traditions

Today, the Wadi Bani Awf is a special highlight for you. This wadi is also better known under the name “Snake Gorge” due to the variety of desert snake living there. Let’s go – high up to the summit, where you will visit the mountain village of Bilad Al Sayt. The village of Al Misfah is a typical Omani village that will give you a good insight into how the locals lived centuries ago. The peculiarity of the village is a Falaj system, an irrigation system. Their path leads close to the water canals, a traditional Omani way of watering the fields. These canals are like lifelines: depending on requirements, the water is diverted and other fields watered with it. You will then visit the Bait Al Safah Museum - an open-air museum that introduces you to ancient traditions. The next destination is the Omani Grand Canyon called Jebel Shams, which is the highest mountain in Oman at an altitude of 3,000 meters. Jebel Shams is called Sonnenberg and offers you a beautiful view of the canyon. Admire the wild and untouched landscape! Overnight in Hail Al Shas.

Day 4 Mountains around Jebel Akhdar

The journey continues to Jebel Akhdar, where you will move into your beautiful hotel today. On the way, stop at Wadi Bani Habib, a dry riverbed that stretches for about 30 km and on the sides of which small pretty villages line up. Overnight in Jebel Akhdar.

5th day fortress over Nizvah

You are going to Nizvah today. Above the rooftops of Nizwa, the largest fortress of the Arabian peninsula was built in 1668. The fortress was once used as the headquarters of Iman. Today you have a wonderful view of buildings and the bustling market. Then head to Jabreen Castle, one of the most beautiful castles built towards the end of the 17th century. They also stop at the Bahla Fortress, the oldest and first fortress built in Islamic style. Overnight in Jebel Akhdar.

Day 6: Three Village Walk

Al Aqr, Al Ayn and Ash Shirayjah are the most important archaeological sites in Oman. The scenic wonder owes the area to the irrigation system, which makes the cliffs terraced. Pomegranates, pears, grapes, garlic and onions are grown and harvested in the region. You can also pick aromatic Damascus roses if you wish. Visit this beautiful area and let yourself be enchanted! Overnight in Jebel Akhdar.

Day 7: A Night in the Desert

After breakfast, on your way through the desert, you will pass the old oasis town of Birkat al Mauz. This area used to be very popular for banana cultivation, today mainly dates are grown. Here are still old ruins of a former settlement. Then continue on to the desert to Wahiba Sands. Before you reach it, stop at Wadi Bani Khalid and enjoy the deep blue waters during a break. Here you will experience on your desert safari an adventurous ride through white and red sand dunes that rise up to 200 meters in height. In the desert there are many animals and plants. Here also live numerous Bedouins and nomads, who will surely meet you again and again with their camels. Arriving at the desert camp, you can enjoy the breathtaking scenery in peace. Despite its drought, the desert shows a breathtaking flora and fauna. For the evening, enjoy an Arabic dinner, Omani coffee and delicious dates before spending the night under the dreamlike starry sky. Overnight in Wahiba Sands.

Day 8: Watching Turtles

Enjoy the Wadi Bani Khalid, which already awaits you in oriental surroundings. It is not only a real piece of jewelry, but also offers with its deep blue water a very special oasis of tranquility. Here is the ideal place to relax in the shade of the palm trees. Then continue on to your hotel. In the evening they will be against 21.00 Clock picked up by rangers at your hotel and start your 15-minute walk. Your destination is the green turtles, which can be found in the tranquility of Ras Al Jinz beach. Enjoy this wonderful experience while watching the turtles at their egg shelves. Overnight in Al Hadd.

Day 9 Along the coast to Muscat

Today you drive along the coastal road back to Muscat. Along the way, visit Wadi Tiwi, a beautiful and turquoise-blue natural pool landscape. You will also see Hawiyyat Nadschm, an equally enchanting place that invites you to swim. The mountain hole is filled with green blue sea water. Finally, you will pass the village of Quriyat, which is protected by its fortress built in the 18th century. You can reach Muscat in the afternoon and then fly on to Salalah in the evening. Overnight in Salalah.

10th Day City Tour in Salalah

Today you will take a city tour in Salalah. First, you will visit the Museum of the Incense Land. Here you will find out everything you need to know about the Weauchstraße. The museum was opened in 2007 and is located in the Archaeological Park of Al Balid, another UNESCO World Heritage site in Salalah. Then stop for a photo stop at Sultan Qaboos Mosque and see the Sultan’s Palace. Afterwards, visit the bazaar of the city and stroll through the alleys. Overnight in Salalah.

Day 11 - On the road in East and West Salalah

Your day begins with a visit to the ancient fishing village of Taqah, famous for its dried sardines and traditional old Dhorari houses. Afterwards, visit the evergreen Wadi Darbat where you can take a walk. To the west of Salalah, visit the Qara Mountains and the tomb of the Prophet Job. Another detour will take you to the vast beach of Mughsail. Here the waves blow the water through natural holes, a beautiful natural spectacle. Take a short walk around the area before returning to your hotel. Overnight in Salalah.

Day 12 A visit to the "empty quarter"

Today's excursion will take you to the so-called "empty quarter", Rub Al Khali. You start with an all-wheel drive vehicle into the untouched dunes. On your way through the Qara Mountains you will find spectacular views. Visit the Lost City - Ubar, a UNESCO World Heritage Site located 175 km from Salalah. The city is now considered the "Atlantis of the Desert" as it is literally sunk in the sand. Explore one of the world’s largest sand deserts before returning to Salalah. On the way back, stop at Wadi Dawkah. The Wadi Dawkah has been the origin of incense since ancient times. Overnight in Salalah.

13th day of free time

Without guidance

This day is free for you. Enjoy the amenities of your hotel or take a snorkeling trip and explore the underwater world. Overnight in Salalah.

Day 14 Back to Muscat

Today you will be back to the airport for your return flight to Muscat. Tucked away between the Hajjar Mountains and the coast, a jewel is hidden among the luxury hotels in Muscat, Oman – the Al Bustan Palace, a Ritz-Carlton Hotel. This seaside retreat welcomes you with a harmonious combination of ancient traditions and state-of-the-art comfort. All 250 rooms and suites of the luxury hotel have a private balcony. The Al Bustan Palace is perfect for couples, business travelers and families, offering everything for beautiful memories that will last a lifetime. In Omani houses, it is a tradition to offer every guest a coffee as soon as they enter the house. So also in the Al Bustan Palace, one of the most elegant resorts in Muscat, Oman. Here, the welcome gesture is carefully performed by the “Coffee Man”, the cultural host and ambassador of the Omani beach resort. His ornate handling of the spices has a centuries-old tradition and gives you new experiences and insights into the local way of life. Overnight in Muscat.

15th - 17th day of free time

Without guidance

These days are at your disposal. Enjoy your free time on the beach. Overnight in Muscat.

18th day Departure from Oman

Your time out from everyday life is coming to an end. Even if your holiday is soon over, the precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories of your trip will accompany you forever. Look forward to letting your friends and family share your experiences in the distance at home. Your driver will take you to the airport for your return flight.

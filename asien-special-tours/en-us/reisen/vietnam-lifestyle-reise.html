1st day Arrival in Vietnam & spiritual pilgrimage Yen Tu

Welcome to Vietnam. Upon arrival in Hanoi, you will be welcomed by your guide and drive to Yen Tu. Mount Yen Tu is a well-known Buddhist pilgrimage site with beautiful landscapes, historical relics and ancient pagodas. According to legend, about 2000 years ago, a Taoist hermit - An Ky Sinh - traveled to Mount Yen Tu in search of medicinal herbs. In 1299, King Tran Nhan Tong came here, where he studied as a Buddhist monk and founded the Truc Lam Zen Buddhist sect, considered the only Buddhist school in Vietnam. Since then, the area has become a Buddhist pilgrimage site and attracts thousands of pilgrims annually. Over the centuries, Buddhist followers have built hundreds of historically significant relics, pagodas, monasteries and temples around the Yen Tu Mountains. The extravagant Hotel Legacy Yen Tu is an inspiring journey back in time as this place is rooted in Vietnam’s deep history, Tran Dynasty heritage and the spirit of Truc Lam Yen Zen Buddhism. Overnight in Yen Tu.

Day 2 Breath Meditation & Conscious Eating

Start your day early and gather new life forces during a yoga session and a breathing meditation. Afterwards, you will be served a tasty breakfast where you can strengthen yourself for the day. In the morning, learn about applied Buddhism in daily life, which helps improve health and mind. After lunch at the Tung Lam Restaurant, a rest meditation & tea meditation with the Truc Lam Zen Master awaits you. Feel a deep relaxation and inner peace. In the afternoon, take part in an aroma workshop before you can relax with your high tea. Dinner is all about conscious food and is served at Tho Quang Restaurant. Late at night you can still participate in an Agarwood meditation before going to bed relaxed. Overnight in Yen Tu.

Day 3rd Sunrise at Mount Yen Tu & Klangschalen Meditation

Beware early risers! An unforgettable moment awaits you at sunrise on Mount Ye Tu. Then hike to the “Foot of the King”, the Dong Pagoda. If it is not so sporty, an electric car is available on request as well as the use of the cable car (for an additional cost). Then return to your hotel where you can relax with a yoga session and a singing bowl meditation. After breakfast, it continues culinary, because today there is a special cooking course with the topic of conscious food and home cooking. Your vegan lunch will be served at the vegetarian restaurant. The activities in the afternoon are a rest meditation and conversations about mindfulness practices. Then you can be pampered with a foot massage and herbal treatments (subject to availability). For dinner, a detox menu is served at the Tung Lam Restaurant. In the evening there will be a Zen community event – “Thien Ca” Singing Zen Songs. Overnight in Yen Tu. 

Day 4 Walking Meditation & Truc Lam Meditation Center

Early in the morning you will take part in a walking meditation. After breakfast, visit the Herb Garden and Truc Lam Meditation Center. You can also visit the Yen Tu Pilgrimage Village. In the afternoon, relax with a rest meditation and have conversations about mindfulness practices. The rest of the day is at your disposal. Explore the area on your own or relax in the hotel. Overnight in Yen Tu.

Day 5 Start of the Cruise & Enchanting Luxury Ship Ylang

Enjoy one last morning yoga session and a subsequent summary of the Zen Master. After breakfast, it’s time to pack suitcases, as your journey will take you on to Lan Ha Bay, where your luxurious cruise begins. Get on board and feel the special atmosphere of your luxury ship. Enjoy a tasty lunch while the island world passes you by. Afterwards you can refresh yourself in the swimming pool or be pampered in the ship's own spa. In the south of Cat Ba Island, go ashore and visit the charming village of Viet Hai. With bicycles you go through lush tropical forests on an exploration tour. Take the opportunity and get in touch with the friendly residents. They are happy to show you their everyday life in an area where time seems to have stood still. Back on board you will anchor in a quiet place for the night. For dinner, you will be served a multi-course dinner. Let the evening relax on the terrace deck under the open stars or try your luck at squid fishing. Overnight on the ship.

Day 6 Tai Chi, Yoga and Jungle Walk

Feel the magical atmosphere on board your ship early in the morning. With tai chi exercises on the sun deck, you awaken the spirits of life and can start the day completely relaxed. Enjoy your breakfast privately in your cabin or dining room. Freshly reinforced, visit one of the still existing fishing villages and learn about the way of life and everyday life of the families who live here on the water. At Cat Ba National Park, you will take a hike in the midst of lush nature. Feel and smell the allure of the jungle. Back on board you will be served a tasty lunch. The afternoon is yours alone. Enjoy an extensive sunbathing, go swimming or do something good with a wellness application. Let your eyes wander into the distance and treat your soul to a time out. Overnight on the ship.

Day 7 End of the Cruise & Return to Hanoi

Early in the morning you can best experience the absolute silence and clear air in the bay. Let it be a relaxed approach with a cup of coffee and use the opportunity for a tai chi or yoga hour on board the ship again today. In the morning, you can go on an exploration tour with a kayak or swim a round in the sea. Afterwards you will go back to the port, where you will have solid ground under your feet a little later. Your car is ready for you and will take you back to Hanoi. Enjoy the free afternoon in this charming city and let yourself drift. Narrow alleys and traditional shops, lakes, parks, boulevards and monuments invite you to explore. Overnight in Hanoi.

8th day Flight to Nha Trang & Paradise bathing stay

After breakfast, you will be taken to the airport and fly on to Nha Trang, where your paradisiacal bathing stay begins. Lonely island feeling meets unique design and first-class service! Welcome to Six Senses Ninh Van Bay! From the airport in Nha Trang it is about an hour by car to the boat dock in the north of Nha Trang. From there, you can reach this resort in just 20 minutes by motorboat. Feel in your villa like the last remaining people on earth, stranded in paradise. Surrounded by white sand beaches, tropical vegetation and impressive rock formations, the resort offers a unique atmosphere. The 59 spacious villas of the Six Senses Ninh Van Bay are built in a mixture of modern and native Vietnamese architecture and grant a lot of privacy due to their special location: nestled in rock formations, quietly situated in a tropical garden or high above the water overlooking the bay, each villa is an oasis of tranquility. Overnight in Nha Trang.

9.-11. Day Exclusive bathing in Nha Trang

Without tour guide & chauffeur

Enjoy the sun, beach and sea and the amenities of your first-class hotel. Go the days slowly, make time for a good book or the sweet nothing-doing. Overnight in Nha Trang.

12th day of travel home

Today, unfortunately, it is time to say goodbye. You will be driven to Nha Trang Airport, where you will board your home or onward flight. There are countless experiences and impressions in your luggage that will accompany you throughout your life.

Vietnam and Laos Travel - Off the beaten track

Vietnam Laos Travel: Experience the rural regions of Laos and Vietnam on this private trip from Asia Special Tours. We will be happy to advise you!

Itinerary & services

This exciting journey to the ethnic groups and colorful mountain peoples takes you into a completely different world and makes you quickly forget the hectic and noisy city life. Huge mountain peaks, rushing rivers, pure nature... Discover Vietnam’s rugged north and mountainous northern Laos on hikes and bike rides far from the beaten tourist trail. This active tour not only takes you through unique landscapes, but also offers you intimate insights into the life of the tribes who have preserved their traditions secluded amidst nature. Enjoy spectacular views, unique natural landscapes, abandoned villages and warm people. Your adventurous journey finally ends in the mystical Luang Prabang, which will certainly quickly captivate you with its special charm.

1st day Arrival in Hanoi & Leisure

Welcome to Vietnam. Upon arrival in Hanoi, you will already be expected by your guide and taken to your hotel. Hanoi is a unique and charming city with green boulevards, interesting architectural buildings in French colonial style, idyllic rivers and fascinating temples. In recent years, the city has grown into one of the modern art centers within Asia. Relax a little from your arrival and take a leisurely walk through the old town of Hanoi. Your centrally located hotel is the ideal starting point for a first exploration tour. Overnight in Hanoi.

2nd Day City Tour of Hanoi & Lunch in a Cooking Kitchen

After refreshing yourself with breakfast, visit Hanoi’s main attractions: your first stop is the Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum, as well as its private residence from 1954 to 1969. The wooden ethnic minority house built on stilts is surrounded by a beautiful quiet garden. Inside the same complex is the one-stake pagoda, founded by King Ly Thai in 1049, and another highlight is the visit to the Temple of Literature, built in 1070 for the veneration of Confucius, which only a few years later made its name as Vietnam's first university. Next, visit the old town of Hanois, also known as the “36 Streets”. Near a local market, you will be guided to an authentic street kitchen restaurant. There you will have the opportunity to try small grilled meatballs and spring rolls “Hanoi Style” along with green salad, a mountain of fresh herbs and a spicy dip sauce. The evening will ring in a classic Bia Hoi one, the Vietnamese counterpart to the German beer garden. Here you get a wonderfully refreshing light beer from the barrel. Further insights into the unique Vietnamese culture will be offered by performing a traditional water puppet theater. Overnight in Hanoi.

3rd day bicycle tour through the picturesque Mai Chau Valley

In the morning, drive to Mai Chau, passing small villages, rice paddies and sugar cane fields. The place is located in a beautiful valley. For several centuries, the relatives of the tribe of White Thai have lived here. Thais are known for their harmonious community and still maintain many of their centuries-old traditions in modern times. You reach the village of minorities around noon and enjoy a delicious lunch. Then ride your bike from the lodge and begin your tour of discovering the daily life of ethnic minorities in the area. You cycle through peaceful villages with traditional pile buildings, past lush green rice fields, where the locals farm with the help of water buffaloes. In the evening, you will be entertained by a traditional dance of Thai minorities. Overnight in Mai Chau.

4th day walk to Van Mai village & scenic boat ride

Today you start the day with a hike to Van Mai village. The easy-to-manage path takes you along green rice fields and past smaller settlements. Around noon, you will arrive at Van Mai village, where a refreshing lunch will be served. After a short break, head to Co Luong in the afternoon, where you will take a scenic boat tour on the mighty Ma River. Then return by car to Mai Chau, where you can relax the day. Overnight in Mai Chau.

5th day walking in the middle of untouched nature

In the morning we will continue to Pu Luong. Here you can expect untouched nature, dense forests and countless rice terraces. The clocks tick much slower and so this area is a real insider tip for adventurers. Your walk today will take you across a mountain ridge tip of the villages of Dong Deang and Kho Muong. Enjoy gigantic views and moments when time seems to stop. Across the valley, your path leads out of the nature reserve, where you will be invited to lunch with a local family. Relax a little and enjoy authentic insights into the everyday life of the locals. In the afternoon, wander for another two hours through breathtaking landscapes. Your way leads you back to the road, where the vehicle is already waiting for you and takes you to your hotel. Let the evening end according to your wishes. Overnight in Pu Luong.

6th day picnic in the rice terraces

A new day, a new adventure. Your walk today will take you from the Don village to the Uoi village. Follow the paths that the local population takes through the forest and enjoy a picnic view of the rice terraces. In the afternoon, your hike will take you through Ban Cong village to the Suoi Cham River, where you will see numerous water wheels. Then we return to the hotel. Overnight in Pu Luong.

Day 7 - Continue to Laos & Visit the Vieng Xai Caves

Today you will leave Pu Luong and continue to Na Meo, on the border with Laos. Your Laotian guide is already waiting for you and together you will go to Sam Neua. On the way, visit the Vieng Xai Caves. During the Indochina War period, Vieng Xai was the headquarters of the pro-communist liberation movement Pathet Lao. The leadership used the caves in Vieng Xai as apartments and shelters. In and around Vieng Xai there are 102 known caves. In the late afternoon you will finally reach Sam Neua. Overnight in Sam Neua.

8th day Wonderful mountain scenery on the way to Nong Khiaw

After breakfast, drive via Vieng Thong to Nong Khiaw. The ride leads through wonderful mountain scenery, which often offers you fascinating distant views. Again and again you will see villages of different ethnicities, such as those of the Hmong, the Khmu and the Tai Dam. If time permits, visit waterfalls and hot springs that lie along the way. Overnight in Nong Khiaw.

9th day boat trip on the Nam Ou & dreamlike Muang La Resort

Today you board a private longtail boat and sail downstream on one of the most beautiful stretches of river in Laos. Unlike the Mekong, the Nam Ou is viewed more dramatically and wildly and there is much less “traffic”. Enjoy a picnic along the way and see some local villages as you drive downriver. In the late afternoon you will arrive at Muong Khoua, where you will continue by car to Muang La. Muang La Resort has a spa, sauna and steam bath (especially from November to March it gets very cold here in the evening). The resort is next to a natural hot spring and every evening a small pool is filled with the water of the hot spring. The evenings here are a delight: you will be pampered with a 5-course menu that combines Asian and European cuisine, candlelight and the sounds of nature. Overnight at Muang La.

10th day Hike through picturesque mountain landscapes

A short trip on a Jeep will take you to the Nam Pak River, where you will be taken to a small mountain road. This is where your walk begins today. On a narrow mountain path it goes through the lush green forest. Then you arrive at the Kamu village Huay Ho, where life goes on at a very relaxed pace. Your guide will answer your questions and tell you about the lives of the villagers. You will wander through lush green rice fields and reach the Lao Loum village of Phongsavan, located in the heart of a beautiful small valley. Now it is time for a strengthening picnic in the middle of the beautiful landscape of North Laos. After lunch, follow a mountain trail and pass by local farms. Your guide will explain to you the native plants and herbs of the forest, which are used for cooking and also as medicine. Through small bamboo bridges you can reach the Yang village Long Ya. Here you can see how peaceful the life of the villagers is. The village has a small Buddhist temple. In the late afternoon you will return to your hotel, where you can treat yourself to a well-deserved rest. Overnight in Muang La.

Day 11 - Continue via Oudomxay to Luang Prabang

They leave Muang La this morning and travel to Oudomxay province. The journey time is approximately six hours, including a short stop for a picnic on the way. The road is a little uneven in some places, but you will drive through a beautiful landscape that will distract you from the road conditions. Stop whenever you want. This northern region of Laos is very mountainous and overgrown by countless rice fields and plantations that share the otherwise dense jungle. Then you reach the capital of Oudomxay province. It used to be a very sleepy city, but over the years it became an important trade hub and shows the rapid development of this region. In the late afternoon you will reach your daily destination Luang Prabang, where you will be taken to your hotel. In Luang Prabang, the spiritual capital of the country, there is little traffic on the roads. Instead, monks wriggle across the streets in their typical orange robes and with umbrellas stretched out in all directions. The enchanting place with historic temples and colonial buildings was declared a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1995. Overnight in Luang Prabang.

12th Day Bicycle Tour of Luang Prabang & Refreshing Bath in Kuang Si Waterfalls

Luang Prabang is a provincial city where time seems to stand still. Since there is little traffic on Luang Prabang's roads, it is an excellent place for a bike ride. The leisurely bicycle tour takes you to the city center, which is located on a peninsula 300 meters above sea level. There you will find historic buildings, the royal palace, as well as temples and monasteries. At the Wat Mai Temple you make a stopover. Visit one of the most beautiful temples in the city. The Wat Xieng Thong, a large collection of sacred buildings, is one of the jewels of Laotian art. Afterwards, stop at the oldest temple in town: Wat Visoun with its wooden windows is inspired by the Khmer temple Vat Phou. In the afternoon, head to Kuang Si’s rushing waterfalls, 32 km from Luang Prabang. Along the way, visit some villages of ethnic minorities. The falls rush over several levels of a limestone formation, creating a series of turquoise shimmering swimming pools that are perfect for a swim on a hot day. Then return to Luang Prabang. Overnight in Luang Prabang.

13th Day Alms of the Monks & Boat Trip to the Pak Ou Caves

Getting up early is worthwhile in the holy city: then you can watch the monks wandering through the streets in their orange coats in the morning and receiving rice and other food donations from the citizens. Then you will be taken to the pier, where a local boat is already ready for you. Sit back and let yourself pass by on a ride on the mystical Mekong the rural life, lush green landscapes and peaceful scenery on the shores. After a good hour you reach the sacred Pak-Ou caves. The caves are among the most important Buddhist pilgrimage sites in Laos. Inside it are thousands of gold-painted Buddha statues. Take enough time to visit before returning to Luang Prabang. The rest of the day is at your disposal. Let yourself drift in this charming city and enjoy a delicious cup of coffee in one of the numerous cafes. Overnight in Luang Prabang.

Day 14: Farewell to Laos

Even when your holiday is over, the precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories of your trip will accompany you forever. Look forward to letting your friends and family share your experiences in the distance at home. Your chauffeur will take you for your return flight to Luang Prabang airport for your home – or onward flight.

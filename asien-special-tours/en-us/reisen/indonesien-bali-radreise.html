Bali Round Trip - Bicycle Over the Island of the Gods

Bali Round Trip: Explore the Island of the Gods by bicycle on an individual Bali round trip from Asia Special Tours. Ask now!

Experience Bali completely unadulterated: on the bike. In this way, you will have the unique opportunity to get to know the way of life of the locals and the Balinese culture directly and up close. You cannot collect more authentic impressions of this island. Visit beautiful places and fantastic temples. Bali’s tropical nature will immediately captivate you! In charming coastal towns, you can relax on magnificent beaches and recharge for the next stages. Always set your own pace on the bike rides and take breaks wherever you want. An escort vehicle is available to you at any time. Embark on a sporting adventure and discover the enchanting island of the gods on the saddle of a bicycle.

1st day Arrival in Bali

Upon arrival at the island’s capital, Denpasar, you will be greeted by your guide and taken directly to your hotel by your driver. Relax a little from your arrival and enjoy your extremely charming accommodation, which is very tastefully nestled in a tropical green garden with romantic lotus ponds. Overnight in Canggu.

2nd Day Tanah Lot and Royal Temple Taman Ayun

This morning you will visit one of the most famous buildings in Bali, the Sea Temple of Tanah Lot. The sea stamp from the 16th century sits on a rock top formed by the tides in the sea. Only at low tide can the temple of dry foot be reached. Entry into the temple is permitted only practicing Balinese Hindus. Then start your big bicycle adventure through Bali. After briefing and discussing the itinerary, travel through the villages of Nyambu and Tapesan to the peaceful temple of Taman Ayun. Stop in the traditional villages while enjoying a fabulous view of the rice terraces. Lunch is served at a local restaurant in Gulingan. Afterwards you cycle past the monkey forest of Bonkasa and on to Ubud to move into your hotel in the late afternoon. Overnight in Ubud.

3rd day Fascinating mountains & picturesque Bratansee

After breakfast, you will start your bicycle tour into the Balinese mountains. Passing the village of Taman, you will drive up and down the hills and see even more of the breathtakingly beautiful rice fields, coffee and cocoa plantations. After crossing more traditional villages, you will reach Bedugul and the picturesque temple Ulun Danu. Located on a small island in the quiet lake, the temple gives the impression that it is swimming. In Bedugul you will have lunch in a typical restaurant. Afterwards, take a car up a narrow mountain road and enjoy a magnificent view of the Buyan and Tamblingan lakes. Here you can take in the scenery again before traveling on to the magnificent remote area of Munduk in the northern part of Bali. Overnight in Munduk.

4th day coffee plantations in Munduk & hot springs in Banjar

The next stage of your bike ride is already waiting for you. Today you will see plantations where coffee, cloves, vanilla, avocados and much more are grown. Watch the farmers in the fields grow and harvest the fruit. Continue on to the Banjar area where you can take a dip in the natural hot springs. After lunch at a local restaurant, you will ride the bike to Lovina. The small town is located on the north coast of Bali and has a dark, volcanic beach. The rest of the time you can freely arrange according to your ideas. Relax by the pool or take an extensive beach walk. Overnight in Lovina.

5th day bike ride in and around Lovina

After breakfast, you will start on a half-day tour. By bike you can explore Lovina’s surroundings and Lovina itself. Among other things, you will visit a Rambutan fruit plantation. This tropical plant species is related to the lychee tree and is very popular in Asia. Experience the activities of the villagers up close and make contacts with locals. The afternoon is at your disposal for discoveries on your own. Overnight in Lovina.

6th Day Enchanting Northeast Coast & Diving Areas in Tulamben

In the morning, take a car to the starting point of the bike ride, which is located at the Temple Ponjok Batu. In this impressive temple complex, all buildings are built of black stone. The drive passes the villages of Bondalem, Tianyar and Kubu, where you can always watch scenes of daily life. You will continue to the small village of Tulamben, which is just as famous for its overwhelming sea views as it is for the diving areas there. Off the coast of Tulamben is the wreck of the USS Liberty. After your arrival, you can use the offer of diving opportunities and other water sports activities on your own. Overnight in Tulamben.

7th Day Idyllic Amed & Boat Tour with a Prahu

Today you set off for a short bike ride to Amed. Amed is a quiet place by the sea where you can fish or snorkel and dive on the nearby reef. The beach of Amed is black, stony and of volcanic origin. For an hour you will ride along the coast on a wooden Prahu - a typical Bali outrigger boat of fishermen - and gather unique views and impressions. The rest of the day you can spend according to your own wishes in this enchanting small coastal village. Feel the real Balinese life up close here. Overnight in Amed.

8th Day Dreamlike White Sand Beach of Jasri

Back on the saddle of your bike, today it goes along the coast to Manggis. A magnificent view of the sea follows the next. Also watch the many fishermen who go about their work. This type of travel will allow you to gain deep insights into the Balinese culture. Then visit Seraya Ujung, a place that has preserved the history of the former Balinese kingdom to this day. You will continue cycling to the beautiful white sand beach of Jasri, Virgin Beach near Candidasa. This beach is considered one of the most beautiful beaches in Bali. Here you can relax, feel the warm sand under your feet and take a look at the distant islands. Overnight in Manggis.

Day 9 Hike to the traditional Aga village of Tenganan

By car you leave for Kastala to take a two-hour hike through the rice fields to Tenganan from there. At the end of the hike, explore Tenganan, one of Bali’s many traditional villages. It is home to the oldest Balinese people, the Aga. They were the first inhabitants of the island. Here you can discover the original architecture of the village and watch as the villagers weave the rare “double Ikat” pattern and baskets. After that, return to your hotel and have the rest of the day at your disposal. Overnight in Manggis.

10th day Departure from Bali

Your time out from everyday life is coming to an end. Even if your holiday is soon over, the precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories of your trip will accompany you forever. The day is at your leisure before you are taken to Denpasar airport for your return flight. Or would you like to relax on the beautiful beaches of Bali? We are happy to make proposals in this regard.

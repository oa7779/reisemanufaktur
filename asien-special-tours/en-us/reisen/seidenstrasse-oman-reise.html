1st day Arrival in Oman

Welcome to Oman! Upon arrival, you will already be expected and brought to your hotel. Look forward to exciting days full of new impressions! Oman offers you what your heart desires: beaches, deserts, a wildlife and a primordial flora and fauna. Your exclusive hotel, The Chedi Muscat, welcomes you with great views of the Gulf of Oman, a vast garden and Omani-style villas and rooms. Overnight in Muscat.

Day 2 Explore in Muscat

You will start your visit at the large mosque of Muscat. It was built between 1995 and 2001 by Sultan Qabus and since then bears his name. Due to its size of about four hectares, the mosque is one of the largest in the world and at the same time one of the most important buildings of Oman. Afterwards, drive along Embassy Street. Your eventful day will take you to the Royal Opera House, which reflects Oman’s unique contemporary architecture. You will continue to the Muttrah district, which is located on the edge of the old Muscat. The coastal road, also known as Corniche, forms the border with the old town. The coastal road takes you along Oman’s main port, Mina Al Sultan Qaboos and past the souk of Muttrah. Please hop off to let the colors and smells affect you. During a visit to the Bait Al Zubair Museum, you will get an insight into Oman’s former way of life. At the port is the center for the administration of the Omani state. In addition to the palace authorities and government buildings, the palace of Sultan Qaboos can also be found here. Here you can visit the building located at the front of the bay, but only from the outside. If you let your gaze wander over the area, the two fortresses Djalali and Mirani immediately catch your eye. Overnight in Muscat.

3rd day mountain villages and field terraces in Al Hamra

After breakfast, head towards Nakhl. The village is located about 120 km west of Muscat in front of the spectacular backdrop of the Jebel Nakhl Massif. In front of the mighty mountains, large gardens expand, which have given the entire district of Nakhl its name, because "nakhl" means "date palms". The imposing fortress of Nakhl, also called Husn Al Heem, rises above the village on a rock sixty meters high. The next stop is Wabi Bani Habib, a dry river bed that stretches for about 30 km and on the sides of which small pretty villages line up. Afterwards, a special highlight awaits you with the Wadi Bani Awf. This wadi is also better known under the name “Snake Gorge” due to the variety of desert snakes living there. Let’s go – high up to the summit, where you will visit the mountain village of Bilad Al Sayt. Only a few kilometers away you will find the old mountain village of Misfat al Abriyin. Here you will still find traditional houses and alleys and multi-storey houses built of clay. Also known are the beautiful field terraces that surround the mountain together with the old buildings. Overnight in Hail Al Shas.

Day 4 Mountains around Jebel Akhdar

Today, they are heading for Jebel Akhdar. On the way, head to Jabreen Castle, one of the most beautiful castles built towards the end of the 17th century. They also stop at the Bahla Fortress, the oldest and first fortress built in Islamic style. Then you will pass Nizvah. Above the rooftops of Nizwa, the largest fortress of the Arabian peninsula was built in 1668. The fortress was once used as the headquarters of Iman's. Today you have a wonderful view of buildings and the bustling market. The journey continues to Jebel Akhdar, where you will move into your beautiful hotel - the Alila Jabal Akhdar. Enjoy the view of the surrounding mountains and refresh yourself in one of the indoor or outdoor pools. Overnight in Jebel Akhdar.

Day 5: Three Village Walk

Al Aqr, Al Ayn and Ash Shirayjah are the most important archaeological sites in Oman. The scenic wonder owes the area to the irrigation system, which makes the cliffs terraced. Pomegranates, pears, grapes, garlic and onions are grown and harvested in the region. You can also pick aromatic Damascus roses if you wish. Visit this beautiful area and let yourself be enchanted! Overnight in Jebel Akhdar.

Day 6: A Night in the Desert

After breakfast, on your way through the desert, you will pass the old oasis town of Birkat al Mauz. This area used to be very popular for banana cultivation, today mainly dates are grown. Here are still old ruins of a former settlement. Then continue on to the desert to Wahiba Sands. Here you will experience an adventurous ride through white and red sand dunes that rise up to 200 meters. In the desert there are many animals and plants. Here also live the Bedouins and nomads, who will surely meet you again and again with their camels. Arriving at the desert camp you can enjoy the breathtaking scenery in peace. Despite its drought, the desert shows a breathtaking flora and fauna. For the evening, enjoy an Arabic dinner, Omani coffee and delicious dates before spending the night under the dreamlike starry sky. Overnight in Wahiba Sands.

Day 7: Watching Turtles

Today you experience the direct contrast to your desert experience. Jump into deep blue and clear waters and cool down in the middle of the desert. Oman is known for its numerous wadis. These are natural pools that can be found in the desert. Stop at Wadi Bani Khalid while enjoying the deep blue waters during a break. If you like it sporty, you can also go for a swim here. The Wadi Bani Khalid lies within the Wahiba Desert. In the evening they will be against 21.00 Clock picked up by rangers at your hotel and start your 15-minute walk. Your destination is the green turtles, which can be found in the tranquility of Ras Al Jinz beach. Enjoy this wonderful experience while watching the turtles at their egg shelves. Overnight in Al Hadd.

Day 8 Along the coast back to Muscat

After breakfast, continue your journey to Sur. Once there, drive directly to the fishing port to watch the fishermen at work. Here you will visit a factory where the traditional dhows, handmade wooden boats, are made. Then your journey will slowly take you back to Muscat. Along the way, visit Wadi Tiwi, a beautiful and turquoise-blue natural pool landscape. You will also see the Bimah Sinkhole during your trip, which was created by the collapse of a mountain and today is filled with crystal-clear turquoise water. At the end of this impressive day, you will pass the village of Quriyat, which is protected by its fortress built in the 18th century. Overnight in Muscat.

Day 9 Departure from Oman

Your journey is coming to an end. Even if your holiday is soon over, the precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories from your trip will accompany you forever. Your driver will take you to the airport for your return flight to Muscat, where you will board your flight home.

Exotic flower scents and a touch of vanilla are the perfume of these paradisiacal islands in the Pacific. Soundful names like Bora Bora, Moorea and the Marquesasa archipelago, home to Paul Gauguin, await you – who wouldn’t dream of it? The ship Aranui 5 supplies the inhabitants of the archipelago of Marquesas with consumer goods and food. The inhabitants of this remote island await every arrival on their islands with great longing and warm welcome. Become part of this unique, Polynesian lifestyle on the journey of your life. Nowhere do you come as close to the way of life in the South Seas as here. Relax at the end of the journey in a beautiful island resort on Moorea, lined with turquoise sea. Typical for French Polynesia are the Overwater Bungalows. Imagine waking up and hearing the sound of the sea beneath you and being able to glide right from your own terrace into the warm, turquoise blue lagoon.

1st day Arrival in Tahiti

Ia Ora! Today you reach Papeete, the international airport of the main island of Tahiti. Our local staff will be waiting for you at the airport. They are taken directly from the airport to their booked resort and can arrive in peace and fight their jet lag. Overnight in Tahiti.

Day 2 Tahiti - the gateway to French Polynesia

Crowned by majestic mountain peaks, Tahiti, the largest island of French Polynesia, towers like a proud queen over the sea. The mountainous interior is rich in natural impressions with gorges, crystal clear rivers and high waterfalls. The majority of the islanders live along the coast, which left the heart of the island almost untouched and primitive despite its proximity to the bustling capital Papeete. Papeete, which means “water basket”, was once a meeting place for Tahitians who filled their bottle squashes with fresh water. Today, Papeete forms the center of the island nation with world-class resorts, spas, Tahitian and gourmet restaurants, nightclubs, lively markets, museums, pearl shops and boutiques. Overnight in Tahiti.

3.-14. Day your dream cruise on Aranui 5

Explore the charming Marquesas Islands with the cargo and cruise ship Aranui 5. The ship supplies the inhabitants of the archipelago of Marquesas with consumer goods and food. The inhabitants of this remote island await every arrival on their islands with great longing and warm welcome. Become part of this unique, Polynesian lifestyle on the journey of your life. Nowhere do you come as close to the way of life in the South Seas as here. During the two-week cruise, which includes all meals and excursions in the price of the trip, the modern and comfortable ship will be your home. Discover the magic that has attracted Paul Gauguin, Robert Louis Stevenson, Thor Heyerdahl and Jacques Brel. The warm crew members who have been on the ship for years will welcome you and familiarize you with their centuries-old customs. Together with the expert and multilingual guest lecturers and guides, they will answer all your questions as you travel through the fascinating island world.

15th day Disembarkation and crossing to Moorea

Today you will return to Tahiti to land. It will certainly be difficult for you to leave your floating hotel, which has grown to your heart over the last two weeks. But there are other highlights waiting for you! Overnight in Moorea.

Day 16 Your Stay at Manava Beach Resort & Spa Moorea

Manava Beach Resort & Spa Moorea is located right on the beach by the village of Maharepa, in an ideal location, close to the majestic Cook's Bay and the Gulf of Moorea. This traditional and modern Polynesian-style resort offers a restaurant, two friendly bars, an infinity pool, a hotel-owned spa, a boutique and its own diving center. As part of Manava Beach Resort & Spa Moorea’s commitment to environmental protection, a coral garden called “To Nui” has been set up. This park allows a colony of corals to regenerate under perfect ecological conditions. Overnight in Moorea

Day 17 Return to Tahiti

Today, the ferry will take you back to Tahiti. Upon arrival, your driver will take you to your booked hotel. Overnight in Tahiti.

18th day onwards or homewards

Sit back and let the past days pass again mentally. These unforgettable memories will accompany you for a lifetime

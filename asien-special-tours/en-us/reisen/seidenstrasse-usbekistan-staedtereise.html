Uzbekistan Journey - Legendary Cities

Uzbekistan Travel: Legendary cities await you on an individual Uzbekistan trip with Asia Special Tours, your Asia expert. Ask now!

With this trip we will take you to the most famous cities on the Uzbek Silk Road. But also places, of which many Uzbekistan travelers have not yet heard, are on the program. Let yourself be enchanted by the magnificent Samarkand, the medieval oasis town of Bukhara and the desert town of Khiva, where time seems to stand still. We will also let you in on the secrets of the former Buddhist center Termez, visit the “green city” of chess risabs and follow the footsteps of Alexander the Great in Nurata. In the secluded village of Boysun you still follow centuries-old traditions, the mountain landscapes invite you to hike and the Aydarkul Lake to jump in. Look forward to a varied and versatile journey through the land of legendary cities.

1st day Arrival in Tashkent

Welcome to Uzbekistan! When you arrive in Tashkent, you will already be welcomed by your German-speaking tour guide. Together with your private chauffeur you drive to the hotel, where you can arrive in peace. Overnight in Tashkent.

Day 2 Tashkent with all its facets

Enjoy a discovery tour through the capital of Uzbekistan, which will bring you closer to the ancient squares and buildings, but also the modern part of the 2,000-year-old city. First you will visit the Kukeldash Medrese, which still serves as a Koranic school today. You will also see Khasti Imon Square with the beautiful Barak Khan Medrese, Tillya Sheikh Mosque and Kaffal Shashi Mausoleum made of simple brick masonry. A stroll over the huge Chorsu bazaar lets you experience everyday life with all your senses.In the afternoon you will get to know the modern capital and visit the Independence Square, Navoi Park and the Parliament building. Then stroll through Amir Timur Square, named after the famous Central Asian conqueror Tamerlan. Don’t miss an underground metro ride either: Tashkent is the only Central Asian city to have a subway, which is also a small architectural and artistic masterpiece. Its rooms in a villa are already an eye candy with their rich embellishments. Carpets, gold embroidery, ceramics, porcelain, jewellery, small furniture and musical instruments will be exhibited. Overnight in Tashkent.

3rd day Onward journey to one of the oldest cities in the world: Termez

After breakfast, you will be taken to the airport to travel to the south of the country – to Termez. Termez is one of the oldest cities in the world. The city is located on the north bank of Amudarja and thus on the border with Afghanistan. It is especially significant because of its Buddhist heritage. Your guide will show you Fayaz-Tepa, a 1st century BC Buddhist monastic complex. During your exploration tour, you will also visit the breathtaking mausoleum of Hakim-at-Termezi with a white marble tomb and the mausoleum of Sultan Saodat, burial site of the Zaid family, whose members were considered direct descendants of the Prophet Muhammad. The archaeological museum of Termez is one of the best in Uzbekistan – so you should not miss it. Overnight in Termez.

Day 4 Termes - Boysun

Your journey will take you to the village of Boysun, idyllic in a trough, nestled in the beautiful landscape of Surchandarja area. You will believe you are traveling to the past. Experience the pure tradition and immerse yourself in a world where centuries-old culture and customs are still alive. The secluded location has certainly helped to preserve the traditional lifestyle for so long. You will visit the gallery of the painter Tura Shamurasev – facing the Gauguin of Boysun – a local craft center and a bazaar. The village is ideal for talking to the residents and learning more about their everyday life. Spend the night with an Uzbek family (very simple accommodation, without comfort and bathroom).

Day 5 - Chess risabs continue to Samarkand

After a traditional breakfast with your host family, continue the journey towards Samarkand. Along the way, stop to explore Chessrisabs – the “green city” of Central Asia – which is a UNESCO World Heritage Site. You will visit the impressive Ak Saray Palace, which according to stories was built by Amir Temur. The city has even more attractions, such as the Kuk-Gumbaz Mosque and the tomb of Tamerlan’s son: Jakhangir and the Hazrati Imam Mosque. Through a scenic area you drive on to Samarkand. Overnight in Samarkand.

Day 6: Legendary Samarkand

Breakfast – then one of the highlights of this trip awaits you: Registanplatz, framed by the three magnificent medres: Ulugbek, Sher-Dor and Tilla-Kori. Continue to the Gur-Emir mausoleum complex, which is particularly beautiful due to Timuridian monumental architecture. You will also see the excavation site of Afrosiab and the dead city of Shakhi Zinda. If you still have a desire and energy, you should visit the Bibi Khanum Mosque in the afternoon – an absolute must for many Samarkand visitors! Depending on your preference, you can spend the remaining time in the colorful bustle of the Siab Bazaar (closed on Mondays). Overnight in Samarkand.

Day 7 Samarkand – Nurata – Yangigazgan

Today it goes out into nature, first to Nurata. On a small guided tour, you will get to know the historic city more closely. The city of Nurata is a well-known pilgrimage site where you will visit the Chil Ustun Mosque as well as the ruins of Alexander the Great’s fortress. The place is also known for its chashma spring – it is sacred along with the fish floating in the water and the destination of many pilgrims. Afterwards we continue to the yurt camp in Yangigazgan, where you will spend the night. Before dinner at the campfire, take advantage of the opportunity and ride on a camel through the camp. Overnight at the yurt camp.

Day 8 Yangigazgan – Lake Aydarkul – Bukhara

Did you sleep well in the yurt? Grab strong at breakfast, because the exciting adventure continues. You drive a short distance to the dreamlike Aydarkul Lake. Just lie down in the sun, walk by the lake or – if it is warm enough – jump into the water calmly. Then continue on towards the oasis city of Bukhara. Along the way, visit the town of Gijduvan, best known for its excellent ceramic craftsmanship. The ceramic styles there are particularly beautiful to look at due to the elaborate ornaments and combinations of geometric figures. In the schools you can watch the ceramic production live. Overnight in Bukhara.

Day 9 Bukhara's Miracle

After all the nature, there is another exciting city tour that takes you to the most magnificent sights of Bukhara. You will see the mausoleum of the former Samanid ruler Ismael Samani from the 9th and 10th centuries, Chashmai Ayub and the beautiful Bolo Hauz Mosque. Other highlights are the gigantic Ark Citadel in the ground plan shape of the constellation “Great Chariot” and the Poi Kalon complex, which consists of a minaret, a mosque and the beautiful Miri Arab Medrese. Also worth seeing is the Ulugbek Medrese, which was named after the famous astronomer. In addition, you can experience the daily activity in the three overcoupled bazaars and at the silk market up close. Your guide also introduces you to the secrets of the complex Labi Hauz, which invites you to linger: Around the water basin “Hauz” several tea rooms are grouped, in which the Bucharers meet for the game of dominoes. Once, almost all inhabitants of the city were supplied with water with the help of the water basin. The fitting conclusion of a perfect day is the dance performance in a medrese. Overnight in Bukhara.

Day 10 - Through the desert to Khiva

An exciting ride ahead of you today. On an ancient caravan road, cross the Kyzyilkum Desert, cruising the Amu-Darja River to reach the ancient city of Khiva. On the way, there are always opportunities for photo stops. After accommodation at your hotel in Chiwa, you can familiarize yourself with this breathtaking city on a first walk.

Day 11 Khiva – Urgench – Tashkent

After breakfast, you are now completely devoted to exploring Chiwa. The small oasis town in the middle of the desert has preserved the fairy-tale atmosphere of an ancient, oriental city to this day. You will visit the old town of Khiva – Itchan Kala – with the beautifully decorated Islam Khodja Minaret, Pakhlavan Mahmoud Mausoleum and Juma Mosque. Enjoy the medres of Mohammed Amin-Khan and Rachim-Khan, the castle of Khan Tash-Hauli – where a cobra is said to still guard the gold treasure of the last Khan – and other fascinating buildings. Everywhere in the old town you will encounter fine, intricate wood carvings – an art that has been cultivated in Khiva since ancient times. Keep your camera at hand! In the evening you will be taken to Urgentsch Airport, from where you will return by domestic flight to Tashkent. Overnight in Tashkent.

Day 12 Departure from Tashkent

Today, unfortunately, we say goodbye. With 1001 new impressions and experiences in your luggage, you will be taken to the airport of Tashkent, from where you will start your journey home. The beautiful memories will always remain for you and often beautify everyday life.

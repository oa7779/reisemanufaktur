General travel conditions

The following conditions serve to clearly present and regulate the legal relationships between the customer and ART even after the entry into force of the new version of the travel law, in particular §§ 651 a ff BGB, taking into account the legal regulations. All cited paragraphs refer to the version of the BGB valid from 1.7.2018.

2. Executing air carrier

EU Regulation No 2111 of 14.12.2005 requires tour operators, travel agents and carriers of transport contracts to inform their customers of the identity of each operating airline before the relevant air transport takes place as soon as it is established. To the extent that this is not yet the case at the time of booking, the likely operating airline must first be indicated. In the event of change of the operating airline after booking, the customer shall be informed immediately.

3. Booking of a trip by ART

1. A contract is concluded according to the legal regulation only at the time when completely identical declarations of intent of the contracting parties (offer and acceptance of this offer) are available, the acceptance must have taken place in good time. Only statements of interest on both sides (booking request or travel proposal) are not yet an offer, but are merely invitations to submit offers. An offer can be limited in time, otherwise it can only be accepted within the usual period, which is 10 days without any special circumstances. A delayed acceptance represents a new offer, so that the roles can change when submitting the contractual declarations. After your booking request, we usually prepare a travel proposal first, which, in consultation with you, ultimately leads to an individual offer in text form under determination of an acceptance period. With your timely acceptance of our offer, which can take place in text or form-free, the travel contract is concluded.

2. Changing or supplementary agreements to the described services as well as to the travel conditions require an express agreement with ART. It should be made in writing for reasons of evidence. Travel agents shall not be authorised to make any undertaking derogating from the content of the travel description or the travel conditions or to make any amending or supplementary arrangements.

4. Payment

1. All payments on the travel price, i.e. also the down payment, are to be made only against delivery of the security note within the meaning of § 651 r BGB. You will receive this security note with the travel confirmation/invoice. Upon receipt of the security note, a deposit of 20% of the travel price is due.

2. The balance must be paid no later than 28 days before departure. In the case of contracts concluded later than 29 days before departure, the entire travel price is due immediately upon delivery of the security note. The travel documents are sent after payment, alternatively the documents can be collected at or after payment.

5. Resignation by the customer/rebooking/replacement participant

5.1 If there are unavoidable and exceptional circumstances at or near the destination of the trip which significantly affect the performance of the trip or the transport of persons to the destination, you may withdraw from the contract free of charge before the start of the trip. Inevitable and exceptional circumstances exist if they are not subject to the control of the Contracting Party, which refers to them and their consequences could not have been avoided even by all reasonable precautions (§ 651 h para. 3 BGB). Such a right to withdraw free of charge also exists under the conditions set out in paragraph 7.4 (price increase above 8%) or in the event of a significant change in an essential part of the travel service.

5.2 Apart from the cases dealt with in point 5.1, you may withdraw at any time before the start of your trip. However, ART then has the statutory right to adequate compensation (§ 651 h para. 1 and 2 BGB), the amount of the compensation required must be justified upon your request. Since, depending on the booked services of the trip, high compensation amounts may arise in individual cases, we point out in this context to paragraph 8 of these conditions.

5.3 In all cases of withdrawal, ART loses the right to the agreed travel price and must refund any amounts already paid on it immediately.

5.4 The customer's entitlement to changes after conclusion of the contract generally does not exist. Only rebooking requests of the customer without legal claim, if their execution is at all possible, are made only as cancellation by the travel participant under the conditions of section 5.2 with simultaneous registration.

5.5 Within a reasonable period of time, but usually no later than seven days before the start of the journey, you can use a durable medium (e.g. E. g. Letter, e-mail, fax) require a third party named by you to enter into the rights and obligations arising from the travel contract. ART may object to entry if the third party does not meet the contractual travel requirements. In the event of entry, original and new participants will be jointly liable as a total debtor for the travel price and the additional costs incurred by the entry. The original travel participant shall be provided with proof as to the amount of additional costs incurred by the third party. Additional costs may only be required to an appropriate extent and ART as an organiser must have actually arisen.

6. One-sided termination by ART

Is ART due to unavoidable, exceptional circumstances (cf. Paragraph 5.1.) prevents the performance of the contract, ART may declare its withdrawal without delay after knowledge of the reason for its withdrawal before the start of the trip. Payments already received on the travel price must be refunded immediately thereafter.

10. Rights and obligations in the event of defects

10.1 A lack of travel must be reported immediately (see also 10.5). Remedial requests and notification of defects must be addressed to our local agency/tour guide (name and address can be found in the travel documents). Where possible and reasonable, they shall be addressed to ART.

10.2 If the trip is not performed in accordance with the contract, the customer may request a remedy from ART. ART may refuse the remedy if it is impossible or is disproportionately burdensome, taking into account the extent of the defect and the value of the travel service concerned.

10.3 If ART does not provide the required remedy within a reasonable period specified by the customer, without being entitled to do so in accordance with clause 10.2., it may itself provide remedy and demand compensation for necessary expenses. The setting of time limits is unnecessary if ART refuses remedy or needs immediate remedy.

10.4 If ART is entitled to refuse the remedy under point 10.2, however, the lack of travel concerns a significant part of the travel services, ART must provide adequate replacement services. If this does not achieve an equivalent quality of the trip, ART shall grant an appropriate reduction of the travel price (reduction) according to clause 10.5.. If the replacement services are not comparable to those originally owed or if the reduction offered is not appropriate, the customer may reject the replacement service. In this case or if ART is unable to offer replacement services, the further legal consequences are also directed without notice (paragraph 10.6.) according to § 651 l para. 2 and 3 BGB.

10.5 For the duration of a non-contractual travel service, the customer may: in so far as the remedy has not been thwarted by a culpable omission of the notice of defect, claim a right to a reduction in the travel price (reduction). Restrictions on the legal consequences of a defect in cases of unavoidable exceptional circumstances arise from § § 651 k para. 4 u.5.

10.6 If the journey is significantly affected by a defect, the customer may terminate the travel contract. Prior to that, it must set a reasonable time limit for remedying the situation. The time limit is unnecessary if ART is refused or the immediate remedy is necessary.

If the contract has been terminated, ART shall be obliged to take the necessary measures as a result of the termination of the contract, in particular if the contract includes the carriage of goods, without delay, and the means of transport used for this purpose shall be equivalent to those agreed in the contract. The additional costs for the return transport are to be borne by ART. However, we retain the right to the (shared) travel price, claims for reduction, damages and compensation for futile expenses (for restrictions on claims for damages and reimbursement of expenses see clause 10.5 sentence two). With regard to travel services no longer to be provided, ART's entitlement to the agreed travel price shall be waived; in so far as payments already made shall be refunded.

2. Insofar as it is not based on intent or gross negligence, the liability of ART towards the travel participant for damages due to tort shall be limited to three times the travel price for damages that are not bodily damages; however, the liability amount shall be at least € 4.100,00.

Form to inform the traveler in the case of a package trip to § 651a of the Civil Code

The combination of travel services offered to you is a package tour within the meaning of the Directive (EU) 2015/2302.

Therefore, you can avail yourself of all EU rights that apply to package tours. The company Africa Royal Tours GmbH bears full responsibility for the proper execution of the entire package.

In addition, the company Africa Royal Tours GmbH has the statutory protection for the repayment of your payments and, if the transport is included in the package, to ensure your return transport in the event of its insolvency.

Main rights under the Directive (EU) 2015/2302

  • Travelers will receive all essential information about the package before the conclusion of the package travel contract.
  • At least one entrepreneur is always liable for the proper provision of all travel services included in the contract.
  • Passengers will receive an emergency telephone number or information about a contact point through which they can contact the tour operator or the travel agency.
  • Travelers may transfer the package to another person within a reasonable period of time and possibly at additional costs.
  • The price of the package may only be increased if certain costs (e.g. fuel prices) increase and if this is expressly provided for in the contract and in any case not later than 20 days before the start of the package. If the price increase exceeds 8% of the package travel price, the traveler may withdraw from the contract. If a tour operator reserves the right to raise prices, the traveler has the right to reduce prices if the corresponding costs are reduced.
  • Travelers may withdraw from the contract without paying a withdrawal fee and receive a full refund of all payments if one of the essential elements of the package, with the exception of the price, is significantly changed. If the contractor responsible for the package cancels the package before the start of the package, travelers are entitled to reimbursement and, in some cases, compensation.
  • Passengers may withdraw from the contract in exceptional circumstances prior to the start of the package without paying a withdrawal fee, for example if there are serious safety problems at the place of destination which are likely to affect the package.
  • In addition, passengers can withdraw from the contract at any time before the start of the package tour against payment of a reasonable and reasonable withdrawal fee
  • Where, after the start of the package, essential elements of the package cannot be carried out in accordance with the agreement, appropriate other arrangements shall be offered to the traveler without any additional costs. The traveler can withdraw from the contract without paying a withdrawal fee (in the Federal Republic of Germany this right is called "dismissal") if services are not provided in accordance with the contract and this has a significant impact on the provision of the contractual package travel services and the tour operator fails to remedy it.
  • The traveler shall be entitled to a reduction in the price and/or compensation if the travel services are not or are not provided properly.
  • The tour operator shall assist the traveler if he is in difficulty.
  • Payments shall be refunded in the event of the insolvency of the tour operator or, in some Member States, of the travel agent. If the insolvency of the tour operator or, if relevant, of the travel agent occurs after the start of the package and the transport is part of the package, the return carriage of the travelers is guaranteed: Africa Royal Tours GmbH has completed an insolvency insurance with R+V Allgemeine Versicherung AG. Travelers can use this facility (Raiffeisenplatz 1, 65189 Wiesbaden, Germany). ruv@~@ruv.de, tel.: 0611-5330) or, where applicable, contact the competent authority if they are denied services due to the insolvency of Africa Royal Tours GmbH.

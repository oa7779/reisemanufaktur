Guest reviews

Here you will find references from customers who have already traveled with us.

W. Bernold: Namibia trip

"Our conclusion: brilliant land, warm people, great lodges and a perfectly organized journey."

What a trip! Namibia is a magnificent country and our trip was perfectly organized. We actually didn't have to take care of anything and could just enjoy it.

Already on the Fish River we had the first unforgettable moments with a great tour and even a bath in the river. Wolwedans was sensational, Little Kulala with his starry sky and the Sundowner beautiful and the trip to the Sossusvlei simply fantastic.

An absolute highlight was our panoramic flight to Swakopmund: over the dunes, along the coast, past seals, wrecks and even flying flamingos. Our accommodation in Swakopmund, the Pearls Beach Pods were so beautiful that we would have preferred to make an offer.

The animal world also impressed us: desert elephants from close up, giraffes with boys, rhinos, lions, cheetahs and finally in Okonjima even a leopard with boys. Incredible experiences that we will not forget.

A very special praise goes to our guide P.J., which we have really closed to the heart. A walking lexicon, incredibly helpful and for us a mixture of Crocodile Dundee and Indiana Jones.

Our conclusion: ingenious country, warm people, great lodges and a perfectly organized trip.
Thank you again for the organization, Mrs. Raith!

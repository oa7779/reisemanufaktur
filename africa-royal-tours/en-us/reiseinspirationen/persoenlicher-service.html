Personal service

Personal service is our credo. We will present you with a personal contact person on the trip, during and after the trip. With Africa Royal Tours, your dream of an individual Africa round trip comes true.

Personal service

Do you also know this when you have a concern and first end up in a waiting loop without knowing if you will ever get out of the right contact person? With us you will be forwarded directly, according to your travel request, to the corresponding country specialist, who takes a lot of time for you and your wishes.

  • A contact person before, during and after the trip. With us you will receive directly a personal supervisor who will accompany you from beginning to end.
  • Short response times, short-term changes and quick help are guaranteed.
  • Everything is possible and can also be arranged at short notice.
  • As a rule, the successful, first joint travel planning is the beginning of a long-standing and trusting partnership for us.

Their satisfaction is the greatest reward and the most beautiful praise for us.

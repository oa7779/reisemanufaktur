Onguma Game Reserve – Experience Namibia's animals up close - 10/09/2018

The Onguma Game Reserve is located in the eastern part of the Etosha National Park and is one of the best kept secrets of Namibia. Guests have the opportunity to experience Africa in all its beauty and diversity, because the reserve is a nature reserve of more than 34,000 hectares with over 30 different species of animals.

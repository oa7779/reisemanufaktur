Ms Bayer: South Africa trip

"Our South Africa trip was just beautiful, perfectly planned – just perfect."

Good afternoon, Mrs. Listl,

today I take the time to give you a short travel report, otherwise there will be nothing at all.

Our South Africa trip was just beautiful, perfectly planned – just perfect. On every stage of the journey we were provided with all our guides, the individual tours were always adapted to our wishes and also the vehicles were all very well in the shot and very spacious. Especially our guide Ellen, who took us from Cape Town to Addo Park, was extremely careful, always caring for us and spoiled us in every way – also with her Mercedes Viano, which was an ideal vehicle for us. But also the small transfers, from and to the airport e.g. B. were always punctual and the drivers all very friendly and helpful.

The hotels were all very good, starting in Cape Town until the end, excellent was of course the "Woodall Country House", where we felt like little kings. The entire complex was impressive, but even better was our room – better to say, our small apartment. The service in the restaurant and the food were excellent, we even got our own menu every day – for Mrs. and Mr. Bayer. There we could also enjoy our bottle of wine, which had been ready for us on arrival in Cape Town. Thank you for this nice surprise, the wine was very good. From there we took our trips to Addo Elephant Park, its wildlife was already very impressive, especially the elephants and zebras.

In the "Blyde River Canyon Lodge" we were unfortunately only overnight, but there we would have liked to stay longer, it was also a very magical accommodation.

Our cottage in the Idube Lodge in the Sabi Sand Park was also very nice, very spacious and comfortable, especially it was fascinating to be surrounded all day by jumping goats, wart boars and monkeys.

The safaris in the Sabi Sand Park were unforgettable, we have seen almost all animals, rhinos, hippos, crocodiles, buffalo, elephants, hyenas, zebras – unfortunately no lions and giraffes. But leopards, in the morning in the tree at breakfast and in the evening a leopard came to us very close – almost within reach. The wildlife in the two parks you had chosen for us was very different and so the rides were very interesting.

We were also very well accommodated in the "A" Zambezi River Lodge, the people there were also very nice and accommodating, but as spoiled as in South Africa we were not there.

We were and are still particularly excited that everything was organized so well, the journeys, the entrance fees were already paid – even in the Viktoria cases, where there was a bigger rush, it worked smoothly with the entrance. Also the helicopter flight worked without problems, the Viktoria Falls from above were the last highlight on this journey rich in highlights.

In summary, I have to thank you very much for organizing this trip so excellently for us – everything worked smoothly and we enjoyed it every day. The impressions we have collected in South Africa have only to be processed gradually, at least they were unforgettable days. Above all, the friendliness of the people in South Africa remains in pleasant memory, they were always nice, accommodating and always laughed.

My husband and I thank you again for the beautifully assembled trip, we were allowed to take home unique experiences.

I wish you another week...

Yours sincerely

Margot Bayer

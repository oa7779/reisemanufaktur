Ms. Maier: Botswana Round Trip

"We liked this camp very much because the people were so warm, the food very good and the guide very friendly."

Dear Mrs Krencker,

At the beginning of February I planned my last big trip to Botswana. At the suggestion of my nephew, who so swarmed for Botswana, I researched the Internet for suitable offers. As a result, I also encountered Africa Royal Tours. I also said that I was 85 years old, but that wasn't a problem and I was able to handle everything well. I was offered an offer that I accepted by arrangement with my son and with him.

On May 2nd, 2017, we started our trip from Munich to Johannesburg. Arriving at Johannesburg Airport, they took care of me in a touching way, because they provided me with a wheelchair and drove me past the waiting crowd. Arrived in Maun we went directly by plane to our first camp. After we brought our luggage to the lodge, we were allowed to drive through the high reeds of the Okavango Delta with our guide in the installation. He told us to speak very quietly so as not to scare away animals. We liked this camp very much, because the people were so warm, the food very good and the guide very friendly.

In the morning and in the late afternoon we went on the hunt. After two days we continued with the bush hopper into the Khwai area. There we experienced two exciting days on safari in the national parks. We saw and experienced a lot, after which we flew on to Bakwena Lodge. Unfortunately, this accommodation was under the trees, yet it was very comfortable and there were no wishes. There, too, we went looking for wildlife. The guides used radios to keep up to date with where the animals were. After that, we went back to the lodge for a refreshment and then set off for a water safari. There were also many animals to see. So we stayed until the magnificent sunset. Unfortunately, the day was coming to an end very quickly.

The next day we went on to Zimbabwe, where we went to Victoria Falls. After that we visited the famous Viktoria Falls by taxi. Loud noise received us and the first sight was overwhelming. About an hour or so we stayed on the grounds. It was really an overwhelming experience. Last night we went on a boat trip. It was a beautiful conclusion, because there we experienced another fabulous sunset.

The next day we started our flight home and the beautiful journey was over.

Love greetings
Christa Maier

Mr. Geisselhardt: Botswana, Zambia & Zimbabwe Travel

"In the shortest possible time, we were put together an individual program tailored to our needs and presented in such a way that we could hardly wait for the start of the journey."

At the end of 2016, we started planning a trip to southern Africa. The target was the Victoria Falls in Zimbabwe/Zambia, an extensive safari in remote camps in Botswana and a multi-day stay in the Cape Town region. Already at the first contact with Mrs Davina Listl we felt at Africa Royal Tours very well. Within a very short time, an individual program tailored to our needs was put together and presented in such a way that we could hardly wait for the start of the trip.

The high expectations so aroused were not only met in spring 2017, but exceeded. The excellent selection of lodges/hotels, the skilfully chosen itinerary and the competent and sympathetic guides and hosts at all stations were able to inspire us from day to day. The wildlife observations by off-road vehicle or boat in Chobe National Park and Okawanga Delta brought us many unforgettable and immediate experiences – not only thanks to the "Big Five", which has been seen several times and quoted a lot.

The unexpected little attentions that have been given to us have also contributed to our enthusiasm. We have greatly appreciated the outstanding all-round care provided by Mrs Davina Listl and also by Mrs Silke Wahl in Cape Town by Africa Royal Tours as well as all partners on site and will gladly count on their services again on the next major trip.

Couple with 2 sons, 15 and 18 years

Friendly greetings from Switzerland
Daniel Geisselhardt

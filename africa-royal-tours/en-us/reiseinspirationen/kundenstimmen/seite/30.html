Poremba family: South Africa and Mauritius trip

"The vacation was one of the most beautiful we have made in recent years. Our expectations have been exceeded."

We had booked a week Cape Town with additional stay in the Sanbona Wildlife Reserve with subsequent bathing vacation in Mauritius. Here we describe our impressions:

Satisfaction related to Care before/during/after our trip:

Better care before, during and after the trip is hard to imagine. We always had a contact person at our disposal. Before the trip we were informed and advised very comprehensively, during the trip encountered problems (autopanne, loss of a travel utensil etc.) could be solved very quickly. The hotels in Cape Town (Blackheath Lodge) and in the Sanbona Wildlife Reserve (Godwana Family Lodge) were excellent. The rooms were large, bright and equipped with very nice bathrooms. The staff was always very nice and helpful. Dinner at the Godwana Family Lodge was varied and delicious.

In Mauritius we had booked the Outrigger Mauritius Resort in the south. The south of Mauritius offers fantastic beaches, but unfortunately also a lot of rain. The hotel is by no means recommended. Especially when you're traveling with a family. The so-called children's rooms are no more than a tiny room next to the master bedroom, in which a high bed is placed and our third child should sleep in a drawer. In addition, the room and bathroom were full of mold. Thanks to our great support through Africa Royal Tours we were able to change the hotel after a gruesome night. The Maritim Resort & Spa Mauritius in the north of the island was a dream and fully met our desire for recreation.

Quality of planned activities:

The highlight of our activities was certainly the two-day tours in the Sanbona Wildlife Reserve. Our guide, who accompanied us throughout the stay, was very competent, spoke good English and was very committed.

Very nice were also the trips we made around Cape Town. Here we have followed the recommendations of Africa Royal Tours and have had very good experiences. This was true for the tours as well as for the restaurants and wineries.

Quality of the itinerary, suggestions for improvement:

The quality of the itinerary was great. The only change that we might make next time is a slightly longer stay at the Sanbona Wildlife Reserve. To do this, you could easily shorten your bathing vacation.

What has moved us to book at Africa Royal Tours? First your appearance on the Internet, then the extremely committed work of your employees.

Were our expectations met or exceeded? The vacation was one of the most beautiful we have made in recent years. Our expectations have been exceeded.

What could Africa Royal Tours have done even better? Nothing !!!!!!!!!!!!

Yours sincerely,
Poremba family

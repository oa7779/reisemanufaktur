Bill and Helga Slivka: Zimbabwe and Botswana trip 的 17.10.2024

"I can't do better. "

Mrs. Raith, you have organized the trip PERFEKT. I can't do better. That was our fourth trip with your company, and every trip was incredibly good. You can't be praised enough.

All flights were perfect, mostly we were alone with the pilot! Also all lodges could not be better. Of course, we were enthusiastic about the Hotel "Viktoria Falls". Then came the Tlouwana Camp, very cozy, small and amiable people. After that, the Stetari Camp was my favorite camp. The house, the food, the management, everything great. You must recommend this to everyone. Then the Mima Tesbe Camp, also very good, great view. Then Deception Valley Lodge. Great house, management should be a little better. Since there were only 4 people there, they didn't try so hard. We almost missed the plane because no one cared. Thanks to the excellent guide who had broken off his safari, we were able to reach the plane. Last Camp Kalahari, also very good in everything.

We thank you for your effort and skill to put together a not quite simple journey so well. I hope this wasn't the last trip with your company.

Yours sincerely,
Bill and Helga Slivka

Sandra Scherb: South Africa trip

"The memories will remain for a long time."

Dear Mrs. Sweet,

we landed well again in Germany on Saturday, and I wanted to thank you again! It was a great trip that you put together here for us! The lodges in the parks were perfect (and exceeded our expectations by far) and the Garden Route was also super beautiful! Another big compliment to the travel documents - the tour descriptions were great, and the clues where to drive and which areas to avoid! That was a huge help and I've never seen that before - THANK YOU!

Thanks also for the invitation to the Champagne Sunset Cruise - we gladly accepted this and thus end the second evening in Cape Town!

Now the seriousness of life starts again with us all today - but the memories will remain for a long time (as well as the work to reduce the thousands of images of giraffes, elephants and zebras again.

Best regards,
Sandra Scherb

Customer: South Africa, Namibia & Botswana Tour

"We all now have the "Africa fever" and are already planning several more trips on this wonderful continent. For this we will certainly use the services of Africa Royal Tours again, because with such excellent experience we will certainly book with you again."

Dear Mrs. Sweet,

First of all, it is worth noting that everything organized worked out perfectly. This is a great achievement that has made traveling very easier and enjoyable.

Mrs Plasan had made two reservations for us in restaurants: one in Cape Town and one in Franschhoek. Both restaurants were wonderful! We enjoyed the show in Cape Town and the exclusive ambience in Franschhoek and thank you very much for these reservations. Great!

The township tour in Cape Town and the wine tour in Franschhoek both worked excellently and were wonderful! The personal commitment of the guide in the Cape Town-Township was impressive and inspiring! The guide in Franschhoek was funny, entertaining and very knowledgeable about places, which came very close to us.

The flights in small aircraft in Namibia were an extraordinary experience! The pilots were very competent and friendly. They met and enjoyed the company. That was all very good! The fact that the flights are very shaky can sometimes also make you a little afraid. We felt this to be unpleasant, but we endured it. We weren't really aware that it would be so shaky before. I think it would be good for inexperienced travelers in the future if this fact were raised a little more clearly when planning their journeys. Some would probably prefer to choose smaller distances and prefer to drive by car, I think. In any case, this is what we plan to do for our next trip to Namibia: rent a car in Windhoek and travel only to the north and west of Namibia; every other day maybe 3 to 4 hours by car to the next lodge (i.e. approx.180-240 km) and spend two nights in a lodge in between and join the hunting trips. But we'll be happy to discuss this with Mrs Plasan when the time comes.

The Fish River Canyon Lodge was very good, but not as high from the level as three of the following lodges. In particular, there was no personal welcome upon arrival. They were standing around a bit strange until someone noticed us. Then everything was fine.

A highlight of the trip was definitely the lodge Klein Kulala! So beautifully located, so wonderful staff, so dedicated management! In addition, as with every lodge on our trip: excellent hunting trips with educated, funny, very shaming guides, who told us a lot about everything and showed us many animals. In Klein Kulala we celebrated four together with the staff until midnight disco party in the restaurant area of the lodge. We all loved it!

Overall, the trip worked very well, especially thanks to the excellent organization of Mrs Plasan. For this again a heartfelt thank you!

In the meantime, we all have the "Africa fever" and are already planning several more trips on this wonderful continent. For this we will certainly use the services of Africa Royal Tours again, because with such excellent experience we will certainly book with you again.

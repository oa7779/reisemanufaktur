Wilfried and Cornelia Otto: South Africa trip

"You have planned a very well organized visit for us from the beginning and it was all just great all around!"

Dear Mrs. Raith,

We have settled in well again and speak almost daily of our great vacation in South Africa - with the many wonderful experiences!

They have planned a very well organized visit for us from the beginning and it was all just super class all around!
After our flight we were received by the very nice employee (who could help us in cash things) and the driver.

The night in Johannesburg in the very modern and pretty hotel with very good dinner was a great start! The lodge in the safari park Pilanisberg was very nice and immediately went on safari, simply unique! We were lucky enough to have our own driver with Jeep with the family (6 people) - Johainis, our guide, was so nice and has so much knowledge of nature and animals in Pilanisberg Park - fabulous. We were amazed several times and had a lot of fun and learned a lot. An experience with the Big Five! Then the beautiful city of Cape Town - we haven't been there the last time!

Super great hotel, also located here wonderfully. Our visit to some beautiful wine regions with wine tastings was perfect and rounded off our visit. Above all, our sympathetic driver/co-operator Christiane was very great; we exchanged ideas and got a lot of shows, for a whole day - fantastic. Also very well planned by you! On the next visit more of the wine towns.... The return flight worked very well and all in all - an unforgettable journey!

Thanks again for the very good planning and we greet warmly, until next time.

Wilfried and Cornelia Otto

Paul and Katharina Teiche: Namibia trip with excursion to the Victoria Falls

"Our trip through Namibia and the trip to Victoria Falls is certainly one of the greatest trips we've ever made."

Our trip through Namibia and the trip to Victoria Falls is certainly one of the greatest trips we have ever made. From the planning to the organization and the care on site everything was perfectly organized by Mrs. Raith. Thank you very much.

All lodges were perfect. Every lodge had something special. Whether it was the trip from the Namib Outpost Lodge to the dunes with the ascent of Big Daddy or the sensational balloon ride at sunrise in Sossusvlei. This balloon ride is a must! About Swakopmund with his great aquatic life we went to Damaraland to the Onduli Ridge Lodge. I can't do better! We were accommodated in the villa with buttler service and private cook. Best of all, when you go to sleep in the evening after a unique dinner under the South Cross.

The safari rides were a great experience every time. The ride through the Etosha Park with its animals to the
Onguma The Fort. Also this lodge with African-Morrocan flair exceptional. We used the days here to relax by the pool. You don't believe it if you haven't seen it yourself, you're sitting by the pool and across the street there are 12 lions at the waterfront and elephants, giraffes marching between the lodges. It gives you the feeling of being beyond Africa and you expect Robert Redford to walk in as Finch Hatton.

After a short stop at the wonderful Hotel Heinitzburg, great food, great wine cellar and great hospitality we went to the Matetsi River Lodge to see the Viktoria Falls

Overall a dream trip that you should have made absolutely.
Thanks again to Mrs. Raith and the Royal Africa Tours team
Paul and Katharina Teiche

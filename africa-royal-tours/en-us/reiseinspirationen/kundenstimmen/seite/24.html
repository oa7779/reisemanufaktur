Melanie and Georg: Tanzania's trip to Zanzibar with a swim-response 29.10.2018

"If you can enjoy the sunrise and the endless expanse in the Highlands in the morning with a cup of coffee, that's something special."

Our first safari went to Tanzania and was perfect all around. The organization of Mrs. Listl was great and even when we were on site, she reorganized something for us at short notice and we didn't have to worry about anything.
Our guide Mashaka was just great. In almost perfect German he brought us his country with much joy and incredibly much knowledge.
The accommodations and the food were above average and each lodge/camp had its own charm and was special in its own way.
The safari itself is hard to put into words. She was unique and also overwhelming and gave me some wet eyes with joy. If you can enjoy the sunrise and the endless expanse in the Highlands in the morning with a cup of coffee, that is something special.
Thank you very much, Mrs Listl, for this experience. We will remember it for a long time to come.

Client: Zimbabwe Travel 的 27.08.2018

"Overall, an unforgettable experience again."

We traveled with ART for the second time. After the first trip had already been optimally planned and wonderful, we decided on our Zimbabwe trip again for ART. The planning process was uncomplicated, Ms. Plasan responded to all our wishes and so we traveled towards Harare in early July. We were kindly received, led to the Domestic Terminal – which we had for ourselves – and flew on to the Mana Pools National Park without any delay. On our 90 minute flight we got the first impression of this beautiful country. At low altitude in our propeller engine, mountains, savannah and finally the majestic Zambesi offered us. Our camp was right on the river and the safaris on land and water were a treat thanks to the landscape and our guides. No less beautiful, although the landscape was completely different, our second camp in the Huwange National Park; and elephants until you could no longer count. Finally, Vic Falls. Stunning lodge and an insane spectacle of nature.
Overall an unforgettable experience again. Thanks to ART and Mrs Plasan.
C.M.

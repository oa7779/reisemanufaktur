Mr. Fandrich: Namibia Round Trip 的 15.11.2017

"Namibia was really a great experience for us - we've really been around a lot."

Hello, Mrs. Listl,

as promised here a few words about our trip:

In general, everything was very well organized and Corinna from your contact office was there half an hour after arrival at the hotel and explained everything to us and handed over the vouchers. Every single day was described with detailed directions on individual pages. Only with the route description to the Okonjima Plains Camp we discovered a mistake. The distance indicated was about 30km further than in reality. But since the driveway was right on the road, we didn't let it confuse us.

On the first afternoon we had to get into the city and shop a bit, e.g. Hats against the sun. Very unusual for me, the last time I had a cap on as a baby ... but was a reasonable measure, because apart from Swakopmund and Walvisbay we rarely had less than 30-35 degrees.

Absolute highlights were the balloon flight over the Namib, the excursion with the catamaran (even sighted a whale), the candlelight dinner on a small mountain near the Hoodia Lodge and of course the Etosha National Park. Getting so many animals so close to the camera was just great. We haven't seen rhinos, but otherwise all the other types of four-legged and two-legged.

I was also impressed by the lack of car rental. I came back with two healing spare tires and was very proud. They only looked at the car when they returned it from a distance ... I know that differently.

Namibia was really a great experience for us - we've come around a lot already ....It was expensive, but worth every cent!

So thanks again!
Yours sincerely
Mr Fandrich

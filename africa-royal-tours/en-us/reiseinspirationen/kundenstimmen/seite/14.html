D.R.: Zimbabwe and Botswana trip

"...that it was the best and most exciting trip we have ever made."

Dear Mrs. Moos,

I wanted to thank you again in writing for the good organization of the trip.
I and my husband agree that it was the best and most exciting trip we've ever made.

We've seen so many animals and so close... that was incredible. A month later, everything resembles a beautiful dream, you know? We were so lucky to see all these animals. At Machaba Camp it was most exciting, since I even met a big elephant cow on the way to the tent and a bull who crossed the river studied me for a long time until he went on. We were really close. And the roaring of the lions on the first night was incredible, it took us a long time to get to sleep.

It was all wonderful, of course, staying in a tent without fences or protection was the first stop and the first time for us, so it is probably more influential. The Elephant Coffee was then at the end of the hammer to interact with wild elephants so as to touch them as your skin is so, and how attentively they watched you was truly incredible. But the other two camps were also incredible. And then the Falls, it was a childhood dream of me to see them. I had seen these on ZDF in a documentary series and there I was 9 or so and that I was allowed to see them from above was really emotional. The nature, the sun, everything in Africa is a wonderful photo motif, the colors...just incredible.

Which also impressed us very much, was in the first two camps, the food!! It was right, no exceptionally good. As I said on the phone, here in South Tyrol we enjoy really wonderful Tyrolean but also Mediterranean cuisine. This camp was just as wonderful, everything gluten-free and it all tasted so delicious.
We were accompanied from Maun to back at the airport in Viktoria Falls, just lovely and very professional and we really didn't miss anything......as said.....a dream
We can't wait to book the next trip with you! Thanks again for the great organization and for this great experience.

Best regards from the sunny and finally really hot South Tyrol and hopefully until very soon

Simone & Kai - 28.03.2024

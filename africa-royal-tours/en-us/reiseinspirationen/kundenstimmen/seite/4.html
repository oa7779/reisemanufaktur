Hellbarth-Busch: Egypt trip

"A successful journey all around. Thank you very much for the very good preparation."

The flight was quiet and quite punctual, the plane had to be emptied and then it started.
Our pick-up service worked very well and perfectly. Here you can express a great praise to the appropriate agency. We were elegantly driven past all the snakes, the entry formalities were perfectly prepared and the entry went easily. Our luggage came quickly and the vehicle was ready to take us to the Four Seasons. Adventure traffic conditions in Cairo. Everyone was glad not to have to drive here themselves.
That's a great recommendation! Thank you so much for the nice Wellcare basket in all three rooms. The kids tried everything, of course.

We used the Italian and Arab restaurants. Everything very tasty and the friendliness of the service staff, also towards the children was phenomenal. We enjoyed the New Year's Eve menu in the Arab restaurant, with surprise bag for all children and women. The kids could use anything! Even the children made it this time until midnight (for us actually only 11 pm) and everyone enjoyed the great view of the fireworks and the ship parade on the Nile.

The visit of the "Old Egyptian Museum" was very instructive and interesting, the children looked at the mummies very carefully. The tour of the bazaar was great and was honestly surprised by the restraint of the dealers. The guide spoke German very well and had an incredible knowledge. During his visit to the mosque, he gave us much knowledge of the Koran and the various faiths.

The trip to the pyramids and the sphinx was beautiful. No intrusive merchants as we expected. The city of Cairo has done a lot to ensure that tourists were able to see everything almost lightly. The pyramid interior in the Cheops pyramid was a highlight for everyone, especially the children were really impressed (I saved myself this trip into the interior, because I don't like going into grave chambers so much. Thanks to the many photos from the inside that our family took, I had a good impression.)

In the afternoon we went to the New Egyptian Museum, so, so impressive the construction and the exhibits. The museum is really worth it and it is worth it again.

Well, and then it went off to the White Desert. We were picked up by two SUVs and then started on the new upgraded highway. We stopped in the Crystal Desert and Black Desert and were still in a mummie museum, to the delight of our grandchildren. Well, and then about 20 km before the desert camp we went across the desert on sand tracks and the SUVs and their drivers had to show what they could. (We would have liked to have read this in your travel description before, then we could have better adapted to it. But since this destination is probably relatively new, there is probably not so much information about this desert safari.)

Finally arrived we moved in our tents, already a special experience: far and wide only desert. I would describe your description of the camp as comfortable at a next booking (luxurious it is not really ;-)). For the night we all dressed in functional clothes and partly with hat. We really couldn't imagine how sharp the temperature difference will be. After a cold night we drove the next morning to the highlights of this desert, real natural wonders! Highly recommended. Well, and then we went to the desert at sunset in the late afternoon, especially impressive in this clear desert air with simultaneous full moon ascent. No more romantic than that.

The return trip to Cairo airport worked well, so that we arrived in Frankfurt with a delay, due to the winter conditions in Frankfurt.

The drivers of the SUVs were good and experienced drivers and knew each other well in the desert. And our travel companion had again known so much to tell us that we could be amazed. All around a successful journey. Thank you very much for the very good preparation, Mrs Plasan. We like your style, just perfect.

Winter greetings to Munich

Mr. Michael Piazolo: Uganda trip

"Uganda at its best perfectly presented by Africa Royal Tours. I'll just say, "Thank you!"

Dear Mrs. Herian,

now it took a long time, my Uganda trip is a little bit ago, but nice memories stay a little longer. So I'll just send you a few lines below.

"Uganda - the pearl of Africa!" That's what it said in the travel guide. And like a pearl Uganda presented itself to me. I arrived in the night and the morning afterward the view from Entebbe to Lake Victoria unfolded in front of me with all its shimmering charms, hour after hour in different light and diverse perspectives.And changeable, unbelievably varied, the further journey first turned north and then west.

Accompanied by a tour operator's best-chosen guide, in personal union driver, organizer, country explainer as well as animal and human understanders, I had the impression in less than a week of getting to know a good piece of this wonderful country in the heart of Africa in an easy way already quite properly. Pleasantly orchestrated was the stay by the successful, individually coordinated route planning as well as carefully selected lodges, which were distinguished by their unobtrusive originality, the friendly people and the extremely tasty dishes.

Exciting safaris on foot, by boat and in the car brought the diversity of the animal world to full fruition with the special experiences for Uganda is known: excited chimpanzees in the green brown undergrowth of a gorge, rich lions high in the trees of the savannah and relaxed gorillas in the foggy jungle.

Uganda at its best perfectly presented by Africa Royal Tours.
I'm just saying, thank you!

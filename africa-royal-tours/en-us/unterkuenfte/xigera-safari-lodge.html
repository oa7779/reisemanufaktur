Xigera Safari Lodge

For an unforgettable stay at the Okavango Delta Botswana, Africa Royal Tours recommends the Xigeria Safari Lodge. Enquire now without obligation!

Xigera Safari Lodge

Moremi National Park, Botswana
Luxury lodge

Information

The Xigera Safari Lodge is located in the water-rich part of the Okavango Delta in Moremi National Park and is one of the most exclusive lodges in Botswana. The newly designed design lodge combines extraordinary safari experiences with comfort, many furniture and objects come from artists. With just twelve buildings – eleven suites for two guests and a family suite – the atmosphere remains personal and private. Each suite has a bathroom with outdoor shower and a terrace with river views. Safaris on land and water, mokoro rides, fly fishing or a night in sleepout under the stars open up a variety of experiences. Excursions in neighbouring concessions with off-road and walking safaris expand the perspectives on nature and wildlife. The stay depends on the rhythm of the guests: meals and safaris are planned individually, on request with private vehicle; helicopter flights offer spectacular views of the Delta. Xigera is a quiet, stylish retreat in the middle of nature – with privacy and a special combination of design and wilderness.

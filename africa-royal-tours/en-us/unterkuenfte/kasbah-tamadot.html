Kasbah Tamadot

Atlas Mountains, Morocco
Luxury hotel

Information

The luxurious Kasbah Tamadot is located in Asni, in the upper part of a valley of the Atlas Mountains overlooking Mount Toubkal, the highest mountain in Morocco, as well as some of the region's traditional Berber villages. Special attention should be paid to the unique design of all rooms and Berber tents with high-quality materials and antiques. On request you have a private pool. The Kanoun Restaurant serves fantastic local and international dishes. In addition to numerous wellness treatments, you can also enjoy the tennis court or a cooking class.

Familiarization trip · December 16, 2025 · South Africa

Franziska’s journey from Cape Town along the Garden Route.

Our Travel Atelier Advisor shares a personal trip with a friend: visiting accommodations firsthand, meeting local partners, and experiencing South Africa’s contrasts along the way.

This report summarizes Franziska’s publicly documented familiarization trip in December 2025. These are personal impressions; routes, hotels, and availability must be checked afresh for every inquiry.

Cape Town: city, coast, and your own pace

The journey began in Cape Town. Franziska describes the blend of Table Mountain, ocean views, and city life, with walks through Company’s Garden, Greenmarket Square, and the Malay Quarter. A chauffeured excursion to the Cape of Good Hope offered a relaxed way to enjoy the coastal scenery along Chapman’s Peak Drive.

Sanbona: safari in the Little Karoo

After three days in Cape Town, the road trip continued into the Little Karoo and Sanbona Wildlife Reserve. Semi-desert scenery and rock formations offered a contrast to the coast. Travel Atelier Advisor Franziska visited three different lodges: Gondwana Family Lodge, Tilney Manor, and Dwyka Lodge, each with a distinct atmosphere and focus.

Knysna, Plettenberg Bay, and Grootbos

The route continued through Knysna and Plettenberg Bay, with lagoon views, coastal walks, and time by the sea. At Grootbos Nature Reserve, Franziska experienced a botanical safari with local guides, adding a new perspective on nature to traditional wildlife viewing.

Franschhoek: a winelands finale

The journey ended in Franschhoek with wineries, tastings, and the wine tram. The personal takeaway: South Africa offers many ways to combine city life, safaris, coast, nature, and good food and wine. Driving times and the length of each stay determine how relaxed that combination feels.

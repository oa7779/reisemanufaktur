Travel Atelier insight · Africa

A good safari needs the right rhythm.

African journeys often combine vast landscapes and different modes of transport. Thoughtful planning considers not only highlights, but also drive times, nights in camp, and time to pause.

01

Time in camp is part of the journey

The daily rhythm, location of a lodge, and number of nights shape a safari. Fewer changes can leave more time for game drives and rest.

02

Read distances realistically

Mileage alone does not determine drive time. Road conditions, border crossings, and local schedules can all influence the order of a route.

03

Choose fly-in travel carefully

Small aircraft can shorten long overland transfers. Baggage limits and ground connections vary by operator and should be checked for the specific itinerary.

Good to know

Questions about private travel in Africa

From finding inspiration to choosing the right safari pace.

How many nights should I plan for a safari?

It depends on the reserve, arrival route, and preferred pace. Staying several nights in one place can reduce transfers and leave time for activities at different times of day. A specific recommendation depends on route and availability.

When is the best time for wildlife viewing?

Wildlife sightings in nature cannot be guaranteed. Season, rainfall, water sources, and animal movements vary by region. Check current information for the specific area when choosing dates.

What should I know about a fly-in safari?

Before booking, check small-aircraft baggage limits, permitted bag types, transfers, and possible weather dependence. Rules differ by route and operator and must be confirmed for the trip.

Can I combine safari and a beach stay?

A combination may work when flights, season, and available days align. Additional stops require time buffers; specific connections and services should be checked before a proposal is prepared.

How flexible are the published African itineraries?

The catalog presents published trip ideas and itineraries. These are starting points, not fixed group programs. We work with you to tailor the route, trip length, private guiding, accommodations, and other services.

How do you assess your recommendations and partners?

Our Travel Atelier Advisors visit their destinations, continue their training, and assess local partners. This helps us address specific questions about lodges, distances, and how the journey will unfold.

What is a realistic budget for a long-haul trip?

Our tailor-made long-haul travel planning generally starts at a total budget of EUR 5,000 per person, including international flights. Prices and services depend on your travel dates, route, accommodations, and preferred comfort level. For lower budgets, our form directs you to suitable European trip ideas — without transferring your personal details.

Can I combine a safari with relaxation or a beach stay?

Yes. Alongside safari and touring ideas, the catalog includes combinations with relaxation and island stays. The right combination depends on your trip length, season, preferred pace, and availability.

South Africa, Zimbabwe and Botswana Journey: Dream Paths through Southern Africa

South Africa, Zimbabwe and Botswana Travel: This travel idea of Africa Royal Tours takes you on the dream trails in southern Africa. Now request.

Zimbabwe, Botswana and South Africa await you with their highlights during this extraordinary tour. Discover not only natural beauties such as the breathtaking Victoria Falls and the Okavango Delta, but also the vibrant metropolis of Cape Town – a city that will delight you. On one of the numerous safaris you have the opportunity to see the Big 5 at close range. There are unforgettable moments and certainly unique encounters waiting for you, which will remind you of this dream journey for a long time.

1st day Arrival in Johannesburg

Welcome to Johannesburg! After your arrival you will be welcomed by your representative of Africa Royal Tours and you will discuss the further journey. Afterwards, the plane will take you directly to Zimbabwe to the world-famous Victoria Falls, which are located on the Sambesi River on the border between Zambia and Zimbabwe. Upon arrival at the airport your transfer will take place to the Matetsi River Lodge where you will stay the next two nights. Overnight at Matetsi River Lodge.

2nd Day Majestic Victoria Falls

Today is at your free disposal to explore the area around the Victoria Falls National Park on your own exploration tours. An absolute must is the visit to the world famous Victoria Falls. The vast masses of water, the infinite depths and the calm streams of water that lead to small lagoons will certainly impress you. In the evening, a romantic sunset cruise awaits you on the Sambesi. Overnight at Matetsi River Lodge.

3.-4. Day River Cruise Zambezi Queen

In the morning you will drive to Kasane, where the crew of the Zambezi Queen will welcome you for your next adventure. Here you will spend the next two nights. The river cruise begins on the river Chobe, in the small village of Kasane. The Chobe flows into the four-country corner of Botswana, Namibia, Zambia and Zimbabwe and then flows into the Sambesi. Your journey will take you upriver while offering you the unique opportunity to watch animals from the water and land in Chobe National Park. The National Park hosts some of the largest animal populations in southern Africa. In addition to countless elephants, many leopards and lions, with a little luck you will discover huge buffalo herds and giraffes – not to mention the crocodiles and hippos lurking in the water. Let the first evening end comfortably on the sunset ride. Another highlight is the opportunity to take part on a half-day trip to a traditional village, on a half day game-watching trip on land in Chobe National Park or on a bird-watching tour from smaller boats. Overnight on board the Zambezi Queen.

5.-7. Tag Unique Okavango Delta

After a short morning cruise on the Zambezi Queen you return to Kasane, from where you fly with a small charter machine to the Okavango Delta. Then transfer to your booked accommodation where you will spend the next three nights. The Okavango Delta is the world's largest domestic river delta and is considered one of Africa's most impressive natural sites. The Delta is the habitat of many mammals such as the Delta. Buffalo, various antelope species and predators as well as many birds. Your lodge's private property offers you excellent opportunities to observe animals. The lodge has 4x4 safari vehicles, "Mokoros" – the traditional canu-like built-in boats and a motor boat. Safaris are of course also possible on foot. You can choose from two activities on land or on water under the guidance of professional guides per day, enabling you to explore the unique flora and fauna perfectly. Overnight at &Beyond Nxabega Okavango Tented Camp.

8th day of Cape Town

Let yourself be awakened one last time by the sounds of the wilderness, because today it is said goodbye to Botswana - in great anticipation of South Africa. With the charter machine it goes back to Maun and from there via Johannesburg to Cape Town. A representative of Africa Royal Tours awaits you there, which will take you to the Silo Hotel. The small and luxurious boutique hotel, located in the exclusive Bantry Bay district, offers you a perfect retreat in Cape Town. The only seven suites have all the amenities you can wish for, the outdoor area with its own pool and views over the Atlantic will delight you. Enjoy one of the magnificent sunsets on the hotel terrace with a Sundowner and Canapés. Overnight at the Silo Hotel.

9.-12. Day Explore Cape Town and the surrounding area

Over the next four days, your German-speaking tour guide will be at your free disposal together with a vehicle to explore Cape Town in an individual way. Located on the southern tip of Africa, Cape Town is often compared with the metropolises of Rio de Janeiro or San Francisco and can undoubtedly compete with them. Discover the many facets of the port city during your explorations and be enchanted by Cape Town, which reflects the entire rainbow nation. The Cape of Good Hope, Table Mountain, the V&A Waterfront, the beaches of Camps Bay & Clifton, downtown with Long Street, the Botanical Garden, the Lions Head and Signal Hill mountains and much, much more await you. If after the hustle and bustle of the city after some rest and relaxation, it is possible to visit the well-known, nearby wine-growing areas. In addition to excellent wines, you can also get to know delicacies from South African cuisine, old and modern architecture as well as a varied landscape. Your personal guide will be happy to help you plan the activities. Overnight at the Silo Hotel.

13th day Kruger National Park

After breakfast you will be taken to the airport and travel via Johannesburg to Skukuza Airport, in the middle of the Kruger National Park. Here you will be warmly welcomed and taken to the private Londolozi Wild Reserve. The reserve extends over 14,000 hectares of privately run area and is located in the heart of the Sabi Sand Game Reserve. Londolozi is known especially for his many leopards. The sand river, which runs through the reserve, attracts the most diverse wildlife and predators to drink, bathe and grass. Your stay guarantees you a private and exclusive experience, as the park rangers are not bound by the strict national park regulations; and so you can follow the animals away from the paths. Its small and exclusive lodge offers a maximum of comfort and elegance in the midst of African wilderness. Overnight at Londolozi Private Granite Suites.

14-15. Tag Safari time

Enjoy the following two days the unique beauty of this area, such as on one of the safari tours organised by the lodge, which are offered in the morning and evening. You also have the opportunity for bush hikes. If you prefer a little quieter, relax with a yoga hour or let yourself be pampered with a soothing massage. Overnight at Londolozi Private Granite Suites.

16th day return flight or bath extension

After 15 unforgettable days in southern Africa it's time to say goodbye today. After breakfast, your driver will take you back to Skukuza Airport from where you will take your individual return flight to Germany. We are happy to organize a bathing extension in Mozambique or on the island of Mauritius.

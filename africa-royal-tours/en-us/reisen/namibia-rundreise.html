A Fly-In Safari across Namibia

Namibia flight safari: travel idea for a unique flight safari through Namibia with Africa Royal Tours. Treat yourself to something special. Now request.

16 days

Your trip inspiration

A sample itinerary — tailored to you

This flight safari combines the highlights of Namibia from the impressive Fish River Canyon to spectacular deserts, fascinating landscapes and unique colors to the animal-rich Etosha National Park. Pick up and discover Namibia with different eyes! Enjoy on board the small plane chartered for you the feeling of infinite space and unlimited freedom. Go on a unique journey that will give you unforgettable moments.

Trip information

Itinerary

16 days Windhoek – Fish River Canyon – Lüderitz – Namib Desert – Swakopmund – Ongava Wild Reserve – Skeleton Coast – Etosha National Park – Otjiwa Collection – Windhoek

Your personal highlights

  • Experience Namibia from a bird's eye view
  • Stunning natural landscapes
  • Enjoy the endless expanse and solitude of the desert
  • Fly in private small aircraft to the highlights of Namibia
  • Let yourself be inspired by the feeling of boundless freedom
  • Wildlife observations in the famous Etosha National Park
  • Sufficient time to get to know the visited places in detail

After your arrival at the international airport in Windhoek you will be warmly welcomed by our local colleagues and taken to your booked hotel. Relax at the hotel for the rest of the day or take the time to explore Namibia's capital (if you wish, we will be happy to organize a guide). You will stay at the Hotel Heinitzburg. The Schlosshotel combines the European charm of the 19th century with modern comfort and has a total of 16 comfortably equipped rooms. The idyllic terrace with a unique view of the city invites you to a romantic sunset. Overnight at the Hotel Heinitzburg.

Today you will be picked up by your hotel in Windhoek and taken to Eros National Airport, from where your flight safari starts. During the two-and-a-half-hour flight you will see the Hardap reservoir, the Kalahari desert, the extinct volcano Brukkaros and the fascinating expanses of the south from the air. Its destination is the Fish River Canyon, which is located at the bottom of the fish river and was built in millions of years. It is the second largest canyon in the world with a length of about 160 kilometers, a width of up to 27 kilometers and a depth of some over 500 meters after the Grand Canyon. The Fish River Lodge, your accommodation for the next two nights, is located between huge granite rocks right in the private 45,000-hectare Canyon Nature Park. It is the only lodge located directly on the canyon, the distance to the edge is only eight meters. From the terrace with pool you can enjoy breathtaking views of the canyon as well as magical sunrises and sunsets. The 20 spacious chalets feature skylights from which you can observe the unique starry sky. Dinner and overnight at Fish River Lodge.

Today you will start with a tour of the Fish River Canyon, to which you will be taken with a vehicle of the lodge together with other guests. Let yourself be enchanted by the impressive natural beauty, while you stand anxiously at the edge of the gorge and look into the depth of the canyon. You can truly still feel the incredible powers of water that worked a long time ago. Let the day end with a leisurely sunset ride through the breathtaking nature in the surroundings. Dinner and overnight stay like the day before at Fish River Lodge.

A short flight will take you to Lüderitz on the Atlantic coast. Arriving there, you will embark on a short orientation tour through the old German port town with its many colonial buildings. For dinner we recommend one of the excellent fish restaurants with fresh Atlantic fish, oysters and lobsters (optional). You will stay at the comfortable Lüderitz Nest Hotel, which is located directly on Lüderitz Bay. The high-quality rooms all have direct sea views. The hotel is the ideal starting point to explore the many attractions in and around Lüderitz Bay. For example, visit the museum, the Goerke House, the famous rock church, the new Waterfront or the Agate Beach, where you will find, with a little luck, crystallised plaster sand roses. Overnight at the Lüderitz Nest Hotel.

Today, the famous ghost town of Kolmanskop awaits you. Move back into the last century to explore this formerly thriving diamond city from the 19th century. The abandoned houses with their broken windows and crumbling walls, which gradually sink into the sand, give the city its own charm. Visit the old two-story casino with the still functioning bowling alley or the small museum where historical photos of the city's history are exhibited. Then you return to Lüderitz. In the afternoon you will take a drive to the various bays and to the Diaz peak. Overnight at the Lüderitz Nest Hotel.

Continue your journey with the small plane towards Sossusvlei. You will be flown across rugged, winding gorges – a unique experience. From the air you can see the western edge of the country, the picturesque Tsaris mountains and the fascinating landscape of the Namib desert. Your accommodation, the Little Kulala Camp, invites you to fully accommodate the surrounding peace and quiet and to completely escape your everyday life. The Little Kulala Camp is small and so exquisite that it is the flagship of all lodges in the Kulala Wildlife Reserve. Adjacent to the vast Namib Naukluft Park, the Kullala Wildlife Reserve with its 210 km² area guarantees a sense of seclusion enhanced by the vastness of the desert, the desert dunes and rough mountains. The eleven suites were built on elevated terraces and have a private Plunge pool and an open-air shower. Do you want to observe the breathtaking starry sky that seems to extend endlessly over you in an undisturbed and romantic way? You can do this completely privately from the roof of your clay-built bathroom. You can even sleep here if you feel like it.

In the cool morning hours you will visit one of Namibia's most beautiful landscapes and at the same time the highlight of the Namib desert – the Sossusvlei, a huge clay floor sink surrounded by the highest dunes in the world (up to 300 meters). Enjoy the unique color play of endless blue sky and the red dunes, which is especially impressive just at sunrise. At noon you will return to your lodge. Here you have the opportunity to enjoy this picturesque ambience by the pool and to review the previous travel experiences. In the late afternoon, together with other guests, you will take a ride into the surrounding area to experience the unique sunset and the color play of the desert. Overnight as on the previous day at Little Kulala Camp.

After breakfast, go to the airfield from where you fly to Swakopmund. From a bird's eye view, you have the unique opportunity to marvel at the spectacular dunes from above before you reach your destination today via Conception Bay and Sandwich Harbour: Swakopmund. Once there, you will be picked up by our colleagues on site and taken to your hotel. In the popular city between desert and sea, the remnants from the German colonial era are still clearly recognizable: Historical buildings and old street names give this coastal town a special flair. You will stay at the Strand Hotel, which is located directly on Swakopmund's pier. Overnight at the Strand Hotel.

An impressive boat tour awaits you today. With a little luck you can see fur seals, pelicans and dolphins. Namibia's oysters and a glass of ice-cold sparkling wine are your culinary accompaniment. After your return to Swakopmund, you will have free time. Use these to explore the German-influenced town on your own or choose from one of the many leisure activities; e.g. A quad tour through the desert, deep sea fishing or a parachute jump. You won't get bored in Swakopmund. Overnight stay as the previous day in the Strand Hotel.

Red mountains, yellow desert: If you want to discover the most windy skeleton coast in the north of Namibia, do it neither on water nor on land, but from the air. The area on the border with Angola is inaccessible, hostile to life - and incredibly beautiful. Skeleton Coast National Park, a 30 km wide and 400 km long coastline consisting of nothing but sand and rock. Over the past three centuries, more than a hundred ships have been stranded in the wild floods of the Benguela River. Half buried, her skeletons still rise out of the desert sand today. You will spend the next two nights in the unique Shipwreck Lodge. Located between the dramatic dunes of Namibia's Skeleton Coast, Shipwreck Lodge offers guests a unique and exciting concept, distinctive design, an incomparable location in the park and the fantastic opportunity for wildlife and desert adventures. The lodge itself and each of the rooms are designed to look like the shipwrecks for which this region is famous. So they blend into the overwhelmingly beautiful surroundings and give this untouched wilderness a sense of fun. Overnight at Shipwreck Lodge.

Today after breakfast you will leave and explore the desert area together with a guide. They start with a visit to the seal colony in Seagull Bay. Afterwards, along the beach, you will be shown the remains of the Suiderkus shipwreck as well as the Karimona shipwrecking and you will explore the deserted Westies Diamond Mine. During this trip, enjoy the fascinating landscape and the seclusion in the midst of golden sand dunes. Overnight at Shipwreck Lodge.

Another flight will take you to Etosha National Park today. From above, enjoy the magnificent view of the notorious Skeleton Coast, the seal colony on the Cross Cape, several shipwrecks, dry river courses and with a little luck also on desert elephants. Upon arrival at your lodge (outside the Etosha National Park) you will have the opportunity to participate in a game observation tour in the private game reserve. Your accommodation for the next two days is the Ongava Lodge, which is located in the private Ongava Wild Reserve on the southern border to the Etosha National Park. The chalets and lodges built out of natural materials in the surrounding area as well as the general area allow plenty of space for relaxation before and after the safaris. All rooms at Ongava Lodge have glazed doors that lead to a private covered terrace from which you have a wonderful view of the African bush. Overnight at Ongava Lodge.

Let's go to the realm of wild animals. They make a game observation trip in the Etosha National Park (with other travelers) where the highest concentration of wildlife is found throughout Namibia. More than 130 different mammal and 340 different bird species live in a unique landscape. Watch in the wild alongside elephants, rhinoceros, lions and leopards numerous other animals such as antelopes, hyenas, cheetahs, giraffes, zebras and many, many more. At noon you will go back to your lodge, where you will have the rest of the day at your leisure or take part in another game drive in the Ongava Wild Reserve. Overnight stay as on the previous day at Ongava Lodge.

After a pleasant breakfast a short flight to the Otjiwa Private Game Reserve, one of the oldest private game reserves in Namibia, takes place. You will spend the next two nights at Otjiwa Lodge. In the afternoon you will take a first drive through the extensive reserve and experience the varied flora and fauna of the Namibian savannah. The following day you will have the opportunity to participate in further wildlife observation activities. Otjiwa is particularly well-known for its rhinoceros protection program and the opportunity to experience these impressive animals in a guided tracking at close range. Overnight at Otjiwa Lodge.

A short flight will take you to the international airport of Windhoek, from where you will start your journey home.

Luxury South Africa — Exceptional Golf & Fine Food

Luxury trip South Africa: Golf dreams and culinary delights is an idea for your individual luxury trip with Africa Royal Tours. Now let's get some advice.

14 days

Your trip inspiration

A sample itinerary — tailored to you

  • Africa Royal Tours
  • South Africa
  • Round trips South Africa

Experience the fascinating diversity of South Africa on a journey that combines pleasure, nature, golf and safari. Starting point is the vibrant Cape Town – a city full of contrasts between Atlantic coast, Table Mountain and cultural diversity. Along the beautiful Cape Coast you will find breathtaking views and picturesque bays. In the wine regions around Stellenbosch you can enjoy noble drops amidst gentle hills and elegant wineries. In between, exclusive golf courses invite you to exercise. The crowning conclusion is a stylish safari in a private game reserve – with luxurious lodges and guided game drives. This journey combines relaxation and adventure, joie de vivre and nature experience – unforgettable, inspiring and full of emotion.

Your exclusive golf and taste journey in South Africa begins in Mother City Cape Town, one of the most fascinating cities in the world. When you arrive at the airport, you will be warmly welcomed by your chauffeur, who will take you to your luxurious boutique hotel Clarendon Fresnaye. It is located in the exclusive Fresnaye district, above the Atlantic Ocean and at the foot of Lions Head. From here, many restaurants, shops and the promenade of Sea Point are within walking distance. The guest suites and the family unit are individually and stylishly furnished and offer a wonderful view. After an exciting day in the metropolis, the pool area invites you to relax. Overnight at The Clarendon Fresnaye.

Today you immerse yourself in the vibrant heart of Cape Town. Together with your German-speaking guide, explore the colourful Cape malayen district of Bo-Kaap, stroll across Greenmarket Square and enjoy a short break in the Company's Garden – the city's green oasis. A visit to Signal Hill opens impressive views over Cape Town and the Atlantic Ocean. In good weather, the cable car will take you to the 1,085-meter-high Table Mountain. At the top you can expect panoramic paths with views of False Bay and a variety of vegetation – with a little luck also Klippdachse. On the eastern slope lies the renowned Kirstenbosch garden, which offers exciting insights into the native plant world. In the evening, the lively V&A Waterfront offers music, cuisine and shops – or you can go on a catamaran trip or visit the historic Robben Island. An eventful day full of contrasts and impressions comes to an end. Overnight at The Clarendon Fresnaye.

Look forward to an eventful day full of natural beauties on your guided tour to the legendary Cape of Good Hope. The trip begins with a detour to the Bay of Hout Bay. Your adventure will take you along the spectacular Chapman's Peak Drives, one of the most beautiful coastal roads in the world. Breathtaking panoramic views accompany you on your way to the Cape of Good Hope. Decide for yourself: Walk to the most popular vantage point in the province or take yourself comfortably to the top with the historic funicular, the Flying Dutchman Funicular, to enjoy the panorama. In the afternoon, another highlight awaits you: the charming penguin colony at Boulders Beach. Watch the drolly eyeglass penguins at close range. On the return trip to Cape Town you have the opportunity to visit Muizenberg. Walk along the charming promenades and feel the casual flair of this coastal idyll. Overnight at The Clarendon Fresnaye.

Your trip will take you to Cape Winelands, a region with rolling hills, extensive vineyards and historic towns such as Stellenbosch, Paarl and Franschhoek. Guests can stay at Pearl Valley Hotel by Mantis, in a quiet location surrounded by well-kept Fynbos gardens and within walking distance of Pearl Valley Clubhouse. Relax at the spa, enjoy a drink at the café or explore the wide paths and mountain bike trails. A highlight of your stay is a golf day at the Pearl Valley Golf & Country Club. Designed by Jack Nicklaus, the course is one of the country's most prestigious golf courses. The well-kept fairways are nestled between mountains and vineyards and offer a scenic setting for every round of golf. Of course, a wine tasting is part of your journey. On a guided half-day tour you will get to know selected wineries and taste a selection of South African white and red wines. Overnight at Pearl Valley Hotel by Mantis.

Enjoy two days of exciting discovery tours in the 11,000-hectare Gondwana Game Reserve on the Gardenroute. On guided game drives with experienced guides you will experience spectacular animal observations, including the Big Five and rare and endangered animal species. Picturesque stops in the middle of the bush offer the opportunity to enjoy the sunrise or sunset in an impressive setting with a hot drink or sundowner. Dreamy Fynbos bush hikes or the San Trail invite you to discover the Cape Floral Kingdom and the history of the region actively. You will stay at Kwena Lodge, which is characterized by its unique design and culturally inspired architecture, reminiscent of the nomadic lifestyle of the San people. The lodge offers an infinity pool oasis with private sunbeds, an outdoor pool side bar and cozy lounge areas that invite you to relax after an exciting day in the bush. Overnight at Kwena Lodge.

After breakfast, continue your journey along the Garden Route. On the way, stop at Knysna, known for its lagoon, oyster breeding and, depending on the season, good whale watching. Walk through the city center or along the Waterfront and enjoy regional specialities. In the afternoon you can reach the stylish Fancourt Hotel & Spa near George, idyllically located at the foot of the Outeniqua Mountains. As one of the country's leading golf resorts, Fancourt has three places, including Gary Player's award-winning Montagu Course. The place is characterized by a varied design with modelled terrain structures, water hazards and extensive, well protected greens. An extensive exercise area with driving range, chipping area and bunker impact zone is available for the training. Away from the golf course, tennis courts, walking trails and bird watching invite you to relax. Overnight at Fancourt Hotel & Spa.

Today you will travel through the lush forested Tsitsikamma region, one of South Africa's most impressive natural paradises, along the coast to the Sunshine Coast. You will stop at Tsitsikamma National Park on the way, where you will set off for a walk to the spectacular suspension bridge across the Storms River Gorge. Another stop awaits you at the impressive Knysna Heads. Finally you reach the exclusive coastal town of St. Francis Bay, where the relaxed flair of the south combines with stylish serenity and natural beauty. Here, the boutique guest house The Sands awaits you – a refuge for those who love the sea. Located directly on a pristine sandy beach, the garden stretches down to the ocean – a place to relax. Individually designed rooms with high-quality materials, stylish details and a private balcony await you. Overnight at The Sands at St. Francis.

Start the day with a beach walk along the wide curved coast of Cape St. Francis. The Cape St. Francis Resort offers a variety of opportunities to both relax and become active. A possible activity in St. Francis Bay is a boat trip through the canal system of St. Francis. On request, the trip is accompanied by a Calamari tasting or a traditional South African barbecue ("Braai"). Golf lovers also have the opportunity to visit St. Francis Links Golf Course to play. The modern course is embedded in the dune landscape of the coast and is one of the most prestigious golf courses in the country. The sophisticated design and scenic landscape offers a golf experience of the highest class. Overnight in The Sands at St. Francis

Today your journey through South Africa is coming to an end. Do you want more? We are happy to make an extension for you – for example in Mauritius, where tropical beaches and Creole joie de vivre await you. When it's time to return, your driver will bring you full of memories of breathtaking experiences, to the airport in Port Elizabeth, where you will take your return flight. We say thank you and "Totsiens" – goodbye and see you soon!

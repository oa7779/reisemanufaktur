Tanzania — Exploring the South

Experience the pristine south of Tanzania: Exclusive safari in Ruaha & Nyerere National Park and luxury beach vacation on Pemba Island. Now request!

14 days

Your trip inspiration

A sample itinerary — tailored to you

  • Africa Royal Tours
  • Tanzania
  • Tanzania Round trips

Tanzania, a country that touches your soul! A top-class safari destination in eastern Africa that has preserved vast expanses and impressive traditions. The silent majesty of Ruaha National Park invites you to experience the seclusion of the African wilderness. In Nyerere National Park, on the other hand, the magic of nature is brought close to you along the Rufiji River with hippos, elephant herds and exotic bird species. Let yourself be enchanted by the scents and sounds of the African bush and immerse yourself in a piece of paradise on earth.

Trip information

Itinerary

14 days Dar Es Salaam – Ruaha National Park – Nyerere National Park – Pemba Island

Your personal highlights

  • Discover fascinating Baobab forests of Ruaha National Park
  • Look out for wild dogs in Nyerere National Park
  • Exclusive bathing extension on Pemba Island
  • Especially selected luxury lodges for you
  • Refuel energy in a breathtaking atmosphere
  • Personal contact person before, during and after your trip

Karibu Sana! Welcome to Tanzania. Upon arrival at Dar Es Salaam Airport, you will be expected by an Africa Royal Tours representative and driven to your accommodation in the city center. Dar es Salaam, the pulsating heart of the country, serves as a gateway to the national parks of the southern areas of Tanzania and the Indian Ocean. Enjoy a walk along the magnificent beaches such as Coco Beach, where you can swim in the sun or enjoy fresh seafood from local sellers. Discover historical sites such as the National Museum and the nearby village museum that showcase Tanzania's diverse culture. The lively markets of the city, such as the Kariakoo market, offer a feast for the senses with a variety of colors, sounds and scents – ideal for acquiring local craftsmanship and spices. Your accommodation for this night is the Four Points by Sheraton Dar Es Salaam. Stylish rooms with views of the port and city, a rooftop restaurant, five other restaurants and bars, an outdoor swimming pool and a 24-hour gym provide your comfort. Overnight at Four Points by Sheraton Dar Es Salaam.

Today your trip continues to Ruaha National Park. After a pleasant breakfast you will be taken to Julius Nyerere International Airport, from where you can fly directly to the Ruaha National Park. Upon arrival, you will be warmly welcomed by your Jabali Ridge Guide and taken to the camp. Once there, you can refresh yourself and enjoy a warm lunch before you find yourself in the relaxed atmosphere of your luxurious lodge. In the afternoon you will embark on an exciting game drive that will take you deep into the untouched wilderness and promise unforgettable impressions until the evening. Ruaha National Park inspires with its seclusion and exclusivity: with just a few lodges and a manageable number of visitors, you can enjoy an exceptionally private safari experience in Tanzania's largest national park. Back at Jabali Ridge Lodge you have the opportunity to review the impressions of the day in a luxurious ambience. Located on a rocky promontory, the lodge offers spectacular views of the surrounding landscapes. Relax between the infinity swimming pool and the Asilia Spa. Overnight at Jabali Ridge Lodge.

After a hearty breakfast you leave for the morning game drive. With an area of 22,226 km², the Ruaha National Park forms a fascinating transition zone in which eastern and western plants meet. More than 1,650 plant and 500 bird species find their home here. The park owes its name to the Ruaha River, which marks the southern border. Here you can expect spectacular animal observations: over 10,000 elephants, lions, cheetahs, leopards, hyenas, large buffalo herds and hippos live here. With some luck you can even discover the rare African wild dogs – a highlight for nature and animal lovers! In the coming days you will immerse yourself intensively in the wilderness and beauty of the Ruaha National Park. Two game drives per day, accompanied by the experienced guides of your camp, open up unforgettable moments and unique animal encounters. The wild landscape with Baobab-lined hills and rocky steep slopes is just as impressive as the animal inhabitants. Among the main attractions are the large and small Kudu, as well as the majestic Rappes and Horse Antelopes, which you will experience with a little luck at close range. Overnight at Jabali Ridge Lodge.

After breakfast, you will set off for another giant in the south – Nyerere National Park. You will be taken to the runway where your flight to East Africa's largest national park starts. With an impressive area of around 50,000 km², the Nyerere National Park welcomes you with the expanse of the East African savannah. Here black and white colobus monkeys live in the surrounding river forests, while magnificent sable and kudu antelopes use the shady bushes as pastures. After arriving at the property, which is picturesquely located on a small peninsula overlooking Lake Nzerakera, you can enjoy your lunch there. Thereafter, a game drive is on the program. In the late afternoon hours you will return to Roho ya Selous Camp. The luxurious tent accommodation offers highest comfort with private bathrooms as well as indoor and outdoor showers. An innovative cooling system above the beds ensures relaxing nights, even in the hot months. Guests can enjoy views from the open lounge and dining areas. The pool offers a refreshing break after a safari or hike. Overnight at Roho ya Selous Camp.

The next two days you will spend two unforgettable days in the midst of the impressive Nyerere National Park! This is where the pure adventure awaits you: your days begin with exciting game drives along the banks of the mighty Rufiji River, a vital source of water for the wildlife of the region. Watch hippos stroll in the waterholes and discover majestic elephant herds as well as a variety of waterfowl that have found their home on the banks of the river. At lunch time you will return to your camp where lunch can be enjoyed, or your guide will have lunch for you! You have the opportunity to experience the impressive predatory cats of the park. Filled with breathtaking experiences, you can enjoy a relaxed dinner at your camp in the evening. Overnight at Roho ya Selous Camp.

Goodbye wonderful Tanzanian wilderness. After breakfast you leave the mainland. From the runway in Nyerere National Park you fly a small plane to Pemba Island. Experience the breathtaking diversity of Tanzania from a bird's eye view: the endless golden savannas and winding rivers of the park seamlessly pass into the small islands and coral reefs of the Indian Ocean before finally appearing the lush, green vegetation and palm-lined beaches of Pemba Island. Upon arrival, you will be welcomed by your representative of Africa Royal Tours and taken to your accommodation, the exclusive The Aiyana Resort & Spa. Located on the northernmost tip of Pemba Island, just 30 minutes' flight from Zanzibar, this resort combines luxury with freedom, simplicity and sensuality. There are 30 stylishly designed villas, each a private retreat with breathtaking panoramic views of the Indian Ocean. The harmonious design ensures maximum comfort and blends perfectly into the natural environment. Overnight at The Aiyana Resort & Spa.

Pemba, next to Zanzibar, the second largest island in Tanzania, is a true paradise for peace seekers. Spend five days in a quiet atmosphere surrounded by fragrant spice and fruit trees. The unique charm of the island unfolds in the numerous pristine bays, idyllic beaches and excellent diving and snorkeling areas. Discover the colourful coral gardens in the Misali Island Conservation Area, the lush Ngezi Forest or the fascinating historic ruins. The irregular coastline with its mangrove forests, steep slopes and deep valleys underlines the wild, natural landscape of the island. Known as the Carnation Island of the Indian Ocean, Pemba offers an aromatic and optical pleasure experience. Treat yourself to your well-deserved break at the property: book relaxing wellness treatments at the spa, choose between massages or beauty treatments (optional), or enjoy the day on the beach or by the pool. A refreshing bath in the sea or a quiet moment with a good book in the hammock – your batteries are definitely recharged here. Overnight at The Aiyana Resort & Spa.

Today it's time to say goodbye to the dream island of Pemba. After breakfast you will arrive at Pemba Airport, from where you will travel home via Zanzibar – with a suitcase full of unforgettable experiences and impressive impressions.

Captivating Namibia — A Self-Drive Journey

Namibia Car Rental Tour: Experience your tailor-made Namibia luxury trip with Africa Royal Tours. Now let non-binding advice.

Go on a very special journey: explore one of the most impressive countries in Africa, travel comfortably with your own rental car and experience the highlights of Namibia on your own. Discover the incredible expanse and loneliness as well as the incomparable variety of wild animals. Travel stylishly at your own pace and fulfill your dream of Africa on this journey.

Trip information

Itinerary

11 days Windhoek – Namib Desert – Swakopmund – Damaraland – Ongava Wild Reserve – Onguma Wild Reserve – Okonjima Nature Reserve – Windhoek

Your personal highlights

  • Unlimited freedom with your own four-wheel rental car
  • Spectacular animal experiences and breathtaking landscapes
  • Picturesque sunrise in the dunes of the Namib Naukluft National Park
  • Seeing the famous Welwitschia plant with your own eyes
  • Selected small lodges and camps with authentic flair
  • Discover foreign cultures, landscapes and lifestyles on your own
  • Time-independent travel

After your arrival in Namibia's capital you will be welcomed by one of our partners on site and taken to the car rental company where you will be helped to take over the car. Then you drive to your hotel and can use the rest of the day to get there and acclimatize. You will stay at Galton House, which boasts only nine modern rooms decorated in warm colours and a private and cozy atmosphere. Overnight at Galton House.

Early in the morning you leave Windhoek – through the impressive Khomas highlands to the gates of Namib Naukluft Park, where the gigantic Sossusvlei dunes are located. Here you will spend the next two nights. The Namib Naukluft Park with its impressive landscape and diverse flora and fauna, which has perfectly adapted to the extreme conditions of life, will impress you. Your accommodation for the next two nights is the Sossusvlei Lodge. It borders directly on the oldest desert in the world and offers an ideal starting point for excursions to the famous Sossusvlei and Sesriem Canyon. Held in red and orange tones, the lodge perfectly adapts to your surroundings with its individually standing cottages, which form a combination of tent and wall walls. On the third day of your trip, you will leave early and drive along with a ranger and other guests of the lodge to the entrance of the park in a four-wheeler car – on time for sunrise when the gates are opened. Take a small hike to "Dead Vlei" – the dead pan – or climb one of the surrounding dunes on Sossusvlei to enjoy the views of the seemingly infinite horizon – the reward for the effort of ascent. Overnight stays at Sossusvlei Lodge.

Your trip takes you today to the Atlantic coast to Swakopmund. Wide streets, familiar street names, German cafés and bakeries can be found here. Here German way of life combines with Namibian serenity. For the next day, one of the following activities is included: Take a boat trip to the pelicans and seals from Walvis Bay, a Living Desert tour or a half-day tour that will take you among other things to an olive farm and a gin distillery. Here you can also taste some of Swakopmund's beers and enjoy local specialties. Or would you rather blow the quad over the dunes? Overnight stays in Swakopmund.

A short walk along the Atlantic coast first, then head inland towards Damaraland. The Brandberg massif – with Namibia's highest mountain peak – appears so close and yet so far on the horizon. A visit to the millennia-old rock art of Twyfelfonteins is worthwhile – and this is best done as early as possible. The rock drawings and engravings are between 2,000 and 6,000 years old and still remarkably well preserved. The "burned mountain" as well as the "rocky forest" and "the organ pipes" – a volcanic mass of vertical basalt columns – should also be visited. The Mowani Mountain Camp is well hidden and perfectly nestled in the middle of the huge boulders on a hill from which you can enjoy fantastic views of the below landscape of volcanic origin. When the sun sets, it plunges the landscape into all shades of red – a unique sight. Overnight in Damaraland.

You will continue to your lodge south of the Etosha National Park. In the afternoon, you will have the opportunity to take a first round trip to the Ongava Wild Reserve together with a ranger and other guests of the lodge. Or take a first tour of the National Park in your own car rental. The Etosha National Park, with an area of over 22,000 square kilometers, was founded in 1907 and now houses more than 114 different species of mammals and 340 different species of birds. In the center of the park is the Etosha Pan, an extensive salt pan that is almost always dry. You will stay at the Ongava Tented Camp within the private Ongava Wild Reserve, which is located south of the Etosha National Park. The small traditional camp with eight comfortable East African-style tents has its own nearby water hole, so you can watch the animals directly from your private terrace. Overnight at the Ongava Wild Reserve.

Before going to your next accommodation today, you will have the opportunity to go on a game drive to the Etosha National Park early in the morning together with the Ranger of the Lodge and other guests. On your way to the Onguma Wild Reserve, cross the Etosha National Park at your own pace. Stops at the waterholes on your way offer themselves time and again. Be sure to leave the National Park in time. On the 9th day you will explore the flora and fauna from a completely different perspective (together with other guests) during a Bush Walk on foot in the morning. In the afternoon, an open-air off-road vehicle is on the agenda for a game drive organized by your lodge to the Etosha National Park (together with other guests). A unique experience here in Onguma is the Onkolo Hide – a kind of bunker that allows you to hide the animals at the water hole and observe them in peace and quiet from nearby. At sunset, you will leave the park and reach your accommodation for the next two nights, Lodge Onguma The Fort, which is located in the Onguma Wild Reserve adjacent to the Etosha National Park.

You've always wanted cheetah eyes in front of you? Then Okonjima is the highlight of your tour through Namibia. Okonjima is known for the AfriCat Foundation, the farm's leopard and cheetah project. AfriCat is dedicated to the conservation of the large cats of Namibia. In the early years, mainly cheetahs and leopards were rescued. Meanwhile, the Foundation is also dedicated to the protection of other animals and nature. Depending on the arrival time, you can participate in one of the activities offered by Okonjima in the afternoon. The eight chalets of the Luxury Bush Camp are designed in earthy tones and equipped with African design elements. Between the chalets are at least 80 meters – so every guest enjoys absolute privacy. For those who want to enjoy the magnificent view over the bush, the windows and tent walls can be completely opened. This ensures the ultimate bush feeling.

Depending on your departure time in Windhoek, you leave Okonjima and drive to Windhoek to the city office of the rental car company, where you return your rental car. You will be chauffeured to the airport and will take your return flight from there. Have a good trip!

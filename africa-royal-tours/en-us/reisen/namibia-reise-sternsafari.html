Namibia Self-Drive — Starry Skies & Memorable Hikes

Discover Namibia’s extraordinary night sky on a stargazing safari planned with your Travel Atelier Advisor at Africa Royal Tours. Inquire here.

14 days

Your trip inspiration

A sample itinerary — tailored to you

  • Individual trips to Namibia

On this journey, the breathtaking starry sky of Namibia will captivate you. In your own four-wheel rental car you travel at your own pace. Your gaze wanders up to the sparkling constellations, from an observatory or quite comfortably from your bed under the open sky. You can enjoy active moments during your hikes on lonely dune ridges and through impressive rock landscapes. On this trip you are as close to nature as never before.

Trip information

Itinerary

14 days Windhoek - Hakosberge - Kalahari - NamibRand Nature Reserve - Namib Desert - Swakopmund - Damaraland - Erongo Mountains - Windhoek

Your personal highlights

  • Unlimited freedom with your own four-wheel rental car
  • Flexible on the move at your own pace
  • Spectacular natural experiences and breathtaking landscapes
  • Enjoy the sunset in the Kalahari Desert
  • Discover the fascinating starry sky
  • Nights in the open sky
  • Hikes on the Tok Tokkie Trail and in the Erongo area

Welcome to Namibia! After your arrival at the international airport in Windhoek you will be expected and taken to the office of the rental car company in the city. Here you will receive your Africa Royal Tours representative and help you take over the car. Should you land in Windhoek in the afternoon, we will book you your first overnight stay in Windhuc or the surrounding area. Then you start to your first accommodation, to the Hakos guest farm with a unique view of the Hako mountains. The Hakosberge remind a little of the Drakensberge and in the surrounding area you can - if time permits - take extensive hikes on the extensive paths of the farm. At an additional cost, you can take part in a four-wheel drive from the guest farm to explore the surrounding area. Meet wild animals such as mountain zebras, kudus or Oryx antelopes. Your accommodation is even equipped with two starkeepers. Tonight, take a first look at the sparkling firmament during your star tour.

The next stage of your Namibia tour is on the agenda: it goes into the Kalahari Desert. This extends over South Africa, Botswana and Namibia and continues to spread towards the Congo. It is characterized by red sand dunes, which represent a beautiful contrast to the blue sky and the yellow grass of the wide dune valleys. In this magnificent landscape you will find, among other things, chalices, jumping goats, jackals, ostriches and spoon dogs. Discover the wildlife during your drive in an open-air off-road vehicle and stop at sunset in a beautiful place where you can enjoy a cool drink and let the view wander over the red dunes. On the morning of the 3rd day you can participate in an optional Bushman hike. Or do you rather feel like a ride or another stargazing safari in the observatory? You decide how you want to design your free time.

Go to Tok Tokkie Farmhouse in time to arrive at 2:00 on time. We are happy to book a lunch for today on request. Personal things - such as camera, binoculars and sun protection - everyone wears in their own day backpack. The big luggage is transported by the 的return car". The vehicle sets you and the other hikers after a short drive in the middle of the sand dunes. Here we go! Today's stage is perfect for getting in and takes about one to two hours. On foot it goes over gentle dunes and with "fee circles" covered surfaces. In the camp you will be rewarded with a sundowner. You spend the night on fieldbeds under a sparkling starry sky. An unforgettable experience in which you will begin to feel the desert.

Before sunrise you will be awakened with a cup of tea or coffee. After a light breakfast, start your hike in the freshness of the morning when the desert awakens. On the way, you will learn what it is like with barking geckos or spider wasps, and how some plants can survive in this harsh climate. Your guide will explain some of the wonders of the Namib on the way. With a little luck, a spoon dog or cape crosses your way. Springbucks, oryx antelopes and ostriches also live here. After about four hours you will find a shady place to rest during the hottest time of the day. A picnic lunch is provided. In the afternoon it goes again about two to three hours over the dunes to the sunset and the second overnight place. After a hearty dinner you will go back to bed in the dunes...and exactly... you have guessed to marvel at the stars.

It's said that when the sun rises in Namibia, the world shines in gold, you're gonna test if it's just a rumor this morning. Again you will be awakened shortly before sunrise for your last easy hike (about three hours). In the morning you will reach the Tok Tokkie Farmhouse. From here you drive to your nearest accommodation. Contrast program: After a field bed and bucket shower, the &Beyond Sossusvlei Desert Lodge awaits you with tasteful luxury. Depending on your arrival time, you will take part in a first activity organized from the lodge in the afternoon.

This day also starts very early – because it goes to the famous Sossusvlei. Surrounded by the majestic star dunes, the salt pan and the surrounding dunes have their special charm in the early morning hours. The "Dead Vlei" offers unique photo motifs with its dead trees. In the afternoon you can relax, sleep, enjoy a massage or take part in another activity from the lodge. When the sun sets and the night sky unfolds its full splendor, you have the chance to admire it either with a telescope or directly from your suite – a roof hatch allows for a clear view of the star sea.

Your trip takes you today to the Atlantic coast to Swakopmund. The townscape of the small town is characterized by numerous historical buildings from the German colonial period. Wide streets, familiar street names, German cafés and bakeries can be found here. Here German way of life combines with Namibian serenity. You can enjoy apple strudel in Café Anton with a view of the lighthouse from German colonial times. He might as well like Sylt if there weren't many palm trees on the promenade.

After all the desert experiences, it's on the water for you today. During a morning boat trip with a speedboat or catamaran you have the chance to discover pelicans, seals and dolphins. Around 12:30 a.m., when you're safe again, we'll take a 4x4 vehicle to Sandwich Harbour. Experience the contrast between desert and water close up. In the late afternoon you will be back in Walvis Bay. For dinner, we will be happy to reserve a table to a restaurant of your choice in Swakopmund.

A short walk along the Atlantic coast first, then head inland towards Damaraland. The Brandberg massif appears so close and yet so far on the horizon. Due to the varied mountain formations and hill ranges, the Damaraland is scenically very attractive. We recommend a visit to the thousands of years old rock art Twyfelfonteins. Thousands of rock paintings are gathered in a narrow space. Of course, you can also take a detour to the "burned mountain" and the "destroyed forest" and the" organ pipes". Design your leisure time according to your mood. And there is also a surprise: the beds of your accommodation Doro Nawas can be rolled onto the private veranda, so that you can enjoy the feeling of absolute freedom when you fall asleep.

Through the rock rubble of the Damara it goes for you today to the east into the Erongo area. The Erongo Mountain is the remnant of an ancient volcano of huge size. Today, satellite images show the shape of the volcano with its diameter of approx. I guess 30 kilometers. Its current accommodation, the Hohenstein Lodge, is located about 25 km north of Usakos at the foot of the highest mountain in the Erongo Massif. You should not arrive here later than 2 pm to make the afternoon as relaxing as possible. After you have moved in your room, in the afternoon you go up through the spectacular rock landscape of the "Boulder Forest" past huge granite rocks. Here on the steep slopes, the small miners are looking for tourmalines, aquamarines, rock crystals and other treasures. On the way back there are magnificent views towards the west, where the sun sinks behind the Spitzkoppe.

Enjoy your rich breakfast at the Hohenstein Lodge and then take your rental car to the Anibib Farm, which is about 45 kilometers away. Along the Erongo massif you drive for about an hour on one of Namibia's most beautiful roads. Then park your vehicle and be picked up by your guide, because the last about 18 kilometers to the Etemba Wilderness Camp through more difficult terrain you will be chauffeured. In the afternoon you can enjoy your guided hike on well-developed hiking trails to the famous rock paintings. In the evening, enjoy a traditional "Braai" in the camp and end the day at the campfire.

Depending on the booked flight, we will return to Windhoek today, where you will return your rental car to the city office. You will then be taken to the international airport from where you will be returning.

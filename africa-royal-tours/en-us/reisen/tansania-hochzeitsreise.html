Tanzania Honeymoon — Romance in Lion Country

Tanzania Honeymoon: Enjoy a unique honeymoon in Tanzania with Africa Royal Tours and experience an unforgettable combination of beach and safari.

14 days

Your trip inspiration

A sample itinerary — tailored to you

  • Africa Royal Tours
  • Tanzania
  • Tanzania Round trips

After your wedding, treat yourself to a special time-out: Exciting animal observations and exotic beach feeling await you in Tanzania and Zanzibar. In the private four-wheeled vehicle you are on the road with your own driver guide. Enjoy your time as a couple without strangers – and with the Big 5. Take a glass of champagne while you sit on your private terrace and let the view wander into the distance. Afterwards, Mnemba Island is a romantic luxury paradise in the Indian Ocean.

Trip information

Itinerary

14 days Arusha – Tarangire National Park – Ngorongoro Crater – Serengeti National Park – Zanzibar – Mnemba Island – Zansibar

Your personal highlights

  • Safaris in private four-wheel vehicle with own driver guide
  • Carefully selected, first class accommodations with a lot of privacy
  • Romantic bush breakfast in Tarangire National Park
  • Overnight at the Fly Camp
  • Balloon ride over the Serengeti
  • Barefoot Luxury on Mnemba Island

Karibu! Welcome to Tanzania! At Kilimanjaro Airport, your Africa Royal Tours representative is waiting for you and will help you enter. You will then be taken to your accommodation at the foot of Mount Meru, where you can relax after your arrival. Enjoy your first African sunset with a good glass of wine. Overnight in Arusha.

After breakfast, on today's 2nd day of your honeymoon, pick up your private driver guide and start your exciting safari tour together. Your first stop is the Tarangire National Park, which will delight you with its natural surroundings and wildlife. After lunch in your camp, take your first game drive. In the heart of Tarangire National Park, the Little Oliver's Camp is perfectly located for spectacular wildlife viewing. With its only five tents, it is an exclusive and intimate retreat. Overnight in Arusha.

Today, too, is full of wild animals. Explore the various vegetation zones of the Tarangire National Park on foot and in your private vehicle together with your driver guide. Huge elephant herds are found at the river to drink, while wandering gnu, zebra, buffalo, gazelle, cow and elenantilope flocks are pushing towards the dwindling water holes. For your night in Tarangire Nationapark, we have considered a very special experience for you. Far away from any civilization, away from Main Camp, set up your tent - Fly Camping is trendy! The campfire is raging, the stars are shining above you – closer to nature is not possible! Of course, you do not have to miss a warm shower! And of course your physical well-being is also taken care of. Overnight in the bush.

Enjoy your breakfast today in the middle of the wilderness before heading back to your camp. There you can refresh yourself, and the next highlight is already on the agenda: the world-famous Ngorongoro crater. The afternoon is free to take advantage of the amenities of your next camp. Entamanu Ngorongoro is located in an exclusive location on the northwest edge of the crater. Sit on your private veranda overlooking the crater and enjoy the views that are available to you. In the evening, a candlelight dinner awaits you in a romantic atmosphere. Overnight Ngorongoro.

Finally – today we go to the bottom of the world famous Ngorongoro crater. It is hard to believe that the volcano, which has now been extinguished and collapsed, was still active many millions of years ago. So many animals roam here. Together with your guide, you will walk down to the crater where you pick up your vehicle and then take a game ride with you. Let yourself be impressed by the large zebra and gnu herds that graze peacefully inside. Lions rot in the sun and rhinoceros dreary forward, always in search of fresh grass. Overnight Ngorongoro.

One highlight chases the next, and so on the 6th day you continue your honeymoon towards Serengeti. On the way, keep your eyes and ears open, because maybe leopards or cheetahs cross your way. The 7th day you start very early in the morning, because you take a balloon ride, where you can enjoy fantastic views of the landscapes of the Serengeti. A completely different perspective! In the afternoon you will go on the hunt, and you will also use the full 8th day to walk on a Bush Walk and a game drive in the footsteps of elephant, zebra, lion and co. Dreamlike sunsets can be enjoyed from your generously designed tent. Serengeti Bushtops is a luxurious oasis here in the Serengeti and offers the perfect combination of safari adventure, first class equipment and absolute relaxation. Pure relaxation can be enjoyed with an aromatherapy massage – and your soul breathes up. Overnight stay in the Serengeti.

The "wild side of Tanzania" leaves behind you today and flies in a small propeller machine from the Serengeti to Zanzibar (intermediate stops possible). There you are already expected, and in the private car we go to the northeast of the island. The last stage is by boat. And then you have arrived in paradise, where you will end your honeymoon in the coming days. Overnight on Mnemba Island.

Romantic solitude, fine sandy beach, perfect service – all this is Mnemba Island – and much more. This private gem in the Indian Ocean has Mnemba Island guests exclusively for themselves. The total of 12 Bandas all overlook the Mnemba Atoll and blend perfectly into the tropical environment and vegetation of the island. And not only extensive beach walks, also various water sports activities such as diving, stand up paddling, snorkelling and Dhau rides are possible here. Bury your feet in the sand and review the experiences of the last few days. Let yourself be pampered! The past safari days started early – here you sleep out and welcome the day at your own pace. Overnight on Mnemba Island.

Today your precious time ends for two on your unique honeymoon in Tanzania. Even if your vacation is soon over – the precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories of your trip will accompany you forever. Look forward to sharing your experiences in the distance with your friends and family at home. By boat and by land we go back to Zanzibar City, from where you start your return trip.

Tanzania & Kenya Fly-In Safari — Above Africa’s Wide-Open Spaces

Experience Africa from the air: 11 days flight safari through Serengeti, Ngorongoro & Masai Mara. With unique lodges. Now request!

11 days

Tanzania and Kenya, a wilderness that will touch your heart! Rise into the air with the small aircraft and experience the fascinating diversity of East Africa from a new perspective. Our exclusive flight safari takes you to the glamorous highlights of the unique natural landscapes and national parks. From the picturesque Lake Manyara National Park to the breathtaking expanses of the Ngorongoro crater to the endless plains of the Serengeti and the majestic Masai Mara, you will find an impressive wildlife of all its diversity. Look forward to unforgettable moments full of spectacular animal encounters and the endless beauty of these wild landscapes.

Your dream trip to Tanzania and Kenya starts with your arrival at Kilimanjaro International Airport! A representative of Africa Royal Tours will welcome you to the airport and will chauffeur you to your accommodation for the coming night, the Arusha Coffee Lodge. Here you have time to fully recover from the trip. The Arusha Coffee Lodge is located on gentle hills at the foot of Mount Meru, amidst one of Tanzania's largest coffee plantations. The total of 30 plantations are perfectly decorated in the typical style of the manor house from 1900. In your suite with private bathroom you will find numerous coffee elements in style, decor and color nuances, equipped with all amenities. Overnight at the Arusha Coffee Lodge.

After an extensive breakfast at Arusha Coffee Lodge, you will be taken to Arusha Airport from where you will take your scheduled flight to the Manyara runway. Once there, the driver of the camp awaits you, who will accompany you to Lake Manyara National Park for a half-day game drive. Ernest Hemingway once called Lake Manyara "the most delightful lake in Africa." Look forward to your first safari in the jewel of this park, home to various wildlife such as hippos, impalas, elephants, gnus, buffalo, zebras, wart pigs and giraffes, birds and dense vegetation. With a little luck you will even discover the tree-climbing lions. In the afternoon you will reach the Wilderness Collections Craters Edge Lodge for the next three nights in the Ngorongoro Protected Area. Just in time for lunch you will check in there and have the opportunity to enjoy the rest of the afternoon according to your mood. The lodge combines luxury and nature to create a unique experience. In the midst of the seclusion of the Ngorongoro crater, wake up with the birds' chirping and enjoy the view of the Caldera directly from your tent suite. Overnight at the Wilderness Collection Craters Edge Lodge.

Today you visit Mto wa Mbu to visit the red banana plantations. They learn about the innovative cultivation methods of the villagers of Mto wa Mbu and learn more about the irrigation system that local crops receive, including the 32 varieties of bananas. Interact with local rice farmers and learn more about rice, vegetables and fruit growing. A walk through the village offers a good introduction to everyday life in Tanzania. Here you will get an insight into the houses and the exquisite craft of the many tribes of Mto wa Mbu. You have the opportunity to meet locals and get information about banana beer. After these knowledgeable experiences, you will return to your accommodation where you will have time to relax or enjoy the lodge's amenities.

Your day begins with a morning game drive after breakfast. With your driver and guide, you will start early in the morning to the world famous Ngorongoro Caldera, also known as the Garden of Eden of Africa. It is hard to believe that this now extinct volcano, whose gigantic crater structure collapsed, was still active millions of years ago. This place is full of wild animals! Let yourself be enchanted by the large herds of zebras and gnus that graze peacefully in the crater interior, watch lions groaning in the sun, and look out for the rare and threatened rhinoceros. In the early evening you will return to the camp where you can look forward to a hot shower before dinner is served and you will end the evening. Overnight at the Wilderness Collection Craters Edge Lodge.

Today, after your extensive breakfast, you will be taken to the Manyara runway for your flight to Serengeti National Park. Look forward to breathtaking moments. You will meet our driver and guide and start directly into your safari adventure to experience the great animal hike. At noon you will reach the Ubuntu Migration Camp, where a lunch is waiting for you before you leave for another game drive that lasts until the evening. Their camp has no fixed location, but follows the giant Gnu herds on their annual journey between the Serengeti and the Massai Mara in Kenya. "Ubuntu" means humanity – a word that fits the intimate camp with 10 safari tents made of canvas. The tent camp is designed so that it leaves no traces and offers no classic luxury. They are comfortably equipped and moments for eternity are guaranteed. Overnight at Ubuntu Migration Camp.

Today and tomorrow you spend in the bush to explore the vast Serengeti. The Serengeti is known for its millions of times the number of Gnus – all of them driven by the same ancient rhythm of the relentless cycle of life. Tanzania's oldest and most popular national park is famous for its annual repetitive hiking season. But even outside the hiking period, the Serengeti undoubtedly offers the most impressive animal show in Africa: large buffalo herds, smaller groups of elephants and giraffes as well as thousands and thousands of Elenantilopes, topaz, cow antelopes, Impalas and Grant Gazelles. A ubiquitous experience in Tanzania's largest national park is the struggle for life and death between predators and predators. Gold-mown lion roodles make rich prey thanks to the abundance of grass-eaters. Lonely leopards seek the acacia tree-lined Seronera river, while many cheetahs roam the southeastern plains. Overnight at the camp.

After breakfast at the camp you will be taken to the runway today and will fly to Masai Mara, where you will be welcomed by a driver of the camp at the Olseki runway. After a short transfer, you can reach your accommodation in time for lunch for the next two days, the Hemingways Ol Seki Mara Camp. The camp sometimes enjoys the best location in the Masai Mara thanks to its hillside location and the round tent constructions with beautiful views. Beyond the immediate view, the Ngama hills line the horizon to the east and behind it lie the plains of the Maasai Mara. In the afternoon is a game drive to the Masai Mara program. This impressive wildlife reserve captivates with its magnificent landscape: wide, open plains, crossed by marshland, rivers such as the Mara River and picturesque woodlands. A special highlight is the Great Animal Migration, during which millions of Gnus and Zebras move to the neighbouring Serengeti in Tanzania every year. But not only this natural wonder makes the Masai Mara unique: In addition to the famous "Big Five", you can watch cheetahs, baboons, ostrichs, spoon dogs, jackals, elenantilopes as well as hippos and crocodiles in the wild. After an eventful day you will return to the camp for dinner.

After breakfast you leave for today for the exciting game drives at Naibosho Conservancy. This private wildlife reserve on the edge of the Masai Mara offers a fantastic opportunity to explore an unspoilt wilderness, with an area of 200 km² it also boasts the highest lion density in Africa. Known for its rich biodiversity, impressive wildlife observations and low visitor numbers, Naibosho Conservancy promises a particularly exclusive and intimate safari experience that stands out from the Masai Mara itself. When the sun sets, return to your camp. On the way, your guide might discover together with you nocturnal animals such as owls or bush babies, who are looking for food. Arrived at the camp, enjoy a well-deserved dinner and review the adventures of the day. Overnight at the camp.

The last day of your trip starts with a breakfast followed by a transfer to the Ol-Seki runway for your flight to Wilson Airport in Nairobi. Enjoy a delicious lunch at the famous Carnivore Restaurant before taking you to the Eka Hotel, where a day room is available (if booked) – perfect to rest before your international night flight. You will then transfer to Jomo Kenyatta International Airport for your return flight home. If you would like to extend your stay with a bath extension, e.g. to Zanzibar, we would be happy to advise you on the possibilities.

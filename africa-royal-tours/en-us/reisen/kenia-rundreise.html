In the heart of Kenya - where luxury meets adventure

14-day Kenya journey: Safari in Tsavo, Amboseli & Samburu, Mount Kenya & Naboisho – with exclusive lodges & private guide.

Experience the heart of Kenya in a journey that takes you directly into the arms of the majestic wilderness. From the elegant Hemingways Nairobi, to the pristine expanses of Tsavo West, Amboseli and Samburu – here you will encounter the proud elephant and whispering savannahs under Africa's endless skies. In luxurious camps and lodges, enjoy the tranquility of nature and the vibrant variety of life that unfolds every day in front of your eyes. Let Kenya enchant you, where adventure and relaxation merge in perfect harmony.

1st day of Nairobi

Jambo! Welcome to Kenya! Look forward to a warm welcome at Jomo Kenyatta International Airport, where your representative of Africa Royal Tours is waiting for you. Your driver welcomes you and takes you directly to the stylish Hemingways Nairobi. This exclusive retreat provides the perfect setting to relax from your trip and to make Kenya's first impressions feel good. Immerse yourself in the life and work of the famous author at the Karen Blixen Museum or get close to the majestic giraffes at the Giraffe Center. Nairobi, a vibrant metropolis that combines vibrant urban life with unspoilt nature, promises an exciting start to your Kenyan adventure. Enjoy the evening at Manzili House, a luxury boutique hotel with Swahilian architecture, idyllic garden, swimming pool and a quiet, intimate atmosphere. Overnight at Manzili House.

2.-3. Day to Tsavo West

After your hearty breakfast you will be taken to Wilson Airport and will fly to the spectacular Tsavo West National Park. The rough beauty of the landscape unfolds with expansive plains and dramatic lava flows even when approaching. In the exclusive Finch Hatton's Luxury Tented Camp, your accommodation for the next two nights, you can enjoy your lunch before you leave for your first exciting game drive in the afternoon. Your dinner awaits you back at the camp. The following day you will dive deep into the fascinating world of Tsavo West. A highlight are the Mzima Springs, where you can watch the life of hippos and crocodiles up close through an underwater window. Continue to the huge, rugged Shetani lava streams. Keep your eyes open for the majestic red elephants, who move gracefully through the savannah and whose reddish coloring makes them distinctive. After a day full of unique encounters, return to the luxurious camp and enjoy your dinner. Overnight at Finch Hatton's Luxury Tented Camp.

4.-5. Day Adventure Amboseli National Park

Today, after your breakfast, the trip calls to the Amboseli National Park, known for its impressive elephant herds and the silhouette of Kilimanjaros. When you arrive at Angama Amboseli, an exclusive experience awaits you on the edge of the wilderness. This elegant refuge offers a perfect blend of natural beauty and luxurious comfort. In the afternoon, dive deep into the fascinating wildlife and the impressive panoramas of the park on a game drive. Meet elephants, zebras and maybe even lions. The evening will end in style in the tranquility of your luxurious suite, followed by a dinner under the stars. On the second day, your program is dedicated to the breathtaking wild observations during the early morning and late afternoon rides. Between the safaris, the lodge invites you to relax and relax. Enjoy the peace and luxury of your accommodation and let the impressions of the day pass during your dinner under the African starry sky. Overnight at Angama Amboseli Lodge.

6th day Departure to Samburu Protected Area

After a last breakfast in Amboseli, your way will take you to Wilson Airport and further to the Samburu Protected Area, known for its rich wildlife and cultural diversity. In Saruni Samburu, an exclusive lodge with impressive villas that blend harmoniously into the countryside, you will be greeted with your well-deserved lunch. This lodge combines luxury with nature and offers ideal conditions for game drives. Their afternoon drive reveals the exotic wildlife of Samburus, including rare Grevy Zebras and Reticulated Giraffes. The day ends with a dinner under the African starry sky and an overnight stay in the luxurious villa of Saruni Samburu, surrounded by unspoiled nature. Overnight at Saruni Samburu.

7.-8. Day Journey of discovery in the heart of Samburus

Spend two unforgettable days in Samburu with game drives and bush walks that will take you deep into the diverse flora and fauna of this unique region. Under the guidance of your guide, explore the fascinating wildlife and learn more about the rich traditions and culture of the Samburu. Between your adventures, your lodge offers your retreat - with a wonderful infinity pool and spectacular sunsets, you can relax perfectly. Enjoy the evenings with a dinner under the stars and stay in your luxurious villa, which blends harmoniously into the untouched nature of Samburus. This seclusion and calm invites you to completely turn off and review the impressions of the day. Overnight at Saruni Samburu.

9.-10. Day Highlights at Mount Kenya

After your breakfast today, leave the wilderness of Samburus and drive to the legendary Mount Kenya Safari Club. The exclusive lodge is known for its impressive gardens, luxurious rooms and breathtaking views of majestic Mount Kenya. Immerse yourself in the atmosphere of this historic estate, once founded by Hollywood legend William Holden. The afternoon is yours: stroll through the magnificent gardens or enjoy a game of golf on the well-kept courses with mountain views. The evening is crowned by the dinner in the stylish atmosphere of the club. An adventure awaits you in Mount Kenya National Park the next day. Explore the dense mountain forests, crystal clear rivers and grassy plains. If you want to experience nature even more intensively, you have the opportunity to participate in a ride, trout fishing or a guided hike. Let the impressions of the day pass through at an enjoyable dinner. Overnight at Mount Kenya Safari Club. 

Day 11: Travel to the Naboisho Conservancy

After breakfast, you will be taken to the runway in Nanyuki and fly to Naboisho Conservancy, which is adjacent to the Masai Mara National Reserve – a hidden paradise where untouched wilderness and absolute tranquility reign. Arriving at the camp, a refreshment and a strengthening meal awaits you before you leave for your first game drive in the afternoon. Let yourself be enchanted by the impressive beauty of the landscape as you delve deeper into this protected wilderness. Meet animals in their natural environment and experience the special magic of this unique reserve. You can spend the evening at Hemingway Ol-Seki Mara. At a dinner under the stars, enjoy the African night and get ready for the upcoming adventures. Overnight at Hemingway Ol-Seki Mara.

12-13. Day Experience Naboisho Conservancy

Spend two impressive days in the pristine wilderness of Naboisho Conservancy. Early in the morning and late in the afternoon, you will embark on exciting game drives, where you will find extraordinary animal observations. Look out for lion's pack, elegant cheetahs and imposing elephants that wander through the endless expanses of the savannah. In addition to the rides, you have the opportunity to take part in guided bush walks, where you will discover the smaller wonders of nature – from plants with healing powers to animal tracks in the sand. Between the safaris, the Hemingway Ol-Seki Mara Camp invites you to relax. In the evening you can enjoy a relaxed dinner in your accommodation while you review the impressions of the day. Overnight at Hemingway Ol-Seki Mara.

Day 14 Goodbye Kenya!

After many unforgettable days your transfer takes place today from your lodge to the runway, where you start your scheduled flight back to Nairobi. Once there, you have the opportunity to design your last day in Kenya according to your wishes. Enjoy an optional lunch in one of the first class restaurants or stroll through the lively local markets to collect souvenirs and last impressions. Immerse yourself in the colourful and vibrant atmosphere of Nairobi. In the afternoon or evening you will be taken to Jomo Kenyatta International Airport. Now it's time to say goodbye to Kenya. With a heart full of unforgettable memories of spectacular landscapes, impressive wildlife observations and unique experiences, you will take your return flight. If you want a bath extension, for example on the dreamy spice island Zanzibar, we will be happy to advise you.

Kenya — Hidden Treasures

Experience Kenya's hidden treasures up close – safari adventures, giraffes in the Giraffe Manor & dream beaches in Watamu. Exclusive & unforgettable!

12 days

Your trip inspiration

A sample itinerary — tailored to you

Kenya – a dream of wild beauty and tropical magic! Immerse yourself in the adventure of Kenya, where endless savannahs, impressive animal encounters and paradisiacal beaches await you! Experience the unspoilt wilderness of Laikipias, where elephants pass through the vast plains and leopards sneak silently through the high grass. Explore the Naboisho Protected Area on exciting game drives and guided hiking safaris with experienced Maasai guides before you let your soul wander on the bright white beaches of Watamu. Luxurious accommodations surrounded by nature, spectacular landscapes and exclusive domestic flights make this journey an unforgettable experience – intense, relaxed and full of magic.

Trip information

Itinerary

12 days Nairobi - Laikipia National Park - Mara Naboisho Conservancy – Watamu

Your personal highlights

  • Meet the Rothschild giraffe in the legendary Giraffe Manor
  • Explore the wilderness of Laikipias on game drives and camel safaris
  • Walk through the Naboisho Protected Area with experienced Maasai guides
  • Immerse yourself in the secrets of the savannah
  • Relax on the beautiful beaches of Watamu
  • Experience the fascinating underwater world at Watamu Marine National Park
  • Especially selected luxury lodges for you
  • A personal contact before, during, and after your trip

Welcome to Nairobi, the vibrant gateway to the wonders of Kenya! After your arrival at Jomo Kenyatta International Airport, you will be warmly welcomed by your Africa Royal Tours representative. Your private driver will drive you directly to the unique, extraordinary Giraffe Manor. This boutique hotel is famous for its charismatic inhabitants: the elegant Rothschild giraffes. While relaxing in the luxurious environment, you might be lucky enough to watch these majestic animals curiously stretch their long necks through the windows, hoping to get a friendly welcome or a treat from the guests. Set yourself in this extraordinary ambience and enjoy a relaxing afternoon with a cup of tea in the company of these gentle giants. Giraffe Manor promises luxurious comfort in an incomparable magical setting. Overnight at the Giraffe Manor.

Start your day with a strengthening breakfast in the giraffe society – an experience that touches all the senses. Then visit the David Sheldrick Wildlife Trust, a refuge for rescued baby elephants. Here you have the unique opportunity to get close to this lovable orphan and learn first-hand about the vital protection measures for these endangered animals. In the afternoon, there are a number of options available: Dive into history while visiting the Karen Blixen Museum or explore the colourful local craft markets to discover unique memorabilia. You will experience breathtaking moments. The evening ends in the Giraffe Manor, with a dinner in a pleasant atmosphere and surrounded by the quiet atmosphere of the house. Overnight at the Giraffe Manor.

After breakfast, you will be taken to Wilson Airport, where your domestic flight to Laikipia starts. Upon arrival at Nanyuki Airport, your driver will be waiting to take you to your accommodation. On the way you can enjoy a first game ride through the wide plains before enjoying a meal with spectacular views. In the afternoon you will go on a guided bush hike with camels, which offers you a special access to the fascinating flora and fauna of Laikipias. Then let the day end relaxed with a dinner at the campfire. You will stay at Tumaren Camp, a charming tent camp perfectly nestled in the natural environment. The spacious suite tents are stylishly furnished and offer comfortable beds, traditional safari furniture and a private bathroom with outdoor bush shower. From your private terrace you can enjoy the view of the unspoilt wilderness. Overnight at Tumaren Camp.

Experience two days of authentic safari adventures in the pristine wilderness of Laikipias. On exciting wildlife viewing rides through the vast plains and along hidden waterholes you will encounter elephant herds, majestic lions and with a little luck even the rare African wild dogs. Your guide shares the knowledge about the complex ecosystem and the tracking techniques that have been used for generations by the local Samburu and Maasai. In addition to the game drives, you can also explore the savannah on foot – an intense nature experience where you can discover the smaller, often overlooked wonders of the wilderness: from delicate wild flowers to artistic termite hills to the traces of nocturnal predators. If you like, you can go back to a special camel safari and enjoy the landscape silently from an elevated perspective. You spend the evenings under the endless African starry sky. Overnight at Tumaren Camp.

Today, after your breakfast, you will leave Laikipia and fly to the Naboisho Landebahn, where you will already be expected and drive to the Saruni Leopard Hill Camp, your accommodation for tonight. The stylish camp blends harmoniously into the landscape of the Naboisho Protected Area and offers a wide view of the savannah. After your arrival you can enjoy a refreshing break and let your first impressions take effect. Then go on the magnificent hunt, especially leopards and cheetahs are native to this region and with a little luck you can spot one of these shy predators while wildlife viewing or resting on a branch. In the evening you will return to the camp and enjoy the cozy atmosphere. Take a look at the experiences of the day and strengthen yourself at the well-deserved dinner. From the unique open-air roof that can be opened, you can then observe the stars directly from your bed.

After breakfast, a special adventure awaits you: Walking Safari! You pack up your most important things and set off with your Ranger and Maasai guide to explore the Mara Naboisho Conservancy on foot. After a few hours on foot, your guide will choose a suitable place for your next night and lunch will be served. In the afternoon there is still time to either go on foot again on a hunt or to do a game drive. Today you will stay in a simpler camping tent, which will be built for you. Meals are prepared for you here. Dorobo is the result of years of admiration and respect for the wandering hunters and collectors of the Maasai. On foot with your guide you will also get an authentic insight into the culture. Listen to the Maasai buzzing at night at the campfire and the stories told after a day in the wilderness of Africa. Overnight at Basecamp Dorobo Mobile Camp.

After breakfast you will be brought comfortably by car back to the main camp. Once there you have some time to relax and relax. Reinforced you then set off again to a full-day safari. Look out for the Big 5: elephants, lions, rhinoceros, buffalos and leopards and many other wildlife such as zebras, hippos and giraffes. After your safari you will return to the camp on time for dinner. Enjoy a final evening in the wilderness before you come to rest filled with unforgettable experiences. Overnight at Saruni Leopard Hill Camp.

After breakfast, say goodbye to the wilderness and fly to the picturesque coast of Watamus, where your driver will drive you to the elegant Hemingways Watamu. This luxurious beach resort captivates with its first-class service, stylish suites and a fantastic location right on the Indian Ocean. The afternoon is at your disposal – relax by the pool with a refreshing cocktail, pamper yourself at the spa or take a walk along the enchanting white sandy beach. Listen to the gentle sound of the waves and enjoy the relaxed coastal flair. Spend your night in a stylish suite with spectacular views of the sea – the perfect place to relax. Overnight at Hemingways Watamu.

Experience two days of relaxation and exclusive experiences on the fantastic coast of Watamus. Let yourself be awakened by the gentle sea noise and start in a day full of possibilities: explore the colorful underwater world of the Watamu Marine National Park while snorkeling or diving and encounter dazzling fish swarms, gently gliding rays and perhaps even a sea turtle. Alternatively, you can relax in the resort's first-class spa or simply relax by the pool and enjoy the view over the azure Indian Ocean. In the evening you can enjoy a delicious dinner with fresh seafood, accompanied by a light breeze and the sound of the waves. Spend your nights in your stylish North Wing room again, where you can enjoy the magnificent view of the sea from the balcony. Overnight at Hemingways Watamu.

Enjoy your breakfast for the last time today with a view of the Indian Ocean and let the impressions of the past few days pass again. Then your driver will take you to the airport in Mombasa, from where you will travel home or onward. Your trip with Africa Royal Tours may end at this point, but the memories of these extraordinary experiences will accompany you for a long time – and who knows, maybe your next adventure will take you back to Africa soon. With a heart full of unforgettable moments you say goodbye to Kenya. Have a good trip and goodbye!

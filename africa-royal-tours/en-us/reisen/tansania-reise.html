Tanzania — A Treasure Trove of Experiences

Tanzania Round Trip with Africa Royal Tours: Let yourself be inspired by this idea for a Tanzania journey. Now ask for advice.

13 days

Your trip inspiration

A sample itinerary — tailored to you

Are you ready for a journey that enchants your senses and touches your soul? Tanzania seduces with breathtaking landscapes and a wildlife that is unparalleled. The "Great Migration", where millions of gnus and zebras travel through the vast Serengeti plains, is a natural spectacle that will impress you deeply. Experience contrasting spectacles: endless savannas, green crater landscapes and sparkling lakes with flamingos. Tanzania is wilderness in its purest form – a country that revives your senses and leaves unforgettable memories.

Trip information

Itinerary

13 days Kilimanjaro Airport – Tarangire National Park – Serengeti – Ruaha National Park – Nyerere National Park – Continue or Departure from Tanzania

Welcome to Tanzania! When you arrive at Kilimanjaro Airport, you will be welcomed by your English-language driver guide (on request in German) and taken to Tarangire National Park, where you will spend the first three nights. Let yourself be enchanted by the African wilderness, while elephant herds of up to 300 animals dig for water veins and hiking gnu, zebra, buffalo, gazelle and elenantilope herds search the water holes. The Tarangire River runs water all year round, attracting the largest collection of wild animals outside the Serengeti ecosystem towards the end of the dry season – a true paradise for predators. In the coming days you can expect unforgettable safaris in your private car with your driver guide. A highlight is a bush breakfast under a more than 3,000-year-old Baobab tree – a magical encounter with nature. Accommodation at Lemala Mpingo Ridge offers luxurious suites with private deck and stunning views over the valley of the Tarangire River. Look forward to game drives, sundowners and exciting night safaris with experienced guides. Overnight at Lemala Mpingo Ridge.

After three adventurous days in the African wilderness, you will be taken to the Kuro runway and fly into the endless expanses of the Serengeti. Welcome to the world-famous Serengeti. After landing at the Seronera runway, you will be welcomed by your guide and escorted to your lodge. After a hearty lunch in your lodge, take a first exploration tour in the afternoon. The Serengeti, Tanzania's oldest and most famous national park, is world famous for the annual migration of millions of gnus, zebras and gazelles. Experience the dramatic life cycle with mating battles, river crossings and the birth of thousands of calves. Even outside migration, an impressive spectacle awaits you with large buffalo herds, elephants, giraffes and numerous antelope species. In the next two days, explore the breathtaking wildlife on Game Drives and directly from your lodge. You will stay at the Lemala Nanyuki Lodge, picturesquely situated on a grassy savannah with ancient acacia trees. The luxurious tent suites offer spectacular views of the endless plains of Serengeti. Overnight at Lemala Nanyukie Lodge.

After breakfast, you will continue to the unspoiled Ruaha National Park, known for its seclusion, impressive wilderness and rich wildlife. After arriving at the runway, you will be taken to the Laba Jongomero Camp, where a delicious lunch awaits you. In the afternoon your first game drive through this huge park begins with 22,226km2, home to over 10,000 elephants, predatory cats such as lions and leopards as well as numerous other wildlife. The next few days you will experience intense safari moments and explore the diverse flora and fauna around the Ruaha River. Enjoy stunning sunrises and sunsets in the safari jeep before staying in your luxurious tent suite at the Laba Jongomero Camp, built on elevated wooden platforms. The view of the riverbed will impress you. Overnight at the Laba Jongomer Camp.

After the unforgettable experiences in the Ruaha National Park, your journey continues to the impressive Nyerere National Park. After a relaxing breakfast you will be taken to the runway, where your flight to the largest national park in East Africa with about 50,000 km² is already waiting for you. During the flight you can enjoy spectacular views of the endless savannahs and the fascinating wildlife. Arrived at the exclusive Siwandu Camp, you can enjoy a strengthening lunch before you leave for your first exciting game drive in the afternoon. Over the next two days, explore the park with all its unspoilt wilderness: rivers, plains and lakes invite you to exciting game drives and bird watching tours. Your daily routine could include an exciting morning breeze, a bush lunch and a birdwatching tour in the late afternoon. Relax with a siesta in the shade of your veranda, before you go on a search again. Experience the unique atmosphere of southern Tanzania – a true African dream. Overnight at the Laba Siwandu Camp.

Day 13: Departure or onward travel

Today, after breakfast, say goodbye to Tanzania's wilderness and fly to Dar es Salaam for your trip home or extend your stay with a relaxing beach vacation on Zanzibar – also a wonderful end to your unforgettable Africa trip.

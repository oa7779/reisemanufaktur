South Africa — Wildlife & the Luxury of a Bygone Era

Enjoy the luxury of a bygone era and South Africa’s wildlife on a tailor-made journey with your Travel Atelier Advisor at Africa Royal Tours. Inquire today.

11 days

A sample itinerary — tailored to you

On this exclusive South Africa tour, you will not only get to know the unique wildlife of Africa, but also travel from Johannesburg to Cape Town with the world's most luxurious train – the Rovos Rail – and embark on a journey back in time into a past century. Pass unique landscapes, witness fascinating cultures and conquer one of the most beautiful cities in the world - Cape Town. The contrast to the vibrant Cape Town is a visit to the idyllic wine-growing area around Stellenbosch, where you should definitely try one of the noble drops. Go on a wonderfully varied journey to the "most beautiful end of the world".

Trip information

Itinerary

(11 days) Johannesburg – Madikwe Private Game Reserve – Pretoria – Train ride: Rovos Rail – Cape Town

Your personal highlights

  • Animal observations in the Madikwe Game Reserve
  • Train ride in the famous Rovos Rail
  • Guarantee and safety with maximum freedom
  • Exclusive hotels with African flair
  • Experience Mother City Cape Town
  • Impressive natural landscapes

After your arrival at Johannesburg airport, the small plane continues to the Madikwe Private Game Reserve. Here you are already expected by the colleagues of the Jamala Madikwe Lodge and taken to the lodge in an open off-road vehicle. Keep your eyes open here! The Madikwe Game Reserve was opened in 1991 and is one of the "younger" parks in South Africa. The malaria-free reserve borders Botswana and offers unique wildlife viewing opportunities. Besides the Big Five – lions, leopards, elephants, rhinos and buffaloes, you also have the chance to see the Super Seven – including cheetahs and wild dogs. With 130 mammal and 300 bird species, there are plenty of wildlife on offer here! Day 2 and day 3 are available for wildlife viewing in the reserve. In the mornings and evenings, you can go in the open-air off-road car on a pick-up or enjoy the lodge's amenities for relaxation. Cooling down can be found in your private Plunge pool, with which your villa is equipped. Or would you rather have a shower under the stars? "Jamala" is Swahili and means "natural elegance" and is absolutely in keeping with the ambience of the camp. On day 1 dinner is included, followed by full board. Nights spent in the Madikwe Game Reserve.

If time permits, it goes on again early in the morning. Then it's time to say good-bye, because from the airfield we go back to Johannesburg, where you are already expected and taken to your hotel in Pretoria (private transfer). Here you end the day at AM Milner Guest House. It is located in the exclusive suburb of Waterkloof and is with its only seven rooms a small and cozy accommodation that offers all amenities. Overnight in Pretoria.

After breakfast you will be chauffeured to the railway station, which is specially operated for Rovos Rail (transfer, possibly together with other guests), when entering, you will already feel displaced by a century. Among connoisseurs, the Rovos Rail is considered a nostalgic train for the highest demands. In an extremely elegant way, you will embark on a comfortable journey through southern Africa in a time machine. Friendly, careful staff will pamper you from early to late royal. The beautifully renovated dining wagon serves excellent food and wines, and the panoramic wagon offers a good view of the passing countryside. Accommodation in your Deluxe Suite (approx. 10 sqm) combines the opulent way of traveling in the 1920s with modern amenities. It has a double bed (different configurations), sitting area, safe, air conditioning as well as bathroom with shower and toilet. Overnight on board the Rovos Rail.

From 7 a.m. on, breakfast is served in the dining car, and around 10:00 a.m, the train arrives at Kimberley, where you will visit the Big Hole and the Diamond Mine Museum. The train continues its journey around 12.30 pm via De Aar and Beaufort West towards Matjiesfontein and in the afternoon you have the opportunity to enjoy the High Tea. From 7.30 p.m. you will be spoiled in the dining car with an excellent dinner. Overnight on board the train.

At around 08:15 you can take a guided walk through the historic village of Matjesfontein if you wish. The place has its origins in 1884 and will enchant you with its beauty. Then the journey continues via Touws River and Worcester to Cape Town, where you will arrive around 6 pm in the evening. There you are already expected by a representative of the Ellerman House at the platform and chauffeured to your accommodation, the Ellermann House (together with other guests). The Ellerman House is one of the most luxurious hotels in Cape Town with first-class views of the Atlantic Ocean. Art enthusiasts can look forward to a selected collection of South African art. Its 100 sqm Ellerman House Suite has not only a phenomenal sea view, but also a private terrace and a spacious lounge. Overnight in Cape Town.

The following two days you will have the opportunity to conquer Cape Town with all its beauties and facets. After breakfast on the 8th day your German-speaking tour guide awaits you and takes you along with you your half-day city tour through Cape Town. The program includes the Company's Garden, City Hall, Parliament and Greenmarket Square. Of course, it also goes up to Table Mountain. Please note that the ascent to Table Mountain is weather-related; in case of bad weather or strong wind, the ascent is not possible. On the 9th day we take Clifton and Camps Bay on Chapman's Peak Drive to the Cape of Good Hope (full-day excursion). What would be a Cape Peninsula tour without stopping at Cape Point with its rugged cliffs and small beaches? On the way back you stop in Simons Town, the charming coastal town that is particularly known for the penguin colonies at Boulders Beach. Over Kalk Bay and Muizenberg you drive to the Botanical Garden of Kirstenbosch and the circle finally closes again in your accommodation in Cape Town. Nights in Cape Town.

For today, a day trip with your German-speaking guide to the Cape wine-growing areas is on the agenda. You have the opportunity to taste the noble drops of the region itself. Go on a culinary journey and enjoy with all your senses. Among other things, the many buildings of Cape Holland architecture are particularly attractive, which you will see on the leisurely drive through the small and often sleepy places of the wine-growing areas. The Paarl-Stellenbosch-Franschhoek Triangle is the cradle of South African wine growing. Overnight in Cape Town.

After breakfast it's time to say goodbye to fantastically unforgettable days in South Africa. Until your airport transfer (together with other guests) for your return flight, the time is at your free disposal. We are happy to offer you a relaxing bathing stay in Mozambique, in the Seychelles or on the island of Mauritius (price on request).

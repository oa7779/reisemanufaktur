South Africa, Zimbabwe & Botswana Round Trip - The Pearls of Africa

A dreamlike and unforgettable journey through the three varied countries of South Africa, Zimbabwe and Botswana awaits you. A highlight of your trip is the ride on the Rovos Rail, which will take you back a century on your train journey. Immerse yourself in the romantic atmosphere of a past era. Other attractions such as the world-famous Victoria Falls in Zimbabwe, the unique animal paradises in Chobe National Park and the Okavango Delta in Botswana and Cape Town, probably the most beautiful city in the world, will not disappoint you. With the highlights of the continent, this journey will become a versatile and beautiful Africa experience and will remain in your memory for a long time.

1st day Arrival in Cape Town

Upon your arrival at Cape Town Airport, you will be welcomed by a representative of Africa Royal Tours. You will then be taken to your accommodation for the next few days. The rest of the day is free to relax and acclimatize – or you use the time to make your first explorations in the "Mother City" Cape Town. For dinner, we recommend the V&A Waterfront or the Kloof or Kloof. Long Street with its many restaurants. Upon entering the hotel, you will immediately be transferred to another world, even though they are located right in the center of Cape Town. The over 100-year-old hotel, which has been extensively renovated, impresses with its comfort and the lovingly coordinated design. Let yourself be enchanted by the unique service of this luxury hotel. It is said that Mount Nelson has some of the best service forces in the world. Overnight at Belmond Mount Nelson.

Day 2 – 3 Discover Cape Town

On these two days you will get to know Cape Town better. On a day of your choice, explore Cape Town together with a private tour guide. They visit, among other things, the Bo-Kaap, also called Cape-Malayenviertel. Minarette and pastel-coloured houses characterize the image of the residential area created in the late 18th century. You walk through the green lung of Cape Town, the Company's Garden, and see the Town Hall and the old fort where the first settlers found refuge. Of course, a visit to the colorful Greenmarket Square and its market must not be missing. In fine weather, take the Table Mountain Railway to Table Mountain - Cape Town's landmark. Here you can spend a lot of time and enjoy the panoramas of various viewpoints. Overnight in Cape Town. 

Day 4 of Pretoria

After breakfast you will be taken to Cape Town Airport for your onward flight to Johannesburg. At the airport, you are already expected by your personal driver and taken to your nearest accommodation in Pretoria. Pretoria is one of the three main cities of South Africa, it serves as an executive and is the capital of the country. It is popularly called Jacaranda City, because of the thousand jacaranda trees in the streets, parks and gardens. The city is located about 55 km northeast of Johannesburg. Your accommodation - the AM Milner Guest House - is a luxurious boutique hotel located in the elegant district of Pretoria. From here you can enjoy impressive views over Pretoria and in the beautifully landscaped outdoor area you will find a swimming pool. The hotel continues to offer a small but fine restaurant. Overnight in Pretoria.

5th – 7th day Nostalgia train Rovos Rail

After breakfast you will be taken to Rovos Rail station. When you enter the station's lobby, you will feel displaced for a century – at a time when only the wealthy upper class had the pleasure of experiencing such a travel adventure. Go aboard the Rovos Rail, where you will spend the next three nights on board. Rovos Rail, the world's most luxurious train, is known for its first-class travel sensations as well as its exclusive elegance and finest cuisine. Feel the magic and secrets of Africa in a relaxed and elegant environment on board the nostalgia train. Depending on the route, the trains are pulled with steam, diesel or electric locomotives and offer space for up to 72 passengers in their 36 high-quality suites. Your three-day drive from Johannesburg to Botswana through the capital Gabrone, crossing the turning circle of Capricorn, continues to Francistown to Zimbabwe, where you cross the Hwang National Park. Here you have the unique opportunity to observe animals directly from the train, or you can also go on a guided game drive until you finally reach your destination: the incomparable Victoria Falls. Overnight on board the train in a Deluxe Suite.

Day 8 Arrival in Victoria Falls

After arriving by Rovos Rail at Victoria Falls Train Station, check in to Victoria Falls Hotels directly across the street. This 5-star colonial-style hotel was built in 1904 and is considered the best place to stay in Zimbabwe. The hotel is surrounded by tropical gardens, water lily ponds and hundred-year-old trees. The Edwardian-style rooms offer all modern amenities and will leave nothing to be desired. Enjoy the sunset on the terrace from where you have a magnificent view of the Victoria Falls Bridge. A ten-minute walk along a private footpath leads you to the unique Victoria Falls. Overnight at Victoria Falls Hotel.

Day 9: The Victoria Falls

Today is at your disposal. Explore the Victoria Falls, the world's largest, uniformly falling waterfalls, which are also among the seven wonders of the world. The gigantic natural spectacle will delight you. The locals affectionately call the cases "Mosi-Oa-Tunya", which means "Thinking smoke" and alludes to the huge spray that arises when it falls into the depths. There are numerous leisure activities (all for a fee) such as helicopter sightseeing flights, bungee jumping, rafting tours or Lion Walks. Overnight at Victoria Falls Hotel.

Day 10 – Day 11 Chobe National Park

After breakfast private transfer with border crossing to Kasane in Botswana, followed by a charter flight to Chobe National Park, where you will spend the next two days. The Chobe National Park offers unrivalled unique wildlife viewing opportunities, because the game stock in the Chobe is immense. The elephant population, with more than 50,000 animals, is one of the largest elephant refugees in the world. But also buffaloes, hippos, antelopes and many other animal species are native here. With over 440 bird species, Chobe National Park is also a mecca for bird lovers. Together with experienced guides from the lodge, you can explore the National Park with open safari vehicles twice a day in small groups. Furthermore, you have the opportunity to enjoy a game watching on foot, or if you prefer it a little quieter an exciting bird watching. A unique experience is a boat trip at the time of sunset. They are housed in the Savute Elephant Camp. In addition to a complete luxury feeling, it offers a spectacular view of the surrounding area. Enjoy the magnificent sunset or watch animals from their private sun terrace, which is equipped with armchairs and hammock. Overnight in Chobe National Park.

Day 12 – Day 13 Moremi National Park

After breakfast charter flight by small plane to the camp of the Khwai River Lodge in the Moremi Wild Reserve, where you will spend the next two days. The reserve is surrounded by the wildlife reserves of the Okavango Delta. The special features of the Khwai Reserve are the possible night trips and the walking safaris. The dry landscape, which changes depending on the season with permanent floodplains, offers optimal living conditions for a very diverse animal and bird world. In the mornings and evenings you can expect exciting game drives in an open off-road vehicle with guides from the lodge, in small groups. Romantics we recommend a boat safari or a moonlight safari. Its luxurious accommodation, the Khwai River Lodge, is located on the edge of a forest with leadwood and fig trees and offers great views of the floodplains of the Khwai River and over the Moremi Wildlife Reserve. All luxury tents have been built on wooden platforms, offering spectacular views and cool shade under the traditional African reed roof. Your tent has luxurious equipment and offers every comfort. Its spacious sun terrace with loungers and a hammock, is particularly suitable for animal watching. Overnight at Belmond Khwai River Lodge.

14th – 16th day Okavango Delta

After breakfast, a small plane will take you to the Eagle Island Camp in the Okavango Delta. This will be your accommodation for the next two days. The Okavango Delta is one of the largest inland deltas in the world and is located in the largest sand-filled area of the world – the Kalahari Basin. A wealth of animal species has adapted to these contrasting conditions of life, which are determined on the one hand by the harsh Kalahari desert and on the other hand by the annual arrival of the delta river from Angola. The landscape, possible activities and animal observations differ considerably depending on the season and water level as well as the whereabouts in the delta. On the following days, you will undertake two daily safari activities. You can choose from excursions to the Okavango Delta such as wildlife or bird watching trips on a motor boat or in the Mokoro (traditionally built-in) of the lodge. You will stay at Eagle Island Camp on Xaxaxba Island, a small paradise on the edge of a lagoon. The twelve luxury tents are built on elevated wooden platforms in the shade of traditional, African straw roofs and offer a breathtaking view of the Okavango Delta. Overnight at Belmond Eagle Island Lodge.

17th day Departure

A fascinating and unique journey with the highlights of the African continent comes to an end. They are taken one last time to an airstrip and hop from there with a small propeller machine to Maun. Enjoy the fabulous view one last time before you have civilization again! If you have not yet collected enough of new impressions or are still looking for some relaxation before your trip home, we are happy to help you with the creation of a relaxing bathing extension in Mozambique, Mauritius or the Seychelles.

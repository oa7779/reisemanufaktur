South Africa & Mauritius — Safari Wonders & Beautiful Beaches

South Africa Mauritius Trip: Enjoy an individual trip from Africa Royal Tours and discover the safari magic and beach jewels.

14 days

Your trip inspiration

A sample itinerary — tailored to you

  • Africa Royal Tours
  • South Africa
  • Round trips South Africa

This exclusive tour perfectly combines the best of exclusive safari and bathing experience. The program includes exciting Big 5 safaris in two different malaria-free areas in South Africa: the Marackle National Park and the Madikwe Game Reserve. In the open off-road car and on foot, follow the footsteps of Leo, Leopard and Co. and get very close to nature. Afterwards you can enjoy peace and relaxation in Mauritius. The One&Only Le Saint Géran is the perfect place to end your vacation at the highest level.

Welcome to South Africa! Upon arrival at Johannesburg Airport, you will be received directly by your Africa Royal Tours representative at the machine, which will guide you through passport and customs control quickly and conveniently. Continue on to the Grand Central Airport (transfer takes place together with other guests) and from there you start at noon in a small propeller machine towards the Marakele National Park. In the middle of this dramatic landscape is the Marataba Safari Lodge. Surrounded by high mountains and an abundance of wildlife, you can make your dream of Africa come true here. The fifteen luxurious suites with tent roof all have a private terrace with fantastic views over the extensive landscape. You can enjoy your shower as usual indoors or outdoors – of course perfectly protected from foreign views. Here you become one with nature. An excellent gourmet cuisine with African influences awaits you as well as fabulous panoramic views. Recreational seekers will be thrilled by the pool with fantastic views of the surrounding countryside. Overnight at the Marataba Safari Lodge.

These two days you will use to explore elephant, lion, leopard and co. in two safari activities per day. When you're wildlife viewing in an open-air off-road car, you and other guests of the lodge and your English-speaking ranger will bump over the floor and the stone. From a completely different perspective, you can experience the wildlife on a foot safari or a boat trip on the Matlabas River. Or you can enjoy the fabulous view into the starry sky. They adapt to the rhythm of the animals and start early in the day. Around 05:00 you will be awakened, and after a nice hot cup of coffee or tea you will start your animal watching. After that you strengthen yourself with a delicious brunch. Of course, a hearty lunch must not be missing. Get some sleep before it is late afternoon (approx. 4:30 pm) again means: It ́s Safari-Time! You can review the experiences of the day with a relaxing massage in your own tent suite. Overnight at the Marataba Safari Lodge.

Say goodbye to Marataba this afternoon and are chauffeured by an employee of the lodge to the Airstrip, from where your small plane stands out towards the Madikwe Game Reserve. Of course, you are already expected there and chauffeured to your next safari accommodation: the Molori Safari Lodge. The rest of the day is free. If the time permits, you'll be doing a first round trip this afternoon. After your stay at Molori Safari Lodge you will redefine luxury, exclusivity and privacy. The word Molori means "dreaming in the language of the Setswana – the name was chosen with good reason. Molori has only five individually furnished suites, so privacy is guaranteed. Experience the fusion of African nature and culture with the service and equipment of a first-class accommodation, nestled in an elegant, modern ambience. As soon as you enter Molori, you will feel its magic. Go on a culinary journey: elements of international dishes as well as typical African ingredients merge into a first-class symbiosis. At the Molori Safari Spa, you can pamper your body and mind – and your soul breathes up. Overnight at Molori Safari Lodge.

Early in the morning, when the wilderness awakens, your safari days also begin. Here in Molori, two safari activities (together with other guests and under the guidance of an English-speaking Ranger of the Lodge) are also included. In the open off-road car, it goes on the hunt, and your ranger explains everything worth knowing about the animal and plant world that surrounds you. Wild dogs, giraffes, elephants, zebras, lions, cheetahs, elephant, hyenas, various bird species – the abundance of animals will delight you! During a Bush Walk, the focus is on small things and animals – a great addition to the four-wheel drive. The heart of every photographer will beat faster in the Molori Hide – with a view of the water hole from which you can observe the animals just a few meters away and take unique pictures. Right next door to it – the "water hole" for people: a whiskey bar with panoramic glazing, if you have become thirsty yourself. Overnight at Molori Safari Lodge.

The "wild side of South Africa" leaves you behind today and flies to Johannesburg at noon. Johannesburg is the city of gold and New York Africa with glittering skyline. Here life runs fast like the shops on the stock market – a vibrant city that never sleeps! Your English-language driver guide is waiting for you to go on a sightseeing tour with you this afternoon. Then he chauffeurs you to your hotel where you're staying tonight. The Saxon Hotel is an exclusive refuge in the Sandhurst district. The award-winning spa is a great place to relax. The excellent cuisine ensures a culinary pampering program. Overnight at the Saxon Boutique Hotel, Villa & Spa.

Your private chauffeur will take you to Mauritius in time for your flight and take you to the island paradise. Of course you are already expected here and your driver will take you to your new home for the next six nights. The One&Only Le Saint Géran, the legendary "Grande Dame" of Mauritius, shines in a new splendour after elaborate renovations (2018). Located on a private peninsula on the east coast near Belle Mare, it offers comfort, elegance and relaxation at the highest level. 24 hectares of tropical garden, the turquoise Indian Ocean - a sea of green and blue tones. On the nearly two kilometers long, fine sandy beach you will find a place to soak up the sun. Strip off your shoes and bury your toes in velvety soft sand. Or do you prefer to be active? Various water and land activities are available. In the spa area, international spa experts offer treatments tailored to your personal needs. And culinary is also provided for variety and variety - five different restaurants are available. You can design the rest of the day according to your mood. Overnight at One&Only Le Saint Géran.

Mauritius – land of rainbows, waterfalls and shooting stars and a harmonious mosaic of cultures and religions. It is considered one of the most beautiful dream islands in the Indian Ocean and is perfect for a relaxing and luxurious bathing vacation. Take advantage of these free days (without a vehicle, without a guide) for extensive beach walks, let the fine white sand trickle between your toes and feel moments when time seems to stand still around you. It certainly does not get boring, because with various water and land sports activities you can power yourself according to mood. Your Beachfront Room is characterized by direct beach access. Overnight at One&Only Le Saint Géran.

Even the most beautiful vacation time is over, and so you take leave of Mauritius today. Depending on the booked flight, you will be taken to the airport in time from where you will be returning.

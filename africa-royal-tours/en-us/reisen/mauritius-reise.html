Mauritius — Tropical Diversity & Timeless Elegance

Experience Mauritius in perfectly coordinated facets: first the green highlands of Chamarel, then the fine sandy beach of Grand Baie.

11 days

This journey combines two fascinating sides of the island: the quiet, original nature of the green highlands and elegant beach luxury on the Indian Ocean. In Chamarel you will experience an oasis of silence surrounded by tropical greenery, gentle hills and further nature – a place of deceleration and harmony. A world of fine sand, turquoise water and stylish lightness opens up on the coast. Excellent service, culinary diversity and relaxed moments of luxury characterize every day. This creates a harmonious interplay of nature and elegance – first the green soul of the island, then the radiant blue of the ocean. Mauritius in its most beautiful, intensely experienceable form.

After your arrival you will be warmly welcomed and personally accompanied to your first stage destination – the path does not lead directly to the sea, but into the green heart of the island. The journey into the interior of the island opens up impressive views of gentle hills, lush vegetation and the relaxed lifestyle of Mauritius. Arrived at Lakaz Chamarel Exclusive Lodge, a very special retreat awaits you – embedded in the "green lungs" of the island. Surrounded by tropical nature, birdsong and wide sky, your journey begins here with peace, ease and a feeling of arriving far away from the coast. A gentle start that shows Mauritius from its most original side – authentic, decelerating and full of natural beauty

The coming days invite you to discover the original Mauritius. The Lakaz Chamarel Exclusive Lodge is the ideal starting point for excursions into the impressive nature of the island's interior. Take a hike to Piton Canot and enjoy wonderful views over the green mountain landscape of the southwest. Not far away you will find the spectacular Chamarel waterfall and the famous seven-coloured earth, one of the island's most famous natural wonders. A visit to the Rhumerie de Chamarel also offers exciting insights into the production of Mauritian rum and the opportunity to taste selected varieties. In between, there is plenty of time to relax by the pool, enjoy the tranquility of the tropical environment and get to know Mauritius from its authentic and decelerated side.

After exciting days in the green hills of Chamarel, a new section of your Mauritius trip begins today. Together with your driver guide, you will set off for the north of the island and use the transfer to get to know more facets of Mauritius. On the way the landscape changes noticeably: lush mountain panoramas soft extensive sugarcane fields, lively villages and dreamlike coastal sections. Depending on the route, a stop at the picturesque Cap Malheureux, with its famous red church and beautiful views of the offshore islands, is one of the most famous photographic motifs in Mauritius. In the afternoon, you can reach the elegant Royal Palm Beachcomber Luxury in Grand Baie. The traditional luxury resort is one of the best addresses on the island and will be your exclusive home by the sea for the next five nights.

The coming days at the Royal Palm Beachcomber Luxury are all about recreation and selected discoveries. Enjoy the exclusive atmosphere of the resort, relaxing hours on the fine sandy beach and first-class service. At the same time you can reach the lively Grand Baie, the charming and grown vacation resort in the north of Mauritius. With its cafés, boutiques, restaurants and relaxed atmosphere, Grand Baie invites you to stroll and enjoy. Two included full-day excursions ensure special highlights: a catamaran trip along the beautiful west coast with BBQ lunch and drinks on board as well as the private island tour "Kreola's Punch". You will visit the magnificent Botanical Garden of Pamplemousses, the historic sugar world of L'Aventure du Sucre and the versatile capital of Port Louis with its cultural and colonial sights. So your stay at the Royal Palm combines relaxing moments by the sea with fascinating insights into the culture, history and way of life of Mauritius.

With a private transfer, you will be taken to the airport where your trip through Mauritius ends. In the luggage maybe a bottle of noble Mauritian rum, fragrant vanilla pods or a selection of exotic spices – but above all the most beautiful memories of tropical landscapes, warm encounters and unforgettable moments on this versatile island in the Indian Ocean.

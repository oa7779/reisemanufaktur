Rwanda Deluxe — The Land of a Thousand Hills

Experience Rwanda's diversity up close – with mountain gorillas, safari, helicopter tour & luxury lodges. Authentic, exclusive & unforgettable.

11 days

Your trip inspiration

A sample itinerary — tailored to you

  • Africa Royal Tours
  • Uganda & Rwanda
  • Uganda Roundtrips

Let yourself be enchanted by the fascinating diversity and unspoiltness of Rwanda during your journey. In the dense rainforests and savannahs of this impressive country, you can explore wild mountain gorillas, gold sea cats, chimpanzees and numerous animal species such as lions or elephants. On this varied tour you will explore the scenic and cultural highlights of Rwanda – on foot, by helicopter, on the water or in a safari vehicle. Away from the usual paths, you will experience an authentic Africa, combined with luxurious accommodation and the comfort of an experienced, private driver guide. Rwanda, the land of a thousand hills, awaits you!

Itinerary

After your landing you will be warmly welcomed by your driver guide. Upon request, he takes care of your entry formalities and organizes the collection of your luggage. Then you will be taken to your luxurious accommodation, the Governor's Residence. This boutique hotel in the heart of Kigali combines modern comfort with traditional charm, creating a unique atmosphere. The spacious rooms and suites are elegantly designed and offer a perfect retreat thanks to their equipment and the view of the idyllic pool landscape. The central location of the Governors Residence makes it the ideal starting point to discover Kigali. Let the day end relaxed and enjoy the tranquility and elegance of your accommodation. Overnight at the Governor's Residence.

Today is dedicated to the multifaceted capital of Rwanda. Your tour takes you to the Genocide Memorial, an important place of remembrance and learning, which documents the events of 1994 and shows how the country has found new strength since then. In addition to the Memorial, you can explore lively markets full of colours and flavors, where you can discover handicrafts, clothing and fresh products. Finally, visit art galleries such as the prestigious Inema Arts Center. Of course, time for a city stroll is possible. Relax and let the evening end in your accommodation. Overnight at the Governor ́s Residence.

After your breakfast, your driver will pick you up, and together you will make your way to the fascinating Akagera National Park – a picturesque trip of about three hours. Once there, get your luxurious accommodation for the next three nights. Magashi Camp with elegant tent suites offers stunning views of Lake Rwanyakazinga and stylish furnishings to feel good and relax. It impresses with its exclusive location in the private, northeastern part of the park, which perfectly combines peace and nature experience. In the afternoon, you will embark on a first exciting game drive, where you will discover the rich wildlife of the park. Let the day end with the sounds of nature and the enchanting view of the lake. Overnight at Magashi Camp.

The coming days will be marked by the fascinating wildlife and landscape of Akagera National Park, an impressive mix of savannahs, swamps, lakes and mountains. Look forward to exciting game drives where you can watch elephants, giraffes, zebras, buffalos, lions or leopards and over 500 bird species. Exclusive night trips also allow you to gain insights into the life of nocturnal animals such as hyenas or cibet cats. A highlight is the boat safari on Lake Rwanyakazinga, where hippos and crocodiles can be seen at close range, while ospreys circle over the water. For bird lovers, the lake offers fantastic photo motifs. The variety of activities guarantees unforgettable moments in the midst of this impressive nature. Overnight at Magashi Camp.

After breakfast, your exclusive adventure begins with an impressive helicopter flight to Nyungwe National Park. During the flight, enjoy spectacular views of the rolling hills and lush green forests of Rwanda until you land directly at the luxurious One&Only Nyungwe House. Surrounded by lush tea plantations and the fascinating rainforest of Nyungwe National Park, the exclusive lodge welcomes you with an incomparable ambience. The elegant rooms and suites combine modern comfort with local design elements and offer fantastic views of the surrounding forests and plantations. Enjoy the afternoon with a guided tea plantation tour, where you can learn more about the cultivation and processing of the famous Rwandan tea, or relax at the infinity pool overlooking the endless expanses of the forest. A gourmet dinner awaits you in the evening. Accompanied by the evening forest sounds, you can review the impressions of the day. Overnight at One&Only Nyungwe House.

Early in the morning you start today to one of the unforgettable highlights of your trip: the chimpanzee trekking. Already at 05:00 you will meet with your rangers who will introduce you to the secrets of the Nyungwe rainforest. After a short drive you will reach the starting point of your hike. The Nyungwe Forest houses about 500 chimpanzees, two of whom were specially used to people and can be visited. The trekking lasts between 2 and 6 hours. Once you have found the chimpanzees, watch how the intelligent primates communicate with each other, act playfully and turn through the treetops. After an hour in their vicinity, you will return to the lodge with unique memories. After a break and lunch, another adventure awaits you: the famous Canopy Walk. This 90-meter long and 50-meter-high hanging path winds through the treetops and offers breathtaking views of nature. End the day in the evening in peace. Dinner is already waiting for you back at the hotel. Overnight at One&Only Nyungwe House.

After your breakfast today you take off again by helicopter, on the way towards Volcanoes National Park a highlight of your trip. During the flight you can enjoy breathtaking views of the unspoilt landscape of Rwanda with its green hills and volcanic chains. Upon arrival, you will be taken to the exclusive Bisate Lodge, surrounded by the spectacular volcanic landscape of Volcanoes National Park. This award-winning lodge offers an incomparable experience in the heart of Rwanda. With only six luxurious forest villas inspired by the royal architecture of Rwanda, it is the perfect starting point to experience the majestic mountain gorillas of the Virungas up close. Sustainability, warm hospitality and breathtaking views of the volcanoes Bisoke, Karisimbi and Mikeno make your stay here an unforgettable adventure of your trip. Experience nature and bird watching, wine tasting and insights into the region's impressive biodiversity. Overnight in the Wilderness Bisate.

A highlight chases the next. Today, another exciting encounter is on the agenda: the Golden Monkey Trekking. It starts with a brief briefing in the park headquarters in Kinigi. You will receive important information about the Gold Sea cats and the rules of conduct during the observation. Then a hike with experienced guides and trackers takes you through the bamboo forests, where these rare primates live in groups of up to 100 animals. The hike usually takes about 2 to 4 hours. Then follows the magical moment: For an hour, watch the Golden Monkeys play and eat. Her bright golden back and playful style make this encounter an unforgettable experience. This trekking not only enables a unique encounter with these rare primates in their natural habitat, but also makes an important contribution to its protection. After this adventure, return to the camp, where the rest of the day is at your leisure – enjoy the peace of nature and let the impressions of the day pass relaxedly overnight in the Wilderness Bisate.

Go on the trail of the gentle creatures today. After an early breakfast, your guide will take you to the meeting point where gorilla trekking starts in the early morning. Then follows a guided hike through dense bamboo forests and the impressive volcanic landscape to the animals. As soon as you reach the gorillas, you can spend an hour near them – an intense and emotional moment that makes the special features of these fascinating animals felt. The trekking, which can take between one and several hours, requires fitness and preparation, as the paths are often steep and impervious. This adventure combines breathtaking nature and close-to-skin wildlife observation to an unforgettable highlight of your journey. Overnight in the Wilderness Bisate.

After breakfast, you can say goodbye to this fascinating region today. Your driver will take you to the helicopter and you will have the opportunity to enjoy the breathtaking views from the bird's eye view of the impressive landscape of Rwanda. You can reach the airport in Kigali on time for your international flight. What a trip! With unique impressions and beautiful memories in your luggage, you will start your journey home. Should you complete this unforgettable experience with an extension, for example an exclusive safari in Tanzania or a relaxing bathing vacation in the Seychelles, we will be happy to advise you. We create an individual offer that is perfectly tailored to your needs. Goodbye!

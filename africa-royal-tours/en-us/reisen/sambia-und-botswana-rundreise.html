Enchanting Zambia and Botswana

Botswana and Zambia journey with Africa Royal Tours. The trip is an inspiration for your individual Botswana and Zambia trip. Inquire here.

On this trip you will get to know the two countries of Zambia and Botswana, which are known for their balance and peace. Discover the diverse cultures in this varied part of Africa. Your journey starts with the mighty Victoria Falls in Zambia, watching the life of large elephant herds and a colorful bird world on the banks of the Chobe River, flying over calm watercourses and palm-lined islands, and then going on wildlife observation and boat trips on the Okavango Delta watercourses. At the end you will experience the unique landscape and atmosphere in the vastness of the Kalahari desert. Embark on an eventful journey that will bring you closer to the originality of these two countries and will certainly remain in your memory for a long time.

Day 1 Arrival in Livingstone

Welcome to Zambia! After your landing at the airport, you will be warmly welcomed by your Africa Royal Tours representative and then your driver will take you to your first accommodation. The rest of the day is available for relaxing and acclimatizing. Livingston is known for the Victoria Falls, which were declared a World Heritage Site in 1989. The locals affectionately call the waterfall "Mosi-oa-Tunya", which means as much as thundering smoke. The name comes from the spray mist, which rises up to 300 m and can still be seen at 30 km distance. This occurs when the water masses of the Sambesi pour over a 110 m deep, falling rock face on a width of 1,708 m. Thus, the Victoria Falls are the longest uniformly falling waterfalls of the earth. The dreamlike, adjacent rainforest owes its existence to the constant spray mist. You will stay at the Royal Livingstone Hotel, located directly on the banks of the Sambesis River. The hotel offers a maximum of luxury and service combined with timeless elegance. Overnight at the Royal Livingstone Hotel.

2nd Day Majestic Victoria Falls

Discover the Victoria Falls today. Numerous optional activities are offered, relaxing walks on the edge of the waterfalls, elephant rides on the back of the majestic giants, relaxed boat rides in the sunset, impressive helicopter flights over the waterfalls and thrilling white water rides in canoes and lively market visits. You can also take a nostalgic train ride with a historic steam train. Overnight at the Royal Livingstone Hotel.

Day 3 - Day 4 Chobe National Park

After breakfast you will be taken to Kasane. Here you cross the border to Botswana and will be chauffeured to your nearest accommodation on the edge of Chobe National Park. Already after your arrival and the next day you will participate in the program and activities of the lodge. As the third largest nature reserve in the northeast of Botswana, the park with its four completely different landscape areas is known as home to huge elephant herds and also offers unique wildlife observations of lions, leopards, cheetahs, hippos, buffaloes, various antelope species, more than 400 bird species and an impressive flora. The area around the Chobe River is rich in different vegetation and offers a spectacular view up to the adjacent river banks of Namibia. Watch the hustle and bustle of large elephant herds during a boat trip, marvel at the unforgettable sunsets, experience an evening game drive and discover and feel the African natural wonders with all your senses during a morning footprint. You will also have the opportunity to get to know the culture of the local population when visiting a nearby village and its school. Overnight at the Ngoma Safari Lodge.

5th - 8th day Okavango Delta

After breakfast you will be taken to Kasane by private transfer. By small plane we continue to the heart of the Okavango Delta, where you will spend the next three nights. The Okavango Delta, also known as the "Jewel of Botswana", is the world's largest river delta. It is considered one of the most breathtaking natural sites in Africa. Over 1.6 million hectares are covered by an endless landscape of water and islands. The delta thus forms the basis of life for an impressive number of wild animals and birds. From the air it resembles an infinite labyrinth of waterways, islands, seasonal flood plains and bushwork. Its seclusion and size has led to mass tourism in this fascinating area passing almost without trace, and a safari on land or water has something to do with adventure. Go on a safari in an open-air off-road vehicle, walk through the bush, take a boat tour by canoe or motor boat. Your next camp is located on the 7,000-hectare private property on the western border of the exceptionally wild Moremi Reserve in the Okavango Delta. Overnight at &Beyond Nxabega Okavango Tented Camp.

Day 9 - Day 11 Kalahari Desert

The journey takes you today by small plane into the vastness of the Kalahari desert. The world's largest sand basin shows a wide variety of vegetation zones and offers a home to numerous animal and plant species. The adaptation of flora and fauna to the extreme anti-survival climate conditions is fascinating: in the sparse landscape you often find organisms and plant species that survive long drought periods. The often occurring and unmistakable red sand areas and dunes, the black-maned, wild lions, as well as large herds of stately Oryx antelopes and petite jumpers attract at all times of the day with magnificent photo motifs. In the next three days you will have enough time to explore the surroundings through various activities organized by the lodge. Take, for example, a natural exploration on foot or go wildlife viewing in the afternoons where you can watch steppes, lions, cheetahs and brown hyenas and many other wildlife with a little luck. A special experience is a night safari under the clear, Namibian night sky. The bird world of the Kalahari is considered unique and invites to observations. Overnight at Deception Valley Lodge.

Day 12 Departure

Today it's time to say goodbye to an eventful journey. By small plane it goes back to Maun, from where you start your journey home. If you wish, you can also spend a few days on the beach in Mozambique, on the island of Mauritius or in the Seychelles. We will gladly put together a program for you (price on request).

Discover Ethiopia! The country on the Horn of Africa impresses with breathtakingly beautiful landscapes, lived traditions and a fascinating, cultural wealth. Walk in beautiful churches on historical tracks. Enjoy the finest taste experiences in the home of coffee. In Ethiopia, the perfect blend of modern and past, natural wonders and World Heritage sites awaits you.

1st day Arrival in Addis Ababa

After your arrival in Addis Ababa in the early morning, your German speaking tour guide from Africa Royal Tours welcomes you and guides you to your hotel. After breakfast you still have some time to get used to the altitude, because Addis Ababa is located at about 2,300 meters above sea level. In the afternoon your adventure Ethiopia starts with a three-hour city tour. The young capital of Ethiopia offers a fascinating mix of tradition and modernity. Finally, you can enjoy a magnificent panoramic view of the city from the 3,200 m high Mount Entoto. At dinner, you will discuss the final details of your upcoming tour. Overnight in Addis Ababa at the Saro-Maria Hotel.

2nd day after Adigrat

In the morning you will fly on to Mekele, the center of the Tigray region. In the Palace Museum, marvel at old robes, crowns and manuscripts before continuing on to Wukro. See Wukro Cherkos, the supposedly oldest rock church of Tigray as well as the picturesque Abreha Atsbeha church with its fantastic murals. Here the prayer cross of Frumentius, founder of the Ethiopian Church, is to be preserved. You drive on over the plateau, past typical rectangular stone houses. It follows Negash, an important Islamic pilgrimage site of Ethiopia. Visit the small mosque of Negash amidst ancient tombstones. Overnight at Agoro Lodge in Adigrat.
Route information: about 2,500 meters above sea level, about 145 km, about 3 hours

Day 3 - The Axums Mountains

In Adigrat, the center of Ethiopian Catholics, a mural by Ethiopian artist Aferwerk Tekle adorns the Redeemer Church. Serpentines take you over the Ahleqa Pass towards Axum. Explore Yeha, probably the center of the Ethio-Sabean empire Diamat, and marvel at the moon temple in Diamat. Completely built without mortar, it is the only preserved monument of pre-axumitic times. The oldest inscriptions of Ethiopia can be found at the church dedicated to Abba Asfe, one of the Nine Saints. Over the historic Adwa Mountains – the victory over Italy’s troops in 1896 secured Ethiopia as the only African country to achieve independence – you reach Axum. Overnight at the Yared Zema International Hotel in Axum.
Route information: about 2,150 m above sea level, about 125 km, about 3.5 h

Day 4 Discover the Rome of Ethiopia

Axum is considered the Rome of Ethiopia, Holy City of Orthodox Christians, coronation place of their emperors, residence of the legendary Queen of Saba. The ruins and monuments of the ancient world empire of Axum await you with the Stelenpark, the largest processed monoliths of the world, tombs of the kings, the stele of Ezana and the palace of the Queen of Saba. A chapel preserves the largest sanctuary in Ethiopia, the original federal ark with the stone tablets of Moses. For lunch or sunset, take a look from the terrace of the traditional Yeha Hotel on Stelenfeld and the Marienkirche. This view is simply unique. Overnight stay as the previous day at Yared Zema International Hotel in Axum. 

Day 5 Simien Mountains

From the highlands it goes down today to the wide river plain of the Tekeze River. Serpentines lead back up through bizarre basalt formations. It offers you unique views of the mountain massif of the “chess figures”, part of the Simien Mountains. The Simien Mountains are UNESCO World Heritage Sites and National Park for the protection of the endemic Ethiopian Capricorn, Simien Wolf, Gelada Baboon and several raptor species. Via the Cloudfit Pass with 3,100 m and past waterfalls you reach the roof of Africa after countless hairpin turns. You are staying on this night in the highest hotel in Africa – enjoy a dreamlike view. Overnight at Simien Mountains National Park at Simien Lodge.
Route information: about 3,300 m above sea level, about 250 km, about 5-6 hours.

6th Day Things to Do in Simien Mountains National Park

Today you can discover the special features of the park, depending on the condition of your walks or hikes. The stages are coordinated with your personal guide. They walk on centuries-old paths between the villages, encounter shepherd children with their cattle and sheep. The Simien Mountains National Park is home to wart pigs, sea cats, 137 bird species, monkeys and majestic wild cats such as leopards. The route along the west side of the massif leads you to particularly impressive viewpoints. With a mixture of barren rock, meadows, fields and the unique afro-alpine flora, the National Park is a real feast for the eyes. Overnight stay as on the previous day in Simien Lodge in Simien Mountains National Park.

Day 7 - Continue to Gondar

After your stay in the National Park, your journey continues to the Camelot of Ethiopia, Gondar, which you will explore in the afternoon. It is still considered one of the religious centers today. You will visit the palace complex, which is a World Heritage site and conveys the former power and splendor of the former rulers. Following is one of the most beautiful churches in Ethiopia: Debre Birhan Selassie with the famous ceiling painting of rows of winged angel heads and cross ornaments. The wall paintings are among the most wonderful of Ethiopia’s iconography. You will also visit the Bath of Fasilides, located just outside. The enchanted little water castle served as a summer residence for the rulers. From the nearby ruins of the “widow seat” Kusquam you can enjoy a unique view of Gondar. Overnight at Goha Hotel in Gondar.
Route information: about 2,300 m above sea level, about 120 km, about 2.5 hours.

Day 8 - Bahar Dar

Today you will drive to Bahar Dar on the south shore of Lake Tana. Your first destination on the way is Gorgora. There you will visit the church Debre Sina Mariam, founded in the 14th century, on a peninsula directly on the lake. After crossing the valley of the Garno River, you finally reach the vibrant city of Bahar Dar. Let yourself be surprised by avenues lined with date palms (unique at this height) and lush vegetation. Overnight at the Jacaranda Hotel in Bahar Dar.
Route information: about 1,800 m above sea level, about 285 km, about 6 hours.

Day 9 Explore the city of Bahar Dar

In the morning you will take a boat trip on Lake Tana, the highest lake in Africa and the largest lake in Ethiopia. You will visit the Zeghie peninsula with the monastery complex Ura Kidane Mihret. The circular church with huge cone roof contains colourful murals and countless Bible scenes. Your boat tour then leads you to the outflow of the Blue Nile from Lake Tana. On the lake you will encounter papyrus boats, which have been used as a means of transport for firewood, for fishing and as a ferry for ever. You may even see hippos or a swarm of pelicans. In the afternoon, a one-and-a-half-hour walk to the waterfalls of the Blue Nile is planned. The water level varies depending on the season. Enjoy the scenic highlight. Overnight stay as the previous day at Jacaranda Hotel in Bahar Dar.

Day 10 - Continue to Lalibela

On the way to Lalibela, you will cross the ancient province of Begemder, the Amharic heartland and pass through places that were already in antiquity along important trade routes. The ride impresses with changing landscapes and unique panoramas, deep valleys and striking rock breaks. King Lalibela left the 12./13Cutting the unique churches deep into the rock – a 8World wonders and world heritage sites. Shortly before your destination, you will visit the Grotto Church of Neakuto Leab. The water dripping from the cracks of the rock ceiling into the stone shells is even said to have a healing effect. Overnight at the Maribela Hotel in Lalibela.
Route information: about 2,500 m above sea level, about 335 km, about 6-7 hours.

Day 11 The highlights of Lalibela

According to legend, in the 12th century King Lalibela fulfilled his dream of carving a second Jerusalem from the rock with the help of angels. So well hidden were the churches that they survived the upheavals of history intact. Their names contain references to the holy sites of Palestine. Each of the 12 churches is unique, carved several stories deep into the volcanic tuff, hollowed out, and decorated with windows, columns, and reliefs. Explore the first group of churches in the morning. In the afternoon, discover Lalibela’s old town with its characteristic two-story, mud-plastered round houses. A traditional coffee ceremony follows. Overnight at Maribela Hotel in Lalibela.

Day 12 in Lalibela

You start the day with an excursion to the church of Asheten Mariam, which is enthroned on a rock spur above Lalibela. As desired on foot or comfortably on the back of a mule. Not only a beautiful church awaits you, but also the best panoramic view of the country. Or you drive about 40 km to the church of Yemrehanna Krestos, which is also high above Lalibela on Mount Abune Yosef. After a short walk you can reach the church built in a grotto, which differs in its construction from the churches of Lalibela. In the afternoon you will visit the second group of rock churches and St. George's church. Overnight in Lalibela.

Day 13 Back to Addis Ababa

In the morning you fly back to the metropolis Addis Ababa and move into your hotel room. Take advantage of the afternoon to relax or let yourself be shown more attractions of Addis. For example, visit the Ethnological Museum or the City Museum in the old Ras Birru Palace, a gallery of works by contemporary Ethiopian artists. Your day room is ready for you. 18 The clock is available. In the early evening, you will enjoy typical Ethiopian dishes with traditional music and dances from each region. After that, you will be chauffeured for your flight home to the airport. Even if your trip is now over, the experiences in Ethiopia will still accompany you in your everyday life for a long time.

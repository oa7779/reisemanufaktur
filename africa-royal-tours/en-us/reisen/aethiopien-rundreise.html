Ethiopia — A Journey through History

Discover Ethiopia! The country on the Horn of Africa impresses with breathtakingly beautiful landscapes, lived traditions and a fascinating, cultural wealth. Walk in beautiful churches on historical tracks. Enjoy the finest taste experiences in the home of coffee. In Ethiopia, the perfect blend of modern and past, natural wonders and World Heritage sites awaits you.

Trip information

Itinerary

13 days Addis Ababa – Adigrat – Axum – Simien Mountains National Park – Gondar – Bahar Dar – Lalibela – Addis Abba

Your personal highlights

  • For you selected accommodations to relax after exploring
  • A private chauffeur and German tour guide
  • Maximum flexibility and individuality during your trip
  • Experience a mix of tradition and modernity in Addis Ababa
  • Take a boat trip on Lake Tana
  • Let yourself be enchanted by the palaces of the imperial city of Gondar
  • Enjoy unique landscapes in Simien Mountains National Park
  • Discover Lalibela's famous Grotto Churches, beaten in the rocks

After your arrival in Addis Ababa in the early morning, your German speaking tour guide from Africa Royal Tours welcomes you and guides you to your hotel. After breakfast you still have some time to get used to the altitude, because Addis Ababa is located at about 2,300 meters above sea level. In the afternoon your adventure Ethiopia starts with a three-hour city tour. The young capital of Ethiopia offers a fascinating mix of tradition and modernity. Finally, you can enjoy a magnificent panoramic view of the city from the 3,200 m high Mount Entoto. At dinner, you will discuss the final details of your upcoming tour. Overnight in Addis Ababa at the Saro-Maria Hotel.

You fly on to Mekele, center of the Tigray region in the morning. In the palace museum you will marvel at old robes, crowns and manuscripts before you continue your journey to Wukro. Consider Wukro Cherkos, Tigray's oldest rock church, and the picturesque Abreha Atsbeha church with its fantastic murals. The prayer cross of Frumentius, the founder of the Ethiopian Church, is to be kept here. You continue across the plateau, past typical rectangular broken stone houses. It follows Negash, an important Islamic pilgrimage site in Ethiopia. Visit the small mosque of Negash in the middle of old tombstones. Overnight at Agoro Lodge in Adigrat. Route information: approx. 2,500 meters above sea level, approx. 145 km, approx. 3 hours

In Adigrat, the center of Ethiopian Catholics, a mural by the Ethiopian artist Aferwerk Tekle adorns the Church of the Redeemer. Serpentines will lead you over the Ahleqa pass towards Axum. Explore Yeha, probably the center of the Ethio-Sabaean empire Diamat and marvel at the moon temple in Diamat. Built entirely without mortar, it is the only preserved monument of pre-axumite times. The oldest inscriptions of Ethiopia can be found at the church dedicated to Abba Asfe, one of the Nine Saints. Through the historic Adwa Mountains – the victory over Italy's troops in 1896, Ethiopia was the only African country to secure independence – you reach Axum. Overnight at Yared Zema International Hotel in Axum. Route information: approx. 2,150 m above sea level, approx. 125 km, approx. 3.5 hours

Axum is considered the Rome of Ethiopia, Holy City of Orthodox Christians, coronation place of their emperors, residence of the legendary Queen of Saba. The ruins and monuments of the ancient world empire of Axum await you with the Stelenpark, the largest processed monoliths of the world, tombs of the kings, the stele of Ezana and the palace of the Queen of Saba. A chapel preserves the largest sanctuary in Ethiopia, the original federal ark with the stone tablets of Moses. For lunch or sunset, take a look from the terrace of the traditional Yeha Hotel on Stelenfeld and the Marienkirche. This view is simply unique. Overnight stay as the previous day at Yared Zema International Hotel in Axum.

From the highlands it goes down today into the wide river plain of the Tekeze River. Serpentines lead up again through bizarre basalt formations. There are unique views of the mountain massif of the "Chess Figures", part of the Simien Mountains. The Simien Mountains are UNESCO World Heritage Sites and National Park for the protection of the Endemites Ethiopian Capricorn, Simien Wolf, Gelada Baboon and several species of birds of prey. Over the Wolkefit Pass at 3,100 m and past waterfalls you reach the roof of Africa after countless hairpin bends. You are staying at the highest hotel in Africa that night – enjoy a fantastic view. Overnight at Simien Mountains National Park in Simien Lodge. Route information: approx. 3,300 m above sea level, approx.250 km, approx. 5-6 hours

Today you can discover the special features of the park, depending on the condition of your walks or hikes. The stages are coordinated with your personal guide. They walk on centuries-old paths between the villages, encounter shepherd children with their cattle and sheep. The Simien Mountains National Park is home to wart pigs, sea cats, 137 bird species, monkeys and majestic wild cats such as leopards. The route along the west side of the massif leads you to particularly impressive viewpoints. With a mixture of barren rock, meadows, fields and the unique afro-alpine flora, the National Park is a real feast for the eyes. Overnight stay as on the previous day in Simien Lodge in Simien Mountains National Park.

After your stay in the National Park, your tour continues to the Camelot Ethiopia, Gondar, which you will explore in the afternoon. It is still considered one of the religious centers today. You will visit the World Heritage site of the palace, which conveys the former power and splendor of the former rulers. It follows one of the most beautiful churches in Ethiopia: Debre Birhan Selassie with the famous ceiling painting of rows of winged angel heads and cross ornaments. The mural paintings are among the most wonderful of the iconography of Ethiopia. You will also visit the outside bath of the Fasilides. The enchanted little water castle served the rulers as a summer residence. From the nearby ruin of the "Witwensitze" Kusquam you can enjoy a unique view of Gondar. Overnight at Goha Hotel in Gondar. Route information: approx. 2,300 m above sea level, approx. 120 km, approx. 2.5 hours

Today you drive to Bahar Dar on the southern shore of Lake Tana. Your first destination on the way is Gorgora. There you can visit the church of Debre Sina Mariam, which was founded in the 14th century, on a peninsula right by the lake. After crossing the valley of the Garno River, you will finally reach the vibrant city of Bahar Dar. Discover date palm-lined avenues, unusual at this elevation, and lush vegetation. Overnight at Jacaranda Hotel in Bahar Dar. Route information: elevation approximately 1,800 m above sea level; distance approximately 285 km; driving time approximately 6 hours.

In the morning you will take a boat trip on Lake Tana, the highest lake in Africa and the largest lake in Ethiopia. You will visit the Zeghie peninsula with the monastery complex Ura Kidane Mihret. The circular church with huge cone roof contains colourful murals and countless Bible scenes. Your boat tour then leads you to the outflow of the Blue Nile from Lake Tana. On the lake you will encounter papyrus boats, which have been used as a means of transport for firewood, for fishing and as a ferry for ever. You may even see hippos or a swarm of pelicans. In the afternoon, a one-and-a-half-hour walk to the waterfalls of the Blue Nile is planned. The water level varies depending on the season. Enjoy the scenic highlight. Overnight stay as the previous day at Jacaranda Hotel in Bahar Dar.

On the way to Lalibela you will cross the old province of Begemder, the Amharic core country and pass places that were already lying in ancient times along important trade routes. The ride impresses with varied landscapes and unique panoramas, deep valleys and striking rock breaks. King Lalibela had the unique churches cut deep into the rock in the 12th and 13th centuries – an 8th World Wonder and World Heritage Site. Just before your destination you will visit the Grottenkirche Neakuto Leab. The water dripping from the cracks of the rock cover into the stone shells is said to have a healing effect. Overnight at Maribela Hotel in Lalibela. Route information: approx. 2,500 m above sea level, approx. 335 km, approx. 6-7 hours

According to legend, in the 12th century King Lalibela fulfilled his dream of carving a second Jerusalem from the rock with the help of angels. So well hidden were the churches that they survived the upheavals of history intact. Their names contain references to the holy sites of Palestine. Each of the 12 churches is unique, carved several stories deep into the volcanic tuff, hollowed out, and decorated with windows, columns, and reliefs. Explore the first group of churches in the morning. In the afternoon, discover Lalibela’s old town with its characteristic two-story, mud-plastered round houses. A traditional coffee ceremony follows. Overnight at Maribela Hotel in Lalibela.

You start the day with an excursion to the church of Asheten Mariam, which is enthroned on a rock spur above Lalibela. As desired on foot or comfortably on the back of a mule. Not only a beautiful church awaits you, but also the best panoramic view of the country. Or you drive about 40 km to the church of Yemrehanna Krestos, which is also high above Lalibela on Mount Abune Yosef. After a short walk you can reach the church built in a grotto, which differs in its construction from the churches of Lalibela. In the afternoon you will visit the second group of rock churches and St. George's church. Overnight in Lalibela.

In the morning you fly back to the metropolis Addis Ababa and get your hotel room. Take advantage of the afternoon to relax or let us show you more attractions of Addis. For example, visit the Ethnological Museum or the City Museum in the old Ras Birru Palace, a gallery with works by contemporary Ethiopian artists. Your day room is available until 6 pm. In the early evening, you can enjoy typical Ethiopian dishes with traditional music and dances from each region at a farewell dinner. After that you will be chauffeured to the airport for your flight home. Even if your journey is over, the experiences in Ethiopia will accompany you for a long time in your everyday life.

South Africa Self-Drive — Wellness, Wine & Wildlife

South Africa journey: Wellness, wine and wild animals is an idea of Africa Royal Tours, your Africa expert from Munich. Now request!

15 days

Your trip inspiration

A sample itinerary — tailored to you

  • Africa Royal Tours

This self-drive tour around the Cape starts in one of the most beautiful cities in Africa – Cape Town. Decide for yourself whether you want to explore the unique city and surroundings or whether you prefer to be pampered in the excellent spa of your hotel. A detour to Franschhoek will give you an insight into one of South Africa's most famous wine regions, before heading into the Private Sanbona Wild Reserve. Here you can expect a unique nature as well as a variety of animals in the wild. Go on a wonderful and varied trip around the Cape.

Trip information

Itinerary

15 days Cape Town – Franschhoek – Small Karoo – Cedarberg region – Cape Town

Your personal highlights

  • Travel spontaneously and flexibly with your own rental car
  • Explore the dream city of Cape Town
  • Let yourself be pampered in the spa
  • Discovery tour through the South African wine-growing area
  • Impressive animal observations in the Sanbona Wild Reserve
  • Balanced combination of adventure and relaxation
  • Walk on strange paths and experience adventure

Your journey through South Africa begins in the exciting metropolis of Cape Town. After arriving at the airport, you will be picked up and taken to your hotel, the Belmond Mount Nelson Hotel. A Group K AVIS (Hyundai IX35 or similar) rental car will be delivered to your hotel during the day or the next morning. Your accommodation for the next nights is the Belmond Mount Nelson Hotel, one of the best addresses for a stay in Cape Town. Upon entering the luxurious, elaborately renovated hotel, you will be immediately transferred to another world, even though they are located right in the center of Cape Town. It impresses with its comfort and the lovingly coordinated design. Let yourself be enchanted by the unique service. Especially well known is the "Afternoon Tea" – don't miss this pleasure. Overnight at Belmond Mount Nelson Hotel.

The next three days will be available for exploring Cape Town or exploring the surrounding countryside. On request, we will be happy to organize a guide for this time. If you need some rest and relaxation, the two swimming pools at the hotel or the Librisa Spa invite you. Discover why Cape Town, with its fascinating blend of African and European culture, is undoubtedly one of the most beautiful cities in the world. The Tafelberg, Company, the Malay district in the Bo-Kaap, the Castle of Good Hope and the Victoria & Alfred Waterfront offer a visit to the city center. Especially recommended for a trip is the round trip around the Cape Peninsula to the Cape of Good Hope in combination with a visit to the penguins at the well-known Boulders Beach and the botanical garden in Kirstenbosch. Overnight at Belmond Mount Nelson Hotel.

After breakfast you will drive to the picturesque Cape wine-growing area of Franschhoek, where you will spend the next three nights. Design your stay individually according to your wishes. We recommend a discovery tour through the Franschhoek, Stellenbosch and Paarl triangle, which is considered the cradle of South African wine growing. The rich, fertile soil of the area has made the white and red wines from there, the sherry, the port wines and the brandy world famous. There are various "wine routes" on which there are many beautiful wineries that offer almost all cellar tours. Wine tastings in dark, cool and pleasant cellars smelling of wine and wood are among the highlights of every trip to the Cape. The many buildings of Cape Holland architecture, which can be seen during a leisurely drive through the small and often sleepy places of the wine-growing areas, are also attractive. You will stay at Boutique Hotel La Residence, located on a large estate in Franschhoek, with vineyards, plum plantations, its own pond and fragrant gardens. Overnight stays at La Residence.

Your journey will take you to the Sanbona Private Game Reserve today. For the next three days, you will find the amenities of your lodge, whose surroundings invite you to numerous activities. The Sanbona Wild Reserve is located in the heart of the small Karoo at the foot of the Warmwaterberg between Montagu and Barrydale. The 54,000-hectare reserve is characterized by gentle mountains and plains of different vegetation, stellar information and the expanse of the semi-desert. Explore this unique nature with experienced guides from Dwyka Tented Lodge and learn a lot about the local flora and fauna. In recent years, Sanbona has re-established a large number of wild animals, including some species that originally had their habitat in the area, such as: E. g. Cape buffaloes, lions, rhinoceros, cheetahs, zebras and many antelopes. These – as well as the more than 160 different bird species, including the rare Black Eagle – can be observed from an open four-wheeled vehicle. Overnight stays at Dwyka Tented Lodge.

Enjoy the morning game-watching ride and the subsequent breakfast before continuing to Bushmanskloof in the Cedarberge region. Only 270 km north of Cape Town is the private Bushmanskloof Nature Reserve. Here you have the opportunity to be put back into an almost forgotten time. For more than 10,000 years, the San Bushmen have returned to this area to hunt and search for water. When the early settlers settled in the area in the 19th century, the San were forced to move on. Still today, however, numerous murals remind us of their culture. Overnight at Bushmans Kloof & Spa.

Today it's time to say goodbye to a fascinating country. Until your return flight from Cape Town, time is at your free disposal. Afterwards you can spend a few relaxing bathing days in Mozambique, on the island of Mauritius or in the Seychelles (prices on request).

The Garden Route in South Africa is the ideal route to explore this unique country with its paradisiacal landscapes, unique vegetation and diverse wildlife as a self-driver. Trip information

Itinerary

20 days Johannesburg – Pretoria – Mpumalanga – Blyde River Canyon – Kruger National Park – Eswatini – Phinda Private Game Reserve – Umhlanga Rocks – Port Elizabeth – Knysna – Oudtshoorn – Paarl – Cape Town

After your arrival at the airport in Johannesburg you will be warmly welcomed. Your Africa Royal Tours representative will help you take over your rental car, which will take you to the AM Milner Guest House. AM Milner Guest House awaits you with a combination of French charm and modern luxury. It is an award-winning boutique hotel located on a quiet street in the heart of the chic diplomatic district of Waterkloof, in Pretoria. It features lovingly furnished rooms, a swimming area for cooling down, as well as a restaurant where you can optionally enjoy delicious dishes from the local chef in the evening. Overnight at AM Milner Guest House.

Today you pass through the majestic Mpumalanga area, which is repeatedly found in stories, myths and legends. Here you spend the next two days. An absolute highlight is the visit to the famous Blyde River Canyon. The 22,667-hectare Blyde River Canyon Nature Reserve begins north of Graskop and ends in the north at Swadini at Blyderivierspoort Damm. The heart of the river is the vast Blyde River gorge, which reaches 700 m deep in some places. At a distance of 26 km the Blyde River has dug into the rocky landscape of the Transvaal Drakensberge with a total of 1,000m altitude difference. The nature reserve is known for its lush and varied flora and fauna as well as for its particularly spectacular viewpoints. Especially worth seeing are the three Potholes Rondavels, God's Window and the Bourke's Luck Potholes. Potholes are deep leaching in the rock caused by the confluence of the Blyde River with the Treur River over a period of millions of years by water erosion. A circular path leads you to the most beautiful places. Overnight at Umvangati House.

The next destination of your trip is the Singita Private Game Reserve in the world-famous Kruger National Park. Here you will spend two wonderful days. The Singita Private Game Reserve is located in the world famous Sabi Sands animal reserve on the western edge of the Kruger National Park and is approx. 18,000 ha. The activities focus on wildlife observation trips in the Sabi Sand Wildlife Reserve. There are two daily game drives in open 4x4 safari vehicles, one in the early morning and another in the late afternoon, which after a sundowner turns into a night trip, so that you can get an impression of the nocturnal animals. There are also guided bush walks during which you can observe lions, elephants, buffalos, leopards, giraffes, zebras, waterbucks, hippos, gnus, kudus and other antelope species at close range. It is located in the Singita Boulders Lodge, one of the most famous and famous lodges in South Africa. Overnight at the Singita Boulders Lodge.

After a last morning game drive and breakfast, the trip continues to Eswatini. The small kingdom holds many natural beauties and is particularly proud of its cultural heritage. The life of the Swazi is still strongly linked to old traditions. On many occasions you can see the locals in colorful traditional dresses that they wear with a lot of self-confidence. Eswatini gained independence in 1968 and since then the young King Mswati III has ruled the country. This tends to be a debauchery and currently has over 11 women. Together with the royal family, he enjoys a high reputation and authority among the population. The friendly Swazis live mainly in small village communities with intact polygamous family ties and strict morality. Overnight at Summerfield Botanical Garden & Exclusive Resort.

After breakfast, leave the Kingdom of Eswatini and return to South Africa to the Phinda Private Game Reserve. The next two days will be devoted to wildlife observation. Twice a day, you have the opportunity to watch the game in the lodge's open off-road car; an experienced ranger at your side. You can also explore the bush on foot along with your guide. The Phinda Game Reserve is located in Zululand, about 350 km north of Durban and comprises about 22,000 hectares of different ecosystems such as bush-savanne, river landscapes and mountain regions. To the north, it borders the state's Mkuzi Wild Reserve and to the east and south, the St. Lucia Greater Wetland Park. The rich wildlife, including the "Big Five" and over 415 different bird species, call this paradise their home and are waiting to be discovered. The &Beyond Phinda Vlei Lodge has 6 luxurious African-style bungalows. All rooms have their own small swimming pool, where you can relax between the individual game drives. Overnight at the &Beyond Phinda Vlei Lodge.

After the last morning drive and a delicious breakfast, drive to Umhlanga Rocks, a seaside resort of Durban. The next two days will be available to you here. For example, take advantage of the hotel's amenities for relaxation or take wonderful walks along the beach promenade. Umhlanga Rocks (debate: umschlanga) is located about 20 kilometers north of Durban. Wide, wide and well protected sandy beaches are the attraction of this fashionable suburb. Luxurious hotels and apartment houses can be found here on the beachfront. On the O'Conner Promenade, which extends from the Durban View Park in the south past the Umhlanga Lighthouse from 1954 to the Breakers Resort in the north, you can stroll relaxed along the sea and enjoy the view. Here begins the Umhlanga Lagoon Nature Reserve. Hiking trails and footbridges lead through dense subtropical coastal forests around the Umhlanga lagoon, with many monkeys, bush goats and numerous birds. Surfers from all over the world travel to Umhlanga and you can watch the waves and your arts on the water from the promenade. Overnight at The Oyster Box Hotel.

During the morning you will fly to Port Elizabeth. Here you can accept your new rental car. The drive takes you to the popular place Knysna, where you will spend the next two days exploring the nearby area. On the way you will pass the world-famous Tsitsikamma Forest and the town of Plettenberg Bay, where you should stop in both places. The beautiful and wild Tsitsikamma National Park, Africa's first marine conservation park, includes a narrow coastal plain bordered by cliffs and beaches. Its deep gorges, evergreen forests and amber-coloured rivers will impress you. Plettenberg Bay has the most beautiful beaches on the Garden Route and is world famous for its picturesque scenery. The beautiful sandy beaches stretch over many kilometers along the bay. Water sports are varied and whales and dolphins can be observed depending on the season. Knysna – the charming town is a popular vacation home, nestled between forests and a lagoon, framed by two rocks that rise directly from the sea, the Knysno Heads. On site you can watch a variety of fish, waterbirds, shrimps and crabs and even seahorses. Overnight at Kanonkop House.

The next destination of your trip is the Oudtshoorn ostrich center. The place is located in the enchanting "Little Karoo". Oudtshoorn is the largest town of the Little Karoo and the stronghold of ostrich breeding. Even today, the stately palaces of the "outer barons" during the boom still adorn the cityscape. A visit to an ostrich farm is worthwhile and should not be missed on your trip. Another absolute must is the visit to the Cango Caves, limestone-shaped stalactite caves of a gigantic size. The caves are among the most beautiful in the world. You will stay at Rosenhof Country House, a Victorian guest house. It is located in the middle of a generous rose and herb garden with fountains and just outside Oudtshoorn on the way to the world famous Cango dripstone caves. The decor of the house is tasteful and dignified; antiques and pictures of South African painters decorate the rooms and warm colors create a cozy ambience. Overnight at Rosenhof Country House.

After breakfast you continue to the picturesque wine-growing area of Paarl. The next two days will be available for exploring the surrounding area. The rich, fertile soil of the area has made the white and red wines, the sherry, the port wines and the brandy from here world famous. There are various wine routes, on which are many very beautiful wineries, which offer almost all cellar tours. Wine tastings in dark, cool and pleasant cellars smelling of wine and wood are among the highlights of every trip to the Cape. The many buildings of Cape Holland architecture, which can be seen during a leisurely drive through the small and often sleepy places of the wine-growing areas, are also attractive. What you should not miss is a discovery tour through the Paarl, Franschhoek and Stellenbosch triangle, which are considered the cradle of South African wine growing. The Grande Roche Hotel is your accommodation for the next two nights.

Today you will go to the last part of your trip, which ends in Cape Town. In the dreamlike port city you will spend your last four days of your trip. Use the time for excursions in the surrounding area. Discover one of the most beautiful cities in the world, with the perfect blend of African and European culture. The Tafelberg, Company, the Malay Quarter in the Bo-Kaap district, the Castle of Good Hope and the Victoria & Alfred Waterfront offer a visit to the city center. As a day trip the round trip around the Cape Peninsula to the Cape of Good Hope is recommended. On the way back you should definitely visit the penguins at the famous Boulders Beach as well as the botanical garden in Kirstenbosch. Your hotel, the One & Only Cape Town, has a central location in the harbour area right on the Victoria & Alfred Waterfront. Immerse yourself in an oasis of international style, elegant ambience and contemporary art. The 122 spacious rooms, equipped with African woods and contemporary African art, offer all other amenities and a unique view of Table Mountain. The exclusive spa area, located on an offshore island, invites you to relax and relax. Overnight at One & Only Cape Town.

Today it is sad to say goodbye to unforgettable three weeks in South Africa. Until your return flight, time is at your free disposal. Drive to Cape Town airport and return the car there. Then return flight to your home airport. If you would like to enjoy some rest, we will be happy to help you organize an exclusive bathing extension in Mozambique, on the island of Mauritius or in the Seychelles (price on request). On request, this tour can also be booked with a German speaking driver guide (price on request).

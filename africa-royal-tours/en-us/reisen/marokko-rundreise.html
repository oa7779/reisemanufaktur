Morocco — Wild Beauty

Morocco Trip: Let Africa Royal Tours take you into the fairy tale of 1001 night with the wild beauty of Morocco. Now request.

8 days

Your trip inspiration

A sample itinerary — tailored to you

  • Africa Royal Tours

Experience a dream trip away from the usual tourist trails. Follow the traces of the Berber peoples into the heart of the extraordinary landscape of the Atlas Mountains. Enjoy the cool and crystal clear waters of Lake Bin-El-Ouidane and the surrounding waterfalls. Stop at fertile oases in the Agafay Desert and enjoy a special place of rest in cool shaded places. On your journey you will experience the original landscape and the natural beauty of this unique country.

After your arrival at Marrakech airport, you will be warmly welcomed and taken to the hotel by your private tour guide. The rest of the day is at your disposal. Your tour guide will be happy to advise you on sightseeing or excursions. Marrakech is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and the millennia-old history transforms the Medina into a large open-air theatre every day. Because of the small and red plastered labyrinth-like streets, Marrakech is also called "The Red City". Particularly worth seeing are Jemaa El Fna Square, the Saadian Tombs as well as the Royal Palace and the Bahia Palace. Your hotel is a true pearl, the design is inspired by the traditional riads: the rooms overlook the central courtyard, which acts as a meeting place and at the same time as a resting place. Located in the heart of the hustle and bustle of the old town, the panoramic terrace and the hotel's heated outdoor pool offer wonderful relaxation possibilities. Private cooking classes are also offered. The suites are individually decorated with oriental and elegant elements. Overnight at La Sultana Marrakech.

Enjoy an extensive breakfast and start your trip to the Ouzoud waterfalls with an all-wheel drive. The waterfalls are considered to be the most beautiful and, with a height of 110 meters, the highest in Morocco. At the foot of the falls you can take a small boat tour or you have the opportunity to make a small hike along the river. After this experience, continue your journey towards the Bin-El-Ouidane reservoir. The route leads past the Cathédrale du Rocher and along the longest river in the Atlas Mountains, the Assif Ahansal, until you finally reach the 130 meter deep reservoir. The crystal clear water and the reddish-brown sandbanks create a picturesque backdrop, embedded in the majestic peaks of the Atlas Mountains. After a short stop at Hotel Widiane Suites & Spa, your accommodation for the next two nights, take a boat trip on the lake (depending on your arrival time) to the fascinating gorges of Bin-El-Ouidane. Your accommodation, the Hotel Widiane Suites & Spa, is idyllically located in the rolling hills of the Middle Atlas. The view of the lake is breathtaking, and the stylishly modern ambience of your room invites you to linger and relax. Overnight at Hotel Widiane Suites & Spa Resort.

Today you will discover the fascinating surroundings around Lake Bin-El-Ouidane on a guided tour. Accompanied by the scent of almond trees (depending on the season), walk in front of the picturesque lake landscape and let yourself be enchanted by the beauty of nature. After lunch in the hotel you can make your time according to your wishes: How about an action-packed round of jet ski or water ski? Alternatively, you can choose from other exciting activities such as mountain biking, trekking or paragliding (for a fee). For peace seekers, the spa offers a pampering program – enjoy treatments, the hammam or the hot tub with breathtaking views of the lake. In the evening you can relax with a delicious dinner at the Widiane Restaurant. Overnight at Hotel Widiane Suites & Spa Resort.

Enjoy an extensive breakfast before continuing your journey towards the Ait-Bouguez Valley. This idyllic valley forms the heart of the High Atlas, the southern mountain range of Morocco with the highest peaks in the country. During the ride impressive landscapes pass by you and let anticipation come up for the coming. The remote mountain valley Ait-Bouguemez is located at an altitude of 1,800 meters and is characterized by small, charming villages, whose mosques, shops and cordial residents invite you to explore. Your accommodation, the authentic Ecolodge Dar Itrane, is surrounded by a gentle hilly landscape and welcomes you with cozy rooms and breathtaking views. The lodge is the ideal place to experience the region as well as the culture and traditions of the Berber peoples. After your arrival, the rest of the day is at your disposal to enjoy the surroundings in peace. Overnight at Dar Itrane Lodge.

Today you have the opportunity to meet locals in one of the surrounding villages. First of all, you will visit a craft cooperative run by women. You will also visit the Ecole Vivante School and Education Center, which gives people the opportunity to discover their talents. In addition to the values of the Muslim faith, culture and tradition, the students are also supported in their personal development and prepared for a life in exchange with foreign languages and the world. After the visit you should not miss the Holy Shrine at the top of the mountain Sidi Moussa. From the complex, which also serves as a burial site, there is a fantastic view over the wide hills and the valley. Lunch is served in the form of a picnic in a garden in the Ait-Bouguez Valley. Then take a walk through the gardens, along wild flowers and mountain streams, to the last seven remaining water mills in the valley. In the evening we return to your accommodation in time for dinner. Let yourself be pampered by Moroccan delicacies and Berber specialties. Overnight at Dar Itrane Lodge.

Another highlight of your trip awaits you today: the Agafay stone desert, which is only 30 km away from Marrakech. Far away from the hustle and bustle of the city, you will experience a special landscape experience here. While the Agafay Desert is not as famous as the "big sister" – the Sahara in southern Morocco – you can feel the seemingly endless expanse, feel freedom and enjoy peace and quiet here. After check-in to your hotel, drive into the stone desert and stop at an oasis where you can refresh yourself and enjoy the views of the distance – a great experience in the midst of unspoilt nature. The La Pause will be your accommodation for the next two nights. Surrounded by the oasis, the walls of the hotel are built of straw and clay according to the traditional construction and provide pleasant cooling especially in the hot months. The rooms are decorated in a mixture of tradition and simplicity – this creates an authentic Moroccan flair. Relax in one of the hammocks stretched between the olive trees, refresh yourself in the pool or spend the night in a nomad tent near the hotel, one night under the starry desert sky (optional). Overnight at the La Pause.

After a relaxing night at the hotel, your day begins with a guided camel tour into the stone desert. Experience the breathtaking colors when the sun dips the surrounding hills in all the shades of red tones and feel the special expanse and beauty of the desert landscape. For lunch, stop in an oasis where your food is served in the shade of trees. For the afternoon you have the choice between a two-hour desert ride or a two- hour horse ride (optional). In the evening a romantic candle-light dinner awaits you in a magical atmosphere. Overnight stay as on the previous day in La Pause.

Unfortunately, your eventful journey through Morocco is coming to an end today. So it is time to say goodbye to the unique landscape, the beguiling smells and the culinary highlights. After breakfast you will be taken to the airport to start your journey home from there. We are happy to create a relaxed bathing extension on the coast of Morocco, such as in the fishing village of Essaouira or in Agadir (price on request).

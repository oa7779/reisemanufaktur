Uganda — Off the Beaten Path

Uganda Round Trip: Enjoy Uganda on unknown paths on an individual trip with Africa Royal Tours. Now make a non-binding request.

14 days

A sample itinerary — tailored to you

Experience Uganda – wild, original and touching! This extraordinary journey takes you deep into the heart of Africa: from the origin of the Nile to spectacular waterfalls and original mountain landscapes to the vast savannahs of the Kidepo Valley National Park. They encounter chimpanzees in the rainforest, follow the footsteps of Uganda's last rhinoceros on foot and let themselves be captivated by the roaring natural spectacle of the Murchison Falls. On the way you stay in exclusive lodges close to nature – partly on private islands, partly in the middle of the wilderness – always with style, comfort and something. This journey combines intense nature experiences with authentic encounters, cultural depth and the quiet magic of untouched landscapes.

Trip information

Itinerary

14 days Jinja – Sipi Falls – Mount Elgon National Park – Sipi Fall – Moroto – Kidepo Valley National Park – Murchison Falls National Park – Ziwa rhinoceros reserve – Kampala – Entebbe

Your personal highlights

  • Exclusive start at the Nile with boat trip at sunset
  • Adventure waterfall hike in Sipi Mountains
  • Trekking in Mount Elgon National Park
  • Superlative Safari in the Kidepo Valley National Park
  • Visit to the Karamojong – fascinating culture in northeastern Uganda
  • Wildlife viewing & Boat Safari in Murchison Falls National Park
  • Chimpanzee tracking and rhinoceros tracking
  • Own accommodations selected for you
  • A personal contact before, during, and after your trip

"Mwebale kujja mu Uganda – Welcome! After your arrival in Entebbe you will be welcomed by your representative of Africa Royal Tours, whereupon you will continue directly. Your destination for the next two nights is Jinja. They pass through villages, small towns and lonely huts, embedded in the wonderful vegetation of the country – Uganda is also called the "green lung of East Africa". Your accommodation in Jinja is the exclusive Lemala Wild Waters Lodge, a true gem on the banks of the Nile. This special lodge, which can be reached by boat, is located on a private island surrounded by rushing wild water. The boat trip there becomes a first adventure and lets you fully immerse yourself in the natural beauty of Uganda. In the spacious and stylish wooden villas that stand on elevated platforms, you can enjoy privacy as well as breathtaking views of the Nile. The crowning end of the day is a relaxing boat trip on the Nile in the evening. Overnight at Wildwaters Lodge.

After breakfast, an exciting day full of opportunities for active exploration awaits you today. Whether you're looking for the thrill of white water rafting or kayaking along the wild waters of the Nile, plunge into the depths of bungee jumping or want to explore the shore area on a quad or mountain bike tour – in Jinja adventure is guaranteed. Would you like to make the day a little more relaxed? Then a guided village hike is recommended, where you gain authentic insights into the daily life of the local population. Alternatively, on a guided ride along the Nile, you will experience the impressive landscape from a different perspective – quiet, close to nature and impressive. Many of these activities can be booked flexibly on site and can be designed according to your wishes. Overnight at Wildwaters Lodge

Today you leave Jinja behind and continue on your way to the scenic east of Uganda – a region that is still considered to be a real insider tip for tourists. Your journey will take you through lush green hills, past small villages and tea plantations, until you finally reach the Sipi area at the foot of the mighty Mount Elgon. In addition to the three impressive waterfalls, the area is known for its bird wealth and some of the best Arabica coffees in the world. Today's accommodation is the authentic Sipi Heritage Lodge, idyllically located at the foot of Mount Elgon. The lodge boasts individually designed cottages and bandas, each room with private bathroom and private veranda. After your arrival you will have enough time to set off on a guided hike to the waterfalls. On narrow paths, you pass terraced fields, banana plantations and traditional clay huts until you finally reach the imposing Sipi waterfalls. In several steps they plunge into the depths with a roaring roar – a breathtaking sight and an ideal place for a little breather. Guests can enjoy a dinner at your lodge. Overnight at Sipi Herritage Lodge.

Get ready today after breakfast – the first day of your trekking begins! With a 7.1 km hike, you start from Elgon Trekkers Base Camp at 1,720 m above sea level up to Hunwick-Camp at 2,882 m – a height difference of 1,162 m. Chapeau. In even serpentines, the trail continues uphill to the park boundary, which lies directly below the impressive Nabunyifu Falls. On the further way, breathtaking views over the valley and the distant heights open up to you again and again. Continue through the Dirigana valley, into the dense mountain forest with ancient Podocarpus trees and an impressive lush green vegetation. Finally, you reach your destination today: Hunwick-s Camp at an altitude of 2,882 m – Bergheil! On a picturesque clearing, Hunwick is waiting for you, it is simply equipped. The tents stand on wooden platforms and there is a small fireplace – ideal to warm up after a long day of hiking. The tranquillity of the mountains, the clear starry sky and the fresh mountain air make this place a special stage destination in the middle of unspoilt nature. Overnight at Hunwick ́s Camp.

You are already in the flow, that's why today after your breakfast it's going on to 2,880 m altitude. Overall, a rather moderate stage. You walk through a beautiful mountain forest, whose peaceful atmosphere invites you to breathe through. The path leads you up gently, accompanied by the chirping of rare bird species and the quiet noise of the nearby water. Soon you branch off and descend into the valley of the Dirigana river, which winds through the dense green. Time and again you pass small waterfalls that fall in cascades between the rocks. The path winds through the forest until you finally reach the Sasa River Camp at an altitude of 2,880 meters. Nestled in the lush vegetation of the mountain forest, this simple camp is surrounded by nature. Rest, enjoy the silence and let the day pass by the fire with a warm drink. Overnight at the Sasa River Camp.

After breakfast you start the descent today. You walk along the Sasa Trail, nestled in the dense forest and reach the top of the Dirigana Falls. After this impressive natural spectacle, continue your way to the Elgon Trekkers Base Camp in Bugiboni, from where you will return to your familiar accommodation, the Sipi Herritage Lodge. Overnight at Sipi Heritage Lodge. The next morning your adventure continues after breakfast towards the northeast. From the lush green highlands around the Sipi Falls, switch to the wide, drier plains of the Karamoja region. On the way, a safari highlight awaits you: a game ride through the largely untouched Pian Upe Wildlife Reserve. Look for zebras, giraffes, buffaloes, antelopes, leopards or even cheetahs. In the afternoon you will reach the small town of Moroto at the foot of the imposing Mount Moroto, the highest mountain range in the region. You will stay at Kara-Tunga Camp, a small, authentic accommodation with only a few rooms and a focus on local culture. The camp is run by the local Karamoja community and gives you a first insight into the self-confident life of the Karamojong. Accommodation at Kara-Tunga Camp.

Today you will continue your journey to the Kidepo Valley National Park, Uganda's most remote national park, where the Sudanese dry-savannah combines with the impressive mountain massifs of Uganda. On a rocky promontory lies the impressive Apoka Lodge, your home for the next two nights. After your arrival, you will have some time for yourself, and you will leave for your first game drive in the afternoon. The Kidepo Valley National Park, considered one of Uganda's wildest parks, is home to a variety of wildlife, including elephants, giraffes, buffaloes and lions. Let the experiences of the day pass in the tranquility of the Apoka Lodge. The following morning starts early with a safari at sunrise. After breakfast in the lodge, visit the Karamojong, a semi-nomadic herd in the dry northeastern region of Uganda. This visit gives you deep insights into a way of life that is firmly rooted in traditional values and livestock farming. Overnight at the Apoka Safari Lodge.

Today, after breakfast, head to Murchison Falls National Park – Uganda's largest and most scenic national park. Finally, you will reach your accommodation for the next two nights, the authentic, classic Bushcamp Papa, idyllically located on the south bank of the Nile, nestled in shadowy fig trees and named after Ernest "Papa" Hemingway. Strengthen yourself with your well-deserved dinner. The next morning can start – according to your wishes – already in the early morning hours. You have the opportunity to set off on a Sunrise safari - a magical experience if the wildlife are particularly active. Maybe you'll discover a resting lion in a gentle light or spot a leopard on his morning roam. Breakfast awaits you back at the camp before embarking on an impressive boat trip on the Nile to the vast base of the Murchison waterfalls. The closer you get to the cases, the more intense the roar of the water, which is squeezed through a narrow crevice and plunges 43 meters into the depth. In the evening you will return to Camp. Overnight at Papa's Camp.

After a hearty breakfast, your adventure in the tropical rainforest begins today – in the footsteps of chimpanzees. Together with experienced rangers, you roam the thicket, always looking for the calling groups of these fascinating primates. Patience is required, because chimpanzees are constantly on the move. Even away from the chimpanzees, the forest offers surprises: with a little luck you will encounter the lively Diadem sea cats, the elegant Kronenmeerkatzen or the eye-catching full-bearded sea cats. Afterwards take a course on the Ziwa rhinoceros reserve, here is the only way to see rhinos in Uganda in the wild. Then you can reach your accommodation – the charming Amuka Lodge, nestled in the forest area and the heart of the protected area. Whether a walk through the bush or a relaxing drink by the pool – Amuka Lodge is a place where time seems to stand still. Overnight at Amuka Lodge.

The day begins with a unique nature experience: Together with an experienced ranger, you will make your way to a footpath through the Ziwa rhinoceros reserve in the morning. Slowly and with vigilant eyes you approach the impressive rhinoceros – until you finally face these powerful animals at a respectful distance. Then continue your journey towards Kampala, the vibrant capital of Uganda. Here you can check in at the stylish boutique hotel Latitude 0°, your accommodation for tonight. Enjoy the panoramic views from the Rooftop Bar, relax in the elegant spa or take a dip in the pool. Design the afternoon to your liking. In the evening it is said: sit back, enjoy, track down. Close your eyes again in Uganda – a journey full of impressions, encounters and contrasts is beginning to end. Overnight at Latidude 0°.

After breakfast it's time to say goodbye. During the day, your guide will take you to the Entebbe airport. Here you will be passed- with a warm "Tambula bulungi", Good Journey. Full of unforgettable moments, unique encounters and impressive nature experiences, you will start your journey home. If you wish, we would be happy to plan a gorilla tracking for you and extend your trip for this intense nature experience. In addition, you can end your journey with a relaxing bathing extension to the Seychelles – surrounded by powder sugar white beaches, turquoise water and tropical flair – we will gladly put together a tailor-made connecting program for you. May the longing for distant places soon lead you back to new adventures – we look forward to seeing you again!

A perfect mixture of animal watching and stylish living in outstanding and elegant safari camps that leave nothing to be desired. Day 1 Nairobi - Masai Mara

Today your dreamlike Kenya and Tanzania journey begins. You will be picked up by an Africa Royal Tours representative at the airport or hotel in Nairobi in the morning and will fly by small plane to the Masai Mara Game Reserve. After a short flight, you will land near your camp, the Mara Bushtops Camp, where you will be immediately driven. Here you spend the next three nights. The camp, with a sensational location on the Mara River, is located in the middle of an 80-hectare private protected area of the Masai Mara. The river is the scene of the famous big hike, whose train is joined by thousands of gnus, zebras and gazelles every year. In addition to the Big Five (elephant, rhinoceros, buffalo, lion and leopard), you can watch many more wildlife. After check-in and lunch, you will be introduced to the comprehensive wildlife of the bush on your first game drive in the community Mara Bushtops vehicle. Overnight at Mara Bushtops Camp.

2.-3. Tag: Masai Mara

The next two days will be spent in the Masai Mara, which you can explore by shared car at the property. Lunch is served as a picnic. In the early morning and late afternoon, the best time to see the spectacular wildlife such as lions, hunting cheetahs, the gnu hike and elephant and buffalo herds. An antelope that keeps watch on an ant hill, zebras, impalas and waterbucks – you will be amazed at how many exciting and wonderful moments there are to watch. Enjoy the amenities at the camp in your free time. Overnight at Mara Bushtops Camp the day before. 

Day 4 - On to Tanzania

After breakfast it is time to say goodbye to the Masai Mara in anticipation of the Serengeti. In the morning you will be taken to the Kogatende airfield. After a short flight, a driver of Serengeti Bushtops Camp will be waiting for you on site to take you to your camp. Already this afternoon the first game drive awaits you. You will go with a lodge vehicle and other guests in search of the BIG 5! The Serengeti Bushtops Camp is a 5-star luxury oasis in one of the most impressive places on earth – the Serengeti. A real Tanzanian dream for the real safari seekers, because the new camp combines seductive luxury and comfort, with twelve spacious and exclusive tents. The large terrace with private hot tub offers an exceptional view of the sunrise. Enjoy the service of a 24-hour butler and have the possibility of private dinners and massage treatments on your terrace (for a fee).Your accommodation is located in the northwest of the Serengeti National Park in the middle of a UNESCO World Heritage Site approx. 25 km from the Mara River. Overnight at Serengeti Bushtops Camp.

5.-6. Tag Serengeti

After breakfast, departure to the park, with a picnic lunch in your luggage. With the communal Serengeti Bushtops vehicle you will explore the Serengeti together with your driver guide. With 14,763 sqm, the Serengeti National Park is the largest in Tanzania. Depending on the season, the Serengeti is the scene of the annual "Great Migration" of the animals from the Serengiti to the Masai Mara - animals as far as the eye can see! – simply unimaginable. Even outside this time, the landscape offers many wildlife. The "kopjes" (cops-shaped stone formations) and rainwater pools offer a favorable habitat for lions, leopards, cheetahs, but also hyenas, wild dogs and jackals. In addition, elephants, buffaloes and waterbucks love the hills as well as hippos, giraffes and crocodiles the rivers and wetlands. In the late afternoon return to your camp. Discover the peace and quiet of your camp in your leisure time. Overnight at the Serengeti Bushtops Camp the day before.

Day 7 Farewell from Tanzania

Today it is sad to say goodbye to seven wonderfully eventful days in Kenya and Tanzania. After breakfast you will be taken to the Kogatende airfield, from where you will fly to Arusha. From there continue flight to Kilimanjaro airport to fly to your connecting destination. If you would like to relax for a few days, we recommend a bath extension on the enchanting island of Zanzibar (price on request).

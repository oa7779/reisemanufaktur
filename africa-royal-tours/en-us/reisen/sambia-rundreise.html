Zambia — Thundering Waters

Explore Zambia on a tailor-made journey with your Travel Atelier Advisor at Africa Royal Tours. Request a no-obligation consultation.

9 days

Your trip inspiration

A sample itinerary — tailored to you

  • Africa Royal Tours
  • Zambia & Zimbabwe
  • Zimbabwe & Zambia Travel

On this trip you will enter the still unknown Zambia, with its scenic highlights. Experience the famous Victoria Falls as a highlight of the journey and then enjoy original nature. In the South Luangwa National Park and on the Sambesi River you will get to know the wildlife of Zambia. Let yourself be enchanted by Zambia and take home the intense feeling of life.

Trip information

Itinerary

9 days Livingstone – Victoria Falls – Lusaka – Mfuwe – South Luangwa National Park – Lusaca – Lower Sambesi National Park –Lusaka

Your personal highlights

  • Varied, untouched Zambia
  • Victoria Falls with their thundering water masses
  • Experiential wildlife observations in South Luangwa National Park
  • Meet the special at the Sambesi River and experience unforgettable moments
  • Daily activities put together directly from your accommodation
  • Comfortable and authentic accommodations
  • Enjoy the vastness and loneliness away from the tourist streams

After your arrival at Livingstone Airport, you will be welcomed by your Africa Royal Tours representative and taken to your accommodation where you will spend the next two days. Activities offered by the lodge include game drives in the Mosi-oa-Tunya National Park, a trip to a local village or a boat trip on the Sambesi at sunset. With a little luck, you can spot white rhinos, elephants, buffalos and giraffes and immerse yourself in the untouchedness of Zambia. For active lovers the possibility of a foot safari or a canoe tour is possible. The Sanctuary Sussi & Chuma is named after the faithful companion David Livingstones. The 12 luxurious and elegantly furnished houses, all built in the crowns of ebony trees, offer a unique view of the Sambesi. Overnight at Sanctuary Sussi & Chuma.

The visit to the majestic Victoria Falls should not be missing on your trip. The Victoria Falls are located on the border between Zambia and Zimbabwe and plunge into the gigantic canyon, 108 m deep, on a width of 1,700 m with a huge roar. You'll be thrilled! The resulting mist rises up to 300 meters high and can still be seen from a distance of 30 kilometers. This fog is the source and source of life for the rainforest at the foot of the falls. Overnight at Sanctuary Sussi & Chuma.

After breakfast you will be taken back to Livingstone Airport, from where you will fly with a short stopover in Lusaka to Mfuwe, in the South Luangwa National Park. There you are already awaited by a representative of the Tena Tena Camp, who will then chauffeur you to your nearest accommodation. The South Luangwa National Park is the most famous wildlife reserve in Zambia and covers just under 9,000 km² and is home to more than 230 different mammals and more than 400 bird species. The park is known for its numerous leopards and lions. The next three days you will have enough time to conquer this animal paradise. The program includes wildlife watching trips, 4x4 off-road vehicles, day and night, and safaris on foot. The experienced guides of the camp are passionate trackers who will read and show you all the signs and traces of the wild animals. The Tena Tena Camp enchants with its fantastic location at a bend of the river Luangwa. Tena Tena means "a temporary home" and this is exactly what the camp reflects. The five comfortable safari tents offer you a quiet and stylish retreat. You are located between a group of mahogany trees that give you pleasant shade in the midday sun. Enjoy the magnificent view of the river landscape from your terrace. Overnight at Tena Tena Camp.

From Mfuwe you fly today after breakfast to your next highlight, the Lower Zambezi National Park. Upon arrival, you will be welcomed by a representative of the Potato Bushcamp and then taken to your accommodation. The beautiful Lower Zambezi National Park was declared a National Park only in 1983 and is therefore one of the youngest and little tourist national parks in Zambia. With its 350 different bird species, the park is a true paradise for bird lovers. The large stock of wildlife such as elephants, buffalos, lions, hippos, antelopes, zebras and others makes a tour of the national park an experience. The next three days will give you plenty of time for the activities offered at the camp, such as canoeing and boating tours on the Sambesi, bush walks, and game drives, all accompanied by experienced guides of the camp. Your accommodation for the next three nights is the small and exclusive Potato Buschcamp. The four spacious and comfortable safari tents offer a family atmosphere and a particularly exclusive safari experience. Wooden bridges connect the individual tents and the main tent. Let yourself be impressed by the magnificent panoramic view of the Sambesi and let the soul dangle in your hammock in front of the tent. Your private Plungepool provides a pleasant cooling. Overnight at Potato Bushcamp.

Today it is sad to say goodbye to the beautiful Zambia. In the morning, until the departure to the airfield, there is still time for game drives. You fly a small plane to Lusaka, from where you start your journey home. We would be happy to advise you if you would like to complement your trip with other highlights in Africa such as Zimbabwe or Botswana. We are happy to create a relaxing bathing stay on the island of Mauritius or the Seychelles for you.

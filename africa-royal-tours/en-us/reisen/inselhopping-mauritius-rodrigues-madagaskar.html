1st day Arrival in Mauritius, the island of diversity

Upon arrival, you will be welcomed at the airport and taken to your accommodation on the southeastern coast of Mauritius. The perfect place to relax after the flight and arrive in peace on the island. Just off the coast is one of the most beautiful snorkeling areas in Mauritius. Crystal clear water, colorful corals and protected bays invite you to swim, snorkel and enjoy. A relaxed start to your journey – close to nature, relaxing and full of tropical island feeling. 

2nd Day Historic Coastal Town in Southeast Mauritius

The first days are all about arriving and enjoying. Let yourself be driven by the relaxed rhythm of the southeast coast, explore the dreamlike beaches around Le Chaland or immerse yourself in the fascinating underwater world right in front of your hotel. Just a few minutes away is the historic coastal town of Mahébourg, which is considered one of the most authentic places on the island. On a walk along the waterfront, through the small alleys or across the colourful market, you will experience the original Mauritius away from the big tourist resorts. Here fishing boats, Creole joie de vivre and the picturesque lagoon characterize the picture. A special highlight of your stay is the already included speedboat tour "One Love". Explore the historic islands of the Grand Port Bay, including Île de la Passe and Île au Phare, enjoy snorkelling stops in the crystal clear waters of the lagoon and visit the impressive Grand River South East waterfall. At the paradisiacal Île aux Cerfs you will then find a BBQ lunch with fresh giant shrimps, accompanied by traditional Sega music and Mauritian joie de vivre.

Day 3 Rodrigues - the original island paradise

With a 90-minute flight from Mauritius, you can reach Rodrigues, the original and quiet "little sister" of Mauritius. On arrival you can feel the relaxed rhythm of the island. Softly curved green hills, characteristic Vakoa trees and fantastic views of the turquoise lagoon characterize the landscape and give Rodrigues their very special charm. Arriving at the stylish Tekoma Rodrigues, enjoy the first impressions of this authentic island pearl and immerse yourself in a world full of serenity, nature and original joie de vivre.

4-6. Day Rodrigues – time for island enjoyment

The next few days will be at your disposal to discover Rodrigues at your own pace. Walk along natural beaches, enjoy spectacular views of the turquoise lagoon or explore the island's varied coastal landscape. The crystal clear waters invite you to swim and snorkel, while small fishing villages offer an authentic insight into the island life characterized by the sea. A special highlight is the full-day island tour with private driver. You can visit the François Leguat Reserve, one of the island's most important nature reserves, where giant turtles and the original flora of Rodrigues can be admired. A joint lunch completes this eventful day. On numerous coastal and panoramic paths, the original beauty of Rodrigues is revealed, as well as the fascinating coral and limestone formations that have always shaped the landscape of the island. Far from mass tourism, you will experience an island full of serenity, warm hospitality and genuine charm – a place where nature and tradition merge in a special way.

Day 7 Back to Mauritius – Luxury and Enjoyment

After the relaxing days on Rodrigues, return to Mauritius and move your room into one of the island's most prestigious luxury hotels. Nestled between tropical gardens, a nature reserve and a fantastic lagoon, an oasis of peace and elegance awaits you. You'll stay at exclusive Constance Prince Maurice.

8.-10. Day Mauritius - Magical Morning Hours on the Indian Ocean

Design the coming days according to your wishes: relax on the beach or by the pool, enjoy the special atmosphere of the lagoon and let yourself be pampered in the excellent restaurants. A special highlight is the famous floating pontoon restaurant, which offers culinary experiences in unique surroundings. Golf lovers benefit from privileged games on two of Mauritius' most beautiful championship golf courses. During your stay, you will also find two varied private excursions that will bring you closer to the different facets of the island. The excursion "Kreola's Punch" takes you to some of the cultural and scenic highlights of Mauritius. You will get exciting insights into the Creole way of life, visit selected sights of the island and enjoy a typical lunch. With "Contrastes & Nuances", you will discover the fascinating contrasts of the island: from lush green landscapes and impressive viewpoints to charming places and lively markets. The Constance Prince Maurice offers the perfect setting for enjoyable days between relaxation, discoveries and a Mauritian way of life before the next highlight of your journey awaits you.

11th day Island world full of adventure and natural wonders

Your trip will take you either via Antananarivo or the French island of La Réunion to Nosy Be – the tropical pearl off the northwest coast of Madagascar. From there, a boat ride over the glittering sea takes you to the dreamlike private island of Tsarabanjina. A true paradise awaits you far from the usual routes: untouched nature, powder-white sandy beaches and a fascinating underwater world. Snorkel over brilliant coral reefs, discover hidden islands and let yourself be enchanted by the serenity and beauty of this unique tropical oasis. Here peace, nature and exclusivity merge into an unforgettable vacation experience. 

12-14. Tag Tsarabanjina – Robinson feeling in the island paradise

The next few days are all about relaxation, enjoyment and tropical island magic. Tsarabanjina is a place where time seems to pass more slowly. 
Take relaxed beach walks along fine sandy coves, explore the small paths through the tropical island forest or simply enjoy the peace under palm trees overlooking the turquoise sea. The crystal clear waters invite you to snorkel, dive, kayaking and stand-up paddling. During boat trips to the neighboring islands, you will discover hidden beaches, secluded coves and the fascinating island world of the Mitsio archipelago. If you like it even more relaxed, enjoy a soothing massage, a sundowner on the beach or let the day end with an atmospheric dinner under the starry sky. Tsarabanjina is the perfect place to fully enjoy the last days of vacation in the midst of nature, exclusivity and tropical serenity. 

Day 15 Veloma – Madagascar!

Enjoy your breakfast today with the fantastic view of the Indian Ocean and take a look at the unforgettable moments of your days on Tsarabanjina. After these special island experiences, it's time to say goodbye to this little paradise. Depending on your wishes, your transfer takes place by boat or helicopter flight back to Nosy Be, from where you start your journey home or onward. As the island slowly disappears behind you, memories of turquoise blue water, white sandy beaches and the relaxed atmosphere of Madagascar remain for a long time. With a heart full of special impressions you say goodbye to Tsarabanjina – Veloma and goodbye!

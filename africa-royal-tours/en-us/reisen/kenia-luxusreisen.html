1st day of Nairobi

"Karibu sana: Welcome! Upon your arrival in Nairobi, a representative of Africa Royal Tours will be waiting for you to take you to your first accommodation. The day is at your disposal: explore Nairobi on your own or relax after your arrival. On request we will gladly organize a program for you. Nairobi, Kenya's vibrant capital and one of Africa's largest cities, combines modern urbanity with fascinating nature and an eventful history. Recommended attractions include Nairobi National Park, the Sheldrick Wildlife Trust, the Giraffe Center or the Karen Blixen Museum. Your hotel for today, the Boutique Hotel Hemingways Nairobi, awaits you in an elegant property. Treat yourself to the Michelin-starred restaurant, brasserie, high tea or bar. The day ends with a dinner. Overnight at Hemingways Nairobi.

2-4. Day Safari Adventures at Amboseli National Park

After breakfast at Hemingways Nairobi, your trip to the Amboseli National Park begins. The charming and eco-friendly Tawi Lodge, a boutique lodge will be your accommodation for the next three nights. The lodge consists of twelve cottages with organic Makuti roofs. From your veranda you can enjoy a spectacular view of the Kilimanjaro in clear weather. After lunch, start your first afternoon safari in the Amboseli National Park, known for elephant herds, buffaloes, zebras and the impressive mountain scenery. Back at the lodge, relax before the day ends with a dinner. The next few days will start with morning game drives, where you will see elephant herds and a species-rich bird world – an experience that will let you experience Africa up close. A visit to Observation Hill offers spectacular views of the Amboseli ecosystem. In the evening, end the day at the campfire. Overnight at Tawi Lodge. 

5.-6. Day Discover the Magic of Masai Mara

After your breakfast, a guide will take you to the runway in Amboseli, from where you can fly to the Masai Mara. Upon arrival, the driver of the camp welcomes you and takes you directly to your first safari. Around noon you can reach the exclusive Governors IL Moran Camp, where a delicious lunch awaits you. After an exciting safari in the afternoon, let the day end relaxed at dinner. The next morning you will start an exciting game drive through the vast plains, forests and riverscapes of the Masai Mara. With a little luck you will discover lions, leopards, elephants, buffaloes and rhinos as well as cheetahs, giraffes and zebras. Especially during the Great Migration, when millions of Gnus and Zebras cross the Serengeti, the Masai Mara unfolds all its magic. The Governors IL Moran Camp, with its ten luxurious tents on the Mara River, offers a peaceful atmosphere in the middle of a river forest. Enjoy the view of hippos and crocodiles or relax with private massage treatments. Overnight at Governors IL Moran Camp.

7.-8. Day Masai Mara – Panoramic views from Oloololo hill

After breakfast, you will set off with your guide to the luxurious Angama Mara – an exceptional lodge high up on the Oloololo hills. After your arrival you can enjoy a delicious lunch with a panoramic view before you leave for an exciting game drive. The Angama Mara is known for its exclusive location, spacious glass front suites and personal service at the highest level. Inspired by the legendary film scene from beyond Africa, the lodge offers an elegant, modern design. The Masai Mara Reserve is one of the most impressive wildlife reserves in the world. Here you can experience the great migration, observe huge herds of gnus and zebras and discover one of Africa's highest predatory densities. Both today and the following day, you can enjoy the unspoilt wilderness during morning and evening game drives. Overnight at Angama Mara.

9th day Farewell from Kenya

Today we will take you to the nearby runway for your return flight to Nairobi after breakfast. There, our driver will take you to Karen Blixen Coffee Garden for lunch. Then we continue to the Karen Blixen Museum. Located in a quiet suburb of Nairobi, close to the city center, it offers a comprehensive insight into the person of Karen Blixen against the backdrop of your beloved Ngong Hills. It belongs to a different era in Kenya's history and gained international recognition through the film "Beyond Africa". After these exciting impressions, it's time to say goodbye. We will drive you to Jomo Kenyatta Airport, where you will travel home or to your next destination. If you want to complete your stay with a bath extension, for example on the dreamlike spice island Zanzibar, we will be happy to advise you on the possibilities.

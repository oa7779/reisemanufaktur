During this journey you can look forward to varied landscapes, fascinating wildlife and paradisiacal sandy beaches. Immerse yourself in the world of the "King of Lions." Day 1 Karibu Africa - Welcome to Tanzania

Karibu! Welcome to Tanzania. After your arrival at Kilimanjaro Airport, you will be welcomed by your private, German speaking driver guide and you will start directly to the first highlight of your trip, the Tarangire National Park. Already on the way to your accommodation you can take a look at the breathtaking landscape. The Tarangire National Park is located between Dodoma and Arusha. 120 km from Tanzania's capital. The natural environment and the wildlife of this region will delight you. Depending on the time of arrival, the afternoon starts with a hunt with your driver guide.

2nd day Tarangire National Park

You've been waiting a long time for this, now you're finally going to go: your safari time in the midst of lions, elephants and co. During your game drives in your own vehicle with your guide in the morning and in the afternoon you will have the opportunity to explore the various vegetation zones of the Tarangire National Park. Huge elephant herds are found at the river to drink, while wandering gnu, zebra, buffalo, gazelle, cow and elenantilope flocks are pushing towards the dwindling water holes. Surrounded by gigantic acacia trees, the Sanctuary Swala Camp with its twelve luxurious pavilions is located in the animal-rich Tarangire National Park. In the shade of the acacia trees, you can relax wonderfully - watch what is going on at the water hole, take a shower under the open sky or relax in peace in the infinity pool. Safari in Style is the motto here!

3rd Day Animal Diversity at Lake Manyara National Park

After a last morning game drive in Tarangire National Park, we continue towards Lake Manyara National Park. He can't keep up with his neighbours in terms of area, but he takes it up with them in terms of botanical diversity and shines through the most diverse habitats and animal wealth in the smallest space. Lake Alkali and the surrounding floodplains as well as acacia forests are home to many wild animals such as hippos, impalas, elephants, gnus, buffaloes, zebras, wart pigs and giraffes. With some luck you can also discover the tree-climbing lions. In the afternoon you will reach your wonderfully located accommodation for the next two nights in the Ngorongoro/Karatu region. Neptune Ngorongoro Luxury Lodge is located in the middle of Tanzanian jungle and surrounded by the volcanic landscape of the Ngorongore area. Each of the 20 luxurious and with approx. 130 sqm very generously designed suites offers a spacious viewing terrace, from which you have a breathtaking panoramic view of the wonderful nature. After a long safari day, you can find the perfect relaxation for body and mind at Earth & Rain Spa and your soul breathes up.

Day 4 Ngorongoro Crater

With your Driverguide, you will leave this early morning for the world-famous Ngorongoro crater, also known as "Africa's Garden of Eden." It is hard to believe that the volcano, which has now been extinguished and collapsed, was still active many millions of years ago. So many wild animals roam here! Let yourself be impressed by the large zebra and gnu herds, which graze peacefully inside, as well as by the lions lying in the sun and the black rhinoceros threatened with extinction.

5th Day World Famous Serengeti

Your day starts today with an extensive breakfast. Then your journey continues to the Serengeti National Park. Keep your eyes and ears open, because here in Tanzania there are always exciting scenery on the go. Together with your private driver guide, for the first time, look out for the large herds of gnu that travel through this area every year in search of water (detailed information on request), but also for lions, elephants and zebras. After an eventful day at your luxury lodge, the Serengeti Pioneer Camp. The small and fine camp (a total of 12 safari tents) is located in the central south of the Serengeti and offers a unique view of the Moru Kopjes and the wide savannah landscape. Your luxurious tent accommodation is furnished in the style of the 30s. Feel placed in the time of conquerors and explorers when you stay at Serengeti Pioneer Camp. Want to have a dinner under the sparkling starry sky, listening to the noises of the bush? A unique experience!

6.-7. Day on the way in the kingdom of wild animals

These two days are dedicated to the exciting animal observations together with your driver guide. Tanzania's oldest and most popular national park, the Serengeti, is famous for its annual hiking season. At this time six million hooves roam over the open plains and more than 200,000 zebras and 300,000 Thomsonga cells join the train of the gnus after fresh pastures. But even outside the hiking season, the Serengeti undoubtedly offers the most impressive animal show in Africa: large buffalo herds, smaller groups of elephants and giraffes, as well as thousands and thousands of Elenantilopes, topaz, cow antelopes, Impalas and Grant Gazelles. Let this unique nature and the variety of animals affect you during your game drives.

8th day flight to Zanzibar

Today you say goodbye to your driver guide, because a small propeller plane will take you to Zanzibar (intermediate stops possible). Look forward to the next days beach vacation on the enchanting Spice Island Zanzibar! The island will fascinate you! At the airport in Zanzibar City you will be welcomed and taken to your hotel. The rest of the day is at your disposal. Enjoy the beach paradise Zanzibar.

9.-13. Day of pure relaxation in Zanzibar

Enjoy the relaxed beach atmosphere and the island life of Zanzibar in the next few days. Do what you like. Zuri Zansibar offers relaxation between extensive tropical gardens and the ocean. Modern and local elements reflect the unique atmosphere of the island. Cool down, will you? The hotel's beach is located directly on a large lagoon and offers optimal conditions for swimming, regardless of tides.

14th day Departure from Tanzania

Their time-out from everyday life is coming to an end. Even if your vacation is soon over – the precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories of your trip will accompany you forever. Look forward to sharing your experiences in the distance with your friends and family at home. Depending on the departure you will be taken to the airport of Zanzibar and say goodbye to the beach paradise in the direction of home.

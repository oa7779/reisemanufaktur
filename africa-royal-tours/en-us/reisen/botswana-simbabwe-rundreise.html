Botswana & Zimbabwe Travel - Wild Africa

Botswana and Zimbabwe Round trip through Wild Africa with the trip Experts Africa Royal Tours. Individual and tailor-made. Now request.

This varied journey through Zimbabwe and the still original Botswana awaits you with exciting animal observations, exceptional and luxurious accommodation as well as a spectacular natural landscape. Marvel at the thunderous Victoria Falls, explore the elephant-rich Chobe National Park on a river cruise and discover the fascinating historical Tsodilo Hills, which are UNESCO World Heritage Sites. At Okavango Panhandle you will experience the water-rich lifeline of the Delta before exploring both the northern and southern parts of the unique Okavango Delta with its diverse landscapes and impressive wildlife. A journey full of unforgettable nature experiences and exclusive safari moments awaits you.

1st day Arrival in Victoria Falls

After your arrival at the airport in Victoria Falls you will be picked up by a representative of Africa Royal Tours and taken to your booked hotel. You will stay at the legendary Victoria Falls Hotel. The luxurious hotel, which opened in 1904, is located directly in Victoria Falls National Park and offers private access to the waterfalls. Enjoy on the hotel terrace one of the breathtaking sunsets and the fantastic view of the bridge of the Victoria Falls. Overnight at Victoria Falls Hotel.

2nd Day Legendary Victoria Falls

After breakfast you will be picked up at the hotel and start your guided tour to the famous Victoria Falls. The waterfalls are the largest in Africa and will impress you with their roaring waters. The Victoria Falls were discovered by David Livingstone, a Scottish missionary who had heard of the cases and finally set out to conquer them in 1855. Deeply impressed by the beauty, he described the waterfall as "the most beautiful thing he ever saw in Africa," and named it "Victoria Falls" in honor of Queen Victoria. In the late afternoon you will be picked up for a cruise on the Sambesi and shiver on the small and fine boat Ra-Icane towards the sunset. Overnight at Victoria Falls Hotel.

3.-4. Day River Cruise on the Chobe Princess

Today you travel from Zimbabwe across the border to Kasane in Botswana (journey time approx. 1.5 to 2 hours plus VAT). (e.g. border formalities). After a short transfer to the investor and entry to Namibia, you will board the Chobe Princess, your floating home for the next two nights. The Chobe Princess is on its way along the Chobe River, which separates the Chobe National Park in Botswana from the Zambian region of Namibia. The Chobe Riverfront is one of the most animal-rich regions in Africa and is especially known for its large elephant herds. With various boat activities along the shores, you will experience the fascinating wilderness at close range and have the opportunity to observe elephants, buffaloes, hippos, crocodiles, numerous antelope species and an impressive bird world. Enjoy the unique perspective of a safari from the water and let the passing landscape feel relaxed from your private cabin or one of the observation decks. The small and elegant Chobe Princess combines personal service, relaxed ambience and exceptional wildlife observations to create a very special safari experience. Nights aboard the Chobe Princess.

5.-7. Tag Okavango Panhandle and Tsodilo Hills

After a last morning cruise on the Chobe River, return to Kasane. From there, a small plane will take you to the remote Okavango Panhandle, the water-rich lifeline of the Okavangodeltas. This unique region captivates with extensive papyrus landscapes and quiet waterways. A special highlight is the excursion to the famous Tsodilo Hills, which belong to the UNESCO World Heritage. The distinctive rock formations rise from the Kalahari plain and host thousands of prehistoric rock paintings that testify to the long cultural history of southern Africa. On a guided hike you will discover this fascinating landscape and learn more about the spiritual significance of the hills for the San. If you are looking for something special, you can spend a night in a comfortable sleep-out camp. Far away you will experience the silence of the African wilderness and an impressive starry sky that makes this extraordinary natural experience unforgettable. Overnight at Okavango Panhandle. 

8.-10. Day in the heart of the northern Okavango Delta

Today you fly by small plane to the northern part of the Okavangodeltas. After landing, you will be taken to your camp at Shinde Concession, a region where water landscapes, open plains and island worlds alternate in a particularly attractive way. The coming three nights offer enough time to immerse yourself in the rhythm of the delta and not only to see its different facets, but to experience them truly. Sometimes you glide silently by boat through the waterways, sometimes you explore the surroundings by wildlife viewing. This variety makes the stay so special, because no day resembles the other. You may observe an elephant herd on the shore, follow fresh tracks in the sand or experience the moment when the landscape changes in the warm light of the late afternoon and the bush life slowly becomes more active. In addition to animal observations, it is often the small moments that remain in memory: the deep grunting of hippos at night, the conversation with your guide or the silence of an early morning on the water. The personal atmosphere of the camp and the diverse possibilities to get to know the delta from different perspectives give this stay a special depth. Overnight at Shinde Footsteps.

11.-13. Day in the Natural Paradise of the Southern Okavango Delta

Your bush flyer will take you deeper into the Okavango Delta. At the runway you are expected and brought to your accommodation for the following three nights. The 16,000 km² Okavango Delta is one of the world's largest inland waters of its kind and a true oasis of life amidst the dry Kalahari desert. The delta consists of countless rivers, tributaries, lagoons, floodplains and islands, which are constantly changing as a result of the annual floods. In the southern Okavango Delta you will find a fascinating mosaic of glittering waterways, palm-lined islands, seasonal floodplains and open plains. In this region, Kanana Nyana has recently opened a small, personal camp. It blends harmoniously into the surrounding area and offers a stylish retreat between safari activities. The limited number of guests ensures a particularly relaxed atmosphere and creates space to review the experiences of the day in peace and quiet. Take advantage of the next few days to explore this unique natural paradise by a trip with the mokoro (a traditional, local built-in boat), by foot safari or by boat. Overnight at Kanana Nyana.

14th day Departure or Extension Program

Even the most beautiful vacation is finished once. One last flight in the small machine will take you today to Maun, from where you will start your journey home. If you don't have enough of Africa yet, we will be happy to assist you with an extension program such as "Mother City" Cape Town or a bathing extension in Mozambique, on the island of Nosy Be in Madagascar, on Mauritius or the Seychelles.

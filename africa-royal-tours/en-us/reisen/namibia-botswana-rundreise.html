Namibia & Botswana — Lands of Wonder

Namibia and Botswana Round Trip: Experience an exclusive trip with the experts from Africa Royal Tours. Let's have personal advice now.

16 days

Your trip inspiration

A sample itinerary — tailored to you

  • Africa Royal Tours

On your trip in Namibia, a barren, rough country awaits you – like from another world. Grandiose landscapes, the magical desert and the endless expanse will captivate you. The wildlife reserves in the north with their diversity promise exciting game drives. Botswana guarantees you extra-class wildlife and safaris. Look forward to a journey through these diverse countries and enjoy the unique impressions that will remain in your memory for a long time.

Trip information

Itinerary

16 days Windhoek – Ongava Wild Reserve – Etosha NP – Onguma Wild Reserve –Divundu–Konola – Houseboat Tour: Chobe Princess – Victoria Falls – Okavangodelta – Maun

Your personal highlights

  • On safari in the Etosha National Park
  • Wildlife observations in the private Ongava Wild Reserve
  • Beautiful landscape and nature around the Kwando River
  • Houseboat tour with the Chobe Princess along the river Chobe
  • The famous Victoria Falls
  • Small, exclusive tent camps with African flair
  • Experience the unique animal paradises of Botswana

After your arrival at Windhoek International Airport, your local German-speaking driver guide will welcome you. As soon as the luggage is stowed away, the journey takes you through the farm landscape to the capital of Namibia – Windhoek, where you check in to your hotel and start a city tour (depending on the arrival time of your international flight). You will stay at the Hotel Heinitzburg, which was built in 1914 by the Count of Schwerin for his fiancée Margarethe von Heinitz. The romantic castle hotel with a dream view over Windhoek, in which the European charm of the 19th century combines with modern comfort, has a total of 16 spacious rooms. From the terrace with sun deck you have an impressive view of the city. Overnight in Windhoek.

On your way to the north you cross the small towns of Okahandja and Otjiwarongo, which are characterized by lively activity. In the early afternoon you will reach your lodge at the gates of the Etosha National Park. You should keep your camera at hand, because in the afternoon you will take the first game drive to the park for wildlife watching in your private vehicle. Your accommodation for today is the Ongava Lodge, which is located in the private Ongava Wild Reserve in the immediate vicinity of the Etosha National Park. The comfortably equipped, reetdecked chalets offer optimal opportunities to relax in a natural atmosphere. Directly from the terrace, the animals can be observed wonderfully at the water place. Overnight at the Ongava Wild Reserve.

Along the gigantic salt pan you drive across the Etosha National Park from water to water to the eastern border of the park to discover as many animals as possible. The word "Etosha" means "great white place". Today's pan is a remnant of a once large, flat lake, which has now dried up. In the morning of the fourth day, explore the Etoshapark with an open off-road vehicle of the lodge (together with other guests). If you have not yet seen enough animals, there is the possibility of another game drive in the afternoon together with your guide in a private car. With sunset you leave the park and reach your accommodation for the next two nights, Onguma The Fort. Onguma The Fort is located on the east side of the Etosha National Park in the Onguma Game Reserve. The fort will surprise you in many ways. Classic African design awaits you in combination with a touch of Moroccan and Indian influences. From your terrace you have an enchanting view of the water hole. And don't miss taking a shower in the open air. Nights spent in the Onguma Wild Reserve.

A contrast program awaits you today. Early in the morning you leave the dry and barren Etosha region. From Rundu, the Okavango River runs from Angola to the River Dance Lodge, where you arrive in the late afternoon. After the long drive, the open terrace overlooking the river invites you to a relaxed Sundowner drink. Overnight at Divundu.

You cross the Okavango, which now flows further into the delta, and cross the Bwabwata National Park. After arriving at Nambwa Tented Lodge, check in for the next two nights. In the afternoon, the first wildlife viewing boat ride takes place on the Kwando River. On the second day you will explore the unspoiled nature again by boat and on an open safari ride together with other guests. The region around the Kwando River is known for its large elephant herds, with up to two hundred animals. The landscape around you is simply magnificent: kilometers wide Ried and Papyrus areas, secluded bays and lagoons with flowering water lilies. Let yourself be enchanted by the more than 400 bird species and the elephants as well as hippos, which you can get very close to up to a few meters. Overnight stays in the region of Congola.

You leave early, reach the border to Botswana, enter there and drive to the airport of Kasane. Your driver guide will say goodbye to you here. A Chobe Princess employee will take you from the airport to the immigration office. Then you continue by boat and after your short trip to Botswana you will return to Namibia. Upon arrival at Chobe Princess, your captain will instruct you. Let's go linen – your river cruise along the river Chobe begins. Be on the lookout, because there are good opportunities for wildlife watching. The first day in the African wilderness on board the houseboat will end with a sundowner drink. On the second day, breakfast is available early in the morning. Thereafter, activities such as fishing or animal watching from a small dinghy are offered. For your lunch, return to the houseboat. After the afternoon coffee you can take part in a boat tour with a unique sunset, or relax on board the luxurious houseboat. Directly on board, you can observe wildlife in their natural environment. Nights spent on Chobe Princess.

As you enjoy your breakfast, the landscape will pass you and you will take a last look at the fabulous wildlife. In the dinghy you will return to the immigration authority of Namibia and Botswana. Your Africa Royal Tours representative will be waiting for you in Kasane and will accompany you across the border to Zimbabwe. After checking in at the Victoria Falls Hotel, in the early afternoon you will go to the majestic Victoria Falls (together with other guests). The magnificent Victoria Falls are located on the border between Zimbabwe and Zambia. The 2 km wide and over 110 meters high Victoria Falls belong to the world's highest waterfalls. Let yourself be enchanted by this unique place. Rain caps are available at the entrance. Your accommodation for the next nights will be the famous Victoria Falls Hotel. This 5-star colonial-style hotel was built in 1904 and is considered the best place to stay in Zimbabwe. Enjoy the sunset on the terrace from where you have a magnificent view of the Victoria Falls Bridge and the spray rising from the falls. Overnight stays in Victoria Falls.

Your Africa Royal Tours representative will take you back across the border to Kasane in Botswana today. Here you will already find a small plane that will take you off towards Okavangodelta. From the bird's perspective you get an impression of the vastness of the delta with its many water arms. During the next two days, together with other guests of the lodge, you can go for a round trip in an open off-road vehicle, boat tours, hikes and mokoro excursions. Your accommodation for this time is Shinde Camp, nestled in the shadows of ancient trees, figs and acacias on the edge of the permanent flood plains of the Okavango Delta. Shinde is a magical refuge in the private Shinde concession blessed with animal wealth. The camp is surrounded by clear watercourses, with a variety of birds and wildlife. Leopards and lions are regularly spotted in this region. Please note: All activities are subject to seasonal changes and weather conditions and therefore depend on the travel time. Foot safaris and mokoro activities can be compromised especially during the rainy season due to dense vegetation and limited visibility. Overnight at Shinde Camp.

A short flight in a small propeller machine will take you to Kanana Camp in the southwestern part of the Okavango Delta. Here you will spend the next two nights in the midst of grassy islands formed by fertile termite hills. The Kanana area includes a chain of islands in the river Xudum, north-east of the Pom Pom lagoon, and with its watercourses winding through the open lagoons, it is a fantastic place where time seems to stand still. The water level varies throughout the year and with it the activities. But the camp managers know how to create optimally filled safari days for you with a diverse activity program. You'll find game drives, mokoro and boat trips and bush walks. Don't miss it as much as possible, because it is a unique experience to explore the wildlife from a completely different perspective. The Kanana Camp is one of the few in the Delta that has access to a private breeding colony where many migratory birds nest from the month of July. Among the bird species that you can see there are Marabu, Yellow Saddle and Clap Beak Storks. The sight of thousands of nesting birds is absolutely fascinating and an impression that you will not forget. Overnight at Kanana Camp.

In the morning you will be taken to the runway from where your bus pilot will take you to Maun. This is where your dream of Africa ends and you step on or off. Go home. Have a good flight!

Uganda and Tanzania Trip - In the Heart of East Africa

Uganda and Tanzania Round trip: With your expert Africa Royal Tours you will experience your tailor-made trip in the heart of East Africa. Now let's get some advice.

A dream of Africa – discover the natural beauties of Uganda and Tanzania on this trip, right in the heart of East Africa. In the rainforests of Uganda, explore the last wild mountain gorillas and let yourself be impressed by the diverse wildlife in the Serengeti and Ngorongoro craters on a safari. The incredible expanse and loneliness as well as the warm inhabitants of both countries will impress you. In addition to unique animal observations, during a stay at Zanzibar you will have enough time to swim, relax and get in touch with the country and people. You travel stylishly, at your own pace and log in selected accommodations.

Day 1 Arrival in Entebbe

Welcome to Uganda! At Entebbe Airport you are already awaited at the airport and warmly welcomed. Your driver will accompany you to Karibu Guesthouse. The very lovingly furnished Karibu Guesthouse in Entebbe is an ideal place to arrive after a long flight in peace in Uganda. Hospitality is important here, so that you feel completely comfortable from the first second. Overnight at Karibu Guesthouse.

Day 2 Kibale Forest National Park

After a short drive to the Kajjansi airfield, fly today to the Kibale Forest National Park. The park is home to more than 13 different species of monkeys, including more than 1,400 chimpanzees and other primates. Upon arrival, you can recover something from the flight and then start to an unforgettable crater lake hike. You pass small villages and walk around the lake in a breathtaking landscape. With fantastic views of the rainforest, the Rwenzori Mountains and Queen Elizabeth National Park, Isunga Lodge welcomes you. Relax in one of the only seven cottages of the lodge and enjoy the breathtaking view that offers you the elevated location. Overnight at Isunga Lodge.

3rd day Chimpanzee hike & Bigodi moor hike

Put on firm shoes, because today it goes over stick and stone into the depths of the African rainforest. With a ranger you will discover the incredible plant and animal diversity of Uganda's green lungs on your two to three hour hike. Immerse yourself in the world of colobus monkeys, redtail sea cats and chimpanzees, who jump high up in the treetops and play together. Can you hear the calls of the chimpanzee's family elder? Not long before you will experience the impressive apes of people very close - a unique adventure. In the afternoon you can take a hike to the Bigodi swamps. A hike through the Bigodi swamps south of the Kibale Forest is an absolute must for hiking enthusiasts like you. Keep your eyes open for kingfishers, giant Turakos and other bird species while you walk along wooden paths through the diverse vegetation. Also different species of monkeys and other animals will certainly cross your path. Your ranger will gladly explain to you the local wildlife and the varied nature. Overnight stay as on the previous day in the Isunga Lodge.

Day 4: Queen Elizabeth National Park

Today, your trip takes you from Isunga Lodge to Queen Elizabeth National Park, one of Uganda's most beautiful natural landscapes. The first stop is at Katwe Salzsee, where you will learn more about Uganda's oldest industry – salt extraction. Even today, the lake's water is channeled into large reservoirs where the water can evaporate and salt is extracted from it. On your trip to your accommodation for the next two nights you will explore the species-rich bird sanctuary, where you can observe among others Wiesenraller, Zwergflamigos, Kampfadler and saddle storks. In the afternoon you will embark on a boat tour on the Kanzinga Canal. Here you have the opportunity to get closer to the water-loving animals such as hippos, buffaloes, crocodiles, pelicans and other animals of the bird world. Marvel at the local fishermen who paddle out on their small wooden boats on Lake Edward and do their daily work. In the evening you will come back to the Marafiki Lodge. The Marafiki Safari Lodge offers you unique luxury safari tents and wooden huts with open-air showers. Look forward to a dinner under the stars of Uganda! Overnight at Marafiki Lodge.

Day 5 Ishasha Sector

The southern part of Queen Elizabeth National Park is famous for its lions, because they live on the trees. Marvel at the unusual peculiarity of these animals in this incredibly beautiful environment. On the way from the north to the southern part of the park, step by step you enter another world. The acacia trees stand free in the savannah and together form a very interesting and distinctive landscape. During the game drive you can get a picture of the beauty of the Ishasha sector. Tonight you will spend at Enjojo Lodge. It is located in a 40-hectare area bordering the Ishasha sector of Queen Elizabeth Park and situated in the middle of an acacia forest. From the straw-covered restaurant and bar you can enjoy homemade food and scenic beauty as well as the view of a small natural lake. Overnight at Enjojo Lodge.

Day 6 Bwindi Impenetrable National Park

In the morning you will receive a transfer to the airfield of Kasese where it continues by flight. On site, your tour guide awaits you, which will take you to your exceptional accommodation, the Engagi Lodge. On the way you can enjoy the beauty of the Bwindi Impenetrable National Park. The 300 km² national park, located in the southwest of Uganda, has been protected by Uganda's forest and wildlife authorities since 1961 and is home to almost half of all wild mountain gorillas worldwide. Officially, the park was declared a national park and UNESCO World Heritage Site only in 1994, thanks in particular to the zoologist and behavioral researcher Dian Fossey, who has dealt with the life and behavior of the mountain gorillas. The Bwindi Impenetrable National Park is very old and originally, difficult to penetrate, which also gave it the name "impenetrable" = impenetrable. The Engagi Lodge is located just outside the National Park and offers a fantastic view of the surrounding forest. From your own veranda you can watch the colorful bird world and review the experiences of the day. Overnight at Engagi Lodge.

Day 7 Bwindi Impenetrable National Park: Gorilla Trekking

Today it is time to embark on a unique tour at sunrise. After a short briefing by the park ranger, the gorilla tracking starts. They are looking for the last mountain gorillas of Uganda. The tour can take between two and six hours and depends on where the Gorilla family has migrated from their last camp. Most of the time, the rangers can track down the gorillas due to their many years of experience and expertise – but there is no guarantee. If you discover the gorillas, you can watch the impressive monkeys and let yourself be inspired by their beauty and stately size. Experience some very special moments near the animals, to which you will remember for a long time. The observation time is limited to one hour in order not to expose the animals to possible stress factors and protect them for too long. After this unique experience, head back to the camp, the rest of the day is available for free design. Overnight stay as on the previous day at Engagi Lodge.

8th day of Arusha

Today it is said to say goodbye from Uganda in eager anticipation of Tanzania. After breakfast, head to Kigali, where you can fly to Kilimanjaro Airport in Tanzania. If time permits, your guide will take you on a guided tour in the capital of Rwanda. In Tanzania, your Africa Royal Tours representative awaits you and will take you to your accommodation. On your way to the Arusha Coffee Lodge you will get a first impression of Tanzania's varied and colourful landscape. Especially the northern part of the country hosts the most famous and most beautiful national parks and animal protection areas, which offer impressive nature and wildlife experiences. At the edge of the city of Arusha, the lodge is surrounded by one of the largest coffee plantations in the country overlooking Mount Meru. The rooms of the Arusha Coffee Lodge are distributed within the Burka coffee plantation and feature a cozy open wood stove and comfortable and homely amenities. An ideal start for some exciting safari days in Tanzania. Overnight at the Arusha Coffee Lodge.

Day 9 Ngorongoro Crater

After breakfast check-out and transfer to the airport, followed by a flight to Manyara, where a driver of the &Beyond Ngorongoro Crater Lodge awaits you and will drive you to your accommodation. The Ngorongoro crater is known for its large number of wildlife and the population of mammals is estimated at over 25,000 and is therefore also known as the Garden of Eden. The Ngorongoro Protected Area is located within the Olduvai crater and an impressive highlands plain, which covers about 8,300 km². On the game drives offered by the lodge's safari vehicles, you can be inspired by the wildlife life in the crater. In the evening, you will then receive your accommodation with its amenities. The design of the &Beyond Ngorongoro Crater Lodge skilfully combines the traditional construction of the Massai with the amenities of a Baroque castle; the location on the edge of the crater with dream views is just the icing. Featuring a private veranda with views of the crater, noble Victorian room amenities with African design elements, ensuite bathroom with spacious showers, the accommodation offers you a maximum of style and comfort. Overnight at &Beyond Ngorongoro Crater Lodge.

Day 10 Serengeti

In the morning you will embark on an early morning hunt in the crater. Together with your experienced guide, you will drive through the forests along the crater rim, down to the plains of Ngorongoro. With the high density of wild animals living within the crater, they can experience exceptional animal sightings with a little luck. The swamp landscape offers numerous birds a home and will delight bird lovers. With a little bit of luck you can observe the "Big Five" of the animal world in a single day, including elephants, lions, rhinos, buffaloes and leopards. After returning to the lodge you can enjoy lunch and then drive to the Manyara airfield. From here you fly to Ndutu in the Serengeti National Park. The Camp &Beyond Serengeti Under Canvas is built depending on the course of the migration of the animals and thus gives you the opportunity to be close to the animals. The tents are simple but luxuriously equipped. Staying in the midst of the African bush will enrich you with a unique experience that will never let you go again. Enjoy the experience of lying in bed in the morning when the bush awakens in its immediate vicinity to life. Overnight at the &Beyond Serengeti Under Canvas.

Day 11 Serengeti National Park

After your tent camp is positioned in the Serengeti after the animal walk, you can usually reach the herds of the Gnus in only a few minutes by car during the game drives in the common vehicle of the &Beyond Serengeti Under Canvas. Depending on the rainy season, the animals stay in different places and there is always a bit of luck to find the herds. If the gnuherds don't show up, your experienced guide will organize an alternative game drive due to his many years of experience. In the Serengeti there are of course many other domestic animals besides the masses of Gnus, Zebras and Thompson Gazelles. These include some hyenas, antelopes and the five most difficult animals in the animal world: lion, elephant, buffalo, rhinoceros and leopard. During your stay in the camp you start a game drive in the early morning and in the afternoon. So you have the opportunity to observe both the morning active herds of the "Great Migration" and the night active animals in the evening. For meals, return to the camp. Overnight stay like the previous day in the &Beyond Serengeti Under Canvas.

12.-15. Day Dreamlike island of Zanzibar

Today you will travel to Zanzibar. From the camp you will be taken to the Kogatende airfield and can experience the impressive Serengeti one last time. From there you will now go to the island of Zanzibar. Zanzibar is known not only by its oriental markets and Arab influences, the beaches of the island are with the most beautiful in the world. Take a discovery tour during your stay and experience the relaxed atmosphere in the streets of the capital Stone Town with the impressive mosques and its warm inhabitants. Your accommodation will be the Diamonds La Gemma dell`Est, located on the northwestern coast of the magical island of Zanzibar. The 138 rooms are surrounded by lush tropical vegetation. Enjoy the relaxed atmosphere and the view of the turquoise Indian Ocean. The hotel is known for its remarkable service. You can also treat yourself to culinary delights here - various restaurants invite you to travel through different kitchens. The treatments at the Mvua African Rain Spa are based on African, Asian and European traditions and rejuvenate the body, strengthen the soul and revive the mind.

16th day Departure

Today it says "Goodbye", Africa. After an eventful 16 days it goes back to home. Just in time for your booked flight you will be taken to the airport on Zanzibar, from where you will travel home.

Uganda & Rwanda — Encounters with Mountain Gorillas

Uganda Trip: Encounters with the mountain gorillas is an idea for an individual Uganda tour with Africa Royal Tours. Now request.

15 days

Your trip inspiration

A sample itinerary — tailored to you

  • Africa Royal Tours
  • Uganda & Rwanda
  • Uganda Roundtrips

Discover the unspoilt nature of Uganda and Rwanda! In its lush rainforests you can expect fascinating encounters with imposing mountain gorillas and lively chimpanzees, who live freely in their native wilderness. Spectacular safaris take you through vast savannahs, glittering lakes and along the Kazinga Canal, where elephants, lions and hippos are close to grabbing. In addition to the fascinating wildlife, you immerse yourself in the rich culture and experience the warm hospitality. From the volcanic landscapes of Rwanda to the green hills of Uganda, Africa unfolds with its wilderness and magic. Feel the incomparable fascination of these two countries, which give unforgettable moments with untouched nature and moving encounters. Uganda and Rwanda – an adventure that touches deeply!

Trip information

Itinerary

15 Days Entebbe – Kibale National Park – Queen Elizabeth National Park – Mgahinga National Park – Volcanoes National Park – Lake Muhazi – Kigali

Your personal highlights

  • Chimpanzee Trekking in Kibale Forest National Park
  • Safari experiences on land and water in Queen Elizabeth National Park
  • Boat tour on the Kazinga Canal
  • Canyon hike in Kyambura
  • Unforgettable encounter with the mountain gorillas in the Mgahinga National Park
  • Impressive volcanic landscapes in Volcanoes National Park
  • Relaxation at Lake Muhazi
  • Especially selected luxury lodges for you
  • A personal contact before, during, and after your trip

After your arrival at Entebbe International Airport, your German-speaking driver guide will welcome you. Together you drive to your first accommodation and adjust to the upcoming experiences. Karibu Guesthouse, a charming villa surrounded by tropical gardens, is just minutes from the airport. Each room is furnished with attention to detail and promises a personal and warm atmosphere. On the inviting terrace you will have the opportunity to relax and to calmly tune in to the upcoming adventures. Take advantage of the remaining time for initial explorations, such as a visit to the nearby botanical gardens in Entebbe, where you can immerse yourself in the exotic flora of Uganda. Overnight at Karibu Guest House.

Today you drive to the heart of Uganda, where the Kibale Forest National Park awaits you with its breathtaking flora and fauna. More than 1,200 chimpanzees live under the park's dense leaf roof, of which a group of about 80 are used to the presence of visitors. In addition to these fascinating primates, other species of monkeys, such as black-and-white and red colobus as well as the full-bearded sea cats, can also be observed. With a little luck you can even see the rare forest elephants in their natural environment. Your accommodation for the next two nights, the exclusive Kibale Lodge, was built in a unique Volcanoes style by a team of African artisans. Each of the eight Bandas offers stunning views of the majestic Ruwenzori Mountains, Queen Elizabeth Park and the Kazinga Channel. The lodge is the perfect retreat to relax after an eventful day and enjoy the beauty of the surrounding area. Enjoy the silence and let your soul dangle while watching the sunset from the terrace above the tops of the Kibale Forest. Overnight at the Kibale Lodge.

Start your day with an early breakfast, full of anticipation of the upcoming nature experience. In the heart of the Kibale Forest National Park, embark on an exciting expedition to the chimpanzees, our fascinating and lively relatives. Under the expert guidance of a ranger you immerse yourself deeply in the green of the forest, in search of chimpanzees. Once you come across a group, you get the rare opportunity to observe their natural behaviors at close range for an hour and capture this unforgettable moment with your camera. At lunchtime we invite you to the charming house of Tinka. Here you will be presented various delicacies of Ugandan cuisine to discover the rich flavors and tastes of Uganda. Let yourself be enchanted by the warm hospitality and taste a variety of traditional delicacies. In the afternoon a hike leads you through the mystical Bigodi swamp, where the path leads you through picturesque pastures, idyllic villages and past lush green tea plantations. On your path you usually observe several species of monkeys and with a little luck your eye sees the magnificent Great Blue Turaco – a true jewel of the forest! Overnight at the Kibale Lodge.

Start your day with an unforgettable hike to a crater lake in Kibale, where you can enjoy the breathtaking scenery on picturesque paths. Small, charming villages line your path and offer insights into the quiet rural life of Uganda. After this impressive nature experience, leave the dense forest of Kibale at noon and continue your journey to Queen Elizabeth National Park. There is an exciting safari where you can watch not only gazelles, wart pigs and waterbucks, but also lions, hippos, elephants and buffalo in front of your camera. The next three nights will be spent at the exquisite Kyambura Gorge Lodge, which blends harmoniously between the Kyambur Gorge and the vast plains of Queen Elizabeth National Park. The lodge offers eight luxurious Bandas, nestled on the hillside and offering impressive views of the gorge or the lush savannah that stretches to the majestic Ruwenzori mountains. Enjoy breathtaking moments! Overnight at Kyambura Gorge Lodge.

Today your day begins in the early hours of the morning with an exciting game drive through the National Park. Keep your eyes open – the chance to discover lions is great. Be excited about these unique moments! After returning to the lodge, a well-deserved lunch break awaits you, where you can review the impressions of the morning and relax. In the afternoon, another highlight of your trip will take you on a boat trip along the Kazinga Canal, connecting Lake Edward and Lake George. On this impressive ride you will be able to observe numerous hippos, playfully strolling in the water. Look out for numerous wildlife that can be found for drinking or bathing on the shore. In addition, you will have the opportunity to observe an impressive variety of magnificent birds. This boat tour will undoubtedly be a breathtaking experience of your trip! Overnight at Kyambura Gorge Lodge.

Today, a hike through the breathtaking Kyambura gorge, a sunken natural wonder surrounded by cascade-like cliffs and dry savannah, awaits you. The gorge looks like a lost world, a place full of fascination and secrets. Surrounded by 100 meters high rock walls, a dense jungle with swamps and crater lakes reveals itself to you, which gives the feeling of having stepped back into a prehistoric period. A river flowing through the reserve, connected to the Kazinga Canal, will lead you through this unique ecosystem. The Kyambura Gorge is the only place in Queen Elizabeth National Park where you can meet chimpanzees. Although a sighting of these fascinating animals cannot be guaranteed, you have a good chance to experience the sounds and perhaps also the presence of chimpanzees. Overnight at Kyambura Gorge Lodge.

Today the drive leads you through dense forests and picturesque landscapes to the breathtaking home of the mountain gorillas in the Mgahinga National Park. The bumpy streets are just a small part of the adventure, as the diverse impressions of the surrounding area – past lush banana plantations and with breathtaking panoramas – enrich your journey in an incomparable way. Look forward to two nights at Mount Gahinga Lodge, it is not only the perfect starting point to discover the rare gorillas and gold sea cats on the Ugandan side of the Virungas, but also to climb volcanoes, to wander through the surrounding villages and experience the culture of Batwa – the oldest inhabitants of the Central African rainforest. The lounge areas, the private veranda and the lovingly maintained gardens invite you to linger and reflect the beautiful hand-built bandas, the aesthetic traditions of the Bufimbira people. Overnight stays at Mount Gahinga Lodge.

Today, an unforgettable moment awaits you: the encounter with the gentle giants of the Mgahinga National Park – the rare mountain gorillas. This experience is one of the most moving highlights of your Uganda trip. These gorillas, accustomed to human presence, let you share in their lives for a short time. Your hike leads through moss-covered slopes, interwoven lianas and lush bamboo forests, while the tension slowly increases – until you finally enter the quiet presence of these majestic beings. An experienced ranger accompanies you, deciphers the mysterious traces of the gorillas and gently introduces you to their world. When your gaze gets caught in the deep, thoughtful eyes of a gorilla, a connection emerges that can hardly be expressed in words. Every moment of this encounter is filled with awe and a deep bond with nature. For an hour, you can observe these impressive creatures at close range – an experience that makes your heart beat faster and remains unforgettable. Overnight stays at Mount Gahinga Lodge.

Today you leave Uganda after breakfast and continue your adventure in the heart of Rwanda, where nature and luxury merge into perfect harmony. Its destination for today is Volcanoes National Park, a refuge of ancient, untouched rainforests and the protected home of majestic mountain gorillas. Your accommodation for the next two days is the legendary Virunga Lodge, which sits majestically on a mountain ridge and offers breathtaking views: the gentle hills of Rwanda, the distinctive peaks of the Virunga volcanoes and the glittering waters of Lake Bulera and Lake Ruhondo stretch to the horizon. Each of the ten stylish Bandas has a private terrace from which you can admire the dramatic landscape and experience the silence and inspiration of nature first hand. Design the day tomorrow according to your wishes. Do you want to face the challenge of a volcanic ascent? Or do you prefer to spend time with the fascinating gorillas again? Book your preferred activity in advance or decide spontaneously on site. Overnight at Virunga Lodge.

Today your trip to Lake Muhazi will take you. The long and flat lake winds over 40 km through a flooded valley and reaches its eastern end near the Akagera National Park, directly on the road to Nyagatare. This place is ideal for a break. Enjoy a strengthening lunch with a great view of the lake. Discover dozens of traditional villages along the coast and experience the rich flora and fauna, including a diverse bird world. Immerse yourself in this atmospheric and relaxing atmosphere and let yourself be enchanted by the special magic of the place. The long, narrow waters of Muhazi Lake are surrounded by lush vegetation and terraced farms. Your nearest accommodation, the Umva Muhazi Lodge, sits on freestanding ramparts right on the shore. Known for its warm hospitality and its fantastic location, the Lodge offers comfortable bungalows, all nestled in this oasis. Each offers a wonderful view of the calm lake – the perfect place to recharge your batteries, relax or experience outdoor adventures. Overnight at Umva Muhazi Lodge.

Use the two days to design them according to your wishes. How about a guided birdwatching or an invigorating hike? You may also be attracted to a yoga session to start the day in peace. A kayaking tour is then possible, where you can explore the gentle waters of the lake. Or try stand-up paddling: Standing on the board, glide over the water, enjoy the fresh sea air or even take on yoga exercises to blend perfectly with nature. Tennis lovers also get their money's worth and can make an exciting match on the Clay Court. The Umwa Lodge offers numerous offers. No matter what you choose – between all these activities you will certainly have time to simply relax. Design your days exactly as you like and enjoy two unforgettable days in the Umwa Lodge – you deserve it! Overnight at Umwa Lodge.

Today your journey will take you to Kigali, the vibrant capital of Rwanda, after your breakfast. A moving visit to the genocide memorial, in which more than 250,000 victims of the genocide have found their final resting place, is on the agenda. Afterwards you will discover the most important sights of this fascinating metropolis during a city tour. Your accommodation today will be the Hôtel des Mille Collines. Opened in 1973 as the first Grand Hotel in Rwanda, it is one of the country's most famous hotels. During the Tutsi genocide, it provided protection for around 2,000 people and has since been an important symbol of Rwandan history. Everywhere in the house you will find artworks by Rwandan artists that not only enrich the ambience, but are also available for sale to support the local art scene. Sustainability is also a top priority at the hotel – 90% of the products used come from Rwanda. Enjoy your break in this historic place. Overnight at the Hotel des Mille Collines.

Grateful and filled with all the wonderful impressions, your driver will drive you to Kigali Airport after breakfast. What an impressive time! If you wish to experience even more of Africa, be it an exciting safari in Kenya or a relaxing bathing extension in the Seychelles, we will gladly put together an individual offer for you. With a heart full of unforgettable moments you will return home. " Murabeho "- Goodbye and see you on the next adventure!

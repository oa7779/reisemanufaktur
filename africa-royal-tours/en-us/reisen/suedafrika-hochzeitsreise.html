South Africa and Mozambique honeymoon - Safari & Honeymoon

Unique South Africa and Mozambique Honeymoon: Experience the magic of safari and bathing holidays on your tailor-made honeymoon with Africa Royal Tours.

This exclusive honeymoon perfectly combines the best of high-end safari and bathing experience for newlyweds. Enjoy romantic togetherness in selected accommodations with absolute privacy. The program includes exciting Big 5 safaris in an open off-road vehicle or on foot. The breathtaking starry sky sparkles over you. Then enjoy peace and relaxation on Benguerra Island in Mozambique. The sunset is dreamy in a dhau or you can enjoy it at your private beach dinner and meet your wedding with a glass of champagne.

1st day Arrival in South Africa and flight to the kingdom of wild animals

Welcome to South Africa! When you arrive at Johannesburg Airport, you will be taken directly to the plane by your Africa Royal Tours representative, who will guide you quickly and conveniently through the passport and customs control to your onward flight. We continue with South African Airlink o. Like Hoedspuit. There you are already expected by a colleague of the Singita Lebombo Lodge and taken to your safari lodge. The next three nights will be spent at the Singita Lebombo Lodge, which is located in a private, extremely animal-rich concession within the Kruger National Park. The design of the lodge was inspired by the beauty of the African landscape and Earth-affiliation. From one of the 13 One Bedroom suites, enjoy stunning views of the N ́Wanetsi River and the surrounding nature. Overnight at the Singita Lebombo Lodge.

2.-3. Day in the footsteps of the Big 5

These two days you will use to explore elephant, lion, leopard and co. in two safari activities per day. During your game drives in an open-air off-road vehicle, you will walk with other guests of the lodge and your English-speaking, professional guide and tracker over floor and stone. From a completely different perspective, you will experience the wildlife in a foot safari. Especially adventurous guests can take a mountain bike tour. Or you can enjoy the fabulous view into the starry sky. Feel true freedom – and the desire for new things is awakened. Experiences of the day can be experienced with a relaxing pampering treatment at the spa or in your own suite. Cooling down can be found in the pool in the central area of the lodge and for sports enthusiasts there is a gym with the most important equipment and weights available. Here in Singita Lebombo, the extra mile is going everywhere to make you as comfortable as possible. This top experience is rounded off by culinary highlights, so you will immerse yourself in a world full of unique moments with all your senses. Overnight stays at the Singita Lebombo Lodge.

Day 4: Sabi Sand Private Game Reserve

Today you say goodbye to Singita Lebombo and are chauffeured by an employee of the lodge to the Airstrip, from where your small plane (Federal Air o. e.g. towards Sabi Sand Private Game Reserve. Of course, you are already expected there and chauffeured into your next safari accommodation: the Singita Boulders Lodge. The rest of the day is free. If the time permits, you'll be doing a first round trip this afternoon. When designing the lodge, the ancient boulders and the course of the river were included in the design. Natural coal and copper tones reflect the four elements of fire, earth, air and water. Thus, the lodge blends seamlessly into the game of light, shadow and different moods – a perfect retreat for travelers like you, looking for time for two. Overnight at the Singita Boulders Lodge.

5.-6. Day animals, animals, animals!

Early in the morning, when the wilderness awakens, your safari days also start Here at the Singita Boulders Lodge, two safari activities (together with other guests and guided by an English-language guide and a tracker of the lodge) are also included for you. In the open off-road vehicle, you'll see the gag, and your guide explains everything you need to know about the flora and fauna that surrounds you. Wild dogs, elephants, zebras, lions, cheetahs, elephants and leopards, various bird species – the wealth of animals will delight you! In the afternoon, the hunt goes into the evening and in the light of the lights the nocturnal magic of the wilderness unfolds. During a Bush Walk, the focus is on small things and animals – a great addition to the four-wheel drive. Overnight stays at the Singita Boulders Lodge.

Day 7: Wilderness to the Indian Ocean

The "wild side of South Africa" leaves behind you today and flies to Kruger Mpumalanga Airport in the small "Buschhupfer". From there you fly with South African Airlink to Vilanculos in Mozambique. The last stage is in the helicopter. Here you will find fantastic views of the paradise, in which you will end your honeymoon in the coming days. Overnight in Azura Benguerra Island.

8.-12. Day honeys in the beach paradise

What a fantastic setting – take a pristine island, white, powdery sandy beaches with gently windy palm trees, bright sun and turquoise blue ocean and your beach paradise Azura Benguerra Island is ready. Not to forget, of course, the colourful underwater world – a paradise for every diver and snorkeler. The 20 villas stretch along a well-kept beach secret of the world. Each villa has a private pool and is located a few steps from the beach. Enjoy Robinson Crusoe feeling and award-winning, relaxed barefoot luxury at the same time. A romantic beach dinner awaits you on the beach. And of course, we'll come up with more surprises that will make your stay here an unforgettable experience. Overnight stay in Azura Benguerra Island.

13th day Departure from Benguerra Island

Today your valuable vacation time ends, but this is only the beginning of your common future as a married couple. They say goodbye to Mozambique today. Depending on the flight booked, you will be taken in time by helicopter to Vilanculos airport, from where you will start your return journey

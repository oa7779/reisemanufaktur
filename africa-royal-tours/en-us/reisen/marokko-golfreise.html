Morocco Golf Journey — Golf at Its Finest

Morocco Travel: Golfing at its finest in Morocco. Experience your Morocco tour with Africa Royal Tours, the Africa expert. Now request.

If you are a passionate golfer and would like to improve your handicap, this trip to Morocco is just the thing for golf enthusiasts. For a whole week, a personal professional golf coach and a caddy will be available for you and will support you with advice and action. Refine your basics by a professional training and sound trainer knowledge. On this trip from Africa Royal Tours, golf and comfortable accommodation are combined with culinary highlights and exciting activities. Let the magic of Marrakech inspire you!

Bienvenue in Marrakech! After you have been received by your Africa Royal Tours representative, you will be taken to your exclusive golf hotel. Your accommodation for the next six nights is the well-known Hotel Fairmont Royal Palm, which is one of the "Leading Hotels of the World". On an extensive area of palm trees and hundred-year-old olive trees, Fairmont Royal Palm Marrakech invites you to an oasis of luxury, style and adventure. Ideally located twelve kilometers from the lively old medina of Marrakech, the hotel offers elegant rooms, suites and villas. They were designed to be great and yet intimate. They offer a breathtaking view over the resort's 18-hole golf course and the Atlas Mountains behind it. Culinary cuisine is well served in one of the four exceptional restaurants serving regional specialities and international cuisine. Between the training sessions you can relax by the pool or in the spacious spa. Overnight at Hotel Fairmont Royal Palm.

After breakfast your private golf course begins. On the first day you can play the 18-hole Atlas golf course. This is designed in accordance with Moroccan traditions: classical architecture combined with modern comfort, while warmth and sunshine are complemented by cooling, shaded places in an oriental-looking environment. The flat profile of the course is ideal for players of all levels. Your professional trainer will first familiarize himself with your technical skills and then start personalized training sessions. You get one and a half hours of training every day to work on your game, your rhythm and the movement of your tee. Your coach also accompanies you daily with an 18-hole game over nine holes, with the emphasis on the most important golf clubs. Of course, you will always be driven with a golf cart to the most attractive greens of the "Red City". So you have every day the opportunity to gain new experience on different facilities. Overnight at Hotel Fairmont Royal Palm.

After breakfast, pick up your chauffeur and drive to Al Maaden Golf Resort in the south of Marrakech. Designed by star architect Kyle Philips, the golf course is located in the heart of a natural valley and combines Moroccan oase elements with a Scottish touch in an amazing way. In front of the impressive backdrop of the Atlas Mountains, the 72-hectare square with 18 holes is suitable for all levels of play. In addition to a 300 meter long driving range, there are numerous water barriers and some elevated greens, surrounded by almond trees, cypresses and palm trees. Today, the focus is on the wide game, followed by an 18-hole game where your coach accompanies you over 9 holes. Dinner is served in a typical Moroccan riad. A riad, characteristic of Morocco, is characterized by a traditionally designed courtyard and is located in the heart of the Medina. There you will be spoiled with typical local delicacies in an oriental atmosphere and with lots of charm. Overnight at Hotel Fairmont Royal Palm.

Today you immerse yourself in the world of the oldest golf course in Marrakech, which was opened in 1923. Celebrities such as Winston Churchill and David Lloyd George as well as numerous well-known golfers have already played here. The legendary 27-hole complex is surrounded by century-old eucalyptus, olive and orange trees as well as palm trees and Aleppo pines. A particularly attractive hole even bears the name Brigitte Bardots. Enjoy a round of golf on this historic course. As the day before, you will focus on the wide game, followed by an 18-hole game where your instructor will accompany you in 9 holes. For lunch, the restaurant Haras is located in Selman Marrakech, where you can be inspired by Moroccan cuisine. This lunch can also take place on another day, depending on your schedule. Dinner is served in the Atlas Golf Club Marrakech restaurant, where fine French cuisine is served. Eat in a festive atmosphere and then take part in a "personal star hour", in which an astronomy expert will bring you closer to the constellations of the sky. Overnight at Hotel Fairmont Royal Palm.

Today you will play on the spectacular 27-hole square of Amelkis, designed by the renowned architect Cabell B. Robinson. The name of the square comes from a former queen of a Berber tribe who lived in the Atlas. Located near the Royal Golf Club, the hilly course offers a fantastic view of the Atlas Mountains. The square, with its water hazards and bunkers, is a challenge for every type of player and is surrounded by palm groves and elements of Moroccan architecture. The fairways are lined with luxurious villas, centuries-old palm trees and huge papyrus reeds. Practice your way around and test your progress in a subsequent 27-hole game, accompanied by your coach over 9 holes. After golfing, you can relax in your hotel or explore the Medina. For dinner, the Palais Jad Mahal will charm you with its Moroccan flair and a dance show. Let the evening end while you let the energy of traditional sounds and dances shine on you. Overnight at Hotel Fairmont Royal Palm.

Your last day of golf starts with the trip to Palmaraie Golf Club in northern Marrakech. In the immediate vicinity of the square you can admire the historic bridge over the Oued Tensift. The golf course, a masterpiece by Robert Trent Jones Senior, blends harmoniously into the picturesque palm garden with its greens, fairways and lakes. The entire area and the clubhouse, which is like a small Moroccan palace in the heart of the golf course, offer constantly impressive views of the Atlas Mountains. The golf resort has been awarded the "Best Golf Resort in Africa" annually since 2006. After an intense wide-play exercise, you have the last chance to prove your skills in an 18-hole game today. Your coach will accompany you through 9 holes. Dinner is held today in Nobu – a first-class restaurant in the stylish Hivernage district. Nobu combines traditional elements with modern elegance and offers an exclusive rooftop terrace with garden bar. In this relaxed atmosphere you can enjoy excellent food and order cocktails at will, while DJs and artists provide entertainment.

Today it is time to say goodbye to the fascinating Marrakech. With a suitcase full of new experiences you start your journey home. Your private chauffeur will take you to the airport in time for your return flight. If you would like to relax on the Atlantic coast for a few days after your discovery, we will be happy to offer you a suitable bathing extension in Essaouira or Agadir.

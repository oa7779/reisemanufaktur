Day 1 South Luangwa National Park

Zambia warmly welcomes you. Upon arrival at Lusaka Airport, you will be welcomed by your Africa Royal Tours representative and taken to Mfuwe Airport for your charter flight to South Luangwa National Park. There you will be welcomed by a driver of your accommodation, the Kakuli Camp, and taken to your accommodation in a shared vehicle. The Kakuli Camp is a tent camp overlooking a plain at the confluence of the rivers Luwi and Luangwa. Although wildlife observation is very popular everywhere in southern Luangwa, it is exceptional in the vicinity of the Kakuli Camp. There are 4 tents, so the camp can accommodate up to 8 people. Each of the tents offers a breathtaking view and the open-air bathrooms behind it are all very spacious. Wild observation can be carried out either on foot or by vehicle. Overnight at Kakuli Camp.

2nd - 3rd day adventure in South Luangwa National Park

The next two days will give you time to explore South Luangwa National Park. The national park is the best known wildlife reserve in Zambia and covers just under 9,000 km² and is home to more than 230 different mammals and over 400 different bird species. The unique landscape characterized by the river course of the Luangwa makes the park a true El Dorado for wildlife. Depending on the rainy season, the river course changes and offers new habitats for the animals. Visit this unique national park on game-watching trips in 4x4 off-road vehicles, day and night as well as on guided walks in the bush and experience authentic wildlife encounters in one of Africa's last paradises. Overnight at Kakuli Camp.

Day 4 Kafue National Park

If time permits, you’ll have the chance of a final morning game drive in South Luangwa National Park. Today, the trip takes you by small plane via Lusaka to Kafue Airport. A special highlight awaits you from here: by helicopter of the camp, the Busanga Bush Camp goes to your accommodation. Enjoy the unique arrival, collect first impressions from the air and admire the diverse landscape of Kafue National Park from above. With barely 22,400 km² is the park, largest and oldest national park in Zambia. In the 50Founded by the legendary Norman Carr, the park still offers many undiscovered and untouched spots. Thanks to its size and diverse landscape, Kafue National Park has a fantastic variety of animals and offers a home to all cats, elephants, buffaloes, wildebeest and numerous antelope species. Through the park flow the rivers Kafue and Lufupa, which provide for large hippo deposits. On the way to your accommodation, Busanga Bush Camp, you can already see some wildlife with a bit of luck. The camp is located in the heart of Busanga Plains. The only four well-equipped luxury tents have all the amenities you can imagine. Overnight at Busanga Bush Camp.

5th – 6th day discover Kafue National Park

The next two days you will have plenty of time for game drives in the Kafue National Park, which are organized twice daily by the camp. Immerse yourself in a wild and unspoilt natural landscape like you haven't experienced yet. When the flood plains are dried up (in the Zambian summer), the camp is ideally located to observe in the plains a large number of wildlife such as buffalo, gnus, puku and letschweantilopes, followed by their hunters, the lions. A special sight are also the herds of the proud horse antelopes. For bird lovers, the Kafue National Park is also a special place, as the park has over 491 bird species, including the endemic fig bird. During the game drives in the open safari vehicle at day and night, you can watch both the day-active and the night-active animals. Alternatively, the camp also offers birdwatching, boat tours and full-day picnic tours in the bush. Optionally, hot air balloon rides can be booked on site. After an eventful day in the bush, you can really relax in the camp. Nights like the day before at Bushanga Bush Camp.

7th day Livingstone

After breakfast you fly back to Kafue by helicopter and from there by small plane to Livingstone. A representative of Africa Royal Tours awaits you there, who will take you to your accommodation for the following night, the Toka Leya Camp. Located in the Mosi-Oa-Tunya National Park, just 12 km from the huge Victoria Falls, the camp is an ideal starting point for exploring. The accommodation impresses with its location with a wide view over the vast Sambesi River. In the afternoon, take a guided tour of the majestic Victoria Falls, which are part of the UNESCO World Heritage Site. On the border between Zambia and Zimbabwe, the falls flow into the 108 m deep gorge at a width of 1,700 m with a deafening noise. The water mist, the spray nebula created by the fall, rises up to 300 meters high and can still be seen from a distance of 30 kilometers. In the late afternoon, a romantic sunset ride awaits you on the Sambesi. The camp consists of 12 spacious safari-style ensuite tents and stylish furnishings in a modern-African mix. Their spacious terrace is covered by a canvas and so you can experience wonderfully relaxing moments overlooking the river plains.

8th day Farewell from Zambia

After breakfast it is unfortunately time to say goodbye to unforgettable eight days in Zambia. You will be taken to Livingstone Airport, where you will be flying home, or if you have not yet received enough of Africa, we will be happy to work out a connecting program for you, for example with a bathing extension on a private island in Mozambique?

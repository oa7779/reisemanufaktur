Travel on a beautiful journey with your rental car and discover the absolute highlights of Namibia, Botswana and Zimbabwe on your route. Enjoy the incredible expanse and tranquility of the Kalahari desert, experience breathtaking animals in the Etosha National Park, discover the diverse landscapes of the Caprivi Strip and be inspired by the thundering Victoria Falls in Zimbabwe.

Trip information

After your arrival at the International Airport in Windhoek, you will be warmly welcomed and accompanied to take over your rental car. Then start to your hotel in Windhoek, where you can relax from your long journey. For example, you can enjoy your dinner in Joe's Beerhouse and get in tune with the Namibian cuisine with a cool beer. Olive Exclusive Boutique Hotel is a luxurious accommodation that offers sophisticated guests every comfort. The Boutique Hotel is surrounded by olive trees in one of the most exclusive districts of Windhoeks. The attentive service, both in the hotel as well as in the restaurant and the wellness center, prepares the guests an unforgettable stay. Overnight in Windhoek.

After your breakfast drive towards Sossusvlei. Namibia's scenic highlight in the middle of the Namib desert – the Sossusvlei in the Namib Naukluft Park – is a salt-crusted clay sink surrounded by mighty sand dunes. Your accommodation for the next two nights is Little Kulala Camp. The camp is a small, luxurious camp in the Kulala Reserve, bordering the vast Namib Naukluft Park and with its 210 km² area, conveys a sense of seclusion and exclusivity, enhanced by the vastness of the desert, the orphaned dunes and rough mountains. Adapted to environmental conditions, the eleven suites were built on elevated decks and equipped with straw roofs, tent cloth walls, air conditioning, private refreshment pool and spacious bathrooms. The suites have a flat roof, where you can sleep on request and experience the romantic atmosphere of the desert under the free starry sky. The modernly furnished main lodge also stands on an elevated terrace. Through the side walls, which can be opened, you look at the water point and the desert behind it. Overnight at Little Kulala Camp.

In the morning you will be picked up and drive towards Sossusvlei, where the highest dunes in the world are located. These rise from the ground to 300 m altitude. In Sossusvlei there are mainly star dunes, which are up to 25 km long. The name "Sossus" comes from the Nama language and is called "Water Collection Place" or "The Place of Water". With the four-wheeled vehicle it goes into the heart of the dune landscape. The rest of the day is at your disposal; we recommend a hike or a visit to the Sesriem Canyon. The canyon is a rare natural spectacle in the otherwise dry desert. Over the last two million years, the Tsauchab River has created a ditch, the Sesriem Canyon, about a kilometer long and up to 30 meters deep, due to heavy desert rain. Overnight at Little Kulala Camp.

After breakfast, continue towards Swakopmund. On the way you stop in Solitaire and the local "Tante Emma Store" which is known for its excellent apple strudel. Continue down the Gaub Pass to Kuiseb Canyon, which is located in Namib Naukluft National Park in the Namib Desert. In the nature reserve there are a lot of wildlife such as ostrichs, jumping goats, oryx antelopes and numerous bird species. There you can also admire the unique plant Welwitschia, which certainly represents a high point in the Namib desert. The leaves of the plant growing on the ground can be up to 2.5 m long and can absorb water from the barren desert soil via dewdrops and their large root system. In the immediate vicinity and on the way to Swakopmund are some very beautiful viewpoints, from which one can see the "moon landscape" well known for the region. The Swakopmund Luxury Suites are centrally located in the city and close to the beach. The modern and stylishly furnished hotel offers spacious and comfortable suites. On the ground floor, the suites also have a small terrace. Overnight in Swakopmund.

A day of sea and dunes awaits you today. A combination of the morning boat tour in Walvis Bay and a Sandwich Harbour 4x4 excursion awaits you. During the boat trip with a speedboat or catamaran you have the chance to see pelicans, seals, dolphins, the port of Walvis Bay, old Russian fishing boats and oyster farms. At 12.30 p.m., when you have finished the boat trip, we continue with our 4x4 to Sandwich Harbour. Lunch including fresh oysters and cold sparkling wine are served first on the boat, and then again in the dunes or on the beach. At 4:30 p.m. you'll be back at the Walvis Bay Waterfront.

On today's day, your journey only goes north past Wlotzkasbaken to Henties Bay, this place has developed into a small resort, but – unlike in Wlotzskasbaken – many inhabitants live here permanently. Through the beautiful Damaraland landscape you come to the sights of Twyfelfontein: the well-known rock engravings up to 6,000 years old, of which there are the 3,000 of their kind, the valley of the organ pipes, in which basalt columns are located, which are located before approx. 120 million years were created when lava penetrated the slate rock and solidified to square columns (not including entrances). The Mowani Mountain Camp, your accommodation for today, is located between the river beds of Huab and Ugab on a small hill and offers breathtaking views of the pristine wilderness with fascinating desert landscape. The total of 12 luxury tents, created in the form of an African village, have all the comforts. Overnight at the Mowani Mountain Camp.

Your trip will take you to the Ongava Game Reserve. Three accommodations named "Ongava" are located in the game reserve of the same name, in the immediate vicinity of the Etosha National Park. From the entrance to the private reserve it is only a few hundred meters to the southern entrance to the famous Etosha National Park. On the same day, you can also enjoy your first game drive in the Ongava Wild Reserve. Your accommodation for the next two days is the Little Ongava Camp. The camp is located on the top of a hill. The kilometer-wide view over the lowlands offers a fantastic view that extends up to the horizon. Little Ongava Camp specializes in travel to the Etosha National Park and convinces with its extraordinary experiences. This private camp has three spacious suites, each with its own swimming pool, bathroom and outdoor shower (al fresco shower). From the living and dining area you have a wonderful view of the camp and its water hole, which promises a stylish dinner under the African starry sky. Overnight at the Ongava Wild Reserve.

Today's day revolves around animal observation. During your safaris in an open-air off-road vehicle, you will visit several water points where you can see with a little luck a wide range of wild animals and birds. Discover African wildlife in the morning in the Etosha National Park. Watch a lioness hunt and read the traces of the leopard. Discover the white rhinoceros or let yourself be enchanted by the majestic beauty of the elephants who pass by in the sublime rhythm on the paths of their ancestors. In the afternoon your guided game drive starts in the Ongava Game Reserve. The wildlife in the reserve is varied with particularly good lion and rhinoceros populations. Overnight at the Ongava Wild Reserve.

Timely departure after breakfast to the Etosha National Park, where the safari starts immediately. From water hole to water hole, hoping to observe all wild species, the path leads through the steppes of the park. The Etosha National Park is a highlight of Namibia with its animal wealth and is one of the largest protected areas on earth. The special charm of Etosha is not in the abundance, but in the palpable void and incredible expanse. Just before sunset, leave the National Park and drive to your accommodation in the Onguma Game Reserve. The Onguma Nature Reserve covers an area of 20,000 hectares on the eastern edge of the Etosha National Park. Wild animals, such as elephants, rhinoceros, lions, cheetahs and leopards, as well as over 300 bird species are found in the Onguma Reserve and can be observed in open-air off-road vehicles. You're staying at Onguma Tented Camp today. The camp is stylish and comfortable. All seven tent suites offer views of a water hole for animal observation.

Adjacent to Angola, on the banks of the Okavango River in northern Namibia, the city of Rundu serves as the capital of the eastern Kavango region. Rundu is known for its local woodcarver market as well as the numerous woodcarver huts on the roadside. The city is a great stopover for visitors who want to go to Katima Mulilo, as well as an excellent starting point to explore the beautiful surroundings. Visitors to Rundu can enjoy the beautiful surroundings, including the magnificent Popa Falls and the Mahangu Game Reserve. Other popular activities are: great wildlife watching, excellent bird watching and kayaking on the spectacular Kavango River. Your night quarters are the Taranga Safari Lodge. The lodge is one of the most luxurious lodges on the trip to Caprivi. A combination of seclusion on the banks of the Okavango River, luxury amenities and high-quality service awaits you. The complex is built almost exclusively on stilts and has many observation decks with views of the river area.

After breakfast your journey continues towards Okavango River in the Mahango Game Reserve. As you cross Kavangoland and Caprivi you will have the opportunity to stop by woodcarvers and potters and admire their handicrafts on the way. Your accommodation for today is the Divava Lodge in the Kavango region, on the banks of the mighty Okavango River, which is a very beautiful and above all green region of Namibia. From the local lovingly called "Paradish Ghomumbiru" – "little paradise", you experience a lush plant and animal world there just as it is not common in Namibia. Experience pure Africa! On site you will find a varied activity program of the lodge (everything for a fee). Today, however, a boat trip is already included for you. Just before sunset, drive upstream past the resort to the Popa Falls, the ideal place to enjoy the sunset with a drink and a few snacks. The 20 luxurious and tastefully furnished chalets guarantee you a relaxing stay. Overnight at Divava Lodge.

A pleasant journey through the Caprivi strip and the Bwabwata National Park, to your lodge, where you will take a game drive and a boat trip on the Kwando River. The region is known for its large elephant herds with up to two hundred specimens. On a boat cruise you drive up the Kwando River, which springs in Angola, flows to Namibia and finally leaks into the Linyanti swamps. Enjoy the varied landscape with kilometers of Ried and Papyrus areas, secluded bays and lagoons with flowering water lilies. Let yourself be enchanted by the more than 400 bird species and the elephants as well as by hippos, to which we come very close to up to a few meters. Their accommodation is the Nambwa Tented Lodge. The exclusive tent suites of the Nambwa Lodge were built on platforms in the crowns of wide-spread trees. Nambwa offers retreat space for about 330 bird species and many mammals, including elephants, lions, buffalos, hippos, rappers and horse antelopes, letswee, leopards, hyenas and the rare African wild dogs. Overnight at Nambwa Tented Lodge.

Today you cross the border to Botswana, where you will return your rental car by prior arrangement. During your 2-day stay in the Chobe National Park you will experience this unique national park with the highest elephant density in the world on a game drive and a boat trip. The next two nights will be spent in one of the most luxurious lodges in Botswana – the Sanctuary Chobe Chilwero Lodge. The 15 very spacious cottages with reed roof are stone and have a private garden with terrace from which you can enjoy the beautiful sunset over the Chobe River. Overnight stays at the Sanctuary Chobe Chilwero Lodge.

After breakfast you will be picked up at the hotel and taken over the border by private transfer to Victoria Falls in Zimbabwe, where a boat trip on the Zambezi awaits you at sunset. On the second day, an expert guide will guide you through the rainforest to see one of the seven natural wonders with your own eyes. Follow the footsteps of David Livingstone to experience the indomitability and fascinating natural spectacle of the Victoria Falls, which are among the UNESCO World Heritage Sites. The cases thunder into the depth on a width of 1,700 m and over 110 m, so that one is usually wrapped in a cloud of water mist. On site you will find numerous other activities that you can do on your own, such as a helicopter flight over the waterfalls, bungee jumping, elephant riding and much more (all activities for a fee). You will stay at Victoria Falls Hotel. The traditional colonial-style hotel is an oasis of peace in a lush tropical garden. The 2 restaurants spoil with classic and African cuisine in an elegant ambience or with views of the roaring Victoria Falls. Overnight at Victoria Falls Hotel.

Time is available until departure. Private transfer to the International Airport to your departure home. If you would like to enjoy a few more relaxing days by the sea, we would be happy to arrange a bathing extension in Mozambique or Mauritius or Seychelles (price on request).

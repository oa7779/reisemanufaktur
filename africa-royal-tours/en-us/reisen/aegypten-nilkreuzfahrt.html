Egypt Nile Cruise — Water, Desert & Wonders

Experience an exclusive, tailor-made Egypt Nile cruise: from Cairo to Luxor to Aswan. Discover temples, pyramids & the magic of the Nile in 9 days luxury.

Discover Egypt – a land of legends, wonders and fascinating history! This extraordinary journey takes you on a journey through time, leading you to the most impressive and mysterious sites of antiquity: from the majestic pyramids of Giza to the magnificent temples of Luxor to the ancient treasures of the Nile. On an exclusive Dahabiya cruise, glide gently along the Nile and immerse yourself in the fascinating world of the Pharaohs.

Ahlan - Welcome to Cairo! After your arrival in Cairo, you will be warmly welcomed by your Africa Royal representative and taken to the luxurious Sofitel Downtown Nile Cairo, where you will spend the night. The hotel offers spectacular views of the Nile River and the world-famous Tahrir Square. Enjoy the day to collect first impressions of the vibrant metropolis on the Nile according to your own rhythm. Look forward to the coming days full of history, magic and great views.

Today your exploration of the legendary wonders of the world begins: on the Giza Plateau you stand in front of the imposing pyramids of Cheops, Chephren and Mykerinos, the last witnesses of the "Seven Wonders of the Ancients". Just a few steps away you can see the mysterious Sphinx, silent guardian of the necropolis for over four millennia. Look forward to the camel ride experience or the visit of the Great Pyramid. You will then visit the Grand Egyptian Museum (GEM), a masterpiece of museum culture. With over 100,000 artifacts, including the complete treasure of Tutankhamun and the colossal statue of Ramses II, the museum becomes a real treasure house. You can also be impressed by the breathtaking panoramic views of the pyramids.

Today, after your breakfast, your driver will drive you to the airport, whereupon you will go to Luxor. Arriving there, you board your elegant Dahabiya Gaia. The ship, a true masterpiece of traditional craftsmanship, welcomes you with elegance and a touch of history. There are eight luxurious cabins and suites on board that are flooded with light and equipped with large windows and a private balcony – ideal for enjoying the first breathtaking view of the majestic Nile. After lunch, a cultural highlight awaits you: the temple of Karnak, a 62-hectare complex with gigantic columns and thousands of years-old inscriptions. Afterwards you will visit the magically illuminated Luxor Temple, once the site of the feast of Opet. The first evening on board marks a journey that combines tradition and luxury in perfect harmony. Enjoy dinner while the ship glides gently down the Nile.

Your adventure will take you today to the west bank of Luxor, where the mysterious Valley of Kings awaits you. Here the Pharaohs created their last resting places, including the artistically decorated tomb of Pharaoh Seti I and the world famous tomb of Tutankhamun, discovered by Howard Carter in 1922. The visit to these impressive tombs lets you immerse yourself deeply in the history of ancient Egypt. Then you drive to the terrace temple of Queen Hatshepsut, a masterpiece of architecture that nestles majestically on the desert cliffs. This temple was built in honor of the only female pharaoh of the New Empire and offers a spectacular photo motif. The morning ends with a photo stop at the venerable Memnon Colossians, who once represented the Pharaoh Amenhotep III. The mighty statues are an impressive testimony of ancient architecture. The afternoon is full of relaxation on board.

After breakfast, the journey will take you today to the magnificent temple of Dendera, dedicated to the goddess Hathor. A highlight is the famous Zodiak of Dendera, which provides insights into the Egyptian understanding of astrology. When exploring the temple, you immerse yourself deeply in the mysticism and history of this unique place. After your visit you will return on board and continue your journey towards Esna. End the afternoon with a refreshing drink on the sun deck and enjoy the view of the calm water while the ship continues along the river.

Today the discovery of the fascinating temple complex of Esna is imminent. This Roman-influenced site is below today's street level and provides a unique insight into the religious and architectural history of the region. The temple complex, dedicated to the goddess Neith, impresses with its well-preserved reliefs and the magnificent column hall, which tells of the past epochs. Then continue your journey and go to Edfu, where you will visit the impressive temple of Horus. As one of the best preserved temples in Egypt, it offers a breathtaking insight into the mythological scenes and rituals of ancient Egypt. Look forward to monumental architecture and fascinating reliefs. Experience the incorruptible symbolism of ancient Egypt to the fullest. Back on board, enjoy your dinner.

In the morning you reach the remote Gebel El Silsila, where the most important quarries of the Pharaohs once lay. Still today, numerous inscriptions and small chapels are carved into the rocks, which testify to the important role of this place in Egyptian history. Impressive relics from ancient times provide an insight into the working world of the pharaohs and the religious practices of that time. Then visit the double temple of Kom Ombo, dedicated to the crocodile god Sobek and the god Haroeris, with your guide. Particularly impressive is the neighbouring museum, which houses a fascinating collection of mummified crocodiles – relics from a time when the crocodile god Sobek was venerated. After this cultural highlight you will return on board.

At the end of your journey, the island of Agilkia awaits you with the fantastically located Philae Temple, a masterpiece of the Ptolemy period built in honor of the goddess Isis. Afterwards you will visit the imposing Aswan Dam, which once electrified all of Egypt – an example of modern engineering. Finally, the imposing, unfinished obelisk awaits you in the ancient granite quarries of Aswan, an impressive testimony of past architecture. The last evening on board will be a special highlight when you enjoy the crowning end of your trip at a stylish dinner and a lively Nubian show

After breakfast it is time to say goodbye to your Dahabiya and the fascinating Nile cruise. Your guide will accompany you to Aswan Airport, where you will be filled with the last impressions of this unique journey. Here you will say goodbye with a warm Ma'a as-salama and a smile that will preserve the memory of the unforgettable experiences in Egypt. Full of impressive encounters, breathtaking temples and majestic landscapes, you will start your journey home. If you would like to extend your adventure, we would be happy to offer you a luxurious bathing extension at the Red Sea, surrounded by beautiful beaches and turquoise blue water. May the journey continue in your heart and soon lead you to new adventures – we look forward to seeing you again!

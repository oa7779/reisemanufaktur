Zimbabwe — A Journey of Wonder

Zimbabwe Round Trip: This trip inspires you for your individual and private trip with the Africa expert Africa Royal Tours. Now request.

Zimbabwe is an interesting and above all undiscovered safari destination. In addition to historical highlights and varied landscapes, Zimbabwe is characterized above all by its animal-rich national parks Matopos and Hwange. The crowning conclusion is the visit to the majestic Victoria Falls, which you will certainly enchant. Join us on a discovery tour and get to know an original country and the real Africa.

Your unique trip to Zimbabwe begins with your arrival at Harare International Airport. There, a representative of Africa Royal Tours warmly welcomes you and takes you to your accommodation for the following night: the Armadale Lodge. Relax from the flight and refresh yourself a little. The rest of the day is at your disposal. Originally the lodge was built as a farmhouse in the middle of a large landscaped garden. Today it is located in a quiet suburb of Harare. The beautiful gardens have an incredible charm. For its guests, Armadale Lodge offers nine elegantly furnished suites, each with either a private veranda or a private garden. On an (optional) excursion you will discover the "Sunshine City", just 15 minutes away, with its tree-lined streets and sights. With the National Gallery of Zimbabwe, the Mbare Nusika Market and the many city parks, Harare offers a collection of attractions. Overnight at Armadale Lodge.

Today will take you to Masvingo – also known as Fort Victoria until 1982. Masvingo is a nice town and the capital of the province of Masvingo. During the three and a half hour drive you will get an impression of the lush savannah landscape as well as the rich flora and fauna. In the afternoon you can reach your Lodge at the Ancient City. With its unique reed roof and stone architecture, it is reminiscent of African royal palaces from ancient times and exudes the splendor of the mysterious Great Zimbabwe Monument – the atmosphere will give you a chance to visit the Great Ruins tomorrow. In the architecture of the lodge built on a granite rock ledge, existing boulders were perfectly integrated into the surrounding forest. The spacious chalets are equipped with spacious bathrooms; large, heavy wooden doors remind of old palace entrances. The main area of the lodge is slightly higher – there is a lounge, a dining area and a bar. From the many viewing platforms and even from the swimming pool you have an unforgettable view of the unspoilt wilderness of Zimbabwe. You can explore the nearby Mutirikwi Lake on a game drive or foot safari organised by the lodge. Overnight at Lodge at the Ancient City.

Go on a tour of the Great Zimbabwe Ruins today. The ruins are divided into three groups: the hill complex, the valley complex and the Great enclosure. This UNESCO World Heritage Site was discovered only in 1871 and is considered one of the most important monuments in Africa. From here, your journey continues to Bulawayo, the second largest city in Zimbabwe, to Harare, known as "the city of smoking fires." In Bulawayo, the majority of the population consists of the ethnic group of the Ndebele tribe. On your trip to Bulawayo, take a break to eat your lunch in the form of a packed lunch. After an exciting day, you will arrive at Banff Lodge, which is just ten minutes from the center of Bulawayo. The family-run lodge is located in a quiet location, surrounded by a lush garden and thus guarantees a relaxing stay. Overnight at Banff Lodge.

You have all day at your free disposal. Relax in your hotel, explore the city or choose from one of the many hotel activities and spend your day in the surrounding area. We recommend a full-day visit to Matopos National Park, where you can see San Rock paintings and Cecil John Rhodes' tomb. Cecil Rhodes was the name given to Zimbabwe, originally known as Rhodesia. Matopos has been a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2003 and is one of the most important nature reserves in Zimbabwe. The National Park forms the core of the Matobo or Matopos Hills, whose areas are characterized by granite rocks and wooded valleys. The hills were formed over two billion years ago when granite was pushed to the surface from the depths of the earth. The so-called broken "Kopjes" (rocks) were scattered along with ste information in the vegetation thicket. Experience a fascinating wealth of colours and shapes that are of great importance to the locals. In addition to its beautiful nature, Matopos National Park is known for its many wild animals, including rhinos, zebras, giraffes, gnus and leopards. Overnight at Banff Lodge.

After breakfast (depending on opening hours and optional) visit the National Museum of Bulawayo. Nature follows the culture: Your journey then leads you north into Hwange National Park, Zimbabwe's largest national park, with many wild animals. The park is located in the west, between Bulawayo and the Victoria Falls, was founded in 1928 and is very varied in landscape. In the afternoon, you can go on a game drive to the park. The chances of spotting the "Big 5" and also the rarer animals such as the African wildcats, white-tailed mongooses and bat-eared foxes are great. Especially at the waterholes you should get buffalo, elephant herds, crocodiles and hippos in front of the lens. Near your accommodation, the Ivory Safari Lodge, is one of the most popular drinking holes; since the lodge is built on stilts in the crowns of the trees, you can watch the herds of animals and enjoy the view of the surroundings from the platforms. Overnight at Ivory Safari Lodge.

The wild savannah, such as Impalas, Kudus, Elan antelopes, bushbucks and waterbucks, are often found in herds in Hwange National Park. The park is also known for its amazing predatory occurrences, including lions, leopards, cheetahs, wild dogs, hyenas and birds of prey. Also large herds of elephants and buffalos can be observed well, sometimes even in groups with well over 200 animals! Since there is so much to see here, spend the whole day in Hwange National Park. Ivory is a family-friendly lodge; a maximum of 18 guests are accommodated here at the same time. The construction on stilts has several advantages: on the one hand, you can better observe the animals – and at the same time are better protected from them. But the temperature also plays a role: the increased construction ensures cooling in the hot and a better heat effect in the cold months. Each room is located between one and five meters above the ground and offers a unique view of the water holes. To lie in bed and look at the elephants at eye level – simply unforgettable. Each unit has a terrace with seating. So you can enjoy the sunsets in complete privacy. Overnight at Ivory Safari Lodge.

The morning begins with a morning game drive; then we continue directly to the Victoria Falls. These are located on the Sambesi River on the border between Zambia and Zimbabwe and are undoubtedly among the most impressive natural wonders on earth. Once the British naturalist Stanley Livingstone discovered the cases and named them Queen Victoria in honor of the Queen of Great Britain. From a distance you can see the spray rising up to 25 meters. Due to the constant "water smoke" the locals also call the cases "dinner smoke" ("Mosi-oa-Tunya"). The adjacent national park of the same name is located on the Zambian side of the Victoria Falls and extends over a length of about twelve kilometers. Once you arrive at your lodge in Victoria Falls, you can enjoy the amenities of your Ilala Lodgee. Overnight at Ilala Lodge.

Today you will take a tour to the Victoria Falls on the Zimbabwean side. Give yourself a lot of time to let the dimensions of the huge waterfalls affect you. Back in your lodge you can just relax or book one of the many optional excursion options. Overnight at Ilala Lodge.

Today, unfortunately, it is time to say goodbye to the past eventful days in Zimbabwe. After the check-out your journey ends with the transfer to Victoria Falls Airport. If you haven't had enough, how about an extension in Botswana? We are happy to work out an extension program for you.

The Seychelles – a paradise in the Indian Ocean awaits you! Discover the dreamlike island world of this unique archipelago: from the famous granite rocks and white sand beaches of La Digue to the tropical landscapes of Mahé and Praslin to the remote Outer Islands, scattered in the endless blue of the Indian Ocean. Enjoy crystal clear water, colorful coral reefs and a fascinating wildlife of animals and plants. Explore the islands comfortably by plane or ferry and experience the Seychelles relaxed, exclusive and full of unforgettable nature experiences.

Day 1: Welcome to Mahé Island – Anantara MAIA Seychelles Villas

Experience Mahé – the largest and most versatile island in the Seychelles – in a perfect combination of exclusivity, comfort and tropical authenticity. 
When you arrive, you will experience special comfort: the VIP service at the airport accompanies you through your arrival and enables a fast and pleasant handling. After that, the transfer to your resort takes place and you already enjoy a relaxed start on the way there. 

2.-5. Day Between Tropical Elegance and Island Adventures

The coming days in the exclusive Anantara MAIA Seychelles Villas invite you to get to know the Seychelles from its most beautiful side. Located high above the turquoise Indian Ocean, the resort combines luxurious privacy, stunning nature and exceptional personal service. Your villa with private pool becomes a very special retreat. At the same time, Mahé offers countless opportunities for small and large discoveries. With the three-day rental car, you can explore the island individually and follow the spectacular coastal roads to hidden coves, palm-lined beaches and impressive viewpoints. A visit to the charming capital Victoria, the colourful market or the famous beaches of the island can not be missed as well as culinary delights with fresh fish and Creole specialties. If you want to experience nature actively, you will find beautiful trails through tropical rainforests in Morne-Seychellois National Park, while the crystal clear waters around Mahé invite you to snorkel and swim. In the months of September and October there is also a rare opportunity to observe whale sharks off the coast with a little luck.

6.-9. Day Desroches Island – Exclusivity in the middle of the sea

After a relaxing breakfast at Anantara MAIA Seychelles Villas, the next highlight of your Seychelles trip awaits you. The convenient transfer to the airport and a short flight will take you beyond the shimmering Indian Ocean until the private coral island of Desroches Island appears on the horizon – a tropical paradise far from all the hustle and bustle. Four Seasons Resort Seychelles at Desroches Island offers exclusive comfort in the midst of the unspoilt island world of Seychelles. Wide sandy beaches, crystal clear water and a high level of privacy create a calm and relaxed atmosphere. You spend the days on Desroches Island in your own rhythm. Explore the island by bike, discover the fascinating underwater world by snorkeling, stroll along the miles of sandy beaches or simply indulge yourself in the luxury of doing nothing at all and letting the view wander over the endless blue of the Indian Ocean. A special landmark of the island is the historic lighthouse of Desroches, from which a wonderful view over the palm landscape and the surrounding sea opens up. Excellent cuisine and the exceptional nature of this private island make your stay one of the highlights of your trip. 

10-14. Day Felicite Island - Days full of island magic

After a relaxing breakfast on Desroches Island, we head back to Mahé, where your helicopter is already ready to fly on to Félicité Island. This flight offers special views: among you is the unique island world of the Seychelles - turquoise lagoons, shimmering coral reefs, small sand islands and the characteristic granite islands of the Innerislands - a panorama that you will not forget so quickly. Arriving at Félicité, the exclusive Six Senses Zil Pasyon awaits you, spectacularly nestled between mighty granite rocks and tropical vegetation. Enjoy exceptional privacy, generous comfort and fantastic views over the Indian Ocean. Also the impressively integrated Six Senses Spa invites you to relax hours in a special ambience. Spend the days according to your own rhythm. Start the morning with a relaxing breakfast with sea views, explore the small paths of the island later or simply enjoy the tranquility of your spacious villa and the view of the ocean. Félicité is a place where you can do a lot – but sometimes doing nothing is the most beautiful. 

Day 15 Farewell to the Seychelles

With a last helicopter flight back to Mahé and the subsequent transfer to the international airport, your extraordinary journey to the Seychelles ends. Behind you are days full of unique impressions – from lush tropical forests and spectacular granite landscapes to turquoise lagoons and secluded dream beaches to some of the most exclusive island retreats in the Indian Ocean. The Seychelles show once more that they are far more than just an island paradise for bathing holidaymakers. Each island has its own character, its own history and its very special natural wonders. They start their journey home with unforgettable memories of a fascinating island world, which will have a long lasting effect with its diversity, its beauty and its relaxed way of life.

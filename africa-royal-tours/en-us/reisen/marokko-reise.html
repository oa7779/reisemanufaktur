Luxury Morocco — An Ocean Breeze

A tailor-made luxury journey along Morocco’s coast with the Travel Atelier Advisors at Africa Royal Tours in Munich. Inquire today.

Your batteries are empty and you're looking for a vacation where you're guaranteed not to think of the Dow Jones? Then this trip is the right thing. You can relax in selected luxury accommodations with the best spa and thalasso centres on the Atlantic coast of Morocco. Let your skin be pampered by the fresh sea air and uniquely pleasant treatments. This journey is ideal for combining calm and serenity in the Mediterranean sunny climate with short discovery tours into the oriental world of Morocco – a well-being journey for all senses.

In Agadir you will already be welcomed by an Africa Royal Tours representative who will help you with the entry formalities. Your chauffeur, who is at your disposal throughout your stay in Morocco, awaits you outside the airport and will take you directly to your luxurious accommodation, The View Agadir, right on the private beach. There you spend the next three nights. The View Agadir impresses with comfort and exclusivity. Elegant rooms, excellent cuisine and the enchanting Amaya wellness oasis. On 1500 m2 your senses are enchanted with soothing massages, authentic Hammam ceremonies, saunas, whirlpools, a heated indoor swimming pool and a stylish relaxation area. As the capital of the Souss Massa region, Agadir offers a diverse cultural offer. The area is perfect for exploring, because there is something for every traveler: the Berber culture, craftsmanship and architectural styles Agadirs, encounters with the local population, the almost endless expanse and the beaches right outside the door, including the mild sea climate. On the second day you are looking forward to a full day trip to the beautiful beaches of Mirleft and Legzira. On your way back to the hotel, stop in the charming town of Tiznit. Overnight stays at The View Agadir.

On the itinerary between Agadir and the next large coastal town of Essaouira lies the small coastal village of Imi Ouaddar. Here there are lonely sandy beaches to the horizon. In paradisiacal surroundings you spend two days here to live in the rhythm of the ocean. In the afternoon you can explore the nearby Paradiestal with your private guide, where you can enjoy a picnic in wonderful surroundings after a small hike. Once you have arrived at your Paradis Plage Surf Yoga & Spa Resort, the rest of the day is at your disposal. Take, for example, a yoga class. Or find relaxation in the treatments in the in-house spa. Of course, you can also do it a bit more sporty and fall into the Atlantic waves together with your private surf instructor. Your Hotel Paradis Plage is the first and only surf, yoga and spa resort in Morocco and is located in the Agadir region. It is located in the middle of a hilly landscape and is surrounded by a five-kilometer-long beach. Thanks to the many lounges and seating options, the restaurant and the beach bar as well as the swimming pool and the surf house right on the beach, you will surely also find your favorite place. Overnight at Paradis Plage.

Today your journey continues along the coast to the north into the "City of the Passatwinde", Essaouira, also known as the pearl of the Atlantic Ocean. This place enchants with its special charm, characterized by white-blue houses, historic city walls and culinary delights. Once known as Mogador, Essaouira is a mecca of colors, art galleries and the scent of spices lies in the air. You will stay at the wonderful L'Heure Bleue Palais, a historic riad and a member of the prestigious Relais & Châteaux Association. The hotel offers spacious rooms and suites with elegant furnishings, marble bathrooms and partly Italian showers or balconies. Enjoy Moroccan cuisine outdoors on the Courtyard Patio or light lunch on the panoramic terrace. Relax in the English Lounge with piano and fireplace or use the Hammam and massage room for perfect relaxation. Overnight at L ́Heure Bleue Palais.

Experience Essaouira from its colorful side today. First, take part in an art and spa experience. Then explore the medina of Essaouira with its many small art galleries and local craft businesses. Experience the colourful and vibrant souks of the city – their sales and the booming for the attention of potential customers are a truly exotic spectacle and a must-see during every visit to Morocco. End your trip at the famous beach of Sidi Kaouki to enjoy the fresh sea air one last time. By the way, Essaouira is a UNESCO World Heritage Site – not without reason: In the Medina, an architectural masterpiece of the next one follows. For example, the fortress is very impressive. For dinner, return to the hotel. Afterwards, the extensive offer in the "So Spa" (for a fee) is available. Take the opportunity to end the evening with a refreshing dip in the swimming pool. Overnight at L'Heure Bleue Palais.

The small village of Oualidia is famous for its oyster breeding and the flocks of flamingos. This idyllic fishing village is one of the preferred destinations for wealthy Moroccans. Oualidia is nevertheless an insider tip that offers peace and quiet throughout the year, whether fishing, sailing or bird watching. The fresh seafood, which are among the best on the Atlantic coast, is also a treat. La Sultana Oualidia is located a good bit outside the village – so close to the sea that its outer walls are almost flushed by the water at high tide. The luxurious wellness hotel offers numerous amenities, including an infinity pool, a sun deck with hot tub and cozy lounges. The pool's water seems to merge with the lagoon in front of it. Over the garden the path leads up to the twelve beautiful rooms. Each individual radiates an individual character: in bright colours, with artworks and special furnishings, they complete the elegant and equally unobtrusive Moroccan charm of the resort. From your own terrace you have a fantastic view of the lagoon. Overnight stays in La Sultana Oualidia.

Goodbye Morocco! After breakfast, your driver will take you back to Agadir Airport. You haven't had enough of fascinating Morocco yet? Why don't you spend a few more days in Marrakech? We like to work out a connection program for you. The price is available on request.

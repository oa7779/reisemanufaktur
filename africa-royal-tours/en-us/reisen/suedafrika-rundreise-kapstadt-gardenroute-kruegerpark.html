Cape Town & Kruger — Along South Africa’s Most Scenic Routes

South Africa Round Trip: With Africa Royal Tours you will discover the most beautiful routes in South Africa - from Kruger National Park to Cape Town. Now request.

16 days

Your trip inspiration

A sample itinerary — tailored to you

  • Africa Royal Tours
  • South Africa
  • Round trips South Africa

A varied journey that ideally combines South Africa's natural and cultural treasures awaits you. We have prepared a trip exclusively for you along the two most beautiful routes in South Africa: the Garden Route and the Panorama Route. Paradise landscapes, impressive national parks and beautiful sandy beaches are on the way. You will stay in small, authentic hotels and lodges and have plenty of time to explore and enjoy the surroundings in peace. Let yourself be enchanted by the beauty and versatility of the rainbow nation South Africa.

Trip information

Itinerary

16 days Johannesburg – Panorama route – Kruger National Park – Eswatini (Swasiland) – St.Lucia – Durban – Port Elizabeth – St. Francis Bay – Knysna – Gansbaai – Cape Town

Your personal highlights

  • Stunning natural landscapes
  • Dreamy sandy beaches
  • Small, authentic hotels and lodges
  • Let yourself be inspired by the feeling of boundless freedom
  • Wildlife observations in the famous Kruger National Park
  • Sufficient time to get to know the visited places in detail

After your arrival at Johannesburg airport, you will be warmly welcomed by your representative of Africa Royal Tours. Then you drive to your hotel together. Once there, the rest of the day is at your free disposal. The Peech Boutique Hotel is located in the Melrose district, just outside Johannesburg. With only 16 rooms it has a very intimate atmosphere. In the beautiful garden with swimming pool you can really relax from the long flight. Overnight at The Peech Boutique Hotel.

Together with your Driverguide you start today towards the Mpumalanga province. In the late afternoon you will reach your hotel. The Blyde River Canyon Nature Reserve with the spectacular nature features of Blyde river canyon, God's Window and Bourke's Luck Potholes is located in the immediate vicinity and will be visited by you on Day 3. Overnight at Olivers Restaurant & Lodge.

The next highlight of your trip awaits you: the Kruger National Park, where you will spend the next two nights. The park is the largest, oldest and most popular park in South Africa. The grandiose and diverse wildlife can be observed particularly well here – the whole in front of an impressive backdrop with extensive steppes and grass landscapes as well as rivers and water holes that attract the wildlife to drink. With a little luck, huge herds with buffalos, zebras, gnus and antelopes and many more await you here. Afterwards, you will have the opportunity to explore the exclusive Lukimbi Safari Lodge and rest a bit. In the afternoon you start your first game drive, which takes place together with other guests in an open off-road car with an experienced ranger of the lodge. Together, you are looking for the Big Five. On Day 5, you will take a safari on foot, where you will experience the untouched nature first hand from a completely different perspective. The focus is not necessarily on the big animals, but rather on their traces and the smaller bush dwellers. If you like, you can also go on another game drive. Overnight stays at the Lukimbi Safari Lodge.

After an early breakfast we say goodbye to the Kruger National Park. The next highlight is already waiting for you: It continues towards Eswatini. Eswatini is an independent small kingdom that holds many natural beauties and is particularly proud of its cultural heritage. You will visit the Mantenga Cultural Village and the Ezulwini Craft Market. Overnight in Eswatini.

Your journey takes you to the coast – it continues towards St. Lucia. Here it can happen that wart pigs and hippos cross the road – these of course always have an advance. On the St. Lucia See take a boat trip and spot hippos, crocodiles and, of course, many different bird species. Overnight at Serene-estate Boutique Guesthouse.

After breakfast, say goodbye to St. Lucia and follow the coast of the Indian Ocean towards the south. On the way, sugarcane fields, small coastal towns and views of the ocean pass by you. In the afternoon you will reach Durban, the lively metropolis of KwaZulu-Natal with its relaxed atmosphere and multicultural flair. Overnight at Lalaria Lodge.

Ihr Chauffeur bringt Sie zum Flughafen von Durban und Sie fliegen von dort nach Port Elizabeth. Vor Ort werden Sie von Ihrem neuen Fahrer-Guide erwartet und setzen Ihre Tour nach St. Francis Bay fort. Der beliebte Küstenort am Indischen Ozean ist ideal, um die Strände und die nahegelegenen Naturreservate zu erkunden. Ein besonderes Highlight: der Leuchtturm von Cape St. Francis aus dem Jahr 1878. Mit der schroffen Felsenküste im Hintergrund ist er ein tolles Fotomotiv. Der Garten Ihrer heutigen Unterkunft reicht bis zum Sandstrand hinab. Genießen Sie die Ausblicke auf den Indischen Ozean. Übernachtung in The Sands @ St Francis.

This day is dedicated to the Garden Route. You drive along the picturesque coast through the Tsitsikamma National Park, where you can also see the impressive Stormsriver suspension bridge. If you like, take a hike to the famous viewpoint. On the ride you will pass the popular place of Plettenberg Bay or "Plett" – as the fashionable seaside resort of South Africans is called. It has the most beautiful beaches on the Gardenroute and is world famous for its picturesque scenery. The water sports are varied and depending on the season you can watch whales and dolphins. In the afternoon you will reach your destination – the charming place Knysna. It is nestled between forests and a lagoon, framed by two rocks that rise directly from the sea, the Knysna Heads. In the bay is the largest oyster breeding in South Africa. Your Boutique Hotel – The Turbine Boutique Hotel & Spa – has 24 rooms, all of which are decorated differently and create a cozy atmosphere with modern and simply elegant style elements and warm tones. The former power station has a certain wow effect. Overnight at The Turbine Boutique Hotel & Spa.

Today, the Outeniqua Pass leads to Oudtshoorn, where the Cango Caves can be seen. They belong to the largest stalactite cave system in the world. The beauty of this "underworld" will impress you. In addition to the regular tour, there is also a more demanding variant for adventurers, where you have to crawl and slip through narrow crevices. Afterwards you visit one of the ostrich farms in Oudtshoorn. In the evening you will return to Knysna. Overnight at The Turbine Boutique Hotel & Spa.

Enjoy fantastic views and the breathtaking wild romantic scenery of the coast on your trip to Gansbaai. Your destination for today is Gootbos Forest Lodge. The 1,000-hectare nature reserve extends over the foothills of the Walker Bay dunes and offers panoramic views to the Cape of Good Hope. The gourmet cuisine is characterized by fine and fresh ingredients, seafood and other South African specialties and is accompanied by first-class South African wines. Guests can experience a variety of culinary delights combined with unique hospitality and professionalism while enjoying the fascinating view of Walker Bay. The 16 spacious suites in the modern and elegant Forest Lodge are bright and friendly decorated in natural tones. Each suite offers a cozy lounge with the finest furnishings, a spacious bedroom, a private veranda with stunning views over Walker Bay and an elegant bathroom. In the afternoon and in the morning you can participate in guided walks or Nature Drives through the nature reserve (included in the price). In the whale season you can book an optional boat trip to watch the whale. Overnight at Forest Lodge.

Not only wine lovers will get their money's worth today: your route will take you through the well-known wine-growing areas at the gates of Cape Town, on the way you will make a stop in one of the renowned wineries of South Africa for a wine tasting. At the Vergelegen Winery you can taste delicious white and red wines and enjoy a gourmet picnic. Get to know South Africa's wine specialities and the secrets of the Celts. In the evening you can reach Cape Town at the foot of Table Mountain. Overnight at Blackheath Lodge.

Today you drive to the Cape of Good Hope. An absolute must on your dream trip! You will travel along one of the most beautiful routes in South Africa along the beautiful coastal road to the bays of Camps Bay, Hout Bay and across the famous panoramic coastal road "Chapman's Peak" at False Bay to the Cape of Good Hope – the most southwestern point of the African continent. Your driver guide will show you the most beautiful corners of the Cape. On the way back, you will pass Boulders Beach by the penguins and, if the weather permits, you will be able to join you in the water. Overnight in Cape Town.

Today is available for explorations on your own (without driver guide and vehicle). A wealth of highlights awaits you: the beautiful beaches, such as Clifton or Camps Bay, invite you to relax and sunbathe. For excursions the Table Mountain, Robben Island, Kirstenbosch and many other attractions offer. Overnight at Blackheath Lodge.

Today, unfortunately, it is time to say goodbye to the enchanting South Africa. Just in time for your return flight, your driver will take you to the Cape Town airport from where you will travel home. We are happy to organize a bath extension e.g. on the Seychelles or Mauritius for you (price on request).

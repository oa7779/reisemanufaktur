South Africa Self-Drive — The Finest of South Africa

Car Rental Round Trip South Africa: Enjoy Africa at its finest with Africa Royal Tours, your Africa expert from Munich. Now request.

We invite you to get to know the breathtaking South Africa in a very special way - take your own journey of discovery with your rental car. Your route leads you along the most beautiful places and you have enough time for your own exploration tours. Enjoy the exclusive hotels, exciting excursions and the culinary specialties of the country. This trip will give you extensive insights into beautiful South Africa, which will remain in your memory for a long time to come.

Trip information

Itinerary

15 days Cape Town – Hermanus – Robertson – Oudtshoorn – Plettenberg Bay – Swellenda – Stellenbosch – Cape Town

Your personal highlights

  • Roadster Feeling and driving fun with your own convertible in Cape Town
  • Wine-growing areas
  • Culinary specialities
  • Gardenroute
  • Spend several days in a place without a tour guide
  • Get to know the daily life on site and country & people

After your arrival in Cape Town, you will be welcomed by your chauffeur from Africa Royal Tours and you will receive all other important information. Then the transfer to the rental car rental, where you will receive your rental car for the next few days. The goal for today is the beautiful Hermanus. We recommend to drive over the lookout road Clarence Drive. This route is spectacular again and again and so the path is the destination. On the way you can make nice stops to enjoy local specialties; delicious wines are available in the nearby Hemel & Aarde Valley. Hermanus is known worldwide for its great spots on the mainland, from which you can observe whales very well. The luxurious Hotel Birkenhead House & Villa is the perfect place for anyone who longs for a life by the sea. The hotel impresses with the great blend of Asian and African antiques, the great service and the relaxed but classic atmosphere – here you can really indulge yourself and enjoy life to the fullest. The area around Walker Bay has a lot to offer: original, rough landscapes and rugged coastal areas crossed by hiking trails, which can be easily reached from the hotel. Overnight at Birkenhead House & Villa.

Your exciting journey continues and leads you today to the traditional Robertson, where many things revolve around wine growing. The journey takes you through the hinterland via Route 62, the longest wine route in the world. The breathtaking passes, small villages, wine and fruit farms as well as the varied landscapes will impress you. They should definitely look for the blue cranes, which occur here numerous and have been chosen because of their beauty and rarity as a national bird of South Africa. In Robertson you can take great culinary excursions through fantastic landscapes. Here you will also spend the next two nights at the Robertson Small Hotel, which is located right on Route 62 in the heart of Robertson. In this 5-star hotel you will find pure luxury and excellent cuisine. The area around Robertson offers great wineries for sightseeing and nice driving routes. Overnight at Robertson Small Hotel.

The drive continues to the ostrich metropolis Oudtshoorn. It is a relatively long route, but definitely worth it. From a landscape point of view, the route 62 is definitely more spectacular than the route N2 – but even the motorways are very pittoresque in this area. Decide for yourself which route you prefer. The next two days are available for exploring the area or simply for relaxing. You will stay at Rosenhof Country House. The country house in luxurious style owes its name to the wonderful rose garden from the 18th century. With its stylishly furnished rooms, excellent cuisine and excellent service, Rosenhof has established itself as one of the best addresses in the region. Nearby are the Cango Wildlife Ranch, as well as the Buffelsdrift Game Lodge and the Safari Ostrich Farm, where a visit is definitely worthwhile. Overnight at Rosenhof Country House.

Your next destination: Plettenberg Bay. On today's stage, the pearl of the Gardenroute awaits you: crossing the most beautiful pass – the Outeniqua Pass. Your trip will take you to Plettenberg Bay, or simply "Plett" – as the chic seaside resort is simply called by South Africans. The place can offer the most beautiful beaches on the Garden Route. On your trip to your accommodation, a stop in Tsitsikamma National Parks is recommended, where you can also see the impressive Storms River suspension bridge. The next two days will be at your free disposal to make trips to the beautiful surroundings with your car rental or to enjoy the amenities of your hotel. Overnight at Tsala Treetop Lodge.

Now we're heading back to "Mother City" Cape Town, but before that you're going to make two more stopovers, including in Swellendam. The route is very nice and always offers something for the eye. On such beautiful rides you can only see how diverse the landscape of South Africa is – the whole world unites in a single country! Swellendam has a lot to offer historically, especially since it is the third oldest city in the country. Eavesdropping tea gardens invite you to linger and relax. You stay at the Schoone Oordt Country House. The country house is located in Swellendam on a 5,000 square meter garden plot. The Victorian property is now a popular landmark of Swellendam. All rooms are lovingly furnished with antique furniture, on hot summer days a pool invites you to cool down and relax. Overnight at Schoone Oordt Country House.

After breakfast, your journey continues straight to Stellenbosch, your last stop before you can look forward to the big city flair in Cape Town. Simon van der Stel is the patron saint of this famous university town, although the wines of the region are probably much more famous. Stellenbosch, Paarl and Franschhoek represent top quality wines, Cape Dutch mansions, culinary delights and also history rich in tradition. During a walk through Stellenbosch you can explore the town with beautiful cafes and shops. The wineries in the area create the best wines in the country and you should definitely test this with a wine tasting. You will stay at Delaire Graff Estate in an exclusive villa with pool. The property also has a very nice wellness area and a top restaurant. Overnight at Delaire Graff Estate.

Today you're going back to Cape Town. The city awaits with its abundance of highlights. To finish off, switch your rental car to a convertible and you can enjoy the Roadster Feeling in and around Cape Town and let the air blow around your nose during your discovery tours. We are happy to help you plan your activities in Cape Town – you won't get bored. The Waterfront is always great and the beautiful beaches such as Clifton Bay or Camps Bay invite you to relax and sunbathe. Exciting activities such as Table Mountain, Cape of Good Hope and many other attractions await you. The next three nights will be spent at the exclusive Hotel The Silo, which is located directly in Cape Town Harbour. The interior is made up of a diverse collection of art artifacts. Each suite is unique and has been furnished with great attention to detail. In the Silo SPA you can enjoy a massage or use the gym and sauna. The outdoor pool on the roof of the hotel as well as the associated bar give you a fabulous view over Cape Town. The restaurants serve culinary delights. Overnight at The Silo.

Until your return flight, the day is at your free disposal. You will return to Cape Town where you will return your rental car. We are happy to offer you a relaxing bathing vacation in Mozambique or on the island of Mauritius. Prices on request.

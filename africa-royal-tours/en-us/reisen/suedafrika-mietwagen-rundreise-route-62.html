1st day Arrival in Cape Town

Welcome to South Africa! After your arrival at Cape Town Airport, you will be warmly welcomed by your German-speaking Africa Royal Tours representative and taken to your hotel, which is located in the heart of the "Mothercity".

2nd day Cape Town and Lions Head

Today, together with your German-speaking guide, you will take a city tour of Cape Town on foot. You will discover the cobbled streets and colorful houses of the Cape-Malayen district, explore the Company Garden, the green lung of Cape Town and visit the Castle, the first building on the Cape. You will stay today and the next nights at Blackheath Lodge. The two-story Victorian guest house is located in the Three Anchor Bay district below Signal Hills, near the city center, the port and the famous beaches. Built in 1880, this house has spacious rooms, high ceilings and wooden floors and impresses with its unmistakable African style and charm. It offers elegant rooms, excellent personal service and warm hospitality. Overnight in Cape Town.

Day 3 Cape of Good Hope

Enjoy the next few days in Cape Town and explore the impressive metropolis of Cape Town and its surroundings free of schedules and time pressure on your own. Your rental car will be delivered directly to the hotel. Why don't you go to the Cape of Good Hope today? An absolute must on your dream trip! You will travel along one of the most beautiful routes in South Africa along the beautiful coastal road to the bays of Camps Bay, Hout Bay and across the famous panoramic coastal road "Chapman's Peak" at False Bay to the Cape of Good Hope – the most southwestern point of the African continent. Here you have the opportunity to go up to Cape Point by cable car. On the way back, you should definitely stop by the penguins at Boulders Beach and, if the weather allows, join you right in the water. Overnight in Cape Town.

Day 4 of Table Mountain

What would Cape Town be without Table Mountain? You can't imagine. Make it a driveway to Table Mountain this morning. (weather dependent, in the case of too strong wind the railway is closed). Arriving at the top, you have a panoramic view of its kind. With the cable car it goes up and down comfortably. Then you start to the next highlight of your trip. Just outside Cape Town are the beautiful wine areas: Green rolling hills covered with full vines and old houses in Cape Dutch style. Several hundred years old, many of these wineries produce wines that have won many awards and are known all over the world. Delightful wine cities are Stellenbosch and Franschhoek, which always lure their visitors back. You will spend the next two nights at Jordan Wine Estate, a beautiful winery near Stellenbosch. You can enjoy your breakfast on your own terrace overlooking the vineyards. At noon and in the evening you have the option to eat at the in-house restaurant. Overnight at Stellenbosch.

Day 5 Explore the Wine Region

Who likes it a little more sporty and wants to experience the nature of the Winelands so right, is right here! While the warm air blows around the ears, one drives in a small group through the beautiful wine fields of the goods. One or the other mountain can already push the sweat on your forehead, but it is always worth the closeness to nature. At the various stops on the wine farms, the best drops of the good can be tried and at the same time new power can be filled. Overnight in Stellenbosch.

Day 6 In harmony with nature

Route 62 is one of the most beautiful routes through South Africa, and at the same time one of the least known and busy roads. Travelers on the way from Cape Town to Port Elizabeth choose this route as an interesting, relaxing alternative to the heavily frequented N2, which runs further south through the coastal strip. The country road winds through the fertile valleys of the Kleine Karoo, past rugged rock massifs, whose summits are covered with snow in winter. Again and again steep passes are overcome with spectacular views of a wide landscape. Its first stop is the 54,000-hectare Sanbona Wildlife Reserve, one of the largest private reserves in South Africa, characterized by gentle mountains, diverse vegetation, stealing and the vastness of the semi-desert. Explore this unique nature with experienced guides and learn a lot about the local flora and fauna and the many unique wildlife. The Dwyka Tented Lodge is located in an overwhelming landscape surrounded by impressive stone formations and on the edge of a gorge in the dry semi-desert Karoo. Nine luxurious and private tent chalets exude the charm of the pioneering period and are equipped with their own small pool. Overnight at the Sanbona Wildlife Reserve.

7th day Sanbona Wildlife Reserve

Explore the Sanbona Wildlife Reserve with an experienced guide on an open four-wheel drive, go on a guided hiking tour or take part in a boat safari. The Sanbona Wildlife Reserve has a lot to offer! Then you end the day at your own pool, while enjoying the view of the beautiful panorama. Overnight at the Sanbona Wildlife Reserve.

8th day Oudtshoorn

The next destination of your trip is the ostrich center Oudtshoorn. The place is located in the enchanting "Little Karoo". Oudtshoorn is the largest town of the Little Karoo and the stronghold of ostrich breeding. Even today, the stately palaces of the "outer barons" during the boom still adorn the cityscape. A visit to an ostrich farm is worthwhile and should not be missed on your trip. An absolute must is also the visit of the Cango Caves. This is a limestone shaped stalactite cave with gigantic dimensions. The caves are among the most beautiful in the world. You're staying at De Denne Country Guest House today. The guest house offers its guests peace and quiet, spectacular views, special moments, unique sunsets and hospitality that makes you feel at home. Overnight in Oudtshoorn.

9th day Onward journey to Tsitsikamma National Park

The traditional name comes from the Khoi language and means clear or sparkling water. The National Park area comprises a narrow section along the coast, 113 kilometers long. The area is characterized by dense forests with very old trees, high rainfall, many streams and rivers, gorges and a picturesque cliffs. After receiving your picturesque Dolphin Trail Chalet, you can enjoy the leisure time. There are a number of short hiking trails, including the start of the Otter Trail, which you can explore on your own! In the evening there is a meeting place with your English-speaking Dolphin Trail Guide and your fellow travelers in the restaurant of the National Park. Here you get information for the next day and can enjoy a dinner while the waves break on the nearby beach. Overnight in the Tsitsikamma National Park.

10th day walking

Early risers can enjoy the magnificent view of the surrounding area and are cordially invited to watch birds or walk to the restaurant and to strengthen themselves first. Your luggage will be taken to your nearest accommodation. All you need is your day pack. Today you walk to Storms River Mouth. After crossing the Storms River on the suspension bridge, follow the coast with a magnificent view towards Plettenberg Bay. The path continues straight ahead to a long descent at sea level through the centuries-old rainforest. This is the perfect place for your picnic, either in the shade of trees or on the banks of the river on the rocks in the sun. The subsequent ascent is quite strenuous and follows immediately. Once you have climbed the summit, it is a comfortable forest walk to Misty Mountain Reserve. Here you refer to your chalet with 180-degree view brought to the Indian Ocean. Enjoy the beautiful pool or whirlpool of the complex. In the evening you can relax with chilled drinks at sunset and a nice dinner at the lodge. Overnight in the Tsitsikamma National Park.

Day 11: Enchanting Tsitsikamma National Park

After breakfast, follow a steep path from their chalets straight down to the rock pools. Today's stage is especially at sea level and the path along the coast is great. Enjoy the beautiful flora and rocky landscape, and look for traces of the Cape small claw otter. Swimming and snorkeling are possible in the rock pools, you must bring your bathing clothes with you. The lunch picnic is served in peace at a beautiful place in the forest. The last 3 kilometers of the trail lead through Fynbos and Waldfarne. The Fernery is located in the afternoon. Various activities are offered here, such as boating, cycling and bird watching. A swimming pool, jacuzzi and sauna are also available. Dinner is served at the Fernery Lodge's restaurant. Overnight in the Tsitsikamma National Park.

Day 12 Idyllic Knysna

After a delicious breakfast you will be brought back to your car to continue your trip to Knysna. The charming town is a popular vacation home and is nestled between forests and a lagoon, framed by two rocks, the Knysna Heads, that rise directly from the sea. On site you can watch a variety of fish, waterbirds, shrimps and crabs and even seahorses. The beautiful lagoon and more than eight beaches invite you to swim, surf, fish, a jet ski ride, a canoe or boat trip or a beach walk. In the bay there is the largest oyster breeding of South Africa and nowhere in the country you can get such delicious fresh oysters. In the expanded port, the Waterfront, there are good restaurants and shopping facilities. Today's home is Leisure Isle Lodge. Only connected by a narrow dam road to the mainland, it is an island of peace and relaxation. Here you can be awakened by birdsongs, listen to the sea noise and enjoy your breakfast or a Sundowner drink with a postcard-ready view over the lagoon. Overnight in Knysna.

13th day of Wilderness

On the evergreen Garden Route you continue today to Wilderness. Known for its wonderful lagoons, lakes, rivers and picturesque mountains, Wilderness is the ideal place to relax. Take a beach walk on the long sandy beach of Wilderness or hike in the Wilderness National Park. On The Beach is a new boutique guesthouse located right on the beach of Wilderness. From the house it is only a few steps until guests feel the fine sand under their feet. But if you want, you can relax on your own sundeck. On The Beach has 7 luxurious rooms, including 6 with sea views. Overnight in Wilderness.

Day 14: Travel to Gansbaai

Today you continue to the Grootbos Private Nature Reserve. When the morning fog banks dissolve and the view becomes clear on the deep blue Atlantic Ocean, the rugged coastal landscape of Walker Bay and the endless flower fields, pure happiness spreads in you. Grootbos – this is Afrikaans and means "big forest" – offers its guests a unique natural landscape worldwide. Mountains, forests, beach and sea, an incredibly rich fauna, the marine "Big Five" (seasonal dependent: whales, sharks, dolphins, seals and penguins) and the magic of the Milkwood forest make Grootbos a unique experience. Distraction – there is no such thing. No restaurants, no bars, no shops, no cars – the Grootbos Private Nature Reserve is the perfect hideaway from the world in an exclusive atmosphere. Forest Lodge offers its guests accommodation in 16 detached chalets on the edge of a 1,500-year-old Milkwood forest. The Forest Lodge is a masterpiece of design art and exudes an elitist yet cozy ambience. The suites continue this consistently and are certainly among the best accommodations in the Cape region. Grootbos' cuisine is one of the best in the whole Cape region and the wine cellars offer a very wide selection of top wines. Overnight at Grootbos Private Nature Reserve.

15th day Grootbos Nature Reserve

During your stay at the Forest Lodge various activities are available (included in the price). Discover the bird world with more than a hundred different bird species, or the plant world with over five hundred different plant species, with an expert nature guide. You can ride on horseback on the beach or in the hills, or take a two-hour nature ride in the closed four-wheel drive through the Fynbos in a varied landscape. In addition, a 1 to 2 hour walk through the fairytale Milkwood forest, the largest of its kind in Africa, will give you an interesting insight into the flora of the country. Overnight at Grootbos Nature Reserve.

16th day Departure

Your trip today goes to Cape Town airport, where you also return your rental car. Today you have to say goodbye to South Africa. Even if your vacation is over: the beautiful souvenir photos and small souvenirs will always remind you of the time in South Africa.

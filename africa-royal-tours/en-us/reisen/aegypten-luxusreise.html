Egypt Luxury Journey - Glory and Heritage of the Pharaoh's Kingdom

Experience an exclusive Egypt luxury trip: pyramids, temples, Nile & culture up close. Discover Cairo, Alexandria and Hurghada in 12 days luxury.

Discover Egypt – a land of legends, wonders and fascinating history! This extraordinary journey will take you to the most important and mysterious sites of ancient times, where history comes to life. From the monumental heritage of the pyramids of Giza to the festive citadel of Cairo to the historical treasures of Alexandria, you will immerse yourself in a world full of mystical magic and impressive culture. Enjoy exclusive visits, from the ancient Sphinx to the National Museum of Egyptian Civilization. This journey promises not only unforgettable experiences, but also a deep connection to Egypt's unique heritage. Immerse yourself and let yourself be enchanted by the history and culture of this fascinating country!

1st Day Welcome to Cairo!

After your arrival in Cairo, you will be warmly welcomed by your Africa Royal representative and taken to the luxurious Four Seasons Hotel Cairo at The First Residence, where you will spend the following nights. This elegant hotel on the western bank of the Nile in Giza offers a variety of shopping and restaurants. Enjoy stunning views of the Nile River, while just minutes away from the vibrant city center. Guests can relax in the outdoor pool overlooking the city or be pampered by soothing spa treatments.

2nd Day Time Travel to Ancient Egypt

Today, a highlight of your journey awaits you: you will visit the famous pyramids of Giza, built 4,500 years ago for the Pharaohs of Cheops, Chephren and Mykerinos. Stand in front of the Great Pyramid, the last preserved miracle of antiquity, and let yourself be impressed by the mysterious Sphinx. Optionally, you have the possibility to enter the Cheops pyramid or take a camel ride. Then visit the impressive Grand Egyptian Museum (GEM), which is a true jewel of Egyptian history with over 100,000 artifacts, including the entire treasure of Tutankhamun. The panoramic view of the pyramids from the museum will take your breath away.

3rd day of multifaceted Cairo

Your adventure will take you today to visit the Citadel, a medieval fortress built in 1176 to protect Cairo from the Crusaders. This impressive structure sits high above the city and today houses important mosques and museums, including the National Military Museum. The mosque of Sultan Hassan, completed in 1363, is considered one of the most magnificent in Egypt. Continue to the mosque of the Refaie, which was completed in 1912 and impresses with its lively, well-preserved decoration. The day ends with a visit to the famous Khan El-Khalili Bazaar, the largest Bazaar in the Middle East, where you can discover handmade goods in a maze of narrow streets. 

Day 4 Alexandria - The City of History and Culture

Go to Alexandria today after your breakfast. They begin with a visit to the catacombs of Kom El Shugafa, Egypt's largest Roman burial site. This fascinating site, carved into the rocks, combines ancient Egyptian motifs with Greek-Roman style. Continue to the Roman theatre, recently discovered and impressed with its well-preserved marble steps and mosaics. The Alexandria National Museum offers a unique overview of the history of Alexandria, from the pharaoh period to the modern age. Visit also the Bibliotheca Alexandrina, a modern homage to the ancient library of Alexandria, and enjoy the breathtaking view from Qait Bay Fort, which guards the entrance to the legendary port.

Day 5: An Excursion to the Green Heart of Egypt

Today you will discover Fayoum, Egypt's largest oasis. This fertile region is known for its agriculture and its historic water wheels, which since the Ptolemaic period bring the water to the fields. In the afternoon, visit the nearby beautiful Wadi Rayan, a desert with picturesque waterfalls and shady ponds that offer a quiet retreat in the heart of the Egyptian desert. Then you will be driven back to Cairo.

Day 6 Coptic Cairo and the Treasures of Egyptian Culture

Start today in Coptic Cairo, the oldest quarter of the city, and discover the fortress of Babylon, the oldest preserved structure of Cairo, as well as the church of St. Sergius and the hanging church, which for centuries served the Coptic patriarchs as its seat. You can also visit the Church of St. Barbara, a beautiful monument from the 4th century. In the afternoon you will visit the National Museum of Egyptian Civilization (NMEC), which shows Egypt's rich history from ancient Egypt to modern times. Especially impressive is the Royal Mummy Hall, where the mummies of Egyptian pharaohs are exhibited. The museum is a fascinating experience and offers you a deep insight into Egypt's cultural heritage.

7.-11. Day Change of scenery: Flight to Hurghada

After breakfast, say goodbye to the fascinating metropolis of Cairo and fly on to the picturesque coast of the Red Sea. The paradisiacal beaches, colourful coral reefs and exotic sea creatures transform this region into one of the most impressive diving and snorkeling paradises in the world. Arriving in Hurghada, you will be warmly welcomed by your driver and taken to your luxurious beach resort: the renowned The Oberoi Beach Resort, Sahl Hasheesh – a place where relaxation and comfort merge. The noise of the waves and the glittering water create the perfect backdrop to immerse yourself in the moment. Do you like to discover the surroundings actively? Take a snorkeling or diving tour to the colorful coral reefs, explore the fascinating underwater world, on a glass bottom boat trip or let the wind blow over your nose on a private boat tour. 

12th Day Goodbye to Egypt

After a last breakfast at your hotel, we say goodbye to the wonderful moments of this unique journey. Your driver will take you to the airport in Hurghada while you reflect on the impressions of this voyage of discovery through Egypt. At the airport you say goodbye with "Ma'a as-salama". Full of moving encounters and experiences you will start your journey home. We look forward to seeing you soon!

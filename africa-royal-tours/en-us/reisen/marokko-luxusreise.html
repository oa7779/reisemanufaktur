Luxury Morocco — The Imperial Cities

Experience the royal cities of Marrakech, Fes & Rabat on a private 11-day luxury trip to Morocco – with exclusive riads, personal chauffeur & culture.

11 days

Your trip inspiration

A sample itinerary — tailored to you

  • Morocco
  • Morocco Travel

Morocco awaits you with a unique blend of culture, history and nature! Experience the lively Marrakech with its mysterious streets, the spiritual Fes with its centuries-old traditions and the impressive architecture of Meknes. The royal capital Rabat delights with magnificent monuments and fresh breezes. In addition, there are the majestic heights of the Atlas Mountains and the endless expanses of the desert. Let yourself be enchanted by the contrasts and magic of Morocco – an adventure that combines tradition, culture and nature. To the crowning conclusion awaits you a luxurious beach resort, which offers you the perfect place for perfect relaxation!

Welcome to Marrakech, the magical "Pearl of the South"! Already on your arrival, the magic of this royal city surrounds you, which fascinates with its oriental flair and vibrant colors. Your private chauffeur will be waiting for you at the airport to accompany you to your riad. La Sultana Marrakech, a jewel of traditional Moroccan architecture combined with luxurious comfort, offers the ideal retreat in the vibrant heart of the city. Treat yourself to a soothing hammam, or take the opportunity to get a first impression of Marrakech. A relaxing walk on the edge of the medina gives you a taste of the historic city. The view of the buildings characterized by the characteristic red plaster, will let you immerse yourself in the atmosphere of 1001 night. Enjoy the evening with a relaxed dinner on one of the rooftop terraces. Overnight at La Sultana Marrakech.

After a delicious breakfast in your riad, your day begins with a fascinating half-day guided tour with your local guide. Admire the majestic Koutoubia Mosque from the outside and the magnificent Bahia Palace, once considered a masterpiece of Moroccan architecture. In the historic Medersa Ben Youssef you can marvel at the subtleties of Islamic art and architecture, while the charm of the Badiaa Palace takes you back to the days of the glorious Moroccan Empire. Immerse yourself in the heart of the pulsating medina. This maze of colourful markets will captivate you with its scents, sounds and unparalleled energy. Experience the hustle and bustle of the merchants, the artfully processed goods and the aromatic food offered at every corner. The afternoon is at your disposal. You may want to enjoy the tranquility of the well-known gardens such as the Majorelle Garden or the Menara Garden. Or how about a visit to the numerous art galleries? Overnight at La Sultana Marrakech.

Your adventure continues with the journey from the lively city out into the untouched nature. After breakfast, your driver will pick you up at the Riyadh and you will travel through the picturesque Haouz plain. A first stop in the market town of Demnate (on Sundays weekly market) captivates by the impressive rock bridge of Imi N'Ifri, a gigantic rock cave through which a river flows – a natural wonder with an almost mystical atmosphere. Nearby are the petrified dinosaur prints of Iroutlane-Iwarids, relics of a long time ago. Continue your journey to the breathtaking waterfalls of Ouzoud, with 110 meters the highest and for some the most beautiful Morocco. Here you have the opportunity to go up to the falls with a small boat or walk along the river. After this experience, your adventure will take you to the Bin-El-Ouidane reservoir, the lake blends harmoniously into the surrounding mountain landscape. Finally, approach your final destination, the Widiane Resort, a retreat full of peace and elegance. End the day with a dinner. Overnight at Widiane Resort.

After breakfast, say goodbye to the peaceful atmosphere and head towards Afourer. Through Khenifra you will travel to the sources of Oum R'bia, one of Morocco's wateriest rivers. Surrounded by lush nature, dozens of springs sprung from the rock and formed the riverbed – ideal for exploring. Then the trail continues, past Ain Leuh, Azrou and finally to Ifrane, a small town at 1,650 meters above sea level. Often referred to as "Switzerland of Morocco", it is known for its cool mountain air and the renowned Al Akhawayn University. In the afternoon you will reach Fes, the oldest of Morocco's four royal cities. This city is a living testimony of Moroccan history and culture – a place where Jewish influence, Islamic learning, traditional crafts and culinary delights are combined. Your accommodation for the night is the Karawan Riad, a historic townhouse that captivates with incomparable charm and elegance. Hidden in the old town of Fes, the exclusive Riad combines authentic Moroccan architecture with modern comfort. Overnight at the Caravan Riyadh.

After breakfast, your day begins full of discoveries in the oldest of the four royal cities. Your local guide will pick you up at the Riyadh and take you on a fascinating journey through the three faces of Fes: Fes-el-Bali, the winding old town from the 9th century, where almost all historical treasures are located, Fes-l-Jedid, the "new Fes", and the modern Ville Nouvelle with French influence. From Borj Sud you can enjoy a breathtaking view over the city before immersing yourself in the lively medina. Your guide will take you through the Andalusian Quarter, the artfully decorated Koranic schools Medersa Attarine and Bou Inania, and Nejjarine Square with its impressive fountain. The Karaouin Mosque, one of the oldest universities in the world, radiates calmness. In the bustling Souks you will experience the colourful market activity. Another stop will take you to the Royal Palace with its golden gates, the magnificent Bab Boujloud gate and the pottery district, where artful ceramics are created. In the afternoon you have time for your own discoveries. Overnight in Karawan Riyadh.

Today your journey continues to Meknes, the smallest of the four royal cities and former residence of Moulay Ismaël. The city is an important commercial center and known for its excellent wines and the best olive oil of Morocco. You will explore the magnificent Bab el Mansour, one of the most beautiful city gates in the country, as well as Bab el Khemis Gate and Moulay Ismaël Tomb Mosque, an impressive testimony to the city's former size. A culinary highlight awaits you on your way to Volubilis: a delicious lunch with wine tasting where you can taste the noble drops of the region. You will reach the ancient city of Volubilis, which with its well-preserved Roman ruins belongs to the UNESCO World Heritage Site. Once the capital of the westernmost province of the Roman Empire, the imposing remains of prosperity and thriving trade testify. After the tour, you will continue your journey and return to your accommodation where you will end the day. Overnight stay in Karawan Riad

After your breakfast today, leave your hotel and head for Rabat, Morocco's elegant capital and seat of the king. After lunch, explore Rabat together with your guide. You will stop at the Royal Palace, the official seat of the Moroccan monarch, before visiting the Challah, the tomb of the Merinids. Surrounded by lush vegetation and ancient walls, this place unfolds a special atmosphere. Then we continue to the famous Hassan Tower, the unfinished minaret of a once planned huge mosque. Its impressive architecture and location on a hill make it one of the city's landmarks. Directly opposite is the magnificent mausoleum of King Mohammed V, a masterpiece of Moroccan architecture. After this cultural journey of discovery, you will find your room at The White Palace Rabat, a luxurious boutique hotel known for its elegance, stylish design and excellent service. Overnight at The White Palace.

Your journey now takes you to the picturesque Mediterranean Sea and the fascinating city of Tetouan. With its winding streets, the Royal Palace and the unique Old Town, which is a UNESCO World Heritage Site, Tetouan will immediately captivate you. Stroll through the colourful markets, where the scents of spices and freshly prepared bread lie in the air. Banyan Tree Tamouda Bay, your exclusive home for the next three nights awaits you. This luxurious beach resort offers you an oasis of peace and comfort. The elegant villas, each with its own pool and stunning views of the turquoise waters, offer perfect retreats. Enjoy the spacious spa area and end the day with a dinner with Moroccan specialties, while admiring the sunset over the Mediterranean Sea.

Start the morning at breakfast and enjoy the peaceful atmosphere of Banyan Tree Tamouda Bay. Spend the day at the private beach or pool, let yourself be pampered at the spa or enjoy a yoga hour to reconcile body and mind. Do you feel like taking a guided tour of Tetouan? Book these and explore the Old Town with the Royal Palace and lively markets. Alternatively, you can take a trip to Tangier to experience the fascinating mixture of Moroccan and European culture during a city tour. Overnight in Banyan Tree Tamouda Bay.

After breakfast, say goodbye to Morocco today. You will be driven to the airport in Tangier and take the last unique impressions of the city and landscape with you on your journey. With wonderful memories and unforgettable experiences in your luggage, you can start your journey home. Goodbye!

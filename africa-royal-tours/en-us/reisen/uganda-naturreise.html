Uganda Trip - Highlights of Uganda

Uganda Round Trip: Experience Uganda's highlights on an individual journey with Africa Royal Tours. Now make a non-binding request.

Let yourself be enchanted by the unspoilt wildness of Uganda! In the dense primeval and rainforests of this mysterious country you have the unique opportunity to roam in the footsteps of the majestic gorillas and the lively chimpanzees. On this versatile journey you will explore the fascinating wildlife not only on foot, but also by boat and on country trails. Discover hidden paths and experience an authentic Africa that shows you in all its original splendor. Uganda, a country of contrasts and unexpected wonders, is waiting to be discovered by you!

1st day Entebbe

After your landing at Entebbe airport, you will be warmly welcomed by your German-speaking driver guide and driven to your hotel. At Hotel No.5, located in the quiet suburbs of Entebbe surrounded by lush gardens, you can relax after the flight. This stylish boutique hotel offers an elegant and intimate atmosphere. Enjoy relaxing moments by the pool and leave everyday life behind you – exciting days are ahead. Overnight at Hotel N.5.

Day 2 Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary

Today your journey leads you north to Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary, through the impressive and diverse landscape of Uganda, often referred to as the 'pearl of Africa'. During the ride, you will have the opportunity to get exciting insights into the natural diversity of this region from your private driver guide. In the afternoon you will reach the Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary, which was founded more than 20 years ago to protect the last broadmouth rhinos. Together with an experienced ranger, follow the footsteps of these majestic animals and experience them at close range. But the Sanctuary is home to more than just rhinos; it also offers a protected habitat for many bird species, antelopes and crocodiles. After this exciting day, after a scenic drive, the Nile Safari Lodge awaits you, your luxurious accommodation on the banks of the Nile. The lodge, located in an elevated position within Murchison Falls National Park, offers a perfect mix of relaxation and adventure in one of Uganda's most beautiful natural landscapes. Refresh yourself in the infinity pool and enjoy your dinner in the open air. Overnight at the Nile Safari Lodge.

Day 3 Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary - Murchison Falls National Park

Continue to the Murchison Falls National Park, where you will immerse yourself in the heart of this great nature reserve in the afternoon. Look forward to your first game drive and encounter the breathtaking variety of flora and fauna that makes Uganda's largest national park so unique. Under the expert guidance of your experienced guide, explore the vast savannahs and dense forests where elephants, giraffes and numerous antelope species move freely. Listen to the reputation of exotic birds and look out for the hidden leopards who call this wilderness their home. This drive offers you not only exciting encounters with the animal world, but also unforgettable impressions of the lush landscapes that extend around the mighty Murchison waterfalls. After a day full of breathtaking moments, you will return to your lodge to let the experiences resonate in peace. Overnight at the Nile Safari Lodge.

Day 4 Murchison Falls National Park

Already in the early hours of the morning, you set off today with your private driver guide. Experience up close elephant herds, giraffes and buffaloes and look out for lions and leopards. After this exciting land tour, continue your adventure on the water. By boat you drive to the foot of the stunning Murchison Falls, undoubtedly one of the highlights of your trip. Let yourself be enchanted by the picturesque surroundings and watch a variety of wildlife and bird species. You might even get a look at the huge Nile crocodiles and the rare shoe-snatch, which offer perfect motifs for exceptional photos. After the boat trip, a 45-minute hike leads you to the origin of the cases, where you can enjoy a spectacular view. Overnight at the Nile Safari Lodge.

Day 5 Kibale Forest National Park

Today, one of the longer stages of your journey will take you through the picturesque heart of Uganda. After breakfast, leave the impressive Murchison Falls National Park and head for Kibale Forest National Park, famous for its impressive chimpanzee population. Your adventure will take you through varied landscapes, past vast tea plantations and rural villages that characterize rural Uganda. Half way you take a well-deserved break in the small town of Hoima. Take the opportunity to taste local specialties or simply stretch your legs. Finally, you will reach your home for the next three days, the Ndali Lodge. This magical lodge is majestically situated on a crater rim and offers breathtaking views over the crater-shaped Ndali Kasenda Lake. The cottages are made of natural materials and have private verandas for relaxing moments with a view of nature. Enjoy the atmosphere and strengthen yourself with a well-deserved dinner. Overnight at Ndali Lodge.

6.-7. Day Swamp Migration and Chimpanzee Trekking

On the sixth day of your trip, tie up the hiking shoes, as it takes about three hours to explore the Bigodi swamps. Keep your eyes open for kingfishers, giant Turakos and other bird species, while you walk along wooden paths through the diverse vegetation. The evening will be crowned by a fascinating night hike with a ranger, where you will have the opportunity to discover the nocturnal animals of the Kibale Forest. This exciting tour takes about 1.5 to 2 hours and offers you unique insights into the nightly bustle of the forest. The following day starts early in the morning not to miss a second of chimpanzee tracking experience. This extraordinary experience allows you to experience chimpanzees intensively in their natural environment and to study their behavior at close range. The time you spend with these intelligent beings will remain unforgettable. Return to your lodge in the evening with wonderful impressions. Overnight at Ndali Lodge.

8.-9. Queen Elizabeth National Park

On the eighth day, your trip will take you to the Queen Elizabeth National Park, one of Uganda's most beautiful and diverse natural landscapes. The park's impressive ecosystems combine vast savannahs, tropical rainforests, fertile bogs and idyllic lake landscapes to create a unique natural paradise. On the way, stop at the equator – a popular photo point that symbolically marks the transition between the northern and southern hemispheres. Afterwards you will reach your idyllically located accommodation, with only a few cottages, which were built stylishly from natural materials and are located on a hill with fantastic views of the wide plains. In the afternoon, you will embark on a first game drive, where you will be able to observe elephants, lions, antelopes and many other residents of the park with some luck. The ninth day also begins with an exciting morning game drive, during which you experience the savannah in its special morning light. In the afternoon another highlight awaits you: an adventurous boat tour on the Kazinga Canal. As you glide over the water, hippos, crocodiles, buffaloes and a variety of waterbirds – including pelicans, kingfishers and shriek-sea eagles – meet you. With unforgettable impressions you return to your accommodation in the evening. Overnight at Honey Bear Bush Camp.

10th Day Ishasha Sector of Queen Elizabeth National Park

The southern part of the Queen Elizabeth National Park enchants with a very special spectacle – here you have the opportunity to experience the famous tree-climbing lions, who rest with impressive elegance in the branches. High up in the shady crowns of the fig trees they escape from the glowing heat of the savannah. On the way from the north to the southern part of the park, the landscape changes almost imperceptibly until you suddenly find yourself in a world of vast plains and isolated acacias that are gentle in the wind. The Ishasha sector unfolds all its magic during a hunt – here the harsh beauty of nature merges with the fascination of wildlife. Finally, on the banks of the gently flowing Ntungwe River, the Ishasha Wilderness Camp, a small, authentic safari camp that blends harmoniously into the natural environment. The only ten spacious tent chalets offer a wonderful view into the green – sometimes even an elephant herd moves directly past the camp. Stop and let the quiet beauty of this place affect you. Overnight at Ishasha Wilderness Camp.

Day 11 Bwindi Impenetrable National Park

Break into the impressive Bwindi Impenetrable National Park today. Since 1994, this ancient rainforest has been a UNESCO World Heritage Site and is considered one of Africa's most species-rich forests. Bwindi became famous especially for its gentle giants, the mountain gorillas threatened with extinction. An important contribution to the protection of these fascinating animals was made by the well-known zoologist and behavioral researcher Dian Fossey, who devoted herself with extraordinary dedication to the life and behavior of the mountain gorillas. With patience and great sensitivity, she gained the confidence of the animals by learning to understand their gestures, social rules and emotions – her moving life's work was later immortalized in the book and the film of the same name Gorillas in the fog. The Bwindi Impenetrable National Park is very old and originally, difficult to penetrate, which also gave it the name "impenetrable", impenetrable. In addition to the gorillas, there are nine other primate species, including chimpanzees, Anubis-Paviane, Guerezas-seacats and half-monkeys as well as forest elephants and over 340 bird species.

Day 12 Gorilla Trekking

Today, a very special highlight of your trip awaits you: before sunrise, you will embark on an unforgettable tour that will take you to the gentle giant Bwindis. After a brief briefing by the experienced park ranger, your gorilla tracking adventure begins. The duration of the hike varies between two and six hours – depending on where the Gorilla family has moved since its last camp. Thanks to the expertise and fine sense of rangers, the chances of encountering the gorillas are very good – but there is no guarantee. Once you have discovered the gorillas, there is time to enjoy the closeness of the animals and to experience some very special emotional moments. Their majestic appearance and peaceful coexistence touch in a way that is hard to put into words. The observation time is limited to one hour in order not to expose the gorillas to unnecessary stress and to ensure their protection. After this unique experience you return to the camp full of impressions. Overnight at Chameleon Hill Lodge.

13th day of Lake Mutanda

After the rain forest adventure, a relaxing day awaits you today at picturesque Lake Mutanda. In the midst of the wonderful silence of this idyllic lake, it is easy to leave everyday life behind. During a boat trip you will explore the fascinating island world and enjoy the fantastic scenery that unfolds around the lake. Even Winston Churchill once referred to Uganda as the "pearl of Africa", and with every look at the diverse landscape it becomes clear why. A special stop will take you to the legendary Skeleton Island, whose history has remained alive to this day. Afterwards, you will have the opportunity to participate in other optional activities at an additional cost, such as an exciting Golden Monkey Tracking or a hike to the imposing Mount Sabinyo. Overnight at Chameleon Hill Lodge.

14-15. Tag: Lake Mburo

On the 14th day you will continue to Lake Mburo – small but impressive, because it is home to a variety of bird species, zebras, antelopes, hippos, leopards and many other animals. The wet biotope, embedded in the expanses of the savannah, offers a perfect home for this animal world. In the high-quality, family-run lodge, you can move into your room and enjoy the breathtaking view before going for a game drive in the late afternoon. Look out, because with the sunset, the predators wake up and go wildlife viewing. The next day we will go on a game drive again, followed by an exciting boat trip, where you will experience the surroundings from a completely new perspective. Overnight at Mihingo Lodge.

16th day Departure

"Weeraba", Goodbye Uganda. Exciting and eventful days are behind you. Today, your path will take you back to Entebbe, where you will stop at the Equator Monument to experience the fascinating experiment with water. You will then be taken to the airport to start your journey home. If you have the desire to experience even more of Africa, whether it is an exciting safari in the Serengeti in Tanzania or a relaxing bathing extension in the Seychelles, we will be happy to put together an individual offer. With a heart full of unforgettable moments, you return home. Goodbye and until the next adventure!

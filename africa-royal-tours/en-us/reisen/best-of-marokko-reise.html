High mountains in the Atlas Mountains, the vastness of the desert, lively hustle and bustle in the city and the fresh breeze of the Atlantic – all of this can be experienced during this unique journey through Morocco. You will visit a Berber village and learn more about the traditional life in the High Atlas. A green oasis awaits you in the endlessly shining desert, where you can explore the surrounding dune landscape on the back of camels. Immerse yourself in the enchanting Marrakech with its millennia-old history, labyrinthine alleys and medina. At the end of the journey, you can review what you have experienced while sucking in the fresh Atlantic breeze.

Day 1 Welcome to Marrakech

After your arrival at Marrakech airport, you will be warmly welcomed and driven to the hotel by your private chauffeur. The rest of the day is at your disposal. Marrakech is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and the millennia-old history transforms the Medina into a large open-air theatre every day. Because of the small and red plastered labyrinth-like streets, Marrakech is also called "The Red City". Particularly worth seeing are Jemaa El Fna Square, the Saadian Tombs as well as the Royal Palace and the Bahia Palace. Your hotel is a true pearl: the Villa des Orangers, a traditional Moroccan 5-star riad. With a refined atmosphere and a harmonious combination of shimmering colors, elegant fabrics and artfully carved wood, with an inner courtyard and three swimming pools, you will find your oasis of peace in the midst of the lively medina. Overnight in Marrakech.

2.-3. Day The Atlas Mountains is waiting for you

After your breakfast you will leave with your driver towards the Atlas Mountains. Today's destination is the small Berber village Imlil. In Imlil you will meet your local guide, who will take you on a small tour of the village and introduce you to some locals. Together you will then take a short hike through the mountains around Imlil. With a little luck you will get a view of the 4,176-meter-high Jbel Toubkal, the highest mountain in Morocco. Enjoy the beauty of the Toubkal valley and breathe deeply. Your accommodation for the next two nights is the Kasbah Tamadot, an award-winning refuge in the Moroccan Atlas Mountains with breathtaking views of the majestic Toubkal mountain. The accommodation features individually designed bedrooms, berber tents and riads. The next day you decide alone how you want to spend your time. Enjoy the fresh mountain air while strolling through the beautiful gardens, relax by the pool or take a hike through the High Atlas. Overnight in Asni.

4.-5. Day About Ait Benhaddou to the most beautiful oasis of Morocco

Enjoy an extensive breakfast and then start with your guide towards Skoura - the most beautiful oasis in Morocco. On the way stop in Ait Benhaddou, Morocco's most interesting and famous Kasbah (UNESCO World Heritage Site). The clay settlement of the Berber tribe of Ben-Haddou gained fame mainly through various Hollywood productions such as "Lawrence of Arabia", "Gladiator", or "The Bible". On an impressive road you continue to Skoura. This thriving oasis, fed by two rivers, enjoys a great natural wealth. The impressive Kasbah Amridil, a citadel from the 17th century, is one of the main attractions of this lush green region and adorns the banknotes of the national currency. Use the next day to explore this unique area on your own. Overnight in Skoura.

6.-7. Day Sand dunes and desert magic

One highlight chases the other: Together with your guide you will explore the Todra Gorge and the Dades Valley today. Even the drive through this canyon is worth a trip. The high rock walls rise to the left and right of the Todra River to some 400 meters in height. After a hike in the valley, a contrast program awaits you: it goes via Erfoud towards the desert to Merzouga. The desert village lies on the dunes of the Erg Chebbi, a fairytale dune landscape. In Erfoud, change to your four-wheeled vehicle and set the last stage to your desert camp. The Merzouga Luxury Desert Camp, located right in the quiet dunes of the Erg Chebbi, is the perfect retreat for an unforgettable break under the stars. Immerse yourself in the silence of the Sahara and stay stylishly in the comfortably furnished luxury desert tents. In such a place you just have to try a desert ship! The next day starts very early, because you swing towards the sunrise on the camel's back. After an hour's ride, enjoy a delicious breakfast in your camp. At lunchtime, you'll blow over the sand dunes with an all-wheel drive. A romantic picnic completes your safari and in the afternoon you can relax in your accommodation. Overnight in Merzouga/Erg Chebbi.

8.-10. Day of Mysterious Marrakech

After a last breakfast in the desert, return to Marrakech today. In the afternoon you can reach the Villa des Orangers, your already familiar accommodation from the first night. Enjoy the return to this charming retreat in the heart of Marrakech. At the beginning of your tour you could already "snap" into the atmosphere of the beautiful Marrakech. After your arrival today, you will now have the next two days extra time to devote yourself more intensively to the sights. On one of these days you will explore the city with your private guide, which will bring you closer to the hidden treasures of Marrakech. The Médina Marrakech belongs to the UNESCO World Heritage Site and the millennia-old history transforms the Medina daily into a large open-air theatre. Overnight in Marrakech.

11.-13. Day between Medina and the sea

From Marrakech it goes today to the Atlantic coast in the charming coastal town of Essaouira. The blue and whitewashed houses still give an insight into the Portuguese history of the city. In the old town you can see Andalusian trains. Design your last days in Morocco according to your mood. For example, visit the harbour in the morning when the fishermen return with their first catch of the day, walk through the mighty walls of the Medina, stroll through the alleys of the old town or walk along the beach, where a constantly blowing wind attracts many surfers. Here in Essaouira, Medina and the sea are close to each other and your accommodation, the L-Heure Bleue Palais, offers you at the same time the proximity to the old town and also panoramic views of the sea, which can be reached in just a few minutes. In addition, it has a pool where you can relax. Overnight in Essaouira.

14th day Departure or Extension Program

Their eventful journey through Morocco is coming to an end today. Say goodbye to these unique landscapes, the enchanting scents and the culinary highlights. After breakfast you will be taken to the airport to start your journey home from there. We are happy to create a relaxed bathing extension on the coast of Morocco for you.

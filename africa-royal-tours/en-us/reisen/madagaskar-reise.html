Madagascar Journey – In the realm of wild beauty

18-day Madagascar luxury trip: Experience Indris in Andasibe, the Dornwald of Mandrare, Anjajavy and the private island of Nosy Ankao at Miavana Resort.

Itinerary & services

This journey combines four of Madagascar's most fascinating natural spaces. You will discover the famous Andasibe National Park, home to the Indri Indri, the world's largest lemur species living today. The journey then takes you to the Dornwald of Mandrare, where you can observe the dancing Verreaux-Sifakas in their natural habitat. Another highlight awaits you in the Anjajavy region, which combines some of Madagascar's most impressive natural wonders: majestic baobabs, spectacular Tsingy rock formations and a fascinating wildlife characterize this extraordinary landscape. The crowning end of your journey is finally Nosy Ankao, an exclusive private island off the northeastern coast of Madagascar. Surrounded by turquoise sea, pristine beaches and an impressive underwater world, this island paradise combines luxurious relaxation, unique natural experiences and the incomparable beauty of the Indian Ocean. A perfect finale for an extraordinary journey through Madagascar's diverse landscapes.

1st day Arrival to Madagascar

With your arrival in Madagascar, a trip to one of the most fascinating island worlds on earth begins. The fourth largest island in the world inspires with an extraordinary nature, impressive landscapes and a unique endemic animal and plant world that exists only here. After landing, you will complete the entry and baggage formalities before you begin your journey of discovery through the diverse Madagascar. From tropical rainforests to spectacular rock formations to dreamy coasts, a land full of contrasts and authentic encounters awaits you. Look forward to unforgettable travel experiences in one of Africa's most original and exciting destinations. You stay in Antananarivo. 

2nd Day Royal Heritage and Rainforest Adventures

In the morning you will immerse yourself in the moving history of Madagascar. In the old districts of Antananarivo and around the former Royal Palace Rova you will experience the capital from its most authentic side. We deliberately put this visit at the beginning of your journey, because it provides a first understanding of the island's culture, history and everyday life. The journey then leads through the charming landscape of the Malagasy highlands to Andasibe. On the way, the Peyrieras Reserve offers you a special start to your nature experiences in Madagascar. Chameleons, geckos, frogs and the first lemurs can often be observed in close proximity. In the late afternoon you will reach Andasibe, one of the island's most famous rainforest areas. A special highlight awaits you after sunset: the night hike in the jointly run VOIMMA reserve. Equipped with flashlights follow the paths through the forest and discover with some luck mouse makis, sleeping chameleons and colorful frogs. The reserve, founded by the local population, also enables a direct contribution to the protection of the rainforest and creates authentic encounters with people who preserve their homeland. Overnight in Andasibe.

3rd day Encounter with the Indris and life in the rainforest

Today one of the special highlights of your time in Andasibe awaits you. In the Analamazaotra Reserve, you will be looking for the Indri Indri, the world's largest lemur. When the characteristic calls of the animals echo through the rainforest, a unique atmosphere emerges, which many travelers describe as their most impressive moment in Madagascar. With a little luck you will also encounter other lemur species, chameleons and numerous bird species in their natural habitat. In the afternoon you will get to know the village of Andasibe. The walk through the small streets opens up insights into the everyday life of people and complements the nature experiences with a personal and authentic encounter with the region. Especially the interaction of extraordinary wildlife and the contact with the local people makes the stay in Andasibe so special. Another night in the rainforest gives you time to relax and enjoy the special atmosphere of this fascinating part of the country. Overnight in Andasibe. 

4th day Lemurs up close and return to the capital

At the end of your time in Andasibe another special nature experience awaits you. In the private Vakona Reserve, you will have the rare opportunity to meet different types of lemurs on a small island in close proximity. After the observations in the rainforest, you will experience the animals here from a different perspective and have enough time to observe their curious behavior and capture special memory photos. Then you say goodbye to the rainforest regions of Madagascar and drive back to Antananarivo. The route through the highlands offers an opportunity to review the impressions of the past days. Passing rice fields, small villages and the gentle landscapes of the highlands, you will once again experience the diversity of the island. In the late afternoon you will reach the capital, where your stay between nature, animal watching and authentic encounters will be rounded off. Overnight in Antananarivo.

Day 5 In the Land of Antandroy

Today you fly by charter to the remote south of Madagascar. The long journey is worth it, because here you will experience a side of the island, which is characterized by original landscapes, the unique Dornwald and the traditions of the Antandroy. While the east is dominated by tropical rainforest, here you can expect Baobabs, vast plains and a culture whose connection to the ancestors is ubiquitous until today. After your arrival in Ifotaka you will be taken to your lodge on the banks of the Mandrare River. Nestled between mighty Tamarinden trees and overlooking the sacred mountains of the region, it is the ideal starting point to get to know the south of Madagascar. In the afternoon you will take a guided walk through the Dornwald. Between exceptional plants, ancient baobabs and the ancestor tombs of Antandroy, you will gain insights into a culture whose life is still shaped by its ancestors and traditions. The artistically designed tombs tell of family stories, faith and identity and open up access to this region, which goes far beyond the landscape. End the day with a sundowner at the Mandrare River. Overnight at the Mandrare River. 

6th day Lemur Paradise on the Mandrare River

After breakfast, explore the Sacred Gallery Forest, a forest along the Mandrare River. Depending on the water level, you can reach the area on foot or by boat. Between mighty Tamarinden trees you look for Kattas and Verreaux-Sifakas, which can often be observed from close up here. The quiet atmosphere in the morning and the history of this forest, which is important for the local population, give the visit its own unique character. At lunchtime you will return to the lodge. The hours at the Mandrare River provide an opportunity to rekindle the impressions of the south and to observe life by the river. In the late afternoon you will take a guided night walk through the Dornwald of Zenavo. As soon as the dawn begins, the region appears from another side. With a little luck you will discover nocturnal lemurs, sleeping chameleons and other inhabitants of this unusual ecosystem. The silence of the forest, the sounds of the night and the common search for the often well-hidden animals give this excursion a very special atmosphere. Overnight at the Mandrare River. 

Day 7 In the Dornenwald and encounter with the Antandroy

When the first rays of the sun immerse the octopus trees and the characteristic vegetation of the spiny forest in warm light, this creates a very special atmosphere. With a bit of luck, you will discover some of the rare bird species that only occur in the south of Madagascar. 
Then visit a local market or a traditional village of Antandroy. Here you will experience the everyday life of people far away from the big cities, observe the bustle of the market and get insights into the way of life of a region that is still shaped by its own traditions and values. Lunch hours will be spent at the lodge on the Mandrare River before departing for a sundowner between the Baobabs in the evening. As the sun slowly sinks behind the mountains, the light changes and immerses the landscape in warm colors. The end of the day is traditional dances and chants of Antandroy. Music and dance are an integral part of community life and are cultivated at festivals, celebrations and special occasions. The performances provide an authentic insight into the culture of the South and round off your stay on the Mandrare River in an atmospheric way. 

Day 8 Discover the natural wonders of the Mandrare Valley

Today belongs to the Mandrare Valley and its many facets. Depending on the time of day, light and perspective, the landscape of the south changes over and over again and invites you to discover the surroundings at your own pace. Characteristic thorn forests, striking baobabs and the special vegetation characterize the image of this region. In your explorations, look for lemurs, endemic bird species and other animal inhabitants of the south. At the same time, there are opportunities to meet people from the region and get to know life along the Mandrare River. It is precisely this mixture of spontaneous encounters and time for own discoveries that makes the appeal of this day. Enjoy another day at the Mandrare River before your stay in southern Madagascar slowly comes to an end.

Day 9 Anjajavy – Where Luxury Meets Untouched Nature

Today you fly to the northwest coast of Madagascar to Anjajavy. The flight takes about five hours with a tank stop in Antananarivo. The journey takes you to a remote part of the island, whose original character has been preserved to this day. After your arrival you will be welcomed by the lodge and will move into your villa. Anjajavy is located on a private peninsula on the Indian Ocean and combines nature experiences with relaxing days by the sea. Around the lodge there is a protected reserve with dry forests, baobabs, mangroves, tsingy formations and small bays. This diversity makes it possible to discover a wide variety of landscapes without having to change locations. At the same time, nature often starts right in front of its own villa. Lemurs, numerous bird species and many endemic plants are part of everyday life in this protected area. After the intense impressions of the past days, Anjajavy consciously offers space to experience the journey at its own pace: exploring the reserve, walking on the beach and relaxing hours by the sea. 

10.-12. Day relaxation and enjoyment of nature in Anjajavy

Enjoy the coming days in Anjajavy. Relax on the beautiful beaches, explore the idyllic bays of the peninsula or take varied trips to the impressive nature reserve. On well laid out paths you will discover the unique landscape with dry forests, mangroves, spectacular coastal sections and traditional fishing villages. There are always excellent opportunities to observe lemurs, chameleons, birds and other endemic animal species in their natural environment. If you want to be active, you can do various water activities or boat trips. In the afternoon, the beautiful oasis of the resort invites you to a relaxing tea hour – the perfect place to enjoy the impressions of the day. Anjajavy combines nature, tranquility and exclusivity in a unique way and forms another highlight of your journey through Madagascar. 

13-17. Hidden island paradise in the Indian Ocean

The coming days will be spent on Nosy Ankao, surrounded by coral reefs and the warm waters of the Indian Ocean. The island offers space to enjoy to the full the "mora mora" of Madagascar - the pleasant serenity and deceleration that you have encountered again and again during your journey. The location between sea, pristine coastal areas and the mountains of the mainland gives this place its own character. Every day can be designed according to your personal interests. Explore the island on long beach walks, take excursions on and in the water or discover the fascinating underwater world by snorkeling. Miavana Private Island stands for a stay in which exceptional natural experiences and complex luxury naturally complement each other. It is not only the generous villas and the attentive service, but also the location in the protected archipelago, the proximity to the sea and the vastness of the landscape that characterizes this place. Let the view wander over the ocean, watch the changing light over the water and enjoy the freedom to make these last days of your journey according to your own rhythm. 

18th day Farewell to Madagascar

Today we say goodbye to Nosy Ankao. You fly back to Nosy Be by helicopter and will then be taken to the international airport in good time. Perhaps it will be the calls of the Indris in the rainforest, the encounters with the people in the south, a Sundowner on the Mandrare River, the calm of Anjajavy or the vastness of the Indian Ocean before Nosy Ankao, which you first remember. What at the end was your personal highlight of this trip will only be able to answer you yourself. Madagascar has shown you in many facets – from the rainforests in the east to the landscapes and traditions of the south to the coasts and islands of the Indian Ocean. With many special impressions in your luggage you will start your journey home. We wish you a pleasant return trip and a long time to enjoy the memories of this extraordinary trip.

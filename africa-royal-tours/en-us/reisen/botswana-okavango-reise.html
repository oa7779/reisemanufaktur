Botswana & Zimbabwe — Immersed in Nature

Discover Botswana and Zimbabwe on a tailor-made journey with your Travel Atelier Advisor at Africa Royal Tours. Start planning today.

10 days

Discover Botswana, its unique original nature and the fascinating wildlife. Come to enjoy comfortable travel with the small aircraft from highlight to highlight and be accommodated in mobile, well-equipped camps. In contrast to the impressive Okavango Delta, the animal-rich areas of the Moremi Wild Reserve and the Chobe National Park, you will be enchanted by the roaring Victoria Falls in Zimbabwe.

Trip information

Itinerary

10 days Victoria Falls – Sambesi National Park – Serondela Region – Chobe National Park: Savute Region – Moremi Wild Reserve – Okavango Delta - Maun

Welcome to Zimbabwe! Upon arrival, you will be welcomed by your Africa Royal Tours representative and taken to your first accommodation. The transfer to your lodge takes place by boat and on the way you can already marvel at the beautiful nature. In the afternoon you will have the opportunity to experience the magic of the majestic Victoria Falls up close and let yourself be enchanted. The waterfalls were discovered by Scottish missionary David Livingston. When he heard of the cases, he set out in 1855 to conquer them. Deeply impressed by the beauty, he described the waterfall as "the most beautiful thing he ever saw in Africa," and named it "Victoria Falls" in honor of Queen Victoria of Britain. The locals affectionately call their waterfall "Mosi-Oa-Tunya", which means "Throwing smoke" and alludes to the huge water spray. Your accommodation is the Old Drift Lodge. Located upstream of Victoria Falls, the lodge offers luxurious suites and fantastic views of the Sambesi National Park. Overnight in the Sambesi National Park.

Let's start your safari tour! The day begins with the trip to the northern area of the Chobe National Park, which is also called the Serondela Region. On the way you can experience the unique nature up close and see many different wildlife. Upon arrival, you will have time to relax and rest in your camp. In the next few days, you can start to the game drives and other wildlife observation activities accompanied by experienced guides. Serondela is considered "the land of elephants". Spend hours watching the large herds of pachyderms gathering around the Chobe River. In addition, you can either opt for a cruise on land or take a private boat trip on the river. Enjoy the varied flora and fauna, the beautiful scenery of the surrounding area and the magnificent sunsets of the northern Chobe National Park. During your journey through Botswana, you will be accommodated in private, mobile & Beyond tent camps. The tents are comfortably equipped and have an ensuite bathroom and a wicker shower with fresh water. Watch the wildlife around the tent camp from your comfortable canvas chairs and enjoy your meals in the middle of the bush in the dining area of the camp. Overnight at the & Beyond Tent Camp in Serondela.

After breakfast you set off for a cross-country trip to the Savute region within the Chobe National Park. The landscape changes between vegetation-rich streams and the Savute area, which is known for its elephant herds and the Chobe river. During a sunset ride, the many hippos looking for a cooling down in the river can be observed particularly well. Their destination is the Savute Channel, a river course that has surprisingly returned to water since 2008 after drying out in the 80s. Now there are huge marshland areas along the river that are home to many native wildlife. The area is especially known for its exciting predator sightings. Your accommodation for the next two nights is a tent camp near the Savute Channel. Overnight at & Beyond Tent Camp in Savute.

Reinforced by breakfast, continue your journey into the wilderness. This morning you will continue to the Moremi Wild Reserve in the east of the Okavango Delta. The reserve covers about a third of the Okavango Delta and so permanent waterways with drier areas alternate here. A dreamlike landscape of unpredictable contrasts and diverse wildlife awaits you. The forests on the edge of the rivers, the large mopans of trees and the ever-expanding wetlands combined with majestic palm trees make the Moremi Wild Reserve one of the most scenic wildlife areas. It is particularly impressive to observe giraffes in the middle of the reed vegetation, which form a dreamlike silhouette on the horizon. The many different habitats are an ideal basis for your voyage of discovery to combine a typical savannah safari with river rides along the lagoons and canals as well as bird watching. They spend two nights in a camp tent and make morning and evening observation tours. Overnight at the & Beyond Tent Camp in the Moremi Wild Reserve.

After an early morning game drive, go to the airfield. You now fly with a small plane to the Nxabega landing runway. There you are already expected and driven to your camp. Take advantage of the opportunity and participate in the many wildlife observation activities. Explore Mokoro's crystal clear lagoons, islands and waterways of the Okavango Delta along with your experienced ranger or take part in an exciting walking safari on one of the river islands. Depending on the seasonal dry or rainy season, the different wildlife species in the Okavango Delta also change. Discover the ‘Big Five' of the wildlife as well as cheetahs, hippos, crocodiles, various antelope and goat species as well as hyenas, zebras and wart pigs. With a little luck, an African wild dog, threatened with extinction, is crossing your way. Your camp is located directly under a collection of large ebony trees. Wake up in your bed to the birds' morning whistle concert and the apes' cry. A special experience that will never let you go again. Overnight at the & Beyond Tent Camp in the Okavango Delta.

Let yourself be awakened one last time by the sounds of the African wilderness and enjoy a last game ride. Today it is sad to say goodbye to unforgettable ten days in Botswana and the magical Victoria Falls. After breakfast, you will be taken to Victoria Falls Airport for your onward journey. If you want to experience even more of the dreamlike Africa, we will be happy to work out an individual extension program such as the "Mother City" Cape Town or a bathing extension in Mozambique, on the island of Mauritius or the Seychelles (price on request).

Uganda: Eye in eye with the silver back

Experience the mountain gorillas in the wild on an individual Uganda trip with Africa Royal Tours. Enquire now and get advice.

26.11.2013

Uganda is still one of the less touristy developed countries in Africa. It is home to ten national parks with countless animal and plant species. From half the world, countless migratory species fly in here to winter and breed in the climatically pleasant Uganda. Even if the coat of arms bird of Uganda is the crowned crane, a species here is the very special attention: the gorillas. Especially the male silver ridge fascinate thousands of visitors to Uganda, who come to the country because of them. A guided trekking tour to these rather shy apes is an incomparable experience.

Gorillas in Uganda

About 350 of these fascinating primates still live in the rainforest-covered mountains. Like other regions Africa Unfortunately, they are also endangered in Uganda in their existence. It is estimated that 100,000 copies are still available worldwide. Since 1975, the Gorillas have been protected by the Washington Convention, but the situation remains threatening. Destruction of habitats, civil war-like conditions and also poaching make the protection of these animals a difficult task.

The mountain gorillas of Uganda came to world fame by the American zoologist Dian Fossey, who visited and researched them regularly since the 1960s. After Fossey's murder in 1985, the film "Gorillas im Nebel", based on her life story, reached people in cinemas around the world in 1988. This still attracts tourists to Uganda. Especially popular are guided trekking tours to the gorillas in the mountains. These live in groups of up to 30 animals, which are always led by a so-called silver ridge.

The silver back is the boss

Gorillas in the wild become 1.25 meters to 1.75 meters in size depending on species and sex and weigh between 70 kilograms and 200 kilograms. The silver ridges are particularly impressive: this is what is called the male gorillas with an age of over 12 years. The name silverback stems from the light color of the back skin, which is characteristic of this age.In some species in West Africa, this coat drawing also extends to the hips and thighs. The silver ridges have long canines and a pronounced crest. Also the pronounced throat sac, which strengthens the sound propagation at the imposing thoracic knock, is a characteristic of the silver back. The mostly peaceful giants are nevertheless exclusive herbivores. In the wilderness they grow up to 40 years old.

The silver ridges are absolute sole rulers over the group, which consists mainly of the harem of female gorillas. Non-dominant gorillas are tolerated. However, they try to challenge the dominant silver back. Such ranking battles under silver backs can end fatally. It is estimated that a silver ridge usually only remains the leader of such a group for five years. So there are always silver ridges that roam alone in search of a group that they may be able to take over.

So the trekking tour to the gorillas will be a success

Uganda is very pleasant for Europeans with its temperate climate. Even if it can be up to 35 degrees Celsius during the day, it is pleasantly fresh to sleep at night with 12 to 18 degrees Celsius. The rainforest does not have its name by chance, so rains are necessary. In case of rain, but also otherwise, such a trekking tour is physically exhausting and requires a certain fitness. In addition to rainsuits, good trekking shoes, which go high over the ankles, are a must. Long hiking socks, which can be pulled over the pants, are also highly recommended.

The armed security guides offer preventive protection against dangers, but not against the small, aggressive red fire ants. And these bear their name rightly!

Firm gloves, as they are available in the hardware store, are also helpful to hold onto and be protected. Sticks, which do good service to free and keep the way, are available on site. On a long hike, they also make walking much more comfortable.

The prescribed vaccinations should be respected in any case. Travel medical advice is therefore always useful. For the photographers, it is thought of an equipment adapted to the circumstances: The light conditions in the rainforest are dark and use of flashes absolutely forbidden. An extremely bright camera is a must as well as a tripod if you want to take impressive pictures of a silver back.

Those who take these tips will be able to keep their tours in Uganda safe in the heart and for the photo archive. The way to the silver ridge is rewarded by unforgettable encounters with giants of a special kind.

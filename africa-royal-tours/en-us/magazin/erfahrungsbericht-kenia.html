Experience report from Kenya – Wildlife, Lodges & Coastal Paradises

Experience Kenya through the eyes of our experts Christiane and Beate: safari, wildlife, exclusive lodges and relaxing beach days in a unique country.

03.03.2026

Finally it was time: Our journey to Kenya began. From Munich, the route took us over Doha to Nairobi, the capital of Kenya. Before midnight we landed at Jomo Kenyatta International Airport, where we were warmly welcomed and taken to our first accommodation.

A short time later we reached Fairmont The Norfolk. The location is ideal, the rooms practical with a touch of colonial style. The fantastic garden area is particularly impressive – it is hard to believe that you are here in the middle of the city.

First impressions in Nairobi

We started our sightseeing program the next day with great anticipation. On our travels we take a personal look at numerous hotels and lodges to discover special insider tips and the best recommendations for our guests.

In Nairobi, the legendary Giraffe Manor, the elegant Villa Rosa Kempinski, the charming Manzili House and the stylish Hemingways Nairobi awaited us. Each house had its own character – from colonial flair to modern luxury.

The adventure begins – Safari in the Amboseli

The next morning, our safari adventure started. With our experienced driver guide, we headed for the Amboseli National Park. After about four hours driving south we reached our destination: the luxurious, modern Angama Amboseli – the only camp in the private Kimana Sanctuary.

With a warm "Jambo" we were greeted at the entrance of the Conservancy. Already the way to the lodge became the first little hunting trip: Zebras, giraffes, Impalas and Gnus crossed our way. The lodge blends harmoniously into the surrounding area. We enjoyed the tranquility of nature for a moment – until suddenly a dictation, a dwarf antelope, sprang out in front of us. Here, in the midst of this landscape, there is no boredom.

Oase in the bush

Then we went to Tawi Lodge, which is located in the Conservancy, adjacent to the Amboseli National Park. A real oasis in the bush.

Separated by a fence, we watched giraffes approaching and grazing peacefully. The camp, built in African style, impresses with great attention to detail. The spacious bathrooms, the stylish decoration and the wooden paths that connect the individual areas create a special atmosphere – we would have liked to stay longer.

On the way on, we experienced what gave the park its name. "Amboseli" means in a local language as much as "salt dust" – and exactly this fine dust accompanied us through the dry, impressive landscape. Between vast plains and acacia trees, we discovered zebras, buffaloes, wart pigs and gnus – Africa as you dream.

Luxury with a view of Kilimanjaro

Finally we reached the Elewana Tortilis Camp Amboseli, an exclusive camp in the private Kitirua Conservancy. "Karibu!" – the warm welcome made us arrive immediately. The tastefully furnished tents and areas of stay quickly conquered our heart.

The coronation followed the next morning: the long-awaited view of the majestic Kilimanjaro.

A subsequent Game Drive led us past newly created lakes that have formed in recent years. We discovered flamingos, pelicans and hippos as well as giraffes, zebras and various antelope species.

The Great Rift Valley and the legendary Masai Mara

Our journey led us further to Naivasha, down to the spectacular Great Rift Valley. The view over the fertile, green landscape from the edge of the ditch was fascinating. Afterwards we went to the legendary Masai Mara – wide plains, an impressive animal world and this incomparable feeling of freedom. It was exciting to learn how closely the Maasai are connected with their animals and the protected wilderness around the National Park. We reached the Entim Private Camp, an open tent camp in the middle of nature. It is guarded by Maasai day and night. At the pool we enjoyed a spectacular sunset.

The next day we visited the new Paradise Plains Camp, which pays tribute to its name. By golf cart we explored the facility and were enthusiastic about the luxurious, colonial-kenian design. On our hunting trips we experienced the full range of wildlife: lions, elephants, buffaloes, antelopes, zebras, giraffes and gnus. Time and again we met the Maasai with her cow and goat herds.

At the end we reached the elegant Olonana Lodge directly on the Mara River. Here, hippos greeted us with loud grunts. Fascinated, we watched the activity of these giants and let the day end in this spectacular setting.

From the savannah to the Indian Ocean

We left the Masai Mara, passed Maasai villages and the Great Rift Valley Rand and reached the busy capital Nairobi again. With the Madaraka Express we drove across the country to Mombasa. On the way we once again saw the majestic Kilimanjaro and passed the extremely impressive Tsavo National Park – a program for our next trip! The first-class train was in a very well-kept condition and arrived in Mombasa on time in the evening. A driver welcomed us at the station and took us to Diani Beach in a good hour, where we spent our last two nights in Neptune Village. 

After arriving in Mombasa, a driver took us to the dreamlike Diani Beach, where we spent our last days on the Indian Ocean. Another recommendation is the Swahili Beach Resort. It convinced with its Omanian flair: large, comfortable rooms, a fantastic pool area, great restaurants and of course the beautiful beach on the Indian Ocean – here every trip to Kenya can end wonderfully.

Chale Island – The perfect conclusion

The last highlight of our trip was Chale Island. At the southern end of the Galu beach, depending on the water level, the "Limousine" – a tractor – is replaced by a speedboat or low water. At tide, swimming is certainly wonderful, we thought, at low tide, it is worth a walk to the reef to discover the underwater world. On the last day we took a ride with the glass bottom boat to discover the underwater world of the reef. Finally, we relaxed on our beachbeds under the high palm trees.

Our conclusion

In the evening we flew with a slightly wistful but happy heart full of breathtaking moments from Mombasa Moi International Airport via Nairobi and Doha back to Munich. What a trip! We can definitely recommend Kenya to you – it is a country full of adventure, luxury & nature!

Onguma Game Reserve – fascinating animals of Namibia up close

The Onguma Game Reserve is located in the eastern part of the Etosha National Park and is one of Namibia's best protected nature reserves! Guests have the opportunity to experience Africa in all its beauty and diversity, as the Onguma Game Reserve is a more than 34,000-hectare nature reserve with over 30 different species, including Kudus, Giraffes, Antelopes, Oryx, Zebras, Impalas and above all elegant predators such as lions, cheetahs and leopards. A special highlight is a family of endangered rhinos that has been successfully relocated to the Onguma Game Reserve! Bird lovers will no longer come out of the amazement: more than 300 bird species migrate through the wetlands of the reserve especially in the rainy summer months, caused by seasonal rains and river systems. By the way, Onguma means "place you don't want to leave - here's the name program.

Pure luxury in the middle of rough wilderness

The exclusive lodge Onguma The FortLocated in the middle of the Onguma Game Reserve, it is a lodge of the highest class. With one of the most beautiful views of the colourful sunset of Namibia, The Fort convinces with the highest comfort, delicate food and spectacular animal watching during the dinner. Twelve bush suites, equipped with loving Moroccan details and a bathtub overlooking the savannah, form a wonderful combination of oriental flair and African traditions.

Onkolo Hide - Hotspot for photographers

During a stay at Onguma The Fort, guests have the opportunity to "Once in a LifetimeI experience. In absolute harmony with the surrounding nature, the hideout "Onkolo Hide" offers the optimal opportunity to experience the animals of the Onguma Game Reserve close to a large water hole without disturbing them. The flat building is kept in the colors of the landscape, has narrow but sufficiently large windows through which guests can observe the animals up close. This, in combination with unique lighting conditions and sufficient space within the hiding place, allows incredible video and photo recordings.

Experience the magic of the Namib in the Sonop Lodge

The Namib desert in Namibia is one of the places of longing for Africa. A wonderful place to enjoy its silence and expanse is Zannier Hotels Sonop Lodge.

02.06.2022

The Namib desert is one of Africa's longing places. A wonderful place to enjoy its silence and expanse is Zannier Hotels Sonop Lodge. It offers contemporary luxury as well as old world charm, wild nature and fine dining. Guests can experience wildlife and thrills or meditative moments as desired. 

When thinking of the Namib desert, the cinema starts immediately: immense expanses, an endless horizon, the feeling of freedom and adventure. The Sonop Lodge is in the middle of the film.

Pure Africa: the Namib Desert

Experts estimate the age of the Namib to be around 50 million years. A desert that is dust-dry, but not hostile to life. 70 reptile species, countless bird species, wild horses, jackals, leopards, hyenas or zebras live here and outsmart nature day after day. The animals defy extreme conditions and temperatures and have developed creative methods to collect water and food. You will see: The Namib is a special place full of beauty – as created to absorb the power of nature and leave the stress of everyday life behind. 

A temporary home: Zannier Hotels Sonop Lodge

A fascinating starting point for exploring the Namib is the dreamlike Sonop Lodge. 10 tent suites with rollable tarpaulin, designed in the nostalgic colonial style of the 1920s, are distributed on a granite rock in the middle of the desert. They have one or two bedrooms as stylish as well as generously sized, and the private terrace overlooks the vastness of the Namib. Here guests experience the natural moods first hand, and after dark the starry sky opens up over the desert. 

A pampering program for body & soul

From breakfast on the sand dunes to the five-course gala dinner in the elegant dining room, the lodge combines first-class cuisine and a special ambience. The spectacular infinity pool is a favorite place of all guests, which nestles wonderfully in the landscape. Daily yoga lessons in the magical mood of sunrise or sunset strengthen body, mind and soul. Massages in the spa are further elements of the wellness program.

The sky over the desert

Zannier Hotels Sonop Lodge is located in one of only 16 designated Dark Sky reservations on Earth. Far from light pollution, billions of stars sparkle in the evening sky. The Milky Way is clear and powerful in its overwhelming beauty. The vast expanses of space make us humble and speechless.

The Namib from above – with helicopter and hot air balloon

Just get in the air? With the helicopter, guests of the lodge can float like a golden eagle over the desert and watch the red dunes of the Namib – where the desert meets the sea – from a bird's eye view. You can also observe animals in different habitats from the air without disturbing them. 

Or take off to the Fish River Canyon, the largest canyon in Africa, which you can experience first hand after landing on four wheels. That sounds like a lot of German to them? Right, in Lüderitz – known for colonial architecture with Art Nouveau elements – you will travel to the end of your trip to the German past of Namibia. 

Another excursion takes you to Swakopmund, Namibia's largest coastal city with its chic waterfront promenade, rich cultural heritage and famous restaurants. On the flight along the coast, you will also fly over Deadvlei, a mystical place with sandstone terraces where petrified trees grow from the majestic dunes. 

A trip with the hot air balloon is particularly romantic. When the sun greets the day, you float towards the sky and across the unique landscapes of the Namib. Dreamy photos will remind you forever of a great experience.

The Namib from below – hikes and more

Even if you prefer to stay on the ground, exciting experiences await. On guided hikes you will discover some of the secrets of the Namib with their guides. And when you climb the ridge of a red sand dune, you feel the magic of the desert close up. Horse-riding or excursions with e-fat bikes are further possibilities for exploring the surroundings. The best way to experience the wildlife of the region is on private sunrise or sunset safaris.

The sister lodge Zannier Hotel Omanda

The boutique lodge with 10 individually designed Owambo-style straw and clay huts is located in the heart of the Zannier Reserve, the 22 000-hectare nature reserve of the hotel chain half an hour from Windhoek. From your terraces or from the pool you can look over the savannah. And in Omanda as well as in Sonop, unforgettable experiences await you from breakfast with meerkats to wildlife safaris in the footsteps of big cats, elephants, rhinoceros and other wildlife animals.

Madikwe Game Reserve South Africa

The Madikwe Game Reserve Nature Park is located in the Bojanala Platinum district of northwestern South Africa. The land in this area is not productive in agriculture, one reason the South African government decided to promote the development of the region by establishing a wildlife reserve. The Madikwe Game Reserve was opened in 1991. The project was financed by a cooperation of private investors and the Nature Conservation Agency of the Northwest Province. Madikwe Game Reserve hosts a variety of exclusive lodges. Most of them are located in the east and west gates of the protected area.

Interesting facts

The area of the Madikwe Game Reserve covers a total of about 75,000 hectares of bushland, pasture land and wide grass plains. This open bush landscape is interrupted by isolated island mountains. The area provides only limited water resources: in the east, the Marico River runs through the country. Therefore, the construction of additional dams was necessary to ensure water supply. In the south, the Madikwe Game Reserve is bounded by the Dwarsberg Mountains. To the north, the game reserve extends from the small town of Groot-Marico to the border with Botswana. All Game Lodges and hotels in the Madikwe Game Reserve are privately owned. The operators pay concession fees to the parking authority, which is responsible for the overall management. The staff, which makes the stay in the hotels and lodges pleasant to the guests, comes from the surrounding villages. This scheme contributes to improving living conditions in the region. The profits of the Madikwe Game Reserve are partly channelled into projects to improve the infrastructure of the surrounding communities. The Madikwe Game Reserve is located about 400 km from Johannesburg and about 90 km from Zeerust. Madikwe has its own runway for private aircraft and is served daily from Johannesburg by a shuttle service.

In 1993, the Madikwe Game Reserve began to relocate about 10,000 mammals from other nature parks. In 1996, predators such as hyenas, cheetahs and African wild dogs were added. The first lion populations came from Namibia and the neighbouring Pilanesberg National Park. When a catastrophic drought threatened the elephant population in Zimbabwe, 180 elephants were moved from this area to the Madikwe Game Reserve. The elephants have well integrated - now more than 250 pachyderms are counted in the Madikwe Wild Reserve. The Madikwe Game Reserve is the fifth largest nature reserve in the country. Also important to know: The Madikwe Game Reserve is considered malaria-free. The park rules stipulate that a visit to the Madikwe Game Reserve is bound to at least one night's stay at one of the excellently managed and beautifully located lodges or one of the exclusive hotels. Pre-booking is required.

Fascinating hunting trips

The Madikwe Game Reserve has grown to more than 12,000 animals. Of these, 66 are large mammals. On the hunts of the Madikwe Game Reserve, you can observe all large game species at a close distance in their natural environment: an impressive natural experience that you will like to remember. The colourful African wild dogs with their white tails are particularly interesting: These highly endangered animals have increased well after their introduction in the Madikwe Game Reserve. Meanwhile, three pack of African wild dogs roam the area. The rare animals are used to encounter the safari vehicles, so they can usually be well observed and photographed. Buffalo, many antelope species, lions and cheetahs, broad and pointed rhinoceros, zebras and giraffes: The Madikwe Game Reserve offers many fascinating opportunities to meet these impressively large wild animals in their original environment. The bird world also shows itself in all its diversity: more than 350 different bird species live in the Madikwe Game Reserve. The natural diversity of its creatures combined with the exclusive hospitality of the lodges and hotels make the Madikwe Game Reserve a gem among the South African nature parks.

Climate and travel times

In the Madikwe Game Reserve, every season has its special charms: in spring and summer, you have to expect rain, but Madikwe game reserve enchants with lush vegetation at this time of year. In addition, during this time, many of the animals are young: attractive sights that offer many options for unforgettable photo motifs. In autumn and winter, clear, dry days and overwhelmingly beautiful, starry nights await you. The vegetation in the Madikwe Game Reserve is quite dry at this time, so the animals gather for drinking and bathing at the water holes: Wonderful observation possibilities and exciting photo motifs are guaranteed in the autumn and winter season. For cool mornings and evenings you need warm clothes all year round! Nevertheless, you should not forget the sun hat, sunscreen and sunglasses even in the winter season. The gates of the Madikwe Game Reserve are opened around six in the morning and closed around six. If you come after this, you must be accompanied by an escort to your lodge or hotel, in accordance with the regulations of the Park Authority.

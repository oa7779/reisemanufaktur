North Island – Paradise on Earth

The sun sends its first warm rays over the white sandy beach and the guests who slowly move through a series of elegant yoga figures. On the horizon you can see a small sailboat that runs towards the sunrise: the perfect start to another exciting day in paradise.

Located in the middle of the inner granite islands of the Seychelles, North Island is a fertile oasis that deeply touches and inspires its visitors. The biodiversity on land and in the sea alone is a masterpiece of evolution and can best be admired during extensive walks or dives.

Out of love for nature

For decades, the island's inhabitants and environmental team have been the main objective of protecting nature and the animals living in it. Therefore, there is only one exclusive and unique resort on the island, created by a long-standing and trusting collaboration between the North Island and Wilderness Safaris. Like any Wilderness Safaris Camp, this on North Island is a dream come true with luxury, comfort and sustainability. The eleven private guest villas, give each guest an incomparable feeling of absolute exclusivity and privacy.

All villas have direct access to the beach and offer a wonderful view of the sea. The villas consist mostly of local materials and have the unique characteristic architectural style called "barfoot luxury": open spaces, large windows, bright colors and the natural materials allow the villas to merge with the surrounding nature.

Barefoot into happiness

Pure relaxation also offers the hotel's spa area. With a private consultation with the dedicated therapists, a tailor-made spa experience is created according to the needs and preferences of each guest. Each treatment begins with a barefoot ritual, which is used to relieve everyday stress and seamlessly slip into the barefoot luxury of the island. All treatments can take place either directly in the spa, with stunning views of the glittering ocean or in the privacy of your own villa.

The spoils of choice

The many restaurants on the island offer a range of culinary experiences: Piazza a romantic candle light dinner, which is accompanied by the gentle sea noise and the West Beach Bar serves freshly prepared pizza, salads and tapas barbecue snacks that can be enjoyed at sunset, a cocktail and relaxed music.

A special highlight is a very private picnic at one of the many secluded places that the island has to offer. With a cozy campfire and colorful sunset, couples in love, or even families at Canapés or a stylish barbecue under the stars, can end the evening wonderfully.

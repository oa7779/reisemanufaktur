Zanzibar - The Spice Island

Discover with Africa Royal Tours where pepper grows: the picturesque Zanzibar. The palm-lined paradise of East Africa awaits you on the spice island of Zanzibar off the coast of Tanzania.

Destination Zanzibar

With its exotic palm beaches and the azure Indian Ocean, the island of Zanzibar looks like a true travel dream, like the template for beautiful beach pictures and beautiful kitsch postcards. Located about 40 kilometers off the coast of Tanzania, the Spice Island is a dream destination for nature lovers, divers and romantics. The two larger islands, Unguja and Pempa, which together form Zanzibar, present themselves as an African hot spot for a relaxing swim after a spectacular safari in Tanzania. Thanks to the best climatic conditions Zanzibar enjoys a delightfully diverse flora. The African island is particularly well known for its lush pepper occurrence. To wish someone where the pepper is growing is, therefore, anything but a curse – just look at this paradise. In addition to pepper, there are also carnations, cardamom, ginger, cinnamon, vanilla and nutmeg. So the island delights with a beguiling fragrance composition, which enticingly surrounds the fairy tale island. 

The white sand fine as powdered sugar, the sea all year round wonderfully bath tub warm and turquoise blue, palm trees lined with coconuts that weigh gently in the wind: Zanzibar is clearly a place to fall in love. Many parts of the island's beach are still so untapped touristic that you can sometimes walk for miles without meeting a human soul. That's how you imagine paradise. With its oriental-looking streets and the graceful, magnificent palaces, the fairytale island awakens dreams of 1001 nights. Today, there are still evidences everywhere of the island's moving history, of its weddings as a place of transhipment for ivory, spices and even slave trade. Unlike many paradisiacal beach destinations such as the Maldives or the Seychelles, Zanzibar is not yet blessed with a perfectly developed infrastructure. The beauty of it: so one has the chance to explore many of the beautiful spots of Zanzibar, which are just waiting to be discovered, as the very first. In addition, it offers travelers the opportunity to get in touch with the locals of the island much more easily, to get to know land and people as authentically as possible. Zanzibar enchanted as an island with its own life.

Stone Town: in Zanzibar's capital

Stone Town, in the heart of Zanzibar City, has been a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2000. With its houses of coral stone and lived traditions, Stone Town is immersed in times long past. There you will experience Zanzibar from its original side. Half veiled Sansibari women sit laughing and chatting on stone benches in front of their homes and offer refreshing sugar cane juice with lemon and ginger. Old Sansibari play the local board game "Bao, curious children run through the alleys, the whispers of praying Muslims in the mosques can be heard. This city lives – and its joie de vivre is contagious. In the historical center, marvel at the stone houses built 150 years ago from coral rock, which still exude this very special charm, although already marked by the years. In the narrow streets, the shops line up and while you wander curiously through the old town of Zanzibar City, sooner or later you usually end up on the lively Darahani market. There, everything that the island has to offer in delicious food is offered. In addition to fresh fish and meat, the Zanzibar fruit and vegetables are offered for sale. 

Taste the local delicacies and let Zanzibar melt on your tongue. Here you can expect a taste intensity, as tasty as freshly harvested, especially with fruits and vegetables. In addition to the Darahani market, you can also visit St. Joseph Cathedral, whose interior enchants with its pink columns and unusually modern colours. In the historical footsteps of Zanzibar, you walk in the palace museum of the Sultan and in the Arab Fort. Or visit the House of Wonders, a Sultan's palace built at the end of the 19th century. Since it was then the first building in Zanzibar that had electricity and running water, it was considered the house of miracles on the island. A visit to a Dhaus, a traditional wooden ship or a trip to the giant turtles living on Prison Island is also highly recommended. And, how could it be different on the island of spices, a Spice Tour is a must. On such a tour you will be guided through the plantations, learning exciting about the origin and peculiarities of the individual fruits. And of course you can try everything. This tour is really a very special excursion for the senses. Experience Zanzibar from all its beautiful sides.

Hotel on Zanzibar

Partly still spoiled by untouched nature, there is no oversupply of hotels on Zanzibar. Most of the resorts on the African island are built in traditional style, but still luxurious. A wonderful example of this architectural union of past and future design: the Meliá Zanzibar on the northeast coast. 

This is not only a luxury hotel, but also an architectural wonder. It was planned according to the vision of a modern, luxurious version of an ancient stone town. The hotel reflects the romance of its role models in its white washed coral stones, narrow avenues, traditional Swahili gates and windows. Decorative brass and wooden wall claddings, hidden backyards with splashing fountains and naturally incident light as well as terraces with panoramic views provide a magical atmosphere that makes the magic of Zanzibar visible. Meliá Zanzibar is located on a 300 meter long, fine white sandy beach, bordered by a natural coral reef and beautifully adapted to the nature of Zanzi Bar. From private villas with their own garden and magnificent infinity pool to romantic rooms with outdoor rain showers, private terrace and sea views, Meliá Zanzibar boasts 124 lovingly designed luxury oases. Especially exquisitely reside in one of the hotel's seven noble Romance Ocean Pavilions. Standing on a rocky promontory, the pavilions offer privacy and tranquility and are ideal for a romantic honeymoon in intimate togetherness.

The glass front of the pavilion offers a gigantic view of the open sea, which you can enjoy from the four-poster bed as well as in the free standing bathtub. The Romance Ocean Pavilions are covered with the typical local "Makuti" roofs – so the noble design combines with the traditional architecture of Zanzibar. Your stay at the Meliá Zanzibar is crowned by an extensive, varied range of cuisine and the excellent Anantara Spa. In five different restaurants and four bars you will find almost every desire for food. Let yourself be enchanted by the chefs of Meliá Zanzibar or enjoy a drink in front of the picturesque backdrop of the fairytale island of Zanzi Bar. And the hotel's spa also makes it really easy to switch off from everyday life. Anantara means "without limits" and describes so very well the harmonious combination of different treatments in the spa. Thai massage, Kilimanjaro peeling, Anantara milk bar – let yourself be pampered at Meliá Zanzibar.

Zanzibar's cuisine

Due to its diverse history, Zanzibar's cuisine today also has a very special variety. Arab, Indian and European influences shape the food on the East African island. The treats of Zanzibar are enhanced by all the spices that flourish so magnificently there. Cardamom in particular is found in almost all dishes, and also in Sansibarian coffee. However, it is so delicately dosed that it always supports the taste of the food to the perfect extent, instead of pushing itself dominantly into the foreground. Besides the other Zanzibar spices cinnamon, vanilla, ginger, nutmeg, pepper and cloves, the coconut in Zanzibar's kitchens also plays an important role. Their refreshing coconut water is used as well as fruit flesh and milk of the coconut. The classics of the Zanzibar cuisine are Chapati, an unsweetened fried and absolutely saturating pancake, which is served as a side dish to many dishes and Pilau, with raisins and cardamom seasoned rice with potatoes. Pilau is often served fresh fish, vegetables or beef masalla, seasoned meat. The most famous restaurant on the East African island is "The Rock" at Michanwi Pingwe Beach. The special thing about it is that "The Rock" is located a few meters from the beach in the sea. 

This small island of cuisine sits on a rock and offers a wonderful view of the sea. So if you want to dine here, you have to either translate with the boat or swim. But this unusual path is well worth it. The cuisine in The Rock is not at the level of a star cuisine, but still truly delicious. The menu includes fresh seafood, accompanied by fine wines. On a visit to Zanzibar truly a must! The Forodhani Garden, also known as the Night Market, offers a small culinary adventure at the harbour of Stone Town. After dark the Forodhani turns into a paradise for curious people, connoisseurs and gourmets. Between stone benches and green areas, the most delicious regional dishes are offered at food stalls. Besides various fish varieties, grilled or fried, the locals offer some treats that are particularly typical of Zanzibar. A tasting is absolutely part of a Zanzibar trip. Taste the Zanzibar Pizza, a dish that has little in common with the well-known pizza. At the Zanzibar Pizza a dough bag is filled, whether with minced meat and vegetables, seafood or as dessert with fruit and chocolate cream, and then fried over the grill. Or venture to the also very popular Zanzibar soup, a well seasoned potato soup with a wide variety of insoles such as vegetables or meat. Enjoy a refreshing, cool sugarcane juice, a glass of freshly prepared Lime Juice or the favorite hot drink of the Sansibari, a chai.

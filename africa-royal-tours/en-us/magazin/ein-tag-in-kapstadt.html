One day in Cape Town: Table Mountain, Bo-Kaap & Penguins

Experience the "Mother City" Cape Town. We will show you the highlights of the metropolis – from penguins at Boulders Beach to the Sundowner at Cape.

19.02.2026

Cape Town is geographically unique. Located at the southwestern end of Africa, where the cold Atlantic meets the warm influences of the Indian Ocean, a metropolis has developed that directly connects nature and high-end lifestyle. In Cape Town it is the landscape that sets the rhythm. We take you on an active day through the city and its unique surroundings.

Geology and Panorama: The Table Mountain

The day begins in the heart of Victoria & Alfred Waterfront, right in the exclusive One&Only Cape Town. From there we make our way to the landmark of the city, Table Mountain. In order to avoid the waiting times at the valley station, our German-speaking tour guide picks us up early and takes us to the Cape Town City Bowl. With the Rotair cable car, whose cabins rotate 360 degrees during the journey, we reach the plateau at 1,085 meters altitude. Table Mountain is one of the "New 7 Wonders of Nature" and, at about 260 million years old, is one of Earth's oldest mountains. A remarkable fact for connoisseurs: With the Fynbos, the area houses a flora that is so rich in species that it forms its own of only six so-called floral kingdoms worldwide, a region with a completely independent and unique plant world. After a tour with a view of the Waterfront and the prison island of Robben Island, we go back down again.

The heart of the city: Bo-Kaap, Greenmarket Square & Company

Back in the valley we explore the colourful Cape malayen district Bo-Kaap. The historic quarter is known for its cobbled streets and the colourful house facades, which are now listed as a monument. Our tour guide tells us more about this cultural center of the Kapmalai community and thus gives us a direct insight into the complex history of the city. We stroll across historic Greenmarket Square with its local handicrafts and enjoy a short break in the Company's Garden. This green oasis in the city center was built in the 17th century to supply ships on their way to India with fresh provisions. Today the park is a botanical gem and offers a quiet counterpoint to the bustling center

Culinary delights at the ocean: Lunch in Camps Bay

The route now leads us along the coastal road to the south. Passing the exclusive bays of Clifton we reach Camps Bay. In a seafood restaurant right on the boulevard, a table is reserved for us. The cuisine at the Cape is characterised by the quality of the local waters – on the map you will find West Coast Rock Lobster, oysters from Saldanha Bay and freshly caught Kingklip. While enjoying our lunch, we look at the white sandy beach and the distinctive mountain range of the "Twelve Apostles". The atmosphere here is comparable to Saint-Tropez or Malibu, but with the harsh backdrop of the South African Atlantic.

Penguins and Sundowner: Boulders Beach & Chapmans Peak

After lunch, the driver will take the ride on one of the most spectacular roads in the world: Chapman's Peak Drive. The track was carved directly into the granite rock and winds along 114 curves above the ocean. We stop at selected vantage points for photos of Hout Bay before heading to Simon's Town. The destination is Boulders Beach, which is part of Table Mountain National Park. Here lives a colony of about 2,000 African penguins. Instead of a classic safari, you will experience the animals here from close up in a sheltered bay between massive granite rocks – a rare sight in a subtropical climate zone.

On the way back from the penguin colonies we make an exclusive stop on Chapman's Peak Drive. As the sun slowly sinks into the Atlantic, we take a small Sundowner at this spectacular vantage point. Here you can find a Méthode Cap Classique (MCC) – the South African counterpart to champagne. From up here, with the steep walls in the back and the endless ocean ahead, the geographical dimension of the Cape Peninsula becomes particularly impressive.

World-class dinner in the City Bowl

The culinary finale takes place in the center of the city, the City Bowl. Cape Town has developed into a global gourmet destination in recent years. We have reserved a place in a fine dining restaurant. The chefs here combine modern European techniques with South African ingredients such as Springbock, Fynbos herbs or Cape Malay spices. The menu is accompanied by top wines from the nearby regions of Stellenbosch and Franschhoek. It is an evening at the top international level that reflects the city's versatility.

A toast to Mother City

The evening will end either in one of the city's discreet gin bars – South Africa produces some of the best gins in the world – or in an elegant lounge on the waterfront. With a warm Lekker Slaap (Good night) this intensive tour ends through one of the most fascinating metropolises in the southern hemisphere.

This day deliberately includes a tight schedule to show the whole range of the region. For a more relaxed experience, it is recommended to discover Cape Town over several days. Depending on the interest, the activities can then be distributed over several days or further travel ideas built in. How about, for example, these three proposals below. 

Cape Point & Lighthouse

An extensive tour takes you to Cape Point in Table Mountain National Park, the most southwestern point of the African continent. A historic path or the cog railway takes you to the famous lighthouse from where you can enjoy the view over the roaring surf and the apparent union of two world seas.

Wine-growing area Constantia

Only twenty minutes from the city center is Constantia, the oldest wine-growing area in South Africa with its magnificent Cape Holland mansions. With a tasting you can taste among other things the legendary sweet wine "Vin de Constance".

Architecture & Art: The Zeitz MOCAA

Visit the Zeitz MOCAA on the Waterfront, the world's largest contemporary African art museum. It is an architectural masterpiece in which a grain silo was spectacularly hollowed out in the form of a huge corn grain. Inside you will discover pioneering works by internationally acclaimed artists.

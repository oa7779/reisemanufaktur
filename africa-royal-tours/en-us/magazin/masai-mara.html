Masai Mara - Kenya's natural paradise

For many, the Masai Mara National Reserve embodies the dream of Africa and is the scene of the great animal migration from Tanzania to Kenya every year.

19.04.2020

Masai Mara - Kenya's natural paradise

The Masai Mara National Reserve in Kenya embodies the dream of Africa for many. What is the first thing that comes to your mind when you think of this fascinating continent? Are they lush bush-savannahs? Do you think of endless expanses and barren steppes that are dipped in a golden light by the sun? Or do giant herds of animals come into your head, whirling the dust under you with their hooves? The Masai Mara unites all of this and is therefore rightly considered one of Kenya's most popular nature reserves.

Masai Mara: The spotted landscape

Already the name points to the particular landscape, which many certainly associate with Africa since their youth and the film King of the Lions. Masa means in the language of the Massai as much as "spotted" and refers to the umbrella acacias that cover the golden savannah with isolated, green color accents. These characteristic trees along with the golden plains are characteristic of Africa and a popular photo motif to capture the magic of the continent in a picture.

But not only these rather sparse areas are discovered on the more than 1500 square kilometers of the Masai Mara. Green grasslands characterize the landscape as well as the gallery forests near the Mara River. A special experience is a hot air balloon ride over the Masai Mara.

You can find this breathtaking natural jewel in the southwest of the country. As a nature reserve, the area of Masai Mara is not enclosed by fences. The surrounding areas are managed by the Massai on the Kenyan side. On the Tanzanian side, the Masai Mara borders directly on the Serengeti National Park.

Animals in the Masai Mara

The great awareness of the Masai Mara also comes from the biodiversity of the nature reserve. It is considered to be the most animal-rich area in Kenya, where more than 90 mammalian species and around 400 bird species gather. Elephants, buffaloes, zebras, giraffes, crocodiles, warthogs, baboons, lions – the list of animal contemporaries is long. Especially common are ungulates. Anyone who has always wanted to watch giant herds of gnu, is in the right place here.

Interesting for many: Can one meet all the representatives of the Big Five in the Masai Mara? That's quite possible! In fact, the nature reserve is considered a good place to take a look at the majestic lion. Leopard, elephant and buffalo can also be found here. In recent years, however, rhinos are very rare to see. As always on Safari, there is no guarantee, but this is also the reason for the adventure and the charm.

The big animal hike: Once-in-a-liftime experience

Let's come to the reason why Masai Mara is considered one of the great recommendations – for some, this is even the only incentive to visit the country. Of course, it is a matter of observing the great migration of animals with one's own eyes in life.

This hike, also known as Great Migration known as one of the most interesting natural spectacles in the world. In search of green pastures, a gigantic number of animals roam from the southern part of the Serengeti to the Masai Mara in Kenya. With the gnus alone, there are more than a million animals every year. The hike starts in Tanzania around March. In July you will usually find the river crossing stat, which is certainly known from numerous animal documentations. From that date, i.e. in July and August, the animal masses reach the Masai Mara. They stay there until the end of the dry season and migrate back to their starting point in about October and November – until the cycle of life takes its course again next year.

While the Masai Mara can be the setting for animal encounters and safaris all year round, the best time to travel can be determined from the end of July to November due to this natural spectacle.

Other impressive national parks in Kenya

- In the Amboseli National Park there are especially many elephants to watch. In addition, there is a wonderful view of Kilimanjaro.

- The Tsavo National Park is Kenya's largest national park, which is very suitable for classic safaris.

- The Mount Kenya National Park is suitable for safari enthusiasts as well as enthusiastic hikers and mountaineers. Mount Kenya is the second highest mountain in Africa.

- The Nairobi National Park is located just a few kilometers from the capital. In a safari you can thus take an unusual memory photo, in the foreground the wild animals, while in the background the silhouette of the big city emerges.

Also interesting is a country combination with Tanzania, as the Serengeti National Park is directly adjacent to the Masai Mara.

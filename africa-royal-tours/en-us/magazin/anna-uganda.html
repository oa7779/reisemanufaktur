Our Africa expert Anna in Uganda

Our Africa expert Anna Plasan fulfilled a dream of life in the heart of East Africa: she traveled to Uganda – the land of gentle hills, dense forests and rare gorillas.

03.12.2025

Our Africa expert Anna Plasan fulfilled a dream of life in the heart of East Africa: she traveled to Uganda – the land of gentle hills, dense forests and rare gorillas.

Africa calls - On to Entebbe

After a flight over Brussels, I finally landed in Entebbe. Already at the first meeting with my Ugandan guide I realized: Uganda will surprise and inspire me with its warmth and openness. My first night at the Karibu Guesthouse offered me a gentle start before it really started.

In the Ziwa rhino park

The next morning, my adventure began with the trip to the Ziwa rhinoceros reserve. I was immediately thrilled: Watching rhinos from close up is just magical. At the same time, the mighty animals seemed peaceful and impressive and I felt deeply connected with African nature. This experience was the first highlight of my trip and a foretaste of everything that was to come.

Murchison Falls – Nile, hippos and baboons

The drive to Murchison Falls National Park was breathtaking. Upon arrival, I was greeted by numerous baboons at the entrance as if they were welcoming me. My accommodation was right on the Nile, and from the restaurant I could watch hippos and crocodiles. In the evening the hippos even came closer to the shore. I sat fascinated on the terrace and enjoyed this peaceful, wild atmosphere.  

The full day in Murchison Falls National Park was indescribable. In the morning I went on a hunt - elephants, buffaloes and rare antelopes accompanied me through the endless savannahs. In the afternoon I went by boat to the origin of the Murchison Falls. On the way elephants came close to the water, hippos and crocodiles were present everywhere. The sight of the roaring water left me astonished: pure power, pure nature. The day ended with a sundowner at the top of the waterfall, while the sun stretched a beautiful rainbow over the falls.  

Kibale Forest – chimpanzees close up

My journey led me on to the Kibale Forest National Park. At the first step into the dense rainforest, I felt like I was entering a completely different world. Before I saw the chimpanzees, I heard their loud screams from afar. The curious, playful primates moved fast on the forest floor and I was completely banished. Every moment with the animals tied me up anew and let me experience their liveliness up close. 

Queen Elizabeth National Park - boat trips and African savannah

A boat safari on the Kazinga Canal brought me hippos and countless bird species very close. I also found impressive and earthing to watch the local fishermen and to see how they master their lives alongside the wild animals by the water. On a hunting trip I saw my first lions in Uganda. The endless savannah landscapes with baobabs and acacias, the wide sky – I could hardly get tired of it. Every moment was like a dream. 

Bwindi Impenetrable Forest – Gorilla Trekking

The absolute highlight of my journey was in Bwindi. At the Chameleon Hill Lodge overlooking Lake Mutanda, I was preparing for the gorilla trekking. The night before was sleepless – since my youth I have dreamed of this moment, and now it should finally become a reality. 

After the introduction at the gate, I set off with a group of eight people on my way through the dense jungle, while our guide with the machete cleared the way. 

Even the way through the forest was impressive: the silence, the rustling of the leaves, the damp air – everything made my heart beat faster. And then suddenly I was in the middle of a 14-headed gorilla family. Unlike the chimpanzees, there was absolute calm. My heart pounded violently when I discovered the first silver ridge – powerful, impressive, incredibly alive. Every movement, every look of the gorillas captivated me, and I put every moment in me. 

These 60 minutes I will always carry in my heart – an unforgettable moment full of wonder at these wonderful animals and with the feeling of how special and valuable our planet is.

Lake Mburo National Park – Farewell to Uganda

The last days I spent in Lake Mburo National Park. Once again I went on a hunt and met zebras, giraffes and impalas. The sun slowly descended behind the hills, and I let the impressions of the past few weeks seem calm to me. Last night at Mihingo Lodge was a moment of gratitude and amazement – a quiet, perfect conclusion to an unforgettable journey.

Return to Entebbe

Last morning, I got up early so I wouldn't miss a second. My coffee was brought to my room and I enjoyed it on the terrace together with the baboons. After that I swam a round in the pool overlooking a nearby water hole where zebras and wart pigs gathered. 

All the other guests were still asleep and I had this last moment in Uganda all alone. The encounters with wild animals, the unspoilt nature and the warmth of people have made my life dream a reality.

Memories for my life

My trip to Uganda was more intense, beautiful and moving than I ever dreamed. From the majestic rhinos in Ziwa Park to the powerful noise of the Murchison Falls, the curious chimpanzees in the Kibale Forest to the Gorillas in Bwindi. 

I was deeply impressed by the variety of landscapes, the animals and the people of Uganda. Your cordiality and joie de vivre have made this journey even more unforgettable. It will have a special place in my heart forever.

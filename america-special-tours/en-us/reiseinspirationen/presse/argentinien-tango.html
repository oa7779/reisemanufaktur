Press release: Argentina – Tango, wine and wellness

Argentina Theme Trip - Tango, Wine & Wellness

Munich, 05 November 2019. Rhythmic dances and noble drops: The Argentina themed trip of the Munich tour operator América Special Tours makes South American passion experienceable in all its facets. Travelers will explore the vineyards in Cafayate and take a dance course in Buenos Aires.

The 10-day journey begins in the capital of Argentina. Buenos Aires is also known as the "Paris of South America" and "World Capital of Tango". A tour with German-speaking guides leads to the classics of the city, including Plaza de Mayo, the government seat Casa Rosada, the colorful district of La Boca and the antique market near Plaza Dorrego.

Then the travelers immerse themselves in the tango scene. In an authentic dance club, a Milonga, they swing their dance legs together with the locals. An English-language tour guide provides the appropriate guidance and tells at the same time about the history of dance. Real professionals can be seen at the famous Tango Show Rojo Tango, which is accompanied by live music and a three-course menu. By domestic flight the journey continues to Salta. A special feature of the city are the numerous buildings in the colonial Baroque. A special highlight and landmark of Salta is the Iglesia San Francisco.

The route leads to Cafayate, past the Los Cardones National Park with its huge cacti and the tranquil village of Cachi, where a short walk is taken. 360 sunny days per year and the location between two mountain ranges of the Andes make the area an optimal wine-growing area. The travelers walk through the vineyards and convince themselves of the quality of Argentine wines during a subsequent wine tasting.

Two other highlights are on the program of the individual trip, both of which have a scenic appeal. Bizarre rock formations await in the Quebrada de las Conchas, the gorge of shells, which is a UNESCO World Heritage Site. In the Uco Valley there are old vineyards and mountain peaks of the Cordillera near the The Vines Resort & Spawhere the travelers stay. There, the trip can be completed with numerous wellness treatments and Argentine cuisine.

This journey is to be understood as an inspiration and can be adapted at any time to the customer's wishes.

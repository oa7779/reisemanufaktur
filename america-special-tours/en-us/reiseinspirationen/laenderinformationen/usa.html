USA: Country information A-Z

Best time/climate

Due to the enormous size of the USA and the different geographical landscapes, the climate varies significantly between regions. According to the latitude, the USA has a predominantly temperate climate. The regional climate is strongly influenced by the north-south orientation of the large mountain ranges and the ratio of land mass to adjacent water areas. In general, it can be said that the further south you come, the more north and inland the seasons are shaping. In many parts of the country there is a strong continental climate with hot summers and cold winters. Very characteristic here are sudden temperature falls. This is due to the lack of "cross-mountains", such as the Alps in Europe. As a result, both polar cold air and tropical warm air can penetrate far into the south or north, whereby in the area where the winds meet, violent storms and tornadoes can form. The coastal plains in the southeast and the Appalachians are green and wooded, depending on the altitude of mixed or coniferous forests. To the west, with increasing drought, the forest regions are first transformed into the treeless grasslands of the prairies. Further west in the Great Basin near Nevada there are also deserted regions in the rain shadow of the mountains.

One can roughly divide the USA into eight climate regions: the North-West Pacific, the Middle East and the Middle East. South Pacific, Middle West, Northeast, Southeast, Southwest, Alaska and Hawaii.

Middle Pacific and South Pacific (e.g. California, Idaho, Monatana, Wyoming, Utah, Colorado): In these states you can enjoy fantastic, dry summer weather. In the northern federal states it can become much cooler in winter. While snow is very rare in California, temperatures in Wyoming, Montana and Idaho can often fall below zero degrees Celsius and in Colorado and Utah there are excellent opportunities for skiing in winter.

The climate varies between a cold, windy and snowy winter and a warm and humid summer. Rain is expected especially in late spring.

==References== Washington D.C., Ohio, Pennsylvania, Massachussetts, Maine, Vermont): The northeast of the USA is characterized by pleasantly warm and mild summers, as well as rough and cold winters with lots of snowfall. The more north the state is, the colder the weather is. Especially worth a trip in autumn, because the deciduous forest dives into the most beautiful orange and red tones these months.

==References== Florida, Geogia, Kentucky, Alabama, Tenessee, Arkansas, Lousiana): Here is a pleasant climate all year round, as the Gulf of Mexico exerts its influence here. The Atlantic provides mild temperatures and high humidity as well as the danger of hurricanes in autumn. The winters are mild and short-lived, spring and autumn pleasantly warm and the summer warm to hot. Only in California and Florida can the summers become very hot and the states are already part of the subtropical climate zone. The largest area of California, however, is determined by the Mediterranean climate with some heavy rainfalls, which mainly fall between October and April. There is a subtropical climate on the Key Islands off Florida and it is almost always sunny and warm. Besides, there's always a refreshing sea breeze here. The average temperature is about 25°C during the day: in summer temperatures rise up to 32°C and in winter it doesn't get much colder than 18°C. The number of rainy days can be counted almost on one hand in winter.

In the semi-deserts and deserts of the southwest, strong temperature fluctuations between day and night are to be expected. The winters are short and pass quickly. The temperatures in spring and autumn are very pleasant. In summer, however, the temperature indicator remains at over 35 °C on most days. The southwest coast, described above as "South Pacific", enjoys temperate temperatures that are stable all year round thanks to the Pacific Ocean.

Alaska: In Alaska you will find subarctic and arctic climates with permanent frost soils. The long period of permanent frost is interrupted by a very short summer, which lasts only a few weeks and in which the temperatures rise above the freezing point, and it can warm up to 30 °C. The winters become extremely cold with temperatures of up to -50 °C. However, Alaska is a state covered by snow and ice all year round. There are four seasons and the climate varies depending on the region. On the south and west coast there are also rains in summer rather than snowfalls and the temperatures become warm in summer. In the northern region, on the other hand, it is very cold and there are average temperatures of -25 °C. In the months between May and August, the sun never sets. A fascinating event of nature.

Hawaii: It's warmest in August and September. The thermometer then shows 30 °C during the day, at night about 24 °C. Exceptions are the higher areas, here the temperature can fall below 10 °C. It is coolest on the Hawaiian islands in February. The Hurricane Season is about from 1 June to 30 November.

Entry and customs regulations/visa

Basically, there are two options for entering the USA with tourist intentions: a visitor visa B or entry as a participant in the Visa Waiver program using the ESTA form.

Visa Waiver program:

The entry with the ESTA certificate is slightly less complex. The prerequisite for this is that you have an electronic passport, a valid ESTA permit and a valid further or return ticket. The ESTA is currently available for nationals of 38 different countries, including Germany, Austria and Switzerland.

In principle, the Visa Waiter Programme excludes those who are either nationals of Iran, Iraq, North Korea, Syria or Sudan, or have resided privately or on business in one of these countries or Libya, Yemen or Somalia since 1 March 2011. In this case, you must apply for a visa B for the USA regardless of the purpose of the trip.

The ESTA permit allows you to enter the USA multiple times once and is valid for 2 years. For the application of the ESTA there is a fee of $14 and personal data, travel dates and data are requested to the personal background. As a participant of the Visa Waiter program, the USA grants you entry for 90 days.

Visitors Visa B:

This visa must be requested in advance at the US Embassy or at one of the consulates. For this purpose, the DS-160 form must first be filled out on the website of the US Embassy, an online registration must be carried out, the visa fee paid and a personal appointment agreed. The personal appointment at the embassy is mandatory for all visa applicants between 14 and 79 years. Applicants outside this age range may also send further documents by post. At the date, the completed DS-160 form, a passport valid for at least 6 months on entry, confirmation of the date, proof of the request of the trip and evidence that there is no immigration project will be brought along. The visitor visa B applies for a maximum of 180 days.

Neither a valid ESTA permit nor a valid visitor visa justify a right of entry into the USA. The final decision on entry is taken by the U.S. border official. There is no appeal against its decision. Unfortunately, the German missions abroad are also unable to work towards lifting a refusal of entry.

Special security measures:

Increased security measures apply to all flights to the USA and there may be interviews at the departure airport. We therefore recommend to schedule enough time (at least three hours) to pass the checks in advance of departure. In addition, pay attention to the hand baggage regulations specified by the airline.

The US border authorities reserve the right to investigate electronic devices and, if necessary, to retain them. Often there are also suitcase checks, which is why it is not recommended to lock the luggage, as otherwise the suitcases can be opened violently if necessary.

Upon entry, the address you are staying at during your trip to the USA will be requested. The first address is valid for round trips. Travelers who do not provide an address may be refused entry.

When you enter, your fingerprints will also be scanned and a biometric photo taken by you. You can expect further interviews and checks at this point. It is important to answer the questions of the border officials kindly, objectively and completely and to follow the instructions of the officers.

Trips from/to Cuba:

Be careful when entering Cuba directly, because these are prohibited in principle under U.S. law (better regulations) and only approved under certain conditions. Further information can only be provided by the relevant US authorities (short-term changes are possible).

Import requirements:

In general, imports of national and foreign currencies are not subject to declaration up to $10,000. However, you should refrain from importing meat products or plants. An official certificate is required for the import and export of pets. At entry duty free are the take-away of 200 cigarettes, one litre of alcohol (for this you must be at least 21 years old) and gifts that do not exceed the value of $100.

Food & Drink

The typical American Breakfast (American breakfast) consists of eggs, bacon, ham, sausages and cornflakes, juice, coffee or tea and a small dessert such as cake or similar. Coffee can be given indefinitely. Crispy rolls and black bread are an absolute rarity in the USA. Here, rather crisp toasts, croissants, muffins and pancakes can be found on the plates of the breakfast ends.

Typically USA is also the bagle, round rolls with a hole in the middle. Originally from Jewish cuisine, they have developed into an alternative for sandwiches in the USA. Before baking with yeast dough, the wrinkles are immersed in boiling water. This gives the Bagles their typical character: outside a thin crust, inside a fresh, dense core with bite. For a snack in between or as a food for a day trip, the sandwich is popular in the USA. You get them in every supermarket, at the snack bar or at fast food chains like Subway.

You are happy to combine the idea of American food with the concept of steak, which stands out over the plate. The reality is, of course, a little more modest. Really huge, inexpensive portions may be obtained in rural areas, but the average local provides mostly European steak sizes. However, unlike Europeans, Americans use the entire beef ridge for steaks. For a particularly juicy specimen, choose the "T-Bone-Steak", for especially delicate and fine meat the "Filet Mignon" is recommended.

The US-Americans also like to eat meat from the grill. At the barbecue family and friends come together and grill and smoke their food over the charcoal grill. Chickens, shrimps and above all the saverips are also particularly popular. The special thing about barbecue, however, is less the meat, the sauce is crucial. Before it wanders on the grill, the meat is marinated for a long time in the barbecue sauce and is then pinned several times during the preparation. Successful sauce recipes are guarded like state secrets.

Even the famous hamburger can't be forgotten from American cuisine. It is available in countless flavors and sizes. French fries or a baked potato, with sour cream and chives, are the most common side dishes. For the preparations of hamburgers, as for steak, it is possible to obtain these rare, medium rare or welldone.

In the coastal regions, besides beef and chicken, fish and seafood are on the menu. Shellfish, such as lobsters and crabs, are particularly popular and can be enjoyed excellently with a glass of Chardonnay.

Order vegetables as a side dish in a restaurant, you should not expect European standards. The selection is often small and usually limited to broccoli or corn. In order not to miss out on the green vitamin suppliers, you should put your focus on the salad. This is an integral part of almost every meal and is served as an appetizer. In many restaurants you can also find salad bars with a variety of salads and dressings.

While the Americans like to make their lunch smaller, the dinner usually consists of a sumptuous meal. The usual meal time is between 6 and 9 p.m. If you go to a restaurant, you are often asked at the beginning of the dinner if you want to drink a cocktail. Tap water is offered free of charge in almost all restaurants.

Of course, the multicultural composition of American society is also reflected in the kitchen. The range ranges from Indian, Chinese, Italian, Japanese to Greek. In the popular food courts, they are often found counters at counters, all side by side. They share a dining area and are mainly located in malls or train stations.

Electricity

It is important to put an adapter in the case, which you can get at the specialist store in your home country. The current voltage in the USA is 110 volts, unlike in most European countries. Therefore, adjust your electronic devices to this voltage. Some devices, like most laptops, do this automatically.

Filming/Photography

Accessories for your camera can be found in all major cities in the USA. In general, the Americans are very photo-friendly. However, if you want to take a picture of foreign people, a brief question is whether this is okay. Especially in Indian reservations there are strict photo and video regulations, which are usually clearly defined at the entrance of the reservation.

Money/currency

The official currency in the US is the dollar ($).

The easiest way to pay in the US is by credit card, whereby all major credit cards are accepted. Bank cards with V-Pay logo, however, cannot be read here. In order to exchange your euro notes, you should visit a currency exchange office, as not every bank is able to do so. At best, you'll bring a dollar note that has been exchanged from home.

Health/vaccination

No compulsory vaccinations are provided for when entering Germany, Austria and Switzerland. Vaccination against hepatitis A and polio is recommended.

Medical care in the USA is very well comparable to European conditions. However, the traveler should not underestimate the great distances that may have to be overcome by the nearest hospital. Treatments are usually expensive and take place against advance payment or direct payment. Therefore, for the duration of your stay abroad, you must complete an international travel health and return insurance covering the USA and pay attention to a sufficient credit frame for your credit card for the necessary advance payment.

Car hire

Car rental agencies usually only rent to persons at least 25 years old. In addition to the national driving licence, an international driving licence must be presented at the time of rental.

Some traffic rules differ from European ones. Thus, turning at red lights to the right is permitted if the car has been stopped before and no other person is put in danger. In exceptional cases, however, this may also be prohibited, then it is pointed out with the sign ‘No turn on red'. Get to a crossing with "All the wayStop sign-- you and all other vehicles must stop at the stop line to enter the intersection in order to stop. Do not be afraid if you are overtaken by the right, which is different in the United States than in Europe. No passing shall be permitted on the bus with warning flashing lights on. This also applies to vehicles coming from the opposite direction.

Do not use mobile phones without a hands-free device while driving. This behavior is banned in many states and is punishable by high fines. It is recommended to adhere strictly to the speed limits, which are significantly lower here than in Germany, as otherwise high fines threaten. On some interstates, the "rush hour" of the left lane is reserved for cars with at least two occupants. For tanks, it should be noted that the indicated price always refers to a gallon (3.78 liters). Major motorways, such as private-sector "turnpikes, Bridges or tunnels are often chargeable. The toll "toll" can be paid at toll stations in cash or partly also with digital payment options.

Parking tickets for wrong parking or excessive speed must be paid, if possible before your departure. In case of non-payment, there may be inconvenience for future entry and stays. The authority that issued the ticket is also responsible for information after leaving the USA. If you are stopped by the police, remain seated in the vehicle, open the window, hold your hands on the steering wheel and follow the police's instructions. Do not step out unsolicited as this could be perceived as a threat and lead to countermeasures. In larger cities, it is always possible to lock the car doors in order to protect yourself from possible robbery.

Language

American English is official in 32 states and unofficially in use as an official language and is the most important language in all states. Second place is Spanish. Through the US border with Mexico and the numerous immigrants, especially in the southern states, the language is also taught at most schools and thus frequently spoken. Immigrants from Asian countries have also taken their language from home and teach your children. Above all, Mandarin is mentioned here, followed by Vietnamese and Korean. According to the history of the United States, not only British but also French immigrants had an influence in colonial times. This influence has been preserved to this day, which has led especially in the southern states like Louisiana to speak French as well as English. Many other international languages can be found in the United States, which have brought immigrants from all over the world. The native languages used to be widespread, but these days they are slowly dying out. This is due to the expulsion of the natives, which still shows their impact today. Currently, these native American languages are mostly only spoken in the reserves.

Most hotels and restaurants have adapted to the residents' communication needs and offer free Wi-Fi access. If you need Wi-Fi on the go, McDonalds, Starbucks and Apple outlets are a good point of contact for free internet. To make a phone call, it is possible to buy a prepaid card.

Tips

Tipping is very important for the Americans, as this is an elementary part of the salary for the staff. Often waiters receive less than the minimum wage and finance themselves primarily through the tip income. That's why Americans themselves tip a lot. Usually the tip in restaurants is not yet included in the price, but a look at the bill is also worth it here, because in some tourist places the service is already on the bill. Usually 15% of the accounts are used, in some restaurants the percentage can also be up to 20%, but this is indicated as a tip proposal on the bill and sometimes even calculated directly to make it easier for the customer to calculate. You can either leave your gift on the table, hand it to the waiter, or open it at the credit card bill.

Also the maid is pleased with a small attention, from $1-2 and the carrier gets $1 per piece of luggage. This is not only a recognition of the helpful service, but also an integral part of wages.

Depending on the satisfaction, group size and length of the tour, the permanent and local tour guide receives a total of $20 for his work. Please note that this is a recommendation.

Behavior

Kindness, a smile on the lips and small talk. The children in the USA learn this very quickly, because everyone knows it here. The hotel clerk at the reception, the waiter in the restaurant or the lady in the clothes store, they will all ask you with a "How are you today?" how you are doing. A -Fine, thanks, and you?- is enough to answer this. This is merely a polite phrase and not an attempt to talk with depth. Don't be surprised if you're overcrowded with compliments, too, this is a form of courtesy. Most Americans are true artists of friendly and unobtrusive small talk. The point is: simply participate, then you get in the conversation quickly with locals. The answer to the question of how the food tasted is "good" almost as criticism. Everything can be a little exaggerated to the courtesy of love and so "good" quickly becomes "great" or "awesome" . In the restaurant, you will usually find a sign at the entrance asking: "Please wait to be seated" (Please wait until you are placed). You should also stick to this if you do not find a sign.

The non-smoking law has also found its enforcement in the US. Smoking is prohibited in most public buildings and squares, as well as in restaurants and bars. Smoking will be particularly expensive in the hotel room, as in this case high cleaning costs arise. The laws vary, however, depending on the US state. The best place to smoke is on site. The minimum age to legally purchase cigarettes is 18 years.

In the US, there are strict rules on the purchase and consumption of alcohol. Drinking beer, wine and others in public is punishable. In principle, alcohol consumption (including beer and wine) is only permitted from 21 years of age, which is why it may still be possible for you to be asked about the ID card in restaurants or bars. Alcohol at the wheel is an absolute taboo and the 0 promille limit applies. Carrying alcohol in the car is only allowed if it is in the trunk.

Time difference

As the US extends across a very large area, it includes six time zones.

Eastern Standard Time: 6 hours behind Central European time: Connecticut, Delaware, District of Columbia, Florida - with exceptions, Georgia, Indiana - with exceptions, Kentucky - only eastern parts, Maine, Maryland, Massachusetts, Michigan - with exceptions, New Hampshire, New Jersey, New York, North Carolina, Ohio, Pennsylvania, Rhode Island, South Carolina, Tennessee, Vermont, Virginia, West Virginia

Central Standard Time: 7 hours behind Central European time : Alabama, Arkansas, Florida, Illinois, Indiana, Iowa, Kansas, Kentucky, Louisiana, Michigan, Minnesota, Mississippi, Missouri, Nebraska, North Dakota, Oklahoma, South Dakota, Tennessee, Texas, Wisconsin

Mountain Standard Time: 8 hours behind Central European time: Arizona, Colorado, Idaho, Kansas, Montana, Nebraska, New Mexico, North Dakota, Oregon, South Dakota, Texas, Utah, Wyoming

Pacific Standard Time: 9 hours behind the Central European time

Alaska: 10 hours behind the Central European period

Hawaii: 12 hours behind Central European time

Caral - the archaeological sensations of South America

North of Peru's capital is Caral, the historically oldest city in America. The UNESCO World Heritage Site was established 2,600 years before Christ. At the same time as the pyramids were created in Egypt, pyramids were also built in Caral independently. In addition, two other ceremonial places, an amphitheatre and a huge irrigation system were discovered.

Privacy notice for the public team preview

How data is processed when visiting this non-indexed but publicly accessible preview.

Reviewed: 30 October 2026 · América Special Tours

Team preview, not a production website: please do not enter real contact, travel, or other personal data. The legal operator details and actual use of the social media accounts have not yet been confirmed.

Controller and approval status

The operator and privacy details imported from earlier website versions have not been confirmed for this preview. Before production use, verify the controller's legal name, address, privacy contact, and—where applicable—data protection officer against the legal notice. Until then, this page is not an approved production notice.

Hosting and technical access data

This preview is published using GitHub Pages. GitHub's documentation states that a visitor's IP address is logged and stored for security purposes when a GitHub Pages site is visited. GitHub handles this processing under its own privacy information. The preview is publicly accessible; noindex metadata does not prevent direct access.

Externally loaded fonts and media

The pages load Google Fonts from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com. Depending on the page, editorial images are loaded from the media hosts listed below and from images.unsplash.com. The relevant server receives technical connection data, including the visitor's IP address and browser information. These connections are made when the page loads.

Forms and email

The inquiry forms are prototypes. Submitting a form does not send an inquiry to a website server; it opens an email draft on the visitor's device or in their email application. The information in that draft is sent to the recipient and through the selected email service only if the visitor sends it. Please do not enter real information in this preview.

Analytics, advertising, and embedded social content

The reviewed preview code contains no analytics or advertising pixels and no embedded social media feeds. The externally loaded font and image files described above are separate resources. The technical review must be repeated before any change or publication.

Data rights and details still to be confirmed

To handle requests for access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, or complaints to a data protection authority, the controller, purposes, legal bases, retention periods, and recipients must be confirmed. These details cannot reliably be established from source code. Please consult the respective privacy information published by GitHub, Google, Unsplash, or the social media providers for their processing.

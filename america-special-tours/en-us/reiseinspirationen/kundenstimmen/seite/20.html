Erwin Härtl: Peru round trip, 24.10.2017

"I haven't experienced such a thing in this concentrated form of professional care personally, and summa summarum was thus the whole journey completely stress-free and therefore even more beautiful than anyway."

Mr Rüger,

After our return from Peru, I would like to thank you again in all form for this wonderful trip for the following reasons, because it is simply no longer to be topped in the overall assessment:

Even in the preliminary stages of travel planning, not only were your suggestions impressive, but you have taken all individual wishes into consideration from the outset.

The stay in Peru was then perfectly organized, the timing fantastic and exactly matched to our wishes for time, all excellent German speaking guides and also the chauffeurs really competent, extremely helpful, also special requests easily accessible, the means of transport spacious and pleasant, the care above average well. Everywhere we were accompanied, of course also to the banks etc., good restaurants were recommended on request and reserved for us.

And when you had to get up very early because of Machu Picchu, in order to stand in line for the tickets and shuttle buses for quite a long time, we were able to sleep anyway and only longer because our guide Elisabeth stood in line for us two hours !!! That pays tribute, which is far more than the usual good customer service as you know it.

The tour of the winery was a very pleasant surprise and the wine tasting was very instructive, should be guided on every round trip as well as the entertaining cooking course.Of course an unforgettable highlight was the ride and overnight stay in Andean Explorer, pure luxury and nostalgia.

But here, as well as during the entire journey, we were always provided with valuable tips and were also closely cared for and accompanied at trains or airports until the last possible second.

I have not experienced such a thing in this concentrated form of professional care personally, and summa summarum was thus the whole trip completely stress-free and therefore even more beautiful than anyway.

It was my first trip with you, but certainly not my last. Last but not least, you are a tour operator, not a travel agent, and this can be worth gold in case of any problems that may arise and save a lot of trouble. But, everything went 100% really smoothly as promised, and the travel times that are important to us, not starting too early, not too late at the end of the day tours, were super adhered to.

It goes without saying that I am not only very happy to thank you, but I will highly recommend you.

Best regards from Berlin

Mr. R. and Mr. L.: Guatemala Round Trip

"It was our third trip with Asia or América Special Tours and we feel closely connected to your agency. Not only did the perfect organization of travel contribute to this, but also the individual care that is not a promise of advertising."

Dear Mr Rüger,

first again a warm thank you for the dream trip to Guatemala and many thanks for the intensive follow-up discussion. It was now our third trip with Asia or América Special Tours and we feel closely connected to your agency. Not only did the perfect organization of travel contribute to this, but also the individual care that is not an advertising promise (where is it?).

The fact that we have now decided to write a few lines about Guatemala is due to the fact that Guatemala is obviously still too short, and wrongly! So now a little blog.

We had chosen three main areas of focus in the planning: attractive landscapes, colorful markets and above all the Mayan culture. The highland with its numerous volcanoes, especially the Atitlán Lake, but also the rainforest region in Petén, is scenically attractive. The markets are an Eldorado for photographers; it is not only worth visiting the most famous in Chichicastenango, but also in Almolonga, Zunil and above all in Solalá (Atitlán Lake).

If you expect architectural highlights in terms of picturesque towns and cities in Guatemala, Antigua must be enjoyed, because after that there is not much left. The charm of the villages lies in the authentic hustle and bustle, such as on festive occasions to Maria Ascension. The authenticity is quickly lost when a particular tourism comes in; Panajachel and Flores change their face and become small party miles for backpackers. So hurry up and go! Sensational are the old Mayan ruins and the vitality of elements of traditional Mayan culture in everyday life.

Those who expect a rush at the Hotspot Tikal like at Neuschwanstein will be amazed: According to statistics, only 250,000 visitors come per year. We felt like we almost had the park for ourselves. The sites are located in an extensive protected area for the rainforest, which had once again been significantly expanded at the beginning of the millennium thanks to the development aid initiative of former Foreign Minister Joschka Fischer. If you climb temples in Yaxha and Tikal you get a film-ready view. Between the temples jungle-feeling pure with toucans, parrots and roar monkeys. If you can't get enough of Mayan ruins, you should definitely plan the "stripper" to Copán (Honduras). Absolutely worth seeing there also the museum and the free living macaws at the entrance. By the way: The town of San José de Copán is also quite picturesque. Recommendation: It should be two to three days already in Copán!

The elements of traditional Mayan culture remain in everyday life. In addition to the indigenous clothing of the indigenous population (three regions are always dominated by them, a singularity in Latin America), there are colorful cemeteries with Indian rites that do not immediately reveal themselves as such; the cross symbol also existed in Mayan symbolism and is interpreted quite ambiguously. If you are interested in naive painting (with world class) full of symbolism, you have to go to the artist's village San Juán Comalapa; it is actually still an insider tip. Oscar Perén has become familiar with his picture of the inner life of a "Chicken Bus" (a strikingly colorful overland bus, where passengers are shuffled like chickens). An absolute curiosity, however, is the strange Saint Maximón (San Simón) with his insatiable hunger for cigarettes and schnapps, to which (as also experienced in numerous churches) fervent prayers are fervently prayed. By the way, during Holy Week in Santiago Atitlán, Maximón is hung up in front of the Catholic Church as Judas, before he finds shelter again for a year in another house. What a syncretism (mixing of religions)!

In summary, it remains to be said. Drive! Everything is changing. It gets better rarely. And there is this guide on site called Luis Ramirez; he is familiar in all things (even reading Maya glyphs!) and speaks many languages, of course also very good German. First choice!

Best regards

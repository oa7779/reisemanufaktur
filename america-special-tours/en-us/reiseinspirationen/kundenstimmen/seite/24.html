"Thank you very much for the great elaboration of an exceptional tour through North Peru and the organization of a smooth logistics."

Dear Mr Rüger,

now we are well-reserved back in Germany after a very nice, informative and excellently organized tour through North Peru. It all worked out excellently Thanks also to the very good guides who brought us closer by their amazing good background knowledge history and nature of Peru.

The hike to Kuelap was cancelled (1400 meters in altitude) as we promised ourselves more of an intensive tour of the old facilities (3 hours), which would certainly not have made us so much pleasure after a strenuous mountain tour.

Thank you for the great elaboration of an exceptional tour through North Peru and the organization of a smooth logistics.

Yours sincerely,
Martin & Marianne Engelhard

Rainer H.: Ecuador Round Trip 的 24.09.2016

"We would like to thank you and your colleagues again for the very competent and friendly advice on planning and the very reliable organization of our trip to Ecuador and the good tips for Galapagos. The trip was an absolute highlight."

Hello Mrs Deller,

Before I completely forget...
We would like to thank you once again and your colleagues for their very competent and friendly advice on planning and the very reliable organization of our trip to Ecuador and the good tips for Galapagos. The trip was an absolute highlight.

A few points that we found particularly impressive:

1. Obviously very good knowledge of the destination (specialization is just a fine thing)
2. Support also after booking and payment of the trip (there many travel agencies lose the
Interest)
3. Enormous fast response to requests (our girlfriend had the Florida part of the trip at
booked by another provider and sometimes waited enormously long for answers – the
then came so late that there was no point anymore)
4. Very good advice also with the part of the trip that we booked ourselves
5. Very good travel documents (even if I didn't have time to really read them – but the
Children found this class)
6. Very good tour guide - not "only" friendly and German speaking (because of the children not
unimportant), but also competent and adapted by the pace "to our ideas"

What would we do again or otherwise (but it is whining at a super high level):

1. Cotopaxi and Hacienda were great; but as long as you didn't get very close to the volcano
may, I would tighten it a little (it doesn't make any sense after 10 p.m. in Quito
To arrive, to drive into the city and then the next day again towards Cotopaxi
I'd rather sleep in the new hotel at the airport
2. One more day in Quito would have been good (at any other place of the trip but also...).
Quito was really a very pleasant surprise.
3. The "market" outside Quito was rather uncomfortable; not as impressive as in
Morocco, but more like a rancid supermarket. You can see, but mE only if
you have a lot of time or you have to pass it anyway. Excursion to the equator (center of the world) is
certainly interesting for children and when you are there... As a result, no "must"
sea
4. The Hotel "Casa el Eden" was absolutely great – maybe not everything from a European point of view
perfect, but almost everything; insanely friendly and familiar. I also like the location, too
if you might want to take a taxi to the hotel at night.
5. Overall everything was great, but you could have set the priorities a little different
(is of course a very personal assessment).

Greetings and until the next trip.

Her Rainer H.

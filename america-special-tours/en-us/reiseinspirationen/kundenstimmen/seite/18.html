Nicole and Leonhard Rezic: Argentina and Chile round trip

"We are often asked what was so "the" highlight of the trip. This is not easy to answer, because there were so many."

Mr Rüger,
We were very happy about your "Welcome home" postcard and would like to take the opportunity to thank you for the really perfectly planned trip.
The trip to South America (that was the "tasting" for you) planned as a surprise for my wife, with all of our ideas and wishes, you have more than fulfilled and every sighted urlaus photo (and there is a lot of it) awakens great memories and brings us back to beautiful Patagonia.
The personal care by local guides and drivers worked wonderfully and all looked after us very competently and with a lot of passion for land and people - and football! And even though all the excursions were already planned, we were able to make spontaneous wishes flow in on site. Especially if you can't spend a lot of time in one place, this is the perfect way to explore it and get an extensive first overview.
We are often asked what was so "the" highlight of the trip. That's not easy to answer, because there were so many.
Starting from the beautiful and multi-layered Buenos Aires, with the magnificent Gran Cafe Tortoni, Recoleta, the bookstore in El Ateneo and the Boca district - to name but a few.
And then, of course, the glaciers in Patagonia. They're so powerful, and you can hardly get tired of them. With a little luck, they also calve when you stand in front of them. Wow - what natural forces and what color games!
Then the breathtaking flight on and around "End of the World" in Ushuaia, before it went on the 5 day cruise with the state-of-the-art expedition ship Australis Ventus. From Ushuaia to Kaphorn, the glaciers Pia, Garibaldi and Condor, continue to Magdalena Island with the Magellan Penguins and via the Magellan road to Punta Arenas. Highly recommended.
The Hotel "The Singular" in Puerto Natales is an experience of its own (must be seen) and the excursions offered by the hotel in the nearby National Park "Torres del Paine" are also highly recommended. It is an uplifting feeling if you have the luck on the Condor-Hike to get so close to the master of the air and then he glides silently over you with his big wings.
Unfortunately, every trip comes to an end at some point, but when you say "there we go again", with America-Special-Tours, that actually expresses everything.
We will be happy to recommend you to Mr. Rüger and the América Special Tours and look forward to a new adventure with you!
Best regards
Nicole and Leonard Rezic

King couple: Costa Rica trip

"We have met the Pacific and Caribbean side. The diverse nature and the species-rich wildlife of Costa Rica have fascinated us again and again."

Dear Mrs Stork,

Costa Rica is worth a trip! We had a very active holiday. Starting with exciting hikes in several national parks, in volcanic areas and of course in the famous cloud forest Monteverde. This forest really did his name all honour - during our hike a misty mist curtain accompanied us. Due to the extreme humidity a unique plant growth has developed.

In the Rincón de la Vieja National Park, which is home to an active volcano, our path passed by bubbling mud holes, hot springs and some waterfalls. During a tour to another active volcano called "Arenal" we see the lava tongue created in 1968, which still bears witness to the enormous eruption at that time. We also visit the Nauyaca Falls, which consist of two individual waterfalls. The upper one is 45 meters high and the river course has searched for its way 40 million years ago. The nature of Costa Rica makes you wonder again and again!

On our trip through Costa Rica we were able to make numerous animal observations. From iguanas and the rare skull monkeys in the Manuel Antonio National Park, to sloths – which really do their name credit – to roar monkeys, nasal bears and crocodiles. We were also lucky enough to watch a humpback whale with offspring, it was a fascinating natural spectacle. We've never seen a whale watching this close up.

Especially in the Tortuguero National Park, which is crossed by lagoons and canals, the lush green of the plants, the changing animal sounds and the small floating water gardens enchanted us. You're almost in a different world.

We have met the Costa Rican population as very friendly and helpful. Both the guided tours with the local guides and our private guide were all very informative and informative in terms of nature and wildlife. All booked transfers via the local provider were excellently organized.

In the rainforest areas we had to fight with the high humidity at times. All wardrobes, towels and bed linen were and remained damp, which was not always pleasant.

The accommodations you selected were mostly in line with our standard.

Some accommodations were exceptional due to their unique location and facilities. The crowning conclusion of our journey was the Pacuare lodge and that extreme rafting on the river of the same name.

We met the Pacific and Caribbean side. The diverse nature and the species-rich wildlife of Costa Rica have fascinated us again and again.

Yours sincerely,
King married couple

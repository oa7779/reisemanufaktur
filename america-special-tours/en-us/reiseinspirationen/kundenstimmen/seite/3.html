Guest Experiences

Here you will find references from customers who have already traveled with us.

E. J.: Argentina, Chile and Bolivia

"We look back on a top-organized individual trip"

After a good 2 weeks we are back from a highly interesting and varied trip to Argentina, Chile and Bolivia.
We look back on a top-organized individual journey, which has given us many unique impressions and experiences in these countries.
We enjoyed the European-influenced Buenos Aires, experienced the Atacama desert in Chile with the peak of the Geyser field "El Tatio" and the expanse of the highlands of Bolivia with the many different lagoons.
After many kilometers in untouched nature of the highlands we reached our peak in Bolivia, the "Salar Uyuni". A white salt desert as far as the eye can see, just incredible.
After so much nature we experienced a great graduation in La Paz.
Our thanks go to Mrs Reichert for the advice and compilation of the trip. Here our wishes were supplemented with great ideas.
We experienced a smooth organization with excellent local guides (Karsten in Chile and Margot in Bolivia) that brought us closer to the respective regions with their motivation and deep local knowledge.
In short – a successful holiday trip.

E.M. from Austria: Cuba trip

"The organization was perfect"

Already at the airport we learned the situation of the country, although we had been very informed. Everything from the times of our parents and grandparents. No suitcases can be found, no information, no internet to exchange with our tour guide. After a few hours, the luggage finally arrived. Thank God our driver Robert and our guide Adalberto were waiting for us. The two deserve great praise. Both were trusting, serious and pleasant. Roberto our driver was always punctual and always had gasoline!!Adalberto our guide is very educated and has brought us perfectly Cuba, with his history closer. The landscapes, the beaches are beautiful. The cities, which is still preserved, are beautiful as in the film. The Tropicana show in Havana was very impressive, a relic from the past, the ride with the classic cars a pleasure, great sunsets and well-chosen places. the drive from Havana to Trinidad via Cienfuegos and to Varadero, we enjoyed very much.
The accommodations were fine. Breakfast was fine, too. The situation in the country must be taken into account. The living situation of the population is sad. Politics has actually ruined the country.
The organization was perfect, everything worked out great. Thank you to Mrs. Ess!

Clients: Peru and Chile Round Trip

"Thank you again for putting together the great Peru/Chile trip and, above all, that you have responded to our wishes and integrated them into the beautiful journey."

Dear Mr. Rüger!

Thanks again for the compilation of the great Peru/Chile trip and above all that you have responded to our wishes and integrated them into the beautiful journey.

It all worked perfectly. The tour guides were very nice and always had personal tips for our leisure program, which enriched our trip.The selected hotels were top and the staff very busy. This is not so often experienced in Europe. We felt very comfortable everywhere. Since we love to stay in nature, the Inka Trail was an experience, although very demanding, as we didn't just want to drive through the area by car. Machu Picchu was of course a highlight.

Since we were there very early we could enjoy the sight in peace and are crawled up everywhere and took beautiful photos. The other Inca buildings were also very impressive. The Condore have also made us expectance. Lake Titicaca with its floating islands (which are inhabited almost only for tourists), the Tatio Geysires, the salt saline (where we took a piece) and the salt lakes and much more have left unforgettable impressions that we first processed at home. We managed well with the height, but you can feel that you are at 4000 m. Planning with exhausting car rides and subsequent relaxing days was just right. So the days on the island of Suasi and Cusco, where we could do something on our own, were to our liking. Even the train rides, with fashion show of the train staff, will always remain in our memory.

Chile with the Atacama desert, where there are all kinds of desert, to marvel at and the great hotel complex we liked very much. The relaxing conclusion was our unaccompanied days in the beautiful Valparaiso (Paradise Valley) where we explored the city and drove up and down the hills with some of the famous and already very antique looking ascensores (lifts or funiculars).

Thank you for the many work in assembling and the great organization, also on site, of our trip. We are happy to let you seduce us on a journey again.

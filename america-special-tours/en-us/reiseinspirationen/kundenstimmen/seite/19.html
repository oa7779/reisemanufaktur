Couple Tribe: Peru Round Trip

"The trip was very well organized by you and the timing was perfect."

Dear Mrs Deller,

with a little delay we would like to send you our impressions of our Peru trip.
The trip was organized by you very well and the timing was perfect. Our thanks go to the tour guides Sandra, Jack, Paulina, Elizabeth, who were all expert, happy and helpful. Also the drivers drove very safely and you could enjoy the trip relaxed.

Our trip took us from Lima to Paracas, where we visited the Islas Ballestas (small Galapagos). The island was populated by thousands of birds, penguins and sea lions. A second highlight was the flight over the huge Nascas Lines, a unique experience.

Our next stop was the white city of Arequipa with its impressive colonial buildings. The trip to the unique Colca Canyon and the 12 Condore that circled over us were fascinating. We went on to Lake Titicaca, where we rested after visiting the unique floating islands of the Uros, 3 days on the idyllic island of Suasi. It is amazing that on 4000m still so many plants and flowers grow. This city impresses with its colonial splendour and the Catholic churches are very worth seeing. Our hotel was located in a former monastery.

Another highlight was Machu Picchu. The journey with Peru Rails led through a beautiful landscape. In the afternoon we had a very interesting tour with Liza in the Inca site and the next morning we let the unique place with a little less tourists re-act on us. The salt terraces of Moray from the Inca period were also worth a visit.

The last few days we spent in the rainforest of Puerto Maldonado. The Corto Malteses Lodge was beautifully located directly on the river and ideal for trips to the rainforest. One day (depart at 5 am) we spent at Sandoval Lake, where we watched monkeys, exotic birds and playing otters. The next day we could observe a large flock of parrots flying to the salt lick early in the morning.
We highly recommend América Special Tours and have booked our Namibia trip through your sister company Africa Royal Tours.

Dear Mrs Deller,
thanks again for putting together this individual trip with such reliable guides and drivers. We enjoyed the trip very much and often remember it.

Kind greetings and all the best,
Couple Tribe

Familie Schweinberger: Argentina, Brazil, Colombia and Peru round trip 的 06.11.2017

"It was a fantastic journey with an incredible amount of impressions that have awakened our desire for much more South America."

Dear Mr Rüger,

This is how the week can begin! We all came back on 30.10.2017 safely. It was a fantastic journey with an incredible amount of impressions, which have awakened us still desire for much more South America.

It all worked out very well and perfectly planned dramaturgically with the highlights Iguazu and Rio at the end of the trip. You have fulfilled our ideas and wishes magnificently. Your suggestion to gradually get used to the heights was just right. With Bogota , Lima , Arequipa at the beginning , we could get used to the different heights.

Their selected tour guides were all so motivated, competent and so kind that we not only took a lot of technical activities with us, but also experienced a lot of positive human experiences on site. A special thank you to our travel guide Elisabeth, who accompanied us for four days and was particularly committed to fulfilling all our wishes!

The hotel selection was almost perfect, especially we enjoyed the upgrade in Aranwa Sacred Valley. You could have spent another night there. But also the Libertador at Lake Titicaca with the magnificent lake view and the Miramar in Rio were very nice. To end the holiday on the terrace with a view of sugar cane was fantastic. One of the many highlights was undoubtedly a visit to the Central in Lima, sensationally the kitchen with 17 courses. Also thanks for the small surprises, Pisco Sour, restaurant in Rio, etc.

From the organization everything worked perfectly, 10 flights, all on time, all suitcases were there, the hotels were prepared and we had nothing to worry about and could enjoy much more South America.

Mr. Rüger, we are happy to recommend you further, you have planned and organized this very well and I am sure that we will plan the next South America trip, which will certainly come, together with you again.

Greetings Thank you Klaus Schweinberger

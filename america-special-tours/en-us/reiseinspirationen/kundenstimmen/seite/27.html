Claudia Gerds: Brazil round trip

"And today I would like to use the time and again in writing to express my thanks and my joy: YOU MAKE A WONDERABLE JOB!"

Mr Rüger,

I haven't forgotten you! And today I want to use the time and again in writing to express my thanks and my joy: YOU MAKE A WONDERABLE JOB!

From my first request to the call after the trip, we always felt wonderfully cared for. You have responded to all our wishes, every small change has been recorded and included in the holiday planning. The written summary of the trip, the sending of the guide and also the help with the arrival of the visa - we could always count on you.

We really want to express our thanks for this wonderful service. You've always been kind and accommodating, and I don't want to smear any honey around your mouth, I mean it really. I work myself in the reservation of a hotel and I know how strenuous this job is - therefore hat off!!!

Also when a small problem with the room occurred during the holiday, we could rely on you and got such a wonderful upgrade. Mr. Rüger, you have made us an amazing pleasure !!! Thanks again for that!

You can be sure that we will think of you on the next long distance trip!

However, I would have a tiny remark: all the planned trips were really great. From the organization, through the guide to food - always great. Except for the trip to the turtle farm... maybe it's funny for children to look at, but it's nothing for adults. You don't really know whether you want to go or not. We had actually been looking forward to a relaxing day in the so-called "fisher's village". Unfortunately, this city is no longer a fishing village, but a single tourist point. I just want to tell you this because you may not have to have it in your program. But that's my personal assessment.

Thanks again for everything and warm greetings from the Baltic Sea

Claudia Gerds

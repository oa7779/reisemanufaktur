Susan Baumann & Martin Kohstall: Ecuador Travel 的 06.01.2023

"There was not a single delay, it was all very well planned and executed."

Last night we landed in Munich on time and relaxed. This morning I go through our mail and see the dear postcard they sent us and I am very happy about your lines. Then I thought that I was about to take the opportunity to write to you how grateful we are for this fantastic travel planning and the incredible experiences we were allowed to have.

Actually, I don't know where to start! I'm gonna start in Quito.
Our tour guide, Sylvana was a real pearl. She was super nice, super punctual, very knowing, responded to all our wishes and it just made huge fun with her.
We found Quito super exciting and it was a real highlight to ride with the cable car up on the Pichincha and enjoy the incredible view! We were totally lucky with the weather and saw all the snow covered volcano peaks - that is not supposed to be a matter of course. We even walked down and enjoyed it very much!!

Both hotels were beautiful, our rooms spacious and stylish and the breakfast and dinner TOTAL delicious!!

On Galápagos one highlight followed the next:
Our motor yacht "Dominika" and the crew and captain were just great!
Our Enviro guide Henzel was incredibly knowing, super nice and very hard for us all to take advantage of every second and show us everything Galápagos has to offer.

The food on the ship was very varied, prepared with lots of love and really delicious! Next to me, as a vegetarian, was a vegan on board and they ALWAYS cooked something extra for us, which was of high quality and tasty.

During the hikes, snorkelling trips, kayaking tours and motor boat trips every single day a new SUPERLATIVE...

We have seen so many animals, fish and birds, we have seen sea lions swimming, playing, sucking, old, young, a baby shortly after birth. We've seen sharks, from the yacht, in the canal, snorkeling (!), we've seen hundreds of sea turtles, two different species, eating, swimming, pairing (!!), we've seen various rays, thousands of colorful fish, penguins on land, in the sea, hunting, quite, a lot of iguanas, runny boobs on land, in the air, diving, mating dance, many other birds, incredible landscapes, starry sky, fantastic beaches... it was really incredibly beautiful.

The short stop in Guayaquil at the end was just right! We also liked the city very much, our driver, transfer attendant and our guide Corina were all fantastic. Corina went with us cable car at our request and accompanied us to the handicraft market so that we could take a nice hat from Ecuador

The hotel was simply great and, the fact that you had already organized the late check-out for us, just perfect.

In conclusion, I can only say that it is always an absolute pleasure to embark on a journey organized by America/, Asia Special Tours. There was not a single delay, it was all very well planned and executed, the travel guides were perfect and the hotels sensational. What can I say besides THANK YOU- MUCHOS GRACIAS!!

Mittmann married couple: Ecuador trip

"The journey is unforgettable for us, we have taken many beautiful memories home"

After a year of waiting as a result of the Corona effects, we were able to start the trip to Ecuador prepared by América Special Tours under the direction of Mrs. Zehnder in May/June 2022. Our specifications/ideas for travel such as We were able to determine the length of the trip, places/spots/hotels/lodges in advance, and Mrs. Zehnder then designed the trip around these fixed points. It was our first trip ever that a travel company "knitted us, but we would do it again and again. As a result of the lack of Spanish and local knowledge, the organization was important to us through experienced experts in the country. Our personal German-speaking guide in Quito was just the right person to guide us through the country. In the individual destinations of the trip we had local English speaking guides, which we like to remember.

With Quito as a central point of departure, we visited the Amazon area, got to know the cloud forest, drove into the Andes to the Cotopaxi and set off for a cruise to the Galapagos Islands. We can't create a ranking, each destination had something special, but it was ALWAYS impressive.

The journey is unforgettable for us, we have taken many beautiful memories home and thousands of photos.

At this point again thanks to Mrs. Zehnder and her team, it was perfect to simply drop in Ecuador and enjoy the organized journey.

We highly recommend Ecuador to those who are looking for a holiday close to nature, and on our trip with perfect hotels with great rooms, dedicated and attentive staff and great cuisine that was regional and nevertheless international. And one more thing, there are nowhere as good fresh fruit juices as in Ecuador, which we will miss as many others.

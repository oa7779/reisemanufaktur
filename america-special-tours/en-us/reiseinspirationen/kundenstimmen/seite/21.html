Nadin Dörk and Reto Mohr: Patagonia Round Trip

"Our ideas were used as a basis and complemented by wonderful destinations, exciting excursions and organized tours. "

Dear Mrs Deller

In November 2016, during América Special Tours, we asked about travel inspiration on the Internet about activities, destinations and transportation in Patagonia. We quickly realized that we were rather late for our planned travel window in January 2017. Therefore, we were very happy to receive the professional advice and support in the travel planning of Mrs Deller.

Our ideas were used as a basis and complemented by wonderful destinations, exciting excursions and organized tours.

So we first visited Buenos Aires on our own and after five days flew further to El Calafate. Three guided full days were at our disposal to marvel at the Perito Moreno glacier. A mini trekking on the eternal ice, watching the calving of the glacier at close range and a boat trip to the Upsala Glacier triggered a constant enthusiasm.

Then we went by bus to the Torres Del Paine National Park. The transport worked perfectly and was a highlight in itself: on the way we saw countless Guanakos, Rheas and some condors. The Hotel Las Torres was delightfully nestled in the countryside and an ideal starting point for hikes in the National Park. The wind, which blew us around the ears at up to 120 km/h, must not be underestimated! The food was excellent and came partly from the own vegetable garden. We enjoyed the evenings in one of the many lounges and watched the countless released horses grazing.

Afterwards we went again by bus to Punta Arenas. The half-day drive through the barren Chilean Plateau was little entertaining, but passed quickly. We were all the more pleased when we arrived in the port of Punta Arenas. A private guide brought us closer to the charm and history of the town and showed us the historical monuments. From an elevated point we were able to survey the city as a whole. However, only the prices of the hotel's own laundry service were withdrawn; in search of a laundromat we were found in a side street.

The next day we sailed with the Stella Australis for a cruise in the direction of Ushuaia. Over the next five days, a highlight for the others, including a hike in the sub-Antarctic Magellanur Forest, a visit to a penguin colony on Tucker Island, a passage along the Pia Glacier, a ride through the "Gletscherallee" in the northwestern arm of the Beagle Canal with musical accompaniment and typical snacks inspired by the names of the glaciers, sighting two humpback whales and finally the passage at Cape Hoorn. All experiences were accompanied by excellent on-board service, friendly and knowledgeable expedition personnel and excellent regional and international cuisine.

The arrival in Ushuaia early in the morning in the best weather made it possible for us to observe the hustle and bustle at the harbor. The disembarkation then went smoothly. Our private driver stood ready and brought us to Hotel Arakur, which on the hillside offered an incomparable view of the city. The food was excellent, but the massage did not quite meet our expectations.

The two days we used to stroll in the southernmost city of the world, for a hiking trip to Laguna Esmeralda and a trip to Harberton Ranch and Martillo Island in the Beagle Canal. There we were once again awaited by a lively penguin drive: countless Magellan penguins, donkey penguins and two of the three King Penguins living on the island we were able to admire firsthand. This was one of the most beautiful moments of our South America trip, with which even the heavy rain, wind and uncomfortable cold temperatures could not change.

The next day it was said goodbye. We left Ushuaia in the direction of Matanzas to the coast of Chile. There we enjoyed another couple of days of warm weather, a cozy relaxing on the beach and delicious food... wouldn't a forest fire break out seven kilometers south of our hotel on the third day. Due to poor air quality, this forced us to leave prematurely.

In short, we decided to take a look at Santiago de Chile for two days, which set a beautiful final point of our trip. Greetings and thanks again for your valuable support in travel planning!

Nadin & Reto

Jürgen Gies: Ecuador and Galapagos Tour

"The resume of my journey: just amazing!"

Dear Mr Rüger,

back again over 2 weeks after my almost 3-week Ecuador/Galapagos trip, but no day passes when I don't think about this extraordinary trip.

I would particularly like to thank you for the extremely pleasant, indeed friendly way in our conversations with which we have always worked on my trip.

You have all fulfilled my very special wishes excellently. In particular, I will remember the train ride with the "Tren Crusero" along the road of the volcanoes, a very extraordinary, not everyday experience. Not to forget the days on Galapagos ! Sustainable impressions with my daily boat cruises on the different islands, partly with breathtaking landscapes and special wildlife.

Also a big praise to the partner agencies on site. With the optimal punctuality and friendly care my more than 10 transfers and 5 domestic flights went without problems. This is not always a matter of course when traveling.

The Resumee of My Trip : Just Amazing !

All the more I am looking forward to my further trips to Uzbekistan, Java and Panama/Costa Rica with you.

Best regards from Kaiserslautern,

Jürgen Gies

Max Fischer: Argentina, Peru and Uruguay round trip

"Even three weeks after our return from Peru, we are still raving about our wonderful journey."

Dear Mr Rüger,

also three weeks after our return from Peru we still rave about our wonderful journey.

We had many special wishes, which you could all very well fulfill. From the individual flight routes to the desired days of relaxation with the beach and the sea, especially at the beginning of our journey, to the sometimes very special hotel wishes, you and your team have mastered everything and yet still gives the impression that it makes you tremendously fun!

So the Hotel Playa Vik in Uruguay was a true design dream with relaxation guarantee and "Wow! factor", right on the endless long sandy beach. In Buenos Aires you had accommodated us in contrast in the middle of Palermo's busiest neighborhood - that was just right for us night owls after the quiet days in Uruguay. For Lima you had some insider tips anyway and the subsequent ten-day Peru trip from Cusco and the sacred valley, the absolute highlight Machu Picchu up to Lake Titicaca was just perfectly organized, with the perfect mixture of interesting tours, expert explanations, visits and still plenty of space for recreation and own discoveries.

The hotels "Nazarenas" in Cusco for the incomparable personal service and the "Titilaka" for the fantastic location and the unique tours offered there on Lake Titicaca are worth mentioning.

The German-language tour guides you provided were all incredibly competent, absolutely reliable, but flexible for our concerns, very friendly and extremely committed to our well being. All transfers - whether flight, train, ship or car - worked smoothly.

We are happy to recommend you further and come - perhaps once for a trip to Colombia? - back to you!

Greetings,

Max Fischer

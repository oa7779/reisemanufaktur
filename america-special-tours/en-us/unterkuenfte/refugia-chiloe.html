Refugia Chiloé

San Jose, Chile
Luxury Boutique Hotel

Information

The new and luxurious boutique hotel Refugia Chiloé Hotel & Spa is located on the mystical island of Chiloé with picturesque fishing villages in the northern part of Chile's Patagonia. The beauty of this destination lies in the countryside as well as in the islanders with their deep-rooted culture and timeless traditions. In the true Tierra style, the characteristic architecture of the lodge was designed in harmony with the breathtaking surroundings. The own boat, the "Williche" is available for hire at any time and lets you explore the waters around the hotel. The rooms are modern and very stylish and have a fantastic lake view on request. The restaurant includes delicious chiloé dishes prepared with excellent local food from land and sea. The hotel's spa includes sauna, steam bath, a large jacuzzi and numerous wellness treatments.

Morgan's Rock Hacienda & Ecolodge

For your perfect stay in Nicaragua, the experts at América Special Tours recommend the 5-star Morgan's Rock Hacienda & Ecolodge in Playa Ocotal.

Morgan's Rock Hacienda & Ecolodge

Playa Ocotal, Nicaragua, Central America
Luxury Ecolodge

Information

Located on a 4000-hectare area of the Nicaraguan jungle, almost half of the huge property of Morgan's Rock Hacienda and Ecolodge is a private nature reserve. The lodge has been carefully built to minimally influence the environment, offering a unique blend of tropical liveliness and architectural comfort. The stylish bungalows are very spacious and lovingly furnished. On request you have either a sea or forest view. In any case, the view is unique. The lodge has a great private beach, which is visited all year round by sea turtles. Special highlights are the hotel's restaurant and farmhouse, where traditional and natural dishes are served with ingredients that mostly come from its own cultivation. Enjoy the fantastic view over the sea during your dinner.

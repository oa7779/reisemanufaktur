Le Commandant Charcot

Travel sustainably and luxuriously to the most remote places in the world with your partner América Special Tours at Le Commandant Charcot.

Le Commandant Charcot

Ushuaia, Antarctica
Luxury cruise ship

Information

Le Commandant Charcot is the world's first luxury expedition ship with hybrid drive and ice class PC2, which will take you safely to the geographic North Pole or deep into Antarctica. On board, state-of-the-art environmental technology and Scandinavian elegance merge into a unique base camp for adventurers. All cabins and suites have private balconies that offer lodges for the spectacular ice world. While you participate in scientific projects during the day or explore unspoilt landscapes, in the evening you will be spoiled with first-class gastronomy at a star level. A heated outdoor pool and the spacious spa area offer the perfect contrast to the rugged wilderness of the polar regions. Join your journey into the eternal ice worlds and experience the comfort of an exceptional luxury icebreaker.

Additional information

Length: 150 m
Capacity: Max. 245 guests / 215 crew
Cabins & Suites: 123, all with private balcony or terrace
Expedition: Zodiacs, 2 scientific laboratories and more than 100 m² of research area
Wellness: Spa, fitness, indoor and outdoor pool
Cuisine: 2 restaurants, several lounges and bars, 24-hour service in the cabin
Technique: Hybrid electric drive with LNG, ice class PC2

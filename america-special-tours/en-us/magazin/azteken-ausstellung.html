Aztecs – Large State Exhibition Baden-Württemberg

As a cooperation partner of the Linden Museum in Stuttgart, we would like to encourage you to take part in the Grand National Exhibition "Aztecen". From 12 October 2019 to 3 May 2020 (extended until 16 August 2020), you have the opportunity to get a deep insight into the life of the Aztecs. Join us on this exciting journey of discovery!

What: Aztecs – Large State Exhibition Baden-Württemberg
When: 12 October 2019 to 03 May 2020 (extended until 16 August 2020)
Where: Linden-Museum Stuttgart
State Museum of Ethnology
Hegelplatz 1
70174 Stuttgart

Insight into the world of Aztecs

On the occasion of the 500th anniversary of the arrival of Spanish conqueror Hernán Cortés in the Gulf of Mexico, the Linden Museum in Stuttgart takes visitors on a journey into the Aztec world. "Aztecen" is a joint exhibition of the Linden Museum and its cooperation partner, the Nationaal Museum van Wereldculturen in the Netherlands. The exhibition is under the patronage of Federal President Frank-Walter Steinmeier.

Time travel along the route of the conquerors

The dramaturgy of the Aztec exhibition follows the itinerary of Hernán Cortés and leads from the provinces to the heart of the holy capital Tenochtitlan. Discover the Aztec Empire with its cultural and natural diversity, magnificent palaces of rulers and the innermost sacred district with the main Templo Mayor temple. The focus of the exhibition is on a unique green stone figure and two of the Aztecs' four preserved spring shields worldwide. Let yourself be enchanted by impressive stone sculptures and admire their natural and detailed representation. Marvel at precious mosaics and imposing gold jewellery that once adorned the magnificent palaces of the Aztec rulers.

Unique excavations and loans

The Aztec exhibition has yet another highlight. Excavations that have never been shown before will be presented to the public for the first time at the large national exhibition. Look forward to offerings recently discovered at Templo Mayor.

The exhibition is complemented by around 150 loans from Europe and Mexico. The Museo Templo Mayor in Mexico City, the National Museum of Denmark and the Vienna World Museum sometimes support the Linden Museum in Stuttgart and the Aztec exhibition.

Further information on the exhibition can be found on the website of the Linden Museum in Stuttgart.

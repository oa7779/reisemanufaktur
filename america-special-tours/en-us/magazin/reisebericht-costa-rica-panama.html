Our American expert Laia in Costa Rica and Panama

Our American expert Laia Bergadá reports on her trip to Costa Rica and Panama. An inspiring insight into two impressive destinations.

11.12.2025

Our American expert Laia Bergada has been on the road for you in Costa Rica and Panama – between rainforest, ocean and exclusive eco-lodges. Your personal travel report shows why these two countries are among the most fascinating destinations in Central America.

Getting to the Pacific Coast and first impressions

It all began on the Pacific coast of Costa Rica, a region known for its lush nature, endless beaches, warm hospitality of people and a special mix of relaxation and adventure. Getting there via the coastal road from San José to Manuel Antonio was a special experience. The hilly road led through dense tropical forest. The noise of the sea accompanied the ride and in the dusk the lights of small villages appeared from time to time. The humid, warm tropical air already let me guess which lush vegetation would expect me the next morning.

Arenas del Mar – nature, comfort and sustainable luxury

My first accommodation, Arenas del Mar, immediately attracted me. This resort is nestled in a private nature reserve in Manuel Antonio, terraced on a steep hill from which you get directly to the beach of the National Park. This privilege is only granted to a few houses in the region and from the very first moment it made me feel like I was in a particularly protected place. The first time I looked out of my room over the tops of the trees at the sea, I realized how extraordinary this spot is. Colorful birds coughed from branch to branch, capuchin monkeys played in the treetops and in the distance I could even observe how a single whale majestically passed through the water. An atmosphere that at the same time was calming and invigorating.

The rooms at the resort are generously decorated, modernly furnished and harmoniously integrated into the natural environment. Especially the breakfast at Mirador Restaurant was a daily highlight. Freshly squeezed juices, handmade smoothies, tropical fruit that was so aromatic that it tasted almost like a dessert, Gallo Pinto in all its variants and freshly prepared egg dishes made every morning a culinary pleasure. The service was warm and at the same time impressively professional, and the staff talked with a noticeable enthusiasm about the nature conservation projects of the hotel and the animals that regularly stop by the resort. An unforgettable experience was undoubtedly the night walk through the hotel's own rainforest. Equipped with a flashlight, I discovered frogs, spiders, glowing insects and other mysterious night dwellers. It was a truly authentic natural experience.

Experiences in the Manuel Antonio National Park

Although the Manuel Antonio National Park is often well visited, I felt that my visit there was worthwhile. The mixture of dense rainforests, white sandy beaches, turquoise water and exceptional wildlife is unique. I saw sloths, iguanas, different species of monkeys and an impressive variety of exotic birds. I also remember our guide, who was super competent and really enriched the tour with exciting and lively explanations. Thanks to his experience and enthusiasm, I was able to discover many details about the flora and fauna that would otherwise have escaped me. I also found it very impressive how much the national park pays attention to protecting the environment. It is strictly forbidden to bring in plastic of any kind, not even water bottles, sunscreens or other packaging are allowed. This consistent attitude shows how seriously the park takes its responsibility for nature and the ocean.

Continue to Uvita – Kurà: architecture, silence and panorama

After a few days of nature experiences, my journey continued to Kurà, an exclusive Adults-Only boutique hotel high above Uvita. The approach mediated the special location of the house. Tight serpentines that stroll through the green landscape opened spectacular panoramas across the coast. Kurà impresses with a modern, almost avant-garde design. Black walls, purple accents, clear lines and an infinity pool that seems to pass directly into the ocean shape the architecture. Each room offers a breathtaking view of the coast and the tropical forest, and it became clear when you entered that this place is fully oriented towards peace, exclusivity and a deep connection to nature.

Isla Palenque – Tropical island paradise and pure deceleration

From Kurà I continued my trip to Panama. After the passport check at the border, a very friendly guide from the Hotel Isla Palenque awaited us, who warmly welcomed us. While continuing to Boca Chica, he spoiled us with freshly prepared empanadas from the hotel and homemade fruit juices, which made the transfer particularly pleasant and entertaining. In Boca Chica there was already a speedboat waiting for us to get to the island.

My accommodation was slightly elevated above the sea and offered a fantastic view of the ocean. Every day was filled with exciting but at the same time relaxed activities. Kayaking tours led to a small sea cave where bats lived, walks through the rainforest offered numerous animal observations, and time and again I met capuchin monkeys, iguanas, parrots and colorful birds. A special experience was the rum tasting on the beach at sunset.

The island also convinced culinaryly on the whole line. The kitchen was fresh, regional and of high quality. Fish, seafood and tropical fruits formed the basis of many dishes, and everything was prepared with a noticeable attention to detail.  

Return to Costa Rica – Caribbean feeling in Puerto Viejo

After this paradisiacal experience we went back to Costa Rica, more precisely to the Caribbean region around Puerto Viejo. There the Hotel Aguas Claras was waiting for me. The hotel's lovingly designed bungalows are nestled in the jungle, and although the hotel is not right on the beach, the hotel's Beach Club is just a few minutes' walk away. The atmosphere is relaxed, Caribbean and warm. 

Cahuita National Park – wildlife, beaches and impressive diversity

A special highlight was the visit to the Cahuita National Park. The trail leads directly along the sea and offers countless opportunities to observe the wildlife at close range. sloths, toucans, turtles and even rays showed up during my visit. The contrast between the light sand, turquoise water and dense vegetation created an image that is hard to beat in its beauty. 

Kayaking tour in Punta Uva followed by a walk to the beach

The tour offered a varied combination of activity and nature experience. During the kayaking tour we could hear the sounds of monkeys and even watch turtles, which made the experience even more special. After kayaking, a small footpath led to a beautiful beach. There we could enjoy a particularly atmospheric sunset.

My personal conclusion

After this intense and enriching journey I draw a consistently positive conclusion. Costa Rica and Panama are two exceptional destinations that combine natural treasures, sustainable hospitality, excellent service and genuine warmth.

In addition to the varied nature experiences, the trip also offered insights into culture and the importance of sustainable tourism in both countries. I was able to observe how Cayuga hotels rely on regionally grown products and work closely with local producers. Every accommodation I visited was not only luxurious, but also a deep awareness of the protection of nature and respect for the people who live and work here.  

Pura vida and gracias at Costa Rica and Panama!

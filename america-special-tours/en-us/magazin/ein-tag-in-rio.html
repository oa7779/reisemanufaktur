One day in Rio: Corcovado, Samba, sugarloaf

Experience Rio de Janeiro: From Christ at Corcovado to a Samba school until sunset on the sugarloaf. Let's get inspired.

23.02.2026

Rio de Janeiro is a geographical and historical exception. Founded in January 1502 by Portuguese seafarers who thought Guanabara Bay was a estuary – hence the name "January River", the metropolis was Brazil's capital for almost 200 years. Today, Rio is captivated by the immediate vicinity of rainforest, colonial architecture and vibrant life. We take you on a route that combines the glamour of postcard motifs with the profound soul of the city.

Above the roofs: Corcovado & Cristo Redentor

We start early, because today's program is as tight as it is fascinating. With the cog railway it goes up to the 710 m high Corcovado, the "Buckligen". In the midst of Tijuca National Park, the largest city forest in the world, the 30-meter-high statue of Christ, one of the "New Seven Wonders of the World" sits here. We enjoy the breathtaking panoramic views of Guanabara Bay and the famous beaches. A remarkable detail: The statue is covered with millions of small soapstone mosaics, which since 1931 retain their bright radiance and give the monument an almost silky texture. As we stand up there, we look at the sea of houses nestled between the steep granite rocks.

Back in the valley, we explore the historic heart of Rio in a compact stroll. We focus on the absolute milestones: The Real Gabinete Português de Leitura, with its magnificent neo-Gothic reading room, looks like a cathedral of knowledge and is a must for aesthetics. After a short photo stop at the elegant Confeitaria Colombo, where we breathe the spirit of Belle Époque, the path leads us to the famous Escadaria Selarón. This mosaic artwork made of tiles from all over the world is the perfect example of the creative energy that drives this city.

Rhythm & Tradition: Cidade do Samba

Now we go to the Gamboa district of Cidade do Samba. In this vast complex, the best samba schools prepare for the big carnival all year round. In the warehouse of the Acadêmicos da Grande Rio we get exciting insights into the history of the famous parade. We explore the workshops, observe the construction of the huge, colorful moving cars and even try on the magnificent costumes. The crowning conclusion is a tasting of the famous Caipirinhas and a percussion and dance workshop. We learn the Afro and Samba rhythms and feel the joy of living that makes this part of Brazilian culture so unique.

The sugar cane at sunset

At the end of the day, we head for the 396 meter high sugar cane. The mountain owes its name to the cone shapes used in sugar production in the 16th century. We take the two glass cable cars that take us to the summit at the western entrance of the bay. We have deliberately chosen this moment: when the sun slowly sinks behind the mountains and the lights of Copacabana and the city below us begin to sparkle, the magical side of Rio reveals itself. It is the perfect place to review the day's impressions in peace, while the "Wonderful City" is immersed in a golden light.

Culinary conclusion: The flavors of Brazil

We end the evening with a first-class dinner. Brazilian cuisine is a revelation of regional treasures: we enjoy the combination of traditional ingredients such as manioc and aromatic Dendê palm oil with modern techniques. Whether delicate picanha from the grill or a refined moqueca (a traditional fish stew), accompanied by first-class wines from the highlands of South Brazil – it is the stylish setting for our last toast to the metropolis on the sugarloaf.

An active day is coming to an end. Of course, we recommend that you take more time for this fascinating metropolis; so the program can be enjoyed more relaxed and supplemented with other highlights. Here are more ideas for your stay in Rio:

Helicopter flight over Rio

Experience the spectacular topography of Rio on a private helicopter flight along the Classic Route. As you circle the Corcovado at eye level and glide across the white beaches of Ipanema and Copacabana, the fascinating interplay of granite rocks, rainforest and ocean from a bird's eye view becomes really tangible.

Imperial heritage in Petrópolis

A day trip leads up to the cooler mountain regions to the iconic pink imperial palace, the former summer residence of the imperial family. In today's National Museum you can see the magnificent crown and sceptre of Dom Pedro II.

Cycling tour to Jardim Botânico

Cycle relaxed to the botanical garden. Between majestic palm avenues, exotic birds and wild animals, you can experience the botanical diversity of Brazil up close. With its avenue from over 100 majestic imperial palms, it is one of the most important retreats in the city. Between rare orchids, historical fountains and the lush green of the rainforest, you can experience Brazil's botanical diversity in an atmosphere of absolute silence.

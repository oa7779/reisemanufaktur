Awasi Iguazu - a luxury hideaway in Argentina's rainforest

Latin American expert Tobias Rüger on Panama City and the Panama Canal. Get individual advice from América Special Tours today.

07.06.2022

Iguazu is famous for its intoxicating waterfalls, which are among the great natural wonders of the world. But the region has more than the "big water" to offer: in the tropical-green Atlantic rain forest, which is located along the coast of Brazil A fascinating animal world moves to Argentina. A real natural paradise. In the middle of the jungle is a luxury lodge where travelers can end their South American trip in style: Awasi Iguazu.

Iguazu Waterfalls

Great waters called the Guaraní Indians the magical place in the rainforest – with sense of understatement. On a width of 2.7 kilometers 270 waterfalls fall over the cliffs up to 80 meters into the depth. Annoying, theatrical and unforgettable – a unique spectacle of nature. The border runs through the middle, so that visitors can enjoy thunderstorms and water spectacles from both Brazilian and Argentinean perspectives. Surrounded by countless butterflies, you can walk through the jungle green on paths and piers and enjoy the water wonder world on a real spin. Thrills to the finale: the view into the Devil's Dragon.

The Atlantic Rainforest

National parks on both sides of the waterfalls have long been included in the UNESCO World Heritage List. They protect one of the last remains of "Mata Atlântica", the Atlantic rainforest. He once moved as a powerful green band along the coast from Brazil to Argentina. Extermination of nature endangered its existence, but it is still a place of superlatives, because almost nowhere in the world so many species live in confined spaces: 24 monkey species, 298 mammal species and over 1000 bird species, many of which only occur here. A green paradise that invites you to discover.

A soul place in the jungle – the Hideaway Awasi Iguazu

A world journey away from the hustle and bustle of everyday life: In the midst of the lush nature of Atlantic Rainforest lies a place of longing that is not of this world. Take off your shoes, close your eyes and listen to the sound of the rainforest – a ritual upon arrival in the jungle lodge. Green-winged altator, green-billed tucan, Elstertangare, blue-breasted pipra and many other tropical birds tune into the bird choir. Monkeys are rustling through the bushes and jumping exuberantly from tree to tree. Orchids put color accents in the eternal green of the jungle. An oasis of peace for all senses – perfect for slowing down.

Living in Awasi Iguazu

14 villas with private diving pools hide in a tropical forest, just 20 minutes from the Iguazu waterfalls. They stand on stilts – a traditional and sustainable construction – and guarantee maximum privacy. The interior decoration highlights the braiding and textile work of the native Guarani, who are master craftsmen. From the terrace, guests of the lodge can see the Atlantic Ocean and rainforest – a real favorite place. The main lodge offers in restaurant and bar the possibility to mingle with the other guests, and is easily accessible from each villa.

Kitchen from the rainforest

Marinated Pacú with mango and fennel salad or shrimps with banana curry, Creole sauce and Cassava croc – does that sound tempting? From the jungle directly to the plate, the motto of the chef is. As far as possible, the cuisine is local and seasonal – sometimes refined, sometimes down-to-earth. Only one international thing has no chance in the cuisine of Awasi Iguazu.

Explore the rainforest with locals

The guests decide where to go. Each villa has a private guide, including four-wheel drive, the destination and time of your excursions to the surrounding area. Of course, they can go on a spin to the Iguazu Falls as often as they want and stroll through the tropical green at their own pace. Even a selfie in front of the water wonder world, when spray and sun paint rainbows to the sky, must be easy. But many other little adventures are also enticing: with the guide on silent paths to explore the rainforest or by canoe to jungle pools with crystal clear water, for example. There are guaranteed moments of happiness for the soul.

Experience the culture of Guarani

Although nature is the focus – the local heritage of the Guarani communities that have lived in the region since ancient times is also fascinating. The team of the Awazi Iguazu has developed authentic and sustainable experiences that allow guests to get in touch with the locals and learn first-hand about their traditions and values.

Caral - the oldest city in America
 

To the north of Peru's capital is an archaeological sensation, where Caral, the oldest city in America, is located. The UNESCO World Heritage Site was established 2,600 years before Christ.

Taking into account the age and weather conditions at that time, it is impressive how well this legendary place is preserved. At the same time as the famous pyramids were created in Egypt, pyramids were also built in Caral independently. To this day, six pyramids have been exposed in laborious handwork. In addition, two majestic ceremonial sites, a mystical amphitheatre and a huge irrigation system were discovered. Caral covers an area of 60 hectares, on which dozens of temples and arable land are located. According to researchers, this historic city is said to have passed through the Andes to the jungle.

The amazing Alter Carals could only be determined precisely to 2627 BC by modern technology and a special analysis technique. As a result, the oldest city in America is 1,000 years older than originally thought and is therefore one of the high cultures of the Egyptians and Mesopotamians. This time is often called "the cradle of modern civilization". Caral was not given much attention during the 1905 discovery. It was not until the discovery of the main pyramid by Peruvian archaeologist Dr. Ruth Shady that Caral's significance in American history was known.

In addition to the magnificent pyramids, archaeologists also found various small utensils, such as textiles, offerings, musical instruments, vessels and tools. These findings were further evidence of a very developed culture. However, no weapons were discovered, which in turn suggests a very peaceful people. The reason for this was probably the remote location of Carals, because it is not located in the coastal region.

The choice of this location is unclear for the time being, since Caral lies in the middle of the desert and therefore offers hardly any fertile soil. But in the coastal areas there was often extreme rain that the inhabitants of Carals could keep by intelligent irrigation systems. The first irrigation system was also discovered, with which cotton, beans and pumpkins were grown.

Already at this time there was a social hierarchy, because the ancient settlement could be divided into a clearly separate upper and lower city. The upper class formed the religious and political elite as well as the leadership of society. The middle class was responsible for agriculture, architecture and irrigation systems. The underclass finally, for manual work and manual work.

Caral is located about two to three hours drive from the capital Lima and is ideal for a day trip. In addition, there is the possibility to marvel at other sights on the way between Lima and Caral, such as the statue of Christ on the cliffs or Barranca Playa. Due to the importance of Caral, it is only possible to visit the facilities in a guided tour.

Salar de Uyuni: Learn more about the endless glittering expanse of the world's largest salt desert in southwest Bolivia. A highlight on every Bolivian trip!

28.05.2025

Salar de Uyuni - Largest salt desert in the world

South America is the home of numerous natural wonders that you must have seen with your own eyes to fully capture their beauty. One of these places is the Salar de Uyuni. The largest salt desert in the world is located in the southwest of Bolivia. Spectacular photos of the Salar de Uyuni are like sand by the sea - but to look at the endless glittering expanse on site is an experience that images cannot do justice to.

Now the Salar de Uyuni is not the only salt desert on the globe, but the dimensions of the Bolivian variant break all borders. The vast salt desert covers 10,000 square kilometers in the Bolivian highlands, called Altiplano. If you want to treat yourself to a little break from the hectic everyday life, find in it a successful destination. Just look at the distance and enjoy the silence and beauty of nature – the Salar de Uyuni makes it possible.

The origin of the Salar de Uyuni

How did this extraordinary natural spectacle come about? Salt deserts form in a closed terrain, where rainwater can not or hardly seep into the soil. In a humid climate, a lake would form at this point over the years. In the desert, however, the water evaporates while the minerals and salt are left behind.

If the story is told in Bolivia, the origin of the story is far more mystical. So the mountains that surround the Salar de Uyuni were once gods. The two mountains Tunupa and Kusku were even a couple, with Kusu one day being abandoned by Tunupa. The salt of their divine tears finally formed the salt desert.

What does one expect in the world's largest salt desert?

Those who set out to visit this tourist attraction in Bolivia, expect adventure Pur. Traveling through the vast expanses with the four-wheeler will inspire the spirit of discovery! The area is isolated, far away from civilization and hardly equipped with real roads. The region is very dry and the weather conditions are rough on some days. In view of the lasting memories that the Salar de Uyuni has given you, this is no longer important.

The must-sees within the salt desert include Incahuasi Island, a rocky area in the middle of the desert from which you have a breathtaking 360 degree view. In the southwest there are several lagoons, such as the Laguna Colorada (Red Lagoon) or the Laguna Verde (Green Lagoons). On your way through the salt desert, with a little luck, you also make extraordinary animal encounters including lamas and of course the flamingos known for the region.

Incomparable memory photos guaranteed

Of course you don't have to always have the camera at your fingertips on every trip, but in the Salar de Uyuni it is still worth taking some probably planned snapshots. Here you can take advantage of the visual effects that nature offers. The Salar de Uyuni is one of the flattest areas on Earth, with a little exercise you can play with perspective in your photos. During the rainy season you also have the opportunity to use the reflections of rainwater that gathers on the soil of the salt desert. The Salar de Uyuni turns into a huge mirror, so to speak!

Overnight stay in the Salar de Uyuni

In the Salar de Uyuni, it is best to spend several days exploring them extensively. The overnight stay in the middle of the salt desert is, of course, one of the highlights if you choose the right accommodation. On an individual round trip through Bolivia, we focus on your interests – how about, for example, the Deluxe Airstream Camper? 

Best time to travel to the Salar de Uyuni

You can visit the largest salt desert in the world all year round, but your experience will vary greatly depending on the season. In the dry season from April to November, you will naturally have fewer rainy days and warmer temperatures up to 21 degrees Celsius in November. The rain season, on the other hand, offers them the mirror effect described. However, some areas may not be accessible due to the water.

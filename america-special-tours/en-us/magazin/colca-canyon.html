The Colca Canyon in Peru – On the trail of the condor

Scattered cliffs, extinct volcanoes, incredible panoramic views: About 100 kilometers north of Arequipa is the second largest canyon in the world: the Colca Canyon. His most famous "inhabitant" is the majestic Andenkondor, in whose search one can go on a visit to this natural spectacle.

Colca-Canyon: Second lowest gorge in the world

The Colca Canyon (or Cañon de Colca in Spanish) offers Peruvian travelers a chance of a real adventure. There it is necessary to overcome some altitude meters, but anyone who has successfully completed the ascent to the viewing platforms is guaranteed to be amazed. The Colca Valley is truly a superlative attraction. It extends over almost 100 kilometers and falls at the deepest point 3300 meters. For comparison: The much more well-known Grand Canyon is just about 1800 meters deep.

It is one of the younger canyons with its 100 million years and formed by the elevation of the Andes and the excavation of the river flowing through the valley, the eponymous Río Colca. Its present form was also obtained by volcanic deposits. 

But not only rugged rock masses shape the image of the Colca Canyon. Green areas with corn and quinoa fields lie far in front of the rocky slopes. The area is considered to be extremely fertile and has been used for agriculture for thousands of years.

Home of the King of the Airs

Even if you're not usually interested in bird watching, the world's largest birds of prey will simply delight you. Despite its enormous size and a wingspan of over three meters, you seem to float effortlessly and gracefully at an airy height.

On your way to the Colca Canyon it is worth taking a stop in the small villages of Salinas y Aguada Blanca National Park to learn more about life in the region. In addition, in the area you have the chance to meet another animal representative of the region, Vicuña.

Last but not least, you can relax in one of the hot springs scattered around the Colca Valley and formed by the seismic activities.

A visit to the Colca Canyon is possible all year round. From May to October is dry season with less rain and cooler temperatures, so these months are highly recommended.

Sustainable travel in the eco-paradise of Costa Rica

In the eco-paradise of Costa Rica, rainforests, fascinating wildlife and dream beaches invite you to discover. Eco-lodges such as active tourism on soft soles are part of the concept.

14.04.2022

Dream beaches between Caribbean and Pacific in powder sugar white and lava black, steaming rain forests and smoking volcanic cones – a landscape spectrum of the extra class awaits visitors in small Costa Rica. Biodiversity is also unparalleled: as in paradise, colorful butterflies and tropical birds, turtles and sloths live. And everything in the green area – almost a third of the country's surface is protected. Lonely tops worldwide! It is clear that tourism also comes on quiet soles. 

Costa Rica's natural paradise – the champion in sustainability

Meetings with wildlife are pre-programmed on a trip through Costa Rica: after all, the small country in Central America – hardly larger than Switzerland – can come up with 30 national parks and many other nature reserves. Over 900 different bird species chirping, creaking and singing in the rainforest. With a little luck even the Quetzal, the colorful Mayan Godbird, can be seen. In addition, dancing butterflies, crocodiles in lurking, monkeys that run through the trees, and sea turtles on the beaches of both oceans. But the favourites of many visitors are jungle dwellers who are always in relaxation mode: sloths. 

Even outside the national parks, sustainability in Costa Rica is not just a marketing fleet. More than 98 percent of the country's energy comes from renewable sources – hydropower, geothermal energy, sun, wind and biomass. Greener is hardly possible. This was rewarded by the UN: since 2019 Costa Rica has been allowed to decorate itself with the title "Champions of the Earth". In 2021 Costa Rica was also one of the winners of the Earthshot Environmental Award.

If you want to do a little more, you can compensate for your carbon footprint for the flight and car rides on site. Tour operators are informed about the different programmes that finance nature conservation projects in the country. 

Active tourism in a gentle way and "Turismo Rural"

In addition to nature lovers, travelers who like to power themselves out also get their money's worth in Costa Rica. Slide through river landscapes with a kayak and discover the slowness or push the adrenaline level at white water rafting or canyoning at waterfalls – anything is possible. Divers have two world seas to choose from, hikers can climb fire mountains like the Chirripó – with almost 4000 meters of the highest summit in the country – or walk through the treetops of the rainforest giants on suspension bridges. Always with regard to nature, of course. 

The accommodations in Costa Rica are also sustainable. Stylish eco-lodges and boutique hotels with only a few rooms are located in the most beautiful places of the country, where only sea noises or birdsongs are the background music. Contact with the population is guaranteed in rural tourism (Turismo Rural), because the accommodations are embedded in the municipalities. When visiting coffee plantations, cocoa plantations or banana plantings, you will learn where and how our small energy bombs grow in everyday life and how farmers combine the economy and protection of the rainforest.  

Wellness for body and soul

Immersion in nature is a constant source of energy. We can charge our batteries while hiking, cycling or diving. A favorite place for nature lovers – the Arenal volcano, where travelers can choose between kayak trips on Lake Arenal, hikes to waterfalls and lava streams, canopy tours through the rainforest and horseback riding up to horse riding. 

La Fortuna at the foot of the volcano is also a great place to simply get pampered by stroke and thread. Costa Rica also has a lot in store for wellness fans. Vulcan forces join forces in volcanic spas, where guests can relax in hot thermal springs or skin themselves with volcanic mud while peeling. Relaxation units for the soul like Yoga & Co. belong to the program as well. 

Fantastic finale on the beach

During a break on the beach after the round trip, you can let your experiences resuscitate. The question is, Caribbean or Pacific? Maybe both! The selection is huge: White powdered sugar beaches as well as black volcanic beaches, waves for surfers as well as coral bays for divers and snorkelers attract. The favourites on the Pacific coast include beach destinations with good infrastructure such as the Nicoya Peninsula or Manuel Antonio. Those who like it a bit wilder will love the bays of Bahía Drake or Golfo Dulce. 

A special experience awaits you on the Pacific coast in the Ostional National Wildlife Refuge, where thousands of olive-ridley turtles meet to lay eggs together. A phenomenon that can only be observed closely in a few places in the world. 

 On the Caribbean coast of Costa Rica, the rainforest often reaches to the sea in places like Puerto Viejo or Cahuita – magical! A caribbean lifestyle is the icing on the i.

Pura Vida – the lifestyle of Costa Rica

In Costa Rica, environmental engagement combines with joie de vivre. "Pura Vida" is the motto of the country with which locals welcome travelers. An expression of a sense of life in which confidence and cheerfulness play the leading role. In Costa Rica, visitors will immerse themselves in the feeling of well-being for a few weeks and enjoy a break from everyday life – with many little moments of happiness for the soul. From a tailor-made round trip you can take a bit of Pura Vida home with you.

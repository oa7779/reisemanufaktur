Firsthand travel report · December 10, 2025 · Colombia & Peru

Katharina's journey from the Andes to the Caribbean and the Amazon.

Katharina Reichert traveled through Colombia in October 2025 and then continued to the Peruvian Amazon. Her report shows how experiences differ between cities, rainforest, and coast.

This page summarizes Katharina Reichert's publicly available travel report. Her personal observations are not a guarantee of availability or safety for future trips.

Bogotá: culture in a high-altitude capital

The report begins in Bogotá, at an elevation of around 8,500 feet (2,600 meters). Katharina describes the city's architectural eras, the Gold Museum, and her first culinary impressions. Altitude and a comfortable pace on arrival are important considerations when planning your own trip.

Medellín: understanding history through conversation

In Medellín, Katharina explored the city with a local guide who was born and raised there. Their conversation helped her understand the city's recent history and transformation through a personal perspective, not just from books.

La Macarena and Caño Cristales

After the cities, the journey continued to La Macarena. The report describes hikes and the distinctive seasonal colors of Caño Cristales, with a stay at La Manigua Lodge. Access and suitable travel times must be checked for your specific dates.

Cartagena and the Islas del Rosario

In Cartagena, the scenery shifted to colonial architecture and Caribbean city life. Street food, the historic center, and a boat trip to the Islas del Rosario rounded out the Colombian part of the journey.

Onward to the Peruvian Amazon

Another highlight in Katharina's report is a river cruise aboard the Pure Amazon. The journey combined wildlife observation, paddling, personal encounters, and insights into life along the river. This part takes place in Peru and requires separate destination and logistics planning.

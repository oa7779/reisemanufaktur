Ecuador — Haciendas in the Land of Volcanoes

On this trip you will experience the natural beauties and traditions of Ecuador. While on a diverse route you will see the impressive highlands of the Andes, crystal clear lakes and mighty volcanoes, you will always stay at traditional haciendas, the South American country estates. Here you will experience rural life in a very comfortable way. In addition to rich nature with a multitude of animals, you can expect the historic cities of Quito and Cuenca, a train ride along steep abysses and the pilgrimage village of Baños.

Trip Information

Itinerary

12 days Quito – Otavalo – Cotopaxi – Riobamba – Cuenca – El Cajas – Guayaquil

Your Trip Highlights

  • Carefully selected hotels for you

  • Country life, very luxurious: Overnight on comfortable haciendas
  • The impressive mountain world of the Andes
  • Fantastic natural spectacles in various national parks

Welcome to Ecuador! At the airport, you will be warmly welcomed by your private guide and chauffeur. They are located in a special geographical location. On the one hand, you have landed today in the highest capital in the world – at 2,850 meters – and on the other hand, you can stand at the center-of-the-world monument on both hemispheres of the earth at the same time. The city also has a turbulent and powerful history behind it: in the 13th century Quito was the flourishing capital of the Kingdom of Quitus and later the northern capital of the Inca Empire. The beautiful colonial-style temples, monasteries and villas you pass by date back to the 16th and 17th centuries. UNESCO has declared the old town as the first city ever to be a World Heritage Site. Overnight in Quito.

Today, explore Quito on a walk through the streets. First, however, you get an overview of Quito’s tangle of houses from a viewpoint – this is where your driver takes you. Together with your guide, you will then follow the history of this exciting city – starting with the indigenous people who lived here before the spread of the Inca Empire and decisively shaped Ecuadorian culture. The city was later founded by the Spanish colonial rulers on the ruins of an Inca settlement and today boasts one of the best-preserved colonial urban centers in Latin America. Particularly impressive is the baroque Jesuit church La Compañía. Enjoy an exquisite chocolate tasting and immerse yourself in the fascinating world of quality chocolate. Learn about the art of chocolate making and the impressive history of cocoa. Discover why Ecuador is considered the best country to grow fine cocoa and let yourself be enchanted by its unique aromas and tastes. For lunch, head to a local restaurant. The rest of the day is at your disposal. Overnight in Quito.

The next stop of your trip is the Otavalo region, an idyllic patch of earth. Otavalo is located in a fertile valley and is surrounded by three volcanoes: Imbabura, Cotacachi and Mojanda. The city is one of the most important places in Ecuador due to its rich cultural heritage. Myths and customs are still alive here, and the unique landscape of the Andes provides the perfect backdrop for your guide’s stories. You will also visit an artisanal market and reach the village of Peguche with its eucalyptus forest in the afternoon. Your accommodation is a highlight in itself: you stay at the Hacienda Zuleta, an extensive property with fabulous views of the Andes, genuine Ecuadorian cuisine and authentic insights into the everyday life of a farm. The 16th century farm of 1,600 hectares has been family-owned for more than 100 years. In the evening, guests gather around the rustic wooden table on which freshly caught trout, prepared according to an old family recipe, and other delicious dishes are applied. Overnight in Otavalo.

As beautiful as the decor of your accommodation may be: The real highlights await you outside. On the property, for example, there is a dairy where you can taste locally produced cheese, a rehabilitation clinic for Andean condors and a fair trade embroidery that allows an insight into the local craftsmanship. If you want to delve deeper into the history of the region, you can also visit the 1300-year-old excavation site of the Caranqui tribe. For excursions into nature, there are various hiking routes on which you can spot some representatives of the rich bird world with a bit of luck. For an additional fee, your hacienda also offers rides or cooking classes. Overnight in Otavalo.

An excursion today takes you to Lake Cuicocha, a crater lake at the foot of the Cotacachi volcano in the Andes of Ecuador. With a diameter of three kilometers, it is one of the largest and most spectacular crater lakes in the country. The name Cuicacho means Guinea Pig Lake. The lagoon owes its name to the shape of its largest island in the crater lake. Since its last eruption 3,100 years ago, the volcano has been dormant, but – just like the eruptions of the surrounding volcanoes – it is responsible for fertile vegetation. Four lava domes jut out of Lake Cuicocha, forming two forested islands – but people are not allowed to enter them. The lush vegetation on the islands offers a protected habitat for a wide variety of wildlife. In the dense reed lives the silver lobe diver, a bird species that occurs everywhere in the Andes, of which only about 44 specimens can be found on the Cuicocha. Take your time to enjoy the unique nature on the lake shore. Overnight in Otavalo.

Today you will visit one of the most fascinating places in the highlands of Ecuador: Quilotoa Crater Lake. This emerald-green lagoon, which lies at an altitude of 3,900 m, was created by the collapse of a volcano. Its name, derived from the Quechua words "Quiru" (tooth) and "Toa" (queen), reflects the special shape of the lagoon. Enjoy the impressive landscape and the diverse flora and fauna around the lagoon. Afterwards, your journey leads south along the “Street of Volcanoes” to the Chimborazo region. The capital of the province, Riobamba, is located in the geographical center of Ecuador and is the most important agricultural center of the Andes. From here you can enjoy breathtaking views of the surrounding volcanoes Chimborazo, Tungurahua and Altar – the most impressive rock massif of the Eastern Cordillera. Overnight at Riobamba.

It goes upwards: the next stop of your journey takes you up to the highlands of the Andes, where Vicuñas graze between herbs and rosette trees. The fields draw a fascinating play of colors on the hilltops, giving the impression that they were plowed for decoration rather than agriculture. Today there are two short hikes on the Chimborazo, the highest volcano in Ecuador. For acclimatization, stop at the two refugios (refuge huts) before you go up to 5,000 meters. You will spend the night near Riobamba on a historic hacienda with whitewashed walls and lots of wood that is listed. Here you can enjoy your dinner in a cozy atmosphere, for which you can treat yourself as an escort a noble drop from the wine cellar. Overnight at Riobamba.

A new day begins and a new stage begins. Your guide and chauffeur are looking forward to taking you on your way to Cuenca today. Along the way, visit the ruins of Ingapirca (“Inca Stone Walls” on Cañari), the most important monument of pre-Hispanic cultures in Ecuador. The site is located on a grassy hill at 3,100 m altitude and was already of great spiritual importance for the Incas before the construction of the Sun Temple between 1450 and 1480. During a walk over the former ceremonial, residential and resting place, you will learn about the faith and rituals, as well as the construction of the puma-shaped sanctuary before reaching your accommodation in Cuenca in the late afternoon. The old town of Cuenca has been on the World Heritage List since 1999. The “Athens of Ecuador” impresses with cobblestone streets, whitewashed houses with red-brown tiled roofs and pretty squares. Your traditional hotel is located in the heart of the old town, so you can explore it in the evening with a small walk. Overnight in Cuenca.

Those who stroll comfortably through the small streets of Cuenca do not necessarily come up with the idea of being in Ecuador’s third largest city. In tranquil alleys, long-established merchants offer their goods - no building must tower over the monumental cathedral with its blue domes. You will start your tour with your guide at the Artist Market in Plaza de San Sebastian, where regional craftsmanship is offered. In front of the whitewashed church of El Carmen de la Asunción, there is a colorful flower market, which you choose as a starting point for a walk to the main square, above which rises the imposing cathedral. A ride along the Rio Tomebamba will take you to the “Hanging Houses”, some buildings built so close to the river that they are believed to crash at any moment. El Turi is the ideal viewpoint to experience Cuenca from above! Enjoy the panoramic view of the colonial old town, the red tile roofs and the surrounding Andes. Finally, visit a hat factory and learn how the famous Panama hats are made. The afternoon is at your disposal for another stroll through the alleys and squares. Overnight in Cuenca.

Today will be a very contrasting day: first your driver will take you to the El Cajas National Park, which is only about 30 kilometers west of Cuenca. Up here at 3,100 to 4,450 meters altitude is a protected area formed by volcanic and glacier activity and houses over 200 lakes as well as some Inca ruins. With a little patience and luck, you can discover some of the many endangered animals that have found a home here: hummingbirds, condors, Andean tapirs or ocelots. From this windy, foggy natural paradise, your chauffeur will take you to the coast. Arriving at Hacienda La Danesa, you will be warmly welcomed by your host who will bring you closer to the traditional history of this charming property. The hacienda is surrounded by lush gardens, cocoa and sugar cane plantations as well as grazing cattle that complete the picture of a classic hacienda. Afterwards, enjoy a refreshing drink and a traditional snack. The afternoon is all yours: explore the hacienda by bicycle or glide down the river while tubing, surrounded by magnificent nature. Overnight at Naranjito.

Today you can expect a variety of experiences at the Hacienda La Danesa. Your journey of discovery begins with a visit to the in-house cocoa plantation, where you will delve deep into the fascinating world of Ecuadorian cocoa. Let yourself be enchanted by the traditional history and the entire processing process. In a workshop with a local chocolatier, you will learn how to make chocolate in the traditional way. Roll up your sleeves and create your own tablet that you can take home as a delicious memory. For lunch, pamper yourself with the fresh and regional delicacies of the hacienda. Afterwards, you will have the special opportunity to test your skills at traditional cow milking. The Hacienda is known for its award-winning dairy cows and produces about 1,200 liters of milk every day. Then enjoy your coffee refined with fresh, even warm milk – a unique treat that brings you closer to the nature of this special place. Overnight at Naranjito.

Guayaquil, the vibrant port metropolis of Ecuador, combines modern city life with historical charm and tropical coastal atmosphere. As the largest port in the country, Guayaquil is an important economic center and at the same time a gateway to the Galápagos Islands. The city offers numerous attractions, such as the picturesque “Malecón 2000” waterfront, which extends along the Guayas River. Depending on the departure time, your chauffeur will take you to the airport in Guayaquil, because today your journey through Ecuador ends. Many more impressions you take your flight home.

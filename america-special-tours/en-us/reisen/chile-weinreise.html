Chile Individual Tour - Vino de Chile

Chile Individual tour with Latin American experts América Special Tours for all wine lovers. Inquiries, get personal advice and experience Chile for yourself.

Chile: trapped between the Andes and the Pacific, with a maximum width of 400 kilometers – but 4300 kilometers long – this unusual country is a single thread-thin stretch of coastline. The exceptional geographical location is ideal for wine growing – and produces such good wines that even the French are in distress. This trip will take you to the Maipo, Cachapoal, Colchagua and Casablanca wine valleys. You will learn all about the cultivation and production of the noble drops and can of course convince yourself of the quality with a glass. In the picturesque coastal town of Isla Negra, you can also see the collecting passion of Nobel Prize winner Pablo Neruda with your own eyes.

1st day of Santiago de Chile

Welcome to Chile! Your journey begins in the capital Santiago de Chile. Upon arrival at the airport, you will be warmly welcomed and taken to your hotel. Then join your guide for a walk through some markets – starting point is the historic Franciscan Church in Recoleta. Later, your cooking class starts at the El Galeon restaurant. In order to be well prepared for this, get the necessary ingredients on the market La Venga. Your guide can tell you all kinds of interesting things about the food, the city’s markets and the country’s dishes. Continue to the historic Mercado Central, where most restaurants specialize in seafood. Once all the errands are done, start your cooking class at El Galeon – it starts with a glass of Pisco Sour made up of grape brandy, sugar syrup and protein. After the introduction by the chef, prepare a three-course meal, which you will of course enjoy for dinner. Overnight in Santiago de Chile.

2nd day wine tasting in the Maipo valley

About 40 kilometers from Santiago, in the Maipo Valley is one of the best wine-growing regions in the world. The Santa Rita winery was founded in 1880 by Domingo Fernandez Concha, who built the houses and chapel standing on the estate. Today, the old family house is surrounded by an area of almost 250,000 square meters with beautiful parks and gardens – the estate has a stately wine-growing area of 800 hectares. On an international level, Santa Rita has already received many awards in terms of the quality of the wine produced there. After a sightseeing tour of the winery, you will be invited to a wine tasting. We recommend that you have your lunch in the restaurant of the estate “La Casa de Doña Paula”. Afterwards you can visit the Museum Andino Alto Jahuel. The Andean Museum has a private collection of valuable objects from the pre-Columbian period. The aim of the museum is to preserve the heritage of this culture. The building is located next to the colonial house of the Santa Rita winery, but is itself built in a modern style. The gray facade is reminiscent of Inca constructions. Overnight in Santiago de Chile.

Day 3 The exclusive wineries of the South

After a delicious breakfast, the Vik winery in the Cachapoal Valley awaits you. The valley is named after the river that rises in the Andes and flows west into Rapel Lake. Most wineries are located on the edge of the Andean cordillera, where the large differences in day and night temperatures offer ideal conditions for growing red wine. Due to the higher coastal cordillera, however, the cool winds of the ocean do not reach the valley. This is why this is the hottest and driest wine region in Chile. It is mainly grown by Cabernet Sauvignon, alongside Merlot, Sauvignon Blanc, Carménère and Chardonnay. During a walk through the estate you will learn all about the special features of the region. And of course you taste one or the other noble drop. After this experience, your private chauffeur will take you to the Colchagua valley, the winery Casa Silva. Casa Silva is one of the first wineries in the Colchagua Valley – the vines brought from France were planted here more than 90 years ago. Mario Silva is recognized worldwide as an oenologist and is considered one of the best in his field. During the visit you will get to know the old wine cellars and the “treasure chamber” of the winery, after which you will see the production facilities. Afterwards, an exquisite wine tasting awaits you in the antique wine tasting room with a view of the wine barrels. Overnight in the Colchagua Valley.

Day 4 The vineyards of Lapostolle

In the morning you will have the opportunity to explore the impressive vineyards of the Lapostolle family by mountain bike or horseback and afterwards be pampered with a relaxing massage. Overnight in the Colchagua Valley.

Day 5 The technique behind the wine

The Casa Lopostolle winery was founded by the French family Mannier Lapostolle and the Chilean family Rabat. The Lopostolle family is known worldwide for its liqueur production (Grand Marnier brand), but also looks back on many years of experience in wine cultivation. In Chile, the family found the ideal climatic conditions and soil to grow French vines and produce excellent wine. The Casa Lapostolle winery covers 350 hectares of cultivation in three different wine fields. Cabernet Sauvignon, Chardonnay, Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot and Carmenere are grown – and exported to over 20 different countries. Overnight in the Colchagua Valley.

Day 6 Guest at Pablo Neruda

The fishing village of Isla Negra, where your path leads you first today, is a pilgrimage site for literature lovers. Because here stands the most beautiful of Pablo Neruda's three houses. Inside him, a great hodgepodge of treasures gathered by the Nobel Prize winner in literature is overwhelming. If you have looked around in peace, continue to Valparaíso. Like a huge amphitheater, the city nestles on 42 hills on a wide bay that opens up to the Pacific Ocean. Your city tour begins at the scenic Plaza Sotomayor, where the imposing monument to the heroes of the Battle of Iquique, built in 1879, stands. From here, make your way to the southern part of the city to explore the famous old inclined elevators. Once upstairs, you will be rewarded with a breathtaking view of the harbor and the glittering bay. Your journey continues along the scenic coastal road to neighboring Viña del Mar, considered the country’s most popular seaside resort. After a visit to the beach of Concón, continue your exploration. On the way back to Viña del Mar, you will cross a charming residential area and visit the lively market square of Vergara as well as the inviting shopping street. Finally, return to Valparaíso. Overnight in Valparaíso.

Day 7 - The Sea of Valparaíso

(Without guide and chauffeur)

Enjoy this day in Valparaíso according to your wishes. The place by the sea is ideal for finding relaxation. We also recommend you to explore the famous hills of Valparaíso, discover the steep sloping elevators and visit the Museo al Aire Libre (open-air museum). Overnight in Valparaíso.

Day 8 in the Casablanca Valley

Today you will return to Santiago de Chile. Along the way, stop at the Casablanca Valley to visit the beautiful boutique winery Kingston Family. The potential of the Casablanca Valley was discovered by Pablo Morande in the early 80s. The oenologist was looking for cooler areas that are particularly suitable for the cultivation of white wines, such as Sauvignon Blanc and Chardonnay. In the Casablanca valley he found an ideal microclimate, influenced by the proximity of the coast and the suitable soil. Due to the excellent results, the cultivation areas were soon expanded, so that today they have an area of 3,500 hectares. In the early 20th century, the Kingston family came to Chile in search of copper and gold. About 20 years later they settled in the Casablanca valley. Since the Kingston Family winery produces only a small amount of wine, it puts all efforts into the qualitative preparation. On a tour, the winery will first be presented and the wine production processes explained before you can convince yourself of the high quality of the premium wines. Overnight in Santiago de Chile.

9th Day Goodbye Chile

Today it is unfortunately necessary to say goodbye to the sun-drenched vineyards of Chile and the unique beautiful landscapes. Your chauffeur will take you to the airport and you will start your journey home. In your luggage you have numerous unique memories – and determine one or the other bottle of wine.

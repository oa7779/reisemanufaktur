Architecture in Brazil — Life Is a Breath

Architectural journey to the masterpieces of Brazil. South America specialist América Special Tours creates your individual Brazil trip. Now ask me.

13 days

Brazil – a country like a continent, as contrasting and spectacular as no other. In addition to breathtaking natural spectacles, the spectacular carnival, the most beautiful beaches and much more, it also produced the visionary and philosopher Oscar Niemeyer. Shortly before his death in Rio de Janeiro, he said life was just a touch. Just a short moment, and we are to enjoy the easy moments. And it is always contagious, the Brazilian ease. Let yourself be taken away to vibrant metropolises, monumental buildings and historical sites. An unforgettable experience in the footsteps of a century architect!

Trip Information

Itinerary

13 Days São Paulo - Curitiba - Brasília - Belo Horizonte - Ouro Preto - Tiradentes - Rio de Janeiro

Your Trip Highlights

  • Private, German or English speaking tour guide
  • Carefully selected hotels for you
  • Travel in the footsteps of the architect of the century Oscar Niemeyer
  • Urban jungle of São Paulo
  • Visit gardens in honor of Germany and Poland in Curitiba
  • Brasília: The monument of modern architecture par excellence
  • The Cathedral of the Capital: One of the Most Extraordinary Churches in the World
  • Baroque Old Town in Ouro Preto
  • Impressive landscape architecture in and at Belo Horizonte
  • Historic Rio de Janeiro and Maracanã

Welcome to São Paulo! You will begin your architectural journey through Brazil in the country’s largest city. Around 21 million people live in the Grande São Paulo metropolitan area. At the airport you will be warmly welcomed by your private guide. After a short refreshment, you will begin your discovery tour through the architectural heritage of São Paulo. Experience the historic center of São Paulo, the financial center along the famous Paulista Avenue and the elegant neighbourhoods of Morumbi and Jardins, known for their refined architecture and atmospheric streets. An absolute highlight of this tour is a visit to the Presidential Palace. The market halls and the Japanese quarter offer not only a cultural insight, but also architectural discoveries that enrich the urban fabric of São Paulo. The stroll along Oscar Freire Street takes you through an elegant neighborhood known for its luxurious boutiques and impressive urban design. In addition, there are the beautiful Trianon and Ibirapuera Parks. The visit to the crypt of the Sé Cathedral completes the tour.

Your path leads you to the Museu de Arte de São Paulo, whose new building was planned by Lina Bo Bardi – your husband is co-founder of the museum. Four steel beams carry the building, which offers a beautiful view of the city center. Paintings and sculptures from the 14th century to today can be found inside. In the restaurant of the museum you have the opportunity to eat lunch. It continues with a large brick building, it houses the Pinacoteca de São Paulo – especially Brazilian art from the 19th century can be admired here. In 2007 and 2008, paintings disappeared here, among others by Picasso. Overnight in São Paulo.

A domestic flight will take you to Curitiba. Here you will be warmly welcomed again and brought to your hotel. They have arrived in the capital of the state of Paraná – one of the most prosperous states in the country. Siemens, Robert Bosch and Volkswagen have company headquarters here. The city offers its inhabitants the highest standard of living and is at the same time the city with the strongest European influence.Together with your guide go on an exploration tour in Curitiba. You will see Tiradentes Square, named after the Brazilian freedom fighter, the cultural center “Paço da Liberdade” and the oldest university in the country (with antique columned front). At the Oscar Niemeyer Museum, learn about the architect who pioneered modern Brazilian architecture. You will also see the Guaira Theatre and the opera, which was built from steel tubes, on your tour. Nature lovers will also like Curitiba: walking through the botanical garden or the Tanguá and Tingui park, you can leave the bustling city behind. It is alive again in the pedestrian zone “Rua das Flores”. “Largo do Ordem” is an urban area where you can admire traditional architecture. Overnight in Curitiba.

After familiarizing yourself with the architecture of Brazilian cities in recent days, a real architecture mecca awaits you today: a domestic flight will take you to the capital Brasília. According to UNESCO, it is the best example when it comes to characterizing the architectural movement of the 20th century. Of course, your guide will introduce you to the secrets of the buildings. The most famous church is the Circular Cathedral of Brasília (Catedral Metropolitana Nossa Senhora Aparecida). It was named after the largest pilgrimage site in the country. Particularly striking is its hyperbolic shape – one of the few non-cubic buildings in the city – which is formed by 16 concrete columns. Their acoustics are also special: You can easily talk at normal volume over a distance of 25 meters. No less spectacular is the Santuário Dom Bosco – a single-aisled church whose supportless interior is spectacularly lit. You will get to know the place of the three powers, where the executive, legislative and judicial branches meet in an architecturally striking manner. In the immediate vicinity is the 224 meter high television tower of Lúcio Costa, which gives you an impressive view over the city. Overnight in Brasília.

Yesterday you were able to get a first impression of the city, which you will deepen together with your guide on this day. The Palácio da Alvorada – the palace of dawn – is the official residence of all presidents of Brazil since Juscelino Kubitschek and was the first building in Brasilia. Surrounded by a lake and tropical plants is the Itamaraty Palace. Brasília’s court consists of six “smart” buildings – your guide will gladly inaugurate you with the technical refinements. The city’s campus brings together projects of architectural greats such as Oscar Niemeyer, Paulo Zimbres, João Gama Filgueiras Lima and Roberto Burle Marx. The National Library and the National Museum – in the form of a large dome – form a complex. Like a cut-off pyramid, however, the National Theatre looks. After all these buildings from the founding period of the city, the JK Bridge from 2003 should not be missing. It spans Lake Paranoá and connects the districts of São Sebastião Paranoá with the Plano Piloto and the central east-west axis of the capital (Eixo Monumental). Overnight in Brasília.

Your chauffeur will take you to the airport and continue on to Belo Horizonte – one of the Brazilian cities with the highest quality of life. Streets for strolling and shopping, beautiful places, a colorful nightlife and wooded areas offer something to every traveler. It is hard to believe that today’s economic and cultural center was founded by a gold prospector as a farm. At Confins Airport, you will be picked up by your guide and taken to your hotel in the city center, from where you can discover the architectural treasures of the city. An absolute highlight for architecture enthusiasts is the district of Pampulha, known for his early works by legendary architect Oscar Niemeyer. Visit the iconic church of São Francisco. The construction of the church was a controversial project in his time and was first opened for a mass in 1956. But not only the church is important: Niemeyer designed several striking buildings around the picturesque Lake Pampulha, including the legendary casino, the yacht club building and the modern art museum (temporarily closed). These buildings, together with the gardens designed by Burle Marx, form a harmonious work of art. Particularly fascinating are the murals created by Cândido Portinari, one of the greatest painters in Brazil. Overnight in Belo Horizonte.

Just outside the city, in Brumadinho, you will find the Inhotim Contemporary Art Center, which exhibits contemporary art from Brazil. The collection – 450 works by more than 100 artists from 30 nations – is embedded in a beautiful garden that shows you a rich biodiversity in addition to art. Here the famous landscape architect Roberto Burle Marx had his fingers in the game. He created a place of meditation, knowledge and pleasure. The focus has been on art since the 1960s: from paintings to photographs to installations. Art finds its exhibition space inside and outside, be it in the water, in the forest or in its own pavilions. Afterwards you will drive to Ouro Preto. Overnight in Ouro Preto.

On your guided tour today, you will feel like you are in an open-air museum. Here you will see the famous Baroque old town: numerous monuments and above all 13 large churches. The most famous are the Igreja São Francisco de Assis, the Igreja Nossa Senhora do Carmo and the opulent Matriz de Nossa Senhora da Conceiçao de Antônio Dias, which is beautifully located between two parks. After visiting the traditional urban mine, you can use the afternoon for your own explorations in Ouro Preto. Overnight in Ouro Preto.

For today, leave Ouro Preto and make a first stop in Congonhas to visit the Santuário de Bom Jesus de Matosinho, where you will find one of Aleijadinho’s masterpieces, the Doze Profetas sculpture series. Aleijadinho is a well-known architect and sculptor of the Brazilian Baroque period. You will continue to São João del Rei, where you will see famous monuments such as the Church of São Francisco with works and projects by Aleijadinho and the landscaping by Burle Marx. The last item on the agenda is the trip to Tiradentes, the most charming city of Minas Gerais and gastronomic capital of this state. It has a rich collection from the first half of the eighteenth century and is home to one of the best restaurants in Brazil. Overnight in Tiradentes.

After breakfast, drive to the airport in Belo Horizonte today. Your journey will take you to the city that was Brazil’s capital for almost 200 years: the metropolis Rio de Janeiro. Rio is located on the Guanabara Bay in southeast Brazil and involuntarily raises images of lightly clothed Sambat dancers and the Copacabana’s dream beach. The name Rio de Janeiro – the “river of January” – comes from Portuguese sailors who discovered the bay in January 1502 and thought it was the mouth of a river. That Rio is more than carnival and colorful nightlife, you quickly notice as you walk through the streets lined with Art Deco buildings. Here the beauty and secrets of this city are revealed in the architectural dress. Accompanied by your guide, explore the first attraction in Rio: The Sugar Loaf. The Glockenberg owes its name to its appearance, which is reminiscent of cone shapes that were used for sugar production in the 16th century. It is located at the western entrance of Guanabara Bay and measures 396 meters. The gondola takes you upstairs, from where you can enjoy the view of the city. Overnight in Rio de Janeiro.

Your day begins with Rio’s most famous postcard motif: the 710-meter-high Corcovado. The 30-meter-high statue of Christo Redentor, one of the “new wonders of the world”, perched here above the Tijuca National Park and offers a breathtaking view. In the afternoon, a fascinating architectural journey through the city center awaits you. At the historic Praça XV you can admire magnificent colonial buildings, followed by the picturesque Arco Teles arch. The sacred diversity of Rio is revealed in the Baroque Igreja de Nossa Senhora da Lapa dos Mercadores and the majestic neoclassical Candelária Church. Another highlight is the magnificent São Bento Monastery with its golden Baroque interior. Cultural accents are set by the CCBB, a lively cultural center in historical guise, and the Real Gabinete Português de Leitura – this neo-Gothic library is considered one of the most beautiful reading rooms in the world. After a photo stop at the elegant Confeitaria Colombo (Belle Époque), you will visit the modern Metropolitan Cathedral, whose cylindrical shape characterizes the skyline. The colorful ending is the famous Escadaria Selarón, a mosaic artwork from all over the world. Overnight in Rio de Janeiro.

One last time, follow Niemeyer’s footsteps when you visit Niterói today. The city is the only one in the country that was founded by an Indian. Along the beach Guanabara stretches the so-called Niemeyer path, which connects buildings of the architect. The most striking is certainly the Museum of Contemporary Art, which stands in the area like a landed UFO. From its windows you can also let the view wander over the beach. The glass terrace on the second floor offers a view of the Sugar Loaf. It continues with momentum: The Oscar-Niemeyer-Foundation has the shape of a spiral and can be reached via a ramp. Like an igloo, the Roberto-Silveira monument ducks above the ground. Here visitors are informed about the city and the state of Rio de Janeiro. A beautiful view over the city, Rio's coastline and the bridge connecting Rio and Niterói can be enjoyed from Juscelino-Kubitschek Square. The port of Charitas is your last stop. Overnight in Rio de Janeiro.

Today ends your journey dedicated to Brazil’s great architecture. Your chauffeur will take you to the airport, from where you will start your journey home.

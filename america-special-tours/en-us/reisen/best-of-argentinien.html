Best of Argentina

24 Days Best of Argentina: Tango in Buenos Aires, glaciers in Patagonia, vineyards in the north and experience the spectacular Iguazú waterfalls up close.

Itinerary & services

Hardly any destination combines so many different landscapes and cultures as Argentina. From the subtropical regions in the north to the icy expanses of Patagonia, a world full of unforgettable impressions opens up. Experience the power of nature at the spectacular waterfalls of Iguazú, discover the colorful mountains and traditions of the northwest and enjoy the tranquility of the endless pampas. Along the majestic Andes await renowned wine regions where some of the best wines in South America are produced. In the heart of the country, charming cities and hospitable people invite you to experience the Argentinian joy of life up close. Further south, the impressive expanse of Patagonia awaits you with its majestic glaciers, rugged landscapes and a unique wildlife that captivates every nature lover. Of course, Buenos Aires must not be missed: a vibrant metropolis full of history, culture and passion. Let yourself be carried away by the sounds of tango, enjoy the famous Argentine cuisine and experience the special atmosphere of this fascinating city. Argentina combines spectacular nature, vibrant traditions and extraordinary experiences to create a journey that surpasses even the highest expectations.

1st day Arrival in the vibrant metropolis of Buenos Aires

Upon arrival at Ministro Pistarini International Airport, you will be warmly welcomed and comfortably transported to your exclusive hotel. Buenos Aires combines European flair with Latin American joie de vivre and impresses as a fascinating metropolis full of contrasts. The city on the banks of the Río de la Plata impresses with its unique mix of historical quarters, modern skyline, lively art scene and traditional culture. Discover magnificent boulevards, charming cafes and stylish boutiques or be inspired by the numerous museums and green parks during the first days of your trip. In the evening, Buenos Aires unfolds its special charm: stylish restaurants, lively tango bars and a varied nightlife make the city a place full of energy and emotions. Overnight in Buenos Aires.

2nd day Buenos Aires in the sign of culture, history and tango

Experience Buenos Aires on an exclusive city tour of one of the most fascinating metropolises in South America. Along the majestic Avenida 9 de Julio, you will pass the famous Teatro Colón and reach the historic Plaza de Mayo with its important government buildings and imposing cathedral. Continue to a traditional café like the legendary Café Tortoni, where coffee has always been considered a cultural ritual of the city. In the colorful district of La Boca with the famous Caminito street, you will experience the lively soul of tango before discovering the modern port district of Puerto Madero with its elegant architecture and the iconic Puente de la Mujer. Afterwards, the tour will take you to the green and elegant Palermo as well as to Recoleta, where you will visit the famous cemetery with the tomb of Eva Perón. In the evening, enjoy an exclusive dinner and an impressive tango show at the legendary Rojo Tango full of passion, music and atmosphere. Overnight in Buenos Aires.

3rd day of discovery in the Paraná Delta

On the third day of your trip, you will leave Buenos Aires and head north to San Isidro and Tigre, where you will get a first impression of the elegant suburbs and green shorescapes of the Río de la Plata. In Tigre, your exclusive boat trip begins through the extensive Paraná Delta with its canals, rivers and idyllic islands. On the private island of El Descanso you will be warmly welcomed and enjoy a relaxed stay in the middle of untouched nature. After a welcome snack, you’ll have time for walks and nature viewing before serving a high-quality 3-course wine lunch. The afternoon is at your leisure to enjoy the tranquility of the island or take part in optional activities. In the late afternoon you will be served tea with homemade cake before returning to Buenos Aires. Along the delta, you will once again enjoy the special atmosphere of this unique water landscape before reaching your hotel. Overnight in Buenos Aires.

Day 4 Into the fascinating world of Patagonia

After a rich breakfast at the hotel, you will be conveniently transferred to Buenos Aires National Airport, from where your flight departs for Patagonia. Upon arrival, private transfer to the exclusive Estancia Rincón Chico (open from mid-September to early April), a unique lodge in a privileged location on the famous peninsula of Valdés. It is located in a private coastal reserve with about 20 kilometers of untouched coastline and offers privileged access to one of the most biodiverse marine regions in the world. The Valdés Peninsula, UNESCO World Heritage Site and Biosphere Reserve, is home to elephants, sea lions, orcas, magellan penguins, whales and numerous bird species. During your stay at Estancia, you will enjoy full board with carefully selected regional specialties as well as varied excursions accompanied by experienced English-speaking guides. Each activity is designed to bring you closer to the extraordinary wildlife and fascinating landscapes of this unique region. The lodge, personally managed by the owner families, combines authentic Patagonian charm with the highest comfort and guarantees an exceptionally private nature experience thanks to its exclusive location. Overnight on the peninsula Valdés.

5th day Exclusive wildlife observations on the Valdés peninsula

Enjoy another unforgettable day in the surroundings of the Valdés Peninsula. Surrounded by the unspoilt beauty of Patagonia, Estancia Rincón Chico offers you the perfect opportunity to experience the tranquility, vastness and fascinating wildlife of this extraordinary region according to your own wishes. Together with naturalist guides and biologists, you will have the opportunity to explore the private coastal landscape of the Estancia and experience the fascinating biodiversity of the region up close. Take advantage of the day to explore the spectacular coastal landscape, enjoy the unique atmosphere of the Estancia or simply let the impressive nature affect you. Depending on the season, southern capers, orcas, elephants, sea lions, dolphins and magellan penguins can be observed (whale season from July to December, penguin season from November to March). Walks along the spectacular cliffs and through the Patagonian steppe open up views of the coast and enable encounters with guanakos, maras and numerous bird species. Between the excursions, the cozy lodge with its terrace, library and views of the Patagonian landscape invites you to relax. In the evening, enjoy regional specialties. Overnight on the peninsula Valdés.

6th day of relaxation and enjoyment of nature on the Valdés peninsula

Another day is at your disposal to fully enjoy the incomparable atmosphere of the Valdés Peninsula. Far from everyday life, you will experience the vastness of Patagonia, the tranquility of the coastal landscapes and the special connection to nature that makes this place so unique. Let the soul dangle, observe the fascinating wildlife or enjoy the exclusive amenities of your Estancia. Take another guided exploration along the coast, observe the wildlife directly from the lodge or learn about the conservation projects of the Península Valdés Foundation dedicated to preserving this extraordinary ecosystem. With your stay, you also make a contribution to the protection of the region’s unique flora and fauna. The family atmosphere, the excellent cuisine with regional products and the privileged location in the middle of one of the most important wildlife reserves in South America make this stay an unforgettable experience for nature lovers and discerning travelers. Overnight on the peninsula Valdés.

7th Day to the End of the World

After breakfast, say goodbye to the impressive wildlife of the Valdés Peninsula and be taken to Trelew Airport. From there, fly on to Ushuaia, the legendary “City at the End of the World”. Nestled between the snow-capped peaks of the Andes and the waters of the famous Beagle Canal, Ushuaia delights with a unique combination of spectacular landscapes, unspoiled nature and fascinating history. As the southernmost city in the world and the most important gateway to Antarctica, it has always had a special attraction for explorers and nature lovers. The region offers an extraordinary variety of natural treasures: majestic mountains, dense southern beech forests, glaciers, crystal clear lakes and an impressive wildlife characterize the landscape. At the same time, Ushuaia tells the exciting story of the first explorers, the indigenous peoples and the pioneers who settled in this remote region. Upon arrival, you will be taken to your hotel. The exclusive Arakur Ushuaia Resort & Spa is spectacularly located on a natural viewpoint above the city and offers unrivalled panoramic views of the Beagle Canal, the surrounding mountains and the unspoiled nature of Tierra del Fuego. Overnight in Ushuaia.

Day 8 Between mountains, forests and the end of the Panamericana

Experience one of the most impressive highlights of your trip to Argentina: the fascinating Tierra del Fuego National Park, just a few kilometers from Ushuaia. Surrounded by majestic mountains, crystal clear lakes, wild rivers and the unique sub-Antarctic forests, a landscape of extraordinary beauty opens up to you. During the guided excursion you will discover the most beautiful places of the National Park, including the picturesque Ensenada Bay, Lake Acigami, Laguna Verde and the famous Lapataia Bay, where the legendary Panamericana ends. With a little luck, you can observe the diverse wildlife of the region and experience the unspoiled nature of Patagonia up close. Overnight in Ushuaia.

9th Day Penguins, Sea Lions and the Beagle Canal

Today, one of Patagonia’s most fascinating excursions awaits you: a boat trip through the legendary water world of the Beagle Canal. From the port of Ushuaias you will travel along spectacular coastal landscapes, framed by the snow-capped peaks of Tierra del Fuego. Along the way, you will encounter an impressive wildlife. On the Bird Island, you will be lucky to observe albatrosses, cormorants and numerous seabird species in their natural habitat. Shortly afterwards you will reach the Sea Lion Island, where entire colonies of these charismatic marine mammals bask on the rocks. Another highlight is the famous Les Éclaireurs lighthouse, the landmark of the Beagle Canal. From here, continue your journey to Martillo Island, where you will have the rare opportunity to spot Magellan and Donkey penguins in the wild. On the way back, enjoy once again the unique backdrop of Tierra del Fuego and discover historic places along the coast before returning to Ushuaia. Overnight in Ushuaia.

10 Day Arrival in the Heart of the Glacier World

After breakfast, transfer to the airport for your onward flight to El Calafate, the gateway to the spectacular landscapes of Patagonia. Upon arrival, you will be taken to the exclusive Pristine camps located on a private estancia in the middle of the Patagonian steppe (open from October to March). Outside the high season we can offer alternative accommodation. Just the journey promises unforgettable views of Lake Argentino and the huge backdrop of the Perito-Moreno Glacier. You move into your stylish Premium Dome with a direct view of the most famous glacier in South America. The luxurious suites combine comfort, sustainability and nature in a unique way, offering an exceptional atmosphere far away from mass tourism. The rest of the day is at your disposal to enjoy the tranquility of the area, admire the spectacular views from your private terrace or get first impressions of the Patagonian wilderness. In the evening, a culinary experience awaits you with regional specialties and selected Argentine wines. Overnight in El Calafate.

Day 11 The Perito-Moreno Glacier up close

Today you will experience one of the absolute highlights of your Patagonia trip: a visit to the famous Los Glaciares National Park. During an impressive boat ride, you will approach the mighty south face of Perito Moreno Glacier. From close up you will experience the huge ice formations and, with a little luck, hear the spectacular thunder of breaking off ice blocks. Afterwards, you will explore the panoramic piers of the National Park, which provide breathtaking views of the glacier and the surrounding mountains. Hardly any other place in South America conveys the power and beauty of nature so impressively. Back at the camp you will enjoy the exclusive atmosphere of the Estancia. End the day with a glass of wine and regional delicacies as the sun sets slowly over the Patagonian landscape. Overnight in El Calafate.

Day 12 Authentic Experiences in the Wilderness of Patagonia

Today is all about authentic Patagonian experiences. Choose from various activities that are already included in the Full Pristine program. Discover the unspoilt nature on guided hikes or mountain bike tours through the expanses of the Patagonian steppe. Learn more about Argentine culture at a mate tasting or take part in a cooking class that prepares regional specialties. Those who want to experience the landscape in a traditional way can take part in a ride and explore the area together with local guides. Between the activities there is enough time to enjoy the unique location of the camp. From your cathedral, the view wanders across the endless expanse of Patagonia to the majestic Perito-Moreno Glacier – a panorama you won’t soon forget. Overnight in El Calafate.

Day 13 Back to the vibrant metropolis of Buenos Aires

After a final breakfast in the impressive natural setting of Patagonia, transfer to the national airport of El Calafate for your return flight to Buenos Aires. Arriving in the Argentine capital, you will be welcomed at Aeroparque Jorge Newbery Airport and comfortably transported to your hotel. The change could hardly be more contrasting: from the quiet expanse of Patagonia back to the living heart of South America. Take advantage of the rest of the day to arrive in peace, enjoy the amenities of your hotel or take a stroll through the elegant city. Overnight in Buenos Aires.

Day 14 From the South to the North: Arrival in Colorful Salta

After breakfast, transfer to Aeroparque Jorge Newbery Airport for your onward flight to Salta in northern Argentina. Upon arrival at Salta airport, you will be taken to your hotel and begin your discovery of one of the most fascinating regions in the country. Salta, known as “La Linda”, is nestled in a fertile valley and is considered the gateway to the wild northwest of Argentina. The city, founded in 1582, delights with its colonial architecture, cobbled streets and a relaxed atmosphere that harmoniously combines history and modern life. The region is rich in indigenous traditions and spectacular landscapes. Here you will find the impressive Quebrada de Humahuaca (UNESCO World Heritage Site) with its colorful mountains, mud houses, historic chapels and millennia-old cultures. Equally fascinating is the region “La Puna”, characterized by vast plateaus, salt lakes and traces of the ancient Inca streets. Salta itself offers a lively cultural life with charming cafes, restaurants, markets and a warm atmosphere. Overnight in Salta.

Day 15 Into the Silence of the Andes

After breakfast, you will leave Salta and begin your journey to one of the most impressive and at the same time most original regions of northern Argentina: the Calchaquí valleys. The ride takes you through ever-changing landscapes: from green valleys to dramatic mountain ranges and wide, almost surreal high plateaus. Along the way, a world of silence, expanse and intense colors will open up for you that makes the north of Argentina so unique. Your destination is the exclusive Bodega Colomé, one of the oldest wineries in the country, nestled in a spectacular mountain landscape at high altitude. Here viticulture combines with nature, tradition and absolute tranquility. Upon arrival, you will move into your room at the charming Hotel Bodega Colomé and have time to enjoy the special atmosphere of the surrounding area. The remoteness, the light of the Andes and the vastness of the landscape create a place of deceleration and enjoyment. In the evening, a dinner in a stylish setting awaits you, accompanied by exquisite wines of the house. Overnight in Molinos.

16th day Nature, wine and absolute tranquility

Today is marked by an extraordinary retreat in the heart of the Calchaquí valleys: the Estancia Colomé, one of Argentina’s most exclusive wineries and boutique hotels. At an altitude of around 2,300 meters, surrounded by untouched nature and endless Andean landscapes, a place of peace, breadth and inspiration awaits you. The Estancia is one of the oldest wineries in the country and is known for its exceptional high-altitude wines, its historical significance and its close connection between nature, art and culture. Here you experience a special form of deceleration. Colonial-style architecture blends harmoniously into the landscape, while only a few suites offer absolute privacy and a wide view over vineyards, mountains and the quiet expanse of the valleys. A special highlight is the combination of wine and art: the property houses the James Turrell Museum, whose light installations change the perception of space and nature in a unique way and underline the special atmosphere of this place. The surroundings invite you to discover nature actively or relaxed: walking through the vineyards, easy hikes or simply enjoying the silence of the Andes. Overnight in Molinos.

17th day Wine landscapes and Andean splendor in Cafayate

After a relaxing breakfast in the unique atmosphere of Estancia Colomé, continue your journey through the spectacular Calchaquí valleys. The drive to Cafayate takes you through one of the most impressive landscapes in northern Argentina. Huge mountain formations, colorful rock landscapes and endless expanses characterize this region, which is known for its extraordinary beauty and wine tradition. Along the way, you will experience a fascinating interplay of light, colors and nature that makes the Calchaquí valleys so unique. In the afternoon you will arrive at Cafayate, the charming wine town in the heart of the Argentine High Andes. The place is famous for its elegant white wines, especially the Torrontés, and combines colonial charm with a relaxed, Mediterranean-looking atmosphere. Here you move into your stylish hotel and have time to enjoy the surroundings in peace, walk through the small streets or simply let the special atmosphere of this place affect you. Overnight in Cafayate.

Day 18 Between vineyards and rock formations

After breakfast in Cafayate, leave the charming wine region and return to Salta. The route takes you once again through the impressive landscapes of the Calchaquí valleys, characterized by color-intensive rock formations, wide plains and ever-changing panoramas. A special highlight of the route is the crossing of the famous Quebrada de las Conchas, a unique gorge landscape that with its sculpturally shaped rocks is one of the most spectacular natural backdrops in Argentina. Wind and time have created a landscape that is unparalleled in its variety of colors and forms. In the afternoon you will reach Salta and be taken to your accommodation. House of Jasmines Ranch welcomes you as a stylish retreat on the outskirts of the city, nestled in a quiet countryside overlooking the Andes. The combination of colonial charm, modern elegance and rural tranquility makes this place an ideal retreat after eventful travel days. The rest of the day is at your disposal to enjoy the atmosphere of the ranch or simply relax. Overnight in Salta.

Day 19 Where the mountains tell of colors

Early in the morning you will leave Salta and start one of the most impressive stages of your journey through northern Argentina. The ride will take you to the province of Jujuy and into the legendary Quebrada de Humahuaca, a UNESCO World Heritage Site that impresses with its spectacular color contrasts, traditional villages and deep-rooted indigenous culture. Along the way, you will pass through small villages such as Yala, Volcán and Tumbaya before reaching Purmamarca. Purmamarca is famous for the world-famous hill of seven colors, whose color palette creates a unique landscape. Continue through Tilcara and Humahuaca, where time is set aside for individual exploration and lunch at your own expense. In the afternoon, the journey continues into even more spectacular altitudes. Via a mountain route you reach the remote highland region of the Salinas Grandes, where an endless white salt area spreads under the sky. In this surreal landscape you will experience one of the most impressive natural panoramas in South America. In the late afternoon you will reach your exclusive camp, which is harmoniously integrated into the landscape. The Pristine Domes offer comfort in the middle of the silence of the Andes. Overnight in Purmamarca.

Day 20 A day in the heart of the white salt plain

Today is all about the unique landscape of the Salinas Grandes, one of the largest salt plains in South America. At an altitude of more than 3,000 meters, you will experience an almost surreal world of light, sky and infinite expanse. The special location of your camp allows you to enjoy the surroundings in complete peace. The day is free and offers space for relaxation, nature experiences and individual discovery. Take walks across the glittering salt surface, watch the extraordinary play of light and reflections or simply enjoy the silence of this secluded region. The landscape is constantly changing throughout the day, always offering new perspectives on the majestic backdrop of the Andes. Your stay at Pristine Camps combines exceptional comfort with an intense nature experience. Culinary delights will spoil you with regionally inspired dishes and carefully selected products. Overnight in Purmamarca.

Day 21 Where the Rainforest Begins

After breakfast, transfer to Purmamarca Airport for your onward flight to Puerto Iguazú in the subtropical north of Argentina. With your arrival, the landscape changes dramatically: the dry expanse of the Andes gives way to the lush, green Atlantic rainforest, which houses one of the most impressive natural wonders in the world. At the airport, you will be personally welcomed and taken to your exclusive retreat: Awasi Iguazú, one of the most prestigious boutique hotels in South America. The private villas are harmoniously embedded in nature and offer maximum privacy and highest comfort in the middle of the rainforest. Your stay follows a fully tailor-made concept: each guest has a private guide and their own four-wheel drive vehicle to make the daily excursions flexible according to individual interests. Whether intense nature experiences, animal observations or relaxed moments in the jungle, each experience is curated personally. Cuisine awaits you in the in-house Relais & Châteaux restaurant, accompanied by an open bar with a selection of wines and local drinks. Overnight in Puerto Iguazú.

Day 22 The Waterfalls in the Heart of the Rainforest

Today is at your leisure and offers the opportunity to experience one of the world’s greatest natural wonders in your own rhythm: the Iguazú waterfalls in the national park of the same name. Together with your private guide, you will explore the Iguazú National Park, which is a UNESCO World Heritage site and opens up on the Argentine side through an extensive network of jetties and viewpoints. Surrounded by the dense Atlantic rainforest, new perspectives on the enormous water masses that extend over more than 270 individual waterfalls open up again and again. A special highlight is the proximity to the famous Garganta del Diablo (devil’s throat), where nature shows itself in all its strength and drama. The thunderous water, the rising fog and the tropical vegetation create an intense nature experience that is unparalleled. In addition to the waterfalls themselves, the park also offers the opportunity to discover the diverse flora and fauna of the rainforest: from exotic birds to the typical sounds of the jungle that accompany you at every turn. In the afternoon return to your private villa at Awasi Iguazú, where you can enjoy the peace and privacy of your accommodation. Overnight in Puerto Iguazú.

23rd day A paradise of water, forest and silence

This day is also completely at your disposal in the exclusive Awasi Iguazú. Take the opportunity to further discover the impressive nature of the Iguazú area or simply enjoy the tranquility of your private villa. The subtropical rainforest offers countless possibilities for individual exploration: from animal observations to easy hikes to relaxed moments in the middle of nature. Each experience is designed together with your private guide according to your interests. Awasi Iguazú combines maximum privacy with exceptional service and allows you to experience the rainforest in a very personal and intense way. Overnight in Puerto Iguazú.

24 Days When a Great Journey Ends

After days of experience in the tropical rainforest of Iguazú, your extraordinary trip through Argentina is coming to an end. After breakfast, you will be picked up from your villa depending on the departure time and taken to Puerto Iguazú Airport. With the departure you will take unforgettable impressions: the vibrant metropolis of Buenos Aires, the majestic landscapes of Patagonia, the expanse of the Andes, the colors of northern Argentina and the overwhelming power of the Iguazú waterfalls. A journey full of contrasts, natural wonders and exclusive experiences comes to an end.

Central America Journey - The Lights of Central America

Central America Travel with América Special Tours. Tailored and individual. With own chauffeur and private tour guide. Now ask me.

On this tour of Central America you will get to know the most charming corners of the three countries Guatemala, Costa Rica and Panama. Embark on a journey that shows you the many facets of these countries and let yourself be enchanted by the last untouched pieces of jewelry in Latin America. Historical explorations in Guatemala take you on the trail of the Maya to Tikal and Copán. Typical experiences await you at the authentic highland markets in Guatemala. In addition, a boat trip on the most beautiful lake in Latin America is on the program - as well as numerous scenic highlights and a stay in the Caribbean. In Costa Rica, the famous Arenal volcano and its backdrop fascinate. After a few relaxing days on the beach, you will end your trip by experiencing the gorgeous Panama City. An important highlight is the exclusive and dreamlike hotels and lodges that you will inhabit and enjoy during this round trip.

1st Day Welcome to Guatemala

Your journey through Central America begins in Guatemala City. At the airport you will be welcomed by your German-speaking guide and your private chauffeur and chauffeured to your hotel. Casa Santo Domingo is picturesquely located in the not far away town of Antigua. Look forward to your charming boutique hotel. Enjoy the wonderful atmosphere of the hotel or take a trip organized by Casa Santo Domingo. Discover colonial art, visit an archaeological museum, a pottery, a wax workshop, a chapel or Antigua’s largest jade factory with a museum. Overnight in Antigua.

Day 2 Discover one of America’s oldest cities

Today will take you through one of the most historically significant and architecturally unique cities not only in Guatemala, but all of Central America. Antigua is one of the oldest cities in America and formerly colonial capital of all Central America. Founded in 1543 and almost completely destroyed by an earthquake in 1773, it finally became a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1979. After the bustling main square and the cathedral, visit the ruins of a monastery. Scattered throughout Antigua are numerous monasteries, churches and convents. Some of these ruins have been restored, some converted into hotels, but most of them were left as ruins due to lack of money. After so much history and architecture, a gustatory highlight awaits you. You will visit a coffee finca and learn all about the tasty black gold. Especially in the southern area around the volcanic mountain slopes of the Sierra Madre and in the Antigua area, Guatemalan coffee thrives. On these fertile soils, the exquisite, famous Arabica beans are harvested. Overnight in Antigua.

Day 3 A fantastic view awaits you

Today, drive north of Antigua up to Cerro de la Cruz, the Cross Hill. Arriving at the top, you will find a fantastic view of Antigua and the majestic Agua volcano. Take a walk through the breathtaking landscape up here. Your guide will be looking forward to telling you entertaining anecdotes about his home. Then drive back to Antigua. The rest of the day is at your disposal. Overnight in Antigua.

Day 4 - The Sololá Market

Today you will visit the charming town of Sololá, where there is always market day on Tuesdays and Fridays. Stroll through the market and experience the authentic life of Guatemala. Afterwards, visit some architectural masterpieces such as the Church of Nuestra Señora de la Asunción, whose towers rise high above the rooftops of the city. The silverwork inside the church is truly worth seeing. You will now drive via Panajachel on Lake Atitlán to Santa Catarina Palopo. Here is another, enchanting hotel waiting for you. Overnight in Panajachel (Santa Catarina Palopo).

Day 5: A boat trip on the lake

Aldous Huxley called Lake Atitlán “the most beautiful lake in Latin America”. Fertile hills and three mighty volcanoes give the lake a fairytale atmosphere. On a boat trip, you will get to know the beauty of the lake and its surroundings. The destination of the boat trip is the village of Santiago Atitlán, located at the foot of the volcanoes and center of the Tzutujíl Maya. The village looks back on a centuries-old Mayan past. Despite the arrival of tourism, this past is still reflected today in tradition, way of life and of course also in life on the streets and markets. Continue to the town of San Antonio Palopó, known for its textile production. To this day, the women use the traditional hip loom there. Residents continue to make a living by growing onions and anise. After this interesting insight, visit the village of San Juan La Laguna before returning to Santa Catarina Palopo. Overnight in Panajachel (Santa Catarina Palopo).

Day 6: Ancient Mayan Rites

Your daily destination is the famous Chichicastenango Highland Market. Chichi, as it is affectionately called by its inhabitants, is especially well known in two ways. Not only has it been and still is an important supra-regional marketplace. It is also a place where pre-Christian, shamanistic rituals are practiced that give the city its very own mysticism. Arriving in Chichicastenango you will have the opportunity to stroll over the most beautiful and colorful market of the Highland Indians. There is an incredible variety of country-typical, handcrafted products offered – the perfect souvenirs of a trip to authentic Guatemala. After your stroll you will go to the more than 400 years old church “Santo Tomás”. Here you will have the opportunity to attend Catholic and ancient Mayan rites that merge into a mystical syncretism. Afterwards you will be chauffeured back to Santa Catarina Palopo. Overnight in Panajachel (Santa Catarina Palopo).

7th day hike on Lake Atitlán

This morning you will start with a private boat trip to the town of Santa Cruz and from there to the small town of Tzununá. Tzununá, in the Mayan language means “House of the hummingbird”. The approximately two and a half hour hike along Atitlán Lake takes you up to a small hill 150 meters above the lake. From there you will have a wonderful view of the volcanoes, which will also accompany you on the further path. By land, you will return to Santa Catarina Palopo for a trip to Guatemala City. Here you are already expected at the Hotel Vista Real. Take advantage of the rest of the day to explore Guatemala City. Overnight in Guatemala City.

Day 8: In the footsteps of the Maya in Tikal

Today, your guide will pick you up early from the hotel to catch an early flight from Guatemala City to Flores. Flores is the capital of El Petén County, where Guatemala’s lowland jungle is located. Enjoy the dreamlike landscapes, to which your flight already gives you a brief taste. Once in Flores, enjoy a delicious breakfast before the bus takes you to the famous Maya site Tikal, in the middle of the tropical rainforest of Petén. This archaeological jewel is one of the most important and impressive Mayan sites, formerly home to over 100,000 Maya. After a guided tour of the most interesting parts of the complex, such as complex “Q”, North and Central Acropolis, Central Square, Temple IV and “Mundo Perdido” you will arrive at the “Restaurante El Mesón” for lunch. Before making your way to your Petén hotel, visit the Sylvanus G. Morley Museum. Overnight in Petén(Tikal).

Day 9 Explore the Yaxhá Mayan Site

This morning you will visit the Mayan site Yaxhá. This vast Mayan city is located on a high altitude above the northern shore of the lagoon of the same name. Yaxhá - "Green Water" in the Mayan language - is one of the largest Mayan places in the Petén lowland jungle. The Yaxhá Lagoon is an area with the richest biodiversity. From the top of the structure of Temple 216 you have an impressive view over the jungle roof. After the tour, drive back to the hotel. The rest of the day is at your disposal. Overnight in Petén (Tikal).

Day 10 in the Caribbean

After breakfast, head south. Arriving at the River Dulce, board your private boat and sail down the river. On the way, you can relax and enjoy the lush vegetation and variety of exotic birds. Particularly impressive are the water lilies fields and mangrove forests. Before you reach the mouth of the river to the sea, you will be offered another impressive natural spectacle: you will pass through the gorge of the Dulce River. Arriving at Amatique Bay, and thus in the Caribbean, you reach the village of Livingston. There, Hotel Villa Caribe welcomes you, amidst beautiful gardens, with outdoor pool and views of Lake Izabal and the Caribbean Sea. Enjoy private beach access and the quiet location in Livingston on the Delta des Ríos Dulce. Each of the spacious rooms is decorated in the Caribbean style and offers a balcony with sea or lake views. Balabala Restaurant will spoil you with local cuisine and many popular fish dishes during your stay. Just one kilometer from the hotel is the Biotopo Protegido Chocón Machacas nature reserve. Enjoy the nature protected there or book a boat transfer to Puerto Barrios. Overnight in Livingston.

Day 11 - Continue towards Honduras

This morning you will take your private boat back to the Río Dulce. From here, head to the Carretera al Atlántico and head west to Quiriguá, a small archaeological site in the middle of a huge banana plantation. Their value comes from gigantic sandstone steles and the so-called “zoomorphs”, huge sandstone blocks covered all around with relief work. The history of the Mayan site is closely linked to that of Copán. Particularly interesting is Stele C, on which the creation of the universe can be traced in the idea of the Maya, which for the Maya in 3114 B.C. It was in our era. Afterwards, your journey continues towards Honduras. Take advantage of the drive to the small town of Copán Ruinas to marvel at the beautiful, varied natural and cultural landscape of this region. Overnight in Copán.

Day 12 At the ruins of Copán

The ruins of Copán, along with Tikal, Chichen Itzá and Uxmal, are among the most outstanding structures that the Maya have ever created. These ruins are located in a beautiful wide valley surrounded by extensive tobacco plantings. You will visit the Acropolis, the worth seeing ball playground, the famous glyph staircase and the Plaza Central with its artistic steles. The volcano-andesit stone in Copán allowed the stonemasons of that time to produce exact glyphs, which are so well preserved today that the epigraphers can trace a 400-year history more accurately than in any other place. Before returning to the hotel, visit the Sculpture Museum in Copán. Overnight in Copán.

Day 13 Back to Guatemala City

Sleep today and enjoy a good breakfast in the tropical jungle of Copán. Around noon, your chauffeur will pick you up and take you back to Guatemala City. Here you will spend one more night before flying on to Costa Rica for your nature adventure the next day. Overnight in Guatemala City.

Day 14 La Pura Vida in Costa Rica

From Guatemala City, fly on to Costa Rica today. At the airport, an English-speaking assistant awaits you, who will help you with luggage and customs authorities if necessary. You will then be met by your private chauffeur and driven to your hotel. The beautiful Finca Rosa Blanca Resort is located above the impressive Central Valley in the hills above San José. Nestled in a tropical landscape of countless fruit trees and surrounded by exotic plants, the small hotel jewel offers spectacular views of volcanoes, cloud forest and coffee plantations. The elegant rooms exude their very own charm with their round arches and traditional murals. The baths are an oasis of relaxation thanks to detailed mosaics and luxurious jacuzzis. Enjoy the culinary highlights, with care and creativity from the abundance of your own orchards and greenhouses. Relax in the evening hours with a crackling fire and round off your day perfectly at the luxurious Finca Rosa Blanca Resort. A visit to the idyllic La Paz Falls or a discovery tour of fascinating butterfly species are also great day activities. Overnight in Heredia (San José).

Day 15: A Symphony of Nature Wellness

After breakfast, pick up your rental car and drive to your next destination: Bajos del Toro. A beautiful view will accompany you until you reach El Silencio Lodge, which lies in the lush greenery of the cloud forest. Just 90 minutes’ drive from San José Airport, immerse yourself in a completely different world. The El Silencio Lodge & Spa is quiet and idyllic among the tree giants of the cloud forest. With great care and consideration for the delicate ecosystem, this eco-lodge was created, which promises its guests a "symphony of natural wellness". This does not only describe the spa applications, but rather the overall experience that awaits you. In your private, heated jacuzzi, watch the scraps of fog slowly roaming over the treetops. And from your bed, thanks to the panoramic fronts, enjoy a magnificent view of this ancient cloud forest that lures with all its secrets. On guided hikes, you can learn more about traditional medicinal plants, discover a completely different wildlife at night and seek adventure while rafting, waterfall hiking or on the treetop trail. Wellness and action, 5-star luxury and naturalness – all this keeps a wonderful balance at El Silencio Lodge & Spa. Overnight in Bajos del Toro.

16th day tortillas made by themselves

The town of Los Bajos del Toro is located off the tourist routes and is therefore still relatively unknown. Perhaps this is the basis for the originality of his villagers, who cultivate their traditions in everyday life. Your English-speaking guide will take you along with other guests to the cultural hubs of the community: the church, the school and the football pitch. The “Pulperia”, the Aunt Emma shop of Doña Olga, can also be considered a communal center, as it not only sells food, but is also popular for its self-made baked goods and fresh tortillas. If you want, you can make your own tortilla. Don Martin Porras also lives in the village. His love for orchids is evident in his collection, which includes over 400 different species. He is happy to guide you through his garden to show you the different and also curious specimens, such as “women’s shoes” and tiny mini-orchids. Equipped with a magnifying glass, you will not miss the smallest detail on this orchid safari. Overnight in Bajos del Toro.

Day 17 Volcano of El Arenal

(Without a guide, without a chauffeur)

After breakfast, you can still enjoy some fresh mountain air before departing for your next destination. With a bit of weather luck, you can already recognize the volcano silhouette. The rest of the afternoon is at your disposal. Relax by the pool or visit one of the thermal springs in the zone. The Arenal volcano and the lush rainforest form the impressive backdrop for the first-class Arenal Nayara Resort. Experience a luxurious atmosphere in the middle of one of the most beautiful rainforest regions. All 24 detached bungalows of the boutique hotel offer from the private balcony with whirlpool unique views of Costa Rica’s lush nature and its own tropical garden with outdoor shower. The rooms blend perfectly into the jungle with natural materials and earthy shades. From the lodge you are spoilt for choice, whether offering countless exciting activities such as hikes to the foot of the Arenal volcano, horse riding excursions or adventure tours to the game reserve Caño Negro. Experience jungle romance at the luxury hotel Arenal Nayara. Overnight at Arenal Volcano National Park.

18th day Volcanoes, lakes and thermal springs

(Without a guide, without a chauffeur)

Undoubtedly, beautiful La Fortuna is one of the most visited destinations in Costa Rica. It is peacefully located, surrounded by mountains and the beautiful Arenalsee. Around the volcano there are numerous hotels, restaurants, bars, clubs and shops. Use your rental car and explore the area on your own. The area offers many attractions and La Fortuna is an ideal starting point for interesting excursions. These include, for example, hikes on the cold lava fields in Arenal National Park or over the Arenal suspension bridges, hot springs, bird watching, mountain bike tours, riding trips, river rafting, visiting the Venado caves, hiking or horseback riding to the waterfall of La Fortuna, an adrenaline-containing canopy tour or a boat trip in the Caño Negro sanctuary for observing crocodiles, monkeys, sloths and birds. After an eventful day of excursion, you can relax in one of the thermal springs and end the day in the warm water with a tropical cocktail in your hand. Overnight at Arenal Volcano National Park.

19-21. Day off to the beach

Today you leave the mountains of Costa Rica and drive with your rental car further west towards the Pacific coast. The 170 kilometers to the coast pass by in the magnificent landscapes of Costa Rica as if in flight, especially when the anticipation is the drive. Because at the destination you can expect a magnificent hotel by the sea. Sapphire blue water, dense jungle and easy accessibility – the peninsula Papagayo brings everything you would expect from a successful bathing holiday in Costa Rica. Explore the island or be pampered in your first-class hotel. Overnight stays at Peninsula Papagayo.

Day 22 Back to San José

Today you travel with your rental car to San José – back to your beautiful Finca Rosa Blanca. Upon arrival, you can rent your car until 18 Return the clock on site and enjoy the view of the charming San José. Overnight in Heredia (San José).

Day 23 - Continue to Panama

From Juan Santamaria, fly to Panama City in less than 90 minutes today. Your German-speaking guide awaits you in the arrival hall and takes you to your hotel. This lovingly furnished American Trade Hotel is located in the historic Casco Antiguo district, a UNESCO World Heritage Site. The Casco has been completely renovated in recent years and has become the country’s center of art, design and cultural upswing. Many new bars, restaurants and hotels have opened here. Built in 1917 during the boom years after completion of the Panama Canal, the building of the American Trade Hotel once housed branches of several companies. A mixture of neoclassical and Art Nouveau details, it became Panama’s first luxury home. In the meantime, it is wonderfully blossomed, lovingly restored and allows the elegance of the old world to meet modern decor. The American Trade Hotel opened in late 2013 and is now the largest hotel in the historic old town with 50 rooms. Overnight in Panama City.

Day 24 Oh how beautiful Panama is

In the morning you will start to visit the fascinating capital of Panama. The gorgeous backdrop of skyscrapers, noble villas and green hills will delight you. The tour begins with a visit to the ruins of Panamá Viejo, the first settlement from the 16th century. Afterwards we go along the shore to the old town of Casco Antiguo. During the tour, you will see unique buildings of the 17th and 18th centuries, the remarkable cathedral, which was dedicated in April 1796 after a construction period of 108 years, and the San José Church with its magnificent gold altar. The journey then leads to the Causeway, from where you can enjoy a magnificent view of the Panama City skyline. Afterwards, visit the Miraflores Locks. Here you will get insights into the workings and dimensions of the famous Panama Canal. As a special end to your trip, you will have the opportunity to admire the capital Panama from high altitude on a one-hour helicopter flight. Watch the busy city life and marvel at the gigantic skyline. The highlight of the scenic flight is the detour to the canal, where you can see from above how the large container ships maneuver through the narrow waterway and its locks. Overnight in Panama City.

25th day of Hasta pronto

Unfortunately, even the most beautiful journey comes to an end. But before your driver takes you to Panama City Airport, you can use today to spend unforgettable hours in Panama City. In the evening, your plane finally takes off towards Europe. Goodbye to Central America!

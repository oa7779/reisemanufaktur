The world’s only luxury icebreaker, Le Commandant Charcot, is on the verge of a historic milestone: in 2028, this extraordinary expedition ship will orbit Antarctica for the first time. A real world premiere and a milestone in the history of polar travel. Designed specifically for extreme conditions, Le Commandant Charcot combines state-of-the-art ice technology with maximum comfort. As a hybrid icebreaker of the latest generation, it enables routes that were previously unattainable and opens up completely new perspectives on the white continent for even experienced explorers. Le Commandant Charcot is the only passenger ship in the world that combines the most advanced technologies and electric propulsion with liquefied natural gas. As a result, it reduces its ecological footprint and enables more responsible shipping. It is also equipped with two research laboratories open to international scientists who will introduce you to the most important current topics surrounding the development of the polar regions. The complete circumnavigation of Antarctica is considered one of the last major nautical challenges. Huge ice shelf landscapes, monumental glaciers, remote stretches of coastline and a fascinating wildlife make this expedition an unparalleled experience. At the same time, guests on board enjoy first-class service, spacious suites, excellent gastronomy and a scientifically accompanied expedition program. This extraordinary journey is for everyone who wants to not only read history, but experience it for themselves.

The Expedition of Your Life

The complete circumnavigation of Antarctica expands the frontiers of modern polar exploration and opens up travelers to one of the world’s rarest expeditions. This extraordinary experience has been specially designed for the Polar Expedition Yacht Le Commandant Charcot, an innovative vessel that can safely and comfortably reach even the most remote regions of the Southern Arctic Sea. On this unique journey, you will discover the unspoiled beauty of the white continent from a perspective reserved for few people. Huge ice landscapes, majestic glaciers, huge table icebergs and a fascinating wildlife accompany you along a route around Antarctica. With a bit of luck, observe penguin colonies, whales and seabirds in their natural habitat. On board, guests enjoy the highest comfort, excellent gastronomy and a comprehensive expedition program with experienced polar experts, naturalists and guides. Lectures, Zodiac excursions and landings allow you to experience Antarctica in depth while learning more about climate, geography and the history of polar research.
 

The Monumental Ross Ice Shelf

Drive through the spectacular Ross Sea and experience one of the most remote and unspoilt regions on our planet. Along the vast Ross Ice Shelf, the largest ice barrier in the world, a landscape of endless ice sheets, monumental glaciers and deep blue polar sea opens up. The Ross Sea is considered the largest marine protected area on earth and is often referred to as “the last ocean” because its ecosystem has remained largely intact to this day. Here you will discover an exceptionally rich wildlife with emperor penguins, seals, whales and numerous seabird species living in this unique environment. This passage is one of the most impressive moments of an Antarctic expedition: a rare opportunity to explore a region that few travelers ever reach. An unforgettable experience for everyone who wants to experience the pure power and beauty of the polar world up close.

Hidden Coasts and Distant Archipelago

Immerse yourself in the unspoilt wilderness of Antarctica and discover the most remote coastlines on our planet. On this extraordinary expedition, explore regions such as George V, Queen Mary and Queen Maud Land, the mysterious area of the shipwreck of the Endurance, the fascinating Charcot Island, the sprawling Marie Bird Land, Wilkesland, Adélieland and the remote Balleny Islands. Each of these places tells its own story from the pioneering period of polar research, shaped by explorers, adventures and overwhelming nature. Huge glaciers, endless ice sheets, dramatic coastlines and a rich wildlife with penguins, seals and whales await you on landings and Zodiac excursions. On board your expedition ship, you will enjoy the highest comfort, excellent gastronomy and an expert expedition team that will give you insights into the geography, wildlife and history of Antarctica. This unique adventure is aimed at explorers, nature lovers and travelers who want to experience the extraordinary feeling of being among the few people who see these remote regions.

The untamed wildlife of Antarctica

Experience the fascinating and diverse wildlife of Antarctica up close and let yourself be enchanted by the untouched polar landscape. On this unique expedition, you’ll encounter penguins in their lively colonies, watch majestic humpback whales and playful dwarf whales gliding through the icy waters, and perhaps curious orcas cruise your way. Seals, elephants and sea lions rest on the ice beaches while storm birds and other seabirds circle above you acoustically underline the barren beauty of this secluded region. In this strictly protected ecosystem, unforgettable moments of observation open up, which show the interplay between wildlife, water, ice and sky in its purest form. Every encounter becomes a unique experience: from watching penguins feed, to watching whales emerge from the water, to the elegant flight movements of birds over the shimmering glaciers. Under the guidance of experienced naturalist guides, you will learn more about animals’ behaviour, habitats and adaptation strategies to the extreme climate.

Mount Erebus: Unique Volcano in the Ice

Mount Erebus on Ross Island is not only the southernmost active volcano on Earth, but also one of Antarctica’s most impressive natural wonders. With its steady plume of smoke, the glowing lava lakes and the ice-covered slopes, it draws scientists, adventurers and travelers alike into its spell. Already at the beginning of the 20th century, Mount Erebus served as an important point of reference for the major expeditions to the South Pole and played a central role in exploring the white continent.nToday, guests of an Antarctic expedition can experience the majestic presence of this volcano up close, whether on Zodiac excursions along the coast, on guided excursions on Ross Island or simply from the deck of the expedition ship. The combination of rough polar landscape, active volcanology and Arctic wildlife makes Mount Erebus one of the most spectacular highlights of any Antarctic voyage. Here history, science and adventure merge into an unforgettable experience that only a few people in the world have been granted.

Historic Sites of Antarctic Pioneers

At the foot of Mount Erebus on Ross Island, travelers can enjoy a fascinating glimpse into the history of major polar expeditions. Here you can visit the original surviving cabins of Robert Falcon Scott, which he used during his famous Discovery Expedition (1901–1904) and Terra Nova Expedition (1910–1913), as well as the cabins of Ernest Shackleton, which are testimonies to his extraordinary pioneering spirit. These historic sites provide an impressive picture of how the early explorers lived, worked and explored the icy wilderness of Antarctica under extreme conditions. Every step between the old huts, the campsites and the remains of the equipment brings to life the stories of courage, perseverance and human determination. For visitors, this becomes a moving experience that combines history, adventure and nature, highlighting the incredible challenges that pioneers of polar research have had to face.

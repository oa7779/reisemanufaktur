Mexico Tour - A Caribbean Dream

Mexico Round trip with Caribbean flair of Latin America specialist América Special Tours. Here you can directly request your individual Mexico trip.

Discover Mexico in the relaxed way: a day on the beach helps you to gain distance from everyday life after arrival. A small round trip through colonial cities and Mayan ruins will then give you an insight into Mexico’s cultural diversity. During a cooking course you can enjoy the country cuisine. The crowning highlight is a few days on the secluded island of Holbox, where you can enjoy the beauty of Caribbean beaches far away from the tourist crowds.

Day 1 Welcome to Cancún

Your journey through Yucatán begins on arrival in Cancún. Forty years ago, this was little more than a sandbank on Mexico's Caribbean coast. It was not until the seventies that people recognized the region's potential, with its turquoise sea, white sand, and fascinating remains of the vanished Maya civilization. Many visitors use Cancún as a starting point for exploring Maya history. On arrival, you are collected at the airport and taken to your beach hotel on the Riviera Maya. After the long journey, enjoy a swim in the clear sea. Overnight on the Riviera Maya.

2nd day of relaxation on the Riviera Maya

At the beginning of your holiday, enjoy a day of relaxation on the beautiful beach of Playa Maroma to gain distance from everyday life. Your resort offers the best conditions for this. Hotel Chablé Maroma is a magnificent retreat in a secluded stretch of jungle and beach. Each of the independent casitas has a private pool with access to the vast 17,000 square meter property. Natural materials mix with the tropical vegetation and form an oasis for guests who want to relax and relax. The first-class spa offers a variety of applications, some of which also include ancient, handed down Mayan techniques. Also culinary you come here fully at your expense. The restaurants at Hotel Chablé were designed in collaboration with chef Jorge Vallejo, whose Quintonil restaurant in Mexico City has been named one of the best in the world. Overnight at the Riviera Maya.

Day 3 One of the “New Seven Wonders of the World”

Today you take a trip into the past – into the era of the Maya. Your first stop is Chichén Itzá, which you can reach after about two and a half hours of driving. This ruined site is one of the best restored places of the Maya and was chosen among the “new seven wonders of the world”. In the middle of dense jungles lie the impressive Kukulcán Pyramid, some smaller temples and ruins of various buildings. During a walk through the site, let the stories of your guide revive the past in this place. Afterwards, visit one of the cenotes typical of Yucatán. These natural freshwater limestone fountains have already played an important religious role for the Maya and today provide a welcome opportunity for refreshment. In the afternoon you will arrive at today’s retreat: the exclusive Chablé Yucatán, nestled in the tranquility of a former Henequén hacienda. Located in the middle of the jungle, Chablé Yucatán combines thousands of years of traditions of Mayan culture with luxurious comfort at the highest level and breathtaking nature. The resort offers a unique golf course and three gourmet restaurants, as well as one of the largest tequila collections in the world. Overnight in Chocholá.

Day 4: The White City of Mérida

After breakfast, drive to the nearby colonial town of Mérida. The capital of Yucatán was built more than 450 years ago on the site of an ancient Mayan city and is considered the gateway to the world of Maya. Today, on a city tour, you will dedicate yourself to the sights, such as the mighty Cathedral of San Ildefonso, for the construction of which the stones of old Mayate temples were "recycled". It is one of the oldest Christian churches in Latin America and contains murals illustrating the meeting between the founder of the city, Francisco de Montejo, and the Mayan king Tutl Xiu. You will also get to know the Government Palace in the historic center of the city and stroll along the Paseo de Montejo boulevard with its magnificent houses. A special experience awaits you at lunchtime: During a private cooking class, you will immerse yourself in the traditional cuisine of the Maya. Together with local hosts, you will prepare typical dishes according to traditional recipes and get an exciting insight into the culinary traditions of the region. Afterwards, enjoy the self-prepared specialties for lunch. The rest of the afternoon is free for individual explorations in Mérida. Overnight in Chocholá.

Day 5 Uxmal and the world of cocoa

Today you will discover Uxmal, one of the most beautiful and impressive Mayan sites in Mexico. The ruined city is considered a masterpiece of the so-called Puuc architecture and fascinates with its harmonious proportions as well as the ornately decorated facades. Architectural highlights include the Pyramid of the Soothsayer and the magnificent Governor’s Palace, which impressively testify to the cultural richness of the Maya. Then visit the Choco-Story-Museum. Here you will learn about the millennia-old history of cocoa and its importance to the Maya, who used the cocoa bean not only as a means of payment and merchandise, but also for ritual ceremonies. In the afternoon, return to your hotel and enjoy time to relax in the special atmosphere of Chablé Yucatán. Overnight in Chocholá.

Day 6 Welcome to Paradise

After breakfast, say goodbye to the interior of Yucatán and drive first to Valladolid. The charming colonial town is one of the most beautiful places on the peninsula and impresses with its colorful facades, historic churches and the relaxed flair of its squares and alleys. After a short walking tour, continue your journey north. In Chiquilá, the guided part of your tour ends. From here you can take the ferry to the island of Holbox. Away from large hotel complexes, you can expect a relaxed Caribbean paradise with sandy streets, colorful houses and endless natural beaches. Even the small town of the island does not spread any hectic. Almost everything can be reached on foot here, the roads are not very busy. The next few days are all about relaxation, nature and Caribbean serenity. Overnight at Holbox.

7.-8. Day rest and relaxation

Feel like Robinson Crusoe and enjoy the tranquility and originality of the Caribbean. Holbox is ideal for lazy days on the beach and your unique boutique hotel will not miss anything you need to relax. Relax in the hammock and be pampered with delicious cocktails and fresh fish and seafood culinary. Are you looking for more action? Explore the island by bicycle and stroll through the colorful center or take a kayak or boat tour to the surrounding islets and lagoons. A special highlight can be experienced between June and mid-September: During these months, snorkeling and diving excursions with whale sharks start from Holbox, which find an ideal habitat around the island. These up to 13 meters long fish, unlike other sharks, are completely harmless to humans – they feed mainly on plankton. However, the gentle giants are always impressive. With a little luck, you will also discover turtles, rays and dolphins. Overnight at Holbox.

9th day of travel home

Today your journey through Yucatán ends. After the ferry crossing from Holbox back to Chiquilá, you will be transferred to Cancún airport for your return flight. Alternatively, you have the option to extend your stay with an individual bathing extension on the Riviera Maya. Even if your journey comes to an end, the many impressions will continue to work for a long time: the fascinating world of the Maya, unforgettable moments of enjoyment, dreamlike cenotes, colonial cities and the relaxed days on the Caribbean Sea. With numerous memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories in your luggage, you will embark on a well-rested journey home or onwards and look forward to sharing your experiences with family and friends.

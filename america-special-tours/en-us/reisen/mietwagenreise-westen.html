US Road Trip — Jewels of the West

USA West Coast rental car trip: Experience the jewels of the West Coast of the USA on an individual round trip of América Special Tours! Ask now!

21 days

Your trip idea

The western United States has always been synonymous with adventure, vibrant cities and untamed wilderness. From the sun-drenched beaches of California to the spectacular national parks of the Southwest, a journey full of contrasts and unforgettable experiences awaits you. Discover the laid-back coastal resorts of Santa Monica, Carmel and Santa Barbara, experience the fascinating desert world of Palm Springs and Las Vegas and immerse yourself in the vast natural scenery of the Grand Canyon, Monument Valley, Zion, Bryce Canyon, Arches and Yosemite National Parks. A journey through the most beautiful facets of the American West that will be remembered for a long time.

Trip Information

Itinerary

21 Days Los Angeles – Santa Monica – San Diego – Palm Springs – Grand Canyon – Moab – Zion – Las Vegas – Santa Barbara – Carmel – Big Sur – Yosemite National Park – Napa Valley – San Francisco

Your Trip Highlights

  • Palm Springs & the spectacular desert landscapes of California
  • Drive along the legendary Route 66
  • Experiencing the Grand Canyon
  • Monument Valley – Icon of the Wild West
  • Canyonlands National Park and Its Huge Gorges
  • Arches National Park with its spectacular sandstone arches
  • Bryce Canyon with the famous red hoodoos
  • Las Vegas – Glamour, Entertainment and Luxury Resorts
  • Big Sur - Between Coastal Sequence Trees and Pacific Views
  • Yosemite National Park with Glacier Point, Yosemite Falls & El Capitan
  • Napa Valley – California’s most famous wine region

Welcome to California! After arriving in Los Angeles, take your rental car and drive to the popular coastal town of Santa Monica. Just a few steps from the famous Santa Monica Pier, the elegant Regent Santa Monica is located right on the Pacific Ocean. Enjoy the relaxed atmosphere of the Californian coast, listen to the sound of the waves and let the first impressions of your journey take effect. The perfect atmosphere for an unforgettable adventure through the American Southwest. Overnight in Santa Monica.

Today you will discover Santa Monica in a special way. During a guided bicycle tour, follow the scenic coastal paths along the beaches of Los Angeles. Passing palm trees, surfers and the impressive Pacific coast, you will experience the Californian lifestyle up close. On the way, there are numerous opportunities for photo stops and fascinating views of the ocean. Afterwards, the afternoon is at your disposal. Enjoy relaxing hours on the beach, stroll through the shopping streets of Santa Monica or watch the sunset over the Pacific Ocean. Overnight in Santa Monica.

Along the coast of California, you will travel to San Diego today. Your destination is the exclusive Rancho Valencia, nestled in the rolling hills north of the city. The luxurious complex impresses with extensive gardens, Mediterranean ambience and an exceptionally relaxed atmosphere. Enjoy the evening amidst lush vegetation and experience the warm hospitality of Southern California. Overnight in San Diego.

Today is all about recovery. Take advantage of the numerous amenities of your resort and enjoy Californian serenity in its most beautiful form. Relax by the pool, treat yourself to a soothing spa treatment or explore the complex’s vast gardens and olive groves. Those who want to stay active will find the best opportunities on the excellent tennis facilities. Overnight in San Diego.

Today, the journey takes you towards Palm Springs. The landscape is constantly changing as the coast gives way to the impressive desert panoramas of Southern California. Set against the backdrop of the San Jacinto Mountains, move into your room at the luxurious Ritz-Carlton Rancho Mirage. Situated high above the valley, the resort offers spectacular views of the surrounding desert landscape. Enjoy the evening in a stylish atmosphere and experience the special magic of the Californian desert. Overnight in Palm Springs.

An impressive ride through the vast expanses of the American Southwest awaits you today. Passing historic locations along the legendary Route 66, the route passes through fascinating desert landscapes to the Grand Canyon. At the southern edge of the national park you move into your hotel and are already in the immediate vicinity of one of the greatest natural wonders on earth. Enjoy huge views and unforgettable nature experiences. Overnight at the Grand Canyon.

Together with a private nature guide, you will discover the Grand Canyon today. From spectacular viewpoints, the view opens up to the vast layers of rock formed over millions of years. Your guide will provide exciting insights into the geology, wildlife and plant life of this unique landscape. After a picnic in an extraordinary setting, a hike below the edge of the canyon leads to completely new perspectives on this tremendous natural wonder. Afterwards, there is time for your own discoveries along the South Rim. Overnight at the Grand Canyon.

Today you will drive through some of the most famous landscapes of the American Southwest. A special highlight is the visit to Monument Valley with its huge red sandstone formations, which are known from countless Western films. The sacred landscape of the Navajo impresses with its vastness, bright colors and deep cultural significance. Afterwards, reach Moab, the gateway to Utah’s spectacular national parks. Overnight in Moab.

Together with a private nature guide, you will discover two of Utah’s most impressive national parks. Canyonlands National Park offers massive canyons, deep gorges and magnificent views of the Colorado River landscape. From the famous plateau “Iceland in the Sky” opens a panorama that is one of the most impressive in the American West. The journey then takes you to Arches National Park, where wind and weather have created a fascinating world of sandstone over millions of years. Huge rock arches, bizarre rock formations and red glowing cliffs characterize the landscape. While exploring, enjoy a picnic in the midst of this unique natural setting before returning to Moab in the afternoon. Overnight in Moab.

Today you leave Moab and drive across the fascinating landscapes of Utah. Along the way, another highlight of the American Southwest awaits you: Bryce Canyon National Park. The vast natural amphitheaters with their thousands of red, orange and golden rock towers are among the most extraordinary landscapes in North America. During a walk along the canyon edge, new perspectives on the fairy-tale backdrop always open up. Then continue your journey to Zion National Park, where you will stay at the idyllic Cliffrose Lodge on the Virgin River. Surrounded by imposing rock walls, you can enjoy the special atmosphere of this natural paradise. Overnight at Zion National Park.

Together with your private nature guide, explore the impressive landscape of Zion National Park today. On scenic hiking trails you will discover huge sandstone formations in red, cream and pink shades that rise spectacularly above the valley. Along the way, you will learn about the geological formation of this unique landscape, the animal and plant world as well as the exciting history of the region. The hike is individually adapted to your wishes and your pace. Upon your return, there will be plenty of time to enjoy the tranquility of your hotel or explore the impressive surroundings on your own. Overnight at Zion National Park.

After the nature experiences of the past few days, you can reach Las Vegas today. The dazzling metropolis forms an exciting contrast to the national parks of the southwest. Your home for the night is the luxurious Wynn Las Vegas, one of the city’s most prestigious resorts. Take advantage of the evening for a stroll along the famous Las Vegas Strip, enjoy the excellent gastronomy or experience the unique flair of this extraordinary desert city. Las Vegas delights with spectacular shows, glamorous hotels and an atmosphere that is unparalleled worldwide. Overnight in Las Vegas.

Today you leave Nevada and return to the California coast. Your destination is Santa Barbara, a charming coastal town with Mediterranean flair, elegant architecture and dreamlike beaches. At the exclusive Rosewood Miramar Beach, located directly on the Pacific Ocean, you can enjoy the highest comfort in a stylish atmosphere. Stroll along the beach, enjoy the view of the sea or end the day with a relaxing dinner. Santa Barbara is one of the most beautiful coastal resorts in California and enchants with its relaxed elegance. Overnight in Santa Barbara.

Today, follow the famous California coast towards the north. Your destination is the idyllic coastal town of Carmel-by-the-Sea, which delights with its galleries, boutiques and white sand beaches. Amidst rolling hills and olive groves, the elegant Bernardus Lodge awaits you. The luxurious retreat is located in the heart of the wine country and offers the perfect setting for relaxing hours. Enjoy the tranquility of the surroundings and end the day in a stylish atmosphere. Overnight in Carmel.

Today, together with a private nature guide, you will explore the spectacular landscape of Big Sur. Hiking trails lead through ancient coastal sequoia forests, past waterfalls, small streams and deeply cut gorges. During the hike, you will learn interesting things about the region’s unique ecosystems and fascinating wildlife. The scent of sage and laurel accompanies you through one of the most beautiful coastal landscapes in North America. Upon your return to the lodge, the rest of the day will be free. Overnight in Carmel.

Today you will leave the Pacific coast and head inland to the famous Yosemite National Park. The ride will take you through varied landscapes before reaching your stylish Firefall Ranch retreat. Nestled in nature, the lodge offers the ideal starting point for the discoveries of the coming days. Enjoy the fresh mountain air and get attuned to the impressive natural wonders of Yosemite National Park. Overnight in Yosemite National Park.

Together with your private nature guide you will discover the highlights of Yosemite National Park today. Among the most famous attractions are the mighty Yosemite Falls and the legendary El Capitan, whose massive granite wall attracts climbers from all over the world every year. After a picnic in the middle of nature, head to the famous Glacier Point. From here you can enjoy one of the most spectacular views of Yosemite Valley. Walks and explorations are individually adapted to your wishes and combine nature experience with relaxed enjoyment. Overnight in Yosemite National Park.

Today, say goodbye to the massive granite rocks and waterfalls of Yosemite National Park and travel back to Napa Valley. The renowned wine region impresses with gentle hills, well-kept vineyards and Mediterranean flair. Their stylish resort Solage is located in the heart of the region and offers the best conditions for relaxing hours. Enjoy the special atmosphere of Californian wine country and end the day in a quiet environment. Overnight in the Napa Valley.

Together with an experienced guide you will visit three renowned wineries, which are accessible only by prior notice. In a personal atmosphere, get to know winemakers and taste some of California’s finest wines. At lunchtime, enjoy a multi-course gourmet lunch at one of Napa Valley’s best restaurants. Fresh local ingredients and creative cuisine make this day a culinary highlight of your trip. Overnight in the Napa Valley.

At the end of your trip, you will travel to San Francisco. The city delights with its unique location, historic quarters and world-famous landmarks. The Four Seasons Embarcadero is an elegant luxury hotel with spectacular views over the skyline and San Francisco Bay. Take advantage of the last evening of your trip for a walk along the waterfront or enjoy the special atmosphere of this fascinating metropolis. Overnight in San Francisco.

Today, your journey through some of the most beautiful landscapes of the American Southwest ends. After breakfast, drive to the San Francisco airport, return your rental car and start your journey home or on. With countless impressions, spectacular nature experiences and enjoyable moments in the luggage, this trip will remain in memory for a long time.

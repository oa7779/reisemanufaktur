Ecuador Travel - Chocolate and Volcanoes

Ecuador travel - chocolate and volcanoes. With Latin America expert América Special Tours you will experience your very individual Ecuador trip. Now ask me.

When you travel Ecuador, you can experience an incredible scenic diversity in a small space. The Andes, the tropical coasts, the Amazon basin and the paradisiacal Galapagos Islands characterize the country. This trip will give you an intense insight into the natural treasures: You will visit the Cotopaxi volcano and a cocoa plantation in the Amazon rainforest. The fact that the land of cocoa has some chocolatey treats for you to enjoy and relax, goes without saying.

1st day Arrival in the idyllic Otavalo

Welcome to Ecuador! At the airport, you will be warmly welcomed by your private guide and chauffeur and taken directly to Otavalo. On the trip you get a first impression of the country and people and learn exciting information and anecdotes first hand. The route first leads over winding slopes, through rain-poor bush and cactus landscape to the wide, oasis-like valley of Guayllabamba. With a bit of luck, you can take a look at the snow-capped peak of the Cayambe volcano. On the way through this beautiful landscape you can marvel at Lake San Pablo with the colossal rock massif of Taita Imbabura (Father Imbabura) in the background, which is an excellent photo motif! Overnight in Otavalo.

2nd day Otavalo Indian town

After an extensive breakfast, immerse yourself in the world of crafts today. Of course, a visit to the world-famous Indian Market of Otavalo should not be missed. Enjoy the colorful market activity at Plaza de los Ponchos and marvel at the weaving art of the Otavalo Indians: From hand-woven carpets, wall hangings with various motifs, knitting sweaters, colorful belts, hammocks and mountains of wool, and fabric products everything is there. Before ending the day in peace at your hotel, visit two regional craft centers. You see Peguche, which is famous for its textiles. You will have the opportunity to look over the shoulders of local weavers at their work and learn about the traditional art of weaving. Afterwards, you will be introduced to an indigenous family from Otavalo who decided more than ten years ago to actively promote the Kichwa culture and bring it closer to visitors. Together with the hearty Claudia and her mother, you will immerse yourself in the world of Kichwa cuisine while preparing and enjoying delicious local dishes. Overnight in Otavalo.

Day 3 The Craterlagune Cuicocha

An excursion today takes you to Lake Cuicocha, a crater lake at the foot of the Cotacachi volcano in the Andes of Ecuador. With a diameter of three kilometers, it is one of the largest and most spectacular crater lakes in the country. Four lava domes jut out of Lake Cuicocha, forming two forested islands – but people are not allowed to enter them. The lush vegetation on the islands offers a protected habitat for a wide variety of wildlife. On an easy hike, you will have time to enjoy the unique nature on the lakeshore. Afterwards, the small town of Cotacachi invites you to stroll through the narrow streets and shops with high-quality leather goods. On your journey to Quito, stop at the “Quitsato” equator monument, the only solar equator monument built directly on top of the equator. The main attraction is an impressive sundial with a diameter of 54 meters, which lies precisely on the equator line. Here you will learn about the course of the sun, the origin of the seasons and the fascinating history of the calendar – a unique place that combines science, history and culture. Overnight in Quito.

Day 4 History and Art Quitos

Start your tour at the pre-Columbian Museum Casa del Alabado, which aims to showcase the richness of culture before the arrival of Europeans. Afterwards you will visit a jewel of the South American Baroque: the Jesuit church “La Compañía”. The detailed carved and gold-covered altars, the finely crafted sculptures and paintings of the art school of Quito will convince you of the mastery of Ecuadorian craft artists. Furthermore, you have the opportunity to visit the church of San Francisco. Continue on the magnificent road in the district of La Ronda, which is experiencing a revitalization. You will visit artists who, as part of the revitalization project, will bring you closer to their craft products – candles or sweets, for example. What would an authentic tour of Quito be without a touch of chocolate? Stop by a chocolatier and immerse yourself in the history of cocoa and the production of high-quality chocolate. Of course, you can also taste one of the finest chocolates in the world. Overnight in Quito.

Day 5 at Cotopaxi Volcano

Today, follow in the footsteps of Alexander von Humboldt and learn about the road of volcanoes he discovered in 1802. No wonder the Spanish conquerors settled here. You will visit Cotopaxi National Park, an ecological sanctuary that extends around the volcano Cotopaxi, which rises nearly 6000 meters in height. The evergreen landscape of the Ecuadorian highlands is a major attraction of the country. In the national park you will learn more about the flora and fauna of the Andean moorland. In the Limpiopungo Lagoon you will have the opportunity to observe a wide variety of birds and – with a bit of luck – wild horses. On clear days, you can admire the fantastic silhouette of the volcano in the background. If you don’t have enough of the breathtaking surroundings yet, you can take a horseback ride from your hacienda in the afternoon. Visit Andean towns and villages on old inkapfaden – and experience the landscape in a special way. For example, a route leads along the volcano Cotopaxi through a pine forest and to a plain from which you can let the view wander over the adjacent mountains. Overnight at Cotopaxi National Park.

Day 6 Quilotoa - Hiking at the Crater Rim

Quilotoa Crater Lake is ideal for active nature lovers. You can hike down to the lake from the volcano and get taken up by donkey for a five-hour hike along the crater rim. Quilotoa Lagoon is located 66 kilometers south of the city of Latacunga and is part of the Ilinizas Reserve. The water glows emerald green – and can shimmer in a blue or even yellow depending on the season. Continue south and enjoy the magnificent scenery surrounded by some of the most beautiful and tallest mountains and volcanoes of the Ecuadorian highlands until you reach the small town of Baños, located at 1,800 m altitude. Baños, also called “The Gate to the Oriente”, is only 60 km from the rainforest and spoils its visitors by its mild spring climate, where you will certainly feel comfortable in the small street cafes, restaurants and while strolling through the numerous souvenir shops. Overnight in Baños.

7th Day Noisy Waterfalls in Amazon Proximity

East of Baños, the road goes downhill and offers a magnificent view over the Amazon basin. Along the road you can marvel at beautiful waterfalls. You will take a hike to the famous waterfall “Pailon del Diablo” before continuing towards the jungle. As the temperature rises, you can find ferns and orchids on the trees. Overnight in Misahualli.

Day 8 Visit to the Kichwa

Today you get to know the Kichwa community in the Amazon. You will spend a whole day with the indigenous people of Ecuador, who are very proud of their roots and traditions. Immerse yourself in their traditional way of life and culture and learn about their customs, their own language and ritual dances. Overnight in Misahualli.

Day 9 The Cradle of Chocolate

Today you will get to know Ecuador as a land of the cocoa tree. After getting a glimpse into the processing of chocolate in Quito, her way leads you today to the municipality of Santa Rita near Archidona. Learn more about the Pacari Agro Project here. The chocolate manufacturer Pacari buys the cocoa at Fairtrade conditions and provides the technology to improve the cultivation of the beans and the variety of varieties. They learn on site how the harvest proceeds and finally cocoa is produced. Your self-produced chocolate paste can of course also be tried. Then travel to Papallacta and reach the Termas de Papallacta Resort and Spa, which also has volcanic thermal springs to offer. The area is known for its harsh, bizarre-looking Páramo landscape. The baths of Papallacta are among the most beautiful and cleanest in the whole country. If you are a real chocolate lover, you must not miss a relaxing application with chocolate (optional). Overnight in Papallacta.

Day 10 Back to Quito

After breakfast, you will return to Quito. After ten unforgettable days, it is time to say goodbye to a small paradise. Your chauffeur will take you to the airport, and with many memories you will head home again.

Ecuador Nature Journey — Cloud Forest & the Galápagos

Nature Tour Ecuador and Galapagos with América Special Tours. Inquire here directly and experience the beauty of Ecuador on your individual journey for yourself.

12 days

Small hummingbirds in rugged mountains, colorful orchids in the mystical cloud forest and the unique biodiversity of the Galapagos Islands: this journey will take you to the natural highlights of Ecuador. You get to know the artists of Quito and take exciting hikes far away from civilization and a ride on the Sky Bike through the lush jungle.

Trip Information

Itinerary

12 Days Quito – Mashpi Lodge – Quito – Galapagos Islands – Guayaquil

Welcome to Ecuador! At the airport, you will be warmly welcomed by your private guide and chauffeur. They are located in a special geographical location. On the one hand, you have landed today in the highest capital in the world – at 2,850 meters – and on the other hand, you can stand at the center-of-the-world monument on both hemispheres of the earth at the same time. The city is beautifully surrounded by snow-capped volcanoes – and has a turbulent and powerful history behind it: in the 13th century Quito was the flourishing capital of the Kingdom of Quitus and later the northern capital of the Inca Empire. The beautiful colonial-style temples, monasteries and villas you pass by date back to the 16th and 17th centuries. UNESCO has declared the old town as the first city ever to be a World Heritage Site. Your hotel “Casa Gangotena” is located in the middle of this splendor, near the Plaza San Francisco, where wealthy families settled – including the Gangotena family. But the city is also extremely creative in the present: In modern galleries and boutiques you will find contemporary painting, sculptures and craftsmanship. Overnight in Quito.

Together with your guide, you will explore the old town of Quito today. During your walk, you will get a good impression of people’s everyday lives. You can learn more about the typical food of the city and the country during a market visit. Continue on the magnificent road in the district of La Ronda. Here you can see the houses with their typical balconies full of geraniums. Let yourself be carried away into the time after the Spanish conquest. You will visit artists who will bring their craft products to you as part of the revitalization project. What would an authentic tour of Quito be without a touch of chocolate? You can stop by a chocolatier and try one of the finest chocolates in the world. Through the old town we go to Plaza Grande. There you will see a jewel of the South American Baroque: the Jesuit church La Compañía. The detailed carved and gold-covered altars, the finely carved sculptures and paintings of the art school of Quito will convince you of the mastery of Ecuadorian craft artists. Of course, you will also visit the Church of San Francisco. The afternoon is at your disposal. Overnight in Quito.

Today your nature adventure begins in Ecuador. Mashpi Lodge is located three hours from Quito in an evergreen flank of the Andes. As you overcome a height difference of 1800 meters, you will experience an incredibly varied landscape. Through the Pomasqui valley, you can reach in lush rainforest and finally to the lodge. All of this can be admired from a vantage point that you head to first: the Pululahua Crater. Another highlight on your way is the ruins of Tulipe, which date back to the pre-Inca civilization of the Yumbo and are set in a magical environment. Here you can fortify yourself with a typical Ecuadorian snack with fresh coffee. You can learn more about the biodiversity and the cloud forest in the lodge, where you will meet your nature guide before lunch. In the afternoon, an eventful hike along the Napa path awaits you. Meanwhile, your naturalist guide will introduce you to the enormous importance of this area for nature conservation. If you want to deepen your knowledge of the fascinating Ecuadorian cloud forest, you will have the opportunity to participate in a reading at Mashpi Lodge in the evening. Overnight in the Mashpi Reserve.

Before breakfast, you will wander from the lodge to particularly old trees in the forest. Already in the early hours of the morning, the forest comes to life, and the variety of bird voices fills the air. Let your experienced naturalist guide introduce you to the secrets of this unique ecosystem. The destination of the hike is the Life Centre. On the wooden terrace of the “Life Center” you will enjoy a magnificent panoramic view and observe colorful birds and curious mammals who arrive at feeding time. After breakfast you will learn more about the different ecosystems. Among other things, you will see how a colorful butterfly develops from a caterpillar. After lunch back at the lodge, explore the forest on a hike that requires you to climb a slope – artificially created steps make the path easier. Your destination is a large waterfall. Your excursion lasts a total of two hours. When the sun goes down, it gives the impression that the forest is transforming. The smells and sounds seem to get more intense, frogs croak and insects match a polyphonic buzz. Visually, you will be offered something despite the darkness, when fireflies swirl through the air and bioluminiscent mushrooms wrap the soil in soft light. Upon your return to the lodge, you will have dinner. Overnight in the Mashpi Reserve.

Decide with your guide how to spend today. Your lodge provides you with various activities to choose from. Overnight in the Mashpi Reserve.

Use the last morning in the cloud forest for bird watching again: you walk to the animals' feeding stations and can watch them at breakfast. After strengthening with your own breakfast, explore the Copa waterfall. Bromelia and orchids line your path, which will take you through the wild for two hours. Back at the lodge, take your lunch before being taken back to Quito. Overnight in Quito.

After the enchanting nature of the cloud forest, you will get to know the longing destination of many travelers in the following days. About 1000 kilometers west of the Ecuadorian border lies a small paradise with a unique flora and fauna: the Galapagos Islands – no wonder that they have been named UNESCO World Heritage and are under strict nature conservation. The Spaniards, who discovered them in the 15th century, gave them the name “enchanted islands” because of their remote location. It was not until the 19th century that they were renamed and today bear the name of the giant tortoises native to them. Your journey begins on the most famous island of the archipelago – Santa Cruz. A flight from Quito will first take you to the neighboring island of Baltra, from where you will take the ferry to Santa Cruz. At your lodge, a light lunch is already waiting for you. The rest of the day is at your disposal. So you have time to relax and familiarize yourself with the amenities of the lodge. You can swim in the Infinity Pool, work out at the gym or enjoy massage, sauna or hot tub at the Lodge Spa. Overnight on Santa Cruz.

Today, a yacht trip will take you to Seymor Norte. The profile flat island, like all other Galapagos Islands, was also formed by volcanic activity. Their coastline is formed by cliffs that are only a few meters high, but are nevertheless extremely popular with gulls and frigate birds with their red dazzling throat bags. On the island, Palo-Santo trees without a leaf dress are waiting for rain that makes them bloom. The island, uninhabited by humans, is spraying with life! Sea lions enjoy a dip in the sun and blue-footed boobies might just be busy nesting when you come to visit. You can see the island life up close on your hike as you observe the largest breeding colony of frigate birds of all the Galapagos Islands. Not only is the color scheme of the acrobats impressive, their wingspan of two meters is also remarkable. After your visit, you will sail to the Itabaca Canal, from where you will return by car to the hotel. Overnight on Santa Cruz.

On Santa Cruz you can encounter the famous Galapagos giant tortoises – here is the second largest population of turtles in the islands. Every year, the turtles that inhabit the island take a natural route during the dry season from June to September to get to wetter areas (and then in the rainy season to return). During the rest of the year, they prefer the reddish ponds or muddy shores for their stay. In the waters you can also admire a variety of finches, woodpeckers and snacks. A morning visit is worthwhile! During your visit to the research station “Charles Darwin” you will learn more about the breeding program for the giant turtles. Lunch overlooks the island before visiting a small farm where cocoa beans, coffee and sugar cane are grown, harvested and processed. While learning about sustainable management, you can also try the products. Overnight on Santa Cruz.

The island of Santa Fe – in the center of the island world – is one of the particularly beautiful bays of the archipelago. The turquoise lagoon is protected by small offshore islets. Even today you can see on Santa Fe where the lava once stepped up and cooled. To get you there, take your yacht to Puerto Ayora – just a few minutes from your hotel. On the island you will see a sea lion colony. The small island also has a lot to offer in terms of landscape: A cactus forest and Palo-Santo trees are waiting for your visit. His wood is said to have healing powers, he should be able to convert negative energies into positive energies. The island has a very special inhabitant who lives only here: the Santa Fe-Drusenkopf. The animals can measure up to 1.10 meters and recall with their comb of mythical creatures from the fairy tale book: Dragons. During your hike, you can also see Galapagos snakes crossing your path – don’t worry, the animals belonging to the Natter family are peaceful. After the active part of the day, take the opportunity to swim in the bay or see the tropical underwater world on a snorkeling trip. Overnight on Santa Cruz.

Unfortunately, you have to leave this little paradise again today. On the way back to Baltra, stop at the twin pit craters – geological depressions created by slow sinking. Over time, they have discovered mosses, orchids and ferns for themselves. From Baltra, take your flight to the port metropolis of Guayaquil. Overnight in Guayaquil.

Today your journey through Ecuador ends. Many more impressions you take your flight home. Your chauffeur will take you to the airport.

Peru Individual travel - Peru's highlights

Individual tour to the highlights of Peru with América Special Tours. Let yourself be personally advised by the Latin American specialist and experience Peru.

Machu Picchu is not without reason Peru’s most famous attraction – but the country has much more to offer. On this trip, you will experience the Andean state from a wide variety of perspectives: you will overlook the mighty Colca Canyon, get to know the indigenous people of Lake Titicaca, ride on a unique train through the Andes and stroll through Arequipa's pretty alleys.

1st day Arrival in Lima

Your journey through Peru begins in the capital Lima, whose old town – legacy of the colonial era – has been a World Heritage Site since 1991. The “City of Kings” carries a dark legacy as the center of the Spanish Inquisition. Due to the geographical conditions, impressive cloud formations regularly show up in the sky of the desert city on the sea. At the airport, you will be expected by your German-speaking guide when you arrive and taken to your hotel in the city. On the way, you will learn first interesting anecdotes about Peru and its capital. Depending on the arrival time, you can take afternoon tea at your hotel to arrive relaxed. Or you can use the time after your arrival for small explorations in Lima on your own before continuing your journey tomorrow. Don’t miss out on eating well: Lima has become a mecca for gourmets. Overnight in Lima.

2nd day onwards to Arequipa

Today, your journey will take you further to the south of the country. Located here, overwhelmed by high volcanoes, Arequipa, the capital of the region of the same name. Prehistoric tribes as well as the Inca had settled there before the region was conquered by the conquistadors in the 16th century. Around the city, which is characterised by colonial architecture, there is a wild stretch of land with snow-capped volcanoes, hot springs, gorges and deserts. Overnight in Arequipa.

3rd Day World Heritage Site of Arequipa

Today you will dedicate yourself to the “White City” Arequipa. The visit takes you to the imposing cathedral and the church of Compania y Claustros. The most important religious building of the colonial period is the Monastery of Santa Catalina. Many rich families once sent one of their daughters to the monastery. Due to its vastness, the monastery is also called a “city within a city”. After 400 years of life behind closed doors, they were only opened to visitors in 1970. In the San Lázaro district, the oldest area of the city, you can expect small houses along steep, narrow streets. Yanahuara impresses especially with its buildings of bright volcanic rocks, Cayma is a pretty, lively district, which shows itself almost village-like in some corners. After you have surely become hungry from your explorations, afterwards it is time to get to know Peru from its culinary side. With your guide, you will first walk through the San Camial Market where you can taste one of the exotic fruits and absorb the scent of foreign herbs and spices. Your guide will be happy to explain more about their use in regional cuisine. However, it is best to experience them in a very practical way by stopping for dinner in one of the many “picanterías”. Overnight in Arequipa.

Day 4 Continue to the Colca Valley

Rugged rock slopes, extinct volcanoes, unique views: Around 100 kilometers north of Arequipa lies the second largest canyon in the world: the Colca Valley. It extends over a length of 100 kilometers and drops 3,400 meters at the lowest point. On the way to this unique natural spectacle, you will make a stopover in three villages in the middle of the Aguada Blanca National Park. Here you will not only learn more about the village life of the region, but with a bit of luck you can also observe some animals that are native to the Andes – such as vicuñas or viscachas, a rodent similar to the chinchilla. Via Chuccura and Patapampa you will reach a viewpoint from where an impressive panorama of the canyon spreads beneath you. From here it is not far to Chivay, where you will have lunch at an altitude of 3600 meters in a restaurant. In Chivay you will also take a short detour to a local market. If you wish, you can still visit some villages in the area on the way to your hotel. In the evening you can review the impressions of the day with a relaxed bath in the warm water of the hotel's thermal springs. Overnight in Colca.

5th day King of the skies

After an early breakfast, visit Cruz del Condor (Condor Cross) and Tapay. Cruz del Condor is located at the lowest point of the canyon. The view from there is fantastic and with a bit of luck you can see the condors floating through the canyon in the early morning. Via the small villages of Pinchollo, Maca, Antahuilque and Yanque you drive back to Chivay and then leave for Puno. Overnight in Puno.

Day 6 - The highest lake in the world

With an area of over 8,000 square meters, Titicaca is South America’s second largest lake and the tallest navigable lake in the world. On a trip along Lake Titicaca, you will cross the border to Bolivia to the enchanting city of Copacabana, one of the most spiritual cities in the region. Here, immerse yourself in the local culture while admiring the famous basilica with the Black Virgin shrine. Stroll across the lively main square and discover the treasures of the small local market. Continue on board a private motorboat in the direction of Sonneninsel. In the southern part of the island, explore Pilcocaina, arguably the most spectacular ruin complex on the island. At the end of this unforgettable morning, enjoy a traditional Apthapi lunch in a cozy restaurant. Dinner at your Ecolodge is included for you today. Overnight on the Sun Island.

Note: The boat trip takes place together with other travelers and in English.

7The Mystical North of the Sun Island

Aboard a private motorboat, glide gently across the shimmering blue of Lake Titicaca into the northern part of the island where the mysterious Chinkana ruins await you. This fascinating site, known as "La Chinkana" or "the labyrinth", reveals an ancient structure surrounded by mystery and legend to this day. A network of small stone walls, winding corridors and hidden “rooms” captivates every visitor and invites them to experience the spiritual atmosphere on a leisurely walk. At the foot of Chinkana lies the sacred rock, a place of great importance in Andean mythology. It is said that the first Inca princes were created here by the sun god. The special energy that surrounds this place can be felt as you wander through the ruins and experience the connection to the past intensely. Dinner at the Ecolodge is included for you today. Overnight on the Sun Island.

Day 8 Moon Island & Return to Puno

Your journey takes you further into a mystical world full of legends and history. Once again, board your private motorboat and head to nearby Moon Island, a mysterious place that often stands in the shadow of the famous Sun Island but is just as fascinating. Here you will find the Temple of the Sun Virgins, "Iñak Uyu" - a remarkably well-preserved testimony of the past. Another highlight awaits you at the fountain of eternal youth. Here, fresh water from a natural source flows through three ornately designed stone canals that represent the three energy levels in the Andean Cosmovision. An elaborately constructed Inca staircase will take you to this magical place. Afterwards, return across the border to Peru and spend the evening again in Puno, the charming city on the shores of Lake Titicaca.

Note: The boat trip takes place together with other travelers and in English.

9th day panoramic ride with the Titicaca Train

Your journey will take you further north today. Due to the rough terrain – with roads that are only a few inches away from deep slopes – a journey through the Andes is often more adventure than a walk. On the other hand, you can move around completely relaxed with the Titicaca Train, which takes you comfortably through the impressive landscape of the High Andes. Imposing peaks and deep gorges will pass you as you take a 3-course lunch in the dining car. Arriving in Cusco, you will be welcomed and taken to the hotel.

Note: The train journey takes place without a guide. The train staff speaks English. The train journey takes place only on Mondays, Thursdays and Saturdays.

10th day Visit to the ruins of Cusco

Cusco and the region of the same name in the Andean highlands (3,400 meters altitude) was once the dominion of the Inca Empire. Today you will visit the Sacsayhuaman Fortress. From here you have a unique panoramic view over the city. Then continue to Koricancha, the ancient Inca Sun Temple. Here you have enough time to explore the building, whose walls are said to have been encased in gold and silver. The Catholic monastery of Santo Domingo was built on the same site and some Inca walls of the temple were used for the monastery. Then visit the Cathedral, built on the ruins of the Inca Wiracocha Palace and known for its collection of colonial works of art from the Cusqueña School. A visit to the local market of San Pedro offers you the opportunity to get to know the variety of Peruvian fruits, vegetables and other products. If you feel like exploring the city for the first time, stroll a little through the narrow alleys after your return. By the way, the city is an insider tip for vegetarians and vegans: Overnight in Cusco.

11th day of legendary Machu Picchu

Machu Picchu is rightly the highlight of most Peru trips. Reaching the sanctuary in the middle of the Andes takes some time: you first go to Poroy train station, where you board the panoramic train to Aguas Calientes. Upon arrival, a shuttle bus will take you up the Serpentine Road to the ruins. Machu Picchu is said to have been built in the 15th century under the reign of the Inca ruler Pachacútec Yupanqui. The ruins are surrounded by an aura of mystery: the city was “lost” for many years before it was rediscovered in the early 20th century. During a two and a half hour guided tour, your guide will explain more about the history and significance of this place. After the visit, your guide will take you back to Cusco. Overnight in Cusco.

12th day flight home from Lima

After almost two weeks in Peru, your fascinating journey through the Andean state is coming to an end today. Your guide will take you to Cusco Airport for your flight home via Lima. Even if you leave Peru behind today: The memories of the country will always be with you.

Optional Extension Paracas: Every goodbye is difficult. Hesitate a bit and treat yourself to a few more days on the Pacific. More specifically, in Paracas. The coastal town just under four hours from Lima is a popular destination for the numerous inhabitants of Lima to enjoy the mostly sunny weather in this area. From here you can make numerous excursions, for example to the Islas Ballestas, an offshore island archipelago where sea lions, penguins and cormorants roam. It is not for nothing that the islands are also called “Little Galápagos”. Would you like it to be spectacular? Then fly on a light plane over the mysterious Nazca Lines.

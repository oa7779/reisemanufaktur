US Road Trip — Golden California

USA Car Rental: Experience the diversity of California with unparalleled landscapes and glittering metropolises on this personalized journey from América Special Tours!

16 days

Your trip idea

A sample itinerary—tailored to you

  • Home
  • USA

Sunny beaches, big surfer waves, culture and entertainment in the glamorous cities, an exquisite culinary scene, unparalleled landscapes and breathtaking views: California is the complete package. Enjoy a unique road trip through golden California. You start your journey in the metropolis of Los Angeles and shortly afterwards drive through the eternal expanses of the Californian desert into the dazzling entertainment metropolis of Las Vegas. In Death Valley and Mammoth Lakes, you will experience an unparalleled natural diversity. You will cross the stunning Yosemite National Park and visit California’s most famous wine area, the Napa Valley. Enjoy a gourmet menu as you ride through the vineyards on a historic panoramic train. Experience the relaxed and multicultural atmosphere of San Francisco and take a break on the California coast at the end of your trip. In this varied journey, the golden federal state pays tribute to its name.

Trip Information

Itinerary

16 Days Los Angeles - Las Vegas - Death Valley - Mammoth Lakes - Yosemite Valley - Napa Valley - San Francisco - Big Sur - Santa Barbara - Los Angeles

Welcome to the metropolis of Los Angeles. Arriving at the airport, you will receive your rental car and make your own way to your hotel. Los Angeles is a city with a fascinating history and rich cultural heritage. The cultural attractions of the city are unmatched and so you are guaranteed not to get bored. Be it the Space Shuttle Endeavour, Walt Disney Concert Hall, Getty Center or art galleries and urban art. With legendary studios, a vibrant nightlife and prestigious theatre productions, you’ll experience the true entertainment capital of the world. Arrive in peace and enjoy the first evening in the USA at your free disposal. Overnight in Los Angeles, Santa Monica.

Los Angeles is a city with many facets – also in terms of culinary diversity. Start this morning on a street food tour of one of the city’s landmarks, the Original Farmers Market at 3rd Street, Mid Town Los Angeles. Stroll along the bustling 3rd Street and stop at various market stalls to taste the delicacies on offer. In the afternoon, a very special experience awaits you: An approx. 15-minute helicopter flight over Hollywood. You’ll see Universial Studios, the Walk of Fame, the Griffith Observatory and the world-famous Hollywood Sign from a bird’s eye view. This phenomenal view of the city will surely remain in your memory for a long time. Overnight in Los Angeles, Santa Monica.

Your next destination is Las Vegas. You go on Highway 15 and always head northeast. Marvel at this surreal desert landscape and make a stop over and over again to get your legs up a bit and become aware of the vastness and solitude of the desert. In the afternoon you will finally reach the dazzling city of Las Vegas. Overnight in Las Vegas.

Enjoy the many entertainment options in Las Vegas during the day at your own pace. Highly recommended is a ride with the Ferris wheel directly on the Strip. The views of the city are unique. In the evening you will visit the famous show Le Reve directly in your hotel. The newly interpreted and shining with new music, costumes and choreography show takes you into an exciting fantasy world. Marvel at the exciting performance of the numerous acrobats and synchronized swimmers on the unique, rounded water stage. Rightly, this show was voted the best show in Las Vegas by the Nevada Concierge Association for 9 consecutive years. Overnight in Las Vegas.

As entertaining as the city may be, outside the city limits there is much more to discover. Thanks to its location in the rocky and rough Mojave Desert, Las Vegas is actually closer than you think to the outdoors. In search of fresh air and adventure, you will continue towards Death Valley, the lowest point in the Western Hemisphere. Situated nearly 90m below sea level, it offers great scenery and an interesting history. Enjoy views from Zabriskie Point east of Death Valley. Expand bizarre erosion landscapes consisting of rocks from Furnace Creek Lake, which dried up 5 million years ago. You should also visit Badwater Basin, the lowest point in North America. A surreal landscape with a huge salt plain. The basin is located 86m below sea level, where a temporary lake can form after heavy rains. Overnight in Death Valley.

Death Valley may be the hottest and driest place in North America, but that’s not to stop you from cruising through the beautiful Death Valley National Park. Despite its name, there is a great variety of life forms in Death Valley. Rare rainstorms bring vast fields of wildflowers to light, and the occasional oases are home to tiny fish and other wildlife. Your daily destination today is Mammoth Lakes. The city is a popular hiking and winter sports destination. So something is offered at every season. Unpack your walking sticks and go into nature. The possibilities for hiking are almost limitless. Your hotel also offers a variety of activities in nature, such as panoramic cable car rides, guided hikes, mountain biking, an adventure center, boat tours, fishing and a golf course. Overnight in Mammoth Lakes.

Today you will cross Yosemite National Park on the famous Tigoa Road. It was founded in 1890 and is known worldwide for its spectacular waterfalls, enchanting natural scenery and breathtaking views. Numerous hiking trails and theme trails wind through the park, waiting to be explored by you. The Yosemite road guide is available from the bookshop of the visiting center at the entrance to the park. In this guide you will find all the streets of the park listed as well as useful tips for rest breaks and photo stops. In the evening you will reach your lodge at the western entrance of the park. Overnight at the edge of Yosemite National Park.

Today you will enjoy an exciting day in Yosemite National Park. Marvel at the breathtaking landscape. The park is full of landmarks. Marvel at Half Dome, Yosemite’s nearly 2,700-meter-high trademark and El Capitan, the largest granite rock on Earth. Both locations attract droves of climbers. If climbing is not on your wish list, visit the magnificent Yosemite waterfalls, which are the highest in the continent at almost 740 meters. Optionally, you can book a guided tour with a Park Ranger on site. Let yourself be charmed by the nature of the park and its history. Overnight at the edge of Yosemite National Park.

After those impressive days in California and Arizona’s natural jewels, you’ll now experience the relaxing part of your round trip. You drive west, past the capital Sacramento to the famous wine region of the Napa Valley. Here you will find the perfect place to relax and enjoy the world-famous wines and exquisite cuisine. On your tour of the vineyards, learn about the history of the region surrounding wine growing and look over the shoulders of the winemakers in their daily work. Enjoy wine tastings, cask tastings and a guided tour with your wine expert. Stay on the verandah of your hotel overlooking the peaceful valley and enjoy a relaxed evening atmosphere – how else – with a glass of wine. Overnight in the Napa Valley.

Today you will explore the wonderful area and its numerous wineries in a very special way. Leave your car and take a four-hour break on board the Napa Valley Wine Train. Enjoy the ride on the 1915s-style vintage train on the historic railway line. At noon, you can expect true palate delights at a 3-course gourmet lunch. After the main course, visit the Grgich Hills Winery and get an exclusive tour of the property, wine tasting included, of course. After that, return to your places on the historic train and look forward to your dessert. In the afternoon, the tour ends in Napa. Let your day end with a walk through the nice place.

After relaxing days between vineyards, a city awaits you again today for a change - San Francisco, where you will spend the next two nights. Even the journey is impressive, because coming from the north you will have the opportunity to drive over the most famous landmark in the city - the Golden Gate Bridge. San Francisco is a city of contrasts - old and new, modern and relaxed, urban and pastoral. The city is constantly evolving. Visit iconic attractions such as the Golden Gate Bridge, Fisherman’s Wharf, Alcatraz Island and many fascinating neighborhoods that are as individual as they are dynamic. San Francisco is also one of the best culinary places in the world thanks to its diverse cultural influences and proximity to fresh ingredients. Overnight in San Francisco.

Today you can use it to explore the beautiful city on your own. Due to its checkerboard-like street layout, San Francisco is quite manageable. With a sense of direction, you will quickly find your way around. In addition, the center can be easily explored with the cable car train. The main districts such as the Financial District, Chinatown, Telegraph Hill, Nob Hill and Downtown with Union Square are centrally located. One quarter is more fascinating and individual than the other, you will be amazed. The city also has a lot to offer in culinary delights thanks to its diverse cultural influences. Overnight in San Francisco.

With your rental car, it goes a little further south for you today. On the way, you should always make small stops, such as in historic Monterey or in the lovely Carmel-by-the-Sea. Both places offer a great coastal location and are worth a visit. Imagine coastal cliffs and pristine beaches from which you can observe whales, sea otters and migratory birds. In addition to the epic backdrop, enjoy unforgettable aromas from the region’s award-winning gastronomy and wine scene. There are also countless recreational opportunities, including golf, shopping, kayaking and hiking. Don’t miss the 17-mile drive along one of the most beautiful coastlines, where beaches and stately estates take turns. Overnight in Big Sur.

Enjoy today relaxed in your hotel. The Ventana is an exclusive 5-star resort situated amid 160 hectares of forest on the rough Pacific coast. An almost magical place to switch off from everyday life and reconcile your body and mind. A feel-good oasis for all needs, whether you’re looking for romance and connection or more interested in solitary reflection. Explore the hills and forests around the hotel on a gentle hike and watch the sunset over the ocean. Whatever you are looking for, in this hotel you will find inspiration. Overnight in Big Sur.

Your journey is coming to an end and so you are slowly making your way back to Los Angeles. A nice drive on Highway 1 will first take you to Santa Barbara, where you will make a last overnight stop. In the enchanting city, take a walk on State Street or alternatively visit the historic Mission Santa Barbara and the Country Court House, which provides an interesting insight into the history and Spanish heritage of the city. Have a delicious dinner at one of the fresh seafood restaurants. Enjoy the beautiful sunset over the sea on a beach walk. Overnight in Santa Barbara.

Even the most beautiful trip comes to an end and so it is today to say goodbye to the impressive West of the USA. Even if your holiday is over, the precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories of your trip will accompany you forever. Look forward to letting your friends and family share your experiences in the distance at home. You drive to the Los Angeles airport and drop off your rental car there.

Costa Rica Nature Tour - The Jungle Adventure

Discover Costa Rica’s impressive flora and fauna on this personalized nature tour by Latin American specialist América Special Tours.

Itinerary & services

The small Costa Rica is one of the most biodiverse countries on earth. You will experience his impressive natural treasures up close on this trip – and very comfortably. You will spend the night in charming lodges, located in the middle of beautiful landscapes and form the cozy starting point for small adventures in nature. Take a romp on hikes through the rainforest, wildlife viewing, riding and rafting, and then relax in your comfortably equipped lodges and retreats. The stress of everyday life is far away here.

1st Day Welcome to Costa Rica

Your journey through Costa Rica starts at the airport of San José. Here you will already be welcomed at the passport control by a local partner who is at your side as a contact. Your private guide awaits you at the airport exit. Enjoy a welcome drink in your comfortable vehicle that will take you to Silencio Lodge in around 1.5 hours. Here you will experience the enjoyment of nature far away from all the hustle and bustle of civilization. You should not expect television or a swimming pool here – you will find a lot of peace and quiet for that. You will reside in one of only 16 luxuriously equipped suites with whirlpool, fireplace and private terrace. In the restaurant you can be pampered with delicious, healthy food. The spa area offers relaxing massages as well as yoga classes outdoors. Overnight at El Silencio Lodge.

2nd day Active and relaxed through the jungle

They are awakened this morning by the sounds of the surrounding nature, which usually happens relatively early. But morning hours have gold in their mouths! This is especially true for those guests who want to observe some of the more than 350 bird species that call Bajos del Toro their home before breakfast. Your guide will take you on a 1.5-hour hike after a leisurely breakfast. This begins in the in-house “Garden of the Senses”, in which you are tuned to nature with sounds, scents and tastes. From here, follow the Mystical Trail, the mystical path, through the cloud forest and reach your destination, the Melodía Waterfall, so named because of the birdsongs that fill the air here. On the way back, your senses are once again challenged in search of traces of the forest dwellers. With a bit of luck, you can spot nose and raccoons as well as Tepezquintes – a kind of giant sea pig. In the afternoon, relax from your hike with a 90-minute relaxing stone massage at the spa. Overnight at El Silencio Lodge.

Day 3 At the foot of the volcano

Costa Rica is part of the volcanic mountain range along the Pacific coast that runs through Central America. Here lies the next destination of your journey: the mighty Arenal Volcano. Your journey first takes you to the northern lowlands of Costa Rica. To the left and right of the road, you will see sprawling plantanges growing pineapples, oranges, coconut and other tropical fruits. Of course, you will stop from time to time to try the juicy fruits. In the afternoon you will approach the active Arenal volcano. In good weather, you can already admire the almost perfect cone from a distance. You can move into your room today at Hotel Nayara Springs, the best on site. Your guide will suggest some activities for tomorrow so you can think about what you want. Overnight at Hotel Arenal Nayara.

4th day adventure around Arenal

Another exciting day is ahead of you, which you can design spontaneously on site. Whether you opt for a kayak or a floating excursion, want to take a riding or treetop trail tour, or prefer a walk over the Arenal suspension bridges - you will definitely experience an unforgettable day in the middle of tropical nature. If you want to tackle the day a little more relaxed, you can relax at the hotel pool, in the spa or in the thermal baths. Overnight at Hotel Arenal Nayara.

Day 5: Travel to Tortuguero National Park

Today, your journey will take you further to the east coast of the country. After breakfast, your guide will take you to the air slope at La Fortuna, where your private charter plane is already waiting for you. The panoramic flight along Costa Rica’s Caribbean coast takes around 45 minutes; then you will land on the beach of Tortuguero, from where you will be taken by boat to Manatus Lodge. Tortuguero is one of the most famous national parks in Costa Rica and protects an extensive rainforest area, which is crossed by hundreds of small lifelines – the famous canals of Tortuguero. The beaches of the national park are also a frequent nest of soup turtles, who visit the beaches at night between June and October to lay their eggs there. Today, the village has dedicated itself to tourism and thus has a great interest in the protection of reptiles. During a walk through the village, your guide will explain more about its history and the everyday life of its inhabitants. The afternoon is at your leisure to explore the area or relax in the hotel. Overnight at Hotel Manatus.

6th day On the way through the Tortugueros canal network

Today you should come out of the feathers early to see how nature awakens in the morning to new life. Before breakfast, your guide will organize a canoe trip through the Tortuguero canals: at dawn is the best time for wildlife viewing. So that you can not only discover animals, but also learn a lot about them, you will be accompanied by a biologist. With a bit of luck you can spot .B. crocodiles, caimans, lizards, monkeys, sloths, freshwater turtles and a large variety of birds. After breakfast, you can sit back for another canoe trip. In the afternoon you will get to know the rainforest in more detail on a short walk. Overnight at Hotel Manatus.

7th day onwards to Turrialba

A boat will take you through canals and rivers to the port, where you board a vehicle. During the trip, you can let the vast banana plantations of the region pass by and learn a lot of interesting things about Costa Rica from your guide. Your next stop is the green and agricultural region of Turrialba, where the boutique hotel Casa Turire is nestled in sugarcane, macadamia and coffee plantations. Overnight at Casa Turire.

Day 8 Excursion into the past: Guayabo

Costa Rica is mainly known as a destination for nature lovers. However, in Turrialba there is also an important archaeological site from pre-Columbian times: Guayabo de Turrialba. Researchers suspect that the site was once an important cultural, political and religious center, but there are still many mysteries surrounding the site. After this excursion into the past, you can use the afternoon to relax by the pool or in the beautiful garden of your hotel. Overnight at Casa Turire.

9th Day to Pacuare Lodge

Today it goes for you deep into the wilderness. In the middle of the rainforest, wild rapids and waterfalls await you. A four-wheel drive vehicle takes you to the Pacuare River, where you will change into a passenger gondola to finally reach the remote Pacuare Lodge. If you wish, we can also arrange for you to reach the lodge on a small rafting tour. Your accommodation offers the highest comfort in the middle of untouched nature. In the afternoon, you can take a walk to a nearby waterfall to refresh yourself there. Afterwards, enjoy a delicious dinner in an unforgettable ambience. Overnight at Pacuare Lodge.

10th day jungle as desired

Decide spontaneously today what you want. How about an adrenaline kick in the in-house treetop trail or on a canoe trip? Do you want to approach it more relaxed? Then treat yourself to a relaxing day in the hammock and listen to the sounds of nature. If you want to immerse yourself in a completely foreign everyday life, we recommend a hike to the Cabecar tribe, the largest group of indigenous people in Costa Rica, which preserves many ancient traditions. They live in small family groups and attach great importance to their shamans. The paths to the settlements of the Indians are a bit onerous and people are very reserved towards strangers – but this is precisely why the experience of meeting them is particularly fascinating. Overnight at Pacuare Lodge.

11th day rafting on the Pacuare

Today, a final adventure awaits you: your day begins with a 3-4 hour white water rafting (category III – IV) on one of the most beautiful rivers in the world. You will travel through unspoiled nature, observe birds and other animals, enjoying direct contact with the river and its deep green surroundings. At noon you will have a picnic on the river bank before continuing your exciting journey to Siquirres. There you can change to dry clothes and refresh yourself a little before continuing your approximately 2.5-hour drive to San José. Costa Rica’s capital lies at 1,150 meters above sea level. In the north, several volcanoes rise above the silhouette of the city, in the south the rugged Talamanca mountains. In San José, you will encounter many European influences – primarily thanks to the Spanish immigrants who arrived here after Columbus’ discovery. In addition, the financially better off traveled often and gladly to Europe in the 19th century and brought a lot with them. The architecture in particular reflects this: the National Theatre could easily be located in Germany. Upon arrival at the hotel, your guide will say goodbye to you. Overnight in San José.

12th day of travel home

Your adventure Costa Rica is coming to an end today. However, the precious memories of your time in Central America will accompany you forever. Your driver will take you to San José airport for the flight home. Here you can comfortably wait in the VIP lounge for departure.

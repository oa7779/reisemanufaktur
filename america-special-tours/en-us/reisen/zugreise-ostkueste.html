US Rail Journey — Cities of the East Coast

USA Train Journey: Experience the cities of the East Coast on an individual train journey with América Special Tours. Ask now without obligation!

10 days

Your trip idea

A sample itinerary—tailored to you

  • USA
  • USA - Travel Inspirations

Look forward to a train journey that takes you through fascinating cities in the cultural melting pot of the East Coast and holds the most legendary landmarks in the USA for you. You will start your journey in the state capital Washington D.C., which will delight you with famous landmarks and varied neighborhoods. Via Maryland and Delaware, your train journey will continue until you reach Philadelphia. The city is known for its historical sights but also for a trendy gastro scene. Unforgettable moments can then be experienced in New York City, not only on a private city tour but also on a helicopter flight. A truly unforgettable experience: The vibrant world metropolis from the air! Your trip will round off with your stay in Boston and its historic neighbourhoods as well as the bustling harbor.

Trip Information

Itinerary

10 Days Washington, D.C. - Philadelphia - New York City - Boston

Your Trip Highlights

  • Safety and flexibility through a private, German-speaking tour guide and chauffeur
  • Carefully selected hotels for you
  • Train ride along the fascinating cities of the east coast
  • Culture and local charm in Washington D.C.
  • In the treasure trove of American culture and history: Philadelphia
  • Enjoy the legendary skyline of New York from a helicopter
  • Explore the city of Boston along the Freedom Trail

Today you will reach the state capital Washington D.C. You will be met by your guide and driven to your hotel. Enjoy the next few hours at your leisure and embark on your first exploration tours to the famous landmarks and varied neighbourhoods. Perhaps one of the many renowned museums is also on your program. Overnight in Washington DC

As part of your bicycle tour, explore landmarks from the time of Jefferson, Washington and Adams. They are witnesses to the connection between the new republic and classical architectural styles. With your guide, you’ll cycle to major landmarks such as the White House, FBI Building, National Archives, Capitol, U.S. Senate, Lincoln Memorial and Washington Memorial. You should also pay a visit to Arlington Cemetery – it is the Kennedy family’s final resting place. Then conquer Georgetown, a beautiful and lively 18th century harbor. The program also includes the Smithsonian Institution’s National Air and Space Museum, the largest collection of historic aircraft and spacecraft in the world. Cryptologists and former officials from the CIA, FBI and KGB are among the espionage museum’s advisors. After glimpse into the world of veiling, enjoy art and culture. The National Gallery of Art is home to significant achievements from the Middle Ages to the present. An eventful day is behind you and so you can look forward to a dinner cruise with the Odyssey. Enjoy the sight of the monuments from the observation deck with a delicious cocktail and a three-course menu. Overnight in Washington DC

From Washington, take the train to Philadelphia. If time permits, a stopover in Baltimore can be made. The heart of downtown Baltimore is the Inner Harbor, a vibrant and beautiful waterfront. Upon arrival in Philadelphia, you will embark directly on a private city tour. Philadelphia is one of the most historically important cities in the United States, because it is considered America's cradle of freedom and democracy. Here Congress announced the Declaration of Independence and the Constitution of the United States was also published. This vibrant city with its historic center combines modern skyscrapers with cobblestone streets and conveys a unique atmosphere with the charm of 18/19. century and the developments of the 20th century. You will visit the historic area, Benjamin Franklin Parkway, with the Franklin Institute, Fairmont Park, Elfreths Alley and Society Hill, National Historic Independence Park with Liberty Bell, Independence Hall, City Hall and Logan Circle. Also on your route are the Philadelphia Art Museum, the Rodin Museum, Germantown and Franz Daniel Pastorius Monument and many more attractions. Overnight in Philadelphia. Today's day distance: 230km

The ‘City of Civil Love’ literally bursts before cultural and historical sights. Art, culture, diversity and of course the spirit of Rocky live on in South Philly! As part of a private tour, you will experience how diverse this city is today and visit one of the most enchanting neighborhoods in the city with South Philly. From Jewish delicacies to Italian markets, churches, cemeteries and public art, South Philly is a piece of the real Philadelphia Philly. Discover the hidden treasures of some of the most historic quarters in the country. Admire the beautiful mosaics of Isaiah Zagar in Magic Gardens and get to know the real South Philly from a local perspective. Enjoy a stroll through the colorful stalls and take a look at the many shops. Treat yourself to a taste experience in one of the cafés or restaurants on one of the world’s most famous Italian markets. Overnight in Philadelphia.

Today you travel by train through the ‘Garden State’ New Jersey to New York. The so-called Garden State offers a variety of ecosystems, hardwood forests and extensive freshwater wetlands. New Jersey is not only home to the famous Jersey Shore, where tranquil coastal towns and sandy beaches take turns, but also home to over 265 species of birds. Around Princeton University there is a lush green landscape. Upon arrival in the vibrant metropolis of New York City, you will be met by your guide and driven to your hotel. Overnight in New York City. Today's day distance: 155km

With a population of over 8.2 million, New York City is the largest city in the USA. The center of the New York metropolitan area is one of the largest urban areas in the world and is classified as an alpha world city due to its global influences in media, politics, education, entertainment and fashion. The city’s cultural centers for art are among the most influential in the nation. Today you get to know the city that never sleeps as part of a private city tour. On your program are, for example, the Statue of Liberty with Ellis Island, Wall Street, Times Square but also Central Park, Empire State and the Chrysler Building. For the evening we are happy to organize tickets for one of the famous Broadway musicals or for the Metropolitan Opera. Overnight in New York City.

Today has two highlights ready for you. First, experience the incomparable city from a new, luxurious perspective, as a 30-minute helicopter flight awaits you. Not only do you fly over the island, but you also get close to places like the CNN/Time Warner Building, Times Square, Central Park, Fifth Avenue and Broadway. They fly near the Empire State and the Chrysler Building. You will also see the United Nations building with the incredible skyline of the city in the background. Enjoy this unique photo opportunity to capture the most extravagant and exciting sights of this particular city. After you have enjoyed a few hours at your disposal, you can look forward to another special experience in the evening. As part of a private tour, visit the secret bars from the time of Prohibition. Even when the production and sale of alcohol was prohibited in the 1920s, the city was still considered a party metropolis. Some of these secret bars are still preserved today. Let your guide take you on an exciting discovery tour and experience the atmosphere from this forbidden time with some drinks and snacks. Overnight in New York City.

Today, your journey continues to Boston. For travel, a stopover at the famous Yale University is recommended. Here we are happy to arrange a private guided tour for you. If time permits, we also include a visit to Newport, RI. This beautifully located city is called “America’s First Resort” after many of America’s richest people (including Vanderbilt’s) built magnificent summer homes here. Many of these villas are now museums and can be visited. In the afternoon you will arrive in Boston. Your guide will greet you at the train station and then escort you to your hotel. Overnight in Boston. Today's day distance: 350km

The friendly capital of Massachusetts breathes history. Look forward to a day in the city where history mixes with modernity but also with contemporary and curious. The most important historical landmarks can be found along the so-called Freedom Trail. Together with your private German-speaking tour guide, you will get to know the main attractions marked by a red brick line throughout the city: Boston Common, Massachusetts State House, Park Street Church, Grain Store, King's Chapel, Old South Meeting House, Old State House, Faneuil Hall and Quincy Market, among others. Then end the evening with a summer ball game at Fenwey Park or enjoy an Italian meal in the city’s North End. Overnight in Boston.

Even the most beautiful trip comes to an end and so it is today to say goodbye to the east coast of the United States. Even if your holiday is over, the precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories of your trip will accompany you forever. Look forward to letting your friends and family share your experiences in the distance at home. For your return flight, your driver will take you to Boston Airport.

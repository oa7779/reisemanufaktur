US Road Trip — The Mighty Mississippi

USA Car Rental: Travel along the mighty Mississippi River from Chicago to New Orleans with América Special Tours. Ask now!

17 days

Your trip idea

A sample itinerary—tailored to you

  • Home
  • USA
  • USA - Travel Inspirations

The Midwestern United States is known as the heart of America and is the link between vast, fertile plains, tranquil small towns and the metropolis of Chicago. The southern states are a mix of different origins and cultures and look back on a turbulent history. With your rental car, you embark on a two-week adventure trip that takes you along the Mississippi from the north to the extreme south. Start in the metropolis of Chicago and drive along the legendary Route 66 to St. Louis, the gate of the west. On the route, you can enjoy the typical American small-town flair in numerous places. Take a detour to two famous music metropolises: Nashville, the capital of country music and Memphis, the birthplace of blues and rock ́n ́roll. Back on the Mississippi, your car rental trip takes you deeper into the south of the USA. You will pass cities like Vicksburg, Natchez and Baton Rouge, which will take you back to the time before and during the Civil War. Numerous surviving plantation houses, antebellum-style estates and monuments are reminiscent of this exciting time. Experience the hospitality of the southern states and pamper your palate with the extraordinary southern cuisine. At the end of your trip, the lively city of New Orleans awaits you with its colorful cultural mix, numerous jazz bars and exotic Cajun cuisine.

Trip Information

Itinerary

17 Days Chicago - Springfield - St. Louis - Kuttawa - Bowling Green - Nashville - Memphis - Vicksburg - Natchez - Baton Rouge - New Orleans

Welcome to the metropolis of the Midwest - Chicago. Arriving at the airport, you will receive your rental car and make your own way to your hotel. Chicago is generally considered the cultural epicenter of the United States. Stunning buildings, innovative star restaurants and world-class museums – Chicago has a lot to offer. A special highlight is the 40 km long shoreline on Lake Michigan, which is only a few steps from the bustling business and shopping center. Visit Millenium Park and discover the famous Cloud Gate, the famous sculpture of artist Anish Kapoor. Also in the outskirts of Chicago you can explore unique attractions, such as the architectural masterpieces of Frank Lloyd Wright or visit one of the largest and best shopping centers in the USA. Overnight in Chicago.

Today, together with your private guide, you will embark on an exploration tour of the city. Chicago is also known as the “Windy City” and is famous all over the world for its special architecture, excellent museums, companion jazz and blues clubs, and the legendary friendliness and hospitality of the Midwest. Stroll along Michigan Avenue and the Magnificent Mile, which offer world-class shopping and marvel at the impressive architecture of the bustling business district. The Willis Tower has been the tallest building in Chicago and the Western Hemisphere since its completion in 1974, with 108 floors. A 70-second elevator ride will take you to an observation deck on the 103rd floor. Here you can see Lake Michigan, the northern and southern parts of the city and the surrounding suburbs from a bird’s eye view. Overnight in Chicago.

Probably the most legendary highway in the USA starts here in Chicago. Experience nostalgia during your journey on Route 66 towards Springfield. On the route you will pass classic diners, drive-in cinemas and numerous “road side” attractions, such as the two life-size statues of the “Blues Brothers” or the historic gas station in Odell. It is also worth a visit to the Pontiac Oakland Automobile Museum. You will reach Springfield, home of Abraham Lincoln. Here you can admire the former president’s memorial of honor and the street block with Lincoln’s home, which still looks like it was in the 19th century. Overnight in Springfield.

Your journey will take you on Route 66 to the city of St. Louis. Here you will meet the Mississippi River for the first time, also known as the “Old Man River”. It is the largest river in the United States. The city of St. Louis is known as the gateway to the west and her most famous landmark is St. Louis. Louis Gateway Arch. The city was the scene of moving events, such as St. Louis World Exhibition 1904 and the first Olympic Games held in the USA. From here Charles Lindbergh took off for his first transatlantic flight. The modern St. Louis stands for thrilling blues music, barbecue traditions, brewing and baseball. Overnight in St. Louis.

Today you will continue to the country between the lakes up to the town of Kuttawa. It is located halfway between St. Louis and Nashville. Around the city there is a beautiful recreation area, which offers a variety of outdoor activities. Enjoy your time together on a walk along the Cumberland River. Overnight in Kuttawa.

Today, take a detour to the town of Bowling Green, Kentucky. The pretty downtown area and its friendly locals exude the typical charm of the American Southern States. The attractive destination also has a special surprise for you: the National Corvette Museum. For adventure fans, it’s worth a visit to Lost River Cave. Here you can take an underground boat trip and take great hikes around the cave or visit Mammoth Cave National Park with its numerous underground attractions. Overnight at Bowling Green.

Welcome to the Music City! The New York Times, Condé Nast Traveler and Travel + Leisure agree: Nashville is the top destination you simply must have seen. Explore the world capital of country music, its music-related museums and many other attractions. How about a visit to the Country Music Hall of Fame or the Johnny Cash Museum? There’s also an up-and-coming cuisine scene to explore, from traditional Southern cuisine to haute cuisine for every taste. Put on your dance shoes tonight, because when visiting a show in the famous Grand Ole Opry you will certainly not be able to keep your feet still. Overnight in Nashville.

Nashville is a city of songwriters. Literally thousands of people move to the city every year to live out their passion and become successful with their music with the dream. Today you start with a singer or songwriter on a very special exploration tour of Nashville. The three-hour tour presents the highlights of the “Music City” from the point of view of a singer/songwriter. Enjoy a very personal appearance of your guide in a picturesque place on the way. You will also get insights into the art of songwriting. You will learn how to make a big hit, from the first idea to recording in the studio. A real "ONLY IN NASHVILLE" experience. Overnight in Nashville.

Your tour will go into the next round. You drive along the Music Highway to your current day’s destination Memphis. Here you will again meet the Mississippi River, which runs through your journey like a red thread. Memphis is the cradle of rock ‘n’ roll, and the blues has also taken its first steps here. The most famous heritage is Graceland, Elvis Presley’s estate with the final resting place. Important milestones of the civil rights movement of the 1950s and 1960s also took place in Memphis. The National Civil Rights Museum provides illustrative evidence. Your hotel for the next two days is the Guesthouse at Graceland. Just a few steps from Elvis Presley’s estate, the hotel opens its doors to true music fans. Overnight in Memphis.

Visit the Stax Museum, Rock ́n ́Roll Museum or Sun Studios today to discover the backdrop of Memphis’ moving musical history. As a culmination, join a guided VIP audio tour of the Graceland property. On this tour, in addition to the property itself, you will visit various museums and exhibitions surrounding the legend Elvis Presley. Ending an exciting day of music, stroll along Beale Street in Downtown Memphis towards the evening, where you’ll find numerous blues clubs. Overnight in Memphis.

Their journey continues. You drive your rental car through the picturesque Mississippi Delta and reach the village of Indianola after some time. Here you stop to the B.B. King Museum and Delta Interpretive Center. The museum is dedicated to the life and musical legacy of probably the most famous musician in the history of the blues. Continue to the city of Vicksburg, where you will spend the night. This city is best known for its architectural antebellum style estates and for the settings during the American Civil War. Overnight in Vicksburg.

On your journey to Natchez today, you’ll arrive just south of Port Gibson on the scenic Natchez Trace Parkway, a beautiful panoramic road that runs through green landscapes and dense forests. The original path system of the Old Trace was used by the indigenous people, hunters and traders. In the afternoon you will reach the city of Natchez. It is considered the oldest European settlement on the Mississippi. Here you will find numerous old plantation houses, such as the Frogmore Cotton Plantation & Gins. Make a visit to the property. Overnight in Natchez.

Continue on to the capital from Louisiana to Baton Rouge. In Louisiana, you will feel the unique mix of nations and cultures. The locals have French, Spanish and African roots, which is reflected in the mixed traditions as well as in the cuisine. Especially famous is the Louisiana Carnival, Mardis Gras, where colorfully dressed fools roam the streets of New Orleans. The character of the city of Baton Rouge is characterized by its historical plantations, monuments and museums. Visit the Old State Capitol and Old Governor’s Mansion to learn Louisiana’s turbulent history. Do not miss to try the fantastic Cajun cuisine in one of the numerous restaurants. Overnight in Baton Rouge.

On your way along the Great River Road, which winds leisurely along the Mississippi River, visit two traditional plantation houses. The Houmas House Historic Plantation is one of the jewels among the time-honored estates. On a guided tour of the mansion, catch impressive insights into life on a plantation. After the tour, you can taste the traditional Southern cuisine in one of the three restaurants. Then take a final walk through the magnificent gardens before continuing your journey. After this immersive experience, continue along the Great River Road and reach the famous Oak Alley Plantation. This plantation is called the "Grande Dame" of the plantations, because it still exudes the charm and elegance of the old south. Particularly impressive is the Eichenallee in front of the main house. Here you take part in a guided tour of the property. In the evening you will arrive in New Orleans. Treat yourself to a drink at the hotel bar and review the impressions of today. Overnight in New Orleans.

New Orleans has more than 300 years of history. The Spanish, French, African and Caribbean influences create a mix of distinctive architecture, immersive music and creative cuisine that only New Orleans knows how to serve. On every corner, the timeless city offers an opportunity to discover new things, whether it is a jazz combo or the centuries-old tram. Look forward to a city that never sleeps, where music resounds around the clock from over 400 bars, lively street corners or open-air festivals. But the city has a lot to offer not only musically, but also historically and culturally guests get their money's worth. Admire the beautiful architecture on a tram or horse-drawn carriage ride, dine in one of the 1,400 restaurants and enjoy the imaginative cuisine. At the gates of this unforgettable city are the large plantations from before the American Civil War. In the evening, a special highlight awaits you: You ride on a genuine paddle steamer, the Natchez, out onto the Mississippi River and enjoy the view of the city. During your buffet dinner, listen to the jazz and Dixieland music in the background. Overnight in New Orleans.

Today, embark on a tour of Louisiana’s origins. For a boat tour through the marsh area, you will first go out to the Mississippi in a motorboat. From there you will get to the truly unique swampland of Bayou. Your specialized guide takes you into a mystical legendary world of the marshes and teaches you the "joie de vivre" of the Cajun culture. You will pass the nesting sites of alligators and herons, with a little luck you will also see a glimpse of a raccoon, the rare beaver rats and various snakes or with even more luck you will catch a glimpse of the mysterious Rougarou on the way: According to the legends of the Cajuns, this fiery, werewolf-like creature roams through the marshes and hunts down naughty children and Catholics who violate the fasting commandment. Overnight in New Orleans.

Even the most beautiful journey comes to an end and so it is said to say goodbye today. Even if your holiday is over, the precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories of your trip will accompany you forever. Look forward to letting your friends and family share your experiences in the distance at home. You drive to the New Orleans airport and drop off your rental car there.

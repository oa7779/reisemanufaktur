Wide steppes, crystal clear lakes, steep mountains and glaciers sparkling in the sun: The wild south of Argentina is a unique natural paradise of rugged beauty. After a short detour to the colorful capital Buenos Aires, you will explore Patagonia's landscapes in a very luxurious way: you will spend the night in the midst of grandiose natural scenery in top-class hotels, where you can relax between excursions and the varied impressions of your trip.

Day 1 Arrival in Buenos Aires

Your journey starts in the Argentine capital Buenos Aires, where you will already be expected at the airport by your German-speaking tour guide. So you can already learn first anecdotes about the city on the journey to your hotel. Buenos Aires is huge, but anything but a faceless metropolis. The world capital of tango has a lot to offer culturally – it is said to have more theater halls than any other place in the world. In many corners, Buenos Aires is also astonishingly European: here is not only the largest Italian enclave outside Italy. Numerous other immigrants from Europe have also shaped the cityscape over the years. In keeping with the nickname “Paris of South America”, Buenos Aires also attracts the country’s bohemian and alternative scene today. Their luxurious accommodation for the next two nights is located in the heart of Recoleta, one of the most expensive and elegant neighborhoods, and is home to an exquisite cigar bar among other things. Overnight in Buenos Aires.

Day 2 Explore the capital of Tango

Buenos Aires is waiting for you: embark on an exploration tour of the capital. At the crossroads of Avenida Corrientes and Avenida 9 de Julio lies the obelisk of Buenos Aires. From here, your driver will take you to Plaza de Mayo in the center. Another piece of history awaits you in San Telmo, which with its colonial buildings and cobblestones is one of the most traditional neighborhoods of Buenos Aires. La Boca in the east of the city is more colorful, and the district was created at the end of the 19th century during a great wave of Italian immigration. Many houses were built from dismantled ships and decorated with ship paint. In the midst of this backdrop, artists praise their works on the sidewalks, and the tour ends with the northern neighborhoods. Drive to the green area of Palermo with its parks and artificial lakes. Quietly it is at the neoclassical cemetery La Recoleta, where in magnificent mausoleums numerous celebrities, including Eva Perón, found their final resting place. The upscale district around the cemetery attracts with numerous bars, cafes and restaurants. In the evening you will experience the fascination of Argentine tango at the unique show of Rojo Tango in one of the famous tango houses El Cabaret. Overnight in Buenos Aires.

3rd day onwards to Bariloche

Today you leave the Argentine capital behind and fly on to Bariloche at the foot of the Andes. The place and its surroundings are a popular destination for skiers and snowboarders in winter. In summer, the mountains and lakes invite you to hike, kayaking, fishing and bathing. At the airport, you will be expected by your guide and taken to your hotel, which is idyllic nestled between lakes and mountain peaks. Your resort offers the best conditions to relax in this beautiful natural setting: The cozy traditional hotel has several pools, a spa and fitness club as well as an in-house golf course. Here you can relax in style between your adventures of the next few days. Overnight at Bariloche.

4th day kayaking on Bariloches Lakes

Around Bariloche are beautiful lakes that are best explored from the water. Enjoy the magnificent surroundings of your hotel in the morning before departing for Lake Gutierrez in the afternoon. Here, after a brief briefing by your professional guide, you’ll board your kayaks and begin the journey along the coast and hidden coves that are hardly accessible elsewhere. After about two hours, you will go ashore for a small picnic on one of the remote beaches before continuing your exploration of the unique nature and admiring the sunset from the water. Overnight at Bariloche.

Day 5 Hike to the Tronador Glaciers

Today, a little adventure in nature awaits you: a hike up Mount Tronador, one of the most famous mountain peaks of the Andean region. On the hike through the Nahuel Huapi National Park you will pass breathtaking landscapes from forests, lakes, waterfalls, beaches, mountains and small Andean valleys. The three peaks of Mount Tronador form the natural border between Argentina and Chile. Marvel at the Ventisquero Negro, one of the glaciers on the mountain top where the Manso River springs, which winds down in serpentines and flows to the Pacific Ocean. In the evening you will return to your hotel. Overnight at Bariloche.

Day 6 Relaxation in Bariloche

Without tour guide and chauffeur

Take advantage of this day to enjoy the varied amenities of your resort "Llao Llao Bariloche Hotel & Resort" with a game of golf, a soothing spa app or a walk through the park. Overnight at Bariloche.

7th day flight to El Calafate

Today your journey takes you by plane a good 1500 kilometers further south. Surrounded by impressive glaciers, the city of El Calafate is the starting point for many tourists to the spectacular Los Glaciares National Park. Upon arrival at the airport, you will be picked up and taken to your hotel, which is located on a huge property in a valley not far from the famous Perito Moreno glacier. Overnight at El Calafate.

Day 8 Shipping to the Glaciers

Today, set out to explore Patagonia’s impressive glacier landscape. In the morning you will be taken with other guests to Punta Bandera, where you will board your ship at the port of La Soledad. The accumulation of the ice floes in various colors and shapes foreshadow the proximity to the Upsala glacier. The Upsala Canal is currently blocked by an ice floe barrier, where you will stop to take a look at the Upsala Glacier in good weather conditions. In the direction of Lago Argentino we continue on the Canal Spegazzini to the glaciers of the same name. In front of the imposing backdrop of the Spegazzini glaciers, a gourmet 3-course menu is served. After lunch, the tranquil Puesto de Las Vacas bay will be visited, where you will moor for a guided walk. Return on board through Brazo Norte to the starting point La Soledad. Overnight at El Calafate.

9th day of glacier migration

Today, set out to explore Patagonia’s impressive glacier landscape. In the morning you will be taken (together with other guests) to the Bay of Bajo de las Sombras, where you will board a ship. It takes you across Lake Rico and offers unique views of the Perito Moreno glacier and the Canal de Témpanos – the Iceberg Canal. After about twenty minutes, get off and embark on a small hike along the shore that takes you to the glacier. During an approximately two-hour walk on the glacier, you will see various ice formations and learn from your English-speaking guide more about the flora and fauna of the area. Finally, you can take a walk through the Magellan forest as you please. Overnight at El Calafate.

Day 10: Travel to Ushuaia

The last stop on your tour is Ushuaia. The southernmost city of Argentina is located in the south of the Tierra del Fuego Peninsula, not far from the national park of the same name. Overnight at Ushuaia.

Day 11 The Tierra del Fuego National Park

Waterfalls, dense forests, majestic mountain peaks and glaciers shining in the sun: The Tierra del Fuego National Park impresses with dramatic landscapes. A variety of plants and animals – including condors, guanacos and jackals – find a protected home here. You will explore the protected area today on an excursion with your German-speaking guide. Take a walk through the forest, look for beaver dams and take in the beauty of Lapataia Bay. Overnight at Ushuaia.

12th Day Adventurous Jeep Tour Around the Lakes

Early in the morning, your guide will pick you and other guests up for an adventurous off-road Jeep tour. With a Land Rover Defender, you will traverse deep forests and rivers and catch a panoramic view of Lake Escondido from the Cordillera, hidden at the end of a valley. The destination of the tour is the huge Fagnano Lake. This stretches over a length of almost 100 kilometers to Chile. For lunch, you will arrive at a typical mountain hut. Overnight at Ushuaia.

Day 13 Back to Buenos Aires

Today we say goodbye to Tierra del Fuego. You will fly back to Buenos Aires, where you can finish your last evening in Argentina. Overnight in Buenos Aires. 

14th day home flight

After two weeks, your break from everyday life is coming to an end today. Your chauffeur will take you to the airport, from where you will travel home with many impressions richer.

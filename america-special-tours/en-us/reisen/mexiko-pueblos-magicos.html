Mexico Tour - Pueblos Mágicos

Individual round trip to the Pueblos Mágicos with South America expert América Special Tours. Here directly inquire, consult and experience for yourself.

Deep in the heart of Mexico you will find the fascinating “Pueblos Mágicos”, the magical villages that live up to its name. Incomparable natural treasures, small colorful alleys, historical buildings and unique traditions make these places particularly magical and therefore more than worth seeing. Here you will experience the real Mexico and immerse yourself in the authentic culture of a country that could hardly be more diverse. From time-honored pyramid sites, seemingly endless agave fields and idyllic villages, you will arrive at the absolute insider tip Baja California Sur, which awaits you with untouched beaches, gentle giants and luxurious hotels. A trip away from the usual tourist paths!

Day 1 Arrival in Mexico City

Your journey starts in the eponymous capital of Mexico, where you will already be expected at the airport by your German-speaking tour guide and chauffeur. After receiving your luggage and leaving the airport, let the bright, warm sun shine on your skin outside - Welcome to the unique Mexico! Together with your personal chauffeur, you will embark on a warm welcome into the vibrant heart of the almost nine million inhabitants of the metropolis. Overnight in Mexico City.

Day 2 Exciting History

Together with your private German-speaking guide, you will make your way to the most important sites of the metropolis, such as the famous main square Zocalo, the colonial government palace or the impressive cathedral. Of course, the beautiful building of the Palacio de Bellas Artes should not be missing. After a stroll through the nearby craft market, you will travel along the Paseo de la Reforma to the Museum of Anthropology, where you will have time to learn more about the Aztec culture, of which only a few remains remain in its former capital. If you still have energy after these many new impressions, we recommend a refreshing margarita in one of the many exciting bars of Mexico City in the evening. Just ask your guide for tips on the capital’s nightlife, as there is plenty to see and an unforgettable evening is guaranteed! Overnight in Mexico City.

Day 3 In the footsteps of Frida Kahlo and Diego Rivera

In the morning you will get a VIP museum tour of the Museum of Contemporary Art. On the side, you will enjoy a delicious cocktail and be inspired by the works of art. Afterwards, explore the southern districts of Mexico City. Your guide will take you through the streets of San Angel and Coyoacán, once a colonial village in the south of the city that exudes a cozy atmosphere with its narrow alleys, bustling squares and pretty cafes. In Xochimilco you will visit the floating gardens, which are also UNESCO World Heritage Sites. Afterwards you will walk in the footsteps of the most famous artists in Mexico, because here the well-known painters Frida Kahlo and Diego Rivera were born. During a short walking tour, you will follow their exciting lives and visit the house where Frida Kahlo spent most of her life. Overnight in Mexico City.

Day 4 So Close to Heaven

Early in the morning your chauffeur will pick you up at the hotel, because today is an absolute highlight on the program: Your hot air balloon ride over the pyramid complex of Teotihuacán! Experience the pyramids from a whole new angle - from above - and have the unique opportunity to take a breathtaking look at the City of the Gods and the Pyramids of Teotihuacán. Experience the cultural heritage of the Mayans as few have experienced it before you! Back on the ground, you first fortify yourself with a delicious breakfast and then set off to explore the historic site on foot. As always, you will be accompanied by your German-speaking guide, who is well versed in the history of the ancient pyramid complex. Following your extensive visit to the impressive ruins of Teotihuacán, your chauffeur will take you to your next stop in the colonial San Miguel de Allende, which is one of Mexico’s most magical places. Overnight in San Miguel de Allende

5th Day Painting San Miguel de Allende

The tranquil city of San Miguel de Allende is located in the Mexican state of Guanajuato, about 1,900 meters above sea level on the river Rio de la Lara about 90 kilometers in the southeast of the city of Guanajuato. The city got its name from a famous fellow citizen, Ignacio Allende, who led the army of insurgents during the struggles for independence. Since 2008, it has been a UNESCO World Heritage Site. The multitude of small churches and patio houses, the numerous picturesque hotels and restaurants as well as the warmth and hospitality of the residents contribute to the charm of San Miguel de Allende, as you experience yourself during your walk. Your guide accompanies you and tells you everything you need to know about the colorful city. Overnight in San Miguel de Allende

Day 6 Underground Guanajuato

The center of Guanajuato consists of numerous European-style squares, connected by cobbled streets and alleys that wind up and down the mountain slope, many of them suitable only for pedestrians and too narrow for vehicles. Driving in the streets of Guanajuato is a real challenge for drivers. In the past, the river Guanajuato ran beneath the city and there was frequent flooding of the roads, especially during the rainy season. In the mid-20th century, engineers built a dam to divert the river into underground caves, limiting flooding. Thus, under the colonial city of Guanajuato today runs an extraordinary network of paths underground. Later, this tunnel network was transformed into roads to help the traffic flow of the city. In addition to various alleys and squares, you will also visit the Hidalgo market. This is located in a huge building in the middle of the center and rather gives the impression of a cathedral. Overnight in San Miguel de Allende.

Day 7 Tequila, Tequila

Mexico without tequila would be like Bavaria without beer - simply unimaginable! Therefore, during the next two days, you will devote yourself completely to the famous national drink. With your private guide and chauffeur, say goodbye to the colonial San Miguel de Allende and make your way to Santiago de Tequila, about four hours away. This place is of course not for nothing just like the agave liquor. Rather, it is the birthplace of the tequila, which is why it also bears the name of the city. Today you immerse yourself in the world of tequila and become a “tequilero”. Of course, a real tequila expert accompanies you and is happy to help you with the mixture of different aromas and scents. Experiment a little and make your own personal tequila. Of course, you take a bottle home with you, after all, your loved ones will also want to taste your masterpiece. Overnight in Santiago de Tequila.

8th Day More Tequila

After you were allowed to participate in the tequila production yesterday, you also dedicate today to the Mexican national drink. Your tequila expert will take you on an exclusive day tour of the magical Santiago de Tequila. Be ready for the fascinating and world-famous “Spirit of Jalisco”, the real tequila! Your tequila experience begins with a visit to the agave fields. Afterwards, a private tour with tasting and exclusive access is waiting for you in the Tequila Fortaleza, where the fifth generation of tequila is traditionally produced. At lunch at the local market, regional delicacies await you before proceeding with your tour. After acquainted with the traditional production process of the Sauza Dynasty in the morning, in the afternoon you will visit La Tequileña, the modern boutique distillery of the legendary Fonseca family. Here you will be introduced to the differences of the modern production process and of course also taste one or the other agave liquor. Enrique Fonseca, head of the family, is considered the most brilliant “tequilero” in the industry. He is the inventor of some famous tequila varieties such as Fuenteseca, Pura Sangre, Zapata, Tequila Cimarron and Xalixco. Overnight in Tequila.

Day 9 - Go to Baja California Sur

Today, leave Central Mexico behind. Your journey will take you to the offshore peninsula of Baja California Sur, a real insider tip. From your chauffeur you will be taken to the airport in Guadalajara, from where you will fly to La Paz. Here, as usual, you will also be expected by your private chauffeur and guide and escorted to your hotel. Arrive comfortably and enjoy a refreshing swim in the pool as the glowing ball of sun sinks into the infinite Pacific Ocean. Overnight in La Paz.

10th Day Island of the Holy Spirit

A visit to Isla Espiritu Santo (English: Holy Spirit Island) is a must in Baja California Sur! The small island of volcanic origin has dozens of beautiful coves, an incredible underwater world and a unique flora and fauna. It is also home to one of Mexico’s best-preserved ecosystems. There are five different species of mammals and reptiles, including a ringtailed cat, a goat, and the endemic black hares. You can reach the island by boat. On the way you will be instructed by your guide and receive a short briefing, as well as a lot of information about the island itself. After about one and a half hours you reach a sea lion colony. Here you have plenty of time to play, snorkel or swim with the bustling sea lions in the water. Back on board, navigate around the island, always looking for the most immaculate beach. After choosing one of the beautiful, unspoilt beaches, you will be pampered with a fabulous lunch. Keep your eyes peeled during the way back, because you may be surprised by dolphins or even a whale. Overnight in La Paz.

Day 11 Gentle Giants

Whale sharks are the true gentle giants of the oceans. Even though it is the largest fish on earth, it is a peaceful shark with a greyish blue back and a light belly. It is very easy to distinguish it from other sharks, it stands out especially for its white points and a wide, flat snout. Since he likes to be near the water surface, he is easy to discover. Especially off the coasts of Baja California, because due to the excellent water quality and corresponding food richness, the whale shark feels particularly well in the Mexican Pacific Streams. Today you join the Whale Shark México team, which has been closely monitoring, protecting and exploring marine life for years under the direction of scientist Deni Ramírez and sharing its knowledge with you for once. To conclude this unique experience, enjoy your lunchtime picnic on one of the beautiful, unspoilt beaches on the Gulf of California. Buried with your toes in the sand, a cool drink in your hand and a view of the seemingly infinite Pacific Ocean, it can definitely be endured here. Overnight in La Paz.

12th day Los Cabos

From La Paz, make your way to the beautiful beaches of Los Cabos today. On the way, you should not miss the small town of Todos Santos, also part of the “Pueblos Mágicos” and is considered one of the most beautiful places in Mexico. In addition to passionate surfers and perfect waves, Todos Santos has enchanting alleys with colorful houses and numerous art galleries to offer. The perfect place to enjoy a delicious lunch and a leisurely digestive walk and take a deep breath with fresh sea breeze. In the afternoon you will finally reach your luxurious beach hotel, which already awaits you with relaxing days. Overnight in Los Cabos.

13-14. Day Free days

The next few days are entirely yours. Let yourself be pampered with a specially tailored spa experience, enjoy the sparkling sun rays on your skin and the warm, white sand under your feet. At Hotel One & Only Palmilla you will not miss anything! Alternatively, you will have the opportunity to explore the unique and incredibly species-rich underwater world on snorkeling trips and cruise on a luxurious sailing yacht off Los Cabos. Or take an adventure off road jeep tour to nearby idyllic mountain villages. Overnight in Los Cabos.

15th day home

Even the most beautiful journey comes to an end and so it is today to say goodbye to the incomparable Mexico. Even if your holiday is over, the precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories of your trip will accompany you forever. Look forward to letting your friends and family share your experiences in the distance at home. For your return flight you will be taken to the airport of Los Cabos.

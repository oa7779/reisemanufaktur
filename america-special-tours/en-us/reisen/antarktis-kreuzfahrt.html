Antarctic Cruise - To the End of the World

Join América Special Tours on a luxurious Antarctic cruise with the Magellan Explorer and let yourself be charmed by an imposing world of ice.

Make your way to the stormy end of the world. An Antarctic cruise on the Magellan Explorer is an unforgettable ice and glacier experience – and it’s all luxurious. Following in the footsteps of Francis Drake and Otto Nordenskjöld, set out in the Chilean city of Punta Arenas, the gateway to Antarctica, to marvel at the stunning, endless white glacier, fjord and mountain world. By plane you will cross the legendary Drake Passage in just two hours and land directly on King George Island – quickly, comfortably and without sea conditions. On the way, you will explore nature on shore excursions and get exciting insights into the wildlife.

1st day Departure into the great adventure

Bienvenidos a Punta Arenas, Chile! This is where your adventure expedition to the end of the world starts. But before you get started, take part in an information session where you will learn everything important about your upcoming flight cruise. You are welcome to ask last questions here before it goes on board the next day. In the evening you get to know your fellow travelers at the joint welcome dinner. Dinner at Explorer's House, Antarctica's Welcome Center21, instead – only five minutes’ walk from your hotel. Enjoy delicious specialties from the region and a relaxed start to your adventure! Overnight in Punta Arenas. Note: In order to have enough time for the introduction and preparation of the expedition, the journey to Punta Arenas must always be made before 14 The clock.
 

Day 2 The expedition begins

Your Antarctic adventure begins with an approximately two-hour flight from Punta Arenas to King George Island in the Southern Shetland Islands. As soon as you leave the plane, you will feel the clear, fresh air of Antarctica – an unforgettable moment! Afterwards you get on a transfer vehicle and drive past the Chilean research station Frei and the Russian station Bellingshausen to the coast. There you will board a Zodiac inflatable boat that will safely take you to your comfortable expedition ship – your floating home for the coming days in the fascinating world of ice and silence. Overnight on the Magellan Explorer.

Day 3 - 6 Discoveries in Antarctica

Antarctica – the land of superlatives. You will sail through fjords filled with ice, past majestic icebergs, accompanied by seabirds, penguins, seals and whales. Every day you go ashore with the Zodiac and explore the fascinating ice landscape together with experienced polar guides. On board, an exciting program of expert lectures awaits you as you share your experiences with your fellow passengers in the cozy lounge with breathtaking views. The expedition team re-plans the route every day to take advantage of the best opportunities offered by wind, weather and ice. This creates a very special, individual adventure every time. Depending on the conditions, you can discover different regions, including the Southern Shetland Islands, Antarctic Sound, Gerlache Street or Penola Street. The Shetland Islands consist of about 20 small islands, discovered by Captain William Smith in 1819, are a strolling ground for penguin colonies and offer beaches dotted with fur seals and lake elephants. The narrow entrance into the interior of the annular volcanic island of Deception is a unique adventure! Carefully approach the nesting sites of penguins and seal colonies. As close as here you rarely come to the cute swimmers. Humpback and minke whales are also frequent companions to your cruise, and even orca sightings are possible. In addition to the animal world, there are also historically and scientifically exciting places here. Overnight on the Magellan Explorer. 

Day 7 Back to Chile

Return to King George Island and say goodbye to the breathtaking world of Antarctica – one last glimpse of the endless ice before returning to Punta Arenas. Leaving the Magellan Explorer will end your Antarctic adventure, but these exciting and impressive experiences will surely not be forgotten. Upon arrival, the transfer will take place to your hotel where you will spend the night. Overnight in Punta Arenas.

Day 8 Saying Goodbye to Another World

After a final breakfast in Punta Arenas, transfer to the airport. Let this unique adventure pass once again on your journey home. Good trip home!

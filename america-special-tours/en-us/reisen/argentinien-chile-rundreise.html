Argentina and Chile Round trip - Our insider tip in the High Andes

Discover the High Andes on an adventurous Argentina and Chile round trip. A real insider tip from América Special Tours. Let's get some advice now!

Itinerary & services

This trip is perfect for all nature lovers who like to actively make their trip. You will explore the northwest of Argentina, which is still a true insider tip. A first highlight of the trip is the exclusive, multi-day Jeeptour through the Puna plateau in the middle of the Andes. At this civilization-unfamiliar place at an altitude of more than 4,500 meters, time does not matter. Here you will feel untouched nature in your perfection and find a place where you will forget your everyday stress with just a few breaths. Enjoy a few days at a winery in Cafayate before setting off for a stunning Andean crossing to Chile. In the desert oasis of San Pedro de Atacama you can expect active days. The Hotel Explora offers you a special outdoor philosophy for explorers in addition to outstanding service: you choose from numerous hikes, bicycle tours, horseback riding tours and jeep tours and actively explore the wonderful surroundings away from the crowds. The cultural framework is formed by two large cities of world-class: Buenos Aires and Santiago de Chile. Pack binoculars, photo camera and walking sticks and embark on an unforgettable adventure in the high Andes of Argentina and Chile!

Day 1 Bienvenidos a Buenos Aires!

Your journey through Argentina begins in the capital Buenos Aires, which also bears the nickname of the Paris of South America. Numerous immigrants from Europe and the rest of the world have helped shape the cityscape over the years and shaped the tango out of a merger of different cultural influences. Buenos Aires is surprisingly European in many corners, including the largest Italian enclave outside Italy. Upon arrival at the airport, you will be welcomed by your guide and taken to the hotel by your chauffeur. So you can already learn first anecdotes about this fascinating city on the journey. Together with your German-speaking guide and chauffeur, you will then embark on a first exploration tour of the southern urban areas. You will visit the center with attractions such as Avenida de Mayo, Plaza de Mayo, Casa Rosada and Metropolitan Cathedral. You will also visit the historic district of San Telmo, the bustling La Boca and the modern urban area around the port of Puerto Madero. Overnight in Buenos Aires.

2nd Day Modere and Tradition in Buenos Aires

The cosmopolitan ambience and the different peculiarities of the individual districts fascinate every visitor and you should not miss a second city tour in the northern urban areas. You will start your tour in Palermo, a very green district with beautiful parks and lake facilities, the Botanical and Zoological Garden, the Argentine horse racing track and the national polo field. Afterwards you will visit the district of Recoleta, also called “Barrio Norte”. The wealthier population of the capital has settled in this well-kept district, which is why you will also find numerous exclusive boutiques and restaurants here. Your tour takes you past the Centro Cultural Recoleta, in the Cemetery of Recoleta you can visit the tomb of Eva Duarte, better known as Eva Peron ("Evita"), as well as numerous architectural jewels, the mausoleums and pantheons. Overnight in Buenos Aires.

Day 3 Onward journey to Salta: “The Pretty”

Today you will leave the metropolis of Buenos Aires, because unforgettable days await you in the northwest of Argentina. By domestic flight you can reach the city of Salta. Together with your English-speaking driver guide, you will get to know the capital of the province of the same name, which was founded in 1582 and is affectionately called “La Linda” (the pretty ones). No other Argentinian city offers such an abundance of historical buildings in the colonial baroque period. This cultural wealth is framed by a spectacular mountain backdrop. By no means should you miss Salta’s most impressive landmark, the San Francisco Cathedral. The streets invite you to take a stroll, the pretty squares, such as the magnificent Plaza 9 de Julio, are ideal for sitting down and watching the city bustle over a glass of wine from nearby growing areas. For an excursion outside the city, the village of San Lorenzo offers its art and craft market. Overnight in Salta.

Day 4: An unforgettable road trip begins

Today you start on an adventurous 5-day tour through the spectacular landscapes around Salta. Together with your guide, you will cross the gorge Quebrada del Toro, which connects the green Lerma Valley with the desert plateau Puna. After some time you will arrive at San Antonio de los Cobres, the last inhabited place before the plateau and starting point for your expedition to the desert. After passing the unspoilt Desierto del Laberinto (desert labyrinth) in the afternoon, you reach your current destination Tolar Grande. From the small village you can enjoy unique views over the western ridge of the Cordillera as well as over the imposing volcanic chain, which in some places is over 6,000 m high. Overnight in Tolar Grande.

Day 5: Rest and solitude in the desert

Today you can enjoy the peace and solitude of this remote region. Together with your guide, you will embark on a day tour of the desert and visit Caipe’s abandoned train station, Rio Grande salt lakes and the Casualidad mine. At the end of this exciting day and with numerous new impressions, you will return to your accommodation. Overnight in Tolar Grande.

Day 6: Feel Freedom, Breathe Freedom

In the morning your desert tour goes into the next round. You pass through the Arizaro Salt Lake and reach the desert oasis Antofalla at noon. It is located directly on the longest salt lake in the world and is surrounded by countless lava tongues and red mountains. While crossing the Vega Colorada, a green pasture landscape at 4,200 m, you will always catch breathtaking views of the Antofalla volcano as well as grazing lamas, suri-alpakas and vicuñas. You will also visit the valley Quebrada de Calalaste, which is only a few kilometers from Vega Colorada. At the end of this impressive day you will reach Antofagasta de la Sierra and your daily destination El Peñón. Overnight in El Peñón.

Day 7 Colourful lakes, volcanic rocks and flamingos

Today, take a trip to Campo de Piedra Pomez where you can marvel at impressive rock landscapes of volcanic origin. During the summer months from October to April, you will also explore the Laguna Grande. This salt lake is located at approx. 4,150 m on the way to the crater of the volcano Galan. In the South American winter, the lake is frozen over, but as spring approaches, you are lucky to marvel at the rare puna flamingoes in the wild. Today you will return to Hostería El Peñón. Overnight in El Peñón.

Day 8 - Continue to Cafayate in the vineyards

Today it is time to leave the plateau, because you make your way to Cafayate together with your guide. Along the way, stop at Santa Maria to fortify yourself with the most famous Argentine specialty: the Asado. As a lover of grilled meat dishes, you will surely get your money's worth here. In the afternoon you will reach the quiet village of Cafayate, built in colonial style and surrounded by picturesque vineyards. The mild and dry climate as well as 360 sunny days a year are perfect conditions for wine production. Overnight in Cafayate.

Day 9 Recreation, Wellness and Wine

After experiencing a variety of impressive and unique landscapes over the past few days, and traveling at dizzying altitudes for days, you’ll have today at your leisure to unwind from your high plateau tour. Sleep in, have a late breakfast and enjoy the hotel’s amenities. Let yourself be pampered in the wellness area or fill up with sun during a walk through the vineyards. A recommendation and a real treat for the palate is a wine tasting offered at the various wineries of the village. Overnight in Cafayate

Day 10: Travel to Humahuaca Gorge

Today is again a travel day with spectacular views ahead of you. After breakfast, you will be picked up by your driver guide and drive towards Salta. On the way, you will cross the Quebrada de las Conchas (Gorge of Shells). Due to its bizarre rock formations and multi-colored mountains, it was named a World Heritage Site by UNESCO. Particularly worth seeing are some extraordinary formations, such as the Garganta del Diablo, the Anfiteatro, El Sapo and El Obelisco. After a lunch break in Salta, continue north into the valley of Quebrada de Humahuaca. This canyon landscape has also been declared a World Heritage Site by UNESCO due to its colorful layers of rock and local customs and traditions. You will reach your daily destination Purmamarca, a small village right at the foot of the seven-colored mountain Cerro de los Siete Colores. The original village will charm you with its authentic charm. Overnight in Purmamarca.

Day 11 Visiting the community of Hornaditas

Today you will experience a varied excursion through the Humahuaca Valley. First, visit the Sapagua Gorge where you will take a small hike to the Pintado de Sapagua rock paintings. It is believed that these paintings date back to the Inca period. Afterwards you will visit the village of Hornaditas, where you will get an insight into the everyday work of the locals. The people here have adapted to the harsh area and still practice agriculture with the ancient techniques of their forefathers. After this authentic experience, return to Purmamarca. Overnight in Purmamarca.

Day 12 Spectacular Salt Lakes

Today your adventure journey goes into the next round: from Purmamarca follow the serpentine road. You are already used to the altitude from your tour in the Puna plateau, so you can concentrate fully on the breathtaking views. After some time you will arrive at the fascinating Salinas Grandes. These salt lakes are reminiscent of an endless white sea that seems to stretch to the horizon. The salt crystals break the light in a wide variety of colors and underline the unreality of this environment. Here you will find information about the various salt extraction techniques. Somewhere in the quiet desert solitude runs the Argentine-Chilean border. After crossing the border, your journey will now continue in Chile. Exciting days in the driest desert in the world await you. In the evening you will reach the oasis of San Pedro de Atacama. Overnight in San Pedro de Atacama.

13 - 15th day In harmony with nature

In the next few days you will get to know numerous highlights of the region, for example active geysers, lakes that stand out for their intense colors or dazzling white salt deserts. You are staying at the exclusive Hotel Explora Atacama, which, as the name suggests, offers you a special outdoor philosophy for explorers in addition to outstanding service: you choose your favourites from numerous hikes, bicycle tours, riding tours and jeep tours and actively explore the wonderful surroundings of San Pedro de Atacama. To relax your tired muscles after a long day, the hotel's spa area is at your disposal or you can enjoy a bath in the Puritama springs. For all star fans, the hotel’s own observatory offers guided tours – as Chile is one of the best regions in the world for stargazing. In this oasis in the middle of the driest desert in the world, you will experience moments of happiness for the soul. Overnight in San Pedro de Atacama.

Day 16 Farewell to the Magical Desert

Today, your active adventure journey through the deserts of Argentina and Chile is slowly coming to an end. After a final breakfast in the desert, you will be taken to the airport and travel to Santiago, the capital of Chile. After almost two weeks in the solitude of desert and mountain landscapes, your empty batteries are recharged and the bustle of the city is sure to be a welcome change. Enjoy an evening walk through the bustling streets of the city. Overnight in Santiago de Chile.

17Santiago – a city of contrasts

Santiago is a city of contrasts. Here are buildings in colonial style next to ultra-modern high-rise buildings. Today you will start with your guide and chauffeur on a city tour to experience exactly these contrasts and get an impression of the city and its inhabitants. You will visit, among other things, the Plaza de Armas with its cathedral and the viewpoint on the Cerro San Cristobal, from which you will have a fantastic panoramic view of the seemingly endless sea of houses of Santiago and the impressive backdrop of the Andean chain. Afterwards you will drive to one of the most beautiful artisanal markets within Santiago. This is built like a Chilean village, you will find the various shops in small cottages. A large number of ceramics, leather goods, copper figures, clay pots, skins up to the lapis lazuli are offered, certainly one or the other souvenir for the loved ones at home can be purchased here. Overnight in Santiago de Chile.

18 Your journey ends

Today you say goodbye to Argentina and Chile. You will be taken to the airport and travel home from Santiago. Even if your adventure journey is over, the precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories will accompany you forever. Look forward to letting your friends and family share your experiences at home in the most remote regions of Argentina and Chile.

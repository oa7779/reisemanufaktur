Peru Luxury Tour - Amazon Cruise with the Dolphin I

Those who want to experience the Amazon away from the tourist flows and observe the Peruvian wildlife up close are in the right place on the “Dolphin I”. From Iquitos you will go to the jungle, get to know pink dolphins, notorious piranhas and posing monkeys. Whether on a hike or by paddle boat – you will uncover some secrets of the Amazon. If you wish, let your trip end on the dream beach of Máncora.

1st day Arrival in Lima

Welcome to Peru! Your journey through Peru begins in the capital Lima, whose old town – legacy of the colonial era – has been a World Heritage Site since 1991. The “City of Kings” carries a dark legacy as the center of the Spanish Inquisition. Due to the geographical conditions, Lima’s sky is also grim: The desert city on the sea is almost always overshadowed by clouds. Your German-speaking tour guide will welcome you at the airport and take you to your hotel, where you can arrive in peace and use the rest of the day for relaxation. Overnight in Lima.

2nd day Arrival in the Amazon: Your journey on the “Delfin I” begins

This morning, head to the airport where you board a plane to Iquitos – and after landing in the Peruvian Amazon. Here you will be expected by a representative of the cruise ship “Delfin I”, who will escort you to the port of Nauta (95 kilometers from Iquitos). For on the road, a small lunchbox is included for you. On the way, you will see “terra firma”, unflooded rainforest, and get a first glimpse of the jungle landscape. On board the ship you first move into your cabin, which offers you, among other things, the luxury of floor-to-ceiling windows and a private bathroom with hot water rain shower. Only twelve passengers travel on the “Dolphin I” – (almost) undisturbed enjoyment is thus guaranteed. While the Dolphin I runs out of Nauta, you can enjoy the view from the elegantly equipped outdoor lounge on the second deck with an exotic cocktail. You will get an insight into the activities offered and the exact schedule. Overnight on the ship.

Day 3 Meeting the Pink Dolphins

On your first morning on board, it’s worth getting up early as you head out to the only Canopy Walkway in this area. At an altitude of over 25 meters and a length of 500 meters, the Canopy Walkway offers a view of the rainforest from the treetops. The suspended walkway stretches between 14 of the area’s largest rainforest trees and is one of the longest canopy walkways in the world. Afterwards you will have lunch, followed by the Yanayacu Pucate. This is one of the best preserved and protected zones of the Pacaya Samiria National Reserve. Get into a canoe and paddle on the river. With a bit of luck, you’ll even have the chance to swim in the jungle river along with the famous pink dolphins! As the night breaks, join your guide on a night safari to look for animals that shy away from daylight: caimans and nocturnal birds. Overnight on the ship.

4th day piranhas fishing and night safaris

Today also begins early, but the first impressions of the Pacaya-Samiria National Park will certainly make up for it. The nature rarely appears as magnificent as in the tropics of the Amazon – especially in the early morning, at sunrise, the animal world is the most active. Take a walk on the Fundo Casual trail. You will wander over solid soil – “terra firma” – and your guide will show you different plants and animals along the way. After lunch, visit Nauta Caño Creek during the flood season between November and May, where you can kayak. Here you will fish for the famous piranhas of the Amazon! Even if the animals are not as aggressive as you might know from movies - they are precise hunters, and fishing for the animals will surely chase a little shiver down your back. During the low water season between June and October, you will continue exploring the Pucate River. Overnight on the ship.

5th day paddling tour on the Yarapa River

You start your morning by visiting an Amazon village. This is designated by approx. 500 inhabitants still quite peculiar, although it is not far from the city of Nauta. During your visit to this community you will be welcomed by smiling faces and friendly people. Learn here about the process of peeling and rubbing cassava and rice cultivation. Then go on a paddle tour on the Yarapa River. Listen to the sounds of the jungle and keep your eyes open to absorb the green nature around you and discover animals in the trees and river. The river branch, which leads to a beautiful lagoon, is the ideal place to spend the afternoon searching for birds and different species of monkeys roaming in the trees. Your guide will help you discover the animals in the foliage, but also look closely – after some time in the jungle and with a well-trained eye, you may already be able to make discoveries in the trees yourself! Overnight on the ship.

6th day Melipona bees and the underwater world of Amazonia

After breakfast, it is already time to disembark. Back at the port of Nauta, a vehicle is waiting to take you to the airport of Iquitos. Along the way, you’ll find an exciting, interactive introduction to the world of stingless Melipona bees and their important role in the Amazon ecosystem. Learn more about these fascinating animals and their contribution to preserving the region’s unique biodiversity. Afterwards we will visit the Amazonian Research Centre for Ornamental Fish. There you will discover the impressive underwater world of the Amazon – from the gigantic Arapaima to the colorful freshwater rays. This last item on the agenda offers a wonderful farewell to the extraordinary biodiversity of the Amazon. In the late afternoon, arrive at Iquitos airport and board the plane to Lima. Overnight in Lima.

7th day Departure or bathing in Máncora

Together with your guide, you will get to know colonial Lima this morning as part of a private guided tour.

Then you have the choice: Either your trip through Peru ends today in Lima or you travel by plane to Máncora, a Peruvian fishing village and popular surfing area that will beautify your life with kilometers of fine sandy beaches. Palm trees line the beach, and the waves can be heard rushing from the terrace of your luxurious accommodation. They spend the night in ArennasThe resort will pamper you with plenty of space. Your Junior Villa is generously designed with 90 square meters. You will also enjoy a private courtyard with an outdoor shower and your own pool on your terrace. The fantastic view is included. If you prefer to tackle one or the other day a little more actively – no problem: The Arennas offers excursions for whale watching, horse riding on the beach or deep sea fishing. The hotel's restaurant provides delicacies from the sea for your physical well-being. Overnight in Máncora.

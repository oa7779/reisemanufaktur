Peru Individual Tour - Mystical Peru

Individual trip to mystical Peru with your specialist América Special Tours. Ask and experience now!

Go in search of traces of the Inca and feel the atmosphere in its sacred sites – above all Machu Picchu. You will experience the nature in the Andes especially intensively as you hike along the Salkantay Trail. Here you will visit the Incaruines of Tarawasi and the beautiful Humantay Lake. You do not have to give up luxury at any time. After the hikes, relax at the exclusive Mountain Lodges and enjoy views of Lake Titicaca, the highest lake on earth from your suite.

1st day Arrival in Lima

Welcome to Peru! Your journey begins in the capital Lima, whose old town – legacy of the colonial era – has been a World Heritage Site since 1991. Your German-speaking tour guide will welcome you at the airport and take you to your hotel, where you can first get fresh. Afterwards, you will embark on a 4-hour round trip through the historic and modern districts of Lima. You will first visit the colonial city center with the “Plaza de Armas”, the historic main square where many magnificent buildings are located, such as the Government Palace, the City Hall, the Archbishop’s Palace and the Cathedral. Then visit the San Francisco Monastery. After the visit, we continue to the colonial building Casa Aliaga. It was built in 1535 and is one of the oldest houses on the continent. Finally, visit the modern residential districts of San Isidro and Miraflores. Your last stop is the “Parque del Amor” (Park of Love) in Miraflores. It is located directly on the Pacific Ocean and from the promenade you have a wonderful view of the sea and the coast. Overnight in Lima.

Day 2 Cusco - Capital of the Incas

You leave Lima today; your chauffeur will take you to the airport for your onward flight to Cusco. Immediately after your arrival, set out to explore the city in the middle of the Peruvian Andean Highlands (3,400 meters). As the former capital of the vast Inca Empire, Cusco is one of the most culturally important cities in South America. First, visit the center of the Inca Empire, the main square “Plaza de Armas”. There you will find the cathedral, which was built on the ruins of the Inca Palace. Look closely at the painting of the Lord’s Supper – do you discover the differences that the artist has allowed himself to incorporate? Continue to the old town with beautifully restored buildings in colonial style, which today partly house hotels, restaurants and shops. Another highlight is a visit to Koricancha, the ancient Inca Sun Temple. The temple walls are said to have been surrounded by gold and silver. Here you will have plenty of time to explore the building before finally making your way to your hotel. Overnight in Cusco.

3rd day excursion to the salt works of Maras

Your excursion today begins with the visit of the little ones, ca. 28 kilometers north of Cusco city Chinchero. It is located at an altitude of 3,762 meters and its home mountain is the snow-capped chicon. The main square has an enormous Inca wall with 10 large trapezoids in very good condition. It is also home to one of the most important colonial churches in the region with baroque altars and richly painted walls. Especially recommended is a visit to the Sunday market of Chinchero, where you can immerse yourself in everyday life. Continue the tour to the ruins of Moray. Here the Incas are said to have operated a large agricultural laboratory, where they were able to produce different microclimates and thus breed a large number of different grain species. Near Moray lies the picturesque village of Maras, which served as a transhipment point for salt during the Inca period and was a resort for the wealthy. The salt from the salt pans is still traditionally extracted today, as in Inca times. Go with your guide on a little time travel. In the evening, get to know your guide for the upcoming trekking adventure, who will give you a short introduction about what awaits you in the next few days. Overnight in Cusco.

4th day trekking along the Salkantay Trail

Today it is time to get up early! Already at 7:00 am you will be picked up at your hotel and set off for Salkantay Lodge in Soraypampa. Along the way, visit the Tarawasi Incaruines, which are about 1.5 hours from Cusco. In this place, both the living and the dead found a resting place: Tarawasi was an interim station for Incabots on their way to Cusco. Chinchaysuyo – and offered tombs for mummies. Ceremonies and rituals were also performed here. In the small mountain village of Mollepata, take a short coffee break before driving to Marcoccasa for another 30 minutes. From here, your 6-hour hike starts on the “Camino Real”, the “Royal Path”, up to your first lodge. At noon, fortify yourself on the way with a picnic. After arriving at the lodge, you will first set up in your room before you can put your feet up with tea and pastries. In front of the fireplace, listen to an introduction from your trekking leader. Overnight at Salkantay Lodge.

Day 5 Hike to Lake Humantay

Today, for acclimatization, take a hike to Lake Humantay, which is impressive and peaceful in the middle of the mountain landscape. The water is fed by the surrounding glaciers of Humantay. If you prefer to relax, you can rest at the lodge. For lunch, return from your hike to the lodge and can arrange the afternoon as you wish. In the evening, your guide prepares you for tomorrow. Overnight at Salkantay Lodge.

Day 6 Up to Salkantay Pass

Today is certainly the hardest part, but also the highlight of the trekking route. After an early start to the day, hike up the Rio Blanco Valley. The highest point of the route is the Salkantay Pass, which stands at 4,638 meters in altitude. Here you can wander the view over the snow-capped peaks of the Vilcabamba mountain range and look for the imposing Andean condors that can be found here. From the Salkantay Pass, you will descend to Wayra Lodge, where you will spend the night. Overnight at Wayra Lodge.

7th day Colpa Lodge

Today you will have a leisurely breakfast and then descend along the Salkantay River through an increasingly green landscape. Upon arrival at Colpa Lodge, you will be greeted by a traditional “Pachamanca”, a Peruvian feast cooked in an earth oven with hot stones and made from stacked meat and vegetables. The rest of the afternoon you can relax and enjoy the magnificent view. Take advantage of the inviting "Outdoor Jacuzzis". Overnight at Colpa Lodge.

8th day Lucma Lodge

Today’s hike takes you along the Santa Teresa river valley, past plantations of bananas, granadilla (a kind of sweet maracuja), avocado and coffee. Enjoy a warm picnic near the river. After a short break, hike for another hour to a meeting point where you will be expected by a private vehicle that will take you to the starting point of the Llactapata Inca Trail (about 30 minutes drive). From this point, hike to Lucma Lodge, located in the middle of an avocado plantation. Here you can meet the owner, who will gladly answer all your questions. Overnight at Lucma Lodge.

9th Day Aguas Calientes

After a hearty breakfast, you will tackle the last stage of the trek. It goes 2 to 3 hours uphill to the Llactapata Pass at 2,736 meters. From here you have a breathtaking view of Machu Picchu from a distance. You will visit the nearby archaeological site Llactapata and then enjoy lunch overlooking Machu Picchu. You start the descent through dense bamboo forests and coffee plantations up to the river Aobamba. Take the train to Aguas Calientes, the small village at the foot of Machu Picchu. Here you check into your hotel and celebrate the challenges of the last few days. Overnight in Aguas Calientes.

Day 10 Mysterious Machu Picchu

To reach your current destination – Machu Picchu – take the shuttle bus from Aguas Calientes. Machu Picchu is said to have been built in the fifteenth century under the reign of the Inca ruler Pachacútec Yupanqui. To this day, science is divided as to what the city with its terraces connected by innumerable steps should serve at all: as a retreat for the king, as a sanctuary for the “virgins of the sun”, or even as a place of veneration for the sacred landscape around the summit? Machu Picchu is surrounded by an aura of mystery: the city was "lost" for many years before it was "rediscovered" at the beginning of the 20th century. For the descendants of the Incas, however, the place has never really been forgotten – it is still their most sacred site today. Upon arrival at the site, join your walking guide for a 2-hour guided tour and then have free time to explore the ruins on your own. For a late lunch, head down to Aguas Calientes and then board the train to Ollantaytambo. At the train station, you will be expected by a private vehicle that will take you back to Cusco. Overnight stay in Cusco

Day 11 Panoramic Train to Puno

Due to the rough terrain with roads, some of which run only a few inches away from deep slopes, a journey through the Andes is often more adventure than a walk. On the other hand, you can move around very relaxed with the Titicaca Train, which takes you comfortably through the impressive landscape of the High Andes. Imposing peaks and deep gorges pass you as you eat your lunch in the dining car and take a leisurely tea break in the afternoon. The desire for high-production is satisfied in the bar cart (but drinks are not included in the price). After about ten hours of driving, you will reach the port city of Puno on the shores of Lake Titicaca, where you can catch a breath after your eventful round trip in an impressive natural setting. Overnight in Puno.

Day 12 On the shores of Lake Titicaca

With an area of over 8,000 square meters, Titicaca is South America’s second largest lake. Around Puno there is a protected area that protects the biodiversity of the flora and fauna of the water. Your lodge offers various excursions with an English-speaking guide, which you can take at no extra cost. For example, you can visit the Necropolis of Sillustani, take a hike on an ancient path of the Incas, visit the Uros Islands or the island Taquile or learn more about life on the shore of the lake on the beach of Socca or on a rowing boat trip through the reeds. Overnight in Puno.

Day 13 Puno - Juliaca - Lima

Unfortunately, you are leaving Peru today. Your chauffeur will take you to Juliaca Airport for your return flight to Lima and international connection.

Chile — Beneath a Sky Full of Stars

Tailored Chile trip with América Special Tours. Inquire now, get personal advice and experience the glory of the stars in Chile for yourself.

Close to the sky – not many places convey this feeling as much as the stations of this journey. Chile is home to some of the best regions for stargazing in the world: In the mostly cloudless skies over the Atacama Desert and the Elqui Valley, for example, the stars sparkle as bright as you would never experience as a Central European urbanite. It is not for nothing that some of the world's most prestigious observatories are located in Chile. This journey is dedicated to both heaven and earth: you observe the day and night sky through powerful telescopes and enjoy evenings under the shimmer of the Milky Way. In addition, you can expect impressive landscapes that sometimes seem to be out of this world, including steaming geysers or the lunar landscape of the Atacama Desert.

Trip Information

Itinerary

10 days Santiago de Chile – Cajon del Maipo – Elqui Valley – Ovalle – San Pedro de Atacama

Your Trip Highlights

  • Private, German-speaking tour guide and own chauffeur
  • Carefully selected hotels for you
  • Beds that provide views of the starry sky
  • Observation of the day sky by a solar telescope in Santiago
  • Guided tour of a Pisco distillery with tasting
  • Hikes through unique nature reserves
  • Special souvenir photos during a visit to the Cruz del Sur Observatory
  • Oasis in the Atacama Desert: full board with delicate food and wine
  • Excursions to Tatio geysers and desert salt lakes
  • Sunset over the Moon Valley

Your astronomical journey through Chile begins in the capital Santiago. Immediately behind the city, the Andes rise and thus form an imposing backdrop. Santiago de Chile is for the visitor one of the most manageable capitals in Latin America. Relatively few high-rise buildings, but extensive settlements give Santiago an expansive character. Upon arrival in Santiago you will be welcomed and taken to your hotel, where you can first get fresh. Together with your guide, you will then take a trip to the Maipo Valley, where almost all of the country’s historic wineries were created – thanks to the Mediterranean climate, the best conditions for viticulture prevail here. You will visit the Undurraga estate. It was one of the first wineries to export its wine to North America in 1903. During the visit of the winery, you will visit the historic estate and the old bodegas. You will learn more about wine cultivation, storage and production of the noble drop. A wine tasting will conclude the visit, as well as enjoying the tranquility while walking through the estate’s park, which was created at the end of the 19th century under the direction of a Frenchman. Overnight in Santiago de Chile.

Today you will take a trip to the imposing Andes near Santiago. The Cajon del Maipo is a green valley in the middle of the Andean chain and a popular local recreation area. In addition to numerous hikes, friends of extreme sports come here at full expense: rafting, canyoning, bungee jumping makes every Andrenalin heart beat faster. But also high on horseback or by bicycle the special landscape can be explored. You have chosen an easy hike to the viewing plateau. Start your tour at the foot of Las Animas waterfall up to the viewpoint. With a bit of luck, catch a glimpse of the King of the Sky, the Andean Condor. In the gorge “Manzanito” you have a spectacular view of the valley and the surrounding mountains. After a breather, return to the starting point of your hike. In the afternoon you will reach your hotel.

Today, leave Santiago behind and reach La Serena after a domestic flight north, where you are already expected. Your chauffeur will take you to the Elqui Valley, a green oasis surrounded by rugged mountains and dry landscape. Here is not only one of the most important wine regions in Chile, but – directly on one of the earth meridians – an ideal area for stargazing. The sky is star clear and cloudless almost every night. Whether this is the reason why especially many UFOs were sighted here, remains to be seen. However, it is undeniable that the Elqui Valley is surrounded by a touch of mystery. Before you dedicate yourself to the stars the next day, visit a piscode distillery today where you can track how wine is turned into the famous South American grape snap. After a walk through the cellars, you will of course also try one of the noble drops. In the evening, you can watch the stars from your hotel’s in-house observatory. You will stay in the only hotel in the Southern Hemisphere that is completely dedicated to astronomy. The rooms are designed to allow you to observe the night sky from your bed. Overnight in the Elqui Valley.

On the way to the next stop of your trip, visit the Bosque de Fray Jorge National Park, particularly known for its fog forests. The coastal fog accumulates on the mountain slopes and thus enables a green oasis in the middle of a desert-like area. After learning more about the background of this World Heritage site in the Information Office, take a walk through the protected area that takes you through varied landscapes. The destination of your journey today is the Limarí valley in the middle of a region known worldwide for the best conditions for stargazing. On more than 300 nights a year, the cloud-clear sky opens up a unique view into space. In the evening you can see for yourself: you visit the Cruz del Sur observatory (Cross of the South) in Combarbalá. The observatory has up to 16-inch telescopes, four domes to which photo cameras can be connected and an amphitheater for stargazing. Overnight in Ovalle.

The provincial town of Ovalle lies off the big tourist trails and is therefore still very authentic. The region is known not only for its phenomenal stargazing opportunities, but also as a world-class wine region. The people here live in simple conditions, mainly from the cultivation of grapes and agriculture. Numerous residents of the city work in the large mines in the north of the country and live only temporarily in Ovalle. During a city tour, you will get an authentic insight into the lives of the people. Visit the Modelo Market, one of the largest agricultural markets in the region. At the main square you pay a visit to the Archaeological Museum and then enjoy a typical lunch. In the afternoon it means to see, smell and taste with all your senses. You will visit one of the nearby wineries. You get a guided tour of the factory grounds and conclude with a tasting of the wines. In the evening you will be returned to your hotel.

Few places on Earth are as closely related to astronomy as the Atacama Desert: Several large observatories observe the skies from here, and the European Extremely Large Telescope with a main mirror with a diameter of 39 meters is being built. In addition, several Mars probes have already been tested in the desert. In the next few days, the Awasi will spoil you with exquisite comfort and warm hospitality. It is located in the heart of the small village of San Pedro de Atacama and offers you a private, family atmosphere with only 8 rooms. The all-round service of Awasi includes not only delicate meals and drinks included in the price, among other things from the exquisite wine collection, but also a separate guide with a vehicle per room, who is well versed in the area and together with you the excursion program individually put together. You will stay in a traditionally built round cottage made of clay and wood, which combines luxury and rustic coziness in the best way. After the long journey, treat yourself to a massage, a bath in the pool and a cooling under your private outdoor shower. Then end your first day in the desert with a good dinner. Overnight in San Pedro de Atacama.

Today you set off with your guide for a first exploration tour: South of San Pedro lies against the fantastic backdrop of snow-capped volcanic peaks of the Salar de Atacama, a salt lake of about 3000 square kilometers. This seemingly hostile environment is an intact retreat for flamingos. They observe the beautiful birds while searching for food. Then follow the Ruta 23, an important pass road to Argentina. After some time the road suddenly leads steeply uphill. You can observe how the landscape changes with each kilometer and with increasing altitude. Barren stone deserts with occasionally luminous, tiny flowers are detached from the steppe-like landscape of the Andean highlands. Again and again there are impressive views of the expanse of the white flickering Salar de Atacama, which lies deep below you. You cross a mountain crest and suddenly you have an overwhelming view of the twin lagoons Meñiques and Miscanti. In the evening you can set off together with a small group of hotel guests for stargazing in the middle of the desert. Here you will study the stars of the southern sky with a high-performance telescope. Overnight in San Pedro de Atacama.

Today you have to get up early to watch one of the most impressive natural spectacles in the area: It is best to leave at around 04:00 in the morning for the Tatio geysers, which are about 90 kilometers north. You will receive breakfast in the form of a lunchbox. Just in time for sunrise you reach the high valley of Tatio at 4321 meters altitude. It hisses, blubbers and steams tremendously up here at the highest geyser field in the world. Getting up early is worthwhile, because especially in the backlight of the first rays of the sun you can shoot fantastic photos of this natural spectacle, which will give you the feeling that they are on a foreign planet. With a bit of luck, watch Vicuñas, the wild relatives of the lamas. For their fur, which is considered the second finest tissue after silk, the petite animals have been hunted almost to extinction in recent decades. In Chile, vicuñas are now under nature conservation and multiply again magnificently. On your way downhill, visit the beautiful Baños de Puritama, hot springs located in a green gorge where you can enjoy a relaxing swim. Overnight in San Pedro de Atacama.

After you have started the last day so early, you can sleep in today and let it go very quietly. Towards evening, set off for a small bicycle tour with your guide to watch the sunset over Valle de la Luna, the valley of the moon. It offers fantastic views over bizarrely shaped desert rocks to the backdrop of snow-capped volcanoes on the horizon, the most impressive of which is the perfectly shaped cone of Licancabur. The last rays of the sun give the silent landscape bright color shades from orange over red to violet. Overnight in San Pedro de Atacama.

Today, your exploration of heaven and earth in Chile is coming to an end. After a final breakfast in the desert, you will be taken to the airport and travel back home via Santiago.

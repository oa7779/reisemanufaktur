Embark on a fascinating journey through Brazil and discover the highlights of this diverse country. Experience the energy of Rio de Janeiro, the massive Iguaçu waterfalls, the Amazon rainforest adventure and the cultural richness of Salvador de Bahia. Day 1 Rio de Janeiro: Welcome to the Cidade Maravilhosa

Welcome to Brazil! At the airport of Rio de Janeiro you will be warmly welcomed by your private guide and chauffeur. Rio is located on the Guanabara Bay in southeast Brazil and involuntarily raises images of lightly clothed Sambat dancers and the Copacabana’s dream beach. Its name Rio de Janeiro – the “river of January” – comes from Portuguese sailors who discovered the bay in January 1502 and thought it was the mouth of a river. After the transfer to your hotel, the rest of the day is at your disposal – arrange the afternoon according to your wishes. Relax, take a first walk along the Copacabana or simply enjoy the Brazilian flair. Overnight in Rio de Janeiro.

Day 2 Rio de Janeiro: Statue of Christ and Sugar Loaf

As part of a city tour, take the cog train through the lush Tijuca rainforest up to Corcovado Mountain, where the world-famous statue of Christ “Christ the Redeemer” awaits you. From virtually every place in Rio, you’ll see one of the country’s most popular postcard motifs: the 710-meter-high Mount Corcovado (the “humpback”) with the 30-meter-high statue of Christo Redentor. This was voted one of the “Seven New Wonders of the World”. Under it stretches the Tijuca National Park, the largest urban forest in the world. Until 1981, the monument was the highest statue of Christ in the world – then it had to leave this place to the blessing Christ of Manado. From there you can enjoy a spectacular panoramic view over Rio de Janeiro. For your lunch, you can choose to visit a Churrascaria, a typical Brazilian barbecue restaurant. In the afternoon, visit the famous Sugar Loaf. The Glockenberg owes its name to its appearance, which is reminiscent of cone shapes that were used for sugar production in the 16th century. It is located at the western entrance of Guanabara Bay and measures 396 meters. The gondola takes you upstairs, from where you can enjoy the view of the city. Overnight in Rio de Janeiro.

Day 3 Rio de Janeiro: A day for your own discoveries

Today is free and offers the opportunity to discover Rio on your own or take part in one of the optional excursions. We recommend a full-day excursion to the charming city of Petrópolis, former summer residence of the Brazilian imperial family, with a visit to the palace and cathedral. Alternatively, take a half-day excursion to the Olympic Boulevard with modern architecture, impressive street art and a visit to the Museum of the Future and the Rio Art Museum. If you want to experience Brazilian joie de vivre up close, you have the opportunity to visit a Samba school – including thrilling rhythms and a caipirinha tasting. Overnight in Rio de Janeiro.

Day 4 From the big city to the Iguazú waterfalls

Today you leave the metropolis to explore Brazil’s natural beauties. A domestic flight will take you to Foz do Iguaçu, where you will be warmly welcomed again. Together you will continue towards one of the most impressive natural spectacles in South America: The Iguazú Waterfalls. The falls stretch across the southern border of Brazil and northern Argentina in the middle of the national park of the same name. The Brazilian side of the Falls offers you a wonderful panoramic view of the rushing beauty. During a hike, you can take a picture of yourself. Your hotel, Belmond das Cataratas, is also worth a visit regardless of its location near the falls: The vast terrain includes tropical gardens, tennis courts, a large pool, palm trees and almost 200 guest rooms and suites. The furnishings are thoroughly noble and do without overdesigned bells and whistles – this is how the Portuguese-inspired elements come into play: hand painted tiles, marble and furniture made of heavy wood. Overnight in Iguaçú.

Day 5: A panorama of water, forest and wonder

Worldwide, there are only a handful of other natural wonders as impressive as the Iguazú Falls. You too will surely be amazed by the sight of the 275 thunderous waterfalls. Today you are dedicating yourself to the Argentine side. Your walk will guarantee you close contact with the rocks and water. Especially the main waterfall – the devil’s throat – is absolutely impressive. Overnight in Iguaçú.

Day 6 An opera house in the Amazon

Arriving in Manaus, your new guide is already waiting for you and will take you to your hotel. Manaus is the capital of the state of Amazonas and lies at the mouth of the Rio Negro in the Amazon. The attraction of the city is actually the jungle surrounding it – it is considered to be the region with the greatest biodiversity in the world. Another attraction is the Teatro Amazonas, a magnificent theater building in the middle of the rainforest. Overnight in Manaus.

7th day Piranhas and the Amazon at night

Your private chauffeur will take you to Anavilhanas Lodge. If you have checked in, make a biting acquaintance right at the beginning of your stay. In wooden canoes you go on piranha hunting on the Igarapé. Even if the animals are not as dangerous as movies make us believe from time to time – a small goosebumps will surely give you the skillful robbers. An intimate experience awaits you in the evening when it begins to dawn: In a canoe where two people can be seated, you will once again sail the river and watch the scenery change in the darkness. With a little luck, you will encounter a crocodile. But even if not: The nocturnal soundscape will not be forgotten so quickly. Overnight at Anavilhanas Lodge.

8th Day Experience with Pink River Dolphins

A hike will take you through the rainforest. Your knowledgeable guide will tell you all about the trees and fruits you pass by. In addition, you will find out which plants provide medical assistance in an emergency and how the locals orient themselves based on the weather conditions. Later you will make an animal acquaintance: You will visit a floating platform, where the pink river dolphins come close to search for food. If you wish, you can swim a round with the animals and feed them. Later in the day, you can buy something for your loved ones at home in the craft center or simply admire wooden toys and furnishings. Overnight at Anavilhanas Lodge.

9th day visit to indigenous peoples

Today you will set off by boat to navigate some of the countless canals and rivers of the Anavilhanas archipelago and visit some islands. After all, you are in one of the largest river archipelagos in the world: Almost 400 islands and islets can be seen in low water. They are all overgrown by virgin forest, which becomes a floating forest during the rainy season. On the Rio Negro you can reach the municipality of Cabocla in Tiririca. Take the opportunity to share their lifestyle and the traditions of the locals. Your guide will be happy to help you with your understanding. In the afternoon, return to the lodge. Overnight at Anavilhanas Lodge.

Day 10: A Contemplative Sunrise

At the end of your time in the Amazon, you will enjoy a sunrise over the rainforest. Early in the morning, head to an island and watch as the first birds of the day swing into the air and the new day begins. Afterwards it is time to say goodbye, because your chauffeur will take you back to Manaus. Overnight in Manaus.

Day 11 Salvador: The Black Heart of Brazil

From the airport in Manaus, leave for Salvador. In Salvador, you will already be expected by your new guide and chauffeur and accompanied to your hotel. Salvador was the first capital of the country and is the third largest city in Brazil. Since it was built on a mountain range, there is an upper and lower city. Overnight in Salvador.

12th day Historic coastal town with gilded church

Salvador offers architecturally significant ensembles as well as a rich culture. The city is a hotspot for cuisine, music and religion: this world will delight you. After pickup, your exclusive discovery tour begins in the Barra district. Enjoy views of the Atlantic Ocean and the Barra Lighthouse in the Santo Antônio da Barra Fortress – the first lighthouse on the American continent. During a walk, immerse yourself in the maritime atmosphere before heading to the Dique do Tororó. There the imposing sculptures of the Orixás are reflected in the water, powerful symbols of Afro-Brazilian spirituality. On the way, you will pass the modern Fonte Nova football stadium. Afterwards, the route will take you to the lower town to the peninsula of Itapagipe, where life is deliberately slower. You will travel to the famous church of Nosso Senhor do Bonfim, one of Brazil’s most important pilgrimage sites and a strong symbol of religious devotion, closely linked to the Candomblé tradition. Another highlight is the Mont Serrat fortress with its spectacular panoramic views. The final stage is the Ribeira district, followed by the iconic Lacerda elevator and the Mercado Modelo market, where history, crafts and local culture come together. Overnight in Salvador.

13th Day Goodbye!

Even the best time is over. Today you have to say goodbye to Brazil. Your chauffeur will take you to the airport, where you will begin your journey home. Even if your holiday is now over: The beautiful souvenir photos and small souvenirs will always remind you of the time in South America.

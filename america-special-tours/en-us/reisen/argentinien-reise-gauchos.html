Feel the wind blowing through your hair as you ride on horseback through Argentina’s pampa. Galops carry the hooves below you to the land of the gauchos, where unforgettable experiences await you. On your journey, you will get to know the exciting life of the gauchos and experience the impressive landscapes of Argentina. Be pampered with Argentine delicacies in traditional and authentic estancias and immerse yourself in the world of gauchos. The happiness of the earth lies on the backs of the horses.

Day 1 Bienvenidos - Welcome to the Land of Gauchos

Today you will begin your exciting journey to the most beautiful places in Argentina, which will take you to the wild pampa. There you will meet real gauchos, fantastic horses and indescribably good food. In this little paradise, your private chauffeur will take you directly upon arrival at the airport of Buenos Aires. As soon as you leave the city, the wildly romantic Pampa draws you into its spell. From a distance you can already recognize the beautiful Estancia La Bandada. The Estancia is truly a small paradise: in addition to the buildings in the typical Pampa style as well as the large suites, a refreshing pool and the seemingly infinite meadows, fields and forests ensure an absolute feel-good atmosphere. After you have made yourself a bit comfortable, you go to the fantastic Pampa. You pick one of the great horses and after familiarizing yourself, you set off with one of the gauchos of the Estancia for a unique ride. As the sun slowly sinks between the tall grasses, you feel what freedom really means! Overnight at Buenos Aires.

Day 2 Behind the Scenes: Fantastic Country Life

You will be awakened by the Argentine sun and start another exciting day with a very delicious breakfast. Well strengthened, you are ready to take a look behind the scenes. An exciting insight into the real, Argentinean country life awaits you. They saddle the horses and accompany the gauchos on their daily work. Gauchos are the traditional, mounted cattle herders in the South American pampa. Equipped with a Lasso, you help them with cattle driving. The sweat-driven work makes one thing above all: fun! Surrounded by wonderful nature, gallop freely like the wind through the pampa and enjoy together with the gauchos a homemade and energetic mate. After your siesta, another highlight awaits you: an exclusive polo hour. The elite sport wants to be learned, because it is usually more demanding than it looks. Under professional guidance, you will learn all about polo. In the end, there will certainly be some players to put what they have learned into practice in a small match. No matter who wins, you will all celebrate together at the end with an excellent dinner and a delightfully cool Quilmes beer or a glass of full-bodied Malbec wine. Overnight at Buenos Aires.

Day 3 - Estancia Los Potreros

After an extensive breakfast, today you will continue your dreamlike journey in the footsteps of the gauchos. A short domestic flight will take you inland, to Cordoba. Outside the city, in the transition area between the mountain ranges of the Sierra and the two plains of Pampa and Chaco lies the Estancia Los Potreros. This Estancia is an absolute must for any horse lover! Upon arrival, you will already be welcomed by your hosts Louisa and Kevin Begg. Then the farmers show you their jewelry, the cattle farm. Particularly unique makes the Estancia Los Potreros its breathtaking views. Due to the slightly elevated position, you look across the expanse of the plain and onto the mighty peaks of the snow-capped Andes. Here you feel gripped by a comfortable feeling of wild romantic coziness. The overall picture is rounded off by the traditional Argentinian country style in perfect harmony with modern luxury. Now it is time to get to know one of the main attractions: The beautiful horses! Strong criollos, gentle Peruvian pas offerings, balanced quarter horses and agile and fast Poloponies. The herd of the Estancia is a feast for the eyes. Overnight at Cordoba.

Day 4 The Happiness of the Earth...

It is on the back of the horses. After a leisurely breakfast, you saddle your horse together with one of the gauchos and set off for a ride in the beautiful surroundings of the farm. Back on the Estancia, you will also find a delicious asado with cuts of meat of the best, Argentinian quality roasted over the fire. Delicious! After a well-deserved siesta, you decide for yourself how you want to arrange your afternoon. You are spoilt for choice between relaxing or sweating activities, such as an adventurous mountain bike tour or hike, another riding trip or helping out in the field. For relaxation, you can use the spa and fitness area with a large pool. At the end of a wonderful day, your chef will spoil you with delicious Argentine specialties. Overnight at Cordoba.

Day 5 Like real gauchos

Strengthen yourself extensively at breakfast, because today again an exciting day awaits you. They accompany the gauchos of the Estancia and support you in their daily work. They drive the cattle from one pasture to another or bring them salt. Of course, there is also some time for a small competition among real gauchos. When throwing Lasso, you can prove all your skill. A delicious picnic in the wilderness ensures the deserved break. In the afternoon you will ride back to the Estancia. In the evening, try your hand at some of Argentina’s most famous wines. Your owner is already looking forward to presenting you his beloved treasures and is eager to see how they taste. Overnight at Cordoba.

Day 6 Along the Andean Chain to Salta

You will fly from Cordoba to the north. Always along the impressive Andean chain you can reach Salta. Here your German-speaking guide and chauffeur await you to take you to your wonderful country estate. Salta is also affectionately called "La Linda" (the Pretty) and offers an abundance of historical buildings in the style of the colonial baroque like no other Argentinean city. This cultural richness is framed by a magnificent mountain backdrop. Perfectly located at the foot of the Andes, you will find the elegant House of Jasmines. The luxury boutique hotel is reminiscent of an original, noble country estate. White and brown natural tones complement the original tile floors and wooden shutters, creating a luxurious, rustic charm. In the high-quality spa it is perfectly relaxed. The kitchen will enchant you with down-to-earth, but at the same time extremely fine cuisine. It is not for nothing that the House of Jasmines is one of the excellent hotels of Relais & Chateaux. Of course, the in-house horses are always available for a ride along the mountain ranges. Overnight at Salta.

Day 7 The gorge of shells

Today, an impressive natural spectacle awaits you on a trip to Cafayate: On the way south, you pass small villages and colorful landscapes and finally cross the Quebrada de las Conchas, in German the gorge of shells. In the approximately 75 kilometers long section of the valley of the Rio Guachipas stretches a bizarre landscape with unusually shaped, different colored rock formations, which quite rightly counts as a UNESCO World Heritage site. Particularly worth seeing is the rock kettle Garganta del Diablo (devil's sword), which has a very special acoustic due to its narrow shape. Finally, you will reach the small and sunny town of Cafayate, nestled between two mountain ranges of the Andean Precordillera, surrounded by idyllic vineyards. Of course, these are not only beautiful to look at, but also produce a variety of high-quality wines. You can see for yourself today at a wine tasting at a local winery. In the afternoon, when the sun begins to sink slowly, return to Salta via the same route and experience the unique panorama of the Quebrada gorge in the evening light. Overnight in Salta.

Day 8 Stunning: The Sparkling Salt Lakes

The Humahuaca Gorge is an enormous valley in Jujuy province. The gorge is famous for its multi-colored mountains, its rock formations and historical villages located there. You will visit the village of Purmamarca, with its famous 7-colored mountain range. The small village at the foot of the "Cerro de los Siete Colores" has preserved its original charm and will enchant you not only with a magnificent view but also with its cozy atmosphere. Afterwards, take Ruta 52 via Lipan Pass (4,170 meters above sea level). This is the breaking point between the eastern and western Andes, between which lies the high altitude plateau La Puna. In the valley in between you will find the Salinas Grandes, a white, glittering salt mirror that you can recognize from a long distance by its glow. Arriving at the Salinas, you will be amazed and only on closer inspection realize that it is not about snow or ice, but about vast amounts of salt, which has collected on the surface to large, about hexagonal fragments. A truly unique natural phenomenon! After an exciting day, you will return speechlessly to Purmamarca. Overnight in Purmamarca.

9th day Small colonial villages in front of colorful mountain scenery

In the morning you should take the opportunity to get up early, because in the morning light the seven-colored mountain shines, thanks to minerals in the most beautiful yellow, green, purple and red shades. Together with your German-speaking guide, after a refreshing breakfast, you will explore this wonderful area and first go to the small colonial village of Tilcara. There you will visit Pucará, an ancient indigenous fortress framed by a spectacular landscape of exotic cactus and colorful mountains. Explore the ruins and the Archaeological Museum on a walk. Continue on to Humahuaca, where you can stroll through the handicraft market and grab the last souvenirs. On the way back, visit the city of Jujuy before ending your last evening at the luxurious Salta estate. Overnight in Salta.

10 Days of Adios Argentinia! See you soon!

Beautiful and exciting days are behind you. They looked behind the scenes and discovered Argentina from its wild side. At heart, you will return home as a gaucho and will always remember your unique journey to the most beautiful places in Argentina.

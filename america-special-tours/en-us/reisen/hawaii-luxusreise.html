Luxury Hawaii — An Island Escape

Hawaii Luxury Tour: Explore the exotic islands of Hawaii with their volcanoes, waterfalls and beaches on this customized car rental tour with América Special Tours.

17 days

Your trip idea

A sample itinerary—tailored to you

Aloha and welcome to Hawaii! With the rental car you conquer four of the Hawaiian islands, which can hardly be surpassed in terms of versatility and dreamlike nature experiences. Start your journey in Honolulu on Oahu Island and familiarize yourself with Polynesian culture. Travel on to Big Island via island flight and enjoy an excellent dinner at sunset as part of a small cruise. With the garden island Kaui, breathtaking beaches, steep cliffs, waterfalls and one or the other sea creature await you on a catamaran ride. The island hopping round off with your stay on Maui and enjoy fabulous views on a helicopter flight over Haleakala Crater. Be guided by your adventurous spirit and enjoy unforgettable moments in the Pacific State of the United States - Hawaii.

After a long flight, today you reach your exotic longing destination: Hawaii. Honolulu on the island of Oahu is the capital of the state of Hawaii and forms the vibrant center of the island with its famous Waikiki Beach. Upon arrival at the airport, you will be received by your guide and receive valuable information for your trip. Afterwards, pick up your rental car and drive to your hotel on your own. Overnight on the island of Oahu.

Thrown by the sun, embark on an island tour through Oahu with your rental car today. Take the day to explore the laid-back Honolulu metropolis and historic downtown. The local cuisine is varied, so there is certainly something for every taste. Afterwards, head to Pearl Harbor and visit the monument erected at the site of the attack on December 7, 1941 in honor of the dead soldiers. In the two adjacent museums you can learn more about military history. Stroll through the gardens of Dole Plantation and take the train through the plantation. If you are interested in the culture of Polynesia, you will certainly get your money's worth at the Polynesian Cultural Center. Alternatively, take a hike to Diamond Head, an extinct volcanic crater. After the effort, enjoy a spectacular view of the island’s coastline, which is away from the wind. Of course, the many white sand beaches of the island should also be on your program today. Stay in the calm waters of Waikiki Beach or enjoy your time at Sunset Beach, Waimea Bay or Hanauma Bay. Overnight on the island of Oahu.

After exploring the unique island of Oahu on your own yesterday, today we will take you on a small hiking adventure. It takes you through a private tropical rainforest in the Manoa Valley, where you will experience it with its typical plants, trees and flowers. The hiking trail also offers you unique views of glittering waterfalls. Perhaps your local guide will also reveal one or the other secret of Hawaii’s breathtaking scenery. Overnight on the island of Oahu.

Today, take a short island flight and fly from Honolulu to Big Island, Hawaii’s youngest island. You might already see the huge Kilauea Caldera crater from the plane. Big Island is the largest island in the Hawaiian island chain and impresses with a natural diversity that can not be found on any of the other islands. Here you have the unique opportunity to experience almost all climates in the world. With your rental car you also have the opportunity to get to know the different regions on this island and to be captivated by the beauty. Enjoy the rest of the day at your free disposal. Overnight on Big Island.

There is much to discover on Big Island: beaches, volcanoes and lush tropical rainforest. With the Hamakua Coast you will find in the northeast of the island one of the most scenic areas of the island. Here you will find not only breathtaking waterfalls, green valleys and tropical rainforest but also gardens full of exotic plants. In the area of the Hamakua Coast, sugar raw was mainly planted in the 19th and 20th centuries. Today, you will find a lively city that boasts many shops and restaurants as well as art galleries and numerous museums. To the east of the island is Puna, a region traversed by lava formations and black sand beaches. The bath in one of the geothermal basins is a good idea. Do you want to dive or snorkel? Then embark on a small water adventure and marvel at Mantas up close. The coast of Kona is sheltered from the wind, so here you will find the ideal place for snorkeling, diving and sailing. For today we recommend you to discover Big Island by boat. From the water you will get a nice impression of the coast of the island. Then relax on one of the beaches of Hawaii’s main island. Overnight on Big Island.

Enjoy today on your own with your rental car. In the evening, a very special dinner awaits you. Along the Kona coast you can take the boat approx. 12 miles to Captain Cook’s memorial as a historian shows you over 50 landmarks and historic sites along the way. You will arrive at Kealakekua Bay, where you will be served a typical Hawaiian dinner. Accompanied by live music, enjoy a beautiful sunset. The approximately three-hour dinner cruise includes buffet and a free cocktail. Overnight on Big Island.

For the following two days, Hawaii Volcanoes National Park is on your agenda. You will find here a bizarre landscape that was or is shaped by still active volcanoes. About 45 minutes from the city of Hilo, you can witness one of Hawaii’s most fascinating natural wonders. There are two volcanoes in the park, one of which is one of the most active in the world. Visitors are provided with a 240km network of trails. Here you have the opportunity to walk through volcanic craters and desert-like lava fields but also through rainforests. In 1987, the park was recognized by UNESCO as a World Heritage Site – for its unique landscape and diverse nature. Start your parking stay at the Kilauea Visitor Center, where you can not only find maps to take with you and updates on the hiking trails and activities of the volcanoes, but also book a tour guided by a ranger. Then follow the ring road to get to the main attractions such as Kilauea Overlook, Halemaumau Crater or Jaggar Museum. Overnight on Big Island.

Today it is important to say goodbye to Big Island because a short island flight will take you on to Kauai, which is the smallest island in Hawaii, unlike Big Island. It also bears the name ‘Garden Island’. Rugged coastlines, endless sandy beaches, tropical rainforests and majestic mountain peaks make this island an unforgettable natural highlight. Some parts of the island are only accessible by water or air. Here, too, you will receive your rental car again and drive to your hotel on your own. Overnight in Kauai.

Although it is the smallest of the Hawaiian Islands, there is much to do and discover on Kauai: Dreamlike beaches, steep cliffs, waterfalls and breathtaking views over Waimea Canyon, kayaking on the Wailua River or snorkeling offshore are just a few of many options. Explore the island with your rental car during the following two days. Overnight in Kauai.

Today, a beautiful catamaran ride awaits you. On the Lucky Lady you will drive along the majestic coast of Na Pali. Enjoy on board a continental breakfast and delicious deli lunch. In the United States, “deli” lunch means cold food consisting of cold sausage and cheese cuts, fruits, cold pasta, cookies and non-alcoholic beverages. Your captain will tell you Hawaiian stories and legends as you glide the view to the towering cliffs, waterfalls, ocean caves, pristine beaches and remote wild coastlines. Time for snorkeling is also provided. On the way north, you can see the island of Ni’ihau and the ridges of Kokee Waimea State Park. If you are lucky, you will be accompanied by dolphins, sea turtles, seabirds and even humpback whales. The ‘Lucky Lady’ offers various seating around the main deck and on the upper viewing deck. Upholstered seating in the shade is also available. Overnight in Kauai.

Another island flight today will take you from Kauai to Maui, the ‘Island of the Valleys’. The Iao Valley State Park is home to many green valleys, roaring streams and foggy forests, giving the island its name. Maui is famous for its black sand beaches and the Haleakala volcano, which watches over the island. It is the perfect end point for your ‘island hopping’. Maui is known for being a prime location for whale watching. Treat yourself to this fascinating experience and observe these extraordinary animals up close. Another breathtaking experience is a hike up to Haleakala to witness the sunrise. Overnight in Maui.

For more than 20 years, the island of Maui has been voted the best island in the United States by the readers of the Condé Nast Traveler. Enjoy the following two days at your free disposal. Overnight in Maui.

For the last day of your Hawaiian stay, you can look forward to a special experience: A helicopter flight over Maui. During your approx. The 45-minute flight offers impressive views of the world’s largest dormant volcano, the Haleakala (literally ‘House of the Sun’) and its huge moon-like crater. The almost uninhabited "back" of the small village of Hanas in the east of the island is home to the "Seven Pools" and Lindberg's Tomb. The Hana Rainforest stretches over more than 300 square kilometers along the northern coast of Maui and is the largest tropical rainforest in the country. The tour includes views of Hana Old Town, Haleakala National Park, Kipahulu, Ke'anae Taro Fields and fabulous waterfalls on the coast. Overnight in Maui.

Even the most beautiful trip comes to an end and so it is today to say goodbye to the Hawaiian island chain. Even if your holiday is over, the precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories of your trip will accompany you forever. Look forward to letting your friends and family share your experiences in the distance at home.

US Road Trip — Florida Deluxe

Florida car rental trip: Experience the Sunshine State of the USA at your own pace with this individual round trip of América Special Tours. Ask now!

14 days

Your trip idea

A sample itinerary—tailored to you

  • Home
  • USA
  • USA - Travel Inspirations

Welcome to the sunny state of Florida! Picture book weather, endless white sand beaches and a tropical natural backdrop coupled with relaxed, Caribbean-style casualness are waiting for you. Along the way in your convertible, you will always have the sun’s rays and a slight sea breeze on your face as you pass through scenic landscapes. Via the Overseas Highway you get to the Florida Keys, where you can enjoy a snorkeling trip into the colorful underwater world of the Keys. In Palm Beach, immerse yourself in the home of the rich and beautiful. By bike, you will explore historic Naples, enjoying quiet trails, impressive architecture and exciting local stories. In the unique ecosystem of the Everglades, you can experience the natural and wildlife up close on a kayaktour. And in Miami Beach, take a walk through Little Havana with Cuban music, street art, snacks and a cocktail at the Ball & Chain.

Trip Information

Itinerary

14 Days New York – Miami – Key West – Islamorada – Palm Beach – Naples – Miami Beach

Your Trip Highlights

  • Explore the Sunshine State of Florida at the Cabriolet
  • Carefully selected hotels for you
  • Seaplane to Dry Tortugas and Snorkeling in Paradise
  • Visiting the Rich and Beautiful in Palm Beach
  • With the bicycle Naples discover nature, architecture and history
  • Kayaktour by the Everglades
  • Experience Little Havana with Cuban culture, music and specialties

Upon arrival in New York and baggage claim, you will be expected by your private driver who will take you comfortably to the heart of Manhattan. In your stylish suite with breathtaking views of the skyline, you can first arrive and relax. Whether you’re taking a leisurely stroll or ending the day at the rooftop bar overlooking the city’s rooftops, your New York adventure starts with style. Overnight in New York City.

Discover the best of New York City on a private half-day tour designed to suit your interests. After hotel pick-up, your discovery tour will begin through the unique neighborhoods such as SoHo, the Financial District or Chelsea. From the High Line to Central Park, visit famous sights and discover hidden corners along the way. Listen to exciting stories from the locals and feel the unparalleled energy of the city that never sleeps. Overnight in New York City.

Today you trade the skyscrapers of the big city for sea breezes. Your driver will take you to the airport from where you fly to Miami. After your arrival, you will take over your rental car and drive independently to your luxurious hotel directly on the sea. The metropolis of Miami offers you everything you want to start your dream vacation: wonderful beaches, exclusive restaurants, a thriving art and culture scene and a vibrant nightlife. Miami is a melting pot of the cultures that meet in different parts of the city. Everywhere in the city there is a casual and warm Latino atmosphere. Arrive in peace and enjoy your first evening in Miami. Overnight in Miami Beach.

Threatened by the sun, take your rental car on one of the most picturesque panoramic routes in the USA today. The Overseas Highway is the only road that connects the Florida Keys, a 161 km long island chain with the southernmost point in Key West. Between the Gulf of Mexico and the Atlantic Ocean, your path will take you across hundreds of small islands and 42 bridges, including the world-famous Seven Mile Bridge, which seems to drive straight across the blue-glittering waters. While dreamlike landscapes pass you, you are spoiled for choice, where you stop for a photo stop or a break. We recommend you stop for a picnic on the wide beach in Bahia Honda State Park. Finally, you reach today’s destination Key West. Here, Southernmost Point marks the southernmost point on the US mainland. Arriving in Key West, check into your hotel directly on the water and arrange the rest of the day according to your wishes. Stroll through the lively streets of Duval Street or browse small, local shops – the charm of the southernmost city in the US will instantly enchant you. Overnight in Key West.

Experience an unforgettable day trip: just about 110 kilometers off the coast of Key West, Dry Tortugas National Park – one of the most remote and pristine national parks in the USA. On a private flight by seaplane, enjoy breathtaking views over the Lower Keys, secluded coves and turquoise water. After the spectacular landing, immerse yourself directly in the crystal clear, shallow water: snorkel among colorful coral gardens and tropical fish schools – here the underwater world is one of the most beautiful of the entire Florida Keys. After a few relaxing hours on the beach and exploring the historic Fort Jefferson, you’ll fly back to Key West by seaplane – full of impressions you won’t soon forget. End the evening at a cocktail bar and enjoy the live music and unconventional atmosphere of Key West. Overnight in Key West.

After a relaxing morning, leave Key West and head north along the scenic coastal road to Islamorada. The journey takes only about two hours, but takes you to a completely different world: Islamorada is considered one of the most elegant and at the same time relaxed islands in the Florida Keys. The exclusive coastal town impresses with its quiet atmosphere, small boutiques and a strong love for nature and the sea. Upon arrival, check into a modern seafront resort that stretches over nearly a mile of private beach – ideal for tranquility. The rest of the day is at your disposal: enjoy the amenities of your accommodation, relax under palm trees or let the day end with another tropical sunset on the resort’s quiet jetty. Overnight in Islamorada.

You say goodbye to the Keys again and travel on to the Palm Beach bathing paradise. On the way, we recommend that you stop at Fort Lauderdale. Characterized by an extensive network of canals, Fort Lauderdale is also known as the Venice of America on the Atlantic coast. A special experience is a romantic gondola ride on the canals. From the water you have a great view of the luxurious houses and gardens of Fort Lauderdale. Afterwards, continue and reach Palm Beach, home to the rich and beautiful. Palm Beach has retained its character as a luxurious seaside resort with exclusive resorts and miles of untouched beaches. Stay at the exclusive Four Seasons Resort Palm Beach – one of the finest addresses on Florida’s east coast. This stylish luxury resort is located directly on the quiet, expansive sandy beach and offers maximum comfort in a modern, elegant ambience. Surrounded by tropical gardens, the house combines contemporary design with the relaxed atmosphere of a first-class beach retreat. Here you will enjoy excellent service, culinary highlights and the quiet beauty at the highest level. Overnight in Palm Beach.

Let yourself be pampered at your luxury resort today or enjoy the day at Palm Beach’s dream beaches. Feel the sun by the seawater pool, reserve a private beach cabana or take a relaxing bike ride along the coast in the fresh ocean breeze. Take a fitness class or spend time in the five-star spa, which gives you access to quiet relaxation areas, steam baths and quiet refueling retreats. Worth Avenue, recently named one of the 20 most legendary streets in America by USA Today, invites you for a shopping tour. Along the pedestrian zone are exclusive boutiques and upscale restaurants. For culture lovers, “Florida’s Cultural Capital” also offers a wide range of art and cultural facilities with more than 42,000 events per year. Overnight in Palm Beach.

Today you continue your journey towards the Gulf Coast. Naples is located directly on the emerald Gulf of Mexico. Surrounded by pearly white dream beaches on one side and the Everglades on the other, the surroundings of Naples and Marco Island offer everything your heart desires. In the charming city of Naples itself, the cozy, palm-fringed city center surprises with a diverse range of art and culture. Trendy galleries, upscale shops and boutiques as well as fine restaurants along the luxury shopping miles of Fifth Avenue South and Third Street South tempt you to stroll. The Condé Nest Traveler regularly appoints Naples as one of the best foodie cities in the USA. Not surprising at the excellent restaurants serving fresh seafood against the stunning backdrop of the coast. Upon arrival in Naples, check into the exclusive Ritz-Carlton Naples. This luxurious beach resort invites you to rest in the afternoon: enjoy the sun on a three-mile powder-white sandy beach or discover the home’s top-notch amenities – from gourmet restaurants to the elegant spa. Overnight in Naples.

Start your morning with a relaxing, guided bike ride through scenic Naples – one of Florida’s most elegant coastal cities. On this approximately two-hour exploration tour, you will cycle through the historic heart of the city, accompanied by a local guide who will give you fascinating insights and stories. You drive on quiet side roads and hidden paths, past tropically planted gardens, magnificent estates and unspoilt coastal scenery. The peaceful atmosphere and pleasant pace make this tour ideal for experiencing Naples from a whole new perspective. Along the way, stop at major landmarks such as the famous Naples Pier, where dolphins often show up, as well as the charming Palm Cottage, the oldest preserved house in town. There you will learn more about the history and culture of this unique region. At the end of the tour, park your bike in elegant Third Street South – a popular neighborhood full of stylish cafes, exclusive boutiques and small galleries. Enjoy a relaxing walk, perhaps with a coffee in hand, while letting the flair of this special place take hold. Overnight in Naples.

Today, a special nature experience awaits you in the Everglades National Park. From Naples, follow the Tamiami Trail which takes you through the Everglades to Miami. The Everglades National Park is home to one of the most diverse ecosystems in the USA and is therefore a UNESCO World Heritage Site. In the unique natural landscape, consisting of marsh and water areas, alluvial and lowlands as well as mangrove forests, live rare and endangered animal species such as manatees, alligators, turtles and the shy Florida Panther. In Everglades City, swap the car for a kayak and get close to the natural and wildlife of this unique ecosystem on a guided tour of the mangroves. After this impressive nature adventure, continue your journey and finally reach Miami and the beaches of South Beach. Overnight in Miami Beach.

Today we have to discover Miami! Immerse yourself in the vibrant rhythms of Little Havana, the heart of Miami’s Cuban-American culture. On a guided walk along the famous Calle Ocho, you will discover the soul of this colorful district – a place where history, music and enjoyment blend together in a unique way. Your guide will tell you exciting stories about immigration, tradition and community as you stroll past ornate murals, cigar shops and colorful street cafes. Let yourself be infected by the joie de vivre of the locals and discover how deep the cultural roots are anchored here. Along the way, enjoy tropical juices, taste crispy Cuban pasta bags and try a traditional Cuban sandwich – freshly prepared and full of flavor. To culminate, visit the legendary music club Ball & Chain. Enjoy live music, dance to thrilling rhythms and toast to life with an artfully mixed cocktail – that’s what Miami sounds like and tastes like! Overnight in Miami Beach.

Enjoy your last day in Miami according to your rhythm – whether relaxed or active. Let your soul dangle by the pool in a private cabana, enjoy the breathtaking ocean views from the rooftop terrace or find new energy during a soothing yoga session. Take the time to discover the city on your own or indulge in the luxury and tranquility of your hotel – perfect for ending this unforgettable journey in style. Overnight in Miami Beach.

Even the most beautiful trip comes to an end and so today already breaks your last day in Florida. With many new impressions in your luggage, you drive back to the airport, where you drop off your rental car and travel home. Do you want the American-Caribbean feeling of relaxation and serenity to last a little longer? After your round trip, spend relaxing days in southwest Florida in your own luxurious vacation villa in the exclusive Cape Coral neighborhood. Nearby, the dream beaches of Fort Myers, Sanibel and Captiva Island are waiting to be discovered by you. Our recommendation: Villa Maverick, a brand new and modern luxury holiday home, welcomes you with 3 bedrooms and 3 bathrooms as well as a spacious verandah with its own heated pool and spa. Or how about a bathing extension in the Bahamas at the end of your trip? By seaplane you can easily reach the island paradise from Miami. In the Bahamas, the luxurious Hotel The Ocean Club – a hotel belonging to the legendary Four Seasons chain. This Hideaway is located on an 8 km long natural white sand beach on Paradise Island. Let the soul dangle and enjoy the pure Caribbean feeling!

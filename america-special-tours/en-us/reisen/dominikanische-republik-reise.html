Dominican Republic — Discovering Quisqueya

Dominican Republic Travel - Experience the fascination of Quisqueya with your specialist América Special Tours. Ask directly and consult here.

On this round trip, discover ‘Quisqueya’ (the mother of all lands) as the island of Hispaniola, consisting of Haiti and the Dominican Republic, was called by the indigenous people. Known for its dreamlike kilometers of sandy beaches, the Dominican Republic has much more to offer in all parts of the country: In addition to the first city in the New World with many architectural sights, the beauty of the southwest awaits you with the greatest biodiversity and the most extreme natural contrasts in the country. Dominican taste experiences - rum, coffee, cocoa and cigars - must of course not be missing during the journey over the island. The diversity is also evident through the paradisiacal tropical landscape of the Samaná Peninsula, through a labyrinth of waterways in Los Haitises National Park and through unspoilt beaches of Isla Saona. Experience the typical Dominican serenity and enjoy a nature that paints unforgettable pictures.

Trip Information

Itinerary

15 days Santo Domingo - Barahona - Enriquillo - Jarabacoa - Puerto Plata - Cayo Arena - Las Terrenas - Los Haitises - La Romana - Isla Saona - Santo Domingo

Your Trip Highlights

  • Safety and flexibility through a private tour guide and own chauffeur
  • Carefully selected hotels for you
  • Colonial Flair in Santo Domingo: The First City of the New World
  • Flamingos and iguanas up close in the Laguna de Oviedo
  • Dominican trademarks: rum and cigars
  • Mangrove belt in Los Haitises National Park
  • Let the soul dangle on the most beautiful white Caribbean beaches

In the south of the island, upon arrival in the Dominican Republic, the capital Santo Domingo awaits you. Look forward to a city where rich culture and history face modern developments. Built more than 500 years ago, the capital today is a vibrant metropolis of millions with just under three million inhabitants. It is considered the first city in the ‘New World’ and is characterised by modern elegance and the charm of its old town. From 1496 the first Europeans settled at the mouth of the river Ozama, whereupon Christopher Columbus brother Bartolomeo founded the first European city in America here in 1498. The colonial old town ‘Ciudad Colonial’ was declared a World Heritage Site of Humanity by UNESCO in 1990. Arrive in peace and enjoy the rest of the day at your free disposal. Stroll through the alleys and let the atmosphere of America’s oldest surviving city take hold. Overnight in Santo Domingo.

After an extensive breakfast, you will get to know the first European city of America during a guided tour together with your guide. Not only was the first cathedral built here, the first governor also resided here, the first bishop was elected and the first hospital opened. In 1538, the first university on the American continent was founded. You will start your tour in the historic old town, the so-called ‘Zona Colonial’. Your path leads you first to the palace of Diego Columbus, the palace of the governor and captains-general and the cathedral. You will also take a detour to the ‘Parque Colón’, where a bronze monument by Christopher Columbus is waiting for you to photograph. You will then walk through the ‘Calle de las Damas’, the Women’s Street and the pedestrian zone ‘El Conde’ with its many shops, bars and restaurants. Strengthen yourself with a lunch in the old town before visiting the Botanical Garden. So that you do not only get your money’s worth culturally today, take part in a cooking class in the evening. Look forward to a Caribbean taste experience and then enjoy your self-prepared food. Overnight in Santo Domingo.

Today you leave the capital and start your round trip towards the southwest. This part of the country is considered to be the unknown part of the island, which so far few explore. In the bay of Ocoa, the first wine tourism project in the entire Caribbean was inaugurated. This is nestled in a unique landscape and offers you wide views over the dreamlike Caribbean Sea. On this patch of soil, it has been shown that climate and soil offer optimal conditions for growing the highest quality grape vines. Once you have arrived at the plant, you will take a guided tour of the vineyards and learn something worth knowing about processing and production. Of course, the tasting of local products should not be missing. Afterwards, enjoy a delicious lunch in this unforgettable ambience. You will be happy to relax by the pool and enjoy the Caribbean flair away from the tourist flows before your trip takes you on to Barahona, where your hotel is already waiting for you. Let the evening end in this almost untapped stretch of land with a fantastic view over the river and sea. Overnight in Bahoruco.

Continue along the coastal road to Enriquillo, a small fishing village. You will first reach the Laguna de Oviedo, a fresh-salt water lagoon separated from the sea only by a narrow stretch of land. It consists of 22 islands. Here you board a private boat that sails along the shore of the lagoon. Stop at one of the islands and have the unique chance to observe iguanas in the wild. You make a short, approx. 10-minute hike across the island, through a barren and cactus-stocked landscape, to an observation tower. Your boat is already waiting for you on the other side of the island and together you go in search of a Flaming colony. Once back on the shore, drive a little further south. Arriving in Cabo Rojo you will drive approx. 15 minutes by boat to probably the most beautiful natural beach in the country. The beach at the Bahía de las Aguilas (Eagle Bay) in the Jaragua National Park is one of the best on earth. Your lunch will be served in this ambience right on the beach. After this eventful day, head back to your hotel where you can sleep in for the upcoming adventures. Overnight in Bahoruco.

Today is a day of travel ahead of you, because together with your German-speaking guide through San José de Ocoa you go inland and say goodbye for a night from the dreamlike beaches. The journey takes you through typical Dominican villages, which are particularly characterized by agriculture. Stop and photograph one of the many tropical fruit stalls waiting for buyers along the roadside. At noon you will arrive in Bonao, where you will cook with the inhabitants of the village on an open fire and with local ingredients. Enjoy Dominican serenity and learn more about the rural life of the population. After strengthening, take a short hike along the Rio Blanco River. Surrounded by pine forests, mountains and valleys, you can reach Jarabacoa, which is located in the mountain range of the Cordillera Central, also called Dominican Alps. It is considered the center for hiking lovers and nature lovers. Enjoy your stay in what is certainly the easiest but still delightful accommodation during your round trip, a country club style hotel. Overnight in Jarabacoa.

In the Caribbean, excellent and worldwide known coffee thrives. After a relaxing breakfast, head north and be the first to visit the coffee factory ‘Monte Alto’. Here, everything about the cultivation and processing of the coffee is explained to you. Of course, a coffee sample should not be missing here either. Freshly strengthened, your trip will take you to Santiago de los Caballeros, the richest city in the country. Not far from Santiago is Tamboril. As a supplement to your coffee tasting in the morning, you now have the pleasure of visiting a cigar factory there. Here you will be told everything about the history and process of tobacco processing and you will experience up close how the tobacco leaves are rolled and processed into a cigar. After so much coffee and tobacco, you can now look forward to your onward journey to the Amber Coast, as the north coast of the island is also called. By the way, in the province of Puerto Plata are the remains of the first European settlement of America. The three ships of Admiral Christopher Columbus landed here in 1492. Near Puerto Plata, Hotel Casa Colonial is the first member of Small Luxury Hotels in the Dominican Republic. Overnight in Puerto Plata.

Today is at your disposal. Sleep out after the exciting days, have breakfast in peace, relax at the spa, by the pool or on the beach of the hotel. Alternatively, of course, you can also use the time to explore Puerto Plata on your own with its beautiful 19th-century Victorian homes. You will find there, among other things, the famous Amber Museum and the fortress San Felipe from 1577.

Today we take you to the Sandbank Cayo Arena or also called ‘Paradise Island’. It is considered the jewel of the Dominican Republic and is certainly a highlight during your stay in the north of the island. You will go to Punta Rucia and board your boat on the beach. From there it goes in approx. 25 minutes to Cayo Arena. On the small sandbank you will get your snorkeling equipment handed over and then have enough time to discover the beautiful coral reef with many colorful fish, which lies directly around the sand island. Pure Caribbean! Overnight in Puerto Plata.

After breakfast, the only 823-meter-long cable car in the country will take you up to Mount Isabel de Torres. From there, enjoy the view over the port city and the north coast. Then continue with your guide towards Salcedo. On the outskirts of the city, visit the museum of the ‘Tres Hermanas Mirabal’. Along the way, another Dominican taste experience awaits you, as during your journey through fertile valleys you will visit the ‘Sendero de Cacao’, a kind of cocoa educational trail. On the cocoa plantation “Hacienda La Esmeralda”, which supplies world-famous chocolate factories, you have the unique opportunity to experience everything from cocoa beans to chocolate processing. Of course, a cocoa and chocolate tasting is also part of this. As part of the tour, you will also be served lunch. Afterwards you reach Nagua and see the Atlantic Ocean again. The 63 km long and between 10 and 20 km wide peninsula Samaná is characterized by a paradisiacal tropical landscape. The lively center of the northeast coast is Las Terrenas, where you can check in at your hotel and end the evening relaxed. Overnight in Las Terrenas.

After yesterday’s travel day with a mix of nature, culture and sweet delicacies, today is at your disposal. Enjoy it in the hotel complex or on one of the great beaches of Las Terrenas. Let the soul dangle and feel the magic of the Caribbean. Overnight in Las Terrenas.

This morning you will take a private boat tour to the Los Haitises National Park. This is one of the most interesting and also one of the largest still untouched areas of wet tropical jungle in the Caribbean. The excursion takes you to the coastal zone of the more than 200 km² protected area. In pre-Columbian times, the Taínos, the natives of the island, settled here or at least visited this area again and again. You can see for yourself during a detour to one of the numerous caves in the area, where you can admire the petroglyphs (stone carvings) on the walls. The park area is also one of the most important retreats of the native flora and fauna. You will take a small hike in this impressive national park before continuing on your kayak after lunch. Gently glide through the middle of the tropical mangrove forests. Let the tranquility and nature of this peaceful environment affect you and welcome the opportunity to explore nature in a sustainable way. The rest of the afternoon will be relaxed at your hotel. Overnight in Las Terrenas.

Today you have to say goodbye to your little paradise on the north coast of the island, because your ride for you further south. Along the way, stop at the historic Barceló Museum in San Pedro de Macoris, where you and your guide can take a look behind the scenes at one of the most important trademarks of the Dominican Republic: Barceló Rum. The museum, built in the shape of a rum barrel, shows the history of the rum and the Barceló family. Before you make your way to your hotel in La Romana, visit the interesting cave system ‘Cueva de las Maravillas’ (Cave of Wonders) with underground river courses and dripstone caves. It is the largest developed dripstone cave in the Dominican Republic and contains many impressive drawings of the Taínos. After this eventful day you reach your hotel and let the day end comfortably. Overnight La Romana.

Surrounded by turquoise blue, clear water, Saona Island awaits you today. First you will drive to the fishing village of Bayahibe. Once there, board your private speedboat, which will take you to the National Park ‘Parque Nacional del Este’ and the island of Saona, where only a few locals live. Your ride will take you past magnificent, untouched beaches. Arriving on the island, you first take a small walk and buy a small souvenir for your loved ones at home at one of the stands. Depending on the season, you can visit ‘El Negro’, a local who has made it his mission to protect the giant turtles. At noon you will be served a typical barbecue on the beach. Of course, as part of your tour, you can also make a swim stop and visit the Piscina Natural, a large sand plateau in the middle of the sea, where you can not only swim but also snorkel. Throughout your excursion, you can snack on fresh fruit. Overnight in La Romana.

Your luxury hotel Casa de Campo is located right in the complex of Altos de Chavon, an ornate replica of a Mediterranean village. The comfort of the guests comes first here. Enjoy your last day in the Caribbean according to your taste. Dominican hospitality and a wide range of food and recreation options will certainly help you enjoy the day to the fullest. A perfect place to relax and revitalize. Overnight in La Romana.

Even the most beautiful trip comes to an end and so it is today to say goodbye to the island. Even if your holiday is over, the precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories of your trip through the Dominican Republic will accompany you forever. Look forward to letting your friends and family share your experiences in the distance at home. For your return flight, you will be taken to Santo Domingo Airport. If you want the Caribbean feeling of relaxation and serenity to last a little longer, book a bath extension (optional) in the exclusive complex of the Eden Roc Hotel in Cap Cana (Punta Cana). Here you can relax by the private pool and get pampered in one of the exquisite restaurants. The charm of the Caribbean will flatter you for more days and in this hotel no wishes will be left open.

Colombia Travel - Colombia's South

Explore the south of Colombia and discover ancient cultures and impressive landscapes on this personalized Colombia trip from América Special Tours.

Itinerary & services

This journey takes you to the authentic south of Colombia. You will explore the Andean cities of Popayan and San Agustín. A special highlight is the National Park "Alto de los Idolos". Here you will marvel at the tombs and statues of the ancient San Agustín culture, which flourished in the 6th to 8th centuries. You will also visit an indigenous community and discover its traditions and customs. On your way to the famous coffee region, make a short stop in Cali, Colombia’s salsa capital. There you will experience Colombian joie de vivre up close. In the coffee region, you will learn how the black gold is grown and processed. The climate here is ideally suited to relax for a few days. At the end of your trip to Colombia, fly north to the historic Cartagena. Stroll through the colonial, historic old town and let yourself be drifted by the Caribbean flair.

Day 1 – The capital Bogotá

Your journey through Colombia begins in the state capital of Bogotá, the pride of Colombians. Already at the airport you will be expected by your guide, who will accompany you to your hotel in the city, where you can relax with a welcome cocktail from your long flight. Overnight in Bogotá.

Day 2 Highlights in and around Bogotá

This morning you dedicate yourself to the capital Bogotá. The Plaza de Mercado in Paloquemao is a huge market hall with over 1,800 stalls of fresh fruit and vegetables, meat, herbs, flowers and small craft businesses. Afterwards, you will experience the contrast between the modern Bogotá and the historic old town. From Plaza Bolivar, the colonial “main square” with a cathedral, palace of justice, parliament and town hall, it passes through the narrow streets of the Candelaria – as the old town of Bogotá is also called. In the Museo Botero you will see an important collection of contemporary art and, of course, many works by the famous Colombian artist Fernando Botero. Another highlight is the Gold Museum, which contains the world’s most impressive collection of pre-Columbian gold jewelry. In fine weather, at the end of your tour, take the cable car to the Bogotás mountain, the Monserrate, from which you have an excellent view over the city. Overnight in Bogotá.

Day 3 Continue to the South

After breakfast, leave the capital behind. You will be taken to the airport, where you will take a domestic flight to Pitalito. There you will be met as usual by your chauffeur and guide. After about an hour's drive you will reach the attraction for which this region is known, the National Park "Alto de los Idolos". Here you will marvel at the tombs and statues of the ancient San Agustín culture, which flourished in the 6th to 8th centuries. You will explore the Archaeological Park and get a first impression of this mystical region. Overnight in San Agustín.

Day 4 San Agustín UNESCO World Heritage Site

The San Agustín region is the most important archaeological site in Colombia. Only in the 18th century did Spanish missionaries report on the mysterious statues in the forests around San Agustín. Even modern archaeology has not yet discovered much about culture. Mountainous terrain and the lack of written records complicate research. Also today you will visit the Archaeological Park of San Agustín. The ceremonial fountain and the “forest of statues” are the highlights of this archaeological collection. Each of the mysterious statues raises the question of whether it represents a human or an animal, whether it should be threatening or reassuring. Overnight in San Agustín.

5th day riding trip to the Magdalena gorge

Today you will take an almost one-hour riding tour to the platform “La Chaquira”, an astronomical site of San Agustine culture. Situated high above the Río Magdalena offers a magnificent view of the Magdalena gorge. Some steps lead down to the representations of the deities carved in rock. Overnight in San Agustín.

Day 6 - Continue to Popayán

On the way from San Agustín to Popayán you cross the central mountain range of the Andes and drive through the typical high moor landscape of the Andes. During a stopover, see the up to three-meter-high Frailejones plants up close, which can only be found in the high altitudes of Ecuador and Colombia. Enjoy the Colombian countryside and, in fine weather, the view of the Puracé volcano before reaching Popayán. Popayán is a beautiful colonial city called “Ciudad Blanca” because of its two-storey, snow-white facades. In colonial times, Popayán was the most important place between Bogotá and Quito, a commercial center and bishopric. During a walk through the rainy small streets you will experience the meeting of modern and historical Colombia. Explore the historic old town on a city walk and visit the most important architectural monuments of the city. Overnight in Popayán.

Day 7 Visiting the indigenous community of the Guambianos

Today you will visit a village of the indigenous community of Guambianos. It is a colorful sight because the locals wear an eye-catching royal blue skirt, a dark felt hat and a characteristic ruana or cape. The color blue stands for the cosmos and the color black symbolizes Mother Earth. You will learn more about the life of these indigenous peoples in the field of tension between tradition and modern Colombia. Afterwards, continue to Cali, one of the oldest cities in America, also called the City of Salsa. The Caleños are very proud of their vibrant culture, everything moves in the rhythm of salsa and the music unites the many different peoples to a harmonious coexistence. The best way to experience the dance culture is by visiting a salsa show tonight. Overnight in Cali.

Day 8: The Black Gold of Colombia

From Cali, the drive passes through the wide valley of the Cauca River until you reach the coffee zone after about three hours. Of course, a visit to a coffee plantation should not be missing here. You will learn everything about coffee: the entire process of coffee processing from harvest to export. You will learn why Colombian coffee from this area is one of the best highland coffees in the world. You walk through the plantation and see seedlings, seedlings and the adult bushes (the growth stage is seasonal). At harvest time, you can watch the coffee pickers skillfully picking only the ripe coffee cherries. Subsequently, the preparation of the harvested fruit is explained. Finally, you will have the opportunity to taste different types of coffee. Overnight in Armenia.

Day 9: The Magical Cocora Valley

Today you will visit the magical Cocoratal. In the cloud forest of the valley you can admire the national tree of Colombia, the wax palm that grows up to 60 meters high. You take a short hike through the valley, on request you can experience the landscape on a horseback riding tour. Then visit Salento, a picturesque village with the typical architecture of the coffee zone. You can stroll through the alleys and marvel at what is made from the coffee bush. Walking through the "Calle Real", you will come to 250 stairs that lead you to the viewpoint "Alto de la Cruz". From there, you have an excellent view of the Cocora Valley and the mountain scenery of Los Nevados National Park. Overnight in Armenia.

10th day of pure relaxation

Without tour guide and chauffeur

The day is free for you. Enjoy the beautiful complex of the hotel, swim a few rounds in the hotel's swimming pool and let yourself be enchanted by the typical cuisine of the region. The hotel offers numerous activities that you can still choose spontaneously on site. Overnight in Armenia.

Day 11 - Travel to Cartagena

After experiencing exciting nature and cultural experiences in the south of the country and in the coffee zone over the last few days, you will travel on to the Caribbean coast today. You will be taken to Armenia airport in time after breakfast and fly directly to Cartagena in the north of the country. Upon arrival, you will immediately feel the geographical difference. As you step through the plane’s narrow door, the Caribbean heat inevitably strikes you like a hot wall. Inside pleasantly air-conditioned, outside tropical heat. Welcome to the airport of Cartagena! As usual, your chauffeur and guide will take you to your hotel. Overnight in Cartagena.

Day 12 Colonial Cartagena

You will start your visit with a ride up the hill of La Popa. At the highest point in the surroundings of Cartagena, Augustinian monks built a monastery in the 16th century. From here you have a perfect view of the Bay of Cartagena and the most important colonial port city of the Spanish in South America. Then visit the fortress of San Felipe. This was built by the Spanish to protect the rich Cartagena from attacks from the mainland. From the fortress it goes on foot to the colonial old town. There you will visit important historical buildings and churches. Above all, you can orient yourself to go out again on your own later in the afternoon and shop for souvenirs on the city wall and admire the sunset from Cafe del Mar on the city wall. Overnight in Cartagena.

Day 13 Explore the City

Without tour guide and chauffeur

Today you can design according to your taste. Stroll through the streets of Cartagena and let yourself be captivated by the lively atmosphere of the city. Overnight in Cartagena.

14th Adios Colombia!

Your journey is coming to an end today. In due course, you will be picked up at the hotel and taken to Cartagena Airport. Even if your holiday is over: The unique memories of Colombia will always accompany you.

Day 14 – 17 Optional bathing extension on Isla Múcura

After you have gained many exciting impressions of the rich cultural and natural treasures of Colombia as well as its friendly inhabitants during the last few days on your round trip, you can relax once again at the end of your holiday. Spend a few days on the beautiful beach of Isla Múcura and enjoy the amenities in the hotel Punta Faro.

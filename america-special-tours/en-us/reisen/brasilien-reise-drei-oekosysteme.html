Brazil — A Journey Through Three Ecosystems

Discover Brazil’s wilderness: savannah, rainforest and wetland on an exclusive 13-day journey through Cerrado, Amazon and Pantanal.

13 days

Your trip idea

A sample itinerary—tailored to you

  • Home
  • Brazil
  • Travel inspirations in Brazil

Brazil is a country of exceptional socio-ecological diversity and is home to a total of six impressive biomes. On this journey you will discover three of these fascinating natural worlds: the Cerrado, the Amazon and the Pantanal. Each of these ecosystems has its own landscape, wildlife and culture, allowing you to experience Brazil anew between lush nature, aquatic landscapes and authentic encounters. Dreamlike expanses, mighty rivers, waterfalls and crystal-clear lagoons shape the diversity of the country. But Brazil inspires not only by its nature, but also by its people. Traditional communities and local cultures preserve centuries-old knowledge and provide unique insights into the soul of Brazil. Brazil is joie de vivre, rhythm and nature in its most intense form.

Trip Information

Itinerary

13 days Brasília – Cerrado – Amazon – Pantanal

Your Trip Highlights

  • Safety and flexibility through a private tour guide
  • Carefully selected hotels for you
  • Intensive nature experiences in the Cerrado with guided activities
  • Encounters with the Mane Wolf and other iconic animal species
  • Canoeing, hikes and bike safaris in the Cerrado
  • Observation of wild animals from observation towers and by boat
  • Immersive jungle experiences in the Amazon with experienced nature guides
  • Private domestic flight for maximum efficiency and comfort
  • Safari Activities in the Pantanal: Night Watching, Canoeing, Horse Riding
  • Experienced biologists as guides during the excursions

Upon arrival at Brasília International Airport, you will be warmly welcomed by your personal guide and conveniently transferred to your hotel by private transfer. Even the first impressions of the Brazilian capital show you an extraordinary mixture of modern architecture, vastness and urban elegance. Brasília is a UNESCO World Heritage Site designed by Oscar Niemeyer and Lúcio Costa. The city impresses with its futuristic architecture, its clear urban structure in airplane form and a unique combination of politics, culture and open landscapes. This first stay offers you the perfect opportunity to arrive in peace, acclimatize and let the atmosphere of Brasília affect you before your journey through the impressive natural worlds of Brazil begins. Overnight in Brasília.

Start the day with a relaxing breakfast at the hotel and enjoy a quiet morning in the modern capital of Brazil. During your free day, you will have the opportunity to explore the city individually or expand your experience through optional activities. Stroll along the famous Monumental Axis, where you’ll find some of Brazil’s most important buildings, including the National Congress, Palácio do Planalto and the impressive Metropolitan Cathedral. Each of these buildings reflects Niemeyer’s iconic style, making Brasília a total architectural work of art. For those interested in culture, a visit to the Complexo Cultural da República with the National Museum and the National Library, which regularly present exciting exhibitions and cultural events. The Parque da Cidade Sarah Kubitschek with its extensive green spaces invites you to walks, bike rides and relaxed breaks in nature and is one of the largest city parks in the world. Another highlight is the iconic Ponte Juscelino Kubitschek, an impressive bridge whose elegant arches offer an extraordinary photo opportunity, especially at sunset. Overnight in Brasília.

After breakfast, private transfer from your hotel to the airport in Brasília, where your journey into the fascinating nature of the Cerrado begins. Then fly to the exclusive Pousada Trijunção. Already the journey leads you deep into one of the most original landscapes in Brazil. Surrounded by endless, original landscapes, your intense encounter with one of the most biodiverse ecosystems in Brazil begins here. Upon arrival, you will be warmly welcomed in the Pousada and spend the coming nights in the heart of Cerrado, one of the most biodiverse savannah ecosystems in the world. The afternoon is marked by a gentle arrival in nature. Depending on the arrival time and conditions, you can get a first brief insight into the surroundings and let the special atmosphere of the Cerrado affect you at sunset. Overnight in the Cerrado area.

This day is entirely dedicated to the discovery of the Cerrado and offers a selection of guided nature experiences around the Pousada Trijunção. Early in the morning, take part in guided bird watching with specialized biologists and discover the region’s impressive biodiversity. A very special experience of this stay is the encounter with the mane wolf in the wild, one of the most characteristic and rare animals of the Cerrado. During the course of the day, you can expect varied excursions through the landscape, where you will learn about the vegetation, geological features and traditional forms of knowledge of the Cerrado. Depending on the program, you can take part in guided walks, wildlife observations or visits to conservation projects. The focus is on diversity and flexibility, so you can experience the Cerrado from different perspectives and in your own rhythm. Overnight in the Cerrado area.

Your last full day at Cerrado is dedicated to an in-depth nature experience around the Pousada Trijunção. You will choose from different characteristic experiences that will give you new insights into this unique ecosystem. This includes silent observations in particularly animal-rich areas, exploratory nature migrations and active explorations of the vast landscape. Today you have the opportunity to set your own focus and discover the Cerrado at your preferred pace and style. The late hours of the day invite you to enjoy the special light and atmosphere of the savannah at sunset. All activities remain flexible and adapted to your interests, so that each day offers a new, unmistakable experience of the Cerrado. Overnight in the Cerrado area.

After breakfast, the domestic flight from Pousada Trijunção to Brasília airport will take place. Then travel on to Alta Floresta in the southern Amazon. A one-hour drive and a subsequent boat tour over the rivers Teles Pires and Cristalino will give you the first intense impressions of the Amazon rainforest. Arriving at the lodge is already an experience in itself and marks the beginning of your intense jungle immersion in the south of the Amazon. In the evening, enjoy your dinner at the lodge and spend the night in the middle of the rainforest.

Here you will experience the southern Amazon, one of the least developed regions of the rainforest. A particularly impressive experience is canoeing on the Cristalino River. The calm waters are ideal for relaxed paddling tours. Canoeing can often be combined with hikes, which opens up different perspectives on the rainforest. This also increases the chances to observe animals such as herons, icy birds or the white spider monkeys in their natural environment. A further highlight are the guided hikes on the 30 km long nature trails within the protected area. In small groups, you will explore the diverse ecosystems of the rainforest. Also unforgettable is the visit to the Observation Towers. The two towers, about 50 meters high, offer spectacular views over the canopy of the rainforest. In this region, you will encounter iconic animal species such as the harpy eagle, tapirs, giant otters, roar monkeys or saki monkeys as well as an exceptionally diverse plant world, including the famous Brazil nut. Overnight in the Amazon.

Another eventful day in the heart of the Amazon rainforest awaits you, giving you even deeper insights into this extraordinary natural world. At the Cristalino Jungle Lodge, nature itself becomes a space of experience: each activity opens up new perspectives on the impressive biodiversity and original atmosphere of the southern Amazon. You will visit majestic, centuries-old Brazil nut trees, glide silently with the canoe over the Cristalino River or enjoy the special mood of the rainforest in the early hours of the morning. An unforgettable highlight is the sunrise from one of the Observation Towers, when the rainforest slowly awakens and the endless canopy shows in all its vastness and beauty. Overnight in the Amazon.

With the first light of day and a final breakfast in the jungle, your time at Cristalino Jungle Lodge ends. The Amazon says goodbye to you with all its intensity and leaves lasting impressions. After saying goodbye to the lodge, the trail takes you once again through the impressive nature around Alta Floresta before continuing your journey to Campo Grande. There you will already be expected and brought by private transfer to your hotel. Overnight in Campo Grande.

After a private transfer from the hotel to Campo Grande Airport, your journey begins in the Brazilian Pantanal, one of the largest and most biodiverse wetlands in the world. A private charter flight will take you directly to the exclusive Caiman Ecological Refuge in just about an hour. During the flight, spectacular views of the vast wetlands, rivers and savannah landscapes open up. Upon arrival, you will have time to enjoy the special atmosphere of the lodge and the impressive nature of the Pantanal. Depending on the arrival time, the first included activity already takes place. Together with experienced nature guides, you will discover the fascinating wildlife of the Pantanal on a safari, a canoe trip or an initial exploration of the surrounding landscape. With a little luck you can observe toucans, capybaras, caimans or even jaguars. In the evening, enjoy a delicious dinner in the middle of nature, accompanied by the sounds of the Pantanal. The stylish accommodation of the Caiman Ecological Refuge combines comfort and authenticity and offers the perfect setting for an exclusive nature experience away from everyday life. Overnight in the Pantanal area.

With the first light of the day, another unforgettable day begins at the Caiman Ecological Refuge. The early hours of the morning offer the best conditions to observe the fascinating wildlife of the Pantanal in its natural environment. Together with experienced nature guides, take a trip through the impressive landscape and discover the extraordinary biodiversity of this unique region. After breakfast, continue exploring the vast farm area. During the activities, you will experience the untouched nature of the Pantanal up close and learn about the delicate ecosystem as well as the conservation projects of the region. At lunchtime, return to the lodge, where a relaxing lunch and rest will be waiting for you. In the afternoon, when the temperatures become more pleasant and the animals are more active again, another excursion is on the program. The golden lighting conditions of the evening hours and the spectacular sunset make this tour a very special experience. After an eventful day, enjoy the tranquility and unique atmosphere of the Pantanal and let the impressions of the day relax. Overnight in the Pantanal area.

After an extensive breakfast, another eventful day begins in the fascinating Pantanal. Today’s excursion offers once again the opportunity to experience the impressive nature of this unique region intensively. You will explore different areas of the Refúgio Ecológico Caiman and discover the extraordinary variety of plants and animals that make the Pantanal one of the most important natural paradises in South America. By changing the excursion areas, the lodge is pursuing a sustainable approach that helps to protect the fragile ecosystems while providing varied nature experiences. During the activities, you will have the best chance to observe the fascinating wildlife of the Pantanal in its natural environment and enjoy the impressive landscape with its water surfaces, savannahs and forests. In the evening, return to the lodge and relax in the midst of the peace and sounds of nature. The special atmosphere of the Pantanal makes this day an unforgettable experience. Overnight in the Pantanal area.

After many days in the Pantanal, we say goodbye to the Caiman Ecological Refuge today. After breakfast, transfer to the private landing runway for the return flight to Campo Grande. The approximately one-hour private flight offers once again impressive views of the endless wetlands, rivers and savannahs of the Pantanal. This unique perspective makes the way back to a special experience and impressively shows the enormous dimension and beauty of this extraordinary natural region. At the same time, the flight enables a comfortable and time-saving connection, as the otherwise long journey time on land is significantly shortened. After arriving in Campo Grande, your unforgettable adventure at the Pantanal will end with many special impressions and unique encounters with Brazil’s fascinating wildlife.

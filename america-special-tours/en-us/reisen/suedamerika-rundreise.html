South America Tour – Magical Andean Highlands

South America Round Trip: Experience the magical Andean Highlands on a round trip with América Special Tours. Ask now!

Four countries, an unforgettable journey! Enjoy a unique South American adventure with numerous highlights in Peru, Bolivia, Chile and Argentina. The Andean highlands are full of secrets, legends and ancient cultures. Here the lifestyle of the indigenous population has survived in many places and traditional customs and beliefs of the mysterious, ancient civilizations continue to this day. In addition to the cultural richness, you can expect incredible scenic contrasts that can hardly be described with words. In the capitals Lima and La Paz you will encounter vibrant, multifaceted metropolises between colonial past and modern urbanity. You will visit mystical places and meaningful sites in the empire of the Incas, which will take you to the lost city of Machu Picchu. A unique spiritual experience will be attending a traditional shaman ceremony. At Lake Titicaca, you will learn about the rough life on the reed islands on the shores of the lake and in the vast desert between Uyuni and San Pedro de Atacama await endless, surreal landscapes, breathtaking natural spectacles and a starry sky that seeks its same. Stay in special hotels and lodges where you can feel and breathe the freedom in harmony with the surrounding nature. Fuel your strength and Andean energy in these unspoilt places full of wide horizons and let yourself be enchanted by the magic of the Andean highlands!

1st day Bienvenidos á Peru

Bienvenidos - Welcome to Peru! Behind you is a long flight that takes you into the Kingdom of the Sun has brought. Lima’s colonial old town has been a World Heritage Site since 1991. Today you will encounter both the dilapidated colonial splendor and the bubbling vitality of a modern metropolis. Lima is divided into 43 districts, each with its own attraction. Your luxurious accommodation, Hotel B, welcomes you in the trendy Barranco district. In particular, the hearts of all art lovers will beat faster here, because surrounded by numerous galleries, the Boutique Hotel also has its own impressive art collection. The villa enchants with a magnificent facade, extensive terraces and high ceilings with its historical charm. To prepare for your trip, you will enjoy a dinner of Peruvian delicacies at the hotel's restaurant upon arrival. Overnight in Lima.

2nd day Lima – The City of Kings

In the morning you will get to know the old and new Lima on a city tour. You will visit the colonial city center with the Plaza de Armas and the Government Palace, the City Hall, the Archbishop's Palace and the Cathedral to name only the most magnificent buildings in this historic square. Then visit the Monastery of San Francisco and enter a labyrinth of tunnels and catacombs. After this detour into the underworld, you will continue to the modern residential districts of San Isidro and Miraflores with their beautiful parks. Then it's time to say goodbye to Lima. After a short domestic flight, you will reach Cusco and from there the Sacred Valley is about a 40-minute drive. Along the way, stop at Manos de la Comunidad, a project of local families dedicated to wool processing. Here you will also encounter the various South American camelids including lamas, alpacas, guanakos and vicuñas. Overnight in Urubamba.

Day 3 The Sacred Valley of the Inca

You are in a magical place. No matter from which window of your hacienda you look, the Sacred Valley of the Inca almost always spreads out before your eyes from the hill of the lodge. Situated between the Inca capital Cusco and the Holy City of Machu Picchu, the valley was once the heart of the Inca Empire due to its geographical and climatic qualities. The valley owes its shape to the Urubamba River, which Sacred RiverAlong the water are many archaeological remains from Inca times. Today is free for your own explorations. Take part in the hotel’s extensive offerings and walk in the footsteps of the Inca or relax in the hotel’s own spa. Use the evening hours to absorb the mystical effect of the sun between day and night. During a walk on one of the many hiking trails, you can enjoy the sunset and learn about the importance of astronomy in the Andean world. Overnight in Urubamba.

Day 4 The Culture of the Andes

After an extensive breakfast, delve deeper into the Andean culture today. Your first stop is Moray, a pre-Inca complex of concentric agricultural terraces up to 150m deep. In the time of the Incas, the most diverse types of grain were bred here. Nearby is the picturesque village of Maras with over 3,000 salt springs. Even today, the salt is still traditionally extracted by hand and foot from the salt terraces. Afterwards you will drive to Ollantaytambo. The enormous fortress towers protectively over the village, the view is magnificent. Here you will visit a typical family house to get an impression of the everyday life of the people of the region. In order to experience the mystical culture of the Andes up close, you will finally take part in a traditional shaman ceremony. The locals worship them. Pachamama - or Mother Earth - as a holy goddess and life-giver. And thanksgiving was given to the earth. Pago a la tierra While the spirits of the mountains Apus The God of Fire Wilko They were called. To this day, the ancient ritual is an important part of Peruvian culture and for you a unique spiritual experience. Overnight in Urubamba.

Day 5: The Lost City of Machu Picchu

Today, the most important sanctuary of the Incas awaits you and highlight of many Peruvian travels: The lost city of Machu Picchu in the middle of the Andes. Even the journey on the panoramic train from Ollantaytambo to Aguas Calientes is an experience. With snow-capped mountains in the background, drive through a picturesque river valley with small villages and varied flora until you finally arrive at the train station at the foot of Machu Picchu. From here, a shuttle bus will take you up the Serpentine Road. Surrounded by impenetrable mountain fog forest, Machu Picchu towers sublime and majestically on the summit. The city is said to have been built in the fifteenth century under the reign of the Inca ruler Pachacútec Yupanqui, but was considered lost for many years before it was rediscovered at the beginning of the 20th century. To this day, the ruins give researchers many puzzles about the origin and their purpose. During an in-depth visit to the famous site, your guide will explain more about the history and significance of this sacred place. Overnight in Aguas Calientes.

Day 6 Mystical Views at Dawn

In order to internalize the special effect of Machu Picchu, a second visit is on the program today. Early in the morning, take the shuttle bus to the ruins and explore other corners of the citadel at your own pace. You may want to wander up to the famous Sun Gate or catch a photo along with the numerous lamas walking around the site. For sporty people, climbing the Huayna Picchus or Machu Picchu mountain is highly recommended. The effort is rewarded with a fantastic panoramic view. For the return trip, treat yourself to a special class experience as you take the elegantly equipped, 1920s-style Hiram Bingham train. In three and a half hours, the shortest luxury train in the world, named after the American explorer of the legendary Inca city, will take you to Cusco along the dreamlike route from Machu Picchu via Poroy. During the journey you will be served a multi-course menu. In the barwagon, you can listen to live music during a Pisco Sour and review the experiences in Machu Picchu. Overnight in Cusco.

Day 7 Cusco – The former Inca capital

Cusco will also Navel of the World In the heyday of the Incas, there was hardly a path that did not lead to the capital of the Empire. Now you are also drawn to the city at 3,400 m altitude, which combines testimonies of different eras. Not only Inca walls and temples can be admired here, but also colonial magnificent buildings and Catholic churches. Combined with the flair of modernity, Cusco is one of the most fascinating cities in Peru. As part of a city tour, you will explore the most important sights today. You will visit the Sun Temple Koricancha and the Cathedral and walk through the Inca Street Loreto, past historic buildings and remarkably preserved Inca walls. Outside the historic center are the archaeological treasures of the Sacsayhuaman fortress ruin, the Qenqo Sanctuary, the Puca Pucara guard post and the Tambomachay water temple. After the visit, you still have enough time to stroll through the small cobblestone alleys and stop at one of the numerous cafes and restaurants. Overnight in Cusco.

8th day Panoramic train ride through the Andes

Your journey will take you further south today. Due to the rough terrain with roads, some of which run only a few inches away from deep slopes, a journey through the Andes is often more adventure than a walk. On the other hand, you can move around completely relaxed with the Titicaca train, which takes you comfortably through the impressive landscape of the High Andes. Imposing peaks and deep gorges will pass you as you eat lunch in the dining car and listen to a performance of traditional music and dances from the Peruvian highlands. After about ten hours of driving, you will reach Puno. The city is the starting point to visit the legendary Lake Titicaca and its inhabitants. At the train station, you will be warmly welcomed by hotel staff and escorted to your exclusive Titilaka boutique hotel. After the eventful train journey, you can relax here in an impressive natural setting. Overnight in Puno.

Day 9 On the shores of Titicaca Lake

With an area of over 8,000 sqm, Titicaca is South America’s second largest lake. Around Puno there is a protected area that protects the biodiversity of the flora and fauna of the water. Your lodge offers various excursions with an English-speaking guide that you can take advantage of. For example, you can visit the Necropolis of Sillustani, take a hike on an ancient path of the Incas, visit the Uros Reed Islands or the island Taquile or learn more about life on the shore of the lake on the beach of Socca or on a rowing boat trip through the reeds. Overnight in Puno.

10th day Onward journey along the Titicaca Lake

Today, say goodbye to Peru and head to Kasani, where you will cross the Bolivian border. In the religious place Copacabana, located on the shores of Lake Titicaca, you will visit the famous church with the shrine of the Black Virgin and the bustling main square. Afterwards you board an environmentally friendly catamaran and transfer to the southern part of the Sun Island. Here you will visit Pilcocaina, the most spectacular ruin complex on the island. A Kallawaya (Andean healer) invites you to participate in an Aymara ceremony. On the subsequent boat trip to Cocotoni, you will enjoy breathtaking views of the Royal Cordillera and a selection of Bolivian specialties. Please note that the path to the property is walked on Isla del Sol and is associated with an approximately one-hour ascent at around 4,000 m altitude. A normal physical condition is advantageous for this. In the event of rain, the path can be a bit more demanding, which is why firm footwear is recommended. The Ecolodge on the island impresses with its authentic, natural atmosphere and an impressive panoramic view of Lake Titicaca as well as the snow-capped cordillera. Since the lodge is deliberately simple and environmentally friendly, it has no heating. Especially in the evening and night hours it can therefore be fresh, so warm clothes are recommended. Overnight on the Sun Island.

Day 11 La Paz and the Moon Valley

From the Sun Island, you will return to the mainland by motorboat this morning, from where you will be transported to the Bolivian capital La Paz. Together with your guide, you will explore La Paz, the administrative capital of the world with about 4,000 m above sea level, and its surroundings. As a start of your tour, you will visit the famous Witch Market, where, in addition to many herbs and plants, there are also llama fetuses, dried snakes and frogs to discover. Enjoy the lively and colorful activity on the market stalls. Then visit the main attractions of the city: the colonial church of San Francisco and the Plaza Murillo with the cathedral, the Government Palace and the National Congress. After leaving the colonial old town behind, you will traverse the modern neighborhoods of Obrajes, Calacoto and La Florida until you reach the nearby Moon Valley. Here, nature has created bizarre rock formations over thousands of years. Back in La Paz, the day is rounded off with a cable car ride to El Alto. Enjoy the unique panoramic view of the city during the trip. Overnight in La Paz.

Day 12 - On to the largest salt desert in the world

It will certainly be easy for you to get up early today. A short domestic flight will take you to Uyuni, the starting point for your visit to the famous salt desert. The Salar de Uyuni is with 10,500 sqm the largest salt lake in the world. The word white desert is probably more accurate here. The white endlessly extends beyond the horizon. Your Deluxe Airstream Camper will make your stay in Uyuni very special. You will stay in the middle of the salt desert in one of the luxurious campers with private bathroom and running hot water, as well as a cozy, individually designed living area. Enjoy the view through the large panoramic windows of the breathtaking, incomparably surreal landscape of the sunny salt lake. Overnight in Uyuni.

Day 13 Endlessly glittering expanse

The sunrise is one of the most beautiful moments on the salar, because then the light is reflected like millions of small crystals on the surface of the salt desert. Already on awakening you feel and breathe the freedom in harmony with the surrounding nature. Today you can choose between different excursion options of the lodge: for example, visit the Chiquini cave with its fascinating stalagmites and stalactites, which shine through the light play of the sun in a mystical atmosphere and offer a unique insight into the geological history of the region. The ruins of Pukara de Ayque, located on the shores of the Salar, testify to the rich history of Aymara culture and offer breathtaking views of the surrounding desert landscape and the salt lake. In the evening, the last rays of light color the desert into a glowing play of colors from orange over red to violet. With a little luck, even the (full) moon appears on the edge of the salar. With the telescope, you can observe the stars that shine even brighter here in the desert than usual. Overnight in Uyuni.

14th day flamingos in front of snow-capped mountains

You continue your journey, the landscape changes. In the plateau of southern Bolivia, brown and red tones dominate, snow-capped mountains and mountain lakes appear again and again on the horizon. You will pass the small villages of San Cristobal and Alota and stop at the viewpoint of the Ollague volcano. Then you will reach the numerous lagoons of the region, including the Hedionda, Honda and Chiarchota lagoons. The mineral-rich lakes are native to Andean, Chilean and the rare James Flamingos. The surrounding mountains are reflected in the crystal clear water, while the animals strut gracefully through the lagoons. The sight is breathtaking. Towards evening you reach the Sioli Desert, which is already part of the dry Atacama Desert. Here is your last Bolivia destination Ojo de Perdiz. Your hotel today is simple but very authentic in this seemingly uninhabited world where you can enjoy the peace and solitude of the Andean highlands. Overnight in Ojo de Perdiz.

15th day Surreal landscapes, colorful lagoons and seething geysers

Surreal landscapes still shape the picture today. You will quickly realize why the area is also as Salvador Dali Desert is referred to. Like a huge painting of the artist, she stretches before your eyes. Grotesque rock formations in the sand stand out against the blue-white sky. The first destination of your excursion today is the famous stone tree, a huge rock of volcanic origin shaped like a tree. They go on to the Laguna Colorada, whose strong red coloration comes from algae and plankton, for which the mineral-rich water is an ideal breeding ground. The shore is surrounded by bright white deposits and forms a great contrast to the water of the lagoon. Filled with bubbling mud holes, fumaroles and the smell of sulfur, the high geyser basin awaits you. Sol de Mañana (morning sun). The Laguna Verde On the other hand, it shimmers beautifully in turquoise blue colors with the cone of the 5,960 m high volcano Licancabur in the background. They are now in the southwestern corner of Bolivia. Finally, in Hito Cajon, you will pass the border to Chile and reach the exclusive Awasi Atacama Hotel. End your day in the desert with a good dinner. Overnight in San Pedro de Atacama.

Days 16 and 17 An Oasis in the Desert

In the next few days, the Awasi will spoil you with exquisite comfort and warm hospitality. It is located in the heart of the small village of San Pedro de Atacama and offers you a private, family atmosphere with only 8 rooms. After a long journey the previous day, treat yourself to a massage, a bath in the pool or a cooling under your private outdoor shower. The all-round service of the Awasi includes not only delicate meals and drinks included in the price, among others from the exquisite wine collection, but also a separate guide with a vehicle per room, who is well versed in the area and together with you the individual excursion program in the driest desert in the world. The white-shining Salar de Atacama should not be missed as well as the bizarrely shaped desert rocks of the Moon Valley and the overwhelming view of the twin lagoons Meñiques and Miscanti, which stand out for their intense colors. One of the most impressive natural spectacles in the area is probably the active Tatio geysers. On the way back you can enjoy a relaxing bath in one of the hot springs. Overnight in San Pedro de Atacama.

18th day Panoramic cruise across the Andes

After a last breakfast in the desert, it is time to say goodbye from the little paradise. At the end of your unforgettable trip to South America, you will cross the impressive Andean landscape from San Pedro de Atacama to Salta. Discover the Paso de Jama – your gateway to the majestic Andes! The Paso de Jama is known for its impressive views of snow-capped volcanoes, endless salt plains and the fascinating colors of the high mountain world. Along the way, you can also experience the cultural diversity of the region. Visit local markets, taste traditional dishes and immerse yourself in the warm hospitality of the local people. After the impressive landscapes of the Andes and the charming villages, Salta is the perfect place to end your trip. Enjoy a final evening in this lively city, stroll through the picturesque alleys and be enchanted by colonial architecture. Perhaps treat yourself to a delicious Argentine dinner and toast the experiences of your trip. Overnight in Salta.

19th day Farewell to South America

Today your great trip to South America is coming to an end. In due course, you will be taken to the airport to take your return flight. You will have in your luggage the precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories of your journey that will accompany you forever.

The Best of Ecuador with Galápagos

Private round trip through Ecuador: Quito, Amazon rainforest, Andes with Cotopaxi & Cuenca and a Galápagos cruise with unique wildlife experience.

Ecuador is much more than just the gateway to the world famous Galápagos Islands. On this trip you will experience both sides of the country: the fascinating mainland with its volcanoes, Andes and Amazon rainforest as well as the unique island world of Galápagos. Around the colonial capital Quito, some of the world’s tallest active volcanoes rise, while colorful indigenous markets shape life in the Andes. In the west, palm-lined beaches on the Pacific invite for recreation, and in the east, the species-rich Amazon stretches. Ecuador is also characterized by a great cultural diversity, with indigenous groups, mestizos, Afroecuadorians and other population groups. The country owes its name to the equator, which crosses it and determines its special geographical location.

1st Day Welcome to the fascinating capital of Ecuador

Quito welcomes you as one of the most fascinating cities in South America: surrounded by snow-capped volcanoes and only about 22 kilometers south of the equator, the capital of Ecuador combines impressive natural scenery with colonial charm. Due to its exceptionally well-preserved old town and the large number of churches and monasteries, Quito bears the venerable nickname “monastery of America”. The historic old town was declared a “Cultural Heritage of Humanity” by UNESCO in 1978 and is one of the most beautiful colonial city centers on the continent. Overnight in Quito.

2nd Day Colonial Old Town, Chocolate & Equator Line

During today’s city tour, you will discover Quito, the capital of Ecuador at an altitude of 2,850 m, in all its cultural and historical diversity. The city, framed by impressive Andean landscapes, brings its colonial past to life in the excellently preserved old town to this day. Together with your guide, you will immerse yourself in the history of Quito. The tour will take you to the central Independence Square with the Presidential Palace, Archbishop’s Palace and Cathedral. Another highlight is the visit to the magnificent church of La Compañía with its richly decorated baroque facade and its artistically designed interior. Afterwards, enjoy a chocolate tasting and learn about the production of fine chocolate as well as the importance of Ecuador as one of the best growing countries for high-quality cocoa. The journey will take you to the famous equator monument “Middle of the World”. Here you make a photo stop and have the opportunity to stand symbolically with one foot on the north hemisphere and with the other on the south hemisphere. Already in 1736, a French expedition under Charles Marie de la Condamine determined the location of the equator at this point. The monument with its brass ball marks the latitude 0. overnight stay in Quito.

Day 3 First Impressions in the Rainforest

In the morning, your journey towards the Amazon begins. After transfer to Quito Airport, you will first fly to Coca, the gateway to the rainforest. There you will be received and prepared for the way ahead. The subsequent ride in the motorized canoe takes you deep into the green heart of the Amazon. The La Selva Amazon Ecolodge is secluded on the Garzacocha lagoon, surrounded by dense, untouched rainforest. This special location enables immediate nature experiences in one of the most biodiverse habitats on earth. The lodge includes a high observation tower and a butterfly station, which offer exciting insights into local biodiversity. In the afternoon, explore the surroundings together with your nature guide on a path through the rainforest. This will give you fascinating insights into the complex plant world and the delicate ecological balance of this unique habitat. After dark, you will discover the rainforest from a completely new perspective: On a canoe trip over the lagoon, you will encounter the nocturnal wildlife of the Amazon. With a bit of luck, you can watch caimans, bats or owls as the clear starry sky unfolds above you. Overnight in the Amazon rainforest.

4th day experience Amazon up close

(Without tour guide and chauffeur)

Experience an unforgettable day in the heart of the Amazon. After a restorative breakfast, your adventure begins with a short hike through the tropical rainforest. After only about 20 minutes you reach a 37-meter-high observation tower, which offers you a breathtaking view over the endless canopy. Afterwards, you will continue on your way to the idyllic Mandi-Cocha lagoon. During the hike, your experienced naturalist guide will give exciting insights into the world of medicinal plants as well as the diverse flora and fauna of the rainforest. With a little luck, you will even encounter a family of giant otters here. After lunch, another highlight awaits you: a relaxing canoe ride on the Garzacocha Lagoon. Look for the unusual Hoatzin, also known as the “Stinkbird”, as well as various species of monkeys, fish and the fascinating plant world that has adapted perfectly to the conditions of the black water. When the twilight sets in, a very special experience begins: a guided night walk. Equipped with a flashlight, you will discover the mysterious side of the rainforest. Insects, amphibians, reptiles and nocturnal birds come to life, making this trip an unforgettable adventure. Overnight in the Amazon rainforest.

Day 5 in the Kingdom of Parrots

Look forward to another eventful day in the Amazon, which brings you closer to the extraordinary diversity of this unique region. In the morning, visit a parrot salt lick in Yasuní National Park, one of the most biodiverse areas on earth. In good weather conditions, you will find an impressive natural spectacle: hundreds of parrots gather to take in mineral-rich soil. Afterwards you will get to know the life of the local rainforest community Pilchi. Together with your naturalist guide, you will visit an indigenous community that warmly welcomes visitors and provides authentic insights into their everyday lives. Discover traditional homes or a school, learn about current projects and try typical food and drinks. If you want, you can even try your hand at the blowpipe or buy handmade works of art directly from the locals. In the afternoon, a hike along Lake Garzacocha awaits you. Your guide will explain the peculiarities of the animal world as well as the diverse use of medicinal plants. On the subsequent canoe trip, look for nocturnal animals such as caimans and bats that become active at dawn. Overnight in the Amazon rainforest.

Day 6: Last Moments in the Rainforest

Enjoy a final breakfast in the heart of the rainforest before heading back. Early in the morning, your departure begins with a final canoe ride along the Napo River. Once again you will have the opportunity to observe the impressive wildlife. You will then reach the dock and continue your journey in a motorized canoe that will take you back to Coca. From there you will take your individual flight back to Quito. Overnight in Quito.

7th Day Nature Experience in Cotopaxi National Park

After breakfast, leave Quito and head south along the legendary “Vulcan Road” towards Riobamba. Already on the way, you will be accompanied by the impressive backdrop of the Ecuadorian Andes with their snow-capped peaks and wide plateaus. In Cotopaxi National Park, take a hike in one of the most beautiful protected areas in the country. Around the Limpiopungo lagoon at an altitude of almost 4,000 meters, you will discover the characteristic vegetation of the Páramo highlands. The majestic Cotopaxi forms a spectacular backdrop and makes this excursion a special experience for nature and photo lovers. In the afternoon, continue your journey and reach the Riobamba region, located in the heart of Ecuador. The surrounding area impresses with vast landscapes and a unique panorama of some of the highest mountains in the country, including Chimborazo, Tungurahua and the striking altar massif. Here you move into your accommodation and let the day relax in the middle of the Andean landscape. Overnight in Riobamba.

Day 8: In the footsteps of the Incas

After breakfast, continue your journey to the south, deep into the varied landscape of the Ecuadorian Andes. The drive to Cuenca takes you through impressive highland scenery and past wide valleys that have shaped the life of this region for centuries. Along the way, visit the impressive ruins of Ingapirca, considered the most significant testimony to pre-Columbian cultures in Ecuador. Located at an altitude of around 3,100 meters, the complex reveals not only archaeological significance, but also spiritual significance. During a tour of the sprawling site, you will learn about the Inca’s connection to the sun, its architecture and the ritual use of this extraordinary place. In the afternoon you will reach Cuenca, one of the most beautiful cities in the country and a true jewel of colonial architecture. The charming old town with its cobblestone streets, craft traditions and elegant buildings from the 18th and 19th centuries exudes a special atmosphere. Cuenca is not without reason considered a cultural center of Ecuador and has been declared a World Heritage Site by UNESCO. Here you can end the day in a stylish ambience and enjoy the special atmosphere of this historic city. Overnight in Cuenca.

Day 9 Discover Cuenca

After breakfast, immerse yourself in the fascinating world of Ecuador’s traditional craftsmanship. In the Panama hat museum, you will learn about the production of the famous hats and the surprising history of their name. From the viewpoint of El Turi, you will enjoy a wide view over the picturesque colonial city of Cuenca and its surroundings. On a guided walking tour, you will discover the many facets of this historic city that was once an important Cañari settlement. Along the Tomebamba River you will encounter historic buildings, the ruins of Todos Santos, the colorful flower market and the central square with its old and new cathedral. The evening is at your disposal. Stroll through the charming alleys with their whitewashed facades, let the special atmosphere affect you and discover Cuenca at your own pace. Overnight in Cuenca.

Day 10 From the Andes to the Coast

After breakfast, leave Cuenca and continue your journey towards the coast. On the journey, you will experience impressive landscape changes: The barren highland plains with typical Páramo vegetation gradually merge into greener valleys and finally into the tropical lowlands. A special highlight along the way is a photo stop in El Cajas National Park, one of Ecuador’s most impressive natural areas. Between 3,200 and 4,500 meters in height, a fascinating landscape of rugged rock formations, moss-covered areas and over 200 crystal-clear glacier lakes awaits you. The animal world is also diverse. With a little luck, condors and numerous other bird species can be observed here. The further you go towards the coast, the more the environment changes. You will pass extensive plantations with rice, cocoa and bananas and finally reach the vibrant metropolis of Guayaquil. The largest city in Ecuador lies on the Río Guayas and is considered the economic center of the country. Here, trade dynamics, river landscape and tropical climate combine to form a very special character that shapes the city. Overnight in Guayaquil.

Day 11 Arrival at Galápagos Natural Paradise

In the morning, your journey begins in one of the most extraordinary natural landscapes in the world: From Guayaquil you fly to the Galápagos Islands. Here you will encounter a unique animal world that has developed completely independently over thousands of years. Giant turtles, sea lizards, sea lions and an extraordinary variety of bird species live in close proximity to humans, making every moment a special experience. Upon arrival in San Cristóbal, you will be welcomed and taken to your Galápagos catamaran, which will be your floating home for the coming days. In the afternoon, visit the Witch Hill (Cerro Brujo), an impressive coastal landscape with a white sand beach and volcanic backdrop. The environment is rich in life and gives you the opportunity to observe numerous animal species up close, including sea lizards, red cliff crabs and various sea and land birds such as blue-footed boobies, pelicans and Darwin finches. Depending on the conditions, it is also possible to swim or snorkel in crystal clear water. Afterwards, drive around the famous Kicker Rock, a striking rock formation that rises majestically from the sea. The day will end on board your catamaran and enjoy the special atmosphere of this unique natural space. Overnight on board.

12th day Galápagos up close

Motorkatamaran Monique offers you an impressive journey through the heart of the Galápagos Islands, where nature experiences and comfort harmoniously merge. A highlight of the trip is a visit to the island of Santa Fe, one of the oldest islands in the Galápagos. At the protected Barrington Bay, you can expect encounters with sea turtles, eagle rays and a variety of seabirds such as pelicans, blue-footed boobies, nazcat boobles and magnificent frigate birds. Sea lion colonies also shape the image of the island, whose playful young animals often approach the snorkelers curiously. Snorkeling itself reveals a fascinating underwater world with sea lizards, sea urchins and colorful flocks of fish. Equally impressive is the small island of South Plaza, a part of the seabed raised by tectonic forces. Depending on the season, the sesuvium soil plant changes colors and makes the island appear in intense yellow, green or bright red shades. After an eventful day on land and in the water, you will return aboard the motor catamaran Monique, let the impressions resound in a quiet atmosphere and spend the night at sea. Overnight on board.

13th day Volcanic landscapes and wildlife

On this stage of the Galápagos Islands, you will experience with the Motorkatamaran Monique an impressive combination of volcanic landscape and unique wildlife. On the island of Santiago, an almost unreal scenery of solidified lava created by an eruption in 1903 opens up at Sullivan Bay. Walking through the rough fields shows how few plant species could survive under these extreme conditions. On the adjoining beach, the sea invites you to swim or snorkel, while sea lions and coastal birds enliven the scenery. Quite different, but equally impressive, Bartolomé presents himself. The small volcanic island with its striking crater offers wide panoramic views over the archipelago during a hike. Particularly well known is Pinnacle Rock, a dramatic rock formation that is considered the symbol of the Galápagos. On the way, you will encounter typical inhabitants of the island world such as sea lizards, lava lizards and pioneer plants. On the beach, a snorkeling tour rounds off the experience, where with a bit of luck you can also see the rare Galápagos penguins. After an intense day you will return on board the motor catamaran Monique, where calm and sea atmosphere are the perfect conclusion to this natural experience.

14th day Bird Paradise in the heart of the Galápagos Islands

Genovesa is one of the most remote and at the same time the most bird-rich islands of the Galápagos archipelago in the northeast, which you can reach with the motor catamaran Monique. After arriving at the sheltered Darwin Bay, a collapsed volcanic crater with natural harbor shape, you will be greeted by an impressive variety of animals. Red-footed boobies, frigate birds as well as fur seals and sea lions share this special environment. A highlight is the climb over the so-called Prince Philip’s Steps. From there, the view of a barren but fascinating landscape opens up. The trail continues through small balm tree stands and ends at an open lava plain that underscores the wild originality of the island. Even under water, Genovesa shows off her spectacular side: while snorkeling along the cliffs, you will discover colorful flocks of fish, rays and, with a bit of luck, even hammer or galapagoshaie. Back in Darwin Bay, the island shows itself from another side: There is lively life on the beach, where frigate birds, boobies and numerous coastal birds determine the scenery. At the end of the day you will return on board the motor catamaran Monique, where you will end the impressions in peace and spend the night at sea.

15th day Last impressions on Galápagos

The final day of travel begins with breakfast on board before experiencing one last natural highlight of the Galápagos Islands: the Black Turtle Cove on the north coast of Santa Cruz. This remote mangrove lagoon, accessible only by dinghy, is considered one of the quietest and most fascinating coastal landscapes in the archipelago. Between branched mangrove forests and small hidden canals, sea turtles can be observed, which live here in a protected environment and even show their mating rituals in certain months. Pelicans and blue-footed boobies are also regular residents, while whitetip sharks and various species of rays glide in the clear water. Afterwards you will be transferred to Baltra Airport, from where you will take your return flight to the Ecuadorian mainland to Guayaquil or Quito.

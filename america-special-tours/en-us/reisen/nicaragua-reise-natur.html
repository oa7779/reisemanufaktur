Nicaragua - Journey in harmony with nature

Feel the power of nature and slow down on an individual round trip along the highlights of Nicaragua. Let us have a non-binding discussion now.

Nicaragua is still a real insider tip for all travelers who are looking for a special authentic experience and want to decelerate their everyday life. On this trip you will not only marvel at the historic cities of Managua and León, but are more close to nature than ever before. You climb the imposing Masaya Volcano, where you can see the bubbling lava when looking over the crater rim and enjoy a breathtaking sunset on the Telica Volcano. You will meet an indigenous community that lives in harmony with nature and can reflect on yourself and your surroundings during yoga classes or walks in nature. In Nicaragua, the clocks tick slower and you learn to appreciate the simple things of life. Little Corn Island off the Caribbean coast of Nicaragua is the perfect place to relax and revisit the impressive journey.

Day 1 – The capital Managua

Your unique journey to Nicaragua begins! After your transatlantic flight, you will arrive at Augusto Sandino Airport in Managua. When you get out, you immediately breathe pleasant, warm air. You will be met by your personal guide and chauffeur and taken to the hotel. This small colonial-style hotel is located in a nice area with small restaurants, café and bars. Overnight in Managua.

2nd day Managua, stunning Masaya volcano and León

Today it is time to get up early, because from Managua you will go to the first highlight of your trip, the Masaya volcano. The volcano is still active. You’ll get to the crater rim and, with a bit of luck, see the glowing lava too! Afterwards, stop at the Viejo Ranchito restaurant and enjoy a typical and delicious lunch before exploring the capital Managua on a guided tour. Afterwards, the journey continues to the student and colonial city of León. The city is considered an intellectual metropolis of Nicaragua and traditionally stands for the liberal element of the country. It was the center of the revolutionary struggles in 1978/79 and the residence of the famous poet Rubén Darío, who is also buried here in the cathedral. The afternoon is at your disposal. Overnight in León.

Day 3 Colonial León and Unique Sunset

Together with your guide, you will explore the city of León this morning and immerse yourself in its history. During your sightseeing tour, for example, you will visit the Central Square and its wall paintings, climb up to the roof of the cathedral and get a glimpse of Nicaragua’s myths and legends. At noon you can stop at one of the nice restaurants or relax in the hotel. Around the afternoon, a real insider tip and further highlight awaits you; because you go to the volcano Telica, where you experience a dreamlike sunset from the top of the volcano. For the ascent to the summit of the volcano you need approx. 45 minutes. Overnight in León.

Day 4 Boat Tour to Isla Juan Venado

Today you take a trip into nature. Isla Juan Venado is located about 20 minutes from León, near the small fishing village of Las Peñitas. The nature reserve stretches for about 20 kilometers and forms a tropical mangrove forest. The mangroves are the habitat of hundreds of bird species, turtles and crocodiles. With a boat and the guide’s trained eye, you’ll cruise along the canals until you get to a stretch of beach where you’ll swim and enjoy a local lunch. With a little luck, you can watch small turtles hatching on this beach from October to January. Overnight in León. 

Day 5 - The Tisey Estanzuela Nature Reserve

Today your journey goes into the next round. Together with your chauffeur and guide, you will continue today to the region around Esteli, the nature reserve Tisey Estanzuela. You will stay in a beautiful, small boutique hotel in the middle of nature, a magical place with fantastic views. At the end of the day, enjoy a yoga lesson at sunset. Overnight stay at the Tisey Estanzuela Nature Reserve.

Day 6 The municipality of Garnacha and the Finca Jalacete

Today you will take a trip to the Tisey Estanzuela Nature Reserve. You will visit the municipality of La Garnacha and get an insight into the daily life of the people. You will also visit the Finca Jalacete. Here, Don Alberto hammers pictures whispered to him in the dream of God into the rocks. A truly extraordinary meeting. You will be invited here for a delicious local lunch. In the afternoon you will return to your hotel. Overnight stay at the Tisey Estanzuela Nature Reserve.

Day 7 The Cañon Somoto and Rosquillas

Today you start the day early, it goes to the Cañon de Somoto. This gorge is a tributary of the Rio Coco near the city of Somoto in northern Nicaragua, almost on the border with Honduras. It was only discovered in 2004 by a group of Nicaraguan and Czech scientists. Enjoy an exciting hike through the gorges, you will even swim through small sections. In the small town of Somoto you will visit a Rosquillas - production site. Rosquillas are a sweet pastry from the region, made from corn and cheese mass, decorated with sugar cane. They go great with a cup of coffee! If you like, you can also make some yourself. Overnight stay at the Tisey Estanzuela Nature Reserve.

Day 8 Visiting a Cigar Factory

Today starts with a yoga lesson. Afterwards, visit the small cigar factory Casa de Alegria, founded in 2004 by Ralf Westerwick and his Nicaraguan wife, where you will get a glimpse of the art of turning cigars. Manufacture Casa de Alegria produces cigars from particularly well-ripened and deposited tobacco from the world-renowned provinces of Jalapa, Esteli and Condega. Fair trade is especially important to Ralf; you will get to know the workers here personally. With a coffee and the cigar tasting, Ralf explains what is important for the cigar. Afterwards we will go on a small hike in the unspoiled nature of Esteli with beautiful views. Overnight stay at the Tisey Estanzuela Nature Reserve.

Day 9: The Caribbean of Nicaragua

Today we say goodbye to the magnificent nature of the Tisey Estanzuela Nature Reserve. You will be taken to Managua airport in due course. Your guide will say goodbye to you. Afterwards you fly with a propeller machine first on Bi Corn Island. From there, travel by boat to Little Corn Island, where your hotel is located. Your hotel for the next few days is the Yemaya Hotel. Enjoy white sand beaches, the bright sun and refreshing fruit cocktails. Overnight at Little Corn Island.

Day 10: Impressive underwater world

With the boat today you go out to the sea, where you will discover the colorful underwater world equipped with snorkels and fins. Overnight at Little Corn Island.

11. -12. Day bathing days in Little Corn Island

After you have gained many exciting impressions of Nicaragua’s rich cultural and natural treasures as well as its friendly inhabitants during the last few days on your round trip, you can relax once again at the end of your holiday. You will spend a few days on the dreamlike beach of Little Corn Island. Overnight at Little Corn Island.

Day 13 Back to the Capital

Today we say goodbye to the white beaches of Little Corn Island. By boat you will return to Big Corn Island and fly back to Managua. There you will already be expected by your chauffeur, who will take you to the already known Hotel Los Robles. Let your trip pass on your last evening in Nicaragua with a refreshing Flor de Caña revue. Overnight in Managua.

14th day of travel home

Your journey is coming to an end today. In due course, you will be picked up at the hotel and taken to Managua Airport. Even if your holiday is over: The unique memories of Nicaragua will always accompany you.

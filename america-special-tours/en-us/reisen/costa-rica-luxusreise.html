Luxury Costa Rica — Above the Rainforest Canopy

Experience Costa Rica at the highest level: award-winning eco-lodges, private charter flights and a unique nature. Request a tailor-made offer now!

11 days

Your trip idea

A sample itinerary—tailored to you

Experience Costa Rica in its most exclusive form: this journey takes you along award-winning luxury colodges to Costa Rica’s most extraordinary natural landscapes. From rafting on the Pacuare River via the jungle canals of Tortuguero to the rainforests of the Osa Peninsula and the mystical cloud forests of Monteverde, you will experience the impressive diversity of the country. Thanks to private charter flights, you will gain valuable time for the essentials and discover the dramatic natural backdrop of volcanoes and coastlines from a perspective reserved only for a few. Trip Information

Itinerary

11 days San José – Pacuare – Tortuguero – Osa Peninsula – Monteverde – San José

Your Trip Highlights

  • Private, German-speaking tour guide and own chauffeur
  • Stays in award-winning luxury eco lodges
  • Adventurous whitewater rafting on the Pacuare River
  • Exploring the Caribbean wetlands on a safari riverboat
  • Unforgettable flight over the interior to the Osa Peninsula
  • Discovery of the magical mist forests of Monteverde
  • Panoramic views of coasts, volcanoes & mountains thanks to private charter flights

Upon arrival at Juan Santamaría International Airport in Alajuela, you will be greeted by an English-speaking guide and taken to the capital San José. Take advantage of the evening to explore the historic and culinary treasures of the city or relax in your hotel. Overnight in San José.

Your adventure begins early in the morning with an impressive drive through the Central Valley. You will pass the former colonial capital of Cartago before enjoying a traditional breakfast with freshly roasted local coffee. The journey takes you further into the dense mountain rainforests of Braulio Carrillo National Park. Here you have a choice: reach the award-winning Pacuare Lodge on an unforgettable rafting adventure on the Pacuare River (Class II to IV) or enjoy the ride through the lush Talamanca mountains. Overnight at Pacuare Lodge.

Your day begins with the gentle singing of the rainforest and fresh delicacies on the terrace of your suite by the river. Pacuare Lodge offers nine exclusive experiences: bird watching at dawn, hiking on ancient paths to a Cabecar village, a refreshing dip into hidden waterfalls or exciting climbing and abseiling adventures in lush canyons. For the adventurous, a zipline experience beckons over the canopy of the rainforest. Those looking for relaxation can visit a local community, discover sustainable projects or enjoy the tranquility at the Jawa Jüu Spa and infinity pool. Overnight at Pacuare Lodge.

Today, the rainforest adventure takes you to one of Costa Rica’s most remote places: Tortuga Lodge & Gardens in Tortuguero. The journey is made with a small machine from Xplore Air to Tortuguero Airport on the northeastern Caribbean coast. The runway is located near the lodge on the other side of a canal. After landing, a short boat ride across the waters will take you to Tortuga Lodge & Gardens. Along the way, unique insights into the pristine habitats of the National Park are revealed, home to exotic plants and animals such as caimans, manatees, turtles, monkeys, reptiles and hundreds of bird species. Overnight in Tortuguero.

Wake up in harmony with nature surrounded by the species-rich wildlife of a private nature reserve right outside your room. Enjoy an exquisitely prepared Caribbean breakfast overlooking the Tortuguero Canal in Costa Rica. The day is all yours. Choose from the exclusive Tortuguero Jungle Expedition Package tailor-made experiences: an early morning bird and nature hike, a guided boat tour to discover the fascinating flora and fauna, a quiet kayak ride through the mirror-smooth canals and side arms of Tortuguero, the excursion to Cerro Tortuguero or an atmospheric Twilight tour at dusk. A walk through the colorful village of Tortuguero rounds off the day perfectly. The gentle rhythm of reggae music and the seductive scents of Creole cuisine characterize the special atmosphere of this place. If the season permits, an unforgettable experience awaits you after sunset: the guided observation of sea turtles during egg-laying. Overnight in Tortuguero.

Today’s experience makes you part of a great adventure film. A flight over the heart of Costa Rica opens up spectacular views of dense rainforests, striking mountain ranges, emerald green valleys with winding rivers and idyllic rural villages on their way to the sparkling Pacific coast and Golfo Dulce on the Osa Peninsula. Arrival is by direct charter flight. After landing in the small port city of Puerto Jiménez, the gateway to the world-famous Corcovado National Park, you will be welcomed and taken along the coast to the exclusive Lapa Rios Rainforest Lodge. Relocate your stylish accommodation overlooking the Pacific Ocean and arrange the afternoon according to your wishes. Relax in the infinity pool, enjoy an application at the spa or take part in one of the exceptional activities offered directly on site. Overnight on the Osa Peninsula.

With its 400-hectare private sanctuary, the renowned Lapa Rios preserves a slice of true Costa Rica, including its stunning, secluded coastline. Whether you sit on your balcony in the treetops, use the yoga deck, relax in the infinity pool or visit the open restaurant, each place offers an intense nature experience. All activities of the lodge are accompanied by naturalists who are experts in the plant and wildlife of the rainforest in Costa Rica. On site, you can participate in early morning bird watching, the coastal hike Matapalo, stargazing at low tide, a hike on the Osa Trail with waterfalls or an exciting night hike. Guests will also gain insight into the lodge’s sustained commitment, including reforestation projects and support from the local community. Overnight on the Osa Peninsula.

At Lapa Rios, you’ll start the morning with a symphony sung by the wildlife in the treetops outside your room. Enjoy the steady rhythm of tree frogs, the lively screaming of macaws, the harmonious melody of colorful rainforest birds or the impressive shouts of territorial roar monkeys. Enjoy unforgettable moments by the sea or on a hike to a rainforest waterfall and then be pampered by the lodge’s award-winning farm-to-table cuisine. Strengthen your body and mind on the yoga deck overlooking the horizon or under the experienced hands of spa and massage therapists. Overnight on the Osa Peninsula.

Start the day early with a flight to the Monteverde region’s Aranjuez Regional Airport. After the landing follows a comfortable, approximately one-hour drive through the hilly countryside up to the mountain peaks and cloud forests of Monteverde. Upon arrival at the charming Monteverde Lodge & Gardens, you will be able to enjoy the magnificent gardens and winding paths or choose from the customised experience offer. Professional nature guides will accompany you on a tour of the gardens or on a hike to the neighboring cloud forest reserves. Alternatively, you can opt for horse riding trips, a visit to chocolate or coffee farms, a visit to the serpentarium and frog house, a bird watching tour, a canopy zipline adventure in Monteverde or a fascinating night hike. Overnight in Monteverde.

Today, your adventure will take you on a guided tour deep into the mystical cloud forest. The 3,500-hectare Monteverde Cloud Forest Reserve is home to 2.5% of the world’s biodiversity, including flora and fauna from both the Pacific and Atlantic regions. You will learn about hundreds of plant, animal and reptile species that live in the reserve, including monkeys, deer, tapirs, sloths, wild cats, snakes and countless birds such as the magnificent Quetzal. In the afternoon, enjoy the exquisite lodge and solar-heated infinity pool or explore the charming locations of Monteverde and Santa Elena. Overnight in Monteverde.

Today your exploration tour of Costa Rica ends. After check-out, your chauffeur will take you to Aranjuez Regional Airport. From there, the flight takes place on a small plane from Xplore Air to the international airport of San José. After landing, you can easily reach the international terminal for your journey home, rich in unforgettable impressions from the fascinating nature of Costa Rica.

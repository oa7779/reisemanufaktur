Jeep Safari by Lençóis Maranhenses

Individual Jeep Safari through Brazil with América Special Tours. Tailored by long-standing experts and the highest level of service. Now ask me.

The northeast of Brazil is the absolute insider tip for a trip between June and September. On your individual round trip you will encounter dreamlike landscapes, unique sand dunes, traditional villages and a Brazilian way of life, which you will certainly only experience here. Travel from São Luis to Santo Amaro, from there to Barra Grande and as the culmination of your tour, to Jericoacoara. There you are guaranteed adventure, unique experiences and of course the most diverse sights. Embark on a unique journey, wander through dune landscapes you won’t find anywhere else in the world, speedboat over the crystal clear water and enjoy the view of the magnificent lagoons. Numerous highlights are waiting for you, which bring the richness of Brazil to the most diverse natural scenery. In order to review all the impressions and experiences that you collect every day, stay in exclusive hotels that fulfill all your wishes.

1st Day Welcome to the colorful city of São Luis

Your journey through Brazil begins in São Luis, the capital of the state of Maranhão in the northeast of the country. At São Luis Airport you will be welcomed by your German-speaking guide and your private chauffeur and chauffeured to your hotel, the Luzeiros São Luis, of course directly on the Atlantic Ocean. Relax there from the long journey and enjoy the dreamlike sea view. This modern hotel is located on the beautiful bay of São Marcos and shines with excellent service. At your convenience, you will then go with your guide on a tour of the historic center of São Luis, which is of great importance in the architectural sense, especially for the 18th and 19th centuries. Once there, visit the Igreja da Sé, the Benedito Leite and Dom Pedro II Park, the fountain “Fonte do Ribeirão”, the Arthur Azevedo Theater, the Leões Palace, the Rua Portugal (a highlight of Portuguese ceramic art) and also the Rua do Giz with its magnificent colonial-era residences. You will also visit the Catarina Mina and Quebra Bundas streets as well as other places worth seeing. In the meantime, you will pass by picturesque beaches such as Ponta d’Areia, São Marcos and Calhão. Overnight in São Luis.

2nd day Explore the Lencóis Maranhenses National Park

Today we start in the morning towards Santo Amaro - the gateway to Lençóis Maranhenses National Park. Once you have reached your destination, a local guide will be waiting to accompany you on your way through the national park and look forward to telling you all about the magnificent nature and landscape. A special adventure awaits you on a 4x4 open-top truck tour to the Junco lagoons. The journey leads over sandy slopes and through impressive dune landscapes until you finally reach the starting point. From there, take a short walk to the edge of the lagoon. There is time to relax, enjoy the silence of nature and let the crystal clear water affect you. The way back is after sunset, when the dunes are immersed in warm light. Afterwards, your chauffeur will take you to the wonderful Oiá Casa Lençóis in Santo Amaro. The boutique hotel is in perfect harmony with nature and the environment. The playful details create a charming and comfortable environment. In every corner you will find natural materials, works of art, plants and flowers. Enjoy the quiet and cozy atmosphere and let the evening end relaxed. Overnight in Santo Amaro.

Day 3 Unforgettable Adventure in the National Park Lençóis Maranhenses

Today you will discover the area in a very special way: another unforgettable adventure awaits you at the Betânia Circuit. The tour takes you in a 4x4 open-top truck through the impressive landscape of the dunes, past glittering lagoons and lush vegetation that make the ride particularly impressive. Betânia itself is a small, welcoming community, a true oasis in the middle of the national park. Residents are friendly and down-to-earth and warmly welcome visitors. A special highlight is the river Alegre, whose warm, bronze-colored water invites you to a refreshing swim. In between, you will have the opportunity to enjoy a lunch with traditional regional cuisine. Another experience is a boat trip on the lagoons around Betânia, where you can experience the beauty of nature and the fascinating culture of the National Park up close. Overnight in Santo Amaro.

4th day nature experience Parnaíba: wildlife, lagoons and luxury in Barra Grande

The next morning you will break from Santo Amaro towards Porto dos Tatus, passing by fascinating landscapes. From the dunes of the Lençóis Maranhenses, it gradually moves to greener regions, through small villages and typical cities of northeast Brazil, an impressive transition between desert and fertile land. At Porto dos Tatus, board a speedboat for an unforgettable exploration of the Parnaíba Delta, one of America’s few open ocean deltas. On your journey through narrow waterways, past islands and dense mangroves, you can observe the rich wildlife: crabs, herons and the fascinating Guarás with their bright red plumage. The highlight of the tour is the magical moment at sunset when the Guarás return to their nests and immerse the sky in deep red colors. After this unique experience, head to Barra Grande to relax at Hotel Pousada Chic, an oasis of luxury and exclusivity. Overnight in Barra Grande.

5Along the dream coast of northeast Brazil

From Barra Grande we head towards Jericoacoara today. First, take asphalt roads to Camocim, where you will cross the Coreaú River by ferry. After that, the adventure continues along beaches and paths between the dunes until you reach Lagoa da Torta, an ideal place to relax and take a refreshing dip. Here there is also an opportunity for lunch while enjoying the beauty of nature. The journey continues via dunes and beaches to Guriú, where you cross mangroves and take a ferry for the last stretch of coast to Jericoacoara. Arriving in Jericoacoara, spend the following two nights at the Chili Beach Boutique Hotel. The impressive property is located on a beautiful, untouched beach. The Chili Beach Boutique Hotel seduces in a tropical style, with modern decor and luxurious amenities. These include, for example, the open-air gourmet restaurant with Italian and local specialties, of course overlooking the ocean, the luxurious spa, the pool bar and the beach club. An absolute highlight is the access from your room to a tropical garden. The sun deck invites you to enjoy a fruity cocktail or glass of champagne while watching the most beautiful sunset of northeastern Brazil. Overnight in Jericoacoara.

Day 6 Explore Jericoacoara and the unique tranquility

Experience an exciting private buggy adventure through the breathtaking scenery around Jericoacoara. The route leads past Riacho Doce Beach, home to the iconic Preguiça tree, as well as the quiet Preá Beach and crystal clear waters of the Blue Lagoon. Afterwards you will reach the spectacular Lagoon Paradise, an ideal place to relax, swim and enjoy the peaceful surroundings. Here you will also have the opportunity to have lunch at one of the charming restaurants on the lagoon edge and refresh yourself before the adventure continues. The return journey takes you through the scenic dunes of the National Park, with breathtaking views and unforgettable photo opportunities before returning to the village of Jericoacoara. The rest of the day is at your disposal to explore the village in peace. Overnight in Jericoacoara.

7th day Até logo no Brasil

After this wonderful week, characterized by scenic dune landscapes, it is about five hours to Pinto Martins – Fortaleza International Airport. Até logo no Brasil! Goodbye to Brazil...

Brazil Round Trip - Golden Brazil

Brazil Round trip with Latin America specialist América Special Tours. Inquire now, get advice from our experts and experience Brazil for yourself.

The adventurous history of the gold rush between the 17th and 18th centuries is still reflected today, especially in the state of Minas Gerais. In Tiradentes, meet a passionate artist who, despite an incurable disease, erected a true religious monument in Congonhas, and in Inhotim you will learn about a paradise of contemporary art nestled in the jungle. In Rio, experience the Sugar Loaf, the Rocinha favela and the vibrant Samba culture up close. As a icing on the cake, the magnificent nature of Brazil cannot be missed – experience Paraty and its surroundings, a colonial treasure on the green coast with crystal clear waterfalls in the rainforest.

Day 1 Welcome to the “Beautiful Horizon”

Welcome to Belo Horizonte – one of the Brazilian cities with the highest quality of life. Streets for strolling and shopping, beautiful places, a colorful nightlife and wooded areas offer something to every traveler. It is hard to believe that today’s economic and cultural center was founded by a gold prospector – as a farm. Your chauffeur awaits you at the airport and takes you to your hotel. During the journey you will learn first anecdotes and interesting facts about the city. Overnight in Belo Horizonte.

Day 2 Inhotim - Pilgrimage Site for Art Lovers

Your day will be green. In Brumadinho, just outside the city, you will find the Inhotim Contemporary Art Center, which exhibits contemporary art from Brazil. The collection – 450 works by more than 100 artists from 30 nations – is embedded in a beautiful garden that shows you a rich biodiversity in addition to art. Here the famous landscape architect Burle Max had his fingers in the game. He created a place of meditation, knowledge and pleasure. The focus has been on art since the 1960s: from paintings to photographs to installations. Art finds its exhibition space inside and outside, be it in the water, in the forest or in its own pavilions. With 1,500 species, the Botanical Garden is probably the largest collection of palm trees. You can move on a natural path between the open and closed spaces when you explore – there is no strictly prescribed route. After your visit, we will head to Ouro Preto. Overnight in Ouro Preto.

Day 3 Black Gold & Romantic Colonial Buildings

Together with your guide, you will explore Ouro Preto, the former capital of the state of Minas Gerais. It is not only a UNESCO World Heritage Site, but was also the center of the imperial gold and top ash mines. The name means “black gold” because the gold deposits were darkly colored by iron oxide. A rich gold vein was found in 1698: Ouro Preto’s history as a Brazilian Eldorado began. At that time, Ouro Preto was the richest city in the New World. A highlight of your visit to the city is the famous Baroque old town: numerous monuments and especially 13 large churches await there. The most famous are the Igreja São Francisco de Assis, the Maritz do Pilar and the opulent Matriz de Nossa Senhora da Conceiçao de Antônio Dias. In the afternoon you will travel to Mariana, the first official city in Minas Gerais. In addition to tourism, mining is still the strongest industry here. Like Ouro Preto, her appearance is characterised by opulent Baroque. You will see for yourself when you visit the churches of Matriz do Camo, São Pedro and São Francisco de Assis. You will also visit the Passagem gold mine. Overnight in Ouro Preto.

4th Day Masterpiece of Brazilian Michelangelo

Via Congonhas your way leads to Tiradentes. At your stopover, the pilgrimage church Santuário do Bom Jesus de Matozinhos is the star. The most important architect and sculptor of the Brazilian Baroque era, Aleijadinho, created it in 1785. He has also contributed life-size sculptures of the twelve baconstone apostles standing in front of the church. Incidentally, the name of the master – translated “cripple” – goes back to an incurable disease, through which he was finally able to work only under great agony. Overnight in Tiradentes.

Day 5 - Magnificent Baroque gloss

Today you will explore the charming colonial town of Tiradentes. Yesterday you met the master Aleijadinho – today you can look forward to another work: the church of Matriz de Santo Antonio with an organ from Portugal. The church of Nossa Senhora do Rosário dos Pretos, on the other hand, has very different origins: it was built by slaves and shows, among other things, African deities. In the afternoon, your tour continues through the historic district of São João del Rei, which is listed as a monument. Worth seeing are, among other things, the first church for the victims of the Emboaba war (the first Brazilian citizen cemetery) or the historic stone bridges from the 18th century. In this place, too, you will encounter Aleijadinho – in the form of the Church of São Francisco de Assis, which bears witness to its genius with a wealth of detail. The place is also the starting point for the railway line to Tiradentes. Overnight in Tiradentes.

Day 6 Luxury in Nature

Your chauffeur will take you to Reserva do Ibitipoca today in Lima Duarte. Around 4,000 hectares of protected land in the areas Lima Duarte, Bias Fortes, and Santa Rita do Ibitipoca are waiting for you. The resort was opened in 2009 to promote ecotourism and improve the lives of locals through work in tourism: social, economic and ecological aspects are the focus here. Overnight in Lima Duarte.

Day 7 - Experience and Enjoy Nature

Surrounded by waterfalls, caves and crystal clear natural pools, your exclusive accommodation is in the Ibiti project – a special place in harmony with nature and an ideal starting point for discoveries of all kinds. Guided walks are offered in the group every day – depending on the selected package in the morning and/or afternoon. One of these tours will take you to one of the surrounding mountains: the climb may be challenging, but the reward is unforgettable. From the summit, you will experience a breathtaking sunset as the setting sun immerses the landscape in golden light. On your way, walk through a Bromelia garden, visit the Cave of the Rising Sun and encounter the fabled tree of the seven cowboys. Whether on foot, high to horseback or by bicycle: For the adventurous riding excursions, bike rides or jeep rides through the vast landscape are available. And your rest is also taken care of: in the spa area you can expect soothing applications with natural oils and relaxing massages. Overnight in Lima Duarte.

Day 8: The Old Imperial City of Petrópolis

Unfortunately, you have to leave your small refuge today – for that it goes on. Visit the Imperial Museum in Petrópolis, which served as a summer residence for the Imperial family in the 19th century. Today you can admire here, among other things, the sceptre and crown of Dom Pedro II. In addition, you will see numerous other precious works of art that give a good impression of the prosperity of the top layer. By the way, the city was founded by German-speaking immigrants – a native of Mainz was commissioned to plan the summer residence. You will also see the Crystal Palace, where concerts and plays are performed today. Once you have looked around, travel on to Rio de Janeiro. Overnight in Rio de Janeiro.

Day 9 Rios Highlights

Rio is located on the Guanabara Bay in southeast Brazil and involuntarily raises images of lightly clothed Sambat dancers and the Copacabana’s dream beach. The name Rio de Janeiro – the “river of January” – comes from Portuguese sailors who discovered the bay in January 1502 and thought it was the mouth of a river. Together with your guide, you will start your tour through the Tijuca forest and go up to the 710-meter-high Corcovado mountain, also called “the hunchback”. On its summit, the famous 30-meter-high Christ Savior statue ("Cristo Redentor") is towering, which was voted one of the "New Seven Wonders of the World" in 2007. From here you will enjoy the breathtaking panoramic view of the “Wonderful City”, Guanabara Bay, the beaches and the largest urban forest in the world, the Tijuca National Park. Then drive along the picturesque beaches to the Urca district. From there, take two glass cable cars that take you to the famous Sugar Loaf (“Pão de Açúcar”). Once you have arrived at the top, a spectacular view awaits you. Overnight in Rio de Janeiro.

Day 10 Favelas and Carnival up close

Your guide will introduce you to a few social aspects of the city: you will get to know the favelas – the poor settlements on the outskirts of the city. High above the most prosperous districts of Rio lies Brazil’s largest favela. Rocinha is a symbol of contrast. Our guests report that this tour was a formative experience for them. You will immerse yourself in the real Rio and learn more about its history. You will encounter people with modest means but big hearts, roaming lively alleys and experiencing the creative energy of local artisans. In the afternoon, you will leave your hotel again and head to the Gamboa district, near the port. There you will find the “Cidade do Samba” – the lively home of the Rio Carnival. In this complex, the best samba schools in the city are preparing for the big carnival in February. In the warehouse of the “Acadêmicos da Grande Rio” you will get a glimpse into the history of the famous parade: explore the workshops, watch the construction of the moving cars and taste the costumes. Afterwards, enjoy a tasting of the famous caipirinha and participate in a percussion and dance workshop. Overnight in Rio de Janeiro.

Day 11: The Gold Road to the Colonial Treasure

You leave Rio today to visit a colonial-style jewel: Paraty. The city was founded in the 17th century and was important as a place from which gold was sent to Portugal. The paved paths and the many churches tell the story of a city founded for the Portuguese crown and shaped by Freemasons and the Gold Rush. For UNESCO, it is one of the most important testimonies to colonial architecture – the entire city has been elevated to the status of a national monument. And really you can feel transported back to the old days, given the cobblestone streets, car-free zone and flower-decorated houses built around courtyards. Overnight in Paraty.

12th Day Gold, Gems & Cachaça

Today is dedicated to the history of gold. You leave Paraty towards the city of Penha (ca.) 30 minutes away. Here you will embark on a journey through time on the Golden Path (“Caminho do Ouro”), which lies between Paraty and Minas Gerais. In the 18th century, this paved road was built by African slaves to bring gold and gemstones from the interior to the ships of the Portuguese. The hike takes you through the rainforest to beautiful waterfalls. Here you can not only take impressive photos, but also refresh them in a small bath. Round off your stay with a visit to a cachaça distillery, where you will be shown how the popular sugar cane brandy is made in the traditional way. Overnight in Paraty.

Day 13 Salvador: The Black Heart of Brazil

From the airport in Rio de Janeiro, leave for Salvador. In Salvador, you will already be expected by your new guide and chauffeur and accompanied to your hotel. Salvador was the first capital of the country and is the third largest city in Brazil. Since it was built on a mountain range, there is an upper and lower city. Overnight in Salvador.

14th day Historic coastal town with gilded church

Salvador offers tourists some highlights: you can wander across the coastline and immerse yourself in the rich culture and architecture of the region. The city is also a hotspot of contemporary culture in terms of cuisine and literature. The tour begins in the Barra district, with a magnificent view of the Atlantic Ocean and the first lighthouse of the American continent at the São Antônio fortress. The journey continues through the typical districts of Corredor da Vitória and Campo Grande, the latter an important setting for the famous Salvador Carnival. A stop at Praça Municipal will give you the opportunity to visit the traditional Mercado Modelo craft market. Afterwards, take the Lacerda elevator, an impressive 1930 art deco structure that connects the lower and upper cities in just 30 seconds. At Praça Tomé de Souza you can enjoy a wonderful view of Todos os Santos Bay. The tour continues to the historic old town district of Pelourinho, which is one of the most beautiful baroque districts outside Europe and has been declared a World Heritage Site by UNESCO. Here you will visit important sights. Overnight in Salvador.

15th day goodbye!

Even the best time is over. Today you have to say goodbye to Brazil. Your chauffeur will take you to the airport, where you will begin your journey home. Even if your holiday is now over: The beautiful souvenir photos and small souvenirs will always remind you of the time in South America.

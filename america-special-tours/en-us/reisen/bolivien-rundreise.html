Bolivia — South America's Hidden Treasure

Bolivia round trip to South America’s secret treasure with Latin America expert América Special Tours. Ask now and get personal advice.

Bolivia is still almost an insider tip of South America. Due to its extreme poverty, it is usually not considered a destination. But you should not make this mistake, because Bolivia is certainly one of the most beautiful countries on the continent. Authentic and versatile, it presents itself, not only in terms of the breathtaking landscapes, but also the population. Always cheerful and open-minded you will be warmly welcomed on this round trip through the most beautiful places in Bolivia. Let yourself be drifted by the lively, never boring everyday life in La Paz. Enchanted by magical reflections in the seemingly endless Salar de Uyuni, immerse yourself in the ancient traditions of the highlands. A truly unique journey through the secret treasure of South America.

Trip Information

Itinerary

12 Days Santa Cruz - Sucre - Potosi - Salar de Uyuni - La Paz

Your Trip Highlights

  • Private, German-speaking tour guide and own chauffeur
  • Explore the tropical city of Santa Cruz
  • Enjoy the natural paradise Biocentro Güembé
  • The "white" capital Sucre and the authentic Tarabuco
  • Fascinating views of deep valleys and high Andean peaks
  • The unique atmosphere of the former Cerro Rico silver mine
  • Visit to the salt capital Uyuni
  • On the way with your own luxury Airstream Camper
  • Your personal cook accompanies you at Salar de Uyuni
  • Experience bizarre rock formations of the Moon Valley in La Paz
  • The Magic of Titicaca
  • A boat trip to the sunny island "Isla del Sol"

Your journey through Bolivia, located in the heart of South America, begins in Santa Cruz. At about 440 meters in altitude and thus in the lowlands of Bolivia lies the largest city in the country. Together you drive with your personal chauffeur to the hotel. On the short journey, you will learn first anecdotes and small stories about Santa Cruz and Bolivia. To immerse yourself even deeper into the city, after the check-in and a small relaxation and refreshment break together with your guide set out for city exploration. Among other things, you will visit Plaza 24 de Septiembre. Today, the square is a popular meeting place for people of all ages, from all social classes and from all over the world. Take a short break on one of the benches and watch the colorful bustle before dedicating yourself to the history of Bolivia’s development during a visit to the Cathedral and the Folklore and Nature Museum. Overnight in Santa Cruz.

Just 15 minutes drive from the center of Santa Cruz is the natural paradise Biocentro Güembé. Together with your guide, you will travel to this idyllic oasis where the world’s largest butterfly house, an orchid garden, nature trails and natural lagoons suitable for swimming await you. Enjoy the exotic, tropical vegetation and refresh yourself in one of the natural pools. Overnight in Santa Cruz.

Today you leave the tropical lowlands and Santa Cruz behind and fly to the capital Sucre in about 40 minutes. Nestled between the green mountains of the Andes, in the Altiplano of the east lies probably the most beautiful city in Bolivia. The Sucres Centre is listed as a monument. Thus, demolitions and changes are forbidden and the unique colonial charm of the city could be preserved to this day and invites you to stroll. Furthermore, due to the altitude at almost 3,000 meters, there is a pleasant, spring-like climate throughout the year. In the afternoon, you will explore with your guide the “white city”, as Sucre is also called due to the many whitewashed colonial buildings. You will of course also visit the main attractions, such as the San Lázaro Church, the Casa de la Libertad and the Museum La Recoleta. The end will be your varied city tour in the convent of San Felipe Neri. Here you have a breathtaking view over the tile roofs of the white capital. Overnight in Sucre.

After getting to know Sucre yesterday, explore the surroundings today. To do this, drive to Tarabuco, about 60 kilometers away. The small village is famous for its particularly colorful Sunday market. Stroll through the small alleys along packed stalls and be inspired by smells and colors. Since it is not, as with so many markets, a touristic one, the people of the region flock here to buy the things of daily necessities. There is haggling and discussion. It is hardly more authentic than here and you rarely get to see so many different, lush, traditional costumes of the surrounding regions. At the end of an exciting day, return to Sucre. Overnight in Sucre.

Your journey continues and you leave Sucre to travel to the Bolivian highlands. After about 160 kilometers, you can reach Potosí with your driver, which is located at almost 4,100 meters above sea level. Enjoy fascinating views of deep valleys and high Andean peaks along the way. One of them is that of the colorful Cerro Ricos, who already inspired the Inca and once made the Spaniards rich. But before you dedicate yourself to this impressive mountain tomorrow, you will first get to know Potosí. Potosí is also a UNESCO World Heritage Site and your city tour takes you back to the past, glorious times of the city. Probably the most famous landmark of the city is the Casa Nacional de Moneda, also called “Mint”, where once weighed, melted, minted and shipped to Europe. Today it is a highly interesting museum that impresses with original tools, machines and coin collections. Overnight in Potosí.

During the time of the Spanish conquerors, Cerro Rico was the largest silver mine in the world, making Potosí the most precious jewel of the Spanish crown, as well as one of the largest and most prosperous cities in the New World. In the early 17th century, Potosí was the heart of the Spanish Conquistadores empire. The Spanish phrase “eso vale un Potosí” (which is worth a Potosí) was used to describe the value of absolutely priceless things and thus gives insight into the former status of Potosí for Spain. With the great silver crisis from the middle of the 17th century, the decline of Potosí began. The glorious times were over and the population fell by half. The mining business in Potosí has not recovered since then, but today the city is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and a real treasure trove of colonial art and architecture with countless townhouses and estates. The Cerro Rico still exudes a very unique atmosphere that you experience up close today. Together with your German-speaking guide, you will explore Cerro Rico and get an intensive insight into the working conditions underground and the rites of the local population for protection against the “evil spirits of Cerro Rico”. Overnight in Potosí.

From the dark mines in Potosí, travel to the salt capital Uyuni today. Keep your eyes open on the way, as you drive through one of Bolivia’s most fascinating landscapes. With a little luck, you will also see one or the other lama or alpaca graze peacefully on the roadside. After about six hours of driving, you will reach Uyuni on the edge of the salt lake of the same name. The Salar de Uyuni is the largest salt lake in the world with 10,500 square kilometers. The word white desert is probably more accurate here. The white endlessly extends beyond the horizon. Before you dedicate yourself to the glittering salt desert tomorrow, spend this afternoon on a relaxing walk through Uyuni. Overnight in Uyuni.

The salt lake Uyuni in Bolivia is the largest on earth. It consists of a kilometer-long dazzling-white nothing and is considered the number one of the most surrealistic landscapes on earth. And that's where you spend the next two days. Stay in comfortable luxury airstream campers right on the salt lake – the campers and adventure will be shared with other travelers, and the guide and chauffeur will accompany the entire group. A comfortable double bed, heating blankets for the cold nights at 4,000 meters, a bath with hot shower, air conditioning/heating, Ipod connection and a minibar make the facilities complete. With your personal chauffeur and guide you are on the road in a specially suitable 4x4 SUV. Furthermore, your personal cook accompanies you. During the day, you can experience the Salar on a bicycle, on foot or with your guide for interesting sightseeings or visits to the indigenous community. Today you will also visit the island of “Incawasi”. The small island in the middle of the white salt desert is littered with meter-high cacti. Enjoy the absolute silence of the magical place! Overnight at the Salar de Uyuni.

Also today enjoy your stay in the breathtaking Salar de Uyuni. You will visit the Thunupa volcano, whose crater lies at 5,432 meters and borders directly on the northern edge of the salar. Arriving at the foot of the volcano, get your equipment and guidance from your guide and then make your way upstairs. The ascent takes about three to four hours due to the thin high air. They arrive at a cave in the volcano and enter. After your eyes get used to the darkness after the glittering salt landscape, you realize the unique treasure that lies ahead of you. Here in this cave, you will find seven mummies ritually buried according to traditional customs thousands of years ago. After your guide has told you in detail the history of the rites and customs of the Bolivian natives, make your descent and continue to the small There Jirira. Here you get to know the barren life in the salt desert and have the opportunity to stroll a bit in the village. Overnight at the Salar de Uyuni.

Today you say goodbye to your luxurious Airstream camper, because your trip will take you to the most lively city in the country to La Paz. La Paz is the highest “quasi” capital in the world (the real capital of Bolivia is Sucre). It is like a moon crater, as it lies in a narrow valley and extends over areas from 3,000 m to 4,100 m above sea level. Hardly a green spot can be found here and also the oxygen is scarce in this extraordinary city. However, one finds abundant color, life and cultural contrasts among his people. Women with bowler hats and voluminous skirts shape the cityscape just as much as businessmen in suits. At the airport, you will of course be welcomed as usual and taken to the hotel before heading out in the afternoon to explore this fascinating city. In the evening, today you will be abducted into the world of Bolivian folklore. You will visit a typical Peña, a cozy bar with delicious Bolivian food, traditional drinks and most importantly, live music. The rhythms of Altiplano are similar, but each region maintains its own melodies and takes you to the magical realm of the various Andean regions. Overnight in La Paz.

Today you will visit the Sacred Lake of the Inca. Lake Titicaca is considered the origin of the Inca, because according to legend, the sun-god lived on the Sun Island and from there his children Manco Capac and Mama Ocllo sent to found the Inca Empire in the world. The fascination of the Inca for Lake Titicaca is easy to understand, because it exudes a unique magic that will also captivate you. In the blue, clear water the surrounding mountains and the sky are reflected and should clouds be seen over the lake, it immediately changes its color. On the edge of the lake, just before the Peruvian border, is the small town of Copacabana. Once you have arrived here, your private boat awaits you with which you make your way over the glittering, endless lake to the sunny island “Isla del Sol”. These can be reached after about an hour and at the beginning enjoy a delicious lunch with typical dishes of the highlands. Freshly strengthened, you will then make your way to exploring the island. In the afternoon, make your way back to La Paz. Enjoy the ride, as it awaits you one of the most picturesque routes between the Andean highlands and deep blue Lake Titicaca. Overnight in La Paz.

Even the most beautiful journey comes to an end and so it is today to say goodbye to the incomparable Bolivia. Even if your journey is over, the precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories of your trip will accompany you forever. Look forward to sharing your friends and family at home with your experiences in the distance. For your return flight, you will be taken to the airport of La Paz.

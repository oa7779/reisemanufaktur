Tailor-Made Ecuador — The Treasures of the Galápagos

Experience a unique individual trip to Ecuador including a Galapagos cruise on the luxurious catamaran M/C Elite. Let's talk now.

11 days

Paradise beaches, volcanic cones that rise to the sky and an incredible biodiversity: all this awaits you during your trip to the Galapagos Islands. The Spaniards who discovered them in the 15th century gave them the name Enchanted Islands because of their remote location. They were renamed only in the 19th century and today bear the name of the famous giant tortoises here. You are very close to them on your journey, discover the colorful underwater world and walk past mangrove and cactus forests. From island to island you will travel comfortably on board the luxurious catamaran M/C Elite, which will leave nothing to be desired.

Itinerary

11 Days Quito – San Cristóbal – Galapagos Cruise – San Cristóbal – Guayaquil

Bienvenidos - Welcome to Ecuador! On the way from the airport to the hotel, you will get a first impression of the charming capital Quito and learn exciting information and anecdotes first hand from your private guide. Surrounded by snow-capped volcanoes, the city looks back on a turbulent and powerful history: in the 13th century Quito was the flourishing capital of the Kingdom of Quitus and later the northern capital of the Inca Empire. The beautiful colonial-style temples, monasteries and villas you pass by date back to the 16th and 17th centuries. UNESCO has declared the old town as the first city ever to be a World Heritage Site. Your Hotel Casa Gangotena is located in the middle of this splendor, near the Plaza San Francisco, where wealthy families settled – including the Gangotena family, after whom the hotel is named. Overnight in Quito.

They are located in a special geographical location. On the one hand, you are in the highest capital in the world at 2,850 meters, on the other hand, you can stand at the equator monument Mitad del Mundo (center of the world) on both hemispheres at the same time. Exactly to this point – the latitude 0 – brings your journey today. During a chocolate tasting, let yourself be enchanted by the intense aromas and artistic processing and immerse yourself in the fascinating world of chocolate production. Back in the city center, you and your guide will trace Quito’s colonial past. You’ll start your tour at the city’s main square, Independence Square with the presidential and archbishop’s palaces, as well as the impressive cathedral. A jewel of the South American Baroque is the Jesuit church La Compañia, which has an ornate facade and a richly decorated interior. Afterwards, you will get to know the Church of San Francisco. 150 years was built on the colonial ensemble, which is said to be the largest in Latin America. in Quito.

Today the time has come: You set off for the longing destination of many travelers. About 1,000 kilometers west of the Ecuadorian border lies a paradise with a unique animal and plant world: The Galapagos Islands. No wonder that the archipelago has been designated a UNESCO World Heritage Site and is under strict nature conservation. Upon arrival at the airport in San Cristobal, you will be taken to the pier at the port, where you will already be expected by the crew of your luxury catamaran M/C Elite. After you have set up in your cabin, the journey begins. In the highlands in the southwestern part of the island, visit the freshwater-filled crater lagoon El Junco. At an altitude of about 700 meters, the deep blue ocean spreads beneath you. Bahama ducks, pond chickens and frigate birds gather at this unique lagoon, which is one of the few permanent freshwater reserves on the islands. Overnight on board.

After a delightful breakfast on board, this morning you will head to the island of North Seymour, a small plateau of lava elevated from the ocean on northern Seymour Island, separated only by a narrow strait from the more southerly island of Baltra (formerly South Seymour). The 1.5 km long circular route takes you over the rocky, flat and windy island. Nord Seymour is particularly exciting because of the many magnificent frigate birds, whose males inflate their dazzling red throat sac to a considerable size during the courtship rituals. In addition to other frigate species, there are also colonies of blue-footed boobies and sea lions as well as plenty of red cliff crabs, black sea lizards and yellow land iguanas. On the beach of Bachas or the island of Mosquera there is the possibility to go snorkeling. After a lunch break on board, visit the Cerro Dragon – the dragon hill. This is located on the northern side of Santa Cruz Island and was named after the land iguanan colonies that are native to it. In the saltwater lagoon behind the beach you can also observe flamingos, stilt walkers, skewer ducks and other bird species. Take an easy hike up the hill and enjoy a great view over the bay from above. Overnight on board.

After a boat ride from Puerto Villamil, today you reach the rock island of Las Tintoreras. During a walk here you will see hundreds of sea iguanas on the rocks that have their main nesting site nearby. In a lava canal there are often white sharks, often accompanied by other sharks and sea lions. You pass a sandy beach where sea lions sunbathe. During the return trip, with a bit of luck you can also see penguins and blue-footed boobies sitting on the rocks. Your next stop is Sierra Negra, located in the south of Isabela Island between the Alcedo and Cerro Azul volcanoes. Its crater is the largest on Galapagos with a diameter of 10 km and the second largest active volcanic crater in the world. You follow the hiking trail, which takes you to the crater rim on foot in 1.5 hours. From here you have a spectacular view over the crater. Your day will be rounded off with a visit to Isabela’s Turtle Rearing Center. Here you will encounter turtles of different sizes and all ages. Your nature guide will tell you all about the animals, as well as the efforts that are made to protect these animals. Overnight on board.

Today’s excursion will take you to Punta Moreno on the north coast of Isabela Island. The vegetation in this environment is comparatively sparse, the main attraction is the lagoons in the middle of a black lava flow, where they can observe different species of birds. Marine lizards, sea lions and other marine animals also like to join. From here, enjoy a panoramic view of the three most active volcanoes of the Galapagos: Sierra Negra, Cerro Azul and La Cumbre. After a delicious lunch on board, your afternoon tour will take you to Urbina Bay. This is located at the foot of the Alcedo volcano and is characterized by a short black sand beach with lava stones in the background. A short circuit takes you from the beach to the interior of the island, where you can see flightless cormorants, giant turtles, pelicans, land iguanas and smaller species of birds. The bay is also often home to rays and sea turtles. Overnight on board.

In the morning you reach Punta Espinosa, a narrow strip of land where sea lizards gather in large groups on the lava rocks. The famous flightless cormorant can be observed here as well as penguins, pelicans and sea lions. Numerous mangroves protrude into the sea and testify to a healthy, intact ecosystem. Two circuits lead over the island. The eastern circuit leads past breeding sites of sea lizards to several small beaches where sea lions frolic. At the northern tip of the island are the nesting sites of flightless cormorants as well as small groups of penguins. The western circuit leads you into the interior of the island on a cold lava flow at the foot of the volcano La Cumbre. Here you see lava lizards, land iguanas, galapago dusts and mockingbirds. Once you’ve had enough, take your course on Punta Vicente Roca. This place can only be reached by dinghy. During the ride, you can observe hundreds of nesting blue-footed boobies and pelicans, sea turtles, sea lion colonies and land iguanas. Snorkeling in the bay brings you closer to the breathtaking underwater world of Isabela’s coast. Overnight on board.

Today you will visit Cerro Brujo - the witch hill located on a beautiful white sand beach. The volcanic crater has an extremely interesting crater landscape. Red cliff crabs, sea lizards and sea lions are at home here. During a small beach walk you can also observe blue-footed boobies, masked boobies, brown pelicans, coastal birds, mockingbirds, flycatchers and Darwin finches. In the afternoon, visit the Turtle Reserve in San Cristóbal, where you will have the opportunity to observe the impressive Galapagos giant turtles in their natural environment. During a guided tour, you will learn about the conservation programs and the important role of turtles in the islands’ unique ecosystem. This not only provides you with fascinating insights into the lives of these majestic animals, but also the opportunity to contribute to the conservation of this endangered species. Overnight on board.

Today you will visit Cerro Brujo - the witch hill located on a beautiful white sand beach. The volcanic crater has an extremely interesting crater landscape. Red cliff crabs, sea lizards and sea lions are at home here. During a small beach walk you can also observe blue-footed boobies, masked boobies, brown pelicans, coastal birds, mockingbirds, flycatchers and Darwin finches. In the afternoon you will head to the island of Lobos. The name means sea lion island, because here are loud, antics drifting and amuse countless sea lions who welcome you with "open fins" in their home. During a tour of the island, you can observe these friendly and curious animals in detail. The island of Lobos is also a nesting site of blue-footed boobies and an excellent place for snorkeling. Swim with the sea lions for the bet and watch the sea lizards eating under water. Overnight on board.

Today is your last day on board the M/C Elite. In the morning you will visit the Interpretation Centre on the island of San Cristóbal. Opened in 1998, today it presents a good documentation of the history of the Galapagos Islands, their ecosystems and flora and fauna. During your visit, you will also learn a lot about the colonization history and conservation efforts. Then you have to leave this small island paradise again. You will be taken to San Cristóbal Airport and take your flight back to the mainland. Upon arrival at the port city of Guayaquil, your German-speaking tour guide will await you and take you to your city hotel Hotel del Parque. Guayaquil is a large city on the Pacific coast on the west bank of the Guaya River and, due to its important port, the economic and financial center of the country. There is almost a Caribbean feeling here – not only in terms of temperatures: In the parks, iguanas sunbathe in the trees. On the last evening of your trip, you can indulge in the wellness area of your five-star hotel or dine at the award-winning hotel restaurant (not included). Overnight in Guayaquil.

Before you say goodbye to Ecuador today, you will have the opportunity to get to know Guayaquil in the morning. On your city tour, visit the most interesting places of this tropical and bustling Pacific metropolis: you start your tour at the most beautiful square in the city, the Parque Simon Bolívar. On its west side is the neo-Gothic cathedral with a large lead glazed window rosette. The original wooden cathedral, built in 1547, went up in flames during the city fire of 1896. This last great fire destroyed many of the city’s magical wooden houses. Only the recently restored Las Peñas district, located north of the center, has been preserved in its originality and represents the last scenic trace of colonial Guayaquil. On the journey along the river bank of Guaya, you will get to know the beautiful city promenade Malecón decorated with tropical flora, the pride of all Guayaquileños. Depending on the departure time, you will finally be taken back to the airport. With unforgettable memories in your luggage, you will begin your journey home.

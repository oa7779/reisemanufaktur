A Golfer's Dream — Argentina, Brazil & Uruguay

Golf trip to Argentina, Brazil and Uruguay with your Latin American experts. Here you can request your individual round trip and get personal advice.

18 days

A sample itinerary—tailored to you

Above all, South America has one thing to offer: infinitely vast and untouched nature. You can expect eternal ice, mighty waterfalls, dreamlike beaches and landscapes as far as the eye can see. On this trip you will explore the diversity of the continent from a different angle, namely that of the golfer. Diverse places challenge you to a strategic game through the bunkers, water hazards and lagoons of Argentina, Uruguay and Brazil. This golf experience will certainly not be forgotten soon!

Trip Information

Itinerary

18 Days Buenos Aires – Colonia – Montevideo – Bariloche – Iguazú Waterfalls – Rio de Janeiro

  • Killing force of nature: the Iguazú waterfalls
  • World metropolis Rio de Janeiro

Your journey through Argentina begins in the capital. Upon arrival at the airport, you will be welcomed here by your guide and taken to the hotel by your chauffeur. So you can already learn first anecdotes about this fascinating city on the journey. Buenos Aires is huge, but anything but a faceless metropolis. The world capital of tango has a lot to offer culturally – it is said to have more theater halls than any other place in the world. In many corners, Buenos Aires is also astonishingly European and fitting for the nickname “Paris of South America”, Buenos Aires today also attracts the country’s bohemian and alternative scene. Overnight in Buenos Aires.

Your driver will take you to Palermo, the green district of the city. Also here you will find the Argentine horse race track and the national polo field. Then visit the aristocratic district of Recoleta, the most well-kept district of Buenos Aires. The neoclassical cemetery La Recoleta forms the center of the district. Your city tour will now take you to the Plaza de Mayo in the heart of the city, which is lined by the Casa Rosada Government House, the former City Hall and the Cathedral. On Avenida de Mayo you will get to know the oldest coffee house in the country, Café Tortoni. After a small snack and refreshments, head to historic San Telmo, the oldest neighborhood in the city. It is particularly colorful in La Boca in the east of the city. Avenida 9 de Julio, the widest street in the world, will take you past Teatro Colón, one of the most successful opera houses in the world. In the evening your chauffeur will take you to El Cabaret. Here you can enjoy a three-course menu at a fascinating tango show of Rojo Tango with live music and different dance couples. The show delights with passionate dancers and unique, beautiful costumes and rounds off this day dignified. Overnight in Buenos Aires.

Today is your first day of golf on one of the best golf courses in Buenos Aires. Leave the center of Argentina’s capital city and make your way to the Nordelta Golf Club. There you will already be expected by an English-speaking, professional golfer who will support you throughout the day. The golf course in Jack Nicklaus design is one of the most difficult in the country with numerous water hazards, lagoons and several bunkers. From the four different golf teas (black, blue, white and red), you will have the opportunity to recognize the strategic defenses of each of the 18 holes. The modern green spaces with interesting sizes have smooth, but at the same time also "difficult to read" movements. Overnight in Buenos Aires.

Your second day of golf from Buenos Aires is spent at the Pilar Golf Club, north of Buenos Aires. This golf course is the first course designed by Jack Nicklaus in Buenos Aires. The former professional golfer, also known as “The Golden Bear”, specialized in the design of high-profile golf courses worldwide after completing his professional career. He uses his many years of experience as a golfer to make every course a challenging and varied experience. This is also the case with the design of the Pilar Golf Club. In addition to the longest hole in South America (665 yards / par 6) it impresses with plenty of water, undulated fairways and its season-independent, excellent condition. Walk in the footsteps of the only four-time winner of the PGA Senior Tour and prove your skills. Your English-speaking professional golfer will be happy to support you. Overnight in Buenos Aires.

In the morning you will be picked up from your hotel and taken to the port of Buenos Aires. There is already a ferry waiting for you to cross the Rio de la Plata and thus also the border between Argentina and Uruguay. After about an hour's drive you reach the port of Colonia del Sacramento. It is the oldest city in Uruguay and one of the few remaining colonial cities in southern Latin America. It has been a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1995. You will explore the city on a short walking tour, stroll through the cobbled streets and enjoy the special atmosphere of the city. Visit the famous Calle de los Suspiros (Sigh Street), which has often served as a setting for historical films. In addition to its historical value, Colonia offers other attractions such as the lighthouse, the cathedral and the nearby bullring. Before the one-hour drive to Carmelo, you have the opportunity to explore the old town or the beautiful nature on the riverbank on your own. Arriving in Carmelo, you will find the fantastic five-star Hyatt Hotel Carmelo Resort & Spa, which boasts the most beautiful golf course in Uruguay. Overnight in Carmelo.

During the next two days you will again devote yourself completely to the wonderful sport of golf. Since the place of your hotel Carmelo Resort & Spa is considered the most beautiful in the whole country, you should not miss this Uruguayan golfing experience. Designed to be South America’s leading golf course, the 18-hole, par 72 course is set amidst the beauty of nature. Perfectly located in the Uruguayan countryside, the golf course winds around numerous lakes, wetlands, native trees and ornamental grass. The course is a true test for golfers of every category. The course was designed by the international team of architects from American Golf Course Design LTDA, South America’s most prestigious design company. Golfers will appreciate details such as curved putting south of the equator – best hybrid Bermuda fairways, challenging white sand bunkers and vast lakes, all designed and maintained down to the last detail, make for an unforgettable golfing experience. Overnight in Carmelo.

Montevideo is the capital of Uruguay and a modern city with a European flair, which has been able to preserve its old, colonial center. During a half-day city tour, you will get to know the contrasting architecture of the city a little closer. Most attractions, such as the Plaza de Constitución with its cathedral and colonial town hall, are located in the old town. Here you will also see the impressive Teatro Solis and the Plaza de la Independencia, the Square of Independence, with a monument by the national hero José Gervasio Artigas. A unique experience is a visit to the old market hall at the port, the Mercado del Puerto. Then your tour will take you to the Palacio Legislativo, the seat of the Uruguayan Parliament. On your way lies the Mercado Agricola, an old market hall. We round off your tour today with a visit to the prestigious district of Carrasco and the Hotel Casino Carrasco, which is now considered a national cultural heritage and operated as a hotel by a luxury hotel chain. Before your guide takes you to Montevideo Airport for your short flight back to Buenos Aires, stop at Plaza Virgilio from where you can enjoy an excellent view of the Montevideo skyline. Overnight in Buenos Aires.

Your next stop will take you to the south of Argentina. Bariloche, located in the foothills of the Andes, rivals the Swiss Alps. Located on the southern shore of Lake Nahuel Huapi, the ski resort was founded in 1898 and is today the most important city in the region. It is a popular destination for winter sports enthusiasts and mountaineers and known for its chocolate industry and cozy wooden houses. Today’s tour will give you a first glimpse of the beautiful landscape around Bariloche. You will drive along the forests, mountains and lakes in a southeastern direction on Avenida Ezequiel Bustillo. Passing the beach Playa Bonita you reach the station Montagne Campanario, where you can take the chairlift up to the 1,050m high mountain top. Here you have a breathtaking panorama with the lakes Nahuel Huapi and Moreno, the peninsulas St Peter’s, Llao Llao, Isla Victoria and the mountain peaks Otto, Lopez and Goye as well as a beautiful view of the whole city Bariloche. Once again downstairs, drive to the peninsula Llao Llao where the little St. Edward's Chapel and the Llao Llao Hotel are located. Then you will arrive at a panoramic viewpoint with beautiful views over Lake Moreno and the peninsula Llao Llao. Overnight in Bariloche.

The connections of the 18-hole Llao Llao golf course are unique in Argentina and are among the best in the world. The golf course has undulating green areas, fairways seeded with fine grass and is surrounded by Nahuel Huapi Lake and large lush trees. Its perfectly integrated water separators and bunkers create an interesting and balanced combination. Here you have enough opportunities during the next two days to prove your golf skills also in distant Patagonia. Overnight in Bariloche.

Today you will leave the wild Patagonia behind and travel further towards one of the most impressive natural spectacles in South America: the Iguazú Waterfalls. The falls stretch across the southern border of Brazil and northern Argentina in the middle of the national park of the same name. By plane you can reach the Argentinean town of Puerto Iguazú near the country border, where your guide will be waiting for you at the airport and accompanying you to your hotel. The Belmond Hotel Das Cataratas is located directly in the Brazilian part of the National Park, just a few steps away from the falls. Overnight in Iguazú.

Worldwide, there are only a handful of natural wonders as impressive as the Iguazú Falls. You too will surely be amazed by the sight of the 275 thunderous waterfalls. Today, your guide will take you to this impressive World Heritage site. Before you dedicate yourself to the Brazilian side, explore this natural wonder from Argentina today. You can choose which way to be impressed by the waterfalls: on the lower path, you will pass various viewpoints over a distance of 1.2 kilometers, allowing for breathtaking views of the rapids and cascades. For the higher path, you should be vertigo-free as it will take you up to close to the cliffs of the falls. From the lower circular route, take the “Jungle Train” to the Teufelsschlucht station. From there, take a short walk to a balcony on the edge of the falls. Then follow the circular path along the Iguazú Canyon and get to know the flora and fauna of the park a little closer. Overnight in Iguazú.

For today’s golf day, make your way to the Wish Iguassu Resort Golf Course. The 18-hole course was designed by golf architect Erik Larsen, who worked with Arnold Palmer for 28 years. It includes a total of 7,100 yards, par 72 and a driving range. This golf course is a complex of very special character, as it is surrounded by lush vegetation. Overnight in Iguazú.

Today you will experience the Brazilian side of the falls up close! A wooden web system that winds along the rocks frames the huge talc boiler. At the end of the trail, the wooden structure widens into a platform at the foot of the Floriano Waterfall, whose wind blows into the astonished faces of visitors. Over a 60-meter-high rock edge, the water masses plunge into the rainforest in 275 individual cataracts over a width of almost 2.5 kilometers – an image of majestic beauty. Following this breathtaking excursion, say goodbye to the Iguazú Falls and travel on to Rio de Janeiro by domestic flight. There you will be met by your guide at the airport and taken to the beautiful Belmond Copacabana Palace Hotel. Overnight in Rio de Janeiro

Today you will explore the highlights of this city. Your tour starts in downtown Rio and you’ll experience old Rio with its cobblestones and stunning architecture. On your way are the Candlariakirche, the Stadtaqueduct, the Stadttheater and the National Library. Followed by the most popular attraction: The Sugar Loaf. The top of the mountain can be reached from the Urca district, where you board the glazed gondola railway, which takes you to the dizzying altitude of 365 meters with a stopover. Then we continue to the most famous postcard motif of the country – the Christ Statue. You will travel the way there with a cog train and pass the impressive Tijuca rainforest. Arriving at the top, another record awaits, the statue of Christ. With her 30 meters, she was named one of the “7 New Wonders of the World”! Another highlight of this eventful day is the visit to a typical Brazilian barbecue restaurant ("Churrascaria") Although the meat skewers are always mentioned here, which are cut directly on the plate as desired but also for vegetarians there are many delicacies! Overnight in Rio de Janeiro.

Your last day of golf is today. Make your way to the Golf Club Gavea, which is considered Rio de Janeiro’s most prestigious club. The course is a real challenge because the first 9 holes wind through the dense, jungle-like Tijuca rainforest that stretches up to the statue of Cristo Redentor. The second section is located on the dream beaches of São Conrado. Its scenic diversity makes Gavea Golf Club simply unmistakable. Enjoy the variety of rainforest, palm trees, sandy beach and sea. No golfer should miss this 18-hole, par 69 place, because its very different fairway characters are like a reflection of the metropolis living from opposites. Overnight in Rio de Janeiro.

After almost three weeks, your eventful golf trip through South America ends today. Many impressions richer you take the return flight from Rio de Janeiro. Your guide will take you to the airport.

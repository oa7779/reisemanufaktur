Day 1 Bienvenidos a Costa Rica!

Your journey through Costa Rica begins in the capital San José, which is 1150 meters above sea level. In the north, several volcanoes rise above the silhouette of the city, in the south the rugged Talamanca mountains. Your German-speaking guide will welcome you at the airport after passport control to discuss the coming days with you. You will then be chauffeured to your hotel in the city center. Your accommodation is located in San José: Costa Rica Marriott Hotel Hacienda Belén. Overnight in San Jose.

Day 2 From the City to the Jungle

After breakfast, you will meet your guide for the next few days. After a short get-together, you will be taken to your lodge about one and a half hours north of San José. Your accommodation is located in the valley of Bajos del Toro, an Eldorado for nature lovers. It is flanked by volcanoes and enchanted by lush rainforest and hidden waterfalls. Here your lodge – the El Silencio Lodge – offers you natural enjoyment far away from all the hustle and bustle of civilization. You should not expect television or a swimming pool here – you will find a lot of peace and quiet for that. You will reside in one of only 16 luxuriously equipped suites with whirlpool, fireplace and private terrace. In the restaurant you can be pampered with delicious, healthy food. The spa area offers relaxing massages as well as yoga classes outdoors. Your accommodation is in Bajos del Toro: El Silencio Lodge & Spa. Overnight in Bajos del Toro.

Day 3 On a Cloth Feeling with Culture

Today you will visit the nearby village of Los Bajos del Toro, where you will get an insight into everyday life. The town is located away from the tourist routes and is therefore still relatively unknown to foreign holidaymakers. Perhaps this is due to the originality of its villagers, who cultivate their traditions in everyday life. This excursion will take you to the cultural hubs of the community: the church, the school and the football field. Also, the “Pulpería” (which means as much as Aunt Emma shops) of Doña Olga can be called a communal center, as it not only sells food but is also popular for its self-made baked goods and fresh tortillas. If you wish, the nice lady cordially invites you to shape your own tortilla. In the afternoon, relax with a 90-minute stone massage at the spa (not included). Overnight in Bajos del Toro.

Day 4 Continue to Arenal National Park

Today you will continue your journey north, where one of the highlights of Costa Rica’s natural spectacles awaits you: the Parque Nacional Volcán Arenal. Around the Arenal, the most active volcano in the country, a protected area extends over 120 square kilometers. After the 13th century, the Arenal had fallen into a slumber, from which it woke up again in 1968. Since then, smoke has almost always risen from its perfectly conical cone. Your accommodation is in La Fortuna: Hotel Amor Arenal. Overnight in La Fortuna.

Day 5: The Tour to the Volcano

Today, take a closer look at the Arenal. Your tour starts with a drive to the active, western side of the volcano, from where you will embark on a small hike. This will take you through the rainforest and up to the remains of a solidified lava flow reminiscent of an eruption in 1992. Along the way, you can expect a species-rich animal and plant world as well as fascinating formations of lava rock. From the height of the cooled lava flow, you have an impressive view of the volcano and the surrounding area. After all the effort of your hike, you can – if you wish – rest your limbs while bathing in a hot mineral spring (optional). Overnight in La Fortuna.

Day 6 - Rincón de la Vieja National Park

Today you will continue to the sun room of Costa Rica. Your simpler accommodation may remind you of an old ranch in the best Western style. The Hacienda Guachipelín is located in the middle of the so-called tropical dry forest, where a large number of wild animals are native. Primates, wild and big cats, parrots, various rodents and ground-dwelling birds can be observed in the area. The extraordinary hot springs are one of the many highlights of this place. Nowadays, the advantages of a bath in thermal springs are sufficiently known worldwide. Enjoy recovery, relaxation of stressed muscles and your physical and mental well-being. Furthermore, countless activities are offered in the area. Marvel at native trees, exotic flowers and magnificent views of the volcano. Your accommodation is located in Liberia: Hotel Hacienda Guachipelín. Overnight in Liberia.

Day 7 - Continue to Punta Islita

Today, you can enjoy a walk through the Rincón de la Vieja National Park. The protected area around the active Rincón de la Vieja volcano impresses with its great diversity. A particularly interesting area of the National Park is “Las Pailas”, a sector of several dozen hectares on the southern slope of the volcano with numerous geothermal activities. The next stop of your trip is Punta Islita, a charming village located on a beautiful stretch of the Pacific coast. Here you can expect untouched wilderness, rugged hills and beautiful beaches that invite you to a variety of activities in nature – such as kayaking, horse riding or bird watching. There is also something culturally offered here: you can visit the open-air museum about contemporary art, where mosaics, murals, carvings and paintings are exhibited. Optionally, you can also take a trip to observe roar monkeys, the largest primates in South America. If you are more interested in the animals of the sea, at low tide there is a guided walk along the coast where you can discover crabs, sea urchins and various reef fish. Your guide will now say goodbye to you for the return trip to San José. Overnight in Islita Beach.

Day 8 - 9 Paradise Days

The next few days are marked by pure relaxation. Enjoy the exclusive resort surrounded by magnificent nature and views of the Pacific Ocean. With your own terrace and hammock, you can let your soul dangle and enjoy the tropical flair to the fullest. Via a stairway you get to the natural beach. In the name of sustainability, nature has the upper hand here. Enjoy some tropical delicious cocktails in this natural environment and taste the luxury of sweet “do nothing”. Overnight in Islita Beach.

10th day home

Today your exploration tour of Costa Rica ends. Your chauffeur will take you back to San José airport for your trip home.

Great Lakes — A US Road Trip

Great Lakes USA Car Rental: Experience the Great Lakes States on a one-time round trip with América Special Tours. Ask now!

15 days

Your trip idea

A sample itinerary—tailored to you

Start on an unforgettable road trip in the land of great lakes. Stunning landscapes and enchanting cities are waiting to be discovered by you. Marvel at Chicago’s interesting architecture, enjoy the local brewing art in Milwaukee and explore the northern areas of the states of Wisconsin and Michigan. Here you are off the beaten tourist trail and enjoy spectacular views of the lake landscape again and again during the journey. Visit the springtime Traverse City and explore Motor City Detroit. A trip to Toronto in Canada should not be missing. Afterwards, experience the impressive Niagara Falls up close. On your way back to Chicago, visit the Rock’n’Roll Hall of Fame in Cleveland and traverse northern Indiana, home to the third largest Amish community in the United States.

Trip Information

Itinerary

15 Days Chicago – Milwaukee – Marquette – Mackinac Island – Traverse City – Detroit – Toronto – Niagara Falls – Cleveland – Elkhart – Chicago

Your Trip Highlights

  • Explore the Great Lakes Region on your own by rental car
  • Architecture Cruise Over the Chicago River
  • Lived beer brewing culture in Milwaukee
  • Panoramic ride along Lake Michigan
  • A Journey into the Past on Mackinac Island
  • Impressive sand dunes at Traverse City
  • Guests at the wineries north of Traverse City
  • The birthplace of Motown – Detroit
  • High up on the CN Tower in Toronto
  • Boat Tour to Niagara Falls & Speedboat Tour on the Niagara River
  • Rock'n'Roll Hall of Fame in Cleveland
  • Insight into the Amish culture in Elkhart

Day 1 Welcome to Heartland

Welcome to the metropolis of the Midwest - Chicago. Arriving at the airport, you will receive your rental car and make your own way to your hotel. Chicago is generally considered the cultural epicenter of the United States. Impressive buildings, innovative star restaurants and world-class museums – Chicago has a lot to offer. A special highlight is the 40 km long shoreline on Lake Michigan, which is only a few steps from the bustling business and shopping center. In the outskirts of Chicago, you can explore unique attractions such as the architectural masterpieces of the famous Frank Lloyd Wright or visit one of the largest and best malls in the USA. Overnight in Chicago.

Day 2 Experience Chicago's Architecture

Today you will embark on a special exploration tour of the city. Chicago is also known as the “Windy City” and is famous all over the world for its special architecture. As part of an approximately one-hour boat tour, you will embark on an exciting trip to the Chicago River. Professional guides tell the dramatic history of Chicago, its reconstruction after the Great Fire of 1871 and how the city has become the cradle of modern American architecture. You will see the buildings of world-famous architects such as Mies van der Rohe, Skidmore Owings & Merrill and Helmut Jahn. Overnight in Chicago.

3rd day of brewing in Milwaukee

Today you start your tour and drive to your first destination Milwaukee. The city is best known for its local breweries, festivals, numerous activities such as bicycle and kayactours and the world’s only Harley Davidson Museum. Take a relaxing walk along Lake Michigan to the North Point Lighthouse. Enjoy a local beer and feel the fresh sea breeze on your skin. Overnight in Milwaukee.

Day 4 Along Lake Michigan

You continue your journey north. Enjoy fantastic views of Lake Michigan during your drive into the sparsely populated north of the state of Michigan. Today’s destination is Marquette on Lake Superior. After the long drive, walk on the beach and climb the lighthouse of Marquette. Overnight in Marquette.

5th Day Wild Waterfalls and Mackinac Island

Your journey today is a real natural spectacle. You drive along the coastline of Lake Superior and have the opportunity to take a break on the deserted beaches. Be sure to stop at the Tahquamenon Waterfalls and take a small hike here. In the afternoon you will reach the town of St. Ignace, where you will transfer by ferry to Mackinac Island. Cars, traffic jams and smog are incorrect, because “horseless vehicles” have been banned on Mackinac Island since 1898. The island can only be reached by ferry or plane. The movement takes place by horse-drawn carriage, a rented bicycle or on foot. The special charm of Mackinac Island is demonstrated by the lovingly restored Victorian-style houses, the colorful autumn foliage, the rattling hooves of the horses, the seductive aroma of homemade fudge and the rippling of the waves – and the inevitably slower pace gives you the opportunity to enjoy the nostalgic charm of the island all the more intensely. Overnight on Mackinac Island.

Day 6 The Dunes of the Sleeping Bear

With your rental car today it goes on to the south. Enjoy the fantastic view of Lake Michigan on your ride. Before you stop at your Traverse City hotel, continue on to Sleeping Bear Dunes National Lakeshore. The protected park is about 288 km² in size and stretches for 60 km along the northwest shore of Lake Michigan’s lower peninsula. The main attraction of the park are the sand dunes, which tower over the shore up to 140 m high. Treat yourself to a few quiet moments at the viewpoint and let your view wander over the lake with its offshore islands. Afterwards, return to the second largest city in northern Michigan – Traverse City. Overnight in Traverse City.

7th Day Cherries and Wine

Traverse City lies amidst the glacier-shaped landscape surrounded by deep freshwater lakes and rolling hills. The city is mainly known for fruit cultivation – here you will find the largest sour cherry producer in the USA. In addition, the region has also made a name for itself as an important center for wine and craft beer. You will find numerous wineries and wineries on the peninsulas of Leelanau and Old Mission, which you can also visit in of course. Today you have at your disposal to explore the city and its surroundings in peace. Overnight in Traverse City.

Day 8: Motor City Detroit

After exploring wonderful natural landscapes in the last few days, travel on to the metropolis of Detroit today. The city stands for a pronounced car culture and history. It is also the venue for numerous music and sports events and a city in constant change. Explore the city on a stroll along the Detroit River boardwalk, past the Detroit skyline with General Motors’ main building. If you let your view wander to the other side of the shore, you can already see Canada. Go back in time with a visit to the Henry Ford Museum, stroll through Eastern Market’s farmers markets and marvel at modern art at the Detroit Institute of Art. Detroit is the cradle of Motown music and looks back on a moving musical history - best experienced in a bar with live music. Overnight in Detroit.

9th Day Detour to Canada

Today you cross the border with Canada. Drive along Lake Erie to your day’s destination Toronto. This metropolis is one of the hottest and most important cities in Canada. Located on the northwest shore of Lake Ontario, you will find both an impressive skyline with the famous CN Tower in its center and numerous parks and green areas. Queen Street West takes turns with trendy restaurants, boutiques and galleries. Downtown cultural institutions include the Art Gallery of Ontario and the Royal Ontario Museum, as well as the Hockey Hall of Fame and Bell Lightbox, headquarters of the Toronto International Film Festival. After arriving at the hotel, enjoy a walk in the waterfront. Overnight in Toronto.

Day 10 Explore Toronto

Today you have free time to explore the city on your own. A highlight that you should not miss is the CN Tower: If you are looking for an adrenaline-packed experience, the Edge Walk is just right for you: equipped with a climbing belt and secured on a steel bracket, you walk along the Edge Walk directly on the CN Tower. If this experience is too experimental for you, there is the gentler option; enjoying a nice lunch at the 360° restaurant in the tower of the CN Tower, enjoying spectacular views of the city and Lake Ontario. There are also numerous museums and art galleries to discover. Overnight in Toronto.

Day 11 Natural Wonders Niagara Falls

Today it is time to get up early, because your journey leads you today to the most famous waterfalls of the USA and Canada – the Niagara Falls. Walk through Queen Victoria Park on the Canadian side. Along the river bank, spectacular views of the entire extent of the falls open up. Cross the U.S. border and explore the oldest state park in the United States, Niagara Falls State Park. Walk along the various viewpoints, through flowering gardens and past historic sites. You are very close to the falls on the boardwalks of the Cave of the Winds viewpoint. In order to get even closer to the huge falls, take part in a boat tour “Maid of the Mist”. The boat will bring you close to Horseshoe Falls. Feel the thunderous masses of water with all your senses up close. As the culmination of a unique day, embark on an approximately one-hour white water cruise on the Niagara River. Overnight in Niagra-on-the-Lake.

Day 12 - Continue to Cleveland

After your impressive day yesterday, there is another travel day ahead of you today. You drive along the Erie Lake to the city of Cleveland. The city is famous for its Rock ́n ́Roll Hall of Fame. Year after year, over nine million visitors make their pilgrimage to Cleveland to attend concerts and admire exhibits. The architecturally interesting building includes various exhibitions and further education programs around the music. Overnight in Cleveland.

13th day Amish culture in Elkhart

Today’s route takes you to the Amish Land of Ohio and Indiana. You drive along Highway 20 and stop at a typical Amish shop in between. You will find there antiques and a large selection of self-made products. It is also advisable to visit an Amish museum, for example the Menno Hof or the Elkhart County Historical Museum. Visit the Downtown Riverwalk in Elkhart and relax in one of the city’s many parks. Overnight in Elkhart.

Day 14 Saying Goodbye

Even the most beautiful journey comes to an end and so it is today to say goodbye to the impressive and diverse Great Lakes region. Even if your holiday is over, the precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories of your trip will accompany you forever. Look forward to letting your friends and family share your experiences in the distance at home. You drive back to Chicago and drop off your rental car at the airport.

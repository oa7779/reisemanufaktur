New England – Between Romanticism and History

USA Travel New England: Discover the six unique states of New England on your car rental trip with América Special Tours. Let's talk now.

Your trip with your own rental car will take you to New England. The region consisting of six unique states - Connecticut, Maine, Massachusetts, New Hampshire, Rhode Island and Vermont - is at its best with white sand beaches and lighthouses but also with extensive lakes, harbor restaurants and impressive views over the mountains. All places are easily accessible from Boston, so you can enjoy beautiful travel experiences within a few hours by car. Turbulent history, picturesque villages, eclectic culture and fascinating cities - each state will delight you with its unique character!

Day 1 Welcome to Massachusetts

Today you land in Boston, the capital of the state of Massachusetts, which is inextricably linked to the emergence of the USA as we know it today. The Mayflower’s Atlantic crossing and the founding of the Plymouth colony are 400 years old in 2020. You will be met by your guide and driven to your hotel. Arrive in peace and enjoy the rest of the day at your free disposal. Overnight in Boston.

Day 2 Exciting Impressions Along the Freedom Trail

Today, you can look forward to a privately guided tour of Boston for your entry into your New England trip. It awaits you a city where exciting history mixes with modernity but also with curiosity and contemporary. Along the so-called Freedom Trail you will find the most important historical landmarks. The sights are marked by a red brick line throughout the city. Follow the line with your guide and discover many impressive attractions: Massachusetts State House, Boston Common, Park Street Church, King's Chapel, Old South Meeting House, Grain Store, Old State House, Faneuil Hall and Quincy Market. Enjoy an Italian meal in the city’s North End in the evening or end the day with a summer ball game at Fenwey Park. Overnight in Boston.

3rd day open-air museum Plimoth Plantation

Today you will receive your rental car and drive on to Cape Cod. On the way, we recommend a visit to the Plimoth Plantation south of Boston. In this museum you will have the unique opportunity to learn more about the history of Native Americans. A 17th-century English village and the Wampanoag Homesite bring to life the history of one of the first American settlements. The “Living History” model gives you as a visitor the opportunity to visit the recreated settlement site in the Coastal Museum. Plimoth Plantation is a world-class Smithsonian affiliate museum and one of the most popular living history museums in the country. This is how the indigenous people dress in historically correct clothing and show you daily life in the 17th century. The indigenous people play no role, but tell from a modern perspective of the culture and history of Wampanoag. After your detour into a turbulent past, you will finally reach the Cape Cod peninsula. The area is known for pristine beaches, rustic cottages and the typical way of life of New England. Enjoy fantastic views of the Atlantic Ocean and feel the romantic flair of the region. Overnight in Cape Cod/Chatham.

Day 4 Picturesque Nature in Cape Cod

Cape Cod is known for its windswept coastline, where the lighthouses and campfires on the beach shine at night. The 64 km long coastline of the Cape Cod National Seashore with its extensive beaches offers many opportunities for activities, e.g. Beach walks, surfing, swimming or a bike ride over the Shining Sea Bikeway. A special highlight, however, is the whale watching tour included for you today. The waters around Cape Cod are among the ten best whale watching areas in the world. The sighting rate is 99%. Let yourself be enchanted by the natural beauty of the peninsula. Overnight in Cape Cod/Chatham.

5th day Charming coastal town of Newport

Today it is important to say goodbye to the romantic lighthouses of Cape Cod, because your journey takes you a little further south to the state of Rhode Island. You will reach the ‘Sailing Capital’ Newport. So that you can familiarize yourself with the new environment, a guide is at your side during the coming hours. You will experience 250 years of American history in the historic buildings housed in 88-acre gardens and parks. The 11 historic plots and landscapes, seven of which are national historical landmarks, trace America’s architectural and social development from the colonial period to the heyday of the American economy. Not for nothing were the sandy beaches and the rocky coast of Newport between 1870 and 1900 called the ‘Americas First Resort’. The most magnificent of the summer houses is called The Breakers. In the style of the Italian Renaissance, the palace is a special treasure of the wealthy Vanderbilt family. Enjoy your evening in Newport and take a relaxing walk along the Cliff Walk. Here you will see the sea on one side and on the other again the impressive villas. Overnight in Newport.

Day 6: The Berkshires of Massachusetts

With your rental car, you will take a leisurely trip to the Berkshires of Massachusetts today. The Berkshires do justice to the picture book of rural New England with wooded hills, narrow winding streets and compact historic villages. Enjoy the views of shimmering lakes and lush forests. With a fresh mountain air and more than 100 cultural attractions, the region is the perfect destination for every season. Overnight in Stockbridge.

7th day Historic mountain village Stowe

Today you will continue directly towards the historic mountain village of Stowe, in the state of Vermont. This is nestled between the Putnam State Forest and the Mount Mansfield Forest and is especially popular with outdoor fans. Especially in the winter months, it is known as the ‘ski capital of the east’ because it offers the best slope conditions for skiers. But also during the other seasons, the area lures with many possibilities for walks, bike rides and inline kating. Even hikers and golfers get their money's worth in this area. During the autumn months, the leaves along the narrow country roads shine in bright colors. Located in this area is the Lodge at Spruce Peak, Stowe’s only ski-in-ski-out luxury resort and accommodation for the following two nights. Stunning views welcome every guest, as the windows range from the ceiling to the floor. Overnight in Green Mountains/Stowe.

Day 8 Your Day in Outdoor Paradise

Enjoy today and take advantage of the variety of activities offered by the lodge (depending on the season): An immaculate golf course, wellness & spa, rope slides, skiing, hiking trails, canoeing, kayaking, wine tasting & tours, etc. Overnight in Green Mountains/Stowe.

Day 9 - Take the historic railway to the summit of Mount Washington

With Mount Washington, the next state awaits you today: New Hampshire. In the still largely unknown region of Grand North in the north of the state, there are extensive and untouched forest reserves but also snow-capped peaks. Enjoy the stay in this area, because it will probably be the loneliest part of your trip - you will not encounter a human soul for hours. The mountains rise in dramatic relief, covered with forests and crossed by streams. Enjoy beautiful walks through the forest, visit the waterfalls of the region or take part in one of the many outdoor activities. Upon arrival, we will take you on a train ride on a historic railway to the summit of Mount Washington. After about an hour's drive, you will have another hour to explore the top of the mountain. In the visitor center there is a museum, a post office and a small snack bar. Enjoy a 360° view of the surrounding area. In front of you, there are up to five states, Canada and the Atlantic Ocean. Overnight in White Mountains/Bretton Woods.

Day 10 Ogunquit – Maine’s most beautiful place by the sea

Your journey takes you today to the resort of Ogunquit, which is considered the most beautiful place in Maine by the sea. Three and a half miles of protected white sand beaches are waiting for you to explore. Take a walk along the Marginal Way, a path that winds along rocky cliffs overlooking the ocean, or explore the city center with art galleries, fresh seafood and pay a visit to the Ogunquit Playhouse. A small boat trip shows you from the water what the beautiful Casco Bay has to offer. Enjoy the unique opportunity to discover the famous lighthouses, the sights of Portland Harbor and the scenic Diamond Pass waterway. You will first drive through the busy inner harbor and the main shipping channel. You can see up close the oldest and probably most photographed lighthouse – Portland Head Light. Your boat winds back and forth between the islands and leads to the Diamond Pass, which was long considered the ‘Gateway to the Calendar Islands’. Drive past the villages and Victorian cottages on the islands and catch a glimpse of island life. Overnight in Cape Neddick.

11th day of travel home

Even the most beautiful trip comes to an end and so it is today to say goodbye to the romantic states of New England. Even if your holiday is over, the precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories of your trip will accompany you forever. Look forward to letting your friends and family share your experiences in the distance at home. You drive back to Boston and drop off your rental car at the airport.

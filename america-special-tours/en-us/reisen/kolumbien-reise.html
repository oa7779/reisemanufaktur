Colombia Round trip - Colombia's metropolises

This trip will take you to Colombia’s cities. Stroll through one of South America’s most beautiful colonial cities, explore an underground cathedral and one of the country’s most traditional coffee plantations, relax on Caribbean beaches and follow in the footsteps of drug lord Pablo Escobar in Medellín.

Day 1 – The capital Bogotá

Your journey through Colombia begins in the state capital of Bogotá, the pride of Colombians. Already at the airport you will be expected by your guide, who will accompany you to your hotel in the city, where you can relax with a welcome cocktail from your long flight. Overnight in Bogotá.

Day 2 Highlights in and around Bogotá

This morning you dedicate yourself to the capital Bogotá. The Plaza de Mercado in Paloquemao is a huge market hall with over 1,800 stalls of fresh fruit and vegetables, meat, herbs, flowers, as well as small craft businesses. From the colorful outdoor flower market to the numerous varieties of potatoes to the exotic fruit range from the varied smell and taste impressions that you get to know when visiting the market. Afterwards, you will experience the contrast between the modern Bogotá and the historic old town. From Plaza Bolivar, the colonial “main square” with a cathedral, palace of justice, parliament and town hall, it passes through the narrow streets of the Candelaria – as the old town of Bogotá is also called. In the Museo Botero you will see an important collection of contemporary art and, of course, works by the famous Colombian artist Fernando Botero. Another highlight is the Gold Museum, the world’s most impressive collection of pre-Columbian gold jewelry. In nice weather, you first take the cable car to the Bogotás mountain, the Monserrate, from which you have an excellent view over the city of millions. Overnight in Bogotá.

Day 3 The Cathedral of Zipaquirá

After breakfast, leave the capital behind and take a trip north to Zipaquirá. In the beautiful colonial old town of Zipaquirá there are numerous buildings from the 18th century. Particularly impressive is the salt cathedral in a mine, whose cloister and chapels are completely carved from the salt rock. All stations of the Cross are designed differently and use the interplay of lighting and shadow in the vault of the tunnels to create a devout, somewhat mysterious atmosphere. At the end of the Way of the Cross you reach the actual cathedral, which consists of a central nave up to 25 meters high and two side naves. Afterwards, drive to the lake of Guatavita and hike in the footsteps of the legendary Eldorado. After a 30-minute walk through a small forest and typical highland vegetation, you reach the crater rim at an altitude of more than 3,000 m. From there you have a magnificent view of the crater lake, where the coronation ceremony of the Muisca rulers used to take place. Over the Municipio La Calera you drive back over the mountains to Bogotá. Enjoy your dinner and let the view wander over the sea of lights of the capital Colombia. Overnight in Bogotá.

Day 4 - Continue to Medellín

This morning your chauffeur will take you to the airport and you will travel on to Medellín. On the way to the city, take a detour to the colonial village of Guatapé. Along cobbled streets are colorful cottages with individually designed bases: each of them is decorated under its windows with typical motifs of the region, with flowers and geometric figures. Not far from the village is a huge monolith called El Peñón. If you want to, you can climb it on your own over 645 stairs. From here above you have a magnificent view of the vast reservoir on the edge of the place, which has put a large part of the mountains under water. After dinner, explore the lake on a boat tour and take time to visit the village of Guatapé. In the evening you will reach Medellín. The metropolis of millions was once one of the most dangerous cities in the world. In the 80s and 90s, the Medellín cartel was the largest exporter of cocaine in the world. Today, on the other hand, Medellín is an important fashion capital and was awarded “Innovative City of the Year” in 2013 for its diverse projects to improve education, quality of life and transport in the city.

5th day of Medellín

Your tour of Medellín begins at Pueblito Paisa, a replica of a typical village in the Antioquias region. From here you can enjoy a wonderful view over the city. The next destination is the historic center of Medellín. At Parque Berrío you can listen to local musicians playing traditional Guasca music. Afterwards, visit Plaza Botero, where an exhibition by Colombian artist Fernando Botero can be seen. The tour continues through the bustle of downtown Medellín to Parque Bolívar, one of the most beautiful squares in the city. Then continue on a cable car ride (Metro Cable) to the Santo Domingo district, which used to be an area notorious for gangs and violence but is now a popular and safe tourist destination. Visit Comuna 13 and immerse yourself in the exciting history of Medellín’s transformation, which has transformed from one of the most dangerous cities in the world into an exciting cultural place.

Day 6 of Colombia's Black Gold

During your excursion to a coffee plantation today, you will learn everything about coffee: the entire process of coffee processing from harvest to export. You will learn why Colombian coffee from this area is one of the best highland coffees in the world. You walk through the plantation and see seedlings, seedlings and the adult bushes (the growth stage is seasonal). At harvest time, you can watch the coffee pickers skillfully picking only the ripe coffee cherries. Subsequently, the preparation of the harvested fruit is explained. Finally, you will have the opportunity to taste different types of coffee. You will be amazed at what a multi-layered product coffee is.

Day 7 - The Caribbean and its cuisine

They are leaving Medellín behind today. Your chauffeur will take you to the airport, from where you will travel on to Cartagena. This city, with its fortress ring and countless palaces in the Andalusian style, is one of the most beautiful colonial cities in South America. Upon arrival, you will enjoy the Caribbean cuisine. During a cooking class, you will learn how to prepare the traditional dish ceviche. Originally, the dish probably comes from Peru, but now it can be found in every South American country from Argentina to Mexico and also in Colombia. Traditionally, raw fish is cut small and then converted into lemon or citric acid. Lime juice. Buen provecho! Overnight in Cartagena.

Day 8 Colonial Cartagena

You will start your visit with a ride up the hill of La Popa. At the highest point in the surroundings of Cartagena, Augustinian monks built a monastery in the 16th century. From here you have a perfect view of the Bay of Cartagena, once the most important colonial port city of the Spanish in South America. Then visit the fortress of San Felipe. This was built by the Spanish to protect the rich Cartagena from attacks from the mainland. In Cartagena, the treasures of the Incas plundered by the Spaniards and the gold and silver treasures from the mines of Peru and Bolivia were temporarily stored for shipping to Spain. Thus, the city was again and again the object of the desire of numerous pirates. In the winding corridors of the fortress you feel transported back to the time of the pirates. From the fortress it goes on foot to the colonial old town. There you will visit important historical buildings and churches. In the afternoon you will have time to explore Cartagena on your own. Buy souvenirs or admire the sunset from Cafe del Mar on the city walls. Overnight in Cartagena.

9th day to the island paradise Isla Múcura

After you have experienced a lot on your trip through Colombia, treat yourself to two days of relaxation in the middle of the Caribbean. In the morning you will be picked up at your hotel and taken to the port, where your resort’s speedboat awaits you and takes you to the island of Múcura. In approx. 2 hours you reach your dreamlike hotel. Overnight in Múcura.

10 - 11 days of pure relaxation

Without tour guide and chauffeur

Enjoy the next few days on the beautiful white beach of Punta Faro or relax with a massage at the hotel. If you want to do something on the island, there is the possibility to rent a kayak, surfboard or bicycle or get to know the Caribbean underwater world during a dive. The hotel also offers a guided ecological walk around the island or an excursion to the neighbouring island. Overnight in Múcura.

12th day of travel home

Your journey is coming to an end today. The speedboat will take you back to Cartagena and drive you to the airport to start your journey home. Even if your holiday is over: The unique memories of Colombia will always accompany you.

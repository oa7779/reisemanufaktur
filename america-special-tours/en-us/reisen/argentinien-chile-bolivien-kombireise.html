Argentina, Chile & Bolivia – Colorful Mountains, White Salt Deserts

Spectacular landscapes, exclusive boutique hotels & private guided tour. Experience the magical northwest of Argentina, Bolivia and Chile in 28 days.

Itinerary & services

Experience a fascinating journey through South America that takes you from Buenos Aires over the colorful landscapes of northern Argentina to the impressive heights of the Andes. Discover the cultural diversity and European influence of Argentina, enjoy exquisite wines at Cafayate and be amazed by spectacular desert and mountain landscapes around Salta and Purmamarca. Continue to Bolivia, the “Tibet of America”, where you will experience colonial history in Potosí and marvel at the largest salt deserts in the world in Uyuni. The journey also takes you to La Paz and the mystical Lake Titicaca, where indigenous traditions are still alive today and provide deep insights into centuries-old cultures. The crowning conclusion is Chile with the unique Atacama Desert, one of the driest regions on earth. Enjoy breathtaking landscapes, impressive contrasts and authentic encounters.

1st Day Welcome to Buenos Aires: Your first evening full of enjoyment

Dive into the vibrant heart of Buenos Aires, the city of tango and passion. When you arrive, you will be warmly welcomed and brought directly to the hotel thanks to early check-in so that you can start relaxed. In the evening, a highlight of Argentine cuisine awaits you: the exclusive dinner at Fogón Asado. Experience the magic of the asado up close: from crackling fires to world-class pieces of meat to seasonal vegetables and creative traditional recipes. Enjoy a welcome cocktail, meet the chef and other travelers, and be seduced by a 9-course menu. This fits a perfectly coordinated wine accompaniment. Overnight in Buenos Aires.

2nd Day Experience Buenos Aires: City Tour, Café Tortoni & Tango Dinner

Start the day with a delicious breakfast at the hotel before embarking on a fascinating discovery tour of Buenos Aires. Drive along the impressive 9 de Julio, the widest street in the world, admiring the magnificent Colón Theatre. Experience the historic Plaza de Mayo, visit the Metropolitan Cathedral and feel the history of this lively city. Afterwards, enjoy a break in one of the historic cafés such as Café Tortoni, where coffee is not just a drink, but a ritual. Taste typical pastries and be enchanted by the atmosphere. Continue to the colorful district of La Boca with its famous Caminito before passing through Puerto Madero, Palermo and finally Recoleta. Here you will visit the impressive Recoleta cemetery, resting place of national icons, artists and Eva Perón. In the evening, an unforgettable highlight awaits you: dinner and tango at Rojo Tango, located in the stylish Faena Hotel. Let yourself be seduced by an exquisite dinner while artists present music, singing and passionate dance. The show combines nostalgia, imagination and sensuality in an unforgettable experience. After the tango, return to the hotel and end the day in style. Overnight in Buenos Aires.

3rd day Salta & the fascinating northwest of Argentina

Start your day with a breakfast buffet before being taken to the domestic airport. From there you fly to Salta. After your arrival, you will be warmly welcomed and start directly for an exciting city tour. Founded in 1582, the city captivates with its colonial charm, cobbled streets and impressive Spanish architecture. During your approximately three-hour guided tour, you will discover the most important sights. Stroll through the central square 9 de Julio and marvel at magnificent buildings such as the Cathedral, the Archbishop's Palace and the historic Cabildo. A highlight is a visit to San Bernardo hill, from which you can optionally enjoy a breathtaking panoramic view over Salta by cable car. Then drive to San Lorenzo, an idyllic village just a few kilometers outside the city. Salta is also the perfect starting point for exploring the region’s cultural and scenic treasures. In nearby Quebrada de Humahuaca, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, you’ll find colorful mountains, traditional mud houses and pre-Columbian sites. After an eventful day, you will be taken to your hotel. Overnight in Salta.

Day 4 Between Cacti, Colonial Villages and Andean Landscapes

Strengthened after breakfast, you will leave Salta and discover the spectacular landscapes of the Northwest on an impressive ride. Your route takes you along the so-called Cuesta del Obispo up to an altitude of 3,348 meters, where you will discover an old, seemingly timelessly anchored mill. Then you reach the highest point of the route with Piedra del Molino. This distinctive rock formation forms a natural boundary between the Lerma Valley and the Calchaquí Valleys. The onward journey through the Los Cardones National Park impresses with its unique cactus landscape and wide panoramas. Your next stop is the charming village of Cachi, which delights with its colonial architecture and tranquil atmosphere. Stroll across the main square and enjoy the authentic flair. Optionally, you have the opportunity to visit the church or the Archaeological Museum. Afterwards, continue your journey through the fascinating valleys until you finally reach Molinos. In the evening, enjoy a dinner and stay in the stylish Bodega Colomé nestled in one of the highest wine regions in the world. Overnight in Molinos.

Day 5 Relaxation in Molinos

After breakfast at the hotel, the day in Molinos is free. Enjoy the tranquility and special atmosphere of this picturesque region, characterized by the imposing Andean landscapes and the gentle valleys of the Calchaquí valleys. Take the time to stroll quietly through the surroundings, admire the architecture and the charming colonial buildings of the small village or simply let the silence of nature affect you. Wine lovers can take a walk through the vineyards around the Bodega Colomé, where you can experience the impressively high altitudes and special climate of the region. Also the wellness area or the cozy lounges of the Bodega invite you to spend the day relaxed, enjoy a good book or simply let the view wander over the vineyards and mountains. Be inspired by the tranquility and colors of this unique landscape and recharge your energy for the upcoming adventures. Overnight in Molinos.

Day 6 Between Andes and vineyards

After breakfast, continue your scenic drive through the Calchaquí valley and finally reach the charming town of Cafayate where you move into your hotel. The region is known as the “land of sun and good wine” and delights with lush vineyards, framed by imposing mountains and breathtaking landscapes. Cafayate exudes peace and serenity, offering something for everyone. Whether adventurous people riding, hiking or archaeological discoveries, wine lovers tasting the characteristic Torrontés grapes or anyone who simply wants to enjoy the relaxed atmosphere, you will get your money's worth. The region’s Torrontés wines are known for their fruity, aromatic aromas and an absolute highlight for any connoisseur. After your arrival, you will have some free time. Stroll through the cozy streets of the city, browse the local craft market or visit the interesting wine museum to learn more about the wine tradition of this unique region. In the evening, you can sit back and enjoy the views of the vineyards and the surrounding mountains. Overnight in Cafayate.

Day 7: Argentina's Greatest Pre-Columbian Heritage

Encouraged by breakfast, you will embark on an exciting excursion to the Quilmes ruins, located about 53 kilometers from Cafayate in the neighboring province of Tucumán. The Quilmes ruins are the largest pre-Columbian fortification in Argentina and are considered one of the most important pre-Hispanic settlement areas in the country. The city of Quilmes was founded by the Calchaquíes around the year 800 and once housed about 13,000 people. Thanks to their highly developed social and economic structures, they were able to ward off the Spanish conquest for a long time. Only after a protracted siege, in which the inhabitants were cut off from access to water and food, did the Spaniards succeed in defeating the settlement. The surviving 260 families were deported far to the south near Buenos Aires, where the community eventually disappeared. Today, this region in southern Argentina bears the same name “Quilmes” as the well-known national beer. After visiting the ruins, you will have the opportunity to learn about the fascinating history of the region and admire the impressive architecture of the old fortifications. Overnight in Cafayate.

Day 8: Cafayate returns to Salta

With fresh energy from breakfast you start your journey back to Salta. The approximately 197 kilometers long journey leads through the impressive landscape of northwestern Argentina and holds some unforgettable natural highlights. A highlight along the way is the Las Conchas Gorge, a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Admire the colorful rock formations and the quirky, natural forms that give the gorge its distinctive appearance. These fascinating formations are created by millions of years of geological processes and give the landscape a unique, almost fairytale atmosphere. After this impressive stopover, you will continue your journey and finally reach Salta, where you will move into your hotel. Use the remaining time of day to explore the city on your own or simply enjoy the surroundings of your hotel. Overnight in Salta.

9th day Indigenous ruins in front of colorful mountain backdrop

Early in the morning, leave Salta and head north to the border between the provinces of Salta and Jujuy. Your destination is the impressive Quebrada de Humahuaca, a 155 km long mountain valley characterized by colorful hills, wide valleys and the characteristic high plateau. Along the way, you will encounter small villages where indigenous traditions, music and cuisine have been kept alive for generations. A highlight of the day is a visit to Pucará de Tilcara (closed on Mondays), an ancient indigenous fort surrounded by cactus and colorful mountains. The ruins testify to the highly developed culture of the region and offer a fascinating opportunity to immerse yourself in the history of Quebrada. In the afternoon you will arrive at Purmamarca, a charming village known for its lively craft culture. Stroll through the local market, discover traditional handicrafts and marvel at the famous Cerro de los Siete Colores, which spectacularly colors the landscape with its shades of brown, violet and red. Overnight in Purmamarca.

Day 10 On a voyage of discovery through Bolivia

After breakfast, you will be taken to La Quiaca to cross the border and continue your journey through Bolivia. At the border in Villazón, you will be welcomed and continue your journey to Tupiza where you will have lunch at a local restaurant. The route then continues to Potosí, a city rich in history and impressive colonial architecture, before finally reaching Cayara. Here you will find the Hacienda Cayara, a unique museum hotel and historical hacienda that has been preserved since the founding of the Republic. The elegant architecture and lovingly cultivated style of the hacienda make the stay a special experience. The house thrives on its history and welcomes visitors who are looking for an unforgettable ambience. In the evening, enjoy a dinner at the hotel and end the day in the stylish atmosphere of the hacienda. Overnight in Cayara.

Day 11 Cayara: History and Natural Wonders

After an American breakfast at the Hacienda, start the day with a visit to the colonial home and museum that offers a glimpse into the history and culture of this region. The lovingly preserved rooms and exhibits tell stories from the time of the hacienda and bring the past to life. In the afternoon, a trip to the stone city is on the program, which is about six kilometers from the Hacienda. This fascinating natural formation of white sand, stone passages, corridors and structures resembles a small adventure path. Particularly impressive are the geological formations of quartz and volcanic tuff, which give the stone city its unique character. In the evening, enjoy a dinner at the Hacienda and end the day in the quiet, historic atmosphere of the hotel. Overnight in Cayara.

Day 12 experience Potosí and the endless expanses of Uyuni

After an American breakfast, leave Cayara and drive privately to Potosí, one of Bolivia’s most historic cities. On a guided city tour, you will explore Potosí’s historical highlights exclusively from the outside. They admire impressive colonial buildings, magnificent churches and imposing arches and towers that reflect the heritage of this once prosperous silver city. A special highlight is the visit to the Casa de la Moneda, the royal mint of Potosí. For centuries, silver coins traded all over the world were minted here, and the workshops provide a fascinating insight into the technology and life of that time. The exhibition shows historical coins, tools and machines and tells of the enormous importance of Potosí for the Spanish colonial economy. For lunch, stop at the Tenedor de Plata restaurant before continuing your trip to Uyuni. In Uyuni, also known as Bolivia’s “favorite child”, a unique natural wonder awaits you: the world’s largest salt desert. Let yourself be enchanted by the endless, white expanses and the breathtaking landscapes. In the evening, enjoy dinner in the hotel and let the day end relaxing. Overnight in Potosí.

Day 13 From Uyuni to the Andean metropolis

After breakfast, the private transfer to the airport takes place. From here you will fly to La Paz, one of the highest cities in the world at 3,600 meters altitude. The vibrant metropolis impresses with colorful quarters, a strong cultural presence and exceptional landscapes that combine modern buildings with snow-capped mountains and bizarre rock formations. Upon arrival, you will be picked up and taken to the hotel before the tour of La Paz begins. The tour first takes you over the famous Witch Market, where in addition to herbs and plants, traditional curiosities such as llama fetuses or dried snakes and frogs are offered. Afterwards, visit the colonial church of San Francisco and Plaza Murillo with Cathedral, Government Palace and National Congress. After a view of the colonial old town, cruise through the modern neighborhoods of Obrajes, Calacoto and La Florida and finish the tour in the Valley of the Moon. Here, for thousands of years, nature has created bizarre rock formations whose forms are constantly changing. At the end of the visit, you will enjoy a ride on the cable car that offers you a spectacular panoramic view over La Paz. Overnight in La Paz.

14th day adventure on the Sun Island

In the morning, your adventure begins with a private drive from La Paz to the picturesque town of Copacabana on the banks of the majestic Titicaca lake. Stroll across the main square, dive into the vibrant local market and visit the famous church with the Shrine of the Black Virgin, a place where history and spirituality come alive. From Copacabana, transfer by private motorboat to the southern side of the Sun Island. There you will find Pilcocaina, one of the most spectacular ruins on the island. Between winding stone walls and mysterious doors, a breathtaking view of a white sand beach opens up for you. Immerse yourself in the culture of the Andes at a traditional blessing ceremony by a Kallawaya, a wise healer of Aymara. Afterwards, enjoy an apthapi lunch at a local restaurant before departing for your Ecolodge on Sun Island. The climb takes about an hour on a steep, natural path with stone steps and is a small trail full of adventure that takes you from the 3,800 meters high boat dock to the lodge at 4,000 meters. In the evening, a delicious dinner at the hotel awaits you. Overnight at Lake Titicaca.

15th Day Between Heaven and Water

Start the day with breakfast on Sun Island before boarding a private motorboat that will take you to Moon Island, another Inca sacred site on Lake Titicaca. Along the way, visit the spring of eternal youth where fresh water from a natural spring flows through three ornately landscaped stone gutters. According to Andean cosmology, they symbolize the three energy levels and are located next to an artfully designed Inca staircase, a place full of myth and power. Once on the Moon Island, discover the Palace of Sun Maidens Iñak Uyo, a well-preserved building with 35 rooms and ornately carved doors that impressively reflects the island’s importance in Inca culture. Afterwards, you will return by motorboat to Copacabana, where lunch will be waiting for you before you embark on the private transfer to La Paz. Overnight in La Paz.

Day 16 In the World of Salt and Light

After breakfast, a private transfer will take you to the airport from where you will fly to Uyuni. Upon arrival, you will feel the unique magic of the largest salt desert in the world. Your first stop is Colchani, a small village where salt has shaped people’s lives for generations. With pickaxes, shovels and axes, the inhabitants gain salt, which not only provides food and building materials, but is also artfully processed into souvenirs and craftsmanship. Everywhere you can feel the connection between nature, tradition and everyday life. On the salt surfaces you will discover the mysterious water eyes, small holes from which salty water bubbling up to the surface from the depths. In addition, you will encounter impressive salt monuments that seem like small islands of light and white in the infinite expanse of the desert. Continue on to the island of Incahuasi, known for its gigantic cacti and volcanic landscape. Watch the curious Vizcachas sunbathe and enjoy lunch in the middle of the endless salt surface, an unforgettable experience. In the afternoon, return to Colchani and check in at your salt hotel, where you’ll end the day with a leisurely dinner. Overnight in Uyuni.

Day 17 Secrets of the Salt Desert

The day begins with a trip around Uyuni. You will travel to Tahua, cross the endless, glittering surfaces of salt and arrive at the spot where historical mummies were discovered. A short walk of about 20 minutes will allow you to experience the history of this fascinating region up close. Then visit the Chantani Museum, which delights with its ornate ceramics and quirky rock formations that seem to grow out of the salt landscape like animals. In the middle of the endless salt surfaces, you can enjoy a packed lunch and let the unique expanse between heaven and earth work in peace. In the afternoon return to Colchani and check in at your salt hotel. Dinner in the hotel and time to let the special atmosphere of the salt desert affect you. Overnight in Uyuni.

Day 18 The Wonders of the Siloli Desertland

After breakfast, visit the Uyuni Train Cemetery, located in the southwest of the city. Here you can admire the impressive collection of old steam locomotives and wagons, many from France and England, telling stories from the early days of the railway. Afterwards, you will leave for Ojo de Perdiz. Along the way, stop in San Cristobal and at the viewpoint of Ollague. You will visit the Hedionda lagoon, whose name indicates the sulphurous smell, as well as the Honda and Chiarchota lagoons. These mineral-rich lakes are home to Andean, Chilean and James Flamingos. The surrounding hills look like melted chocolate ice cream cups and create a surreal landscape. A packed lunch on the way provides energy as you traverse the desert. Finally, you will reach the Siloli Desert and your destination for the night, the Ecolodge Tayka del Desierto, where a dinner awaits you. Overnight in the Atacama Desert.

Day 19: From the Uyuni Desert to the Magic of the Atacama

After breakfast, depart from Ojo de Perdiz towards Hito Cajón and immerse yourself in the surreal landscapes of Eduardo Avaroa National Park along the way. You will pass the famous stone tree, a huge volcanic rock in the shape of a tree, and marvel at the bright red Laguna Colorada, whose colors come to life through algae and minerals. Continue to Sol de Mañana’s seething mud springs filled with steaming fumaroles and the intense smell of sulfur before experiencing the tranquil beauty of Laguna Verde against the backdrop of the majestic Licancabur volcano. In the afternoon, reach the border at Hito Cajón and continue on to San Pedro de Atacama, a charming town on the edge of the driest desert in the world, where you will immerse yourself in the expanse of the Atacama, admiring the quiet lagoons, volcanic rocks and sparkling salt plains, immersing yourself in the local culture and enjoying the clear air and starry skies. Your stay at Awasi Atacama offers you comfort, tranquility and the opportunity to customize the adventure. Overnight in San Pedro de Atacama.

20-23. Tag Atacama: Land of Extremes

You will spend the next few days in San Pedro de Atacama, nestled between endless desert landscapes, imposing volcanoes and clear lagoons. Your stay at Awasi Atacama offers you all the amenities of an exclusive all-inclusive program while giving you the freedom to choose from a variety of individual excursions. The Atacama unfolds in all its diversity: vast lunar landscapes, bubbling geysers and small, traditional villages invite you to discover new wonders every day. In between, enjoy peace, comfort and culinary delights in your hotel, let the expanse of the Atacama affect you and watch spectacular sunrises and sparkling star nights. Overnight in San Pedro de Atacama.

Day 24 From the Atacama to the glittering salt surfaces

After breakfast, you will be picked up from your hotel in San Pedro de Atacama and travel towards Salta until you finally reach the shimmering expanses of the Salinas Grandes. There you will find the luxurious Pristine Salinas Grandes Camp, nestled in a breathtaking landscape of endless white salt surfaces that glitter like liquid light under the sun. You will enjoy culinary delights made from regional products and the special atmosphere of the camp, which combines traditional Andean culture with modern exclusivity. Explore the salt surfaces on electric bicycles, visit the mysterious pools of the salt flats or let yourself be told the stories of the Andean constellations while looking into the sparkling starry skies. During a longer stay, additional excursions into the area open up, where mysterious ruins and idyllic mountain villages await you, always accompanied by fine gourmet picnics. Overnight in Purmamarca.

25th day of relaxation in Purmamarca

Today is a day full of relaxation, inspiration and small adventures. You can walk through the quiet salty areas, let the expanse of the landscape affect you or, on request, make trips to archaeological sites and picturesque mountain villages, accompanied by delicious gourmet picnics. Explore the pools of the salt flats, enjoy regional specialties or simply let the special atmosphere of the camp affect you. Every moment in the Salinas Grandes offers peace, wonder and unforgettable impressions. Overnight in Purmamarca.

26th day Drive through the Andean landscape to Salta

After breakfast, say goodbye to the glittering salty areas and special atmosphere of Pristine Salinas Grandes Camp. The journey takes you to the charming town of Salta, where colonial architecture, vibrant places and Andean culture await you. The journey through the diverse landscape invites you to feel the experiences of the last days once again. Overnight in Salta.

27th day flight to the metropolis of tango

After breakfast, say goodbye to the picturesque city of Salta and travel to the airport for your flight to the vibrant capital of Buenos Aires. Upon arrival, you will feel the vibrant flair of the city that combines colonial charm, modern architecture and Latin American temperament. With the private transfer you reach your hotel and can relax and absorb the special atmosphere of Buenos Aires. Overnight in Buenos Aires.

Day 28: Farewell to Buenos Aires

It is time to say goodbye to Buenos Aires. A private transfer will take you to the international airport where your journey home begins. Take advantage of the last moments to recapture the lively atmosphere of the city before your unforgettable adventure ends.

Florida Vacation Villa — One Retreat, a Thousand Possibilities

Discover the Cape Coral vacation paradise on this customized USA trip from your exclusive Florida vacation villa. Let's talk now.

In the southwest of Florida, holiday dreams come true. In addition to award-winning mussel beaches and untouched landscapes, the tropical island paradise impresses with a variety of outdoor activities on land and water as well as first-class golf courses. In your waterfront villa with private pool and direct boat access to the Gulf of Mexico, you will enjoy exclusivity and the Caribbean-loose lifestyle on the waterfront, typical of the Fort Myers and Sanibel Island region. More than 100 offshore islands invite you to island hopping and the more than 80 km long dreamy white sand and shell beaches lure you to extensive beach walks. From the boat or kayak, observe dolphins and manatees up close in their natural environment. In the evening, feast on fresh fish and seafood while admiring fantastic sunsets. Experience the feeling of boundless freedom and pure luxury in your exclusive holiday villa in the holiday paradise Cape Coral – a retreat, a thousand possibilities.

Itinerary

10 days Cape Coral and surrounding area

Your Trip Highlights

  • An exclusive villa in the holiday paradise Cape Coral
  • Explore the Unknown Southwest Florida by Rental Car or Boat
  • Island paradise with award-winning mussel beaches & unspoiled landscapes
  • Romantic dinner on the beach and fantastic sunsets
  • Private boat tour to the offshore islands
  • Golf pleasure on first-class and well-known golf courses
  • Collecting mussels on Sanibel & Captiva Island
  • Kayaking tour of the mangroves in the J.N. Ding Darling Wildlife Refuge
  • Culture, Art & Shopping in Fort Myers and surroundings
  • Private Sailing Tour to Sunset

Welcome to the Florida holiday paradise. Upon arrival at Fort Myers International Airport, you will receive your rental car and reach your Cape Coral holiday home after about half an hour's drive. The popular destination for discerning holidaymakers is located opposite Fort Myers and directly on the Caloosahatchee River. Set in this exclusive neighborhood, the Living Coral holiday villa has direct boat access to the Gulf of Mexico. The spacious and quietly furnished villa with a maximum occupancy of 6 people has a living space of 205m2 with a large, partially covered veranda and its own heated pool and whirlpool. Equipped with three air-conditioned bedrooms and three bathrooms, a fully equipped kitchen, an office, and a cozy living and dining area, the home offers everything you need for your stay in southwest Florida. On the spacious pool terrace there is a gas grill, a lounge area and an outdoor dining set as well as sun loungers ready for you. Sit back and feel at home! Overnight in Cape Coral.

Thrown by the Florida sun, take advantage of today to arrive at your vacation and villa and explore the surrounding area. The region offers a variety of outdoor activities on land and water. Cape Coral, also affectionately known as “The Cape” by the locals, is not for nothing regarded as the Venice of Florida - with over 600 km of waterways, the casual community invites you to magnificent discovery tours on the water. The bicycle-friendly city can also be explored with a rented bike. The various cycle routes along the Caloosahatchee River connect nature reserves and scenic canals, as well as exclusive marinas and golf courses. Close to Cape Coral are the dream beaches of Fort Myers Beach, Sanibel and Captiva Island. How about ending tonight at a romantic dinner on the beach? Overnight in Cape Coral.

Today, explore the island world off the coast of Fort Myers from the water. Your captain will take you on his small boat on an individual boat trip. Visit Cayo Costa State Park, North Captiva, Cabbage Key or Pine Island together and let yourself be infected by the certified naturalist’s passion for his home. On your tour you will learn about the plants, shells and (sea) creatures of the islands and listen to exciting stories about the indigenous people and the first settlers of the region. Your captain knows the islands and their special features and as a professional photographer he can show you the best spots for your souvenir photos. For lunch, you can reach the romantic island of Cabbage Key and enjoy a delicious lunch with panoramic views of the tropical backdrop of Pine Island Sound in the traditional restaurant with the adjacent well-known “bar with all the money on the walls” (lunch not included). Don’t miss out on immortalizing yourself on the famous bar wall as well before heading back. Overnight in Cape Coral.

How about a world-class game of golf against an impressive natural backdrop under the Florida sun? The tropical island paradise around Fort Myers has over 30 renowned golf courses with spectacular views of the Gulf of Mexico or idyllic waterways and canals ready for you. The 18-hole golf course The Old Corkscrew Golf Club in Estero, designed by Jack Nicklaus, offers a landscape of cypresses, pines, old oak trees as well as resident storks and herons. A real Florida golf experience! Overnight in Cape Coral.

Today, the natural dream islands of Sanibel and Captiva Island lure. Enjoy a relaxed lifestyle while walking barefoot along the wide white sand beaches in the pleasantly warm waters of the Gulf of Mexico – no building can be higher than the tallest palm tree. The award-winning beaches range from Lighthouse Park at one end of Sanibel to the romantic beaches of Captiva. But the special thing about the beaches are the many shells that are washed on the beach. Over 400 different species of mussels can be found here. Before you set off for your beach walk, it’s worth a visit to the exciting Bailey-Matthews Shell Museum, which houses the largest collection of shells in North America. Join a scientist on a beachwalk and learn about the local shells, mollusks and other marine life found on Sanibel Island. River otters, sea turtles, dolphins, manatees, bald eagles and hundreds of native birds seek refuge here. Then you can dedicate yourself at Bowman’s Beach to shelling – as mussel collecting is called there. And best of all, you can also take home the artworks of the sea as a souvenir – unless there are live animals in the shell. Overnight in Cape Coral.

The J.N. Ding Darling National Wildlife Refuge is a true paradise for nature lovers. The approximately 2,500-hectare nature reserve is home to one of the largest natural mangrove forests in the country and is home to a variety of animals and more than 230 bird species. To get to know this unique ecosystem, we recommend a kayaking tour in the Tarpon Bay and coastal area. Together with an experienced naturalist, paddle through the mangrove forest along the Commodore Creek Trail and experience the habitat of the mangroves up close. After the one and a half hour guided tour you can go on your own with the kayak. The park can also be explored on foot or by bike on the well-developed paths with observation towers for animal observation. Overnight in Cape Coral.

Today you are once again fully dedicated to the wonderful sport of golf. You are spoiled for choice as to which golf course you want to play. On Sanibel Island, the award-winning 18-hole championship course is The Dunes Golf & Tennis Club, nestled in a tropical natural landscape with spectacular views and challenging greens. The golf course borders a nature reserve and the individual fairways are lined with lakes and waterways. Spectacular villas enliven the demanding picture. But you can also follow in the footsteps of the famous inventor Thomas Edison and his best friend, car magnate Henry Ford, who were already enthusiastic about the Fort Myers Country Club in the heart of Fort Myers in the 1920s. Among the top facilities in Southwest Florida is the 27-hole Del Tura Country Club golf course in North Fort Myers. Here, water hazards and excellent greens provide the right challenge. If you don’t have enough of golf yet, we are happy to recommend more first-class courses in the region. Overnight in Cape Coral.

Not only nature fans, also culture lovers come in the southwest of Florida fully at your expense. Explore the River District - Fort Myers' beautiful historical and cultural center along the promenade of the Caloosahatchee River. Innovative restaurants, trendy bars and small cafés invite you to stay. In the antique shops and boutiques at The Open Door Shoppes on Hendry Street, you’ll find extraordinary souvenirs. Art buffs visit the Sidney & Berne Davis Art Center, a performing arts venue. Just a few minutes' drive from downtown is the winter home of inventor Thomas Edison and entrepreneur Henry Ford. On a guided tour of the Edison & Ford Winter Estate, you can visit the men’s tropical gardens and laboratories in addition to the apartment buildings. Shopaholics is drawn to the Miromar Outlets in Estero. If you want to leave the hustle and bustle of the city behind, head towards Pine Island. Here you will find a fishing village idyll as from the picture book. The heart forms the colorful artist enclave Matlacha, which is located on its own small island. This artists’ paradise is characterized by colorful shops and galleries, fish markets and hip restaurants on the waterfront. Overnight in Cape Coral.

At the end of your trip, treat yourself to another day on the beach in the dreamlike island paradise. The region has the right place for all beach lovers, whether it is for relaxation and soul dwelling, getting active or for romantic beach walks. Fort Myers Beach is located on Estero Island, a seven-mile coastline with gently sloping beaches into the sea. Here families and sports friends come fully at your expense. Along the lively pier there are many small restaurants, bars and souvenir shops and in the evening there is a vibrant nightlife. It gets quieter and more romantic on the beach of Lover’s Key, nestled in the wild surroundings of the eponymous State Park between Fort Myers Beach and Bonita Beach. Only by ferry or boat, on the other hand, can you reach the 14 km long sandy beaches of Cayo Cayo Costa, one of the offshore islands north of Captiva and a true natural paradise for walkers and mussel collectors. After your beach day, you will experience the picturesque sunset from the water in the evening. On a private catamaran you sail towards the setting sun. The perfect end of an eventful day in southwest Florida. Overnight in Cape Coral.

Even the most beautiful trip comes to an end and so today already breaks your last day in the southwest of Florida. Enjoy an extensive breakfast on your terrace, a refreshment in your private pool and the warm sunbeams on your skin before heading back to the airport. After you have dropped off your rental car, you will start your journey home relaxed and full of new impressions. Would you like to extend your holiday by a few more days? So much the better. From Cape Coral you can reach with the rental car within a few hours many more famous excursion destinations and cities of Florida. How about a visit to the fascinating metropolis of Miami and the Florida Keys or a visit to Disney World in Orlando? Sarasota lures as the cultural capital of Florida and a trip to Naples promises exclusive shopping experiences and palate delights in the palm-fringed city center. Talk to us – we have the right recommendations for every place.

In the first millennium AD, the Maya had built a considerable empire and a highly developed culture in Central America: they were masters of art, architecture, mathematics and astronomy. Even before the arrival of the Spanish conquistadors there was a decline of the empire, for which there is still no generally accepted theory. What remains is a large number of archaeological sites that testify to the greatness of the Maya. On this trip through Yucatán, you will get exciting insights into the heritage of the Maya in various places. For variety and relaxation the beautiful nature of the peninsula, especially its beautiful beaches.

Day 1 Welcome to Cancún

Your journey on the Yucatán Peninsula begins in Cancún. Forty years ago, this was little more than a sandbank on Mexico's Caribbean coast. It was not until the seventies that people discovered the region's potential, with its turquoise sea, white sand, and fascinating remains of the vanished Maya civilization. Cancún has since become a busy beach resort that many visitors use as a starting point for exploring Maya history. On arrival, you are met at the airport and accompanied south to your Riviera hotel by your private German-speaking guide. After the long journey, enjoy a swim in the clear sea before starting your explorations tomorrow. Overnight in Playa del Carmen.

Day 2 Your Mayan adventure begins: Chichén Itzá

Today begins your journey back in time to the Mayan era. Your first stop is Chichén Itzá, which you can reach after about two and a half hours of driving. This ruined site is one of the best restored places of the Maya and was chosen among the “new seven wonders of the world”. In the middle of dense jungles lie the impressive Kukulcán Pyramid, some smaller temples and ruins of various buildings. During a walk through the site, let the stories of your guide revive the past in this place. Before you reach Mérida, make another stop in Izamal. Visit the small colonial town, which consists mainly of yellow painted colonial buildings, including the old and beautiful Franciscan monastery. Afterwards, you will travel to Mérida, which once emerged from an ancient Mayan city and is now Yucatán’s capital. The bustling place has preserved the colonial charm with its richly decorated cottages in pastel tones. Overnight in Mérida.

Day 3 Pink Flamingos in Celestún

Today, let the heritage of the Maya rest for a day and dedicate yourself to Mexico’s impressive nature. Together with your guide, you will head to the fishing village of Celestún in the west of Mérida, where a biosphere reserve gives a protected home to various plants and animals. The protected area is particularly distinguished by its bird wealth. During a boat trip, look for flamingos, jabirus and king vultures. Cooling provides a bath in a cool fresh water source. Overnight in Mérida.

Day 4: The White City of Mérida

Mérida was built more than 450 years ago on the site of an ancient Mayan city. This morning you will dedicate yourself to the city’s sights, such as the mighty Cathedral of San Ildefonso, for the construction of which the stones of old Mayate temples were “recycled”. It is one of the oldest Christian churches in Latin America and contains murals illustrating the meeting between the founder of the city, Francisco de Montejo, and the Mayan king Tutl Xiu. After a stroll across the pretty Plaza Principal, you will drive along the Paseo Montejo Boulevard, which leans in style to the Parisian Champs Elysees. Afterwards you will visit a traditional Mayan village. Here you will have the opportunity to gain insights into the way of life and traditions of the Maya and participate in a ceremony. Overnight in Mérida.

5th Day Temples and Ballgrounds of the Maya

This morning, another Mayan cultural heritage awaits you: Uxmal. In the middle of a karst landscape lie the ruins of a once important city, whose name may be derived from the Mayan word for “three times” – apparently the place was rebuilt and extended again and again. The Maya were not only excellent mathematicians and craftsmen, but also had a soft spot for ball sports. On your exploration tour today, visiting various temples, you will not only get insights into the spiritual heritage of this civilization, but you will also learn more about their favorite recreational pastime on ancient indigenous ball courts. Apparently, not only various ball sports were held here, but also fights reminiscent of wrestling and festivals. The fact that the ball courts were partly in the sacred parts of the cities suggests that rituals also took place here. For lunch, you can try a piece of Mayan culture: Pibil, a dish made from pork that has its origin in the cuisine of high culture. After this reinforcement, you will continue to the next archaeological site of Kabáh, which indisputably dates back to the time of the early Classical period. The palace is a complex consisting of almost twelve constructions. Overnight in Uxmal.

Day 6 - The Pirate Town of Campeche

Today you leave Uxmal behind and continue your journey to Campeche. The city was founded in 1540 by the Spanish conquistadors on a Mayan city and between the 16th and 18th centuries it represented the main seaport of the Yucatán peninsula. As such, he attracted pirates so that the Spanish Crown built a powerful fort for the protection of the city. Some of the fortress walls that once surrounded the entire city are still preserved; the ruins give the city something audaciously romantic as a legacy of the pirate era. During a visit to the old fort and a museum, you will learn more about the history of the city. Don’t miss the famous Campeche sunset over the Gulf of Mexico. It is considered one of the most beautiful in the country and is wonderful to observe from the waterfront. Overnight in Campeche.

Day 7: Continue to Palenque

Your journey today takes you further south, where inland lies Palenque in the foothills of the Tumbala mountains, an ancient Mayan city that overlooks from a hillside the swampy lowlands that stretches up to the Gulf Coast. On the way, you will make a stop in the city of Ciudad del Carmen. This is located on the island of del Carmen, which can be reached from both sides via bridges. Between the island and the mainland is the beautiful Laguna de Terminos. With a boat you will explore the lagoon, which is one of the largest in all of Mexico and served as a former pirate shelter. The lagoon is known mainly for its numerous dolphins, which you can observe here with a little luck up close. Afterwards you will continue to Palenque where you will be dropped off at your hotel in the afternoon. Overnight in Palenque.

Day 8 Excursion to Bonampak and Yaxchilan

Your day begins very early, as your excursion takes you to the Bonampak, about 150 kilometers from Palenque and surrounded by dense jungles, which hid from the outside world until 1946. The city is best known for its colorful murals that uniquely evoke a variety of scenes from mythology and Mayan life, including rituals, wars, offerings and trials. After you have gained further insights into the life of the Maya together with your guide, you will continue to the border town of Frontera Corozal, where you will board a boat and travel along the border with Guatemala on the Usumacinta to Yaxchilan. The city was once very powerful and ruled over various places in the area. Explore the temples before returning to Palenque. Overnight in Palenque.

Day 9 in the middle of the tropical rainforest - Palenque

Today you will dedicate yourself to the archaeological site of Palenque, which consists of around 500 buildings spread over 15 square kilometers in the rainforest and inhabited by toucans and roaring monkeys. Your guide will show you the highlights of this once important city, including the magnificent Sun Temple and the palace with its labyrinth of rooms and corridors. The city flourished between 500 and 700 AD. Even today, finely crafted reliefs and elegant buildings testify to the high culture that once ruled this fertile land. Let yourself be fascinated by the impressive architecture, the art of temples and the imposing size of the complex. Archaeologists estimate that about 1200 more temples are hidden in the jungle. Afterwards we continue towards Calakmul, where they arrive in the late afternoon at your jungle hotel. Enjoy the evening red over the rainforest and let the day end with the sounds of the jungle. Overnight in Calakmul.

Day 10 Bacalar – The Lagoon of Seven Colors

With the awakening of the jungle, we head to the excavation site Calakmul this morning. They drive approx. 60 km through the jungle of the “Biosfera Calakmul” to the ruins of the same name. This place is located in the middle of the rainforest and is still one of the less discovered excavation sites. Calakmul was once the most important and influential Mayan power center and at the same time the largest of all Mayan cities. Experience how the jungle awakens and climb the pyramids. The view from above compensates for every effort. Afterwards, make your way to the Laguna Bacalar, also known as the Lagoon of 7 Colors. This lights up especially in the afternoon in all its blue, turquoise and green tones and invites you to relax in the Maya hammock. Fortunately, this impressive place has been spared large hotel complexes and you will find here only small eco-resorts that attach great importance to the preservation of the unspoiled landscape. Overnight in Bacalar.

Day 11 - Continue to Tulum

After a leisurely breakfast with a view of the lagoon, take an approx. 3-hour boat trip where you will visit the most interesting places of the lagoon. Among them are the mangroves, which are home to numerous bird species, as well as the cenotes, which supply the lagoon with water through their underground channels. Those who want can also snorkel or enjoy a mineral-containing mud bath. After that, you can rent kayaks or paddleboards and explore the lagoon even further. In the afternoon you will be taken to your hotel in Tulum. Overnight in Tulum.

Day 12 Tulum, Cobá and the Mayan Coast

This morning you will be picked up and make your way to the archaeological site of Cobá, which in the dense rainforest and in the extensive terrain with its birdscry, cicadas and iguanas still gives a unique feeling of a Yucatec bush forest. If you wish, you can climb the tallest Pyramid Nohoch Mul. The view over the jungle compensates for every effort. Afterwards, drive to the unique archaeological site of Tulum, a site located directly on the sea and surrounded by thick walls. When exploring the castle and pyramid directly on the shore of the turquoise sea, you will have beautiful views over the coastline. Overnight in Tulum.

13th Day of Caribbean Enjoyment

After so many impressions, enjoy a last day of sun and sea on the beach. Overnight in Tulum.

14th day of travel home

Her time out in the Mayan realm is coming to an end. Today, your chauffeur will take you back to Cancún in time for your flight. Even if your journey is over – the precious memories will accompany you forever.

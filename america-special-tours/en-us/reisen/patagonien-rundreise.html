Patagonia — The Wild Landscapes of Argentina & Chile

Two countries, one spectacular trip: Experience the natural wonders of Argentina and Chile on this luxurious Patagonia tour - with América Special Tours.

19 days

Your trip idea

A sample itinerary—tailored to you

  • Home
  • Argentina
  • Travel inspirations in Argentina

Wide steppes, crystal clear lakes, towering mountains, a unique flora and fauna and glaciers sparkling in the sun: all this and much more is Patagonia. Framed by the two capitals of Buenos Aires and Santiago de Chile, we will take you on this journey to the beauties of Patagonia, in a unique natural paradise at the end of the world. Explore Patagonia’s nature in a very luxurious way: you spend the night in the midst of magnificent natural scenery in top-class hotels and board the Ventus Australis. On your cruise you will visit Cape Horn, where the Atlantic and Pacific Oceans meet at the southern tip of South America.

Trip Information

Itinerary

19 Days Buenos Aires – El Chaltén – El Calafate – Ushuaia – Punta Arenas – National Park Torres del Paine – Santiago de Chile

Your Trip Highlights

  • Private, German speaking tour guide and chauffeur
  • Carefully selected hotels and lodges
  • Experience the metropolises of Buenos Aires and Santiago de Chile
  • Argentina's Natural Wonder: The Perito Moreno Glacier
  • Patagonia A-la-Carte – determine your excursions yourself
  • Tierra del Fuego's Wild Nature: Exploring the National Park
  • Stunning cruise to the end of the world
  • Space for relaxation and spontaneous activities

Your journey starts in Buenos Aires, where you will be expected at the airport by your German-speaking tour guide. After you have moved into your room, get to know Buenos Aires on a private city tour with chauffeur and German-speaking guide. At the crossroads of Avenida Corrientes and Avenida 9 de Julio lies the Obelisk of Buenos Aires, one of the city's landmarks. From here, your driver will take you to the Plaza de Mayo in the center, which is lined by Casa Rosada, the Cathedral and Cabildo, the former seat of government. You have the opportunity to visit the cathedral from inside. Colorful it is in La Boca in the east of the city. Many houses were built from parts of dismantled ships and decorated with ship paint. The tour ends with the northern neighbourhoods. You will drive to the green area of Palermo with its parks and artificial lakes. Quietness also takes place in the neoclassical cemetery La Recoleta, where numerous celebrities found their final resting place in magnificent mausoleums. Your luxurious accommodation for this night is located in the modern Puerto Madero district, on the banks of the Rio de la Plata. Overnight in Buenos Aires.

Today, leave the Argentine capital behind and fly on to El Calafate, in the heart of Patagonia. At El Calafate Airport, you will be expected and taken to the unique Aguas Arriba Lodge, near the trekking capital of El Chaltén. The lodge can only be reached by boat or on foot. Either use the boat or, if you like to move after the flight and experience the nature of Patagonia up close, you can also hike through the stone beech forest along the lake shore to the lodge. Aguas Arriba Lodge captivates with its unique location above the lake and offers fantastic views of the famous Fitz Roy, Cerro Torre and Vespignani Glacier. Here it is wonderful to switch off and enjoy the imposing landscape of Patagonia. Overnight at Lago del Desierto.

On this day, take advantage of the offers of Aguas Arriba Lodge. It is the ideal place for varied hikes into incredible nature, fly fishing to get in tune with nature, relaxing, meditating, reading, smelling fresh flavors, hearing wild sounds and just diving your soul... Dive in and experience Patagonia with all your senses. Overnight at Lago del Desierto.

You leave Aguas Arriba Lodge and continue on your way south, always towards the end of the world. Your chauffeur will take you to your Hotel Eolo Lodge near El Calafate. It is picturesquely located in the valley of La Anita and is the starting point for your visit tomorrow to the world-famous Perito Moreno glacier in the breathtaking Los Galciares National Park. Built in the style of an old Estancia, the rooms offer a beautiful view into the vastness of Patagonia. Overnight at El Calafate.

Today you will find not only one of the highlights of Patagonia, but also of Argentina: the Perito Moreno Glacier. It is 30 km long, 5 km wide and 60 m high and is one of the few glaciers on earth that still grows continuously; approx. 2 meters in one day. Leave your lodge and make your way to Los Glaciares National Park to experience the Perito Moreno. Walk over the wooden walkways and admire this imposing, dynamic, azure-blue glacier from various viewpoints. A true goosebump experience awaits you when the glacier calves with loud noise into Lake Argentino and a small iceberg is born. After this impressive excursion, return to Eolo Lodge. Overnight at El Calafate.

Take advantage of the day to discover the beautiful surroundings on a hike, on an eventful bike ride or on the back of a horse – or enjoy the amenities of your unique lodge Eolo Patagonia’s Spirit and let yourself be pampered all around. Overnight at El Calafate.

Today your journey takes you by plane even further south, towards the end of the world. On the southern edge of South America, in the mystical Tierra del Fuego and surrounded by the waters of the Beagle Canal, lies Ushuaia. Ushuaia is the starting point for many expedition ships to Antarctica and for your cruise to Cape Horn. Overnight at Ushuaia.

Waterfalls, dense forests, majestic mountain peaks and glaciers shining in the sun: The Tierra del Fuego National Park impresses with dramatic landscapes. A variety of plants and animals – including condors, guanacos and jackals – find a protected home here. You will explore the protected area today on an excursion with your German-speaking guide. Take a walk through the forest, look for beaver dams and take in the beauty of Lapataia Bay. Overnight at Ushuaia.

Take advantage of the free morning to stroll through Ushuaia or take a relaxing dip in one of the hotel’s pools with spectacular views over the Beagel Canal before it gets exciting in the afternoon: you embark on the Ventus Australis. A unique, unforgettable journey through the fjords of Patagonia - legendary waters and unspoilt nature awaits you. After check-in, you will be warmly welcomed by the captain and his crew. A solemn and exciting moment when the ship leaves the port of Ushuaia and your cruise begins. Overnight on the ship.

Early in the morning you will reach the first – and most important – destination of your cruise, Cape Horn National Park. Located at the absolute southern tip of the South American continent, the National Park is known for its remote and difficult-to-access location and moody weather. Cape Horn Island is a wild, rocky headland that rises 425 m from the sea where the Atlantic and Pacific meet. Even from the sea you have an impressive view of the Cape – a landing is an absolutely unique, unforgettable experience! If you spot albatrosses here, you will see – according to legend – the souls of the dead sailors who live on in the birds. In the afternoon, disembark once more and explore Wulaia Bay. Once lived here the Yangan, a lake nomad people. Not only will you learn about the settlement of Tierra del Fuego, but you will also have the opportunity to hike through this unique vegetation to a viewpoint from where you will have a breathtaking view over the bay. Overnight on the ship.

Via the northwestern arm of the Beagle Canal, you reach Pia Fjord, where you transfer to Zodiacs to land and visit the beautiful Pia Glacier. For this, hike to a viewpoint from where you have a breathtaking view of the Pia Glacier, whose impressive glacier tongue slides from the mountain range down into the sea. In the afternoon, climb the Zodiacs again to approach the glacier wall of the Porter Glacier. A spectacular sight from this perspective. You feel very small in the face of the huge, all-powerful glacier. Stay in front of the glacier wall to admire the colors, hear its sounds, and, with a bit of luck, see the glacier calve. Overnight on the ship.

Another exciting day in the Patagonian fjords begins. As you cruise through the Cockburn Canal and Agostini Fjord, you will be accompanied by the dramatic backdrop of the imposing peaks and glaciers of the Darwin Corridors. There is a lot to see today: After breakfast, you will embark on a unique hike that will take you to the meltwater lagoon of the Aguila Glacier. The Aguila Glacier is another highlight of Patagonia – it pushes down from the heights of the Darwin corridor into the Agostini fjord. In the afternoon, the next nature adventure awaits you. With the Zodiacs you drive to the Condor Glacier. The glacier owes its name to the Andean condors, which have their habitat here. With a bit of luck, you’ll spot these huge, impressive birds and watch them on their majestic flight over southern Patagonia. Overnight on the ship.

In the middle of the famous Magellan road lies the final destination of the cruise – the Isla Magdalena. Those who travel to Patagonia must not miss a visit to a penguin colony. On the island you will experience the Magellan penguins in their natural habitat. Here you will observe the lovely creatures together with other bird species. Before going back on board, wander up to the small lighthouse San Isidro, which once guided the explorers and adventurers of distant times through the infamous Magellan road. After the past, exciting days, your cruise ends in Punta Arenas in Chile. After completing all the formalities, disembark and make your way through the beautiful Patagonian nature to your last highlight on this trip – the Torres del Paine National Park. Have you ever felt very small in the midst of nature’s limitlessness? You will feel the same here between turquoise green waters, glaciers and impressive granite horns. Your Hotel Tierra Patagonia blends perfectly into the magnificent landscape and offers breathtaking views. Overnight at the edge of Torres del Paine National Park.

Immerse yourself in the Torres del Paine National Park with all your senses in the next few days. Take advantage of the many excursion opportunities that Tierra Patagonia offers you. Whether on foot, on the bike, on the back of a horse or with the four-wheel drive car, everyone gets their money’s worth here and can discover the national park individually. Granite columns that rise almost vertically above the Patagonian steppe are just one feature of perhaps the most beautiful national park in South America. Also shimmering, turquoise-colored lakes, roaring rivers, cascading waterfalls, ever expanding glaciers, dense forests and a rich wildlife make the park so special. The 242,000-hectare National Park, recognized as a Biosphere Reserve by UNESCO in 1978, offers a wide network of hiking trails that invite you to explore. Overnight at the edge of Torres del Paine National Park.

On the way back to Punta Arenas, we say goodbye to Patagonia. Enjoy the scenery again before taking the plane north, to Santiago de Chile. The lively capital of Chile lies at the foot of the Andes and, unlike the sophisticated Buenos Aires, looks more manageable, yet cosmopolitan and modern. Chile's capital impresses with colonial buildings next to modern high-rise buildings, framed by the ubiquitous Andes. After refreshing yourself in the middle of the city at your Hotel The Singular, get to know Santiago better with your private guide. You start your tour on the Alameda, the lifeline in the heart of the city. The street is lined with interesting buildings, such as the University, the Church of San Francisco, the National Library and the Santa Lucia hill. Visit the vibrant Plaza de Armas and immerse yourself in the tangle of Mercado Central. Through the Bellavista artist and nightlife district, you reach Cerro San Cristobal, from where you have a beautiful view of the city and the high cordillera off the Andes. Before returning to your hotel, visit the city’s modern neighbourhoods. Overnight in Santiago de Chile.

At some point the best time will come to an end. After almost three weeks, your time off from everyday life is coming to an end today. Your chauffeur will take you to the airport, from where you will travel home with many impressions and experiences and many adventures in your luggage.

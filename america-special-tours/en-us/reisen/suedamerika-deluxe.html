South America Deluxe – A dream of life in five countries

The Ultimate Grand Tour: Experience South America’s icons such as Machu Picchu, Uyuni & Patagonia in 30 days privately & luxuriously.

Five countries, one dream: Join us on the ultimate "Grand Tour" through Peru, Bolivia, Chile, Argentina and Brazil. For 30 days you will encounter the icons of the continent in a framework that leaves nothing to be desired. Experience the magic of the Incas in the Sacred Valley and Machu Picchu, stroll through Cusco and travel to the dazzling Lake Titicaca on the luxurious Titicaca Train. In La Paz, feel Bolivia’s vibrant heights before discovering the surreal landscapes of Salar de Uyuni. From the Atacama Desert, head to Patagonia where you will experience spectacular natural wonders in the Torres del Paine National Park and Perito Moreno Glacier. After the flair of Buenos Aires, the Iguazú Falls await before your trip to Rio de Janeiro between Sugar Loaf and Copacabana ends.

1st day Bienvenidos á Peru

Your journey through Peru begins in the capital Lima, whose old town – legacy of the colonial era – has been a World Heritage Site since 1991. The “City of Kings” carries a dark legacy as the center of the Spanish Inquisition. Due to the geographical conditions, impressive cloud formations regularly show up in the sky of the desert city on the sea. At the airport, you will be expected by your German-speaking guide on arrival and taken to your hotel in the city. On the way, you will learn first interesting anecdotes about Peru and its capital. Depending on the arrival time, you can take afternoon tea at your hotel to arrive relaxed. Or you can use the time after your arrival for small explorations in Lima on your own before continuing your journey tomorrow. Don’t miss out on eating well: Lima has become a mecca for gourmets. Overnight in Lima.

2nd day Lima – The City of Kings

Enjoy your breakfast in the morning before you are picked up by your guide for a city tour of the old and new Lima. In the 17th century, Lima, founded by Francisco Pizarro in 1535, was considered one of the richest cities in the world. It was the residence of the Spanish viceroy, who initially ruled over an area ranging from Nicaragua to Cape Horn (9,000 km). First, visit the colonial downtown with Plaza de Armas and the Government Palace, City Hall, Archbishop’s Palace and Cathedral. Visit the San Francisco Monastery, built over a labyrinth of tunnels and catacombs that served as a mass graveyard. Afterwards you will drive to the modern residential districts of San Isidro and Miraflores. On the coast in Miraflores is the “Parque del Amor”, from which you can enjoy a great view of the cliffs and the Pacific Ocean. Afterwards, the fascinating Museum Larco Herrera awaits. With more than 45,000 objects, it is one of the most impressive collections of pre-Columbian ceramics, textiles and pieces of gold and silver. In the evening, another wonderful menu awaits you, this time in the well-known restaurant Astrid y Gaston with a fusion of French, international and typical Peruvian cuisine. Gastón to one of the most renowned chefs in Latin America. With all your senses, you will experience a fabulous evening! Overnight in Lima.

Day 3 In the Heart of the Inca Empire

Today you will be taken directly to the Sacred Valley. The Sacred Valley of the Inca (Valle Sagrado) is a fascinating cultural landscape in the Peruvian Andes that stretches along the Urubamba River. Once the agricultural center of the Inca Empire, today the region enchants with spectacular terrace fields, traditional villages and impressive ruins. Majestic sites such as Pisac, with its ornate terraces, and Ollantaytambo, whose massive stone fortress was once the scene of heroic resistance against the Spanish, bring the glorious past to life. In addition to its rich history, the valley delights with its colorful markets where indigenous communities showcase their craftsmanship, as well as the breathtaking natural backdrop that accompanies travelers on the way to Machu Picchu. Overnight in Sacred Valley.

Day 4: The Inca on the Trail

After an extensive breakfast, embark on a full-day tour of the Urubamba Valley, better known as the Sacred Valley of the Incas. First you visit Moray. This is a pre-Inca complex of concentric agricultural terraces, some of which are 150m deep. Here the Incas are said to have maintained a large agricultural laboratory, where they could produce different microclimates and thus grow a large number of different grain species. Near Moray lies the picturesque village of Maras, which served as a transhipment point for salt and a recreational resort in the times of the Incas. The salt is still traditionally extracted by hand and foot from the salt terraces today. The view over the approx. 3,000 salt springs is impressive. The last item on the agenda today is Ollantaytambo, where the vast fortifications of the Inca are visited. Ollantaytambo is one of the few places where the Spaniards did not receive their will to conquer and lost the battle against the Incas. Ollantaytambo is one of the oldest cities in the Sacred Valley and also known as “the last living Inca site of Peru”. Here you have the unique opportunity to visit a typical house of a local family and get an impression of the everyday life of the people of the region. Overnight in Sacred Valley.

Day 5: The Lost City of Machu Picchu

Machu Picchu is rightly the highlight of most Peru trips. However, reaching the sanctuary in the middle of the Andes takes some time: with your guide you will first go to Ollantaytambo train station, where you will board the train to Aguas Calientes, a village at the foot of Machu Picchu. With snow-capped mountains in the background, drive through a picturesque river valley with small villages and varied flora until you finally arrive at the train station at the foot of Machu Picchu. Upon arrival, a shuttle bus (together with other visitors) will take you up the Serpentine Road to the ruins. Machu Picchu is said to have been built in the fifteenth century under the reign of the Inca ruler Pachacútec Yupanqui. Archaeologists suspect that the city was once able to supply up to 1000 inhabitants. To this day, science is divided as to what the city should serve with its terraces, which are connected by innumerable steps. For the descendants of the Incas, the place is unforgotten to this day and is still considered their most sacred site. During a two and a half hour guided tour, your guide will explain more about the history and significance of this place. You will visit the main square, the Temple of the Three Windows, the Inca Chambers and the sacred sundial. In the afternoon, the shuttle bus will take you back to your hotel. Overnight in Aguas Calientes.

Day 6 Mystical Views at Dawn

A second visit to Machu Picchu is on the agenda today. Early in the morning, take the shuttle bus to the ruins (from 5:30) and explore other corners of the citadel on your own. You may want to wander up to the famous Sun Gate or catch a photo along with the numerous lamas walking around the site. In the afternoon, take the train back to Poroy. Here you can expect your chauffeur and guide, who will bring you back to Cusco in your hotel. End this unique day with a cool glass of Pisco Sour. Overnight in Cusco.

Day 7: The Navel of the World

Sleep, eat breakfast and go! As the former capital of the vast Inca empire, Cusco is one of the most culturally important cities in South America. First, visit with your German-speaking guide the center of the Inca Empire, the main square Plaza de Armas. There you will find the cathedral, which was built on the ruins of the Inca Palace. Here is a small picture puzzle: Look closely at the painting of the Lord’s Supper – do you discover the differences that the artist has allowed himself to incorporate? Continue to the old town with beautifully restored buildings in colonial style, which today partly house hotels, restaurants and shops. Another highlight is a visit to Korikancha, the ancient Inca Sun Temple. Here you will have plenty of time to explore the building and get a taste of a sacred atmosphere before finally making your way to your hotel. The temple walls are said to have been surrounded by gold and silver. Overnight in Cusco.

8th day Panoramic ride with the Titicacatrain

Your journey will take you to Lake Titicaca today. You will be picked up from your hotel and taken to the Cusco train station, where the next highlight is waiting for you. A panoramic ride through the Altiplano. Due to the rough terrain – with roads that are only a few inches away from deep slopes – a journey through the Andes is often more adventure than a walk. On the other hand, you can move around completely relaxed with the Titicaca Train, which takes you comfortably through the impressive landscape of the High Andes. Imposing peaks and deep gorges pass you as you eat your lunch in the dining car and take a leisurely tea break in the afternoon. The desire for high-production is satisfied in the bar cart (but drinks are not included in the price). After about ten hours of driving, you will reach the Puno on Lake Titicaca and will be taken directly to your hotel by your next guide. Overnight in Puno.

Day 9 The floating islands of the Uros

With an area of over 8,000 square meters, Titicaca is South America’s second largest lake and the highest navigable lake in the world. Today you go on an excursion to the islands of the Uros. The Uros are an ethnic group of indigenous people on Lake Titicaca near Puno in Peru, currently about 2000 people. The Uros live on currently 40 floating islands, which they make from dried Totora reeds. The same plant is among other things also raw material for reed boats and the houses on the islands. The islands, where only a few hundred Uros live, are located 5 km west of the port Puno. After visiting the Uros, glide on the huge and sun-flooded Andean Sea to the Mediterranean-looking island of Taquile. There you will take a small hike to magnificent viewpoints over the deep blue lake and learn about the traditional life of the approximately 2,000 taquileños who have lived together in collective cooperatives for centuries. The people here wear colorful, traditional dresses that they make themselves and sell in cooperative shops on the island. Famous are the knitting men of Taquile, who can be seen everywhere with knitwear in hand and make fine goods. They will invite you and serve you a typical lunch. Overnight in Puno.

Day 10 The birthplace of the Incas on Lake Titicaca

Today your journey goes into the next round. You will be picked up from the hotel and taken to Kasani, where you will cross the border into Bolivia. From there you will continue to the religious city of Copacabana, where you will visit the famous church with the shrine of the Black Virgin as well as the main square. Afterwards you board the environmentally friendly catamaran and transfer to the southern part of the Sun Island. Here you will visit Pilcocaina, the most spectacular ruin complex on the island. This labyrinth of stone walls and tiny doorways offers a view of a beautiful white beach washed by the deep blue water. You will visit the ruins and take part in an Aymara ceremony led by a Kallawaya (Andean healer). Afterwards, take a local barge to the Yumani berth. From here, climb the Inca stairs and visit the island’s fountain of youth, where fresh water flows from a natural spring. On the subsequent catamaran ride to Cocotoni, you will enjoy breathtaking views of the King Cordillera and a selection of delicious dishes including Bolivian specialties carefully prepared by the chef. After arriving in Cocotoni, take a boat trip on Lake Titicaca in a traditional Totora reed boat. Your eventful day ends in La Paz. Overnight in La Paz.

Day 11 Explore La Paz

The start of this tour is a visit to the famous Witch Market, where, in addition to many herbs and plants, there are also llama fetuses and dried snakes and frogs. The colonial church of San Francisco will then be on the program before going to Plaza Murillo with the cathedral, the government pa-last and the National Congress. The most beautiful lane of La Paz, Calle Jaen, is then suitable for strolling. Next, take a cable car ride to El Alto, the twin town of La Paz. During the journey you can enjoy the unique panoramic view of the city. After the colonial old town, travel through the modern districts of Obrajes, Calacoto and La Florida. You also have a very special view of the twin cities of La Paz and El Alto from the “Teleferico”. This is a brand new cable car that connects La Paz, located in the valley, and El Alto, located at an altitude of more than 4,000 meters. Over the heads of the busy Cholitas you will float up from the crater and be amazed by this breathtaking view over the sea of houses nestling on the slopes. The end of the tour is a visit to the so-called Mondtale. Here, in thousands of years, nature has created bizarre rock formations that are subject to constant change. Overnight in La Paz.

Day 12 The “Travesía” begins

You will be taken to the airport in time for a short domestic flight to Uyuni. Today begins for you the “Travesía”, a special onward journey that takes you from Bolivia to Chile. This is the perfect start to the upcoming transformative journey. Prepare your body and mind to discover the numerous wonders. On the first day of this “Travesía” you will cross the impressive salt desert. Uyuni was founded in 1889 by former Bolivian President Aniceto Arce. Most residents of the city work for the state or in the mines, although tourism is experiencing an upswing. Uyuni is also called the “favorite daughter of Bolivia”. Finally, we will reach our Jirira Lodge, which is located at the foot of the majestic Tunupa volcano. After a warm welcome, you will receive an introduction about the destination. Then plan together with your guide where and what you want to explore in the coming days. Overnight in Uyuni.

Day 13: The World's Largest Salt Desert

Today, enjoy the breathtaking view of the salt desert of Uyuni from the comfort of the lodge. What are you looking for today? You can opt for a contemplative excursion or a bicycle tour. Possible activities include climbing to the colorful slopes of the Tunupa volcano up to 5.432 m altitude, a bike ride over part of the salt desert or exploring the foothills of the volcano with visits to the villages along the way. Overnight in Uyuni.

Day 14: Uyuni to Chituca

Today you have the opportunity to hike on the “Isla del Pescado”. This is a fascinating island in the middle of the endless expanse of the salt desert of Uyuni. The island is known for its huge, partly centuries-old cactus and offers spectacular views over the shining white salt sea that surrounds it. Afterwards we will continue the journey towards Chituca, where we will learn more about the culture and way of life of the local population. Overnight in Chituca.

15th day Adventures and discoveries in the Altiplano

After a restorative breakfast, set off for an exploration of your choice. You can walk through the cactus forest and visit the nearby old agricultural terraces. Alternatively, it is possible to visit the village of “San Pedro de Quemes”, a former Spanish settlement with breathtaking panoramic views of the Altiplano and the salt desert. Then continue your journey to the “Gruta de las Galaxias”, a remarkable cave with unusual geological formations, where you will discover and experience a fascinating part of Andean history. They are heading towards Ramaditas. On the way, you walk through old mines and valleys. The Turquiri lagoon, with its great variety of birds and its impressive rock formations, offers the perfect setting for a relaxing lunch. Now it is time to explore the region more intensively. Choose between a longer hike or a combination of short walks and journeys by vehicle. Then continue your journey to our Mountain Lodge, where you will rest to recharge for a new day. When you close your eyes, you can feel and hear the vast silence around you. Overnight in Ramaditas.

16th day Border crossing

On the way, the exploration continues. You will visit the geysers “Sol de la Mañana” as well as various salt deserts, wetlands and lagoons. The “Laguna Colorada” is the perfect place to see flamingos in their natural habitat and at the same time take a lunch break here while enjoying the impressive variety of colors that characterize the Altiplano. Your journey through the Andes begins and takes you to the border at Hito Cajón, 47 km from your starting point. Now you are in Chile. Together with your guide, change the vehicle and continue your adventures. Arrival in the oasis of San Pedro de Atacama, located at 2,407 m above sea level. M. Their arrival marks the beginning of a deep immersion in the nature of seclusion. Overnight in San Pedro de Atacama.

17-18. Day of Desert Adventure in San Pedro de Atacama

Immerse yourself in the world of the driest desert on earth and feel its impenetrable aura in every landscape. The plan you created together with your guide unfolds at its own pace. Activities range from simple to advanced levels of difficulty, with short or long durations and various methods of exploration: hiking, horse riding, cycling, alternating vehicle routes with short walks and even high mountain tours for the most adventurous. Remember: every weather provides the perfect day to explore. In clear skies, however, head to the fresh desert night for an astronomy session at the lodge’s observatory. Overnight in San Pedro de Atacama.

19th Day to the South

From Explora Atacama Lodge, head to Calama Airport, the journey takes about an hour to get you in time for your flight. Today is a travel day ahead of you. Via Santiago it goes south to Puerto Natales. Here you will be expected and taken to your hotel in the Torres del Paine National Park. Have you ever felt very small in the midst of nature’s limitlessness? This is exactly how you will feel here in the Torres del Paine National Park between turquoise green waters, glaciers and impressive granite horns – a highlight of any trip to Chile. The windstorm Patagonian plain meets here suddenly on the summits of the South Cordillera, on the steeply towering mountains, which rise like an impregnable rock castle with granite tips. They rise more than 2,000 meters from the level. There are numerous blue-green glacier lakes in the park. In one of them, Lake Grey, thick white-blue ice blocks float even in summer time – again and again, meter-high ice blocks break out of the ice wall of the Grey glacier under great roar. Around 120 species of birds, including condors and nandus, are at home here, guanacos can be met every step, foxes and pumas live in the more remote regions. Overnight at Torres del Paine National Park.

20-21. Day your days at Torres del Paine

In the next two days, you will have the freedom to design your explorations according to your interests. Choose from a variety of fascinating excursions and immerse yourself in the impressive landscape of Torres del Paine National Park. Let yourself be enchanted by the magic of this incomparable region and discover it at your own pace. Overnight at Torres del Paine National Park.

22nd Day Ice El Calafate

In the morning, you will leave the spectacular scenery of Torres del Paine National Park and make your way to El Calafate in Argentina. The ride will take you through Patagonia’s vast, windswept steppe, a seemingly endless expanse of golden grasslands, rolling hills and occasional Guanako herds curiously lingering on the roadside. After crossing the border at Cerro Castillo, the landscape gradually changes – the panorama is characterised by vast plains and distant mountain ranges as the turquoise Lago Argentino draws ever closer. With a bit of luck, you can spot Nandus or even condors circling majestically in the sky on the ride. After about five to six hours drive, you will reach El Calafate, the charming gateway to the famous Perito Moreno Glacier. The small town is idyllic on the shores of Lake Argentino and welcomes you with a relaxed atmosphere, cozy cafes and rustic restaurants serving regional specialties such as grilled lamb and homemade empanadas. Take advantage of the rest of the day for a relaxing stroll along the lake promenade or visit the Glaciarium, a modern museum that offers a fascinating insight into the world of glaciers. Overnight in El Calafate.

Day 23 The Calving Glacier

A natural wonder that seeks its own kind awaits you today. After an extensive breakfast, make your way to Los Glaciares National Park with your guide. Your exclusive tour with your own driver and German-speaking guide will take you through the Patagonian Andean Forest to the Curva de los Suspiros. Here you get a panoramic view over the fabulous landscape and immediately notice what awaits you today – the Perito-Moreno Glacier. This huge and world-famous ice plateau is better known as the “calving glacier”. It is part of the Patagonian continental ice field, from which 13 glaciers flow down to the Atlantic side and finally flow into the lakes Lago Viedma and Lago Argentino. The Perito Moreno Glacier, 5 km long and perched up to 80 m high above Lake Argentino, is one of the few glaciers in the world that gradually forms a dam and blocks the water masses of the Brazo Rico river arm. Every four to five years, the pressure of the water is so strong that the icy dam bursts into a thousand pieces. To get a better look at this unimaginable spectacle, your guide will take you to the observation platforms across from the Perito Moreno Glacier. In the afternoon, return to El Calafate. Overnight in El Calafate.

Day 24 Buenos Aires – The pulsating soul of Argentina

Once the time has come, you will be taken to the airport for your flight to Buenos Aires. Arriving in Buenos Aires, you will already be expected and taken to your hotel. Buenos Aires fascinates with a mixture of European flair, Latin American passion and deep-rooted tango culture. The city on the Río de la Plata impresses with impressive architecture, green boulevards and a variety of cuisine. Historic districts such as San Telmo with its antique markets or the colorful La Boca with the famous Caminito street offer nostalgic charm, while the elegant Recoleta and trendy Palermo exude cosmopolitan flair. Puerto Madero shows the modern side of the metropolis. Whether a tango show, traditional milonga or an excellent steak – Buenos Aires combines culture, history and enjoyment to create an unforgettable experience. Overnight in Buenos Aires.

Day 25: Paris of South America

You leave your hotel and immerse yourself in the vibrant heart of Buenos Aires. Your first destination is Plaza de Mayo, the historic center of the city. Here you will find the imposing Casa Rosada, seat of the Argentine government, as well as the first town hall, the Cabildo. In the impressive cathedral of Buenos Aires, admire the tall vaults, ornate stained glass and elegant marble columns. Afterwards we will go to the colorful district of La Boca with its lively street art and the colourfully painted corrugated tin houses. Stroll through the legendary Caminito Street, a lively open-air museum where tango dancers and street performers create a unique flair. Continue on to Puerto Madero. Here, historical harbor atmosphere meets contemporary flair. Afterwards you will drive through Palermo, an oasis of tranquility with vast parks, artificial lakes and magnificent avenues. The end of your tour is the elegant Recoleta. This evening you can look forward to a unique tango experience. The unique show of Rojo Tango takes place at El Cabaret. The show delights with passionate dancers and unique, beautiful costumes. Dinner includes a 3-course menu and drinks. After the show, you will be taken back to your hotel around midnight. Overnight in Buenos Aires.

Day 26 The Iguazu Falls - A Force of Nature

In the morning, you will be transferred from the hotel to the airport, where your onward flight to Puerto Iguazú begins. After landing in Puerto Iguazú, immerse yourself in the relaxed atmosphere of this small, charming town located at the mouth of the Iguazú and Paraná rivers. A transfer will take you directly to the Argentine National Park Iguazú to your hotel. From your hotel, you can walk to the visitor center where you will board a small train that will take you through the tropical rainforest to the upper circuit. Already on the way, a spectacular panorama of the waterfalls opens up. The highlight of the day is a visit to the devil’s throat (“Garganta del Diablo”) – the heart of the falls. With a width of 150 meters and a height of 80 meters, the water plunges thunderingly into the depths here and creates a huge fog veil that makes this natural spectacle even more impressive. After this unforgettable experience, you will wander along the gorge of the Iguazú River about 3 kilometers to the lower circular path, where you can not only admire the majestic waterfalls up close, but also discover the rich flora and fauna of the tropical rainforest. Overnight in Puerto Iguazú.

27th day Noisy water on the Brazilian side

In the morning, you will drive through Iguazú National Park and cross the border into Brazil to experience the falls from a new, breathtaking perspective. On the Brazilian side, a spectacular panoramic view of the roaring water masses and lush tropical vegetation opens up. The highlight of your visit is the viewing platform with direct views of the Devil’s Throat (“Garganta del Diablo”) – a breathtaking natural spectacle that remains unforgettable with its huge spray and thunderous water masses. After this impressive experience, you will be transferred to the airport of Foz do Iguaçu, where you will take your onward flight. Arriving in Rio de Janeiro, you are already expected and comfortably brought to your hotel – ready for the next chapter of your journey. Rio de Janeiro delights with breathtaking landscapes characterized by majestic mountains, lush forests and dreamlike beaches. The Cariocas, the friendly locals, are known for their joy of life and hospitality. Featuring delicious local cuisine and a vibrant lifestyle, Rio de Janeiro combines natural beauty and cultural richness – an unforgettable destination! Overnight in Rio de Janeiro.

Day 28 Rio de Janeiro - Cidade Maravilhosa

Today you will explore the highlights of Rio. We start with the most popular attraction: The Sugar Loaf. The mountain owes its name to its appearance, which is reminiscent of the cone shapes used for sugar production in the 16th century. The top of the mountain can be reached from the Urca district, where you board the glazed gondola railway, which takes you to the dizzying altitude of 396 meters with a stopover. Back on flat ground, you’ll now get a glimpse of the city’s history as you stroll through the narrow 19th century streets, admire magnificent baroque churches and the ruins of the old harbor. Not far from the historic center of the city is a striking district, the artistic district of Santa Teresa. Characteristic of Santa Teresa is above all the colorful staircase of the artist Selarón. In 1994, the Chilean began to form a staircase of hundreds of tiles from around 60 countries into a colorful work of art, which is now a popular photo motif. Continue to the most famous postcard motif of the country – the Christ Statue. You will travel the way there with a cog train and pass the impressive Tijuca rainforest. Overnight in Rio de Janeiro.

Day 29 Your day in Rio de Janeiro

After your impressive trip, enjoy a relaxing day in Rio de Janeiro. Let yourself be driven by the rhythmic lifestyle of the “Cidade Maravilhosa” – whether on a leisurely walk along the legendary Copacabana, a relaxing afternoon at the pool of your hotel or with a delicious caipirinha overlooking the sparkling sea. Overnight in Rio de Janeiro.

30th day your journey comes to an end

Your journey is coming to an end. Even if your adventure journey is over, the precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories will accompany you forever. Your driver will take you to the airport for your return flight.

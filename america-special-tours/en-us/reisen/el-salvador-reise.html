El Salvador & Nicaragua – The Hidden Heart of Central America

Private round trip through El Salvador & Nicaragua: experiencing volcanoes, colonial cities such as León and Granada, the Ruta de las Flores and the island of Ometepe up close.

El Salvador and Nicaragua combine the most beautiful facets of Central America on this extraordinary journey. Discover the impressive volcanic landscapes of El Salvador, stroll through charming colonial towns and immerse yourself in the fascinating history of the Maya and other pre-Columbian cultures. From the crater rim of the mighty Santa Ana volcano, you can enjoy spectacular views while the picturesque Ruta de las Flores delights with its coffee plantations and colorful mountain villages. Afterwards, the journey will take you across the Gulf of Fonseca to Nicaragua, the land of lakes and volcanoes. Here you will find the colonial treasures of León and Granada, the active volcanic landscape of the Masaya National Park and the unique island of Ometepe with its two majestic volcanoes. Boat trips through mangrove forests and island archipelago always open up new perspectives on the impressive nature of the region. The perfect ending is a stay at the exclusive natural paradise Morgan’s Rock on the Pacific coast. Surrounded by tropical forest and unspoilt beaches, you can enjoy relaxing days in the midst of a fascinating wildlife. This journey blends culture, history, nature and recreation in an ideal way, offering authentic insights into two of Central America’s most original and fascinating countries.

Day 1: First meeting with El Salvador

Your unique journey to El Salvador and Nicaragua begins! You start your exclusive journey in El Salvador, a still little-known jewel of Central America. Upon arrival at San Salvador International Airport, you will be met by your personal guide and chauffeur and taken to the hotel. During the ride, you will get first impressions of the dynamic capital, where modern architecture, exclusive shopping districts and the warm hospitality of the country meet. Depending on the arrival time, you will have the opportunity to enjoy the elegant atmosphere of San Salvador or relax in peace after your long-haul flight. Overnight in San Salvador. 

Day 2 San Salvador and the El Boquerón Volcano

After a relaxing breakfast, meet your private guide in the hotel lobby and embark on a fascinating journey of discovery through the capital El Salvador. The first step will be a visit to the El Boquerón National Park, located on the crater edge of the imposing San Salvador volcano. During a walk through the lush vegetation, spectacular views of the huge crater open up. Afterwards, you will discover the cultural and historical highlights of San Salvador during a detailed city tour. In the historic center, you can expect important buildings such as the Metropolitana Cathedral, the National Palace and the most beautiful squares in the city. Your guide will tell you about the eventful history of El Salvador: from indigenous cultures to the Spanish colonial period and the modern development of the country. In addition, you will experience the elegant and contemporary side of the capital with its exclusive residential districts, modern business quarters and lively boulevards. In the late afternoon you will return to your hotel with many new impressions and enjoy the rest of the day according to your own ideas. Overnight in San Salvador.

3rd day Joya de Cerén & the colonial Suchitoto

After breakfast, leave San Salvador and embark on a journey to two of El Salvador’s most fascinating places. Your first stop is Joya de Cerén, an impressive UNESCO World Heritage site often referred to as the “Pompeii of Central America” due to its exceptional state of conservation. More than 1,400 years ago, the Mayan village was covered by a layer of volcanic ash and thus conserved for posterity. During an exciting visit you will discover original houses, storerooms and places of worship and get a unique insight into the everyday life of the Maya. Afterwards, the journey will take you through the gentle hills of Central El Salvador to the picturesque Suchitoto, considered the most beautiful colonial city in the country and the cultural heart of El Salvador. During a city tour, discover historic squares, ornate churches and small galleries that make Suchitoto a very special destination. In the afternoon, another highlight awaits you: a relaxed boat trip on the idyllic Lago Suchitlán. Today, the largest lake in the country is an important natural paradise and home to numerous bird species. Overnight in Suchitoto.

4th day Cihuatán, thermal springs & the picturesque Ruta de las Flores

Today you will discover the fascinating history of El Salvador and experience some of the most beautiful landscapes in the country. Its first highlight is the archaeological site of Cihuatán, considered the most important pre-Columbian center in the country. During a tour you will discover imposing pyramids, spacious squares and the remains of former residential districts. Then one of the most beautiful scenic streets in El Salvador awaits you: the famous Ruta de las Flores. The varied route leads through green mountain landscapes, past extensive coffee plantations, colorful flower gardens and charming villages that delight with their authentic character. Along the way, enjoy a relaxing break in the thermal springs of Santa Teresa. Nestled in lush tropical vegetation, the pleasantly warm natural pools invite you to relax your soul and recharge your energy. In the afternoon you will reach Concepción de Ataco, one of the most beautiful mountain villages in El Salvador. Colorful murals decorate the historic facades, small cafés exude the scent of freshly roasted coffee and lovingly guided craft shops invite you to browse. Overnight in Concepción de Ataco.

5th day Nahuizalco & hike to the Santa Ana Volcano

Today you will find a day full of unforgettable impressions, which combines the cultural roots and the impressive nature of El Salvador in a unique way. Your first stop is Nahuizalco, a traditional community with a strong indigenous heritage. In the lively market, you will discover artfully crafted crafts made of wood, basket braid and natural fibres, which are still produced according to traditional techniques. Afterwards you will drive to the Cerro Verde National Park, where one of the great natural highlights of your trip awaits you: the hike to the imposing Santa Ana volcano. At 2,381 meters, it is the highest volcano in El Salvador and is considered one of the most impressive in the entire country. The well-developed hiking trail leads through varied vegetation and opens up spectacular panoramic views of the surrounding volcanoes, the Coatepeque Lake and the green mountain landscape. At the summit, you will be rewarded with an unforgettable view: deep in the crater, the intensely turquoise-green crater lake shimmers. With a clear view, the view reaches to the Pacific Ocean and over large parts of the country. After this extraordinary nature experience, you will return to your hotel on Cerro Verde where you can end the day in a relaxed way. Overnight in Cerro Verde.

Day 6 Through eastern El Salvador to the Gulf of Fonseca

Today, your journey takes you from the volcanic west of El Salvador to the original and much less visited east of the country. Along the way, you will experience the impressive scenic diversity of El Salvador: fertile plains, small villages, extensive pasture landscapes and always striking volcanic cones accompany your journey. With every kilometer towards the coast, the landscape changes: lush tropical vegetation and palm trees now shape the picture. Finally, you will reach the Gulf of Fonseca, a fascinating sea bay that shares El Salvador with Honduras and Nicaragua. The region is one of the most scenic, but at the same time one of the still little discovered areas of the country. Your hotel is located directly on the water and offers a magnificent view of the offshore islands and the impressive volcanic backdrop. The rest of the afternoon is at your disposal. Take a relaxing walk along the coast, enjoy the tranquility of this original region or let the soul dangle by the pool. When the sky turns into bright red, orange and gold in the evening, you will experience one of El Salvador’s most beautiful sunsets. Overnight in El Jaguey.

Day 7 - Over the Gulf of Fonseca to Nicaragua

Early in the morning, a very special stage of your journey begins: you leave El Salvador and sail from La Unión by boat across the Gulf of Fonseca to Nicaragua. During the journey, a fascinating coastal landscape opens up to you with mangrove forests, small islands and imposing volcanoes on the horizon. The Gulf of Fonseca has been an important fishing area for centuries and at the same time an important habitat for numerous bird species. Upon arrival in Nicaragua, another highlight awaits you: a visit to the traditional Flor de Caña Rum distillery. During a private guided tour, you will get exciting insights into the history of the family business as well as into the production and maturation of the internationally known rum. Then continue your journey towards León. During the journey, you will be accompanied by impressive volcanic landscapes that have given Nicaragua the nickname “Land of Lakes and Volcanoes”. In the late afternoon you will reach the historic colonial and university city of León, which delights with its magnificent architecture, vibrant culture and special atmosphere. Overnight in León.

Day 8 Colonial City of León and the Mangrove World by Juan Venado

Today you will discover two very different facets of Nicaragua: the fascinating colonial past and the impressive nature of the Pacific coast. León is considered the cultural heart of the country and inspires with its rich history, lively atmosphere and magnificent architecture. During a city tour, you will explore the main attractions of the former capital. These include the imposing León Cathedral, which is a UNESCO World Heritage Site, historic churches and charming colonial-era squares and streets. At the same time, you will experience the authentic flair of a lively university city where tradition and modern Nicaraguan life are harmoniously linked. In the afternoon, a special nature experience awaits you at the Juan Venado Nature Reserve. With a small fishing boat you travel through a fascinating labyrinth of mangrove canals, which offers a protected habitat for numerous animal species. Watch exotic birds, iguanas and with a bit of luck also caimans, sea turtles or dolphins in the coastal waters. The tranquil atmosphere of the mangrove landscape and the untouched nature are a perfect contrast to the cultural experience of the morning. Overnight in León.

9th day Managua, Masaya volcano & Granada's colonial beauty

A varied day full of contrasts awaits you: from the lively capital over fascinating volcanic landscapes to the colonial pearl Granada. First of all, the trip leads to Managua, where you will get a first impression of the capital of Nicaragua during an orientating city tour. You will visit, among other things, the historic Plaza de la Revolución, a place of great cultural and historical importance. Afterwards we will continue to the National Park Masaya Volcano, one of the most spectacular nature experiences in Central America. Even the Spanish conquerors were fascinated by the glowing appearances in the crater and gave it the name “Gateway to Hell”. Continue to the viewpoint of Catarina, from where a breathtaking panorama over the deep blue Laguna de Apoyo opens. In the afternoon you will reach Granada, one of the oldest colonial cities on the American continent. At the end of the day, you will enjoy a relaxing boat ride through the famous Isletas of Granada: a fascinating archipelago of more than 300 small islands created by a historic eruption of the Mombacho volcano. Between lush vegetation and tropical nature, you will enjoy a special insight into this unique landscape. Overnight in Granada.

Day 10 Colonial City of Granada & the Cloud Forest at Mombacho Volcano

Granada is one of the most beautiful cities in Central America and enchants with colorful colonial buildings, magnificent churches and lively squares. During a tour of the historic old town, you will discover the most important sights and immerse yourself in the fascinating history of this traditional city. Attacked several times by pirates, Granada has preserved its unique colonial charm to this day and is considered one of Nicaragua’s cultural highlights. In the afternoon, an impressive nature experience awaits you at the Mombacho Volcano. The extinct volcano rises majestically over Granada and is home to a unique cloud forest that delights with its extraordinary biodiversity. On well-developed nature trails you wander through a mystical landscape with lush vegetation, colorful orchids and tropical bromeliads. On the way there are always spectacular views of Lake Nicaragua, the famous Isletas of Granada and the surrounding volcanic chains. The pleasant climate in the high altitudes and the fascinating nature make this excursion an unforgettable experience between culture and adventure. Overnight in Granada.

Day 11 - Ferry to Ometepe Island

Today you will leave Granada and continue your journey to the fascinating island of Ometepe. From the port of San Jorge you can easily reach the island by ferry. Already during the crossing you will enjoy impressive views of the two majestic volcanoes Concepción and Maderas, which characterize the unique silhouette Ometepes. Ometepe is one of the most extraordinary natural wonders in Nicaragua and is considered one of the largest volcanic islands in a freshwater lake worldwide. The special combination of volcanic landscape, tropical vegetation and original atmosphere makes the island an unforgettable highlight of the trip. In the afternoon, visit Finca Magdalena at the foot of the Maderas volcano. During an exciting hike, you will discover centuries-old pre-Columbian petroglyphs left behind by the island’s early inhabitants. Your guide will give you interesting insights into the meaning of these mysterious stone drawings and into the history of Ometepe’s indigenous cultures. On the way, there are always beautiful views of the glittering Lake Nicaragua. An authentic experience that combines nature, history and culture. Overnight in Altagracia.

Day 12 From the island of Ometepe to the Pacific coast

The morning invites you to enjoy the special peace and natural beauty of Ometepe once again to the fullest. The fertile volcanic soils make the island one of Nicaragua’s most important agricultural regions. Lush banana plantations, green pasture landscapes and small villages characterize the idyllic landscape. Then take the ferry back to the mainland and travel along the Pacific coast towards San Juan del Sur. The varied route leads through tropical landscapes and always opens up beautiful views of the coastal region. From San Juan del Sur you will continue to the exclusive nature resort Morgan’s Rock. Nestled in a private sanctuary with tropical dry forest and an expansive sandy beach, the lodge offers an exceptional combination of nature, comfort and sustainability. Already upon arrival you will feel the special atmosphere of this unique place. The harmonious combination of untouched nature, stylish accommodation and spectacular location creates the ideal setting to end the last days of your trip in a relaxed way. Overnight in San Juan del Sur.

Day 13: Morgan's Rock Natural Paradise

Today is all about relaxation, nature and special moments. Nestled in a private nature reserve, Morgan’s Rock offers a unique retreat on Nicaragua’s Pacific coast. The species-rich environment is home to numerous animal species: with a bit of luck you will discover roaring monkeys, capuchin monkeys, armadillos and an impressive variety of tropical birds. On the extensive nature trails you can explore the area individually and enjoy the special tranquility of the rainforest and dry forest. Alternatively, the natural sandy beach invites you to relax and let the relaxed atmosphere of the Pacific coast affect you. Various optional activities are available for active travelers, including kayaking, horseback riding or guided walks through the reserve’s fascinating nature. After the numerous experiences of the past few days, Morgan’s Rock offers the perfect setting to review the impressions of the journey. In the evening you will experience an atmospheric sunset over the Pacific Ocean. Overnight in San Juan del Sur.

Day 14: Farewell to Nicaragua

At the end of your trip, private transfer to Managua International Airport will take place. With unforgettable impressions of imposing volcanoes, historic colonial cities, tropical landscapes and warm encounters, a varied journey of discovery through two fascinating countries of Central America ends.

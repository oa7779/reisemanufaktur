For many Peru travelers, Machu Picchu is an absolute must! But what about the unknown north? Here lie true secrets, lost cultures and dreamlike beautiful landscapes. Embark on an exciting journey to the unknown north of Peru and follow in the footsteps of pre-Inca civilizations. Hike to one of the highest waterfalls in the world – the breathtaking Gocta and learn about the exotic variety of Peruvian cuisine. Immerse yourself in a world full of unknown traditions and ancient culture that dominated Peru before the Incas.

1st day Arrival in Lima

Welcome to Peru! You start your journey in the capital Lima, whose old town – legacy of the colonial period – has been a World Heritage Site since 1991. The “City of Kings” impresses with Spanish colonial buildings and magnificent squares. Due to the special geographical conditions, impressive cloud formations regularly show up in the sky of the desert city on the sea. Your German-speaking tour guide will welcome you at the airport and take you to your hotel in your private, air-conditioned vehicle, where you can freshen up. Overnight in Lima.

2nd Day Lima – Culinary, Colonial & Modern

It is best to get to know new cultures through their culinary characteristics. Therefore, look forward to a stroll through the Peruvian cooking pots today. Your start in the day starts at a typical Peruvian market. Taste exotic fruits, such as the Aguayamanto or Lucuma and familiarize yourself with the fascinating variety of Peruvian cuisine. Afterwards, you will have the chance to take part in the tasting of the famous “Pisco Sours” and a “Ceviche” at the restaurant “El Bolivariano”. Finally, you will be served an exquisite lunch of Peruvian dishes.
Then visit the colonial center of Lima with all its magnificent churches, casonas (mansions), ornate palaces and squares left here by the colonial masters as well as the monastery of San Francisco. The tour ends with a visit to the modern neighbourhoods of San Isidro and Miraflores. In the former is the Parque Olivar, where old olive trees rise up next to large shopping centers. On the coast in Miraflores lies the "Parque del Amor" (English: Park of Love), from which you can enjoy a great view of the cliffs and the Pacific Ocean. Overnight in Lima.

Day 3 Larco Museum and on to Trujillo

Before you leave Lima and travel on to Trujillo, visit the Larco Museum with your private guide. The visit to the award-winning museum takes you into the world of pre-Columbian cultures. Here you will get an interesting insight into the hidden cultures of the North, which you will encounter in the next few days. Hidden treasures, ceramics and filigree jewelry are only a small part of the world’s largest collection of pre-Columbian works. And if you need a moment of peace, the museum’s tropical garden looks like a small feel-good oasis in the middle of the bustle of the city. Before you embark on your journey to the north, a delicious Peruvian lunch awaits you at the museum’s café. Strengthened, make your way to the airport with your guide for your onward flight to Trujillo. A short flight will finally take you to the capital of the north, where you are already happily awaited by your local guide. Overnight in Trujillo.

Day 4: Tracing the Moche Culture in Trujillo

Already in the morning you will go in search of the legacy of the Moche culture. The Moche were farmers whose culture flourished in the desert plains between the Andes and the Pacific in the first millennium AD. Since the Moche people have captured daily life since the 1st century in very realistic and expressive pottery figures, your first stop in Trujillo will be a pottery workshop. You will then drive directly to the Moche Temples. The spectacular Huacas, as the temples are called, captivate every visitor. Built entirely of mud bricks, the massive sun temple “Huaca del Sol” can already be seen from a distance. Another highlight is the moon temple with its more than 6000 m2 painted murals. With your guide, you will travel to a small fishing village called Huanchaco, known for its traditional “straw horses” (boats). As soon as you see the boats, you will be able to understand the nickname. To review the impressive day in peace, your guide will take you to a local restaurant for dinner today. Overnight in Trujillo.

Day 5 Ceremonial Temple “El Brujo”

Peru’s north is full of secrets and carries the history of many advanced cultures. How modern and progressive people’s thinking was back then, you will learn today on your way to Cajamarca during your visit to “El Brujos”. In the colossal mud-brick pyramid, researchers came across a mummy almost 2000 years old of a woman of the Moche people. Who was she? Perhaps the queen of this ancient Peruvian warrior people? Many assumptions and finds in her grave suggest this at least, and researchers are sure that the high cultures of the north were also shaped by women. Then continue your journey and travel to Cajamarca today. Passing small mountain villages and mountain lakes, you can enjoy the beautiful landscape of the northern highlands on this trip. Step by step you gain altitude and finally arrive in the late afternoon to Cajamarca. The proud city is often referred to as the Cusco of the North. Overnight in Cajamarca.

Day 6 Cumbemayo and City Tour in Cajamarca

Do you believe in giants? If you discover Cumbemayo with your guide today, you could at least suspect that these must have been formed by giants, because the impressive arrangement of the massive volcanic rocks also hardly suggest otherwise. Surrounded by a massive forest of rocks, this place truly seems as if it came from an imaginative adventure story. Actually, this is one of the most remarkable aqueducts of the Andes between the Pacific and Atlantic Oceans. Then strengthen yourself with a lunch before your guide takes you to the “Ventanillas de Otuzco”. These are pre-Columbian rock tombs, which were knocked like windows into the rock interior. Before going back to the hotel, take a city tour of Cajamarca. Discover the “Room of the Ransom” (Gold & Silver Collection for the Liberation of the Incas Atahualpa 1533) and the churches of different religions built in the colonial period. Overnight in Cajamarca.

Day 7 - Maranon Canyon

In addition to the fascinating cultures in northern Peru, the overwhelming landscape will also captivate you. Therefore, look forward to your onward journey to Leymebamba, on one of the most beautiful panoramic streets in Peru. From the highlands to more than 3500 meters, you will descend into the Peruvian rainforest and soon see the mighty Marañón Canyon, which is one of the deepest canyons in the world. Lovingly, it is often referred to as the Grand Canyon of Peru! On its spectacular slopes, you will descend and cross the wild river, which is considered the main source river of the Amazon. Afterwards, the roads wind up again at an altitude of more than 3000 meters and finally lead to the small village of Leymebamba. Deeply embedded in the valleys of Amazonia and surrounded by lush rainforests, Leymebamba lies in a scenic area. After the long drive, you are certainly looking forward to your cozy hotel to end the day. Overnight in Leymebamba.

8th day of Leymebamba

Today you will be off the usual tourist trails, because Leymebamba is still a true insider tip that most would not even find on a map. Why Leymebamba should definitely be visited is due to the numerous historical finds made in the region. The museum that exhibits these finds is therefore on your program today in the morning. But here you will be amazed above all about the largest collection of Peruvian quipus, an ancient knot writing of the Inca, with which everything that was countable was counted. In principle, therefore, the accounting of the Inca Empire. After this interesting insight, continue with your guide to the rock tombs of Revash. In the middle of the rocks, these house-like mausoleums were beaten and overlook a breathtaking landscape. Your journey will then take you to Chachapoyas, where the evening is at your disposal. Overnight in Chachapoyas.

Day 9 Kuelap – The fortress of Chachapoyas

Far from any civilization, in the north of Peru, where the Andes slowly fall off and merge into the rainforest, the ruins of a time long past appear as if out of nowhere. Behind this masterpiece of architecture lies the Chachapoya people. High above the valleys of the region, this vast ruin rises up and is often enveloped by the rising fog fields of the steaming rainforest. This probably led the Chachapoyas to the name “cloud warriors”. The former fortress was built from 500 n. Built and located at a dizzying altitude of 3000 meters, surrounded by an imposing, ca. 600 meters long and 20 meters high wall. Let yourself be enchanted by this mystical contemporary witness of an awesome high culture and feel the magic that surrounds these walls. Afterwards, continue to Cocoachimba. Overnight in Cocachimba.

Day 10: The Gocta Waterfall

If there was an award for the most beautiful view from a hotel room, Gocta Lodge would certainly win the prize. In front of you spreads the Peruvian rainforest, on the horizon of which rises a huge mountain wall. A breathtaking waterfall makes for a fantastic panoramic picture. You will certainly not forget the sight so quickly. Directly at the Gocta Lodge starts the hiking trail to the waterfall, which you will take after breakfast together with your guide. They walk past fields and orchards as well as huge trees. With a bit of luck, you might even see a "Gallito de las Rocas" (Andean cliff bird), the national bird of Peru, until you finally see Gocta. It consists of two waterfalls that make Gocta one of the tallest waterfalls in the world with a height of 771 meters. The water comes from the Upa and Zuta rivers, which come from the Bongará region. You get almost to the foot of the waterfall, which wraps you in a cool mist with a roaring force. Overnight in Cocachimba.

Day 11 – Crossing the “El Paso de Porculla”

From the mountains you drive back to the Pacific today. You will cross the highest point of the northern Andes of Peru. Watch as the landscape is increasingly changing. From the rainforests of the north, you get back to the desert bit by bit. Soon you will be surrounded by rock and stone before finally reaching Chiclayo. Overnight in Chiclayo.

12th day King Tombs of Sipan

Today, you will once again immerse yourself in the past of Peru and make your first trip to the Pyramid Valley of Tucume. Here is a large number of pyramids, dated from 1530 to 1000 BC. The valley is said to have been the second capital of Lambayeque and Chimu as well as the regional center of the Incas. In the temple Las Balsa, which is now also open to the public, the central theme is the marine world. Waves, the mystical bird man, gods on a boat with fishing nets, sea birds and of course fish can be found in the depictions. After lunch in a typical restaurant, visit the Museum of Tumbas Reales, one of the most impressive and modern museums in South America. Here the largest part of the finds from the tomb of the "Lord of Sipán" is shown. The discovery of the royal tombs of Sipán marked an important milestone in the archaeology of America, because for the first time, intact and without signs of looting, a royal tomb of a pre-Inca civilization was discovered. Overnight in Chiclayo.

Day 13 Relax in Máncora

Your final stage is: Máncora. The Peruvian fishing village in the north of the country offers the perfect end to your trip. Here you can relax for another day on the beach and enjoy the sun. Overnight in Máncora.

Day 14: The Last Day in Peru

If you want to be a little more active on your last day, you can take an exciting whale watching tour on the idyllic coast. Overnight in Mancora.

15th Day Goodbye Peru

Today your dream trip to Peru ends, after breakfast you will be taken to the airport of Tumbes. From here we go back to home.

Antarctica & Chile — Ice and Fire

From eternal glaciers to the driest desert in the world: Experience Antarctica by expedition ship & the Atacama Desert including glamping. Ask now!

18 days

Your trip idea

A sample itinerary—tailored to you

  • Home
  • Antarctica

This extraordinary journey connects two of the most fascinating regions on our planet: the unspoilt wilderness of Antarctica and the spectacular landscapes of the Atacama Desert. Exclusive charter flights take you to the most remote corners of the southern continent and gain valuable time for intense nature experiences between huge icebergs, penguin colonies and impressive glaciers. Aboard the luxurious Silver Endeavour, explore the Antarctic Peninsula and the Southern Shetland Islands up close, accompanied by experienced polar experts. Afterwards, discover the cultural and culinary facets of Chile in Santiago de Chile before heading to the driest desert in the world. At the exclusive Nayara Alto Atacama, spectacular desert landscapes, unforgettable excursions and a special glamping night under one of the clearest starry skies on earth await you. A journey of extremes that combines comfort, adventure and some of the most impressive natural spaces on our planet in a unique way.

Your journey to the end of the world begins in the vibrant metropolis of Santiago de Chile. After arriving in Santiago de Chile, you will be welcomed at the airport and taken to your hotel. The rest of the day is at your disposal to explore the Chilean capital on your own or to relax and prepare for the upcoming Antarctic expedition.

After breakfast, transfer to Silversea’s private terminal. Then fly on an exclusive charter flight to the extreme south of Chile to Puerto Williams. On the banks of the Beagle Canal and surrounded by the spectacular peaks of the Dientes-de-Navarino mountains, one of the most remote places in Patagonia awaits you. Enjoy the special atmosphere of this small place and get ready for the upcoming Antarctic expedition. Overnight in Puerto Williams.

Today your adventure begins on the Antarctic continent. Depending on weather conditions, the charter flight will be from Puerto Williams to King George Island. During the flight, spectacular views of the unspoiled landscapes of Antarctica are already opening up. Upon arrival, you will meet the Silversea Expedition Team and begin your journey through one of the most fascinating regions on Earth. Overnight on board the Silver Endeavour.

The coming days are all about unforgettable expedition adventures in the eternal ice. On the Antarctic Peninsula, it is not a rigid timetable that determines what happens, but the elements: wind, ice conditions and nature set the pace. The experienced Silversea Expedition Team chooses the most fascinating coves, fjords and waterways every day to give you spectacular experiences. On daily Zodiac excursions you maneuver between gigantic, deep blue glowing icebergs and come breathtakingly close to the imposing breaking edges of powerful glaciers. During guided landings you stand in the middle of bustling colonies of Adélie, donkey and rein penguins and observe their fascinating behavior at close range. Keep an eye out on the water for dormant Weddell and crab-eating seals, as well as majestic humpback and dwarf whales that appear surprisingly close to the boats. If you like it even more actively, explore the silent backdrop on optional kayaking tours. Every day brings new, unpredictable discoveries in one of the last untouched wildernesses on Earth. Overnight on board the Silver Endeavour.

To conclude your expedition cruise, head to the rugged Southern Shetland Islands, which are considered the outer gateway to Antarctica. This remote archipelago fascinates with its impressive volcanic geology, steep towering rocky coasts, dark volcanic beaches and huge glacier backdrops. During further exciting Zodiac trips and landings, you will immerse yourself once again in this unique polar landscape. You will visit places steeped in history and spectacular penguin colonies where thousands of animals go about their everyday lives unimpressed. Even stately elephants and fur seals can be observed excellently on the protected beaches. While the Silver Endeavour finally slowly takes its course on King George Island, there is plenty of time on board to review the grandiose natural spectacles in exchange with the lectors. Enjoy the last hours in the eternal ice on the viewing decks as the infinite expanse of Antarctica passes you. Overnight on board the Silver Endeavour.

Today we say goodbye to your ship and the white continent. Depending on the weather, the charter flight from King George Island back to Chile. Once again, there are impressive views of the vast ice landscapes of Antarctica before returning to Puerto Williams. There is time to review the experiences of the past days and to end the extraordinary adventure at the end of the world. Overnight in Puerto Williams.

After breakfast, the charter flight from Puerto Williams returns to Santiago de Chile. During the flight, you will once again enjoy impressive views of the endless expanses of Patagonia and review the experiences of the past days. Upon arrival, you will be taken to your hotel and enjoy a last evening in the Chilean capital. Overnight in Santiago de Chile.

Today you will discover the Chilean capital during a varied city tour. Santiago impresses with a fascinating mix of colonial heritage and modern architecture against the impressive backdrop of the Andes. You will visit the historic center with the Plaza de Armas, the Cathedral and the Government Palace La Moneda and get to know important attractions such as the National Library and Santa Lucia Hill. Also a visit to the famous Mercado Central conclusion forms a magnificent panoramic view over the metropolis of millions. Overnight in Santiago de Chile.

Today, immerse yourself in the world of one of Chile’s most prestigious wineries. In the historic Don Melchor winery, you will learn more about the art of winemaking and taste selected top wines. A special highlight is the opportunity to create your own cuvée and get to know the secrets of one of the most famous Cabernet Sauvignon in South America. Overnight in Santiago de Chile.

After breakfast transfer to the airport and flight to Calama. From there, the transfer takes place to San Pedro de Atacama. Nestled in the green Catarpe valley, Nayara Alto Atacama is a stylish oasis set amidst the spectacular desert landscape. Take advantage of the rest of the day to enjoy the special atmosphere of this exceptional place. Overnight in San Pedro de Atacama.

Today you will discover the fascinating landscapes of the Atacama Desert as part of your lodge’s excursion program. Depending on the chosen activity, you will explore spectacular salt lakes, colorful valleys, impressive volcanic backdrops or traditional Andean villages. Highlights include the lagoons of Salar de Atacama, the colorful Valle Arcoíris, the geysers of El Tatio or the impressive landscapes of Piedras Rojas. Each excursion opens up new insights into the unique natural and cultural world of Chilean Altiplano. Overnight in San Pedro de Atacama.

A special experience awaits you today with an exclusive overnight stay in the stylish desert camp. Far away from any light pollution, enjoy the unique peace and expanse of the Atacama Desert. The comfortable tents blend harmoniously into the spectacular landscape and offer unrivalled views of the surrounding desert. In the evening, an atmospheric open-air dinner awaits you before one of the most impressive starry skies in the world unfolds over you. Thanks to the exceptionally dry air and remote location, you will experience the magic of the Atacama particularly intensely as the Milky Way and millions of stars illuminate the night sky. Overnight at the Atacama Desert Camp.

After a special morning in the desert, return to Nayara Alto Atacama. Take the opportunity to get to know more facets of this extraordinary region. Take advantage of the day to get to know more facets of the Atacama Desert. Majestic volcanoes, colorful valleys, sparkling salt lakes and the expanse of the Altiplano form the backdrop for unforgettable discoveries. Overnight in San Pedro de Atacama.

Transfer to Calama airport and return flight via Santiago de Chile. With unforgettable impressions from Antarctica and the Atacama Desert, you will begin your journey home.

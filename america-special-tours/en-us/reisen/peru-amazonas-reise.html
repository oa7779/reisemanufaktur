Peru Journey - Peru's Wild Amazon

Most people do not connect Peru with the Amazon – the country is not only the source of the river, but also offers the best conditions to experience life along the mighty stream up close.
This journey will take you to the Tambopata National Park, whose incredible wealth of species you can comfortably explore from your luxurious lodge. Your accommodation offers various excursions that you can spontaneously choose on site – or you can sit back in one of the hammocks and listen to the sounds of the jungle.

1st day Arrival in Lima

Your journey through Peru begins in the capital Lima, whose old town – legacy of the colonial era – has been a World Heritage Site since 1991. The “City of Kings” carries a dark legacy as the center of the Spanish Inquisition. Due to the geographical conditions, Lima’s sky is also grim: The desert city on the sea is almost always overshadowed by clouds. At the airport, you will be expected by your German-speaking guide when you arrive and taken to your hotel in the city. On the way, you will learn first interesting anecdotes about Peru and its capital. Use the time after your arrival for small explorations in Lima on your own before continuing your journey tomorrow. Don’t miss out on eating well: Lima has become a mecca for gourmets. Overnight in Lima.

2nd day from the Amazon: Puerto Maldonado

You leave the Peruvian capital behind and set off for the fascinating Amazon region. A domestic flight will take you via Cusco to Puerto Maldonado. Your accommodation for the next few days – the Inkaterra Reserva Amazonica Lodge – will take you on a 45-minute boat ride on the Madre de Dios, where you will get your first glimpses of the region’s rich ecosystem. After you have set up in your comfortable wooden hut in the middle of the forest, together with your guide you can create an exciting program for the next few days from the various excursions that your lodge offers. For the first day we recommend a guided walk in the surrounding nature reserve, where you get to know the Amazon basin better. So that you also experience how much the tropical forest changes with dark, we recommend a boat trip later. In the afternoon you go on board. As the day-active birds and mammals retreat into their hiding places for the night, time is dawning for the creatures of the night. After dinner, you can retreat to your private hot tub suite or learn more about living in the middle of the rainforest. Overnight at the Inkaterra Reserva Amazonica Lodge.

Day 3 Over the Tops of the Rainforest

Start your first full day in the middle of the Amazon as you wish: early risers who want to avoid the heat during their nature observations can already have breakfast at half past six in the morning. Of course, you can also sleep in and tackle the day very relaxed. For today’s excursion, for example, you could head out with your guide to Lake Sandoval, which is located in the middle of the Tambopata Sanctuary. In a wooden canoe, you will glide through a species-rich region where, in addition to the endangered giant otters, various monkey species, anacondas and turtles live. After lunch at the lodge, it is time to get to know the rainforest from a whole new perspective: 29 meters above the forest floor, a path begins over suspension bridges and platforms through the tops of the trees. You share the view with toucans, woodpeckers, trogons, monkeys and sloths that you will spot with some luck among the foliage of the trees. If you wish, you can also watch the sunset from breezy heights before heading to the dining room for a cocktail and dinner. Overnight at the Inkaterra Reserva Amazonica Lodge.

Day 4 The Plant and Animal World of the Amazon

If you want, a boat will also take you deeper into the rainforest today. Along the winding river branch Gamitana, in whose water piranhas, caimans and turtles frolic, you will explore the rich flora and fauna on the shore of the water. You will then learn more about the life of the people of the region by visiting a farm where vegetables and fruit are grown. After lunch, take a walk through your resort’s rainforest garden to learn more about the local plant world. For ten years, the complex has been an educational show garden with various types of orchids, trees and flowers. Many of them are not only pretty to look at, but very useful: they are used, for example, as building materials – also for your lodge – or for the production of textiles. In the evening, a trip to the nocturnal rainforest is worthwhile, during which you have another opportunity to observe the nocturnal inhabitants of the Amazon. Overnight at the Inkaterra Reserva Amazonica Lodge.

Day 5 Last Day at the Amazon

Even today, you can learn more about the animal and plant world of the Amazon. After a delicious breakfast, your adventure begins with an idyllic 35-minute upriver boat ride that takes you directly to Hacienda Concepción, the sister lodge of Inkaterra. This historic place, once built on the foundations of an old cocoa and rubber plantation, offers you a fascinating insight into the rich natural and cultural history of the region. Here you will experience up close why the rainforest is considered the largest pharmacy in the world: You will discover the healing powers and secrets of the plants growing there and get deep insights into their traditional use – a journey full of aha moments and inspiring nature experiences! Afterwards, enjoy the day on a canoe trip, where you look for turtles and water birds. Very interesting is also an excursion to the wetlands, where the forest passes into marshland and a large number of animals find their habitat. Let your last evening in the lodge end comfortably and once again take in the unique soundscape of the Amazon. Overnight at the Inkaterra Reserva Amazonica Lodge.

Day 6 Farewell to the Amazon

Today, your adventure in the Amazon is coming to an end. After breakfast, you will be taken by boat back to Puerto Maldonado, from where you will travel home via Lima. However, in your memories and photos from this trip, you take home part of your Amazon adventure.

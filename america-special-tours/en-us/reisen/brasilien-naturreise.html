Brazil – a country with many facets. One of them, and probably the most significant, is the breathtakingly beautiful nature. Explore the heart of South America. As the largest wetland in the world, the Pantanal is unique with its labyrinth of river arms and its unspeakable lushness. Marvel at the great waters of Iguazú – wider than Victoria Falls, higher than Niagara Falls and more impressive than both combined. Get to know Rio de Janeiro’s green side with its botanical garden, which is one of the ten most important of its kind in the world as a biosphere reserve. The crowning conclusion is a wonder of the world – the Amazon: an ecosystem that is incredible in its biodiversity. Let yourself be carried away by the unforgettable adventure Brazil!

Itinerary

12 Days of Rio de Janeiro – Iguaçú – Pantanal – Amazon

Your Trip Highlights

  • Safety and flexibility through a private German or English-speaking tour guide
  • Carefully selected hotels for you
  • Explore Rio de Janeiro – and its green surroundings
  • See and feel the Iguazú waterfalls up close
  • Hike and safari in the marsh landscape Pantanal
  • Night boat trip on the Amazon
  • Encounters with alligators and parrots
  • Close to the Pink River Dolphins
  • Get to know the life of a local community

Welcome to Brazil! At the airport, you will be warmly welcomed by your private guide and chauffeur. During a coffee break at the Confeitaria Colombo, your guide will tell you first facts and anecdotes about the country. Rio is located on the Guanabara Bay in southeast Brazil and involuntarily raises images of lightly clothed Sambat dancers and the Copacabana’s dream beach. Together with your guide, you will explore the first attraction in Rio: The Sugar Loaf. The Glockenberg owes its name to its appearance, which is reminiscent of cone shapes that were used for sugar production in the 16th century. The gondola takes you upstairs, from where you can enjoy the view of the city. Lunch is scheduled for a visit to a Churrascaria – more specifically, the Assador Rio, located in Aterro do Flamengo opposite Guanabara Bay. The restaurant is a real gastronomic recommendation with unlimited service (eat as much as you want). The menu includes a selection of first-class meats, side dishes, salads and desserts. Spend the afternoon as you wish. Overnight in Rio de Janeiro.

From virtually every place in Rio, you’ll see one of the country’s most popular postcard motifs: the 710-meter-high Mount Corcovado (the “humpback”) with the 30-meter-high statue of Christo Redentor. This was voted one of the “Seven New Wonders of the World”. Under it stretches the Tijuca National Park, the largest urban forest in the world. Until 1981, the monument was the tallest statue of Christ in the world. In 1808, the King of Portugal created a botanical garden on 137 hectares. Walk past orchids, bromeliads and lianas and stop at one of the lakes. After visiting the Botanical Gardens, you will have lunch at Casa Camolese, a sophisticated gastronomy and entertainment center. Located in the revived Vila Portugal, within the Jockey Club, the house comprises two historic 18th-century buildings whose facade has been preserved as a listed building. Overnight in Rio de Janeiro.

Today you leave the metropolis to explore Brazil’s natural beauties. A domestic flight will take you to Foz do Iguaçu, where you will be warmly welcomed again. Together you will continue towards one of the most impressive natural spectacles in South America: The Iguazú Waterfalls. The falls stretch across the southern border of Brazil and northern Argentina. The Brazilian side of the Falls offers you a wonderful panoramic view of the rushing beauty. During a hike, you can take a picture of yourself. Your hotel, Belmond das Cataratas, is also worth a visit regardless of its location near the falls: The vast terrain includes tropical gardens, tennis courts, a large pool, palm trees and almost 200 guest rooms and suites. You will stay in a stylish deluxe room with direct views of the falls. Overnight in Iguazú. Our tip - Moonlight Walk: If you are lucky enough to visit this place by full moon, be sure to take a night tour to see a moon rainbow.

Worldwide, there are only a handful of other natural wonders as impressive as the Iguazú Falls. You too will surely be amazed by the sight of the 275 thunderous waterfalls. Today you are dedicating yourself to the Argentine side. Your walk will guarantee you close contact with the rocks and water. Especially the main waterfall – the devil’s throat – is absolutely impressive. Afterwards you will embark on a small adventure – first in the truck, then by boat. You go on a safari in the jungle and ride a boat along the river Iguazú. Rapids and waves caused by the falls give your tour the special kick. Along the river, you will spend about an hour exploring Iguazú National Park with its subtropical flora and fauna. In a Zodiac, you can experience the power of the river with high-speed – your contact with the falls can hardly be more direct. On a sunny day, your tour ends with some luck quite poetically when you say goodbye to the falls with a rainbow.

You can get to Pantanal by plane. Pantanal means “swamp” and is one of the largest inland wetlands in the world. An abundance of wildlife has found its home here – 650 species of birds (more than in the whole of Europe!), 240 fish species, 50 reptile species and 80 mammalian species live in the 140,000 square kilometer marsh area. You will stay at the Caiman Lodge, which blends harmoniously into the Pantanal. From your private balcony, you can wonderfully observe the nature around you. Explore the local wildlife around the lodge on a whale hike. On a jeep safari or the picturesque photo safari, discover more remote areas of the farm. Special experiences are provided by a canoe trip at sunset, where you will experience nature up close, as well as a night-time exploration tour, where you could encounter glowing caimans. During your explorations, you will be looked after by the lodge’s well-trained English-speaking nature guides. Overnight in Pantanal de Miranda (South Pantannal).

You can choose the best for yourself from its multitude of activities. Would you like to know more about the Onçafari-Jaguar project, which aims to promote eco-tourism in the Pantanal? How about the Experience Hyacinth Macaw Institute? On this ecological safari, you will accompany biologists of the initiative to nesting sites of the Hyacinth macaw and learn about these colorful birds that shape the skies of the Pantanal. Overnight in Pantanal de Miranda (South Pantannal).

Today, too, you have the choice of how you want to spend the day. A recommended option is the Kaimane Tour – a hike around the ranch, past enclosures, the Research Centre and the Centre for Environmental Education. The destination is Paizinho’s Bridge, one of the most traditional places in the Caiman Sanctuary and a fantastic place to watch the impressive spectacle of the caimans up close. Overnight in Pantanal de Miranda (South Pantannal).

By car you will be taken to Campo Grande Airport. Arriving in Manaus, your new guide is already waiting for you and will take you to your hotel. Manaus is the capital of the state of Amazonas and lies at the mouth of the Rio Negro in the Amazon. The attraction of the city is actually the jungle surrounding it – it is considered to be the region with the greatest biodiversity in the world. Overnight in Manaus.

Your private chauffeur will take you to Anavilhanas Lodge. If you have checked in, make a biting acquaintance right at the beginning of your stay. In wooden canoes you go on piranha hunting on the Igarapé. Even if the animals are not as dangerous as movies make us believe from time to time – a small goosebumps will surely give you the skillful robbers. An intimate experience awaits you in the evening when it begins to dawn: In a canoe where two people can be seated, you will once again sail the river and watch the scenery change in the darkness. With a little luck, you will encounter a crocodile. But even if not: The nocturnal soundscape will not be forgotten so quickly. Overnight at Anavilhanas Lodge.

A hike will take you through the rainforest. Your knowledgeable guide will tell you all about the trees and fruits you pass by. In addition, you will find out which plants provide medical assistance in an emergency and how the locals orient themselves based on the weather conditions. Later you will make an animal acquaintance: You will visit a floating platform, where the pink river dolphins come close to search for food. If you wish, you can swim a round with the animals and feed them. Later in the day, you can buy something for your loved ones at home in the craft center or simply admire wooden toys and furnishings. Overnight at Anavilhanas Lodge.

Today you will set off by boat to navigate some of the countless canals and rivers of the Anavilhanas archipelago and visit some islands. After all, you are in one of the largest river archipelagos in the world: Almost 400 islands and islets can be seen in low water. They are all overgrown by virgin forest, which becomes a floating forest during the rainy season. On the Rio Negro you can reach the municipality of Cabocla in Tiririca. Take the opportunity to share their lifestyle, traditions and social structures of the local people. Your guide will be happy to help you with your understanding. In the afternoon, return to the lodge. Overnight at Anavilhanas Lodge.

At the end of your trip, you will come to the colorful enjoyment of a sunrise over the rainforest. Early in the morning, head to an island and watch as the first birds of the day swing into the air and the new day begins. Afterwards, it is time to say goodbye, because your chauffeur will take you to the airport for your trip home.

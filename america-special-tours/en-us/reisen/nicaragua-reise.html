Nicaragua Round Trip - In the Land of Lakes and Volcanoes

Exclusive Nicaragua round trip to the land of lakes and volcanoes with América Special Tours. Get advice and go on your individual Nicaragua trip.

Itinerary & services

Nicaragua combines colonial heritage, spectacular volcanic landscapes and authentic nature experiences like no other country in Central America on this extraordinary journey. You will discover the historic cities of León and Granada, glide through the mangroves of the Juan Venado Nature Reserve, experience the thrill of volcanic boarding at Cerro Negro and explore the mysterious island of Ometepe with its majestic twin volcanoes. Exclusive ecolodges, private island accommodation and selected boutique hotels ensure maximum comfort. Encounters with monkeys, sloths and exotic bird species, sunsets over Lake Nicaragua and relaxing hours on the Pacific coast make this trip a perfect combination of adventure, nature enjoyment and stylish travel.

1st day Arrival in the capital Managua

Welcome to Nicaragua, the fascinating land of volcanoes and lakes. After landing at the international airport of Managua, you will already be received by your driver. Together you will drive to your hotel, which is in a quiet location just outside the city center. During the short drive you will gain first impressions of the lively capital and its special atmosphere. Upon arrival, the rest of the day will be at your disposal. In the evening, the restaurants of your hotel invite you to a pleasurable end of the day. Enjoy international specialties, fresh seafood or a relaxed drink in a stylish ambience and get attuned to the upcoming travel experiences. Overnight in Managua.

2nd day Historical Managua and colonial León

After breakfast, embark on a discovery tour of Managua and learn about the eventful history of the Nicaraguan capital. Highlights include the Plaza de la Revolución with its imposing old cathedral and the National Palace, as well as the hill of Loma de Tiscapa. From here you can enjoy an impressive panoramic view over Managua to the majestic Momotombo volcano. Then continue your journey to León Viejo. The ruins of the former colonial city are a UNESCO World Heritage site and provide fascinating insights into the beginnings of Spanish settlement in Nicaragua. After the visit, the journey through traditional villages and rural regions leads to the historic city of León, which is today considered the cultural and intellectual center of the country. In the afternoon, another nature experience awaits you: by boat you can explore the protected mangrove area of the Juan Venado Nature Reserve. Between waterways and lush vegetation, numerous species of birds, turtles and even crocodiles can be observed with a bit of luck. At the end of the day, enjoy the relaxed atmosphere on the Pacific coast and experience an atmospheric sunset on the beach. Overnight in León.

3rd day Cathedral of León and adventure at Cerro Negro

Today you will discover the cultural and scenic highlights around León. In the morning you will explore the former colonial city on a guided tour. A special highlight is the Cathedral of León, which is considered the largest church in Central America. From its bright white roof you can enjoy a spectacular view over the city and the surrounding volcanic chains. During your walk through the historic streets, you will encounter colorful murals, important buildings and museums that tell of Nicaragua’s turbulent history and cultural roots. Then one of the most extraordinary adventures in the country awaits you. After a trip to the Cerro Negro volcano, Nicaragua’s youngest and most active volcano, the approximately one-hour ascent begins through the striking black volcanic landscape. Once at the top, you will be rewarded with impressive views of the surrounding volcanoes and vast landscapes. If you want, you can take a look at the volcanic crater before the adrenaline peak of the day follows. Equipped with protective clothing and board, it goes down the volcanic ash slope in a spectacular way. The famous volcanic boarding on Cerro Negro is one of the most unique experiences in Central America. Overnight in León.

Day 4 - Magical Ometepe

Drive along the Panamericana to San Jorge Port. From there you will take the ferry to the fascinating volcanic island of Ometepe, which is characterized by the two imposing volcanoes Concepción and Maderas. From the upper deck of the ferry you can enjoy an impressive all-round view of the island and its legendary natural landscape. After about an hour you will reach Moyogalpa, one of the two main towns on the island. From here, the journey continues to a unique natural phenomenon: Punta Jesús María. This narrow sandbank juts far into Lake Nicaragua and offers a spectacular panoramic view of Ometepe with its two volcanoes. With a little luck, you can experience one of Nicaragua’s most beautiful sunsets here. Afterwards, they will be brought to their accommodation. Your charming Cabaña is located directly on an idyllic private beach and offers a magnificent view of Lake Nicaragua. Let the eventful day end with a delicious dinner in a relaxed atmosphere. Overnight in Ometepe.

5th day volcanic scenery and petroglyphs

After breakfast, head to Altagracia, the second largest town on the island. Here you will get exciting insights into the daily life of the islanders. Ometepe looks back on a long pre-Columbian history and is especially known for its numerous petroglyphs. One of the best places to discover these fascinating rock engravings is the Finca El Porvenir. Afterwards, you will have the opportunity to enjoy a lunch (not included) directly on site with a magnificent view of the majestic Concepción volcano or to savor the relaxed atmosphere on the beach. Then continue your journey towards the port. Along the way, you will stop at the legendary Charco Verde. During an easy walk through the nature reserve, you will discover the famous emerald lagoon and can observe numerous bird species. With a little luck you will even encounter the local roar monkeys. After the hike, we say goodbye to Ometepe. With the ferry you return to the mainland and continue to Playa Ocotal. End the day with a glass of wine on your property’s private bungalow terrace and enjoy the spectacular sunset over the Pacific Ocean. Overnight in Playa Ocotal.

6th day Farmers breakfast and boat trip to San Juan del Sur

Your ecolodge includes its own farm, where a large portion of the fresh fruit and vegetables for the lodge’s restaurant are grown. This is where your day begins with a very special breakfast experience. After the drive to the farm, collect your eggs yourself in the chicken coop and get exciting insights into Nicaraguan rural life. Afterwards, try milking the cows before learning how to hand-prepare Nicaraguan tortillas in a traditional country kitchen with fire pit. After breakfast, return to your accommodation. A private boat awaits you there, which takes you along the impressive Pacific coast to the coastal village of San Juan del Sur, about twelve kilometers away. Recommended is the climb to the viewpoint Cristo de la Misericordia, from which a spectacular view over the entire bay opens up. Afterwards, take the boat back to your lodge. The rest of the afternoon is at your disposal. After sunset, another special experience awaits you: On a guided night hike, you will discover the fascinating wildlife of the tropical night with a flashlight and experienced guide. Listen to the sounds of the forest. Overnight in Playa Ocotal.

7th day jungle adventure in Jicaro

In the morning, you will set off into the tropical jungle with an all-terrain vehicle and go in search of the fascinating roar and capuchin monkeys that are native to this region. With a little luck, you can observe the animals in the treetops and listen to their characteristic calls. Then continue on to one of the scenic highlights of your Nicaragua trip. On a boat trip through Lake Nicaragua, you will explore the famous Isletas de Granada, an archipelago of some 365 small islands created by a historic eruption of the Mombacho volcano. During the ride, enjoy the impressive backdrop of lush vegetation, glittering water and volcanic landscapes. Your scenic boat tour ends at the exclusive Jicaro Island Ecolodge, located on a private island in Lake Nicaragua. Here you can expect a very special nature experience in the middle of a quiet and idyllic environment. Just a few steps away is a fantastic viewing platform from which you can experience breathtaking sunsets overlooking the Mombacho Volcano. End the day with a delicious dinner at the lodge’s restaurant. Overnight on Jicaro Island.

8th day of free time on Jicaro Island

Today is all about relaxation, nature experiences and sustainable travel. The exclusive Jicaro Island Ecolodge offers the perfect combination of relaxation and active discovery of the unique flora and fauna of Lake Nicaragua. Explore the area on your own by kayak or stand-up paddle board and glide through the scenic islands. If you want to take a look behind the scenes of the Ecolodge, you can participate in an informative sustainability tour. You will learn how water is treated, what measures are implemented to prevent waste and conserve resources and how the lodge keeps its ecological footprint as low as possible. For those who want to deal even more intensively with the social projects of the region, there is also the opportunity to visit some of the surrounding islands. There you will learn about local initiatives and get insights into the lodge’s collaboration with local communities, including support for a local primary school. Enjoy the unique tranquility of this tropical island paradise and let the impressions of your Nicaragua trip in a relaxed atmosphere. Overnight on Jicaro Island.

Day 9 Explore Colonial City of Granada

After breakfast, a boat will take you back to the mainland, where your guide is already waiting for you. Then continue your journey towards Granada, one of the most beautiful and oldest colonial cities in Latin America. During a city tour with a colourfully decorated horse-drawn carriage, you will discover the charming streets, colorful facades and historic squares of the city. At the end of your visit to the city, you will be guided by the renowned cigar manufacturer Doña Elba Cigars. Here you will learn about the traditional art of cigar production and can observe the craftsmen in their precise work. Around noon you will continue to the idyllic Laguna de Apoyo. The turquoise crater lake lies in an extinct volcano. Here you will enjoy your lunch in magnificent surroundings and then have time to explore the lake while swimming or kayaking and enjoying the relaxed atmosphere. In the afternoon we continue to the Masaya Volcano National Park. Established in 1979, the protected area includes the Nindirí and Masaya volcanoes with a total of five craters. From an observation deck you have an impressive view. After an eventful day, return to Granada. Overnight in Granada.

Day 10 Excursion to Mombacho Volcano

Today you will discover another impressive volcano of Nicaragua: the Mombacho Volcano. With a special terrain truck you drive up from the foot of the volcano to the higher regions of the protected area. Along the way, make a stop at a coffee finca where you will have the opportunity to taste a freshly prepared, homemade coffee. Once you reach the summit, take a one- to two-hour hike through the unique cloud forest. Then continue to the Pueblos Blancos, the famous “White Villages” of Nicaragua. They owe their name to the traditionally white-limed Adobe houses that once shaped the townscape. Today, the villages present themselves in vibrant colors, but have preserved their original charm. You will visit three of the most famous places in this region: San Juan de Oriente, which is famous for its ceramic art; Catarina with its viewpoint over the Laguna de Apoyo; and Niquinohomo, a place steeped in history with cultural heritage. Here you will get insights into the traditional crafts and life of the local population. Back in Granada, enjoy your last evening in a special atmosphere with traditional music and a performance of a folkloric dance group. Overnight in Granada.

11th day Hasta pronto Nicaragua

Even the most beautiful journey comes to an end. After days full of impressive nature, fascinating culture and unforgettable encounters, we say goodbye to Nicaragua today. Take advantage of the last few hours to review the impressions of your trip. From the majestic volcanoes and colonial cities to paradisiacal beaches to the warm encounters with the people of the country, you have got to know the many facets of Nicaragua. At the agreed time, your driver will pick you up at your hotel and bring you comfortably to the international airport. In time for your return flight, your journey through the land of lakes and volcanoes will end. We wish you a pleasant journey home and hope that you will take home many beautiful memories. ¡Hasta pronto y buen viaje! Goodbye, Nicaragua!

Patagonia Journey - The Wonders of Patagonia

Patagonia Travel to the Wonders of Patagonia with América Special Tours. Luxury, private guide and chauffeur included. Ask now and experience for yourself.

Snow-covered volcanoes, mighty glaciers, crystal clear lakes and romantic towns with wooden churches: Chile has a lot to offer over its 4,300 kilometers. You will make acquaintance with cottages that will make you think of home, one of the wildest rivers in the country and the majestic Osorno volcano. The highlight of this trip is your stay at the Torres del Paine National Park. Here you can put together your activities according to your taste with glaciers, lagoons and horse riding – in any case, an impressive landscape awaits you.

1st day of Santiago de Chile

Welcome to Chile! Your journey begins in the capital – Santiago de Chile. Upon arrival at the airport, you will be warmly welcomed and taken to your hotel. Immediately behind the metropolis, the Andes rise and thus form an imposing backdrop. Santiago de Chile is for the visitor one of the most manageable capitals in Latin America. Despite its 5.9 million inhabitants, it has almost provincial atmosphere. Relatively few high-rise buildings, but extensive settlements give Santiago an expansive character.
On the first day of your trip, you will see for yourself the first-class quality of Chilean wines. Together with your guide you will visit the winery Cousiño Macul, located southwest of Santiago. It was founded in 1856 by Matias Cousiño and developed into a first-class company by his only son. On a tour of the estate’s imposing old wine cellars, you will learn more about its history. Overnight in Santiago de Chile.

Day 2 The “Chilean Switzerland”

The plane goes to Puerto Montt. Here you will be warmly welcomed and taken to Puerto Varas, where your hotel is located – not without getting to know Puerto Montt on the way. The city is an important fishing port and starting point for ship journeys through the archipelago to the south. The lake area of Chile, also known as “Chilean Switzerland”, with its blue-green glowing lakes, untouched rainforests, snow-capped volcanoes and rushing crystal-clear waterfalls will certainly offer something to every visitor. If you wish, you can walk through the Fish Market Angelmó in the Port of Puerto Montts. In addition to fresh fish, vegetables, cheese, sausage and crafts are also offered for sale. Here you can also observe how a typical curanto – a stew of seafood, smoked meat, poultry and sausage – is prepared. In the afternoon, enjoy a beautiful panoramic ride along Llanquihue Lake. Overnight in Puerto Varas.

Day 3 The Wild River

On a dreamlike ride along the lake Llanquihue you will marvel at the lush landscape of the lake area. You will pass the small village of Ensenada to finally reach the Vicente Pérez Rosales National Park. In Chile’s oldest nature reserve, take a walk along the Laguna Verde (“Green Lagoon”). The national park is located in the Andes and extends to Argentina. The Osorno volcano is its highest point. In this area, pumas, Chilean wild cats or pudus – the smallest deer in the world – sneak through the bushes. Then visit the rapids of the Rio Petrohué, one of the wildest rivers in Chile. He rushes through his uneven bed. With the mighty Osorno in the background and the rainforest around you, you will enjoy a breathtaking (and partly deafening) backdrop. Afterwards, discover the lake Todos Los Santos during a small boat trip. The emerald green shimmering waters are for many visitors the most beautiful lake in Chile. Take a walk along the lava beach before returning to Puerto Varas. Overnight in Puerto Varas.

Day 4 Chiloé – the island of myths and legends

The Chiloé archipelago consists of almost a hundred islands and is rich in bird species and marine life. It is the land of myths and colorful wooden churches – some of which are UNSESCO World Heritage sites – a place where vegetables grow twice as large as elsewhere. There are 400 species of potatoes growing on the islands – and perhaps this is where the tubers originated; research has not yet been able to uncover this. Your path will take you across the Panamericana past Puerto Montt to Pargua, where you will board the ferry that takes you to Chacao via the Chacao Canal. Your Hotel Tierra Chiloé is nestled in the picturesque landscape of the Chilean archipelago of Chiloé and offers a perfect balance of luxurious comfort and authentic nature experience. Various excursion options are available for the next few days. Overnight in Chiloé.

Day 5 Castro: Experiencing the Cultural Heart of Chiloé

The vibrant heart of Chiloé is revealed in Castro, where traditional stilt houses, impressive churches and authentic craft markets characterise the special atmosphere. Once a strategic stronghold of the Chilean colonial government in the 16th century, Castro was the starting point for the development of the island. This historic city today invites you on an exciting journey of discovery, where the cultural roots and influences come alive. On a tour based on the rhythm of the tides, you will explore the island capital and its characteristic stake houses. A visit to the picturesque Yumbel market offers an insight into the colorful bustle of the islanders. The colorful Church of San Francisco, an impressive UNESCO World Heritage Site and National Monument, is another highlight of the city. The tour will take you to the coastal yard before finally reaching the Church of Nercón – a valuable example of Chilota School of Wooden Architecture, reflecting Chiloé’s artisanal tradition and cultural richness. Overnight in Chiloé.

Day 6 Kayaking Adventures to Rilán

Strengthen yourself at breakfast for an active day! From the dock, your journey to the south of the island begins on a scenic waterway that offers you the opportunity to experience Chiloé’s life and culture from a very special perspective. They follow in the footsteps of the Jesuit missionaries who once shaped the Chilote population with their missions throughout the archipelago. After a paddling tour of about 8 kilometers, you will reach the charming village of Rilán. There you can visit the UNESCO World Heritage-listed church and enjoy the atmosphere of the place while walking through the small streets. During this tour you will have the opportunity to observe the diverse wildlife of the peninsula, including numerous bird species and – with a bit of luck – Chilean dolphins. Return to the Hotel Tierra Chiloé and return as desired either comfortably by car or actively by kayak. Overnight in Chiloé.

7th Day Island Discoveries on Chelin and Quehui

This tour takes you by boat heading southwest to the idyllic islands of Chelin and Quehui. Your first stop is Chelin, a magical island with charming, original homes that stretch along nostalgic, old streets. Here you will find the neoclassical church, a UNESCO World Heritage Site and lovingly restored by the careful work of local craftsmen and architects. Then visit the cemetery of the island, enjoy the view of the surrounding islands from a viewpoint and wander through a small forest. The journey continues to the island of Quehui, where you can reach the island center via the calm waters of the Pindo estuary. There you can decide whether to explore the coast by kayak or zodiac, or take a bike ride and enjoy the spectacular views before heading back to the hotel. Overnight in Chiloé.

Day 8: Torres del Paine National Park

Today you will leave Chiloé to travel on to the Torres del Paine National Park. Granite columns that rise almost vertically above the Patagonian steppe are just one feature of perhaps the most beautiful national park in South America. Also shimmering, turquoise-colored lakes, roaring rivers, cascading waterfalls, ever expanding glaciers, dense forests and a rich wildlife make the park so special. A domestic flight will take you from Puerto Montt Airport to Punta Arenas. You will spend the next six nights at Hotel Awasi, located in a private reserve near the National Park. Here you will also get to know your private guide for the next few days. Together you will discuss the excursions – a 4x4 vehicle with chauffeur is at your disposal. Enjoy the impressive views of Torres del Paine and Lake Sarmiento. In your villa – whose design is based on the traditional ranches – you can enjoy plenty of privacy on 80 square meters – for example in the living room in front of the fireplace. Overnight at the National Park.

Day 9 at the Torres del Paine

How about a trip to the peninsula Grey? Enjoy the birdsong as you wander through the forest to Grey Beach. There you can admire the huge icebergs that were driven there by the strong winds from nearby glacier Grey. Overnight at the National Park.
Difficulty: low
Duration of the journey: 2 hours
Duration of the excursion: 2.5 hours

Day 10 at the Torres del Paine

Are you ready to be a summit striker? The challenging hike to the Mirador Cumbre viewpoint in the center of the park, east of Lake Pehoé, is rewarded with a spectacular 360-degree view of the park. In the northwest, the southern ice sheet can be seen, in the north the Paine massif and the famous horns (Cuernos del Paine) with the Nordenskjöld Lake at their foot, in the east the Sierra Baguales and in the south the Sierra del Toro. With a little luck, you can see condors spinning their rounds above your heads high up. Overnight at the National Park.
Difficulty: high
Duration of the journey: 1.5 hours
Duration of the excursion: 2 hours

Day 11 at the Torres del Paine

The Laguna Azul is a paradise for bird watchers. Various specimens have found a home here: Caiquén (highland goose), Pato Juarjual (Schopfente) and Tagua (yellow-billed chicken). Furthermore, you can also encounter guanakos and nandus. You will be fascinated by the breathtaking views over the national park – as well as the Patagonian steppe and forest. Overnight at the National Park.
Difficulty: low
Duration of the journey: 1 hour
Duration of the excursion: 2 hours

Day 12 at the Torres del Paine

Your tour of China Falls starts in the northeastern region of Estancia. If the weather permits, in addition to the Las Chinas waterfalls, you can also admire the Laguna Azul with the Torres del Paine in the background or the Sarmiento Lake. Overnight at the National Park.
Difficulty: Medium
Duration of the journey: 15 minutes
Duration of the excursion: 5 hours

Day 13 at the Torres del Paine

You will get a good overview of the park during a photo safari. You will be fascinated by the beautiful views that Torres del Paine offers – geology and flora are so easy to admire. To bring home many beautiful memories, visit the most popular places in the National Park. Overnight at the National Park.
Difficulty: low
Duration of the journey: variable

Day 14 Departure or onward journey

Today you will be taken from your hotel in Torres del Paine via Puerto Natales to the airport or the port in Punta Arenas. If you wish, from here you can take part in a cruise where you get to know Cape Horn – and thus a world of snow and ice.

Brazil Individual Tour - The Soul of Brazil

Brazil Individual tour with América Special Tours to the soul of Brazil. Here directly inquiries, get personal advice and experience for yourself.

This trip brings you closer to some facets of this varied country and is simply ideal for getting to know and love Brazil. You can marvel at colonial architecture, be introduced to the art of Brazilian cuisine and percussion, and learn about the breathtaking nature of Chapada Diamantina. From the flowering period as a diamond region, tranquil towns have remained; here you can explore table mountains and waterfalls. The culmination is the visit to a cocoa plantation, which offers you a unique insight into the sweet culture of the Cocoa coast and rounds off your trip in a particularly enjoyable way.

Day 1 Welcome to Brazil’s most Brazilian city!

Welcome to Brazil! At the airport, your guide and chauffeur are already waiting for you and together you drive to your hotel. On the way, you will already learn first facts and anecdotes about status and country. Salvador de Bahia was the first capital of the country and is the third largest city in Brazil. Since it was built on a mountain range, there is an upper and lower city. Overnight in Salvador de Bahia.

2nd day panoramic experience Salvador

There is no question about it: Salvador is blessed and offers tourists some highlights: you can stroll past architecturally important ensembles, let your view wander across the coastline and immerse yourself in the rich culture of the region. You will not only find valuable architectural and religiously significant collections here, but the city is also a hotspot of contemporary culture in terms of cuisine, literature and simply all arts. Be it the music scene, the delicious cuisine or the religion: This other world will surely delight you. Today your guide will show you the most beautiful corners of the city – go on a little journey back in time and get to know Salvador from its beginnings to the present day as the third largest city in the country. Overnight in Salvador de Bahia.

3rd day of colonial splendor in Cachoeira

110 kilometers from Salvador, on the river Paraguaçu you will find the city of Cachoeira, the destination of your excursion today. Along the way, stop at the historic Santo Amaro to visit the cathedral and the large market. Cachoeira is famous for its colonial architecture complex, which is one of the most beautiful in Latin America. Its craft, folklore and good cuisine make this city even more appealing. After lunch at a typical restaurant on site, you will have time to stroll through the charming alleys and enjoy the special flair of the city. Overnight in Salvador de Bahia.

Day 4 Bahian Cuisine and Percussion Course

Dedicate the morning to the delights of the Bahi cuisine. Here it is less Portuguese than in other parts of the country, also African influences are noticeable. This includes spices such as ginger, coriander, nutmeg and red pepper. You choose your ingredients first at the market and then prepare a delicious lunch under guidance. In the afternoon, another cultural highlight awaits you: in Bahia, the sound of the drums is much more than music – it is heartbeat, expression and joie de vivre. In this inspiring workshop, immerse yourself in the vibrant world of percussion, deeply rooted in African roots and one of the most powerful expressions of Brazilian culture today. Under the guidance of experienced musicians, you will experience how sounds are created, how Samba reggae carries body and soul – a rhythm that even world stars like Michael Jackson inspired – and how these sound worlds combine with influences from jazz and other genres. Overnight in Salvador de Bahia.

5th Day Fishing Village and Turtles

You will set off to visit a former fishing village – Praia do Forte. The ride takes you around 83 kilometers along the coast, with a stop at Itapoan Beach where you can admire the surrounding sand dunes and mangrove landscapes. On the way, you will also visit an old hippie village on the beach of Arembepe. Magical sandy beaches lined with many coconut palms make Praia do Forte popular for recreation seekers. Animal lovers are also drawn here – they visit the sea turtle protection station (TAMAR), which is committed to the conservation and protection of these animals. Today, the project is also committed to sharks and other marine animals and offers informative educational programs. After a lunch of local specialties at a local restaurant, you’ll have time to stroll and relax on the beach. Overnight in Praia do Forte.

Day 6 Brazilian Paradise

You will enjoy today according to your taste in the magical village of Praia do Forte with azure sea, dazzling coral reefs, forests of coconut palms, tropical gardens, the typical rainforest Mata Atlântica. Quiet beaches provide the ideal backdrop for an unforgettable day off. Overnight in Praia do Forte.

7th day diamond town Lençóis

Today, your journey takes you to the magnificent nature: you fly from Salvador to Lençóis, to the Chapada Diamantina National Park, an area that is particularly green as the surrounding mountains keep rainfall in the region. You can see the result in countless waterfalls and a lush green flora. Even more: From the time of diamond mining, there are many small historical towns and villages that were abandoned after the boom. The small town of Lençóis, today one of the main tourist attractions of Bahia, was founded in 1844 in the second wave of diamond mining. The city with around 11,500 inhabitants still preserves the charm of bygone times with pavement streets and 19th century buildings. In the afternoon, take a first small hike accompanied by your guide. First, we head to the Rio Serrano, where there is a natural slide of smoothly ground stones that flows into a delightful water basin. Afterwards, visit the Cachoeirinha waterfalls, Primavera and Paraiso. Overnight in Lençóis.

Day 8 of Caves and Table Mountains

Today’s day trip will take you to the sights in the northern part of Chapada Diamantina. By car we first go to the Lapa Doce cave in Iraquara. On the approximately one-hour hike you can marvel at the most fantastic rock formations before visiting the silver cave Pratinha and the adjoining lake Pratinha with its crystal clear water. Here you have also found the ideal place for bathing, relaxing and for your lunch picnic. At the end of the day you climb on an approx. 20-minute hike the famous table mountain Pai Inácio, from which you can enjoy a wonderful view of the mountains and gorges. With a little luck, you can also experience an unforgettable sunset. Overnight in Lençóis.

Day 9 The highest waterfall or the mini-pantanal

Today you can choose between two optional excursions. Overnight in Lençóis.
Option 1 – Cachoeira da Fumaça (only for experienced hikers)
Explore the highest waterfall in Brazil! By car, you will first go to the Capão Valley, where your approximately six-kilometer hike begins. We recommend this tour only to experienced hikers, because the first two kilometers lead very steeply uphill. Once upstairs, however, you will be rewarded with a fantastic view over the surrounding table mountain landscape. Continue flat until the Fumaça waterfall, which you reach from its upper end.
Option 2 – “Mini-Pantanal” Marimbus
Get active and go on canoe trip through the alluvial land of Santo Antônio. Because of its similarity to the famous Pantanal, the alluvial is also called mini-pantanal – officially called Marimbus. From Lençois, you will drive for a good 1.5 hours to the fishing village of Remanso, where your boat excursion starts. Paddles will reach the river Roncador, and you will have to deal with one or the other cascade.

10th day Sliding of stone

A short hike will take you in the morning to a very special swimming pool with slide. You will refresh yourself in the natural basin of the Ribeirão, which you can reach via a stone slide. At the end of exciting days in the National Park, you will embark on a flight to Salvador. Overnight in Salvador.

11th day Ilhéus, the capital of cocoa

After your transfer from the hotel to Salvador Airport, Ilhéus will welcome you to this charming coastal city. Ilhéus enchants with dreamlike strands, a rich history and as the home of legendary writer Jorge Amado. Here the magnificent colonial architecture merges with the lush tropical nature of Bahia into a unique experience. Discover the soul of cocoa – a region full of sensuality, culture and nature that you will enjoy with all your senses. Overnight in Ilhéus.

Day 12 Jorge Amado & the Paradise of the Cocoa Coast

Today your guide will show you the locations of the novels by Jorge Amado, who grew up here. He worked as a journalist and campaigned for the oppressed. His father was a plantation owner and he himself helped out as a teenager. You will see, for example, the bar Vesúvio and the cathedral. After the tour you will visit the famous chocolate factory of Ilhéus. The city’s heyday came in the early 20th century with the cultivation and export of cocoa. At the end of the day you will reach Itacaré, a town in the south of Bahia on the so-called Cocoa coast. Itacaré impresses with its tropical climate with sunny, pleasant weather all year round. The area is well preserved and is home to Jorge Amado, the Afro-Brazilian religion of Candomblé, the martial arts of Capoeira and Samba. Itacaré has 16 different beaches. A special highlight is the rocky mountain, embedded in the Atlantic Rainforest, with a diverse flora and fauna as well as river estuaries and crystal clear waterfalls. Overnight in Itacaré.

Day 13 Cocoa Experience

Cocoa, the second: After breakfast, head to Taboquinhas to visit another local cocoa plantation. This program has been developed by small farmers to give you the unique opportunity to make your own chocolate. You wander to the cocoa plantation of a small farmer in the neighborhood to learn from the cocoa culture. After harvesting the fruit, continue the traditional process by breaking the hard shell and manually extracting the seeds to deposit them in a wooden box. Then the withdrawal of the honey is prepared. The seeds wrapped in pulp are carefully and gradually squeezed until they release a liquid called cocoa honey. After that, roast and peel the cores manually to then beat them on the mast and bring the cocoa mass to the wood stove. Mixed with other ingredients, you now get the real chocolate. Overnight in Itacaré.

14th day enjoyment time on the beach

Let yourself be pampered by the Brazilian sun today and enjoy your time out before returning to Germany. Overnight in Itacaré.

15th day Brazil says goodbye!

Even the best time is over. Today you have to say goodbye to Brazil. Your chauffeur will take you to the airport, from where you will begin your journey home. Even if your holiday is now over: The beautiful souvenir photos and small souvenirs will always remind you of the time in South America.

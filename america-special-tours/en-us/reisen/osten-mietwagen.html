US Road Trip — Eastern Cities and Natural Wonders

USA Rental car trip to the east of the USA: Experience world metropolises and natural phenomena on an individual journey with América Special Tours!

11 days

Your trip idea

A sample itinerary—tailored to you

  • Home
  • USA
  • USA - Travel Inspirations

Have you ever wanted to float in a hot air balloon over the Grand Canyon of the East? Or look over your shoulders at the Corning Museum of Glass and visit award-winning wineries from the Finger Lakes area? If so, then you should not miss a trip to the eastern triangle of the USA, because the area between the impressive metropolis of New York and the equally fascinating Niagara Falls has a lot to offer. From the city that never sleeps, you will first travel to the Finger Lakes region with waterfalls and water mills before setting out to experience the natural phenomenon of Niagara Falls. You dare a small detour and spend the night on the Canadian side. On the way back to the coast, you will get to know the Grand Canyon of the East and cross regions as if from ancient times, because here the Amish live according to their old traditions. Back on the coast, experience pure history and magic in Washington D.C. and Philadelphia!

Day 1 Welcome to New York

After your long-haul flight, today you will reach the city that never sleeps - New York. You will be met by your guide and driven to your hotel. With a population of over 8.2 million, New York City is the largest city in the USA. The center of the New York metropolitan area is one of the largest urban areas in the world and is classified as an alpha world city due to its global influences in media, politics, education, entertainment and fashion. The city’s cultural centers for art are among the most influential in the nation. Overnight in New York City.

Day 2 New York City – The city that never sleeps

Today you get to know the impressive metropolis in a unique and stylish way, in an old-fashioned car. Hop on and listen to fascinating stories about New York and feel transported back to the world of the 20s thanks to a suitable car, a driver dressed according to the era and other contemporary accessories. Sit back, your private chauffeur will do the rest. Following your classic car tour, we invite you to a cocktail course held in one of New York’s legendary Speakeasy. This one approx. 90-minute course in the intimate jazz age is led by an experienced mixologist. They create 3 to 5 signature cocktails, including interesting stories about the time of Prohibition and the preparation of incredible drinks. For the evening we are happy to organize tickets for one of the famous Broadway musicals or for the Metropolitan Opera. Overnight in New York City.

3rd day Get out of the city – into nature

Today you will receive your rental car and go on your own to the Finger Lakes region, Ithaca, which is characterized by beautiful waterfalls and water mills. The region with more than 23,300km2 is a large adventure playground that enchants visitors – no matter the season. Eleven freshwater lakes run through the region like fingers – hence the name. According to a legend of the indigenous people, the lakes were created by the blessing of the Great Spirit. But not only the beautiful nature will impress you, but also the many different paths. Walk along the wine, art or museum path. There is also a path for cheese lovers. For example, visit the goat farm Lively Run Goat Dairy. The farm is under German management and offers visitors the opportunity to learn more about the cheese production process. You can visit the barn and goats; of course, a cheese tasting should not be missing. Also interesting for a visit is the Cornel University campus and the botanical garden of Cornel. Overnight in Ithaca.

Day 4 Unique Natural Wonder – The Niagara Falls

Today it is time to get up early, because your trip will take you to the most famous waterfalls of the USA and Canada – the Niagara Falls. No trip to this part of the world would be complete if Niagara Falls were not visited. This magnificent spectacle lies on American and Canadian terrain and should be visited by both sides. To get close to the massive falls, take part in the boat tour “Maid of the Mist”. The boat will bring you close to Horseshoe Falls. Feel the thunderous masses of water with all your senses up close. As the culmination of a unique day, embark on an approximately one-hour white water cruise on the Niagara River. For tonight, sleep in a hotel on the Canadian side. Overnight in Niagara-on-the-lake.

Day 5 Hot Air Balloon Ride Over the Grand Canyon of the East

Today you will travel again towards the Finger Lakes region, but this time not to Ithaca but to Corning, where a visit to the glass museum should be on your list. Sounds boring – no way! It is the world's largest glass museum and offers you the opportunity to watch live presentations blowing vases, bowls and figures. You can also take part in workshops and make a glass figure yourself – maybe a nice souvenir for home...? Before you practice glass blowing in Corning, however, a highlight awaits you along the way: A hot air balloon ride over Letchwork State Park, known as the Grand Canyon of the East. During the one-hour drive, enjoy unique and spectacular views of waterfalls, the gorge and experience a unique backdrop. Overnight in Corning.

Day 6 Drive through the Appalachians

Your route will take you through the Appalachians to Lancaster today. Along the way, you’ll experience rolling hills with lush grasses and crops, farms with windmills on the horizon, and horses sharing the streets with you in an old-fashioned way. Overnight in Lancaster.

Day 7 in another world

Today you will drive through Pennsylvania Dutch County, where Amish families have settled. They are descendants of German settlers who live according to ancient tradition without electricity, telephones or cars. As part of your premium tour, take a look behind the scenes of this tradition. You will experience a 25-minute guided tour of one of the houses, a 90-minute tour by backroad bus and a self-directed tour of the 12-hectare farm with schoolhouse, barn and forge. On the way, it is also recommended to visit Georgetown. In the evening you will reach the state capital Washington D.C, with famous landmarks and varied neighborhoods. Overnight in Washington DC

Day 8 - Bicycle along impressive monuments

As part of your private bicycle tour, explore the landmarks from the time of Jefferson, Washington and Adams (difficulty = easy). These landmarks are all relatively close together and can be easily reached by bicycle. They are witnesses to the connection between the new republic and the classical architectural styles of Athens and Rome. Together with your guide, you will travel through the neighborhood where the embassies are located and bike to major landmarks such as the White House, FBI Building, National Archives, Capitol, U.S. Senate, Lincoln Memorial and Washington Memorial. You should also pay a visit to Arlington Cemetery – it is the Kennedy family’s final resting place. Overnight in Washington DC

Day 9: Travel to Philadelphia

From Washington, travel to Philadelphia today via Maryland and Delaware. Philadelphia is one of the most historically important cities in the United States, because it is considered America's cradle of freedom and democracy. It was founded in 1682 by William Penn. Here Congress announced the Declaration of Independence and the Constitution of the United States was also published here. This vibrant city with its historic center combines modern skyscrapers with cobblestone streets and conveys a unique atmosphere with the charm of 18/19. century and the developments of the 20th century. Overnight in Philadelphia.

10th day treats at the famous Italian market Phillys

Before you head back to the starting point of your trip, New York City, you will experience a tour in the morning at the oldest and largest open-air market in the country – for foodies. Try all kinds of snacks, fill your shopping bag with cheese, chocolates, delicacies and baked goods and get to know the many merchants who have shaped the character of the city for generations. You’ll start the tour on the ever-colorful South Street, cruising through the Bella Vista district, and let yourself be attracted by the sounds and scents of the famous Italian market. After leaving the market, you will drive to various Mexican shops. If this area sounds familiar to you, it’s probably because Rocky ran through the streets here during one of his famous training runs. Nearby you will also find a selection of Asian shops with flavors and sights that will delight the senses. Your culinary experience will round off with a cheese steak typical of Philly. Then continue on to New York. Overnight in New York City.

11th day of travel home

Even the most beautiful journey comes to an end and so it is today to say goodbye to the impressive eastern triangle. Although your holiday is now over, the precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories of your trip will accompany you forever. Look forward to letting your friends and family share your experiences in the distance at home.

Enjoyment journey through Mexico

Mexico Culinary Circles: Enjoy the culinary sides of Mexico on a unique individual tour with América Special Tours. Ask now!

Mexican cuisine is varied, rich and complex. In this country, the culinary world of Mexico is often confused with Tex-Mex cuisine and so we usually only think of corn and red beans. As you will experience on this culinary journey, Mexico’s cuisine is far more diverse. For example, did you know that without Mexico we would be a lot poorer in culinary terms? Because what would we be without chili, tomatoes, vanilla and chocolate? In order to highlight the culinary techniques and customs of the cuisine, which consists of countless salsas, tacos, enchiladas, mole poblano and guacamole, it was declared in 2010 by UNESCO as an intangible world heritage site worthy of special protection. Be curious about a journey that takes you through colonial Mexico from the capital to the Pacific coast. Enjoy the many culinary impressions that the journey with tacos, tequila ice cream, corn bags, cheese and wine has in store for you.

Day 1 Bienvenidos a Mexico!

Your enjoyment trip in Mexico begins with your arrival in the Mexican capital of the same name, where you will already be expected at the airport by your German-speaking tour guide and chauffeur. Mexico City is located in one of the most important marshes of pre-Hispanic Mexico and is known as the “City of Palaces”. In the central highlands, at 2,240 meters above sea level - an unusual altitude for such a large city - the whole climate is mild almost all year round. Before you set off for your first exploration tours in this metropolis of millions tomorrow, enjoy the hours after your arrival at your leisure. Overnight in Mexico City.

2nd day World metropolis and tacos

After a leisurely breakfast, your tour of the city begins in the morning. After the long flight yesterday, you are sure to look forward to first exploring the historic center of the city on foot. You will get to know the huge Zócalo, the famous Metropolitana Cathedral and the Government Palace with its impressive wall paintings by Diego Rivera. We round off your excursion with an interesting visit to the anthropological museum, which is located on the edge of the beautiful Chapultepec Park. Time for relaxation and a lunch (not included) is scheduled for you. In the evening you finally immerse yourself in the nocturnal culinary world of the city. In total, you will visit three traditional, rustic taco stands or small restaurants, so-called “taquerias”, where you can taste various tacos and other typical specialties. You will be amazed by the variety of spices and the intensity of the taste. They come into direct contact with the local population, who frolic with their families at the stands until the early hours of the morning. It is not for nothing that Mexican cuisine has been named a UNESCO World Heritage Site. To conclude, of course, the missing - Mezcal, the big brother of the tequilas. Your tour ends late in the evening and you will then be taken back to your hotel. Overnight in Mexico City.

Day 3 Teotihuacan – City of the Gods

Today, with the visit of Teotihuacán, a real highlight awaits you! After breakfast, first visit the famous Basílica de Guadalupe with the image of the Virgin of Guadalupe. It has been the holiest place in Mexico since colonial times and is the most important pilgrimage church in the country. Afterwards you will go for the first time on the traces of the indigenous peoples of Mexico. Drive to the city of the gods Teotihuacán, located 50 kilometers northeast of Mexico City. It is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and, at 22 km2, the largest ruined city in Latin America, with most of it still unexplored and not yet excavated. You should not miss a climb to the Sun or Moon Pyramid, because from above you will enjoy a unique view over the City of the Gods and get an impression of its actual extent. Afterwards you will go back to your hotel, where you can arrange the evening according to your taste. Overnight in Mexico City.

4th Day Whispering Markets and Street Food

After yesterday dedicated itself to the traces of the indigenous peoples of Mexico, today you will discover the culinary secrets of the historic center of Mexico City, which only real gourmets know. To welcome you try "Antojitos Oaxaqueños", typical specialties from Oaxaca. During the following walk, you will experience the wonderful flavours of the cuisine of different ethnic groups in Mexico. Your stops include the antique and oldest candy shop in Mexico and the best seafood status of the historic center, as well as one of the most traditional cantinas in the city. At the delicacy market San Juan you can taste many exotic treats. The evening is at your disposal. Overnight in Mexico City.

Day 5 Magical Powers at the Monolith Peña de Bernal

Today you say goodbye to the world metropolis and first go to Bernal. In the small colonial village, you will climb the 288-meter-high monolith Peña de Bernal, which is the third largest in the world after Ayers Rock and the Sugar Loaf. They say he has magical powers and gives a long life and the fabulous view over the Sierra Querétaros compensates for every effort. Afterwards, you will have time to get to know Bernal extensively and taste Bernal’s famous sweets and homemade gorditas (maistas with any filling). However, the state of Querétaro is not only known for its colonial beauty and vibrant history. And so today you leave the city and embark on the surrounding wine & cheese route. Querétaro has gained notoriety for its delicious Mexican and European cheeses in recent years and is also the second largest wine producer in Mexico. In the afternoon you will head to your last destination, the “Puerta del Lobo” winery, where you will also stay today. After a small walking tour, relax with an extensive wine tasting and a delicious dinner. Overnight in Querétaro.

Day 6 Opale, Wine and Cheese

After a leisurely breakfast at the winery, make your way to an opal mine. To get there, an adventurous ride in the Jeep is necessary. They descend into the mine to observe the mining process and to beat an opal themselves. Afterwards you will visit the workshop where the valuable stone is processed. Your next stop is the “Rancho Santa Marina” cheese factory, which produces goat cheese in many variants. Here you look behind the scenes of cheese production and enjoy a small cheese and wine tasting. Strengthened, continue to San Miguel de Allende. Overnight in San Miguel de Allende.

7th day Capital of Culture 2019 – San Miguel de Allende

San Miguel de Allende is known for numerous art galleries, breathtaking rooftop terraces, outstanding restaurants and exclusive boutique hotels. Not least because of the good quality of life and the pleasant climate, it is the destination number 1 Canadian and American immigrants. The beautiful colonial city with its unique Gothic-style cathedral became the American Capital of Culture through the “International Bureau of Capitals of Culture” in Barcelona. 2019 explained. After a leisurely breakfast you will enjoy a detailed guided tour of the city. Afterwards you will continue to Guanajuato, which with its tangle of streets and alleys is Mexico’s most beautiful mining city. You can use the afternoon to explore Guanajuato on your own. Overnight in Guanajuato.

Day 8 Guadalajara - Most Mexican of all Mexican cities

After breakfast, take an extensive walking tour of Guanajuato before continuing to Guadalajara. The capital of the state of Jalisco and Mexico’s second largest city is known for its typical and still preserved Mexican traditions, its mariachi music and, of course, the tequila. Here you will be driven to your hotel in the middle of the historic center and enjoy a guided tour in the afternoon in the “most Mexican of all Mexican cities”. Overnight in Guadalajara.

Day 9 Pueblo Mágico – a magical village

Today we will go to Tlaquepaque after breakfast. The beautiful and almost unspeakable village is one of the colorful and lively Pueblos Magicos of Mexico. So-called magical villages. And it truly enchants with its colorful houses and impressive handicraft, which can be found everywhere. You stroll through the streets and discover this dreamy place. You’ll also sample the culinary highlights like tequila ice cream before heading back to the hotel in the late afternoon. Overnight in Guadalajara.

Day 10 Culinary Bicycle Tour

Today you can conquer the culinary world of Guadalajara not on foot, but by bicycle. After you have picked up your bicycles, the first culinary highlight awaits you – a regional breakfast, typical of the state of Jalisco. Strengthened it then goes through the different districts of the city. Here you get to know the history of the respective districts and explore the typical “tapatio” buildings. Your tour program includes the statue of Jose Clemente Orozco, the Mercado Juarez, the Templo Expiatorio, the Palacio de Gobierno and the Plaza Fundadores. Next stops will take you to traditional restaurants where you will taste “Carne en su Jugo” and the typical dish from Guadalajara “Torta Ahogada”. Throughout the tour you will get information about the well-known districts of Guadalajara as well as the stories of each dish. The tour lasts about three hours and extends over a distance of approx. 14 kilometers. Overnight in Guadalajara.

Day 11 Tequila, Tequila

Before the relaxing part of your trip begins today, make a stop on the way to Puerto Vallarta in an authentic traditionally guided tequila distillery where the tequila is produced organically without chemical additives. Here you will learn everything you need to know about the exquisite drink that represents Mexico worldwide on an exciting guided tour and enjoy a tequila tasting. Afterwards you will get to know the village of the same name and Pueblo Mágico Tequila and then continue on towards the Pacific coast. (journey time approx.) 5 hours. Puerto Vallarta is one of Mexico’s most vibrant and noble resorts. Here you can take advantage of the rest of the day to relax comfortably on the beach or explore the colonial city center on your own. Overnight in Puerto Vallarta.

Day 12 Relaxation on the Pacific Coast

Today is at your disposal. Relax on the beach, go shopping or take advantage of one of the many opportunities that Puerto Vallarta offers to entertain you. Overnight in Puerto Vallarta.

13th day of free time in Puerto Vallarta

Also today you can arrange to your liking and enjoy the last moments in Mexico at your own pace. Optionally, we are happy to organize a trip to the Sierra Madre: This expedition is intended for all guests who dare to drive deeper into the tropical jungle with special vehicles from Mercedes Benz. After breakfast and a brief briefing on the vehicles, the excursion to the Sierra Madre starts. First, drive through typical Mexican villages and the hinterland of Puerto Vallarta, make some stops and your guide will tell you about the locals, flora and fauna of this region before reaching the heart of the Sierra Madre. Combined with a short hike you will discover the secrets of the Sierra Madre and with good luck you will observe the animals of the West Coast living there. But of course the journey does not end here in the jungle. It continues with an exciting ride through the unique landscape until you have arrived at a beautiful beach in Nayarit. The stay is connected with a delicious picnic, there is enough time to refresh and relax in the sea. Overnight in Puerto Vallarta.

14th day of travel home

Even the most beautiful trip comes to an end and so it is today to say goodbye to Mexico. Even if your holiday is over, the precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories of your pleasure trip through the country will accompany you forever. Look forward to letting your friends and family share your experiences in the distance at home. For your return flight, your English-speaking driver will take you to Puerto Vallarta Airport.

Argentina Travel - Unique Retreats in the Northeast

Experience the unique retreats in northeast Argentina on an individually planned Argentina trip from the expert. Let's get some advice now!

Argentina is different... Experience this fascinating country away from the classic tourist trails and treat yourself to a short break in the northeast of the country. A few selected stations, exceptional hotels, authentic experiences and an exciting flora and fauna await you. After arriving in this unique land of contrasts, explore Buenos Aires on a city tour and take your first tango steps in the evening while visiting a milonga. Leave the bustling capital behind you and recharge while being spoiled on a typical Gaucho-Estancia. A unique highlight in the northeast of the country are the Iguazu waterfalls, which you must not miss in any case. According to your own taste, you will experience the area in and around Iguazu thanks to the special service of the Awasi Hotel. At the end of the trip, you will enjoy traditionally warm Argentine hospitality at the Iberá Nature Reserve. The region is still considered a true insider tip, look forward to exciting activities and / or pure deceleration in the Hotel Puerto Valle.

Day 1: Your journey begins!

Your journey through Argentina begins in the capital Buenos Aires, which also bears the nickname of the Paris of South America. Numerous immigrants from Europe and the rest of the world have helped shape the cityscape over the years and shaped the tango out of a merger of different cultural influences. Buenos Aires is surprisingly European in many corners, including the largest Italian enclave outside Italy. Upon arrival at the airport, you will be welcomed by your guide and taken to the hotel by your chauffeur. So you can already learn first anecdotes about this fascinating city on the journey. Arrive in peace and enjoy your first evening in the modern Puerto Madero district, on the banks of the Rio de la Plata. Overnight in Buenos Aires.

Day 2 Modernity, Tradition and Tango in Buenos Aires

The cosmopolitan ambience and the characteristics of the individual Barrios (Neighborhood) fascinates every visitor and you should not miss a city tour. You will start at the famous Avenida de Mayo, known as the widest street in the world. You will pass the Teatro Colon and stop at Casa Rosada and the Cathedral before visiting the colorful La Boca district. Your tour ends with the northern neighbourhoods of Palermo and Recoleta. In the cemetery of Recoleta you can visit the tomb of Eva Duarte, better known as Eva Peron ("Evita"), as well as numerous architectural jewels, the mausoleums and pantheons. In the evening, immerse yourself in the vibrant tango scene of the capital, as an authentic tango experience awaits you within the framework of a milonga. Before you can dance tango together with the locals, your English-speaking tango teacher will show you the most important steps. During the dance class, you will also receive an introduction to the history, course and norms of tango so that you can understand, see and feel what tango is all about. After the dance evening, you will be taken back to your hotel. Overnight in Buenos Aires.

3rd day Get out of town ... into the pampa

Today you will leave the metropolis of Buenos Aires, because you can expect unforgettable days in the Pampa. Your private chauffeur will take you to San Antonio de Areco, about two hours away, the prettiest place in Argentina’s Pampa. Here you will be welcomed by a peaceful atmosphere and picturesque, colonial streets. The place was founded in the early 18th century and is very proud of its Gaucho traditions, which are still maintained today, and of course also famous for artistic saddlery crafts. In the heart of the Pampa and near San Antonio de Areco lies one of the oldest Estancias in Argentina: The Estancia La Bamba de Areco, which has been renovated with great care. The traditions of the Argentine gauchos are also lived on in La Bamba de Areco, from the traditional asado to Argentine bocchia play and mate tastings to polo and horse whispering workshops, guests at the Estancia La Bamba de Areco can immerse themselves in the world of gauchos, Argentine cattle herders and folklore customs. Overnight in San Antonio de Areco.

Day 4 – 5 Unforgettable Days in the Argentine Pampa

During the following two days, enjoy the warm ambience in this historic and traditional hotel. As a member of the Relais & Chateaux Hotel Association, Estancia is not only a hotel with exquisite cuisine and refined service but also a timeless place to relax. Gaucho traditions and horse racing also contribute to the magic of La Bamba. Overnight in San Antonio de Areco.

Day 6 Indescribable Natural Wonder – the Iguazu Waterfalls

Today your round trip will take you by domestic flight to the border of Argentina, Brazil and Paraguay, where you will visit the unique waterfalls of Iguazu. They consist of 20 larger and 255 smaller waterfalls on an extent of about 2.7 km. The falls are separated by several larger and smaller islands and are located in both the Argentine National Park Iguazú and the Brazilian National Park Iguaçu. Upon arrival, you will be driven to your accommodation, the exclusive Hotel Awasi (Relais & Châteaux). In the middle of the Atlantic Rainforest, there are 14 stylishly furnished villas that guarantee a high level of privacy through private terraces with their own plunge pool. The service of Awasi is also unique, as each villa has a private English-speaking guide and its own SUV for excursions into the area. Experience and discover the area in and around Iguazu at your own pace and according to your personal interests. Overnight in Puerto Iguazu.

7th – 8th day Feeling freedom, breathing freedom

During the following two days you decide together with your guide which program items should be on your list. You can choose between full day trips with lunch (from 4 to 6 hours) or two half-day trips (between 2 and 5 hours; lunch at the hotel). Of course, the visit to the Iguazu National Park should not be missed – a unique advantage of the Awasi Hotel: hotel guests are allowed to enter the national park half an hour before official admission. Other excursion options include visiting locals of the Mbya Guarani, kayaking or a boat trip on the Alto Parana River, a visit to the Yacu-i reserve and hikes in the Urugua-i Provincial Park. Overnight in Puerto Iguazu.

9th day Onward journey to the Iberá Nature Reserve

Impressive days in Buenos Aires, in the Pampa and at the rushing waterfalls of Iguazu are behind you – finally, a destination that is still relatively unknown to most travelers: the Iberá Nature Reserve. A private transfer takes you from Puerto Iguazu to the next days hotel, Puerto Valle. Along the way, stop at the ruins of San Ignacio, a former Jesuit missionary station. The Iberá Nature Reserve contains one of the largest drinking water reserves on the entire continent and is also the largest nature reserve in Argentina. Its total area is 1.3 million hectares and houses animal species that have been declared a natural heritage of the province of Corrientes: the South American otter, the mane wolf and pampas and swamp deer. Not to be forgotten are the more than 350 different bird species. On the northeastern border of the nature reserve lies Puerto Valle, where a private access is the ideal starting point to discover the wondrous world of wetlands. The small and very fine hotel offers its guests traditionally warm Argentine hospitality and many day trips exploring the fascinating wildlife of the region. Overnight in Ituzaingó

Day 10 – 12 Unique Retreat – Puerto Valle

Enjoy the activities of your accommodation during the following days or let the soul dangle by the pool. A park of over 14 hectares, designed by landscape architect Carlos Thays, offers habitat for native and exotic species and invites you to relax. From the swimming pool you overlook the huge river, tanning beds attract sunbathing, while the shadow and a light breeze roam the armchairs under a Palapa Palm roof. You can also actively arrange your days and participate from one of the activities offered by the property. Take a motorboat through the various waterways of the Paraná River, enjoy a silent adventure on a kayak ride, experience the surroundings of the Estancia on an easy ride through the pine and eucalyptus forests or visit the extraordinary flora and fauna of the Iberá marshes. Overnight in Ituzaingó.

Day 13 Visiting Argentina

Today it is time to say goodbye to your little time out in the Iberrá Nature Reserve, because it goes by domestic flight back to the capital of the country. For your last evening on Argentinian soil, you can still look forward to a special experience, because you enjoy a dinner in a homey atmosphere – as a guest with Argentinians. A warm embrace on the doorstep, Hello, welcome, walked in. Neither guest nor host has seen each other before, so are the rules of the game Home Hosted DinnerEnjoying a privately prepared dinner is a unique opportunity to get to know the people of Argentina even more closely. You will be served a typical Argentinean 3-course menu, accompanied by the wines of the country, a real taste of the local kitchen. But food and drink are not alone. Who sits opposite me, how do I deal with him, what should I tell about myself? The hosts have experience - for the guests it is only a prerequisite that their own English is sufficient for a conversation with the hosts. After an eventful evening, you will be greeted warmly - out into a country that has moved a little closer to you. Overnight in Buenos Aires.

Day 14 Farewell to the Magical Northeast

Today, say goodbye to Argentina: you will be taken to the airport and go home from Buenos Aires. Even if your journey is over, the precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories will accompany you forever. Look forward to sharing your friends and family at home with your experiences in the far northeast of Argentina.

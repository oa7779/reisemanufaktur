La Pura Vida — A Costa Rica Road Trip

Costa Rica car rental trip with Latin America specialist América Special Tours. Individual and customized in the heart of Munich. Now ask me.

19 days

Your trip idea

A sample itinerary—tailored to you

  • Home
  • Costa Rica
  • Travel inspiration from Costa Rica

On this journey through Costa Rica, you will find a magnificent natural backdrop that will take your breath away. Overwhelming panoramic views and adventurous ventures will make your vacation an experience you’ll never forget. Marvel at ancient volcanic landscapes, untouched rainforests, raging waterfalls and steaming geysers. Get to know the exotic wildlife of Costa Rica - Pumas, Cebu cattle, nose bears, sloths, roar monkeys, iguanas and so many more are at home here. Take advantage of the unique opportunity and wander to the indigenous people of the country. Kayaking or snorkelling, playing a game of golf, learning all about making coffee, taking a night hike through the cloud forest and looking for whales and dolphins. This trip offers you countless possibilities. Idyllic hotels invite you to relax in complete privacy and enjoy the beautiful beaches of the country.

Trip Information

Itinerary

19 Days San José – Pacuare – Arenal Volcano National Park – Rincón de la Vieja National Park – Peninsula Papagayo – Monteverde – Uvita – Puerto Jiménez – San José

Your Trip Highlights

  • Private, German-speaking tour guide and own chauffeur
  • Personal contact before, during and after your trip
  • Exclusive and extraordinary hotels in a breathtaking nature
  • Adventure Rafting to Pacuare Lodge
  • Canopy or canyoning tours at Pacuare Lodge
  • Hike to magical waterfalls in the cloud forest of Monteverde
  • Relaxation in the thermal water sources of the majestic Arenal Volcano
  • Mysterious Night Walk through the Cloud Forest
  • Exclusive coffee tasting

Your journey through Costa Rica begins in the capital San José, which is located at 1,150 meters above sea level. While in the north several volcanoes rise above the city, in the south are the rugged Talamanca mountains. Your German-speaking guide will welcome you at the airport after passport control to discuss the coming days with you. You will then be chauffeured to your hotel in the city center. Enjoy the charming Grano de Oro, which exudes a particularly cozy atmosphere in Victorian style with only 40 rooms. On the hotel roof, an oasis awaits you to relax with a unique view of the city. After recovering from the long journey, explore San José. Visit the Art Museum located in La Sabana Park or stroll through the Plaza de Cultura, where you can visit the National Theatre and the Gold Museum. Overnight in San José.

Today you leave early in the morning for your lodge, which is located directly in the wilderness on the banks of the Pacuare River. On the way, make a stopover to enjoy a delicious breakfast. You decide for yourself whether you want to reach the lodge as part of a small rafting tour or whether you want to arrive by land. One thing is for sure, the Pacuare River offers one of the best rafting experiences in Latin America and impresses with steep gorges and raging waterfalls. Pacuare Lodge offers the highest and most luxurious comfort in the middle of the unspoilt rainforest, although the lodge hardly needs electricity. In the afternoon, you can finally let yourself be sprinkled by the sounds of the jungle and the sounds of the river. Take a walk to the nearby waterfall and refresh yourself there. Later you will enjoy a delicious dinner in an unforgettable ambience. Overnight in Pacuare.

Today begins for you with a breakfast of a special kind, high above the earth in the so-called “nest” of the lodge. After being brought there with special equipment, enjoy a breathtaking view during your breakfast. Then you decide spontaneously what you want to do. The hike to the Cabecar tribe, the largest group of indigenous people in Costa Rica, will particularly fascinate you. Although the paths to the Indians are arduous, and these strangers are more restrained – you will have a unique experience. If you are more into an adrenaline kick, you can explore the in-house treetop trail. A quieter canoe trip would be another option. Or you can just make yourself comfortable in one of the hammocks and let the day pass by. Overnight in Pacuare.

After breakfast, you will continue to travel. As desired, you raft to Siquirres or are taken by land to the town. Once there, a hearty lunch awaits you. On site, showers and changing rooms are also available for refreshing. Then take your rental car. After completing the formalities with the representative of the car rental company, travel via Saroiqui, Aguas Zarcas and Muelle to La Fortuna, the place at the foot of the impressive Arenal volcano. In good weather, you can already marvel at the almost perfect cone of the volcano from some distance, which depicts the impressive backdrop with the lush rainforest. Overnight at Arenal Volcano National Park.

Today you are spoiled for choice: Would you like to experience a guided hiking tour over suspension bridges? Or do you prefer a boat tour on the Peñas Blancas River, where you can admire magnificent landscapes full of plants, butterflies, beautiful birds and graceful lizards? After your excursion, head to La Fortuna Waterfall and the colorful town of La Fortuna. In the afternoon you will experience another guided hiking tour through the Arenal National Park. After so many adventures, you can expect some relaxing hours in the hot springs – pure bathing pleasure! Let the impressions gained so far be reviewed in peace. The conclusion of this beautiful day will be crowned by a dinner at the restaurant of the thermal springs before returning to your hotel. Overnight at Arenal Volcano National Park.

Your Costa Rica round trip continues today and takes you along the beautiful Arenalsee. During your journey, you will pass through the towns of Nuveo Arenal, Tilaran and Canas. In the immediate vicinity of the town of Canas you have the option to visit the animal reception station "Las Pumas". Meet native big cats of Costa Rica, such as the Ocelot, Pumas and weasel cats – with a little luck even a Jaguar. After your visit to the cat world, your journey continues on the Pan-American Highway, which you follow towards the north, where livestock farming dominates the landscape. In the long pastures you will see Cebu cattle typical of the region, because the province of Guanacaste is the stronghold of cattle drivers. After passing the provincial capital Liberia, the streetscape changes and you turn off on a natural road. The bumpy but varied ride will take you to your Hotel Borinquen. The hotel is located directly on the slopes of the Rincón de la Vieja volcano and has a natural steam bath, as well as mineral sludge, which is ideally suited for an extended sludge bath. Enjoy the unique view from the balcony of your Junior Suite. Overnight in the Rincón de la Vieja National Park.

Today, you can enjoy an exploration tour of the Rincón de la Vieja National Park. In addition to the diverse flora and fauna, there are also various volcanic phenomena in the center. In the middle of the forest, you will encounter bubbling mud holes, steaming geysers and sulfur-containing hot water streams during your tour. Back at the hotel, the afternoon is at your disposal. Take a special ride on horseback or let yourself be pampered in the hotel spa “Anáhuac”. In the Native American language, Bribri means “surrounding water” – the spa was built directly over a crystal clear creek. Overnight in the Rincón de la Vieja National Park.

After breakfast, you will leave Rincón de la Vieja National Park and head to the country’s northern Pacific coast. Your destination is Casa Chameleon Las Catalinas, located on the peninsula of Papagayo, which brings everything you would expect from a successful beach holiday in Costa Rica. The extraordinary architecture of the hotel offers almost cocoonically cozy retreats and pure privacy. After arriving at the hotel, enjoy a tropical cocktail in the infinity pool. The next two days are at your disposal: try your hand in kayaking, stand-up paddling or snorkeling. Or use your rental car and take a trip to the surrounding national parks. Take advantage of the days to let your soul dangle on the beach and indulge in the hotel’s spa area. Overnight at Peninsula Papagayo.

After the extensive recovery period on the Pacific coast, make your way to the mountains of Costa Rica. You will drive across the Panamericana along a hill ridge that will impress you with breathtaking panoramas. You will reach the small town of Santa Elena, which is the starting point for excursions in the Monteverde region. Your hotel is nestled outside the city in the mountain tops of Monteverde Nebelwald, offering you unique experiences full of natural wonders and elegance. Cover your room with a panoramic view of the mountains of the cloud forest and the Gulf of Nicoya. At sunset you start your night hike and get to know the cloud forest from an extraordinary side, because at this time the animals are particularly active. While all day-active creatures are in search of a place to sleep, the nocturnal species are slowly waking up. Most often sloths, nose bears, agunis, tree stitchers, gray foxes, tarantulas, frogs, some insect species and numerous bird species that sleep quietly in the branches. Overnight in Monteverde.

Coffee is indispensable to Costa Rica, also in Monteverde, where high-quality beans are grown and extracted. That’s why this morning you go on a tour where you learn everything about coffee, from cultivation to processing and roasting. Your guide will tell you what is important when sorting the beans and of course you have the opportunity to test different varieties at the end. The coffee tour reveals numerous details about the history of Costa Rica and from now on, the magnificent scent of a cup of freshly brewed coffee will remind you of Costa Rica – Promised! The afternoon is at your disposal. Take the time to hike in one of the fog forest reserves, experience a bat tour or take a tour of the suspension bridges. Do you want to take it easy? Then listen to the sounds of the rainforest in your hotel. Overnight in Monteverde.

After a refreshing breakfast, you will embark on the journey from Monteverde to the central Pacific coast today. Enjoy the views along the coastal road and be excited about the different regions you pass today. If you want to make a stopover, we recommend the Carara National Park. The park is home to many mammals, such as large ant bears, four-eyed polio rats, roar monkeys and raccoons. In addition to crocodiles, there are also basilisks and iguanas in this area, as well as an extensive bird life. Represent your feet a little while on a small walk or walk and then head to your hotel in the extra class, the Kura Design Villas. The boutique hotel with only six villas is a symbiosis of nature and art, surrounded by a uniquely tropical and authentic atmosphere. You can expect fantastic views and absolute privacy, an outdoor pool, a bar and a restaurant. Overnight in Uvita.

Today is at your disposal. Relax in your dream hotel, take long walks on the beach or take a boat trip to the sea to look for whales and dolphins. Your hotel also offers optional diving trips and numerous other excursions into the area. Overnight in Uvita.

Today you will make your way to Puerto Jiménez. Enjoy the last ride along the coast before dropping off your rental car in the city. Afterwards, a transfer service will take you to the Lapa Rios. In the afternoon you will have enough time to explore the surroundings of your hotel and be inspired by the natural charm. The 17 luxurious bungalows are located in the middle of a private section of rainforest on three ridges. Your bungalow is open and offers breathtaking views of the Pacific Ocean. Turn off in the overwhelming nature of the rainforest. Relax in the swimming pool or on the beach, no wishes remain open here. Overnight in Puerto Jiménez.

The next two days are at your disposal. Start the day with breakfast with a breathtaking view of Golfo Dulce. Choose between different walks and tours, depending on the requirements and difficulty. You can take a walk to the wild waterfalls, take a hike in the rainforest or enjoy bird watching at sunrise or sunset. A spectacular night hike is also available. Overnight in Puerto Jiménez.

After a last relaxing breakfast in the rainforest, the hotel shuttle will take you to the Puerto Jiménez air slope in about 30 minutes. From there, fly back to San José, where your chauffeur awaits you at the airport and then return to your city hotel. The rest of the day is at your disposal – explore the nearby city center on your own whim. Overnight in San José.

Today it is time to say goodbye to a fascinating country. Your chauffeur will pick you up at your hotel and take you to the airport from where you board your flight home. You will be accompanied by beautiful impressions, which will surely sweeten your everyday life often. You will begin your return journey, with many memories in your luggage, from which you will live for a long time.

Uruguay, Argentina and Brazil Tour - Everything in the River

Travel Argentina, Brazil and Uruguay on your tailor-made Latin America tour. Let the expert América Special Tours advise you personally.

Experience a varied journey through South America that takes you from Uruguay to Argentina to Brazil, bringing together some of the most fascinating cities and natural landscapes on the continent. Start in Montevideo, the charming capital of Uruguay, where colonial architecture, vibrant squares and relaxed coastal atmosphere meet. Discover the elegant seaside resorts of Piriápolis and Punta del Este with their sophisticated charisma and enjoy the unique backdrop of Casapueblo. Continue to the historic Colonia del Sacramento with its cobbled alleys before reaching the vibrant metropolis of Buenos Aires. Immerse yourself in the cultural heart of Argentina, experience traditional cafés such as the famous Café Tortoni and be enchanted by the passion of an authentic tango evening. Another highlight awaits you at the impressive Iguassú waterfalls, where you will explore both the Argentine and Brazilian sides of this tremendous natural wonder. To culminate, the trip will take you to Rio de Janeiro, one of the most spectacular cities in the world. Situated between lush mountains and the Atlantic Ocean, attractions such as the Sugar Loaf and the Corcovado delight with breathtaking views. Enjoy a perfect combination of culture, nature and South American lifestyle.

1st day Montevideo: The perfect start of your South America trip

Welcome to Montevideo, a city that instantly delights with its relaxed atmosphere, eventful history and charming colonial heritage. As soon as you arrive, your journey of discovery begins through one of the most liveable capitals in South America. During a fascinating city tour, you will experience the most beautiful facets of Montevideo up close. You will visit the historic Plaza de la Constitución with its impressive cathedral and the former Cabildo, a significant testimony to the colonial past. Another highlight is the elegant Teatro Solís, which is one of the most important cultural houses on the continent and delights with its impressive architecture. In the heart of the city, the vibrant Plaza Independencia awaits you, a symbolic place where history and present meet. This is where the impressive monument of the national hero José Gervasio Artigas stands, whose mausoleum offers a deep insight into the identity of Uruguay. Montevideo welcomes you with openness, culture and South American lifestyle, an ideal prelude to an unforgettable journey full of impressions and emotions. Overnight in Montevideo.

2nd day Coastal magic and sophisticated lifestyle

Today, an unforgettable full-day excursion along the beautiful Atlantic coast of Uruguay awaits you. You leave Montevideo and first go to the charming seaside resort of Piriápolis, one of the country’s most traditional seaside resorts. Continue to Punta del Este, the most famous and elegant seaside resort in South America. The place is considered a meeting point of the international jetset scene and impresses with a unique mix of nature, architecture and lifestyle. An absolute highlight is a visit to Casapueblo, the iconic white artist residence of painter and sculptor Carlos Páez Vilaró. The extraordinary structure, reminiscent of Mediterranean-Moorish architecture, perches spectacularly on a cliff over the Atlantic Ocean. Afterwards, the route will take you over the famous “wave-curving” Puente Leonel Viera, the iconic bridge that marks the way to the small coastal village of La Barra. In the center of Punta del Este, enjoy free time for a relaxing lunch or a stroll along the elegant promenade. Let yourself be enchanted by the maritime flair, the exclusive boutiques and the lively street cafes. In the afternoon we will return to Montevideo. Overnight in Montevideo.

Day 3 Your free day full of pleasure and possibilities

Enjoy a relaxing free day in Montevideo and design it according to your personal wishes. After the impressive experiences of the past few days, today offers the perfect opportunity to come to rest or to discover the city on your own. Optionally, a special highlight awaits you for connoisseurs: an excursion to a traditional winery in the area. There you will immerse yourself in Uruguayan wine culture, tasting selected wines directly from the producer and enjoying a typical lunch in the middle of the picturesque landscape. Those who like it more actively can discover the natural side of the region on a ride along the endless beaches of Playa Penino and Playa Pascual. The vast coastal landscapes, the calm sea and the fresh breeze create a unique atmosphere for an unforgettable nature experience. Whether you want to take it easy or do something, this free day gives you the freedom to discover Montevideo and the surroundings according to your own ideas and experience special moments individually. Overnight in Montevideo.

Day 4 - Journey through history and river landscapes

After breakfast, your private transfer from the hotel in Montevideo will take you to the historic colonial town of Colonia del Sacramento. The ride in a comfortable vehicle takes you through the quiet, wide countryside of Uruguay and offers first impressions of the relaxed pace of life of the country. Arriving in Colonia del Sacramento, a guided city tour of the picturesque UNESCO World Heritage site awaits you. Cobbled streets, colorful colonial houses and historic squares tell of the Portuguese and Spanish past, giving the city its unique, romantic character. After sightseeing, your private transfer will take you to the port where you will take the ferry over the Río de la Plata. During the crossing, enjoy relaxed views of the water before arriving in Buenos Aires and immersing yourself in the vibrant atmosphere of the Argentine capital. Overnight in Buenos Aires.

5th day Culture, coffee house flair and passionate tango

Experience Buenos Aires on a varied city tour that brings you closer to the most important sights and the special feeling of life of the city. Between magnificent boulevards, historic districts and lively squares, discover the fascinating mix of European heritage and Latin American passion. A special highlight is the visit to the traditional Café Tortoni, the oldest and most famous literary café in the city. Here you can enjoy a cup of coffee and small snacks in a unique, nostalgic ambience that once housed artists, writers and intellectuals. In the evening, an unforgettable experience awaits you: in a joint group, you will be taken to a traditional tango house, where you will enjoy a stylish dinner and then experience one of the most authentic tango shows in the city. Passionate dances, soulful music and the intimate atmosphere make the soul of the tango feel first hand and make this evening a real highlight of your trip. Overnight in Buenos Aires.

Day 6 Free day full of optional discoveries

Enjoy a free day in Buenos Aires and make it according to your personal wishes. The vibrant metropolis offers countless opportunities to experience the special atmosphere of the city on your own. Optionally, you have the opportunity to participate in varied excursions. Discover the green labyrinth of the Tigre Delta, where small islands, waterways and traditional houses form a unique image of nature and life. 

Alternatively, a trip to the countryside will take you to the traditional Estancia Santa Susana, where you will learn about Argentine gaucho life (feasible on Tuesdays, Fridays and Sundays). Those who prefer the urban flair can visit the famous San Telmo antiques fair. Between cobblestones, street artists and antique stands, a particularly charming and lively atmosphere unfolds here. This day offers you the freedom to discover Buenos Aires according to your own rhythm and to collect new impressions individually. Overnight in Buenos Aires.

Day 7 Arrival in the Natural Wonder of South America

After your flight, you will reach the region around the world-famous Iguazú Falls, one of the most impressive natural wonders on earth. Already the arrival in this tropical environment conveys a feeling of adventure and overwhelming natural power. On the same day you will visit the Argentine side of the Iguazú National Park, which offers you particularly close and intense insights into this spectacular ecosystem. On well-developed paths and jetties you move through the middle of the subtropical rainforest and experience the waterfalls from different perspectives. An absolute highlight is the visit to the famous Garganta del Diablo, the so-called “Devil’s Gorge”. Here the water plunges with tremendous force into the depths and creates an impressive soundscape of thundering water and rising fog. Overnight in Puerto Iguazú.

Day 8 The Brazilian Perspective of Waterfalls

Today you will experience the spectacular Iguazú waterfalls from a completely new perspective. On the Brazilian side of the Iguazú National Park, a wide panoramic view opens up that impressively highlights the full size and power of this natural wonder. Well-developed jetties and viewpoints lead you along the gorge and offer breathtaking views of the roaring water masses. Particularly impressive is the view of the countless waterfalls, which pour over a wide rock edge and are framed by dense rainforest. The Brazilian side perfectly complements the experience of the Argentinean perspective and allows you to capture the full dimension of this UNESCO World Heritage site. Fog, rainbows and the deafening sound of water create an almost magical atmosphere. At the end of the visit, you will return to your hotel with unforgettable impressions. Overnight in Puerto Iguazú.

9th day Arrival in one of the most spectacular metropolises in the world

After your flight, you will arrive in Rio de Janeiro, one of the most impressive cities in the world, nestled between green, wooded mountains and the glittering Atlantic coast. As soon as you arrive, you will feel the unique atmosphere of this metropolis, where nature and urban life merge in a fascinating way. Steep slopes, lush vegetation and wide beaches form a backdrop that makes Rio distinctive and gives it its world-famous character. Rio de Janeiro welcomes you with joie de vivre, rhythm and spectacular views. Overnight in Rio de Janeiro.

10th day Panoramas between Corcovado and Sugar Loaf

Experience Rio de Janeiro on a full-day city tour and discover one of the most spectacular cities in the world from different perspectives. Between tropical hills, golden beaches and the vast Atlantic coastline unfolds a cityscape full of energy and impressive contrasts. A highlight of the day is the scenic drive to Corcovado, where you can experience the famous mountain up close and admire the imposing statue of Christ with its breathtaking views of the city and the surrounding nature. The view over the bays, forests and neighbourhoods is one of the most impressive panoramas in the world. Afterwards we continue to the legendary Sugar Loaf. By cable car you can reach the summit and enjoy an unrivalled 360-degree view over the coast, the beaches of Copacabana and Ipanema as well as the striking silhouette of the city. In the evening, there is an optional option to visit a traditional Brazilian samba show. Rhythmic music, colorful costumes and enthralling dances make Brazil’s joie de vivre feelable up close and round off the day perfectly. Overnight in Rio de Janeiro.

Day 11 Farewell to an unforgettable trip

After many days in Rio de Janeiro, your private transfer to the airport will take place for your flight back home. Once again, the impressions of this unique metropolis draw past you. With many unforgettable moments, cultural experiences and scenic highlights in your luggage, you will embark on your journey home and take home the colors, rhythms and emotions of South America.

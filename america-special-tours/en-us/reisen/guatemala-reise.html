Guatemala Travel - In the Highland Markets of Guatemala

Individual Guatemala trip to the highland markets of Guatemala with América Special Tours. Inquire directly here and experience Guatemala for yourself.

Guatemala – the insider tip in Latin America. Here you will find mystical Mayan ruins in Tikal, the charming colonial city of Antigua, majestic volcanoes and the endless lakes Atitlán and Peten-Itzá. Let yourself be captivated by the traditional culture that you experience especially on the colorful markets of the Guatemalan highlands. As befits a real insider tip, you wander through secluded landscapes, drive on quiet lakes and enjoy undisturbed fabulous views of the unique landscape of Guatemala. As a culmination, spend a few days in Belize on the beach of San Pedro.

1st day Bienvenidos a Guatemala

Bienvenidos a Guatemala – Welcome to Guatemala. After your long-haul flight from Munich, you will reach the country’s capital today: Guatemala City. Upon arrival at La Aurora International Airport, your guide and chauffeur will be waiting for you right after the passport and customs control at the airport exit. From here it goes directly to Antigua. The cozy city with cafés and small restaurants is located in the highlands of Guatemala and has been declared a UNESCO World Heritage Site due to its beautiful colonial architecture. Colorful houses adorn the streetscape and a breathtaking panorama with the volcanoes Agua, Acatenango and the still active volcano Fuego surrounds the city. Your chauffeur will take you directly to your magical boutique hotel, where you can relax after a long journey. Overnight in Antigua.

2nd day Antigua

Today, your guide will take you through one of the most historically significant and architecturally unique cities in Central America. Even if it is hard to believe, Antigua is one of the oldest cities on the American continent and former colonial capital of Central America. Founded in 1543 and almost completely destroyed by an earthquake in 1773, it was finally declared a World Heritage Site by UNESCO in 1979. After visiting the bustling main square, where the cathedral and city administration are also located, you will visit the ruins of a monastery. Scattered throughout the city you will find numerous monasteries, churches and convents. Some of these ruins have been restored, some have been converted into hotels, but most of them have been left in ruins due to lack of money. This is followed by a visit to a coffee finca, because the caffeinated beans are the main export of the country. At the Finca you will learn everything about the black gold of Guatemala. Overnight in Antigua.

Day 3 - The Pacaya Volcano

After breakfast, you will be picked up and embark on a ride to the crucifix "Cerro de la Cruz". Arriving at the top, a breathtaking view of Antigua awaits you with the majestic Agua volcano in the background. Enjoy this beautiful scenery and take a few photos to capture this sight forever. Afterwards we will take you to Finca El Pilar, a coffee finca in the private nature reserve, which is located at an altitude of 1,600 to 2,400 meters. The view of the region is indescribable. In addition, many birdwatchers come to the finca, as the environment has a high variety of different birds. If you wish, you can follow the elaborately constructed path of approx. 1.5 km in length, which makes accessible a narrow valley with coffee plantation, wet deciduous forest and dry forest. The total path length is approximately 3 km. A gravel road can be used for the return route. In addition, the surroundings of the finca are excellent for a small bicycle tour. Horse rides are also possible. In the afternoon you will be returned to your hotel. Overnight in Antigua. 

Day 4 San Juan Comalapa Market

Today you will explore the wonderful and authentic Comalapa market. The market is visited by only a few tourists, which gives you the opportunity to get a very good insight into the life of the local population. The market is located in the city of the same name San Juan Comalapa, at an altitude of more than 2,100 meters in the highlands of Guatemala. From Antigua it is about two hours away by car. Over 90% of the inhabitants are indigenous kaqchikel Maya. The best days to visit are on Tuesdays, Fridays and Sundays when the market is in full swing. People buy and sell various products such as flowers, vegetables, fruits, household goods and clothing. After this interesting insight, your journey continues through the highlands to Lake Atitlán. Aldous Huxley called this lake “the most beautiful lake in Latin America”. Fertile hills and three mighty volcanoes give the lake a fairytale atmosphere. These can be enjoyed during an evening stroll on the lake and in the colorful, lively place. Overnight in Santa Catarina Palopó.

Day 5: Lake Atitlán

The unique atmosphere of Lake Atitlán and its surroundings can be experienced on a boat trip. The destination of the boat trip is the village of Santiago Atitlán, located at the foot of the mighty volcanoes and center of the Tzutujíl Maya. The village looks back on a centuries-old Mayan past. Despite the arrival of tourism, this past is still reflected today in tradition, way of life and of course also in life on the streets and markets. Continue to the town of San Antonio Palopó, known for its textile production. To this day, women use the traditional hip loom. Residents continue to make a living by growing onions and anise. After this interesting insight, visit the village of San Juan La Laguna and return to your hotel with your guide. Overnight in Santa Catarina Palopó.

Day 6 - The Chichicastenango Highland Market

Your daily destination is the famous Chichicastenango Highland Market. Chichi, as it is also called by its inhabitants, is particularly well known in two respects: On the one hand, it is still an important supra-regional marketplace today. And on the other hand, a place where pre-Christian, shamanistic rituals are practiced that give the city its very own mysticism. Arriving in Chichicastenango, you will have the opportunity to stroll through the most beautiful and colorful market of the Highland Indians. An incredible variety of country-typical, handcrafted products is offered. A half-hour walk takes you to the hill La Democracia, where the most famous place of sacrifice in the region is located: Pascual Abaj. On the way to Pascual Abaj there are beautiful views of Chichicastenango. Overnight in Santa Catarina Palopó.

Day 7 Guatemala's Largest Vegetable Market

This morning you will first visit the nearby village of Almolonga. This is particularly known for its fine weaving and the largest vegetable market in Guatemala. The enormous and colorful variety will amaze you. Your guide will bring you closer to the different and partly exotic vegetables. And maybe a nice seller invites you to a taste. After a stroll through the village, you will continue with your guide to Zunil, a pretty market place whose economy and townscape is shaped by agriculture. Here you will also visit the traditional market. The village is located in a beautiful valley, framed by steep slopes, overthroned by the Santa Maria volcano. During the drive from Almolonga downhill, there is a wonderful sight of the whitewashed colonial church, which shines from a sea of red-covered houses. Overnight in Santa Catarina Palopó.

Day 8 - The Sololá Market

In the morning, your chauffeur and guide will be waiting for you to go with you to Sololá, where today is market day. The main attraction of Sololá is the Indian market, which spreads along the narrow streets of the village. It is mainly food and everyday necessities that are traded here. Tourists are rarely seen. The originality of the market stands out favourably from the tourist market in Chichicastenango. Many women still wear the traditional clothing developed during the Spanish colonial period, consisting of a wrapper skirt (uq or corte) and a colored embroidered or woven shirt (po't or huipil). The multilayer folded headgear (tzut), on the other hand, has become rare. Sololá, which has a population of around 20,000, is a very traditional town with an indigenous population of over 90 percent. Then continue your journey towards Honduras. Overnight in Copán.

Day 9 The Ruins of Copán

The ruins of Copán, along with Tikal, Chichen Itzá and Uxmal, are among the most outstanding structures that the Maya have ever created. These ruins are located in a beautiful, wide valley surrounded by vast tobacco plantations. Here you will visit the Acropolis, the interesting ballpark, the famous glyph staircase and the Plaza Central with its artistic steles. The volcano-andesit stone in Copán allowed the stonemasons of that time to produce exact glyphs, which are so well preserved today that the epigraphers can trace a 400-year history more accurately than in any other place. In the heyday of Copán lived about 24,000 people. Without a doubt, it is in the first place of all Mayan cities in terms of the multitude of sculptures, altars and steles. Before returning to the hotel, visit the Sculpture Museum in Copán. Afterwards, visit the “Macaw Mountain Bird Park” bird sanctuary, which advocates for endangered bird species and is home to macaws, parrots, toucans and owls, among others. Overnight in Copán.

Day 10 Continue north to Flores

After breakfast, head north to Guatemala today. You will travel east with your guide to Quiriguá, a small archaeological site set in the middle of a huge banana plantation. Its value comes from the tall, gigantic steles of brown sandstone and the so-called ‘zoomorphs’, huge sandstone blocks that are covered all around with relief works. The history of the Mayan site is closely linked to that of Copán. From here, head to the Carretera al Atlántico and visit the Fuerte San Felipe, which is located at the mouth of Lake Izabal to the Dulce River. The task of this historically significant fortress from colonial days was to protect the entrance to the lake and thus to the interior from pirate attacks. From there, your journey continues north, more precisely to Flores. Once there, you will already be expected and brought to your hotel. The magnificent Las Lagunas Boutique Hotel is located in the middle of the Peten Rainforest and houses its own private reserve. Overnight in Flores.

Day 11 The Mayan ruins of La Blanca and Yaxhá

Today you will immerse yourself in the mystical past of Guatemala. Right at the beginning it will be exciting, because the dignified excavation site of La Blanca is still in the middle of the process of discovery. Again and again, new finds cause excitement – even in professional circles. Unlike the famous Mayan ruins of Tikal, La Blanca is visited only by relatively few tourists. Mostly it is researchers who pursue their discoveries here. With each excavation, Guatemala, still today the country of Central America most heavily influenced by Mayan peoples, rediscovers part of its lost identity. Then visit the Maya site Yaxhá. This vast Mayan city is located on a high altitude above the northern shore of the lagoon of the same name. Yaxhá is recommended for those who want to enjoy a little visited ruins in the forest on a beautiful lagoon. Then drive back to the hotel. Overnight in Flores. Little insider tip: Wait until the sunset. With a cool beer or a fruity cocktail in hand, this becomes a romantic experience.

Day 12 Tikal

Today you will visit the famous Mayan site Tikal, which is located in the middle of the tropical rainforest of Petén. This archaeological jewel is one of the most important and impressive Mayan sites, former home to over 100,000 Maya. According to archaeologists, Tikal’s heyday was between 100 and 900 AD. The former urban area should have an area of approx. 125 km2, on the current excavation area of 16 km2 alone, there are more than 3,000 structures. After a guided tour of the most interesting parts of the complex, such as complex “Q”, North and Central Acropolis, Central Square, Temple IV and “Mundo Perdido” you will arrive at the “Restaurante Del Parque” for lunch. After this relaxing break you will visit the Sylvanus G. Morley Museum and then drive back to the hotel. Overnight in Flores.

Day 13 A free day at Lake Peten-Itzá

Today you can enjoy a free day at the beautiful Peten-Itzá Lake. Take advantage of the day to take advantage of your hotel’s magnificent facilities or perhaps plan a trip to the island town of Flores. With its small, colorful houses, the bright cobblestone streets, the cozy cafes, restaurants and bars, as well as the impressive church on the summit of the island, it is usually more than just a starting point to Tikal for its visitors. Overnight in Flores.

Day 14: On the way to Belize

Today you will first visit the Mayan ruins of La Blanca, which is a dignified excavation site, still in the middle of the process. Then continue to the border with Belize: Melchor de Mencos. From there we continue to San Ignacio. From a trip to San Ignacio, in the Mayan country west of Belize, one expects ancient culture, breathtaking landscapes and a bit of adventure. Less often you will find a small full-service luxury hotel, as is the case with the Ka'Ana Boutique Resort & Spa. A hotel that combines the typical tropical Belize with the style of a cosmopolitan city boutique hotel. It is by far the best accommodation in the area, its seventeen rooms are plush but discreet. I'm sure you'll feel comfortable here. Overnight in San Ignacio Town.

15th day program of Ka’Ana Resort

You can use today to take advantage of one or more offers from your resort. Start with a cooking class, where you will be shown what techniques and tastes Maya used. Another highlight is the caves around your lodge, where the Maya performed sacred rituals. The remains of these ceremonies can still be found today. Cave tubing and jungle ziplining perfectly combine adventure and relaxation. Relax after an eventful day in your villa. Overnight in San Ignacio.

Day 16: A Mayan Site and an Island in the Caribbean

Today we continue to the Mayan site Xunantunich, a Mayan site whose settlement stretched from the pre-classic to the end of the classical period. You’ll see one of Belize’s tallest pyramids, 42 meters high, with a gorgeous stucco frieze and some limestone stilts. From there, you will continue to Belize City to the Belize Municipal Airport, from where you will continue to Caye Ambergris. Caye Ambergris is the largest of over 200 islands off the coast of Belize. The first inhabitants of Ambergris Caye were Maya – the oldest traces date back to the Late Pre-Classics. Next to Ambergris Caye, the Belize Barrier Reef is a good starting point for divers and snorkelers. Overnight in San Pedro.

17-20. Day in the Caribbean to let the soul dangle

The next few days are marked by pure relaxation. Which place in this world would be better than the dreamlike Caribbean. Your hotel is simply ideal for processing the impressions of your trip. The Victoria House Hotel is a place of complete relaxation and seclusion, free from the constraints and stressors of everyday life, and known as one of the best resorts in the area. Amidst the gentle palm trees swaying in the wind and the white sandy beach, this resort, with its charisma of tranquility, privacy and casual elegance, offers you all the services of a luxury hotel without the usual formalities. The types of accommodation offered in Victoria House are varied and one of them will surely meet your needs. In the heart of the resort is the impressive swimming pool, surrounded by tall palm trees and overlooking the white sand beach and blue Caribbean sea. Enjoy the coming days. They deserve it. Overnight stay in San Pedro. 
 

21st day home

After just under three weeks, you have got to know the most beautiful highland markets and the fabulous Mayan ruins of Guatemala. You have climbed one of the most active volcanoes in the world, taken a boat tour on the "most beautiful lake in Latin America" and traveled to neighboring countries Honduras and Belize. Now it is time to say goodbye to these beautiful countries. Your chauffeur will take you to the airport on time. From here you start your journey home.

Baja California Sur – Mexico’s Wild Peninsula

12-Day Private Tour of Baja California Sur: Desert Landscapes, Colonial Places & 3-Day Catamaran Cruise in the Gulf of California. Details & Offer.

Itinerary & services

Experience the wonders of Baja California Sur in a particularly versatile way: this journey combines spectacular desert landscapes, colonial mission sites, charming artist cities and some of Mexico’s most impressive marine regions. From the coasts of Los Cabos and Todos Santos to the majestic Sierra de la Giganta to the turquoise bays of the Gulf of California, discover the extraordinary diversity of the peninsula. A special highlight awaits you with a private catamaran adventure lasting several days on board the Mango Wind. Discover remote islands, sheltered coves and pristine beaches, snorkel in one of the most biodiverse marine regions in the world and enjoy relaxing days on the water accompanied by an experienced crew and culinary delights on board. This journey combines nature, adventure, enjoyment and exclusive comfort to create an unforgettable experience in one of the most beautiful regions of Mexico.

1st day Arrival in Baja California Sur

Welcome to Baja California Sur, a region full of contrasts and fascinating nature. Upon arrival in San José del Cabo, you will be personally welcomed and taken to your hotel. Already on the first kilometers you will experience the special atmosphere of the Baja, where endless desert landscapes meet the deep blue sea. Enjoy the first impressions of this unique peninsula and get ready for a journey full of extraordinary nature experiences, authentic encounters and unforgettable moments. Overnight in San José del Cabo.

2nd day Culinary delights & colonial flair in San José del Cabo

Today you will experience San José del Cabo with all your senses. The day begins at the renowned farm-to-table restaurant Acre, a green oasis amidst tropical vegetation. During a cooking class, you take part in a culinary experience together with other guests and prepare local specialties from fresh, regional ingredients under the guidance. In the afternoon, explore the charming historic center of San José del Cabo. Art galleries, small boutiques and colorful streets give the place its special flair. A visit to the historic Mission San José del Cabo Añuití offers exciting insights into the history of the region. Afterwards, there is time to enjoy the relaxed ambience of the coastal town on your own. Overnight in San José del Cabo.

3rd day Giants of the Sea and "Pueblo Mágico" Todos Santos

It awaits you one of the most impressive nature experiences of Baja California Sur. During whale season (from January to the end of March) you will go in search of majestic humpback whales that visit the region’s coastal waters every year. The encounter with these gentle giants in their natural environment is one of the unforgettable highlights of a journey through the Baja. Out-of-season on a boat trip, discover Los Cabos’ most famous landmarks, including the spectacular El Arco rock arch and the impressive coastal landscape at the confluence of the Pacific and Gulf of California. Afterwards, the journey along the scenic Pacific coast leads to Todos Santos, one of the most beautiful Pueblos Mágicos in Mexico. On a walking tour of the charming artist place, explore historic buildings, colorful art galleries and cozy cafés. The unique mix of desert landscape, culture and proximity to the sea gives Todos Santos its very special character. Overnight in Todos Santos.

Day 4 Along the coast to Loreto

Today you will experience the impressive diversity of Baja California Sur on one of the most beautiful itineraries of the peninsula. The journey leads through fascinating desert landscapes, past isolated stretches of coast and spectacular panoramas to Loreto, the first Spanish capital of California. During the whale season (from January to the end of March) there is an opportunity for a special excursion from Puerto Chale, where gray whales can be seen in the protected lagoons of the region. The encounter with these gentle sea giants in their natural environment is a unique nature experience. A highlight of the day is the crossing of the imposing Sierra de la Giganta. Rugged rock formations, deep canyons and the typical vegetation of the Baja create an impressive backdrop, making this region one of Mexico’s most spectacular natural landscapes. At the end of the day you will reach the charming coastal town of Loreto, which delights with its colonial history, relaxed atmosphere and privileged location on the Gulf of California. Overnight in Loreto.

Day 5 In the footsteps of blue whales

From January to the end of March, you will have the opportunity to look for the impressive blue whales in the Gulf of California. The encounter with the largest living creature on our planet in its natural environment is one of the most extraordinary experiences of Baja California Sur. Outside the whale season, discover the original beauty of the hinterland. During a hike through the spectacular Sierra de la Giganta you can expect impressive rock landscapes, hidden gorges and wide views of the unspoiled nature of the region. Alternatively, experience an authentic Baja adventure on a canyon safari on the back of a mule with a visit to a traditional ranch and insights into the peninsula’s rural life. In the afternoon, explore historic Loreto, one of Mexico’s most beautiful Pueblos Mágicos. Stroll through the colonial alleys, visit the famous Mission Nuestra Señora de Loreto and enjoy the relaxed atmosphere of this charming coastal town on the Gulf of California. Overnight in Loreto.

6th day Oase landscapes & the dreamlike Bahía de Concepción

Today you will discover one of the most surprising and beautiful regions of Baja California Sur. Your first stop is the green oasis Mulegé, which stretches like a hidden paradise between palm groves and the barren desert landscapes of the peninsula. Perched high above the valley is the historic Mission Santa Rosalía de Mulegé, from which a magnificent panoramic view of the oasis and surrounding landscapes opens up. Then continue to the spectacular Bahía de Concepción, which is one of the most beautiful stretches of coast in Mexico. Turquoise blue water, white sand beaches and the impressive backdrop of the surrounding mountains create a unique atmosphere. Enjoy the tranquility of this natural idyll, take a relaxing beach walk or simply let the beauty of the Baja affect you. Overnight in Loreto.

Day 7: Loreto to La Paz

Follow the scenic coastal road along the Gulf of California to La Paz, the charming capital of Baja California Sur. The relaxed coastal city captivates with its authentic Mexican flair, turquoise sea and one of the most beautiful waterfronts in the country. Surrounded by a unique natural setting, La Paz combines culture, history and maritime lifestyle in a fascinating way. During a city walk, you will discover the lively Malecón, the famous waterfront of the city. Artful sculptures, palm trees and magnificent views of the Gulf of California invite you to stroll. Afterwards, explore the historic city center with its charming squares, traditional buildings and the Cathedral of Nuestra Señora de La Paz. A visit to the local Mercado Bravo offers interesting insights into the daily life of the locals. La Paz is considered a gateway to one of the most biodiverse marine regions in the world. The Gulf of California, once called the “Aquarium of the World” by Jacques Cousteau, is home to an extraordinary variety of marine life and makes the region a paradise for nature and ocean lovers. Overnight in La Paz.

Day 8 Start of your private catamaran adventure

The next highlight of your trip is your exclusive catamaran adventure in the Gulf of California. At the port of La Paz, board the Mango Wind, a comfortable Leopard 45 sailing catamaran. The Mango Wind can accommodate a maximum of six guests and has three comfortable cabins with private bathroom, hot water shower and air conditioning. On board, an experienced crew of captain, nature guide and onboard cook will take care of your well-being. While sailing along rugged coasts, unspoilt islands and hidden coves, you will enjoy the tranquility and beauty of this unique region. The route is flexibly adapted to your wishes as well as the weather and natural conditions. Whether a first bathing stop in crystal clear water, snorkelling in colorful underwater worlds, kayaking, stand-up paddling or relaxed hours on deck, every moment is marked by freedom and natural enjoyment. The Gulf of California is one of the most biodiverse marine areas on earth. With a bit of luck, watch dolphins, sea lions, sea turtles, rays, whales or even whale sharks in their natural habitat. On board you will be spoiled with freshly prepared delicacies as part of the full board (alcoholic drinks not included). Overnight on board the private catamaran.

9th Day Islands, Bays & Marine Protected Areas

Wake up amidst the fascinating island world of the Gulf of California and enjoy breakfast on deck surrounded by one of Mexico’s most spectacular natural landscapes. Far from everyday life, the beauty of nature today determines the rhythm of the day. Together with your captain, you will discover remote coves, protected islands and paradisiacal beaches that can only be reached by water. Crystal clear water invites you to swim, snorkel or stand-up paddling. Beneath the water surface opens up a fascinating world with colorful flocks of fish, while with a bit of luck sea lions, dolphins or sea turtles accompany your way. Away from the well-known routes you will experience the original Baja in all its diversity: majestic desert landscapes, volcanic islands and the deep blue sea merge into a unique backdrop. Back on board, enjoy the relaxed atmosphere and be pampered by the crew with freshly prepared specialties as the catamaran glides leisurely through the dreamlike island world. Overnight on board the private catamaran.

Day 10 Exclusive Nature Experiences in the Gulf of California

Once again, wake up in the middle of the impressive island world of the Gulf of California, surrounded by glittering waters, unspoilt coastlines and spectacular landscapes. This day is all about relaxation and individual enjoyment of nature. Take the time for a refreshing swim in the crystal clear sea, explore the fascinating underwater world while snorkeling or discover the quiet coves by kayak or stand-up paddleboard. If you like it more relaxed, enjoy the view on deck and let the unique atmosphere of this extraordinary marine region affect you. The route is flexibly adapted to the best conditions and your personal wishes. There are always new opportunities to experience the beauty of the Gulf of California from a very special perspective and to observe the diverse animal and plant world. At the end of this unforgettable sailing adventure, another highlight awaits you: a spectacular sunset over the sea that immerses the islands and coastal landscapes in warm, golden light and sets a perfect end to your time on board. Overnight on board the private catamaran.

Day 11 - From the coast to the Sierra de la Laguna

After disembarking in La Paz, you will leave the coast and immerse yourself once again in the fascinating landscapes of the hinterland. Your first stop will take you to a cactus reserve, where you will get to know the extraordinary plant world of Baja California Sur on a short hike. Huge columnar cactuses, endemic species and the impressive silence of the desert characterize this unique nature experience. Then visit the historic mining town of El Triunfo, which was once one of the most important silver mining centers on the peninsula. During a walk through the charming streets, you will discover historic buildings, the mine museum and the striking chimneys that still bear witness to the former prosperity of the region. Afterwards, the journey continues into the green mountains of the Sierra de la Laguna. In the surroundings of Santiago you will experience a surprisingly different side of the Baja: lush vegetation, fertile oases and a quiet, original atmosphere form a delightful contrast to the desert and coastal landscapes of the past days. Overnight in Santiago.

12th Day Oases, Waterfalls & Farewell to Baja California Sur

At the end of your trip, you will once again experience the surprising diversity of Baja California Sur. In the picturesque Sierra de la Laguna you will visit the natural thermal springs of the region and discover a landscape that is clearly different from the coastal and desert areas of the past days. A special highlight is the hike to the idyllic waterfall Sol de Mayo. Hidden between rocks and lush vegetation, the crystal clear water forms a small oasis in the middle of the impressive mountain landscape. Enjoy the tranquility of nature and let the varied impressions of your journey pass once again. Afterwards, you will be taken to the airport of San José del Cabo for your journey home. Alternatively, you have the option to extend your stay with some relaxing days at the exclusive Four Seasons Resort Costa Palmas directly by the sea.

Costa Rica & Panama — Rainforest Green and Ocean Blue

From bright jungle green to rich ocean blue: Experience 19 days of Pura Vida, species-rich nature & exclusive resorts in Central America.

19 days · 18 days

Your trip idea

A sample itinerary—tailored to you

Are you ready for the adventure of your life? Immerse yourself in the fascinating worlds of Costa Rica and Panama and discover two of the most beautiful countries in Central America. Enjoy tropical rainforests, fascinating wildlife, dreamlike beaches and authentic encounters. Between adventure and relaxation, you will experience the diversity of this unique region while enjoying selected accommodation, unforgettable nature experiences and the warm hospitality of its inhabitants. A journey full of contrasts that you will remember for a long time.

Trip Information

Itinerary

18 Days Santa Barbara de Heredia – Manuel Antonio – Uvita – Osa Peninsula – Boca Chica – Santa Catalina – Panama City

Your Trip Highlights

  • Every trip is unique - designed according to your wishes
  • Safety and flexibility through private tour guide and chauffeur
  • Carefully selected hotels for you
  • Personal contacts before, during and after your trip
  • Tasting on a sustainable coffee plantation in the hills of Heredias
  • Manuel Antonio National Park with unique wildlife and beaches
  • Deceleration on Costa Rica's most beautiful Pacific beaches
  • Observation of monkeys, sloths, toucans and other animal species
  • Stay at the exclusive island resort Isla Palenque off the coast of Panama
  • Snorkeling trip to Isla Coiba
  • Visit to the famous Monkey Islands on the Panama Canal

Your journey begins with a long-haul flight to Costa Rica, the land of the “Pura Vida”. Sit back relaxed and look forward to the unforgettable experiences that lie ahead of you. Upon arrival at San José International Airport, you will already be warmly welcomed by our airport tour guide. At the exit you will receive your personal travel documents before your private transfer takes you to your charming hotel in Santa Bárbara de Heredia. Nestled in the green hills above the central valley, the property offers the perfect place to arrive after arriving and get your first impressions of Costa Rica. Overnight in Santa Bárbara de Heredia.

The day begins with a walk through the fragrant coffee plantations of your accommodation. You will get exciting insights into the cultivation, harvesting and processing of Costa Rica’s famous coffee beans. Of course, a tasting of the aromatic coffee should not be missing. Meanwhile, you will enjoy magnificent views over the green central valley. Then travel on to the Pacific coast. The journey leads through varied landscapes and gives a first impression of the impressive nature of the country. After your arrival, the rest of the day will be at your disposal. Relax on the beach, take a walk along the coast or simply enjoy the relaxed atmosphere. The gentle sea noise and the fresh breeze invite you to arrive and let the beauty of Costa Rica affect you. Overnight in Manuel Antonio.

Welcome to Manuel Antonio, one of the most beautiful and biodiverse national parks in Costa Rica. Today you have the opportunity to explore the park on your own. On well-designed trails, you will wander through tropical rainforest looking for sloths, capuchin monkeys, iguanas, nose bears and numerous exotic bird species. Again and again, spectacular views of the lushly overgrown coast and the deep blue Pacific Ocean open up. Take enough time to enjoy the beautiful beaches of the national park. Don’t forget your swimsuit, as the two beautiful coves invite you to a refreshing cooling in the Pacific Ocean. Showers and toilets are available on site. The unique combination of rainforest, fascinating wildlife and paradisiacal bathing bays makes Manuel Antonio an unforgettable experience. In the evening, numerous restaurants invite you to get to know the diverse Costa Rican cuisine and to end the day relaxed. Overnight in Manuel Antonio.

Today, the focus is on relaxation. Treat yourself to a well-deserved time out and arrive completely in Costa Rica. Your stylish hotel is located directly on two dreamlike beaches and combines luxurious comfort with a unique natural landscape. Spend the day according to your wishes: enjoy the hotel’s fresh, sustainable cuisine, relax at the infinity pool overlooking the Pacific Ocean or allow yourself to be pampered with a soothing spa application. Those who like can also take a walk on the beach and enjoy the tropical atmosphere to the fullest. Overnight in Manuel Antonio.

Continue your journey along the scenic Pacific Coast. The route from Manuel Antonio to Uvita leads through tropical landscapes with lush rainforest, small coastal villages and always spectacular views of the ocean. Arriving in Uvita, one of Costa Rica’s most beautiful coastal areas awaits you. The place is known for its relaxed atmosphere, extensive beaches and the Marino Ballena National Park. At low tide, the famous whale-shaped sandbank can be recognized here, which has given the national park its name. Take advantage of the rest of the day to enjoy the untouched nature, relax on the beach or explore the surroundings on your own. The tranquility and originality of this region make Uvita a perfect place to relax and enjoy the beauty of the Pacific Coast to the fullest. Overnight in Uvita.

Today you set the pace. Enjoy a relaxing day in your stylish hotel and let the special atmosphere of the Pacific Coast affect you. Relax by the pool, listen to the sounds of nature or enjoy the view of the lush green surroundings. Those who want to explore the region can visit the nearby Marino Ballena National Park. Extensive beaches, tropical rainforest and the famous whale-shaped sandbank characterize this unique protected area. With a little luck, even dolphins or seasonally passing whales can be observed off the coast. Leave everyday life behind you and enjoy the relaxed atmosphere of this special place between rainforest and ocean. Overnight in Uvita.

Your journey will take you to the south of Costa Rica on the remote and original Osa Peninsula. During the journey, tropical landscapes, dense rainforests and small villages pass you by and give an impression of the impressive diversity of this part of the country. Upon arrival in Puerto Jiménez, continue your journey to the idyllic El Remanso Rainforest Wildlife Lodge. Deeply embedded in the tropical rainforest, one of Costa Rica’s most biodiverse natural paradises awaits you here. With a little luck, monkeys, toucans, macaws or nose bears can already be observed on the premises. Enjoy the special atmosphere of this retreat, where untouched nature, authentic jungle experience and relaxed relaxation combine in a unique way. Overnight on the Osa Peninsula.

Today, immerse yourself in the fascinating nature of the Osa Peninsula. Together with experienced naturalist guides, explore the trails around El Remanso Rainforest Wildlife Lodge and discover one of the most biodiverse rainforests in the world. With a bit of luck, watch toucans, macaws, monkeys or even a sloth in its natural habitat. Between huge jungle trees, rushing streams and exotic plants, you will experience the original beauty of Costa Rica up close. After the explorations, there is time to enjoy the special atmosphere of the lodge. Let the view wander over the rainforest and listen to the sounds of nature surrounding this unique place. Overnight on the Osa Peninsula.

Today is at your disposal. Enjoy the tranquility and seclusion of your lodge or explore the area on your own. Take a walk to nearby beaches, relax in the middle of the tropical garden or watch the diverse wildlife right from your accommodation. Those who want to become active can choose from various optional activities and discover the nature of the Osa Peninsula from a new perspective. In the evening, the setting sun immerses the rainforest in a warm light and ensures an atmospheric end to another unforgettable day in the south of Costa Rica. Overnight on the Osa Peninsula.

Today you will leave Costa Rica and travel on to Panama. After transfer to Boca Chica, you will transfer by boat to the exclusive island of Isla Palenque. Already on arrival it becomes clear why this place is considered one of the hidden jewels of Panama: lush tropical forest, secluded beaches and the turquoise shimmering waters of the Pacific form the backdrop for the coming days. At the sustainable luxury resort The Resort at Isla Palenque, you can move into your stylish Casita directly on the beach. Enjoy the special atmosphere of this private island and leave everyday life behind. Lonely coves, dense rainforest and the gentle noise of the Pacific create the perfect backdrop for unforgettable days away from everyday life. A true paradise that invites you to slow down and enjoy. Overnight in Isla Palenque.

Welcome to the island paradise. Today you will have time to let the beauty of Isla Palenque feel quite relaxed. Explore the island’s seven private beaches, enjoy long walks along the coast or find one of the hidden coves to let your soul dwindle. The crystal clear waters of the Pacific Ocean and lush tropical vegetation create a unique atmosphere of peace and seclusion. Those who want to actively experience nature can explore the hiking trails of the island and discover exotic plants, colorful birds and other inhabitants of this protected natural paradise. Overnight in Isla Palenque.

Take advantage of another day on this extraordinary private island according to your wishes. Take a kayak or stand-up paddle tour of the mangroves, explore the coastal landscapes on a boat trip or relax on the beach overlooking the Pacific Ocean. With its unique blend of untouched nature, luxurious comfort and absolute privacy, Isla Palenque offers the perfect setting for unforgettable holidays. End the day with a spectacular sunset and enjoy the special magic of this exclusive retreat. Overnight in Isla Palenque.

After breakfast, say goodbye to Isla Palenque and travel on to the relaxed coastal village of Santa Catalina. The ride takes you through the rural regions of Panama, past green hills, small villages and original landscapes. Once you reach your destination, one of the most charming places on the Pacific Coast awaits you, which has retained its authentic character to this day. Santa Catalina is known for its long sandy beaches, spectacular sunsets and relaxed atmosphere. Take advantage of the rest of the day to stroll through the small town, relax by the sea or let the view wander across the Pacific Ocean. At the same time, Santa Catalina is the gateway to the famous Coiba National Park, one of the most important nature reserves in Central America and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Overnight in Santa Catalina.

Today, an unforgettable adventure awaits you in Coiba National Park, a UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of the Pacific’s most impressive marine reserves. By boat you will sail out to the island world of Coiba, whose waters are known for their extraordinary biodiversity. While snorkeling, you will discover colorful shoals of fish, coral formations and, with a bit of luck, sea turtles, dolphins, manta rays or even reef sharks. Not only under water, Coiba delights with its diversity. The islands and coastal sections provide a protected habitat for numerous bird species and other animals. With a bit of luck, during the whale season from July to October even humpback whales cross your path and provide very special moments on the water. After this eventful day, you will return to Santa Catalina and let the impressions of the day end relaxed. Overnight in Santa Catalina.

Today, you will make the day according to your own ideas. Relax on the beautiful beaches of Santa Catalina, enjoy the relaxed atmosphere of this small coastal village or explore the surroundings on your own. Take a walk along the coast, watch the fishing boats in the harbor or simply let the view wander across the Pacific Ocean. Those who want to become active will find numerous opportunities to get to know the natural beauty of the region more closely. Between golden beaches, spectacular sunsets and the deep blue Pacific Ocean, experience Panama from its most relaxed side. Overnight in Santa Catalina.

After breakfast, say goodbye to the beaches of the Pacific Coast and travel on to the vibrant capital of Panama. Your destination is the historic district of Casco Viejo, which is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and delights with its lovingly restored colonial buildings, charming squares and lively alleys. Upon arrival, you will move into your stylish hotel that combines historic charm with modern comfort and exclusive ambience. Take advantage of the rest of the day to stroll through the scenic streets of Casco Viejo, linger in one of the cozy cafes or enjoy views of the impressive Panama City skyline. The fascinating mix of history, culture and modern metropolitan flair makes Panama City one of the most exciting destinations in Central America and the perfect conclusion to your journey through Costa Rica and Panama. Overnight in Panama City.

Today you will discover a completely different side of Panama. In the morning you will drive to the Soberanía National Park, one of the most biodiverse protected areas in the country. During a guided walk through the tropical rainforest you will experience the impressive nature up close. Look for colorful toucans, parrots, monkeys and many more inhabitants of this fascinating ecosystem. Afterwards we will continue to Gamboa, where your boat is already waiting for you. While cruising over the sprawling Lake Gatún, which is part of the famous Panama Canal, you will be lucky to discover monkeys, sloths, crocodiles and numerous waterfowl. A special experience is the encounter with the massive cargo ships that pass you on their way through the Panama Canal. Finally, visit the well-known Monkey Island, where various monkey species live in the wild. The combination of tropical rainforest, species-rich wildlife and the fascinating backdrops of the Panama Canal makes this excursion one of the highlights of your stay in Panama. Overnight in Panama City.

Before you embark on your journey home, get to know Panama City once again from its most beautiful side. On an exciting city tour, discover the fascinating mix of modern metropolis, colonial heritage and tropical nature that makes Panama’s capital so unique. First we go up to Cerro Ancón, the highest point of the city. From here you can enjoy an impressive view of the skyline, the Panama Canal and the bay of Panama. Then head to Amador Causeway, a scenic waterfront with spectacular views of the city’s modern silhouette and the famous Puente de las Américas. Another highlight is a visit to the lively fish market, where you will get an authentic insight into local life. Afterwards, explore the historic old town of Casco Viejo. Finally, the tour will take you along the Cinta Costera, from which you can admire some of the most impressive skyscrapers in Latin America. Then you will be taken to the airport. With unforgettable memories of the tropical rainforests of Costa Rica, the dream beaches of Panama and the warm hospitality of Central America, you will finally start your journey home.

Argentina — Tango, Wine, and Wellness

Argentina Tour with Tango, Wine and Wellness. Let América Special Tours create your individual Argentina trip.

10 days

Your trip idea

Argentina is passionate: it was where tango was once born – a synthesis of the cultures of numerous immigrants who, despite poverty and unemployment, created one of the most elegant dances ever. On this trip, you will get to know Argentina’s passion in full pleasure: dancing tango in Buenos Aires, enjoying the wines of the country and ending your holiday after a ride through vineyards and a barbecue steak by the campfire under the stars.

Trip Information

Itinerary

10 days Buenos Aires – Salta – Cafayate – Uco Valley/Mendoza

Your Trip Highlights

  • Private, German-speaking tour guide and own chauffeur
  • Carefully selected hotels for you
  • The birthplace of tango: dance class and dinner show in Buenos Aires
  • A journey through time through the “Paris of South America”
  • Colonial heritage in the old town of Salta
  • Overland journey with a view: Over breathtaking mountain passes to Cafayate
  • Noble drops: tasting of fine Argentine wines
  • Bizarre rock formations in the “gorge of shells”
  • Luxurious Western Romance Amidst Vines and Mountains

Your journey through Argentina begins in the capital. Upon arrival at the airport, you will be welcomed here by your guide and taken to the hotel by your chauffeur. So you can already learn first anecdotes about this fascinating city on the journey. The world capital of tango has a lot to offer culturally – it is said to have more theater halls than any other place in the world. In many corners, Buenos Aires is also astonishingly European: here is not only the largest Italian enclave outside Italy. Numerous other immigrants from Europe and the rest of the world have also shaped the cityscape over the years. In keeping with the nickname “Paris of South America”, Buenos Aires also attracts the country’s alternative scene today. In the evening, immerse yourself in the lively tango scene of the capital. Your English-speaking tango teacher will take you to an authentic milonga where you can dance tango together with the locals. During the dance course, you will receive an introduction to the history, course and norms of tango. After the dance evening, you will be taken back to your hotel. Overnight in Buenos Aires.

Together with your guide and chauffeur, you will embark on an exploration tour of the Argentine capital. After viewing the numerous highlights, such as Plaza de Mayo, the Casa Rosada government seat, the legendary antique cart at Plaza Dorrego, the colorful district of La Boca, the green district Palermo and the neoclassical cemetery La Recoleta, visit the unique show of Rojo Tango in the evening. El Cabaret is part of the elegant Faena Hotel & Universe in Puerto Madero. Design and decoration are by Philippe Starck and revive the lifestyle of old times. Here you can enjoy a three-course menu at a fascinating tango show with live music and various dance couples, which rounds off this day worthy. Overnight in Buenos Aires.

Today you leave the Argentine capital behind. You will be picked up from your hotel and driven to the airport for onward travel to Salta. Here your next guide is waiting for you. Together you will get to know the capital of the province of the same name, founded in 1582 and affectionately called “La Linda” (the pretty ones). No other Argentinian city offers such an abundance of historical buildings in the colonial baroque period. This cultural richness is framed by a magnificent mountain backdrop. By no means should you miss Salta’s most impressive landmark: Iglesia San Francisco. The streets invite you to stroll, the pretty squares, such as the magnificent Plaza 9 de Julio, are ideal for sitting down and watching the city bustle over a glass of wine. If you are interested in archaeology, we recommend the excellent Museo de arquelogía de alta Montana (admission not included in the price). It tells more about the culture and history of the Inca through a variety of artifacts. Overnight in Salta.

Today’s ride offers you beautiful views: You leave Salta on serpentine-rich roads through green mountainous lands towards the south. You will be happy that you are not at the wheel, but can focus all your attention on observing the magnificent landscape of the Cuesta del Obispo (Bishops’ Abbey). On your way, you will pass “Recta del Tin Tin”, a straight line that is about 19 kilometers long and was scraped into the ground with astonishing perfection in Inca times. Through the Los Cardones National Park with its huge cactus, you can reach the village of Cachi, which – surrounded by majestic mountains – delights with its beautiful colonial architecture. After a walk through the tranquil place, you will continue traveling so that in the afternoon you reach Cafayate, a quiet colonial-style village between two mountain ranges of the Andes, surrounded by vineyards. With about 360 days of sunshine per year, there is a perfect climate for wine growing. Your hotel is idyllic between the vineyards. Overnight in Cafayate.

The vineyards around Cafayate are of course not only beautiful to look at, but also produce a variety of high-quality wines. You can see this today during a wine tasting in the Bodega del Esteco estate. After an introduction about which wines the estate produces and a guided tour of the cellars, you will taste some wines together with other guests. How about a cycle icon, a mixture of Malbec and Merlot, of vine planted between 1958 and 1972, matured in oak barrels for 15 months? Or a don David Cabernet Sauvignon who matured for twelve months? If all these terms tell you little – don’t worry: The staff will be happy to introduce you to the basic concepts of wine tasting. For the rest of the day, for example, you can take a walk through the vineyards and cool down in the pool. Overnight in Cafayate.

On your way back to Salta, another natural spectacle awaits you today: You cross the Quebrada de las Conchas, the gorge of shells in German. In the approximately 75 kilometers long section of the valley of the Rio Guachipas stretches a bizarre landscape with unusually shaped, different colored rock formations, which quite rightly counts as a UNESCO World Heritage site. With a bit of luck you will discover herds of wild goats that climb exceptionally handsome over the rocks. In the afternoon, when the sun begins to sink slowly and the rocks dip into warm light, the gorge of shells is uniquely beautiful. Particularly worth seeing is the rock cauldron the Garganta del Diablo (devil's veins), which has a very special acoustic due to its narrow shape. In the evening you will return to Salta. Overnight in Salta.

By domestic flight, your journey today takes you about a thousand kilometers to the southwest. Here lies Mendoza, the capital of the province of the same name, which is known as a wine-growing region just like Salta. However, your journey will take you about eighty kilometers south into the Uco Valley. It is framed by the mountain peaks of the Kordillera, between numerous, partly centuries-old wineries and fruit trees flow clear streams. In this picturesque setting is your accommodation for the next few nights. The Vines Resort & Spa Hotel in the middle of a 670-hectare estate with vineyards. Here rustic charm meets modern elegance: from the pool you can look at endless rows of vines, behind which snow-capped peaks inherit. Your villa is comfortably furnished with wood and natural stone and offers a spa-quality bathroom on over 180 square meters, a private terrace and a fully equipped kitchen. The hotel restaurant was named one of the best hotel restaurants by the Condé Nast Traveler’s Hot List 2014. Here much is traditionally prepared over open fire. After a hearty dinner, you can watch from the campfire as the stars rise above the valley – hardly anywhere else is Western romance so luxurious. Overnight in the Uco Valley.

At the end of your trip, treat yourself to two quiet days in the middle of beautiful surroundings. Your resort offers the best conditions for relaxation: let yourself be pampered in the in-house spa, enjoy the view of the winery and mountains on a couch by the pool or from the hot tub – or set out to explore them. The staff will be happy to introduce you to the secrets of wine growing and the winery. Under the guidance of the gauchos, you can take rides or discover the surrounding nature on various hiking trails. Those who are best able to relax actively can let off steam in the well-equipped open-air fitness area or climb the in-house climbing wall. After exercising in the fresh air, the hearty Argentine cuisine, accompanied by a good wine, tastes even better. Overnight in the Uco Valley.

Today, say goodbye to Argentina: you will be taken to the airport and go home via Buenos Aires.

Optional extension: The coast of Puerto Madryn. Our insider tip for you: Instead of flying back directly to Buenos Aires, you can make a stopover in Puerto Madryn. Here you stay in a pretty lodge with gables and turrets just not far from that of a long coastal strip, where a variety of fascinating animals frolic: whales run their courses through the sea, on the rocks lie sea lions and elephants, in the surrounding steppes live pampashas and wild sheep. Enjoy once again the unique nature of Argentina before you go home.

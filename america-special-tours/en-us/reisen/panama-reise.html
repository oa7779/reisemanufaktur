Panama — A Beautiful Discovery

Exclusive Panama round trip with Latin America expert América Special Tours. Individual and customized in the heart of Munich. Ask now and experience for yourself.

7 days

Your trip idea

A sample itinerary—tailored to you

Get to know the paradisiacal little country of Panama, which has a lot to offer with the lively metropolis of Panama City, dreamlike tropical islands and landscapes and the friendly, fun-loving inhabitants, the Panamanians. On this trip you will discover one of the last untouched pieces of jewelry in South America, which will enchant you with its breathtaking variety of flora and fauna. Climb treetops and swing from treetop to treetop like Tarzan, enjoy the full-bodied Panamanian coffee and sail to secluded, paradisiacal beaches at the end of your trip.

Trip Information

Itinerary

7 Days Panama City – Boquete – Bocas del Toro

Your Trip Highlights

  • Private, German-speaking tour guide and own chauffeur
  • Carefully selected hotels for you
  • Capital of the Jewel: Panama City
  • Helicopter flight with unforgettable views
  • Exciting tropical migration
  • World famous waterway: shipping to the Panama Canal
  • Tarzan experience over the treetops
  • Aroma and Crema: The Land of Coffee
  • By boat through crystal clear water and to white sandy beaches

Your journey starts in the eponymous capital of Panama, where you will already be expected at the airport by your German-speaking tour guide and chauffeur. Receive your luggage, leave the airport and let the bright, warm sun shine on your skin outside - Welcome to Panama! Your luxurious accommodation for the next two nights is located in the state-of-the-art heart of Panama City. From here you have the best starting point for an exploration of the vibrant capital. Overnight in Panama City.

In the morning you will start to visit the fascinating capital of Panama. The gorgeous backdrop of skyscrapers, noble villas and green hills will delight you. At the beginning of the tour, visit the ruins of Panamá Viejo (“Old Panama”). Here are numerous remains of the pre-Columbian era of the country, which today are under the protection of UNESCO. Already in earlier times, the city had enormous importance as an intercontinental hub. Afterwards we will go to the old town “Casco Antiguo”. Here you have the opportunity to admire unique buildings of the 17th and 18th centuries. Afterwards, an absolute highlight is on your daily schedule: At the national airport, a helicopter awaits you, which will show you the exciting capital from above. From here you also have the best view of the probably most famous waterway, the Panama Canal. In the evening you will also experience the romantic old town at night. In an exclusive restaurant, you will be enchanted by the aromas of Panamanian cuisine. Overnight in Panama City.

Today, leave Panama City behind for now. Together with your guide, you will make your way to the nearby Soberanía National Park. Here you will get to know the diverse tropical flora on a leisurely hike and your guide will tell you one or the other detail about the exotic plants. With a little luck, you can even observe some animal species. Afterwards, drive to the port of Gamboa. With a small boat you cruise about two and a half hours on the Gatún Lake. You will admire the biodiversity of the lake, because after all, the meaning of the Indian word Panama is “rich in plants and fish”. Here you will also encounter some ships that are just crossing the Panama Canal. The channel runs through Lake Gatún and is the heart of the Panama Canal. On the largest island of the lake, Barro Colorado Island, is probably the most famous research station in the tropics. A paradise for tropical explorers. After a short domestic flight you will arrive at your next travel station, Boquete. Overnight in Boquete.

An adventurous day awaits you today. During an exciting ride on steel ropes, make it like Tarzan and “swing” from treetop to treetop. For this purpose, you will of course be provided with special equipment, as well as a detailed instruction by trained guides who will give you assistance and pay attention to your safety. After this experience, you will have some time to get used to the solid ground under your feet again. Then explore the nearby village before moving up to Ross in the afternoon. During a ride you will enjoy the fresh Panamanian mountain air, which has its very own character. It quickly becomes clear why this region is also called the land of coffee and flowers. You will have your dinner in a restaurant of the village. Here you have the opportunity to talk to locals and at the same time to taste the delicious Panamanian food. Overnight in Boquete.

In the morning we will visit a coffee plantation. Accompanied by your guide, you will be informed about the cultivation, harvesting and processing of the coffee fruit. So you will learn first hand what makes the coffee of Panama so aromatic and popular. At the end of the tour, you have the opportunity to taste the freshly brewed coffee and get your own impression. After lunch, we will take a trip over the Cordillera de la Talamanca towards the Atlantic coast to Almirante. From the port of Chiquita, a water taxi will first take you to Isla Colón, from there you will be taken to the Red Frog Beach Resort. Overnight in Bocas del Toro.

Today we go by boat to Laguna Bocatorito, the home waters of a dolphin colony. Here you admire the graceful animals and watch them play. After that, the drive continues to the most beautiful beaches of Bocas del Toro, where you can expect crystal clear, turquoise water and white sand beaches. Verily Paradise! In the afternoon, visit Coral Cay, a true dream for those who like to snorkel. Overnight in Bocas del Toro.

Today, unfortunately, you say goodbye to Panama: you will be taken to the airport and take your flight home via Panama City. Optional extension: Every goodbye is difficult. Hesitate a little while and experience the truly dreamlike home of the Gunas. With the sailing ship you will get to know some of the 365 islands of the San Blas archipelago and the interesting culture and traditions of the local people. The small islands are among the most colorful and beautiful in the country and invite you to relax, snorkel and swim.

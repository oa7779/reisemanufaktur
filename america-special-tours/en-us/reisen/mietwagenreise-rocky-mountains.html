US Road Trip — Geysers, Mountains, and Bears in Yellowstone & the Rockies

USA Car Rental Tour: Discover the beauty of the Rocky Mountains and Yellowstone National Park on this personalized tour of América Special Tours!

16 days

The Rocky Mountains are considered the most legendary mountains in the USA and attract travelers from all over the world with dramatic mountain landscapes and unique panoramic backdrops. As part of your rental car trip, you will not only experience Rocky Mountain National Park but also the oldest and perhaps most famous national park in the country with Yellowstone National Park. Look forward to natural beauties like geysers, mountains and canyons. You will travel from Salt Lake City to Denver and experience the already mentioned highlights as well as other national parks, authentic cowboy flair in the Wild West and an impressive variety of animals. Enjoy breathtaking nature and an exciting cultural heritage in the shadow of the Rockies!

Trip Information

Itinerary

16 Days Salt Lake City - Dutch John - Jackson - West Yellowstone - Cody - Clearmont - Keystone / Mount Rushmore - Cheyenne - Estes Park - Denver

Welcome to Salt Lake City, the capital of Utah. Framed by mountains, the city welcomes you to a relaxed atmosphere. Upon arrival, you’ll discover why Salt Lake City has recently been named the most laid-back city in the US. Services and amenities are always nearby, the streets easy to navigate, public transport on time and an extensive bicycle program allows you to drive through the city center. The city is ideal as a starting point for your rental car trip in the shadow of the Rocky Mountains. Within a few hours you can reach the most important national parks in the region. Overnight in Salt Lake City.

For lovers of dinosaurs, today’s travel day starts with a highlight. Visit the Dinosaur National Monument, which stretches across the mountains, plateaus and deserts of northeast Utah and northwest Colorado. Looked at on a map, it looks like a triangle and has remote resemblance to the huge footprint of a dinosaur. Visit this place, which boasts more than 1,500 dinosaur bones at the original site – not just for viewing, but even touching. Before you head to your hotel, a stay in Vernal is recommended. The mountain ranges in the background make the lush landscape consisting mainly of corn fields and sunflowers look beautiful. Especially at sunset an unforgettable experience. Annually, festivals as well as rodeos and smaller live music concerts take place here. In winter, Vernal is brimming with snow and is great for cross-country skiing and snowmobiling. If it always takes you outside in your free time, you will feel at home here. The Uinta Mountains offer a wide range of mountain biking. In the rivers and lakes in the region, the days can be spent on boat trips, whitewater rafting and kayaking. Overnight in Dutch John.

Today you are heading for Jackson Hole. Welcome to Wyoming – the Wild West is waiting for you! Founded in 1897, Jackson is located at the south entrance of the Grand Teton National Park and has retained its Wild West flair to this day. Interesting are the four gates of the city park, which are made from hundreds of antlers. With a carriage you can enjoy a short round trip here. The historic town is a wonderful starting point to visit the surrounding national parks. Do you want to dress in style? Use the afternoon to search for cowboy hats and boots. Overnight in Jackson.

Today, Grand Teton National Park is a place of contrasts, where you can take a quiet walk on a nature trail or join a climbing expedition to a summit. Before exploring the park on your own in the afternoon, enjoy the morning on a raft ride on the water. On a peaceful raft ride on the Snake River, you’ll have the best opportunity to catch a glimpse of wildlife before the heat of the day sets in. Unlike on rafting tours, you have your hands free to watch the passing wildlife up close with binoculars. The knowledgeable guides will guide you through this unspoiled landscape, introducing you to the history, geology and wildlife of the region. On your trip you can observe beavers, cliff swallows, fish eagles and bald eagle. With a bit of luck, you’ll even catch a glimpse of a mule deer, moose, or even a grizzly or black bear. Along the Teton Park Road, there are various options for stops and hikes in the afternoon. The largest port of call is Jenny Lake. For a quieter day, the round hike to Taggart is recommended. The Snake River Overlook offers a beautiful view over the valley, mountains and lakes. Overnight in Jackson.

On the program of the next few days is the Yellowstone National Park, a true masterpiece of nature. The central part of the park lies on a wide, volcanic plateau with altitudes ranging from around 2,000 to 2,500 m above sea level, surrounded by the Rocky Mountains. The park is known for its geothermal features as well as the wide variety of animals. Around 200 geysers, 10,000 hot springs and numerous fumaroles (steam outlets) can be found here. The road network through Yellowstone Park is structured like an eight. There are countless natural wonders of various kinds along the road, which are absolutely worth a visit. They are usually equipped with a parking lot and well-developed paths, which can take between a few minutes to several hours. How many you visit and how long you stay will depend on your personal preference, condition, weather and road conditions. In any case, a stop in one of the visitor centers is recommended, not only for maps and toilets, but also for information about possible road closures, forest fires or the next eruption times of the surrounding geysers. Overnight in West Yellowstone.

Your day will be unique, because together with a knowledgeable guide you will also experience the valley off the beaten tourist path. Side by side, start your buggy tour along the National Elk Refuge and Antelope Flats. Drive deep into the Gros Ventre Valley to remote locations such as Gunsight Pass and Crystal Creek. A unique experience with river crossings and the observation of wild animals. Look for bison, antelope and moose and become part of the true wilderness of Wyoming. Overnight at West Yellowstone.

After yesterday’s adventure day, today you can enjoy Yellowstone National Park to your own taste. During your explorations, feel the power of nature and actively shape your free space. The following top highlights from north to south could be on your To Do list: Mammoth Hot Springs Terraces, Norris Geyser Basin, Grand Canyon of the Yellowstone, Midway Geyser Basin/Grand Prismatic Spring, Upper Geyser Basin / Old Faithful. The Hayden Valley is home to at least a few bison and wapiti deer throughout the year. Overnight at West Yellowstone.

After exciting days in Yellowstone National Park, you will embark on a journey into the world of Buffalo Bill. Once you arrive in Cody, immerse yourself in the world of the American Western hero – Buffalo Bill – known worldwide as the personification of the American Western. Cody is known for the Cody Nite Rodeo, which began in 1938 and is the longest running and only night rodeo in the world. As the rodeo capital of the world, Cody knows a thing or two about bull riding, barrel racing and rodeo clowns. Overnight in Cody.

Take the Shell Canyon and Granite Pass towards Sheridan today. On the way there is a trip to the Bighorn National Forest. The Bighorn Mountains are a mountain range east of the Rocky Mountains in the USA. The mountain range runs in a northwest-southeast direction in the north of the US state of Wyoming and in the south of Montana. In the Bighorn Mountains lies, among other things, the Medicine Wheel, a sacred place of various peoples of the prairie Indians. The area around Sheridan is characterised by impressive mountain landscapes, while the historic city center is the focal point for those interested in cowboy culture. Experience unforgettable moments with a unique cowboy flair! Overnight in Clearmont.

After exciting days, you can leave the car today and enjoy the day at your accommodation The Ranch at UCross. The historic ranch was built in 1902 in Victorian style and offers you a majestic view of the surrounding area, vast, unspoiled nature. Relax in the heated outdoor pool overlooking the surrounding landscape, as many Western writers, sculptors and artists have included in their work. For an active day, the ranch offers you opportunities for horseback riding, fishing, hiking, paddle boating or a game of horseshoe throwing. Overnight in Clearmont.

On your way to Keystone, three special stops await you. First, stop at Devils Tower National Monument, a giant monolith that resembles a fossilized tree stump. Located in the southwest of South Dakota, Rapid City awaits. After Sioux Falls, it is the second largest city in the state and the center of a mining, farming, grain cultivation and timber industry area. Rapid City was founded in 1876 when gold was found in the Black Hills. Here in the southwest of South Dakota lies the region that is one of the most historically significant of the Wild West era. You will continue and soon you will be able to catch the first glimpse of Mount Rushmore. High on the cliffs, in the center of South Dakota’s Black Hills, the gigantic busts of the four presidents Washington, Jefferson, Lincoln and Roosevelt have enthroned for more than half a century, overseeing the vast expanse of the Great Plains. While visiting one of the most recognizable landmarks in the US during the day, enjoy the starry sky at night with incredible views of the Milky Way – all from the comfort of your private tent. Experience glamping - stylish camping - by not sacrificing the luxury of a pleasant accommodation and at the same time enjoying a unique outdoor nature experience! Overnight in Keystone.

Your next destination is Cheyenne. On the road, it is recommended to spend the day in Custer State Park and Wind Cave National Park. There is a variety of attractions in Custer State Park. The Needles Highway takes you across forests and past candle straight towering granites. The road is about 22.5 km long. You always have the opportunity to stop at the roadside, the so-called pull-outs, to enjoy the surroundings. The park is home to about 1,500 bison. In addition, in the park live elk, deer, antelopes, coyotes, bighorn sheep, mountain goats and the hardly shy, small donkeys. South of Custer State Park, head to Wind Cave National Park. Among them are the 114 km of galleries, the seventh longest cave in the world. The Wind Cave belongs to a huge cave system that is located under the Black Hills. In the Wind Cave is a labyrinth of unusual, honeycomb-shaped formations (boxwork) and popcorn-like formations. Your day ends in Cheyenne, Wyoming’s largest city. Ever since its founding, the city has been a trading center; at first it was like a Wild West movie, then it became a little more civilised and it has matured into the capital of the state. Overnight in Cheyenne.

For you today, head to Estes Park, the eastern gateway to Rocky Mountain National Park. Without a doubt, Rocky Mountain National Park is one of Colorado’s greatest and most breathtaking natural wonders. About a third of the park is located above the tree line, where tundra dominates. The special features of the park are the differences in flora and fauna at the different altitudes. Experience unique adventures today and tomorrow, stopping at various viewpoints such as the Forest Canyon Overlook or following the Tundra National Trail, a small circular trail. Pass by the Trail Ridge High Point, where the landscape resembles the Arctic. A winding road leads over the 10 Milner Pass, where a sign draws attention to the Continental Divide. The continental watershed runs from north to south through the Rocky Mountains. The water on the western side of the mountains flows into the Pacific Ocean, rivers on the eastern side flow into the Atlantic Ocean. Overnight in Estes Park.

Your day is all about the most legendary mountains in the USA. You and the mountains are in the center of attention! Enjoy the fresh air and unique panoramic views from the top of the world. The park with its 107,000 hectares holds dramatic landscapes and more than 500 kilometers of hiking trails a wide selection of active design options for you. Overnight in Estes Park.

Your journey is coming to an end and so you head towards Denver. Denver is called the Mile High City because it is exactly 1 mile (1,609 m) high. It is the commercial, manufacturing, financial and transportation center of an important ranch and mining region in the Rocky Mountains. The city center of Denver between Civic Center and Union Station is very clean and well maintained and well to explore on foot. There are numerous shops, restaurants and bars. Among the numerous attractions of the city is the Corinthian-style State Capitol. Overnight in Denver.

Even the most beautiful trip comes to an end and so it is today to say goodbye to the impressive nature and animal experiences around the Rocky Mountains. Even if your holiday is over, the precious memories, beautiful photos and exciting stories of your trip will accompany you forever. Look forward to letting your friends and family share your experiences in the distance at home. You drive to the Denver airport and drop off your rental car there.
